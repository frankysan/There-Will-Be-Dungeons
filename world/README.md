## A few major source distinctions

**Played event vs report:** At E048 **Tedna and Bok Bok** are rescued alive. **Tedna dies E050**, while his report that **Angelashi** was killed proves mistaken when she is **found alive and freed E054**. [E048](../episodes/E048.md), [E050](../episodes/E050.md), [E054](../episodes/E054.md).

**What the GM later explained:** Beau's post-arc Q&A says **Buttons loved Stanley** and intended to take him to the Arcanum to free him from Diontalus. That is **retrospective motive explanation**, not an E049 dialogue the party heard. [Bonus dossier](../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md).

**Later Razak Val status, before E064:** **Orp dies and Vorel is acclaimed Great Fang E062; Zercil dies E063.** [E063](../episodes/E063.md) begins screening **200–300 prisoners**, not an already completed mass release, and a **worm is captured from Zercil's bed**. [Razak Val](places/razak-val.md) · [chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md). **E064 outbreak:** widespread mutations threaten the valley; **Omnom remains behind with the Necklace of Fireballs**. **E065:** Rorf **confesses Bramie's killing**, while the party shelters in Fate 4 during an extreme rainstorm and a **T. rex arrives**. [E064](../episodes/E064.md) · [E065](../episodes/E065.md). **E066:** Fate 4 is **destroyed**; **Rorf and Follow die**, and **Sira is abducted**. **E067:** a spectral dragon describes a hypothetical **planet-relocation machine**, and the party tunnels into **Kervalon's basement**. **E068:** the party defeats a mind-flayer-like body and the exposed riflemen, but follows worms to **concealed metal doors with a biting control panel**. **E069–E071** enter the [CHIDS](places/chids.md) complex, where Nash and Hope use illithid corporate headsets, later meet [Alvert](characters/alvert.md), and reach the icy data-center vent. **E072–E073** introduce the withered elf [Cerinthala](characters/cerinthala.md), a **dangerous +1 AC curse binding Stanley**, ice-construct attackers, and a **biological laboratory with a stalled elevator**. **E074–E075** defeat the spider matriarch and **memory-eating red mass**, while **Cerinthala dies, revives and betrays the party**. The group acquires a **[paired portal device](technology/portal-device.md)** and **Vorel faces charging bugbears**, with **portal escape still unplayed**. **Sira remains missing; actual Traversal Drive access unresolved.** [E066](../episodes/E066.md) · [E067](../episodes/E067.md) · [E068](../episodes/E068.md) · [E069](../episodes/E069.md) · [E070](../episodes/E070.md) · [E071](../episodes/E071.md) · [E072](../episodes/E072.md) · [E073](../episodes/E073.md) · [E074](../episodes/E074.md) · [E075](../episodes/E075.md) · [Kervalon](places/kervalon.md).
