# The Wastes — world and character reference (seed edition)

**Coverage:** E000–E001 pilot references plus provisional episode dossiers through **E054** (including **E013.5**). **E043 is a separate holiday one-shot, not a chapter of the Wastes campaign**; the main campaign resumes E042 → E044. The [March 2019 Q&A bonus](../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md) follows E054 as **non-narrative retrospective commentary**, not corrected Wastes E055. **Episode-event spoilers through E054**, but the separate Discord spelling register and the name-evidence appendices can contain **later-campaign name spoilers**. No new passage has been listener-proofread. The machine-readable `entities.json` is still an E000–E001 seed, not a complete index of the added episodes.

This is a **reference for consistency work**, not a complete or independently verified setting encyclopedia. Statements are sourced to approximate timecodes in the supplied podcast transcripts, which themselves still contain ASR errors. Later episodes can confirm, qualify, or contradict these entries.

## Evidence labels

- **GM exposition** — Beau narrating the campaign's circumstances or describing scenes.
- **Character statement/backstory** — a player's character or introduction declaring something (may reflect belief, unreliable memory or aspiration).
- **Session event** — an action described as occurring in the actual-play session.
- **Listener-confirmed** — verified by archive owner; currently limited to identified diarization/timing/omission issues.
- **Spelling pending** — recognized phonetically, but orthography not yet independently corroborated.

See the [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md). Episode/time references are navigational and approximate.

## External reference warning

The linked D&D Beyond sheets are the characters' **end-of-campaign** states, per archive-owner confirmation. See [external sources and chronology policy](../project/EXTERNAL_REFERENCES.md) before incorporating either source into episode-era facts.


**Visual source additions (2026-10-21):** See the [85-file wiki-sourced image inventory](../project/IMAGE_ASSETS.md), which includes a two-page **Kristen Ashton** Fate 4 creator design, a separate RV floorplan and two **undated or later** maps. The regional map provides **candidate written place names** but must **not** be mistaken for the E005 player handout; the E011 Slavetown street map remains unlocated. Image reuse permissions are unverified and artwork can spoil later campaign content. The images did not retroactively verify any transcript.

**Special chronology:** E013.5 was released **between E013 and E014**, but the Hope/Vorel scene it adds belongs in-story **around E008–E009** while Nash and Stanley were underground. Its **Stanley retrospective narration** and newly disclosed **Hope time-travel/dark-pact objective** must be indexed by both *release position* and *in-world scene position*. See [supplemental dossier](../episodes/E013.5.md) and [chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md); neither is listener-audio-verified yet.

**E015–E016 interpretive boundaries:** [E015](../episodes/E015.md) **stages Tylander's death** and the disappearance of a purported mine deed, whereas [E016](../episodes/E016.md) contains **the magistrate's later report** that he is active again; the party does not independently see him returned. E016's **Black Mine/999th level** lead differs from the disputed earlier mine deed, and **Salivating Fields evacuation** does not release Dusthill captives. **Vorel's Fuxi ritual vision** and **Nash's private “Mother” voice/dragon-associated fire** are distinct simultaneous threads whose metaphysics are unresolved. Neither episode has been audio-proofread.

**E017–E018 evidence note:** These newly indexed [E017](../episodes/E017.md) and [E018](../episodes/E018.md) dossiers distinguish Nash's **Pixie Blush hallucinations** from GM-observed **Slavetown fire**, **Nash's staff nail “Freedom”** from the E014 **Freedom firearm**, the **Black Mine guards' slave-uprising report** from the party's **directly witnessed undead**, and the **E018 cliffhanger** from the next episode's combat. They follow the owner's **corrected numbers** while leaving owner spellings [noncanonical](../project/OWNER_EPISODE_NOTES.md) and processed SRTs unaltered.

**E019–E021 evidence boundaries:** The [E019](../episodes/E019.md) undead elevator battle concludes in an **E019 rockfall**, whose consequences and **Tickles's death** are resolved in [E020](../episodes/E020.md). That episode identifies **Tylander's handwritten diary**, *not* the nearby old skeleton, and ends in **magical darkness**. [E021](../episodes/E021.md) introduces **Hope's private “find me” voice**, Dorothy's door, Tylander's reported history and admission about his undead workers, and **two distinct Catherine-related manifestations**: his disguise and the wounded person who identifies herself as Catherine. The final winged figure's precise identity remains **unverified**; use neither later wiki artwork nor the owner's provisional spellings to declare it. All three dossiers are **machine-transcript-only**.

[E024](../episodes/E024.md) contains **GM-confirmed dragon-heart mechanics** for Nash and Soulbroker **artifact/strategic claims** that still need checking; it also resolves the **E021 locked chest in E024** while leaving **Dorothy's smoldering exit and the original mine deed** unresolved. These are provisional processed-SRT references, not audio-verified canon or updated `entities.json`.

**E025–E027 continuity:** [E025](../episodes/E025.md) resolves the **Black Mine physical exit** (while Dorothy remains audible), [E026](../episodes/E026.md) reveals **Bertrand, Trigger and Bok Bok captive** and restores **Fate 4**, and [E027](../episodes/E027.md) introduces the **Umbilical Order**, young **Tedna** and **Diptak's lizardfolk challenge**. Distinguish the party's observed violence from Bertrand's and the sightless orc's **interested accounts**, and keep the challenge outcome for later episodes. **E028–E030 chronology:** [E028](../episodes/E028.md) resolves Diptak's challenge and **makes Buttons's first appearance**, then ends with Hope drawn from camp. **E034–E036 continuity:** [E034](../episodes/E034.md) features **Diontalus explicitly identifying himself as the alternate controller of Stanley's body**, acknowledging **Stanley's refusal and forced union**, and the party's **Gray Socks practice**, not the actual match. **E037–E039 chronological caution:** [E037](../episodes/E037.md) contains **Vorel's buried heist payment**, **Nash/Angel Ashi private magic**, **Charlemagne's evacuation**, **Tedna's partial keep letter**, **Monsoon's first marked cave route and armory**, **and the rocket firing/returned blast at the episode close**. [E038](../episodes/E038.md) contains **the blast's aftermath**, **Blunt Goliver's beheading**, and **Hope's limb amputations**; [E039](../episodes/E039.md) reveals **her functioning prosthetics** and Monsoon's broader **Lactid, Andreeste, Tiat Cala and Gem of Tides** claims, alongside the official **Stanley/Dion wanted announcement**. Do not confuse the **source character's confession of ordering caregivers killed** with audio-verified independent forensics. All dossiers remain processed-SRT-based with noncanonical owner spellings.

**E040–E042 source boundaries:** [E040](../episodes/E040.md) leaves **Twenty-Six Block**, follows **Dion/Stanley's magical Teet/Guzzle disguises** and a failed stealth crossing under keep spotlights, and ends running toward the **Pit of Despair** after a major street fight—not safe inside the keep. [E041](../episodes/E041.md) descends into the barred pit, **directly encounters the froghemoth**, brings **George the Teet Guard** with the group, opens a rusted **side-crevasse exit** and takes **one unidentified glowing sphere** below ground; **Stanley argues against killing Buttons**. [E042](../episodes/E042.md) **cuts the sphere and keeps only its dark core**, records an **Infernal wall inscription delivered privately to Hope**, finds a potentially valuable **old supply cache**, and defeats **two black puddings**, after which **George shoots his own foot**. A supposed singing cave “king” is **not seen or identified by E042 cutoff**. None of these three episodes completes a Teet keep entry, captive rescue, wedding or Gem of Tides recovery. All claims remain **processed-SRT-based, not listener verified**, and source subtitles are preserved unchanged.

**E043–E045 special and campaign boundaries:** [E043](../episodes/E043.md) is a **four-hour holiday one-shot**, with **Jon/John DM**, Beau/Kyle/Kristen playing fictionalized selves, and Scott absent from live play. Its Christmas ghosts, magical house and Vorel action figure are **not Wastes-setting evidence**. The campaign resumes **directly after E042** in [E044](../episodes/E044.md): **George is alive and treated**, the E042 wooden stick becomes a **Wand of Scowls** passed to Nash, and the group meets a **giant self-described river king, Hungui** (name to verify), accepts his conditional **“headache man”** task and recovers **seven magical beans and a skeleton-marked key**. Just before camping **Stanley reveals “Stanley Billings” is a name he chose**, not his original identity. In [E045](../episodes/E045.md), **Dion takes control of the shared body and murders George**, after which the group kills a **basilisk**, sees a hostile **wall shadow**, discovers a **horned tiefling skeleton with privately provided writing**, and encounters/kills a **dark, headache-causing river creature**. Nash's previously pocketed **black core becomes alive and is destroyed**. The group **has not returned to the river king, reached the keep or recovered the Gem of Tides**. These are **processed-SRT-only provisional dossiers**, not audio-confirmed, and structured `entities.json` remains an early seed.


**Cast-written spelling evidence (2026-10-21):** See the [Discord report](../reports/DISCORD_SPELLING_2026-10-21.md), [spelling/consistency ledger](CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md) and [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md). **Diontalus** is **accepted creator-authoritative orthography** on Jon's two messages, while `Dion Talus`/`Deontalus` remain ASR search aliases; no words were audio verified by the report. **Nash Maggard**, **Stanley Billings**, **Teet**, **Slavetown**, **Talia**, **Tedna**, **Pentael Wise** and Kyle's item-title **The Eye of Groxl** have cast-written backing; the **archive owner has now confirmed** that `Grojil`/`Groxel` refer to this **same item** and **Groxl** is canonical. Other claims and identity matches still require episode evidence. **Tietkalla** is a working cast-supported form, not an automatic rewrite of every `Tiat Cala` cue. **Angel Ashi/Angelashi** and **Catoblepraithe/Catobelpraithe** remain open variants. Inverse/Delvers names from shared records do **not** belong to the Wastes reference just because the cast linked them.

**E046–E048 continuity (processed-SRT-only):** [E046](../episodes/E046.md) opens with **more headache-creatures attacking after E045's first kill**. A green beanstalk created with E044's recovered beans and skeleton key opens an **ancient tiefling complex**, while a brief return to the **froghemoth pit grate** confirms its threat remains. [E047](../episodes/E047.md) investigates a magic-laden library/office and **releases an elderly tiefling from a pictorial book**; **Nash** supplies Reduce while **Vorel** pulls him out. [E048](../episodes/E048.md) establishes the elder's claim that **Hope descends from an Akmenos royal family** and he has an army stored through a historic **Soulbroker** bargain. Those allegations, his **twenty-newborn sacrifice**, **Kefeli/Medusa** hypothesis, and **Nash-as-crystal** diagnosis are **NPC testimony**, not independently certified history. **The elder leaves alone through Château Weir**, with army retrieval pending. After a level-up/long rest **Stanley** returns to control and wants to help deliver a keepsake to George's family later. **Nash uses the robe's 24-foot ladder patch to reach the keep prison**, incapacitates two guards (later suffering severe pixie-blush reactions, **death unconfirmed**), and the party **frees Tedna and Bok Bok alive**. Tedna reports **Angelashi** was sent to the pit and allegedly fed to a monster, **not a verified death**. The **Gem of Tides and wedding/Jolt confrontation remain outstanding**. All new details are scene-time-qualified in the [chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md), [ledger](CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md) and [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).

**E049–E051 continuity (processed ASR only):** [E049](../episodes/E049.md) shows the **Buttons/Jolt/Monsoon** betrayal and forced **P0D transformation**; **Vorel kills Buttons**, while P0D and Jolt survive that episode. [E050](../episodes/E050.md) **Diontalus kills P0D** against Nash's wish; **Tedna dies** after E048's successful rescue and temporary stabilization, **Jolt and Dashington are killed**, and the party **recovers Gem of Tides** plus **Fox and Scully/Smokey's belt** for Hope. **Vorel proposes Pillars of Decency**. The party **reaches the old crypt/pool chamber** and encounters **Kefeli**. [E051](../episodes/E051.md) shows **Vorel and Hope both petrified then restored alive** with an improvised **basilisk/eyeball remedy**, and **Kefeli petrified and destroyed** without releasing the other statues. Other **giant pixies invade** during the ongoing siege. **Buttons/P0D/Tedna/Jolt are dead by E051**, **Hope/Vorel live**, and the **Gem's final placement, rumored army and Principal's fate remain unresolved**. Character hallucinations do **not** count as independent proof of a steel afterlife; Kefeli's history remains self-interested testimony. [Review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
