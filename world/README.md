## Evidence labels

- **GM exposition** — Beau narrating the campaign's circumstances or describing scenes.
- **Character statement/backstory** — a player's character or introduction declaring something (may reflect belief, unreliable memory or aspiration).
- **Session event** — an action described as occurring in the actual-play session.
- **Listener-confirmed** — verified by archive owner; currently limited to identified diarization/timing/omission issues.
- **Spelling pending** — recognized phonetically, but orthography not yet independently corroborated.

See the [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md). Episode/time references are navigational and approximate.

## External reference warning

The [fan wiki](https://twbd.fandom.com/wiki/There_Will_Be_Dungeons_Wiki) is a useful spelling and continuity index, but contains campaign-wide spoilers and retrospective accounts. The linked D&D Beyond sheets are the characters' **end-of-campaign** states, per archive-owner confirmation. See [external sources and chronology policy](../project/EXTERNAL_REFERENCES.md) before incorporating either source into episode-era facts.


**Visual source additions (2026-10-21):** See the [85-file wiki-sourced image inventory](../project/IMAGE_ASSETS.md), which includes a two-page **Kristen Ashton** Fate 4 creator design, a separate RV floorplan and two **undated or later** maps. The regional map provides **candidate written place names** but must **not** be mistaken for the E005 player handout; the E011 Slavetown street map remains unlocated. Image reuse permissions are unverified and artwork can spoil later campaign content. The images did not retroactively verify any transcript.

**Special chronology:** E013.5 was released **between E013 and E014**, but the Hope/Vorel scene it adds belongs in-story **around E008–E009** while Nash and Stanley were underground. Its **Stanley retrospective narration** and newly disclosed **Hope time-travel/dark-pact objective** must be indexed by both *release position* and *in-world scene position*. See [supplemental dossier](../episodes/E013.5.md) and [chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md); neither is listener-audio-verified yet.

**E015–E016 interpretive boundaries:** [E015](../episodes/E015.md) **stages Tylander's death** and the disappearance of a purported mine deed, whereas [E016](../episodes/E016.md) contains **the magistrate's later report** that he is active again; the party does not independently see him returned. E016's **Black Mine/999th level** lead differs from the disputed earlier mine deed, and **Salivating Fields evacuation** does not release Dusthill captives. **Vorel's Fuxi ritual vision** and **Nash's private “Mother” voice/dragon-associated fire** are distinct simultaneous threads whose metaphysics are unresolved. Neither episode has been audio-proofread.

**E017–E018 evidence note:** These newly indexed [E017](../episodes/E017.md) and [E018](../episodes/E018.md) dossiers distinguish Nash's **Pixie Blush hallucinations** from GM-observed **Slavetown fire**, **Nash's staff nail “Freedom”** from the E014 **Freedom firearm**, the **Black Mine guards' slave-uprising report** from the party's **directly witnessed undead**, and the **E018 cliffhanger** from the next episode's combat. They follow the owner's **corrected numbers** while leaving owner spellings [noncanonical](../project/OWNER_EPISODE_NOTES.md) and processed SRTs unaltered.

**E019–E021 evidence boundaries:** The [E019](../episodes/E019.md) undead elevator battle concludes in an **E019 rockfall**, whose consequences and **Tickles's death** are resolved in [E020](../episodes/E020.md). That episode identifies **Tylander's handwritten diary**, *not* the nearby old skeleton, and ends in **magical darkness**. [E021](../episodes/E021.md) introduces **Hope's private “find me” voice**, Dorothy's door, Tylander's reported history and admission about his undead workers, and **two distinct Catherine-related manifestations**: his disguise and the wounded person who identifies herself as Catherine. The final winged figure's precise identity remains **unverified**; use neither later wiki artwork nor the owner's provisional spellings to declare it. All three dossiers are **machine-transcript-only**.

**E022–E024 evidence:** [E022](../episodes/E022.md) distinguishes **Quizquaz, beholder and Soulbroker**, and **Vorel's magical charm** from conscious betrayal. [E023](../episodes/E023.md) records **Nash, Stanley and Hope signing** the 1,000-day/nights soul pact, **Vorel refusing**, and Hope's **private mother-dream**. [E024](../episodes/E024.md) contains **GM-confirmed dragon-heart mechanics** for Nash and Soulbroker **artifact/strategic claims** that still need checking; it also resolves the **E021 locked chest in E024** while leaving **Dorothy's smoldering exit and the original mine deed** unresolved. These are provisional processed-SRT references, not audio-verified canon or updated `entities.json`.

**E025–E027 continuity:** [E025](../episodes/E025.md) resolves the **Black Mine physical exit** (while Dorothy remains audible), [E026](../episodes/E026.md) reveals **Bertrand, Trigger and Bok Bok captive** and restores **Fate 4**, and [E027](../episodes/E027.md) introduces the **Umbilical Order**, young **Tedna** and **Diptak's lizardfolk challenge**. Distinguish the party's observed violence from Bertrand's and the sightless orc's **interested accounts**, and keep the challenge outcome for later episodes. No new audio verification or structured-entity reindexing was performed.
