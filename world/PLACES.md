## Travel/story sequence

**Dusthill** → desert pursuit and **Leopold facility** → captive transfer to **Slavetown** → storm and **Black Mine** → supernatural **Château Weir** → return through mine and **Liberators** → Golden Land challenge and **oil sands** → **Trashburg / Tietkalla keep** → underground library, prison, crypt and old throne → split froghemoth's **desert lower carcass** → **devastated Tietkalla** and Hope's accession.

This is **narrative order, not a surveyed map or complete physical road itinerary**. The [Arc One narrative](../narrative/ARC_ONE.md) expands the connections. The [episode chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md) gives time-based detail.

## Source and map cautions

- **Tietkalla** is strongly supported by **Jon's cast-written spelling**, but context matching to `Tiat Cala/Tiat Kala` ASR must be verified per referent. Names such as **Moag Ferhain**, **Esophageal Pass**, ancient town subdivisions and some towns on the E005 map are **wiki-backed or provisional**, not audio-reviewed fact.
- The source inventory includes **fan-wiki-sourced imagery and maps**, some with later-campaign annotations and uncertain licenses. Do not treat an undated map as the identical handout used in E005 or E011. [Asset register](../project/IMAGE_ASSETS.md).
- The associated **Angelashi death report E048** is about a person's assumed fate, not geographic proof she was killed in the Pit. She **is rescued alive E054**.
- **Cross-campaign:** The **Inverse / Delvers** settings and holiday one-shot are separate; the bonus Q&A is not a site visited in the story.
- Every minor place name and map transcription from the original episode-by-episode review remains accessible in the [archived place compendium](../research/legacy/2026-10-21/WORLD_PLACES.md) and [source dossiers](../episodes/E001.md). The short index does **not** declare them disproved.

## Third arc — Sigil (E082–E084)

**[Sigil / City of Doors](places/sigil.md)** becomes the directly visited setting after the shared E081 beam dissolution. In [E082](../episodes/E082.md), Vorel awakens alive below its **impossibly curving, roof-filled skyline**, Nash persists as a floating heart, and **Hope/Stanley are found as inert bodies**. A **Welcome Center** supplies meal/lodging vouchers, and the **Soggy Feather** becomes their base. In [E083](../episodes/E083.md), **Squirts leads them to a crypt-bearing trickster church that vanishes from view after exit**, the two friends' bodies are entrusted to its priest, a **Pelor-linked delegation** visits, and the group registers for marionette fighting at **76 Mulberry Lane**, then visits **Royce Gymnasium** and an **M-sports library**. **No verified portal home, completed resurrection, tournament match or prize occurs.** The [Sigil location profile](places/sigil.md) distinguishes witnessed features from **Binch/Bill/priest claims** about planes, petitioners, Lady of Pain and the Weir. **This is The Wastes, not Inverse.**

In [E084](../episodes/E084.md), the strategy-book theft starts in the **M-sports library**, and Squirts' **trickster church materializes in another neighborhood**, again vanishing from view after departure. The party returns to the **Soggy Feather**, where fixer **Pelvis** privately recruits Nothing. Nothing separately visits a **merchant-district shop at 47 Castamere/Castlemere Lane (?)**, using a pyramid-shaped device to enter a **real, anonymous portal meeting**. **The proposed future destination** is **10 Belshamer Lane (?) in the Hive** and an onward **Abyss portal**; **the kidnapping/delivery is not performed**. At the **Royce Gymnasium/Colosseum** qualification venue, security screens for magic, **Squirts is initially barred**, Nothing slips in during a disturbance, and **Vorel and Nash win first-round marionette matches** while **Nothing double-forfeits**. The **champion's public humiliation, any championship prize and Hope/Stanley's restoration remain pending**. All names and location relationships are [ASR-only](../reports/E084_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## Place and event boundaries

**Gordon Shuttle Bay** is described by Cerinthala but **not visited** in the reviewed E055–E081 episodes; **Elhandar** is the elf's desired refuge, **not a destination reached by the party**. A **space-like scene change** during Dolo Popio's battle is **visibly depicted**, but this alone does not prove CHIDS moved into literal outer space. The skeletal visitors' **warp coil**, CHIDS **portal-pair apparatus** and central **Traversal Drive** are separate objects. The [portal apparatus reference](technology/portal-device.md) records the red/green surface links and the necrotic beam.
