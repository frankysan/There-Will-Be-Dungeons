## Travel/story sequence

**Dusthill** → desert pursuit and **Leopold facility** → captive transfer to **Slavetown** → storm and **Black Mine** → supernatural **Château Weir** → return through mine and **Liberators** → Golden Land challenge and **oil sands** → **Trashburg / Tietkalla keep** → underground library, prison, crypt and old throne → split froghemoth's **desert lower carcass** → **devastated Tietkalla** and Hope's accession.

This is **narrative order, not a surveyed map or complete physical road itinerary**. The [Arc One narrative](../narrative/ARC_ONE.md) expands the connections. The [episode chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md) gives time-based detail.

## Source and map cautions

- **Tietkalla** is strongly supported by **Jon's cast-written spelling**, but context matching to `Tiat Cala/Tiat Kala` ASR must be verified per referent. Names such as **Moag Ferhain**, **Esophageal Pass**, ancient town subdivisions and some towns on the E005 map are **wiki-backed or provisional**, not audio-reviewed fact.
- The source inventory includes **fan-wiki-sourced imagery and maps**, some with later-campaign annotations and uncertain licenses. Do not treat an undated map as the identical handout used in E005 or E011. [Asset register](../project/IMAGE_ASSETS.md).
- The associated **Angelashi death report E048** is about a person's assumed fate, not geographic proof she was killed in the Pit. She **is rescued alive E054**.
- **Cross-campaign:** The **Inverse / Delvers** settings and holiday one-shot are separate; the bonus Q&A is not a site visited in the story.
- Every minor place name and map transcription from the original episode-by-episode review remains accessible in the [archived place compendium](../research/legacy/2026-10-21/WORLD_PLACES.md) and [source dossiers](../episodes/E001.md). The short index does **not** declare them disproved.

## Third arc — Sigil and Outlands (E082–E096)

**[Sigil / City of Doors](places/sigil.md)** becomes the directly visited setting after the shared E081 beam dissolution. In [E082](../episodes/E082.md), Vorel awakens alive below its **impossibly curving, roof-filled skyline**, Nash persists as a floating heart, and **Hope/Stanley are found as inert bodies**. A **Welcome Center** supplies meal/lodging vouchers, and the **Soggy Feather** becomes their base. In [E083](../episodes/E083.md), **Squirts leads them to a crypt-bearing trickster church that vanishes from view after exit**, the two friends' bodies are entrusted to its priest, a **Pelor-linked delegation** visits, and the group registers for marionette fighting at **76 Mulberry Lane**, then visits **Royce Gymnasium** and an **M-sports library**. **No verified portal home, completed resurrection, tournament match or prize occurs.** The [Sigil location profile](places/sigil.md) distinguishes witnessed features from **Binch/Bill/priest claims** about planes, petitioners, Lady of Pain and the Weir. **This is The Wastes, not Inverse.**

In [E084](../episodes/E084.md), the strategy-book theft starts in the **M-sports library**, and Squirts' **trickster church materializes in another neighborhood**, again vanishing from view after departure. The party returns to the **Soggy Feather**, where fixer **Pelvis** privately recruits Nothing. Nothing separately visits a **merchant-district shop at 47 Castamere/Castlemere Lane (?)**, using a pyramid-shaped device to enter a **real, anonymous portal meeting**. **The proposed future destination** is **10 Belshamer Lane (?) in the Hive** and an onward **Abyss portal**; **the kidnapping/delivery is not performed**. At the **Royce Gymnasium/Colosseum** qualification venue, security screens for magic, **Squirts is initially barred**, Nothing slips in during a disturbance, and **Vorel and Nash win first-round marionette matches** while **Nothing double-forfeits**. The **champion's public humiliation, any championship prize and Hope/Stanley's restoration remain pending**. All names and location relationships are [ASR-only](../reports/E084_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

In [E085](../episodes/E085.md), the action remains at **Royce Gymnasium/Colosseum**, where **Vorel becomes local qualifier champion** by defeating Nash and Ventro in played marionette bouts, and **qualifies for the next day's larger event at the Great Colosseum**. The latter venue is **announced, not yet visited**. A violent brawl at Royce ends when an **unidentified huge green woman-like manifestation appears and Ventro is taken through a pinhole in reality**. The force and destination are unknown. At the **Soggy Feather**, a celebratory crowd generates actual wages/tips for Nash and Nothing; Vorel **breaks an upper window escaping unwanted admirers** and later returns. **Two armed intruders then enter their sleeping room via a window**, but their attack is unresolved. Neither a completed resurrection nor an Abyss kidnapping occurs. See the [Sigil subject page](places/sigil.md) and [E085 comparison](../reports/E085_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

[E086](../episodes/E086.md) stays in **Sigil**: masked **Crimson Moon** assassins attack the **Soggy Feather suite** and vanish into dust when defeated, leaving a destroyed mattress and windows. The **Soggy Feather kitchen** becomes the site of the priest/Squirts' **performance-enhancing potion**, which Vorel drinks but does not test. Outside the inn, **Squirts nearly kills a young lizardfolk fan, then stabilizes him; the child flees alive**. The Arcane Spice sponsor leaves with its cape, then Vorel visits the **Great Bazaar**, is referred by rug merchant **Zarfur** to tailor **Lohar**, and receives a **free red Soggy Feather cape**. **Nothing leads Nash to a bazaar spell-key merchant**, who quotes school-based keys at **500gp, with conditional credit**; Nash buys none. The **Great Colosseum finals** remain a **scheduled next destination (3 p.m.), not visited during E086**. **Nash's older 25,000gp is in a Tietkalla vault**, not a Sigil banking visit. [Dossier](../episodes/E086.md) · [ASR report](../reports/E086_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

[E087](../episodes/E087.md) **actually visits the Great Colosseum** for the overall BMF championship (rather than E086's future scheduled venue). The **eight-player finals' lounge**, physician, security checkpoint, giant marionette arena, and **magical-item screening** are observed. **Vorel defeats Mind Master, advances when Tubbs 9K is disqualified, then defeats Kanja and receives 5,000gp and a trophy**. The priest's device displays **miniature operators inside Kanja's apparent body**; it happens here, but the body's true mechanism remains unverified. The **Soggy Feather** holds a second celebration, then provides **30 prepaid nights of lodging after Vorel pays Bill 100gp including repairs**; Bill proposes **selling/co-owning the inn** but neither occurs. Finally the party **sets out from the Soggy Feather toward Kidigree's hidden temple**, **not yet shown entering or performing a resurrection**. [E087](../episodes/E087.md) · [ASR report](../reports/E087_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

[E088](../episodes/E088.md) **actually revisits the shifting Chapel of Laughter and enters its Hall of Reflections**, where **Hope and Stanley emerge alive from ritual mirrors**, with **original inert bodies left on slabs**. A remote mirror shows **Mind Master with Crimson Moon enforcers threatening Kanja**; it does not show the earlier inn assassin commission. **Squirts remains in the church**; **Nothing guides the restored companions back to the Soggy Feather**, where Bill calls Sigil **the Cage** and recommends researching **portal ownership in the Clerk's Ward** for a possible route to Earth. **Clerk's Ward is planned, not visited**. At the **Great Bazaar** an old arcane bookseller redirects Vorel to **Large S's/Largess Appleton (?) magical showroom**, where an ethereal spectrometer is **borrowed for a test but not purchased**; Vorel **pays 1,250gp for an M-sports cabinet and Nash's skull arcane focus**. He visits tailor **Lohar** and orders three outfits at **30gp**, paid on deposit and delivery. The **professional 16-fighter cabinet is actually installed at the Soggy Feather**, and three outfits arrive, but **Vorel still does not buy the inn**. A proposed **eight-day rest for Nash** is not fully shown. [Episode dossier](../episodes/E088.md) · [ASR comparison](../reports/E088_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

**[E089](../episodes/E089.md)** actually visits the **Clerk's Ward Portal Regulatory Agency** and the **Dark Below**. An official locates one *recorded stable* Earth portal stewarded by **Tobacho Fleur (?) in Hive**, but supplies **no exact door/key or transit**. In the sewers the party recovers **Hope's Scully** and working **Weir-marked ethereal spectrometer**; rat-man **Java** traces her second gun to an associate of the **same Hive steward**. The **temporary purple door** appearing near umbrella **Barbara** is an **unknown destination**, not a verified Earth portal. **Neither Hive entry nor return to Earth occurs**. [Dossier](../episodes/E089.md).

[E090](../episodes/E090.md) **actually enters Hive by surface rickshaw**, through a **Harmonium Fifty-Seventh Legion checkpoint** with **gallows/prisoner cages** and an officer's threat to deny all future exit. It visits **Daddy Long Legs** (adult entertainment club; Hope gets directions from red tiefling **Spatulo**) then follows a **central-Hive frog-on-stick vendor** to a **burgundy alley door** and the **underground cantina of huge blue dragon Tobacho Fleur**. Vorel **pays 50gp** to intermediary **Jelver** to arrange an audience; **Hope gifts Scully to the dragon**. He promises **Earth-portal location/access only after an unspecified item-retrieval task**, **not yet explained**. [E091](../episodes/E091.md) continues **inside Tobacho's underground Hive compound**: Jelver leads the four through guarded corridors past a **brief sighting of Mind Master**, then uses a **rune circle** for a **magically staged troll test in apparent desert canyons**. ## Place and event boundaries

**Gordon Shuttle Bay** is described by Cerinthala but **not visited** in the reviewed E055–E081 episodes; **Elhandar** is the elf's desired refuge, **not a destination reached by the party**. A **space-like scene change** during Dolo Popio's battle is **visibly depicted**, but this alone does not prove CHIDS moved into literal outer space. The skeletal visitors' **warp coil**, CHIDS **portal-pair apparatus** and central **Traversal Drive** are separate objects.
