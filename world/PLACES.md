## Travel/story sequence

**Dusthill** → desert pursuit and **Leopold facility** → captive transfer to **Slavetown** → storm and **Black Mine** → supernatural **Château Weir** → return through mine and **Liberators** → Golden Land challenge and **oil sands** → **Trashburg / Tietkalla keep** → underground library, prison, crypt and old throne → split froghemoth's **desert lower carcass** → **devastated Tietkalla** and Hope's accession.

This is **narrative order, not a surveyed map or complete physical road itinerary**. The [Year One narrative](../narrative/YEAR_ONE.md) expands the connections. The [episode chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md) gives time-based detail.

## Source and map cautions

- **Tietkalla** is strongly supported by **Jon's cast-written spelling**, but context matching to `Tiat Cala/Tiat Kala` ASR must be verified per referent. Names such as **Moag Ferhain**, **Esophageal Pass**, ancient town subdivisions and some towns on the E005 map are **wiki-backed or provisional**, not audio-reviewed fact.
- The source inventory includes **fan-wiki-sourced imagery and maps**, some with later-campaign annotations and uncertain licenses. Do not treat an undated map as the identical handout used in E005 or E011. [Asset register](../project/IMAGE_ASSETS.md).
- The associated **Angelashi death report E048** is about a person's assumed fate, not geographic proof she was killed in the Pit. She **is rescued alive E054**.
- **Cross-campaign:** The **Inverse / Delvers** settings and holiday one-shot are separate; the bonus Q&A is not a site visited in the story.
- Every minor place name and map transcription from the original episode-by-episode review remains accessible in the [archived place compendium](../research/legacy/2026-10-21/WORLD_PLACES.md) and [source dossiers](../episodes/E001.md). The short index does **not** declare them disproved.

## New-arc places and routes — E055–E056

The city of [Tietkalla](places/tietkalla.md) has experienced **three months of actual partial reconstruction** and now includes **Vorel's Golden Sands inn/adventurers' guild**, a **Tidebearer Fishbird church**, and functioning **government court/medical work**. A cometlike event then causes **lethal falling stones**, and the **sewer network** becomes an emergency shelter route; the original **ancient laboratory** beneath the keep hosts Hope's ancestral ritual. [E055](../episodes/E055.md) · [E056](../episodes/E056.md).

Visiting dwarven trader **Joderty Strongnee (?)** aboard the **Diamond Stroke** describes **Esophageal Pass**, **Mogen Frogan**, claimed dwarven ruins and **Principle City**. These are **his reported travel descriptions**, not locations the party physically visits during E055–E056. [April 2019 bonus](../episodes/BONUS-2019-04.md) supplies GM naming inspiration for Mogen Frogan, not verified geographical coordinates.

## New place — Razak Val (E059–E065)

[Razak Val](places/razak-val.md) is a **lizardfolk valley settlement with many clans, cave dwellings, a scaling duel arena, priestly Tailway shrine and Cave Fengar**. **Orp** occupies the **Great Fang** title, holds living humans for food and **kills Diptak in E060**. Vorel's **formal challenge is accepted for the following morning**, but is **not yet fought by E060 cutoff**. Its **Council of Twelve** represents local belief and social institutions, not independently proved cosmology. **This is the E060 cutoff only**: by **E062 Vorel has killed Orp and become Great Fang**, and the Cave Fengar explosion has killed many named leaders. In E063 Zercil is killed, **a black worm is found in her bed**, and under the throne the party begins screening **200–300 starving captives** it intends to free. **E064**: many captives **transform into chicken lords**, the city is **engulfed in internal warfare**, and **Omnom stays behind**, so a completed release or extinction of every lizardfolk is not proven. **E065**: the travelers reach the **purple sands** in the occupied **Fate 4**; **Rorf confesses Bramie's murder**, and a torrential rainstorm interrupts the journey toward **Kervalon/Kaer Valon**. **The fate of Razak Val, Omnom and the Dinosaur approaching the RV remains open**. [E059](../episodes/E059.md)–[E065](../episodes/E065.md).

## New route — purple sands, strange forest and Kervalon (E065–E075)

The party continues through the **purple sands** toward the northern Zarks' **[Kervalon / Kaer Valon](places/kervalon.md)**, with an unusual **torrential storm** soaking the area. **Fate 4** is destroyed E066 in the T. rex encounter; Vorel builds a **Fang Fate Five** rickshaw but cannot use it in a subsequently grown **dense forest of black trees bearing living eyeballs**. A **village of skeleton-looking alleged space travelers** is encountered in this forest, and its inhabitants supply a **spectral carriage**. Their claims about other planets and a crashed ship are **unverified testimony**, not mapped terrestrial coordinates. [E066](../episodes/E066.md).

The carriage transports the party to **Kervalon's ruined keep** E067, where **a beam enters the fortress from above** and motionless **rifle-bearing lizardfolk stand along the battlements**. After a **spectral dragon claims an ancient human-built planet-moving machine lies below**, the party physically **tunnels into the keep's old basement**, fights an animated-skeleton-producing flesh monster and scouts an apparent armed lizardfolk ambush. No successful rescue of **Sira**, verification of the alleged machine or destruction of the demon commander by E067. [E067](../episodes/E067.md).

### Beneath Kervalon — magical shaft and concealed lower doors (E068)

In [E068](../episodes/E068.md), **Zark-associated but not independently verified riflemen** meet Vorel's challenge with **pulse-energy gunfire**. Nash walls some melee fighters into an unseen corridor; after the party defeats the exposed guards and an **illithid-looking body**, the prisoners' **knocking stops without proof of their deaths**. The room leads to a spiral staircase surrounding a **sky-to-ground shaft of exceptionally potent magic**. **A thrown stone and corpse disintegrate inside it** and **Hope's leg temporarily twists near it**; the party climbs to a rainy exterior spire and then returns below. **Little black worms emerge from a hidden floor seam** where Nash uncovers **two steel elevator-like doors** with an aggressive biting **button panel**. **The doors do not open by E068 cutoff**. The [August bonus](../episodes/BONUS-2019-08.md) records Beau's **intended deeper-room puzzle clue**, not an inspected machine. [Kervalon reference](places/kervalon.md).

### CHIDS — human-demon research facility beneath Kervalon (E069–E075)

After opening the biting **Door Authority** gate in [E069](../episodes/E069.md), the group descends via a ruined **45° funicular** into a **[Center for Human-Demon Interdisciplinary Studies (CHIDS)](places/chids.md)**. Preserved automated guide **Julia** displays a **blue/red/green rail network** connecting welcome/security, **living pods, food court, oxygen bar, laboratories, executive offices, Archives, data center, engineering** and other facilities. The **precise physical name/sign reading, line map and site layout are session-source-based**, not independently verified cartography. Ancient residents are mostly skeletons; **robotic systems and some transportation still work**. [E069](../episodes/E069.md).

In [E070](../episodes/E070.md), the **executive offices' old conference room** holds eight wired headsets, and Nash/Hope experience an **illithid corporate environment** where a worker named **Charles Dickens** alleges an ancient **Traversal Drive** can merge realms. Real security responds when they intrude. In [E071](../episodes/E071.md), the party travels the **blue route and green-line northern corridor on foot**, opens **Archives** with a credential and persuasion but finds its internal terminals **unpowered**, and chooses the separately located **data center** whose massive front gate is **encased in regenerating ice**. After failing to thaw the main entrance, **all four enter a small overhead vent**. **Alvert the imp stays outside**. The **data center and engineering are distinct unaccessed destinations**, and neither the drive nor Sira is found. [CHIDS detail](places/chids.md).

### Frozen server hall and experimental laboratory levels (E072–E073)

In [E072](../episodes/E072.md), the protagonists enter **CHIDS's data center** when a **frozen ventilation duct collapses**, and **Stanley's Feather Fall** lowers everyone into a gigantic server-farm room. **Computer racks, enormous refrigeration fans and frozen staff** surround a camp inhabited by **[Cerinthala](characters/cerinthala.md)** and her preserved companions **Toby, Carl, Gordon and Benson**. Cerinthala uses a **data pad to remotely unlock engineering access**, which the team **has not reached or tested physically**. Stanley accepts a **+1 AC totem necklace** for a best-effort promise to return her to **Elhandar**, which becomes a **choking/shocking curse** when ice creatures containing frozen bodies attack after a long rest.

In [E073](../episodes/E073.md), ice giants and **animated blue-eyed corpses** fight the group. **Toby still speaks from within the ice**; Cerinthala admits **predatory prior plans**, then guides the party to a **hydroponic Maltaweed room and locker area**. **Vorel melts ~25 feet of ice**, exposing maintenance tunnels beneath the facility with **red emergency light** and electrical/data conduits. A recovered **Tara Mebat/Mebatt ID** opens biological labs with **tall cylinders and active pink-purple fleshlike membrane** that **snaps Nash's staff**. Cerinthala calls these rooms part of old **lizardfolk creation and infernal-wave reproduction programs**, but these **origins and applications remain her statements**, not authenticated machinery. **Vorel refuses a proposed artificial offspring**. A **four-lift elevator bank** provides no safe descent: **one lift crashes**, another **stalls between floors**, and a **giant spider-like form approaches from above**. [CHIDS site](places/chids.md).

**Still unvisited:** the **Traversal Drive**, a confirmed shuttle launch site, and the biological facility's lowest sublevels; **Gordon Shuttle Bay** is only discussed as a possible route off Earth.

### Lower CHIDS armament halls and paired gateways (E074–E075)

**[E074](../episodes/E074.md):** The party descends from a stranded lift while fighting **brain-backed poisonous spiders**; **Hope eventually shoots the giant matriarch dead**, and **Vorel crafts shields from its carapace**. In the lab corridors, **responsive pink membrane** admits some party members, allows **Nash's singing passage**, but **crushes Cerinthala fatally**, leaving her body to be carried. Two **green-scaled energy-rifle lizardfolk** are killed. An illithid control-room operator **secretes a disk and summons a red many-faced memory-devourer**, inflicting memory penalties on Vorel and Nash; **Hope holds the illithid by its neck**.

**[E075](../episodes/E075.md):** The red mass also drains Stanley's memory and knocks him down; **Vorel destroys it**, dissolving its copies, and **Hope with dragon-Nash kills the operator**. Three black **headworms** are removed from that illithid and the riflemen. A second secure lab beyond the operator room contains a **sound-dampened chamber**, linked **buttons/valves/console**, and **one [red/green portal apparatus](technology/portal-device.md)**. **Cerinthala revives**, betrays the party and appears to form ice Toby from a blue gem; **Toby's forms are destroyed** and she **escapes toward the organic corridor**. A pair of compatible wall apertures is **actually demonstrated**; Vorel hands the device to **Hope**. A **bugbear militia and illithid** approaches, Nash's Fireball kills front ranks, and **Vorel's taunt triggers a charge**. **No completed portal escape or Traversal Drive encounter by episode end**.

**Other site details and limits:** [CHIDS](places/chids.md) · [Kervalon](places/kervalon.md) · [Red/green portal device](technology/portal-device.md). **Gordon Shuttle Bay remains only discussed**, and **Sira remains missing**.
