# Places and regions — The Wastes

Travel through the settlements, ruins, planes and distant destinations of The Wastes. Each place has its own inhabitants, history and changing place in the companions' journey.

## Sources and project notes

### Editorial boundary

**T'ietkalla** has an exact on-map spelling and **Tietkalla** has established cast-written support; retain both for search. **Khaervilon** and **Moag Ferhain** are also canonical map spellings; older Kervalon/Kaer Valon forms are source/legacy aliases, not another location. The mapped Moag Ferhain referent has not been proven identical to the underground ruins discussed near Dusthill. **Principal City** is an episode/world-supported city name, but is not independently verified as a printed label on the regional map; **the Principal's Lands** is the mapped territorial label, not a known city boundary. Related **Teet/Trashburg** do not always denote precisely the same administrative area. The large map also prints **the Principal's Lands**; The canonical antagonist is **the Principal**; the old `the-principle.md` path and historic `Principle` spelling remain compatibility/search aliases, not an alternate approved name. [New map source audit](../reports/WASTES_KNOWN_MAP_ORTHOGRAPHY_2026-10-27.md). **Dunebreaker** is not a contemporaneous Arc Five ship. The separate *Inverse* campaign and E043 holiday special are **not** physical regions in the Wastes timeline. Handout/map orthography may require checking against original imagery. [Image provenance](../project/IMAGE_ASSETS.md) · [Review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
