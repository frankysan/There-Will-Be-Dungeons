# Factions, institutions and supernatural powers

Discover the societies, institutions and supernatural powers of The Wastes: what they seek, how they act, and how their influence reaches the companions. Their alliances change across the campaign; wartime cooperation does not make distinct peoples or institutions a single faction.

[^factions-council-faces]: The [played S3E18 finale](../episodes/S3E18.md) shows a chamber of chrome faces on the Moon that identifies itself as ancient humanity's preserved collective, but its origin and claimed mission are the Council's own testimony. It physically forces Nash's chrome transformation and demands that he kill his friends. The companions destroy the **lunar installation**, not the Moon; no separately identified phylactery or remote backup is discovered. These events do not establish an identity with the **Council of Clowns** encountered through Sigil or the **CHIDS living-wall council**. [CHIDS](places/chids.md) · [Sigil](places/sigil.md).
[^factions-principal]: **S3E14–S3E15 historical cutoff:** The Principal returns in a weakened body under Vorel's conditional parole with five powered flight boards, joins the Worldbreaker battle and opens planar portals to save Hope; no settled pardon is then demonstrated. **Later played S3E16–S3E18:** he betrays the allies, his Earth body is destroyed by Vorel, and remaining chrome copies collapse. The separate lunar Council of Faces forcibly recreates Nash as a successor, but Nash destroys the facility with his companions' help and is later resurrected in his original flesh; the fate of every claimed regime agent/backup remains unverified. [Played finale](../episodes/S3E18.md).
[^factions-brett]: **Brett actually takes physical HAND OF VOICE via PRINCIPLE in S3E12 (then gives to female succubus); S3E13 ACTUALLY ACQUIRES NASH DRAGON-METAL HEART after nonresisted extraction, reconstructs own-flesh heart and teleports FOUR home to EARTH without new signed contract.** Claims divine ownership/hierarchy, cosmic payment and potential Earth healing are interested speaker testimony, not independently all achieved. **His “Brent” alias and bartender disguise are played.**
[^factions-soulbroker]: **S3E12 manifested SOULBROKER PHYSICALLY SLAIN by 3,280-Nash mass *Fireball*, GM affirms WEIR–EARTH CONNECTION SUNDERED.** But independent **WORLD-BREAKER EGG/SEED STILL in Earth S3E13 physically creating LAVA/PURPLE GEYSERS**, cosmic/demonic reach beyond link unresolved
[^factions-elhandari]: **S3E14–S3E15 historical cutoff:** Elhandari hold Solar Mines, with personnel guarding unactivated Ruby deep within the core and Admiral Chrysorius favoring the Worldbreaker battle before activation. Hope receives a +2 crossbow and Stanley receives gem-component bowls for *Heroes' Feast*. The canyon ambush is only prepared at the S3E15 cutoff, with severe lizardfolk losses following its S3E16 detonation. **Played S3E18:** Elhandari acknowledge Ruby's activation order, help evacuate and demolish the Council of Faces lunar facility, take part in postwar survivor arrangements and transport resettlers. Stanley later crosses a real Feywild portal from Mars with many Elhandari, while some remain on Mars or return toward Earth; no universal exodus or unified settlement is shown. [Finale](../episodes/S3E18.md).
