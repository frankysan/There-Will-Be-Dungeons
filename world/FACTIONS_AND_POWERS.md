## Sources and project notes

**The Wastes · selected sources through the played S3E18 finale.** Organizations and supernatural claimants are indexed here separately from people, places and possessions. **Rows explicitly dated S3E05–S3E09 remain historical battle cutoffs, not the latest outcome**; later S3E16–S3E18 material updates only the factions whose later actions are documented. Descriptions indicate *observed actions* or *attributed claims* rather than automatically certifying lore. Research is based on provisional processed transcripts. [^factions-brett]: **Brett actually takes physical HAND OF VOICE via PRINCIPLE in S3E12 (then gives to female succubus); S3E13 ACTUALLY ACQUIRES NASH DRAGON-METAL HEART after nonresisted extraction, reconstructs own-flesh heart and teleports FOUR home to EARTH without new signed contract.** Claims divine ownership/hierarchy, cosmic payment and potential Earth healing are interested speaker testimony, not independently all achieved. **His “Brent” alias and bartender disguise are played.**
[^factions-soulbroker]: **S3E12 manifested SOULBROKER PHYSICALLY SLAIN by 3,280-Nash mass *Fireball*, GM affirms WEIR–EARTH CONNECTION SUNDERED.** But independent **WORLD-BREAKER EGG/SEED STILL in Earth S3E13 physically creating LAVA/PURPLE GEYSERS**, cosmic/demonic reach beyond link unresolved

Old Prince alliance criticized by elder Amanthelodar, Stanley still Solely Prime.
