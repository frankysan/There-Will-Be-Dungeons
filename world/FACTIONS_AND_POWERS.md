# Factions, institutions and supernatural powers

Discover the societies, institutions and supernatural powers of The Wastes: what they seek, how they act, and how their influence reaches the companions. Their alliances change across the campaign; wartime cooperation does not make distinct peoples or institutions a single faction.

## Sources and project notes

**The Wastes · selected sources through the played S3E18 finale.** Organizations and supernatural claimants are indexed here separately from people, places and possessions. **Rows explicitly dated S3E05–S3E09 remain historical battle cutoffs, not the latest outcome**; later S3E16–S3E18 material updates only the factions whose later actions are documented. Descriptions indicate *observed actions* or *attributed claims* rather than automatically certifying lore. Research is based on provisional processed transcripts. [^factions-earthly-defenders]: [S2E02](../episodes/S2E02.md) ends when the four companions **arrive at the elder-tree meeting**, with no settled strategy. **S2E03** actually convenes the named Council, including **Amanthalodar, Admiral Chrysorius, Nameless One, Hargomoon, a Dead Fingers birdfolk ruler, Nobieri, Bok Bok and the four companions**; **Monsoon is initially concealed, then volunteers**, and Stanley is separately elected Elhandari leader. The proposed target is **Solar Mines** rather than Firelands or Black Mine; roughly 300 elves, 75 lizardfolk, five giants, 64 birdfolk, Liberator vehicles and other candidates are **planning estimates, not battle-ready roll call**. Hope **refuses to conscript** her exhausted tiefling survivors, though Nobieri volunteers; the search for Tungsten and unfinished Triangulosphere is **assigned**, not finished. [S3E03](../episodes/S3E03.md) announces a further council, which **actually takes place S3E04** with Nobieri's scout testimony, Tungsten's finished Ruby, Dunglestar's short-lived device demonstration, Diontalus's actual attendance, and agreed northern-gate/AA tactics, **not yet performed strikes**. [S3E05](../episodes/S3E05.md) records allied mobilization, including **Bok Bok's physically arrived goblins**, after their earlier recruitment had only been proposed. The **S3E18** survivor-allocation gathering is **observed**, but does not establish perpetual coalition membership, new state sovereignty or completed integration/Clench clearance.

[^factions-umbilical-order]: In [S2E03](../episodes/S2E03.md) enemy Paladins of the **Umbilical Order** are among the Admiral's **forecast** defenders, not yet witnessed by protagonists. In [S3E04](../episodes/S3E04.md), Nobieri **reports** Paladin-equipped towers from reconnaissance, distinct from direct party inspection. In **S3E05**, one Paladin arrives with **eight Kuromokishi** but combat has **not started at that cutoff**. In [S3E06](../episodes/S3E06.md), the eight are actually fought and **Vorel kills the Paladin** with his +2 longsword, while the surviving enemies of that immediate squad are finished by Stanley; **the Order itself is not thereby extinct**. Later Paladins appear in the wider Solar Mines fighting. The [S3E18 leaders' meeting](../episodes/S3E18.md) **commissions** a future assault/clearing of the **remaining Clench/Umbilical Order facility** involving rehabilitating Toyota Host survivors; no departure, entry, facility capture, permanent closure or final garrison accounting is depicted. The Paladin's claims concerning a historical “Chrome Cord” or created/enslaved lizardfolk are **hostile ideology**, not independent proof of species origin. [Lizardfolk](races/lizardfolk.md).

[^factions-arcanum]: [E028](../episodes/E028.md) records Buttons's **first-person institutional and biographical claims**, including a provisional wizard name **Darguth/Jarguth**; no original handout or party inspection confirms that appointment, campus or magical alteration. [E039](../episodes/E039.md) has a **publicly audible accusation** naming an Arcanum mage, **not** verification of its truth or an institutional verdict. [E059](../episodes/E059.md) negotiates **prospective** reciprocal educational trips under the Principal's conditional diplomacy; no tour or actual exchange ensues at that cutoff. The [owner-reviewed private v3 map](../project/WASTES_CARTOGRAPHIC_CROSSWALK.md#source-artifact-and-verification) carries an in-world inscription crediting a cartographer *of the Arcanum Universalis*, not evidence of its current faculty or a real-world artist. The [March 2019 Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md) retrospectively attributes to Buttons a plan to bring Stanley to the Arcanum to remove Diontalus; this is **GM explanation of unrealized intent**, not a played rescue. The [nonplayed S3E019 roundup](../episodes/S3E019.md) adds **unused** flying-citadel survival speculation, not observation or certification of an operational postwar site. Do not conflate this institution with CHIDS, the Earthly Defenders, the Principal's state or all spellcasters; its legal autonomy, precise premises, charter, continued members and final status remain **unknown**.

[^factions-council-faces]: The [played S3E18 finale](../episodes/S3E18.md) shows thirteen opened machine/chrome-making chambers and a separate hall of static faces that animate for Nash in a private experience. Nash's hand opens the sealed hangar, and the Council **actually rewrites his body without consent**, orders the successor to kill, and speaks of **future** production of copies; no new replica is actually shown deployed. Nash **defies its order and helps evacuate the allies**. Bok Bok's explosives destroy the **Moon facility**, not the Moon itself; Nash detonates from ship, then physically dies in the failing chrome body. Subsequent off-world resurrection is distinct. The Council's claimed ancient-human origin/mandate, alleged creator chain, alleged power beyond its destroyed installation and **hypothetical backups** remain testimony/unknown; no identified phylactery is found. Do not identify this Council with the **Council of Clowns**, **CHIDS living-wall minds** or the **wartime Earthly Defenders**. [CHIDS](places/chids.md) · [Sigil](places/sigil.md).
[^factions-principal]: **S3E14–S3E15 historical cutoff:** The Principal returns in a weakened body under Vorel's conditional parole with five powered flight boards, joins the Worldbreaker battle and opens planar portals to save Hope; no settled pardon is then demonstrated. **Later played S3E16–S3E18:** he betrays the allies, his Earth body is destroyed by Vorel, and remaining chrome copies collapse. The separate lunar Council of Faces forcibly recreates Nash as a successor, but Nash destroys the facility with his companions' help and is later resurrected in his original flesh; the fate of every claimed regime agent/backup remains unverified. [^factions-soulbroker]: **S3E12 manifested SOULBROKER PHYSICALLY SLAIN by 3,280-Nash mass *Fireball*, GM affirms WEIR–EARTH CONNECTION SUNDERED.** But independent **WORLD-BREAKER EGG/SEED STILL in Earth S3E13 physically creating LAVA/PURPLE GEYSERS**, cosmic/demonic reach beyond link unresolved
