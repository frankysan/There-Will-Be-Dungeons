# Entity index convention

[`entities.json`](entities.json) is a starter machine-readable name index for future transcript consistency checks. Markdown pages remain the authoritative **editorial notes**; JSON provides stable IDs, aliases, types, source pointers and review status.

Each entity has:

- `id`: stable, ASCII slug that does not change when its display name is corrected.
- `display_name`: provisional written label for generated indexes.
- `type`: `person_character`, `person_npc`, `place`, `artifact_or_technology`, `organization`, `world_concept` or `race_culture` (introduced in v2 for intelligent peoples/cultures, **not** their factions or individual members).
- `aliases`: alternate recognition spellings/phrases that should match in searches; aliases do **not** establish canon.
- `name_status`: `working`, `needs_audio_spelling_check`, `spoken_name_confirmed`, `known_label` or `wiki_spelling_supported` (the latter is **not** audio verification).
- `description`: source-limited summary with careful attribution.
- `sources`: array of `{episode, time, evidence_type}` entries; episode/time is approximate and refers to source audio time.
- `external_sources`: optional array of `{url, evidence_type, chronology, checked_on}` for external orthography and other carefully attributed references.
- `questions`: explicit unresolved issues.
- `page_path` (optional, v2): repository-root-relative Markdown subject page; confirm that the file exists. It is a navigation target, not proof of comprehensive canon coverage.
- `relations` (optional, v2): an array of `{relation, entity_id}` directed from the containing record to another **existing** stable ID. For `race_culture`, `documented_individual` means the target character is an attested member; it neither asserts that every member of the people shares that individual's culture nor creates a membership list. Relation IDs must be unique within a source record, resolvable and type-appropriate.

The index does not claim that the listed spellings are externally official. Do not auto-replace words in old transcripts when a preferred name changes: create a separate reviewed editorial variant and retain the originals.

## Version 2 — evolving, non-exhaustive people index (2026-10-31 onward)

[Decision D-046](../project/DECISIONS.md) authorizes a Races domain distinct from characters, creatures and factions. The owner separately approved an **additive** `race_culture` data migration for exactly [Lizardfolk](races/lizardfolk.md) and [Tieflings](races/tieflings.md). The **initial historical v2 pilot** used `version: 2`, `stage: extended-pilot` and `source_scope_policy: cited-episodes-only`. The later WCL-200 cohort used `version: 2`, `stage: expanded-people-cohort`; the **current registry is version 3** with a separately defined provenance-bearing edge collection. The initial v2 pilot retained the 16 original record IDs and positions and appended two races. The later WCL-200 addition preserves the same 18-record ID prefix and adds six more documented peoples; Stanley's owner-confirmed original name is now a search alias on the **same original stable ID**. Optional `page_path` and `relations` remain nonmandatory on older records. See the [current cohort](#wcl-200--first-people-record-expansion-2026-11-02) and historical [pilot plan](#wcl-099--expansion-inventory-and-compatibility-plan-2026-11-02).

**Scope contract:** `source_scope` is the unique, explicitly listed set of episodes **actually cited by indexed records** (including the earlier E000/E001 pilot). It does *not* mean all characters, claims, peoples, or events in those episodes have been indexed; it does not mean recordings were independently verified by listening. The exact cited episodes of every record must belong to this list, and every listed episode must have at least one record citation. Source references retain approximate time codes and evidence classes; competing accounts, local customs and planned events must remain attributed, not become objective race-wide traits. The initial two, and now eight, race records link to established reader studies but **do not synchronize or generate** those pages. **Lactids remain a research candidate without a standalone population study**. [Harlequinari](races/harlequinari.md) is now indexed as a limited people record on the strength of Blood Skunk's identity and the E113 Council encounter; **the Council of Clowns' entire membership remains unknown**. Existence of an article or machine entry does not establish a complete cultural history.

**Compatibility and upgrade:** The top-level `entities` array and all v1 record fields (`id`, `display_name`, `type`, `aliases`, `name_status`, `description`, `sources`, `questions`, and optional `external_sources`) keep their original meaning. Type-agnostic readers that look up IDs, names and aliases can continue reading them; a consumer that **requires** `version == 1` or rejects unknown types/fields needs an explicit v2 upgrade. New relation targets must resolve to IDs in the same file, and `page_path` must resolve to an existing repository Markdown file. Never change older IDs or transcription spellings automatically.

[Decision D-044](../project/DECISIONS.md) makes **The Principal** the canonical display name (old `the Principle` is a wrong historical alias), **Khaervilon** the canonical location name (old `Kervalon` is a wrong historical alias), and confirms **T'ietkalla**, **Moag Ferhain**, **Ubere Mtaris** and the **Or'Nath** family forms. Before adding records, distinguish *owner-confirmed written form* from `spoken_name_confirmed` (actual audio verification); do not overload the latter to mean map-authoritative. Consider a separate status such as `owner_confirmed_written` or explicit provenance object when a versioned schema migration is approved. Stable IDs, previous paths and aliases must survive changes of display spelling. [Consolidation plan](../project/WASTES_CAMPAIGN_CLEANUP_PLAN.md).

## WCL-099 — expansion inventory and compatibility plan (2026-11-02)

**Planning disposition, not a schema migration:** The present 514-line `entities.json` has **18 unique records**: four `person_character`, four `person_npc`, five `place`, one `artifact_or_technology`, one `organization`, one `world_concept` and two `race_culture`. The original **16 IDs and order** are preserved, followed by `race.lizardfolk` and `race.tieflings`; seven source-episode IDs cover the **33 citations** actually included. There are two directed `documented_individual` relations (Lizardfolk → Vorel; Tieflings → Hope), two `page_path` links, and no machine custody timeline. This is *pilot coverage*, not 18 exhaustive biographies or seven complete episodes. [Current live census](../project/WASTES_WORLD_FILE_CENSUS.json) records the original JSON structural checks, not independent audio verification.

**Consumer audit:** The only direct runtime JSON reader located in tracked `tools/` and `scripts/` is `tools/discord_archive.py::entity_search`. It reads the `entities` array, finds an **exact stable ID**, and queries message text with `display_name` plus `aliases`, deduplicating hits by message ID; it does **not** use `relations`, `page_path`, type or source scope, nor is its search a factual assertion. `tests/test_discord_archive.py::test_version_two_races_preserve_existing_entity_lookup` explicitly asserts `version == 2`, `stage == extended-pilot`, **exactly 18** records, the entire **16+2 ID order**, the two current relations, the cited-episode set and resolved race pages. These tests **must be revised as part of a controlled expansion**, not silently relaxed or bypassed; other external consumers and historical downstream snapshots have **not** been verified.

**Migration sequence and invariants:**

1. **Freeze the 18-record baseline.** Retain every existing ID, its identity, alias search semantics and original record order. For an additive existing-type expansion, append only individually source-reviewed records; preserve an assertion for the immutable first 18 IDs even when later tests allow more than 18. Review any updated `stage` or `version` field explicitly rather than reusing the pilot label for a full graph.
2. **Expand name coverage conservatively.** Create a candidate crosswalk before edits: source name and variant → one existing/new entity ID → approved canonical/provisional display → optional primary `page_path` → cited episodes and evidence class. Prioritize six existing people profiles and established subjects from the directories, but do not imply that every article, technical register or combined index row represents one entity. Add confirmed aliases to existing IDs rather than duplicate records; retain ambiguous words as unresolved candidates, not blind auto-replacements.
3. **Design temporal relationship claims separately.** Existing `{relation, entity_id}` remains supported for the two v2 `documented_individual` edges. Before adding kinship, faction membership, custody, transfer or last-observed edges, decide whether a **versioned provenance-bearing claim structure** is necessary: source episode/time, witnessed versus attributed evidence, effective interval/cutoff, claimant, uncertainty and direction. Do not overload a timeless edge with an event-dependent status. This is [WCL-100](../project/WASTES_CLEANUP_TASKS.md#wcl-100), not already-implemented data.
4. **Upgrade consumers and checks in the same change set as data.** Confirm the source-scope/citation-set invariant, required types/fields, unique IDs, alias conflicts and punctuation, resolved relation targets, valid `page_path`/episode files, and no dangling legacy links. Replace the test's exact length only with a documented version/stage-aware migration assertion, while retaining the fixed 18-record prefix and existing lookup/unknown-ID behaviors. Add targeted tests for new records, uncertain relationships and any changed schema. Re-run the Discord lookup tests and a read-only source/link check; never claim original-audio or outside-consumer compatibility from unit tests alone.
5. **Publish the data boundary honestly.** Markdown biographies and source dossiers remain authoritative editorial records. Any expanded JSON declares which entities and episodes are *actually* indexed, not the whole campaign. Explicitly report original-audio gaps, legacy alias choices and unverified external consumers. Do not create a synchronized historical truth or assign custody from the campaign's final scene without supporting evidence.

**Decision at WCL-099 (historical planning state):** WCL-099 established the expansion inventory and staged contract without changing `entities.json` or test consumers at that point. The follow-on [WCL-200](#wcl-200--first-people-record-expansion-2026-11-02) now implements its first additive cohort; parent WCL-035 remains open for WCL-100's independently validated relationships and temporal custody.

## Version 3 — provenance-bearing relationship and custody evidence (WCL-100, 2026-11-02)

**Explicit versioned migration:** `entities.json` now declares `version: 3` and `stage: evidence-edges-cohort`. The original 24 v2 stable IDs remain in exactly the same order, followed by **four documented subjects**: [P0D](characters/p0d.md) (`npc.p0d`), [Blood Skunk Hendrickson](characters/blood-skunk-hendrickson.md) (`character.blood_skunk`), Delit Or'Nath (`npc.delit_ornath`, without an invented standalone biography), and the [Mask of Eltanfawr](technology/mask-of-eltanfawr.md) (`tech.mask_of_eltanfawr`). The canonical `character.stanley` continues to represent Valiir Or'Nath and is **not** duplicated. The new subjects cite E031, E113, E115 and S3E18 within the existing cited-episodes-only `source_scope`.

**Existing links remain supported:** The v2 per-record `relations: [{relation, entity_id}]` form retains its limited timeless meaning. Added `documented_individual` entries on [Pixies](races/pixies.md) → P0D (directly witnessed E031) and [Harlequinari](races/harlequinari.md) → Blood Skunk (self-identification in E113). Those two links do **not** identify the alternate Pod with deceased P0D, imply Harlequinari Council membership, or create a full people/organization census. The two earlier Vorel/Lizardfolk and Hope/Tieflings relations remain unchanged.

**New top-level `evidence_edges` (v3):** a separate, **non-exhaustive, append-only set of source-attributed claim/event records**; do not reinterpret this as present-day status, a global ownership ledger or proof of every relationship's history. Each row has a unique immutable `id`, `kind`, `relation`, `subject_id`, `episode`, `time` (source-local approximate HH:MM:SS), `evidence_type`, `certainty` and a qualifying `note`. All IDs must resolve in the same `entities` array; episode must exist in `episodes/` **and** be in the cited-episode scope. Names/IDs and citation classes must not be normalized automatically from ASR.

| `kind` | Direction and required additional fields | Meaning and validity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `membership_evidence` | `subject_id` is a `race_culture`; `object_id` is one attested individual; `relation: documented_individual` | The evidence supports a **single individual's** affiliation/identity at the source window. An equivalent v2 `relations` link may be included for discoverability; neither represents a universal attribute or temporal organizational office. |
| `kinship_evidence` | `subject_id` is the child; `object_id` the cited parent; `relation: child_of` | Stanley → Delit Or'Nath is supported by [E115's family discussion](../episodes/E115.md) and owner-confirmed written names. This asserts **only** that father–child connection, not mother's identity, resurrection, remains, a complete genealogy or disputed theories. `certainty` records mixed evidentiary provenance rather than pretending original audio verified the orthography. |
| `custody_event` | `subject_id` identifies a specific artifact; `from_entity_id` and `to_entity_id` identify the people in a physically witnessed transfer; `relation: physical_transfer`, `temporal_scope: at_event_only` | Stanley → Hope physically hands over the [Mask](technology/mask-of-eltanfawr.md) at [S3E18 ~02:15](../episodes/S3E18.md). The scene also shows Hope wear it as a crown; **no new attunement, permanent legal ownership, all-ghost-crew recovery or perpetual later custody** is established. |

**Integrity and compatibility:** Only the four bounded evidence edges described above are indexed, not all possible familial or historical relationships. Every edge references existing stable IDs and the specified episode/time; membership edges agree with optional v2 `relations` for their source race. `world/entities.json` stays a non-exhaustive index; `source_scope` still equals only the distinct episodes in entity `sources`, not every event it could describe. `tools/discord_archive.py::entity_search` remains a type-agnostic exact-ID, display-name and aliases reader and **ignores `evidence_edges`**; it is not yet a graph-query API. Consumers that reject new top-level properties or expect `version == 2` require deliberate upgrades. [The synthetic lookup and integrity regression](../tests/test_discord_archive.py) verifies the immutable 24-ID prefix, four additions, distinct edge IDs, resolved endpoints, source time/episode, fixed kinds, the two existing race links, and the custody event's explicitly time-bound semantics. No current-state reconstruction, inferred reverse edges, audio listening, downstream consumer validation or native-browser proof is implied.

**Remaining limitations:** A documented event is not a blanket permission to assert subsequent ownership. Hand of the Voice's E101/S3E02/S3E12 transfers, other artifacts, faction roles, additional kinship and all unindexed characters remain candidate additions requiring their own dated source reviews. Contested genealogy stays outside this cohort. Complete later audit of all campaign subjects is separate from this **first working graph schema**.
