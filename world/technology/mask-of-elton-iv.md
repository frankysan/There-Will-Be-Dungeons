# Mask of Elton IV (?) — working name

**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Item:** Jeweled black mask found in **Stanley's father's hidden Elhandar study** in E118. Name alternatives in ASR include `Elton Four` and `Eltenvor`; **not canon-certified**.

Stanley is **told to add the mask to his inventory**. Its GM-described **Peer into Plurality** feature can alter selected d20 results at an escalating **psychic cost** (first selection described as 30 psychic damage, subsequent choices doubling), with an unusual four-successful-long-rest attunement requirement and severe described death risk. **No attunement, activated power, damage or resurrection consequence occurs in E118**. Treat the details as an untested GM item read-out rather than proven universal rules. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/E118_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## E126 — spelljammer control revealed, unsuccessful first activation

In [E126](../../episodes/E126.md) ~00:28–00:39, Dalit's animated journal identifies the mask (Qwen **Eltanfawr**, Parakeet **Elton IV**, still audio-uncertain) as the telepathic command interface for **[Villa Ornath](../places/villa-ornath.md)**. **Stanley actually puts it on**; he chooses a narrow, pointed face-mask design bearing **eight repositionable chromatic stones**. The GM describes the mask imparting knowledge of a larger **spelljammer vessel** capable of **dime-size shrinking with its occupants, flight and space travel**, with a claimed air-supplying vessel mode. Stanley's **attempted mental lift makes the villa creak but is resisted**; the item reportedly requires **four consecutive nights** of personal attunement. These advertised features are not actual successful flight, shrinking or vessel-mode use. The existing *Peer into Plurality* description is a **separate untested power**, not the flight activation; the exact relationship of its prior four-successful-rest mechanism to E126's four consecutive nights needs original item text.
