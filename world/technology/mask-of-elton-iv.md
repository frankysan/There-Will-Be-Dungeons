# Mask of Elton IV — legacy spelling and compatibility link

The artifact formerly catalogued as **Mask of Elton IV (?) / Elton Four / Eltenvor** now uses the archive-owner preferred name **[Mask of Eltanfawr](mask-of-eltanfawr.md)**. The names refer to **one jeweled mask**, discovered by Stanley in [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) and described as Villa Ornath's spelljammer interface in [E126](../../episodes/E126.md); **no separate Elton IV item exists in this catalogue**.

This filename is retained to preserve historical incoming links and transcript references. See the [canonical Eltanfawr reference](mask-of-eltanfawr.md) for chronological development, the E128 current status and unresolved mechanics. The exact historical ASR spellings remain in the [pre-reorganization archive](../../research/legacy/MASK_OF_ELTANFAWR_PRE_ARC_REORGANIZATION_2026-10-24.md). This is an **owner-selected editorial spelling**, not audio verification.
