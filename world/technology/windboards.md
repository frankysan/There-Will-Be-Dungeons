# Windboards — Principal's Kuromokishi hovering assault boards

**First physically observed:** [Wastes S3E14 — Sit and Hum](../../episodes/S3E14.md) **~01:16–01:24**. **Five** heavy, engine-driven, metallic **hoverboards / “windboards”** stored **INSIDE THE PRINCIPAL'S CRASH-LANDED EARTH-STYLE ROCKET**, with apparent rear thrusters, multiple foot controls and accelerator. The PRINCIPAL calls them assault vehicles made for his elite **“KUROMOKISHI” (P) / “KARAMUKISHI” (Q)**; exact unit spelling/proper item name **ASR-PROVISIONAL**, original audio pending [P/Q report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E14_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md). **Not Tarrasque loot and not Fate Six's materialization mechanism**.

## S3E14 — First observed flight and training

**HOPE PHYSICALLY lifts out one metal board, STANDS on it, activates pedal, board rises and hovers.** She turns by shifting balance, accelerates and **ACTUALLY CIRCLES CAMP AND SKIMS ABOVE A LITTLE LAVA CRACK WITHOUT CHANGE IN ELEVATION**; with enough speed it can do a **loop-de-loop**, during further practice she **ACTUALLY FALLS once and resumes** (no dramatic injury demonstrated). **S3E14 ~02:03–02:06 HOPE AND STANLEY ACTUALLY LEAVE TOGETHER ON A BOARD** for practice/errands, Stanley initially less assured; no final race performance or duration established. **OTHER FOUR boards are AVAILABLE BY PRINCIPAL'S OFFER**, but which specific companion physically carries each by cutoff not individually inventoried. **Five total is GM's explicit number at ~01:54–02:00**, distinct from number of current hero pilots.

## S3E14 — Proposed use against the Worldbreaker

The PRINCIPAL and party plan to use powered boards to **harass an exceptionally fast TARRASQUE across dangerous LAVA fields**, keeping their more fragile restored **FATE SIX with arcane ballistas** as delayed artillery support. Initial suggested ranged **NASH, STANLEY, PRINCIPAL** from boards, while **HOPE carries VOREL from remote explosive choke point to choke point** in the LEADEN FINGERS; the plan changes conversationally as constraints arise. **No Tarrasque battle, dodged attack, actual boarding of monster, completed ferry carrying Vorel, expended fuel, item charges, attack statistics or magical enchantment value demonstrated during S3E14.** A large metallic hoverboard is **powered artifice with motors**, not necessarily a spell-granted flying carpet, and doesn't inherit Fate Six's interplanar travel capability.

## S3E15 — flight speed clarified; one board ACTUALLY swallowed/destroyed, Stanley rescues Hope

In [played S3E15](../../episodes/S3E15.md), originally **FIVE** power hoverboards; four actual pilots **PRINCIPAL, STANLEY, NASH and HOPE** launch to provoke TARRASQUE and lure toward LEADEN FINGERS while VOREL takes FATE SIX. **NORMAL SPEED 90 feet/round, BOARD CAN FLY freely in three dimensions**; GM later **ACTUALLY REVEALS/RETRO-ADJUDICATES BONUS-ACTION OVERDRIVE SPEED UP TO 180 feet/round**, not ground-bound. HOPE and STANLEY earlier train shared piloting and HOPE **NAT20 PERFORMS TIGHT DUST-SLIDE STOP before battle**. ## S3E15 — Sigil transit is the Principal's portal magic, not a board power

During the [S3E15 fight](../../episodes/S3E15.md), **the Principal personally opens a portal from the Worldbreaker's mouth to Sigil**, which **Hope and the Principal actually cross aboard their powered boards**. He later **opens a second portal back to Earth** and they return through it. This proves those boards can be **carried or ridden through someone else's actual planar portal**, **not** that the windboards generate Gates, travel between planes by themselves, or are interchangeable with Fate Six. Hope's own malfunctioning board is **subsequently destroyed in the same episode**, despite the earlier Sigil passage.

## S3E16 — Three working boards in the canyon chase

In [played S3E16](../../episodes/S3E16.md), **three working flight boards carry four riders** at the opening of the canyon pursuit: Hope pilots with Stanley aboard, while Nash and the Principal each ride another board. This is an observed opening configuration, not a complete census of all surviving boards; the fifth original board's whereabouts were already uncertain. Hope continues piloting during the fight, and Stanley uses her board to pick up the *Polymorphed* Worldbreaker snail. The Principal also uses his board before Vorel grapples him off it; what later happens to that particular board is not independently established.[^windboards-s3e16]

## S3E17 — Hope extracts invisible Nash above the Principal-copy siege

[Played S3E17](../../episodes/S3E17.md): Following S3E15 **ONE HOPE BOARD DESTROYED** and S3E16 continued use of remaining boards, HOPE **ACTUALLY PILOTS ONE WORKING POWERED WINDBOARD WITH STANLEY'S *GREATER-INVISIBLE* NASH ABOARD**. At STANLEY'S SIGNAL (~01:39–01:47), she flies **90FT STRAIGHT UP**, later **ANOTHER 180FT**, and thereafter holds position ~**270FT ABOVE FIGHT**, keeping Nash's *Banishment* concentration safe. She asks about using Fighter *Action Surge* to double a readied movement; GM rejects same-trigger action surge but allows normal board movement. **NASH *BANISHMENT* DOES LAST FULL MINUTE, WORLD BREAKER EXPELLED**. Specific pilot ownership/total inventory of other three/four boards remains unverified; do not invent a recovered destroyed board. [P/Q](../../reports/WASTES_S3E17_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md).

## Current status

**Latest directly witnessed use (S3E17):** Hope pilots **one functioning board**, with invisible Nash aboard, above the fighting as his *Banishment* reaches its completed one-minute cutoff. This establishes **at least one functional board during that scene**, not that Hope owned it permanently or that the particular board's previous or later custodian has been identified. The **inventory evidence is deliberately non-additive**: **five** boards are introduced in S3E14; **one of those five is visibly destroyed** in S3E15 (**at most four not shown destroyed**); **three working boards are simultaneously seen carrying four people** at S3E16's opening; **one working board is directly seen with Hope/Nash** in S3E17. The missing fourth board after S3E15, and **the Principal's board after Vorel pulls him off it in S3E16**, are **not separately confirmed destroyed, repaired, recovered, transferred or usable at the finale**. Do not transform 'at most four not proven destroyed' into 'four working and possessed.' In [S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md), the characters instead make later journeys aboard **Elhandari spacecraft and the rematerialized Fate Six**, with **no demonstrated final windboard transfer, repair, disposal or owner-by-owner count**.[^windboards-final]
