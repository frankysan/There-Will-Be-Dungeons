# Gem of Tides

**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Role:** Blue gem associated with T'ietkalla's old water/oil transformation system. **Observed custody:** Recovered by the party **E050**. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional.

The party searches old chambers beneath the T'ietkalla keep and eventually **physically retrieves the Gem in E050** after the ambush and deaths around the throne conflict. E051 explores the old water-throne installation. The artifact's retrieval is an established played event; **full installation, functioning water conversion and long-term civic restoration are not all independently demonstrated**. [E049](../../episodes/E049.md)–[E051](../../episodes/E051.md) · [T'ietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md).

## Earlier claims, contested ownership and physical recovery (E039–E050)

In [E039](../../episodes/E039.md), **Monsoon Cabbage claims** that the Gem converts contaminated oil into usable water, suspects Jolt of planning to steal it, and wants the artifact for his own political goals. This is **Monsoon's interested interpretation of the Gem's purpose and Jolt's intentions**, not a witnessed test of water conversion. [E049](../../episodes/E049.md) adds Buttons' disputed account of a bargain concerning the Gem, Monsoon and the Principal; none of these negotiations by itself proves a completed Gem transfer or running waterworks.

In [E050](../../episodes/E050.md), the party **actually identifies the glowing blue Gem of Tides in a recovered bag and takes possession** after the confrontation. The old underground chamber they soon enter contains pipes, a pool and a **visibly empty Gem emplacement**. Thus E050 establishes **recovery, not installation or activation**; the immediate physical holder within the group is clarified in the next played episode.

## E051 — Gem carried into the old water-throne chamber

In [E051](../../episodes/E051.md), **Vorel is explicitly carrying the Gem in a bag** as Hope tells **Kefeli** that they came to return it and restore water to T'ietkalla. Kefeli demands the Gem and claims historical authority over the kingdom. The party fights her; **Hope and Vorel are petrified and later restored within this same episode**, and Kefeli is destroyed. The episode establishes the **physical water-throne room, pipework and the party's restoration intention**, but the source dossier **does not independently settle the exact Gem handoff, emplacement or a sustained Gem-driven oil-to-water conversion**. Do not mistake visible water/pump infrastructure or a proposed repair for a verified completed installation.

## S3E18 — restored Earth and T'ietkalla rebuilding are distinct events

In the [played campaign finale](../../episodes/S3E18.md), **the Rubistic Triangulosphere's authorized operation visibly restores rain, greenery and water across Earth**. Later, **Hope returns to the still-ruined T'ietkalla**, takes responsibility for rebuilding and welcomes survivors and settlers. Her arrival and resettlement are real, but **a repaired citywide waterworks or renewed use of the Gem of Tides is not individually shown**. The planetary outcome is credited to the **separate Rubistic Triangulosphere**, not silently to the Gem. No post-E051 direct custody check establishes who holds the blue stone at the finale.
