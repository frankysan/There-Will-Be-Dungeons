**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Scope:** Large ancient installation reached deep in **CHIDS**, distinct from the **portable paired portal device**. **First physical encounter:** E079; controls used E081. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional, no audio review.

## Reaching the machine

A spectral dragon describes a hypothetical Earth-relocation machine in E067. Inside CHIDS, a simulated office worker named Charles Dickens claims to be able to delay it by false progress reports; the party performs an upload, with effectiveness unknown. These **early explanations are testimony**, not proof of exact engineering. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md) · [E070](../../episodes/E070.md) · [CHIDS](../places/chids.md).

The party **actually enters the Drive complex in E079** and fights illithids and a large commander. In E081 it manipulates machine controls and confronts a manifestation of Dolo Popio. **Quizquaz** claims the Drive channels Void energy and frames catastrophic alternatives, including destruction of Earth; the companions reject that option and **choose to absorb the crimson beam**, dispersing and being declared dead at episode end. They later reappear alive in Sigil E082, so **E081 is neither a permanent-death endpoint nor evidence that the machine's planetary effects were fully surveyed**.
