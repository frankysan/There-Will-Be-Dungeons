# Traversal Drive — CHIDS

**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Scope:** Large ancient installation reached deep in **CHIDS**, distinct from the **portable paired portal device**. **First physical encounter:** E079; controls used E081. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional, no audio review.

## Reaching the machine

A spectral dragon describes a hypothetical Earth-relocation machine in E067. Inside CHIDS, a simulated office worker named Charles Dickens claims to be able to delay it by false progress reports; the party performs an upload, with effectiveness unknown. These **early explanations are testimony**, not proof of exact engineering. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md) · [E070](../../episodes/E070.md) · [CHIDS](../places/chids.md).

## First witnessed chamber and interrupted fight (E079–E080)

In [E079](../../episodes/E079.md), the party **physically reaches the vast crystal-transformed engineering chamber**. They see enormous embedded machinery, a luminous vertical beam, chanting illithids, Cerinthala and a dragon-worm figure in a rainbow-colored pool. Hope's two rockets **strike enemies near the beam**, and several visible bodies disintegrate, but **the Drive is not operated or destroyed** at this cutoff. In [E080](../../episodes/E080.md), Nash **actually defeats the surviving large illithid commander**, while **Dolo Popio remains alive** and transforms the fight into a strange space-like arena. Neither the machine's purported planetary purpose nor a literal interplanar relocation is proved by that battlefield change.

## Performed beam operation and E081 cutoff

In [E081](../../episodes/E081.md), Hope's radiant shot **destroys Dolo Popio's manifested body**; the scene returns to the engineering chamber with Stanley, Eddie and Sira temporarily trapped within the light, and **Chance still unlocated after drifting away**. **Hope actually examines the console**, changing a frequency dial and beam-color faders; other settings create hazardous sensations, not a successful safe shutdown. **Quizquaz** claims the machine channels energy from the Void and that allowing it to run could destroy Earth and the Principal; he presents a strong volunteer's sacrifice as an alternative. Those are **interested testimony and proposed consequences**, not independently confirmed planetary mechanics. He explains the flattened waveform needed to pass the beam and the raised-frequency crimson setting; the controls' **immediate responses are subsequently observed**, but lasting consequences remain unmeasured.

The party **rejects the offered planetary destruction**. Eddie and Sira are **physically freed from the beam**; **Stanley, Hope, Vorel and dragon-form Nash deliberately choose to absorb its energy together**, while **Eddie operates the controls outside**. All four take catastrophic damage, **disperse into white light and mist**, and the GM **explicitly declares them dead at the E081 ending**. Eddie and Sira are alive outside; **Chance's fate is unknown**. There is **no witnessed subsequent Earth-wide survey, proven permanent Drive shutdown or confirmed destruction/relocation of the planet**.

## E082 aftermath: four different conditions, not four living returns

In [E082](../../episodes/E082.md), the Sigil aftermath distinguishes **Vorel**, who is conscious and alive in a lizardfolk body; **Nash**, conscious as a **floating heart with an eye/finger, not yet a restored humanoid**; **Hope**, whose body is **motionless** with limbs missing; and **Stanley**, whose body remains **cold and GM-described as dead**. **Do not say all four reappear alive in E082** or treat E081's GM death declaration as a proven permanent end to their stories. Eddie and Sira are not shown again in this episode, and Chance remains missing. The apparent transition to Sigil **does not independently establish the machine's exact plane-travel method, Earth-side damage or final power state**.
