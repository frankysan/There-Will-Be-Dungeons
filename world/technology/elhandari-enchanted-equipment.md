# Elhandari enchanted equipment — allocations, handovers and finite spell bowls

**The Wastes · Mars preparation and Earth campaign, S3E03–S3E18.** This source-led register distinguishes an Elhandari **offer**, a **specific award**, an observed **physical handover**, a **demonstrated use**, and an **unverified later inventory**. It is not an item-by-item list of everything the heroes possess at the finale. [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [Elhandari augmentation cylinder](augmentation-cylinder.md) · [Rejuvenation syringes](rejuvenation-syringe.md) · [Chrome's Bane](chromes-bane.md).

## S3E03 — offers are not individual weapon acquisitions

In [S3E03](../../episodes/S3E03.md), Admiral Chrysorius's Elhandari delegation **offers** +1/+2 weapons, armor, spell reagents and augmentation surgery. **No specific new +2 weapon or armor set is selected or physically issued before the episode's cutoff.** The GM **does allocate a separate rejuvenation syringe to each of the four originals**; these are not new magical swords, feast bowls, or completed augmentations. Stanley's **physical gift of his older enchanted sword *Diplomacy* to Hope in the same episode** is a separate, actual transfer, not an Elhandari +2 gear allocation.

## S3E04 — the first specific awards and two original feast bowls

In [S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md), after Nash, Hope and Stanley **actually undergo the separately offered eight-hour augmentation surgery** while Vorel declines, the following specific gear is awarded:

| Intended user | Confirmed Elhandari award | Boundary on physical custody |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stanley** | **+2 rapier; +2 studded leather armor; two gem-encrusted spell-component bowls; ruby and diamond dust** | His personally selected gear is received. The original **two bowls are finite spell components**, not an unlimited permanent casting tool. |
| **Hope** | **+2 rapier; +2 half plate; +2 shield** | Awarded and instructed to add to her equipment. Her already owned animated *Diplomacy* is a **distinct sword**. |
| **Nash** | **+2 quarterstaff; *Cloak of Protection*** | GM **first objects to the non-generic cloak but then explicitly reverses the ruling and grants it**. Neither a “+2 cloak” nor imaginary “+2 robes” are documented. |
| **Vorel, absent when chosen** | **+2 longsword collected by Stanley; +2 shield collected by Hope** | At this cutoff **Stanley and Hope are the physical collectors**, not yet a witnessed final hand-to-hand delivery to Vorel. |

Stanley **actually casts *Heroes' Feast* during S3E04**. **One of his two originally issued gemmed bowls cracks/is consumed**, and the four original companions **actually eat**, as does invited Union Wheel. The GM adjudicates a **24-hour benefit**, including immunity to poison/poison damage and being frightened, cure of existing diseases/poisons, advantage on Wisdom saves, and an individual **2d10 increase to maximum and current hit points**. The feast removes the party's hangovers but does **not** remove Nash's engineered heart or all his physical anomalies. **Only the first bowl has been expended at this cutoff.**

## S3E05–S3E07 — Vorel's sword and shield enter demonstrated use

In [S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md), **Stanley actually gives Vorel the +2 longsword aboard Fate Six**. Vorel accepts and **practises with it**. **Hope's separately collected +2 shield is not expressly handed to Vorel in that episode**; do not invent a matching transfer scene.

In [S3E06](../../episodes/S3E06.md), **Vorel explicitly confirms he has the +2 shield equipped**, establishing actual later custody without explaining the intervening handover. He **unsheathes the +2 longsword, lands two hits totalling 29 slashing and kills the armored Paladin**. He **considers naming the weapon**, but no name is spoken in that episode. In [S3E07](../../episodes/S3E07.md), he first definitively calls the **sword “Chrome's Bane”** and **physically hurls his +2 shield at an attacking Paladin**. The shield's **later use with a mounted detonator in S3E14–S3E16** does not retroactively narrate its exact intervening pickup. Neither shield nor sword has a proved additional unique anti-chrome enchantment beyond the described +2 award. [Sword history](chromes-bane.md).

## S3E11 — second bowl, second fresh feast

In [S3E11](../../episodes/S3E11.md), Stanley **actually casts a new *Heroes' Feast* after the morning rest**. The party **eats again** and receives the renewed time-limited protection. The GM/player explicitly call this **the second and last of Stanley's two original 1,000-gp gem-encrusted bowls**, and it is **consumed**. The already expired or expiring S3E04 meal is **not merely reheated**, and there is **no third original bowl** left after this cast. A displayed 2d10 roll for one person **must not be attributed to all diners**.

## S3E14–S3E16 — two different +2 crossbows and a replenished feast component

In [S3E14](../../episodes/S3E14.md), Stanley **actually receives his own +2 Elhandari crossbow from the magical armory**, with **ordinary bolts**, distinct from the Principal's chrome bow. Stanley **plans to obtain another 1,000-gp gemmed bowl** and departs with Hope on a windboard; **no bowl purchase, actual collection or new *Heroes' Feast* cast is yet witnessed in S3E14**.

In [S3E15](../../episodes/S3E15.md), **Hope separately receives her own +2 magic crossbow**. Elhandari attendants **also actually supply Stanley with the necessary additional gem-encrusted bowl(s)**; the record **does not demonstrate a Walmart purchase** or a precise number of extra bowls beyond what is needed for casting. Stanley **actually casts a third *Heroes' Feast*** before the battle, and **all four originals eat**, consuming **one bowl**. The Principal's metal-eating body **cannot benefit from that meal**, by GM ruling. The 24-hour protection remains active through the ensuing rest and the next day's Worldbreaker combat; it **does not stack indefinitely** or make anyone permanently immune. The later number of **unused** bowls is **not checked**.

Hope's S3E15 crossbow attacks against the Worldbreaker include **hit 29 for 12 piercing**, **miss 15**, and **hit 26 for 11 piercing**. The table applies the light crossbow's **Loading** limitation, so Hope does **not** gain unlimited shots from Extra Attack. Stanley instead chooses spells at those S3E15 moments; **do not assign Hope's bolts to him**.

In [S3E16](../../episodes/S3E16.md), **Stanley actually fires his separate +2 crossbow**, **hitting 28 for 10 piercing**. Hope **hits another shot** but later rolls **natural 1**, **jams/breaks her own +2 crossbow and abandons it**. No repair, physical retrieval or replacement is performed at that episode cutoff. This is **not** a malfunction or loss of Stanley's separate crossbow. The companions' **frightened immunity from the S3E15 feast remains active** during this battle; allied lizardfolk and goblins **do not automatically share the feast benefits**.

## Finale and inventory boundaries

The maintained [S3E17](../../episodes/S3E17.md)–[S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md) accounts do **not** establish a full owner-by-owner final inventory of the awarded +2 weapons, armor, shield, cloak, crossbows, remaining ruby/diamond dust or possible unused bowls. **Nash dies and is successfully resurrected without personal equipment in S3E18**; that is **not** evidence the earlier +2 quarterstaff or cloak survived in his possession after resurrection, nor proof they were destroyed. Hope's broken, abandoned crossbow is not silently repaired. Vorel's **Chrome's Bane** has its **separately attributed S3E18 custody summary** and should not be conflated with the other gear.

**Component accounting:** Stanley receives **two gem bowls in S3E04 and consumes both by S3E11**; after **a further Elhandari supply in S3E15**, he performs **one additional documented feast using a bowl**. Thus **three actually performed feast casts and at least three expended bowls** across these episodes, **not** three originally allocated bowls and **not** proof of a fourth still available. The **rejuvenation syringes** and **augmentation cylinder** are independent processes with their own source histories, not substitute feast components.
