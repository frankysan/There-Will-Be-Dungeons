# Eye of the Voice

> **The Wastes · sought artifact, never positively acquired through S3E18.** This is an **ASR-provisional source account**, not an inventory entry for a held item. [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [Living Hand of the Voice](hand-of-the-voice.md) · [Voice](../characters/the-voice.md) · [Hope Akmenos](../characters/hope-akmenos.md).

## Identity and source limits

**The Eye of the Voice is a reportedly separate, missing counterpart of the living Hand of the Voice.** Its location, physical appearance, construction, full powers, true owner and final disposition are **not directly observed or confirmed** in the maintained played episodes. It is **not Vorel's actual, implanted [Eye of Groxl](eye-of-groxl.md)**, the distinct **Zorander's Fist gauntlet**, a collector's ring, or the biological eye of an encountered monster. Statements by the Principal, Tobacho, the Council of Clowns, the hooded Voice and Brett are **attributed claims**, not automatically authenticated facts. The names **Pentel Weiss / Pentelvice / Pentalvise (?)** retain source-phonetic uncertainty.

## E106–E113 — leads, a signed bargain and an actual compass

In [E106](../../episodes/E106.md), the **Principal claims** that a separate **Eye of the Voice** exists and advises Hope to seek information from **Tobacho Fleur**. **Neither the Eye nor a reliable location is produced** during the conversation. In [E112](../../episodes/E112.md), Hope and Tobacho **actually sign a written compact** involving help against the Lady of Pain in exchange for **a lead** and bounty concessions; Hope receives a copy. Tobacho explicitly **does not know the Eye's immediate location**, instead proposing that Pandemonium's **Council of Clowns** might know more.

In [E113](../../episodes/E113.md), Hope, Steerious Business, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider **actually reach Pandemonium and appear before the Council**. Bobo recognizes the attached living Hand by its scent and **claims** the Eye is located within a **wizard-built prison, Pentel Weiss/Pentelvice (?)**, in the **phlogiston near the Corinthian Nebula**. This is an **NPC-supplied destination**, not a verified precise coordinate or the Eye itself. A tiny clown car **physically delivers a wind-up jack-in-the-box compass to Hope**, and she **actually takes it**. Bobo says the device is keyed to Hope and points toward the prison; its claimed exclusive-user restriction is **not independently tested on others**. She **does not acquire the Eye** and has **not yet traveled to that prison** at E113's cutoff.

## E116–E118 — attempted logistics, not a completed expedition

[E116](../../episodes/E116.md) resumes with a **scene-boundary discontinuity**: E113 had ended with the group passing through a Gate toward Tobacho's Sigil bar, but E116 restages their departure from the Council's cavern and sends them instead to **Sigil's earlier White Tower departure dock**. Preserve both source cutoffs; do **not** invent an intermediate return trip. Hope **actually asks about a ship and pilot** to reach the prison; the questioned dockmaster does not know Pentelvice or the nebula, and the episode shows **no booked Eye-expedition ship**. A Gate opened toward Earth does not reach a usable spacecraft facility; Hope closes it without crossing. Her **Council compass and signed compact are not shown handed away**, but that silence is not a final inventory.

In [E118](../../episodes/E118.md), the hooded Voice **privately appears to Hope**, her attached Hand burns and feels heavy, and he urges her to travel to Pentelvice immediately. **Hope asks for more time**. The demand is a privately delivered supernatural instruction, **not a completed Gate, physical Eye sighting, or receipt of the Eye**.

## S2E07–S2E12 — prison reached, but no Eye identified or retrieved

The original party's **actual later trip** is distinct from the earlier failed search for a ship. In [S2E07](../../episodes/S2E07.md), **Hope physically checks the Council's jack-in-the-box compass**, which points northeast and leads the ship toward a small portal sphere enclosing the prison. **Fate Six actually enters the sphere, docks beside Pentel Weiss, and the party disembarks**. The compass thus has a **demonstrated directional function**, but does **not** independently prove that the sought Eye is inside the pyramid or mark its precise hiding place.

In [S2E08](../../episodes/S2E08.md), the compass **points to the pyramid regardless of where the party surveys its exterior**, not to a particular door. A gray-haired prison warden actually appears with a **deeply scarred eye**. Hope **speculates** this could indicate the Eye of the Voice, but **no removable artifact is seen or identified**, and the GM directly questions whether Hope's in-character knowledge of the Eye had been established. There is **no successful theft, examination or receipt** of the relic.

Inside the prison in [S2E10](../../episodes/S2E10.md), Vorel glimpses a **large eye embedded in a wall** beside a tentacled, telepathic creature. In [S2E11](../../episodes/S2E11.md), Hope **actually injures its fleshy eye/membrane**, reaches into the wound and finds **only fibrous tissue, not a removable magical lens**. The **separate hooded Voice privately claims** another piece of its power lies at the **top of Pentel Weiss**; the claim does **not identify the eye-creature as the Eye of the Voice**. At the Voice's private suggestion, Hope and companions **sing**, and the tentacled entity **actually liquefies**, without releasing a known Eye artifact. These played outcomes **do not count as Eye recovery or proven destruction of the desired object**.

In [S2E12](../../episodes/S2E12.md), the group **leaves the pyramid aboard Fate Six**, with Tungsten Wheel and other rescued companions. The Voice **privately rebukes Hope for failing to secure the further artifact**; Hope does not produce it and does not return to the alleged top-of-prison location before departure. The party's separately recovered **collector ring** is **not** the Eye.

## S3E12–S3E18 — still demanded, never delivered

In [S3E12](../../episodes/S3E12.md), the Principal **actually delivers the stolen living Hand** to Brett/Asmodeus, who **passes it to a succubus assistant who puts it in her pouch**. Brett **still demands the Eye of the Voice as a separate, unpaid piece**; Vorel's Eye of Groxl remains distinct. No Eye is produced during this witnessed Hand transfer. In [S3E13](../../episodes/S3E13.md), the heroes **discuss** possible future consultants and an **Eye of the Voice expedition**, but **do not undertake that expedition** before their actual return to Earth. The maintained [S3E14](../../episodes/S3E14.md)–[S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md) dossiers show **no subsequent positive identification, handover, activation or inventory receipt of the Eye**. The campaign's ending therefore **does not establish its ultimate whereabouts**, nor that a particular warden, corpse, creature or ally possessed it.

**Evidence boundary:** A reported location is not a confirmed item sighting; the recovered **compass** is an actual held object, not the Eye; reaching the **prison** is an actual completed journey, not a completed Eye retrieval. None of those distinctions changes the separate observed histories of the [Hand](hand-of-the-voice.md) or [Eye of Groxl](eye-of-groxl.md).
