# Rubistic Triangulosphere (“Ruby”) — unfinished planetary sealing device

**The Wastes • introduced as proposal in late Season One, physical unfinished machine encountered in [S2E02](../../episodes/S2E02.md).** Related [Tungsten Wheel](../characters/tungsten-wheel.md) · [Delit Ornath's spelljammer](fate-six.md) · [black dragon hearts](nash-dragon-heart.md). **ASR-provisional; no audio or source machine/engineering handout inspected.**

## Purpose versus achieved function

A dying **natural black dragon** earlier recommends shaping its separate metal heart into an extraordinary **Triangulosphere** and placing it in **Earth's hottest location** to oppose the **Weir**. The party gives the proposed instrument the nickname **“Ruby.”** During the Season One E128 cutoff, the dragon's separate recovered heart is transported toward **Mars** by elves, the engineer **Tungsten Wheel** remains unfound, and **no finished instrument is operating**. **Monsoon Cabbage** suggests in S2E01 that **Solar Mines may reach Earth's mantle and fulfill the placement requirement**, but explicitly offers his own uncertain theory; it is neither verified nor tested.

## S2E02's actual discovery

The party actually enters **Tungsten's preserved workshop in the Theatre of Mortal Bodies on Mars**, with access granted by missing engineer's lover **Dunglestar Ravensbread**. The central device is physically visible, **roughly 10 feet high and six wide** according to Beau, mounted above an annular base with irregular triangles and arcs. It looks nothing like a completed sphere when **inactive/unpowered**. **Stanley's examination of schematics** shows it is intended to **spin its components together rapidly** when running. Both Hope and Nash inspect engineering/arcane characteristics; none can simply complete it. **No test spin, activation, relocation, Bag-of-Holding transport or Earth installation occurs** in S2E02.

A missing connector/material identified phonetically as **Gluptwarp / Glupwarp / Globwarp (?)** is necessary, per plans and Dunglestar, to prevent overheating. Dunglestar's elaborate origin story includes matter shaved from a dying star, falling on a frozen world, **fermentation in an ice giant for a thousand years** and refinement with mithril; that is **NPC-described manufacturing lore**, not a demonstrated experiment. He asserts Tungsten went looking for it, possibly with help from a **chrome man called the Principle**. The party sees a handwritten message identifying **Blast Belly Rock** as where Tungsten apparently sent a warning of danger, and **Pentel Weiss** as a possible source of the missing substance. The message carries glyphs **recognizable as resembling Fate Six helm controls** to Stanley and Hope; their exact destination values are not yet tested. The GM corrects a slip from “turbo pigeon” to **laser pigeon** as delivery mechanism. The whereabouts, health and cooperation of Tungsten are **unconfirmed**.

## S2E03 — Earth-core war plan, still no operation

At the [Mars council](../../episodes/S2E03.md), the Elhandari describe **the core of Earth** as the intended depth for the completed device, for reversing the **Weir corruption** that permits hostile influence to cross into the world. The **Solar Mines** are selected as the most promising route, compared with the Firelands and Black Mine, but their **actual access to the core is unverified**; the project is still missing **Gluptwarp** and missing engineer **Tungsten Wheel**. Hope says she recalls a Solar Mines clue in Tungsten's **diary**, requiring comparison with the **letter physically shown in S2E02**. A military escort plan is drawn up, not carried out. **The World Breaker, identified as an altered Tarrasque, must be dealt with separately even if the Triangulosphere works.** No prototype activation or installation in S2E03. ## S3E03 — actual Mars repair preparations, NOT yet completed or tested

In [S3E03](../../episodes/S3E03.md) ~01:16–01:24, **Fate Six ACTUALLY LANDS ON MARS**, resolves the prior travel plan. **Tungsten Wheel ACTUALLY REUNITES with Dunglestar**, an old lover and keeper of his unfinished apparatus. Tungsten **requests and receives Stanley's authority for at least 64 ELHANDARI ASSISTANTS**, **starts selecting engineering team and physically departs with Dunglestar and Union** to work on the Rubistic Triangulosphere. At ~02:30 Admiral **PREDICTS** the device might be ready by **next day**, but **NO Gluptwarp sample retrieval, actual fitment, activation, verified operating machine, Mars lift-off or Earth-core deployment occurs by this episode cutoff**. Wastes S3E03 ends as original four make choices at **unrelated Mars augmentation cylinder**; the cylinder is **NOT** Ruby. [ASR report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S3E02 — Tungsten claims extradimensional storage; Mars first

In [S3E02](../../episodes/S3E02.md) ~02:34–02:36, Tungsten **CLARIFIES THAT the vital substance (ASR “Glopscorp/Gluptwarp”) is SECRETED IN A PRIVATE MICRO-DIMENSION ASSOCIATED WITH HIS PERSON**, rather than in an ordinary pocket or physically shown vial. He says recovery would be quick with **Dunglestar's workshop tools** if needed, and **not physically dangerous**; **no sample is produced, composition checked, owner/custody transferred to party, instrument activated or Triangulosphere completed**. The party **ACTUALLY CHOOSES MARS FIRST**, with Planet Nash recruitment a later possibility; Fate Six travels through a rift **toward Mars but does not arrive by cutoff**. This confirms only Tungsten's **account**, not that the reagent is actually intact or suitable. [Two-ASR report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S3E01 — Tungsten rescued, says missing reagent concealed, no built machine

Tungsten Wheel is **actually rescued from Pentel Weiss in S2E12** and travels on Fate Six alongside his niece Union. In [S3E01](../../episodes/S3E01.md), he tells Stanley and Hope that the precious difficult-to-produce reagent is **already in his possession**, having remained **concealed somewhere on his person** throughout his years in prison. The name sounds approximately **“Glopscorp/Globscorp”**, perhaps the previous **Gluptwarp/Globwarp** mentioned in schematic accounts; **exact identity/orthography requires checking**, not automatic merging by phonetics alone. Tungsten refuses to disclose the precise place and says **Dunglestar may help him recover it**, but **no sample is taken out, engineering work attempted, workshop revisited or mechanism activated in S3E01**. The ship safely escapes the nebula and is then **harpooned by Hell's vessel before Mars arrival**; **Ruby remains unfinished and the same cross-season storyline is ongoing**.
