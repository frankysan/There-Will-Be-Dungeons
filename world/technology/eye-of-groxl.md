# Eye of Groxl

**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Canonical object identity:** **One artifact**, with owner-confirmed spelling **Eye of Groxl**. Processed ASR names `Grojil` and `Groxel` refer to **this same object**, including the E067 “god killer Grojil” phrase; they are not a second god/item. [D-028/D-034](../../project/DECISIONS.md). **Spoilers:** Through played Wastes S3E09. **Research:** ASR-provisional mechanics and lore.

## S3E09 — defeated Groxl CLAIMS the Eye was fashioned from his removed eye

In [played S3E09](../../episodes/S3E09.md) ~00:04–00:25, Vorel undergoes a **SUPERNATURAL / POSSIBLY AFTERLIFE encounter in “Endless Dunes”** after physically defeating giant. In that scene **his embedded Eye is ABSENT** and a defeated ancient lizardfolk self-identifies as **Groxl**. He tells an **attributed origin story**: humans grew him “in soup,” manipulated, killed and tested him, and ultimately **ONE WARLOCK ACTUALLY REMOVED HIS EYE AND MADE IT INTO A WEAPON / ARTIFACT** later found in **VOREL'S CHEST**. This explains why the Eye might hate or call the MACHINE-ENHANCED GIANT “not Grojil” / “abomination” in S3E08, **but it is Groxl's own narrative, not independent provenance evidence or full mechanical reconciliation of S3E07 identification**. **VOREL AWAKENS IN REAL SOLAR MINES WORLD at ~00:26, and the GM EXPLICITLY CONFIRMS THAT EYE REMAINS IN HIS CHEST**; it was missing only IN THE ENDLESS DUNES VISION. No transfer, eye destruction, replacement or additional observed ability this episode. Groxl **RECOGNIZES VOREL AS “SLAYER OF THE GOD KILLER”** (title of Vorel, not a newly named Eye power), and Vorel refuses his revenge philosophy. [Title register](../characters/vorel-titles.md) · [S3E09 comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E09_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md). No audio listened.

## S3E08 — Eye contradicts apparent certainty and reveals generator dependency

In [played S3E08](../../episodes/S3E08.md) **~00:43–00:46**, as Vorel uses the Eye's **bonus-action learn-one-vulnerability ability**, it **erupts with hatred, issues immediate repeated commands to KILL**, and identifies the immense lizardfolk foe as **“NOT GROJIL” / an “ABOMINATION” sustained by HUMAN “CALCULATOR” MACHINERY**, insisting that the machinery be detached. This **directly complicates**, rather than silently overturns or resolves, **S3E07 GM's explicit statement that Vorel recognizes the legendary Groxl through this SAME Eye**. Possible interpretations include hostile mechanism/augmented vessel, self-serving Eye consciousness or unreliable current wording. **None independently proved**; preserve unresolved identity tension. Actual evidence corroborates equipment dependency: **HOPE identifies power generator, dragon Nash and Stanley DETACH it, giant's external laser/chain blade/cannon FAIL, yet giant remains alive; VOREL ultimately finds a SECOND embedded electric cable running through its HEART**. On striking inner wires Vorel **TAKES 92 LIGHTNING**, with Eye strangely seeming to welcome electricity. After external generator detonates the Eye issues “STRIKE AGAIN”; Vorel **cuts heart cabling with Chrome's Bane for 46 slashing and GIANT PHYSICALLY COLLAPSES**. Eye remains in Vorel's chest at cutoff; **no Eye extraction/transference, defeated god's resurrection or divine cosmology confirmed**. [Full comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E08_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md), audio unverified.

## S3E07 — physically emerged Groxl recognized through Vorel's implanted Eye

In [played Wastes S3E07](../../episodes/S3E07.md), a **huge egg drawn up by the chrome dragon** at the central Solar Mines opens to reveal an actual **mountain-sized LIZARDFOLK with an armed building on his back**, who self-names amid inconsistent ASR renderings such as **Grojil/Grozil/Groshil**. The owner-confirmed display is **Groxl** for this Eye-associated ancient referent, not a second unrelated object. **The GM EXPLICITLY CONFIRMS** after the encounter that **VOREL, THROUGH THE EYE STILL IMPLANTED IN HIS CHEST, KNOWS WITH CERTAINTY that the gigantic being is the legendary Groxl** (~02:19–02:21). This establishes **physical presence and recognized identity**, but **NOT the literal historic truth of the being's own boasts about divine kills, ancient battles or being superior to the Principal**. The large creature ACTUALLY **CRUSHES Fate Six, destroys/throws Elhandari ships, and turns to threaten Hope**. No transfer/removal of the Eye, Eye attack, divine status proof or defeat of Groxl by cutoff. ## E124 — recognition by Malhya and ambiguous lore

**The Eye remains in Vorel's chest**. When Malhya takes fish-scaled Vorel form, it cannot reproduce the Eye. Vorel senses its anger/desire to kill the visitor but an infinitesimal chance of success, then **permits Malhya to touch it**. The visitor recognizes **Groxl**, calls the being dangerous/feared by gods and asks if Vorel came to kill it. **`Grojil/Gergil/Grosjean` are variants of the same identity**, not new objects. **“The one he slew ... he is the very unmaker” (~01:01:47)** has an unresolved antecedent: do not invent a victim or independently certify that cosmology. **Malhya's debuff cure is a separate act**, not Eye removal, transfer or a new Eye attack. ## Contested origin

CHIDS's hive recounts an alleged anti-dragon prototype **GR0X1** and related supernatural experiments. The marked vessel is **physically observed**, but the broad origin story, divine status and reputed ability to kill gods are **speaker-attributed**. A claimed Eye of the Voice in the later Sigil/Hope storyline is **not** this artifact. [E077](../../episodes/E077.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md) · [Ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
