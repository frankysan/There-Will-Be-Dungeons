Processed ASR names `Grojil` and `Groxel` refer to **this same object**, including the E067 “god killer Grojil” phrase; they are not a second god/item. The party's observations include protective effects and, later, the Eye's responses to **Lethanphius** and the **G-R-0-X-1** tank at **CHIDS**. ## Contested origin

CHIDS's hive recounts an alleged anti-dragon prototype **GR0X1** and related supernatural experiments. The marked vessel is **physically observed**, but the broad origin story, divine status and reputed ability to kill gods are **speaker-attributed**. A claimed Eye of the Voice in the later Sigil/Hope storyline is **not** this artifact. [E077](../../episodes/E077.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md) · [Ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
