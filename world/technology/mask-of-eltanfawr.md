# Mask of Eltanfawr

## Introduction

The Mask of Eltanfawr is a dark, jewel-studded face covering discovered among the hidden possessions of [Stanley Billings's](../characters/stanley-billings.md) father. It rested in the impossible upper study of [Villa Or'Nath](../places/villa-ornath.md), beside the [Book of Adjacencies](book-of-adjacencies.md), charts of the stars and a model of Earth. Fashioned from a nearly weightless black material, it had no ordinary straps and was said to fit itself to its wearer. Stanley took possession of it without yet putting it on.[^mask-discovery]

Its first described gift, **Peer into Plurality**, seemed to offer influence over the course of chance, but at a mounting cost to the wearer's mind and body. The description warned of grave consequences should that power prove fatal. Nothing about the mask's appearance revealed how these unusual gifts were connected, and none was demonstrated when it was found.[^mask-plurality]

## Development by arc

### Arc Five — The hidden vessel's command

After Elhandar's fall, the mask's second apparent purpose came to light. Stanley's father's responsive journal identified Villa Or'Nath as a concealed vessel capable, it claimed, of traveling between worlds. The mask was presented as its **telepathic command interface**. When Stanley finally donned it, the covering reshaped itself into a narrow, pointed eye-mask adorned with **eight movable chromatic gems**. Its response to his wishes offered the first tangible sign that its form could change to suit its bearer.[^mask-interface]

The journal attributed extraordinary abilities to the mask and house together: flight, travel through space, a smaller form encompassing the villa and its occupants, and a vessel configuration able to provide air. Yet the distinction between promise and operation remained sharp. Stanley's first attempt to raise the house produced only a creak and resistance. The mask had not yet established the necessary bond with him, and further nights of attunement were said to be required. Its possible influence over chance remained an entirely separate, untested power.[^mask-limits]

## Season Two — actual ship operation and remaining limits

The **four-day attunement eventually completes by S2E01**, after the S1E128 discussions; Villa Or'Nath **actually transforms into a flying elven spelljammer**. In **S2E02**, now named **[Fate Six](fate-six.md)**, it flies atmospheric routes and from Earth to Mars with a ghost crew, living passengers and a demonstrated void-protective envelope. This **supersedes** the earlier E126/E128 “no flight yet” source cutoffs without rewriting those historical observations. The mask's **Peer into Plurality**, claimed time/number manipulation, cost/reset rules, and the vessel's **shrinking-to-dime** mode remain **unperformed/unverified** in these two episodes. Stanley **briefly blinded by enemy flash despite *Foresight*, later recovers**, and his blind *Eldritch Blast* **physically kills first Slow Peter**. ## S3E07 — at-will vessel/cottage transformation actually tested, vessel destroyed

In [played S3E07](../../episodes/S3E07.md), the GM explicitly checks the attuned mask's **Fate Six transformation clause** and confirms that the original **four-day attunement is NOT needed on every change between ship and cottage**. Stanley **ACTUALLY TRANSFORMS airborne Fate Six INTO A FALLING COTTAGE**, causing the ghost crew to disappear temporarily and Vorel to be momentarily exposed to the fall; he **IMMEDIATELY TRANSFORMS THE COTTAGE BACK INTO FATE SIX**, catching Vorel safely. This was **real at-will use but failed to evade** an incoming dragon/eight Paladins. ## S3E14 — Fate Six requires an external planar Gate

In [played S3E14](../../episodes/S3E14.md), the GM clarifies the question left unresolved in S3E13: [Fate Six](fate-six.md) can travel through space within the Prime Material plane, but **cannot cross between different planes on its own**. It needs to find and traverse an **external planar Gate**. This is a confirmed ship-navigation limitation, not evidence of a new mask-powered trip to Sigil; the later [S3E15](../../episodes/S3E15.md) Sigil portals are the Principal's magic, not Fate Six's own travel.

## Latest observed custody and attunement

**At the S3E18 finale, Hope is the witnessed owner and wearer** of the Mask of Eltanfawr, reshaped into a crown. The transfer happens **after** she has flown Fate Six back to Earth. The GM says that, following attunement, she **can command Fate Six and its ghost crew**; the episode does **not** show completion of that new attunement or her first mask-powered command as the new holder. Stanley's demonstrated *Foresight*, vessel summon and ship/cottage transformations belonged to his earlier attuned use, not powers visibly reactivated by Hope before the ending. Stanley leaves on a separate Elhandari vessel; no prior missing ghost crewmember is shown restored by the transfer. [Played S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md) · [Fate Six](fate-six.md).
