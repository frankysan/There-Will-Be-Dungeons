# Mask of Eltanfawr

## Introduction

The Mask of Eltanfawr is a dark, jewel-studded face covering discovered among the hidden possessions of [Stanley Billings's](../characters/stanley-billings.md) father. It rested in the impossible upper study of [Villa Ornath](../places/villa-ornath.md), beside the [Book of Adjacencies](book-of-adjacencies.md), charts of the stars and a model of Earth. Fashioned from a nearly weightless black material, it had no ordinary straps and was said to fit itself to its wearer. Stanley took possession of it without yet putting it on.[^mask-discovery]

Its first described gift, **Peer into Plurality**, seemed to offer influence over the course of chance, but at a mounting cost to the wearer's mind and body. The description warned of grave consequences should that power prove fatal. Nothing about the mask's appearance revealed how these unusual gifts were connected, and none was demonstrated when it was found.[^mask-plurality]

## Development by arc

### Arc Five — The hidden vessel's command

After Elhandar's fall, the mask's second apparent purpose came to light. Stanley's father's responsive journal identified Villa Ornath as a concealed vessel capable, it claimed, of traveling between worlds. The mask was presented as its **telepathic command interface**. When Stanley finally donned it, the covering reshaped itself into a narrow, pointed eye-mask adorned with **eight movable chromatic gems**. Its response to his wishes offered the first tangible sign that its form could change to suit its bearer.[^mask-interface]

The journal attributed extraordinary abilities to the mask and house together: flight, travel through space, a smaller form encompassing the villa and its occupants, and a vessel configuration able to provide air. Yet the distinction between promise and operation remained sharp. Stanley's first attempt to raise the house produced only a creak and resistance. The mask had not yet established the necessary bond with him, and further nights of attunement were said to be required. Its possible influence over chance remained an entirely separate, untested power.[^mask-limits]

## Season Two — actual ship operation and remaining limits

The **four-day attunement eventually completes by S2E01**, after the S1E128 discussions; Villa Ornath **actually transforms into a flying elven spelljammer**. In **S2E02**, now named **[Fate Six](fate-six.md)**, it flies atmospheric routes and from Earth to Mars with a ghost crew, living passengers and a demonstrated void-protective envelope. This **supersedes** the earlier E126/E128 “no flight yet” source cutoffs without rewriting those historical observations. The mask's **Peer into Plurality**, claimed time/number manipulation, cost/reset rules, and the vessel's **shrinking-to-dime** mode remain **unperformed/unverified** in these two episodes. ## Current status

The mask is associated with Stanley's activated **Fate Six**, which **actually flies from Earth to Razak Val and then to Mars in S2E02**. Its initial four-day bond requirement is met between the S1 finale and S2E01's morning ship transformation. The dangerous **Peer into Plurality** option and claimed **miniaturization** remain untested; confirmed flight does not establish them. At the latest reviewed cutoff Stanley and companions are **on Mars**, the ship landed there.[^mask-status]
