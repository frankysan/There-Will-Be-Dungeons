# Fate Six — Villa Ornath's revealed spelljammer

**The Wastes • first visible transformation [S2E01](../../episodes/S2E01.md), first real voyage and naming [S2E02](../../episodes/S2E02.md).** Main-party vessel owned/captained by **Stanley Billings (Valere Ornath)**, created by his father from the family [Villa Ornath](../places/villa-ornath.md) and associated with the [Mask of Eltanfawr](mask-of-eltanfawr.md). **Source confidence:** text-only, ASR-provisional; ship name itself confirmed as an enacted in-play christening. See [full S2E02 comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## Transformation and first voyage

While it was a cottage in Elhandar's forest and later crashed to Earth's desert, the Villa concealed a spelljammer. In **S2E01** it **actually rises into a wooden elven flying ship after four days of attunement**, the former cottage forming the **rear captain's quarters**. In **S2E02**, Stanley and Hope **learn controls**, fly from Earth across the **Dead Fingers** region to **Razak Val** (via a no-landing visual pass of damaged Tietkalla), **land** at the lizardfolk canyon, take on a **large contingent of willing lizardfolk**, ascend into the void and complete the ~**one-story-hour** flight to a **protected Mars jungle biosphere**, then **land**. This is the ship's first demonstrated interplanetary voyage in current review; there is **no confirmed later departure at the S2E02 cutoff**.

The ghost crew's first mate and Stanley's apparent **uncle Sever** introduces approximately **40–50 ghost elves**, magically bound to ship operation and unable to depart or touch mortals. Important roles include helmsman **Crooked Hand Row**, cook **Chef Durgonzola Mamsie**, ghost surgeon **Dr. Splenival Daffodil**, and an enormous one-eyed non-elven cabin boy **Gorgeous George(s)**. Full surnames and whimsical roster spellings remain audio/handout-unverified. **Twaddle, an 87-year-old elf**, agrees to serve as physical assistant to the ghost surgeon in S2E02, writes his farewell letter and travels by speeder with the party toward Mars council; **he has not yet boarded or worked as assistant**.

### Helm, protection and routes

Deck arcane ballistas, below-decks stores, captain's private lounge, engine controls, wheel, dials and elven glyph-keypad are observed on the tour. The helm can present an ethereal chart with only **Earth, Mars and Planet Nash** preprogrammed as actual destination coordinates in S2E02. The DM's briefly displayed additional red marker is an **interface mistake, not a fictional destination**. Arbitrary new destinations require correct coordinates; obtaining glyphs alone is **not proof of successful input or safe travel**. The ship's visible magical/rainbow exhaust and **blue protective shield** allow the crew and mortal passengers to breathe and survive void exposure. Sever's giant-space-hamster stories are explicitly **unwitnessed sailors' legends**, not an encounter.

### The father aboard

Beneath the engine, a physically strange chamber houses **a wooden carving resembling Delit/Dalit Ornath** that speaks as Stanley's father. The voice says Delit gave up his corporeal body after a mortal wound, leaving **his life essence to power the vessel**, and that Stanley's mother died. Sever states Delit is **the ship, not merely the carved face**, and that face provides a comprehensible means of contact. The presence, conversation, and GM-supplied resemblance are **played**, but exact ancient circumstances and the ship's possible access to privacy are not independently witnessed.

### S2E08 — still docked, costume supplies and father-ship testimony

During [S2E08](../../episodes/S2E08.md), Fate Six **remains docked at the enormous Pentel Weiss pyramid** as five travelers plan how to retrieve Badass T/Tungsten. **An actual ~two-hour exterior survey by ship** finds **no visible alternate pyramid entrance**, and the party comes back to the wharf. Uncle Sever supplies **Vorel's nonmagical sword and pirate jacket** and other hooded clothing; **Nash removes Rambunctious Bunch gang armor** for lack of proficiency, Stanley equips studded leather, Hope tests human-shape necklace and Union gets a hood. **Hope privately CONSULTS the vessel's carved father voice**, which claims **it originally recruited Tungsten to build the Triangulosphere** but **cannot determine his prison location**. They then **disembark, race into prison and five of them ACTUALLY jump into the intake shaft**, where Badass T proves alive; **shipbound ghost crew remains aboard, ship not moved beyond portal nor damaged**. Three orcs lost in S2E07 remain dead. [P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E08_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

### S2E07 — rift voyage, lightning losses, and Pentel Weiss

In [S2E07](../../episodes/S2E07.md), **Fate Six ACTUALLY leaves Blast Belly Rock with Tungsten's recovered coordinates**. Three newly recruited orcs come aboard, fear the ghost crew at first, and carry the treasury chest to Stanley's quarters. Ghost poker game lasts about five hours and leaves Stanley/Vorel approximately even. The mortal crew **completes a long rest**, during which Nash recovers old ability scores; Stanley's *Lesser Restoration* cures his Wisdom loss earlier that evening. The ship exits near a **pink/purple nebula**, sends an actual **laser pigeon** with a greeting (no response observed), then enters the lightning-cloud environment. **Hope pilots hazardous safe nodes**, massive apparently nonhostile three-eyed space-whales **conduct lightning into the hull and crew**; **all three recruited orcs DIE** (one impact, two overboard). Ghosts suffer harm too. **Stanley's megaphone feedback drives away one whale**.

Hope's **clown compass** points toward a **six-foot burning sphere**. Nash checks it, physically returns; **Hope actually pilots the whole ship THROUGH portal** and with Crooked Hand Row **prints new return coordinates on parchment, left aboard**. Fate Six docks at **Pentel Weiss's enormous pyramid** in internal pocket space. Shipbound ghost crew remain aboard, ready for escape; **four heroes + Union Wheel + Badass T disembark**. Fate Six remains docked at S2E07 cutoff, **with NO surviving Green Vale orc crew**. [Comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E07_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

### S2E06 — attempted seizure and three new orc crewmembers

The [S2E06](../../episodes/S2E06.md) confrontation on Blast Belly Rock begins while **Fate Six is docked**: **Green Vale marauders intend to seize it**, but are defeated before they take the vessel. **Three surviving orcs surrender and Stanley accepts them as crew**, with GM adding tokens to the ship's crew map. **These three are not the whole defeated warband.** **Badass T is recovered alive** from town cages; no new ghost crew status is conferred upon him or the orcs. The ship's captured/compromised status is **not** established; **no additional rift passage or departure occurs** by cutoff. Stanley has a numbered deposit box from Tungsten's bank desk, and **Hope has retrieved a parchment of Fate Six-compatible coordinates, which remain untested**. [P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E06_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

### S2E05 — actual second rift journey and Blast Belly Rock docking

Fate Six **actually leaves Planet Nash** in [S2E05](../../episodes/S2E05.md) with **Stanley, Nash, Hope, Vorel, Union Wheel and older alternate Badass T**. **Angelashi and four children STAY on Planet Nash**; do not carry the S2E04 aboard roster into this second voyage. Hope pilots with **Crooked Hand Row**, selects actual **Blast Belly Rock** coordinates, **enters and exits rift space after approximately a day's passage**, and **the ship physically docks on the old asteroid mining village of Burp**. The party has an **evening of elven mead and long rests** in transit; shipbound ghost Gorgeous George carries physical tray and cups **via door, not ethereally through deck**. Uncle Sever orders the crew to keep vessel ready for a rapid retreat, **but no departure from Blast Belly Rock occurs by episode end**. The four heroes, Union and Badass T disembark; **Badass T then stays at Wet Whistle**, others at Bank of Burp. No new fuel source, ship damage, transfer of the Triangulosphere or additional ghost crew recruitment is witnessed. [P/Q report](../../reports/WASTES_S2E05_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

### S2E04 — rift-space ship, no fuel and first new destination

[**S2E04**](../../episodes/S2E04.md) provides new **performed** capabilities: Fate Six has **no conventional fuel need**, its fey-enchanted wood sustained by **Delit Ornath's life essence**; if that essence failed the ship could lose power, **but no failure occurs**. Ghosts may **handle solid shipbound objects, food, barrels, doors and provisions** while bound to the vessel, although they **cannot touch mortals or leave it**. **Twaddle actually boards** on Mars to assist the ghost surgeon, with **no surgery shown**. The crew carries **Angelashi and all four Nash children aboard from Mars** (an explicit table continuity correction); Nash Jr is the youngest **daughter**, with unestablished object form.

**Hope is instructed in helm keypad and throttle controls** by Crooked Hand Row and **actually opens/enters/exits rift space**, after the S2E02 high-speed open-void voyage. The ship completes **a roughly six-hour Mars → Planet Nash rift journey** and lands on the two-asteroid settlement. **Blast Belly Rock coordinates are located and entered for map preview** but **NOT selected for the trip**, and the party **does not visit Blast Belly Rock or Pentel Weiss in S2E04**. The family and party disembark to Tower Nash; **Angelashi agrees to stay there to research alternate-Nash recruitment**, but no imported Nashes are aboard or in this timeline. The ship **remains landed at Planet Nash** at the S2E04 cutoff. [Two-export ASR](../../reports/WASTES_S2E04_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

### December 2021 creator Q&A — stored-energy ghost state

In the out-of-game [December 2021 bonus ~00:00–00:02](../../episodes/BONUS-2021-12.md), Beau explains that before the ship awakened the elven ghost sailors were **held as stored energy, in something like stasis**, comparing their existence to a compressed computer file. His afterthought that they might have been playing canasta is a **joke**, not an actual pre-awakening activity. This **creator explanation does not depict a newly played prequel scene or alter S2E01's visible activation**. [ASR audit](../../reports/BONUS_2021-12_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

### Name and cutoff

“Fate Six” is **discussed ~00:34** in reference to Fate Four / Fate Five, then Stanley **formally adopts the name aloud at ~01:25–01:28**, and the ghosts cheer its christening. The motivational explanation Stanley gives the ghost crew is improvised; it should not be used as an accurate historical inventory of exactly six prior ships. **Fate Six is the current name** from then on, not merely a joke or future plan. At the S2E02 cutoff, it is **landed on Mars** in the terraforming biosphere, with ghost crew aboard and the recruited lizardfolk on Mars exploring mud. No specific commitment to ferry Nash's family to Planet Nash has been made. [Dossier](../../episodes/S2E02.md).
