# Grape's magical guitar

> **The Wastes · first directly observed in E126, with later E128 and S3E12 evidence.** **Grape's magical guitar** is the owner-approved *provisional descriptive item label*, not a verified original item-sheet name. Its first owner's given name remains **Henitar/Henatar** in the ASR evidence; the later spoken name sounds like **“Axe of Gilgamar.”** These are one instrument, not three items. [Henitar Grape (provisional)](../characters/henitar-grape.md) · [Stanley](../characters/stanley-billings.md) · [E126 source dossier](../../episodes/E126.md). **Original audio/item card not checked.**

## E126 — The guitar's witnessed attacks and Stanley's seizure

The bounty commander uses an **oversized ornate floating electric guitar** with horn-like shapes, skull-shaped tuning pegs and an illuminated fretboard, controlled by his hands and also magically bound to him without a shoulder strap. As he plays, **sonic waves** batter **Diontalus** into a desert crater and shake even Villa Or'Nath's reinforced construction. During the combat, repeated chords can **charm targets into attacking the nearest being** (Hope and Vorel are compelled to turn on each other), and subject other creatures to sonic **psychic shredding damage**, knockdown or a series of **Magic Missiles**. Treat those as **specific GM adjudications**, not published generic spell text.

Henatar has **six recharging orbiting blue spheres** associated with his beads and defenses. They absorb a variety of incoming spells and emit attempted stunning pulses against close attackers, but **do not stop Nash's dragon acid breath**. Whether the spheres belong to the guitar or to separate equipment/abilities is **not established**, so do not make them inseparable from its ownership.

At **~02:29–02:33**, Stanley **casts Telekinesis on himself**, wins an **Arcana contest** against Henatar, and **pulls the floating guitar into his hands**. Stanley strums it without understanding it, **actually launching a Fireball which a blue sphere absorbs**. Henatar attempts to recover it by telekinetic force but loses another contest and retreats. This is a **witnessed temporary transfer/control**, not settled lawful ownership, successful attunement, destruction or guaranteed later custody. **Nash's second acid breath** subsequently destroys the commander's disk; Henatar falls **limp at ~02:41–02:42**, **not formally pronounced dead**. [E126](../../episodes/E126.md) · [ASR report](../../reports/E126_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md).

## E128 — Stanley retains and cautiously tests the guitar

**Henatar/Henitar Grape is explicitly killed by Nash in E128**, after surrendering—not by the previous E126 acid breath. At ~00:05 the wounded commander sees **the guitar strapped to Stanley's back** and pleads for its return; Stanley **does not give it back**. Around ~01:10 Stanley, alone in the desert, **actually plucks and softly plays the electric guitar**. The GM says it **magnifies/amplifies the sound** with a pleasant timbre but **does not produce the destructive shock/Fireball/charm effects on this attempt**; Stanley's lute skill helps with music but **no proficiency or attunement to the guitar's other powers is proven**. **Custody at the Season One finale: Stanley**, pending any future change. Hope's attempted removal of **one separate blue orb** blasts her away and destroys the sphere; **it does not prove the orbs belong to the guitar or that all were gathered**. [E128](../../episodes/E128.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/E128_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md). **ASR only.**

## S3E12 — “Axe of Gilgamar” is THIS electric guitar used as planned lightning rod

In [played S3E12](../../episodes/S3E12.md) ~00:07–00:10, while PRINCIPAL raises chrome dome during SOULBROKER'S NECROTIC-LIGHTNING ATTACK, STANLEY names an item sounding **“AXE OF GILGAMAR”** whose item-sheet powers purportedly **RECHARGE WHEN ATTACKED BY LIGHTNING MAGIC**. The GM explicitly identifies the **AXE AS THE ELECTRIC GUITAR TAKEN FROM HENATAR GRAPE**, NOT a new battleaxe or separate weapon: **STANLEY ACTUALLY PHYSICALLY EMBEDS GUITAR BODY INTO FORMING CHROME DOME with neck pointing OUTWARD as a LIGHTNING ROD**. This is ACTUAL placement, and the player asks it to catch shock/recharge, but **NO SPECIFIC CONFIRMED NUMBER OF NEW CHARGES, GUITAR ATTACKS, DAMAGE TO GUITAR OR SUCCESSFUL RECHARGE** subsequently measured or played. Exact phonetic “Gilgamar” vs established item-name spelling needs owner/item-sheet/audio; owner-approved provisional descriptive label remains Grape's magical guitar. [S3E12 ASR report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E12_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md), no audio.

## Later custody and verified effect boundaries

**Latest physically observed guitar placement:** In **S3E12**, Stanley **actually inserts the guitar body into the Principal's temporary chrome dome** with its neck pointing outward. That is a demonstrated *placement*, not proof the guitar was struck by a qualifying spell, gained charges or was subsequently retrieved when the dome disappeared. **E128 remains the last explicit personal carrying/strumming confirmation**; S3E12 is later physical possession and use as an intended lightning rod, with **subsequent physical custody unknown**.

**Demonstrated versus unverified mechanics:** Henatar's harmful musical compulsion, forceful notes and *Magic Missiles* are actually played in E126; Stanley himself **triggers a single *Fireball* that a blue sphere absorbs**, and later safely amplifies notes in E128. Neither establishes safe repeatable attacks or full item attunement in Stanley's hands. The **six orbiting magical spheres** have separate witnessed defensive/shocking effects, but **are not verified inseparable guitar components**. “Axe of Gilgamar” is the **S3E12 spoken/item-sheet identification**, not an owner-confirmed orthographic name or a second separate battleaxe. The supposed lightning-recharge rule remains **proposed/reported without a measurable successful recharge**. The maintained [S3E13](../../episodes/S3E13.md)–[S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md) dossiers do not document a new guitar activation, handover or finale inventory; silence does **not prove permanent loss or destruction**.
