# Fate 4 — Hope's vehicle of destiny

## Introduction

**Fate 4** began as a battered recreational vehicle, a neglected relic of the world before the Scorching. In the workshop cut into Dusthill's rocky enclosure, [Hope Akmenos](../characters/hope-akmenos.md) and the mechanic **Jimster** rebuilt it into something that could survive the Wastes. Enormous monster-truck wheels raised its small camper body above the ground. Its unmistakable prow bore a dragon's skull fashioned from metal; solar panels stood on the roof, while exterior rails, handholds and a rear ladder gave travelers ways to move over and around the vehicle.

Inside, the makeshift home was every bit as improvised as its exterior. Weapons filled the place where an ordinary front passenger seat might have been, with more tools and equipment in its compartments. Its removable skull-shaped gearshift, radios and magnet-mounted javelins mingled with water, stores, sleeping places, a hot plate and a toilet. It was at once Hope's workshop creation, a defensible vehicle and a home the companions could carry into hostile lands.[^introduction]

An owner-selected illustration depicts Fate 4 in Jimster's garage. It is an artistic reconstruction, not a photograph, measured design or independent evidence of the vehicle's features.[^illustration]

## Arc One — the road from Dusthill

Hope unveiled Fate 4 as the companions prepared to leave Dusthill in search of people taken by raiders. The vehicle offered a rare combination of mobility, shelter, storage and protection. Its size, unusual fittings and rough mechanical temperament made it an emblem of the party's determination to improvise their way through danger.

That usefulness came with the hazards of crossing the Wastes. A violent engine failure stopped it, and later damage to its wheels left it stranded as the party made do with other transport. Its eventual recovery and repair brought back not merely a means of travel but a recognizable part of the group's shared life.[^arc-one]

## Arc Two — a home patched together

Fate 4 endured increasingly punishing journeys. One explosion overturned it and ripped open the upper driver's-side roof and windshield region. Magic helped right the damaged body, but the breach still needed a practical repair: the travelers fastened a rough covering of raptor hide and scales across the opening, held together with stripped-bone thread. It kept the opening covered but could not bear someone's weight. More damage followed, including another opening in the wall.

For all its wounds, the vehicle remained a place where companions could shelter, quarrel, travel and make repairs while the larger world changed around them. Its patched and battered state was a record of the journeys it had survived.[^arc-two]

## Arc Three — the last journey

The companions later returned to Fate 4 after using other vehicles. Its interior suffered under the strains of carrying an increasingly unusual company, and a stop in wet sand left its enormous wheels struggling for traction. In the purple sands near Khaervilon, a colossal tyrannosaur finally brought the journey to an end. The beast crushed the camper body and sent its wheels flying. Fire and an explosion subsequently consumed what remained of the RV.

Some components survived to serve a different purpose. Wheels, bent axles, a recovered seat and the slain dinosaur's bones became a rough new conveyance, **Fang Fate Five**. That cart was a successor built from salvage, not a restoration of Fate 4.[^arc-three]

## E064–E067 — the original RV's return, destruction and separate successor

**E064 — returned to the party's presence, not simply recovered from storage.** After escaping Razak Val on **four borrowed riding raptors**, the companions hear an engine in the purple sands and **actually see their own Fate 4, now hotwired and driven by a large orc**. In the played post-credit continuation they identify **Follow, his daughter Sira and the driver** aboard. The original heroes **physically board Fate 4 and release their raptors**. Follow asks to borrow it later to protect his child, while Nash protests that it belongs to their group; **no permanent vehicle gift or settled transfer of ownership occurs** in that episode. [E064](../../episodes/E064.md) · [Riding raptors](../creatures/riding-raptors.md).

**E065 — Hope drives the still-operational RV until the cliffhanger.** The orc driver **Rorf** initially struggles to control it in the strange purple-sands rainstorm; **Hope actually takes over the wheel**. The vehicle becomes stuck in mud and **Nash uses *Mold Earth* to free it**. During the overnight stop near Stanley's *Tiny Hut*, a **T. rex approaches and nudges the vehicle**, but **neither a completed dinosaur attack nor the destruction of Fate 4 occurs before E065's cutoff**. [E065](../../episodes/E065.md).

**E066 — the RV is destroyed and its salvage becomes a different vehicle.** The enormous T. rex **stomps and crushes Fate 4** while Vorel tries to draw it toward Nash's pit. An **actual later explosion ignites the wreckage**; the original powered camper is **not operable or rescued as a complete vehicle**. Vorel retrieves **wheels, axles, a seat and other salvage**, combines these with the slain beast's **bones and improvised bindings** and actually builds a **handmade, nonpowered rickshaw called “Fang Fate Five”** (phonetic ASR variants remain). This is a **separate cart**, not an intact fifth model, a motorized Fate 4 rebuilt, or Fate Six. Hope subsequently finds it **unable to negotiate the tightly packed eyeball-tree forest**. The party proceeds through that region using a **different spectral horse-drawn carriage**, not a functioning Fang Fate Five or repaired RV. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

**E067 — recollection, not a later physical restoration.** Hope's encounter with Jimster and an earlier version of Fate 4 occurs **inside a subjective Dusthill memory/scene** during the supernatural carriage journey. It does **not undo the real E066 destruction**, return the original RV to Hope's possession or demonstrate new repairs. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md).

## Current status

**Fate 4 was destroyed.** Its original body no longer serves as a powered vehicle or traveling home. What survived of its metalwork and wheels entered the construction of Fang Fate Five; the completed RV, its work of invention and the journeys it carried remain part of Hope's and her companions' history.[^status]
