# Hand of the Voice

**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

## Identity, attachment and original-party separation

**Identity:** Living, severed supernatural hand attached to **Hope** in **E101**; **not** the Eye of the Voice or the detachable **Zorander's Fist gauntlet**. **Evidence:** Processed ASR, not independently heard.

In the White Tower crisis E101, Hope **cuts away her own hand** and fuses the separate **Hand of the Voice** to herself, changing her body and abilities. This action accompanies **Hope leaving the original party**, the end of Arc Four and beginning of Arc Five **within the same episode**. The gauntlet remains a physically distinct item: Nash reseals it in its case, and **Tobacho actually receives the gauntlet without the Hand in E106**. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [Hope](../characters/hope-akmenos.md).

The hooded Voice continues privately pressing Hope toward an **Eye of the Voice** lead. In E118 the Hand hurts and the Voice demands a trip to **Pentelvice (?)**, but the Eye is **not obtained**, nor is the Hand removed. The Voice's alleged motives and nature are not independently certified; the hand's influence should be distinguished from deliberate acts that Hope herself performs. [E113](../../episodes/E113.md) · [E118](../../episodes/E118.md).

## E123 — private dependency statement and an unperformed ring proposal

In a conversation **with Stanley, away from Nash/Vorel**, Hope says the Hand brings a **dead/forgotten god** who speaks and may withhold its powers, and **she believes removing it would kill her**. Neither removal, fatal consequence nor patron eavesdropping is tested. Stanley **apologizes and welcomes her back** while warning against corrupt promises of good intentions; he does not certify the artifact safe.

Hope separately describes a **travel ring** as a possible blood-cost cross-plane route to Stanley's father. **No ring use, blood expenditure or father summoning happens**. Her retrospective account does not replace E116's observed Gate/calling sequence. The Hand **stays attached**, the Eye remains unrecovered and no new patron deed is completed. [E123](../../episodes/E123.md) · [Hope](../characters/hope-akmenos.md).

## S2E04 — Nobieri's speculative containment/removal idea, not performed

Before leaving Mars on Fate Six, Hope **actually consults Nobieri** about earlier research into the living Hand. He reports an old **rumor of a powerful dead wizard whose life essence might persist in artifacts**, but **does not identify the Voice with that mage/lich as fact**. Nobieri guesses that **cutting off the Hand within a sufficiently strong lead-shielded or arcane-proof chamber** might contain backlash if there is an explosion; he expressly lacks the magical understanding to certify or execute such a remedy. Hope thanks and embraces him, **does not sever, disable or remove the Hand**, and still seeks the separate Eye. [S2E04](../../episodes/S2E04.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E04_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md). The artifact's identity and future consequences remain open.

## S2E05 — private advice and herald's alleged “Lord of Secrets”

On **Planet Nash** when the Chronos herald **Zolfin Sol(?)** examines Hope's attached Hand, **the Voice privately tells Hope** he is powerful, could be tamed later, and that Hope should **cooperate for now**. Other party members are **not told the private instruction** in the played scene. The herald **calls the presence “Lord of Secrets,” claims it is a powerful former mortal wizard who ascended to godhood**, deliberately avoids speaking a true name, and portrays **avarice/control** as its weakness. These are **NPC cosmological assertions**, not corroborated true identity, a performed domination or proof his future plan will succeed. **No Hand severance, Eye recovery or magical containment in S2E05**. ## S2E08 — scarred warden eye and disputed character knowledge

During [S2E08](../../episodes/S2E08.md), a **real gray-cloaked prison warden with a deeply cratered/scarred eye** physically manifests and refuses to supply collector-privileged inmate details. **Hope speculates he might have the separate Eye of the Voice**, but **neither appearance nor his refusal identifies the scar as that relic**, and Hope **does not obtain an Eye or sever her attached Hand**. GM directly **challenges whether Hope's in-character knowledge of an Eye is substantiated**, calls it potentially player **metagaming**, and invites listener recollection—there is **no settled in-world confirmation** in this scene. Keep these reservations alongside prior Hope-only Hand dialogues; do not treat the bonus/character sheet text as spoken in the campaign. Hope's **human necklace disguise drops inside prison**, and ***Darkness* spell fails**, but this does not prove the attached living Hand itself was removed. ## S3E02 — Principal physically STEALS the Hand, Hope survives and regrows an ordinary arm

In [played S3E02](../../episodes/S3E02.md), **the Principal grapples Hope, flows LIQUID CHROME across her attached Hand of Voice, speaks an ASR-uncertain incantation, and ACTUALLY SEVERS/SWALLOWS THE HAND**, which visibly disappears into the Principal's chrome palm/arm. Though the **item's written drawback says removing the Hand kills wearer**, Beau **ACTUALLY adjudicates Hope at ZERO HP and allowed death saving throws**, later ***Healing Word* REVIVES HER for 16 HP**. Do NOT write permanent death, claim she still possesses Hand, that the device exploded away irrecoverably, or that the Voice can still speak to her. The **Principal RETREATS THROUGH PORTAL WITH HAND IN HIS POSSESSION (~01:04)**, alive after acid, cannonfire and Stanley's *Disintegrate*; he names Solar Mines as possible future meeting. **After resting, Stanley ACTUALLY casts seventh-level *Regenerate* on Hope (~02:17–02:19)**, causing a **NEW NORMAL BIOLOGICAL ARM AND HAND** to form—not regrowing supernatural Voice Hand or restoring cold/*Finger of Death* powers. Hope experiences **ordinary bodily warmth, pleasure in food/coffee and sensory comfort** after loss of former artifact. The **separate Zorander's Fist GAUNTLET** had gone to Tobacho in E106; this theft concerns the **living attached Hand**, NOT the previously handed-off gauntlet, a collector ring, or Eye. The Principal's new story about its commissioning remains **NPC testimony**, not independently authenticated origin. [S3E02 ASR report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## Out-of-game creator commentary (not additional played transfers)

## February 2022 creator Q&A — provenance of Zorander's Fist

In the [February 2022 bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2022-02.md) (~00:34–00:37:53), Beau says the name **Euphemius/Euphemia Zorander** originated with **one of Beau's own earlier player characters in a different group**, and the Wastes figure is **not necessarily the same literal person**. The Wastes **Zorander's Fist** was **Beau's homebrewed gauntlet built to sit over the separate living Hand**, an antecedent Beau likens to Hope's ongoing possession and burdens. Beau wrote elaborate punch/uppercut/slam game rules and **hoped Kyle might wield the Fist**, but says this **did not happen in the campaign**. No rules text, verified damage or added abilities are reproduced in this recording. Canon remains that **Hope attached the living Hand in E101**, while **Tobacho received the separate, Hand-less gauntlet in E106**; no return, removal or new activation is performed in the bonus. Exact name ending **Euphemius vs Euphemia** needs audio/written reference. [Two-ASR audit](../../reports/BONUS_2022-02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## May 2022 BONUS — GM confirms the Principal originally commissioned Tobacho Fleur

In [May 2022 supporter bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2022-05.md) **~00:16:08–00:17:52**, **GM BEAU directly states, OUT OF GAME, that the PRINCIPAL WAS THE ORIGINAL CONTRACTOR BEHIND TOBACHO FLEUR'S REQUEST TO ACQUIRE THE LIVING HAND OF THE VOICE**. The Principal's own in-character S3E01 explanation thus receives a distinct source of **GM authorial corroboration**, not a physically played flashback of their contracting conversation. Beau says the villain planned to **recover the Hand and leave**, rather than kill all companions; he was more heavily wounded by their fight than expected. His **specific final purpose for the Hand / motive for cosmic chrome conversion is NOT fully disclosed**, and exact Eye/other piece custodianship remains unresolved. In **played S3E02**, the Principal **ACTUALLY PHYSICALLY STEALS THE HAND AND ESCAPES**. **NO NEW HAND TRANSFER/ACTIVATION IN THIS BONUS**, and **at played S3E05 cutoff Principal still last known holder, Hope has only her later ordinary regenerated arm**.
