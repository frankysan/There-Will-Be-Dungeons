# Book of Adjacencies

**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Item:** Responsive, apparently many-paged scientific notebook/journal of **Dalit/Daliot Ornath (?)**, Stanley's father. **Obtained:** E118, in the hidden study of an Elhandar cottage. **Evidence:** Two processed ASR exports, not independently audio verified.

In E118 a doctor **physically delivers Stanley his father's large key**. Stanley and Hope recite his childhood poem; the key floats into an invisible lock, and they enter an apparently impossible third-floor study. There Stanley is **authorized to add the Book of Adjacencies to his inventory**. Its pages respond to authorized spoken names/topics. This is a **real recovered object**, but it is not a demonstrated omniscient source: queries only reveal journal entries, and private pages aren't automatically known to every character. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Stanley](../characters/stanley-billings.md) · [Elhandar](../places/elhandar.md).

The father writes of an apparent anomalous **N4SH (?)** and speculates about the **Weir** intruding on Earth, temporal “adjacency,” future **Principle** technologies and a region around the **Solar Mines**. The book, letter and marked globe are observed; **the physical theory, foresight and reported future events are unverified character-authored assertions**. A separate jeweled **Mask of Elton IV (?)** is also inventoried. [Mask](mask-of-elton-iv.md) · [E118 ASR comparison](../../reports/E118_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

**Open:** the father's correct personal spelling, dating and provenance of entries, identity of N4SH, and whether any theory survives later direct observation. [Ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
