# Book of Adjacencies

**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Item:** Responsive, apparently many-paged scientific notebook/journal of **Delit Or'Nath**, Stanley's father. **Obtained:** E118, in the hidden study of an Elhandar cottage. **Evidence:** Two processed ASR exports, not independently audio verified.

## E118 — key, hidden study and responsive journal actually acquired

In E118 a doctor **physically delivers Stanley his father's large key**. Stanley and Hope recite his childhood poem; the key floats into an invisible lock, and they enter an apparently impossible third-floor study. There Stanley is **authorized to add the Book of Adjacencies to his inventory**. Its pages respond to authorized spoken names/topics. This is a **real recovered object**, but it is not a demonstrated omniscient source: queries only reveal journal entries, and private pages aren't automatically known to every character. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Stanley](../characters/stanley-billings.md) · [Elhandar](../places/elhandar.md).

The father writes of an apparent anomalous **N4SH (?)** and speculates about the **Weir** intruding on Earth, temporal “adjacency,” future **Principal** technologies and a region around the **Solar Mines**. The book, letter and marked globe are observed; **the physical theory, foresight and reported future events are unverified character-authored assertions**. A separate jeweled **Mask of Eltanfawr (earlier “Elton IV”)** is also inventoried. [Mask](mask-of-eltanfawr.md) · [E118 ASR comparison](../../reports/E118_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

**Evidence boundary:** The **key moves, hidden stairs open, Stanley handles and acquires the journal, and queried pages physically respond** in E118. The father's letter **is genuinely read**, but the alleged foreknowledge of his own death, unknown `N4SH` visitor and theories of Weir-induced time anomalies remain **written first-person testimony**; the apparent red-X/Solar Mines globe correspondence is a **map observation**, not a witnessed time journey or confirmed future event. Hope does **not automatically learn every Elvish/private page** when Stanley opens the Book. [E118 dossier](../../episodes/E118.md).

## E123 — shared journal concerns, not new verified pages

Stanley tells the group about his father's **future-Nash visitor and possible Nash/Principal links**, but **Nash=Principal remains Vorel's speculation**. The GM's dashboard includes an unclear father-related **Prime** phrase. In a later private conversation Stanley questions his father's foresight and physically examines **his dictionary's wear**; he does **not read a new journal page or prove the dictionary's temporal owner**. Hope's offered blood-cost search for his father is unperformed. [E123](../../episodes/E123.md).

## Out-of-game authorial commentary — October 2020

**October 2020 bonus — origin outside the fiction:** Beau explains that he originally wrote a speculative note **about branching time for his own curiosity, apart from the campaign**, and later adapted it into an entry in **Stanley's father's Book of Adjacencies**. The model that proliferating alternatives resemble **“time barf”** is an intentional **authorial theme**, not verified cosmology, correct physics, or proof that every claim in Dalit's journal is true.
