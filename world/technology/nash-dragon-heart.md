# Nash Maggard's engineered dragon heart

**Object:** Implanted **dragon-metal/mechanical heart engine** in Nash Maggard's body, connected to his earlier floating-heart survival, unusual black-dragon transformations and episodic magical reactions. **Latest direct inspection:** **E121**. [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [Nash](../characters/nash-maggard.md) · [Chronology](../CHRONOLOGY.md).

## Prior evidence and boundaries

Nash's implanted heart and regeneration have long been observed and debated, but early experiences of **floating in fire** and claims involving **N4SH**, his origins and **Solar Mines** were not verified explanations of its manufacture. The father-of-Stanley **Book of Adjacencies** makes time-adjacency speculations, and neither it nor prior creator Q&A establishes the heart's complete provenance. ## E121 — the heart visibly opens

During a duel with the returned **Diontalus**, Nash ***Polymorphs* into an adult black dragon** and later suffers uncontrolled fear, fire and acid; he falls unconscious into a **private vision of a heart among flames** and hears a voice calling for its mother. An intervening **scarred black dragon** identifies itself as the **same Earth-desert prisoner** the party once met, says the implanted heart bears **its mother's recognizable draconic signature**, and asks for a meeting. Its account of prior attempts to reach Nash and its ancient-dragonflight rescue plan is **testimony**, not verified causal science.

Nash calls a name provisionally heard as **Kaz Desiree / Cas de Jurey (?)**; **his black-dragon torso opens and the enlarged mechanical heart physically emerges, covered with gauges**, projecting an **orange-gold spectral dragon** who actually caresses the dying-looking black dragon and sings to him. This is a **performed, witnessed apparition**, and a major upgrade from earlier in-world theories, but does **not** prove whether the heart contains the mother's complete consciousness, merely an echo/signature, a technology-mediated summoning, or some other relationship. The son's name sounds **Yoshalar / Shallar (?)**.
