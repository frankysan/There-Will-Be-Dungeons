# Nash Maggard's engineered dragon heart

**Object:** Implanted **dragon-metal/mechanical heart engine** in Nash Maggard's body, connected to his earlier floating-heart survival, unusual black-dragon transformations and episodic magical reactions. **Latest direct inspection:** **E122**. [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [Nash](../characters/nash-maggard.md) · [Chronology](../CHRONOLOGY.md).

## E128 — the *natural* black-dragon heart physically recovered for Mars

The natural **black dragon's massive extracted heart**—a **separate item** from Nash's still-implanted engineered pacemaker—was buried under/near a cave spike in **E122** and was still missing at E126 cutoff. In **[E128](../../episodes/E128.md)** ~00:28–00:33, Stanley informs High Elf Amanthalodar of the buried heart; he **orders** an Elhandari retrieval platoon to search Elhandar's collapsed mountain. **At ~01:05, Admiral Chrysorius expressly confirms the heart has been recovered**, and the surviving elves **carry it aboard the ships actually departing for the Mars terraforming station** with refugees and families. Do not confuse the **initial order** with the **later completed recovery**. **Ruby / Rubistic Triangulosphere remains unbuilt**, and missing engineer **Tungsten Wheel** has not been found; **Dunglestar Ravensbread reportedly on Mars** might supply his location. The dragon's proposal to seal Earth at its hottest point remains **untested**, though the GM offers a late **out-of-character hint** that dragonkind's advice may be the strongest. No removal of Nash's implanted heart, teleportation of the PCs to Mars or completed planetary seal occurs. [Report](../../reports/E128_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md).

## Prior evidence and boundaries

Nash's implanted heart and regeneration have long been observed and debated, but early experiences of **floating in fire** and claims involving **N4SH**, his origins and **Solar Mines** were not verified explanations of its manufacture. The father-of-Stanley **Book of Adjacencies** makes time-adjacency speculations, and neither it nor prior creator Q&A establishes the heart's complete provenance. ## E121 — the heart visibly opens

During a duel with the returned **Diontalus**, Nash ***Polymorphs* into an adult black dragon** and later suffers uncontrolled fear, fire and acid; he falls unconscious into a **private vision of a heart among flames** and hears a voice calling for its mother. An intervening **scarred black dragon** identifies itself as the **same Earth-desert prisoner** the party once met, says the implanted heart bears **its mother's recognizable draconic signature**, and asks for a meeting. Its account of prior attempts to reach Nash and its ancient-dragonflight rescue plan is **testimony**, not verified causal science.

Nash calls a name provisionally heard as **Kaz Desiree / Cas de Jurey (?)**; **his black-dragon torso opens and the enlarged mechanical heart physically emerges, covered with gauges**, projecting an **orange-gold spectral dragon** who actually caresses the dying-looking black dragon and sings to him. This is a **performed, witnessed apparition**, and a major upgrade from earlier in-world theories, but does **not** prove whether the heart contains the mother's complete consciousness, merely an echo/signature, a technology-mediated summoning, or some other relationship. The son's name sounds **Yoshalar / Shallar (?)**. ## E122 — implanted engine versus harvested natural heart

The mother communicates with Nash through **purring and emotional impressions**, including a negative response to being his biological mother. The dying black dragon's **Dragon Sons/Clench/Principle manufacture story and 72/83 donor estimate are attributed testimony**, not direct inspection of Nash's creation. The clause **“that she's you”** is unclear ASR and cannot settle their identity. **When the black dragon dies (~00:40–00:42), the mother returns into Nash's pacemaker, his body closes and heals, and he later resumes human form**. His engineered heart remains implanted.

**A second, distinct heart:** Vorel extracts the dead dragon's **natural black, glassy dragon-metal heart**, roughly **five feet tall**; mass is estimated first as **a couple of tons**, later **five tons**, not measured. The dragon asks for elven construction of a **rubistic/robustic triangulosphere (?)** and melting at Earth's hottest point to finish sealing it against the Weir. **Instructions, expected effects and engineering feasibility remain untested**.

Stanley uses the **rejoined one-minute movable sphere wand** to place that natural heart into Nash's *Mold Earth* cavity **beneath/near a seven-foot black metal spike in the rear cave (~01:35–01:38)**. The spike causes worsening head pressure but is **not identified as a named item**; “staff of disruption” is party shorthand. Nash seals/disguises the earth; **the natural heart and spike remain there as the four leave**. The rest of the dead dragon dissipates. **No mother transfer into the harvested heart, heart delivery, device construction or successful planetary seal occurs**. ## E123 — Ruby is named, not constructed

The GM's dashboard repeats the proposed **impossible-geometry, perpetually moving sealing object**. Vorel calls it **“Ruby” (~01:10:35)** and Stanley accepts that shorthand. The Solar Mines are proposed as a hot destination, **not verified as the required site**. **No elven blueprint, circle sigil, heart retrieval or device construction occurs**; the natural dragon heart **remains buried in the cave**, distinct from Nash's implanted pacemaker.
