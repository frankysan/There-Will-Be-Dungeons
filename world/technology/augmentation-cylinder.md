# Elhandari augmentation cylinder — Mars underground facility

**First played source:** [Wastes S3E03 — Hammer Giggle](../../episodes/S3E03.md) **~02:32:28–02:42:20**, within an **underground laboratory accessible by elevator inside the great terraforming tree on Mars**. Not a version of the **Rubistic Triangulosphere**. [Mars/Triangulosphere](rubistic-triangulosphere.md) · [World items](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md). **Processed-ASR source, no original audio verification.**

**Physical apparatus:** A large cylindrical room with doctors, glass observation office, a bed angled approximately 45 degrees, straps/chains to keep patient still, and **multiple (roughly 8–12) instrument arms from ceiling**. Admiral **Chrysorius(?)** says science/neuroscience/biology salvaged from Elhandar enabled this apparatus; his account of design provenance is attributed speech, while **room and machine are directly shown**.

## S3E03 — proposed augmentation and equipment

**What is OFFERED:** Surgery may revise **which spells and other in-class features the heroes know/use** and provide **level 15** advancement from existing level 14. Player/GM discussion restricts **race, base class and subclass changes**; fighter fighting style and eligible spell choices could change. Separate general Elhandari magical **+1/+2 weapons and armor** are offered; **specific selected or issued replacement items not documented here**. The GM invites decisions in the following session; Vorel considers **but does not perform** Eye-of-Groxl removal, and Nash **refuses** replacing his dragon-metal heart. **At the end of S3E03 none of the four have been operated on, no class features are actually swapped and level-up is a future choice**.

**S3E03 stopping point:** The party ends the episode examining screens and preparing selections. No machine procedure or character-feature swap has been performed at that cutoff. [S3E03 P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S3E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S3E04 — three completed operations

[Played S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md) ~00:03:45–00:32:43: **STANLEY, HOPE and NASH actually consent, are sedated, undergo an eight-hour operation and awaken with GM-confirmed NEW LEVEL-15 POWERS** (exact spell/feature choices NOT all spoken). **VOREL explicitly REFUSES and departs**; GM later informally calls him “level 15,” but no surgery for him was played and his sheet advancement needs checking. The first three need rest afterward; no removal of Eye of Groxl or Nash heart. [S3E04 audit](../../reports/WASTES_S3E04_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

**Separate equipment allocation:** The surgery does not itself account for later issued magic gear. In [S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md) Stanley selects a +2 rapier and +2 studded leather armor; Hope selects a +2 rapier, +2 half plate and +2 shield; Nash selects a +2 quarterstaff and obtains GM approval for a *Cloak of Protection*. Stanley separately collects a +2 longsword and Hope a +2 shield **for absent Vorel**, without a witnessed handover **within S3E04**; the sword's later delivery and shield's later use are documented below.[^augmentation-equipment]

## S3E05–S3E06 — Vorel receives gear without undergoing surgery

The separately collected equipment has its own later custody evidence. In [played S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md), **Stanley actually gives Vorel the +2 longsword acquired for him**, and Vorel acknowledges and practices with it aboard Fate Six. In [S3E06](../../episodes/S3E06.md), Vorel **explicitly says he has the +2 shield equipped** and later **draws the +2 longsword to kill the Umbilical Order Paladin**. The precise intervening **shield hand-to-hand transfer remains unnarrated**, but its carried/equipped status is no longer merely proposed by S3E06. These later physical equipment observations **are not a fourth augmentation surgery**, and the refusal in S3E04 remains intact.

## S3E13 and S3E18 — separate heart and chrome transformations, not cylinder results

In [S3E13](../../episodes/S3E13.md), **Brett/Asmodeus removes and retains Nash's engineered dragon-metal heart in Hell** and builds a beating heart from Nash's **own flesh**. Nash permits that intervention; it is **not a belated Elhandari cylinder operation**, did not occur in S3E04 and does not alter the original three-person surgery count. Nash survives with restored ordinary-heart function, though successful full dragon transformation is not shown after the extraction. [Nash's heart history](nash-dragon-heart.md).

In the [S3E18 finale](../../episodes/S3E18.md), the lunar **Council of Faces forcibly changes Nash into a chrome successor to the Principal despite his resistance**; this is a **different machine, location, agent and involuntary process**. Nash retains agency, escapes the lunar facility and **actually dies as his chrome body melts**. Stanley later uses ***Wish*/*True Resurrection* on Planet Nash to restore his familiar living body**. No Elhandari cylinder is operated, revisited, or identified as the cause of those events; **do not attribute coercive chrome conversion, Nash's death, his resurrection or any unobserved permanent medical effect to the consensual S3E04 augmentation**.

## Lasting result and unresolved level status

The cylinder is **actually used on Stanley, Hope and Nash**, who awaken from an eight-hour procedure with GM-confirmed level-15 powers. **Vorel does not undergo the operation.** His precise character-sheet level cannot be settled from that refusal alone: the GM later describes him as “level fifteen” during S3E04, but no compensating cylinder procedure or special XP award is witnessed; later creator discussion about a possible bonus level is retrospective, not an awarded level.[^augmentation-vorel] No operation removes Vorel's Eye of Groxl or Nash's dragon-metal heart.
