The GM invites decisions in the following session; Vorel considers **but does not perform** Eye-of-Groxl removal, and Nash **refuses** replacing his dragon-metal heart. **At the end of S3E03 none of the four have been operated on, no class features are actually swapped and level-up is a future choice**.
