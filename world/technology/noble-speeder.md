## E125 — three additional speeders on the escape route

After the station's assault begins, the household exits the central spire via **Rainbow Road**. The group finds **six noble speeders parked near the biodome terminal**, with **no explanation of who placed them there**. Three are driven—one each by **Stanley, Hope and Lyrielle**—to **Villa Or'Nath**, where Lyrielle's actual ***Wall of Force*** catches falling biodome wreckage. **This does not mean the earlier borrowed Lodge speeder was somehow recovered from the villa or that the new parked machines are legally owned by the party**. All occupants reach the villa but the later impact scene does not establish survival thereafter. [E125](../../episodes/E125.md) · [comparison](../../reports/E125_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md).

## E124 — villa return and vehicle left behind

**Hope pilots the four from Harbor to Villa Or'Nath**, with Stanley behind, Vorel forward and Nash towed as before. Once the household packs, the group **deliberately leaves the speeder at the villa (~01:30:12)** and walks to Rainbow Road. **Latest custody/location: left at Villa Or'Nath**, not central reception or off station. No refueling, measured range, ownership transfer or return to lodge occurs. ## Identity and permission

The lodge custodian **Tom/Tomsilar Bear (?)** says an earlier hunting group left the vehicle in the shed around a year ago and never returned for it. He allows the party to use it, but **does not establish his ownership or permanently gift it**. His attribution of its repulsing surfaces to local mages is **NPC testimony**, separate from the vehicle's demonstrated operation.

Hope inspects a heavy, ornately carved **elderwood slab**, with elven faces/leaves, two forward poles, handlebars with squeeze grips, a padded seat, a fuel-cap-like fitting, foot controls and a **gemstone activation switch**. It has **no wheels** and floats with glowing surfaces beneath. **Fuel identity, remaining supply, maximum capacity and measured speed/range are unknown**. The party finds no fuel in the shed.

## Demonstrated use in E123

At **~01:38:50**, Hope activates it, fails a Dexterity save and is thrown off for **8 bludgeoning damage**; the vehicle **breaks through the barn wall and lands in trees**. It remains usable. Later **Hope pilots with Stanley as passenger**, testing fast travel, steering and braking through the forest, then returns to the lodge. The GM characterizes it as fast and dangerous, **without a calibrated speed**.

Next morning the final arrangement is **Hope driving, Stanley behind her, Vorel crouched on the forward poles facing backward**, distributing the weight. **Nash floats in his existing garment**, towed by a secure **upper-body/chest harness with roughly seven feet of slack**. Stanley's **Sleight of Hand 25** secures it. A three-foot line and foot-only tether are discussed but not adopted; the group **never tests all four seated together**, so towing does not prove four passengers are categorically impossible.

Hope gradually starts, increases speed and follows the roads to **the Harbor**, arriving after roughly **fifty story-minutes (~02:22–02:26)**. No journey crash occurs. Casual map-hex/mile/minute estimates are **not an independently measured vehicle specification**. Nobieri later loosens Nash's knots when Hope injures a finger trying to untie him.

**E123 custody:** Vehicle **at the Harbor**, party using it with the custodian's permission; **Nash untied**. No destruction, fuel refill, legal ownership transfer, Earth/Planet Nash trip or return to the lodge has been shown.
