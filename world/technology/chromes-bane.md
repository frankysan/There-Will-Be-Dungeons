# Chrome's Bane — Vorel's named +2 longsword

**Identity:** The **+2 longsword assigned to Vorel by the Elhandari on Mars**, physically received/practiced with on Fate Six in [Wastes S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md). **New self-given weapon name “Chrome's Bane” FIRST SPOKEN by Vorel over radio in [S3E07](../../episodes/S3E07.md) ~00:07–00:09**. Distinct from Vorel's older flame swords and from his **personal title/epithet register** ([Vorel titles](../characters/vorel-titles.md)). Two independent ASR versions support the words; exact phonetics not checked against original audio.

**Played provenance:** In [S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md) Stanley collects a new +2 longsword intending it for Vorel, who did not attend augmentation, while Hope collects a +2 shield. By S3E05 on Fate Six Vorel explicitly **handles and practices with the new sword**, establishing physical receipt. At the **Solar Mines north gate in S3E06**, Vorel **unsheathes this +2 longsword and actually kills the Umbilical Order Paladin** with two hits (~29 slashing). He considers naming it but **does NOT speak a name in S3E06**. At the opening of **S3E07**, Vorel **actually names it “Chrome's Bane”** and proclaims that name to the allied radio channel. Later in S3E07 after falling from/clinging to the flying patchwork chrome dragon, Vorel **strikes it twice with Chrome's Bane for ~40 magical slashing and KILLS the dragon**, with *Feather Fall* and Nash's *Enlarge* supporting his position. After the vast Groxl destroys Fate Six, **Vorel still holds/draws the named sword on landing by Groxl's foot**; no transfer, destruction or special additional sword power is demonstrated.

**S3E08 decisive usage:** During [S3E08](../../episodes/S3E08.md), Vorel repeatedly **ACTUALLY ATTACKS THE GIANT'S CUTICLE** using Chrome's Bane, then is **ACTUALLY SWALLOWED** and uses its blade to grip/cut the inner esophagus. He discovers electrified cabling embedded in the giant's **heart/aorta**, suffers **92 lightning damage** on his failed strike, then **SELF-PROCLAIMS new title “Vorel, Savior of the Universe”** and deals **46 MAGICAL SLASHING with two Chrome's Bane strikes, severing the heart cabling and PHYSICALLY KILLING/TOPPLING the giant's body**. This confirms a **named +2 sword delivered an important physical finishing blow**, NOT that the blade has special god-killing properties. Vorel **FLIES OUT OF THE CORPSE IN BLOOD SURGE and remains FALLING** before S3E08 cutoff, sword's precise final post-impact location not verified. [S3E08 report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E08_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md), no audio.

**S3E09 actually retained after return from Endless Dunes:** In [played S3E09](../../episodes/S3E09.md), Vorel **ACTUALLY WAKES ALIVE IN SOLAR MINES camp at 1 HP** after an apparent Endless Dunes meeting with the defeated giant **GROXL**, and **CHECKS HIS ITEMS: GM CONFIRMS CHROME'S BANE / SWORD IS STILL IN HIS HAND**, the **Eye of Groxl still in his chest**. This resolves S3E08 ejection/fall as **physical survival and sword retention**, not recovery by an unknown third party or weapon lost in dead giant. Groxl recognizes Vorel with new rival-conferred personal epithet **“Slayer of the God Killer”**; distinct from the weapon's S3E07 given name. Sword is not shown wielded for a decisive named duel in the Walmart, Vorel instead uses flame/rage. Party later **descends freight elevator**, precise equipment checks below not observed. [S3E09 report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E09_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md), audio unverified.

**Boundaries:** “Chrome's Bane” names this individual piece of equipment **at S3E07**, not Vorel himself, not a formally bestowed military rank, and not automatically a sword that uniquely bypasses chrome or kills gods. The documented magical enhancement is **+2**; extraordinary damage in S3E07 also includes Nash's *Enlarge* bonus and the GM's combat adjudication. No audio verified.
