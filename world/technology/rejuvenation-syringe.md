# Rejuvenation syringe — Elhandari rest booster

**First observed/allocated:** [S3E03 — Hammer Giggle](../../episodes/S3E03.md) **~02:36:35–02:37:55**, offered by Admiral **Chrysorius(?)** in Mars prewar technology bay. Sometimes described at table as **“rest in a can,” “booster,” “rejuvenation syringe.”** [Items](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [augmentation cylinder](augmentation-cylinder.md).

## S3E03–S3E05 — four doses allocated, none administered

**Played GM equipment instruction:** Unlike the **offered but unselected** generic magic weapons and augmentation surgery, the GM **explicitly instructs EVERY ONE OF FOUR ORIGINAL HEROES to ADD A SYRINGE to inventory**; only **four doses** exist. **Originally assigned recipients: Nash, Hope, Stanley and Vorel, one each**[^syringe-allotment-pre77]; no physical passing-hand animation described, but inventory allotment is authoritative tabletop instruction. **NO DOSE ADMINISTERED by S3E03, S3E04 or S3E05 ending.** In [S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md) ~00:34–00:41 Elhandari again supply “long rest in a can”; by ~01:39 Vorel is reminded someone collected one for him. **No injection, consumed dose, actual five-year lifespan loss or reallocation of four allotted doses is depicted.** During [S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md) the party actually flies to Earth and begins a costly fight with two Slow Peters but **NONE of the four allotted syringes is used despite ordinary hit-point/fire damage**.

## S3E10 — discussed and preserved, ordinary short rest instead

**S3E10 update — still unused:** During [played S3E10](../../episodes/S3E10.md) **~00:32–00:38**, the party asks whether the syringes work instantly. GM **explicitly confirms INJECT AS AN ACTION (including IN COMBAT) with immediate LONG-REST resources**, possible **hangover and loss of about FIVE years** (none physically measured). **BOTH NASH AND VOREL EXPLICITLY DECIDE TO KEEP THEIR OWN INJECTION FOR LATER/ACTUAL CRITICAL COMBAT**, not use now. **PARTY INSTEAD TAKES ORDINARY SHORT REST and spends HIT DICE; STANLEY ACTUALLY CASTS 8th-level *HEAL* 90HP on VOREL**. **NO DOSE ADMINISTERED and no HP, spell slot, lost life-years or recharge from this syringe occurs by S3E10 cutoff**. [S3E10 P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S3E10_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md); no audio checked.

## S3E11 — natural long rest, no injections

**S3E11:** All four **ACTUALLY COMPLETE A FULL EIGHT-HOUR LONG REST** in the Principal's guest rooms beneath Solar Mines after negotiated ceasefire; **NO dose of the four syringes is administered to anyone, no years of life lost**, and ***Heroes' Feast* is newly cast by STANLEY after the overnight rest (separate resource)**. Their retained syringes are NOT a replacement for the observed natural long rest. [S3E11 source](../../episodes/S3E11.md) · [P/Q report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E11_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md), audio unchecked.

## S3E17 — Stanley injects his own dose

During the [played S3E17 battle](../../episodes/S3E17.md), Stanley **injects a rejuvenation syringe as an action** and receives the GM-awarded immediate long-rest benefits, restored spell slots and a character-level increase. The estimated cost of about five years of lifespan is **not measured as an observed biological effect**. This witnessed use supersedes the earlier *none used through S3E11* status, without showing that Nash, Hope or Vorel use theirs or establishing the exact remaining party inventory.[^syringe-s3e17]

### Separately reported second syringe: still unspent

**A separate unspent syringe is reported:** The maintained S3E17 dossier distinguishes **Stanley's own dose, which he injects**, from **a second syringe Vorel earlier gave him**; the P/Q comparison also identifies a separate retained dose. The earlier handoff's time and physical circumstances have **not been independently located in the preceding maintained episode dossiers**, so this is a retrospectively reported custody detail, **not** a newly witnessed handoff or a fifth dose beyond the original four. The extra syringe is **not shown injected in S3E17**. [S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md) does not establish any later syringe consumption or a complete final inventory.[^syringe-second-held]

## Mechanics and last evidenced campaign status

**Mechanics as spoken:** Use as **one action** (injection, described into leg) for **all benefits of long rest** without waiting for prolonged battlefield rest. Side effect described as **possible/estimated shortening of remaining lifespan by around FIVE YEARS** per use, not a witnessed numerical reduction. No source proof of repeatability, natural expiration, alternative healing or exact interaction with dragon-heart immortality. Distinguish **assigned syringe** from **used syringe**, and do not backdate it to Mars visit S2E02. [P/Q comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

**S3E18 and final accounting:** The [played finale](../../episodes/S3E18.md) does not separately depict an additional injection, verify a final allocation of the remaining syringes, or measure any eventual lifespan loss. **One actual dose administered** (Stanley's own in S3E17) is confirmed. The additional dose **reported in Stanley's possession after having been given by Vorel** is a different, **unspent** item drawn from the context of the **four originally assigned doses**; its precise handover scene is still not verified. Do not convert the report into a fifth syringe, a second injection, an independently demonstrated physical handoff, or a guaranteed final inventory.
