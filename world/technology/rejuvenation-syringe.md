# Rejuvenation syringe — Elhandari rest booster

**First observed/allocated:** [S3E03 — Hammer Giggle](../../episodes/S3E03.md) **~02:36:35–02:37:55**, offered by Admiral **Chrysorius(?)** in Mars prewar technology bay. Sometimes described at table as **“rest in a can,” “booster,” “rejuvenation syringe.”** [Items](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [augmentation cylinder](augmentation-cylinder.md).

**Played GM equipment instruction:** Unlike the **offered but unselected** generic magic weapons and augmentation surgery, the GM **explicitly instructs EVERY ONE OF FOUR ORIGINAL HEROES to ADD A SYRINGE to inventory**; only **four doses** exist. **Assigned current custodians: Nash, Hope, Stanley and Vorel, one each**; no physical passing-hand animation described, but inventory allotment is authoritative tabletop instruction. **NO DOSE ADMINISTERED by S3E03, S3E04 or S3E05 ending.** In [S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md) ~00:34–00:41 Elhandari again supply “long rest in a can”; by ~01:39 Vorel is reminded someone collected one for him. **No injection, consumed dose, actual five-year lifespan loss or reallocation of four allotted doses is depicted.** During [S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md) the party actually flies to Earth and begins a costly fight with two Slow Peters but **NONE of the four allotted syringes is used despite ordinary hit-point/fire damage**. 

**S3E10 update — still unused:** During [played S3E10](../../episodes/S3E10.md) **~00:32–00:38**, the party asks whether the syringes work instantly. GM **explicitly confirms INJECT AS AN ACTION (including IN COMBAT) with immediate LONG-REST resources**, possible **hangover and loss of about FIVE years** (none physically measured). **BOTH NASH AND VOREL EXPLICITLY DECIDE TO KEEP THEIR OWN INJECTION FOR LATER/ACTUAL CRITICAL COMBAT**, not use now. **PARTY INSTEAD TAKES ORDINARY SHORT REST and spends HIT DICE; STANLEY ACTUALLY CASTS 8th-level *HEAL* 90HP on VOREL**. **NO DOSE ADMINISTERED and no HP, spell slot, lost life-years or recharge from this syringe occurs by S3E10 cutoff**. [S3E10 P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S3E10_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md); no audio checked.

**Mechanics as spoken:** Use as **one action** (injection, described into leg) for **all benefits of long rest** without waiting for prolonged battlefield rest. Side effect described as **possible/estimated shortening of remaining lifespan by around FIVE YEARS** per use, not a witnessed numerical reduction. No source proof of repeatability, natural expiration, alternative healing or exact interaction with dragon-heart immortality. Distinguish **assigned syringe** from **used syringe**, and do not backdate it to Mars visit S2E02. [P/Q comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).
