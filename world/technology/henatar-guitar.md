# Henatar's magical guitar — sound weapon of the three-headed bounty hunter

> **The Wastes · Arc Five · E126 played combat.** The current name is descriptive, not a canon name for the item. [Henitar/Henatar Grape](../characters/henitar-grape.md) · [Stanley](../characters/stanley-billings.md) · [E126 source dossier](../../episodes/E126.md). **ASR-provisional, original audio/item card not checked.**

The bounty commander uses an **oversized ornate floating electric guitar** with horn-like shapes, skull-shaped tuning pegs and an illuminated fretboard, controlled by his hands and also magically bound to him without a shoulder strap. As he plays, **sonic waves** batter **Diontalus** into a desert crater and shake even Villa Ornath's reinforced construction. During the combat, repeated chords can **charm targets into attacking the nearest being** (Hope and Vorel are compelled to turn on each other), and subject other creatures to sonic **psychic shredding damage**, knockdown or a series of **Magic Missiles**. Treat those as **specific GM adjudications**, not published generic spell text.

Henatar has **six recharging orbiting blue spheres** associated with his beads and defenses. They absorb a variety of incoming spells and emit attempted stunning pulses against close attackers, but **do not stop Nash's dragon acid breath**. Whether the spheres belong to the guitar or to separate equipment/abilities is **not established**, so do not make them inseparable from its ownership.

At **~02:29–02:33**, Stanley **casts Telekinesis on himself**, wins an **Arcana contest** against Henatar, and **pulls the floating guitar into his hands**. Stanley strums it without understanding it, **actually launching a Fireball which a blue sphere absorbs**. Henatar attempts to recover it by telekinetic force but loses another contest and retreats. This is a **witnessed temporary transfer/control**, not settled lawful ownership, successful attunement, destruction or guaranteed later custody. **Nash's second acid breath** subsequently destroys the commander's disk; Henatar falls **limp at ~02:41–02:42**, **not formally pronounced dead**. ## E128 — Stanley retains and cautiously tests the guitar

**Henatar/Henitar Grape is explicitly killed by Nash in E128**, after surrendering—not by the previous E126 acid breath. At ~00:05 the wounded commander sees **the guitar strapped to Stanley's back** and pleads for its return; Stanley **does not give it back**. Around ~01:10 Stanley, alone in the desert, **actually plucks and softly plays the electric guitar**. The GM says it **magnifies/amplifies the sound** with a pleasant timbre but **does not produce the destructive shock/Fireball/charm effects on this attempt**; Stanley's lute skill helps with music but **no proficiency or attunement to the guitar's other powers is proven**. **Custody at the Season One finale: Stanley**, pending any future change. Hope's attempted removal of **one separate blue orb** blasts her away and destroys the sphere; **it does not prove the orbs belong to the guitar or that all were gathered**.
