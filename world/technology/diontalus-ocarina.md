# Diontalus's summoning ocarina

> **The Wastes · obtained E125 ~00:19–00:21.** Unique physical artifact, **ASR-provisional**. [Episode dossier](../../episodes/E125.md) · [dual-ASR report](../../reports/E125_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md) · [Diontalus](../characters/diontalus.md) · [Stanley](../characters/stanley-billings.md).

**Acquisition and description.** During the High Elf's audience in E125, after the conditional E124 accord, **Diontalus produces an initially glowing/ethereal ocarina and hands it to Stanley Billings**. In Stanley's hands it becomes **solid, intricately finished, dark chestnut-colored wood**. The GM describes the workmanship as fine and elven. This transfer is **actually played**: Stanley is the last observed holder, not merely the proposed beneficiary.

**Claimed function.** Diontalus says Stanley need only **play a song or hum a few notes** to summon him when the party requires help against **the Principle**. The description does not prove unlimited range, unlimited charges, immunity to interference or that Diontalus will honor every request. **The party does not play the ocarina or test the summoning during E125**. Diontalus later fights the invading fleet after teleporting away, **without any activation of the instrument**.

**Separation of promises and actions.** This is distinct from Stanley's preexisting **Diontalus-associated Otiluke's Resilient Sphere device**, discussed as an escape contingency during the later speeder chase but **not activated**. No future E125 fate for the ocarina is shown after Stanley reaches his father's hidden upper room during the station's fall.
