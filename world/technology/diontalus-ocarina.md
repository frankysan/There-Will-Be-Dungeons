# Diontalus's summoning ocarina

> **The Wastes · obtained E125 ~00:19–00:21.** Unique physical artifact, **ASR-provisional**. [Episode dossier](../../episodes/E125.md) · [dual-ASR report](../../reports/E125_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md) · [Diontalus](../characters/diontalus.md) · [Stanley](../characters/stanley-billings.md).

**Acquisition and description.** During the High Elf's audience in E125, after the conditional E124 accord, **Diontalus produces an initially glowing/ethereal ocarina and hands it to Stanley Billings**. In Stanley's hands it becomes **solid, intricately finished, dark chestnut-colored wood**. The GM describes the workmanship as fine and elven. This transfer is **actually played**: Stanley is the last observed holder, not merely the proposed beneficiary.

**Claimed function.** Diontalus says Stanley need only **play a song or hum a few notes** to summon him when the party requires help against **the Principal**. The description does not prove unlimited range, unlimited charges, immunity to interference or that Diontalus will honor every request. **The party does not play the ocarina or test the summoning during E125**. Diontalus later fights the invading fleet after teleporting away, **without any activation of the instrument**.

**Separation of promises and actions.** This is distinct from Stanley's preexisting **Diontalus-associated Otiluke's Resilient Sphere device**, discussed as an escape contingency during the later speeder chase but **not activated**. No future E125 fate for the ocarina is shown after Stanley reaches his father's hidden upper room during the station's fall.

## E126–E128 — Diontalus appears, falls and is revived without the instrument

In [E126](../../episodes/E126.md), the **spectral Diontalus actually battles Henatar** as Elhandar falls, and the mercenary's magically amplified guitar renders him **motionless in a crater**. This is **not an observed response to Stanley playing or humming into the ocarina**. In [E128](../../episodes/E128.md), Stanley actually casts **third-level *Healing Word*** on the downed Diontalus and the GM describes **12 HP restored**; he rises, speaks and eventually departs after drawing a visible soul-like energy from Henatar's corpse. **Neither return nor departure is an ocarina activation**. Diontalus's continued activity does not independently prove the instrument's promised range, reliability, effect or charges.

## S3E08 — later spectral destruction is not a summoning test

In [played S3E08](../../episodes/S3E08.md), **Diontalus appears as a glowing blue spectral figure in the fight against the giant Groxl**, and Groxl's eye laser **visibly disintegrates that spectral form**. The GM describes an ending and Stanley feels a rupture, but whether the archfey is permanently destroyed is **not independently resolved**. Neither a preceding ocarina performance nor a later attempt to summon him with it is shown; **do not infer that an unplayed attempt succeeded or failed because of this battle**.

## Last witnessed artifact status

**The last direct hand-to-hand possession evidence remains Diontalus giving the physical wooden ocarina to Stanley in E125.** A search of the maintained episode dossiers identifies no separately narrated later **playing, summoning test, physical transfer, loss, destruction or final inventory check** for the instrument through S3E18. This is an **evidence limit**, not proof that Stanley physically held it every later moment or that the claimed summoning function would still operate after S3E08. Keep it separate from Stanley's **Diontalus-related *Otiluke's Resilient Sphere* contingency** and the independently acquired **Henatar guitar**.
