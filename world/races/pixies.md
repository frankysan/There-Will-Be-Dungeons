# Pixies — captivity, choice and lives beyond Trashburg

The Wastes' pixies enter the companions' story through an industry that exploits them. One frightened survivor, **[P0D](../characters/p0d.md)**, asks Nash to help people still in captivity. Later, others are forced into enormous fighting forms during an assault on T'ietkalla. Yet another pixie, from a different history, has a partner and children in a miniature home far from the city. These glimpses show pixies as **people with attachments and choices**, without establishing one shared homeland or a fully documented society.[^scope]

## P0D and the captive pixies

In the poor streets of **[Trashburg and T'ietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md)**, Nash hears a small voice and finds a glowing, winged pixie about a foot tall. She gives her name as **P0D**—the label on her aquarium, with a zero in the middle—and begs not to be sent back to **Farmer Dewey**. She says that pixies are held in numbered tanks and crushed into the dust sold as **Pixie Blush**, the drug that earlier altered Nash's perceptions.[^captivity]

P0D's account is first-person testimony from someone who fears being returned, and the companions treat her as a living person rather than a source of the drug. But they **do not inspect the entire pixie farm** in that encounter. Their discussions of an infiltration and rescue remain plans, not the liberation of all captives. The later cast discussion of a prepared rescue route describes something the players **did not choose or perform**; it cannot replace events actually played.[^plans]

P0D shelters with allies, later accompanies the companions and helps them investigate dangerous parts of the keep. Her friendships and particular abilities belong to her life and need not be taken as a complete account of pixie habits or powers.[^companion]

## The assault and forced transformations

During the confrontation beneath T'ietkalla, **[Monsoon Cabbage](../characters/monsoon-cabbage.md)** issues **Order 26** by radio. P0D visibly undergoes an unwilling transformation into a huge, muscular creature. She becomes confused, distressed and dangerous, yet Nash continues trying to calm and protect her. **Diontalus** deliberately attacks and kills her in the following episode; her body bursts into dust. It is neither a voluntary betrayal nor a transformation she survives in ordinary bodily form.[^death]

Other pixies appear in similarly enlarged forms, bursting through the ruined keep and fighting among its enemies and an altered froghemoth. Some are swallowed, and others emerge against the companions. As Nash carries the party back above the city, several **[Pixie Hulks](../creatures/pixie-hulks.md)** visibly shrink back to smaller pixies and disperse, sometimes invisibly. The adventurers do not establish exactly why the changes end or where every survivor goes. The Hulk is a **forced condition imposed on pixies**, not the name of a separate race or a normal social role.[^hulks]

P0D later appears to Nash as an **ethereal presence** during an ancestor rite. That encounter can matter to the characters without undoing her death or proving that every pixie has the same afterlife.[^spirit]

## Another Pod and a family of her own

The companions later visit **Tower Nash**, where an alternate version of **Pod** lives in a small house with her pixie husband **Roger**, two children and another expected. She is pleased to see Nash, who carefully explains that **he is not the Nash she remembers** and that his own P0D died. She wishes to join a rescue, but remains behind with her family as the travelers depart.[^family]

This is the clearest witnessed pixie household outside the captive system in Trashburg. It establishes that **this alternate Pod has a family and a home**; it does not establish that she is the original P0D resurrected, that all pixies use the same family structure or that Tower Nash represents their ancestral home. Nor does the party's eventual journey toward the Feywild supply a witnessed pixie homeland or governing tradition.[^limits]

## Current status

The original P0D is dead. Other pixies forced into giant forms are shown reverting and scattering, but their complete rescue, future settlement and numbers are not established. The alternate Pod remains alive with Roger and their children at Tower Nash at the last source-verified departure; the current party does not take her through the portal.[^ending]

The wider pixie people therefore remain only partly known. Their story in the Wastes cannot be reduced to **Pixie Blush** or **Pixie Hulks**, but the observed friendships and household do not yet amount to a verified culture shared by every pixie.[^scope]
