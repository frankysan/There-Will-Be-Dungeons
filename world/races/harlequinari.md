# Harlequinari — clowns, customs and outsiders

The Harlequinari are a people known to the travelers through **Blood Skunk Hendrickson** and through the peculiar society surrounding the **Council of Clowns**. Their world appears to value elaborate jokes and performance, but the companions see only a narrow corner of it: one estranged traveler, the words of a ferryman and an audience in Pandemonium. Neither the full extent of the people nor their common appearance is known.[^identity]

## The Council and its audience

When Hope's companions seek an audience with the [Council of Clowns](../FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md) in Pandemonium, they enter a vast auditorium where clowns heckle petitioners, exchange tricks and test what entertains them. Bobo leads a council occupying thirteen seats; its proceedings turn mockery and cruelty into a kind of public performance. Members speak of the wider Harlequinari world and suggest that many Harlequinari might welcome lessons in a new style of humor. The council therefore has a substantial connection with Harlequinari society, but the scene does not establish that **every councilor is Harlequinari**, or that the council governs everyone of that people.[^council]

In Sigil, the priest Kidigree had earlier said that the Council prized tricks played on the grieving. His treatment of travelers suggests that these values have followers beyond the Pandemonium auditorium. Yet an institution's appetite for cruel comedy is not proof that all Harlequinari share it.[^kidigree]

## Blood Skunk and estrangement

The ferryman Hesterbrook recognizes **[Blood Skunk](../characters/blood-skunk-hendrickson.md)** as Harlequinari and regards his work as a hunter as unusual for one of that people. Blood Skunk names himself a Harlequinari but rejects the idea that he is an ordinary example. The ferryman calls him an exile, a description offered by an outsider rather than a fully explained history.[^identity]

During the later audience Blood Skunk rebukes the council and insists that he left the Harlequinari long ago and no longer serves them. The clowns mock his manner, question whether he belongs among his own kind and rebuke him for failing to instruct Hope in their humor. His words establish a personal rupture with this milieu, not the cause of his departure or the terms of a formal banishment.[^estrangement]

## Appearance and unusual gifts

Blood Skunk's pale skin, striking red features and disturbing smile are his own described appearance, not a measured description of all Harlequinari. His peculiar **Critical Fortunes** manifests during his travels, but the companions do not establish that every member of his people possesses the same ability, or how the wider society understands such gifts.[^traits]

## Current status

The council remains capable of hearing petitions and dispensing information when Hope meets it. It gives her a small directional device and a lead toward a hidden prison, though its account of that place remains unverified. Blood Skunk subsequently dies during the confrontation aboard Fate Six. His head is later attached to Steerious's living body without any demonstrated return of his consciousness. Nothing in those events establishes the fate of the wider Harlequinari people or a later change in the council's membership.[^status]
