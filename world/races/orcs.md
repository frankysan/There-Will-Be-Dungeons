# Orcs — orders, outsiders and changing allegiances

Orcs are encountered across the Wastes as soldiers, mercenaries, travelers and people who have broken with the powers that shaped their lives. Some serve the Principal's martial establishment; others oppose it, take work on distant airships or abandon old causes. Their stories show sharply different choices rather than a single orcish state or uniform code.[^overview]

## The martial order and Slavetown

The Principal's forces first overtake the companions on the road to [Slavetown](../places/slavetown.md). A heavily armed orc warrior demands submission, and five orc initiates later fight the travelers in the Rusty Cage. Once the company wins, a magistrate and orc knight acknowledge the judgment. Members known as **Lady Saltbubble** and **Sir Chickenfinger** invite the victors to seek entry into the **Kuromokishi** martial order (working written form). Its ceremonies and invitation make it an institution people can join, not a name for every orc.[^order]

The order is part of a larger web of power. The **Toyota Host** is a broader armed force; Kuromokishi warriors hold a smaller, prestigious position associated with military discipline and claims of honor. The magistrate, town officials, mine owners and armed orders have overlapping interests but are not one organization. The orc soldiers the companions encounter therefore cannot stand in for the customs of all orcs.[^authority]

## Leaving the Principal behind

At the Liberators' camp the companions meet a sightless orc who has renounced the Kuromokishi. He refuses a personal name and says he removed his own eyes in rejection of teachings he once followed. In private conversation with **Vorel**, he speaks of orcish life, the Principal's authority and the possibility of reform while arguing for a cautious campaign against the regime. His rejection is his own act and account; it does not reveal how all former members think, or prove that his earlier order represents traditional orcish culture.[^renegade]

## Mercenaries, crews and strangers

Armed orcs serve as escorts on the travelers' route to Sigil, but their duty to Tobacho's undertaking does not make them members of the Principal's martial order. On the threatened airship, the armed escort is distinct from the mixed crew that actually operates the vessel. Their shifting roles in its disaster belong to the ship's story, not to a common orcish political allegiance.[^escorts]

Much farther away, the **Green Vale/Greenveil** raiders try to seize Blast Belly Rock and the travelers' ship. Their leader threatens residents, and the company defeats them. **Three surviving orcs surrender**, disclose where prisoners are being held and are accepted into the crew of **Fate Six**. They initially fear the ship's ghost sailors but are persuaded to stay. None survives the dangerous subsequent voyage through the nebula: one dies in a violent impact, and the other two are lost overboard. Their brief change of allegiance demonstrates an individual choice, not a settled peace between entire peoples.[^burp]

In the **Gray Waste**, the party also meets an apathetic orc who directs them toward Pointless. After his head is severed, it astonishingly continues to respond. His fate belongs to a supernatural place and a particular encounter; it cannot establish that ordinary orcs generally survive decapitation.[^waste]

## The Kuromokishi in the last war

At the Solar Mines, fighters called **Kuromokishi** serve alongside the Principal's armed regime, including a squad supporting an **Umbilical Order Paladin** and others stationed inside the defended complex. They wield swords and other weapons and fight the coalition opposing the Principal. The regime also supplies extraordinary chrome-associated technology to favored warriors. These scenes establish an elite martial affiliation, not that every orc is transformed into chrome or that all order members possess an identical enhancement.[^war]

After Earth's restoration, the assembled leaders decide to give surviving Kuromokishi a chance to be integrated into another community under **Vorel's** lizardfolk leadership. They are not all summarily executed. Vorel and the group set out toward **[Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md)**, beginning a journey of resettlement and reform. Their completed arrival, individual consent and eventual standing in lizardfolk society are not shown.[^aftermath]

## Current status

There is no single recorded fate for the orcs of the Wastes. The three recruits from Burp are dead or lost, the former Kuromokishi dissenter's eventual fate is not settled by his early conversation, and other orcs remain associated with different factions and places. The final alliance under Vorel creates a possibility for former enemies to live differently without establishing a universal orcish culture, a new nation or a completed reconciliation.[^status]
