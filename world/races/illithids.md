# Illithids — beyond the CHIDS incursion

The companions first know illithids, or mind flayers, as frightening enemies: a floating figure takes a child from her family, and others hold part of the buried CHIDS complex. Yet the Wastes also gives them glimpses of illithids in offices, contests and civic life. **The invasion is the history of a particular hostile group, not proof that every illithid shares its purpose.** Some of the other encounters are brief, and what lies behind the projected workplaces remains uncertain.[^scope]

## The abductors and the buried complex

In the Wastes, a tentacled figure who gives a name heard variously as **Ovaxiad** or **Oubvayx** confronts the companions with apparently controlled lizardfolk. He speaks telepathically, claims allegiance to the dragon-worm **Dolo Popio**, and takes **Sira** while her family tries to save her. When the party later attacks a mind-flayer figure at Khaervilon, its apparent body collapses into skin and black oil. Defeating that manifestation does not prove that the original abductor has permanently died or even conclusively identify every later body as the same individual.[^abduction]

Beneath **[CHIDS](../places/chids.md)**, the companions find other illithids operating control rooms, guarding captives and protecting the approach to the **Traversal Drive**. These adversaries act with armed lizardfolk, bugbears and other defenders. In one chamber an illithid summons a red, many-faced organism that eats memories; another group guards **Chance and Eddie**, suspended over small tentacled creatures. The party fights through them and reaches the larger engineering installation, where Nash eventually destroys a powerful floating illithid commander. The machine's ultimate fate is a separate and less certain question than that visible defeat.[^incursion]

## Workplaces seen through borrowed bodies

The explorers also encounter a very different illithid setting, reached from CHIDS's old executive conference room. Wired headsets leave their real bodies seated in the ruin while showing Nash and Hope a seemingly restored office populated by illithids. Their perceived bodies become **editable illithid avatars**. Colleagues use computers, file reports, answer to superiors and call security when they discover a break-in. Alarms sound in the actual physical CHIDS offices as well, demonstrating a working connection between these experiences.[^office]

One office worker calls himself **[Charles Dickens](../characters/charles-dickens.md)**. He complains that a rival has been favored while he has gone decades without promotion, and he encourages the visitors to plant falsified reporting data. He describes the workplace as part of a **Void Realm**, and says the illithid leadership plans to use CHIDS's ancient machinery to bring that realm to Earth and profit from souls. Those ambitions, the realm's location and the expected length of the sabotage delay are **Charles's claims**, even though the travelers actually upload the file and encounter real consequences. Charles's private resentment is not established as a common illithid value.[^charles]

## An illithid in Sigil

A more independent encounter occurs in **[Sigil](../places/sigil.md)**. Among the competitors at the **M-fighting championship**, the companions meet **Fax/Faks**, an illithid with a conspicuous metal fitting on his head. Fax and another contender, Tubbs 9K, speak with Vorel in the contestants' lounge. The encounter shows an illithid taking part in public recreation, not a soldier visibly obeying CHIDS command.[^sport]

Later, Fax appears as an assistant in the **Portal Regulatory Agency**. He recognizes Vorel, is interested in talking about the sport, and is redirected to the agency's business by his supervisor. Fax is subsequently absent from a scheduled coaching lesson, and no one can locate him at that time. The absence is not proof that he has died, been captured or rejoined the invaders. His appearances establish one illithid's working and social life in Sigil without revealing the customs of a larger settled community.[^fax]

## What the hive is not

The living wall within CHIDS is easily confused with the illithids because it speaks as a collective intelligence. But its representative **claims to preserve thousands of human minds** from the facility's older knowledge system, and it asks the heroes to stop an illithid incursion. Its history and self-description are not wholly independently verified; nonetheless, the wall should **not be labeled an illithid hive**. Likewise, black headworms recovered from an illithid and other guards are observations about particular bodies, not a demonstrated universal illithid life cycle.[^hive]

## Current status

The illithid group at CHIDS suffers repeated defeats, including the loss of the major commander. The companions confront the Traversal Drive's power, but the wider fate of the Void Realm, its inhabitants or any additional illithid communities is not observed. Fax's whereabouts after the missed lesson also remain open. The evidence therefore supports an intelligent people with at least several distinct roles and allegiances, **not** a fully documented shared culture, origin or final destiny.[^limits]
