# Elves — the Elhandari and their scattered communities

The elves of the Wastes are encountered in more than one age and allegiance. Most visibly, they include the **Elhandari**, whose refuge among the stars combines an inhabited forest with immense machinery and magic. The travelers meet an organized elven community capable of welcoming strangers, tending the sick and undertaking work beyond Earth. Its ambitions, customs and political choices provide a window into **one elven society**, not the defining history of every elf.[^arrival]

## Ancient history and the Elhandari mission

The Elhandari remember Earth before its devastation, when their people were engaged in a long effort to understand and heal it. Their elder Amanthalodar speaks of an ancient mission, the loss and corruption that followed, and the dangers that Earth now poses to departing elven souls. This is the history and spiritual teaching the community passes on, not a record independently recovered from the lost age. The forest refuge that survives among the stars shows what the Elhandari have actually preserved: a living landscape sustained by extraordinary technology and magic. Their mission should not be mistaken for the first origin of all elves, whose wider beginnings remain unknown.[^beliefs]

## The forest sanctuary

The Elhandari home of **[Elhandar](../places/elhandar.md)** encloses an actual living woodland within a great constructed station. Thousands of elves receive visiting travelers in a public gathering. Within their community, **Admiral Chrysorius** commands vessels and defenses while an elder known as **Amanthalodar**, or the High Elf, serves as a spiritual leader rather than an unquestioned monarch. The hosts provide places to rest, medical care and an expectation that new arrivals be assigned therapists; the admiral explains that attending therapy is voluntary. The society's technology does not displace its interest in living trees and cultivated landscapes.[^community]

The elder's teachings shape how visitors are received: healing Earth is for the Elhandari both a practical undertaking and a matter of belief. The companions encounter their present-day care and research directly, but neither ancient memories nor a universal law of elven death is demonstrated by this welcome.[^beliefs]

## Families, conflict and refuge

Elhandar is also bound to **[Stanley Billings](../characters/stanley-billings.md)**, a half-elf who recognizes **Valiir Or'Nath** as his birth name while keeping his chosen one. Its records connect him with the Or'Nath family, and his father's lakeside cottage still stands within the living forest. Those family ties matter to Stanley without making his personal history an account of all elves.[^stanley]

The Elhandari offer shelter to tieflings displaced from T'ietkalla, making space for the settlement that Hope calls **[the Harbor](../places/the-harbor.md)**. Not every encounter in the forest is benign: disguised hostile elves attack the companions and help abduct Hope. Their actions, and the uncertainties surrounding the apprentice Dilenoria, do not establish that the Elhandari community as a whole sanctioned those attacks. Other elves, including Cerinthala of the ancient CHIDS laboratories, belong to different historical contexts and should not automatically be counted among Elhandar's people.[^refuge]

## From Elhandar to Mars

A mercenary assault destroys the station's ability to remain a safe home among the stars. Its forest and machinery crash to Earth, while surviving elves gather the injured and arrange evacuation with **High Elf Amanthalodar** and **Admiral Chrysorius**. Their ships take Elhandari survivors and other refugees toward the community's terraforming work on Mars. The loss of the station does not mean that all its inhabitants died, nor does departure alone establish what awaits everyone on the new world.[^exodus]

On Mars, the Elhandari participate in a wider council devoted to Earth's recovery. They publicly elect Stanley, the first half-elf to hold their revived **Prime** office since the Scorching according to the assembled community, and an elder tree visibly transforms his appearance. Stanley accepts the office and later entrusts local Elhandari affairs to Amanthalodar while traveling elsewhere. This is an observed change of leadership within their community, not an elevation of Stanley over every elf anywhere.[^election]

## Current status

The Elhandari survive as a dispersed people rather than a single intact orbital settlement. They help lead the final struggle on Earth and participate in the world's restoration. After the victory, Stanley departs with many Elhandari through an opened **Feywild portal**; other elves remain on Mars or return to Earth. Their choices are not uniform, and the future size, homeland or government of every group is not shown.[^final]

The greater history of elves remains wider than the Elhandari: individual elf travelers, families, scholars and enemies have their own stories, while the customs and beliefs directly observed at Elhandar are evidence about that community. Neither its spiritual teachings, particular forms of magic nor its wartime politics should be treated as innate traits shared by all elves.[^limits]
