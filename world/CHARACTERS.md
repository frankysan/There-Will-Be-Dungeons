## Tietkalla's emerging court and other notable people

**Nobiery/Nobieri** is the ancient **Dead Tongue** rescued from a magical book E047. He claims Hope's Akmenos royal descent and an old Soulbroker army deal; **he actually returns with hundreds of armed tieflings and crowns her E054**. The exact personal/house spelling and the alleged thousand-man count remain open. [E047](../episodes/E047.md), [E048](../episodes/E048.md), [E054](../episodes/E054.md).

**Melgor of Poxis**, **Fula Dentipilius** and **Relium Vanch** are prospective court leaders for **security, farms/food and reconstruction**, with spellings requiring listening review. Orders and plans are not proof projects are complete. **Andreeste/the Nourisher** is a possible divine or supernatural claimant according to Monsoon and Kefeli; **no direct arrival by E054**. [E054](../episodes/E054.md).

**George Miller**, a Teet guard, is **deliberately murdered by Diontalus E045**, not killed by his earlier gun accident. Stanley/Nash discuss returning a child's memento to the family, but that visit is **not shown by E054**. The Q&A explains a radio clue that **might have been discovered** had George survived longer; it is **counterfactual**, not played. [E044](../episodes/E044.md), [E045](../episodes/E045.md), [Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md).

**Dusthill people** include the deceased **Hillmaster**, earlier specter **Peter**, mechanic **Jimster**, water attendant/interim leader **Shun'ti Stoutbarrel**, **Talia** and **Bruce**. In the Black Mine, the captive **Tickles** dies after a ceiling collapse. **Orp** is a giant/cyclops whose future role the GM discusses but does not enact in the Year One Q&A. All of these have additional time-coded entries in the [archived research](../research/legacy/2026-10-21/WORLD_CHARACTERS.md) and [episode dossiers](../episodes/E001.md).

## Reference and source conventions

- **Status is as of E054 unless explicitly dated otherwise**. An earlier “alive,” “missing” or “rumored dead” entry remains correct as history of what was known *then*.
- **Confirmed display orthography:** **Diontalus** ([D-026](../project/DECISIONS.md)), **Eye of Groxl** one artifact ([D-028](../project/DECISIONS.md)). **Tietkalla** has cast-written support in the matched context. No spelling choice replaces audio review.
- **GM Q&A disclosures** about Buttons' romantic motives, Nash's engine-heart origin or unused pixie missions are **retrospective information**, not in-story actions ([BONUS-2019-03](../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md)).
- **Completeness:** This index highlights the principal Year One cast; the complete earlier list of minor NPCs and source-specific first appearances remains in the [archive](../research/legacy/2026-10-21/WORLD_CHARACTERS.md), not erased or declared nonexistent. Names or actor roles not yet corroborated require a separate review.

## New arc — three months later (E055–E056)

**The subject profiles, not a repeated episode-batch chronology, are the live reference.** After E054, an actual **three months elapse**: [Nash](characters/nash-maggard.md) and [Angelashi](characters/angelashi.md) are expecting a baby; [Hope](characters/hope-akmenos.md) governs and implements disputed reforms; [Vorel](characters/vorel.md) and [Bok Bok](characters/bok-bok.md) operate **the Golden Sands**; and [Stanley](characters/stanley-diontalus.md) studies medicine and Tidebearer magic, while Diontalus remains dormant **but still sharing his body**. [E055](../episodes/E055.md).

In [E056](../episodes/E056.md), **George Miller appears as an attacking spirit**, **P0D appears as an ethereal companion** and neither is restored to biological life. **Vorel's spectral aging is cured in the same episode**. Newly significant **Tidebearer priestess Emenil/Eminel** heals Vorel but is then **attacked by an undead figure at Nobiery's command**—her death remains unknown. **Softhead** is now a mutilated **but surviving envoy** whose promises to free Dusthill captives remain unfulfilled on-screen. [April cast roundtable](../episodes/BONUS-2019-04.md) contains **player motives/GM process**, not additional character scenes.

## Status after E063

**[Vorel](characters/vorel.md):** In [E061](../episodes/E061.md) **Orp appoints him sheriff** to investigate Bramie's unsolved murder, then in [E062](../episodes/E062.md) **Vorel kills Orp and is publicly acknowledged as Great Fang**. He remains ruler after **Zercil's E063 death**, although Hope's assistance in her fatal fight creates a question of honor. His titles register distinguishes his **self-proclaimed “Lizard King”**, Orp's **sheriff appointment**, and his **earned Great Fang office**.

**[Orp](characters/orp.md), Mirion, Typalla, Toxar and Zercil:** **Orp dies E062**. The **Cave Fengar rocket blast** kills **Mirion Fengar, priestess Typalla, the Elder of Families, General Toxar, Mola Janda and many others**. **Zercil dies E063** after Hope shoots her with **Scully's Obsidian Nail** while Vorel attempts subdual. **Omnom Cinderborn survives** and becomes an adviser with strong objections to Hope. **Three Fengar children survive**, including Felcha. **Bramie/Baymie dies E061** but his murderer remains **unproven**. [E061](../episodes/E061.md)–[E063](../episodes/E063.md).

**[Nash](characters/nash-maggard.md):** Helps **two apparent future versions of himself steal the preserved Fengar egg** in E061; personally observes Bramie still alive before his departure. **Their future origin is not verified**. In E063 a dragon transformation draws worshipful reactions and leaves Nash with **one level of exhaustion**; it is not proof of actual godhood.

**[Hope](characters/hope-akmenos.md):** Her **E062 rocket** causes mass deaths in the lounge; she **kills Zercil E063** with an Obsidian Nail despite Vorel's nonlethal attempts. She assists in capturing a **live black worm** from Zercil's bed. She remains **Queen of Tietkalla**, separate from Vorel's new title.

**[Stanley](characters/stanley-diontalus.md):** **Secretly heals Vorel E063**, retrieves a **Necklace of Fireballs with five charges** from Orp's corpse, supervises the beginning of medical screening for **200–300 human captives**, and sets apart the puzzling legless prisoner **Follow/Follo**. **Only Stanley sees a young human girl disappear** from the crowd. Neither all captives' freedom nor a magical cause for Follow has been established.
