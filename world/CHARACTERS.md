## New Season Three companions and key survivors

**[Sprout](characters/sprout.md)** — **ACTUAL independently speaking eye and four-toothed mouth** physically attached to Nash's shoulder after his S3E02 regeneration, opposite old baby hand. Nash names it Sprout; origin and biology unknown, **not yet a separate full humanoid or a verified “Jesus Chrome” descendant**.

**[Union Wheel](characters/union-wheel.md)** — Stanley's sworn Elhandari guardian, Tungsten's niece. **Doctor Spider, Stanley and Dr. Daffodil ACTUALLY save her** from catastrophic Hell Knight wounds in S3E02; **alive on crutches** when Fate Six leaves Blast Belly Rock, then **rescues Vorel from a parasite S3E03**; S3E04 **dances with him but visibly leaves upset** at news of Courtship; **reaffirms guardian oath S3E05; S3E08 is *Telekinesis*-saved from lava but LATER crushed by giant alongside Emenil (99 damage/visible remains); S3E09 NO RESURRECTION witnessed, Stanley visibly grieves**.

## How to read these pages

A subject profile owns changing status, relationships and significant events; do **not** append an E### digest to this directory for every newly processed recording. Always distinguish an earlier **reported death** from a later observed survivor, an **on-screen action** from a **plan or accusation**, and **GM/cast retrospective explanations** from what protagonists knew at the time. Performer labels are out-of-world credits: guest Carter Johnson played Eddie, Ben Bumhofer played Chance, and Beau GMed; a diarization label for Scott's character voice in E001 does not establish a sixth performer.
