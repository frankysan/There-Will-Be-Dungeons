# Quizquaz

**Role:** Recurring oil-associated, supernatural interlocutor with sometimes consequential explanations and proposals. **Spoilers:** Through E118. **Evidence:** Source-first provisional ASR, not audio-verified.

The party first encounters a strange presence in the oil below **Dusthill**. Later connections with the Black Mine, the Soulbroker ritual and the ancient history of Tietkalla encourage characters to treat Quizquaz as knowledgeable, but that trust is not independent confirmation of all he says. [E004](../../episodes/E004.md) · [E022](../../episodes/E022.md).

During the post-coronation stonefall he offers a theory involving a dragon and supernatural obligations. **Stonefall and a peculiar crater are observed**, while this proposed cause is not. In the **Traversal Drive** crisis E081, Quizquaz describes Void energy and the purported relation of the Drive to the Principle and soul contracts. The party witnesses controls and the machine, **not a comprehensive validation of the cosmology or every proposed contract loophole**. They reject destroying Earth. [E056](../../episodes/E056.md)–[E058](../../episodes/E058.md) · [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [Drive](../technology/traversal-drive.md).

**Unresolved:** ontology, allegiance, consistency of his historical accounts and any genuine authority over the Soulbroker compact. He must not be equated with the Soulbroker, beholder or Principle without explicit evidence. [Soulbroker](soulbroker.md) · [Ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
