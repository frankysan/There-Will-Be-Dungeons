# Quizquaz — the Dream Drinker

## Introduction

Quizquaz was an oil-dark, winged supernatural figure whose dealings with the travelers began beneath **Dusthill**. Calling himself the **Dream Drinker**, he spoke of dreams, the dwarven queen and the distant past while seeking a bargain involving the sensations of other minds. His explanations could be useful, but they were offered by a being with reasons to manipulate those who listened. When Stanley severed his connection to the queen, the party fought him; Hope's close-range shot ended the encounter in oil and fire.[^quiz-early]

## Servant of the Soulbroker

Quizquaz later appeared again in the Black Mine ritual chamber, still separate from the great beholder concealed beneath the stage. After the beholder's defeat, he took a dark gem from its remains and opened a passage for a much larger, goat-like power. That visitor called himself the **[Soulbroker](soulbroker.md)**, and Quizquaz acted as his subordinate, arranging hospitality for the travelers. Quizquaz's return showed that his earlier defeat had not ended his appearances, but it did not reveal how he survived or whether any resurrection had occurred.[^quiz-mine]

His service to the Soulbroker drew him into the companions' long struggle over bargains and souls. When the party faced a devastating stonefall, Quizquaz approached Hope with claims about supernatural legal disputes, a dragon's supposed fall and a proposed exchange involving Melgor and fifty tiefling souls. Hope declined his offer. The falling stones and strange crater were real; his explanation of their origin was still testimony.[^quiz-stonefall]

## The Traversal Drive

After the confrontation with Dolo Popio, Quizquaz appeared in the altered engineering chamber of Khaervilon, where the [Traversal Drive](../technology/traversal-drive.md) trapped companions in its central beam. He claimed that the machine channeled power from the Void and urged the travelers to let the beam destroy Earth, thereby supposedly satisfying their bargain to kill the Principal. They rejected that proposal. He then described a different possibility: a willing subject might absorb the beam at the cost of their life, and he explained how to adjust its frequency. He refused when Vorel challenged him to sacrifice himself.[^quiz-drive]

Quizquaz used the console to release Eddie, and the group later operated the controls in the way he described, freeing Sira and enabling the four heroes to enter the beam together. That sequence gives substance to his practical guidance, **not** independent proof of every claim about the Void, the contract or the fate of Earth. Quizquaz departed angrily before the companions' collective sacrifice; its outcome was not an act he performed on their behalf.[^quiz-drive]

## Current status

Quizquaz subsequently emerged from shadow behind the throne at **Château Weir** when the four heroes and the Principal arrived seeking a temporary alliance against their greater enemy. He accused **Hope, Nash and Stanley**, the three signers of the old bargain, of breaching their obligation to kill the Principal. When Vorel insisted that he had never signed, Quizquaz expressly acknowledged that **Vorel was not bound by that compact**. He threatened court proceedings, summoned skeletal guards and withdrew into shadow after Stanley defied him by sitting on the throne. The Soulbroker appeared separately afterward. Quizquaz was **not shown slain or captured** in that encounter, and his later whereabouts, true authority and the fate of the threatened proceedings remain unknown.[^quiz-later]
