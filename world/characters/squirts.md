# Squirts — kobold trickster (Arcs Four–Five)

> **First appearance:** [E082](../../episodes/E082.md), unnamed kobold at the Soggy Feather. **Identified and developed:** [E083](../../episodes/E083.md), [processed Parakeet SRT](../../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E083.%20Trash%20Monkey%20and%20The%20Bird.parakeet.processed.srt). **Kristen's new character**, **not** a reincarnated Hope. This profile is **ASR-based, not audio-verified**; contains spoilers through E106; earlier sections retain their episode-era cutoffs.

## E106 — onward with the original travelers

In [E106](../../episodes/E106.md), **Squirts and Chunks** are present with **Nash, Stanley and Vorel** for Tobacho's audience, in which **Zorander's Fist gauntlet is actually handed over without Hope's Hand**. They accompany guide **Badass T** through the **frog seller's basement portal** to a strange port, then onto a **gnome-crewed airship** and to a **paired-asteroid tower**. **No Earth arrival or meeting with Hope is shown**. The identity of the speaking Nash likeness at cutoff remains open; Squirts does **not** independently obtain an Earth portal from Tobacho. [Comparison](../../reports/E106_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## E105 — guide charm, emergency healing and return to Tobacho

In [E105](../../episodes/E105.md), Squirts charms **Bungor/Bungo (?)**, a friendly lizardfolk rickshaw driver, who carries her and helps the party reach the **Hive**. She survives the freight-elevator ambush with **Chunks**, casts **Heal** on badly wounded Stanley for **70 hit points** (as a crumpled-trash-looking spell, **not** a scroll), and later **Spare the Dying** and **Mass Healing Word** to save injured allies including **Fambo Joe**, who is alive again afterward. She reaches **Tobacho's bar** with the others; no Fist delivery or Earth crossing occurs. [Comparison](../../reports/E105_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## Identity and appearance

**Squirts** is a very short female **kobold** (E082 suggests about two and a half feet tall) in a patchwork outfit made of **safety pins**, a **colander helmet**, and an improvised **silverware-and-rubber-band crossbow**. She carries a small floating stone and her infant kobold **Chunks** in a shoe tied to her outfit. The baby is **not** an independent adult party member. Squirts has an excitable, rapid speaking style, relishes jokes and trickery, and prefers sleeping in a wastebasket. She refers to a **trickster god**, early ASR **Uldemara/Old Mara**; in E104 Kristen spells **O-L-I-D-A-M-M-A-R-A**, supporting **Olidammara** in reference prose, still not audio-verified.

## Played development

At the **Soggy Feather** in [E083](../../episodes/E083.md) ~00:08, Squirts responds to Vorel's search for resurrection, says she has seen people return from death, and leads **Vorel, heart-form Nash and Nothing** on a long walk to a hidden church in Sigil. She opens its doors with a humorous sound and knows some attendants. The birdlike priest distrusts her, accusing her of a scheme to take the church, but **her successful Persuasion roll reassures him**. Wearing his crystal viewing device, she reads **Draconic markings indicating Vorel's Fushi affiliation**; infernal **Weir** marks appear around Stanley in the priest's examination. She is not the person performing resurrection magic.

Squirts vigorously opposes the **Pelor** delegation at the inn on the following morning and brandishes a fork, but **Vorel persuades her to tolerate the visit as a supposed trick**. She wants the group to help the priest perform a cosmic humiliation and later tries to steer them back toward trickster business instead of regular work. When they register for the **BMF** marionette tournament, she jokes about the team name **“Mighty Squirts”**, but **she does not register to compete** and the name is not shown accepted by the organisers.

## E084 — sick-sticks, invisibility and deliberate tournament sabotage

In [E084](../../episodes/E084.md), Squirts leads the party back to her **hidden trickster church in a new visible location** after Vorel nonlethally knocks out a library patron. She makes a supposed **forgetfulness drink using crushed petals, saliva, dirt and a salt shaker**, and administers it to the unconscious but living halfling; **whether it alters memory is not checked**. The priest (name **Kidigree/Kidagri** provisional) reveals a scheme to humiliate **BMF's undefeated champion**, provides small **illness-causing syringe-like “six-sticks”**, and invites Squirts to sabotage specific competitors. Squirts agrees. **This is not ordinary neutral guidance** and not proof her god condones or guarantees resurrection.

At the tournament security detects Squirts's **magical forehead stone**, and a swallowing ploy fails: the guards **bar her from entry**. Her priest appears **disguised as a well-dressed human** and admits her by a side entrance, intending to manipulate pairings. Squirts uses **Channel Divinity: Cloak of Shadows** to become invisible, approaches **Vorel's assigned elderly gnome opponent**, and **actually injects him with one needle**, visibly causing discomfort and later loss of concentration in his match. The DM gives a **provisional out-of-combat invisibility duration ruling**, explicitly pending correction; **it is not settled official 5e procedure**. Squirts **never registers to fight**; her role here is **active interference** while carrying infant Chunks. The trickster deity's exact name, remaining needle count and stone identity need audio/primary-sheet confirmation. [Source comparison](../../reports/E084_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## E085 — additional sabotage, successful spells and a new public team

In [E085](../../episodes/E085.md) Squirts **sticks two sickness syringes into Ventro's leg** in the competitors' meal room, drawing his attention and getting grabbed despite momentary invisibility. Ventro **kicks Squirts with Chunks**, dealing **two nonlethal damage to Squirts**, with no lasting infant injury shown. After the announcers pair **Vorel against Nash**, Squirts **actually injects Nash**; Nash makes a **successful CON save and is not sickened**. By the episode's late party, Beau says **her needle stock is exhausted** (initial quantity and exact offscreen-use audit unresolved). During Ventro's stage brawl Squirts **successfully casts Command** to make him grovel and praise Vorel against his will, **Calm Emotions** to stop several attackers, and **Word of Radiance**, and hits Ventro's foot with an improvised **steak-knife crossbow**. **These spells do work while Nash's spells are suppressed**, though the reason is unproven.

After Vorel wins the **Royce local qualifier**, Squirts and Vorel **publicly describe themselves as “Mighty Squirts”**, with Squirts named its **manager** in a newspaper interview. **The name is public now, not proof of a legal guild or earlier official team registration**. She tries to negotiate coaching, but **no fee is collected**. At the Soggy Feather party she gathers a **sketchbook, one yellow and one green gem, and three unidentified elixirs (red, blue, purple)**; their properties are unknown. The group sleeps with Chunks before **armed intruders enter**. [E085](../../episodes/E085.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/E085_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## E086 — the surviving lizardfolk boy and the forbidden potion

During [E086](../../episodes/E086.md), **Squirts tells Nash he needs a “key”** to cast ordinary spells in Sigil; she uses cowering/begging to help allies, and **Command** to freeze a dangerous masked attacker. **The GM's emergency advantage ruling occurred while D&D Beyond was unavailable**, not an audited rules interpretation. Squirts joins the disguised trickster priest **Kidigree/Kidagri (?)** in Bill's kitchen and helps prepare a performance-enhancing stew with biting plants, fungi and an **improvised Blessing of the Trickster**. The priest adds squirrel eyes; **Vorel drinks the mixture** with an expectation of better M-fighting decisions. **No successful finals advantage is demonstrated yet**, and event doping bans remain.

At breakfast a **young lizardfolk fan** repeatedly wants Vorel to be his father. Squirts **bites the boy's tail**, unsuccessfully tries **Command**, then uses **Sacred Flame for seven damage** after his ineffectual attack. The boy **falls motionless, is described as dead**, and Squirts hides him in the alley rubbish. After table discussion about death saves she **returns and casts Spare the Dying**. The boy **speaks and escapes alive, severely burned**, falsely told by Squirts that his actual father saved him. This is **neither harmless play nor a permanent killing**. Onlookers believing he was murdered cause **Arcane Spice to retract Vorel's unpaid promotion**; his ultimate recovery/public correction remain unknown. **Chunks remains with Squirts**. [Two-ASR report](../../reports/E086_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## E087 — public rumor, specimen-test maneuver and poison cured

[E087](../../episodes/E087.md) opens amid public belief Squirts killed the young lizardfolk in E086, although **that boy actually survived and fled after her Spare the Dying**. At the Colosseum, competitors repeat an **invented jealousy/parentage rumor** about Squirts and Vorel; it does **not** establish that they are lovers, Chunks is their child together, or that she actually completed homicide. Squirts **leaves her enchanted forehead stone at the inn** before security, and accompanies the team in a supporter box. The forbidden potion she and Kidigree made in E086 **really produces move precognition** in Vorel's games. When Kanja calls Vorel a cheater, Squirts **urinates on Kanja's leg** and demands a group test; **Kidigree later disguises himself as the urine attendant and replaces Vorel's sample**. This is **actual deception, not proof of a clean potion-free performance**.

After Vorel's **played overall championship victory and physical prize payment**, Squirts makes roughly **25gp in small thefts** at the Soggy Feather celebration. The next day she uses **Protection from Poison** to **neutralize Vorel's lingering assassin-dart poisoning**, the first actual permanent cure observed; the doctor's temporary tablets have worn off. She then **leads the party out toward the trickster's hidden temple** to seek help for Hope and Stanley. **The episode ends before the priest meets them or any ritual occurs.** [E087](../../episodes/E087.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/E087_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## E088 — bargain fulfilled, returning to the Chapel of Laughter

In [E088](../../episodes/E088.md), Squirts guides Vorel, Nash and Nothing through an apparently **relocating alley to the Chapel of Laughter**, unlocking its blank doors with a fart noise. Priest **Carr/Karr** leads them to **Kidigree's Hall of Reflections**; **Kidigree actually restores Hope and Stanley** via ritual mirrors, honoring the humiliating-Kanja bargain that Squirts helped engineer. Hope and Stanley step out as **living player characters**, while old corpses are left for the priest. After the reunion **Squirts remains at the temple with Kidigree and Chunks**, and **Kristen resumes Hope**. This is a **change of active player character, not Squirts's death, disappearance from canon or proof she can never return**. The earlier severe burn to the young lizardfolk child and her later stabilization remain separate E086 consequences. [Dossier](../../episodes/E088.md).

## February 2020 bonus — temporary-PC design after Hope's apparent death

In the [February 2020 cast Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-02.md) (~21–23 min), Kristen says she **expected Hope to return** and therefore made **Squirts intentionally unlike Hope** as a playful temporary character. That expectation is **player confidence**, not a retroactive guarantee of the rescue before Kidigree's **actual E088 mirror ritual**. Squirts's future role, Chunks's fate and whether she ever returns to play remain unestablished by this bonus. [ASR comparison](../../reports/BONUS_2020_02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## E104 — stowaway and successful Divine Intervention

**Squirts and Chunks return aboard the Outlands airship**, hidden among oranges; how and when they boarded is unrevealed. She teases Nash with an orange-juice attack, survives his dragon transformation overturning the crate and learns through **Arcana 17** that the moon is a real transported object, not a simple dispellable illusion. Gripping her **Toyota-symbol necklace**, she invokes **Divine Intervention**, rolls **9**, and requests the moon's removal. Kristen spells the god **O-L-I-D-A-M-M-A-R-A** and distinguishes him from Kidigree. Beau **confirms after a rules-check break that the moon, mechs and surviving Tungan forces disappear**, while wreckage and existing damage remain. **No mecha piloting or verified return of the moon to its home is shown.** The orcs wrongly credit Nash.

During the later wrong-portal disaster, **Squirts uses her own Dimension Door to bring herself and Chunks onto the smaller support craft**. Both remain with the party at its **second portal crossing**, destination unknown. This return supersedes the E088/bonus uncertainty about future play without changing those earlier cutoffs.
