# Squirts

<a id="squirts--kobold-trickster-arcs-fourfive"></a>

Squirts was a small kobold cleric whose cheerful absurdity went hand in hand with mischief that could be cruel. She dressed in safety-pin armor and a colander helmet, carried a cutlery-and-rubber-band crossbow, and traveled with a floating magical stone near her head. Her infant Chunks rode in a shoe strapped to her clothes. She sought tricks and humiliations in the name of her god, yet repeatedly used healing magic to protect travelers who badly needed help. The difference between her compassion and her willingness to hurt strangers never fully disappeared.[^intro]

<a id="identity-and-appearance"></a>
<a id="played-development"></a>

## A strange guide in Sigil

Squirts met Vorel, the disembodied heart of Nash and Nothing after Hope and Stanley's deaths. She knew an elusive trickster chapel where a priest might help restore their friends, and guided the group through Sigil to it. The priest Kidigree offered only a future ritual, contingent on humiliating a prominent opponent. She was a willing partner in the scheme, not herself the resurrection priest. Her early religious name sounded uncertain, but she later spelled **Olidammara** aloud during an actual divine intervention. The name is better supported than the early phonetic renderings, although the original audio and character sheet have not been independently checked.[^sigil]

## The price of resurrection

To win back the two friends, Squirts helped the chapel sabotage a tournament. She administered a supposed forgetfulness mixture to a living, unconscious man, without proof it erased anything. At the contest, invisibility and sickness-inducing needles let her interfere with opponents, even Nash when he faced Vorel. Nash resisted the injected poison; others were affected. When security barred her for her floating magical stone, Kidigree smuggled her inside. **She never entered as a fighter.** Vorel later publicly called his team **Mighty Squirts** and identified her as its manager, but no earlier official team registration was shown.[^sabotage]

Not all of Squirts's tricks were harmless. She and Kidigree concocted a forbidden performance-enhancing potion which really improved Vorel's tournament decisions. The priest later substituted a sample to defeat the doping test. More gravely, Squirts attacked a young lizardfolk who wanted Vorel to become his father, left the badly burned boy in an alley, then returned to stabilize him. **He survived and fled**. Observers believed him murdered, and Vorel lost a proposed sponsorship. Even after his victory she could neither undo the assault nor ensure the rumor was corrected everywhere. Yet she also used magic to cure Vorel's lasting poison and heal endangered companions.[^harm][^finals]

## The chapel fulfills its promise

Kidigree eventually brought Hope and Stanley back in living bodies through ritual mirrors. Squirts had helped win that result through deception, but the restoration itself was witnessed. She chose to stay with the priest and Chunks while the four principal companions continued. Her departure as an active adventurer was **not** a death or an irreversible farewell. A later player's account that Squirts had been created as a temporary alternate character does not change what happened in Sigil.[^restoration]

## Returning aboard the airship

Squirts reappeared with Chunks hidden among oranges aboard the party's Outlands airship; the means by which they boarded were not shown. During a dangerous encounter with a transported moon and Tungan forces, she invoked her trickster god and achieved a striking result: **the moon and remaining hostile forces disappeared**, while the wreckage from the earlier fighting remained. She also brought herself and Chunks to safety with *Dimension Door* during a disastrous portal crossing.[^intervention]

She continued traveling with Vorel, Nash and Stanley through Sigil, strange gateways and an alternate Nash's asteroid tower. She charmed a driver and healed severely wounded companions, including using a powerful healing spell on Stanley and aiding the survival of Fambo Joe. Her readiness to scheme remained, but she was more than a comic passenger: her prayers and quick judgments repeatedly changed the outcome for people around her.[^travels]

## The rescue and her first death

Back in T'ietkalla, Squirts carried Chunks and helped the travelers move through demon-held streets using *Silence* and *Pass without Trace*. At the Golden Sands, Bok Bok greeted Vorel and showed an embarrassed attraction toward Squirts, but no romance or shared parentage was established. During the battle against General Carthax, Squirts used *Mass Healing Word* to aid the wounded Omnom. Carthax's destructive beam then **disintegrated her into ash**. Chunks survived the attack. Vorel collected some ashes in a vial supplied by Stanley, hoping that her loss might somehow be reversed.[^first-death]

On the Elhandari vessel Vorel hoped the elves could restore her, but no resurrection happened there. Later he said the ashes had been lost, perhaps to the vacuum or his fiery weapon; **the actual loss of the vial was not independently witnessed**. At Elhandar the companions held a real memorial, and Bok Bok pledged to raise Chunks as a father. The baby's survival and the new guardian's devotion were her legacy, not evidence that she lived on literally in her child.[^memorial]

## Reawakened in Hell

In a separate later scene in Hell, Brett/Asmodeus claimed that he had recovered Squirts's soul, made arrangements and changed her body. A pod opened and a living-looking Squirts awoke. Brett appointed her leader of a group sent to track Hope. This **physical reappearance was played** and follows her earlier real death, but his account of its mechanics, Squirts's consent, and the nature of her altered body remain uncertain. A later player reflection about unused inspiration in the original fatal encounter did not retroactively change that death.[^hell]

## A contradictory name on the passenger lists

During the Elhandari household's evacuation, the table sometimes called a small companion “Squirts” despite her prior death. One such mention was explicitly corrected to **Chunks**, while some were not corrected. The newly reawakened Squirts was shown separately in Hell; **nothing showed her returning to Elhandar, traveling with Bok Bok or leaving for Mars**. These roster contradictions remain unresolved speech or transcription questions, rather than proof of an unseen transfer, secret revival or a second child.[^roster]

<a id="s3e02--dead-head-grafted-to-steerious-not-resurrected"></a>

## A second death aboard Fate Six

Squirts later led her Hell-aligned companions in a real attack on Fate Six. She used *Harm* against Vorel and healed the attackers. Hope struck her down, Doctor Spider restored her briefly, and Squirts continued fighting. When she fell again, **Doctor Spider's own *Wall of Fire* burned her incapacitated body**, and her final death save failed. This was another witnessed death, not simply the old T'ietkalla event being recounted. No subsequent independent return to life is demonstrated.[^second-death]

After the battle, Doctor Spider grafted **Squirts's dead head** and the dead Blood Skunk's head onto the living minotaur Steerious Business. The host remained alive with three heads, but neither attached head spoke or acted as a revived individual. Vorel noticed the altered cyborg head without achieving positive recognition. Steerious and Spider left the party alive at Blast Belly Rock. That grotesque surgery was **not a third resurrection of Squirts**.[^graft]

<a id="open-questions"></a>

## Current status

Squirts's last independently active appearance ends in her second death aboard Fate Six. Her severed head is subsequently last seen attached to Steerious, lifeless and unresponsive. **Chunks remains a separate surviving child**, cared for by Bok Bok, not a reincarnation of Squirts. No played finale establishes another revival, a later fate for Steerious or the attached head, or a final disposition of Squirts's soul. Those questions remain open.[^final]
