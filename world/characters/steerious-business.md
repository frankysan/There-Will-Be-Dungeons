# Steerious Business

## Introduction

**Steerious Business** was a black-furred minotaur adventurer who joined [Hope Akmenos](hope-akmenos.md) on her independent travels after the party's separation. He favored force, bravado and personal combat, but his allegiance belonged to a paid infernal company rather than permanently to Hope or the companions aboard Fate Six. His extraordinary survival after that company's later attack left him alive with two additional, lifeless heads grafted to his shoulders.[^steerious-intro]

## Hope's infernal company

The casino host [Brett](brett-asmodeus.md), presenting himself as Asmodeus, introduced Steerious to Hope as one of three companions who would accompany her to T'ietkalla. The others were [Blood Skunk Hendrickson](blood-skunk-hendrickson.md) and [Doctor Spider](dr-spider.md). Brett openly admitted that they also served as his eyes and ears; each had a distinct role and his own agency. Steerious's fashionable business clothes and physical stature made the minotaur hard to miss.[^steerious-crew]

The company traveled among T'ietkalla, Sigil and other places. During E112 Steerious attacked two civilians who survived and killed a bounty hunter. He surrendered his greatsword as payment to Hesterbrook for passage on the Styx in E113; Brett gave the sword back in E116. That same episode the group failed in an attempted assault on a cargo ship and withdrew alive. These events establish violence and temporary assignments, not Steerious's final loyalty or a complete account of his motives.[^steerious-travels]

## The battle aboard Fate Six

In **S3E01**, Steerious returned as part of a Hell-aligned force attacking Fate Six. The GM presented Brett's company as considering Hope a deserter, though this was the attack party's rationale rather than proof of a newly witnessed broken pact. Steerious fought [Vorel](vorel.md) in an eager barbarian duel, biting with a transformed jaw and striking with a life-stealing sword. Vorel knocked him unconscious, Doctor Spider healed him back into action, and Vorel **downed him a second time** shortly before the Principal's arrival disrupted the battle. He was gravely injured, **not confirmed dead** at that session's end.[^steerious-duel]

## S3E02 — surviving with two inert heads

In the continuation, Steerious **succeeded on his death saves** and remained alive while Doctor Spider and he were detained in Fate Six's magically concealed brig. Their fallen teammates Squirts and Blood Skunk were dead; their remains were brought to the brig without any successful revival. Doctor Spider later surgically attached **the dead heads of Squirts and Blood Skunk** to Steerious's shoulders. The GM accepted the fantastical anatomy: Steerious was a **living minotaur with three heads**, but the other two were limp, unresponsive and **not additional living personalities**.[^steerious-heads]

The joke name “Steerious Bloodsquirts” was entertained in banter, but did not replace his established name or resurrect either head's original owner. As arranged following Doctor Spider's life-saving treatment of Union Wheel, **Steerious and Spider were released on Blast Belly Rock**. They were not enlisted as Fate Six crew or shown immediately traveling to Mars with the main adventurers.[^steerious-release]

## Current status

**Steerious was last observed alive and released with Doctor Spider on Blast Belly Rock in S3E02, bearing two inert grafted heads.** A later independent fate or lasting allegiance is not demonstrated by the reviewed sources. His original name remains **Steerious Business**, as confirmed by the archive owner; provisional transcript spellings such as Stirius or Sterius do not establish separate characters.[^steerious-status]
