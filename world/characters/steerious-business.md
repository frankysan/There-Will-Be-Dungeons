# Steerious Business

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Role:** Minotaur adventurer travelling with Hope in Arc Five. **Name:** **Steerious Business** is the spelling confirmed by the archive owner; a pun/joke origin is discussed in [June 2020 bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-06.md). Do not silently replace the name with ASR guesses. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional scenes, with owner-confirmed spelling.

Brett, the Asmodeus-claiming host, introduces Steerious among Hope's assigned companions in **E102**. He joins **Blood Skunk Hendrickson** and **Dr. Spider** as her independent party. They travel through Tietkalla, Sigil and other planes; their alliance is contingent and each has a separate personality and agency. [E102](../../episodes/E102.md) · [Hope](hope-akmenos.md).

In **E112** Steerious assaults two civilians who survive and kills a bounty hunter. In **E113** he surrenders his greatsword to Hesterbrook as fare for the Styx passage, and the group reaches Pandemonium and returns toward Sigil. **Brett gives him back the sword in E116.** During E116's attempt to attack a cargo ship, the four retreat alive after failing to seize the vessel. [E112](../../episodes/E112.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md) · [E116](../../episodes/E116.md).

In [played S3E01](../../episodes/S3E01.md), Steerious is **a returning Hell-aligned attack-party minotaur** against Fate Six, with the GM explaining that Brett's team considers Hope a deserter. He revels in a **personal barbarian duel with Vorel**, shapeshifting his jaw to bite and drawing a **life-stealing sword**. Vorel **downs him**, but Doctor Spider's healing brings him back; Vorel **downs him AGAIN near the moment the Principle arrives**. At the episode cutoff he is **unconscious/down and not confirmed dead**, so neither a completed permanent execution nor surrender should be assumed. The Principle's unexpected appearance **surprises Hell's attackers as well as the protagonists**. The correct curated name **Steerious Business** is owner-confirmed; **“Stirius/Sterius” in the S3E01 ASR are approximations, not new minotaurs**.
