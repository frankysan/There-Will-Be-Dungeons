# Buttons — talking cat of the Arcanum

## Introduction

Buttons was an intelligent orange cat with spectacles and a mechanical leg, who joined the travelers aboard **Fate 4** and became especially important to [Stanley Billings](stanley-billings.md). He spoke of the **Arcanum Universalis**, a wizard tied to his own transformation and the larger world beyond the party's travels. He helped direct the journey toward T'ietkalla, accompanied Stanley into the oil sands and offered him a written *Blink* spell. The combination of apparent loyalty, arcane knowledge and guarded intentions made him a companion whose purpose remained difficult to read.[^buttons-early]

## Stanley, Diontalus and divided loyalties

As Stanley's struggles with [Diontalus](diontalus.md) deepened, Buttons became troubled by the changes he saw. Their private conversations in Trashburg revealed affection and distrust as well as disagreement about whom to follow. Buttons' later conduct suggests a divided loyalty: he wanted Stanley separated from the power occupying him, yet chose to act against the companions who had traveled with him.[^buttons-conflict]

At the confrontation with Jolt, Buttons described a counteroffer he claimed to have arranged with [Monsoon Cabbage](monsoon-cabbage.md), involving the **Gem of Tides**, control of T'ietkalla, the [Principal](the-principal.md) and promised rewards. Those details were **Buttons' account of proposed bargains**, not proof that every party had accepted them or that the plans came to pass. The resulting violence brought Buttons into direct conflict with his former companions. Hope restrained and wounded him; **Vorel dealt the fatal sword blow**.[^buttons-ambush]

## What remained after his death

Buttons' death did not end the arguments over his intentions. Stanley later questioned his corpse with *Speak with Dead*, gaining limited answers rather than restoring him to life. Diontalus also drew arcane energy from the remains, but later commentary did not establish an enduring mechanical power transferred from Buttons. After the time jump Stanley still kept objects associated with him, including a dictionary, a strange reference book and a regional map.[^buttons-aftermath]

The later cast discussion brought a different perspective: Beau explained that **Buttons had romantically loved Stanley** and had hoped to bring him to the Arcanum to remove Diontalus. This was retrospective revelation of intended motive, **not evidence Stanley knew it at the time**, that a rescue succeeded or that the ambush had been harmless. When the Principal subsequently cited Buttons' earlier report while extracting Diontalus into a crystal, his account of what Buttons wanted remained the Principal's interpretation of a deceased person's purpose.[^buttons-motive]

## Current status

**Buttons was killed by Vorel in E049 and did not return to life in the later reviewed events.** His corpse was questioned in E050; his possessions, remembered motives and reported information continued to influence Stanley's story. Later references to Buttons are not further appearances of the living cat.[^buttons-status]
