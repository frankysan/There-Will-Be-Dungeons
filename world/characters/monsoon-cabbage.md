# Monsoon Cabbage

In the crowded streets below T'ietkalla's keep, Monsoon Cabbage is a figure of wealth, threats and uneasy bargains. An older tiefling and leader of the Twenty-Sixers, he rules his block through favors and fear while contesting the keep's authority with other gangs. The companions meet him as a dangerous political player, not yet the armored defender of Earth he will become.[^gangs]

<a id="e032e054--twenty-sixers-contested-claims-and-a-witnessed-escape"></a>

## The Twenty-Six Block

Monsoon offers to help the travelers reach the keep if they kill a rival leader. Vorel and the others eventually bring him Blunt Goliver's head, but his hospitality conceals surveillance, coerced medicine and threats. The Toe Rippers, Jolt, Buttons and the wider struggle over T'ietkalla's rulers draw him into schemes whose participants do not always agree on their purpose.[^gangs]

He speaks of the city's ancient past, Kefeli, Andreeste the Nourisher and a council of vampires. These are his accounts of a contested history, not an established explanation of everything beneath the keep. A possible arrangement with Jolt and the Principal is described by Buttons, whose interpretation need not be Monsoon's full plan.[^order]

At the height of the conflict, Monsoon issues **Order 26**, causing P0D and other pixies to become huge, dangerous versions of themselves against their will. The ensuing disaster exceeds the simple appearance of a controlled attack. The later cast discussion says that Monsoon's Order 26 was prepared as a disguised super-soldier move, while the specific froghemoth consequences emerged unexpectedly in play.[^order]

<a id="e055--alleged-death-contradicted-by-later-played-appearances"></a>

## The shattered court and a false report

When the companions reach the ruined throne room, Monsoon holds power over the wreckage. Prince Aladweir lies dead; Suck Master Din is a prisoner; and Angelashi remains alive as a hostage. Monsoon declares that he will yield the city to Hope, but warns that Kefeli's death has supposedly exposed it to Andreeste's claim. His prophecy and professed abdication are not an independently verified transfer of divine power or proof he has relinquished all influence.[^keep]

When Vorel strikes, **Monsoon dissolves into mist and escapes**. A captured Twenty-Sixer later claims Monsoon has been killed, but the rumor cannot overturn the witnessed escape. His continued appearances settle the apparent contradiction: he survives the fall of the court.[^keep]

<a id="s2e01s2e03--physically-returns-exorcises-stanley-and-volunteers-for-earth"></a>

## The visitor at Villa Or'Nath

Long after the battle for T'ietkalla, Monsoon appears at Villa Or'Nath with Softhead confined alive in a cage. He cannot cross the threshold until Stanley invites him inside. He displays a crest that Stanley remembers from his family and speaks of an ancient circle containing Stanley's father, another Nash, Groxl and the Principal. Their supposed fellowship, the Principal's rise to lichhood, and a catastrophic ancient quarrel remain **Monsoon's interested testimony**, rather than witnessed history.[^fellowship]

His offer to help is nevertheless consequential. With the companions' consent, he leads a rite against the obligation binding [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) to the Soulbroker through the Weir. Hope destroys the manifested curse entity, and the GM confirms that the obligation is lifted while older pact benefits persist. Monsoon leaves with Softhead still captive and alive; helping Stanley does not make Monsoon's other claims trustworthy or render his treatment of Softhead harmless.[^exorcism]

<a id="s3e04--physically-on-mars-offered-conditional-farewell-and-approved-tower-assault"></a>

## A reluctant defender

Monsoon later appears at a council of Earth's defenders on Mars and openly offers to fight as a **vampire knight**. He has committed himself, but does not bring a demonstrated vampire army with him.[^volunteers]

Approaching Hope in dark battle armor, he reflects on his bitterness and his seemingly endless hunger. He says he hopes to leave the world for others like himself **after** the war is won. Yet this is a wish for a future departure, not an exit that happens then: he subsequently participates in the defenders' second council and volunteers for the northeastern anti-air position at the Solar Mines. The council's plans, including cooperation with Diontalus against enemy weapons, are not already accomplished victories.[^volunteers]

<a id="s3e05--physically-joins-earth-invasion-and-rides-toward-ne-cannon-success-pending"></a>

## Back on Earth

Monsoon descends to Earth with the allied force, takes an Elhandari hovering speeder and rides toward his assigned northeastern anti-air cannon. Over the radio he reports movement and the changing battle around the northern approach. He is physically deployed, but that day's reports do **not** confirm that he destroys the cannon, kills a Slow Peter or finishes the wider operation.[^deployment]

<a id="s3e09--actually-rescues-all-four-protagonists-stays-to-hold-solar-mines-field"></a>

## Holding the line

After a devastating explosion around the giant Groxl-like foe, the four companions find themselves alive inside an allied defensive circle. Admiral Chrysorius credits **Monsoon with bringing all four to safety**; White Nash subsequently heals the unconscious heroes. The rescue is credited to Monsoon by a present commander, not confused with the healing.[^rescue]

Monsoon has also seen a clean-looking district beneath a glass dome from the air and suggests it may offer another route. That impression does not prove access to the buried complex. As the companions depart on their infiltration mission, he remains with the principal allied force to help hold the line and flies out toward another battle. He does not accompany them into the dome or its underground passages.[^rescue]

<a id="s3e17--actual-final-battle-arrival-claws-and-fate-six-crash"></a>

## The last battle

When the Principal's chrome copies threaten the companions, Monsoon arrives aboard Fate Six in enclosed vampire-knight armor. He leaps into the fighting and strikes at the copies with his claws while Stanley's prismatic barrier still stands. Fate Six crashes through the surrounding enemies and is shattered. **Its destruction does not kill Monsoon**: he is still present and alive when the final day begins.[^battle]

<a id="s3e18--final-witnessed-fate-voluntary-sunlight-death-hope-inherits-helmet"></a>
<a id="connections-and-evidence"></a>

## Current status

With the danger from the Principal's copies overcome, Monsoon comes before Hope. He says the long oath by which he sought to defend Earth has at last been fulfilled, and that he has lived long enough despite the dishonor of earlier years. Then he deliberately opens his protective helmet, steps into full sunlight and casts off his armor. **His vampire body burns and dissolves until nothing remains.** This is his directly witnessed death, not the old rumor of his demise or another escape as mist.[^end]

Hope takes his surviving vampire-knight helmet. Monsoon's words about leaving the realm to her are a farewell and an endorsement, not a magical transfer of office. His death comes shortly **before** Earth's restoration; he is not among those who later rebuild the world he helped protect.[^end]
