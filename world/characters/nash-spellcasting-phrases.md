# Nash Maggard — spellcasting phrases and rhymes

[Nash Maggard](nash-maggard.md), portrayed by **Scott**, improvises incantations, rhymes, song snippets and sometimes abandoned verses around his magic. This is a **source-indexed register**, not an account of every spell Nash casts. Beau praises the effort behind the words in [S1E046](../../episodes/E046.md) ~02:52:57–02:53:10 ([P][046-p] 2797–2801; [Q][046-q] 4622–4629); that does not establish when the habit began.

**Scope:** 45 numbered findings, ordered by episode: **42 in Wastes Season 1 through E126** and **three clearly separated later-season findings/seeds**. The latest episode review is **Wastes S3E10**, with no new original-Nash NSP identifier (NSP-045 was added for S3E06). The older-episode *backfill* stops at **S1E111**; later findings come from regular episode reviews or pre-existing seeds, not an extension of that backfill. Updated **2026-10-27**. **All wording and voice attribution remain ASR-provisional, not audio-verified; the collection is not exhaustive.**

## How to read the register

- Read **top to bottom in episode/time order**. **NSP-001–NSP-044 are permanent discovery identifiers**, *not* chronological numbers; do not renumber them or create a second row for a repeated scene.
- **P / Q** link to the exact **Parakeet processed / Qwen Q8 + Canary CTC + Nemotron** subtitle exports. Export names are not validated run metadata. Timestamps use the **recording clock**; cue references are local to each export.
- Code-formatted text reproduces **ASR wording** (slashes join line/cue breaks), not verified speech or an edited polished rhyme. Keep competing readings, awkward grammar, interrupted endings and speaker-boundary doubts; do not silently invent words to make a couplet scan.
- Every row distinguishes **cast, failed/attempted cast, item, character ability, preparation or someone else's supplied words**. Nash is the contextual performer unless an entry says otherwise. Diarization numbers do not prove who spoke. A rhyme does not prove a successful spell or a mechanical requirement.
- Other castings **without located distinctive wording**, proposals, non-casting poems, and out-of-game recollections are documented separately below. An ASR-text negative is not proof nothing was spoken on the audio.
