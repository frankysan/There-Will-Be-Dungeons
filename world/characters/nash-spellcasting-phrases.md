# Nash Maggard — spellcasting phrases and rhymes

**Character:** [Nash Maggard](nash-maggard.md), portrayed by **Scott**. **Continuity:** The Wastes. **Spoilers:** Selected Season 1 magic through **E111**, later evidence reviews **E114–E123** (including **E120's performed *Haste* rhyme**, and non-casting poems/retrospective memories), plus a marked section of later-season seeds. **Status:** Updated 2026-10-24; **ASR-provisional, not audio-verified or an exhaustive collection**. **Current backfill ceiling: S1E111.**

Nash improvises strange phrases, rhymes and sometimes unfinished verses around his magic. This page collects the wording separately from his character history, following the provenance practice of [Vorel's title register](vorel-titles.md). That recognizes an existing habit; it does **not** establish E046 as its beginning.

## E120 — performed rhymed *Haste* and later spells

**NSP-041 · corrected S1E120 ~00:26:29–00:26:44 · performed *Haste* on Vorel:** The Parakeet text renders the rhyme as `This hunt we're on shall not go to waste / and now give Vorel a healthy dose of haste`; Qwen renders `This hunt we're on shall not go to waste / I now give Vorrel a healthy dose of haste`. The first half is closely shared; **the lead-in “and” versus “I” in the second half and Vorel name spelling are ASR-provisional**. Vorel visibly receives the speed effect. **It is a new performed phrase**, not just a suggestion or uneventful rehearsal. ## Reading and maintaining the register

- **P / Q** link to the exact local **Parakeet processed / Qwen Q8 + Canary CTC + Nemotron** SRT exports. These are filename labels, not verified run metadata. Times are approximate **recording clock**, not story time; cue numbers belong to the named export.
- Wording in code spans is an **ASR excerpt**, with cue breaks joined. It is not a certified quotation. Keep odd wording, unfinished attempts and meaningful competing readings; do not improve a rhyme to make it work. Agreement between exports does not verify speech.
- **Scott performing Nash** is the contextual attribution for the main register, pending listening. Numeric diarizer labels are local to an export; they do not independently identify the performer. Credit another participant separately if they supply or finish a line.
- Record each distinct phrase or meaningful variant with a stable **NSP** identifier, corrected season/episode, clock/cues, spell or ability **when identifiable**, context, source links and confidence. IDs are stable entry keys, not a claim about discovery or performance order. Earlier findings can be inserted chronologically without renumbering existing entries.
- Distinguish **cast/attempted spell**, **character ability**, **magic-item activation**, **proposed wording** and **out-of-game recollection**. A rhyme is not proof of a successful spell or a fixed mechanical requirement. Repeated spells can have different phrases; link unchanged repeats to the existing entry and retain material wording or outcome changes.
