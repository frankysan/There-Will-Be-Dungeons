# Henitar Grape — the three-headed mercenary commander

## Introduction

Henitar Grape appeared before the defenders of Elhandar dressed in purple, three human heads speaking from a single body as he hovered upon a disk. A mobile castle and a fleet of warships accompanied him. He presented himself as commander of a hired army and announced a contract against Hope and Vorel. His arrival was no empty threat: soldiers materialized inside the High Elf's chamber while the fleet fired upon the station.[^assault]

## A commander hired for a private war

Henitar claimed that a Mind Master in Sigil had hired him. He also supplied a story about Tobacho Fleur's supposed imprisonment by the Lady of Pain and accused Vorel of cheating at M Sports. Those were the commander's claims, not independently witnessed changes of power in Sigil. Nor did he clearly establish which of his two targets was required dead and which might be captured. When the defenders resisted, Henitar teleported away from the immediate struggle. His army continued the assault and Elhandar began its catastrophic fall toward Earth.[^assault]

## The battle for the guitar

Henitar later confronted the companions outside Villa Or'Nath as his castle ship crashed into Elhandar. By then he wielded an immense magical electric guitar, whose violent music battered Diontalus until the archfey lay motionless. Six blue spheres circling the commander intercepted spells and struck at those who came close. His music compelled Hope and Vorel to attack one another, while Henitar claimed responsibility for thwarting an attempted teleport.[^battle]

The companions found ways around his defenses. Nash, transformed into a black dragon, burned him with acid breath; the spheres did not absorb the dragon's attack. Stanley wrested the guitar from Henitar through magic and held it against his effort to reclaim it. Henitar withdrew into the sky, but Nash's second acid attack broke his hovering disk and sent him to the sand. He lay limp after the fall. That defeat was not yet a witnessed death.[^battle]

## Surrender and death

The next time the companions addressed him, Henitar was alive and conscious. He admitted that the Mind Master had commissioned the attack, described Diontalus as an unwelcome surprise and pleaded for his life. Stanley wounded his leg without killing him. Nash, still a dragon, nevertheless tore off one of the commander's heads and killed him while he was yielding. The guitar remained with Stanley.[^death]

Diontalus, revived by Stanley, later drew blue soul-like energy from Henitar's corpse and consumed it. A subsequent conversation conducted through *Speak with Dead* produced more claims about the employer, the journey to Earth and the lost castle's return route. Neither those answers nor the visible consumption proved the ultimate metaphysical fate of Henitar's soul.[^death]

## Current status

Henitar was killed after surrender, not by the earlier fall. His castle was lost in the station catastrophe, the guitar passed into Stanley's custody, and the commander left only attributed testimony about his paymaster and their plans. Whether the soul-like essence Diontalus consumed can ever be restored is not established.[^death]
