# Henitar / Henatar Grape (?) — mercenary fleet commander

> **The Wastes · first observed E125 ~00:33–01:03.** Personal name and army title are **ASR-provisional**, not audio-verified. [E125 dossier](../../episodes/E125.md) · [comparison](../../reports/E125_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md).

A purple-robed **human with three heads speaking together** arrives on a levitating disk during an armed assault on **Elhandar**. He introduces himself in the exports as **Henitar/Henatar Grape (?)**, leader of a mercenary force called roughly the **Asphyxiant Army (?)**, whose formation includes a huge mobile castle and many airships. Approximately **one hundred Hive/Sigil mercenaries teleport into the High Elf's chamber** while the fleet bombards the station. He calls the assault a contract to take **Hope and Vorel**, alleging his employer wants one dead and will accept the other alive or dead; the exact assignment **is not stated unambiguously**.

He **claims** the employer is **Mind Master Gerbken Shartlinger** of Sigil, and alleges Gerbken informed the Lady of Pain about **Tobacho Fleur's** designs and caused the dragon's imprisonment in a labyrinth. He also alleges that Vorel cheated in M Sports. **These claims are not separately witnessed or verified**; no independent evidence here settles Tobacho's fate or the employer's actual command structure. The army's attack on Elhandar is itself **played**, however.

**During battle (~01:02)** the commander **teleports away**, leaving his hovering disk behind as an inert metal object; **Vorel does not take it**. The fleet continues attacking. The High Elf reflects some projectiles; **Diontalus visibly damages hostile ships**, and the station begins falling toward Earth. The commander is **not shown killed, captured, converted or deprived of the mobile castle**, and Vorel's threat to take that castle is **only a boast**. [Diontalus](diontalus.md) · [Elhandar](../places/elhandar.md).

## E126 — defeat of Diontalus and desert battle

After the fleet's assault sends **Elhandar** toward Earth, the commander's **castle ship catches fire and crashes into the station**, producing an immense explosion; the fate of every vessel/crew member is not audited. Henatar's hovering disk and **[magical electric guitar](../technology/henatar-guitar.md)** reappear outside **Villa Ornath**, where he **beats spectral Diontalus to the ground** with huge sonic chords; the archfey ends **motionless, not confirmed dead**.

The commander's **six orbiting blue spheres** block several kinds of incoming magic, recharge between turns and attempt stunning intruders within reach; his guitar **charm-compels Hope and Vorel to attack the nearest person**. He blocks Hope's attempted **Teleport** and claims this is a deliberate bounty-hunter defense. Nash turns into a **black dragon** and uses **two acid breaths**, which the spheres cannot absorb. **Stanley magically pulls Henatar's guitar away with Telekinesis** and resists Henatar's attempt to reclaim it, though this is **not proof of permanent ownership**. Henatar flies higher, apparently retreating; the **second acid blast destroys his hovering disk**, and he drops to the sand, **limp at E126 cutoff**. **Death, restraint, interrogation or a renewed escape are not narrated in E126.** [E126](../../episodes/E126.md) · [comparison](../../reports/E126_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md). **ASR-only; exact name/music mechanics require original audio.**

## E128 — executed after surrender; soul drawn from corpse

**E126's actual ending was a fall with this commander limp but not confirmed dead**. In [E128](../../episodes/E128.md) ~00:05–00:19, Nash as a black dragon discovers **Henatar alive, conscious and yielding**. Henatar admits the **Mind Master in Sigil commissioned the attack**, claims the appearance of Diontalus surprised him, and **pleads for a bargain and his life**. Stanley **nonlethally stabs his leg** with Diplomacy but does not grant a pardon. **Nash grips a side head in his jaws, physically tears it loose, and the GM declares Henatar takes his final breath and dies**; a second head is removed **after death**. His guitar **stays with Stanley**, rather than being returned to him. **Diontalus is healed by Stanley**, then **visibly extracts and consumes blue soul-like energy from Henatar's corpse**, leaving a gray husk. A later ***Speak with Dead* actually works** on Henatar's surviving mouth: it alleges a tip to travel to Earth, an **educated guess** about Elhandar, **anti-Vorel M-sports Weekly smear**, **Mind Master now in Tobacho's Hive chair**, and the former castle's **coordinate console** as intended return route. These answers are **corpse testimony**, not independent verification or a recovered functional castle. **Death E128 is verified in play, not E126; magical fate of the consumed soul remains uncertain.** One hazardous orb shocks Hope and is destroyed when touched; **the full orb set is not collected**.
