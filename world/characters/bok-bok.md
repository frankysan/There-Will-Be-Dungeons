# Bok Bok

**Alternative ASR spellings:** `Bokbak`, `Buckbuck`, `Bachbok`; **Bok Bok** appears in the fan wiki and is the established project display. **Identity:** Goblin companion of Vorel. **Arc Two status:** Alive and back with Vorel by E054.

Vorel captures or brings along Bok Bok during Dusthill's post-raid events in E006, and the goblin gradually becomes an enduring companion, admirer and source of unexpected loyalty. He is not a member of the original four PCs and should not be inserted into E001–E005 merely because he later appears in every party summary.

He is separated from the party at points around **Slavetown**, travels on his own and reunites with Vorel. His devotion is tested during the campaign's most dangerous episodes: he is found captive alongside other allies and later follows the party toward **Tietkalla**. In E048 the group frees him **alive** from a keep prison cell, and he joyfully rejoins Vorel.

He directly participates in the chaotic fighting against giant P0D and later watches over **Vorel while Vorel is petrified** in E051. His insistence on staying with his companion should not be confused with a newly granted permanent class or magical ability. In E054 tiefling guards temporarily separate/restrain him, after which Vorel retrieves him and discusses **future squire training**—a plan, not a completed apprenticeship.

In the Year One Q&A Beau reveals that Bok Bok's first appearance was an **improvised NPC encounter** when Vorel explored alone. That production fact explains his narrative origin, **not** an in-world supernatural emergence.

**E117 update:** While everyone is at Elhandar, [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) shows **Bok Bok actually joins Stanley, Vorel and Chunks on a hunt**, welcomes Hope back, and explains he wants to **remain at the family's cottage defending Angelashi and Nash's four children** while **Vorel takes Chunks on their next expedition to train him**. **Vorel subsequently carries Chunks to the tiefling refuge**, while Bok Bok **stays at the cottage**. This **supersedes E115's intended travel arrangement**, but is not legal adoption, biological paternity, a death or irrevocable renunciation of Bok Bok's parental commitment. ## E115 — Chunks’s caretaker and a hand-status correction

**Bok Bok helps clear the medical barricade**, then **disembarks at Elhandar carrying Chunks**. He asks for **Squirts’s funeral**, grieves during Vorel’s remembrance, says he would have made her his wife (**no marriage established**), and throws dynamite into the lake. **No one is hurt by that explosion**. He declares **he is no longer only Vorel’s helper: he will be Chunks’s father/caretaker**. Vorel accepts, and Bok Bok takes Chunks inside, blankets him and **provides care through the completed four recovery days**. This is **a performed caretaker commitment**, not proved biological paternity or formal legal adoption; Chunks remains alive despite a garbled Qwen “dead chunks” fragment.

At **~02:14–02:15**, after discussion of the missing hand, **the GM now treats Bok Bok as having two hands**, admits uncertainty remembering the earlier loss and suggests elven medical assistance. **This is an explicit continuity correction, not a played regrowth scene or named spell**. Preserve **E111’s hand loss and E114’s no-regrowth cutoff** as historical evidence. ## E114 — conscious again and guarding the medical bay

On the Elhandar-bound ship, **Bok Bok begins unconscious and badly burned**. During decompression the GM briefly says “lifeless,” then **explicitly corrects it to “unconscious”**; this is not his death. **Stanley casts Healing Word for eight hit points (~01:01–01:02), bringing him to responsiveness**, with burns improving but **no missing-hand regrowth**. Vorel gives him a fire-retardant canister and tells him to protect Angelashi's medical bay. Bok Bok **stacks canisters into a barricade**, obstructing the door; an attendant later clears enough to close the blast doors, while he continues guarding his work. **Bok Bok and Chunks survive the boarding attack and remain aboard at cutoff**, without reaching Elhandar. [E114 dossier](../../episodes/E114.md) · [comparison](../../reports/E114_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md), **ASR-provisional**.

## E111 — reunion, dynamite and missing hand

After the Tietkalla invasion, [E111](../../episodes/E111.md) **actually finds Bok Bok alive** in the hidden passage beneath the **Golden Sands**, where he has been protecting pregnant **Angelashi**. He joyously embraces **Vorel**, speaks learned Common and behaves bashfully towards kobold **Squirts**. He joins the original party and the Elhandari rescue operation against **General Carthax**. The elf-provided **pink pill** he accepts has a fast-rest effect/cost not fully tracked for him. **Bok Bok runs into Carthax's mouth with dynamite**, setting off an internal explosion; **Stanley uses *Mantle of Majesty* and *Command* (“vomit”)** on the pacified general, who **expels Bok Bok alive**, suffering multiple burns and **the loss of one hand**. This **is not Bok Bok's death**. **Vorel carries him aboard the departing Elhandari spaceship** along with **Squirts's ashes**. No healing of the missing hand or prosthetic is shown. ## New-arc status — E055–E056

**Bok Bok** is Vorel's **kitchen worker/apprentice at the Golden Sands**, helping maintain the adventurers' guild during three months of settled life, though habits about meat and order still cause comic friction. He remains at the inn alongside **Angelashi and Fibel/Fibble** during E056's dangerous stone storm, rather than accompanying the four protagonists into the ancestral rite. No injury/death to Bok Bok is reported by E056. [E055](../../episodes/E055.md) · [E056](../../episodes/E056.md) · [April cast roundtable](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-04.md).
