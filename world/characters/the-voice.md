# The hooded Voice — Hope's patron (identity unresolved)

## Introduction

A face-obscured figure known only as **the Voice** appeared privately to [Hope Akmenos](hope-akmenos.md) as she struggled with the powers of the [Hand of the Voice](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md). The figure offered guidance, issued threats and seemed to seek fragments of its own lost power. Its secret conversations were not automatically heard by the other companions, and its actual name, nature and ultimate designs were never securely established. The attached Hand, the separate [Zorander's Fist](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) gauntlet and [Vorel's Eye of Groxl](../technology/eye-of-groxl.md) were not one interchangeable artifact.[^voice-intro]

## The White Tower and Hope's altered hand

At the White Tower, Hope obtained the Fist, then followed the Voice's private instruction to give up her own right hand and join herself to a severed, living replacement. The **Hand of the Voice** fused to her body and changed her appearance and abilities. In a private moment the hooded figure called her its champion; she expressed her wish to become a god. Vorel glimpsed something of the apparition and witnessed the Eye of Groxl restored to his chest, but the patron's complete conversation with Hope was not shared with the others. The gauntlet came loose again and remained separate from her attached Hand.[^voice-tower]

The figure's interventions continued as Hope embarked on her independent journey. During her encounter with [Brett](brett-asmodeus.md), the Voice encouraged her to demand a stronger travel ring. Its presence and recommendations did not prove that it was Brett, a particular deity or a reliable narrator of its own history.[^voice-casino]

## The uncollected Eye and a refused command

The Voice directed Hope toward an **Eye of the Voice**, associated with the prison called Pentel Weiss or Pentelvice in provisional sources. In E113 the **Council of Clowns** provided an attributed location lead and compass, but the Eye was not recovered. The figure pressed Hope to hurry in E118, causing pain through the attached Hand; Hope did not reach the prison in that episode. Neither the Council's information nor the figure's pressure demonstrated what the Eye actually contained.[^voice-eye]

In E124, when Hope was alone in a private exchange, the Voice demanded that she kill Diontalus and take his power. She **refused twice**. It offered a spoken phrase said to undo an archfey, but the exact words remain unclear in the processed transcripts. Hope did not speak the phrase to Diontalus, kill him or absorb his essence. The Voice's claim that all archfey had such a weakness remained untested.[^voice-diontalus]

## Pentel Weiss and fear of the Principal

At Pentel Weiss, the Voice told Hope privately that singing would destroy an alien eye. The companions later sang and the prison's eye melted, confirming that specific practical weakness without proving every claim the Voice made. It also insisted another fragment of its power lay at the prison's summit. Hope did not collect that missing object, and afterward refused the Voice's demands to return at the expense of her friends.[^voice-prison]

When the [Principal](the-principal.md) came aboard Fate Six in S3E01, the hooded Voice **panicked and urged Hope to flee**. Hope stayed and fought. Its fear was portrayed in Hope's private experience, but the reason for that fear and any past relationship to the Principal remained unknown. The Principal claimed to have commissioned an earlier search for the Hand; that was his testimony, not an observed meeting with its original owner.[^voice-fear]

## The severed Hand and the Hell transfer

In S3E02, the Principal physically seized and severed the attached Hand from Hope, absorbing it into his chrome form before escaping through a portal. Hope survived severe injury, later recovered a **natural arm** through Stanley's *Regenerate* and again experienced warmth and ordinary tastes. Her restored arm was **not** the Voice's Hand; removal of the artifact did not demonstrate whether the hooded figure was freed, killed, trapped or still able to communicate. No later private message from it was established in the reviewed sessions.[^voice-severed]

In S3E12 the Principal produced the stolen Hand in Hell and passed it to Brett, whom he addressed as Asmodeus. Brett then handed it to his female demonic assistant, who put it in a pouch. Brett still demanded the **separate, missing Eye of the Voice**, which was not delivered. The Hand was therefore last directly witnessed **with Brett's assistant**, not returned to Hope or the hooded figure. Neither Vorel's Eye of Groxl nor Hope's spent Hollow Ring satisfied the demand.[^voice-transfer]

## Current status

**The hooded Voice's final identity, whereabouts and physical or metaphysical condition remain unverified.** Its last documented communication with Hope was before the S3E02 removal. The Hand was later seen in Brett's assistant's custody in S3E12; the separate Eye of the Voice remained uncollected. No reunion, restoration, confirmed destruction or completed transfer of the Voice's alleged power was witnessed.[^voice-status]
