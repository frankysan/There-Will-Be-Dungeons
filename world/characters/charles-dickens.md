# Charles Dickens — virtual illithid office rival

> **Appears E070 in a headset-mediated office experience; follows up through a computer in E071.** Name spoken **Charles Dickens** (not proof of identity with any historical writer), **ASR-based and pending listening**. **Spoilers through E071.**

In [E070](../../episodes/E070.md), Nash puts on thick wired **conference-room glasses** beneath Khaervilon, finding himself with an **illithid body/avatar** inside an intact, functioning corporate office. An illithid in **khaki/Bermuda shirt, shorts, flip-flops and glasses** says he is **Charles Dickens** and offers snacks/green coins for a brain-vending machine. He describes himself as a **long-overlooked employee** who has waited **50 years without promotion**, resentful of the commanding illithid (working name **Ovaxiad/Oubvayx**). His reason for helping Earth is chiefly **humiliation and rivalry**, not concern for human life.

Charles says the headsets project a participant's **ethereal self into the Void Realm**, while the body remains in the ancient physical CHIDS office. He claims the illithids are using an ancient human **Traversal Drive** to move their realm into Earth's location and turn souls into revenue. He gives Nash and Hope a **memory stick with falsified report data**, advising them to load it into an unattended office computer before a meeting to **set the project back by months**. The party experiences the editable illithid avatars, with Hope becoming a **cockroach, coffee mug and automobile**. Nash **uploads files** to a server through a wizard; discovery triggers physical and virtual alarms and hostile security response. **Nash and Hope remove the headset membranes safely**, so they are no longer within the apparent virtual realm at E070 cutoff.

In [E071](../../episodes/E071.md), the group's old green-on-black office computer **switches off the alarm after unseen password entry**, then displays a response identifying its sender as **Charles Dickens** and claiming **the data transfer succeeded**. It warns further messages could be compromised and powers off; **a burning disk-drive casing is ejected** nearby. The sender's asserted identity and **reported successful upload** are stronger evidence than the hoped-for months-long delay, which the four have **not observed at the physical machine**.

**Evidence:** headset and computer messages **experienced directly**, larger cosmology and machine mechanics **Charles's claims**, active effect on the Traversal Drive **unmeasured**. Do not collapse Charles into **Ovaxiad** simply because both are illithids, or describe him as a moral ally.
