# Hope Akmenos

**Also reported as:** Oriphi “Hope” Akmenos (fan-wiki form; spoken orthography unverified). **Portrayed by:** Kristen. **Identity:** Tiefling engineer, adventurer, **Queen of Tietkalla** and later bearer of the living **Hand of the Voice**. **Spoilers:** Through E118 and retrospective cast Q&As. **Evidence:** Source-linked processed ASR; individual claims not audio-verified unless specified.

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [Tietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md) · [Hand of the Voice](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md) · [World portal](../README.md).

## Character, ambitions and loyalties

Hope begins in **Dusthill** as a resourceful mechanic whose **Fate 4** vehicle becomes a central part of the company's journeys. She hopes to use powerful old technology to alter a tragic past, but **wishing for time travel is not proof she has achieved it**. She becomes bound to the **Soulbroker compact** in E023 alongside Nash and Stanley (Vorel refuses), and later learns of a supposed connection to the old tiefling royal house **Akmenos**. The elder's full genealogy is *his testimony*, while Hope's actual coronation is directly played. [E001](../../episodes/E001.md) · [E006](../../episodes/E006.md) · [E023](../../episodes/E023.md) · [Tietkalla history](../HISTORY_TIETKALLA.md).

Her body changes substantially over the campaign. After a catastrophic rebound of a **rocket in E038–E039**, Hope loses limbs and uses mechanical replacements and arm-mounted weapons. She later gains specialized **Fox and Scully firearms**, a **Broom of Flying**, appearance-changing magic and—much later—the **Hand of the Voice**. **These are different objects with different custody histories**, and the living Hand is neither **Zorander's Fist gauntlet** nor Vorel's Eye of Groxl. [E038](../../episodes/E038.md)–[E039](../../episodes/E039.md) · [E050](../../episodes/E050.md) · [Artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md).

In a **June 2019 retrospective Q&A**, Kristen discusses the idea that Hope might eventually become powerful enough to **reach godhood and confront or destroy gods**. This is a developing **player ambition**, not accomplished ascension, confirmed godslaying or automatically an in-world declaration heard by the other characters. [June Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md).

## From mechanic to queen (E001–E059)

Hope travels with **Nash, Stanley and Vorel** through Dusthill, Slavetown, the Black Mine and the contested settlements near **Tietkalla**. She resists exploitative power yet sometimes makes harsh choices of her own. The underground water-throne journey involves **Gem of Tides**, **Kefeli** and the bloody collapse of the Tietkalla power structure. **The party recovers the Gem in E050**, but the exact installation and sustained water restoration remain uncertain. In **E054**, the elder **Nobieri** arrives with actual armed tieflings; **Hope is publicly crowned queen**. She also frees **Angelashi alive**, contrary to earlier testimony that Angelashi had died. [E050](../../episodes/E050.md)–[E054](../../episodes/E054.md) · [Gem](../technology/gem-of-tides.md) · [Nobieri](nobieri.md).

After **three in-story months before E055**, she has initiated labor and sanitation reforms, rebuilt portions of city infrastructure and challenged the old serf-like social hierarchy. Yet shortages, rival noble houses and political violence remain. Hope orders **Softhead's tongue removed**, and people are executed under her authority; these actions must not be airbrushed away as a perfectly benevolent reign. A deadly **stonefall** threatens civilians, and her diplomacy with the **Principle** creates disputed trade and railroad prospects. **Actual Dusthill survivors return E059**, but that is not evidence every slave is freed or that a railway was already operational then. [E055](../../episodes/E055.md)–[E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [The Principle](the-principle.md).

## Razak Val, CHIDS and the shared sacrifice (E060–E081)

In **Razak Val**, Hope supports **Vorel's Great Fang challenge**, but her **rocket attack redirects through Vorel** and contributes to a feast catastrophe that kills named leaders and bystanders. The blast's victims and Omnom's later allegations are central to her moral story; her own claim about why she fired is not the same thing as Omnom's accusation of intent. She later offers shelter in Tietkalla to **Follow, Rorf and Sira**, but **Follow and Rorf die E066**, and Sira is abducted instead of reaching refuge. A T. rex **actually crushes and destroys Fate 4 in E066**; the improvised later rickshaw is not the repaired original vehicle. [E060](../../episodes/E060.md)–[E066](../../episodes/E066.md) · [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md) · [Omnom](omnom-cinderborn.md).

At **Kervalon/CHIDS** Hope survives mind-flayer attacks, treacherous transport, spiders and hazardous biological containment. She **kills the giant spider matriarch with shotgun shots E074**, rather than it dying permanently from Stanley's earlier sheep transformation. She gains the **portable red/green portal apparatus E075** and **actually uses it to escape attackers E076**. Later the device demonstrates a dangerous alternate **necrotic-beam** setting, distinct from its paired wall gateways. Hope also acquires **two rockets E076** and expends **both during the E079 Drive battle**. The artifact and resulting limitations are documented separately. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md)–[E079](../../episodes/E079.md) · [Portal device](../technology/portal-device.md).

**E081** concludes the CHIDS confrontation: Hope **destroys the manifested Dolo Popio**, and Sira escapes the Drive beam with Eddie alive. The original four **choose to share the dangerous crimson beam rather than destroy Earth**, vanish into light and are **GM-declared dead at E081's cutoff**. This is a played sacrifice, not proof that the planet is undamaged forever, that a soul contract is canceled, or that all four stay dead. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [CHIDS history](../HISTORY_CHIDS_DRIVE.md).

## Sigil restoration and White Tower (E082–E101)

**E082** shows Hope's **inert, apparently dead body** collected in Sigil after the sacrifice. **E088** actually restores her through a mirror rite; the original inert body remains on the slab. She is **alive afterward** and gains organic limbs; resurrection is a consequential event, not a refutation of the earlier GM death declaration or proof the Weir obligation is void. Later cast Q&A describes its contingency on the party's effort rather than unconditional immunity from death. [E082](../../episodes/E082.md) · [E088](../../episodes/E088.md) · [March 2020 bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-03.md).

She searches for an Earth portal in Sigil, crosses paths with **Tobacho Fleur** and the mysterious **Zorander's Fist** and obtains new magical means of travel. Her **Scully firearm** has several custody changes, including recovery and transfer; a pistol reported missing at one episode cutoff must not be restored to her inventory without an actual scene. At **White Tower** she is repelled by a protective ward on the first infiltration attempt, then becomes involved in the siege and a private **hooded Voice**. While a voice or charm seems to influence her, **her decisions and injuries are still events performed in play**, not proof someone else controlled every act. [E089](../../episodes/E089.md)–[E100](../../episodes/E100.md) · [White Tower](../places/white-tower.md).

**In E101**, Hope severs her right hand and **fuses the living Hand of the Voice to her body**, receiving extraordinary power at a serious spiritual/moral cost. The **separate Fist gauntlet remains behind with Nash, Stanley and Vorel**, and the Eye of Groxl is restored to Vorel by the patron's intervention. Hope **chooses to leave her original companions** through a portal. This is **both the ending of Arc Four and opening of Arc Five**, not the middle of one unchanged group adventure. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [Voice](the-voice.md) · [Hand](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md).

## Hope's separate path (E101–E116)

At the **Kaiser Casino**, Hope encounters a host calling himself **Asmodeus (“Brett”)**. In E101 he offers collaboration and access, but **she does not yet sign a formal bargain**. In E102 he introduces her new companions—**Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider**—and she **Gates back to Tietkalla** with them. The party is physically divided; its two branches must be followed independently. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [E102](../../episodes/E102.md) · [Kaiser Casino](../places/kaiser-casino.md).

Hope returns to an **inhabited Tietkalla E103**, encounters **Celibus Stump's acting chancellorship** and **sets the temporarily closed, empty railway station on fire** with her group. A blue fissure and rain are observed; the station's eventual fate and political consequences are not settled. The **hooded Voice accepts the fire E104**, but the destruction of Tietkalla soon escalates when **General Carthax's forces attack**. Hope opens a Gate addressed to Brett and **flees with her companions**, rather than being shown commanding the demonic invaders. **Omnom later accuses her of deliberately drawing the army**; he does not provide an omniscient verdict. [E103](../../episodes/E103.md) · [E104](../../episodes/E104.md) · [Tietkalla history](../HISTORY_TIETKALLA.md).

In **E106**, Hope meets the **Principle/Vladimir** at Kaiser Casino and hears claims about ancient human processing and the Lich Project, some performed via Brett's illusions. Hope **agrees to cooperate**, then is directed toward the **Eye of the Voice**. Her group returns to Sigil; **Dr. Spider kills Spatulo** during a conflict, and the refugees accompanying the earlier Gate are **not among the four who later leave**. This Eye is **not Vorel's Eye of Groxl**, nor is it presently in Hope's possession. [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [The Principle](the-principle.md).

In **E112**, Hope **signs Tobacho's written compact**, promising to help depose the **Lady of Pain** in exchange for an Eye lead and bounty terms. **No overthrow or Eye acquisition occurs**. She and her new party **actually traverse a House of Delights portal into Gray Waste**. In **E113**, they cross to Pointless, pay **Hesterbrook's River Styx ferry** (Hope relinquishes **Fox**, not Scully), and **reach Pandemonium**. The **Council of Clowns** gives Hope a **compass and a claimed prison lead near Pentelvice/Pentel Weiss (?)**. The E113 ending shows a Gate back to Tobacho's Sigil bar; **E116 replays the cutoff and has Hope instead decline that portal**. These are **different presentation/cutoff versions**, not proof she performed an unseen round trip. [E112](../../episodes/E112.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md) · [E116](../../episodes/E116.md) · [Planar route](../places/gray-waste-and-pandemonium.md).

After the altered E116 departure, the four try to **seize a Sigil cargo vessel** but withdraw without capturing it; **Blood Skunk survives** despite severe injury. From **Dr. Spider's laboratory in Hell**, Hope **casts Gate calling “Nash Maggard of the Solar Mines,” and Nash actually arrives**. This is a real reunion, not merely a promise to find him. She accompanies Nash toward Elhandar and the old group. [E116](../../episodes/E116.md) · [Nash](nash-maggard.md).

## Elhandar, the Harbor and the father's study (E117–E118)

**E117:** Hope physically reunites with **Nash, Stanley and Vorel** at **Elhandar**, encountering Angelashi and Nash's **four changeling children**. **Stanley distrusts Hope**, objects to the attached Hand, cites her responsibility for Tietkalla's deaths and **does not forgive her**. Hope discusses her actions publicly before refugees from Tietkalla and **names their new community “the Harbor”**. Nobieri conducts a fire rite, while the deeper theological meanings remain uncertain. Naming Harborers is a **real founding/declaration**, not a reconstructed city on Earth. [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) · [Harbor](../places/the-harbor.md).

In **E118**, severely scarred **Omnom** physically confronts Hope and attempts to attack her. **Stanley's Hold Person stops him**, so Hope is **not struck**. Omnom accuses her of killing kin through the redirected rocket, burning the railway station and **deliberately inviting demons**. Hope **acknowledges firing the rocket and the fire**, but says the **Hand attracted demons** and that she fled hoping they would follow her away from the city. Neither her explanation nor Omnom's accusation should be silently elevated to settled secret history. The company **plans**, but does not undertake, a joint hunt with Omnom. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Omnom](omnom-cinderborn.md).

Later Hope helps Stanley interpret his father's childhood poem and **actually enters the hidden third-floor study with him**. The ornate key, **Book of Adjacencies** and **Mask of Elton IV (?)** pass into **Stanley's** inventory, not hers. As she leaves, the hooded **Voice privately appears** and presses her toward **Pentelvice**, causing her attached Hand to hurt. **Hope does not Gate to the prison, acquire the Eye, or remove the Hand by the E118 cutoff**. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) · [Book](../technology/book-of-adjacencies.md).

## Relationships, governance and open questions

**Nash, Stanley and Vorel** are longstanding companions, but reconciliation after the E101 break remains incomplete. **Tedna** dies in E050, **Angelashi** survives, and **Nobieri** supplies real soldiers and a contested claim of royal ancestry. **Steerious, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider** are individually acting allies, not extensions of Hope's mind. **The Voice** is a supernatural interlocutor with observable influence, not a certified identity or justification for every act. [Tedna](tedna.md) · [Nobieri](nobieri.md) · [Voice](the-voice.md).

Hope's major items include her changing **mechanical/organic limbs**, vanished **Fate 4**, **Fox/Scully** guns with different transfers, **Broom of Flying**, **Necklace of Infinite Appearances**, travel rings, **the attached Hand**, and the separate quest for the unpossessed **Eye of the Voice**. Custody and expended devices must remain episode-specific. The **Gem of Tides** recovery does not establish uninterrupted civic water service, and a proposed pact cannot be mistaken for completed fulfillment. [Artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [Consistency ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).

**E119:** Hope joins **Vorel, Nash, Stanley, Omnom and Dilo** for the real Elhandar hunt. **Fitz of the Harbor actually recognizes her as queen** and shares berries; she feeds two rations' worth of nuts to the speaking squirrels. She travels with the party into the northern forest but **does not summon demons, acquire the Eye of the Voice, attack Omnom or complete reconciliation**. [E119](../../episodes/E119.md).

**E120:** Hope **pulls Chester down from a tree using its entrails**; the impact **kills the huge animal**, but she is **pinned alive beneath it until Vorel lifts the body**. She later receives misleading thanks from six illusory deer, **stabs a revealed hostile elf in the throat**, is blinded by blood, grapples another, and is briefly made to act like a squirrel by ***Mass Suggestion*** (ended by subsequent damage). **Dilo and an elf mage deliberately abduct her in a sealed floating sphere** while Stanley is preparing ***Destructive Wave***. In her separate played viewpoint she **remains conscious and tries *Teleport* via the Hand**, but **the spell fails and consumes the slot**; Dilo brings the sphere into a mountain cave. **She is not freed, killed, returned to her companions, or shown reaching Pentelvice/Eye**. [E120](../../episodes/E120.md).

**October 2020 bonus — possible futures, not events:** Kristen says she once thought Hope's **E101 Hand departure** might remove the character for a while, and was surprised by her continued story and reunion. She imagines potential branches in which Hope **embraces the Voice's agenda** or **turns against and kills her patron**. Beau discusses the struggle over a person's soul as dramatic potential. **Neither alignment choice, godslaying, patron defeat nor redemption is played in the bonus**, and E120 still ends with Hope **alive but abducted in Dilo's sphere**. [Bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-10.md).

**Latest played status:** **Alive but magically imprisoned and abducted into an Elhandar mountain cave by Dilo E120, still wearing the Hand, ordered by the Voice toward Pentelvice and not fully reconciled with Stanley or Omnom**. The Eye, political fallout in Tietkalla, exact nature of the patron, Soulbroker obligations and later consequences of her bargains remain open.
