# Hope Akmenos

**Also documented as:** Oriphi “Hope” Akmenos, according to the [fan wiki](https://twbd.fandom.com/wiki/Oriphi_%22Hope%22_Akmenos); early spoken `Orifi/Orify` is unverified. **Portrayed by:** Kristen. **Year One status:** Alive, mechanically augmented and **publicly crowned Queen of Tietkalla** in E054. **Spoilers:** E000–E054. **Source quality:** Processed ASR and later retrospective comments, not listener-verified dialogue.

## Character and history

Hope is a **tiefling engineer** who begins in **Dusthill**, where she works on an ambitious vehicle project. Her stated goal of finding a dragon engine and changing a tragic past is an **aspiration**, not proof she can travel through time. The vehicle **Fate 4** makes her work central to the party's travels. The name Akmenos gains new significance much later, when an ancient elder ties it to **Tietkalla's alleged royal bloodline**.

Across Slavetown and the Black Mine, Hope navigates dangerous bargains alongside the others. She **signs the Soulbroker pact** in E023, unlike Vorel. One earlier privately delivered revelation and other character-specific scenes are not fully preserved in the public processed transcripts; summaries should not supply the missing words from later knowledge. Her tiefling heritage, personal pact and reaction to claimed religious authority remain separate from the claims made about her by supernatural strangers.

In Trashburg Hope becomes entangled with the coming royal wedding, Monsoon's gang politics, and Tedna's attempts to help. A **rebounded rocket** badly wounds her during the attack on the Toe Rippers. In E038–E039 she undergoes **amputation of her left arm and leg**, then receives functional mechanical prosthetics, including an enhanced gripping hand. These are injuries and modifications actually played, not a cosmetic costume upgrade. The Year One Q&A also records that Kristen **chose for Hope to refuse becoming a vampire** despite liking vampire stories herself; production preference and character decision must stay distinct.

The old tiefling scholar rescued in E047–E048 mistakes Hope for a former queen, then offers an **Akmenos-lineage claim** and invites her to reclaim the city. That family narrative has an interested speaker and imperfect privately delivered source text. Hope does not immediately become queen through his assertion. She helps free **Tedna and Bok Bok** from the keep in E048; Tedna dies during the following battle in E050, after expressing love to her. **Hope kills Jolt** while suffering intense pixie-blush hallucinations; perceived supernatural images during this scene are not proven literal afterlife events.

In E051 **Kefeli petrifies Hope**, but Nash and Stanley make a one-off remedy from basilisk remains and old preserved materials: **Hope is restored alive before the episode ends**. In E054 the old elder returns with **hundreds of actual armed tieflings**, publicly **places a black crown on Hope**, and the assembled force recognizes her as queen. At her signal, **Vorel executes captive Suck Master Din**. Hope admits non-tiefling companions to a council despite prejudice among her supporters. Her future rule, prospects for rebuilding and the elder's offered remedy for a personal fertility question are **not established achievements at this cutoff**.

## Relationships and contested authority

**Nash, Stanley and Vorel** remain her companions rather than merely subjects. **Tedna** is an important loss, while **Angelashi** is a surviving ally/partner of Nash. **Nobiery/Nobieri** (name unresolved) offers ancestry and troops, and the troops **do arrive**, without validating his every historical claim. **Kefeli** also claimed royal authority, but physical petrification and death do not independently authenticate her account of ancient politics. **Monsoon** escapes; his allegation of a future **Andreeste** claim is still a warning, not a witnessed divine assault. **Gem of Tides** is recovered by the party in E050, but exact installation/water-system status is a source-review topic.

## June bonus — a still-developing divine ambition

In the [June 2019 cast Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md) (~00:16:28–00:16:45), **Kristen says her long-term concept for Hope is to become powerful enough to attain godhood and then destroy the gods**. She explicitly describes the idea as **not yet fully fleshed out**. This is a **player intention**, not a completed ascension, proof that any named gods have been killed, or an already public in-character declaration.

## After Year One — governing and contested truth (E055–E056)

The three-month break **actually occurs before E055**: Hope has **abolished Guzbucks and a serf-like category of houseless subjects**, promoted merit-based employment, issued sanitation measures and rebuilt defenses/vehicles, but **house rivalries, shortages and the ethics of state coercion remain**. At court she orders captured gang members publicly executed, and **orders envoy Softhead's tongue removed while leaving him alive**. A **poisoned assassin** later attacks her in her chambers; she survives through weapons and guards. She is still the **publicly crowned queen**, but authority alone does not secure legitimate methods. [E055](../../episodes/E055.md).

During E056's **fatal stone storm**, Hope returns to the keep and enters an **ethereal ancestral ritual**. She answers difficult questions and **refuses Quizquaz's souls-for-bargain proposal**. His claims about **Andreeste, a destroyer and a killed dragon** are uncorroborated. A Tidebearer priestess learns of hidden demonic relationships and challenges her honesty; **Nobiery orders an undead assault**, with the priestess's final fate unknown at the cutoff. [E056](../../episodes/E056.md).

## Diplomatic bargains and the lucky coin (E057–E060)

In E057 Hope **orders Nobiery to stop the attack on Emenil**, who is bitten but **saved/healed by Stanley**, rather than accepting the killing of a dissenting priestess. She obtains **Coopen/Coupon's Lucky Coin** after Diontalus kills the leprechaun at the impact site. In E058 she has the returning adventurers and **Nash's search pickup** organized and receives the **Principle** as queen. [E057](../../episodes/E057.md) · [E058](../../episodes/E058.md).

She **provisionally agrees to trade, railroad and limited embassy proposals** while seeking **paid labor and local security**, not unconditional subordination or a finished rail system. The Principle appears to extract **Diontalus into a crystal**. In E059, named **Dusthill survivors actually arrive**, including friends from Hope's earlier life; their forced labor and reunion are direct story events. She then travels with Stanley and Nash in **Fate 4** toward Razak Val, where Orp's captivity of humans and the impending scaling duel threaten another political crisis. [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [E060](../../episodes/E060.md).

## Razak Val — a queen supporting a Great Fang (E061–E063)

In **E061**, Hope hears the northern **Zark clan's** warning that the **Demon Vold** may be a portal for destructive forces linked to a name resembling **Dollo Poppio**, and **offers Tietkalla's help** whether Vorel wins the scaling or not. This is a **promise spoken at the feast**, not a played deployment or treaty. [E061](../../episodes/E061.md).

In **E062**, Hope's arm-mounted **single rocket** redirects to Vorel via the Eye of Groxl's protective/attracting effect and explodes in the crowded Cave Fengar lounge. **Many noncombatants as well as fighters die**, including **Mirion, priestess Typalla, the Elder of Families, General Toxar, Mola Janda and many others**; **Omnom survives**. Neither the intent to remove Orp nor Vorel's subsequent solo victory cancels the civilian losses. In **E063**, while Vorel tries to **subdue apparently worm-infested Zercil alive**, Hope instead uses **Scully's Obsidian Nail**, **killing/disintegrating Zercil**. The audible outside intervention raises legitimate scaling-honor grievances, especially from Omnom, who threatens her. A **charge of Coopen/Coupon's Lucky Coin** is used during the confrontation (precise remaining item state awaits handout check). [E062](../../episodes/E062.md) · [E063](../../episodes/E063.md).

Hope helps Vorel **capture a living black worm from Zercil's bedding** in E063, instead of assuming all cases of infestation require killing the host. They meet **three bereaved Fengar children**, and the party begins a plan for **medical screening and eventual release of the humans** previously stockpiled as Orp's food. She remains **Queen of Tietkalla**; Vorel's separate elevation makes the title **“Two Royals”** meaningful, without establishing the ultimate state of any mutual alliance. [E063](../../episodes/E063.md).

## Razak Val's collapse and promised refuge (E064–E065)

Hope escapes [E064](../../episodes/E064.md)'s **pit transformation and six-armed lizardfolk battles**, shoots pursuing chicken lords with **Scully's Obsidian Nails** and survives falling with the rope bridge before climbing out. The attempted liberation of **200–300 captives** is not completed; Razak Val's fate and Omnom's survival remain unknown.

In [E065](../../episodes/E065.md) she offers **Follow, his daughter Sira/Sera and orc Rorf** refuge in **Tietkalla**, conditional on arrangements and safe travel, and Follow accepts. **They have not yet reached Tietkalla.** When **Rorf's driving** and a sudden torrential purple-sands storm endanger **Fate 4**, **Hope takes over the wheel**, then camps under Stanley's *Tiny Hut*. A **private sending-stone reply** says Emenil and Melgor are working toward cooperation while **the Principle's promised men have not arrived**; it is a message, not Hope's own inspection.

In the [July-archived bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-07.md), Kristen says she **was glad to use the E062 rocket**; Beau says that catastrophic intervention changed his plans. These are **out-of-character reactions**, not an erasure of civilian deaths or in-world absolution.

## Losing Fate 4, rejecting a bargain, and the lost girl's rescue (E066–E067)

**E066:** Hope watches the **T. rex crush Fate 4** and its wreckage **explode**, a profound personal loss: the improvised **Fang Fate Five** bone-and-metal rickshaw is no substitute for the actual RV. The tentacled intruder **Oubvayx/Ovaxiad** tells her that **Andreeste wants Tietkalla's souls** and that allowing the demons to take her people could free her from the **Catoblepraithe deal** via killing the Principle. **Hope unequivocally rejects the bargain**—neither the promised discharge nor enemy ownership of Tietkalla becomes a consummated fact. Hope fires **Scully's Puckle rounds** at the apparent apparition, without harming it. The entity **kidnaps Sira**, **kills Rorf**, and leaves **Follow dying**; Hope helps bury Follow. **The sanctuary offered in E065 is never realized for the two men.** [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

**E067:** Hope experiences a **private or subjective Dusthill memory**: Softhead introduces her to mechanic **Jimster**, and she first sees pre-modification **Fate 4** in his garage, with large tires still only an idea. This is **a memorylike sequence during travel**, not proof that the destroyed vehicle is restored or that Hope has physically revisited Dusthill. Later she hears a **spectral dragon** allege a pre-Scorching planet-moving machine beneath Kervalon and Dollo Poppio's designs on Sira. Hope joins Nash in excavating a **covert underground tunnel**, and uses **Scully's radiant ammunition** against skeletons and the flesh-spawner; she subsequently **spots a lizardfolk with a rifle holding an ambush position** farther inside the keep. The mission to rescue Sira and stop the machine remains unfinished. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md).

## Breaking domination and the biting elevator panel (E068 / August bonus)

**E068:** Hope watches the elaborate **Mold Earth / illusion trap** and the real **Polymorph of Vorel into a T. rex**. When the illithid casts **Dominate Monster**, Hope's **two shotgun-arm hits cause a concentration failure**, releasing Vorel; this is **crucial support**, not a mind-flayer kill. A subsequent **Mind Blast stuns Hope for many turns**, and she recovers after the exposed defenders are slain. After a **short rest in Stanley's Tiny Hut**, she approaches the **powerful magical shaft**: a thrown stone vanishes; **her own leg briefly spirals as it nears the energy**, then **returns to normal** as she withdraws. Hope observes worms moving around the stairwell and tracks their trail back to a **hidden alcove and pair of steel elevator-like doors** revealed by Nash. When she presses toward the control buttons with her **flesh hand**, the panel **becomes a biting mouth**; **her Dexterity save succeeds (reported 23)** and **she retains her fingers**. **The doors remain closed**, Sira missing, and the machine's existence/operation unconfirmed. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md).

In the [August bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-08.md), Kristen discusses learning Hope's **Australian-style accent** from Mad Max and old interviews and jokingly describes Hope's desired mechanical/tiefling balance as **“mostly teeth.”** These are **out-of-character performance/hypothetical comments**, not evidence of in-world body surgery in this episode.

## Old-world transport, office-avatar infiltration and the data center (E069–E071)

**E069:** Hope's encounter with the biting **Door Authority panel** leads to a negotiated **Acid Splash by Nash** which finally opens a pair of steel doors. The group traverses a **funicular** where magical flowers **put Hope and Nash to sleep**; **Stanley's Feather Fall** prevents disaster in its crash, and **Vorel wakes Hope**. Hope helps recover **staff identity cards**, takes **four whiskey bottles** from an abandoned bar, and uses a **Tara Mebat card** to activate an oxygen mask, **accidentally poisoning Nash**. At the security barrier Hope's cards fail and **turrets threaten her**; **Vorel bends open the exit**. She drives an ancient railcar into the executive offices, where a tentacle-like object appears behind her—but its **E070 resolution is a plush doll**, not a live enemy. [E069](../../episodes/E069.md).

**E070:** The tentacled object is a **squeaking plush mind flayer**, which **Hope keeps in her bag**. Small wall-passing psychic sprites avoid her and Stanley, but this does **not prove specific guilt or allegiance**. At a console inviting a “game,” Hope follows Nash into wired headsets, taking an **illithid avatar** in the so-called **Void Realm**, according to **Charles Dickens**. She becomes **a cockroach for scouting**, then **a coffee mug for surveillance**, then **a car to distract suspicious employees**, allowing Nash to upload falsified reports. When the virtual security breach triggers physical alarms, **both escape their clinging headset membranes**. During the real hellhound fight Hope uses **Scully radiant ammunition**; her **shotgun-arm mechanism jams** following a critical miss. **The episode ends before the fight resolves**. [E070](../../episodes/E070.md).

**E071:** Hope fires remaining **radiant darts**, and uses **Action Surge to unjam the arm shotgun**, later replenishing radiant charges. She meets **Alvert**, who identifies as a rival of the demons but makes unverified claims about the Principle's genealogy and gene farms. In the abandoned facility, Hope helps with **rail-car/snail decoy controls** and explores the frozen green-line passage, **slipping on ice and taking minor damage**. At **Archives**, another sentient door opens with an executive credential and persuasion, not actual acid. She discovers **a high air duct with thinner ice** above the inaccessible data-center door, providing the eventual entry. With **floating underwear briefly borrowed from Nash/Stanley**, she joins the others in the freezing duct, then passes the item along. The **world-transfer machine and Sira have not been found**, and Alvert remains outside. [E071](../../episodes/E071.md).

## The old elf, a Tietkalla message, and an escape from frozen bodies (E072–E073 / September bonus)

**E072:** The brittle duct into CHIDS's server farm breaks below Hope as Stanley melts blockage with *Heat Metal*. She **grabs a piece of the sheet metal** and descends safely after Stanley **casts Feather Fall**. The group meets **Cerinthala**, an old withered elf apparently living among four preserved humans. During the **long rest inside Tiny Hut**, Hope receives a **distressed message on the sending stone from Tietkalla**; its wording is **very garbled in ASR**, so its sender, alleged crisis and exact political status must **not be canonized from this draft**. Hope **replies with news of the party's mission**. She shares **Malta/Malterweed soup** with Cerinthala, finding it good despite seeing **metal nuts/chain** thrown into the cauldron. The elf claims tieflings were bred by demon-human experiments and expresses concern about **Vorel's Eye of Groxl**; both claims remain **her testimony**. After morning rest, **ice constructs attack and seize Stanley**; Hope runs after his captor, trying unsuccessfully to divert it. [E072](../../episodes/E072.md).

**E073:** Hope loads **Scully's explosive ammunition** and **shoots the ice creature's arm off**, letting Stanley fall to safety. She helps him escape as it retreats. Her shotgun later **destroys a blue-eyed animated frozen corpse** grabbing at Stanley's ankles. Cerinthala's **Toby** responds from within the ice; she confesses to an original scheme to eat/use the group, and reveals that Stanley's amulet is a **curse**. Hope opposes the elf's prejudices about lizardfolk and hears her **infernal-wave reproductive experiment** story; no tiefling or lizardfolk origin is independently verified. She helps the group through the old laboratories and climbs onto a **stalled elevator's roof**, where **she spots a huge spider-like form descending**. [E073](../../episodes/E073.md).

**September bonus:** Kristen explains that a **future replacement for destroyed Fate 4 would be called Fate 5**, perhaps involving a motorcycle, but there is **no actual Fate 5 vehicle in E073**. The joking **Kismet** side-bike is likewise hypothetical. Vorel's abandoned **Fang Fate Five** salvage rickshaw in E066 is a different historical item, not the same proposed replacement. Kristen jokes about **Hope becoming a god** when discussing wishes, but **does not establish an event, prophecy or final destiny**. She reports that changing Hope's current class would mean designing a different character, not a present sheet edit. [Bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-09.md).

## Spider venom, a headworm and the paired escape device (E074–E075)

**E074:** Hope climbs the stalled lift roof and sees a **huge brain-backed spider**; smaller spiders poison her while **Vorel's Eye flames** burn swarms. Repeated bites also impose **one level of exhaustion**, distinct from being *Poisoned*. She survives the lift drop with **Stanley's Feather Fall** and eventually **kills the large spider queen with two shotgun shots** after its temporary sheep transformation. **Vorel crafts a small spider-carapace shield fitted to Hope's additional tiny artificial hand**, which she accepts (no evidence of a large standard magical shield). She witnesses **Cerinthala crushed to death in living membrane** and accompanies the party farther to a control room. There she uses Vorel's launch and her **mechanical leg plus Action Surge** to jump across a platform, **grab a fleeing illithid**, and hold him in a **metal-arm choke**. The illithid remains caught as E074 ends. [E074](../../episodes/E074.md).

**E075:** Hope continues to crush the illithid until **black-dragon Nash tears away the body, killing it**, leaving the head with Hope. **She probes its brain and finds a black worm**, which she **crushes**, and with Vorel prepares **two energy-rifle lizardfolk guard heads**, likewise destroying **one black worm in each**. She recovers an **alphanumeric password-like code** but it **fails to unlock the control console**; the illithid's concealed disk is still **missing**. The group then opens a secure vault, with **Hope operating the main terminal**. She holds **a translucent blue gem recovered from Cerinthala's clothes** in her small hand; **Cerinthala revives and appears to summon ice Toby via the gem**, then **casts Hold Person**, paralyzing Hope until she later succeeds on a save. **Toby's ice and exposed reanimated forms are destroyed**, and Cerinthala escapes. **Vorel's new red/green device proves to be a working paired-portal tool**, not a conventional firearm. He **hands the single device to Hope**, who is holding it at E075 cutoff as **the team plans, but has not yet executed, an escape** while Vorel lures roughly fifty charging bugbears. [E075](../../episodes/E075.md) · [Portal device](../technology/portal-device.md).

**Status:** Hope's **E074 spider venom subsides after the fight**, but the **separate exhaustion level** and its later reduction are not as clearly narrated. Her later **Hold Person ends**; she is not left paralyzed. **Fate 5 remains unbuilt**, notwithstanding the September bonus's future concepts.
