# Diontalus — self-described archfey of carnage

**Identity:** A supernatural agent who **forcibly joins Stanley Billings in E029**, exercises independent control of the shared body, and is **physically extracted into a crystal by the Principle in E059**. **Spelling:** *Diontalus* is the owner-accepted canonical display ([D-026](../../project/DECISIONS.md)); `Dion Talus`, `Deontalus`, `Diantalus` and `Diontalis` are ASR variants, not separate entities. **Spoilers:** Through E121 and retrospective bonuses. **Evidence:** Episode dossiers based on processed ASR, not audio verification.

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [Antagonists](../ANTAGONISTS.md) · [Stanley Billings](stanley-billings.md) · [World portal](../README.md).

## Origin and intrusion — E029–E034

In the **oil sands** between the Golden Land and Tietkalla, Stanley explores while other party members are entranced. He encounters a luminous being identifying himself as **Diontalus**; **Stanley refuses the proposed arrangement**, yet the entity **enters his body by force in E029**. Later scenes, including **E034**, establish a distinct speaking/acting personality capable of taking control. This is **not** Stanley willingly signing a new pact, an alternate name for Stanley, or evidence that the Soulbroker personally caused the intrusion. [E028](../../episodes/E028.md) · [E029](../../episodes/E029.md) · [E034](../../episodes/E034.md).

Diontalus calls himself an **archfey of carnage**. His powers and control are witnessed, but his rank, remote history and implied cosmic authority are **self-description**, not independently established facts. During possession, whether Stanley can perceive or prevent a given action is scene-dependent, and the host's guilt or reaction must be recorded separately from **Diontalus's agency**. [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) · [World](../WORLD.md).

## Conduct and casualties — E045–E057

In **E045**, Diontalus **deliberately kills the guard George Miller** while controlling the body, over Stanley's objections. Stanley later interprets the killing as a way to punish him, but that motive is Stanley's interpretation; the killing itself is observed. In the Tietkalla warehouse catastrophe, Diontalus **kills P0D in E050** even as Nash attempts to save her, and also **kills Dashington**. Those murders deepen the conflict with Nash and Vorel and create serious moral consequences for Stanley, even though Stanley is **not the acting agent** in those moments. [E045](../../episodes/E045.md) · [E049](../../episodes/E049.md) · [E050](../../episodes/E050.md) · [P0D](p0d.md).

Diontalus remains **in Stanley's body at the E054 coronation cutoff**, rather than having been cured by the hidden library, Buttons or the froghemoth's division. During the ensuing **three-month interval**, he is largely quiet. In **E057** he acts again: taking control, **killing the leprechaun Coopen/Coupon (?)** and consuming magical essence. The silence was never proof he had gone. [E054](../../episodes/E054.md) · [E055](../../episodes/E055.md) · [E057](../../episodes/E057.md).

## Extraction — E059

When the **Principle** visits Hope's court in **E059**, he **visibly pulls Diontalus out of Stanley and seals him within a glowing crystal**. Stanley survives and subsequently behaves without the intrusive controlling voice. This is a **played separation**, not a witnessed annihilation or permanent metaphysical death. The Principle claims Diontalus caused the destruction of an ancient metropolis and describes plans to keep and study the crystal for old knowledge. **The destruction story and ultimate custody are interested testimony and open questions**, distinct from the directly observed extraction. [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [The Principle](the-principle.md).

An idea of **sending Diontalus into space** circulates, but later cast discussion clarifies that **Vorel proposed** this and that **the Principle instead discussed imprisonment/examination**. **At the E118 cutoff**, no space launch, release, reunion with Stanley, crystal escape or re-possession had been established; **E121 subsequently reveals the entity's active return, but not the mechanics of that escape**. The principal protagonists' **E081 sacrifice** does not automatically provide a new fate for the already separated Diontalus. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [January 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-01.md).

## Buttons, the Arcanum and relationships

The intelligent cat **Buttons** shows particular concern for Stanley during their time together. In the later **March 2019 cast Q&A**, Beau reveals his intended, previously undisclosed motive: **Buttons loved Stanley** and wanted to take him to the **Arcanum** to remove Diontalus. This is retrospective creator information—not something Stanley necessarily understood during the E049 ambush, nor evidence that such a rescue actually occurred. **Vorel kills Buttons E049**. The Principle later invokes Buttons' report in support of the E059 extraction; the Principle's testimony and Beau's retrospective comment remain distinct evidence. [Buttons](buttons.md) · [E049](../../episodes/E049.md) · [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [March 2019 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md).

**Stanley** wants to remove Diontalus, experiences guilt for the harm done through his body and studies medicine partly toward eventual separation. **Nash** mourns P0D and opposes the entity's cruelty. **Vorel** treats the shared body with suspicion yet his opposition to Diontalus does not prove Stanley approved every murder. The **Soulbroker compact** was signed by **Stanley** (plus Hope and Nash) in E023, **not** by Diontalus as a second consenting signer. [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) · [Soulbroker](soulbroker.md) · [Vorel](vorel.md) · [Nash](nash-maggard.md).

## Creator commentary and disputed fate

In the **February 2020 Q&A**, Jon says he deliberately left Diontalus silent while Stanley attempted to improve Tietkalla, imagining the entity expected reform to fail; a later hypothetical “told you so” is **player characterization, not new acted dialogue**. In the **March 2020 Q&A**, the cast jokes about Diontalus perhaps surviving, but **two ASR exports disagree on a possible container word** (“cat” versus “capsule”); that exchange does **not** establish a new body, location or custody for the crystal. [February 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-02.md) · [March 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-03.md).

**E115–E119 distinction:** In **E115**, the Principle **privately tells Stanley an unnamed old friend survived and might visit**. In **E119**, Stanley recounts this to Vorel, **identifying Diontalus as his own suspected referent** and imagining that the newly eager pupil **Dilo might be a host**. Stanley explicitly admits the Principle may have bluffed. **Neither the private warning nor Stanley's inference establishes a witnessed crystal escape, re-possession, body, location or return**. Dilo accompanies the hunt without demonstrable Diontalus agency. [E115](../../episodes/E115.md) · [E119](../../episodes/E119.md) · [Dilo](dilenoria.md).

**E120 boundary:** **Dilo really betrays the party and kidnaps Hope**, vindicating Stanley's practical mistrust **without proving the specific supernatural explanation**. No one detects Diontalus, identifies his voice, sees the E059 crystal open, or witnesses him enter Dilo. [E120](../../episodes/E120.md) · [Dilo](dilenoria.md).

**E121 — the return is witnessed, the allegiance remains disputed.** In the Elhandar mushroom cave, the Dilo figure **explicitly recognizes Stanley and is identified as Diontalus**, offers to let Hope survive if Stanley surrenders his body, and claims **the Principle helped him**. **When Stanley strikes the young elf with a giant-ape fist, Dilo's body is knocked aside and a distinct blue spectral Diontalus visibly emerges**. Diontalus manifests a necrotic **Grim Reaper scythe**, suppresses/strains the party and **kills Omnom by chain lightning**. Vorel's Eye identifies **radiant vulnerability**; a large formerly captive Earth-desert black dragon intervenes and badly injures Diontalus, who **actually escapes alive through rock**. The statement that the Principle assisted his reconstruction, the promise to bury Hope's Hand in the Feywild and his plan to return there are **Diontalus's claims**, not independent evidence of E059 crystal-release mechanics or of the Principle's true intentions. **Dilo's apparently dead body is separate from the escaped ghost**. [E121](../../episodes/E121.md) · [Dilo](dilenoria.md).

**Latest established state:** **Directly manifested separately from Dilo in E121, wounded and escaped alive through cave rock; prior E059 extraction and how it was undone remain unresolved**. Questions remain about his true nature, claimed ancient massacre, durability and location of the crystal, and whether he can exert influence from confinement. Avoid turning an uncertain or joking Q&A reply into a confirmed return. ## E123 — persistent harm and fears without a new encounter

After a **completed long rest**, Stanley reports **Strength 7/Dexterity 17**, and Vorel's strength loss also remains. **No restoration is performed** before the lake cutoff. The party discusses radiant weapons, possible sunlight effects, multiple hosts and risks to family/refugees; those are **theories and precautions**, not new demonstrated powers or a new attack. The GM dashboard recaps **Diontalus's own claim of trading Sylvan secrets for the Principle's assistance**, not a witnessed crystal release. He does not reappear; **Emenil is not revealed as a host**, and a corrected GM name slip is not evidence of possession. [E123](../../episodes/E123.md).
