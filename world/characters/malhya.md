# Malhya / Malhaya — the Fishbird

## Introduction

Malhya, also called the **Fishbird** or **Shepherd of the Tides**, appeared to the Wastes companions as a whale-sized, rainbow-scaled fish with birdlike wings. Its followers spoke of a savior of the waters, and [Emenil](emenil.md) sought its aid when the party needed relief from the injuries inflicted by [Diontalus](diontalus.md). In the deep-water encounter beneath the Harbor lake, the companions witnessed extraordinary transformations and an actual restoration of their lost strength. The visitor's claimed godhood and account of creation's history, however, remained its own explanations.[^malhya-appearance]

## Faith and the lake rite

Emenil preserved a small supply of fish flakes said to have come from her late husband, a reverend who had died after persecution by T'ietkalla's vampire rulers. She described this as an emergency means of asking the Fishbird for help, though she had never performed the rite herself. Her account of her husband's life and death was testimony rather than a newly witnessed history. Her earlier destruction of a shrine did not by itself prove that the Fishbird had died or that Emenil permanently abandoned her faith.[^malhya-rite]

Emenil and the four companions swallowed the flakes, discovered that they could breathe beneath the Harbor lake and experienced a shared transition into what appeared to be endless water. A vast kraken approached and withdrew without battle. Then a winged, rainbow-scaled fish appeared, answered to Malhya and created a platform of **solid water, not ice**, beneath them. The shared encounter was played; its precise plane or cosmic status was not independently established.[^malhya-encounter]

## Demonstrated encounter outcomes

When Stanley challenged the visitor, Malhya made him disappear; Nash demanded his return and also vanished. Both reported isolation in darkness without a sense of elapsed time. After [Vorel](vorel.md) appealed for them and Emenil's faith was invoked, Malhya returned both men. **Neither disappearance was adjudicated as a death, nor their return as resurrection.**[^malhya-disappearance]

Malhya then took a blue, fish-scaled likeness of Vorel, though it could not reproduce his chest **Eye of Groxl**. With his permission it touched the Eye, recognizing a feared power and warning of danger. The words about “the one he slew” and “the unmaker” have an unresolved referent; they cannot safely establish a particular divine victim, a new title or a complete cosmic history. The visitor next drew blue strands from Vorel and then Stanley. The GM **explicitly confirmed that their Diontalus-inflicted ability debuffs were cured**. Malhya departed, and the party returned to the Harbor lake, where Emenil remained alive and responsive underwater.[^malhya-cure]

## Claims and unfinished engineering

Malhya said that ancient claims over creation, dragon blight and the Earth's heat prevented the waters from returning. It connected the hoped-for recovery to a dying dragon's device of impossible geometry and an unnamed engineer supposedly hidden from the gods, last known near a place rendered in the processed accounts as “faerun” or “Favron”. It directed Stanley toward the Elhandari leader and royal court. These were **directions and theological claims**, not proof that an engineer had been located, a key or blueprint delivered, the proposed device built or Earth's climate restored.[^malhya-claims]

## Current status

**Malhya's last directly witnessed action in E124 was to depart after curing Vorel and Stanley, leaving the companions back beneath the Harbor lake.** Its continued existence was not in doubt during that encounter, but neither its supposed divine rank nor its wider cosmology was independently verified. No completed restoration of Earth's waters followed from the meeting, and the precise spelling of its name remains provisional.[^malhya-status]
