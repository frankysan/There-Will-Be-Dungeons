# Tobacho Fleur (?) — Sigil's dragon power broker

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [Antagonists](../ANTAGONISTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Name:** `Tobacho Fleur` is a working ASR-supported form; confirm from source audio or cast writing. **Role:** Dragon controlling a fortified site and network in **Sigil's Hive**, proposing dangerous exchanges. **Spoilers:** E089–E125. **Evidence:** Provisional ASR.

The party's search for a stable Earth portal leads them toward a figure called Tobacho. The [Portal Regulatory Agency](../../episodes/E089.md) says one recorded portal is under his stewardship, but **no accessible portal or exact key is delivered then**. In E090–E092 the companions reach a guarded Hive compound and undergo trials; claims by staff about portal arrangements are not themselves a crossing to Earth. [Sigil](../places/sigil.md).

**E106:** Stanley opens the sealed **Zorander's Fist** case and **physically hands Tobacho the gauntlet**, but **its living Hand is attached to Hope elsewhere**. Tobacho reacts to its absence, advertises a substantial bounty on Hope and points the companions toward a proposed indirect way home. **E112:** Hope independently **signs a written bargain with Tobacho** promising future help against the **Lady of Pain** and support for his Sigil rule in exchange for a lead toward the **Eye of the Voice** and withdrawal of his bounty. **Neither the Lady's deposition, full cancellation nor Eye acquisition is played**. He directs the group toward Pandemonium's Council of Clowns. [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [E112](../../episodes/E112.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md).

**E125 — reported new Sigil fate, not observed:** During the **Elhandar fleet invasion**, mercenary commander **Henitar/Henatar Grape (?)** claims **Mind Master Gerbken Shartlinger** learned of Tobacho's planned move against the **Lady of Pain**, told her, and she cast Tobacho into a **maze/labyrinth**. This is **an interested enemy's account**, not an on-screen capture, death, independently confirmed status change or proof the existing E112 bargain is formally void. At this cutoff Tobacho is **alleged imprisoned, fate unverified**. [E125](../../episodes/E125.md) · [comparison](../../reports/E125_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md).

**Relationships:** [Hope](hope-akmenos.md), [Hand of the Voice](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md), [factions](../FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md). **Open:** real portal control, reliability of the bargain, later custody of Fist and his ambitions' outcome. [Ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
