# Tobacho Fleur — dragon power broker of Sigil

## Introduction

Tobacho Fleur was a dragon with a fortified compound and a network of followers in **Sigil's Hive**. Travelers searching for a way back to Earth were directed toward a portal said to be under his stewardship. The claim did not immediately put a usable passage or its key in their hands. His guarded domain drew visitors into trials and negotiations in which information was as valuable as treasure.[^tobacho-approach]

## The Fist and the bargains

Tobacho's power rested partly on the things others sought from him. When Stanley opened the sealed case for **Zorander's Fist**, one of Tobacho's stone golems carried the gauntlet to its master. Tobacho inspected and retained it, but the separate living **Hand of the Voice** was still attached to [Hope](hope-akmenos.md) elsewhere. Angry at its absence, he offered a bounty for Hope and suggested an indirect route back to Earth.[^tobacho-fist]

Hope later entered into a written bargain with Tobacho: she promised aid against the **Lady of Pain** and support for his ambition to rule Sigil, while he promised a lead toward the **Eye of the Voice** and withdrawal of his bounty. He directed her group toward the **Council of Clowns** in Pandemonium. The agreement demonstrated his ambition and willingness to trade favors; it did not establish the Lady's downfall, the Eye's recovery or a fulfilled homeward passage.[^tobacho-bargain]

## Current status

During a later assault on Elhandar, the mercenary commander Henitar Grape claimed that a Mind Master had exposed Tobacho's designs to the Lady of Pain, who then trapped the dragon in a maze. **This was an adversary's account, not a witnessed imprisonment or death.** Tobacho's actual condition, the reliability of the report, continued custody of the Fist and the legal fate of Hope's bargain remain unresolved.[^tobacho-rumor]
