# Dilenoria / Dylanoria (?) — “Dilo”

## Introduction

Dilo was a young Elhandari elf who approached [Stanley Billings](stanley-billings.md) in hope of learning from a hero. He studied unarmed combat but wanted something more than fighting forms: judgment, tactics and the courage to face dangerous opponents. His brief place among Stanley's companions would end in the abduction of Hope and the revelation that [Diontalus](diontalus.md) could act through Dilo's body. Whether Dilo himself chose that course remained unknown.[^dilo-intro]

## From proposed pupil to hunt participant

At the Elhandar lakeside cottage, Dilo recalled watching Stanley defend the rescue ship. Stanley invited him to return the next morning but did not formally accept a permanent apprentice. Dilo joined the subsequent forest hunt with [Vorel](vorel.md), [Omnom](omnom-cinderborn.md) and the companions. He sought lessons in strategy rather than technique, received an improvised deer-leg club from Vorel and learned Stanley's joking “lemon tree” intervention phrase. He helped with camp life, identified their quarry as Chester the alleged bearsaurus and showed more curiosity than fighting prowess. His account of a laboratory-made creature that had killed earlier hunters was testimony, not established Elhandari history.[^dilo-early]

## E120 — the pupil is a genuine betrayer

During the hunt, Stanley transformed into a Tyrannosaurus rex and the companions killed Chester. Six seemingly grateful talking deer then approached. Stanley exposed one as an elf under a magical disguise, and all six attacked. Dilo first appeared to aid Hope with unarmed strikes, but as Stanley began *Destructive Wave*, the young elf gave a prearranged signal. One of the attackers sealed Hope in a floating magical sphere and threw Dilo its control wand. Dilo caught the device and escaped with his captive, running far faster than he previously had. Hope's attempted *Teleport* failed inside the sphere.[^dilo-betrayal]

His collusion and the abduction were witnessed. They did **not**, at that moment, identify the person directing him or establish whether he acted freely. Stanley had earlier feared that Dilo might be connected to the dangerous figure mentioned in the [Principal's](the-principal.md) warning; that fear was not yet proof of possession.[^dilo-suspicion]

## E121 — the true agent revealed

The pursuit led to a mountain cave. There the figure using Dilo's body spoke of Stanley's birth name and acknowledged the identity **Diontalus**. During the ensuing duel Stanley, transformed into a giant ape, struck Dilo's physical form. The elf fell, and a separate blue spectral Diontalus emerged. The GM later described Dilo's body as motionless and not breathing, while Diontalus escaped from the larger battle through rock. Dilo and Diontalus were thus **physically distinct**, but the duration of any control, Dilo's consent and the possibility of reviving his body were not settled.[^dilo-cave]

## Disputed danger and the truth boundary

The Principal's warning, Stanley's private suspicions and Dilo's extraordinary escape speed offer context for the betrayal, not an independent account of its origin. Nor does Dilo's description of Chester prove an ancient laboratory experiment or its reported death toll. What the companions witnessed was a promising would-be pupil, an actual collaborator in Hope's capture, and finally a body from which the hostile spectral figure separated.[^dilo-boundaries]

## Current status

**Dilo's last observed physical state was motionless and not breathing in the Elhandar cave in E121.** Diontalus survived as a separate spectral entity. Dilo's precise name spelling, independent agency before the separation and any later revival remain unresolved; no subsequent living return is established by these sources.[^dilo-status]
