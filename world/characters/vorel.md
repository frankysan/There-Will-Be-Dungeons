# Vorel

**Longer written name:** **Vorel Rasvim Kurik** in the fan wiki, not independently confirmed as a full name from Year One spoken dialogue. **Portrayed by:** Kyle. **Identity:** Lizardfolk warrior. **Year One status:** Alive; protector of Bok Bok; bearing the sentient **Eye of Groxl**. **Spoilers:** through E067; retrospective bonus remarks kept separate from played events.

## Character and development

Vorel arrives in Dusthill a powerful desert-wandering lizardfolk trying to trade for water and food. As the campaign unfolds he becomes a warrior increasingly willing to declare himself a **Lizard King**, but his leadership is not a uniformly recognized crown over every lizardfolk community. During the road adventures he brings along **Bok Bok**, a goblin whose persistent devotion starts as a surprise and becomes one of Vorel's closest bonds.

He fights through the slavery and terror of Slavetown and the Black Mine, including a lizardfolk religious/visionary ordeal tied to the name **Fuxi**. At the **Soulbroker compact**, Vorel is the one regular protagonist who **refuses to sign** the soul contract accepted by Nash, Hope and Stanley. His independent judgments are not always gentle: he kills enemies, coerces prisoners, contributes to hard choices and is often suspicious of the people who promise power. A later confrontation with **Diptak** ends with a pledge from the **Golden Land clan**, although no army is thereby on call at every subsequent scene.

As the company reaches Tietkalla, Vorel acquires the distinctive chain greatsword **Mawstorm** (provisional owner-suggested spelling; ASR often has `Ma Storm`) and fights the Toe Rippers and city defenders. His decisions frequently escalate danger, yet he is also among the companions most outraged by **Diontalus's** killing of P0D. He **kills Buttons** in E049, and later proposes a memorial code of conduct called the **Pillars of Decency** (POD). It is a **proposed party standard**, not a magical contract or a binding constitution.

**Kefeli petrifies Vorel** during E051. Nash and Stanley protect him and make a limited remedy from an earlier basilisk corpse, restoring **both Vorel and Hope alive**. In the giant froghemoth E052–E053, he mentally contacts the creature's brain but does not demonstrate lasting mastery over it. He then accepts the living **Eye of Groxl** into his chest. Its will, combat rage, shield and protection effects come with risks of displeasure and influence. The eye is a **single owner-confirmed artifact**, even though ASR transcribes `Grojil` and `Groxel`; the E067 `god killer Grojil` speech is **owner-confirmed as a reference to this same Eye of Groxl**, not to a separate deity or person; its alleged god-killing deeds remain unverified. Vorel's newly controllable **scale color-changing** arises separately from pixie/froghemoth effects, not the eye.

At E054 he attacks the fleeing **Monsoon** but cannot kill him, then **executes captive Suck Master Din at Hope's signal** after Hope's public coronation. Vorel's future ideas about raising Bok Bok as a squire are plans rather than completed training.

## Relationships and items

**Bok Bok** accompanies and protects him, including watching over his petrified form. **Hope** becomes a queen he has helped enthrone, not simply another opponent. **Stanley/Diontalus** remains a fraught relationship because of P0D and George; later cast discussion describes Vorel informally checking who has bodily control. Key equipment includes **Mawstorm** (spelling pending creator-written or audio confirmation), the **+1 AC lizardfolk-skull helmet** obtained in E047, and the later **Eye of Groxl**; **the helmet and eye are separate items**. The hidden desert cache Vorel retrieves in E053 is not equivalent to all its gold being distributed to the town.

## After Year One — Golden Sands and spectral consequences (E055–E056)

By **E055**, Vorel has opened the **Golden Sands inn and adventurers' guild**, with Bok Bok as kitchen apprentice. Its three rules interpret the **Pillars of Decency** as **honest talk, fair deals, kinship**; a “liars' table” allows card-game bluffs. He defends non-tiefling customers in a fistfight against prejudiced patrons. In E056 he helps shelter civilians from falling stones using his **Eye of Groxl's flaming shield** and directs people into sewers. **The ghost of George Miller supernaturally ages him**, but the priestess **Emenil restores his youth with Greater Restoration within E056**. The sentient **Eye of Groxl is displeased but loses a control contest**; it does not possess him in that moment. A comment using `Grojil` in ASR **does not create a different item**. ## Great Fang — succession and first decisions (E061–E063)

The initial sunrise scaling becomes a **three-way contest** when **Zercil/Zershil Fengar** joins following **Bramie/Baymie's murder in E061**. Orp assigns Vorel the temporary role of **sheriff** to investigate. Vorel finds three similar human footprints in the heirloom room but **does not establish the murderer**; he helps disentangle the separate theft of a preserved Fengar egg by two apparent Nash doubles. In E062 Zercil proposes that Vorel help defeat Orp and become **her husband/right hand**, relinquishing Great Fang to her; **Vorel refuses**. [E061](../../episodes/E061.md) · [E062](../../episodes/E062.md).

The captive-feeding system drives Vorel to **challenge Orp immediately**, not at dawn. During a disastrous battle **Hope's rocket is redirected toward Vorel by his Eye of Groxl**, devastates the lounge and launches Vorel and Orp into a witnessed duel outside. **Vorel fatally drives his returning flaming shield into Orp's eye**, survives with only **one hit point**, and is **acclaimed Great Fang** by the crowd and Omnom Cinderborn. **This is the Eye's shield, not the separate Mawstorm greatsword.** The rocket kills many lizardfolk, including **Mirion, Typalla, Elder of Families, Toxar and Mola Janda**; this mass loss is not an inconsequential coronation ceremony. [E062](../../episodes/E062.md).

In E063 Zercil tries to claim the title, but the crowd favors Vorel; she attacks. Vorel **sees wormlike movements behind her eyes** and attempts a **nonlethal defeat**, but **Hope shoots and disintegrates her** with an Obsidian Nail. The visible intervention complicates the honor of the victory; the crowd nonetheless reaffirms Vorel. He **begins dismantling Orp's human-feeding system**, says captives should be free and orders examinations for parasitic influence. He and Hope **capture a live black worm from Zercil's bed**. An announcement of freedom and examinations of the **200–300 people in the man's pit** are **not proof every captive has already been released**. The murderer was **not yet known to Vorel in E063**; his accusation against Zercil was **unsupported**. **Rorf later admits killing Bramie in E065**, which does not retroactively become Vorel's E063 knowledge. ## Fleeing a broken kingdom (E064–E065)

In **E064** the **Sun's Witnesses** in the man's pit mutate into monstrous **chicken lords**; Vorel calls out **Dollo Poppio** to provoke one prisoner, then burns off the **spray of worms** that tries to infest himself and Omnom. Aboveground **six-armed lizardfolk fight other citizens**; Vorel must leave Razak Val despite winning the title of **Great Fang**. He tells Omnom to survive and return to the Zarks at **Kervalon/Kaer Valon** for answers. **Omnom stays behind to fight** and takes the **Necklace of Fireballs** recovered from Orp; his fate is **not observed**. Vorel names **a new velociraptor “Mawstorm,” separately from the weapon**, but the party later releases the mounts. Vorel promises **to return** as Great Fang; actual liberation/reconstruction has **not happened by E065**. [E064](../../episodes/E064.md).

In **E065**, Vorel hears **Rorf, an orc shapeshifter, admit Bramie's murder** while Follow recounts how their group engineered scaling opportunities to escape the pit. This corrects Vorel's earlier suspicion of Zercil, **not an event that clears Nash of helping steal the separate egg**. During the storm in purple sands, Vorel enjoys unexpected rain and then lies **camouflaged beneath Fate 4** while a huge **T. rex sniffs the vehicle**. The encounter remains a cliffhanger. Kyle says in the [July-archived bonus Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-07.md) (~00:13–00:20) that he **does not know** if Vorel will rebuild Razak Val; he considered **paladin multiclassing**, but **has not taken the class**, and he rejects **Tyrant's Bane** as a weapon of defeat. This is **player planning**, not a new in-world office, oath or inventory. [E065](../../episodes/E065.md).

## A destroyed vehicle and the quest beneath Kervalon (E066–E067)

In **E066**, Vorel attempts to lure a giant **T. rex** away from **Fate 4**, but it **crushes and then destroys the RV**. The dinosaur falls into Nash's earlier excavated basin and Vorel **kills it with the Eye of Groxl's returning flaming shield**, after Nash's Fireball. Vorel cuts a **tooth into a personal dagger** and salvages bones, wheels and other wreckage into a **hand-pulled rickshaw**, named **Fang Fate Five**. This is a newly **self-chosen vehicle name**, not a new title for Vorel, a repaired Fate 4, or a renamed Mawstorm sword. When a mind flayer tries to coerce Hope, Vorel calls himself **Fang of the lizardfolk** and shouts for enthralled lizardfolk to **bow before their Fang**: both are **self-assertions of his existing recognized Great Fang office**, not additional independently granted titles. His fire/shield attacks kill an apparent thrall; **mind control/true victim status remains uncertain**. He helps the party confront the forest of eyeball trees and marches toward **Kervalon**, leaving his cart behind because the trees are too dense. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

In **E067**, a **spectral dragon** claims an ancient planet-moving machine lies beneath Kervalon, and tells Vorel that the **Eye of Groxl** is the **“god killer”** in his possession. The owner identifies `Grojil` in this ASR passage as a **pronunciation approximation of Groxl**, definitively referring to the **same Eye**, not a separate person or artifact. The **god-killing claim itself** remains the dragon's testimony. Vorel asks for a blessing, receives a mission rather than power, and commits to rescuing **Sira** and stopping Dollo Poppio. Instead of adopting the discussed **polymorph-into-T. rex plan**, he follows Nash through a tunneled wall and helps defeat a **flesh-and-bone monster with spawning skeletons** using the **Eye's shield**. The party ends E067 beneath the keep, not with its demon commander defeated. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md).

## The brief King Rex and the shaft beneath Kervalon (E068)

Vorel **calls himself “your Fang”** when challenging a large group of armed, seemingly emotionless lizardfolk beneath Kervalon. This is an assertion of his **existing Great Fang office**, not proof the Zark defenders recognize his political authority. After Nash's corridor trap draws pursuers away, **Stanley actually Polymorphs Vorel into a T. rex**, and he kills several guards. While others note that **rex means king**, Vorel jokes **“I accept the title”**: this is a **self-accepted, playfully styled “Rex/King Rex” epithet** to add to the register, **not a new formal royal title**. The mind flayer **briefly dominates the beast** until Hope breaks concentration; a later **Mind Blast stuns him**, and multiple energy-rifle volleys destroy the temporary form. **Vorel survives in his own body**, with damage/healing continuing afterward. The **Eye of Groxl's redirected/reduced rifle damage** remains a GM-described item effect of the same canonical Eye. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md).

The party defeats the exposed defenders, but the **sealed lizardfolk corridor suddenly falls quiet with its occupants unseen**. Vorel investigates the **brilliant light shaft**, attempting to handle the mind flayer's oily skin cautiously and then throwing in an **already-dead lizardfolk**, which twists and disintegrates. The beam is not safe, and no living person deliberately enters it. In the [August-publication Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-08.md), Kyle imagines the Eye someday becoming a **sword, hammer or buzzsaw** on demand; Beau regards the Eye as **integrated into Vorel's being**, but **no new weapon-form switching power is bestowed** by that discussion.
