# Vorel

**Portrayed by:** Kyle. **Identity:** Lizardfolk warrior and leader, protector of **Bok Bok**, bearer of the **Eye of Groxl** and claimant to many personal titles. **Spoilers:** Main Wastes through **E118** and cast Q&As. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional except owner-confirmed item/name orthography.

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [His source-indexed titles](vorel-titles.md) · [Eye of Groxl](../technology/eye-of-groxl.md) · [World portal](../README.md).

## Origin, aspirations and moral commitments

Vorel first appears in **Dusthill** seeking food and water. He values lizardfolk kin, strength and direct speech, but is also invested in mutual obligation. He befriends **Bok Bok**, a goblin who becomes his loyal companion. Vorel increasingly calls himself **Lizard King**, although that self-description and Bok Bok's address **do not confer a crown over all lizardfolk**. In the Golden Land a challenge involving **Diptak** produces a specific clan pledge, not an automatic universal army. [E001](../../episodes/E001.md) · [E006](../../episodes/E006.md) · [E027](../../episodes/E027.md)–[E028](../../episodes/E028.md).

During Slavetown and Black Mine episodes he encounters slavery, political manipulation and mystic experiences connected to **Fuxi**. At **Château Weir E023**, Vorel **refuses the Soulbroker pact** which Hope, Nash and Stanley sign. His skeptical approach to supernatural obligation persists. He proposes a **Pillars of Decency** code (honest talk, fair deals and kinship), later reflected in the rules of the Golden Sands inn. These are a declared ethics project, not proof every act he performs is benevolent or that the Pillars operate as an inviolable spell. [E023](../../episodes/E023.md) · [E026](../../episodes/E026.md) · [E055](../../episodes/E055.md).

Vorel is hostile toward **Diontalus's independent cruelty** and mourns its victims, notably **P0D**. He **kills Buttons in E049** during the Tietkalla betrayal. The distinction between unwilling host **Stanley** and acting intruder **Diontalus** matters to his shifting trust: a body-sharing conflict should not be flattened into Stanley voluntarily killing George or P0D. [E045](../../episodes/E045.md) · [E049](../../episodes/E049.md) · [E050](../../episodes/E050.md) · [Diontalus](diontalus.md).

## The Eye, changes to his body and his titles

Vorel acquires a distinctive **chainsaw-edged sword Mawstorm** (owner-suggested name, not audio-confirmed) and a separate **lizardfolk-skull helmet** in the second arc. He is **petrified by Kefeli in E051** and restored alive through an improvised remedy prepared with the party. Inside the transformed froghemoth he bonds with a **living Eye of Groxl**, implanted in his chest **E053**. The Eye manifests protective effects, a flame shield and later a flaming sword; it also has independent reactions and apparent demands. **Eye of Groxl is one owner-confirmed artifact**, despite `Grojil` and `Groxel` in ASR. Claims of divinity and “God Killer” deeds are not automatically established by title alone. [E037](../../episodes/E037.md) · [E047](../../episodes/E047.md) · [E051](../../episodes/E051.md)–[E053](../../episodes/E053.md) · [Eye profile](../technology/eye-of-groxl.md).

He also undergoes an unusual **color-changing scale transformation** unrelated to merely owning the Eye. In a July 2020 Q&A, Beau entertains the idea of trained, precise per-scale imagery, but **Vorel cannot yet display freely controlled text or animations as a learned feature**. [July 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-07.md).

Vorel accumulates an unusually elaborate mixture of **self-awarded epithets, other people's greetings, formal offices and humorous slogans**. The **source-indexed [title register](vorel-titles.md)** preserves wording, cue windows and speaker/recognition distinctions rather than flooding his biography. Major differences include **Lizard King** (self-claim), **sheriff** (temporary appointment under Orp), **Great Fang of Razak Val** (publicly recognized after defeating Orp), **Sigil M-sports champion** (actual tournament victory), and later statements of being the “Fang of nothing” (self-assessment, not a formal abdication ceremony). [Title audit](../../reports/VOREL_TITLE_AUDIT_2026-10-23.md).

## Tietkalla, Golden Sands and Razak Val (E054–E066)

At **E054**, Vorel attempts to attack **Monsoon Cabbage**, who **escapes as mist**, and **executes captured Suck Master Din at Hope's signal** after her public coronation. In the three-month interval before E055, he develops the **Golden Sands** inn/adventurers' guild with Bok Bok, including rules inspired by the Pillars of Decency. He helps civilians during the stonefall and disputes attempts to weaponize supernatural bargains. [E054](../../episodes/E054.md)–[E056](../../episodes/E056.md) · [Tietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md).

In **Razak Val**, Vorel seeks to protect lizardfolk and challenges the cyclops **Orp**. **Diptak is killed by Orp in E060**. Vorel's succession involves violent trials and a catastrophic **feast explosion following Hope's redirected rocket**, killing other leaders and bystanders. He **defeats Orp E062** and becomes **Great Fang**, but **the Sun's Witnesses crisis erupts** and transforms people into chickenlike monsters. Vorel must leave without resolving the region's full suffering. **Omnom Cinderborn** remains behind to defend others and will survive to confront the party later. [E060](../../episodes/E060.md)–[E065](../../episodes/E065.md) · [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md).

During the party's later journey, Vorel tries to prevent a giant **T. rex** from destroying **Fate 4**, but the RV is actually crushed and wrecked in **E066**. He kills the dinosaur after Nash's preparations and constructs a makeshift **Fang Fate Five** rickshaw from salvaged bones and wreckage. This is a **vehicle name**, not proof that the original was repaired or an extra personal title. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md) · [Hope](hope-akmenos.md).

## Ancient technology and the shared sacrifice (E067–E081)

At Kervalon Vorel is briefly **Polymorphed into a T. rex by Stanley E068** during a battle with energy-rifle guards and mind-flayer control. This does not become a permanent lizardfolk transformation or a formally granted “King Rex” office. In CHIDS he encounters **company-like automated security, deadly environmental hazards, illithids, engineered creatures and the living hive**. He wears specialized **anti-dragon armor**—a nanoweave suit, harpoon harness, jump boots and psychic-resistant helmet—without the suit being exempt from ordinary armor tradeoffs. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md) · [E076](../../episodes/E076.md).

A CHIDS attack severs his hand/digits in **E077**, and **the Monster Mender procedure actually reattaches them in E078**, with assistance from Nash and Stanley. A **hive council** claims CHIDS used pre-existing lizardfolk material to breed anti-dragon soldiers; observed conditioning labs substantiate particular experiments but **not the origin of all living lizardfolk**. The **Eye of Groxl** reacts to marked vessels including **G-R-0-X-1**; its broader divine genealogy remains contested. [E077](../../episodes/E077.md)–[E079](../../episodes/E079.md) · [CHIDS](../places/chids.md).

At the **Traversal Drive E081** climax, Vorel rejects a proposed sacrificial shortcut that would destroy Earth, and suggests the **four friends share the burden** of the crimson beam. They join hands and are **GM-declared dead at the cutoff**, while **Sira escapes alive with Eddie** and Chance is left drifting unlocated. The fate of the entire planet, the Principle's contract and all ancient explanations cannot be proven by the fact of activation. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [CHIDS crisis](../HISTORY_CHIDS_DRIVE.md).

## Sigil and White Tower (E082–E101)

**E082** restores Vorel to an active living body in **Sigil**, after a subjective star-filled separation. His possessions are missing and the **Eye of Groxl appears broken/split**, leaving protective remnants. Later GM comments describe **physical inert Eye matter still embedded in his chest**, not fully removed or confirmed active. In the **E101** tower confrontation the **hooded Voice actually restores Vorel's Eye** and he again summons its blade. These are *different statuses at different cutoffs*, not a contradiction to be silently erased. [E082](../../episodes/E082.md) · [April 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-04.md) · [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [Eye](../technology/eye-of-groxl.md).

Vorel enters **Sigil's M-sports/marionette fighting circuit**, winning a qualifier and ultimately the **E087 Big Time Smackeroo championship**, including **5,000 gold** and a real public prize. The Q&A describes the tournament's out-of-world evolution from Beau's poster joke, not an additional match or secret trophy. Vorel also completes the **trickster church's public humiliation condition** related to Hope and Stanley's restoration, and both friends **actually return alive through mirrors E088**. [E085](../../episodes/E085.md) · [E087](../../episodes/E087.md) · [E088](../../episodes/E088.md) · [March 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-03.md).

He accompanies the party toward **Tobacho** and the **White Tower**, sometimes borrowing **Shatter/Angel Slayer** and employing a Flame Tongue before the Eye is restored. At White Tower a **plague really kills some residents**, and **Vorel prepares/administers the curing broth with Walnut E096**, restoring infected survivors over several hours. He participates in the siege, resists some of Hope's choices, and in **E101 drops the separate Fist gauntlet when stunned**, after which Hope attaches its different living Hand to herself and **leaves the original party**. Vorel's Eye is restored before the three remaining companions depart. [E090](../../episodes/E090.md)–[E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [White Tower](../places/white-tower.md) · [Hand](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md).

## The search for home and survivors (E102–E118)

Vorel accompanies Nash and Stanley aboard an airship toward **Sigil** while retaining the **closed Fist case**. The Tungan moon and saucers attack; Squirts's intervention eliminates that threat in E104. After the alternate portal ritual and the deadly **E105 lift ambush**, they reach Tobacho. **E106:** **Stanley opens the case and Tobacho physically accepts the gauntlet**, without the Hand held by Hope; **Vorel returns borrowed Shatter to Jalver**. The separate case remains with Tobacho, not Vorel's later inventory. [E102](../../episodes/E102.md)–[E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [Tobacho](tobacho-fleur.md).

At **First Nash's asteroid tower E110**, Vorel meets an alternate Tedna and hears of the deaths of *other-timeline* companions, not this world's four. He briefly takes **Hope's guns/belt** but gives them back before departure. The party teleports to **devastated Tietkalla**, where Vorel finds **Bok Bok alive** at Golden Sands E111. He joins the assault on **General Carthax**, who **kills Squirts and Melgor**. Vorel's use of **Elhandari pacification technology** enables the general's **capture alive**, not his killing; Bok Bok survives an internal blast but loses a hand. Vorel places **Squirts's ashes in a vial** and boards the rescue ship with other survivors. [E110](../../episodes/E110.md) · [E111](../../episodes/E111.md) · [Carthax](general-carthax.md).

In **E114**, Vorel still handles Squirts's ashes and defends the elven vessel from undead pirates; **Bok Bok is healed conscious but his lost hand is not regrown**. At **Elhandar E115**, Vorel reports that the ashes are missing, perhaps lost during the voyage, but **the precise loss is not observed**. He mourns his people and doubts his standing as Great Fang, saying in a self-assessment that he is “Fang of nothing.” **This is not evidence every lizardfolk subject died or that a formal abdication happened**. [E114](../../episodes/E114.md) · [E115](../../episodes/E115.md).

**E117:** Vorel witnesses Hope return, supports a chance for dialogue without absolving her and helps look after **Chunks**, Squirts's surviving child. He joins the refugees' ceremony and is called **Keeper of Stories**. He smells evidence that lizardfolk are nearby in the Elhandar forest but does **not** locate Omnom that episode. [E117](../../episodes/E117.md).

**E118:** Vorel **actually finds Omnom Cinderborn alive**, severely injured and grieving Razak Val. Omnom charges Hope, but **Stanley's Hold Person** stops the attack. Vorel takes Omnom aside and refuses his request to be killed; **Omnom recounts his losses and accuses Hope of intentional wrongdoing**, while Hope offers a different explanation. Vorel persuades him to attempt a **hunt the next morning** with the four companions, but **no hunt or full reconciliation occurs before the E118 cutoff**. Omnom addresses Vorel as **Great Fang**, a renewed personal recognition of an existing title rather than new political coronation. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Omnom](omnom-cinderborn.md).

## Relationships, possessions and open questions

**Bok Bok** is Vorel's closest surviving companion and ward. **Omnom**, **Diptak**, **Razak Val** and the **Golden Land** tie him to his people. **Hope** is both a fellow adventurer and the queen whose decisions he challenges. **Stanley** is a friend with a complicated history; the acts of the separate **Diontalus** should remain distinct. **Nash** shares wartime and CHIDS experiences with him. **Squirts's death** and care for Chunks complicate his understanding of leadership. [Bok Bok](bok-bok.md) · [Diontalus](diontalus.md) · [Squirts](squirts.md).

Equipment at different cutoffs includes **Mawstorm**, skull helmet, **Eye of Groxl**, borrowed swords, anti-dragon suit and specialist devices. Vorel's record is **not** one cumulative RPG inventory: items may be transferred, lost or inactive; the Eye changes state between E081, E082 and E101. [Eye](../technology/eye-of-groxl.md) · [Title register](vorel-titles.md).

**E119:** Vorel **actually leads the promised hunt** with Omnom, Dilo and all four original heroes. He makes **mundane improvised weapons**, has Nash bathed, guides the group north, camouflages with his changing scales and **lights and throws a wax-wrapped fish-head lure** into northern forest brush. **Stanley extinguishes the resulting small fire**; Chester is **not sighted or fought before the cliffhanger**. Omnom **shows he still carries the Necklace of Fireballs**, refusing Vorel's suggestion of a transfer. No new formal rank or new wording of a personal title is confirmed. [E119](../../episodes/E119.md) · [title register](vorel-titles.md).

**E120:** Vorel **actually battles and wounds Chester** under Nash's ***Haste*** spell, helping set up the creature's fatal fall when Hope pulls it down. He **lifts Chester's massive dead carcass and physically frees Hope alive**. **Omnom now expressly accepts him as the unquestioned Great Fang**, a renewed recognition rather than fresh official office. After the apparent deer turn into hostile elves, Vorel grapples and bites a caster; **Dilo escapes with Hope**, not killed by Stanley's spell. Vorel follows Stanley's giant eagle to a mountain cave, finds Dilo's unnaturally long stride tracks and **enters with Nash/Stanley/Omnom**. No new personal title name beyond existing **Great Fang** is reliably introduced. [E120](../../episodes/E120.md) · [title register](vorel-titles.md).

**Latest played state:** **Alive in the Elhandar mountain cave E120 alongside Stanley, Nash and Omnom, pursuing Dilo who has abducted Hope**. Bok Bok and Chunks remain at the cottage; **Chester was defeated and killed earlier in this episode**. Exact formal reach of the Great Fang title, the fate of Razak Val, lost remains of Squirts, Eye of Groxl mechanics and Vorel's future obligations remain open.
