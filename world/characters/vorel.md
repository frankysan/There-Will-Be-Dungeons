# Vorel

**Longer written name:** **Vorel Rasvim Kurik** in the fan wiki, not independently confirmed as a full name from Year One spoken dialogue. **Portrayed by:** Kyle. **Identity:** Lizardfolk warrior. **Year One status:** Alive; protector of Bok Bok; bearing the sentient **Eye of Groxl**. **Spoilers:** E000–E054.

## Character and development

Vorel arrives in Dusthill a powerful desert-wandering lizardfolk trying to trade for water and food. As the campaign unfolds he becomes a warrior increasingly willing to declare himself a **Lizard King**, but his leadership is not a uniformly recognized crown over every lizardfolk community. During the road adventures he brings along **Bok Bok**, a goblin whose persistent devotion starts as a surprise and becomes one of Vorel's closest bonds.

He fights through the slavery and terror of Slavetown and the Black Mine, including a lizardfolk religious/visionary ordeal tied to the name **Fuxi**. At the **Soulbroker compact**, Vorel is the one regular protagonist who **refuses to sign** the soul contract accepted by Nash, Hope and Stanley. His independent judgments are not always gentle: he kills enemies, coerces prisoners, contributes to hard choices and is often suspicious of the people who promise power. A later confrontation with **Diptak** ends with a pledge from the **Golden Land clan**, although no army is thereby on call at every subsequent scene.

As the company reaches Tietkalla, Vorel acquires the distinctive chain greatsword **Ma Storm** and fights the Toe Rippers and city defenders. His decisions frequently escalate danger, yet he is also among the companions most outraged by **Diontalus's** killing of P0D. He **kills Buttons** in E049, and later proposes a memorial code of conduct called the **Pillars of Decency** (POD). It is a **proposed party standard**, not a magical contract or a binding constitution.

**Kefeli petrifies Vorel** during E051. Nash and Stanley protect him and make a limited remedy from an earlier basilisk corpse, restoring **both Vorel and Hope alive**. In the giant froghemoth E052–E053, he mentally contacts the creature's brain but does not demonstrate lasting mastery over it. He then accepts the living **Eye of Groxl** into his chest. Its will, combat rage, shield and protection effects come with risks of displeasure and influence. The eye is a **single owner-confirmed artifact**, even though ASR transcribes `Grojil` and `Groxel`; its connection to any separate historical deity remains a source question. Vorel's newly controllable **scale color-changing** arises separately from pixie/froghemoth effects, not the eye.

At E054 he attacks the fleeing **Monsoon** but cannot kill him, then **executes captive Suck Master Din at Hope's signal** after Hope's public coronation. Vorel's future ideas about raising Bok Bok as a squire are plans rather than completed training.

## Relationships and items

**Bok Bok** accompanies and protects him, including watching over his petrified form. **Hope** becomes a queen he has helped enthrone, not simply another opponent. **Stanley/Diontalus** remains a fraught relationship because of P0D and George; later cast discussion describes Vorel informally checking who has bodily control. Key equipment includes **Ma Storm**, the **+1 AC lizardfolk-skull helmet** obtained in E047, and the later **Eye of Groxl**; **the helmet and eye are separate items**. The hidden desert cache Vorel retrieves in E053 is not equivalent to all its gold being distributed to the town.

## After Year One — Golden Sands and spectral consequences (E055–E056)

By **E055**, Vorel has opened the **Golden Sands inn and adventurers' guild**, with Bok Bok as kitchen apprentice. Its three rules interpret the **Pillars of Decency** as **honest talk, fair deals, kinship**; a “liars' table” allows card-game bluffs. He defends non-tiefling customers in a fistfight against prejudiced patrons. In E056 he helps shelter civilians from falling stones using his **Eye of Groxl's flaming shield** and directs people into sewers. **The ghost of George Miller supernaturally ages him**, but the priestess **Emenil restores his youth with Greater Restoration within E056**. The sentient **Eye of Groxl is displeased but loses a control contest**; it does not possess him in that moment. A comment using `Grojil` in ASR **does not create a different item**.
