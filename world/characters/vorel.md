## Origin, aspirations and moral commitments

Vorel first appears in **Dusthill** seeking food and water. He values lizardfolk kin, strength and direct speech, but is also invested in mutual obligation. He befriends **Bok Bok**, a goblin who becomes his loyal companion. Vorel increasingly calls himself **Lizard King**, although that self-description and Bok Bok's address **do not confer a crown over all lizardfolk**. In the Golden Land a challenge involving **Diptak** produces a specific clan pledge, not an automatic universal army. [E001](../../episodes/E001.md) · [E006](../../episodes/E006.md) · [E027](../../episodes/E027.md)–[E028](../../episodes/E028.md).

During Slavetown and Black Mine episodes he encounters slavery, political manipulation and mystic experiences connected to **Fuxi**. At **Château Weir E023**, Vorel **refuses the Soulbroker pact** which Hope, Nash and Stanley sign. His skeptical approach to supernatural obligation persists. He proposes a **Pillars of Decency** code (honest talk, fair deals and kinship), later reflected in the rules of the Golden Sands inn. These are a declared ethics project, not proof every act he performs is benevolent or that the Pillars operate as an inviolable spell. [E023](../../episodes/E023.md) · [E026](../../episodes/E026.md) · [E055](../../episodes/E055.md).
