# Nobieri — the Dead Tongue

## Introduction

**Nobieri** (also rendered *Nobiery*, sometimes *Riddle Nobieri*) is an elderly tiefling scholar, ritual practitioner and political ally of [Hope](hope-akmenos.md). The companions first meet him in **E047**, imprisoned within an enchanted book in the hidden library of **T'ietkalla**. He speaks of a dispossessed Akmenos dynasty and a hidden army; **his story of ancient bargains is his testimony**, not independently certified prehistory. In **E054**, he physically returns with armed tieflings and crowns Hope, making his contribution to the changed city directly observable. [E047](../../episodes/E047.md) · [E054](../../episodes/E054.md).

When the city is devastated again, he helps connect its survivors with an Elhandari refuge and performs a ceremony as the community names **the Harbor**. In later episodes he investigates dangerous specimens and reports on the Solar Mines for the Earthly Defenders. His **last directly described personal action in this curated profile is boarding an allied Liberator vehicle in S3E05**, not surviving every subsequent battle or appearing at the S3E18 rebuilding council. [E111](../../episodes/E111.md) · [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) · [S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md).

## From the hidden book to Hope's court

## Ancient claims and first appearance

The group releases him from an enchanted book in T'ietkalla's hidden library in **E047**. He describes an ancient Akmenos royal family, twelve houses, the **Night Pasture** and an old arrangement with the **Soulbroker**. These are his recollections and assertions, *not* established global history. In E048 he uses the Key to Château Weir to seek a lost army. **Hundreds of armed tieflings actually arrive with him E054**, when he crowns **Hope** queen. This validates a concrete return and public coronation, but not every detail of his alleged original bargain or troop census. [E047](../../episodes/E047.md) · [E048](../../episodes/E048.md) · [E054](../../episodes/E054.md).

## Counsel, diplomacy and exile

He remains involved in the monarchy, spiritual rites and Hope's reforms; **his own knowledge of past sacrifices and the Weir is interested testimony**. E111 shows Nobieri and Melgor physically return during the demonic siege alongside arriving Elhandari rescuers. By E115 he visits the Elhandar cottage, reports other survivors and asks after Hope. In **E117** he performs a fire ceremony as Hope names **the Harbor**; a fire figure called Abegir appears, but its divinity and afterlife statements cannot be established from its self-presentation. ## Refuge, samples and reconnaissance

## E123 — samples received, mending and permission to investigate

At the Harbor **Hope gives Nobieri the collected mushroom bag**. When she breaks a finger trying to untie Nash, he **magically loosens the ropes and mends the finger with an Infernal prayer**; **no hit points are restored**. This does not cure Stanley/Vorel's scythe-inflicted ability losses. He observes that **one of each specimen may be insufficient**, receives Hope's **landmark directions toward the northern cave** and her warning about a possessing spirit/veined eyes, then asks permission to learn more.

Hope **authorizes the inquiry**, and Nobieri promises to keep participant numbers low so the Harbor can survive if it goes badly. **No expedition is shown departing or returning, extra mushrooms obtained, preparation consumed or buried heart knowingly revealed to him as such**. He does not automatically hear the later private warning to Emenil and is **back in his own house at the lake cutoff**. [E123](../../episodes/E123.md) · [Harbor](../places/the-harbor.md).

## E124 — cellstone and refugees staying

After the party's lake encounter, **Nobieri gives Hope a cellstone** and hears a group account. He affirms the refugees can fight and stand beside their queen; **they remain at the Harbor**, and he returns to his study. His E123 limited mushroom investigation **is not shown departing or returning**. No extra mushrooms or heart custody acquired; he does not accompany the household to the core. [E124](../../episodes/E124.md) · [Comparison](../../reports/E124_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md), **ASR-provisional, no listening**.

## S3E04 — actual Earth reconnaissance, reporting at Mars second war council

In [S3E04 — The Plan](../../episodes/S3E04.md) ~01:56–02:04, Nobieri(?) **PHYSICALLY ATTENDS the SECOND MARS COUNCIL of Earthly Defenders** and **REPORTS that he traveled to Earth as SCOUT** while the heroes rescued Tungsten. He **PERSONALLY DESCRIBES** the Solar Mines valley, government offices, Workerberg, domed City of the Chosen, Information Library, lava-ringed entrance, and **four anti-air cannon towers**. Claims paladins of the Umbilical Order watch with **magical senses**, **7–8 enormous Slow Peters** and approx **1,000–2,000 Toyota Host** plus Kuromokishi orc squads. **These are HIS surveillance estimates, not a demonstrated fight, exact enemy census, established AA cannon range or successful infiltration**. He explicitly **cannot say how far guns shoot**, confirms no shield effect seen. Council uses his report to agree **future** diversion and tower assaults, not yet executed. [Two-ASR source audit](../../reports/WASTES_S3E04_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S3E05 — actually accompanies Earthly Defenders deployment, no reported personal fight

During [S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md) ~01:19:20–01:19:40 **Nobieri physically boards one of the Liberator vehicles with tiefling/other ground forces during Earth landing and march toward northern gate**. The **two gate Slow Peters physically attack the convoy**, but **no individualized injury, death, spell, scouting beyond S3E04's report or solo breakthrough by Nobieri is established**. Hope leads the ground column, and the regional battle plan has yet to reach interior mines. [S3E05 source review](../../reports/WASTES_S3E05_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## Current status and evidence boundaries

**Last directly observed scene: S3E05.** Nobieri boards a Liberator vehicle during the allied landing and approach to the Solar Mines. He is **not shown personally wounded, slain or leading an independent assault** in that episode. The greater campaign continues through S3E18, but **no confirmed later individual Nobieri fate** follows from the indexed sources; his participation in one convoy cannot be converted into a final survival, death or postwar appointment.

**Earlier roles:** His return with armed tieflings and Hope's E054 coronation are played events. His reports about the old kingdom, the Night Pasture and ancestral politics are testimony. His E123 permission to study mushrooms is **not a witnessed expedition**; the S3E04 report supplies estimates from scouting, not an exact enemy census or completed gun-battery assault. The Harbor fire manifestation occurs, but **its claimed divine identity remains unverified**.

**Name:** `Nobieri`, `Nobiery`, and `Riddle Nobieri` remain source-provisional alternatives. The heading and legacy file path are preserved; no pronunciation, genealogical certainty or universal religious truth is inferred from his assertions.
