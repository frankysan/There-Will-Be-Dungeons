# Nobieri / Nobiery (?) — the Dead Tongue

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Identity:** Elder tiefling scholar and political intermediary. His name is transcribed as `Nobiery`, `Nobieri`, and sometimes `Riddle Nobieri`; the exact spelling and some titles await listening/written-source review. **Spoilers:** Through E124. **Evidence:** Processed ASR, not independent audio verification.

## E124 — cellstone and refugees staying

After the party's lake encounter, **Nobieri gives Hope a cellstone** and hears a group account. He affirms the refugees can fight and stand beside their queen; **they remain at the Harbor**, and he returns to his study. His E123 limited mushroom investigation **is not shown departing or returning**. No extra mushrooms or heart custody acquired; he does not accompany the household to the core. [E124](../../episodes/E124.md) · [Comparison](../../reports/E124_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md), **ASR-provisional, no listening**.

## Ancient claims and first appearance

The group releases him from an enchanted book in Tietkalla's hidden library in **E047**. He describes an ancient Akmenos royal family, twelve houses, the **Night Pasture** and an old arrangement with the **Soulbroker**. These are his recollections and assertions, *not* established global history. In E048 he uses the Key to Château Weir to seek a lost army. **Hundreds of armed tieflings actually arrive with him E054**, when he crowns **Hope** queen. This validates a concrete return and public coronation, but not every detail of his alleged original bargain or troop census. [E047](../../episodes/E047.md) · [E048](../../episodes/E048.md) · [E054](../../episodes/E054.md).

## Counsel, diplomacy and exile

He remains involved in the monarchy, spiritual rites and Hope's reforms; **his own knowledge of past sacrifices and the Weir is interested testimony**. E111 shows Nobieri and Melgor physically return during the demonic siege alongside arriving Elhandari rescuers. By E115 he visits the Elhandar cottage, reports other survivors and asks after Hope. In **E117** he performs a fire ceremony as Hope names **the Harbor**; a fire figure called Abegir appears, but its divinity and afterlife statements cannot be established from its self-presentation. ## E123 — samples received, mending and permission to investigate

At the Harbor **Hope gives Nobieri the collected mushroom bag**. When she breaks a finger trying to untie Nash, he **magically loosens the ropes and mends the finger with an Infernal prayer**; **no hit points are restored**. This does not cure Stanley/Vorel's scythe-inflicted ability losses. He observes that **one of each specimen may be insufficient**, receives Hope's **landmark directions toward the northern cave** and her warning about a possessing spirit/veined eyes, then asks permission to learn more.

Hope **authorizes the inquiry**, and Nobieri promises to keep participant numbers low so the Harbor can survive if it goes badly. **No expedition is shown departing or returning, extra mushrooms obtained, preparation consumed or buried heart knowingly revealed to him as such**. He does not automatically hear the later private warning to Emenil and is **back in his own house at the lake cutoff**. [E123](../../episodes/E123.md) · [Harbor](../places/the-harbor.md).
