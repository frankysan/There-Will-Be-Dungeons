# Nobieri / Nobiery (?) — the Dead Tongue

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Identity:** Elder tiefling scholar and political intermediary. His name is transcribed as `Nobiery`, `Nobieri`, and sometimes `Riddle Nobieri`; the exact spelling and some titles await listening/written-source review. **Spoilers:** Through E118. **Evidence:** Processed ASR, not independent audio verification.

## Ancient claims and first appearance

The group releases him from an enchanted book in Tietkalla's hidden library in **E047**. He describes an ancient Akmenos royal family, twelve houses, the **Night Pasture** and an old arrangement with the **Soulbroker**. These are his recollections and assertions, *not* established global history. In E048 he uses the Key to Château Weir to seek a lost army. **Hundreds of armed tieflings actually arrive with him E054**, when he crowns **Hope** queen. This validates a concrete return and public coronation, but not every detail of his alleged original bargain or troop census. [E047](../../episodes/E047.md) · [E048](../../episodes/E048.md) · [E054](../../episodes/E054.md).

## Counsel, diplomacy and exile

He remains involved in the monarchy, spiritual rites and Hope's reforms; **his own knowledge of past sacrifices and the Weir is interested testimony**. E111 shows Nobieri and Melgor physically return during the demonic siege alongside arriving Elhandari rescuers. By E115 he visits the Elhandar cottage, reports other survivors and asks after Hope. In **E117** he performs a fire ceremony as Hope names **the Harbor**; a fire figure called Abegir appears, but its divinity and afterlife statements cannot be established from its self-presentation.
