# General Carthax (?)

**Role:** Demonic commander involved in the invasion of Tietkalla. **Last directly played disposition:** **Alive, pacified and captured by Elhandari forces in E111**, not killed. **Spelling:** Provisional processed ASR. **Spoilers:** E094–E111.

The party encounters a general during the White Tower conflict, and later Tietkalla is assaulted by demonic forces. The precise hierarchy among generals, the hooded Voice and other fiends should not be taken as settled solely by an opponent's claims. [E094](../../episodes/E094.md) · [E104](../../episodes/E104.md).

In **E111** Carthax's destructive attack **kills Squirts and Melgor**, while **Chunks survives**. Vorel deploys **Elhandari empathy/anti-aggression devices** in the general's eye and the Elhandari take him alive. **Bok Bok survives a violent internal blast**, though he loses a hand. This is a capture, not a slain-general finale, and does not establish a successful long-term rehabilitation.
