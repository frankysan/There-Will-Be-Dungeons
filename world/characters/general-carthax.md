# General Carthax — commander of the demon host

## Introduction

General Carthax commanded demons during the struggle around the White Tower and later the assault on T'ietkalla. Massive and imposing, he treated mortal captives as leverage in an alliance with devils that was temporary even by his own account. His claims of infernal jurisdiction and orders did not make him a reliable witness to the wider politics of the Abyss.[^siege]

## The uneasy alliance

Hope encountered Carthax after winged soldiers seized her near the White Tower. He questioned her on a raised command platform while a smaller infernal associate, Ulvert, claimed prior knowledge of her. Later, Carthax declared that the demon–devil arrangement had ended and that Orcus had ordered the demons away from the immediate siege. His retreat was visible, but his account of the higher powers' motives was still a commander's testimony, not an independently verified peace among the fiends.[^siege]

## Attack on T'ietkalla

Carthax returned at the head of forces that devastated T'ietkalla. Winged soldiers spread fire through the city and a strike shattered part of the keep. Hope escaped through a gate before he could seize her. When the other companions later reached the city, the general sat above a gruesome throne of tiefling corpses, defying Elhandari teams sent to subdue him.[^return]

He proved lethal. During the attempted rescue, his force beams killed Squirts, Melgor and several Elhandari soldiers; Squirts's infant Chunks survived. Vorel and the others fought back, but the general was not slain by their blows. Bok Bok entered his mouth with explosives and emerged badly injured after Stanley compelled Carthax to expel him.[^capture]

## Current status

The Elhandari supplied magic designed to suppress aggression. Vorel drove a bandolier of the empathy devices into Carthax's eye, leaving him wounded and pacified. Elhandari forces contained him alive in a shrinking tentacled trap. No subsequent custody record, lasting reform or execution is established. His final witnessed disposition is **captured alive**, not killed in the battle.[^capture]
