# Union Wheel — sworn guardian to Stanley

## Introduction

Union Wheel was a young Elhandari elf who served as [Stanley Billings's](stanley-billings.md) sworn guardian after he was chosen as Selle Prime. She identified herself as the niece of engineer [Tungsten Wheel](tungsten-wheel.md), and her loyalty survived injuries, an uneasy closeness with [Vorel](vorel.md), and Stanley's attempts to make her value her own life as much as her oath. Her final stand during the assault on the Solar Mines ended in a witnessed death, after she had already survived a desperate rescue and surgery.[^union-intro]

## An oath and a family reunion

When Stanley accepted the Elhandari leadership at the Mars council, Union took her place beside him as an attendant and protector. In the search at Pentel Weiss she later encountered her long-missing uncle Tungsten and embraced him. The imprisonment proved dangerous: a giant psychic eye rendered her unconscious, Stanley stabilized her, and further fighting in the prison knocked her down again. Stanley and Tungsten provided care in separate moments, allowing her to leave the prison alive with the others aboard Fate Six.[^union-early]

## Injured aboard Fate Six, saved by Doctor Spider

Union was still recuperating when Hell-aligned attackers boarded Fate Six. During the fighting she suffered severe injuries belowdecks, revealed only afterward when Tungsten approached Stanley for help: her spine, chest and organs were badly damaged. Dr. Daffodil helped keep her alive, but her recovery was uncertain. Tungsten, fearing prolonged suffering, suggested letting her go. Stanley refused. With [Doctor Spider](dr-spider.md) temporarily released from the ship's brig under supervision, an extraordinary surgery followed. Spider employed his medical instrument, silk, and spectral assistant Nurse Wilkins while Stanley and Daffodil aided her. **Union survived**; this was a rescue of a living patient, **not a resurrection**.[^union-surgery]

At Blast Belly Rock, she could stand with crutches and a bandaged chest. As Fate Six continued toward Mars, she reached out to Vorel and he took her hand. The gesture showed comfort and affection but proved no marriage or enduring romantic commitment. Nor did her recovery mean that an injury or later death could be retroactively ignored.[^union-recovery]

## Return to Mars and uncertain affection

During the next voyage a green mind-fluff parasite caught the companions in a shared dream. In the waking ship Union pulled one of the creatures off Vorel, helping the others free Stanley, Hope and Nash. Fate Six reached Mars, where Union rejoined Tungsten and received Stanley's permission to help her uncle's engineering team with the developing Rubistic Triangulosphere. The device was still unfinished at that episode's cutoff.[^union-mars]

On the evening before the war, Union invited Vorel to dance and taught him a simple elven two-step. After hearing about his recent Courtship of the Fang, she became visibly distressed, released his hand and left the celebration. She returned the next morning in guardian regalia and later ate Stanley's *Heroes' Feast* with the companions. The dance and hurt were directly witnessed, but **no marriage, explicit breakup or final decision about their relationship** was announced.[^union-dance]

## Her renewed oath and journey to Earth

Before the attack Stanley privately warned Union against throwing away the life she had nearly lost aboard Fate Six. She responded that she **chose** to defend the Selle Prime and what he represented for the Elhandari, even at personal risk. She did not use that conversation to settle her relationship with Vorel. She boarded Fate Six for Earth alongside Stanley, remaining attentive to him through the voyage and his private family concerns. The ensuing battle would test the conflict between her devotion and Stanley's desire that she survive.[^union-oath]

## A rescue followed by a fatal charge

At the Solar Mines in **S3E08**, Union attempted to jump over lava toward Stanley but fell short and was burned. Stanley **rescued her with *Telekinesis***, argued that her life was worth more than dying as his shield, handed her his radio headset and went alone to the giant foe's back. Union nevertheless insisted on her oath. She and [Emenil](emenil.md) then tried to climb or hold the enormous enemy's tail and were thrown clear. As they retreated, the giant **struck and crushed them both**, leaving visible remains. Their deaths were directly narrated; no subsequent healing or resurrection of either occurred in the reviewed battle.[^union-death]

## Current status

**Union Wheel was physically killed alongside Emenil in S3E08, after Stanley had already saved her from the lava.** Earlier recoveries, including Doctor Spider's successful surgery, are part of her life and do not overturn this later outcome. No subsequent restoration was witnessed, although the fate of her soul or the abstract possibility of revival was not adjudicated. Her last choice to keep serving Stanley and her uncompleted personal relationship remain distinct aspects of her story.[^union-status]
