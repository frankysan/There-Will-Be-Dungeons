# Union Wheel — sworn guardian to Stanley

**Identity:** Elhandari elf and the **niece of engineer Tungsten Wheel**, who becomes sworn guardian/attendant of **Stanley Billings** after his selection as Selle Prime in [S2E03](../../episodes/S2E03.md). Her name is rendered variously as *Yunyon/Yuenion* in ASR; **Union Wheel** is the established research label, pending definitive audio. [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) · [Tungsten](tungsten-wheel.md) · [Chronology](../CHRONOLOGY.md). This page distinguishes performed acts from source claims; audio **not** independently listened to.

## S3E03 — rescues Vorel, returns to Mars and helps uncle's engineering effort

In [S3E03 — Hammer Giggle](../../episodes/S3E03.md) **~01:04:20**, **UNION ACTUALLY FREES VOREL** from a horrid green mind-fluff face parasite aboard Fate Six, pulls/stabs organism and begs his help with others after struggling for hours; **Vorel then frees Stanley and Hope**. The GM recounts that Union/Vorel had **laid down beside each other during rest**, explicitly **“nothing happened”** further in GM staging; Vorel later tells friends he found her desire for affection perplexing. This is **evidence of actual shared rest and her attachment to him**, **not verified marriage, consummation, commitment or lasting romantic partnership**. **Fate Six ACTUALLY LANDS ON MARS**, and Union is there with recovered uncle **Tungsten Wheel**, who reunites with lover Dunglestar. Union **ACTUALLY REQUESTS AND RECEIVES STANLEY'S PERMISSION TO JOIN her uncle's engineering group** working on **unfinished Triangulosphere** (~01:23). By episode cutoff the machine is **not completed/activated**, Union is alive on Mars, not reported newly injured. [S3E03 P/Q comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## Seen events and current state

In [S2E09](../../episodes/S2E09.md), Union recognizes and **embraces living uncle Tungsten**, found in Pentel Weiss. During [S2E11](../../episodes/S2E11.md), the giant telepathic eyeball's psychic screams **knock her unconscious**, and **Stanley STABILIZES her**. In [S2E12](../../episodes/S2E12.md), Stanley **actually heals her consciousness with collector-ring-enabled *Healing Word***, the mummy fight **knocks her unconscious AGAIN**, Tungsten stabilizes and Stanley heals her again. Hope's teleport brings Union physically **out of prison onto Fate Six**, and she recovers in ghost doctor **Dr. Daffodil's** cabin during [S3E01](../../episodes/S3E01.md), not combat-ready.

During the continuing Hell-ship boarding melee, Union **SUFFERS AN ADDITIONAL NEAR-FATAL INJURY FROM HELL KNIGHTS BELOWDECKS**. The destructive blow is **reported in [S3E02](../../episodes/S3E02.md) ~01:38**, not shown as a previously resolved death or resurrection: shattered spine, damaged chest and organs, actively kept alive by **Dr. Daffodil and Tungsten**. Tungsten tells Stanley she may die imminently and asks him not to prolong suffering. Stanley **REFUSES TO ABANDON HER** and arranges for captured **Doctor Spider** to operate. Spider **ACTUALLY SURGICALLY RECONSTRUCTS her** with his own silk/spectral Nurse Wilkins/healing device while Stanley helps and casts *Healing Word* when she crashes. GM **declares SUCCESS**; she survives, and on docking at **Blast Belly Rock (~02:25)**, **ACTUALLY STANDS beside Stanley on crutches, wearing a large bandage around her chest**.

As Fate Six leaves the settlement **for Mars** (~02:39), Union quietly **APPROACHES VOREL ON CRUTCHES, HOLDS OUT HER HAND, AND VOREL ACCEPTS**. This is a **real, witnessed affectionate/comforting gesture**, but neither marriage, intimate romance, sworn new office nor permanent disability is established by that scene. She is **alive, recovering, aboard Fate Six** at S3E02 cutoff; **no Mars arrival yet**. [Complete two-ASR audit](../../reports/WASTES_S3E02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).
