# Imprisoned black dragon — survivor of the ancient dragonflights

## Introduction

The scarred black dragon had once been a prisoner in an Earth-desert enclosure. Years later, the companions encountered it again beneath the Elhandari forest, where it emerged from the cave beyond a mushroom chamber as [Diontalus](diontalus.md) fought the travelers. Its old captivity had left missing scales, inscriptions and deep wounds, yet it could seize the otherwise intangible spectral enemy. The struggle drove Diontalus away into stone but left the dragon gravely hurt.[^dragon-return]

## A wounded witness

The dragon identified itself as the earlier desert captive and addressed [Nash](nash-maggard.md) as someone bound to its past. Although bleeding heavily, it conjured a blue restorative mist that healed Nash, Vorel and Stanley, but not itself or the already fallen companions. It believed Nash's artificial dragon heart bore the signature of its mother and said that it had tried to reach him through the frightening bursts of fire that afflicted him. The nature and cause of that connection were **its account**, rather than something the others could inspect for themselves.[^dragon-healing]

The dragon had returned to the forest, it said, to die and nourish the land. At Nash's appeal, a luminous, golden-orange maternal dragon appeared from **Nash's implanted heart**, comforted the wounded creature and sang to it. The apparition was visible; the claimed family history and the names heard in the exchange remained uncertain. Nothing showed that Nash's heart and the dying dragon's own heart were the same object or contained the same spirit.[^dragon-mother]

## The last account

Before dying, the dragon offered a history of ancient human arrivals, a bargain with the [Soulbroker](the-soulbroker.md), the encroachment of the Weir and dragonflights that failed to save Earth. It described the Principal as a servant or pawn of that struggle and believed the Weir had damaged time, while admitting that it did not know the cause of Nash's particular alternate selves. Its tale was valuable testimony from a self-described survivor, not a witnessed account of those ancient events.[^dragon-history]

It also described experiments by the Dragon Sons and alleged that Nash had been constructed from many human donors for the Principal's ambitions. Finally, it asked the companions to bring **its own heart** to Elhandar's elves, who might fashion a strange triangulated device capable, if melted at Earth's hottest point, of sealing the corruption. Neither the genealogy nor the proposed device's effectiveness had been proved, and no such device was built during this meeting.[^dragon-legacy]

## Current status

**The black dragon died in the cave.** Its breathing stopped as the spectral mother withdrew into **Nash's implanted heart**. Vorel then carefully extracted the deceased dragon's separate, natural metal heart. Stanley and Nash concealed that heart in the rear cave beneath or near a painful seven-foot metal spike; they did not carry it to Earth. The rest of the dragon's body later rose and dissipated, including its bones. The last known custody of the extracted heart is therefore its hiding place, not Nash's chest, and no revival of the dragon was witnessed.[^dragon-death]
