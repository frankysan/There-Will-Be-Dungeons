# Tedna

## Introduction

**Tedna** is a young stowaway who joins the companions aboard [Fate 4](../technology/fate-4.md), looking to become an adventurer and drawn especially to [Hope](hope-akmenos.md). His enthusiasm carries him through danger in **T'ietkalla**, where his attempt to help infiltrate the keep leads to imprisonment and a brief rescue. He is wounded in the later warehouse fighting and **dies in E050** after seeking Hope. His death is an event in the original group's history, not a vision or an unresolved disappearance. [E027](../../episodes/E027.md) · [E048](../../episodes/E048.md) · [E050](../../episodes/E050.md).

Another man later appears who resembles him: **[Badass T](badass-t.md)**, a masked, much older alternate-reality counterpart with an independent history. He is **not Tedna restored to life**. This page retains the original character's story and a source-preserving chronological appendix about the alternate; Badass T's own reader-first biography provides his main account.

## The original Tedna — journey, rescue and death

Tedna is introduced as a young **stowaway in Fate 4** during E027, eager to become an adventurer. An overturned vehicle leaves him hurt, but alive. He follows the group toward **T'ietkalla**, survives the oil-sands crisis and takes increasing risks to help Hope.

Within Trashburg he investigates opportunities to infiltrate the keep through its **wedding and catering preparations**. A letter or message from Tedna helps the group identify possible interior routes and political facts, though not every privately transmitted page is preserved publicly. He is detained inside the fortress; in E048 the party **directly finds and frees Tedna and Bok Bok alive** from prison cells. He reports that **Angelashi** was taken to a pit, but this remains **his secondhand account** and is disproved as a death report by her direct rescue in E054.

The reunion is brief. Tedna becomes gravely injured during the warehouse fighting around E049–E050. **Diontalus's Spare the Dying** temporarily stabilizes him, but he still needs urgent healing. In E050 **Tedna dies after a final exchange with Hope**. Hope's distorted vision of his ascent to a steel heaven is **pixie-blush-affected perception**, not confirmation of the cosmology of the setting. His death remains real despite the unreliability of those images.

## The alternate counterpart — Badass T (not a resurrection)

The following episode-bound notes concern the **different person**, Badass T. His earlier appearances during the Sigil and alternate-Planet-Nash route are treated in his [separate biography](badass-t.md); the S2E04–S2E09 material below is retained for source provenance, ordered by when each scene was played rather than by the last reported outcome.

## S2E04 — physically encountered “Badass T” (NOT original Tedna resurrected)

At [Tower Nash on Planet Nash](../../episodes/S2E04.md), Hope encounters an **alternate-reality Tedna** who now calls himself **“Badass T”**. He has a scarred/burned lower face and a lower-face mask, says he crossed from another strand of reality via Sigil, and insists on accompanying Hope's future adventure. **Hope reluctantly PERMITS him to come**, but **he has NOT actually boarded Fate Six** when the episode ends. Vorel reminds him of a prior entrusted item and **Badass T physically returns Smokey's belt with its special ammunition to Hope**. The final clue is Angelashi's question about **how an alternate Tedna reached Planet Nash**; that transfer mechanism is **not explained**. **Do not rewrite the original Tedna's E050 death**: this is a separately living counterpart with divergent history, not an on-screen resurrection. [Two-ASR comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E04_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E05 — alternate Badass T actually boards Fate Six and stays at Wet Whistle

The older alternate Tedna **ACTUALLY boards Fate Six** with the original heroes and elf guardian Union Wheel, then **travels via rift space and disembarks at Blast Belly Rock**. Hope discusses his fighting style aboard ship; he describes fighting for the Liberators and training in a **Toida/Toyota Host (?)**, prefers reckless striking and is **in his thirties**, distinct from the youthful original Tedna. Stanley privately warns him before landing, saying **Stanley killed their original Tedna**, which is **Stanley's present statement, not an observed replay of E050**. At the **Wet Whistle tavern in Burp**, Badass T offers to be a lookout; after argument, heroes **ACTUALLY leave him at the tavern** while **four protagonists and Union Wheel** explore the bank. He is **not killed, kidnapped or present in the bank blob fight** at this cutoff. [S2E05](../../episodes/S2E05.md) · [P/Q report](../../reports/WASTES_S2E05_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E06 — alternate Badass T captured and freed alive

During the [S2E06](../../episodes/S2E06.md) party's bank exploration, **alternate Badass T remains near Wet Whistle**. **Green Vale marauders capture him and local townsfolk** and keep them in cages instead of killing him. After the four heroes defeat the raiders, surviving orcs reveal their captives and **Hope actually unlocks cages and frees Badass T, Ailboon and the others**. Badass T says he did not fight off the entire band needlessly; he is **alive and not part of the orc raiding force**. No onward ship voyage occurs by episode end. This has **no bearing on original Tedna's E050 death**. [ASR audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E06_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E07 — alternate Badass T enters Pentel Weiss shaft; fate unresolved

After the [S2E07](../../episodes/S2E07.md) nebula voyage on Fate Six, **alternate Badass T physically enters the pyramid of Pentel Weiss** with four heroes and Union Wheel. The prison warden refuses to recognize them as authorized collectors, and rejects **Tedna Garfield** (Badass T's reluctantly stated *former surname*, ASR-provisional) as a listed criminal. **Hope asks him to test the intake shaft**, and he **actually jumps into the deep opening with knives drawn**, the sound disappearing. **The episode stops before revealing whether he survives, is arrested, escapes or reaches any prisoner**. He is **not verified dead**, and no evidence reverses the **original Tedna's E050 death**. [P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E07_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E08 — alternate Badass T confirmed ALIVE beyond prison intake

The [S2E08](../../episodes/S2E08.md) cutoff resolves his S2E07 cliffhanger: **Badass T survives jumping alone into Pentel Weiss's intake shaft**. By the time the original four and Union Wheel descend, **he has met a halfling (Zipty/Ziptery) and other local camp residents**, and **ACTUALLY throws them a rope** during their underwater tentacle-monster escape. He says he spent roughly **five hours waiting for the others**; the party had meanwhile spent hours searching/disguising, so no demonstrated unusual time dilation. He **stays with the six-person living group in the underground camp**, where the prisoners demand meat and finally **trap them by slamming a gate and transforming into werecreatures**. **Badass T is ALIVE at episode cutoff, his actual fight status after the cliffhanger unplayed**; he has not returned to ship, found Tungsten or become a registered criminal. Original Tedna's E050 death remains separate. [P/Q report](../../reports/WASTES_S2E08_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E09 — alternate Badass T left in werefolk care, still alive

In [played S2E09](../../episodes/S2E09.md), alternate **Badass T is severely injured by Torbeer the werebear**, thrown into a wall/cage and falls unconscious. **Stanley actually STABILIZES him** after a failed death save. **New fur begins growing from under his mask**, and Grandma Tilly **CLAIMS he contracted bear lycanthropy** from the bite; **no complete werebear change, healed missing lips, or reversal is depicted**. Four heroes and Union **ACTUALLY leave unconscious Badass T with the werefolk** and proceed to Tungsten. This **does not resurrect original Tedna**, nor prove his alternate will be safe or cured. [P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E09_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

**March 2022 creator Q&A:** Listener asks whether the altered alternate T will **regrow missing lips or other parts**. Beau explicitly says **the bite was not planned and he is still deciding what lycanthropy will do**. **No regrowth announced, no follow-up played scene and no confirmed complete transformation**. Beau's joking explanation that werefolk might avoid consuming mages because of unpredictable adverse magical effects is **speculation**, not proof T has eaten wizards or been cured. ## Current status and source limits

**Original Tedna:** dead in **E050**, after his rescue from T'ietkalla's keep in E048. His last exchange with Hope and the witnessed death should not be overwritten by a later alternate's appearance; the pixie-blush vision does not independently prove an afterlife.

**Badass T, separate counterpart:** last directly witnessed in **S2E09** alive but unconscious, stabilized by Stanley and left with the werefolk after a severe bite. Fur growth is observed; Grandma Tilly's claims of full werebear transformation and the March 2022 creator's tentative remarks about lost lips do not establish a completed change, recovery or return to Hope. His [own current-status section](badass-t.md#current-status) holds the latest curated outcome; there is no known direct personal return through S3E18, but that is not an independently listened full-transcript absence claim.

**Names and evidence:** `Tedna` has cast-written spelling support; `Ted` is an earlier variant, while the alternate's old surname, training and planar origin remain ASR-/speaker-qualified. The detailed chronological appendix retains sourced, episode-local observations, not fresh claims from original-audio listening.

**Other names/ASR:** `Ted` in some early contexts. The name **Tedna** has cast-written evidence from Beau ([Discord report](../../reports/DISCORD_SPELLING_2026-10-21.md)). **Identity:** Young follower of the group, especially attached to Hope. **Status:** **Dead in E050**, after being **rescued alive in E048**. **Spoilers:** Through played S2E09 and March 2022 out-of-game bonus (original Tedna dies E050; separate alternate counterpart survives).
