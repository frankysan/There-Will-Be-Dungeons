# Omnom Cinderborn — Razak Val survivor

> **The Wastes · lizardfolk warrior of Razak Val; first directly re-encountered in the Elhandar forest in [E118](../../episodes/E118.md).** Owner/reference spelling **Omnom Cinderborn** matches the Qwen-labelled ASR; variants include `Amnom`, `I'm nom`. [E118 full-export comparison](../../reports/E118_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). **Text-ASR evidence, no audio verified.**

## History and E118's firsthand meeting

**Earlier Razak Val events:** In [E062](../../episodes/E062.md) at Razak Val's Cave Fengar, a rocket **fired by Hope** is diverted to Vorel by the Eye of Groxl protection/attraction effect, resulting in substantial casualties including noncombatants and Omnom's family. A previous lizardfolk struggle against **chicken lords** continues after Vorel's departure. **Do not treat E118's allegation as the first time that earlier battle happened**. Omnom later retreats to **Tietkalla**, then experiences the demon attack. **E115** has only **Nobieri's report that Omnom is recovering at Elhandar**; **E117** ends with Vorel detecting an unidentified lizardfolk scent. Neither is a played E118-style reunion.

**E118 (~01:02–01:29):** Elhandari admiral tells Vorel Omnom has been treated and released but is adjusting poorly; **Vorel calls him out of hiding** in the living forest. **Omnom actually appears**, alive, **part of his mouth/face torn away**, with missing scales, scars and bandaged/casted arm/leg. Omnom says **he fought to save Razak Val for five nights**, saw brethren die of combat wounds and parasitic infestation, then chose survival and retreat over dying in battle. This is **his reported history**, not a new E118 flashback. On seeing Hope, he **charges to attack her**, but **Stanley's *Hold Person* succeeds before any blow lands**. Vorel **carries him away**, hears him, then calls Hope and the others in to talk.

Omnom **accuses Hope of killing his family with her rocket**, of burning Tietkalla's rail station and **intentionally drawing the demon army into the city before fleeing**. **Hope admits the rocket and fire** but says she expected demons to follow her **Hand's power** out through her Gate, intending to spare Tietkalla. Omnom is an interested, hurt witness with real firsthand perceptions but **does not have complete knowledge of Hope's motive or the demons' orders**; his sincere belief is not independently confirmed intentional betrayal. **Vorel is disturbed by learning Hope's role in the rocket and does not declare her innocent**. Omnom weeps, calls his survival shameful and **asks Vorel to kill him**. **Vorel refuses**, describing him as a bearer of Razak Val's surviving stories and encouraging purpose beyond death in battle. Omnom abandons his immediate wish to attack Hope, agrees to **a joint hunt planned for next morning**, and **walks off to find shelter in the evening**. He has **not been healed or forgiven or restored as war-chief**, and **the proposed hunt does not occur** before E118 closes.

**E119 — first actual joint hunt:** **Omnom physically accompanies Vorel, Nash, Stanley, Hope and Dilo** into the Elhandar forest after agreeing in E118. His disfiguring injuries remain relevant: he **scrapes his leg while advancing stealthily**, risking discovery. He agrees to face **Chester**, an alleged engineered apex predator, with Vorel but **no animal is killed or grief cured before the E119 cutoff**. He **actually produces the earlier Necklace of Fireballs** from a concealed skin/scales pouch; **Vorel does not take it back**, leaving custody with Omnom and charge count untested. Omnom's E118 accusations against Hope are **not resolved** by sharing an expedition. [E119](../../episodes/E119.md).

**E120 — the hunt and recognition:** Chester is **actually fought and killed** before Omnom manages to deliver a meaningful strike. Omnom **explicitly renounces his own remaining thoughts of being Great Fang and acknowledges Vorel's superiority** after witnessing Vorel and the party defeat Chester; this is **renewed personal recognition**, not a formal second coronation. He starts **eating the creature's raw flesh**, seeks relief from grief and derides Dilo's inexperience; proposed lethal sparring **does not happen**. During the later disguised-deer ambush, ***Mass Suggestion*** compels Omnom to **behave like a squirrel**; he subsequently recovers and follows the others to the cave. His **Necklace of Fireballs was not passed away in E119 and no E120 transfer/use is depicted**. **Hope is abducted by Dilo**, and Omnom's earlier hostility to her has not become a played killing or settled forgiveness. [E120](../../episodes/E120.md).

**Titles and relationship:** Omnom uses **“Great Fang”** for **Vorel**, affirming the previously earned title; this is **repeat recognition**, not a new rank awarded in E118. He respects Vorel's judgment despite his anger, and their connection now links Razak Val's losses to Hope's unresolved responsibility. **Further destiny, recruitment, and whether Omnom can trust her remain open**.
