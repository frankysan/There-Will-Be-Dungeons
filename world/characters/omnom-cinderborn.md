# Omnom Cinderborn — Razak Val survivor

Omnom Cinderborn was a lizardfolk warrior of [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md), loyal to the people whose Great Fang had become [Vorel](vorel.md). The cost of the succession struggle was personal: Hope's rocket, diverted in battle by the protection of Vorel's Eye of Groxl, killed many at Cave Fengar, including members of Omnom's family. Omnom survived, but his loyalty to Vorel did not erase his grief or his anger toward [Hope](hope-akmenos.md).[^omnom-early]

<a id="history-and-e118s-firsthand-meeting"></a>

## A wounded survivor

Omnom later fought to defend Razak Val, according to his own account, then retreated as the fighting and losses mounted. He passed through T'ietkalla during the demon attack and eventually reached the Elhandari forest. When Vorel found him there, Omnom was alive but badly injured, with part of his face missing and an arm and leg bandaged. He had survived when other warriors had died, a choice he regarded with shame.[^omnom-return]

The meeting with Hope nearly became another tragedy. Omnom rushed at her, but [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) restrained him with *Hold Person* before he could strike. Omnom accused Hope of killing his family and deliberately exposing T'ietkalla to demons. Hope admitted the rocket and the fire at the station but disputed the intent he assigned to her, saying she expected the demons to follow the Hand's power through her Gate. Omnom's experience of loss was real; his account of Hope's motives remained contested. Vorel did not simply absolve her.[^omnom-accusation]

Omnom broke down and asked Vorel to kill him. Vorel refused and urged him to live as a bearer of his people's history. The warrior agreed to hunt with the others the following morning. This was a fragile step away from his immediate demand for death, **not a completed reconciliation with Hope or a cure for grief**.[^omnom-accusation]

## The last journey with Vorel

Omnom did join Vorel and the others in the forest. He carried a concealed **Necklace of Fireballs** that Vorel initially left with him. After the group defeated the creature Chester, Omnom acknowledged Vorel as the better Great Fang and abandoned his remaining personal claim to that position. It was renewed recognition of an existing ruler, not a new coronation. His anger and losses had not vanished.[^omnom-hunt]

In the cave beyond the forest, Omnom fought alongside the companions against the spectral [Diontalus](diontalus.md). Vorel retrieved the entire necklace from Omnom's pouch and expended it in the fight; it was not left intact on Omnom's body. Diontalus's devastating magic killed Omnom, leaving only his two feet. Vorel recovered those remains and placed them beside Dilo's motionless body. No restoration followed.[^omnom-death]

## Current status

**Omnom died in the cave confrontation.** His renewed trust in Vorel had been demonstrated, but he did not live to reach a settled understanding with Hope. Omnom's feet were last seen where Vorel placed them; no performed recovery, funeral or revival is established. A later suggestion that they could become memorial boots was cast commentary about an unrealized idea, not another event in Omnom's life.[^omnom-after]
