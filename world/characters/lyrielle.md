# Lyrielle — elven doctor (provisional name)

## Introduction

Lyrielle was an elven doctor aboard the Elhandari rescue ship that carried [Nash](nash-maggard.md), [Angelashi](angelashi.md) and their companions away from the dangers of T'ietkalla. Golden-haired, green-eyed and accompanied by a white mink familiar, she became a healer, guide and protector of the growing Maggard household. Her skill with protective magic mattered first during childbirth under attack and later when the family fled the collapsing Elhandar station.[^lyrielle-intro]

## The four deliveries and the breached ship

During the rescue voyage, Lyrielle brought Nash and Angelashi to the medical bay and helped organize care for Angelashi's extraordinary pregnancy. An examination suggested forms resembling a fork, boot and smoking pipe; a proposed pathology scan was not shown being completed. Lyrielle tried *Animate Objects* on the apparent objects, but the GM ruled it unsuccessful. The crew faced a pirate assault and a breached hull while the deliveries continued. Lyrielle cast *Wall of Force* across the breach, keeping the medical group from being swept away, and later arranged for a barricade at the blast doors to be partly cleared so the doors could close.[^lyrielle-birth]

She assisted the births of four infants: three first appeared as a fork, a pipe and a boot, and the fourth as a baby. As the apparent objects changed into living babies, Angelashi revealed her changeling appearance. Lyrielle's work was part of a dangerous but successful rescue, not evidence that her failed animation spell created the children. She was a new companion to the family, **not** Squirts returned from death or another incarnation of Hope.[^lyrielle-babies]

## E114 cutoff and open checks (historical)

By the end of the voyage's first played session, Lyrielle, Angelashi and all four living babies remained aboard a ship still heading toward Elhandar. A different crew member, Doctor Evany/Vani/Avigne (name uncertain), was swept into space during the fighting; that event must not be assigned to Lyrielle. The children's later individual name-and-form descriptions also belong to subsequent scenes.[^lyrielle-e114]

## E115 — cottage guide, Acorn and remembered seafoam

After the ship reached Elhandar, Lyrielle served as a guide and host at the forest-biodome cottage. She helped care for Angelashi, Nash, the children and their companions through four days of recovery. She cooked, cleaned, mended, used *Mage Hand* and *Prestidigitation*, and lit the memorial flame for Squirts with *Fire Bolt*. Her white mink familiar was called **Acorn**. The station offered therapy, but Lyrielle was not shown formally conducting a therapy session.[^lyrielle-cottage]

Lyrielle spoke of having once been a mermaid, bargaining with a sea witch, turning into seafoam and returning through children's prayers. This was **her own account**, not an independently played earlier life or a demonstrated law of resurrection. She also knew of a permanent teleportation circle in the station's science-and-magic district, but did not provide its sigils or transport the family through it then.[^lyrielle-memories]

## E124 — guide to the core and reception caretaker

When Stanley and the others prepared to approach the High Elf, Lyrielle agreed to accompany the family after hearing a limited warning of danger. She guided them along Rainbow Road to the central core, soothed Vorel with magically produced harp music in the lift and helped clean up after his sickness. Lyrielle stayed with Angelashi and the children in reception while **the four adventurers entered the private audience**. Her presence on the journey did not give her knowledge of everything said inside.[^lyrielle-core]

## E125 — family evacuation and protective spell

As mercenaries attacked Elhandar, Lyrielle remained beside the household. The party escaped a falling spire elevator, where she used *Mage Hand* to steady Vorel while he worked on an exit. She then **piloted one of three noble speeders**, carrying Nash, Angelashi and their four children. When huge pieces of the biodome fell, she cast a real *Wall of Force* canopy to protect the vehicles and help them reach Villa Or'Nath. The household sheltered in Stanley's father's hidden chamber and braced for impact; the episode ended before their condition afterward was shown.[^lyrielle-escape]

## E128 — survives station and departs with household

The next played resolution established that Lyrielle survived the impact inside Villa Or'Nath with Angelashi and the children. After the mercenary's defeat, she helped the family and other survivors board Elhandari transports. The ships **actually departed bound for the Mars terraforming station**. Lyrielle did not remain on Earth with the four principal adventurers; neither the landing at Mars nor any later medical work was shown in that departure scene.[^lyrielle-departure]

## September 2020 bonus — princess, wizard and name inspiration

In a retrospective conversation, Kristen described developing a bookish, Disney-princess-like wizard and a name inspired by fantasy novels and *The Little Mermaid*. This helps explain the portrayal, but does not settle Lyrielle's remembered mermaid adventures or establish exact spellings of a surname or literary references.[^lyrielle-bonus]

## S3E019 Round Up — princess or well-read imaginative elf remains deliberately undecided

In the later cast roundtable, Jon asked whether Lyrielle had truly been a princess or had imagined a grand history from stories she read. Beau said he first conceived her as a real princess but came to like the alternative and **never chose between them**. Her royal ancestry and the truth behind her memories therefore remain deliberately undecided. This retrospective discussion is not a new event in the fictional world.[^lyrielle-princess]

## Current status

**Lyrielle's last directly established state is alive and departing Elhandar aboard an Elhandari vessel bound for Mars in E128.** She remained with Angelashi and Nash's children rather than the adventuring party on Earth. Her ultimate arrival, later life, full name spelling, supposed royal birth and remembered mermaid past are not settled by these played scenes.[^lyrielle-status]
