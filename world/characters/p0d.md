# P0D

**Other recognized forms:** `Pod`, `P0d`, `P0D`; spelling/capitalization remains inconsistent across source text and recaps. **Identity:** Pixie companion, with an identifying number, close to Nash. **Status:** **Killed by Diontalus in E050**. **Spoilers:** E031–E054.

P0D becomes known to the group in the poor districts of **Trashburg** during E031. She speaks about the captivity and harvesting of pixies to manufacture **Pixie Blush**, making the city's hidden economy personal to Nash. The party shelters and relies on her, including for scouting. Her firsthand account is significant, but it does not mean every claimed factory detail is independently witnessed.

P0D follows the adventurers into the underground route and helps investigate forgotten chambers. In E049, **Monsoon issues an order over radio (identified as Order 26)**, and she unwillingly enlarges into a dangerous, distressed giant. She fights during the ambush, but **this is not a voluntary betrayal**. Nash tries to calm/save her; **Diontalus deliberately attacks and kills her during E050**, where her form bursts into pixie dust. No intact ordinary body remains. The other huge pixies encountered in E051 and later are **not evidence of her survival**.

Her death worsens the relationship between Nash, Stanley, Diontalus and Vorel. Vorel later proposes the **Pillars of Decency (POD)** partly as a memorial. The Year One Q&A adds that the GM **had prepared an unchosen route to try rescuing pixies**, and that Order 26 was part of Monsoon's planned assault; **this does not create a successful rescue or undo her death**.

## Spirit encounter after death — E056

During the **ancestor rite beneath Tietkalla**, **P0D appears in ethereal form and talks to Nash**, inviting him to play and mentioning a **winged Rory/Vorel-like figure**. Nash does **not** follow her. **This is an in-session spirit manifestation, not the reappearance of a living pixie**; the killing in E050 and the later supernatural encounter remain different facts.
