# Cerinthala / Saranthala — withered elf in the data center

## Introduction

Cerinthala was a withered, elderly-looking elf whom the adventurers found living among frozen servers and preserved bodies beneath [CHIDS](../places/chids.md). She offered [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) a way through the facility in exchange for help reaching the distant Elhandari home she longed for. Her lonely hospitality concealed a cursed amulet, a willingness to prey on visitors and an allegiance she eventually revealed through violence. Although the group witnessed her death in E074, **she returned to life in E075, betrayed them and escaped**. The maintained spelling *Cerinthala* remains provisional; her age, origins and knowledge of ancient experiments remain partly her own testimony.[^cerinthala-intro]

## The frozen server room (E072)

The companions encountered a short, deeply wrinkled elf with gray hair, few teeth, a lazy eye and many layers of winter clothing. Her improvised shelter stood within a vast frozen data center beside a fire, a small garden and blocks of ice containing four human bodies she called **Toby, Carl, Gordon and Benson**. She spoke to them as companions, although none was independently heard answering at first. She claimed to have survived since before the Scorching and to be unable to find rest until she returned to Elhandar. Neither that antiquity nor her account of elven death was yet independently verified.[^cerinthala-frozen]

She asked Stanley to help her leave Earth; he promised to do **all he reasonably could**, without guaranteeing an immediate escape or abandoning Sira's rescue. She gave him a wooden totem amulet that the GM said granted **+1 AC**, and he put it on. She admitted having sealed the engineering passage at a mind flayer's request in exchange for supposed freedom, then unlocked it with a data pad for the companions. She suggested possible spacecraft at Gordon Shuttle Bay, without establishing that any could still fly.[^cerinthala-promise]

Her stories about lizardfolk made as anti-dragon soldiers, tiefling creation, blood-drinking experiments, sound-sensitive crystals and an ancient official called “Principal Dombrovski” were **attributed accounts**, not established genealogy or proof that Dombrovski was the later Principal. She treated lizardfolk and tieflings with open prejudice. After the party rested, giant ice forms containing bodies rose from her camp, seized Stanley and attacked. His new amulet began choking him; E072 ended while the attack continued.[^cerinthala-claims]

## Confession, curse and escape through laboratories (E073)

Hope freed Stanley from an ice captor, while Vorel's fire and Nash's *Fireball* broke the attacking constructs. Several frozen corpses emerged as animated, hostile “ice boyfriends”; their individual identities cannot be assigned automatically to all four men in Cerinthala's camp. **Toby continued speaking**, manifesting an icy mouth, and explained that the companions feared their keeper would leave them. Cerinthala said she had not deliberately ordered their attack, but admitted that she **had originally considered consuming or using the adventurers** before believing they might take her to Elhandar.[^cerinthala-companions]

She also confessed that Stanley's amulet was **a cursed bond of her own design**. She described it as reacting to his distrust or opposition, and asserted that it could not be removed; Stanley's attempted cut caused **25 lightning damage**. Her full curse mechanics and any means of release were not independently tested. The amulet remained attached, with the previously awarded AC benefit. Stanley insisted that rescuing Sira and stopping the illithid came before Cerinthala's departure.[^cerinthala-curse]

Cerinthala accompanied the group through her greenhouse into maintenance corridors that Vorel opened by spending hours melting and removing ice. They entered laboratory rooms with large vessels and responsive pink-purple tissue; Nash touched the growth, which broke his staff. Cerinthala characterized the rooms as centers of ancient engineered life. She described an “infernal wave” reproductive device and offered to create a child for Vorel; **he refused**, and no pregnancy or offspring resulted. The companions reached a lift bank, where one elevator fell and another stalled, leaving them facing an approaching giant spider. Cerinthala was still with them, not abandoned in the frozen server hall.[^cerinthala-labs]

## Death, return, betrayal and disappearance (E074–E075)

### E074 — death inside the living corridor

The party escaped the stalled lift during an attack by brain-marked spiders. Vorel made Cerinthala a shield from the dead giant spider's carapace. In a lower laboratory corridor, a responsive pink membrane permitted several companions to pass but barred Cerinthala and Nash. Nash succeeded in passing after singing. Cerinthala's own attempted song failed, and **the membrane closed around and killed her**. The party checked her unresponsive body, found **no pulse or signs of life**, and carried it on the spider shield. An oily black residue irritated those touching her; Nash tasted it and developed a tongue reaction. At **E074's actual cutoff she was dead**, not merely separated from the party.[^cerinthala-death]

### E075 — the employee identity and return

While the companions explored further CHIDS control rooms, they searched Cerinthala's layers of clothing and found a **translucent blue gem** and her **employee ID card with a youthful red-haired photograph**. The ID supported an association with CHIDS but did not authenticate every history she told. Later in the secure laboratory sequence, **Cerinthala visibly regained life**. This reverses the physical death status established at E074, **without proving that all ancient elves revive after death** or revealing her exact mechanism.[^cerinthala-revival]

### E075 — Toby's final confrontation and Cerinthala's escape

The revived elf called for **Toby**; the blue gem recovered from her clothing became or released an ice construct inside the secure chamber. She **cast *Hold Person* on Hope** and claimed an illithid master wished the adventurers kept alive. This was a direct betrayal, not merely a report of an old temptation. The group destroyed Toby's ice casing and his reanimated corpse, while Cerinthala retreated behind a ***Wall of Ice***. Stanley's ***Dispel Magic*** ended the barrier, but the pursuit failed: greasy tracks and icy residue ended near the earlier organic passage. **Cerinthala was alive when last seen escaping**; no further route, recapture, death or teleport was witnessed.[^cerinthala-betrayal]

## Knowledge limits and continuity safeguards

Her apparent ancient memories and claimed genealogy of the races remain attributed testimony. The observed laboratory growths, employee ID and unusual creatures support the existence of old biological experiments, **not** each detail of her account. Her claims about Elhandari immortality remain distinct from the single observed return to life. Toby's later destruction does not establish the fates of Carl, Gordon or Benson. The cursed +1 AC necklace **was last documented on Stanley**, with no confirmed removal or tested long-distance control after Cerinthala fled. Her name also appears in processed transcripts as *Cerenthala, Saranthala* and *Ceranthalis*, none confirmed by original audio or creator-written evidence.[^cerinthala-limits]

## Current status

**Cerinthala's last directly witnessed state is alive and escaping the CHIDS party in E075**, after dying in the living corridor in E074 and reviving in the control-room sequence. Her destination, allegiance beyond her claimed illithid orders, immortality and later fate remain unknown. Stanley's best-effort promise was neither an agreement to sacrifice Sira nor proof that Cerinthala reached Elhandar.[^cerinthala-status]
