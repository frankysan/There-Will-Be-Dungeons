# Cerinthala / Saranthala — withered elf in the data center

> **Working display name: Cerinthala**, with processed-ASR variants `Saranthala`, `Ceranthalis`, `Saranthalis`. **Exact spelling has not been audio verified or supported by a creator-written source.** First heard in [E072](../../episodes/E072.md), accompanies the party in [E073](../../episodes/E073.md). Spoilers through E073.

## The frozen server room (E072)

A short, deeply **withered, wrinkled elf woman** with a lazy eye, gray hair, few teeth, and **many layers of winter clothes** lives in CHIDS's enormous frozen **data-center server hall**. She has created an improvised camp sheltered by canvas from ~60-foot cooling fans, with a fire pit, jars, and a small **Malta/Malterweed hydroponic garden**. She speaks to **four preserved human bodies** in blocks of ice she identifies as **Toby, Carl, Gordon and Benson**. In E072 the party initially sees no independent activity from the frozen people, and assumes she is talking to corpses; subsequent E073 events change that assessment for **Toby at least**.

She says she has remained here since the pre-Scorching period, wants to leave Earth, and **cannot die normally or achieve rest unless returned to Elhandar/Elandar**, her people's home among the stars. She asks **Stanley**, another elf-descended person, to help her return. He promises **to do all in his power**, rather than guaranteeing success or delaying the rescue of **Sira**. In exchange she gives him a **wooden-totem amulet** granting **+1 AC at the table**. He **actually puts it on**. She says a mind flayer persuaded her to block the **engineering access**, offering release; she uses a **data pad to unlock that route** when the party promises aid. She suggests **Gordon Shuttle Bay has spacefaring ships** but provides **no proof the craft are intact, operable or still able to reach Elhandar**.

Her recollections include **anti-dragon genetic experiments on lizardfolk, tiefling creation by human/demon gene manipulation, dangerously reactive organic purple crystals in Laboratory A and an old official named “Principal Dombrovski.”** Each is **attributed antiquarian testimony**, not a verified genetic/cosmological fact. She makes openly **prejudiced comments about lizardfolk as pets/servants and tieflings as enslaved demon products**. Her interest in Stanley and conversations with Hope reveal personal loneliness; Hope tastes a **surprisingly palatable soup** after observing the elf put **metal scraps** into the cauldron. None of this confirms Cerinthala's benign intentions.

At E072's end the camp's frozen humans appear embedded inside **giant ice creatures** that assault the party after the long rest. One abducts Stanley and his **necklace begins choking him**, seemingly related to the elf's binding.

## Confession, curse and escape through laboratories (E073)

Several large ice forms shatter under **Vorel's Eye of Groxl fire** and **Nash's careful Fireball**, releasing animated, blue-eyed corpses nicknamed **“ice boyfriends.”** **Hope frees Stanley** by severing a captor's arm, and the remaining attackers are defeated or flee through ice. **Toby** continues speaking via **an icy mouth/head**. When Cerinthala arrives, **Stanley's necklace loosens**. Toby says the preserved companions attacked because **they feared she would leave them behind**, and Cerinthala says she **did not intentionally order this attack**. This admission is supported by Toby's words, but **does not establish her complete innocence**.

Cerinthala acknowledges she originally planned **to eat/use the party as prey or replacement bodies** before their apparent ability to return her to Elhandar persuaded her otherwise. She further admits that **Stanley's amulet is a cursed object of her own design**, not a standard ancient elf promise necklace. It reacts to **the wearer mistrusting or turning against her**, rather than a strictly objective legal breach of his original promise; she claims the necklace **cannot be removed/broken**, but **its full rules and removal methods have not been independently tested**. When Stanley tries to cut it, it inflicts **25 lightning damage**. **By E073 cutoff the necklace remains worn**, still with its previously stated +1 AC.

She tells additional disturbing stories about **engineered anti-dragon lizardfolk soldiers**, failed **blood-drinking “vampire” variants**, and urges Stanley and others to treat lizardfolk differently; all remain **witness testimony**. Under threat, she agrees that **the rescue of Sira and stopping the illithid take precedence** over her desire for immediate departure.

She leads the party through **garden and locker rooms** above a maintenance shaft. **Vorel spends several hours burning and hauling ice** to open a service tunnel; Cerinthala **travels with the party**, through red-lit cable corridors, to ancient laboratory rooms containing **large cylinders and living pink-purple membrane**, then down towards a multi-elevator bay. She says this is where **lizardfolk were manufactured** and recounts an **“infernal wave” technology** supposedly enabling a full pregnancy in one day. She **offers to make Vorel an offspring from a fertilized egg**, but **he explicitly refuses**. None is created. She also claims **more exotic weaponry** might be available deeper in the labs.

One arriving elevator **crashes**. The party takes another, which **halts between floors**. They escape to its roof as a **large spider-like creature descends** in the dark shaft. **Cerinthala's own exact position on the roof/interior at cliffhanger needs audio/map review**; she has not been abandoned in the server room.

## Knowledge limits and continuity safeguards

- **Name:** No canon spelling yet. `Cerinthala` is editorial convenience, not verification.
- **Toby, Carl, Gordon, Benson:** Frozen men **were used in/connected to animated ice attackers**. Only **Toby's ongoing awareness/speech** is explicitly demonstrated after the fight; do not invent the individual deaths, assignments or survival of every named body.
- **Promise:** Stanley **never promised to succeed at all costs** or abandon Sira. The **self-made curse** reacts to his thoughts/intention as Cerinthala describes, but no verified mechanism forremoval is known.
- **Ancient origin stories:** Human/demon experiments, elven afterlife, tiefling/lizardfolk genealogy, sound-reactive crystals and the **“Principal Dombrovski”** name should **not be presented as indisputable narration**. The **pink membrane and laboratory vessels** are direct observations, strengthening evidence of biological experimentation in general but not proving each specific claim.
- **Purpose:** She is **a coerced/unreliable guide with her own urgent agenda**, not straightforwardly a friend, creator, combat villain or benevolent spirit.
