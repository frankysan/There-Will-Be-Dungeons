# Brett / Brent — Asmodeus, infernal host and contract broker

**Identity:** Same recurring **HELL CASINO HOST/BARGAINING GOD** variously spoken/transcribed **Brett, Brent, Brant, sometimes Brett/Brent interchangeably**; the PRINCIPLE **DIRECTLY ADDRESSES him as “ASMODEUS”** in [played Wastes S3E12](../../episodes/S3E12.md). Does **NOT** refer to the earlier killed WEIR **SOULBROKER CATOBLEPRITH**, the PRINCIPLE, or the unnamed HOODED VOICE. His absolute theology/omnipotence and claims of owning souls should be kept as **interested divine character statements** rather than independently demonstrated universal rules. E101–E102 and E106 include Hope's earlier HELLSCAPE visits and deals; [Kaiser Casino](../places/kaiser-casino.md) is the longstanding location page; **S3E12/S3E13 do NOT newly announce exact formal casino-site name**. Character chronology and comparison evidence: [S3E12 P/Q](../../reports/WASTES_S3E12_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md), [S3E13 P/Q](../../reports/WASTES_S3E13_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md). **Audio unverified**.

## S3E12 — physically takes the Hand of the Voice, then offers unsigned partnership

He **ACTUALLY ARRIVES in HELL'S CHARRED-STONE SOUL/SLAB ROOM in a white dinner jacket with rose**, accompanied by a female demon/succubus. **PRINCIPLE ACTUALLY PRODUCES THE STOLEN HAND OF THE VOICE AND PASSES IT TO BRETT; BRETT ACTUALLY PASSES IT TO THE FEMALE ASSISTANT WHO STORES IT IN A POUCH**. **THE MISSING EYE OF THE VOICE remains UNDELIVERED, and is NOT VOREL'S EYE OF GROXL**. Brett asserts he **LOANED the engineered DRAGON-METAL HEART** to Principle to create Nash and also furnished knowledge/lich-like help, demands it returned. PRINCIPLE proposes executing Nash and consigning another companion to Hell as interim payment; **STANLEY'S PERSUASION31+35 ACTUALLY CONVINCES BRETT TO CHANGE HIS POSITION**, preventing those sacrifices. **BRETT ACTUALLY SNAPS PRINCIPLE OUT OF SIGHT** (destination UNKNOWN, no death shown), releases several captives, and transports four to HELL VIP CASINO, releasing NASH after further discussion. He proposes Nash's heart, the missing Voice Eye, and killing Principle in exchange for release/potential Earth cure, but **NO NEW CONTRACT SIGNED in S3E12, no heart taken yet**.

## S3E13 — barkeep disguise, Nash's heart ACTUALLY taken, four sent back to Earth

In [played S3E13](../../episodes/S3E13.md) ~01:20–01:40, after the group wait at HELL BAR hours, the bartender **ACTUALLY REVEALS HIMSELF AS BRETT**, having personally observed the four failing to succumb to luxury/gluttony/violence. His kitchen served what was presented as TARRASQUE STEAK but STANLEY ***DISPEL MAGIC* REVEALS FALSE MEAT AS HUMAN INTESTINES/BATS/REFUSE**, then replacement unaffected by second *Dispel Magic*; this is in-world Hell hospitality, not Tarrasque harvest. Brett again asks for alliances involving EYE OF THE VOICE and PRINCIPLE; **THE FOUR DO NOT SIGN A PACT OR AGREE TO RETRIEVE THE EYE**, and Hope refuses to become his lieutenant. He agrees to restore Nash's normal heart: **PHYSICALLY SNAPS NASH'S DRAGON-METAL ENGINE OUT THROUGH HIS CHEST**, PLAYER NASH EXPRESSLY **CHOOSES NONRESISTANCE**, **BRETT PHYSICALLY TAKES ENGINE AND MAGICALLY FORMS A NEW BEATING HEART FROM NASH'S OWN FLESH**, closes wound. **BRETT ACTUALLY TELEPORTS ALL FOUR DIRECTLY TO EARTH BY HIS OWN FINGER SNAP** (NOT using Hope's 0-charge Hollow Ring), with NASH ALIVE, *FIREBOLT* and floating still functioning, dragon form untested. **CURRENT OBSERVED PHYSICAL CUSTODY: BRETT HAS DRAGON METAL HEART; BRETT'S FEMALE ASSISTANT HAS HAND OF VOICE IN POUCH from S3E12.** EYE remains missing. **No "new signed partnership" is executed**, and BRETT threatens to recruit other agents, perhaps PRINCIPLE, with no reunion shown. His claim he could cure Earth with gods' power is NOT executed; Worldbreaker grows in northern Earth as four later witness. [S3E13 dossier](../../episodes/S3E13.md).
