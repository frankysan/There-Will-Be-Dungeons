# Brett (Asmodeus) — infernal casino host

## Introduction

Brett, also heard as Brent or Brant, was an urbane infernal casino host who offered [Hope Akmenos](hope-akmenos.md) hospitality, gifts and bargains after her separation from the other adventurers. He presented himself as **Asmodeus**; the [Principal](the-principal.md) later addressed him by that name in person. His demonstrated control over his Hell establishment and several extraordinary acts was real within the played story, but his claims about divine rank, ancient debts and the ownership of souls remain those of an interested participant.[^brett-identity]

## Hope's visit to the casino

After Hope gained the [Hand of the Voice](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md) and left her companions, she accepted an invitation to the [Kaiser Casino](../places/kaiser-casino.md). There Brett privately introduced himself as Asmodeus, asked about her ambitions and warned that the hooded patron behind her Hand had plans of its own. Hope declined a formal pact. On the next visit he tested her judgment through a coercive encounter involving the goblin Pumpo, then gave her a shape-changing appearance necklace and a powerful travel ring. The first offered ring was rejected; she accepted a second, rougher ring and demonstrated one of its travel effects.[^brett-hope]

Brett also introduced Hope to three paid companions—[Steerious Business](steerious-business.md), [Blood Skunk Hendrickson](blood-skunk-hendrickson.md) and [Doctor Spider](dr-spider.md)—while admitting that they reported to him. Hope led them through a portal to T'ietkalla, but their employment did not amount to a proved lifelong allegiance or an unrestricted contract. Brett's stories about his own past, the imprisoned goblin's bargain and the Voice's ultimate designs were not independently established.[^brett-company]

## The Hand, the missing Eye and Nash's heart

When the adventurers were brought before Brett in a Hell chamber in **S3E12**, the Principal physically produced the stolen Hand of the Voice. Brett received it and passed it to his female demonic assistant, who placed it in her pouch. He demanded a second object, the **Eye of the Voice**, which was **not** delivered. This missing Eye was separate from [Vorel's Eye of Groxl](../technology/eye-of-groxl.md). Brett also claimed that he had lent the Principal the engineered dragon-metal heart used in Nash's creation. That earlier arrangement was Brett's account, while the Hand transfer was directly witnessed.[^brett-hand]

The Principal proposed killing Nash and offering another captive in payment. Stanley's arguments convinced Brett to reject immediate sacrifices; he snapped the Principal out of sight, released the prisoners into casino hospitality and continued negotiations. He proposed further work involving the Eye and the Principal in exchange for escape or help healing Earth. **No new agreement was signed**, the Principal's destination was unknown, and neither the Eye nor Nash's heart had yet changed hands in S3E12.[^brett-negotiation]

In **S3E13**, Brett revealed that he had been disguised as the casino bartender during the party's long wait. He again offered arrangements that the adventurers did not accept. Nash nevertheless chose not to resist the immediate heart exchange. Brett **removed the engineered dragon-metal heart from Nash's chest**, held it himself, formed a beating replacement from Nash's **own flesh**, and closed the wound. Nash lived; his *Fire Bolt* and levitation still functioned, though a post-removal dragon transformation was not tested. Brett then sent all four adventurers directly back to Earth by his own magic—not by Hope's spent Hollow Ring.[^brett-heart]

## A soul collected after the Principal's defeat

Near the end of **S3E17**, after the Worldbreaker threat was banished and the Principal's active chrome bodies collapsed, a red-and-black portal opened. A richly adorned, goat-featured devil emerged who **strongly resembled** Brett, perhaps in a different form. Nash did not personally recognize him as Brett, and the scene stopped short of an unqualified identity declaration. The visitor drew purple-green soul-energy from the Principal's corpses into a single sphere, declared the debt due, spoke of restoring the depleted world and departed through his own portal. The collection was witnessed; the precise contract, the visitor's identity and the promised means of healing Earth were not definitively proved.[^brett-soul]

## Current status

**Brett was last unequivocally seen taking Nash's dragon-metal heart and returning the four companions to Earth in S3E13.** The S3E17 soul-collector resembled him strongly and collected the defeated Principal's soul-energy before leaving, but remains an identity-qualified appearance. The Voice's Hand was last seen in Brett's assistant's pouch, its separate Eye remained missing, and no new four-hero pact or completed planetary restoration was demonstrated.[^brett-status]
