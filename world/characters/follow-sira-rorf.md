# Follow, Sira and Rorf — three travelers seeking refuge

## Introduction

Follow, Sira and Rorf traveled together after the destruction of a settlement they called Home. To strangers they appeared an unlikely family: a legless telepath who moved on a wheeled cart, a quiet young girl able to vanish from sight, and an enormous orc capable, by his companions' account, of taking other shapes. Follow called Sira his daughter; their exact blood relationship was never independently established. What united them was the danger they had survived and their determination to find safety.[^origins]

## Captivity, escape and a troubled bargain

Stanley first encountered Follow imprisoned among the Sun's Witnesses near Razak Val. Follow spoke of his traveling troupe, then displayed the ability to communicate within others' minds. Sira appeared and vanished near Stanley, while Rorf later took control of Fate 4 to carry the reunited group away. Follow said that gobbear attackers had destroyed Home and lizardfolk hunters had killed or captured survivors they considered mutants. He had been held for Orp's benefit, while his companions plotted his release.[^origins]

Their methods had harmed others. Rorf confessed to killing Bramie Fengar; Follow described a scheme to remove heirs so rivals could challenge Orp for power. These admissions implicated the adults, but did not establish that Sira personally committed the confessed killing or that every allegation was a witnessed event. Hope offered the three sanctuary in T'ietkalla, and Follow accepted the promise. They never reached that refuge.[^escape]

## Sira and Stanley

Sira bore scars where her pointed ears had been cut. Follow attributed the mutilation to former human guardians who had rejected her, an account for which the companions had no independent record. Stanley spoke to her in Elvish and found that she understood him. Though Follow said she barely spoke, she eventually asked Stanley about songs, and his remembered melody brought her comfort during the storm. Her invisibility and her silence did not make her an interchangeable participant in the adults' bargains or violence.[^sira]

An earlier child seen in one of Nash's visions resembled her enough to invite comparison. A later out-of-game discussion did not establish that they were the same person.[^sira]

## Abduction and the two deaths

While the companions sheltered from the storm, Sira disappeared. A floating illithid, speaking as a servant of Dollo Poppio, held her aloft and used her to threaten Hope. Rorf changed into a large bird and attacked, but was violently crushed and killed. Follow rose toward his daughter and was hurled down with mortal injuries. Before he died he begged Stanley to rescue Sira. Stanley promised, and the companions buried Follow. The illithid departed with the girl still alive.[^abduction]

## Current status

Follow and Rorf remained dead, with no witnessed revival. Sira remained captive through the journey into Khaervilon and CHIDS, but was eventually seen in the Traversal Drive's central beam. With Quizquaz's help she escaped, briefly invisible, and appeared alive outside beside Eddie. She thanked Stanley before the four companions made their own sacrifice; Eddie promised to protect her. Her later destination, safety and guardianship are not established, and the promised sanctuary in T'ietkalla was never shown to be fulfilled.[^rescue]
