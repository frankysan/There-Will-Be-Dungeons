# Nash Maggard

**Portrayed by:** Scott. **Identity:** Wasteland adventurer, spellcaster and bearer of an implanted **dragon-metal engine heart**, capable of black-dragon transformation. **Spoilers:** Played *Wastes* through E118 and retrospective cast Q&As. **Evidence:** Processed ASR and episode dossiers, not independent audio verification.

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [Solar Mines](../places/solar-mines.md) · [Spellcasting phrases](nash-spellcasting-phrases.md) · [World portal](../README.md).

## Life, memories and magical body

Nash introduces himself as a survivor of the **Solar Mines/Blood Canyons**, traumatized by coerced industrial labor, betrayal and the apparent destruction of his old life. His recollections and later histories sometimes conflict. The **implanted metal heart** is a real part of his body and magic, but his alleged earlier betrayer, biological origin and the sequence of implantation are **not fully reconstructed** from direct events. He begins in Dusthill, helps restore its old waterworks, and builds close relationships with **Hope, Stanley and Vorel**. [E000](../../episodes/E000.md) · [E001](../../episodes/E001.md) · [E006](../../episodes/E006.md) · [E024](../../episodes/E024.md).

His metal heart enables extraordinary magical output and **transformations into a black dragon wyrmling and then a young black dragon**. These forms consume effort and have practical costs. He also has unusual bodily changes, such as a smaller additional hand, and later wears a finite **Robe of Useful Items**. Recovered components and individual robe patches should not be assumed permanently present after their source episodes show use. In a post-arc Q&A, Beau explains he conceived the **heart** after Scott improvised uncertainty about Nash having a normal one: *production history*, not a new character memory. [Château Weir](../places/chateau-weir.md) · [June 2019 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md) · [E048](../../episodes/E048.md)–[E053](../../episodes/E053.md).

## The original party and Tietkalla (E001–E059)

Nash becomes a protective and sometimes reckless friend to the company, repeatedly using spontaneous spells and eccentric incantations. **P0D**, a pixie who exposes abuse connected with Pixie Blush, becomes especially important to him. **Diontalus kills her in E050**, despite Nash's efforts to save her; Nash's grief is distinct from the acting entity's cruelty and Stanley's attempts to repair the harm. [P0D](p0d.md) · [Diontalus](diontalus.md) · [E049](../../episodes/E049.md)–[E050](../../episodes/E050.md).

His relationship with **Angelashi** develops through the second arc. When others report her dead in E048, this is not a witnessed death; **she is freed alive in E054**. During the **three-month jump before E055**, Angelashi is pregnant, and Nash's anxieties about family and his old home return through nightmares. An apparent E055 encounter with P0D is **a dream**, not her resurrection. He protects Tietkalla citizens from a deadly stonefall. [Angelashi](angelashi.md) · [E054](../../episodes/E054.md)–[E056](../../episodes/E056.md).

## Razak Val, alternate selves and the Drive (E057–E081)

While investigating the stonefall, Nash fights the **Wormfather** near an anomalous green crater in dragon form; the expedition later finds a brain parasite. He encounters apparently **future versions of Nash** who request a **Fengar egg** at Razak Val. Nash helps them steal it, but their claims of future origin and the rules of their “time barf” travel remain **claims**; this is not proof of a solved deterministic loop. The subsequent **Sun's Witnesses transformation crisis** and flight from Razak Val bring real losses, and in **E066 a T. rex destroys Hope's Fate 4** despite the party's spells. [E057](../../episodes/E057.md)–[E067](../../episodes/E067.md) · [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md).

At **Kervalon/CHIDS**, Nash uses terrain-altering spells, protective illusions, Fireball, dragon form and improvised devices. The first CHIDS funicular crash is a real physical event, not just an office simulation. A shared magical **cooling/flying garment** changes hands and is not an unlimited supply of equipment. The companions discover **a portable red/green portal device**, which Nash recognizes by analogy to an entertainment game; it is **not** the larger Traversal Drive. The living hive's apparent “future Nash” and cosmic warning remain potentially deceptive testimony. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md)–[E078](../../episodes/E078.md) · [CHIDS](../places/chids.md) · [Portal device](../technology/portal-device.md).

In **E078**, Nash receives a **Ring of Spell Storing** and loads *Chromatic Orbs*; he assists Vorel's actual limb reattachment. The group physically finds the **Traversal Drive E079**. **Nash kills the great illithid commander in E080**, while Hope destroys a manifested **Dolo Popio** in E081. Instead of sacrificing Earth, the four original friends **share the crimson beam**, vanishing into white light and being **GM-declared dead at E081 cutoff**. **Sira escapes alive with Eddie**; the planet's lasting condition is not fully observed. [E078](../../episodes/E078.md)–[E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [Drive crisis](../HISTORY_CHIDS_DRIVE.md).

## Sigil, regeneration and the White Tower (E082–E101)

**E082** reveals Nash's consciousness alive in **Sigil** as a **small eye protruding from his metal heart**, without his ordinary body. His gradual **rebuilding of hands, limbs and other anatomy** through subsequent episodes is physical development, not instantaneous restoration at E082. In **E089**, he has regained an ordinary mobile body; his personal account of the Solar Mines remains a report rather than a scene played in those earlier years. Beau's January 2020 bonus remarks anticipate **self-regeneration**, but those comments alone did not accomplish the process. [E082](../../episodes/E082.md)–[E089](../../episodes/E089.md) · [January 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-01.md).

Nash helps the party's Sigil investigations, battles and route planning. In E093 he **voluntarily becomes a young black dragon** while the **Traveler's Twin** crosses the Outlands. He and his companions enter the besieged **White Tower**; the danger includes a plague, a secured Fist relic and a growing moon. A later battle temporarily conjoins Nash's dragon body with a separate demonic body, but **the forms separate in E100**, so he does not permanently retain that shared physiology. The tower's plague is **actually cured in E096** by Walnut/Vorel's ritual. [E093](../../episodes/E093.md)–[E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [White Tower](../places/white-tower.md).

**E101:** Nash survives the last siege, aids Vorel, witnesses Hope replace her own hand with the **Hand of the Voice**, and remains behind when she **leaves the original party**. Nash later works with Stanley and Vorel to **re-seal the separate Zorander's Fist gauntlet**; he removes a medallion key and returns it to Stanley. This is the **Arc Four/Arc Five transition within E101**, not Hope's permanent death. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [Hand of the Voice](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md).

## Separate paths and family reunion (E102–E118)

A **wild-magic-created, city-bearing Tungan moon** becomes a threat to the Outlands; Nash's attempts to reverse or outrun it are not automatically successful. **Squirts's Divine Intervention E104 removes the moon and attacking Tungan ships**. Orcs mistakenly credit Nash, acclaim him **Captain Dragon Lord** and rename their ship **The Great Angelashi** at his instruction. A wrongly executed fruit-portal ritual then **destroys that main ship**; the company survives aboard a smaller vessel. In E105 they reach **Sigil** and Nash receives a **100,000-gold liability claim**, not a paid debt. [E102](../../episodes/E102.md)–[E105](../../episodes/E105.md).

After the party helps deliver the **Fist gauntlet to Tobacho in E106**, an indirect route takes them to an **alternate Nash's asteroid tower**. Its host, **First Nash**, claims lost companions of **his** timeline and later calls the current Nash “Junior”; those memorials are **not deaths of today's party**. Nash uses an unusual vision in E110 to see pregnant Angelashi escaping danger at Tietkalla, but this is **not yet a physical reunion**. He then transports the party to the keep. [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [E110](../../episodes/E110.md).

**E111** brings the actual **reunion with Angelashi at Golden Sands**. Nash sketches a *Teleportation Circle* for a possible getaway, **without casting it** when Elhandari help arrives. During the battle with **General Carthax**, Squirts and Melgor die, while the family and other survivors board an Elhandari ship. In **E114**, Angelashi reveals **four living changeling children**—two boys and two girls—and speaks privately about Nash's own ancestry, which remains **her account** rather than a verified origin. Undead attackers disrupt the voyage, but the rescuers escape and **reach Elhandar E115**. [E111](../../episodes/E111.md) · [E114](../../episodes/E114.md) · [E115](../../episodes/E115.md).

At the end of **E116**, Hope **uses Gate to summon Nash physically into Dr. Spider's laboratory**. The arrival is real, not merely an imagined afterlife reunion. Nash is startled and asks questions; **Hope accompanies him back**, and in **E117 all four protagonists reunite at Elhandar**. Nash introduces Hope to his family, argues against violence in the dispute over her past conduct, and publicly recounts the Solar Mines uprising during the refugees' ceremony. This is a *present-day oral account*, not an enacted ancient flashback. [E116](../../episodes/E116.md) · [E117](../../episodes/E117.md).

In **E118**, Nash joins the cottage dinner and is among the group when severely injured **Omnom** confronts Hope; **Stanley's Hold Person**, not a blow by Nash, stops the attack. After the cottage-key investigation Nash and Vorel remain outside when **Hope and Stanley** enter the hidden study. The subsequent father's journal and Mask belong to **Stanley's E118 inventory**, not Nash's. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md).

## Family, possessions, spellcasting and disputed chronology

**Angelashi** is Nash's surviving partner, and their **four changeling children are physically seen**, not a child solely forecast in earlier Q&As. **P0D** and the original **Tedna** died in Arc Two; alternate versions at First Nash's tower do **not** restore those individuals. **Hope, Stanley and Vorel** remain his companions even when their choices divide them. [Angelashi](angelashi.md) · [P0D](p0d.md) · [E110](../../episodes/E110.md) · [E117](../../episodes/E117.md).

Nash's recurring objects include **his dragon-metal heart, a staff, a limited Robe of Useful Items, a Ring of Spell Storing and the shared cooling/flying underwear** at specific episodes. A late-stage recovery of a counterpart staff in E110 does not undo every earlier breakage. **His unusual spell chants and rhymes have their own source-indexed register** and should not be repeated in each biography update. [Casting phrases](nash-spellcasting-phrases.md) · [E073](../../episodes/E073.md) · [E078](../../episodes/E078.md).

The June/July 2020 Q&As distinguish Scott's creative origins for Nash's personality, Beau's still-withheld heart explanation and hypothetical endings. In E118, Stanley's father **claims** to have encountered **N4SH (?)** before Stanley's birth and speculates about future Principle technology and the Weir; those are **found private writings**, not confirmed laws of time or proof that Nash's alternate selves are all identical. [July 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-07.md) · [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Book of Adjacencies](../technology/book-of-adjacencies.md).

**E119:** Nash thinks infant **Pipe** has gone missing, uses the alternate-tower **red-eye loved-one locator**, and **discovers the child transformed into a pipe in his own pocket**; Pipe resumes infant form safely with Angelashi. Nash **actually bathes** before the hunt, levitates silently to pass Vorel wax-wrapped fish bait, and **keeps ten fish heads (one with an eye missing) and ~250 fish eggs** from the party's camp catch. His earlier heart, biological ancestry and N4SH theories are **not** resolved. [E119](../../episodes/E119.md).

**E120:** Nash **casts a deliberately rhymed *Haste* on Vorel as Chester appears** and a ***Ray of Frost* on Chester**; *Reduce* is mentioned but not used, since the concentration slot is taken. After Chester dies he shares rations with supposed talking deer, later revealed as disguised hostile elves, and **actually casts *Chain Lightning* at four attackers**. As the group pursues Dilo's kidnapping of Hope, Nash **cuts and eats a blue glowing mushroom**, making his **open mouth emit temporarily blinding white-blue light**. The effect is real, but no broader mushroom physiology is confirmed. [E120](../../episodes/E120.md) · [casting phrases](nash-spellcasting-phrases.md).

**October 2020 bonus — Scott's retrospective interpretation:** Scott says Nash is committed to **Angelashi and all four children** but remains startled by immediate fatherhood and his family's shifting forms; his own parenting experiences inform these feelings. He has **not determined how Nash will process his own alleged shifting identity**. This is player characterization and a possible future theme, **not an additional witnessed child transformation, family move or resolved heart origin**. Scott also discusses E120's **mushroom eating as typical Nash behavior**, rather than reporting another meal. [Bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-10.md).

**Latest played status:** **Alive at Elhandar E120, pursuing abducted Hope inside the mountain mushroom cave**, with Angelashi and four children safe back at the cottage. His precise origin, heart engineering, genuine history of the Solar Mines, true identities of alternate Nashes, future of the family and alleged N4SH/time connection remain open.
