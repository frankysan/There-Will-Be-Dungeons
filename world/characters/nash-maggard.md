# Nash Maggard

## Introduction

Nash Maggard came out of the Blood Canyons and the [Solar Mines](../places/solar-mines.md) marked by a violent past. His body carried tumors, old scars and a sightless eye. With worn boots, a staff and a threadbare satchel, he crossed a devastated landscape carrying memories of the mines and of losses he could scarcely explain. He was a powerful, unpredictable wanderer, haunted by what he had done and what had been done to him, but still capable of choosing a different life.[^nash-introduction]

He made his way toward Dusthill, where a shortage of water had brought fear and hardship to the settlement. There he would meet the people whose lives would become bound to his own. His strange physiology, the full history of his childhood and the origins of his magic remained mysteries even to Nash.[^nash-dusthill]

## Development by arc

### Arc One — From wanderer to companion

In Dusthill, Nash's formidable but unpredictable magic found an immediate purpose among people threatened by the failing waterworks. He joined [Hope](hope-akmenos.md), [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) and [Vorel](vorel.md), companions whose different temperaments challenged his impulsiveness. As the group encountered coercion in Slavetown and the Black Mine, Nash's memories of the Solar Mines gave those dangers a personal resonance. He could be reckless and destructive, but his actions also revealed an instinct to protect people caught in such suffering.[^nash-arc-one]

His first lasting transformation was social rather than magical. A solitary survivor began to share risks and responsibilities with others, without yet understanding his scarred body or the origins of his power. The abilities that made him dangerous could also make him useful to the people around him. His new friendships gave him a place in the world before he had any convincing account of where he came from.

### Arc Two — Attachment and the fear of loss

Nash grew fiercely attached to his companions, even as his impulsiveness made him difficult to predict. The pixie [P0D](p0d.md) became especially dear to him. When [Diontalus](diontalus.md) killed her, Nash could not save her despite his formidable magic. His grief exposed the vulnerability beneath his power and complicated his bond with Stanley, whose body Diontalus had used. Nash had reason to fear the archfey without treating his friend as identical to the being responsible for the murder.[^nash-pod]

Love for [Angelashi](angelashi.md) gave Nash another reason to survive. Hearing that she had died seemed to destroy the possibility of life beyond wandering and loss. Her eventual rescue showed the report had been false, but the fright remained part of how deeply he cared. His warmth and fierce loyalty coexisted with erratic behavior and sudden anger. By the time the company had endured Tietkalla's upheavals, Nash had gained people whose well-being mattered more to him than his own safety—and a hope for a future that his earlier life had offered little reason to expect.[^nash-angelashi]

### Arc Three — Responsibility and sacrifice

Angelashi's pregnancy turned Nash's longing for a future into an approaching responsibility. Dreams of the Solar Mines and an apparent meeting with the dead P0D returned him to grief and unanswered questions. Near Razak Val, older counterparts claimed to have passed through time. They offered no settled explanation of Nash's past, but their presence made his uncertainty about himself harder to dismiss. His increasing power, including draconic transformation, mattered beside the prospect of becoming a father rather than in place of it.[^nash-razak]

At [Kervalon and CHIDS](../places/chids.md), Nash confronted forces that could not simply be overcome with greater destructive magic. A [portable portal device](../technology/portal-device.md) offered limited possibilities, while the Traversal Drive threatened consequences far beyond the companions. Nash's care for Vorel emerged as he helped him recover from grave injury; his ferocity remained evident in the struggle against the Drive's defenders.[^nash-chids]

When activating the machinery risked destroying Earth, the friends chose to **share the crimson beam** instead. Nash accepted what appeared to be death alongside the people he had grown to love. That decision marked a distance from the wanderer who first entered Dusthill: he now understood belonging as a commitment that could cost him his life, even with a child on the way.[^nash-drive]

### Arc Four — A body remade in Sigil

Nash returned in [Sigil](../places/sigil.md) as an eye emerging from a metallic heart. The rest of his body gradually regenerated, binding his continued existence to a mysterious engineered organ. His recollections of the Solar Mines were still fragmented, and his apparent reconstruction made questions about parentage and identity unavoidable. Whatever had happened to his body, he recognized his companions and resumed life among them. His loyalties proved more continuous than his physical form.[^nash-regeneration]

He learned to become a young black dragon and survived a temporary fusion with a demonic body. Both transformations made his unusual nature more visible, while his choices remained those of a friend trying to keep others alive. At [White Tower](../places/white-tower.md), the group's unity faced a different kind of threat. Hope departed carrying the living [Hand of the Voice](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md), leaving Nash with Stanley and Vorel. The bond that had endured the crimson beam could not prevent disagreement and separation. Nash continued with those who remained, his concern for Angelashi and their unborn child now sharpened by another loss of certainty.[^nash-white-tower]

### Arc Five — Family, identity and difficult choices

With Hope gone, Nash stayed beside Stanley and Vorel while his unpredictable magic created a dangerous Tungan moon. He named his ship **The Great Angelashi**. Meeting **First Nash**, a counterpart mourning friends lost in another history, exposed the fragility of his own attachments. Seeing Angelashi in danger drew him home.[^nash-tunga][^nash-first]

Their reunion brought new responsibilities. Angelashi revealed that she was a changeling, and their four shapechanging children were born. Nash responded with love, wonder and considerable awkwardness. The short peace at [Elhandar](../places/elhandar.md) and [Villa Ornath](../places/villa-ornath.md) allowed him to experience family life, not merely fight to reclaim it. When Hope returned, he helped restore the original companions' bond and spoke of his Solar Mines past. Little Pipe's appearance in his pocket displayed the humor and unpredictability of the family he now hoped to protect.[^nash-family][^nash-reunion][^nash-hunt]

A dying black dragon then recognized what it claimed was its mother's power inside Nash's manufactured heart. A glowing maternal apparition appeared, but the dragon's tale of flesh pits and many contributors to Nash's making could not prove his origins. Nash faced the possibility of being made rather than born, while wanting above all to belong to Angelashi and the children. The dragon's separate natural heart offered a possible way to defend Earth, not an answer to his identity. His anxiety and playful curiosity persisted, even through the strange encounter with Malhya.[^nash-dragon][^nash-origins][^nash-lake]

Elhandar's fall made Nash's protectiveness both vital and troubling. He helped his family survive, but killed the mercenary Henatar **after the man had surrendered**. His capacity for tenderness did not prevent an act of cruelty.[^nash-siege][^nash-henatar] He later sent Angelashi and the children toward Mars with the surviving elves and the recovered heart, promising to return when they were safer from his enemies. When a vast burrower unleashed a sandstorm, he carried his three companions above it as a black dragon. Nash remained a father, a friend and an uncertain creation whose powers could shelter others, but whose choices still carried a moral cost.[^nash-farewell][^nash-burrow]

## Relationships and abilities

Angelashi and the four children anchor Nash's hopes for a life beyond endless battle. His loyalty to Hope, Stanley and Vorel has survived grief, quarrels and separation. P0D's death remains part of his history; the alternate people encountered in First Nash's tower are not replacements for friends lost in his own world. He has often teased and argued with Stanley, yet their bond has endured moments when their judgments differed sharply.[^nash-relationships]

Nash's powers are inseparable from his unusual body and [engineered dragon-metal heart](../technology/nash-dragon-heart.md). He can summon destructive magic, change into a black dragon and use an assortment of hard-won enchanted objects, including a staff, a Ring of Spell Storing and garments that can carry their wearer through the air. A smaller additional hand and his unpredictable bodily changes have sometimes been as useful as they were unsettling. His improvised spell-phrases are gathered in a separate [record of his spellcasting](nash-spellcasting-phrases.md). The heart's true origin, and the precise limits of its power, remain unknown.[^nash-equipment]

## December 2021 retrospective — character tone and unrevealed mines story

Scott says in the [December 2021 bonus (~00:03–00:07 and ~00:17–00:21)](../../episodes/BONUS-2021-12.md) that Nash **began as a dark, distrustful and solemn sorcerer** with scars, a blind glowing red eye and the broad Solar Mines trauma. His later idiosyncratic humor, curiosity and odd impulses came from collaboration with the rest of the cast, rather than from a planned alternate character. Scott also says **he and one other person escaped the Solar Mines**, without disclosing who the other escapee is now. Beau confirms he deliberately connected the **Principle, the engineered dragon-metal heart and Solar Mines secrets** over time; Nash's remembered ordeal **does not encompass the mines' entire history**, and Scott's question about **updated Slow Peters is speculation**. **Creator commentary, not a new in-world casting, flashback or verified return journey.** [ASR comparison](../../reports/BONUS_2021-12_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## Season Two — reunion on Mars and a new possible refuge

Nash follows Stanley's continuing mission aboard the newly functional **[Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md)**. After the vessel's stop at Razak Val, it **actually completes a spaceflight to the Mars terraforming colony**. In [S2E02](../../episodes/S2E02.md), **Angelashi runs to embrace him, and all four children are physically present, alive and playful in human form**. Although Nash trusts the elves' protection, Angelashi fears that Mars may become a war zone; Nash **privately mentions Planet Nash as a possible future family refuge**. This is neither an implemented move nor an authorized voyage for the family. Nash rejoins companions and physically inspects **Tungsten Wheel's incomplete Triangulosphere** on Mars; he still carries his *engineered* heart, while the dead dragon's *natural* heart remains a different object's recorded custody history. No new improvised spell rhyme is found in the full processed S2E02 text. [P/Q report](../../reports/WASTES_S2E02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E03 — the Solar Mines account before the war council

At the [Mars Council of Earthly Defenders](../../episodes/S2E03.md), Nash becomes the main **witness to Solar Mines history**. He describes upper educational and breeding facilities, children sent underground when judged unable to reproduce, appalling overcrowding and fatal work, **Slow Peter** magic-machine guards, solar panels powering deep extraction, and a masked/hooded **“Emperor With No Name”** whose identity Nash did not learn. His own scars, pain and escape through a gutted construct with aid from fellow captives are **first-person testimony**. Some institutional aims, exact age/census and current security are **recollection, inference or rumors**, not a new played mine visit. The Elhandari also claim an **older/future Nash** provided Delit Ornath with the Triangulosphere solution via time adjacencies; Nash presently lacks that knowledge, and the council's explanation is **not direct proof** of the trip.

He advocates a **rear *Mold Earth* tunneling diversion** combined with frontal attack, warning of severe mines defenses. **He does not cast *Mold Earth* or enter the facility in S2E03.** Nash also objects to a proposed oil-fire attack lest many enslaved miners perish. Stanley's private communion with the elder tree includes a prophecy concerning Nash's possible future death, but **Nash does not hear it and survives the episode**. [ASR report](../../reports/WASTES_S2E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E06 — temporary dragon cognition, but no cure

At [Bank of Burp](../../episodes/S2E06.md), Nash begins still **INT 3 and DEX 3** after S2E05's ability-stealing blobs. ***Reduce* on Vorel** succeeds, with the distinct **NSP-013** flea verse already present in the [spell phrase register](nash-spellcasting-phrases.md); Vorel passes through bank bars and opens the doors. Nash tries tasting old ooze residue to restore intelligence—**no effect**—and ***casts Light* on a food peeling** worn on his head. During subsequent **Green Vale marauder combat**, Nash **actually becomes an adult black dragon**, using breath weapon and physical impact. GM gives dragon form **INT 14**; **this is form-specific, not evidence baseline INT/DEX returned**. Nash remains a dragon at S2E06 end; no restored baseline ability scores, new improvised rhyme, or visit to the newly discovered coordinates. [P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E06_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## Current status

At **S2E07 cutoff**, Nash is in **Pentel Weiss's pyramid intake hall**, with his **normal-form Intelligence and Dexterity restored** after an **actual completed long rest** aboard Fate Six. GM and player cleared both earlier `3` ability-score overrides; this is **not merely adult-dragon-form INT 14**. He **used *Detect Magic* while investigating the small portal**, physically entered and returned from the portal ahead of ship, then ***casts Light* on a food peeling** inside the prison; no new incantation rhyme. **Badass T has just jumped into the intake shaft and his fate is unknown**. Angelashi/four children remain on Planet Nash, ship at the giant pyramid, no Tungsten rescue. [S2E07](../../episodes/S2E07.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E07_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).[^nash-status]

His **manufactured heart remains implanted**; the separate natural dragon heart was transported by elves toward Mars. The party has now **found Tungsten Wheel's incomplete physical sealing device in his preserved workshop**, but **Gluptwarp (?) is missing, and no successful construction/test, coordinate voyage or planetary seal is achieved**. His Solar Mines origin, other Nash counterparts and the question of safety for his family remain open.[^nash-open]
