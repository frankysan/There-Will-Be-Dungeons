# Nash Maggard

**Portrayed by:** Scott. **Identity:** Wasteland traveler and spellcaster, with an implanted dragon-metal heart. **Year One status:** Alive; physically changed, allied with newly crowned Hope. **Spoilers:** Main Wastes through E081; retrospective Q&A comments kept separate from direct play. **Evidence:** Processed SRTs/dossiers, not independently audio-verified.

## Character and history

Nash arrives in Dusthill bearing the scars and memories of the **Solar Mines** and the **Blood Canyons**, with a destructive power that both frightens and sustains him. His early account of captivity and escape is important to who he believes he is, but parts of that history later conflict with experiences induced by other powerful beings; the precise history of the mines is **not completely settled**. He is a central actor in the repair of Dusthill's underground water system, becomes dangerously involved in Slavetown's rivalries, and contributes to the fire there while affected by Pixie Blush.

The meeting with **Catoblepraithe, the Soulbroker**, changes what Nash knows about his own body. He is one of the three signatories to the demand to defeat the **Principle** within a stipulated thousand-day/night period, and he experiences an intense private memory/vision of the Solar Mines. The GM reveals an implanted **dragon-metal heart** capable of unusual mana phenomena and black-dragon transformation. Later he can take the form of a **young black dragon**, but the ability carries substantial exhaustion and other restrictions that should be taken from the contemporary played episode rather than a final-campaign character sheet.

In **Tietkalla**, Nash is especially protective of the pixie **P0D**, whose fate tests his trust in his companions. Monsoon's order triggers her unwilling transformation in E049; Nash tries to calm and rescue her, but **Diontalus kills her** in E050. Nash mourns her loss. His romance with the singer **Angelashi** is interrupted by her disappearance and an unreliable report of death; in E054 he helps break her free from Monsoon's stone-bodied captor, and they reunite **alive**. The froghemoth's unstable magic gives Nash a **small extra arm**, which he retains through E053. His **Robe of Useful Items** supplies one-use ladder, mirror and rowboat patches in different episodes; the patches used are **not still available**.

At the close of E054 he helps usher the party back into the shattered city in dragon form, physically smashes open the throne-room wall, and has not yet completed longer-term plans to aid surviving pixies, visit **George Miller's** family or uncover all of his own history.

## Relationships and significant objects

**Hope** is both a friend and a concern for Nash, including during her near-fatal injuries and political ascension. **Stanley** shares a sometimes argumentative, familial loyalty with him, while **Diontalus's** actions create serious personal danger. **Vorel** remains an ally despite disputes. His bond to **P0D** is particularly painful because it ends with an ally killing her. Nash's **dragon-metal heart**, **Robe of Useful Items**, **Wand of Scowls** and memories of the **Freedom nail/staff** belong to different parts of his story and should not be conflated. The Q&A mentions a **Blink scroll from Buttons** which Nash apparently failed to record in his inventory; its current custody is a **review item**, not proof of loss or use.

## What the Q&A adds

Beau describes conceiving the **dragon-metal engine-heart** after Scott improvised an early uncertainty about Nash having a normal heart. Scott discusses a **betraying Solar Mines partner** as still-partly-unshared player backstory; this is not enough to establish the person's identity or every detail of the mines. The GM also emphasizes intentional contradictions between Nash's memories and later visions.

In the [June 2019 bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md) (~00:05:37–00:07:05), Scott explains that **“Nash Maggard of the Solar Mines” remains Nash's preferred self-identification**, even while living in Tietkalla. The epithet acknowledges his past and its lasting effect on his actions; **“of Tietkalla” does not replace it**. This is a player explanation of an ongoing title, not a newly bestowed in-world honor.

## After Year One — E055–E056

**Three months later**, Nash is **expecting a child with Angelashi**. Their relationship and his weekly **puppet shows** using the small extra hand are played downtime status. Repeated Solar Mines nightmares torment him; his opening E055 truck-crushing, dynamite and **P0D** reunion are **a dream**, not a physical death or return of the pixie. When stones fall he helps civilians evacuate into sewer shelters, later fights **George's hostile ghost**, and speaks to **P0D as a spirit** who asks him to follow her. Nash **stays alive in the material world**. He also helps prepare diamond dust for Vorel's restoration. The ethereal meeting confirms a scene of supernatural communication, **not P0D's bodily resurrection or an independently proved heavenly geography**. ## The crater and the scarless double (E057–E060)

Nash's **young black dragon** fights the winged **Wormfather** pet and bugbears at the **impact crater**, then destroys an observed black **brain leech** with acid. After his flying/scouting route diverges, he follows **bananas** dropped from the companions' returning truck until his transformation ends; a **royal pickup retrieves him alive** before the anticipated multiday walk. He remains concerned for **Angelashi and their unborn child**. [E057](../../episodes/E057.md) · [E058](../../episodes/E058.md).

During the Principle's visit, Nash sees **Diontalus removed from Stanley into a crystal** but remains suspicious that the problem is permanently gone. On the subsequent lizardfolk expedition, **Nash alone** (others' headphones off) encounters a **scarless, red-eyed lookalike** in a latrine, who **claims to be Nash “from the future.”** This is a claim by a mysterious figure, not a verified time-travel fact or a public revelation to Hope, Stanley and Vorel. ## Apparent future Nashes, an egg and the Great Fang (E061–E063)

**E061:** Another similar figure joins the scarless man, and both claim to be **future Nash Maggards** with a secret request. They describe time in terms of **“time barf”** and ask Nash to steal a **preserved Fengar egg** from a family heirloom chest. He **puts two guards to sleep**, helps the two lookalikes enter, and distracts **Bramie/Baymie** as the visitors take the egg and vanish. **Bramie is alive during Nash's last observed encounter with him**, though later found stabbed in the back; Nash's involvement in **theft is direct**, his involvement in **murder is not established**, and the time-travel account remains the visitors' unproven claim. Nash first tells Vorel privately and later speaks in Common within hearing of several lizardfolk, apparently breaking a secrecy warning; **the later effect of the chill at E061's ending is unknown**. [E061](../../episodes/E061.md).

**E062–E063:** Nash witnesses **Hope's rocket devastating Cave Fengar**, the killing of Orp and Vorel's ascent as Great Fang. In E063 he transforms into a **young black dragon** to awe the lizardfolk, many of whom bow; his claim to be a divine figure is **a public performance**, not an established godhood. He returns to human form and incurs **one exhaustion level**. His **Mold Earth** helps prepare rooms to medically screen the **200–300 starving captives** whom Vorel wants to free. Stanley later isolates the suspicious prisoner **Follow/Follo** in one such room. The promised freedom is not an observed completed mass release. [E062](../../episodes/E062.md) · [E063](../../episodes/E063.md).

## Future eggs and Razak Val's fall (E064–E065)

In [E064](../../episodes/E064.md) Nash uses *Mold Earth* to contain a chamber as the **Sun's Witnesses mutate**, then a **Fireball** in the pit kills many transforming prisoners/creatures without ending the outbreak. During the mounted escape he **casts Dimension Door**, carrying himself and his raptor across the ravine. His ownership of **Fate 4** remains disputed when Follow's companions are found driving the RV; **no permanent transfer is agreed**.

In [E065](../../episodes/E065.md), **Rorf confesses to murdering Bramie**, a separate act from Nash's E061 assistance in stealing the Fengar egg. Nash uses *Mold Earth* to free Fate 4 from rain-softened sand and builds a huge runoff basin by **Stanley's Tiny Hut**. Multiple new **claimed future Nashes** arrive, bring **two apparent copies of the Fengar egg**, disagree about needing an **egg versus a magical leg**, and mention a winged employer and a supposedly exploding planet. **One egg remains with the party; another is taken away**. Their future origin, mechanics and forecasts are still unproven.

In the [July-archived Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-07.md), Scott says Nash's unpredictable choices derive from his character, not a dice chart, and that he is **protective of children**. Beau **tentatively doubts** Follow's daughter Sira is the young girl from Nash's **Solar Mines dream**, and distinguishes those heart-related dream episodes from the new claimed time-travel plot.

## Fate 4's destruction, an old dragon name and the underground approach (E066–E067)

**E066:** Nash's **deep *Mold Earth* drainage pit** catches a rain-soaked **T. rex**, which has already **crushed Fate 4**; Nash **casts Fireball** into it and Vorel kills the creature. After a subsequent blast, **Fate 4 is a wreck**, not merely loaned out or stuck in mud. Nash becomes a **young black dragon** during the battle with the mind flayer's thralls and **attacks eyeball trees**. Stanley eventually **polymorphs him into a mouse** out of frustration, then Nash returns to dragon/human form and rests; there is **no permanent mouse condition**. He **licks an eyeball tree**, is told a bug may have been ingested, and feels ill, but **no proved infection or immediate outcome** follows. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

**E067:** During an extraordinary starfield encounter, a **spectral dragon claims Nash carries the energy/name of Kazdejira/Kazdeshira**, seemingly connected to his **dragon-engine heart/mark**; he recalls the name from an item/arm note. **The species, spirit identity and name orthography remain unverified**. The dragon warns of a **pre-Scorching planetary relocation machine** under Kervalon. Nash favors cautious tactics and ultimately **tunnels using *Mold Earth*** from the forest beneath the keep's outer wall, assisted by Hope. They encounter an underground **flesh giant spawning skeletons**; Nash tests *Minor Illusion* and *Ray of Frost*, then **Fireball** helps destroy many minions. The party reaches an underground corridor but has **not found the machine**. The E067 opening's **bat-guano/arcane-focus discussion** is out-of-character rules clarification, not proof of a newly cast free spell. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md).

## Solar Mines backstory and Kervalon's hidden doors (E068 / August bonus)

**E068:** Nash prepares **Mold Earth** walls and a short-lived *Minor Illusion* false passage as an elaborate defense against Kervalon's rifle-bearing lizardfolk. When Vorel emerges as bait, Nash **seals approximately eight melee pursuers into a corridor**; their eventual **abrupt silence is not proof of death**. With **Stanley Polymorphing Vorel into a T. rex** and Hope interrupting the mind flayer's spell concentration, Nash fights the defenders with **Fireball, Ice Storm and Fire Bolt**. The visible mind-flayer form is defeated but its permanent fate is unknown. Following a short rest, Nash studies the **extraordinarily powerful light shaft**; **Mold Earth fails directly beneath it**, apparently opposed by intense force. Later, following **small black worms** from a masonry joint, he **removes stone to expose steel double doors with a hostile control panel**. **No actual elevator activation, hidden-machine discovery or Sira rescue** has occurred. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md).

**August-publication Q&A (spoken as recorded July 27):** Scott clarifies that the **Solar Mines really involved coerced labor collecting solar energy from old panels in extreme radiation**, exposing most workers to **early death from poisoning or cancers** while privileged overseers avoided it. Occasional access to books/media helped retain exploited workers. **The exact precipitating incident behind Nash's escape/anger is deliberately undisclosed**, and these details are **Scott's player/backstory testimony**, not a new speech heard by the other PCs in E068. ## From poisoned tourist to borrowed flight and corporate sabotage (E069–E071)

**E069:** Nash forces entry past a sentient **Door Authority** panel by **actually applying a negotiated Acid Splash**, after threats and persuasion. He enters the CHIDS complex via a **funicular** whose **magical flower spores knock him and Hope unconscious**; **Stanley's Feather Fall** protects them during its wreck and **Vorel shakes them awake**. In employee housing Nash first dons **magically refrigerated/levitating underwear** recovered from a deceased demon scientist (GM gives **10-foot fly speed**). At the oxygen bar an ancient mask emits briny brown gas, causing **6 damage and the Poisoned condition**; **no explicit cure is heard during E069–E071**, so track uncertain persistence rather than assuming normality. Nash helps use dead workers' keycards, passes security and explores executive offices. [E069](../../episodes/E069.md).

**E070:** Nash enters wired conference **VR glasses** and assumes an **illithid avatar**, meeting **Charles Dickens**, a disgruntled mind-flayer office worker who says he wants to humiliate his rival **Ovaxiad/Oubvayx**. Charles's tale about a human **Traversal Drive** threatening Earth is **interested testimony**. Hope joins; Nash uses an unattended office terminal to **upload false reporting data** and sees success before being discovered. After removing a headset that becomes an aggressive membrane, Nash fights **fiery hellhounds** with *Absorb Elements*, and **casts Enlarge on Vorel** so he can wield grabbed hounds as weapons. Under severe pressure Nash **casts Dimension Door out of the melee**, **does not transform into a dragon**, and remains alive but wounded. [E070](../../episodes/E070.md).

**E071:** Nash's cold magic contributes to the final victory over hounds/ape, but **the lasting consequence of his E069 poison is not expressly addressed**. At the old office console, **unseen typing** deactivates alarms and a message identifying itself as **Charles Dickens** confirms the false report upload; Nash **cools a burning ejected disk-drive casing with *Ray of Frost* and pockets it**. Whether this imposes **months of physical delay** is unknown. He leaves his **flying briefs** with companions for the high frozen vent and **Vorel carries him aloft** using them. Nash helps melt the vent obstruction with *Fire Bolt*, while direct engineering access stays closed. **At E071 end Nash no longer has custody of the underwear**. [E071](../../episodes/E071.md).

## Flight briefs, Slow Peters and a snapped focus (E072–E073 / September bonus)

**E072:** After **Vorel returns the shared magical cooling/flight underwear**, Nash again wears the pair as the party navigates CHIDS's enormous frozen server hall. He explores a **keyboard hidden beneath ice** but cannot use it to stop the cooling fans; one input briefly lights a preserved corpse. At camp he listens to Cerinthala's extraordinary stories about ancient humans and demons, with her reliability already questionable. The four take an **actual long rest** in Stanley's Tiny Hut before ice monsters attack. **Nash casts Enlarge on Vorel** to protect Stanley, who is being carried off and choked by Cerinthala's necklace. [E072](../../episodes/E072.md).

**E073:** Nash reports that the **Solar Mines used fire elementals nicknamed “Slow Peters”** for perilous heat-related industrial work; mine workers were warned never to approach them. This is **Nash's in-character memory**, valuable but not independently historical. He casts *Minor Illusion* during the ice battle and then a **Fireball with Careful Spell to protect Vorel**, spending actual **sorcery points**, in contrast to player mail criticism of underusing them. The party frees Stanley, defeats attackers, and takes Cerinthala's **service-tunnel route** into ancient biotech laboratories. **Nash pokes pink, mobile tissue with his staff**; a tendril **pulls it away and breaks it into two pieces**. **The nail-bearing short half remains usable as an arcane focus**; Nash **does not have Mending** and the full staff **is not repaired**. In the stalled elevator he is still alive and facing a large approaching spider-like creature. [E073](../../episodes/E073.md).

**September bonus and memory reliability:** Beau explicitly confirms **the Solar Mines exists on his official campaign map**. He suggests, **conditionally**, that Nash's previous dragon-heart surgical vision may depict a **Frankenstein-like composite body made of several people**, with memories from different individuals. **Beau does not confirm Nash is undead or that this interpretation is literally true**, and Scott says his own Solar Mines memories/backstory are deliberately partial and could be less reliable than they seem. Scott's visual inspiration of **an old Walmart distribution facility in a desert** is **real-world creative inspiration**, not a fixed canonical map location. Scott also expresses a **future wish to destroy the mines and free survivors**, not a completed character action. [Bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-09.md).

**Inventory/condition caveat:** Shared **flight briefs are last worn by Nash from E072**, distinct from Vorel's E071 custody. The E069 oxygen-bar **Poisoned status was not expressly cleared on-screen**; E072 grants a full long rest, but the exact disease/poison condition clearance **still needs sheet/audio reconciliation** rather than silent retroactive declaration.

A secured chamber yields a **two-color sleeve-mounted device**. Nash's **fourth-level Fireball kills the forward bugbear ranks** and triggers retreat, then **Vorel goes out as bait**. ## Containment, winged Lethanphius and the alleged Wall Nash (E076–E077)

In [E076](../../episodes/E076.md), Nash **Dimension Doors past the flesh membrane** when it refuses his song, after the team performs its initial bugbear **portal escape**. He briefly holds the **portal device** to place endpoints across a broken Containment shaft while wearing the single **flying/cooling underwear**, fights a winged predator and **Hope is observed using it again in E077**, although the handoff back is not clearly narrated. He tastes **a drop of Blue Bull**, experiencing short-lived stimulation, not a permanently altered spellcasting ability. His **broken staff** is not repaired.

In [E077](../../episodes/E077.md), Nash helps the party investigate dead lizardfolk vats and a black-winged captive **Lethanphius**, then **casts Enlarge on Vorel** during combat. **Nash's Fire Bolt kills the surviving metallic-boned upper torso** after Hope's void beam tears it apart. Nash **freezes Vorel's two severed digits and a severed hand** and keeps them in his cooling underwear; the GM later uses these recovered parts for **Monster Mender reattachment**.

A **second flesh-made figure impersonates or claims to be an older Nash** who wandered through time and spent centuries absorbed into a CHIDS hive. It describes another timeline's destroyed planet, Soulbroker bargain and supposedly trapped Sira. **Present Nash openly doubts this account**, including its disjunction from Vorel's current injuries. A figure being physically present is **not proof its purported future happened**. At close the GM **awards level nine and directs a full rest**; the previously active **1d4 memory deficit remains an open post-rest mechanics question**. The [October Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-10.md) includes **Scott's optional visual interpretation** of Nash's baby hand as a small dragon claw/wing in dragon form and its sentimental association with P0D—**not a new played ability or resurrected companion**.

## Spell-storage ring, Chromatic Orb and illithid masquerade (E078–E079)

In [E078](../../episodes/E078.md), **Nash returns Vorel's preserved hand/digits** for their actual **Monster Mender reattachment**. Council gifts **a Ring of Spell Storing** to Nash and one to Stanley. Nash **charges his ring with three Chromatic Orbs** before the **eight-hour long rest**, and later uses **Chromatic Orb** against illithids. He dramatizes the spell component by removing his **wedding-ring diamond and incorporating it into his orb**; the [November Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-11.md) clarifies the **component didn't need to be physically transferred** under the table's rules—Scott deliberately chose that moment. Nash wakes from the eight-hour rest feeling **strange aching and lost/rearranged magic**, but its cause and the effects on the old 1d4 memory penalty are **unresolved**; keep separate from the out-of-character spell audit. When old gnome acquaintances **Chance and Eddie** are found captive, Nash uses **Twinned Haste on Hope/Vorel**, fails to banish an alien, and **kills a mind flayer with Chromatic Orb** after an exhausting fight.

In [E079](../../episodes/E079.md), Vorel makes Nash **a hollow illithid-head mask** from a corpse; Nash wears it with dark robe, **flying underwear and projected Minor Illusion speech** to successfully pass two checkpoint security apes. The disguise is a **mortal, rotting prop**, not actual telepathy or transformation. Nash retrieves a **severed dead mind-flayer hand** for a four-finger crystalline lock, but **it fails to open**. Stanley/hope bypass by **Dimension Door and linked portals**. Nash later **drinks Blue Bull** with party ahead of Hope's **two-rocket assault** on the first witnessed Drive chamber. Per November Q&A Beau says **human-form dragon acid breath is once per long rest**, not at-will, despite Scott's previous memory of unlimited use. Scott calls his dragon-form baby claw an optional image, not a new attack.

## E081 — final dragon battle and the crimson beam

[E081](../../episodes/E081.md) opens with a starry battlefield and **Dolo Popio releasing worms as its deferred breath**. Nash remains in **young-black-dragon form**, uses recharged **breath attacks to destroy drifting worms**, removes his frightened condition, and attacks red memory-devourers. **Hope kills Dolo's manifested body**, returning the party to the altered CHIDS engineering bay; **Chance does not return from drifting into the void**. **Quizquaz** then urges acceptance of Earth-destruction or an individual sacrifice. Nash refuses the demon's broad bargain and **joins Stanley, Vorel and Hope in the crimson beam**. He clasps a claw with the others. The machine's damage **reverts him to human form** and deals him further harm; he **turns to white light and disappears along with the other three**. GM explicitly **calls the protagonists dead at the E081 cutoff**. No further destiny for Nash's body, magic, dragon heart or soul is shown; do not retroactively interpret E078's changed spell list as verified cause of the finale.
