# Nash Maggard

## Introduction

Nash Maggard came out of the Blood Canyons and the [Solar Mines](../places/solar-mines.md) marked by a violent past. His body carried tumors, old scars and a sightless eye. With worn boots, a staff and a threadbare satchel, he crossed a devastated landscape carrying memories of the mines and of losses he could scarcely explain. He was a powerful, unpredictable wanderer, haunted by what he had done and what had been done to him, but still capable of choosing a different life.[^nash-introduction]

He made his way toward Dusthill, where a shortage of water had brought fear and hardship to the settlement. There he would meet the people whose lives would become bound to his own. His strange physiology, the full history of his childhood and the origins of his magic remained mysteries even to Nash.[^nash-dusthill]

## Development by arc

### Arc One — Dusthill and the road through the wastes

In Dusthill, Nash joined [Hope](hope-akmenos.md), [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) and [Vorel](vorel.md) as their paths converged around the failing waterworks. They explored the buried systems, helped restore water to the town and followed the trail of its enemies beyond the settlement. The journey carried them through Slavetown and the Black Mine, where the coercion and industrial horrors they encountered echoed Nash's memories of the Solar Mines. His spells could be destructive and erratic, yet he repeatedly turned his strength toward keeping the others alive.[^nash-arc-one]

### Arc Two — Friends, grief and Angelashi

The wanderer grew fiercely protective of his companions, even as his impulsiveness made him difficult to predict. [P0D](p0d.md), a pixie who helped expose abuses surrounding Pixie Blush, became especially dear to him. When [Diontalus](diontalus.md) killed her, Nash could not save her. His grief deepened the divide between the friends and the archfey acting through Stanley's body; what Diontalus had done could not simply be attributed to Stanley himself.[^nash-pod]

His love for [Angelashi](angelashi.md) gave Nash another reason to endure the journey. When word reached him that she had died, the loss seemed to be one more cruelty of the wastes. Yet the report was wrong: she was later found and freed alive. Amid broken settlements, threatened kingdoms and strange transformations, the possibility of a life with Angelashi remained one of Nash's few steady hopes.[^nash-angelashi]

### Arc Three — Razak Val and the crimson beam

After months had passed, Angelashi was pregnant, and dreams drew Nash back toward his childhood and the mines. He even seemed to encounter P0D again, though the meeting belonged to a dream rather than a return from death. When a deadly stonefall threatened Tietkalla, Nash stood beside his companions to protect its people. Near Razak Val, he fought the Wormfather in dragon form, explored the strange green crater and met figures who looked like older versions of himself. They claimed to have crossed time and sought a Fengar egg, but their explanations raised as many questions as they answered.[^nash-razak]

The troubles around the Sun's Witnesses and the flight from Razak Val brought fresh losses. At [Kervalon and CHIDS](../places/chids.md), Nash and the others found themselves amid living machinery, unstable experiments and a series of dangers that no amount of clever spellcasting could fully control. Their discoveries included a [small portable portal device](../technology/portal-device.md), distinct from the much larger Traversal Drive. Even an apparent future Nash within the hive offered warnings whose truth was uncertain.[^nash-chids]

Nash gathered new magical tools and helped Vorel recover from a terrible injury, then fought the forces guarding the Drive. He killed the great illithid commander as the conflict reached its height. Faced with the possibility that activating the machine could destroy Earth, the four original companions chose instead to **share the crimson beam**. They vanished together into white light and were believed dead. The fate of the world beyond that moment remained unseen to them.[^nash-drive]

### Arc Four — A body remade in Sigil

Nash's consciousness returned in [Sigil](../places/sigil.md), not in his old body but as a small eye emerging from his metal heart. Gradually, his hands, limbs and the rest of his anatomy formed again. His existence had become inseparable from the strange engine beating within him, but the process did not tell him who had made him or why. His recollections of the Solar Mines remained fragmentary, and the questions surrounding his parentage were no closer to resolution.[^nash-regeneration]

With his body restored, he aided the others through Sigil and the Outlands. He learned to take the shape of a young black dragon and helped the company travel toward [White Tower](../places/white-tower.md). The tower was besieged and afflicted by a plague; its defenders guarded a powerful gauntlet as threats gathered outside. Nash survived a bizarre temporary fusion with a demonic body, emerged separate again and helped the others defend the tower. Although the plague was eventually cured, the friends could not prevent their company from breaking apart. Hope took the living [Hand of the Voice](../technology/hand-of-the-voice.md) and departed alone, leaving Nash with Stanley and Vorel.[^nash-white-tower]

### Arc Five — Separation, reunion and the fall of Elhandar

The three remaining companions tried to secure the Fist gauntlet and find a way home. Instead, a wild surge of Nash's magic had created a moon bearing a Tungan city, whose machines threatened the Outlands. It was Squirts, rather than Nash, whose appeal to Olidammara made the moon and attacking vessels disappear. Orcish sailors nevertheless hailed Nash as Captain Dragon Lord, and he named their ship **The Great Angelashi**. A misperformed portal rite destroyed that ship soon afterward, leaving the survivors to seek passage on a smaller vessel to Sigil. Nash also inherited a daunting claim against him for the lost ship.[^nash-tunga]

Delivering the Fist to Tobacho led them away from Earth again, toward the asteroid tower of **First Nash**, a counterpart from another history. The older Nash mourned companions who had died in his world; their memorials did not mean the present Stanley, Hope and Vorel had suffered the same fate. The younger Nash looked through a mysterious eye and saw Angelashi, still pregnant, fleeing danger in Tietkalla. Determined to reach her, he returned with the others to the ruined keep.[^nash-first]

At the Golden Sands, Nash finally embraced Angelashi again. Their refuge was short-lived. General Carthax threatened the survivors, and the ensuing fight cost the lives of Squirts and Melgor. Chunks survived his mother's death, and the company escaped aboard an Elhandari rescue ship. During the perilous voyage Angelashi revealed that she was a changeling. Their four children arrived in forms as extraordinary as their parentage: a fork, a boot, a pipe and a child-shaped infant, each alive and capable of changing shape. Nash was overjoyed, bewildered and frightened by the sudden responsibilities of fatherhood.[^nash-family]

The rescue vessel carried them to [Elhandar](../places/elhandar.md), an immense orbital sanctuary containing a living forest. For a brief time Nash could live beside Angelashi and the children in [Villa Ornath](../places/villa-ornath.md). Hope eventually found him again, drawing him through a Gate into Dr. Spider's infernal laboratory before returning with him to Elhandar. The original four companions were reunited. Nash introduced Hope to his family and tried to keep their bitter arguments from becoming another rupture. At the Harbor refugees' gathering he spoke openly of the uprising at the Solar Mines, offering his own memories rather than an answer to every mystery of his past.[^nash-reunion]

The forest gave Nash moments of comic relief and moments of terror. When little Pipe seemed to have vanished, he discovered the child safely transformed into a pipe in his own pocket. On the hunt for Chester, a monstrous bear-dinosaur, Nash hurried to aid Vorel with magic, sampled a luminous mushroom that caused his mouth to shine, and battled elves who had disguised themselves as talking deer. Their betrayal led to Hope's abduction and a pursuit into the mountain caves.[^nash-hunt]

There the spectral Diontalus confronted Stanley. Nash took black-dragon form, but Diontalus's magic and the struggle in the cave left the company battered. An imprisoned black dragon intervened, and Nash's own body unleashed uncontrolled fire and acid before he collapsed into a vision of his floating heart. The dying dragon claimed to recognize the magical signature of its mother within Nash's engineered heart. When Nash called to her, his chest opened and a golden-orange draconic apparition emerged, tenderly greeting the wounded creature. What the others witnessed was extraordinary; what it proved about Nash's birth remained far less certain.[^nash-dragon]

The black dragon spoke of the Principle, flesh pits, Dragon Sons and many supposed contributors to Nash's creation. Nash was shaken by the possibility that he had been fashioned rather than born, but the creature's account could not settle his parentage. Its death left a **natural dragon-metal heart**, distinct from the engine still beating inside Nash. The friends hid the enormous object for a future effort to protect Earth, then took refuge at the Gatherers' Lodge. Nash's anxieties and curiosity soon became entangled in a ridiculous quarrel about ghostly sweets, from which no demonstrated curse emerged.[^nash-origins]

At the Harbor, Nash rejoined his family and learned more of the threats gathering around Elhandar. During a strange rite in the waters of Malhya, he vanished briefly into darkness but returned alive. His love for Angelashi and the children sat uneasily beside the thought that he might have some part in a much larger struggle. He wanted to protect the family he had found, not be made into a symbol of cosmic destiny.[^nash-lake]

The company returned to Elhandar's core with Nash's household. The High Elf shared a possible route to Mars and clues to finding an engineer who might build the proposed planetary seal. Before the friends could pursue that hope, a fleet led by a three-headed musical mercenary descended on the station. Nash fought with lightning, speed magic and his companions' help as the station began to fall. The family escaped toward Villa Ornath, where the hidden study protected them through a devastating impact.[^nash-siege]

The cottage and its occupants survived, though the wreckage around them was terrible. When a second impact struck, Nash helped catch Angelashi and shield their children. He transformed into a black dragon and pursued the mercenary, **Henatar Grape**, whose orbiting spheres had protected him from spells. Nash's acid burned through the defenses and destroyed the commander's hovering disk. Henatar fell to the sand, still alive. When the fallen enemy yielded and begged for mercy, Nash tore one of his heads away. **Henatar died there, after surrendering.** Diontalus later drew a pale soul-like essence from the corpse.[^nash-henatar]

The surviving Elhandari prepared to take refugees to a terraforming platform on Mars. Nash said goodbye to Angelashi and their four children, promising to return when the dangers following him no longer threatened them. Lyrielle, Bok Bok and the surviving Harborers joined them aboard the departing ships. The rescued natural dragon heart went with the elves, while Nash remained on Earth with his companions. Their separation was deliberate and painful, not a reunion already secured.[^nash-farewell]

Before the friends could decide where to go, the Soulbroker opened a violet rupture in the sky. A colossal, gear-scaled creature plunged into the desert and burrowed beneath it, sending a towering wave of sand toward Villa Ornath. Nash took dragon form again and lifted Hope, Stanley and Vorel onto his back. He flew them above the storm until it passed. The cottage still stood; the burrowing creature remained somewhere beneath Earth.[^nash-burrow]

## Relationships and abilities

Angelashi and the four children anchor Nash's hopes for a life beyond endless battle. His loyalty to Hope, Stanley and Vorel has survived grief, quarrels and separation. P0D's death remains part of his history; the alternate people encountered in First Nash's tower are not replacements for friends lost in his own world. He has often teased and argued with Stanley, yet their bond has endured moments when their judgments differed sharply.[^nash-relationships]

Nash's powers are inseparable from his unusual body and [engineered dragon-metal heart](../technology/nash-dragon-heart.md). He can summon destructive magic, change into a black dragon and use an assortment of hard-won enchanted objects, including a staff, a Ring of Spell Storing and garments that can carry their wearer through the air. A smaller additional hand and his unpredictable bodily changes have sometimes been as useful as they were unsettling. His improvised spell-phrases are gathered in a separate [record of his spellcasting](nash-spellcasting-phrases.md). The heart's true origin, and the precise limits of its power, remain unknown.[^nash-equipment]

## Current status

Nash rests on Earth beside Villa Ornath with Hope, Stanley and Vorel, alive after the fall of Elhandar and the arrival of the monstrous burrower. Angelashi and their four children are aboard Elhandari ships bound for Mars, beyond his immediate protection. He has promised to return to them, but their safe arrival and reunion still lie ahead.[^nash-status]

His manufactured heart remains within him; the natural heart recovered from the dead dragon travels with the refugees. The device meant to seal Earth has not yet been built. Nash and his companions have survived another catastrophe and spent a peaceful night together, but they must still decide how to face the Principle, the Mind Master and the hungry powers entering their world. For Nash, the question of what he was made to become remains unresolved beside the question of whom he has chosen to love.[^nash-open]
