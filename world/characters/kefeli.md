# Kefeli — serpent-haired claimant to T'ietkalla's throne

## Introduction

Kefeli was a serpent-haired woman who confronted the travelers in the ancient subterranean throne chamber beneath **T'ietkalla**. Surrounded by petrified figures and the machinery of the old water system, she claimed to be the land's legitimate ruler and demanded the **Gem of Tides**. Her appearance, power and presence there were witnessed; the antiquity and legitimacy of her kingship were matters she argued for, not facts the party had established.[^kefeli-arrival]

## Claims and confrontation

Kefeli described a past in which the old realm had been threatened by cursed vampires and said that **Andreeste, the Nourisher**, had helped transform her to protect her people. She called for the party's service and challenged the authority of [Monsoon Cabbage](monsoon-cabbage.md). Hope rejected the demand that the companions surrender their freedom. The exact ancient bargains, divine roles and family histories behind Kefeli's account remained contested, especially given Monsoon's competing explanation.[^kefeli-claims]

When diplomacy failed, Kefeli fought using metallic serpent hair, water magic and a gaze that truly turned flesh to stone. **Vorel and Hope were both petrified** in the ensuing struggle. Stanley then forced her to look at her own reflection in a steel mirror, and **Kefeli herself turned to stone**. The surviving party members destroyed her statue. Her destruction did **not** automatically release Hope or Vorel; Nash and Stanley later found and used a separate preparation to restore them to life during the same episode.[^kefeli-battle]

## Current status

**Kefeli was petrified and destroyed in E051.** No restoration of her body was played. Hope and Vorel's temporary petrification ended through a separate remedy, not because Kefeli survived or was persuaded to lift it. Monsoon's later warnings about the repercussions of her death remain testimony about the consequences, not evidence that Kefeli physically returned.[^kefeli-status]
