# Blood Skunk Hendrickson — Harlequinari wanderer

## Introduction

Blood Skunk Hendrickson was a clownlike infernal traveler who joined [Hope](hope-akmenos.md), [Steerious Business](steerious-business.md) and [Dr. Spider](dr-spider.md) on a dangerous journey through Sigil and the planes beyond. He described himself as **Harlequinari**, while also saying that he had left their society and no longer served the Council of Clowns. His uneasy affiliation with infernal powers did not make him an interchangeable representative of every member of that people.[^skunk-identity]

## Travels and allegiances

During the crossing through the Gray Waste and the River Styx, Blood Skunk conjured temporary animal companions and surrendered his left little toe as a ferry payment. The wound healed, though regrowth was never established. The Gray Waste drained color and feeling from him and Spider; those effects lifted later in Pandemonium. When Hope needed help in another perilous passage, Blood Skunk offered his outer ears as a magical sacrifice. He survived a failed attempt to seize a cargo ship, wounded but healed before the group withdrew.[^skunk-journey]

His companions knew him as a grotesque, theatrical presence rather than a merely harmless clown. A later recollection described pale patches of skin, startling red lips and blood, and his unsettling grin and breathing. Those details come from a retrospective performance discussion, not a new bodily change in his final battle.[^skunk-appearance]

## Return aboard Fate Six

Blood Skunk eventually came aboard **Fate Six** with the infernal group during an attack carried out under [Brett](brett-asmodeus.md)'s hostile instructions. He marked [Vorel](vorel.md) with magic and fired at him, but Stanley's magic and even Spider's dangerous spells injured their own allies. Blood Skunk fell and failed his final struggle to survive. His body was left ruined, and a grotesque jack-in-the-box emerged from it. Nothing about that image establishes that Blood Skunk lived on inside it.[^skunk-death]

## Current status

**Blood Skunk died aboard Fate Six.** His charred remains were subsequently taken into the ship's magical brig. Spider attached Blood Skunk's severed head to the still-living Steerious, alongside the dead head of Squirts. Neither head spoke, revived or demonstrated a separate continuing consciousness. Steerious and Spider survived and departed; their departure does not reverse Blood Skunk's death.[^skunk-remains]
