**Role:** Companion assigned to Hope's separate Arc Five path by Brett in E102. **Names and mechanics:** Treat episode-specific ASR as provisional; do not infer a different identity from variations. [E102](../../episodes/E102.md).

Blood Skunk travels alongside **Hope, Steerious Business and Dr. Spider** through Sigil, the Gray Waste, River Styx and Pandemonium. **E112:** He summons four temporary animals but does not keep a permanent caravan of all four. **E113:** He relinquishes the **left little toe** as Styx ferry payment; the wound heals, but regrowth is **not shown**. Both he and Spider become gray and emotionally altered in the Gray Waste; their colors restore later in Pandemonium. [E112](../../episodes/E112.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md).

In **E116** he volunteers his **outer ears** for Hope's ring, is downed in the failed cargo-ship raid but **healed and alive** when the party withdraws. These should not be recast as permanent death or a successful ship capture. [E116](../../episodes/E116.md) · [Hope](hope-akmenos.md).

## September 2020 bonus — creator-described appearance

In [BONUS-2020-09](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-09.md), **~00:31:41–00:33:42**, **Scott imagines red/crimson blood**, patchy pale skin, excessively red lips, an unsettling grin and shuddering breath: a **demonic being with clownlike traits**, despite his use of clown tropes. These are **retrospective creator descriptions**, not an observed wound or new played transformation. Candy-cane/frosting blood and the blood-filled nose are banter. The **~32:16–32:22** clause recognized **`not the holiday` / `not the the harlequinari`** is unresolved **within that bonus**, so it establishes neither membership nor nonmembership there. In the later **played [E113](../../episodes/E113.md)**, Blood Skunk explicitly calls himself Harlequinari and says he has left their society and no longer serves the Council of Clowns. This supports his identity and an estranged relationship, not common traits for every Harlequinari or the composition of every council seat.
