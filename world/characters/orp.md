# Orp — cyclops and Great Fang of Razak Val

**Status through E063:** **Killed by Vorel in corrected E062**, after an immediate scaling; replaced as Great Fang of Razak Val by Vorel. At **E060** he was still alive, with a sunrise duel *scheduled but not yet fought*. Name `Orp` established as working display; processed ASR not independently listened.

Orp first appears during the **Slavetown conflict** as an enormous and dangerous cyclops associated with a gemstone/necklace dispute, royal rumors and lethal violence. Later, the adventurers send him away or redirect his path in hopes that he will cease endangering their people. See [E014](../../episodes/E014.md) and other early [Slavetown](../places/slavetown.md) dossiers.

He reappears at **Razak Val** in [E059](../../episodes/E059.md), having become **Great Fang**, apparently accepted/adopted by the **Fengar ruling clan**. **Mirion Fengar** explains that his own father died challenging Orp in a scaling duel and that the clan retained influence by making the cyclops its leader. Orp **keeps chained human captives as food and kills captives before the party**. He recognizes Vorel/Nash as friends and initially objects to hurting Vorel.

In [E060](../../episodes/E060.md), while Vorel and **Diptak** debate the custom and officially raise a **scaling challenge**, **Orp kills Diptak with a single direct blow**. The advisor Mirion then guides the party into clan politics. Orp **accepts a formal sunrise duel at the feast** but **remains alive and undefeated through the episode's ending**. His professed friendship and lack of political sophistication do **not** erase his observed killings or reduce the prisoner system to hearsay.

In **[E062](../../episodes/E062.md)**, Vorel refuses to wait for the arranged sunrise duel while Orp's live captives remain at risk. Hope's single rocket blasts both combatants out of Cave Fengar. Vorel battles the cyclops before an assembling crowd, hurls the **Eye of Groxl's flaming shield** into his eye, and **kills Orp directly**. **Omnom and the gathered lizardfolk recognize Vorel as new Great Fang**. The cyclops is not merely wounded or resting at the E062 ending.

In **[E063](../../episodes/E063.md)**, Orp's corpse is **butchered for food by the settlement**; the party retrieves a **Necklace of Fireballs with five remaining beads/charges** from its earlier concealment on his body. This recovered item is **not** the Eye of Groxl, and its custody needs ordinary inventory tracking. Meanwhile Vorel begins a program to free Orp's **living human captives**, but the hundreds held are **not all verified released by E063 cutoff**.

**Unsettled:** Razak Val's population, precise duration of his reign, complete accounting of Orp's human victims and surviving captives, and ultimate fate of the dismantled feeding system. [Vorel](vorel.md) · [Diptak](diptak.md) · [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md).
