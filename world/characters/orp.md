# Orp — cyclops and Great Fang of Razak Val

Orp was an enormous cyclops whose path crossed that of [Vorel](vorel.md) and his companions during the violent struggles around [Slavetown](../places/slavetown.md). The travelers once tried to steer him away from harming others, but his apparent friendliness toward familiar faces did little to restrain his appetite or his capacity for killing.[^orp-early]

## A friend and a feared ruler

When Vorel next encountered Orp, the cyclops ruled the lizardfolk settlement of [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md) as its **Great Fang**. The Fengar clan had brought him into its political order after the previous Great Fang died in a contest against him. Mirion Fengar explained the arrangement as a way for the family to retain influence. Orp welcomed the people he knew, yet accepted chained human prisoners supplied to him as food and killed captives openly. His uncomplicated affection for Vorel did not make his reign harmless.[^orp-rule]

The warrior [Diptak](diptak.md) demanded that Vorel replace this ruler through the traditional duel of *scaling*. Orp resisted fighting someone he regarded as a friend. When Diptak objected, Orp struck and killed him in one blow. At the subsequent feast, the cyclops agreed to a formal contest at sunrise; that promised duel did not take place as scheduled.[^orp-challenge]

## The fall of the Great Fang

Vorel would not leave living captives in Orp's power until morning. He confronted the cyclops sooner, while the Fengar court's politics were breaking apart. Hope's rocket devastated the gathering before the two fought outside. Vorel drove a burning shield from the **Eye of Groxl** into Orp's eye and killed him before witnesses. Lizardfolk including Omnom recognized Vorel as the new Great Fang; this was an actual change of ruler rather than another boast about a title.[^orp-fall]

## Current status

**Orp died in the battle with Vorel.** His body was later butchered by the settlement, and a **Necklace of Fireballs** with five remaining charges was recovered from where it had been concealed on him. The necklace was not the Eye of Groxl. Vorel began releasing the human captives, but their complete liberation and the lasting reform of Razak Val's feeding system were not established at that point.[^orp-aftermath]
