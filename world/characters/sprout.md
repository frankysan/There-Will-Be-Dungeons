# Sprout — Nash Maggard's speaking shoulder companion

## Introduction

Sprout was a tiny, living mouth and eye that appeared on [Nash Maggard's](nash-maggard.md) shoulder after his severely damaged humanoid body regenerated. Though attached to Nash and dependent on him for food and movement, Sprout could speak, react, ask questions and express preferences of its own. Its origins were a mystery to Nash and the companions, and neither a proven species nor a family relationship explained its sudden arrival.[^sprout-intro]

## A new voice in the cabin

After a battle with Hell's forces aboard Fate Six, Stanley's *Regenerate* had restored Nash's humanoid body. During a shared rest with [Vorel](vorel.md), Nash heard a small voice ask for the light to be switched off. He discovered a face-like protrusion on one shoulder, opposite the older small additional hand he already possessed. Sprout had an eye, sparse hairs and a mouth containing about four teeth. It blinked, spoke, ate and bit Nash's finger when prodded. It had **no separate full body or demonstrated limbs**, and was not an imaginary figure or merely the old baby hand speaking.[^sprout-discovery]

The little being said it knew almost nothing, felt it had been alive for only minutes and wanted food. Nash offered vegetable peels, then grapes, which proved more welcome. Sprout wondered what a book, a name or a father was, and asked whether Nash might be its father. Nash gave the creature the name **Sprout**, which it accepted. When Nash considered what to do with an unexpected additional being on his body, he explicitly promised **not to cut Sprout out**. Nash introduced it to Hope, Stanley and Vorel; its questions and curiosity continued, but no explanation of the new life was established.[^sprout-name]

## The journey through the shared dream

During the next voyage a green mind-fluff parasite struck the companions and induced a shared dream. When the party was rescued, one of the parasites was found with a tentacle **in Sprout's separate shoulder mouth**. This confirmed that Sprout remained physically present on Nash outside the dream. Nash's involuntary waking burst of fire destroyed the parasites; the source did not describe Sprout suffering a permanent new injury. Sprout was still attached as Fate Six reached Mars.[^sprout-parasite]

## A curious meeting with Nash's family

On the Mars terraforming platform, Nash reunited with [Angelashi](angelashi.md) and their four children. Sprout addressed the household independently and asked whether Angelashi was also its wife. Nash and Angelashi rejected that idea. The exchange showed Sprout trying to understand family relationships, **not proof that it was Angelashi's spouse, one of the couple's children or another Nash**. Its presence and questions were witnessed, while the nature of its connection to Nash remained unknown.[^sprout-family]

Later discussion of whether the creature's mouth or Nash's other extra hand might aid spellcasting was hypothetical; no separate trained spellcasting action by Sprout was demonstrated. Nothing in these scenes confirmed that it was the product of the Principal's experiments, the dragon-metal heart, a distinct clone or an expected consequence of *Regenerate*.[^sprout-theories]

## Current status

**Sprout was last directly shown alive, speaking and physically attached to Nash at the Mars station in S3E03.** A later S3E05 conversation considered potential use of its mouth, without demonstrating a new ability or independent change in condition. Its ultimate species, origin, kinship, long-term survival and ability to live apart from Nash remain unresolved.[^sprout-status]
