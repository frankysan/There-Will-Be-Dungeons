This priestess of the **Tidebearers** at the rebuilt T'ietkalla **Fishbird/Malhya church** mentors **Stanley** in magical practice and beliefs concerning the **savior gods** opposed to the Weirlords. In [E056](../../episodes/E056.md) she responds to his plea and uses **Greater Restoration** and diamond dust to reverse **Vorel's ghost-inflicted aging**, then learns of **Hope and Nobiery's concealed demonic bargains**. She is horrified and says the citizens deserve to know. **Nobiery commands undead “47 Carnage” and “Theresa” to attack her** at the cliffhanger.

[E057](../../episodes/E057.md) resolves her fate: **Carnage's bite does land**, but Stanley **heals the wound** and Hope demands the undead cease. Emenil survives and tentatively seeks an explanation for the secrecy. Nobiery admits using the **Soulbroker** while saying his people owed their preservation to **Matamaru**. Emenil's partial agreement to learn more **does not prove wholehearted ideological acceptance or agreement to hide the truth forever**.

She is **alive at the E058 royal audience**, and in [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) volunteers the **Tidebearers/hospital** to screen returned Dusthill survivors. Her ability to cast healing spells is observed; her account of gods, salvation and sacred history remains attributed theology, not neutral cosmology.

**E103 return:** Hope **finds Emenil alive** at T'ietkalla's Fishbird church and hears her account of a struggling regency: **Nobiery's departure from local government, Celibus Stump's appointment as Principal-backed chancellor, attacks on inbound rail supplies and rumors of three political opponents disappearing**. These are **Emenil's reports**, not witnessed arrests or proved perpetrators. The hooded Voice **orders Hope to kill Emenil, but Hope refuses**; the priestess remains alive at this episode's cutoff. ## E124 — first rite, petition and time beneath the lake

**Emenil actually leads all four through fish-flake ingestion, water breathing and a shared endless-water encounter**. She admits this is her **first performance**, recounting her late husband **Lelu/Leilu/Lelou/Lilu (?)**, reverend killed under vampire persecution, as the source of flakes/instructions. **History is her testimony**. She is frightened by the kraken, then delighted by winged **Malhya**, petitions for Stanley/Vorel and witnesses their **explicit cure**. All return to Harbor lake. **She stays underwater but answers Stanley's Message alive**, asking for time and saying she will be fine. No resurrection, romance or renounced faith is established. ## E123 — warning heard, refuge defended and lake communion begun

Emenil **receives all four at her Harbor home**, initially avoiding Stanley's eyes. He warns of Diontalus targeting her/the community, while admitting he cannot prove she is not already possessed; **she is not revealed as Diontalus**. A GM name slip is immediately corrected to Emenil. She challenges plans to leave Elhandar's hosts exposed and insists **Stanley warn his people's leader**. She says she would follow Hope's command to evacuate, but **Stanley and Hope instead agree the refugees should retain their home**.

Emenil still invokes **Malhaya/Fishbird** despite her E117 destruction of the shrine. She proposes communion at the lake, explaining the old church baths and claiming a strong planar connection here. **She disrobes, enters the lake and invites Stanley**, with the GM clarifying the invitation includes everyone. Stanley follows/disrobes; **no Fishbird manifestation, answer, cure, shrine repair, romantic commitment or completed shared rite occurs before fade-out**.
