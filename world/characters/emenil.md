# Emenil / Eminel — Tidebearer priestess

**Name:** `Emenil/Eminel` is provisional ASR; final spelling not listened or author-confirmed. **Status:** **Directly seen alive E117 at the new Elhandar tiefling refuge**, after a survival report in E115. **Scope:** played E055–E059, E103, E117, with E115 report kept distinct.

This priestess of the **Tidebearers** at the rebuilt Tietkalla **Fishbird/Malhya church** mentors **Stanley** in magical practice and beliefs concerning the **savior gods** opposed to the Weirlords. In [E056](../../episodes/E056.md) she responds to his plea and uses **Greater Restoration** and diamond dust to reverse **Vorel's ghost-inflicted aging**, then learns of **Hope and Nobiery's concealed demonic bargains**. She is horrified and says the citizens deserve to know. **Nobiery commands undead “47 Carnage” and “Theresa” to attack her** at the cliffhanger.

[E057](../../episodes/E057.md) resolves her fate: **Carnage's bite does land**, but Stanley **heals the wound** and Hope demands the undead cease. Emenil survives and tentatively seeks an explanation for the secrecy. Nobiery admits using the **Soulbroker** while saying his people owed their preservation to **Matamaru**. Emenil's partial agreement to learn more **does not prove wholehearted ideological acceptance or agreement to hide the truth forever**.

She is **alive at the E058 royal audience**, and in [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) volunteers the **Tidebearers/hospital** to screen returned Dusthill survivors. Her ability to cast healing spells is observed; her account of gods, salvation and sacred history remains attributed theology, not neutral cosmology.

**E103 return:** Hope **finds Emenil alive** at Tietkalla's Fishbird church and hears her account of a struggling regency: **Nobiery's departure from local government, Celibus Stump's appointment as Principle-backed chancellor, attacks on inbound rail supplies and rumors of three political opponents disappearing**. These are **Emenil's reports**, not witnessed arrests or proved perpetrators. The hooded Voice **orders Hope to kill Emenil, but Hope refuses**; the priestess remains alive at this episode's cutoff. [E103 dossier](../../episodes/E103.md) · [ASR report](../../reports/E103_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

**E115 survival report:** During breakfast at the Elhandar cottage, **Nobieri tells Stanley Emenil has asked after him**, recalls their Fishbird prayers, and says she is suffering with the other refugees. **The tieflings are across the clearing**, and he invites a visit. **No actual E115 meeting, prayer scene or new spell by Emenil occurs**; survival/location is **Nobieri’s testimony**, with `Emanil / Emmanuelle` among the ASR alternatives. [E115](../../episodes/E115.md) · [comparison](../../reports/E115_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md), ASR-provisional.

**E117 — direct survivor, love declaration and shrine destroyed:** In [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) **Emenil actually attends the new Harbor community's council and fire/ash ceremony at Elhandar**, confirming Nobieri's E115 survival report. She sits near Stanley, watches **a flaming figure calling itself Abegir (?)** emerge from Nobieri's rite and tells the circle of her **Malhaya/Tidebearer faith** and prayer during Tietkalla's disaster. She advocates **water for all** as the foundation of peace; **no water miracle is performed**. After Hope formally names the community **the Harbor**, Emenil **invites Stanley to her cottage and expressly confesses that she loves him**. He says his survival is important to her and vice versa but **cannot return the love or begin a romance while disoriented about his identity**. After he leaves, he hears a crash; on a **Perception 29**, the GM confirms that **she smashed her Malhaya altar/statue and offering bowls**. **He does not witness the act directly or reenter**. The destruction establishes an emotional reaction, **not that Malhaya lost divinity or that Emenil has publicly converted faiths**. [ASR comparison](../../reports/E117_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). Name spelling still provisional.
