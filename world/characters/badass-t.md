# Badass T

## Introduction

Badass T is a masked guide hired through [Tobacho Fleur](tobacho-fleur.md) to lead travelers onward from [Sigil](../places/sigil.md). Bald, with a wandering eye and a breastplate fashioned from part of a vehicle, he presents an unsettling mixture of familiarity and urgency. He claims to know the companions, insists they move quickly and treats secrecy as a reason for violence.[^badass-introduction]

## A guide from another history

His first service to Nash, Stanley and Vorel includes killing the Mangler, a bounty hunter who proposes pursuing Hope. The companions do not order the attack. Badass T also defends removing witnesses when questioned about the frog vendor they leave behind, although that vendor's death is not directly established. He provides the notebook and ingredients for a strange portal rite, then guides the group aboard an airship whose cannons fire at their route behind them.[^badass-guide]

At First Nash's asteroid tower, he removes his mask and reveals himself as a disfigured counterpart of [Tedna](tedna.md). He says Suck Master Din tore away his lips and insists on the name Badass T. The youthful Tedna the companions knew died in T'ietkalla; this older man has a different history. He wants their help to save Hope, and remains at the tower when they leave. Vorel entrusts him with weapons and a belt associated with her.[^badass-identity]

## An insistent companion

Badass T later meets the reunited company on Planet Nash. Hope initially resists his wish to accompany her, but eventually allows him to join their next expedition. He returns Smokey's special-ammunition belt to her. Tower Nash and a visiting herald attribute his arrival to intervention across realities; their explanations do not make the entire mechanism known.[^badass-return]

He actually boards [Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md) for the voyage to Blast Belly Rock. He describes fighting with the Liberators and receiving warrior training, insists that he is now a man in his thirties and rejects being treated as the young Tedna Hope remembers. The others remain wary of his violence. At the Wet Whistle he stays behind as lookout while the companions investigate the bank. They later find him among captives held by marauders, and Hope frees him from his cage.[^badass-voyage]

## Beneath the prison pyramid

At Pentel Weiss, an attempt to present him as a wanted inmate fails when the warden finds no registration. Hope asks him to test the intake shaft, and he agrees, jumping into the darkness with knives drawn. He survives. When the others eventually descend, he helps throw a rope to them during their escape from the pool's tentacled creature. His recklessness has brought danger, but he is also capable of practical help.[^badass-prison]

The apparent residents of the underground camp become hostile werefolk. Badass T charges into the fight, suffers a bear's bite and severe injuries, and is hurled against a cage or wall. [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) stabilizes him, leaving him unconscious but breathing. Fur begins growing from beneath his mask. Grandma Tilly attributes it to lycanthropy and promises that her people will look after him; the companions leave him in their care while continuing their search.[^badass-injury]

## Current status

Badass T is last seen alive, unconscious and stabilized in the werefolk camp beneath Pentel Weiss. The emerging fur is real, but the full course of the change is unknown. His companions subsequently escape the prison without bringing him along.

Whether he recovers safely, undergoes a complete transformation, regains his missing lips or ever rejoins Hope remains unresolved. Their intention to check on him does not become a known return. His separate life does not undo the original Tedna's death.[^badass-status]
