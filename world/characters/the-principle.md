# The Principle — Vladimir

**Identity:** Usually called **the Principle**, sometimes transcribed “Principal”; identified by an Elhandari high elf as **Vladimir** in E115. In S3E01 Qwen proposes the tentative **new surname “Trix”**, but this is not verified. **Role:** Ancient political/technological power connected to Slavetown, Tietkalla diplomacy and the Soulbroker pact. **Spoilers:** Through Wastes S3E02; this remains the SAME story arc initiated in Season Two. **Evidence:** Provisional ASR; no independent listening.

## Activity and influence

The E013 GM briefing connects the evolution of Pristine into slaveholding **Slavetown** with agents of the Principle and Oilbeard interests. In the June 2019 cast Q&A, Beau says he conceived this figure from the opening episode, describes him as very old and warns against a simple lich identification. These are distinct types of evidence; his actual nature remains uncertain. [E013](../../episodes/E013.md) · [June Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md) · [Slavetown](../places/slavetown.md).

At Tietkalla the Principle or his envoy negotiates prisoner returns, labor and possible rail trade. **Actual Dusthill survivors return in E059**, but a complete railway at that point is not shown. The Principle **physically extracts [Diontalus](diontalus.md) from Stanley into a crystal**, leaving the entity's subsequent fate open. [E055](../../episodes/E055.md) · [E058](../../episodes/E058.md) · [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [Tietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md).

His political reach and claims become important again in Arc Five. In E106 he speaks with Hope at Kaiser Casino and gives a version of ancient pre-Scorching history and **Lich Project** experiments via Brett's illusions; these are claims, not independently inspected archives. He **actually appears at Elhandar in E115**, visibly exercising supernatural abilities, and **claims** responsibility for a great city explosion and newly acquired demigodhood. The High Elf calls him Vladimir and speaks of earlier scientific disagreement. Neither an explosion's cause nor divine status is proven merely by the Principle's self-account. [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [E115](../../episodes/E115.md).

## E128 — the Soulbroker accuses, a new threat visibly enters Earth

At ~01:13–01:28, **the Soulbroker privately tells Stanley** a revised account of the Principle's ancient relationship to the Weir: he allegedly was once an **Earth human who enlisted Weir help during the dragon catastrophe**, broke the bargain and later **shielded the world against Weir entrance**, before turning to a **new aspiration to become a god**. These historical, intentional and metaphysical claims are **the Soulbroker's interested testimony** and must not override the E122 black dragon's different framing as independently verified canon. The GM then **actually shows a purple rift, a colossal gear-scaled creature burrowing into Earth and a massive sandstorm**; the incursion is played, **its alleged cause in the Principle's spiritual protections is not independently demonstrated**. Stanley tells the party about his private conversation afterward. **No direct E128 appearance, achieved ascension, death or final confrontation with the Principle occurs**. ## S3E02 — actually steals Hand, claims Nash as “Jesus Chrome,” escapes alive

In [S3E02](../../episodes/S3E02.md), the Principle **ACTUALLY GRAPPLES Hope and steals her physically attached supernatural HAND OF THE VOICE** through liquid-chrome contact and a contained explosion, leaving her at **0 HP with death saves**. He **ejects faceless, incomplete warm bodies from chrome torso to replenish himself**, but their identity/importance is **NOT established**. **Nash's 53-point dragon ACID BREATH is not resisted and actually melts his chrome**, Vorel's infernal ship cannons hit hard, and ***Stanley's Disintegrate* 74 FORCE** tears off a chrome hand/shoulder. **The Principle is NOT defeated or permanently damaged beyond recovery**, and escapes through a self-opened **PORTAL TAKING HOPE'S STOLEN HAND** (~01:04). He tells Nash to **FIND HIM AT SOLAR MINES** for a new world of steel and offers him a position among his generals.

The Principle **DIRECTLY CLAIMS “I AM YOUR FATHER” and calls Nash “the son of God, JESUS CHROME,”** alleging he found broken Nash in the desert, remade him and set him on the “silver road” to preach the Steel God. **Nash ACTUALLY REJECTS HIM**; no performed parentage test, Nash's prior original father death verification or independent evidence that the villain is literal biological father. Parakeet may insert **“Robert Nash”**, Qwen does not confirm; do NOT canonize that extra name. The already-attested first name **Vladimir** remains; *Trix* from prior Qwen is unverified surname. The Principle **IS ALIVE WITH HAND** at episode cutoff, and his claims about Tobacho/Weir/Jesus Chrome are **interested NPC testimony**. ## Season Three — physically manifests as crowned chrome being

In [S3E01](../../episodes/S3E01.md), the Principle **actually steps through a portal aboard Fate Six** while a Hell crew is already fighting the four main heroes. His chrome body has luminous **blue lines and ten red rings along his fingers**. He **physically alters his form**, growing taller and revealing a **crowned skull-shaped head**, publicly declaiming new titles. The character had already been identified by his first name **Vladimir** in E115. **Qwen's new transcription suggests “Vladimir Trix” at ~02:17, while Parakeet segments only “tricks”; the surname is NOT verified and must not be made a canonical identity without original audio or creator evidence.** His self-applied epithets include a claim to be **“father to Jesus Chrome”** and slayer of flesh gods; **spoken boasts are not independent confirmations of their historical truth**.

He **actually casts *Power Word Stun* on Stanley**, leaving the bard stunned. Hope defies a **private panicked warning from her hooded Voice** and stabs the Principle three times with magical dagger and cold Hand; his chrome body resists the cold damage, though Hope still harms him. Nash, now adult black dragon, actually bites him but **only two points of acid penetrate** and his claw attacks miss. The Principle **claims HE instructed Tobacho to retrieve the Hand of the Voice originally**, asks Hope to surrender it, and says he had hoped she would retrieve the missing part. **Hope refuses**; he remains alive, active and physically confronting her on trapped Fate Six at episode end. **The historical commissioning allegation is new interested testimony**, not a replayed Tobacho scene or proof of the Voice's origin or alliance. The Hell attackers explicitly report **surprise** at his arrival, so their service to Brett/Asmodeus does not automatically mean they knew his plan. ## Opponents, motives and outstanding questions

**E121–E122 bring claims from other interested witnesses, not a new appearance by the Principle.** Diontalus says the Principle helped him recover; E121 shows Diontalus physically manifested and escaped, **not how the earlier crystal was opened**. In E122 the dying black dragon calls the Principle **the Weir's remaining agent/pawn**, and says he commissioned the **Dragon Sons** to create **an invulnerable body**, with Nash among the experiments. Neither claim is independently verified by a laboratory record or the Principle himself. Stanley asks why the Weir would then want their own agent destroyed; **the dragon leaves that contradiction unresolved**. [E121](../../episodes/E121.md) · [E122](../../episodes/E122.md) · [Black dragon](imprisoned-black-dragon.md).

Hope, Nash and Stanley signed a **Soulbroker compact aimed at the Principle**; Vorel refused. **Alvert** later alleges the Principle was a CHIDS scientist associated with human-processing facilities; physical CHIDS experiments exist, but that does not independently validate the entirety of Alvert's account. The father of Stanley records speculative future-Principle/Weir experiments in E118—**a found theory, not a confirmed discovery**.
