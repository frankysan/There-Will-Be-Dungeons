# The Principle — Vladimir

**Identity:** Usually called **the Principle**, sometimes transcribed “Principal”; identified by an Elhandari high elf as **Vladimir** in E115. In S3E01 Qwen proposes the tentative **new surname “Trix”**, but this is not verified. **Role:** Ancient political/technological power connected to Slavetown, Tietkalla diplomacy and the Soulbroker pact. **Spoilers:** Played Wastes through S3E05 plus explicitly attributed May 2022 cast/GM Q&A; this remains the SAME story arc initiated in Season Two. **Evidence:** Provisional ASR; no independent listening.

## May 2022 BONUS — GM confirms original Tobacho Hand commission and uses “lich,” but NOT literal paternity

In [BONUS-2022-05 — May 2022 listener Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2022-05.md) **~00:13:14–00:17:48**, **GM Beau DIRECTLY EXPLAINS as out-of-game creative intent** that **THE PRINCIPLE ORIGINALLY HIRED TOBACHO FLEUR TO ACQUIRE THE HAND OF THE VOICE**. Hope's obtaining the living Hand and proceeding to seek its other piece surprised/impressed the antagonist; by **played S3E01–S3E02**, GM wanted him **TO TAKE THE HAND and depart with it**, not necessarily execute the party or die there. Beau admits dragon Nash's offensive abilities and **Vorel's Hell-ship cannon** damaged the Principle **more than expected** and made the scripted-looking exit precarious, but GM chose not to force a predetermined resolution. The prior [S3E01](../../episodes/S3E01.md) claim **“I commissioned Tobacho”** was CHARACTER testimony then, and now receives **SEPARATE GM RETROSPECTIVE CORROBORATION**, **not an actual recorded historical commissioning conversation**. The **S3E02 theft/portal escape are actual played facts** and do not become separate events in this bonus.

In the same exchange **Beau explicitly calls THE PRINCIPLE “a lich”** and says liches have methods of avoiding final death; this is **GM-level taxonomic/plot framing** and narrows the previously unresolved species category from the **June 2019 Q&A's warning against simplistic lich labeling**. It **DOES NOT PROVIDE a phylactery location, 5e statblock, death/return mechanics, exact past transformation or test of permanent mortality**. He describes a plan to **CHROME the Prime Material/cosmos and assault/chrome the heavens**, but says **the ULTIMATE reason still has not been revealed**, including to some players despite comments to livestream viewers. Importantly, when Beau also recounts **Nash as “Jesus Chrome” and alleged flesh-vat child of the Principle (~00:08–00:09)**, the cast themselves still qualify this **“if you believe the tales” and “allegedly.”** **DO NOT CANONIZE biological paternity, Nash's supposed prior life, the Principle as actual ascended god or the full universe's eventual fate**. **No Principle physically appears or dies in this bonus; latest played S3E05 remains outside the CLOSED north gate**. ## E128 — the Soulbroker accuses, a new threat visibly enters Earth

At ~01:13–01:28, **the Soulbroker privately tells Stanley** a revised account of the Principle's ancient relationship to the Weir: he allegedly was once an **Earth human who enlisted Weir help during the dragon catastrophe**, broke the bargain and later **shielded the world against Weir entrance**, before turning to a **new aspiration to become a god**. These historical, intentional and metaphysical claims are **the Soulbroker's interested testimony** and must not override the E122 black dragon's different framing as independently verified canon. The GM then **actually shows a purple rift, a colossal gear-scaled creature burrowing into Earth and a massive sandstorm**; the incursion is played, **its alleged cause in the Principle's spiritual protections is not independently demonstrated**. Stanley tells the party about his private conversation afterward. **No direct E128 appearance, achieved ascension, death or final confrontation with the Principle occurs**. ## S3E02 — actually steals Hand, claims Nash as “Jesus Chrome,” escapes alive

In [S3E02](../../episodes/S3E02.md), the Principle **ACTUALLY GRAPPLES Hope and steals her physically attached supernatural HAND OF THE VOICE** through liquid-chrome contact and a contained explosion, leaving her at **0 HP with death saves**. He **ejects faceless, incomplete warm bodies from chrome torso to replenish himself**, but their identity/importance is **NOT established**. **Nash's 53-point dragon ACID BREATH is not resisted and actually melts his chrome**, Vorel's infernal ship cannons hit hard, and ***Stanley's Disintegrate* 74 FORCE** tears off a chrome hand/shoulder. **The Principle is NOT defeated or permanently damaged beyond recovery**, and escapes through a self-opened **PORTAL TAKING HOPE'S STOLEN HAND** (~01:04). He tells Nash to **FIND HIM AT SOLAR MINES** for a new world of steel and offers him a position among his generals.

The Principle **DIRECTLY CLAIMS “I AM YOUR FATHER” and calls Nash “the son of God, JESUS CHROME,”** alleging he found broken Nash in the desert, remade him and set him on the “silver road” to preach the Steel God. **Nash ACTUALLY REJECTS HIM**; no performed parentage test, Nash's prior original father death verification or independent evidence that the villain is literal biological father. Parakeet may insert **“Robert Nash”**, Qwen does not confirm; do NOT canonize that extra name. The already-attested first name **Vladimir** remains; *Trix* from prior Qwen is unverified surname. The Principle **IS ALIVE WITH HAND** at episode cutoff, and his claims about Tobacho/Weir/Jesus Chrome are **interested NPC testimony**. ## Season Three — physically manifests as crowned chrome being

In [S3E01](../../episodes/S3E01.md), the Principle **actually steps through a portal aboard Fate Six** while a Hell crew is already fighting the four main heroes. His chrome body has luminous **blue lines and ten red rings along his fingers**. He **physically alters his form**, growing taller and revealing a **crowned skull-shaped head**, publicly declaiming new titles. The character had already been identified by his first name **Vladimir** in E115. **Qwen's new transcription suggests “Vladimir Trix” at ~02:17, while Parakeet segments only “tricks”; the surname is NOT verified and must not be made a canonical identity without original audio or creator evidence.** His self-applied epithets include a claim to be **“father to Jesus Chrome”** and slayer of flesh gods; **spoken boasts are not independent confirmations of their historical truth**.

He **actually casts *Power Word Stun* on Stanley**, leaving the bard stunned. Hope defies a **private panicked warning from her hooded Voice** and stabs the Principle three times with magical dagger and cold Hand; his chrome body resists the cold damage, though Hope still harms him. Nash, now adult black dragon, actually bites him but **only two points of acid penetrate** and his claw attacks miss. The Principle **claims HE instructed Tobacho to retrieve the Hand of the Voice originally**, asks Hope to surrender it, and says he had hoped she would retrieve the missing part. **Hope refuses**; he remains alive, active and physically confronting her on trapped Fate Six at episode end. **The historical commissioning allegation is new interested testimony**, not a replayed Tobacho scene or proof of the Voice's origin or alliance. The Hell attackers explicitly report **surprise** at his arrival, so their service to Brett/Asmodeus does not automatically mean they knew his plan. In E122 the dying black dragon calls the Principle **the Weir's remaining agent/pawn**, and says he commissioned the **Dragon Sons** to create **an invulnerable body**, with Nash among the experiments. Neither claim is independently verified by a laboratory record or the Principle himself. Stanley asks why the Weir would then want their own agent destroyed; **the dragon leaves that contradiction unresolved**.
