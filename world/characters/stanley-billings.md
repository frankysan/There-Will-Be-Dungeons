# Stanley Billings

**Portrayed by:** Jon. **Identity:** Half-elf, persuasive bard and later healer, once unwilling host to **[Diontalus](diontalus.md)**. **Birth name:** A form resembling **Valier/Vale Ornath (?)** is acknowledged by Stanley in **E115**; spelling remains ASR-provisional. He **chooses to remain Stanley Billings**. **Spoilers:** Main campaign through **E122** and separately sourced Q&As. **Evidence:** Processed transcripts, not audio-verified.

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [Diontalus — independent profile](diontalus.md) · [World portal](../README.md).

## Identity and personal outlook

Stanley begins in **Dusthill**, championing civic institutions and the rule of law while often operating through charm, performance and calculated persuasion. He helps resolve the water crisis, navigates Slavetown's predatory politics and **voluntarily signs the Soulbroker compact** in E023 with Hope and Nash, while Vorel refuses. Across the Wastes he comes to question whether hierarchy and law, as enforced by the **Principle**, can coexist with justice when those institutions defend enslavement. In a June 2019 Q&A, Jon describes Stanley's growing **guilt, atonement and distrust of easy civic order** as his *player reading*, not private information known automatically to the party. [E001](../../episodes/E001.md) · [E023](../../episodes/E023.md) · [June 2019 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md).

Stanley's childhood and original identity emerge gradually. In E115 the Elhandari admiral identifies him using a name heard as **Valier/Vale Ornath**, reports a family-record match and names his father **Dalit/Daliot Ornath (?)**; Stanley acknowledges the birth name but keeps his chosen one. His father reportedly died on Earth; no remains are recovered, and the admiral's account of elven souls is **testimony**. Stanley **declines an offered memory-sharing trance** rather than instantly learning his entire past. [E089](../../episodes/E089.md) · [E115](../../episodes/E115.md).

## Diontalus: violation, culpability and separation

In **E029**, Stanley meets a luminous being in the oil sands, **refuses its offer**, yet is **forcibly joined** with it. **Diontalus** speaks and acts independently within Stanley's body; his **E045 murder of George Miller** and **E050 murder of P0D and Dashington** should **not** be recorded as deliberate choices by Stanley. Stanley nonetheless witnesses harm, grieves and feels moral responsibility for actions he could not prevent. Later Beau discloses that **Buttons loved Stanley** and planned to take him to the **Arcanum** to remove Diontalus. That is retrospectively revealed GM intent; no such rescue is played. [E029](../../episodes/E029.md) · [E045](../../episodes/E045.md) · [E050](../../episodes/E050.md) · [Buttons](buttons.md) · [Diontalus](diontalus.md).

During the **three-month post-coronation interval** Stanley helps establish medical and religious institutions and privately studies possible separation. Diontalus remains unusually quiet before acting violently again in E057. **In E059 the Principle visibly extracts Diontalus into a glowing crystal**. Stanley continues without the controlling intruder; **the crystal's permanent fate is unverified**. This extraction is **not** a second Soulbroker compact, a confirmed space launch, or proof the archfey is dead. Further Diontalus history, allegations and Q&A are owned by the [separate profile](diontalus.md). [E055](../../episodes/E055.md) · [E057](../../episodes/E057.md) · [E059](../../episodes/E059.md).

## The four story arcs — played development

### Dusthill through Hope's coronation (E001–E054)

Stanley forms a tense but durable traveling partnership with **Nash, Hope and Vorel**. The group navigates Dusthill, Slavetown, the Black Mine, Château Weir and Tietkalla's underground kingdom. His relationship with the speaking cat Buttons deteriorates toward the **E049 ambush**; Stanley **casts Speak with Dead on Buttons in E050**, a limited postmortem conversation rather than revival. He fights to restore **Hope and Vorel after petrification in E051**, and is present at **Hope's real coronation E054**. The history of the body-sharing supernatural threat is recorded on [Diontalus](diontalus.md), rather than merged with Stanley's own decisions. [E049](../../episodes/E049.md)–[E054](../../episodes/E054.md) · [Tietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md).

### Rebuilding, Razak Val and the CHIDS sacrifice (E055–E081)

Stanley assists with Tietkalla's hospitals, medical library and **Tidebearer/Fishbird** worship. During **Razak Val** he questions the moral costs of experimentation and prison abuse. Following **Follow's death in E066**, he **promises to save Sira**. The struggle with mind flayers at **Kervalon/CHIDS** reveals his resourcefulness: he uses **Feather Fall** during dangerous falls, helps the party through laboratories, and survives the **curse imposed by Cerinthala's amulet**. He and Nash receive **Rings of Spell Storing**; he also takes custody of recovered equipment and assists Vorel's **Monster Mender limb reattachment in E078**. The effects of specific spells are preserved in the episode dossiers rather than promoted to permanent character powers. [E055](../../episodes/E055.md) · [E066](../../episodes/E066.md) · [E069](../../episodes/E069.md)–[E078](../../episodes/E078.md).

In **E081**, Stanley offers himself as a possible substitute to save Sira and uses **Augury invoking Moha** before the four companions finally **join hands in the crimson Traversal Drive beam**. **Sira escapes with Eddie**, and the GM explicitly declares all four protagonists **dead at that episode's cutoff**. Stanley's own agency and sacrifice are demonstrated; the already separated Diontalus is **not shown sharing that fate**. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [CHIDS crisis](../HISTORY_CHIDS_DRIVE.md).

### Sigil: death, revival and his changing faith (E082–E097)

Stanley's inert body appears in **Sigil E082** and is taken into a trickster church's crypt. A priest identifies a potential **Weir-linked inscription**; his explanations about the afterlife remain **in-world testimony**. **In E088 Stanley himself chooses to step out of a formless void and emerges alive through a mirror**, while the earlier body stays on a slab. This is played restoration, not a retroactive statement that E081 never happened or proof the Soulbroker pact has ended. [E082](../../episodes/E082.md) · [E083](../../episodes/E083.md) · [E088](../../episodes/E088.md).

Stanley explores Sigil politics and portals, obtains information in the Hive and continues a difficult relationship with **Emenil** and his own earlier convictions. He helps prepare a route toward the besieged **White Tower**, discusses safer rescue plans and develops a bond with **Knight Commander Alentris Vayne (?)**. In **E096** he is cured of the tower's plague through a performed ritual; **E097** opens the restricted library and withdraws the group's sentence. A prospective peaceful life is **not yet chosen**. [E089](../../episodes/E089.md)–[E097](../../episodes/E097.md) · [White Tower](../places/white-tower.md).

### White Tower, separate paths and the return to Elhandar (E098–E118)

During the White Tower siege, Stanley repeatedly rescues **Alentris**. In **E101**, after falling, he **rolls a natural 20 on a death save**, regains consciousness, heals Alentris and helps hold the tower. Hope **attaches the Hand of the Voice** and leaves the original party; this ends Arc Four and begins Arc Five **inside E101**. [E100](../../episodes/E100.md) · [E101](../../episodes/E101.md).

In **E102**, Stanley **casts Modify Memory on Alentris**, making her believe the party left the Fist behind; this deception troubles Vorel and the others. The party's subsequent airship and portal journeys bring Stanley through **Sigil**, Tobacho's bargain and the **alternate First Nash tower**. He **opens the Fist case and passes the gauntlet to Tobacho E106** (the living Hand is already attached to Hope). At the alternate tower he receives counterparts of **Diplomacy, his megaphone and dictionary**, **not possessions reclaimed from the dead original Stanley of that timeline**. [E102](../../episodes/E102.md) · [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [E110](../../episodes/E110.md).

After Nash teleports the travelers back to ruined **Tietkalla** in E110, Stanley helps survivors and Elhandari rescuers. **General Carthax disintegrates Squirts and Melgor in E111**; Stanley makes Carthax **vomit out Bok Bok** with *Command* after an internal blast. He defends the Elhandar-bound ship from undead in E114, rescues Vorel and uses a fire-retardant canister to seal a breached hull; **the elf who collides with him in decompression is not rescued**. Arrival at **Elhandar E115** confirms his ancestry claim while leaving his father's destiny unsettled. [E110](../../episodes/E110.md) · [E111](../../episodes/E111.md) · [E114](../../episodes/E114.md) · [E115](../../episodes/E115.md).

**E117:** The four protagonists physically reunite in Elhandar, but **Stanley distrusts Hope**, condemns her continued Hand, refuses to treat her as his queen and publicly questions his former faith. **Emenil confesses love**; Stanley values her yet **declines a romantic commitment** until he understands himself. Her destruction of her Malhaya shrine is heard after he departs; he does not witness a full reconciliation. [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) · [Emenil](emenil.md).

**E118:** At the Elhandar cottage a doctor **actually hands Stanley his father's ornate key**. With Hope's help reciting a childhood poem, he opens a **hidden, physically accessible third-floor study**. He takes possession of the **Book of Adjacencies** and **Mask of Elton IV/Eltenvor (?)**. The journal's entries describe a presumed future **N4SH**, the Weir and strange time-adjacency physics, **but remain his father's unverified hypotheses and testimony**; the mask's powerful and dangerous rules are **described but not activated or attuned**. A young elf **Dilo/Dilenoria (?)** asks to become an apprentice; Stanley tells him to return tomorrow, not joining him immediately. Earlier, **Stanley restrains wounded Omnom with Hold Person** when the lizardfolk charges Hope, and no blow lands. At the cutoff **Stanley studies alone**, without traveling to Earth or solving the journal's chronology. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Book](../technology/book-of-adjacencies.md) · [Mask](../technology/mask-of-elton-iv.md).

## Relationships, faith and significant equipment

**The original companions:** Stanley loves and disputes **Nash, Hope and Vorel**; his reaction to Hope's Hand is not erased by their physical E117 reunion. **Buttons** dies before his intended rescue can take place; **P0D and George** are murdered by Diontalus, with Stanley feeling shame. **Emenil** holds intimate importance but their future is uncertain. **Alentris** becomes a valued, complicated ally following the Sigil/White Tower trials. **Stanley's father** emerges chiefly through private letters, names and a hidden study; those writings are not automatically known to the whole party. [Buttons](buttons.md) · [Emenil](emenil.md) · [E117](../../episodes/E117.md)–[E118](../../episodes/E118.md).

**Objects and abilities:** Animated rapier **Diplomacy**, speaking megaphone and dictionary (alternate equivalents acquired in E110); past **spell-storage ring** and medical tools; **father's key, Book of Adjacencies and untested mask** E118. Former items, dropped weapons, consumed charges and transferred keys must be checked by episode and not treated as a single cumulative inventory. The **Zorander's Fist gauntlet** is no longer in the team's custody after E106; the **Hand** remains with Hope. [E078](../../episodes/E078.md) · [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [E110](../../episodes/E110.md) · [E118](../../episodes/E118.md).

## Authorial comments and open questions

Jon's later cast Q&As clarify the **chosen stage name**, Stanley's unrevealed backstory, his desire for a healing separation, his doubts about civic order and the possibility of an “elf side” artifact. These are **player intent or retrospective context**, not hidden scenes or a confirmed prior inventory. Beau says the four-person E081 sacrifice was unexpected. Stanley's **changed feelings after resurrection** are Jon's characterization, not a mechanically proven new soul. [March 2019 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md) · [April 2019 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-04.md) · [June 2019 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md) · [August 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-08.md).

**E119:** **Dilo returns** and Stanley explicitly allows him to **accompany this day's hunt as a provisional pupil**, instructing him on a “lemon tree” combat signal—not permanently enrolling him. More importantly, Stanley privately recounts to **Vorel** the **Principle's E115 whispered warning about a loose and returning old friend**, interpreting it as possibly **Diontalus**. He fears Dilo might be a new host but **states it could be the Principle's bluff**, and no possession is witnessed. On the hunt, when Vorel's **flaming fish-head lure actually ignites brush**, Stanley **uses *Prestidigitation* to snuff out the fire** as heavy footsteps approach. His book, mask and father's theory are **not tested**. [E115](../../episodes/E115.md) · [E119](../../episodes/E119.md) · [Dilo](dilenoria.md) · [Diontalus](diontalus.md).

**E120:** Stanley **actually *Polymorphs* to a Tyrannosaurus rex** and critically bites Chester in the hunt; **the creature is killed when Hope pulls it from a tree**, then Vorel rescues her. Later Stanley detects something wrong with six apparently friendly deer and ***Dispel Magic* reveals hostile disguised elves**. As he prepares ***Destructive Wave***, **Dilo activates a coordinated kidnapping**: Hope is sealed in a moving magical sphere and Dilo escapes. The wave **kills six hostile elves but not Dilo**. Stanley ***Polymorphs* into a giant eagle** to follow the sphere toward a mountain cave and guides the others there. **Dilo's betrayal is confirmed, his possible Diontalus identity/possession remains unverified**. [E120](../../episodes/E120.md) · [Dilo](dilenoria.md).

**E121 — confrontation with his former possessor:** The suspected Dilo figure openly knows Stanley's Elhandari name, threatens Hope and **reveals Diontalus's renewed agency**, claiming the **Principle helped him reconstruct**. Stanley ***Dispel Magics* the prison without effect**, negotiates a one-on-one duel and ***Polymorphs* into a giant ape**; his blow leaves Dilo's physical body motionless and **Diontalus's distinct blue ghost emerges**. Diontalus's scythe drains Stanley's **Strength/Dexterity by GM ruling**, while ***Destructive Wave*** hurts the apparition. Stanley ***Dimension Doors* to the sphere**, grabs the controller and calls **“lemon tree”** to bring friends into the fight; later **snaps the wand, but Hope remains imprisoned**. After an unfamiliar black dragon drives Diontalus off, Nash's involuntary dragon fire knocks Stanley to **0 HP**; Vorel expends **all ten uses of Stanley's healer's kit** stabilizing him, and the newcomer dragon **heals him by 73 HP**. Stanley is **alive**, Diontalus **escaped**, Hope **not freed**, no further father's-book or mask discoveries played. [E121](../../episodes/E121.md).

**E122 — Hope released and heart concealed:** Stanley **presses the detonator and frees Hope unharmed**. He retrieves Dilo's apparently mundane rapier; the **broken wand rejoins**, and study supplies **three dawn-recharging charges, a one-minute movable resilient sphere and no concentration for this item**. He uses it to **move the harvested natural dragon heart into Nash's excavation near the painful metal spike**, leaving it hidden rather than delivered. Stanley pockets **eight empathy grenades**, worries about Diontalus reaching their families, and proposes **a ship journey to Earth**, but **does not leave**. At the lodge he announces the party by megaphone, accepts real water, sees through Hope's necklace prank and helps restrain/offer candy to Nash in play combat. **Strength/Dexterity depletion is still mentioned at the lodge; no restoration is shown**. [E122](../../episodes/E122.md).

**Latest played state:** **Alive with the four at the Gatherers' Lodge E122**, feeding candy toward Nash as play cuts off. Hope freed; Diontalus escaped; heart buried in cave; **Earth departure only planned**, no completed long rest, new mask attunement or solved adjacency theory.
