## S2E08 — no newly bestowed title in the prison

In [S2E08](../../episodes/S2E08.md), Vorel tries to enter Pentel Weiss under a pirate guise, shouting **“I'm under arrest”** as he leaps into the shaft (~01:10), and later remarks on its inhabitants as **“the greatest most wanted criminals in all of space-time”** (~02:32). This is **sarcastic prisoner description**, **NOT** a new self-proclaimed Vorel title. He uses ordinary ship sword, then enchanted ***Diplomacy*** against the pool monster, but receives **no formal honor/epithet or title** in the reviewed primary ASR. [ASR audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E08_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E05 — “Best of All Vorels” repeat, no promotion

At **~00:43–00:45** during the **Vorel's Sandburgers** dispute on Planet Nash, Vorel says **“I am the best of all Vorels”** (Parakeet `best of all varrels`, Qwen likewise). This is a **slight wording variant** and repeat of the self-claimed best-of-multiverse epithet from S2E02–S2E04, not a new social or political title. His refusal to become the sandburger director, interest in an **alternate Vorel manager**, and herald's possible future promise **do not grant the actual Vorel a new office** or physically produce another Vorel. His announcement at **~01:15** on Blast Belly Rock as **Vorel Rasphem Kurix** recites his name rather than a newly bestowed epithet. [S2E05](../../episodes/S2E05.md) · [P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E05_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md). Audio unverified.

## S2E04 — competing projected Vorels, “Greatest of the Time Vorels” variant

During the **Tower Nash alternate-Nash council** on **Planet Nash** (~02:17–02:22), **main Vorel asks for other Vorels to appear as projections**. They do, with different timelines and without his chest-mounted **Eye of Groxl**; he declares himself **“the greatest of the time Vorels”** (source phonetically `best of the time Varels` / `greatest of the time Vorels`), **shortened repeat variant** of his S2E02–S2E03 self-bestowed boast. Each other Vorel challenges the claim. Main Vorel **summons/demonstrates Blade of Groxl** (Parakeet phonetic `blade of Grojil`) to impress them, and they express curiosity. This is **not a conferred best-Vorel rank, actual duel, gained alliance or physical multiverse travel**. [S2E04 episode](../../episodes/S2E04.md) · [text comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E04_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md). Audio unverified.
