# Tungsten Wheel — absent Elhandari engineer (provisional name)

> **The Wastes · main storyline · first identified E125 ~00:10–00:16.** Wording from two ASR exports; **no audio or Elhandari written records verified**. [E125 dossier](../../episodes/E125.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/E125_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md) · [Elhandar](../places/elhandar.md).

**Tungsten Wheel** is the name the High Elf **Amanthalodar/Amenthalodar (?)** gives for a gifted independent-minded **Elhandari engineer**, described as potentially capable of fabricating the proposed **Rubistic Triangular Sphere** from the natural dragon-metal heart. The High Elf credits the engineer with earlier work on **Elhandar's Cadulet/Cadulate spire**, improvements to its shipyards and **empathy-grenade production**. He reportedly pursued dangerous and rare minerals for intricate dimensional constructions and has **not been seen in decades**. This is the High Elf's testimony, not a witnessed recent meeting or verified biography.

The High Elf says the engineer's **lover** is at an Elhandari **terraforming platform on Mars**. The lover's source name sounds roughly **Dungstil/Dungilsta Ravensbread (?)**, inconsistently segmented between exports. **Nash actually receives teleportation-circle glyphs for Mars and the Elhandar spire** in E125, but the party does **not travel to Mars, find the lover, locate Tungsten, commission a build, recover the buried heart or create Ruby** before the episode cutoff.

**Unresolved:** exact name/spelling, engineering record, whether the lover has recent whereabouts, whether the engineer can/will build the sphere, and the wider Elhandari response to the station's E125 descent/impact. Do not identify him with the unnamed mythic engineer whom Malhya mentions in E124 solely because the party believes their search is aligned; it is **a plausible lead, not an on-screen meeting or independently confirmed identity**.
