# Tungsten Wheel — Elhandari engineer

## Introduction

Tungsten Wheel was an Elhandari engineer remembered for unconventional designs, rare materials and work on his people's spire, shipyards and empathy grenades. When the travelers needed someone to construct a device that might save Earth, the High Elf Amanthalodar named Tungsten as a possible maker. The engineer had been missing for decades. His former lover, **Dunglestar Ravensbread**, could be found on an Elhandari station on **Mars**, but neither that lead nor the engineer's reputed brilliance guaranteed that he was still alive or able to finish the work.[^tungsten-intro]

## Traces of an absent inventor

The companions found Dunglestar on Mars, where she kept Tungsten's old workshop. Inside stood a **partially built Rubistic Triangulosphere**, a towering device lacking a rare component whose name was heard uncertainly as *Gluptwarp*. Dunglestar spoke of Tungsten's departure in search of the substance and displayed a later laser-pigeon message from **Blast Belly Rock**, reporting danger and a possible lead at **Pentel Weiss**. It was evidence of earlier contact, not a sign that Tungsten remained at either place.[^tungsten-workshop]

The search soon gained a personal advocate. **[Union Wheel](union-wheel.md)**, assigned as Stanley's Elhandari protector, said Tungsten was her uncle. She joined the effort to locate him, but their claimed family relationship did not itself establish his whereabouts. On Blast Belly Rock, the party followed a tavern keeper's hazy memory of a solitary elf to the **Bank of Burp**. Records eventually confirmed that Tungsten had worked there: his signed contract and desk key led to a numbered deposit box containing navigation coordinates. Those coordinates, followed through an uncharted nebula, brought the party to the prison at Pentel Weiss.[^tungsten-search]

## Found inside Pentel Weiss

The search did not end at the prison doors. Tungsten's reported presence there was initially hearsay; the party entered an oppressive compound without a reliable location for him. At last Stanley's announcement drew a tall, slender, white-haired elf from the mage ward. Union recognized and embraced her uncle. Tungsten invited the rescuers into his equation-covered cell and explained his suspicions about the prison's magic suppression, collector rings and a dangerous creature farther inside. His guesses about a betrayed hideout and possible ways to escape remained hypotheses, not evidence that a culprit or working exit had been found.[^tungsten-discovery]

Tungsten continued with the group into the prison's dangerous inner chambers. He gathered pieces of an animated carpet for later study and fell under a strange influence that Stanley physically restrained. Union helped break another enthrallment. Blindfolded, Tungsten joined the rescue of captives held by a monstrous eye and lent his voice to the attack that destroyed it. His niece suffered repeated injury during these encounters, and Tungsten helped care for her. Once the company escaped aboard **Fate Six**, he recognized the mummy curse afflicting Stanley and removed it with his instruments and a spoken procedure. Rescue had restored Tungsten to the company, **not yet to Dunglestar or his unfinished invention**.[^tungsten-prison]

## A threatened niece and a concealed material

On Fate Six, Tungsten stayed close to the recovering Union. He said that his search for the missing substance had succeeded and that he had kept the reagent concealed on his person even during captivity. He declined to disclose precisely where and thought Dunglestar's workshop might be needed to retrieve it. Although he spoke confidently about the process, no one had yet seen the material extracted or installed.[^tungsten-reagent]

When infernal boarders attacked the ship, Union was left gravely wounded. Tungsten feared she would die. Stanley persuaded **Dr. Spider** to assist Dr. Daffodil and himself in a desperate operation; together they **saved Union's life**. Tungsten remained with her afterward and repeated that he could recover the hidden ingredient once they reached Mars. His anxiety and loyalty to his niece were as important to the search as his technical talent.[^tungsten-union]

## Reunion and the Mars demonstration

Tungsten finally disembarked on Mars and reunited with Dunglestar, embracing and kissing the partner he had long hoped to see again. He also met the High Elf who had recommended him and gained authority from Stanley to assemble a sizable Elhandari repair team. Union was allowed to take part. Tungsten and Dunglestar set to work on the incomplete Triangulosphere; the reunion and recruitment were real, while a functioning device still lay ahead.[^tungsten-reunion]

At the Mars war council, **Tungsten and Dunglestar brought out a completed Triangulosphere** on a hover platform. A modified protective sphere transported it, and Dunglestar briefly activated the machine. The apparatus formed an impossible luminous shape, disturbed the nearby soil and temporarily liquefied Hope's horns before it was switched off. Tungsten argued that placing the active device in the **Solar Mines** lava would reverse a corrupting wave and shut the Weir's influence out of Earth, with consequences for ordinary magic. Those were his predictions. The Mars demonstration established that the machine could operate **locally**, not that the planetary cure had occurred.[^tungsten-test]

## Current status

**Tungsten was alive and working with Dunglestar on Mars at the last directly reviewed encounter.** Their completed device had been demonstrated and a mission to carry it to Earth agreed upon, but it had not yet been deployed there at that cutoff. The exact recovery and use of the concealed reagent were not independently depicted, nor were the proposed planetary effects established by the trial. The engineer's ultimate fate beyond the Mars council remains outside this profile's witnessed horizon.[^tungsten-status]
