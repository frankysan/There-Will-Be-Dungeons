# Tungsten Wheel — absent Elhandari engineer (provisional name)

> **The Wastes · main storyline · first identified E125 ~00:10–00:16.** Wording from two ASR exports; **no audio or Elhandari written records verified**. [E125 dossier](../../episodes/E125.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/E125_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md) · [Elhandar](../places/elhandar.md).

**Tungsten Wheel** is the name the High Elf **Amanthalodar/Amenthalodar (?)** gives for a gifted independent-minded **Elhandari engineer**, described as potentially capable of fabricating the proposed **Rubistic Triangular Sphere** from the natural dragon-metal heart. The High Elf credits the engineer with earlier work on **Elhandar's Cadulet/Cadulate spire**, improvements to its shipyards and **empathy-grenade production**. He reportedly pursued dangerous and rare minerals for intricate dimensional constructions and has **not been seen in decades**. This is the High Elf's testimony, not a witnessed recent meeting or verified biography.

The High Elf says the engineer's **lover** is at an Elhandari **terraforming platform on Mars**. The lover's source name sounds roughly **Dungstil/Dungilsta Ravensbread (?)**, inconsistently segmented between exports. **Nash actually receives teleportation-circle glyphs for Mars and the Elhandar spire** in E125, but the party does **not travel to Mars, find the lover, locate Tungsten, commission a build, recover the buried heart or create Ruby** before the episode cutoff.

## S2E02 — real unfinished apparatus and two leads

The party actually reaches Mars, finds Tungsten's former lover **Dunglestar Ravensbread** working as an eccentric anatomist in the **Theatre of Mortal Bodies**, and is shown the preserved old **engineering workshop**. A **real unpowered ~10-foot-high/6-foot-wide partly constructed Rubistic Triangulosphere** stands there, missing an overheating-prevention component heard as **Gluptwarp/Glupwarp (?)**; this changes **“not yet constructed”** into **“partly constructed, not operational”** at the new cutoff. Dunglestar says Tungsten departed long ago seeking the substance, possibly travelled with the **Principle**, and sent a later **laser-pigeon letter from Blast Belly Rock** alleging peril and a potential material lead at **Pentel Weiss**. The letter is actually displayed; the glyphs are recognized by Hope/Stanley as similar to **Fate Six navigation buttons**, but no coordinate is tested. No sign of Tungsten's present life, death or location is physically established. **Device remains in the workshop**, no rescue/reunion/final repair. ## S2E03 — a purported niece among the Elhandari

When **Stanley/Valere Ornath accepts his newly awarded Selle Prime office**, a spear-bearing elf, source-named **Yunyon/Union Weal/Wheel (?)**, is **actually assigned** as his protective **Belekun/Balelikun (?)**. She explicitly tells Stanley that **Tungsten Wheel is her uncle**. This adds an important **claimant family connection** available to Stanley's party, not proof of Tungsten's present location or even written verification of their genealogy. No reunion, correspondence or travel to Blast Belly Rock is shown. [S2E03](../../episodes/S2E03.md) · [comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E06 — signed contract, desk key and coordinates; Tungsten still missing

The search in [S2E06](../../episodes/S2E06.md) **confirms that Tungsten Wheel historically worked as a teller at the Bank of Burp**: Stanley reads and takes a **signed ten-gold-per-week employment contract**, and a teller ledger supports the identification of Tungsten's desk, from which Stanley **takes a small key**. At the basement vault the key is identified as **724** and opens that deposit box; Hope **opens the box and discovers a rolled parchment of navigation coordinates**. **Neither Tungsten's current location nor the coordinates' destination is revealed in S2E06**; no retrieval of Gluptwarp, rescue, or finished Triangulosphere. Earlier innkeeper Ailboon's uncertain recollection is **corroborated specifically for employment**, not for every other part of his story. [P/Q audit](../../reports/WASTES_S2E06_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S2E05 — party actually reaches Blast Belly Rock; possible bank trail

By [S2E05](../../episodes/S2E05.md), the party **ACTUALLY takes Fate Six from Planet Nash to Blast Belly Rock**, docks at the old mithril settlement **Burp**, and asks around in the **Wet Whistle**. Tavern keeper **Ailboon Hardbottom (?)** remembers a **lone elf who came in every day decades ago waiting for someone**; the elf allegedly **worked at the Bank of Burp**, but the keeper **cannot confirm the person's name was Tungsten**. This is **a decades-old possible lead**, not verification Tungsten once worked there, is alive, located, a prisoner, or about to be rescued. The party **physically enters the abandoned Bank of Burp**, but finds glowing attribute-stealing blobs and ends the session there **before examining bank records, reaching inner rooms or finding the engineer**; Nash is left at **INT 3/DEX 3** from ooze theft. Guardian **Union Wheel**, who claims to be Tungsten's niece, accompanies them. No **Gluptwarp** component or Triangulosphere repair is acquired. [P/Q report](../../reports/WASTES_S2E05_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).
