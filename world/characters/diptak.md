# Diptak — lizardfolk warrior of the Golden Lands

## Introduction

Diptak was a lizardfolk warrior of the Golden Lands who initially confronted [Vorel](vorel.md), then yielded alive and pledged his household's allegiance. The encounter established a bond of obligation that would later draw Vorel into the affairs of Diptak's people.[^diptak-early]

## Return to Razak Val

When Diptak met Vorel again, he urged him to travel west to [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md), where he considered the reigning Great Fang unworthy. He escorted Vorel toward the settlement with swift reptilian riding mounts while the companions traveled in [Fate 4](../technology/fate-4.md). Diptak placed his hopes in Vorel's challenge to the ruler, but the lizardfolk succession custom required a formal contest known as *scaling*.[^diptak-return]

The Great Fang was [Orp](orp.md), a cyclops the travelers had known before, now fed human captives by the settlement's hunters. Diptak insisted on the authority of the custom and its Council of Twelve; Vorel challenged the cruelty of the captive-feeding system. Their disagreement exposed the divide between Diptak's belief in inherited political procedure and Vorel's refusal to accept its human cost. The council's precise authority and Diptak's wider kinship ties remain uncertain.[^diptak-politics]

## Current status

During the dispute over Vorel's challenge, Orp struck Diptak once and killed him outright. Diptak's crushed body was carried away. Nothing in the reviewed account establishes his return to life or the subsequent fate of the household that had pledged itself to Vorel.[^diptak-death]
