# Dr. Spider — infernal physician and experimenter

## Introduction

Dr. Spider was an arachnoid physician and powerful spellcaster traveling with [Hope](hope-akmenos.md), [Steerious Business](steerious-business.md) and [Blood Skunk Hendrickson](blood-skunk-hendrickson.md) in the infernal company assembled by Brett. His interest in experiments and his willingness to use destructive magic made him a dangerous companion, though he could also preserve life when it suited him.[^spider-intro]

## The wandering laboratory

During the group's travels, Spider killed Spatulo with a magical mental prison and preserved a talking brain. He later questioned an already dead accountant through necromancy and set fire to a mansion after the company left. He surrendered his lab coat as passage payment on the River Styx and suffered the Gray Waste's draining influence until the company reached Pandemonium. After a failed effort to seize a Sigil cargo ship, the survivors withdrew to his laboratory in Hell.[^spider-journey]

Spider's scientific zeal was memorable even to those who had only watched his experiments. The performers later recalled his obsessive planning, surgery and unsettling hospitality. Their comparisons with other fictional scientists were reflections on the performance, not evidence of a second identity or a separate history for him.[^spider-retrospective]

## Fate Six and the wounded

Spider returned as part of Brett's assault aboard **Fate Six**. His great field of sickening radiance endangered his own allies as well as the ship's defenders; other hostile spells briefly trapped Nash in an illusion and set the mast ablaze. He also restored fallen companions during the battle. His proposed attempt to travel between planes never came to pass: Nash's dragon-form acid breath brought Spider down before the journey could begin.[^spider-battle]

Spider nevertheless survived. Held in Fate Six's guarded magical brig, he agreed to operate on the severely wounded **Union Wheel** with Stanley and Dr. Daffodil, aided by his equipment and spectral nurse Wilkins. The operation succeeded. Spider later attached the **dead heads** of Squirts and Blood Skunk to the still-living Steerious; neither head returned to consciousness. Hope gave him her former Hell-issued appearance necklace and summoning ring for the journey back toward Brett, distinct from the Pentel Weiss collector ring.[^spider-repair]

## Current status

**Dr. Spider survived and left Fate Six** with Steerious at abandoned Blast Belly Rock. His stated wish to establish a medical practice there remains a proposal rather than a witnessed institution. A separately told holiday experiment involved a person called **Professor Spider**, who was killed, but nothing in that account proves the professor was Dr. Spider or places the doctor physically at that experiment. Spider's whereabouts after the landing remain unconfirmed.[^spider-status]
