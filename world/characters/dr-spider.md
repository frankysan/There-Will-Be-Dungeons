# Dr. Spider

**Browse:** [Characters](../CHARACTERS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Role:** A member of Hope's separate Arc Five group, assigned with Steerious Business and Blood Skunk by **Brett**. **Evidence:** Provisional processed ASR; the name is a character identifier, not proof of medical credentials.

In **E106** Dr. Spider kills **Spatulo** using *Mental Prison*, preserves a talking brain and witnesses Blood Skunk shoot it afterward. This is **Spatulo's played fate**, separate from the two refugees who are left behind before Hope's later Gate. [E106](../../episodes/E106.md).

With Hope's group in **E112**, Spider uses *Speak with Dead* on an **already dead accountant** and sets a mansion on fire after the party leaves; this does **not** mean the spell kills the accountant. In **E113**, Spider gives his lab coat to Hesterbrook as ferry payment and experiences the Gray Waste's color/despair effects before they lift in Pandemonium. [E112](../../episodes/E112.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md).

The group withdraws alive to Spider's **Hell laboratory** after failing to capture a Sigil cargo ship in **E116**. No successful capture or death of the four companions is observed. [E116](../../episodes/E116.md).
