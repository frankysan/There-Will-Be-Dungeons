# The Soulbroker — Catoblepraithe (?)

**Names:** The title **Soulbroker** is secure as a role; personal-name forms including `Catoblepraithe` and `Catobelpraithe` remain ASR-provisional. **Primary site:** [Château Weir](../places/chateau-weir.md). **Evidence:** E022–E024 and subsequent recollections; not audio-verified.

## Encounter and compact

After the Black Mine's deep ritual, the adventurers visit the Soulbroker's uncanny château in **E023**. **Hope, Nash and Stanley each sign** a compact involving action against **the Principle**, with a **one-thousand-day/night** interval described; **Vorel refuses**. These are four separate individual choices, not one unanimous contract. Hospitality, waivers and pact consequences must be distinguished from what is actually witnessed. [E022](../../episodes/E022.md) · [E023](../../episodes/E023.md) · [E024](../../episodes/E024.md).

The Weir is described as a place of great supernatural power and as a claimed destination for souls, but the actual contractual enforcement mechanism and its place in the wider cosmology are not independently established. Nobiery/Nobieri's later story of old military bargains is **his testimony**; an actual army returns in E054, but the precise ancient sacrificial terms are not witnessed. [E048](../../episodes/E048.md) · [E054](../../episodes/E054.md).

In E081 **Quizquaz offers interpretations** of the pact and proposes a catastrophic solution involving Earth. The companions **reject letting Earth die**. The consequences of their sacrifice, revival and later adventures should not be simplified into proof the Weir obligation has been discharged. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [Chronology](../CHRONOLOGY.md).

## Source boundaries

**E122 — the black dragon's ancient-history account:** The dying dragon says human colonists entered a portal disguised as a black hole, bargained with the **Soulbroker** for lordship and promised access to Earth. He describes elven/dragon opposition and calls the Principle the Weir's surviving agent. **These are NPC assertions about unplayed history**, with no named colonist or independent bargain record. Stanley's question about why the Weir then seek the Principle's destruction is **not answered**, and the party's original compact is **not discharged or replaced** by this conversation. [E122](../../episodes/E122.md) · [Dragon](imprisoned-black-dragon.md) · [The Principle](the-principle.md).

The Soulbroker is **not automatically identical with Quizquaz**, the Principle, or the hooded Voice. The original signed terms, eventual deadline and whether undelivered promised souls must still be collected are open.
