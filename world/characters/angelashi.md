# Angelashi

**Name status:** **Angelashi** is used as a working display spelling based on cast-shared recap forms; **Angel Ashi** appears in episode ASR, and the **word division remains unresolved**, unlike the accepted spelling **Diontalus**. **Identity:** Changeling who presents as an elven singer (revealed E114), and an important companion of Nash. **Arc Two status:** **Alive and freed in E054**. **Spoilers:** Played E035–S2E05, plus separately framed November 2020 creator discussion below.

## S3E05 — actual last farewell before Nash leaves Mars for Earth

During [S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md) **~00:42–00:43**, Nash **ACTUALLY RETURNS to ANGELASHI and their FOUR children** before embarking, reassuring them he will return and joking she has ninety-nine possible alternate versions of him as backups. GM indicates she appreciates the dark humor **but is NOT willing to replace her Nash with an alternate**. **The family STAYS ON MARS** while **Nash and 99 alternate Nashes ACTUALLY BOARD Fate Six and FLY TO EARTH**. No deaths, children added, transplantation or new pregnancy shown. [S3E05 ASR report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E05_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## S3E04 — private eve-of-war promises; remains with four children on Mars

In [played S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md) ~00:52 and ~01:03–01:10, Angelashi **ACTUALLY REUNITES PRIVATELY WITH NASH** in their Mars tent; they spend evening with the **SAME FOUR children**, put them to bed and discuss a future elsewhere **if** the war ends safely. ## S3E03 — actual reunion with Nash and four children on Mars

In [played S3E03](../../episodes/S3E03.md) ~01:52–01:58, **ANGELASHI ACTUALLY WALKS FROM MARTIAN FOREST WITH FOUR CHILDREN**, and husband Nash **returns to HUMANOID FORM from adult black dragon, embraces/kisses her and greets all four**. Angelashi says they are doing well after evacuation from danger on Earth and may build a home on Mars; **this is a preference, not a final permanent settlement decision**. **One of their four is DAUGHTER NASH JUNIOR**, who **ACTUALLY MORPHS INTO A HAMMER**, makes a distinct giggle when found, then changes to wood and humanoid girl. Character names briefly discussed as **Pipe, Boot, Fork and Nash Junior** with cast/player uncertainty; do not introduce a fifth child named Hammer. **Sprout, a separate tiny speaking MOUTH/EYE still attached to Nash's shoulder**, introduces itself; Angelashi sees/hears it and expresses discomfort, **no claim it is Angelashi's biological child or spouse**. [S3E03 ASR report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

Angelashi is introduced as a performer at the **Golden Gargoyle** in Trashburg, drawing Nash's interest. She travels with him after the club's violence and later speaks privately about her past, including elven/refugee experiences, travel hopes and her magic. Some of those details are **her own account**, not independent confirmation of an ancient itinerary. She uses temporary flower/plant magic around Nash, and the two develop an intimate relationship.

She disappears during the worsening conflict with the keep. In E048 **Tedna reports** that a detained woman he identifies as Angelashi was branded a traitor and taken toward the Pit of Despair, supposedly to be fed to a beast. This is **a report, not a witnessed death**. In E054 the group **finds Angelashi indisputably alive in the throne room**, held in a stone grip by **Lazlo** under Monsoon. The group breaks her free and she **reunites with Nash**. The E054 outcome should be displayed as **survival and rescue**, never as resurrection from Tedna's mistaken report.

Her future travel, health and place in the new Tietkalla government are not decided merely by their reunion. The Q&A mentions Nash's emotional stakes and plans without adding scenes of Angelashi's life after E054.

## S2E05 — chooses to remain at Tower Nash during the expedition

After the [arrival of herald Zolfin Sol(?) in S2E05](../../episodes/S2E05.md), Angelashi asks the heroes **NOT to summon the proposed 200 alternate Nashes while she and the children are living on Planet Nash**. She fears confusing or frightening her family with so many alternate versions of their father. She **actually decides to stay in Tower Nash**, says farewell to main Nash and goes back indoors to arrange rooms with Tower Nash. **Main Nash, Hope, Stanley, Vorel, Union Wheel and alternate Badass T physically sail away without her** aboard Fate Six, bound for Blast Belly Rock. At **S2E05 cutoff**, Angelashi and all **four children are on Planet Nash**, **not on Blast Belly Rock** and **no army of alternate Nashes has been imported**. The herald describes a theoretical summoning, **not a completed family relocation**. [P/Q report](../../reports/WASTES_S2E05_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## E115 — a home, reconciliation and agreed names

**Angelashi and all four children arrive at Elhandar** and are moved to a **lakeside cottage**, with Lyrielle’s assistance. She explains hiding her changeling identity against exploitation/mistrust in Trashburg, and says Nash is **partly shifter but also human**, with an origin she cannot explain. These are **her interpretations and recollections**, not proof of a newly acquired power; his attempted frog change fails. After hurtful remarks and time apart, **both apologize and agree to forgive**. **Fork, Boot, Pipe and Nash Junior Maggard** become the **parents’ accepted names**, following a successful Persuasion roll, not merely Nash’s private nicknames. She says she has no known surname; this does not independently establish all of her ancestry.

**Four days of recovery actually pass**. At day-five breakfast Angelashi would prefer Nash to retire but **supports him fulfilling his obligations and returning to the family when possible**. She considers helping construct a circle, but **no spell lesson or construction is performed**, and an existing station circle makes that proposal unnecessary for now. **She and the children remain alive at the cottage**, without family relocation to an alternate Nash’s home or an actual teleport journey. ## E114 — four living children and her revealed form

During the rescue ship's voyage toward **Elhandar**, Angelashi goes into labor and **privately tells Nash she is not an elf but a changeling, like him (~00:41–00:42)**. **Lyrielle/Liriel (?)**, Kristen's newly introduced elf doctor, assists while an undead attack threatens the ship. Ultrasound shows a baby and forms resembling **a fork, a smoking pipe and a boot**; **Animate Objects fails because they are not objects**. All four are delivered, with Nash catching the baby-shaped boy. During the family embrace **the three apparent objects change into babies**, establishing **four living changeling children, two girls and two boys (~02:35–02:39)**. **Angelashi's elven appearance visibly falls away**, revealing gray skin, white hair, small pointed ears and an altered face.

**Her changeling form is shown; her assertion that Nash is also a changeling is not independently demonstrated**, and he protests that his dragon heart explains his known transformation. This disclosure does not automatically become Stanley's or Vorel's knowledge. Earlier descriptions of Angelashi as an elf are **her public appearance and episode-era understanding**; earlier elven-history testimony needs reassessment rather than an invented replacement biography. **Mother and four children are alive together aboard ship at cutoff**, not yet in Elhandar. No children's names or complete individual sex/form mapping. [E114 dossier](../../episodes/E114.md) · [comparison](../../reports/E114_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md), **ASR-provisional**.
