# Angelashi

Angelashi first appears as a singer at the Golden Gargoyle in Trashburg. Her warmth, occasional flower magic and tales of distant places catch Nash's attention, but she is cautious about whom to trust. What appears to be an elven performer's history eventually proves to be the guarded life of a changeling. As her relationship with Nash deepens, the fear of separation and the need to protect their children shape her choices more than the adventurers' battles do.[^early]

## The singer beneath the keep

Angelashi and Nash grow close amid the danger of T'ietkalla's rival gangs and the contested keep. She speaks of journeys and hardships she has endured, though the whole of that past is not independently witnessed. Their affection is real even before Nash knows her hidden nature.

When Angelashi disappears during the conflict, Tedna reports that she has been condemned as a traitor and sent toward the Pit of Despair. This is an alarming **report**, not a witnessed death. The companions later find her **alive**, held under Monsoon's power in the throne room, and free her. She and Nash truly reunite; nobody resurrects her.[^early]

<a id="new-arc-status--e055e056"></a>

## Building a life in T'ietkalla

With Hope crowned, Angelashi tries to build a home with Nash while he remains haunted by the Solar Mines. She comforts him through nightmares, and they learn they are expecting a child. The pregnancy makes their future together both more hopeful and more frightening. When a stone storm threatens the city, Angelashi stays with others at the Golden Sands rather than traveling into the danger with the four companions. Neither the child's birth nor loss has happened yet.[^pregnancy]

<a id="e110-vision--e111-physical-rescue--pregnancy-and-the-ship"></a>

## Separated again

Later Nash sees a heavily pregnant Angelashi fighting near T'ietkalla through an extraordinary vision. That sighting is **not a physical reunion**. He actually finds and embraces her only afterward, in a concealed basement at the Golden Sands, where she has sheltered with Bok Bok. Fearing for the unborn child, she asks him to accept refuge from the arriving Elhandari. Nash carries her aboard their spacecraft before returning to the fighting. Her escape is real, but the later voyage and birth still lie ahead.[^rescue]

<a id="e114--four-living-children-and-her-revealed-form"></a>

## A changeling and a mother

The spacecraft comes under attack while Angelashi goes into labor. In private she finally tells Nash she is **not an elf but a changeling**. She believes Nash may share that nature, although he questions the explanation for his own unusual transformations. Assisted by Lyrielle, she delivers **four living children**. One initially looks like an ordinary baby; the others resemble a fork, pipe and boot before becoming infants in their parents' arms. Angelashi's elven disguise falls away, revealing gray skin and pale hair.[^birth]

Her revelation helps Nash understand why she concealed so much. It does not mean that everything she said about her past was false, nor prove his ancestry. The names and individual identities of their children are established gradually, with some conflicting statements preserved as source questions rather than silently rewritten.[^children]

<a id="e115--a-home-reconciliation-and-agreed-names"></a>

## A home and a reconciliation

At Elhandar, Angelashi and the children find shelter in a lakeside cottage. She explains that she disguised herself to avoid the exploitation and distrust she faced in Trashburg. Nash is hurt by what he has not known; after an argument and time apart they apologize and choose to forgive. They agree to call their children **Fork, Boot, Pipe and Nash Junior Maggard**.

Angelashi would prefer that Nash put dangerous missions aside and remain with them. She nevertheless supports his obligations to friends and family, hoping he will return when he can. Their time together allows a glimpse of the ordinary life she wants without promising that it can yet last.[^home]

<a id="e124--household-moves-and-junior-confirmed"></a>

## Shelter as Elhandar falls

As danger approaches, Angelashi, the children and Lyrielle move toward the station's central reception, taking care to carry infants who sometimes appear as objects. When Elhandar is attacked, they flee alongside the companions and eventually reach the hidden room at Villa Or'Nath. They shelter while the damaged station crashes to Earth. Their survival is confirmed after the impact; the earlier desperate escape alone cannot establish it.[^evacuation]

<a id="e125--evacuation-with-the-four-children"></a>

<a id="e128--farewell-on-earth-and-actual-mars-bound-departure"></a>

When the Elhandari offer transport to Mars, Angelashi must part from Nash again. She worries that their children may grow up without him. Nash says the dangers following the companions would make traveling together unsafe, and promises to return. Angelashi kisses his dragon-scaled cheek and boards with **all four children**. The ships physically leave Earth while Nash stays behind; the subsequent safe arrival is a later event.[^mars]

<a id="s2e02s2e05--reunites-with-nash-settles-temporarily-on-planet-nash"></a>

<a id="s2e05--chooses-to-remain-at-tower-nash-during-the-expedition"></a>

## Choosing safety on Planet Nash

Angelashi and the children reach Mars and later reunite with Nash. Wary of another war threatening their refuge, she considers Planet Nash as a safer place, but merely discussing it does not move the family. She and the four children later **actually travel** there with Nash and the others, meeting Tower Nash and learning of his alternate counterparts.

When Nash continues with the expedition, Angelashi elects to **remain with the children in Tower Nash**. She is uneasy with the idea of hundreds of versions of their father surrounding or confusing the family; she values Nash himself, not an interchangeable replacement. Any proposed summoning of additional alternate Nashes at that time must be distinguished from their later appearance elsewhere.[^refuge]

<a id="s3e03--actual-reunion-with-nash-and-four-children-on-mars"></a>

## Homecoming on Mars

When Nash returns to Mars with the party, Angelashi and the same four children meet him near the terraforming forest. The family embraces. Nash Junior demonstrably changes between a girl, wood and a hammer; this is a child's transformation, not another new child. Angelashi also meets the small talking being called Sprout attached to Nash and is unsettled by it. They imagine a peaceful future, but neither Mars nor any other place is irrevocably chosen as their home.[^reunion]

<a id="s3e04--private-eve-of-war-promises-remains-with-four-children-on-mars"></a>

<a id="s3e05--actual-last-farewell-before-nash-leaves-mars-for-earth"></a>

## The last farewell before the war

Before Nash leaves for Earth, he spends a private evening with Angelashi while their children sleep. She asks where they might live afterward and cautions him against taking immortality for granted. She wants him to return alive. Nash later comes back to reassure the family before departing with the defenders; Angelashi **remains with the children** rather than joining the assault. Their parting is genuine, but it is not the campaign's final meeting.[^farewell]

<a id="november-2020-bonus--child-gender-recall-versus-the-birth-source"></a>

## Current status

After the Principal's defeat, Nash dies while destroying the Moon facility that sought to remake him. **Angelashi does not witness his death.** His companions bring what remains of his chrome body to Planet Nash, where Stanley uses powerful magic to restore him. Nash **actually reunites and embraces Angelashi and their four living children**. He chooses to stay with his family instead of continuing adventuring. At their cottage, an apparent second Nash emerges from a locked cupboard, tackles Nash and warns that an unidentified “he” is coming. The figure's identity and Angelashi's response are not shown. Nash's reunion and choice are witnessed, but the family's future safety remains unresolved.[^finale]
