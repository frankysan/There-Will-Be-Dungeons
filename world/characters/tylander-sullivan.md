# Tylander Sullivan

**Role:** Powerful, unreliable source of Slavetown and Black Mine history; associated with ownership claims, slavery and the ritual investigation. **Spoilers:** Early Wastes campaign. **Evidence:** Unverified processed SRTs.

Tylander is closely associated with the **Slavetown** power structure and the disputed history of the **Black Mine**. The party discovers diary material, claims about title and labor, and disturbing events that culminate in the deep ritual and Soulbroker encounter. Tylander's explanations of what happened at the mine should be identified as **his accounts**, not neutral facts; the party encounters actual victims, undead and extraordinary phenomena separately. [E013](../../episodes/E013.md) · [E019](../../episodes/E019.md)–[E023](../../episodes/E023.md).

The claimed rights of ownership, family ties and legal status of a mine deed require verification against the underlying documents/handouts rather than confident reconstruction from paraphrased ASR.
