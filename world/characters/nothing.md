# Nothing — kenku guide of Sigil

## Introduction

Nothing was a small, dark-feathered **kenku** whom the travelers met in the **Soggy Feather** during their stay in [Sigil](../places/sigil.md). Dressed in worn dark clothing and a long-beaked, plague-doctor-like mask, she watched for danger and carried several blades, including a sword that could warn of nearby threats. She communicated chiefly by copying remembered voices, sounds and phrases or by gesture. A borrowed answer was not always her own unambiguous opinion, and her apparent silences concealed an alert, resourceful mind.[^nothing-intro]

At Bill's suggestion, Nothing became a guide for Vorel, Nash and [Squirts](squirts.md). She led them through unfamiliar neighborhoods and a trickster church, warned them against provoking the Lady of Pain, and knew the routes toward the library and **Royce Gymnasium**. She also entered the local **Big Time Smack'em Brothers** competition under the name *Nothing*, while doing odd jobs at the inn. Her careful attempts to communicate were not always understood by her companions.[^nothing-guide]

## Two lives in the city

Nothing had underworld connections that the others did not fully know. A fixer called **Pelvis**, who addressed her by a name heard as *Ebon Skulker*, offered her a highly paid private assignment. Through a magic-shop contact and a strange pyramid device, she spoke with a hidden client who wanted a person abducted from Sigil and delivered through a portal into the **Abyss**. Nothing accepted a deadline and secrecy conditions, but **no kidnapping or delivery was performed in the reviewed episodes**. The client, victim's precise name and faction remained uncertain; her companions were not shown learning the terms.[^nothing-job]

During the qualifying contest, Nothing tried to outwait a rival kenku by copying the opponent's choices. Neither competitor acted before the clock ran out, and **both forfeited**. This was a loss of her entry, not a tournament victory. Nothing stayed involved in the party's struggle on Vorel's behalf: when a defeated champion attempted to strike Vorel with a raised stall, she threw a hammer to interrupt him, used nonlethal force and scattered ball bearings to stop pursuing attackers. At the crowded inn she waited tables, earned wages and tips, and separately took money by swindling patrons.[^nothing-contest]

## Watchfulness and the Crimson Moon

Nothing's enchanted sword warned her of intruders moving across the Soggy Feather's roof. She woke the others, then fought masked attackers who broke into the room. The assailants disappeared into dust and empty clothing when killed. Nothing recognized the crest of the **Order of the Crimson Moon**, a feared assassins' guild, through information known to her privately. She tried to convey the warning through mimicry, drawings and clothing until the others understood the name. Nothing did not know who had hired the assailants, and the raid was **not proved to be part of her separate Pelvis commission**.[^nothing-attack]

She remained Vorel's bodyguard as he advanced in the tournament. After temporarily leaving her enchanted sword behind to satisfy the competition's rules, she saw him defeat the Mind Master and later Kanja, while the trickster priest's illicit assistance affected the contest. Vorel paid her **600 gold** for her service. There were troubling hints connecting the Mind Master's circle to the Crimson Moon: Vorel recognized a woman's eyes from the masked attack, and a later mirror showed the Mind Master with Crimson Moon escorts. Neither observation established that he had commissioned the earlier assault.[^nothing-bodyguard]

Nothing also proved a practical guide when Nash needed help navigating Sigil's restrictions on spells. She brought him to a seller of spell-keys, turning a vague clue into a useful contact, although he did not purchase a key at that meeting. Her growing usefulness came less from long speeches than from recognition, initiative and small acts of protection.[^nothing-help]

## Current status

**Nothing was alive when Hope and Stanley returned** from the Chapel of Laughter's mirror ritual. She assisted the reunited companions, supplied ordinary clothes to Hope, lent a lantern and guided the group back toward the Soggy Feather. With Stanley restored as an active companion, Nothing no longer accompanied the main group in the same role. She still had Pelvis's unfinished Abyss assignment and might have left to attempt it, but **the kidnapping, the promised payment and her subsequent fate were not played or verified**. Her private history, the mark on her sword, the assassins' employer and the full scope of her kenku mimicry remain unresolved.[^nothing-status]
