# Nothing — kenku guide (Arc 3)

> **First appearance:** [E082](../../episodes/E082.md), unnamed kenku in the Soggy Feather. **Name explicitly used:** [E083](../../episodes/E083.md), [processed Parakeet SRT](../../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E083.%20Trash%20Monkey%20and%20The%20Bird.parakeet.processed.srt). **Jon's new character**, **not** resurrected Stanley. This is **provisional processed-ASR research**, not audio verification. Spoilers through E083.

## Identity and traits

**Nothing** is a small, dark-feathered **kenku**, dressed in worn, hooded dark clothes and a modified, long-beaked **plague-doctor-like mask**. The performer describes removing the cloak and mask at night, setting gear aside and planting an **eye-marked sword** in the floor while Nothing sleeps in a chair. The character carries other blades and is vigilant about possible threats. **Nothing communicates largely by mimicking phrases and sounds**; repeated “yes,” “no” or borrowed statements are not consistently plain, independent answers. The narration uses **female pronouns** in E083 even while other characters sometimes guess incorrectly; verify by later material before making broader assumptions.

## Played development

Bill suggests Nothing as a guide at the end of [E082](../../episodes/E082.md). In [E083](../../episodes/E083.md), Nothing accompanies Vorel, Nash and Squirts to the trickster church, mimics its eccentric entrance sound, then returns with them to the **Soggy Feather**. **Bill pays Nothing one gold piece** for helping and asks the kenku to continue guiding the newcomers. Nothing responds cautiously to Pelor's advisers and uses gesture and mimicry to warn **Vorel and Nash not to challenge the Lady of Pain**.

The party tries to discuss **factions, guards and bounty work** with Nothing, often misunderstanding the mimicry. The kenku recognizes the **Fiend's Hoof** as a possible shady business venue, but **the party never visits it in E083**. At **76 Mulberry Lane** Nothing **registers individually for the Big Time Smack'em Brothers tournament under the exact name “Nothing”**, receiving **league number 142748**, alongside Vorel and Nash. Nothing knows the way to **Royce Gymnasium** and the library district; **no tournament match is played** during E083.
