# Nothing — kenku guide (Arc 3)

> **First appearance:** [E082](../../episodes/E082.md), unnamed kenku in the Soggy Feather. **Name explicitly used:** [E083](../../episodes/E083.md), [processed Parakeet SRT](../../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E083.%20Trash%20Monkey%20and%20The%20Bird.parakeet.processed.srt). **Jon's new character**, **not** resurrected Stanley. This is **provisional processed-ASR research**, not audio verification. Spoilers through E083.

## Identity and traits

**Nothing** is a small, dark-feathered **kenku**, dressed in worn, hooded dark clothes and a modified, long-beaked **plague-doctor-like mask**. The performer describes removing the cloak and mask at night, setting gear aside and planting a **sword with an as-yet-unverified hilt marking** in the floor while Nothing sleeps in a chair. The character carries other blades and is vigilant about possible threats. **Nothing communicates largely by mimicking phrases and sounds**; repeated “yes,” “no” or borrowed statements are not consistently plain, independent answers. The narration uses **female pronouns** in E083 even while other characters sometimes guess incorrectly; verify by later material before making broader assumptions.

## Played development

Bill suggests Nothing as a guide at the end of [E082](../../episodes/E082.md). In [E083](../../episodes/E083.md), Nothing accompanies Vorel, Nash and Squirts to the trickster church, mimics its eccentric entrance sound, then returns with them to the **Soggy Feather**. **Bill pays Nothing one gold piece** for helping and asks the kenku to continue guiding the newcomers. Nothing responds cautiously to Pelor's advisers and uses gesture and mimicry to warn **Vorel and Nash not to challenge the Lady of Pain**.

The party tries to discuss **factions, guards and bounty work** with Nothing, often misunderstanding the mimicry. The kenku recognizes the **Fiend's Hoof** as a possible shady business venue, but **the party never visits it in E083**. At **76 Mulberry Lane** Nothing **registers individually for the Big Time Smack'em Brothers tournament under the exact name “Nothing”**, receiving **league number 142748**, alongside Vorel and Nash. Nothing knows the way to **Royce Gymnasium** and the library district; **no tournament match is played** during E083.

## E084 — Ebon Skulker, Pelvis's commission and the kenku double forfeit

In [E084](../../episodes/E084.md), Nothing conceals the stolen champion strategy book, joins Vorel, Nash and Squirts at the trickster church, and studies the marionette rules. At the **Soggy Feather**, a familiar fixer named **Pelvis** (bowler hat, diamond/gold teeth) finds her, calling her **“Ebon/Eben Skulker”**. The GM says she **previously took work through him**. **Without the party overhearing the terms**, she accepts an important job promising **triple the usual fee**, then visits a small magic shop at **47 Castamere/Castlemere Lane (?)** the following morning. An aged **githyanki** supplies a **marble pyramid portal device**, through which Nothing **actually meets an anonymous, hidden and voice-distorted client**.

The client demands **a secret kidnapping and delivery to the Abyss**: take **Stevius Ronkly (?)**, a member of the **“Godsmen” (Parakeet) / “Guardsmen” (Qwen)**, from Sigil's high quarters, transport the target to **10 Belshamer Lane (?) in the Hive**, and use the portal to deliver him to the Abyss. The client sets a **seven-day deadline**, with **only one hour to return from the Abyss** after opening the delivery portal, and insists on no witnesses. **The kidnapping has not happened, the client and employer are not identified, and Nothing has not been paid.** The faction spelling **must stay unresolved pending audio**; neither model is an authoritative spelling source. The other companions are **not shown learning the mission**.

At BMF security Nothing **avoids a magic-item scan**, steals a small doll from the confiscation bin, plants it on an entrant, and orchestrates a scuffle to **slip inside**. In her first qualifying match she meets **another kenku** (ASR **Bockip/Bakit/Bocket**) and selects **Kung** after he locks **Bland**. Each waits for the other to press the controller first, trying to mimic the other's choice. **The clock expires and both characters forfeit by timeout**; Nothing **does not advance via a win**. This table scene should be kept separate from the secret commission. [Comparison](../../reports/E084_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## E085 — the qualifier's end, a fight, earned gold and the warning sword

In [E085](../../episodes/E085.md), Nothing's own BMF entry **remains ended by E084's double timeout**, but the kenku accompanies Vorel/Nash/Squirts through the Royce qualifier and sees **Vorel defeat Nash and local champion Ventro**. When Ventro tries to strike Vorel with the raised marionette stall, **Nothing throws a hammer into his arm**, averting the immediate blow. She later uses fighter/rogue combat manoeuvres and a **nonlethal attack** to disable Ventro, as well as **ball bearings** to trip charging entourage members. She does **not carry out the separate Pelvis/Abyss kidnapping assignment**, and the other characters still lack its full private terms.

After Vorel's victory, Nothing helps **wait tables at the crowded Soggy Feather** and **swindles patrons for 24 gold**; the GM also credits **one gold in wages and twenty gold tips**, distinguishable from the swindled gold. She locks the inn after the party, assists Vorel's late return and cleans up. Overnight her **warning-enchanted sword vibrates** and alerts her to **movement on the roof**, preventing a completely unannounced surprise. She **caws to wake her companions**, and **two sword-bearing ninja-like intruders leap into the room**, ending the episode before any attack is resolved. No motive is assigned to the intruders or tied to her unfinished commission. [E085](../../episodes/E085.md) · [Comparison](../../reports/E085_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## January 2020 Q&A — kenku internal voice and attempted warning

In [BONUS-2020-01](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-01.md), Jon clarifies **Nothing has an internal voice, thoughts and intentions** and her speech relies on **remembered sounds, borrowed voices and gestures** rather than freely composed new words. He interprets a **traditional kenku curse** as the restriction; neither Jon nor the others establishes **who imposed it**. A recognizer produces an ambiguous/contradictory phrase about whether the curse exists, so **do not canonize its exact wording as a new D&D rule without listening**. Jon/Beau also indicate Nothing **tried to convey Nash's E085 spell-suppression problem**, but **Nash did not understand**. This is a **post-episode interpretation**, not evidence Nash learned or fixed the underlying Sigil-specific mechanism. Nothing's **Pelvis commission remains secret and unperformed at E085's cutoff**. ## E086 — Crimson Moon knowledge and first spell-key referral

[E086](../../episodes/E086.md) directly begins with masked assassins breaking into the **Soggy Feather**. Nothing's **warning sword alerted her in E085**; she fights with dual weapons, killing several attackers who **vanish into dust and leave empty clothing**. The GM **privately tells Jon/Nothing** that their howling-wolf/red-moon crest identifies the **Order of the Crimson Moon**, an **extremely costly, legendary and elusive assassins' guild**. **Nothing initially knows this privately**, then tries to communicate the name to Vorel, Nash and Bill through borrowed phrases, red-crescent drawings and rearranging garments. The party **eventually deciphers the name**, but **no employer, price or link to Pelvis is demonstrated**. Nothing's private **Abyss kidnapping remains unperformed**.

At the kitchen cooking session Nothing wears a **chef hat**, chops a grotesque pink mushroom, and assists the trickster priest's potion work; **Vorel drinks the product**. Later the official tournament handler **Pentheon/Pantheon (?)** appears startled or strangely interested in Nothing, but **no recognition or identity is established**. At the Sigil bazaar Nothing leads Nash to **a spell-key seller**, translating Squirts' brief “key” clue into a practical lead. **Nash learns school-specific Sigil costs (500 gold, financed 50×12 with ID), but buys no key**.
