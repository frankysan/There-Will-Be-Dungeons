## Season Two and Three — later turning points

The later campaign moves from the first off-world voyages to the battle for Earth and its aftermath. These are **selected historical changes**, not a replacement for the [episode-by-episode chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md). A command, warning or claimed ancient explanation is not treated as an accomplished event.

| Turning point | Played result and boundary | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Villa Or'Nath becomes Fate Six; the travelers reach Mars and Planet Nash** | After the mask's attunement the villa **actually transforms and flies**; the party reaches the surviving Elhandari on Mars in S2E02. With Angelashi and the children aboard, it later performs **rift-space travel to Planet Nash** in S2E04. Neither journey proves the ship can independently reach every plane. | [Fate Six](technology/fate-six.md) · [S2E02](../episodes/S2E02.md) · [S2E04](../episodes/S2E04.md) |
| **Nash dies, then returns through his engineered heart** | Nash's original body dies at the S2E12 cutoff, while the heart-and-eyestalk survives; Stanley's subsequent *Regenerate* restores a body in early Season Three. His later identities and the heart's ultimate custody are separate events. | [Nash's heart](technology/nash-dragon-heart.md) · [S2E12](../episodes/S2E12.md) · [S3E01](../episodes/S3E01.md) |
| **The Rubistic Triangulosphere is made and tested on Mars** | Tungsten and Dunglestar **physically demonstrate** the completed Ruby in S3E04. Its local effects are real; a cure for Earth and the proposed mine-core deployment remain **untested at that point**. | [Ruby](technology/rubistic-triangulosphere.md) · [S3E04](../episodes/S3E04.md) |
| **The Solar Mines campaign reaches the geothermal chamber** | The allied assault begins in S3E05. Groxl crushes Fate Six in S3E07, but Nash saves Ruby; the giant is later brought down. By S3E11 the party **actually reaches the Principal's core chamber**, with Ruby staged there **but not activated**. | [Solar Mines](places/solar-mines.md) · [S3E07](../episodes/S3E07.md) · [S3E11](../episodes/S3E11.md) |
| **The manifested Soulbroker falls; the Weir–Earth link is severed** | The Soulbroker's manifested opponent is defeated and the GM confirms the connection broken in S3E12. **The Worldbreaker already on Earth remains a threat**: severing the link does not itself remove or banish it. | [Soulbroker](characters/the-soulbroker.md) · [S3E12](../episodes/S3E12.md) |
| **The allies hold the mines while the Worldbreaker advances** | Elhandari forces occupy Solar Mines in S3E13 and **physically guard the still-unactivated Ruby** by S3E14. During S3E15 the heroes prepare a canyon ambush and face the advancing Worldbreaker; a defensive plan is not yet a completed victory. | [Solar Mines](places/solar-mines.md) · [S3E13](../episodes/S3E13.md) · [S3E15](../episodes/S3E15.md) |
| **The Principal betrays the alliance; the Worldbreaker is banished** | Vorel **actually detonates** the canyon explosives in S3E16; the Principal tries to keep the monster available and attacks his former allies. Nash **completes the banishment** in S3E17, after which the remaining chrome copies collapse. The Principal's claims of cosmic necessity are not independently proven. | [The Principal](characters/the-principal.md) · [S3E16](../episodes/S3E16.md) · [S3E17](../episodes/S3E17.md) |
| **Ruby restores Earth's living landscape** | With the Worldbreaker gone, the heroes consent and Stanley **orders Ruby activated** in S3E18. The GM shows actual rain, vegetation and renewed waterfalls, alongside the loss of ordinary Earthbound mana-wave spellcasting. **Physical insertion of Ruby is not separately shown.** Monsoon chooses death in sunlight; Hope takes his helmet. Off-Earth spellcasting continues later in the same finale. | [Ruby](technology/rubistic-triangulosphere.md) · [S3E18](../episodes/S3E18.md) |
| **The Moon's Council of Faces falls; Nash dies and is restored** | On the Moon the Council **forcibly converts Nash** to a chrome successor; the allies destroy its installation, Nash dies, and his companions retain a chrome sample. After four days, on **Planet Nash**, Stanley actually uses *Wish* to produce *True Resurrection*. Nash returns to life; this is not proof the Council's origin story was true. | [Nash](characters/nash-maggard.md) · [S3E18](../episodes/S3E18.md) |
| **The survivors' council plans a postwar order** | At Solar Mines, surviving leaders discuss refuge for former captives, an agreed division of Toyota Host groups between T'ietkalla and Elhandari, Kuromokishi reintegration with Vorel's lizardfolk, a possible mixed township and future clearance of the Clench site. **These are agreements or assignments, not completed reconstruction, reconciliation or site clearance.** | [S3E18](../episodes/S3E18.md) · [Solar Mines](places/solar-mines.md) |
| **The companions take separate paths after the war** | After Fate Six reconstitutes, Nash is **actually resurrected on Planet Nash** and stays with Angelashi and their four children. Hope brings resettlers to **still-damaged T'ietkalla**, receiving Stanley's Mask and wearing it as a crown; her future attunement is not shown. Vorel **sets out** with lizardfolk and Kuromokishi to rebuild Razak Val; Stanley **crosses a Feywild portal** with many Elhandari, while others remain on Mars or return to Earth. No completed city rebuild or reunion is played. | [T'ietkalla](HISTORY_TIETKALLA.md) · [Razak Val](places/razak-val.md) · [S3E18](../episodes/S3E18.md) |
| **The final visitor's warning remains unresolved** | At Nash's family home an apparent second Nash emerges and warns of an unspecified threat. His identity and the target of the warning are **not revealed**; the GM ends the played campaign. The later S3E019 discussion is **not** a story continuation or proof that the Principal has returned. | [S3E18](../episodes/S3E18.md) · [S3E019 cast discussion](../episodes/S3E019.md) |

## Sources and project notes

**The Wastes · selected historical events through the played S3E18 finale.** The first events table follows main-campaign developments through **E128 (end of Season One)**; the separate later table groups important Season Two and Three changes without claiming to index every episode or individual consequence. Use [the full chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md) for the researched episode sequence, [arc narratives](../narrative/README.md) for story-first reading and [episode dossiers](../episodes/E000.md) for evidence. Ancient dates, disputed genealogy and character testimony are **not established historical measurements**. E107–E109 is a played **prequel before E001**, irrespective of publication order; the completed-campaign horizon does **not** certify individual event accuracy beyond the cited processed-ASR sources. Original audio has not been independently listened to.
