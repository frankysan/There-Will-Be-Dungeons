# CHIDS — Center for Human-Demon Interdisciplinary Studies

> **Working formal name:** “Center for Human Demon Interdisciplinary Studies” (spoken repeatedly by the automated guide). **CHIDS** appears spelled out on the welcome-center sign in [E069](../../episodes/E069.md) (`C H I D S` in processed ASR); later automated dialogue may resemble `CETIS` / `C-TEST`. **Orthography needs audio/visual reference; do not silently standardize later spellings.** **Spoilers through E081; dossier derived from processed ASR, not listened to.**

## Introduction

**CHIDS** is a sprawling, largely abandoned research and industrial complex beneath the fortress at [Khaervilon](kervalon.md). Its remaining railways, laboratories, offices, security machinery and living biological corridors were built for a civilization whose own institutional messages describe cooperation between humans and beings from the Weir. That account of its founding is not the same as an independently verified history. By the time the travelers enter in E069, much of the site is ruined or depopulated, yet transit systems, weapons, virtual headsets and some laboratories still work. [E069](../../episodes/E069.md) · [E070](../../episodes/E070.md).

The story follows the party from a guarded service entrance through an old transit network, executive offices, a frozen data center, security and biological research floors, then the chamber of the **[Traversal Drive](../technology/traversal-drive.md)**. Several people and systems offer incompatible descriptions of the place: receptionist Julia's corporate script, a virtual illithid named Charles Dickens, the ancient elf Cerinthala and a talking hive of preserved minds. Their assertions about demon research, the origin of whole species and the alleged power to move or destroy planets remain **attributed testimony**. The directly observed site instead establishes functioning devices, trapped experimental subjects, actual battles and a dangerous machine operated in E081. [E072](../../episodes/E072.md) · [E077](../../episodes/E077.md) · [E081](../../episodes/E081.md).

**Latest witnessed site outcome:** After Hope defeats Dolo Popio's manifested body, **Sira and Eddie leave the central beam alive**, while **Chance remains missing**. All four heroes then voluntarily share the beam's energy and the GM declares them dead at the E081 cutoff. E082 follows different survivals and apparent deaths in **Sigil** rather than a new physical survey of CHIDS. No completed site-wide rescue, verified permanent shutdown or demonstrated planet-wide relocation is documented. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [E082](../../episodes/E082.md).

## Site, age and access

A large **pre-Scorching scientific-industrial complex** reached under [Khaervilon](kervalon.md) after the party discovers **twin steel doors** behind a hidden masonry section, revealed by **following black worms**. In **E069** a **sentient Door Authority panel** bars visitors for alleged crimes against “doormanity”; it yields after the group negotiates and **Nash applies a little Acid Splash**. A **purple-fogged, worm-bearing 45-degree funicular** descends into the complex; the vehicle crashes after **magical flower spores** put two riders to sleep, though **Stanley's Feather Fall** protects them.

The **welcome center** contains **skeletons, terminals, Julia the automated holographic receptionist**, and an old **facility route map** with blue, red and green subway lines. Julia describes work with **demons from the Weir**, **infernal-wave technologies**, and a **“mega cooker”**; she mentions a **United Federation of Continents** identity-registration system. These are **institutional messages from a long-dead era**, not evidence its human research program still operates.

Most dormitory staff are dead; cards recovered belong to **Stefan Borghetty**, **Bobby Bees**, **Dr. Yxtlee/Yxtle**, **Ricky Julian**, **Tara Mebat**, plus a later **Dr. Tara Mebatt** card in the executive offices ([E070](../../episodes/E070.md)). Names are provisional ASR and the two Tara cards could refer to the same person. The preserved **magically refrigerated/flight-granting underwear** originally belonged to a nonhuman scientist; **Nash wears it first**, and later it is shared for flight. The **food court (McDonald's, Subway, Manchu Wok)** and an **oxygen bar** are physically present, but Nash is **poisoned by a foul oxygen-mask output** (E069). Not all old equipment is harmless.

**Security checkpoint:** requires scanning cards and bodily features; **Hope's attempted passage triggers ceiling guns** and Vorel bends open an exit. The party **operates a subway** and passes many blocked stations, including **Laboratory C, storage, shipping/receiving, and administration**. **Gordon Shuttle Bay**, **Laboratory A with humming violet crystals**, and **executive offices** are reachable or partially visible in E069. Station names and route topology reflect the **in-session screen/map**, not independently surveyed geographical coordinates.

## Executive offices, virtual Illithid realm and alarms (E070–E071)

In **E070**, an office console offers a “game” when the protagonists type a name like **Oubvayx/Ovaxiad**. A **conference room has eight wired virtuality headsets**. Nash and Hope use them, experiencing illithid avatar bodies in a **restored version of the office**. The virtual illithid [Charles Dickens](../characters/charles-dickens.md) calls it an **ethereal projection to the Void Realm**, with a live corporate reporting system, a rival illithid command staff and the **Traversal Drive** planning to merge the worlds. **This is an inhabitant's explanation**, not physical confirmation of the device. The [illithid people profile](../races/illithids.md) distinguishes this workplace and the CHIDS incursion from an independent Sigil resident; none of those contexts establishes species-wide beliefs.

Nash **uploads Charles's false data into a reporting server**, with Hope providing cover through shapeshifted avatars. An illithid worker exposes the intrusion and **security alarms sound simultaneously in the real physical office**. The headsets turn into aggressive membranes, but **both escape**. **Hellhounds and demonic security apes** attack. In **E071** the party defeats one ape (the other remains **a Polymorphed snail**) and the hounds. Charles later **claims the upload worked**, remotely deactivates the alarm and ejects a **burning disk component**. **The supposed several-month delay to the Traversal Drive has not been measured or independently observed.**

## Other stations and the inaccessible machine (E071)

A small imp named [Alvert](../characters/alvert.md) says **engineering** likely houses the **Traversal Drive**, but the relevant **face-like powered door is offline**. The group sends a **railcar containing a still-living snail-transformed security ape** back toward unseen hostiles, **without witnessing a crash or reversion**. They proceed on foot along the railway and reach a **cold north transfer station** and the **green line**. The **Archives** station opens through **Door Authority negotiation plus an executive keycard**, but its internal reception terminals and doors **lack power**; no archival data is recovered. At the **data center**, the main steel door is **buried under ~20 feet of continuously refreezing ice**. **Stanley's Heat Metal** and **Vorel's Eye of Groxl** damage it without granting entry, so Hope finds a **high overhead vent** with less ice, breached by Nash and Stanley. **Four protagonists enter that vent at E071 end; Alvert remains outside**. Data center has **not been reached** and power **has not been restored**.

## Frozen data center, Cerinthala and biological labs (E072–E073)

The four adventurers crawl **from the E071 overhead ventilation duct** through a branching icy route with dangerous grates and fans. **Stanley's Heat Metal melts an obstruction, but the duct tears and collapses**; he casts **Feather Fall**, lowering everyone safely into an enormous **server hall** with high computer racks, luminous indicators under frozen flooring, and wall-sized **cooling fans**. A console under ice is **not an HVAC control terminal**, despite Nash's attempts. **Vorel passes the magical flight/cooling underwear back to Nash** while they explore. [E072](../../episodes/E072.md).

An ancient withered elf, **[Cerinthala](../characters/cerinthala.md)** (provisional spelling), lives behind a canvas windbreak in the same hall with four **frozen human figures named Toby, Carl, Gordon and Benson**. She says the mind flayer induced her to **lock the engineering bay** but she **now remotely unlocks it with a data pad** after Stanley promises to help her reach **Elhandar**. The **actual remote action is played**, but the engineering door is **not seen opening** and the Traversal Drive is **not found** in E072–E073. Cerinthala gives Stanley a **+1 AC wooden-totem necklace** whose **choking and electrical retaliation** is revealed to be **her self-made curse**, not a standard benign elven artifact. She originally contemplated **preying on the party**, later states that she changed her mind, and **accompanies the party** when they leave the server room. **The companion ice constructs assault the party following a complete long rest**, several shattering into animated frozen corpses nicknamed **ice boyfriends**. **Toby still speaks as an icy head/mouth** after the fight. [E072](../../episodes/E072.md) · [E073](../../episodes/E073.md).

Cerinthala claims **pre-Scorching researchers created lizardfolk soldiers and failed vampire forms**, made tiefling servants through human/demon genetic dealings, developed **“infernal wave” accelerated pregnancy equipment**, and tested **organic sound-detonating purple crystals in Laboratory A**. She also names a past official **“Principal Dombrovski.”** These are **her recollections**, distinct from direct evidence. The party observes **frozen workers, functional remote controls, a hydroponic Maltaweed garden, red-lit maintenance corridors, biological growths and research vessels**; this supports the existence of biological programs, **not their complete claimed origin stories or identity with today's Principal**.

In E073, Cerinthala leads the party through the **garden and a locker room**, where **Vorel spends hours cutting a ~25-foot deep ice blockage** with the **Eye of Groxl's fire**. They enter an **open maintenance tunnel** and reach laboratories with large vertical **cylinders and living pink-purple fleshlike membrane**. A tentacle **grabs Nash's staff and snaps it in two**, with the **nail-bearing short half still a valid arcane focus** under the GM's ruling. Cerinthala proposes fertilizing a **lizardfolk egg for Vorel** using her alleged accelerated gestation technology; **he rejects the offer**, and the device is **not activated**. An elevator bank has **four lifts and ten indicated sublevels**: the first lift **falls and crashes**; a second lift receives the group but **stalls on fraying cables between floors**. **Vorel opens its roof hatch** and the party spots a **huge spider-like creature coming down the shaft**. This is **the E073 cutoff**, not a completed descent to the deepest laboratory level. [E073](../../episodes/E073.md).

**Updated open site questions:** engineered species' real histories; Cerinthala's birth/name; exact **Elhandar** coordinates and shuttle operability; fate/locations of named frozen humans other than Toby; **engineering access after remote unlock**, source of purple growth and elevator spiders, and whether **“Principal Dombrovski”** is today's Principal. None is resolved by an NPC's assertions alone.

## Elevator shaft, lower weapons level and secure portal device (E074–E075)

**Elevator shaft:** In [E074](../../episodes/E074.md), an enormous **brain-backed spider matriarch** and hundreds of smaller spiders attack around the stalled lift. **Stanley casts Feather Fall** so the group can descend; his **sheep Polymorph** causes spider fall damage, **but Hope's later two shotgun shots kill the matriarch**. Vorel salvages carapace **shields for Cerinthala and Hope's tiny extra arm**, then forces open a **damaged laboratory landing**.

**Responsive flesh corridor:** Pink biological **membrane blocks Cerinthala and Nash but initially allows Hope/Stanley/Vorel**, despite all using the same hall. **Nash sings**, obtains passage, while Cerinthala's failed attempt ends with **her death by membrane enclosure**. The **dead elf is carried**, with black chalk/oil skin residue that causes **Nash's tongue reaction when tasted**. E075 later **shows her return to life**, so E074 should not be described as final death. **A later pursued Cerinthala appears to escape near this same corridor**, leaving **ice residue and greasy tracks**, route unobserved.

**Lower control room:** Two **green-scaled, energy-rifle lizardfolk** are killed ahead of a motion-activated door. An illithid behind it **pockets a disk**, summons a **red many-faced memory-devourer** and attempts escape. **Hope grapples the illithid**, **Vorel destroys the red mass** in E075, and **dragon-Nash kills the operator** with Hope still holding its neck. The red mass produces **tethered copies of Vorel and dragon-Nash**, which vanish with it; **Vorel/Nash/Stanley all suffer ongoing 1d4 memory-drain effects**. **Hope crushes headworms from the illithid and both killed guards**; the pocketed disk **is not recovered**. [E074](../../episodes/E074.md) · [E075](../../episodes/E075.md).

**Inner secure vault:** Behind the first control console a second room houses **unusually silent clean metal containment chambers**, protected by **a sealed main door, two control buttons, multiple valves and an outside terminal**. The party performs the access sequence; a cradle yields **a single white/gray dual-trigger red/green portal apparatus**. **Vorel retrieves it**, then gives it to **Hope** after discovering **two wall marks link as traversable gateways**. It is **a small recovered local device, not the larger Traversal Drive**. Shots fired at bodies do not cause apparent damage; endpoint duration, maximum range and broader limitations unknown. [Portal device](../technology/portal-device.md).

**Cerinthala and Toby:** During vault operation the previously dead elf **visibly revives**, proves to have a **historic CHIDS employee ID** recovered in her coats, appears to **summon ice Toby from the blue gem**, and **paralyzes Hope with Hold Person** while announcing that her illithid contact wants the adventurers **alive**. **The group kills the ice construct and reanimated Toby corpse**, **Stanley dispels her Wall of Ice**, but **Cerinthala escapes**. Her past testimony about engineering lizardfolk remains **unconfirmed by laboratory records**.

**Military pressure at E075 end:** **A mass of armed bugbears** enters this area, apparently burning obstructive flesh with flame weapons. **Nash's fourth-level Fireball kills front ranks**, then **Vorel steps into the hall and taunts perhaps fifty bugbears with an illithid behind them**. They **charge**. Other adventurers spot **an air-blowing vent and possible traversable exit in the secured back-room area**, but do not explore it. They stand ready to **escape using Hope's portal apparatus**, but **neither that escape nor use of the vent has been played**. **Sira missing; no world-moving engine observed**.

## Containment chasm, armory and living hive (E076–E077)

**Escaped pursuit and chasm:** [E076](../../episodes/E076.md) completes **the previously only planned portal evacuation** from the second illithid and the charging bugbears. **Hope shoots the isolated illithid dead**, while the surviving bugbear force is not wiped out. The team enters **Containment** by a flesh-lined passage that initially excludes Nash, requiring **Dimension Door**. A broken bridge over a **deep chasm** and a jammed door force an elaborate **portal crossing** with **Nash's flying underwear**. **Hope shoots down a winged predator** while more beasts call below.

**Phantasms and preparation room:** Farther in, **translucent blue scientist forms** reenact tasks and speech, apparently registering noises or touch but not directly typing on the physical keyboards. Real terminals display **lizardfolk equipment status** including *psy blade/nanoweave* terms. A supply room yields **two rockets for Hope**, code-locked white boxes (still unopened), **Blue Bull** stimulant-like blue vials, **Monster Mender** red healing vials, and **one lizardfolk anti-dragon set** (skin suit, harpoon harness, jump boots, helmet). Vorel dons the set; **the suit interferes with his unarmored AC**, even while supplying anti-dragon protection and psychic-resistant headgear. **The Eye of Groxl reacts with anger** toward deeper containment. Hope pockets **five of each vial**, Vorel an additional **one each**; subsequent use is recorded in E077.

**Subjects and brain training:** In [E077](../../episodes/E077.md), the team sees **rows of deceased lizardfolk floating in green fluid**, panels with *status/instruction/home*, and direct **behavioral/neural conditioning macros**. This is evidence of historic experiments on particular subjects, **not proof all modern lizardfolk were created here**. Two larger vessels carry labels resembling **Lethanphius** and **G-R-0-X-1**. The living wall itself releases **Lethanphius**, a winged humanoid with metal bones, who fights and is **killed by Nash's Fire Bolt** after Hope's **necrotic-beam mode** from the portal apparatus cleaves him. Vorel suffers severe injury; **Monster Mender reattachment is anticipated at E077 end and performed during E078's on-screen procedure**.

**Hive council and engineering:** Living pink flesh **forms a humanoid representative** that claims to be a **self-governing collective of thousands of preserved human minds**, CHIDS's former wisdom archive, now defending the complex from illithids. It says its **leadlike tissues shield against telepathic detection**, and it deliberately opened the vessel after concluding the party might help. It attributes the defeated subject to **anti-dragon heaven-derived research** and says **GR0X1 was a prototype** that escaped; these are the council's claims, not yet independently corroborated archive records. A **second flesh-made figure resembling Nash** alleges it comes from a future in which the world is destroyed; the party **questions the claim**. The council reports an **illithid breach at the engineering site housing the Traversal Drive** and offers access codes and **future transport through flesh conduits to an assault site**. **These offers are not yet fulfilled** and **the party does not reach the Drive by E077**.

## Prepared arsenal, connected-flesh ingress and transformed Drive chamber (E078–E079)

**[E078](../../episodes/E078.md):** Hive council gives **cache codes** and **two Rings of Spell Storing**; it claims the **lizardfolk species predates its own locally engineered soldiers** and that **destroying the Drive directly could destroy the planet**. The party **actually repairs Vorel's severed limbs** using Monster Mender and later completes a **real eight-hour Tiny Hut rest**. All three locked weapon caches yield **20 Vorpal Laser Swords and four bracer blades**, of which **three swords** are individually claimed and **two blades installed on Vorel**; **17 swords, spare bracers and ~11 anti-dragon suits/related equipment** go into Stanley's Bag of Holding. The wall **carries the party through its connected organic structure to the recreation/engineering wing**, ending the earlier 'proposed only' transport. The group kills an initial illithid and laboratory guards, then rescues **Chance and Eddie Manchester III** from a second room holding two illithids, a human caster and a security ape. The ape room contains **a pool of small tentacled creatures**. The gnomes survive, the defenders die, and **the Drive is not yet reached**.

**[E079](../../episodes/E079.md):** Gnomes' account describes **guarded recreation/gym/cafeteria and earlier unusually comfortable captivity**. Party takes **short rest and disguises Nash as an illithid**, passing two ape guards by deception. Blue-purple vapor and crystalline cave begin beyond. A **four-finger biometric-seeming door** rejects living gnome and **dead illithid** hand attempts; **Stanley Dimension Doors with Hope to the far side, then Hope completes the red/green portal pair for the others**, not a successfully unlocked keypad. The engineering cavern beyond appears **substantially remodeled by crystal**, with multiple platforms, **a vertical beam similar to Khaervilon's**, three chanting illithids, Cerinthala, an **Ovaxiad-like large illithid**, ranged lizardfolk guards and a **rotting dragon front fused to a wormlike hind body**. **Hope's two explosive rockets** propel **three illithids and Cerinthala's current body** into the beam, visually disintegrating them while clearing several guards. A **large illithid survives, floats upward**, and **dragon-worm, beam and Sira are not resolved**. These observations supersede E077's "Drive unvisited" statement **at the later cutoff only**.

## The engineering battle after the opening rockets (E080)

In [E080](../../episodes/E080.md), the GM recap places **Sira within the brightly lit central region** beside the apparent dragon-worm **Dolo Popio**. The hovering **large illithid commander (Uvexiad/Ovaxiad spelling pending)** produces Mirror Image doubles, **Mind Blasts the party**, summons previously encountered **red memory-eating monsters**, then fires **black necrotic lightning** that incapacitates both gnomes. **Stanley uses Feather Fall and animates ten nonmagical pipes** because the cache's Vorpal Laser Swords are magical and cannot be targets for this *Animate Objects* adjudication. **Hope's shots explode green worms in chains**. **Eddie recovers on a natural-20 death save and Chance is revived by a Ring of Spell Storing Healing Word**. **Nash, transformed into a young black dragon, ultimately kills the illithid commander with acid breath**, but **not** the dragon-worm.

Dolo Popio invokes an **alleged contract with a garbled name**, and the characters **find themselves on floating rock/crystal platforms in a starry void**. It is **not established** whether CHIDS, Earth or the machine literally moved. The central dragon-worm, pool and **Sira** remain; **Vorel leaps using suit power boots, shapes the Eye of Groxl's flame shield into a flaming greatblade and grapples onto the monster**. Dolo Popio then **begins an enormous breath attack**, whose results are **deferred beyond E080**. Neither **Sira's rescue** nor a **safe shutdown of the Traversal Drive** occurs in this episode.

## Arc-finale machine operation and disappearance (E081)

[E081](../../episodes/E081.md) continues the transformed Drive chamber's **starry void phase**. **Chance slips from a floating platform and remains lost/unlocated**, while **Vorel removes his CHIDS nanoweave suit and binds Dolo Popio's jaws** to obstruct more worm breath. **Eddie nearly dies again but Stanley uses a ring-stored Healing Word** to revive him; Hope removes a burrowing worm from her chest with her **detachable metal horn**. A **fire truck floats through the impossible battlefield**. **Hope fires radiant darts from Scully and destroys Dolo Popio's manifested body**; the party's battlefield returns to **CHIDS engineering**, but **Chance does not reappear**.

**Stanley, Eddie and Sira remain trapped in the central light**. Hope investigates a machine console with **frequency dial, colored beam fader and other hazardous controls**. **Quizquaz, an oil-black winged demon**, appears, claims the **Void-sourced Drive beam could annihilate Earth and the Principal**, and urges accepting that option. He says a powerful subject can **absorb its energy**, and teaches a **flat waveform for passage / high-frequency crimson mode for absorption**. **Eddie and Sira are actually released from the beam**; Sira briefly becomes invisible and later appears beside Eddie, who promises to care for her. Quizquaz leaves after being pressed to volunteer himself.

The heroes **reject Quizquaz's offered planetary destruction**. **Stanley asks Moha by Augury about a self-sacrifice and gets a favorable yet final omen**, then **all four choose to share the beam** instead of leaving him alone. **Eddie operates controls outside**; the four **glow, take enormous damage, and disperse as white light and mist**, with the **GM expressly declaring them dead at E081 cutoff**. **Sira and Eddie remain alive outside**, **Chance unlocated**, and the narrative **does not show later planet-wide surveys, successful safe disassembly, souls' locations or a permanent Drive shutoff**. The exact state after the activation belongs to later material, not this E081 source.

## Current status and source boundary

At the **final directly witnessed CHIDS site cutoff (E081)**, the engineering chamber returns from its impossible star-field surroundings; Dolo Popio's manifested body has been defeated, and the party has operated the dangerous beam. Sira and Eddie **physically escape the beam alive**. Chance is still missing after falling away during the fight. The four adventurers **choose to enter the energy together**, vanish, and are explicitly declared dead by the GM **at that cutoff**. The surviving companions do not inspect the whole complex, clear every living subject, collect a post-event inventory or prove the Traversal Drive can never function again. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md).

**Beyond the site:** [E082](../../episodes/E082.md) describes the four adventurers' divergent fates in **Sigil**: Vorel has a living body, Nash survives as an engineered heart, and Hope and Stanley are found motionless. The subsequent [Sigil account](sigil.md) is not a return visit to CHIDS. Later survivals must not be retroactively treated as if the E081 death declaration never occurred. The hive's accounts of ancient planetary relocation and Quizquaz's claim about destroying the Principal or Earth are still **claims**, not measured results of the machine's activation.

## February 2022 GM design retrospective — intended one-person sacrifice, played all-person choice

In the [February 2022 bonus Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2022-02.md) (~00:23–00:26), Beau explains that **his original design of the E081 Traversal Drive's life-energy mechanism anticipated one character heroically volunteering to die to seal the gateway**. The players instead **all chose to make the sacrifice together**, an outcome GM did not pre-script. Beau was reading **Planescape** and **improvised a path onward to Sigil** rather than permanently ending the story. This account describes **GM preparation and response to unexpected choices**, and does **not replace the directly played E081 ending**: all four sacrifice, are declared dead at that session's cutoff, and later events must be sourced in their later episodes. It also does **not** mean that the mechanism actually required only one death in the presented game. [Comparison](../../reports/BONUS_2022-02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## Unsettled and evidence tiers

- **Observed:** building/rails/map, cards, hologram, automated door, virtual headsets, physical security response, frozen data center exterior.
- **Earlier speaker claims (E067–E071):** ancient partnership/technologies (Julia), VR's astral mechanics / literal Void Realm / data effect (Charles), Traversal Drive location and offlined power (Alvert). **At that earlier cutoff** the party had not inspected the machine. **E079 directly reveals the engineering chamber**, and **E081 the party operates the controls**. The spectral dragon's planet-moving account and Quizquaz's Void-energy explanation remain **attributed claims**, not independently verified technical history.
- **Dates:** exact facility construction, destruction, real age, chronological relation between the **old dragon war** and humanity's demon research still uncertain.
- **Inventory:** E071 **magical flying briefs move from Nash to Stanley to Hope to Vorel**, last worn by **Vorel** in the approach vent; **E072 Vorel returns them to Nash**, who wears them in the server room. They are **one shared item**, not four pairs.
- **Names:** CHIDS/C-TEST, Dr. Yxtlee, Uobvayx/Ovaxiad, station spellings; seek audio/visual handout before canonizing.
