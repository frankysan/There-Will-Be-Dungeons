# Tietkalla — Teet keep and Trashburg

**Spellings:** **Tietkalla** is cast-written by Jon and a strong context-supported historical city/kingdom spelling ([name report](../../reports/DISCORD_SPELLING_2026-10-21.md)); `Tiat Cala`, `Tiat Kala`, `Teet Kala` appear in processed ASR. **Teet** commonly refers to the keep/fortress and **Trashburg** to the poorer settlement around/below it. The exact administrative equivalence across ancient and present-day uses is **not always established**. **Spoilers:** E028–E104 (with separately sourced intermediate developments).

## The city of gangs and a wedding

The party approaches in **Fate 4**, encountering the music and mysteries of the oil sands, then enters **Trashburg** in E031. The settlement's poverty, local coercion and heavy security stand in sharp contrast with the fortress overlooking it. The **Guzzle Gang/Suck Master Din** exercises power from the keep while the **Twenty-Sixers** under **Monsoon Cabbage** and **Toe Rippers** compete below. **Charlemagne's lodging**, a Blood Ball stadium and training ground, the **Golden Gargoyle** music venue, a clandestine clinic, rival gang territories, and the so-called **Pixie Blush** trade all matter to the party.

**Jolt's intended wedding** to a prince becomes a proposed point of entry. The party **trains for Blood Ball**, but a game is **not actually played** in the second arc. Nash meets the pixie **P0D**, who reports the exploitation of pixies in the drug trade; the group repeatedly discusses rescue but does not carry out the prepared full-scale pixie liberation route in Arc Two.

## The Pit and the underground kingdom

Escaping persecution, the party enters the **Pit of Despair**, a punitive vertical pit whose original froghemoth lives beside strange glowing spheres. **George Miller** descends with the party; **Diontalus kills him E045**. The lower oily cave network holds puddings, a singing self-styled river king, a basilisk later used in emergency restorative preparations, a skeleton with a **gag enchanted earring** and an undeciphered private paper, a riddle, enchanted beans and a skeleton-marked key. A **huge green beanstalk** opens an older upper entrance.

Beyond the hatch are **ancient tiefling skeletal remains, enchanted armor, a living illustrated book**, a library, a hidden bureau, a ritual pentagram, a partly broken **prison lift**, and a secret **crypt** linked to the old water-throne infrastructure. The party frees an **elder tiefling**, probably referred to as **Nobiery/Nobieri** but orthography unresolved, who claims a vanished Akmenos kingdom and a hidden army. The party reaches the keep prison via the lift in E048 and **frees Tedna and Bok Bok alive**, though **Tedna later dies E050**.

The old **pool/pipe/Gem of Tides** chamber is reached in E050. **Kefeli**, a woman with petrifying snake hair/gaze, claims legitimacy and says she transformed to protect subjects from an older vampire menace and **Andreeste**. **Hope and Vorel are temporarily petrified E051 and then restored in the same episode**, while Kefeli is petrified and destroyed. Her extraordinary powers are directly encountered; her historical justification, the complete royal genealogy, and the consequences of killing her remain **disputed testimony**.

## The warehouse disaster and the siege

A hidden **grain/food storeroom** hosts Jolt and Buttons' betrayal. **Monsoon's Order 26** causes P0D's unwilling giant transformation E049; **Vorel kills Buttons E049**, **Diontalus kills P0D E050**, and **Tedna, Dashington and Jolt** also die in the aftermath. The party retrieves the **Gem of Tides**. Nearby mechanical/stone-age and ancient chambers provide routes away from the violence.

As the fight spreads, an enlarged multi-eyed **froghemoth** swallows the party, who navigate its digestive system. In E053 the froghemoth **splits**: the **upper cocoon-like portion flies away**, while the **lower portion falls into the desert and decomposes**. This must not be simplified to a witnessed extinction of the whole organism.

## The changed throne at E054

The adventurers return on **Nash in young-black-dragon form**, finding **Trashburg devastated and keep walls breached**. Giant transformed pixies begin returning to ordinary size. The throne room contains **Monsoon** with a dead prince, restrained **Suck Master Din**, and **Angelashi alive as Lazlo's hostage**. Monsoon warns of a coming **Andreeste claim** before **escaping as mist**. The party directly **frees Angelashi alive**, superseding the earlier report of her death.

The old elder **returns with hundreds of armed tieflings**; their acclamation and a black crown make **Hope's coronation public and real**. **Din is executed by Vorel at Hope's signal**. Leaders discuss security sweeps, farms, rebuilding, anti-human prejudice, an heir and the remaining Principal threat. **No completed months-long rebuilding or settled government has yet been played**. The original 1,000-soldier census and purported ancient sacrificial bargain remain unverified.

## Three months of rule and the stone storm — E055–E056

After **three played months elapse between E054 and E055**, Tietkalla has a functioning queen's court, **repaired walls and vehicles**, a new **Tidebearer/Fishbird church**, and the **Golden Sands inn/adventurers' guild**, where Vorel and Bok Bok work. Hope attempts **merit-based offices, abolition of serf-like houseless status, plumbing/toilets and new defense orders**, provoking noble-house competition. The chief engineer reports serious **wood/stone/steel shortages**. Some reconstruction has occurred; the city is not yet a perfectly reformed or self-sufficient state. **Softhead** enters court as a **claimed envoy of the Principle**, offering trade and release of remaining Dusthill slaves, then **has his tongue removed at Hope's order but survives E055**; no returned prisoners or actual summit witnessed. A demon-invoking attacker later wounds Hope before being killed by guards. [E055](../../episodes/E055.md).

A comet-like flash and **stone storm** strike the region. **E056 directly portrays civilian deaths**, panicked shelter movement and the party's attempted evacuation to sewers. Under the keep, **Nobiery stages a ritual in the old laboratory**, enabling Hope's ethereal encounter. **George and P0D appear as ghosts**, not living returned characters. **Vorel's aging is healed in E056**. Priestess **Emenil** reacts to secret Soulbroker/demonic contracts, and **Nobiery commands an undead Carnage to bite her at the cliffhanger**; her fate is **unknown at E056 cutoff**. **Quizquaz's theory** of a dragon struck from the sky and an Andreeste contractor is **interested testimony**, not site-confirmed explanation of the storm. [E056](../../episodes/E056.md).

**External routes:** A traveling trader describes **Mogen Frogan**, the **Diamond Stroke**, **Esophageal Pass** and **Principle City**; only his visit and statements are observed, not the group's travel to those sites in E055. [April bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-04.md) is a GM/cast roundtable, not an additional riot or storm event.

**Settlement naming lead:** In E055 the GM's three-month recap refers to the rebuilt/renamed neighborhood previously called Trashburg using a term transcribed approximately `Orfrewyz/Orferwis`. Its spelling and whether it covers only the lower town or the full capital require verification; **do not backdate it to E031–E054**. [E055](../../episodes/E055.md).

## Rail diplomacy and returned Dusthill survivors — E057–E059

**Emenil is bitten by a raised corpse but healed in E057**, and survives to attend the Principle's formal court visit in E058. The Principle **promises** construction materials and **released Dusthill slaves** while proposing **rail access, a small embassy and Steel God place of worship**, all of which raise security and sovereignty questions. **In E059 several identifiable Dusthill survivors actually arrive**—among them Talia, Bruce Bane, Shun'ti and Jimster—and are directed toward **Sand Sanctuary** screening. The government provisionally agrees to rail **in principle**, not to a railway already operating. **The Principle extracts Diontalus into a crystal and departs with it**; permanent imprisonment remains untested. ## Hope's later return and the damaged rail network — E103

[E103](../../episodes/E103.md) **does not portray an abandoned or destroyed city**. Hope returns to a **populated keep**, with functioning **Golden Sands** (Bok Bok working), a **Fishbird church** and living tieflings. **Celibus Stump**, the former Slavetown businessman, has been appointed acting chancellor during Hope's absence and **dies upon her return**. A ***Speak with Dead*** answer claims **three months** have elapsed, rather than a new time jump invented by the editor. **Jimster and Emenil** report major house disagreements, Nobiery's departure from governance and **rail supply attacks**; **rumors of three vanished political opponents** remain unverified, not a known final casualty list.

Hope and the new henchmen **actually ignite the temporarily closed, empty railway station**, using a magical arrow and ***Wall of Fire***; city residents rush to extinguish the blaze. The building is aflame and rail operations apparently disrupted at cutoff, but **repair, casualties, duration of service outage and citywide political fallout are not shown**. A **blue-white sky fissure** and new rains are visible/mentioned; exact cause unverified. [Dual-ASR](../../reports/E103_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## E104 — Carthax's invasion and Hope's escape

The station burns down to **ash and rubble**; the Voice accepts that deed. **Omnom Cinderborn is physically present and alive**, reporting his failure to retake Razak Val. Hope publicly rejects further dealings with the Principle. Before she can settle affairs, **a floating castle and winged force attack**, burning streets and striking the keep with a meteor that brings down a tower. Hope and Dr. Spider direct residents toward shelter, while Steerious carries two tieflings. **Carthax breaches the courtyard as Hope's group escapes through a ring gate**; the two carried residents and Spatulo go too. Destination unseen. **Celibus's zombie is last encountered during evacuation**. The full casualty list, survival of Bok Bok/Emenil/Jimster/Omnom after the assault and extent of lasting city destruction remain unobserved. The Voice's anticipation is not proof he commanded the invaders. [E104](../../episodes/E104.md) · [comparison](../../reports/E104_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).
