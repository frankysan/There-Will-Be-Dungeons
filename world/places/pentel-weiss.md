# Pentel Weiss — the prison beyond the nebula

The travelers of *The Wastes* encounter **Pentel Weiss** as an immense, glyph-covered **pyramid prison** beyond a dangerous purple nebula. Its entrance can be reached through a strange, tiny blue-edged sphere in space. The prison holds living and undead captives, animated guardians, dangerous sealed chambers and an unevenly enforced **suppression of spellcasting**. In Season Two, the companions search it for the missing Elhandari engineer **Tungsten Wheel** and for clues about the separate **Eye of the Voice**. They succeed in **rescuing Tungsten** and escaping, but do **not** recover the Eye or establish what becomes of the entire prison. [S2E07](../../episodes/S2E07.md) · [S2E12](../../episodes/S2E12.md).

## The route through the nebula

The route begins with navigation coordinates recovered from the **Bank of Burp** on Blast Belly Rock, connected to Tungsten's old work. In [S2E07](../../episodes/S2E07.md), Hope pilots [Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md) into hazardous space, following her **jack-in-the-box direction finder**, which points toward the sought prison. Electrical storms, whale-like creatures and ship damage make the passage costly; **three newly recruited orc sailors die or are lost** en route. A glowing sphere roughly six feet across reveals an apparently distant dark pyramid through distorted space.

Nash first flies through the sphere alone to investigate and returns. Hope then **actually flies Fate Six through it**, a passage that distorts the vessel before it regains its normal appearance. The crew records **separate return coordinates** on board. The ship docks alongside the gigantic prison; the four protagonists, Union Wheel and alternate **Badass T** disembark. This is a **played arrival**, not a prison merely mentioned by a compass or by a rumored Eye location.

## Intake and the warden

The public entrance contains an inscribed stone interior, statues, an administrative **keeper** and a dark **inmate intake shaft**. The keeper denies access to inmate information without credentials belonging to **collectors**. Hope's attempt to use Tungsten's name gains no clearance; it does not establish that he is registered there. At the end of S2E07, Badass T **actually jumps down the shaft** before the party knows whether he survived. [S2E07](../../episodes/S2E07.md).

In [S2E08](../../episodes/S2E08.md), the companions search the pyramid's outside for another entrance, but their extensive sweep finds **no working secret door**. A gray-haired, gray-robed **warden with a damaged or scarred eye** appears and announces that the prison is closed to new business. The warden's statement is **not proof the prison has been physically abandoned or destroyed**: golem staff, captives and internal threats remain present. Hope wonders whether his eye is the **Eye of the Voice**, but its identity is **never established**, and the GM questions what Hope herself knows about that separate relic. The main four and Union later **jump down the intake shaft** and land in icy water. Badass T is **found alive** below and helps rescue them from a large, toothed aquatic creature. The party escapes the water, **not the prison**.

## Inhabitants and the search for Tungsten

Badass T leads the newcomers to a prisoner camp whose residents reveal werecreature forms and lock the party in. [S2E09](../../episodes/S2E09.md) resolves the fight: the wererat Ziptyree (phonetic name) and **Morton the wereboar** die, while **Grandma Tilly and the other named werefolk survive and yield**. Badass T is badly injured, stabilized, and shows fur after a werebear bite; Tilly calls this a developing curse, but a complete transformation or cure is **not played**. The heroes **leave him under Tilly's care**, planning to return but not actually bringing him onward.

A werebear guide shows the party a hidden passage into the wider prison. Its halls include motionless golems, an altar-like junction and a community of detained mages. A self-styled prisoner representative challenges the visitors. **Tungsten Wheel is actually found alive**, and he and his niece Union Wheel **physically reunite**. Tungsten claims that collectors once wore rings able to bypass the prison's suppression, that he was seized from his bed years earlier, and that a dangerous thing in another sector may have consumed collectors. These are **Tungsten's accounts or theories**, not fully verified prison records. He agrees to accompany the party; hostile inmates let them pass after a confrontation. [S2E09](../../episodes/S2E09.md).

## The crypt and the creature beyond the eye

The expedition continues into old crypt corridors in [S2E10](../../episodes/S2E10.md). Animated carpets attack and are defeated. The heroes encounter two ancient, seated speakers who in [S2E11](../../episodes/S2E11.md) call themselves **liches**, with provisional transcriptions **Egnails the Maggot Lord** and **Archeloft the Obtuserizer**. They speak of phylacteries outside the prison and offer dangerous plans for release; the party does **not** kill them to test their claims.

In the adjoining chamber, a huge telepathic, tentacled **eye-like entity** traps visitors in pleasing imagined lives. Those fantasies are **not historical events**. Hope and the others discover that **music**, unlike brute force, can break its control; the entity and its tendrils **actually dissolve** after the group sings. Searching the freed chamber, Nash **retrieves one ring** from a captive corpse and puts it on. This is the only recovered **candidate collector ring**, **not** the Eye of the Voice. A private message from Hope's Voice about further power at the *top* of Pentel Weiss remains a **claim**, not an acquired or identified item. [S2E11](../../episodes/S2E11.md) · [Eye of the Voice](../technology/eye-of-the-voice.md).

## The escape and the prison left behind

In [S2E12](../../episodes/S2E12.md), Archeloft **closes a heavy stone partition** while on the far side, trapping the companions with Egnails and a rising mummy force. Stanley wears the recovered ring and **successfully casts spells inside the suppressed prison**, providing practical evidence for its limited bypass function. **Vorel destroys the mummy lord**; **Egnails is left alive, shut inside a sarcophagus**, while Archeloft is **not shown killed or captured**. Nash genuinely dies during combat; Stanley briefly places the ring on the dead body's finger. The ring's later fate becomes uncertain when Nash's body is transformed/reduced after departure.

Hope **successfully teleports six people to the still-docked Fate Six**: the four original companions, Tungsten and Union, with Nash's then-dead body included. The GM permits this because ship and prison are within the same accessed location; an initial planarity objection is **superseded by the final ruling**. **Badass T is not rescued** from the werefolk and is explicitly absent from the departing group. Fate Six physically leaves the pyramid dock and passes back through the sphere into the nebula; **Tungsten subsequently cures Stanley's mummy rot aboard ship**, and Nash survives in a later observed heart-and-eyestalk form. [S2E12](../../episodes/S2E12.md).

## Current status and limits

**Last witnessed condition (S2E12):** The party and rescued **Tungsten Wheel** have **departed Pentel Weiss aboard Fate Six**. The gigantic pyramid and its remaining occupants **are not shown destroyed, fully evacuated, permanently sealed or freed**. The warden's later whereabouts, administrative authority and damaged eye remain unknown. **Badass T is left with Grandma Tilly's group**, with his possible lycanthropy and long-term fate unconfirmed by this source window. **Egnails remains imprisoned alive within the sarcophagus; Archeloft's final whereabouts are unverified**. The giant telepathic eye creature and mummy lord are actually defeated; neither is proven to be the sought **Eye of the Voice**, which the party **does not obtain**.

This article separates **performed travel, combat, rescue and departure** from **keeper/warden claims, inmate testimony, private instructions to Hope and planned return trips**. The cited episode dossiers derive from processed speech-recognition text; **original audio, GM maps, item sheets and a complete later census of prisoners have not been independently checked**. The prison's alternate phonetic spellings, including **Pentelvice**, remain source variants until further evidence settles the name. A later lack of visits does not prove a permanent shutdown.
