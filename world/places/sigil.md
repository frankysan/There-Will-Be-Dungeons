# Sigil — City of Doors (The Wastes, E082 onward)

> **First Wastes appearance:** [E082 — Binch Better Have My Money](../../episodes/E082.md). Evidence comes from the [processed ASR](../../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E082.%20Binch%20Better%20Have%20My%20Money.parakeet.processed.srt), **not independently audio-verified**. **Sigil is part of The Wastes' third arc, not the separate Inverse reality.**

## Directly witnessed

After [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) shows the four-person beam dissolution, **Vorel awakens alive in a refuse-filled brick sewer**, extremely fatigued and stripped of previous gear. **Nash's mind persists in a floating black stone/metal heart** that sprouts an eye and eventually a finger. **Hope and Stanley are found as inert bodies** by Vorel among the sewer corpse collectors; Hope lacks metal prosthetic arm and leg, and Stanley is described as **cold, blue/bloated and dead**. Vorel refuses to surrender their bodies and pushes both to the surface on a hovering collector's slab.

The city above is **physically depicted** with busy streets that **curve upward and around overhead**, buildings occupying what would otherwise be the sky, diverse travelers and merchants. This is directly shown in [E082](../../episodes/E082.md) ~01:42–01:45.

## E083 — petitioners, the vanished church and work in Sigil

In [E083](../../episodes/E083.md), **Squirts**, a kobold with her infant **Chunks**, and **Nothing**, a mimicry-speaking kenku, accompany Vorel and Nash from the Soggy Feather. Squirts brings the party to a **shabby trickster church** far across the city, **not confirmed to be the Welcome Center's separate “50 Gums Road” referral**. Its birdlike priest, whose name sounds like **Kidigree** (provisional), uses a **crystal viewing device** to examine Stanley and asserts that **Stanley and Hope are petitioners associated with the Weir**, rather than ordinary mortal corpses. **His claims** about the Far Realm and petitioner metaphysics should not be confused with a demonstrated return to life or with independently verified connections to [Château Weir](chateau-weir.md).

The priest offers to **attempt resurrection later** if Vorel helps humiliate an unidentified powerful person before peers and subjects. **Assistant Susu takes the slab and both bodies into purportedly secure crypts**; no ritual is performed. After the group exits, the **road to the church is no longer visible**, replaced by houses when the party looks back. The concealment is observed; its magical mechanism is unknown.

Back at the **Soggy Feather**, **Nothing is paid a gold coin by Bill** for guiding, and Nash awakens after a rest with **a speaking mouth and functional hand** while still in heart form. The Welcome Center sends **three Pelor-affiliated helpers**, who describe Sigil's god-exclusion, Lady of Pain and violent-faction restrictions as local political/religious explanations. Their suggestions of accounts management, leatherwork, fighting or bounty work **do not lead to employment**. Bill identifies the subterranean **Fiend's Hoof** as a possible bounty-business lead, **not visited**.

The party pursues the **Big Time Smack'em Brothers** tournament instead: a **1,000-gold prize is advertised**, **Vorel, Nash and Nothing register at 76 Mulberry Lane**, and the qualifier is assigned to **Royce Gymnasium**. They see marionette controls, decline paid practice, receive a free beginner pamphlet, then visit the **M-sports library** to investigate a popular competitive treatise. They **do not compete, win money, take a bounty, or obtain the sought book** by episode's end. Neither the Lady of Pain nor a portal home is witnessed.

## Current cutoff and unresolved questions

At **E083's end**, **Vorel is still alive and pursuing a way home**; **Nash remains a floating heart but can speak and use one hand**; **Hope and Stanley are still unreanimated and left in a trickster church's crypts** under a conditional promise of future help. **Nothing and Squirts are named**, Squirts is accompanied by infant Chunks, and **three party members have registered for a tournament without competing yet**. The original Eye of Groxl protective-sacrifice status persists; no new proof of its recoverability exists. **The Drive, Earth, the Principle, Chance, Sira and Eddie** are not observed here.

**Evidence policy:** Treat Sigil's physical architecture and characters' played actions as observations; distinguish **Binch's and Bill's planar theories** from demonstrated outcomes. Do not substitute external D&D/Planescape lore for the show's recorded events. Keep both **E081's contemporary death declaration** and **E082's newly shown different outcomes**. ## Later retrospective interpretation — December 2019 cast Q&A

Beau later explains in [the December 2019 bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-12.md) (~00:04–00:06 and ~00:28–00:30, standard-copy times) that **Hope and Stanley's apparent bodies are manifestations of their souls**, interrupted en route to **the Weir**, where their Soulbroker promise directed them. This gives **creator-level retrospective support** to the trickster priest's **petitioner diagnosis in E083**, but is **not new game action**: the bodies remain in crypt custody, no promised ritual succeeds on-screen, and the exact planar mechanism or travel home is not demonstrated. **This knowledge is not backdated to Vorel at E082's first corpse discovery.** The Q&A [ASR comparison](../../reports/BONUS_2019_12_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md) is unverified against audio.
