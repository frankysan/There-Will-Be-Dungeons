# Sigil — City of Doors (The Wastes, E082 onward)

> **First Wastes appearance:** [E082 — Binch Better Have My Money](../../episodes/E082.md). Evidence comes from the [processed ASR](../../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E082.%20Binch%20Better%20Have%20My%20Money.parakeet.processed.srt), **not independently audio-verified**. **Sigil is part of The Wastes' third arc, not the separate Inverse reality.**

## Directly witnessed

After [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) shows the four-person beam dissolution, **Vorel awakens alive in a refuse-filled brick sewer**, extremely fatigued and stripped of previous gear. **Nash's mind persists in a floating black stone/metal heart** that sprouts an eye and eventually a finger. **Hope and Stanley are found as inert bodies** by Vorel among the sewer corpse collectors; Hope lacks metal prosthetic arm and leg, and Stanley is described as **cold, blue/bloated and dead**. Vorel refuses to surrender their bodies and pushes both to the surface on a hovering collector's slab.

The city above is **physically depicted** with busy streets that **curve upward and around overhead**, buildings occupying what would otherwise be the sky, diverse travelers and merchants. This is directly shown in [E082](../../episodes/E082.md) ~01:42–01:45.

## E083 — petitioners, the vanished church and work in Sigil

In [E083](../../episodes/E083.md), **Squirts**, a kobold with her infant **Chunks**, and **Nothing**, a mimicry-speaking kenku, accompany Vorel and Nash from the Soggy Feather. Squirts brings the party to a **shabby trickster church** far across the city, **not confirmed to be the Welcome Center's separate “50 Gums Road” referral**. Its birdlike priest, whose name sounds like **Kidigree** (provisional), uses a **crystal viewing device** to examine Stanley and asserts that **Stanley and Hope are petitioners associated with the Weir**, rather than ordinary mortal corpses. **His claims** about the Far Realm and petitioner metaphysics should not be confused with a demonstrated return to life or with independently verified connections to [Château Weir](chateau-weir.md).

The priest offers to **attempt resurrection later** if Vorel helps humiliate an unidentified powerful person before peers and subjects. **Assistant Susu takes the slab and both bodies into purportedly secure crypts**; no ritual is performed. After the group exits, the **road to the church is no longer visible**, replaced by houses when the party looks back. The concealment is observed; its magical mechanism is unknown.

Back at the **Soggy Feather**, **Nothing is paid a gold coin by Bill** for guiding, and Nash awakens after a rest with **a speaking mouth and functional hand** while still in heart form. The Welcome Center sends **three Pelor-affiliated helpers**, who describe Sigil's god-exclusion, Lady of Pain and violent-faction restrictions as local political/religious explanations. Their suggestions of accounts management, leatherwork, fighting or bounty work **do not lead to employment**. Bill identifies the subterranean **Fiend's Hoof** as a possible bounty-business lead, **not visited**.

The party pursues the **Big Time Smack'em Brothers** tournament instead: a **1,000-gold prize is advertised**, **Vorel, Nash and Nothing register at 76 Mulberry Lane**, and the qualifier is assigned to **Royce Gymnasium**. They see marionette controls, decline paid practice, receive a free beginner pamphlet, then visit the **M-sports library** to investigate a popular competitive treatise. They **do not compete, win money, take a bounty, or obtain the sought book** by episode's end. Neither the Lady of Pain nor a portal home is witnessed.

## E084 — qualifying rounds, the trickster's concealment and an Abyss-linked assignment

[E084](../../episodes/E084.md) demonstrates that the **trickster church can be found from another neighborhood**, again disappearing from the visible alley afterward. The birdlike cleric supplies **forgetfulness powder** to the party for an **unconscious but living library reader**; the drug's effects are **not tested**. He identifies the undefeated marionette champion as his **humiliation target** and proposes **bracket manipulation and covert injections** in return for eventually attempting the resurrection of **Hope and Stanley**. **Squirts actually injects Vorel's opponent at the tournament**. That victim becomes ill; **Vorel wins his opening qualifier and Nash wins separately**, while **Nothing and another kenku both forfeit through inactivity**. None of this completes the promised ritual or awards the advertised prize.

The **Royce Gymnasium/Colosseum** venue has a **magic-item exclusion gate** and a very large network of marionette-fighting stations. **Squirts is denied entry** for a magical forehead stone and then helped in by **the priest disguised as a human**. **Nothing eludes the gate** by orchestrating a disturbance. Organizers cite **malfunctioning marionettes (only Bland, Kung and Rubles available)**, **10,000 entrants at multiple sites** and an **additional 4,000-gold donation**. Treat numerical prize claims as **announcement, not paid proceeds**. The venue's two titles and several proper names need audio confirmation.

Separately, **Nothing privately meets a fixer called Pelvis** and uses a magic shop on **47 Castamere/Castlemere Lane (?)** to open an **actually depicted portal into an anonymous client's chamber**. The client demands a future **abduction of Stevius Ronkly (?)** from the high quarters and delivery through another portal to the **Abyss at 10 Belshamer Lane (?) in the Hive**, with a **seven-day completion deadline and one-hour return window**. **Parakeet calls the man's affiliation “Godsmen” whereas Qwen says “Guardsmen”; do not choose one without audio.** The job is **accepted privately, never executed here**. This is **not** the E082 newcomer-clerk referral and not a route back to Earth.

## E085 — Royce champion, witnessed enforcement, and a new night threat

[E085](../../episodes/E085.md) distinguishes the **Royce Gymnasium local qualifier title** (held by the alligatorlike **Ventro (?)**) from the **next-day larger BMF finals** and overall champion **Kanja (?)**. Vorel **actually defeats Nash and then Ventro** in marionette matches, becomes **Royce qualifier champion**, is featured in **M Sports Weekly** and publicly calls **Mighty Squirts** his team, with Squirts as its manager. His fame results in **unsigned sponsorship offers**, not a paid deal; **the 5,000-gold tournament pool is advertised but remains unwon**. At the Soggy Feather victory celebration, **Nash and Nothing actually earn wages/tips**, while Squirts acquires unknown items. Vorel escapes intrusive admirers by jumping through an upper window and subsequently returns.

More consequentially, **Ventro and his entourage fight the PCs after the marionette defeat**. **Nash's attempted Enlarge, Minor Illusion and Ray of Frost are externally suppressed**, whereas **Squirts' spells succeed** nearby; **no cause is demonstrated**. A **huge, green, woman-shaped statue-like figure appears in the arena**, and **Ventro is pulled through a pinhole in reality and disappears**. The manifestation imposes a **silent felt warning**. This provides **direct evidence of a powerful local enforcement event**, unlike locals' earlier untested claims about the Lady of Pain. But **the apparition never explicitly identifies itself as the Lady, and Ventro's death is not shown**. Neither gods' exclusion nor the precise enforcement mechanism is thereby fully verified.

That night **Nothing's warning sword** alerts her to movement above their room in the **Soggy Feather**, followed by **two armed ninjalike intruders entering through a window**. The episode ends before they are identified or their attack adjudicated. **No evidence connects them to Ventro, Kidigree or Nothing's secret Pelvis/Abyss commission yet**. ## E086 — the Crimson Moon, child survival and magical licenses

In [E086](../../episodes/E086.md), a **masked assassination force** attacks the party's **Soggy Feather suite**. Assailants wear **howling-wolf/red-moon insignia** and disappear into dust when defeated, leaving clothes. **Nothing recognizes them from underworld knowledge as the Order of the Crimson Moon**, a rare and expensive assassins' guild. The party learns the name through Nothing's gestures, **not the identity of its client or the magic of its vanishing bodies**. Innkeeper **Bill** intervenes with a bat, then reproaches Vorel for a smashed mattress, broken windows and flames. He says **Lady of Pain's intervention is not guaranteed in any particular act of violence**, warns against assuming legal immunity and cites hostility toward open faction war as **his testimony**, not a witnessed universal rule. **The Lady does not appear to resolve the intrusion.**

After the night's rest, the inn hosts Vorel's remaining admirers and commercial contacts. **Arcane Spice initially agrees to a 200-gold one-day promotion**, giving Vorel a blue cape; the contract is **withdrawn and the cape taken back without payment** when onlookers witness **Squirts badly burn an adolescent lizardfolk fan** and believe him killed. **Squirts later stabilizes him with Spare the Dying; he speaks and flees alive**, but public belief and business losses remain. Kidigree/Kidagri (?) arrives **disguised as an older human**, uses **Bill's kitchen** for a performance-enhancing concoction, and **Vorel swallows the alleged doping mixture**. Its effect is **untested**. Bill separately has an endangered **Arcane Spice vendor arrangement**, not necessarily the same agreement as Vorel's.

A gaming official named **Pentheon/Pantheon (?)** schedules **escort at 1 p.m.** to a **3 p.m. start** at the **larger Colosseum finals**, and cites performance-enhancing potions, magic items and rival-directed attacks among grounds for disqualification. **No final is fought in E086**. The busy **bazaar** contains a carpet seller **Zarfur/Zarfour (?)** and capemaker brother **Lohar/Lorhar (?)**, who **gives Vorel a red Soggy Feather cape free** after an initially discussed 20-gold price is waived. Nothing brings Nash to **a spell-key merchant** selling **school-specific Sigil magic unlocks for 500 gold each**, conditional **50 gold over twelve payments** with faction-issued ID/credit. Nash **buys none**. In later GM system commentary, **the Lady's magic-dampening field** is identified as the reason for ordinary magic restrictions, and **rarer power keys** are mentioned. **These are creator explanations after the bazaar visit, not a spell key acquired by Nash**. See [ASR source comparison](../../reports/E086_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

## E087 — overall M-fighting champion, exposed Kanja and paid prize

In [E087](../../episodes/E087.md), Sigil's **Great Colosseum** becomes the site of a **played overall BMF championship**. Vorel enters with **delayed Crimson Moon dart poison** emerging on his shoulder; the **tournament physician provides temporary 8–10-hour relief pills** (not permanent cure), allowing Vorel to perform. Officials distinguish **magically constituted participants** like Nash (issued an **M marker**) from banned **enchanted equipment**, which Nothing/Squirts leave at the Soggy Feather. The handler **Pentheon/Pantheon (?)** independently recognizes the **Crimson Moon** insignia but cannot link the assassins to any competition rival; Vorel recognizes familiar **eyes on the Mind Master's female companion**, a **clue rather than verified employment order**.

The final eight include **Mind Master**, **Tubbs 9K** (dwarven ghost), **Fax/Faks** (speaking illithid with metal on his head), **Arsenad/Asenad**, **Kanja/Kenja** and others. **Vorel defeats Mind Master in a played marionette game**, with illicit **opponent-move precognition** from Kidigree/Squirts' E086 potion. In a **group urine screen**, **Kidigree disguised as attendant Jimmy** replaces Vorel's actual specimen with the priest's, making the official negative unreliable; **Tubbs 9K tests positive and is disqualified**, giving Vorel a **bye, not another played victory**. After **Kanja beats Arsenad (narrated)**, Vorel **actually defeats Kanja with Kung versus Bland** in the final. Vorel then **activates Kidigree's disc**: violet light **visually reveals a miniature crowd of workers and mechanisms within Kanja's apparent body**, and an announcer denounces **a team masquerading as one competitor**. Kanja flees humiliated. **The display and crowd reaction are observed**, but true physiology and subsequent official inquiry are unknown. **The promised public humiliation is performed; no Hope/Stanley resurrection follows within E087**.

Officials **hand Vorel two sacks totalling 5,000 gold and a Kung trophy**; he becomes **actual overall Big Time Smackeroo champion**. He returns by carriage to the **Soggy Feather** for a second large party. **Nash earns 86 gold**, **Nothing and Squirts obtain further money**, and Vorel **gives Nothing 600 gold, Nash 500 gold for a future spell key**. **Nash does not buy a spell key**. After the celebration, **Bill receives 100 gold from Vorel** for **damage (40gp) and 30 prepaid nights (60gp)** in the executive suite. Bill and Pam discuss retirement to the **Feywild** and **propose that Vorel might buy the Soggy Feather or become a partner**; no sale occurs. **Nash regrows a second forearm and an ear** in the subsequent rest. **Squirts casts Protection from Poison**, actually curing Vorel after the temporary physician relief has worn off. At E087 cutoff the party **leaves the inn and walks toward Kidigree's hidden temple**, without reaching the priest or witnessing resurrection. ## E088 — Chapel of Laughter resurrection, portal clues and a corrected magic rule

[E088](../../episodes/E088.md) reaches the **Chapel of Laughter** through an alley that has shifted position, where priest **Carr/Karr (?)** escorts the party into Kidigree's subterranean **Hall of Reflections**. Its mirrors show many guises of the same hooded visitor and one window onto **Kanja's remote alley**. There the group sees **Mind Master with Order of the Crimson Moon enforcers threatening 100,000gp repayment within a week** and future killings of Kanja's **tiny internal inhabitants**. This establishes a **mirror-witnessed active association of Mind Master with Crimson Moon**, **not direct proof of who hired the E086 bedroom attackers**. Kidigree **collects Kanja's tears**, admitting he had considered reneging on his bargain, then **actually performs the agreed return ritual**. **Hope and Stanley separately approach lights in a formless void and emerge from mirrors as physically present speaking PCs**, even though the **original corpses remain restrained on slabs**. **Hope has organic limbs and surviving scars but no former gun-augmented metal limbs**. Kidigree promises disposal of the original husks but **does not demonstrate it**. Squirts remains in the chapel; Nothing guides the newly reunited group to the inn.

**Explicit erratum:** While the party leaves the chapel, **Beau says his previous assertion that a Sigil-wide dampening field blocks Nash's ordinary spells was a DM mistake**; the restriction **does not apply within Sigil**. **Nash successfully casts Fire Bolt** without buying a key. The prior E086 spell-key merchant's statements and E087 500gp gift happened in those sessions, but **do not remain a reliable current game rule that Sigil requires school keys for basic casting**. Conditions of possible magical restrictions *elsewhere* are not established. Any general Sigil/planar rules in earlier reference sections must be read subject to this source-timed correction. [ASR discrepancy report](../../reports/E088_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md).

Back at the **Soggy Feather**, **Bill** explains **Sigil's nickname “the Cage”**, concealed portals to many planes (possibly back to **Earth/Wastes**), and suggests **Clerk's Ward records of registered portal passage/ownership** as a lead. **No particular Earth portal is found**, and the **Clerk's Ward is not visited in this recording**. He reacts fearfully to Weir/Kintobal Bray references, offering **NPC testimony** rather than proof of Weir cosmology. Vorel uses a **Treverson (?) ethereal spectrometer at a magical showroom** to see similar white auras on friends and an **Infernal “Stanley Billings” marking on Stanley's neck**; the device is **borrowed for the test only**, not bought, and **the Soulbroker contract is not shown ended**.

The **Great Bazaar** includes **Large S's/Largess Appleton-like magical emporium**, whose toy-shop front leads to an enchanted showroom. **Vorel pays 1,250gp** there for a **professional 16-fighter BMF cabinet (1,200gp)** and **Nash's skull arcane focus (50gp)**. **The cabinet is actually installed at the Soggy Feather with Bill's consent** after a short delivery montage; the inn **is not bought**. **Tailor Lohar delivers three formal outfits** to Hope, Stanley and Vorel at **30gp total, paid 15 down and 15 on delivery**. **No firearms, spectrometer or complete new armor inventory is bought**. The group discusses **about eight days** of recovery and equipment planning; only **a couple of days' deliveries** are clearly narrated before E088 closes. [E088 dossier](../../episodes/E088.md).

## E089 — Clerk's Ward's Earth record, the Dark Below and the Hive steward

[E089](../../episodes/E089.md) begins **after the full eight-day time skip proposed in E088**. **Nash has regrown his full humanoid body** and can cast normally after the earlier GM correction; his scars/red eye remain. A morning **M Sports Weekly** reports **Kanja found murdered** (hanged/burned) and some of his internal tiny population dead, with **Harmonium** investigating. This is **press testimony**, not a witnessed killing or proof that the E088 **Mind Master/Crimson Moon** threat was the perpetrator.

The restored five hire a **rickshaw** from the Soggy Feather's **Merchant Ward** to the **Clerk's Ward**, where a horse-carriage office directs them to the ornate **Portal Regulatory Agency of Sigil**. **Hope is required to have two armed escorts solely because she is a tiefling**, a discriminatory office policy voiced by a halfling clerk; meeting official **Duerto/Dueto (?)** apologizes. His illithid assistant **Fax/Faks** is recognizable from the BMF championship and searches an information device called the **Echo (?)**. Duerto reports **one *registered stable* portal to the party's planet “Earth,” as distinct from the Elemental Plane of Earth**. Its steward **Tobacho Fleur/Tobacco Fleur (?)** can be found in **the Hive**, a poor/dangerous ward. **No exact doorway, key, passage agreement, license or known return journey is provided**. A frog vendor earlier explains that people may guard and profit from **portal location knowledge/controlled access** without formally owning doors; this is **NPC testimony**, not an externally settled legal policy.

The party debates staying in Sigil vs returning to threatened friends, dependents and kingdoms on Earth, then dines at the **Pig's Foot/Big Foot (?)** in Clerks' Ward (**20gp including tip**) before returning to the **Soggy Feather** by rickshaw (**1.5gp**) and re-entering **Dark Below** near the Welcome Center. In the **body-recovery room from E082**, **Stanley fishes Hope's pistol Scully from the sewer water**, and the party finds **a functioning ethereal spectrometer previously used by corpse scavengers**. The device shows **Weir-destined soul inscriptions on Hope and Stanley, with a proprietor and reward-if-found text**, despite their new living bodies. This is **a genuine found device**, not E088's premium **1,400gp unpurchased shop goggles**. Another sewer item called **Barbara** unfolds into an umbrella. A **purple temporary portal opens while Nash experiments with it**; **only objects and Nash's head cross**, briefly revealing a hot dark space and many-legged crawlers. The party remains in Sigil, and **no reproducible connection to the recorded Earth portal is shown**.

In the nearby **arrival chamber**, the party witnesses **multiple corpses and shattered equipment**, while **rat-man Java/Jarva the Carver (?)** attributes them to **Rat King's minions killing the scavenger crew that had bought Vorel's arrival-site rights**. For **9gp in two bribes**, Java says **Hope's second pistol** was sold to **Fedor (?)**, an agent of **the same Tobacho Fleur in the Hive**. Thus the **return-to-Earth lead and missing pistol point to the same provisional steward**, though **neither is reached**. The group considers **surface entry into Hive vs covert passage through cramped rat tunnels in Dark Below**, **not yet attempted**. ## E090 — Harmonium's Hive border, Daddy Long Legs and Tobacho's lair

[E090](../../episodes/E090.md) **actually enters the Hive via the surface**, after the reunited four decide against rat-sized travel through **Dark Below**. **Lohar** in the **Great Bazaar** makes them four matching, threatening gang costumes—brown/black leather with many spikes, belts/ropes, a demon-face metal chest emblem, three-skull back emblem and steel-toed **retractable spike boots**. Each is quoted at **50gp**, and after **two elapsed days all four receive and wear them**, with Stanley's *Prestidigitation* griming the new fabric. **Payment of 200gp is not directly played**. Their cover story is **“Lady Despair and the Rambunctious Bunch/Rascals,”** with **Hope cast as Lady Despair**. **Vorel's BMF coaching** draws customers at the **Soggy Feather**, but pupil **Fax/Faks doesn't arrive**, and Tubbs can't find him. GM explicitly reveals **Nash's thermal underwear has fused into his DNA**, giving **10-foot flight** and armpit cooling despite E089's humanoid regrowth; this is a **character ruling**, not evidence of a normal item still worn.

## E093 — leaving Sigil by airship, now actually in the Outlands

## Later GM commentary — March 2020 public-release bonus: Planescape, M-sports and resurrection

In the [March 2020 bonus Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-03.md) (~04:30–09:15), Beau explicitly distinguishes **homebrew campaign creations—the Weir and its inhabitants including Kitobalpraith—from licensed D&D/Planescape material, including Sigil and its planar framework**. He credits **Manual of the Planes**, **Planescape** and older TSR settings for informing Sigil's planes and factions. He says the Weir has been **alluded to as linked to the published Far Realm**, **not** that its identity, location and portal mechanics have been definitively established to the characters. A question about a possible **Lady of Pain final boss** gets a general answer about the DM's creative freedom; the cast's later claim of a “confirmed boss fight” is **a joke, not an announcement or E085 retcon**.

Beau also explains that **M-sports began as a joke poster** riffing on esports, **without a fighting system at the time**. **Player interest prompted the GM to develop one**. This is **production history**, not a claim that the already witnessed Royce finals or Vorel's championship were unplayed or in-world imaginary. At ~23:10–24:40 Beau says **Sigil's unusual concentrations of magical expertise provided multiple possible avenues for reviving Hope and Stanley**, whereas had they died elsewhere in Caravalon/Daemonvold without appropriate spellcasters, **those routes would not have been available**. The actual E088 resurrection **required party choices and effort**, was not a universal automatic afterlife event, and does not guarantee equivalent options back on Earth. [ASR comparison](../../reports/BONUS_2020_03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). **This is later GM commentary, not a new episode scene or a proved Far Realm crossover.**
