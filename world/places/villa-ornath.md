# Villa Or'Nath — Delit Or'Nath's family cottage and concealed spelljammer

**Name and provenance (owner-confirmed, 2026-10-27):** The family surname is **Or'Nath**, shared by **Valiir Or'Nath** (Stanley Billings's original elven name) and his father **Delit Or'Nath**; the home is therefore **Villa Or'Nath**. Jon's [Valiir Or'Nath poem](https://discord.com/channels/146848379853471744/671473359099527180/755289251192504340) and the project owner's relationship/spelling clarification establish the editorial forms. Earlier `Villa Ornath`, `Delit Ornath`, and ASR `Dalit/Daliot Ornath` alongside the file path `villa-ornath.md` remain historical aliases and compatibility links, **not separate places or people**. The exact words spoken in episodes still require listening.

> **The Wastes · Arc Five · first visited E115, hidden study discovered E118, escape mechanism disclosed E126, Season One refuge through E128.** This is the **family cottage originally inside Elhandar's orbital forest biodome**, not a new house built on Earth. ## Earlier uses — ordinary home and impossible upper floor

At [E115](../../episodes/E115.md), Stanley, Nash, Vorel and their companions live in **the villa/cottage associated with Stanley's father, Delit Or'Nath**, among the forest and Rainbow Road. By [E118](../../episodes/E118.md), a physical **key** is given to Stanley, who invokes his parents' poem and reveals a **hidden third floor** exceeding the geometry of the visible two-story house. There he and Hope find the **Book of Adjacencies**, an **obsidian jeweled mask**, globe and star charts. The father's claims about time/cosmology remain writings, not proven objective history.

## E125 — impact shelter and episode-bound uncertainty

With Elhandar falling toward Earth, the High Elf tells Stanley to protect his household at the villa. **Hope, Nash, Vorel, Stanley, Angelashi, Lyrielle, Nash's four children, Bok Bok and Chunks reach the villa** after the interrupted elevator and biodome escape. Its sealed upper study manifests seats and restraints; they buckle in. **E125 ends at the impact, without proof of post-impact survival**. [E125](../../episodes/E125.md).

## E126 — occupants survive, the house is a spelljammer

The hidden third floor **absorbs the first crash**, keeping the family alive. The party finds dozens of **injured tieflings from the Harbor downstairs**. One says elves assembled them before the disaster and they unexpectedly appeared in the villa; **the mechanism and who brought them are only reported**, not witnessed. A blocked entry and rubble pressing against sound windows establish the house has been **buried within fallen station debris**.

Stanley opens the responsive father's journal by asking about the villa. A magically presented explanation says the entire dwelling is an **especially damage-resistant shell of a spelljammer**, an interstellar vessel. Wearing its associated **Mask of Eltanfawr** (previously transcribed “Elton IV”) grants mental/telepathic controls for **flight, shrinking the villa and its occupants to dime size, and a vessel form which reportedly supplies air**. Stanley **puts on and reshapes the eight-gem mask** but his first attempted lift only makes the house creak: **four consecutive nights of attunement/trust are said to be required**. **No successful lift, shrink, full transformation or actual spaceflight in E126**. The alternative **Teleportation Circle** is discussed and never cast; another attempted short-range **Teleport by Hope is blocked** by the bounty hunter.

**Bok Bok's reusable beauty bomb** produces magical formal clothing and music in the lower rooms. Rubble then shifts and exposes a sunlit path; **Vorel scouts out to Earth's desert and returns**. The cause of the house movement is not independently established. A second burning impact spins the villa, killing **several Harbor tieflings** and wounding other occupants; Angelashi, Lyrielle and Nash **protect the children**, who survive. The house ends **upright and free of rubble on Earth's desert sand**, with debris from Elhandar behind it. Later **Henatar's musical attacks shake its structure and loosen nails**. Its durability is extraordinary, but **not proven literally invincible to every magical attack**.

## E128 — the season-finale refuge on Earth's surface

In [E128](../../episodes/E128.md), **Villa Or'Nath remains intact** after the second impact and the mercenary's death. After the surviving Elhandari land, **four more noble speeders are actually attached to exterior fixtures on the villa**, which belongs to Stanley. **The rescued tieflings and Angelashi's group leave aboard Mars-bound elf ships**, while **Stanley, Nash, Hope and Vorel remain on Earth by the cottage**. Stanley still requires additional **consecutive nights with the obsidian mask** to command the house as a spelljammer; **its vessel flight and shrinking have not been successfully activated**. The Soulbroker later visibly opens a sky-rift and a titanic burrower triggers a **sand tsunami**. The quartet **flies above the storm on dragon-form Nash**; the cottage **survives exactly where it stood**, with one side banked by blown sand and its interior still messy. **A peaceful long rest is completed here** at finale; all four reach **level 13**. The proposed **three additional days** to finish attunement have **not yet elapsed**. The party does not board the Mars ship. ## Season Two — a ship called Fate Six, actual Earth-to-Mars travel

After **three more days on Earth**, the fourth-day transformation **actually takes place in S2E01**: the cottage rises as the captain's quarters of an elven airship. In **[S2E02](../../episodes/S2E02.md)** the vessel's **ghostly first mate Sever, an uncle of Stanley**, introduces roughly 40–50 enchanted ghost elves as crew and the strange under-engine chamber where **a carved face resembling Delit Or'Nath actually speaks as Stanley's father**. The voice and Sever **claim his remaining life essence powers the vessel**, but the ancient transfer is not staged; **neither the first S3E07 destruction nor later S3E13 restoration independently verifies his personal survival or death**. The vessel **flies from Earth's crash site past T'ietkalla to Razak Val**, takes a large contingent of Vorel's living lizardfolk followers aboard, then **enters space and completes an approximately one-hour void crossing to the Mars terraforming biosphere**. Stanley **formally christens the ship Fate Six there**; unlike the E126/E128 descriptions, the flight and Mars landfall are now *actually performed*, while shrinking and the mask's dangerous chance power remain untested. See the canonical [Fate Six vessel study](../technology/fate-six.md) · [S2E02 ASR comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md). At the S2E02 cutoff the party is on Mars and the ship remains landed there, **not on Earth**.

No post-battle custody, permanent house transformation or exact refugee-survivor tally is witnessed. **The villa is on Earth's surface now, not merely proposed to travel there**; the occupants have **not separately crossed to Mars**.
