# Black Mine

The **Black Mine** lies beyond the desert road from [Slavetown](slavetown.md), where an open quarry leads into a vast shaft of worked galleries. When the companions visit in **E018–E026**, it is an **Oilbeard-guarded forced-labor site** with undead miners, abandoned passages and an occult ritual far below. Its **ownership**, the miners' supposed revolt and its mineral deposits remain contested; escaping the site is not equivalent to liberating it.

## Upper workings and descent

After surviving a sandstorm, the party reaches the mountainside quarry in [E018](../../episodes/E018.md). Dwarven overseer **Scrolly/Scrawly Oilbeard** claims the guards own the mine and have blocked the lift to starve **100–200 rebellious slaves** below. The number, motive and ownership are **his testimony**, not an independent census. The guards visibly abuse **Tickles**, a **human** chained and made to behave like a dog.

The four adventurers descend to the stranded lift; undead attack as it ascends. In [E019](../../episodes/E019.md), guards reverse the lift with the heroes aboard, and Tickles **jumps onto it**. A suspension chain fails; **Stanley's *Feather Fall*** saves the party during the long drop, but the legendary **999th floor** is not proved to be their destination. Deeper below, Hope pulls a lever that triggers a ceiling collapse. The other companions survive; **Tickles's death is confirmed in [E020](../../episodes/E020.md)**.

## Lower labyrinth and otherworldly connection

The ruined lower workings contain a dusty bedroom, **unidentified skeleton** and **Tylander Sullivan's diary**. Its account of lost relatives and a subterranean patron is **the author's testimony**, not authenticated ownership history. Beyond a glyph-marked chasm and a wounded displacer beast, animate purple fungi attack. The travelers negotiate with **Dorothy**, a speaking door of uncertain nature, then reach a ruined chapel. [E020](../../episodes/E020.md) · [E021](../../episodes/E021.md).

There **Tylander claims** to have been Pristine's mayor, enslaved and betrayed by the Principal's interests and Oilbeards. He admits transforming captives into undead, but neither the details of his family history nor his legal rights are independently established. He **impersonates Catherine Speckles**; a **separate mutilated prisoner claiming to be Catherine** gives a message for Bertrand and apparently dies. Tylander's apparent throne figure proves insubstantial, while the party defeats eight monstrous followers.

Behind the chapel, **Quizquaz**, a **separate beholder** and Tylander's ritual are encountered. The beholder is defeated in **[E022](../../episodes/E022.md)**; Quizquaz takes a gem and summons **Catoblepraithe, the Soulbroker**. In [E023](../../episodes/E023.md), the Soulbroker brings them through a rift to **[Château Weir](chateau-weir.md)**. **Hope, Nash and Stanley sign his compact; Vorel refuses.** Château Weir is another realm, **not a physical mine level**.

## Return to the surface and rescue

On returning, the group opens a chapel chest and takes coins, gems and **two potions**, **not the deed**. The Soulbroker **claims** the deed was consumed in the rite. They burn Dorothy's wooden door, yet **her voice remains audible**. [E024](../../episodes/E024.md).

In [E025](../../episodes/E025.md), Nash **carries the party up the shaft in dragon form**. They fight Scrolly's Oilbeards and the gun **Bessie**; at least one dwarf escapes. They **injure, heal and injure the captured Scrolly again before throwing him down the shaft**, not a peaceful interrogation. A locked room contains **Bok Bok's voice**. In **[E026](../../episodes/E026.md)** they actually free **Bok Bok, Bertrand and Trigger**, find **Fate 4 repaired**, and depart. An armored visitor enters the mine, but his identity is not established at that cutoff.

## Current status and disputed ownership

At the last witnessed cutoff, the companions and three named prisoners have **left**; no complete worker rescue, new mine government or final inspection occurs. The **Oilbeards' ownership claim** conflicts with Tylander's account and rival Slavetown interests. Tylander's purported deed **turns to smoke in [E015](../../episodes/E015.md)**; the Soulbroker later claims it was used as a ritual ingredient, while Bertrand merely **speculates** that it concerned dragon metal. **No authentic deed is recovered**, and even the site's gold/copper description varies across earlier testimony.

## Connections and gaps

These overseers do not represent every [dwarven community](../races/dwarves.md). [Leopold's desert research facility](leopold-facility.md), visited in **E008–E009**, is **not** the Black Mine; neither is [CHIDS](chids.md). Precise floor plans, casualties, surviving workers, claimant rights and the later fate of Dorothy remain unknown.
