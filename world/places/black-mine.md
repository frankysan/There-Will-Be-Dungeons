# Black Mine

**Type:** Extensive underground extraction site, work camp and supernatural ruin. **Arc:** E018–E026. **Evidence:** Processed-SRT dossier research; site map, level labels and personnel statuses remain provisional unless played.

## Upper workings and descent

The party approaches through a desert sandstorm and reaches a heavily guarded **Oilbeard dwarf** facility. Human **Tickles** is kept in degrading captivity, and miners report a supposed slave uprising and blocked access to lower sections. Those reports express guards' knowledge/interest, not an objective account of every disturbance.

The adventurers descend a great **vertical elevator/shaft** through upper levels and immediately face scores of undead. A struggle over the elevator is followed by failed machinery and a long fall; **Stanley's Feather Fall** saves companions. **Tickles** reaches the deeper passages alive but is later **killed by a ceiling collapse** after Hope operates a lever. The party finds safety mechanisms, abandoned studies and **Tylander Sullivan's diary**. The old skeleton found nearby is **not automatically Tylander**.

## Lower labyrinth and otherworldly connection

Below the worked galleries are chasms, dense fungus, glyphs and magically obscured paths, including a talking door known as **Dorothy**. The adventurers fight creatures such as a tainted displacer beast and eventually confront Tylander's apparent ritual. His own historical narrative about **Pristine**, the city's dead and undead workers is **interested testimony** and may diverge from the prologue or documentary evidence. A captive speaking as **Catherine Speckles** appears separate from an **illusion/impersonation** of her.

A **beholder** battle and the extraction of a gemstone open a passage to **Catoblepraithe, the Soulbroker**, bringing the party into the extradimensional hospitality of **Château Weir**. **Nash, Stanley and Hope** sign a pact there; **Vorel refuses**. The return route to the Black Mine is hazardous and the party eventually **escapes upward in Nash's dragon form**. Armed clashes with Oilbeards, the coercion of **Scrolly**, and a side prison where **Bok Bok, Bertrand and Trigger** are freed complete their departure.

## Connections and gaps

The mine links **Slavetown's** slave economy to ancient workings and old dragon-engine claims. Specific mineral wealth, levels, ownership and the ultimate **mine deed** are disputed or unresolved. A separate **government-controlled or experimental desert laboratory** entered E008–E009 is **not** the same place as the Black Mine merely because both are underground.
