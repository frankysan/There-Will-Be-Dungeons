# Mars terraforming colony and Elhandari refuge

After the fall of the orbital sanctuary [Elhandar](elhandar.md), its survivors maintain a **protected, green terraforming region on Mars**. When the companions land there in [S2E02](../../episodes/S2E02.md), a jungle thrives inside a limited **Elhandari biosphere** while the outside environment is still described as dangerous. It is a refuge for displaced elves, tieflings, Nash's family and allies, as well as the place where the defenders organize their campaign to restore Earth. The witnessed story includes **a landing area, a theatre/workshop, an elder-tree council grove, refugee quarters, a separate underground laboratory and a later Mars platform**. Their presence on Mars does **not** prove that each is one structure, at one coordinate, or continuously occupied through the finale.

## The exodus from Elhandar

In [E128](../../episodes/E128.md), surviving Elhandari ships **actually leave Earth** carrying rescued Harbor tieflings, **Angelashi and all four children**, Lyrielle and others, along with the **natural black dragon's recovered heart**. This is an **outbound voyage**, not yet the group's witnessed landing on Mars. The original four adventurers remain on Earth with Villa Or'Nath. In [S2E01](../../episodes/S2E01.md), the GM establishes that the evacuated family and allies are **already under Elhandari protection on Mars**, although an individual arrival scene for every E128 passenger is not played. The fallen Elhandar sanctuary itself is **not** shown rebuilt in Martian orbit.

## First witnessed visit — jungle, elders and workshop

In [S2E02](../../episodes/S2E02.md), the transformed spelljammer [Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md) reaches Mars after an observed flight from Earth via Razak Val and **physically lands in the local green jungle**. Stanley christens the vessel *Fate Six* on arrival. **Amanthalodar** and **Admiral Chrysorius** greet the crew. Nash **physically reunites with Angelashi and their four living children**. He discusses **Planet Nash as a possible later family refuge**; no move takes place in that conversation. Vorel brings lizardfolk allies, who are welcomed, but the proposed magical construction of a new sandy habitat is **not yet observed**.

The companions visit **Dunglestar Ravensbread's Theatre of Mortal Bodies**, where they seek news of the missing engineer **Tungsten Wheel** and inspect an **incomplete, unpowered** Rubistic Triangulosphere mechanism. Twaddle agrees to assist the ship's ghost surgeon, but must still board Fate Six later. At the large **elder tree**, whose phonetic name is recorded variously as *Sumaslanar*, the group reaches a vine-and-wood council space. The **first war council opens** by the episode's end, but its strategic decisions are not yet complete. [S2E02](../../episodes/S2E02.md) · [Ruby](../technology/rubistic-triangulosphere.md).

## A working refugee settlement, not just a military camp

The subsequent [S2E03](../../episodes/S2E03.md) council **actually meets**, selects the Solar Mines approach and discusses forces and strategy. The older tree and Elhandari presence are real places of assembly; future allocations and proposed recruitment are not already-deployed forces. In [S2E04](../../episodes/S2E04.md), the companions see **refugee tents**, including **Nobieri**, before departing for Planet Nash. **Angelashi and all four children board Fate Six and physically travel away**; they subsequently stay in [Tower Nash](planet-nash.md) in S2E05. This means the Mars home is **not** their uninterrupted permanent address.

In [S3E03](../../episodes/S3E03.md), Fate Six returns and **actually lands at a Mars terraforming platform**. Admiral Chrysorius and Amanthalodar receive the party, and rescued Tungsten **reunites with Dunglestar**. An Elhandari speeder takes the party to a **Martian canyon** where **99 alternate Nashes have physically gathered**. Their earlier transit is attributed to Tower Nash and an apparent time herald, but is **not separately shown**. Nash later reunites with Angelashi and the same four children in the **terraforming forest**. Their movement back from Planet Nash is **not individually staged**. **Charlemagne**, formerly of T'ietkalla, actually operates an **open-air restaurant** among the refugees; this is observed local civilian life, not proof of a finished new city or universal resettlement.

## Beneath the great tree — augmentation and Ruby

In the latter part of [S3E03](../../episodes/S3E03.md), an **underground Elhandari laboratory beneath a great terraforming tree** houses a salvaged **augmentation cylinder**; surgical alterations are **offered** there but not all performed by that episode cutoff. In [S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md), **Stanley, Hope and Nash actually undergo** the selected augmentations, while Vorel declines. The episode also witnesses approximately **75 lizardfolk** in earthen accommodation rather than elven science tents, festivities and family time. A proposed sand habitat from the earlier visit should not be retroactively described as a completed separate settlement.

The **second Council of Earthly Defenders** takes place beneath the elder tree. Here **Tungsten actually presents completed Ruby**, and **Dunglestar demonstrates its protective floating enclosure and a brief, local spin-up on Mars**. The device is switched off afterward; this is **not Earth's later planet-scale healing**. In [S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md), the allied force **actually departs Mars for Earth aboard dropships and Fate Six**, taking the completed device and its controls. Nash parts from Angelashi and the four children, who **remain on Mars at that cutoff**. The exact spatial relationship among theatre, council grove, lab, landing zone and family/refugee dwellings remains **unmapped**.

## The final platform and the Feywild departure

In the [S3E18 finale](../../episodes/S3E18.md), **Stanley does not return to Mars aboard Fate Six**. After leaving his companions on restored Earth, he boards **another Elhandari fleet vessel** for a **Mars platform**. Amanthalodar **actually opens a Feywild portal**. Stanley addresses his people as **Solely Prime**, offers them the choice to remain or return, and **crosses the portal with many Elhandari**. **Some remain on Mars; others return to T'ietkalla**. The scene does **not** document the departure of every Elhandari, a permanently installed interplanar gate, abandonment of Mars, or a rebuilt Elhandar station.

The final platform is a witnessed **Mars location**, but there is no independent proof that it is **the identical S2E02 jungle landing, S3E03 augmentation platform, elder-tree grove, theatre, or subterranean laboratory**. Nor does the surviving colony's existence establish that Stanley's Feywild departure shut down the terraforming technology. [S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md).

## Current status and source boundaries

At the final played cutoff, a **living Elhandari-associated Mars community** has been seen repeatedly. Its earlier refugees, research facilities and military staging grounds are supported by **different episode windows**. Stanley and many Elhandari **actually leave through a summoned portal** in S3E18, while others stay or head toward Earth; **no complete census, final settlement boundaries, sustained portal use or detailed postwar governance** is observed. Angelashi's family, having spent time on Mars, is **reunited with the resurrected Nash on Planet Nash** by the finale—not simultaneously documented as still dwelling in their earlier Martian tent.

The above account comes from the maintained [E128](../../episodes/E128.md), [S2E01](../../episodes/S2E01.md)–[S2E04](../../episodes/S2E04.md), [S3E03](../../episodes/S3E03.md)–[S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md) and [S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md) episode dossiers derived from processed speech-recognition text. **Original audio, Elhandari plans, mapped coordinates and a full refugee register have not been independently checked.** Treat *Sumaslanar*, the precise platform naming and some personal names as source-provisional until stronger material establishes them.
