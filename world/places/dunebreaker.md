# The Dunebreaker — roaming landship of the Wastes (historical prequel)

The **Dunebreaker** is a colossal traveling desert caravan, part marketplace and part fortress. Its engines leave **glassed sand** in its wake; its crew offers scarce food and water, while enforcing secrecy and harsh labor. **Hack Loman, Ramilda Gloryfaded (?) and Al** serve aboard it in the three-part, **played historical prequel [E107](../../episodes/E107.md)–[E109](../../episodes/E109.md)**, explicitly **long before the original four depart Dusthill**. Although released between E106 and E110, these episodes **do not continue the main party's journey**.

## What is actually seen

The roughly **400-foot** machine comprises a **head, three articulated segments and a loading tail**, across four levels. A van **actually drives up its tail ramp**. Engineer **Gens/Genz (?)** describes facilities and the ship's sand-melting underside; his account does not prove inexhaustible supplies or engines.

## Organization and surface claims

**Ember Shalisper (?)** runs the vessel, with lionlike **Captain Lotho (?)** commanding guards. New recruits receive **food, water and lodging, not cash**, and cannot freely travel or disclose plans. A bartender's story that Ember restored an ancient machine remains **unverified**. **Fleece/Fleese (?)** signs Al's contract promising payment for confidential information, but **no secrets are sold or payment received** during the prequel.

## E107 homicide — open at cutoff

At a tournament between **Slavetown and the Teet**, **Hack, Ramilda, Al and NPC Strum** defeat a **Chicken Lord** and win guard jobs. Two injured opponents are stabilized but **not hired**. Below the ship's second segment they find a murdered engineer with **four wounds in his back**. E107 ends with **no known culprit**. [E107](../../episodes/E107.md).

## E108 — solved murder, hidden daughters and rat-worker sentence

Stowaway **Lamulkin Farthing (?)** confesses, but his **older daughter admits she stabbed the engineer**. The murder is **reported after the fact**, not played as a witnessed scene. The guards knowingly conceal both daughters and falsely name Lamulkin alone. Ember **spares him** but assigns dangerous under-ship **tumbler-vent cleaning**—work for laborers nicknamed **“rats.”** The daughters remain hidden; **Hack, Ramilda and Strum bring them food**, not freedom. Proposed faked death and escape by *Feather Fall* **never occur**. [E108](../../episodes/E108.md).

## E109 — the secret exchange and defeat of the giant dessert

Lotho questions the false report because the wounds were in the victim's **back**. He **orders reinvestigation**, but it is **not performed**. Gnome cook **Dipshit** has prepared forbidden fruit gelatin that becomes an **ooze**. It escapes into the drains; **Al damages the plumbing** without killing it. During a desert rendezvous, Ember **actually exchanges goods and papers with armed marauders**, but the terms are unknown. The enlarged ooze attacks; **Hack and Ramilda use deck guns, Al vents superheated steam, and Ramilda destroys the final cherry-bearing globule**. The ship survives; **at least two guards and several marauders die**. Jon formally closes the three-part prequel, with **no main-party crossover**. [E109](../../episodes/E109.md).

## Current status and source boundaries

At E109's end, the Dunebreaker is **operational** and the three player guards survive. The hidden daughters' fate, Lamulkin's later condition, Strum's role, Fleece's employer, the reopened murder inquiry, plumbing repairs and the vessel's supply arrangements **remain unresolved**. Its later main-campaign whereabouts are **not established**.
