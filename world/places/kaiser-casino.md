# Kaiser Casino — Hope's infernal destination

**Browse:** [Places](../PLACES.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Type:** Supernatural gaming and meeting location on Hope's separate Arc Five route. **Spoilers:** Through played Wastes S3E13. **Evidence:** Processed ASR.

After leaving her companions at White Tower **in E101**, Hope follows the hooded Voice's intervention into an infernal landscape. Guided by **Spatulo**, she arrives at **Kaiser Casino**, where a powerful host calling himself **Asmodeus** and known as **Brett** stages extravagant hospitality and makes offers. Hope talks with him but **does not make a formal pact in E101**. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md).

In **E102**, Brett demonstrates power over another petitioner and introduces **Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider** as Hope's prospective helpers. They later leave toward T'ietkalla. **E106** brings Hope back to Kaiser Casino, where she speaks with the **Principal/Vladimir** amid illusions and competing historical claims. Hope does agree to cooperation there; this must not be backdated to a signed Brett compact in E101. [E102](../../episodes/E102.md) · [E106](../../episodes/E106.md).

**S3E12–S3E13 Hell casino sequel:** After the Soulbroker's actual defeat S3E12, PRINCIPAL took all four heroes to HELL.
