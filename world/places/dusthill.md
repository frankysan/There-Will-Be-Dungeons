# Dusthill

**Known source variant:** `Dust Hill` in ASR. **Type:** Protected wasteland settlement and first meeting place of the protagonists. **Spoilers:** Arcs One–Two through E054. **Evidence:** Early GM scene exposition and later episode dossiers, presently processed-SRT-derived.

## Setting

Dusthill lies amid protective red rock and narrow approaches, with farms, a garage, a meeting place, defenses and an essential communal **water delivery system**. Its traditional **Hillmaster** leadership, local guards, dwarven attendants, mechanics and other townspeople depend on an inherited installation buried deep beneath the settlement. The well-like outlet is connected to **ancient subterranean pumps and a dragon engine**, not necessarily to a simple hand-dug groundwater well.

In E001 the Hillmaster brings the four future companions together at a meal, then asks them to investigate a collapse in the water supply. Through traps and a guarded passage, the party discovers a damaged network, a much deeper river/intake, an ancient dwarven site referred to later as **Moag Ferhain** (wiki-supported spelling corroborated by the [later recovered broad regional map](../../reports/WASTES_KNOWN_MAP_ORTHOGRAPHY_2026-10-27.md); the mapped surface referent need not equal the specific underground structures, and audio remains unchecked), a spectral first Hillmaster and an oil-associated being **Quizquaz**. The present Hillmaster dies during the expedition. The party repairs or restarts crucial machinery and **Nash tests the surface water running in E006**. Earlier E005 remarks that the well has *not yet* been tested are historically accurate to that earlier moment, not a continuing problem.

## After the expedition

The settlement is struck by **goblin raiders**; a public meeting in E006 reports **45 people dead and 25 abducted** (figures from session reports), leading to emergency leadership under **Shun'ti Stoutbarrel**, with defenders and **Jimster** involved. **Bok Bok** becomes Vorel's companion in E006, after the opening crisis. The adventurers leave in **Hope's Fate 4** to seek those taken; subsequent discoveries about Slavetown and the Black Mine do **not** prove every Dusthill captive was freed.

## Important people and connected sites

The deceased Hillmaster, earlier specter **Peter**, **Softhead**, **Jimster** (mechanic), **Shun'ti**, **Talia** and **Bruce Bane** are linked to the settlement, though not all have fully verified personal names or statuses. Relevant physical sites are **the garage/Fate 4**, **the water outlet**, **the guarded under-hill route**, **dragon engine**, **shaft and river**, and **ancient dwarven ruins**.

## Survivors finally return — E059

The **Principal promises** the release of Dusthill captives in [E058](../../episodes/E058.md), and **an actual group arrives in T'ietkalla in E059**, including **Talia and child Bruce Bane, Jimster, Shun'ti and other surviving Dusthill citizens**. They are medically received and speak of **forced work at the Salivating Fields**. Their living arrival is directly played, so the entire rescue cannot remain classified as merely diplomatic promise. **The return does not establish that every person taken in the original raid survived or was included**, and reports about the raid's organizer or a ruler's intent remain interested testimony.
