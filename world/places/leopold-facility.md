# Leopold experimental facility — desert installation

**Browse:** [Places](../PLACES.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Type:** Hidden advanced ruin visited in **E008–E009** by Nash and Stanley with guest characters. **Distinct from:** the Black Mine and CHIDS. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional.

The party investigates a **desert research complex** involving security badges, advanced containment, surveillance and an imprisoned extraordinary tree. The installation collapses and a dragon is released during the adventure. The guest characters **Edison “Eddie” Manchester III** and **Chance** are distinct participants and should not be treated as displaced main-party players. [E008](../../episodes/E008.md) · [E009](../../episodes/E009.md).

A subsequent **June 2019 GM Q&A** discusses an **unclaimed**, tree/druid-associated magic staff designed for the site. Beau cannot recall its exact name or mechanics. This is **prepared but unclaimed loot**, not an item secretly owned by Nash, nor Stanley's Immovable Rod. [June 2019 bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md).
