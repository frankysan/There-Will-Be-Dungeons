# Leopold's experimental facility

Beneath a barren stretch of the Wastes stood an ancient, partly buried research and containment complex. An antenna-like metal structure in the desert led into sealed, sand-filled rooms, elevators, laboratories, living quarters and guarded corridors. Machines continued to provide power, food and security long after the original operators had disappeared. The complex's precise original name and the fate of its wider research network remain unknown.[^arrival]

## The keeper of the complex

[Nash](../characters/nash-maggard.md) and [Stanley](../characters/stanley-billings.md) entered the installation with two visiting gnome adventurers, **Edison “Eddie” Manchester III** and **Chance**. A disembodied voice calling itself **Leopold** challenged them, guided them through an elevator and presented itself in an unnaturally lifelike reception-room form. Leopold asserted that he managed a secret containment site, that previous visitors had died, and that the newcomers could not leave. His insistence that he was **not a computer** remained a claim; the facility's later visible *warden AI protocol* did not establish the true nature of its speaker.[^arrival]

The laboratories contained a hostile [green-case mimic](../creatures/green-case-mimic.md), a separate [eight-legged, teleporting animal](../creatures/eight-legged-facility-predator.md), suspended metal objects and a succession of security mechanisms. A great living tree grew through the facility and appealed for release. It claimed to have once been an elf imprisoned in experiments against dragons, but its distant history was not independently witnessed.[^creatures]

## Collapse and escape

Leopold supplied identification badges intended for security robots and sought the adventurers' assistance with power disruptions. They gained access to controls, altered some restrictions and initiated a self-destruct sequence. Leopold then advanced the deadline as the complex came apart. After an encounter with a [gibbering mouther](../creatures/gibbering-mouther.md), Nash and Stanley climbed out, while Eddie and Chance disappeared into the collapsing underground site. Their disappearance was **not an observed death**. A huge black dragon subsequently rose from beneath the sands and flew away, although the mechanism connecting its emergence to the collapse was not established.[^escape]

## Current status

The visited installation collapsed beneath the desert. No later on-site inventory establishes what, if anything, survived in its lower levels, nor whether it connected physically to the [Black Mine](black-mine.md), [CHIDS](chids.md) or other ancient complexes. A later account of an unidentified, tree-associated magical staff concerns **unclaimed prepared loot**, not a recovered possession of Nash or Stanley; it must not be confused with Stanley's Immovable Rod.[^retrospective]
