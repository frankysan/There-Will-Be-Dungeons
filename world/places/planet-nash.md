# Planet Nash and Tower Nash

**Planet Nash** is the name used by the Wastes travelers for a refuge built around **two joined asteroids and a central inhabited tower**. The companions first reach it aboard [Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md) in S2E04, where they meet a speaking **Tower Nash**, its inhabitants and apparently alternate versions of familiar people. By the played S3E18 finale it is the place to which [Nash Maggard](../characters/nash-maggard.md) returns after his death and resurrection, choosing a life with [Angelashi](../characters/angelashi.md) and their four children. Its measured coordinates, jurisdiction, permanent population and relationship to every earlier alternate-Nash tower are **not independently established**.

## From possible refuge to an actual visit

Before leaving Mars, the party discusses Planet Nash as a possible refuge for Nash's family and a place to seek alternate allies for the planned attack on the Solar Mines. This is initially a **proposal**, not evidence the family has moved there. Angelashi and the children are first physically reunited with Nash on **Mars**; the other characters' recruiting plans remain unperformed at that stage. [S2E02](../../episodes/S2E02.md) · [S2E03](../../episodes/S2E03.md).

In [S2E04](../../episodes/S2E04.md), the GM confirms that **Angelashi and all four children actually board Fate Six** with Nash and the companions before departure. Hope inputs known Planet Nash coordinates, initiates a controlled **rift-space passage lasting about six hours**, and lands the ship at the paired asteroids. This is **an accomplished voyage**, not merely a plotted destination or the later departure for Blast Belly Rock. The family and the companions disembark and encounter **Tungans**, who communicate with ghost crewman Gorgeous George; no complete species genealogy follows from that interaction.

## The speaking tower and alternate histories

Nash awakens the tower's carved speaking face, and the figure called **Tower Nash** tells the visitors about his own unsuccessful Solar Mines assault, meeting Delit Or'Nath and being incorporated into the structure late in life. His earlier timeline and account of divergent realities are **the tower's testimony**, not an independently witnessed history. The tower **projects seven differently colored alternate Nashes** to discuss failed or proposed attacks. Their setbacks, deaths and predictions belong to **their claimed timelines**, not automatic outcomes for the main party. The scene **does not physically import an alternate-Nash army**. [S2E04](../../episodes/S2E04.md).

Inside the inhabited tower, the party also meets a living **alternate Pod** with her pixie husband **Roger** and children, and a disfigured alternate Tedna calling himself **Badass T**. They are neither resurrections of the main history's dead Pod and Tedna nor proof that every counterpart shares one biography. Badass T physically returns **Smokey's ammunition belt** to Hope. Angelashi agrees to remain with the four children while Nash continues the mission; their continued safety and a method for recruiting other Nashes are **hopes**, not guaranteed outcomes. [S2E04](../../episodes/S2E04.md).

## Temporary stay, further departures and the Mars reunion

In [S2E05](../../episodes/S2E05.md), the tower is still occupied. A Tungan and a blue Triton argue over **Vorel's Sandburgers**, and an apparent emissary of Chronos arrives after the tower's summons. The emissary's cosmological explanations and proposed means of moving alternate people between realities remain **claims and plans**. Angelashi explicitly dislikes the idea of a crowd of alternate fathers around her children and returns to arrange living space in the tower. The four original heroes, Union Wheel and Badass T **actually depart aboard Fate Six for Blast Belly Rock**, leaving Angelashi and the four children on Planet Nash. No 200-Nash contingent leaves with them.

This stay should **not** be extrapolated into an uninterrupted permanent residence. By [S3E03](../../episodes/S3E03.md), Nash **physically reunites with Angelashi and the same four children on Mars** near the Elhandari terraforming forest, while 99 other Nashes are present elsewhere on Mars. The intermediate movement of each family member is **not separately staged** in the cited episodes. The alternate-Nash gathering on Mars is later evidence, **not** a mass arrival on Planet Nash in S2E04. [Angelashi's family history](../characters/angelashi.md).

## S3E18 — resurrection and the chosen family home

After Earth is restored and the Moon's Council of Faces facility is destroyed, Nash's coerced new chrome body **actually dies** aboard the departing Elhandari ship. Stanley retains a sample. Four days later, the repaired Fate Six carries Stanley, Hope and Vorel to **Planet Nash** with Nash still dead. Outside Earth's mana-suppressed environment, Stanley uses ***Wish*** to reproduce ***True Resurrection*** on the sample, and **Nash genuinely returns alive in familiar flesh, without his carried equipment**. This happens at the Planet Nash destination, not at Solar Mines or on Mars. [S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md) · [Nash](../characters/nash-maggard.md).

At the **Elhandari outpost**, Nash is physically reunited with **Angelashi and four living children**. He chooses to stay with them rather than continue with the other adventurers; the companions depart afterward. In the final **domestic cottage** scene, an **apparent second Nash** emerges from a locked cupboard and warns that **“he's coming.”** Neither the visitor's identity nor the referent of “he” is disclosed. The cottage is shown **on Planet Nash**, but it should **not** silently be equated with the tower's interior or any unvisited building. The unexplained ending does not establish the Principal's return, identify Nash Junior as the visitor or constitute another played season. [S3E18 finale](../../episodes/S3E18.md).

## Current status and source boundaries

At the end of played **S3E18**, Planet Nash is a **visited, inhabited off-Earth place** where the resurrected Nash chooses to live with his spouse and four children. The tower, earlier households, Tungans, changing visitors and Elhandari outpost have different observed windows; a **complete postwar census, continuing government, permanent guarantees of safety and final fate of Tower Nash** are not shown. The family's actual reunion and Nash's decision are firmer evidence than earlier plans to resettle or recruit. The apparent double's warning remains a deliberate unresolved fictional hook.
