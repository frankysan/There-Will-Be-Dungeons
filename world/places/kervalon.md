# Kervalon / Kaer Valon — contested border keep and Demon Vold approach

> **Working name only:** `Kervalon`, `Kaer Valon`, `Caravalon`, `Caeravalon` and similar ASR variants. **Do not silently declare canon orthography from a processed transcript.** Discussed [E061](../../episodes/E061.md), reached physically [E067](../../episodes/E067.md). **Full spoilers through E073**.

## Earlier warnings and route

In **E061**, a northern **Zark clan** envoy at Razak Val reports that **Kervalon** is a defended area near the **Demon Vold** where wardens are losing fighters to demonic incursion. The envoy alleges a formidable enemy with a name reminiscent of **Dollo Poppio**. The party later learns guards attribute the tainted **Sun's Witnesses** delivery to the Zarks, prompting suspicion but **not proving every Zark deliberately caused the outbreak**. In **E064** Vorel and the group flee Razak Val amid transforming prisoners and six-armed lizardfolk, heading toward **Kervalon** to seek explanations. **Omnom stays to fight**, taking the Necklace of Fireballs. [E061](../../episodes/E061.md) · [E064](../../episodes/E064.md).

## The changing purple sands (E065–E066)

A **violent, prolonged thunderstorm** over the purple sands appears unprecedented to several travelers. **Fate 4** bogs down in mud and is freed by Nash; near camp **a T. rex** prowls. In **E066**, it **crushes and later causes destruction of Fate 4**, while Vorel and Nash defeat it. A **mind flayer calling itself roughly Oubvayx/Ovaxiad**, claiming allegiance to **Dollo Poppio**, attempts to extract a deal from Queen Hope and **abducts Sira**, directing the party to find him at **Kervalon**. It **kills Rorf**, fatally injures **Follow**, and sprouts **eyeball-bearing black trees** across the surrounding terrain. The darkness and storm continue long past an ordinary night's rest. These physical phenomena are witnessed; **the mind flayer's control of every lizardfolk, authority to cancel Hope's Soulbroker bargain, or identity as a fully manifested god are not established**. [E065](../../episodes/E065.md) · [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

A settlement of **skeletal-looking travelers** is encountered amid the strange trees. They **claim to be stranded space voyagers**, and a leader named something like **Sergeant T'Lupa/Tulupa** provides a **green spectral horse-and-carriage** that bends the tree trunks aside; the crew also gives the party a supposed **warp-drive cylinder** for a crashed ship. These are **their statements and observed objects**, not verified spaceflight, and no repair/installation occurs in E066. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

## Keep and underground ruins (E067)

On the magical carriage the travelers experience a **starfield encounter with a translucent dragon spirit** which **claims an ancient pre-Scorching human-built device lies beneath the keep**. According to it, the **machine was intended to relocate the entire planet** to evade an ancient dragon assault and is now being abused to help **Oubvayx bring his realm to Earth and incarnate Dollo Poppio through Sira**. The spirit claims its dead body lies beneath the castle and names a dragon associated with Nash's heart as **Kazdejira/Kazdeshira**. All are **important but attributed supernatural testimony**; the party **has not inspected the actual machine or corpse** at E067 cutoff.

At the keep the party sees **stone ruins, a bright beam dropping from above, approximately 30-foot walls and stationary armed lizardfolk on the ramparts**. Rather than carry out hypothetical frontal **T. rex-polymorph or invisible-dragon assault** schemes, **Nash tunnels with Mold Earth beneath the outer wall**; Hope scouts a disused underground chamber. The party seals the route behind them, defeats a **flesh-and-bone creature that continuously spawns animated skeletons**, and advances cautiously through vacant stone rooms and lowered corridors. **Hope observes an armed lizardfolk lying in wait farther down a passage.** E067 ends before they confront that guard or reach the machine, Sira, the mind flayer or its cabal. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md).

## Sealed rifle ambush and hidden elevator (E068)

The apparently waiting rifleman is part of **a larger force with bulky, energy-pulse firearms**. Vorel's shouted **Great Fang** challenge draws attackers into a broad basement battle. **Nash seals roughly eight lizardfolk melee pursuers into a stone passage**; their hammering later **stops suddenly**, but the party **does not open the sealed corridor or prove the occupants dead**. **Stanley Polymorphs Vorel into a T. rex** (executed now, unlike the previously discussed E067 tactic), and Vorel kills several guards. **Hope's shotgun blasts interrupt the mind flayer's Dominate Monster**, then a psychic Mind Blast leaves Hope and Vorel stunned, and rifle fire ends the polymorph. **Stanley's Diplomacy runs through the illithid-like foe**, whose visible body becomes **empty skin and black oil**. Enemy permanence, possession and relation to the E066 kidnapper **are unresolved**; **Sira is still missing**. All exposed riflemen are eventually killed, **not necessarily the entire garrison**. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md).

A **spiral-stair tower contains a searing, vertical shaft of exceptionally strong magic** extending from sky through floor. Objects and an already-dead lizardfolk placed directly in it **disintegrate**. **Hope's approaching leg spirals briefly but recovers** when withdrawn; **Nash's Mold Earth is actively resisted below the shaft**. The party ascends to the rainy roof, descends again and follows **little black worms to a hidden floor seam**. Removing masonry reveals **two solid steel elevator-like doors, a control panel and a round glass feature**, with dozens of worms retreating from light. **Hope reaches to press the panel; it becomes a biting mouth and narrowly misses her fleshy fingers**, leaving the doors **closed and purpose unknown**. The [August bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-08.md) has **GM testimony that the beam was intended to point toward a deeper, hidden room and the worms were clues**, but does not establish the ancient machine's operation. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md).

## Underground CHIDS complex and three routes (E069–E071)

In **E069**, a **sentient Door Authority panel** beneath Kervalon refuses access and calls the party criminals, but **Nash's bargained Acid Splash actually causes it to open**. Beyond it lie a **purple fog-filled 45-degree funicular** and an ancient research complex: **[CHIDS — Center for Human-Demon Interdisciplinary Studies](chids.md)**. **Magical pink flowers/spores** put Hope and Nash to sleep during the funicular descent, but **Stanley's Feather Fall** prevents death in a catastrophic crash. Within the complex, **Julia's holographic welcome guide** explains a **pre-Scorching human/demon science center** and shows a multi-color **subway map**. The group recovers long-dead scientists' **employee ID cards**, meets automated guns at an ID checkpoint, and boards a functional **blue-line railcar**. It passes numerous collapsed stops, an open **Gordon Shuttle Bay**, and **Laboratory A with humming violet crystals**, before the group selects **executive offices**. No reconstructed map coordinates beyond the in-session diagram are independently checked. [E069](../../episodes/E069.md).

In **E070**, the executive office's console and wired conference glasses lead Nash and Hope into a **subjective illithid corporate “Void Realm”** where **Charles Dickens**, a disgruntled employee, claims a rival plans to use a **Traversal Drive** to import their realm and harvest Earth's souls. With Hope's avatar disguises, **Nash uploads fake reports** into a server. The resulting **real-world alarm** summons **flaming hellhounds and demonic ape security**, and the pair narrowly escapes headset membranes. **Stanley Polymorphs one ape into a living snail**, the other becomes invisible; the fight carries into E071. The virtual world's proposed political consequences are **Charles's interested claims**, not confirmed physical evidence of an operating drive. [E070](../../episodes/E070.md).

In **E071**, the party kills the active ape and remaining hellhounds, and meets **Alvert**, an imp claiming membership in the **Underworld Bureau of Investigation**. He identifies **engineering** as his best guess for the Traversal Drive but says the **face-like engineering door has no power**. This is **his report**. A later console message purporting to come from **Charles Dickens** confirms the data upload; the **promised months of delay are not independently verified**. The group launches a **still-live polymorphed-snail ape in an unattended railcar**, **without witnessing any crash or reversion**. They travel by foot to the **north transfer** and along the **green line**, enter the **Archives** with a credential and persuasion of another sentient door (which refers to the **Door Street Journal**), but find the archives' inner doors/terminals **unpowered**. Continuing without a long rest, they reach the **data center**, whose enormous steel gate is **sealed beneath ~20 feet of freezing, regenerating ice**. **Stanley's Heat Metal, the Eye of Groxl and a thinner overhead ventilation duct** make an indirect route. **All four crawl into the icy duct at E071 cutoff**; **Alvert remains outside**, and **data center/engineering are separate destinations**. [E071](../../episodes/E071.md).

## Freezing data center, cursed elf and stalled laboratory elevator (E072–E073)

From the **overhead data-center duct** reached E071, the party crawls through icy vents until **Stanley's Heat Metal destabilizes a passage**; **Feather Fall** rescues them during the fall into **CHIDS's huge frozen server hall**. The hall contains computer racks, immensely tall cooling fans and preserved bodies. A withered elf, **[Cerinthala](../characters/cerinthala.md)** (provisional ASR spelling), is camped behind a windbreak with four frozen human companions **Toby, Carl, Gordon, Benson**. After **Stanley promises best efforts to return her to Elhandar**, she **remotely unlocks engineering via data pad** and gives him an **amulet granting +1 AC**. The party takes a **long rest**, but giant **corpse-containing ice constructs** attack Stanley and his necklace chokes him. [E072](../../episodes/E072.md).

In E073 **Hope frees Stanley** by severing an ice giant's arm; Vorel's **Eye of Groxl** and Nash's **Careful Spell Fireball** defeat multiple icy forms and their **blue-eyed reanimated corpse occupants**. **Toby still speaks after the attack**. Cerinthala reveals she **once considered eating or using the party** and made Stanley's **unremovable, choking and lightning-discharging curse herself**; his **original best-effort promise still stands**, and the item is not removed. She travels with them and directs Vorel to excavate a **frozen ~25-foot maintenance passage** beneath her **Maltaweed garden**, exposing **red-lit service corridors** that lead to a **biological laboratory containing rows of vats and aggressive pink-purple flesh**. A tendril **snaps Nash's staff into two pieces**, while the nail-bearing short portion retains use as a focus. Cerinthala alleges old **lizardfolk/vampire experiments and infernal-wave reproduction**, but these are **her claims**, not independently verified; **Vorel refuses to manufacture a child**. They reach a **four-lift bank**: one lift falls and crashes; a second stalls between floors with fraying cable, and **a giant spider-shaped threat descends toward the party at the roof hatch**. **No machine, Sira rescue, spacecraft launch or engineering door visit by E073**. [E073](../../episodes/E073.md) · [CHIDS site](chids.md).

- **Keep location / Demon Vold portal:** do not collapse these into one structure merely because both concern the Zarks and demon invasion.
- **Dragon's planet-moving-machine claim:** distinguish observed beam and supernatural conversation from verified engineering. Also keep alleged spectral travelers' **warp coil/ship** distinct from the supposed relocation machine.
- **Sira:** abducted alive E066, **not rescued E067**; Follow and Rorf **die E066**.
- **Ruin's defenders:** the previously motionless rampart lizardfolk **might be enthralled**, but the discovered underground guard's allegiance and consciousness are not yet established.
- **Travel:** the ghost carriage disappears at Kervalon E067; **Fate 4 is destroyed E066**, and **Fang Fate Five** is not physically carried through the densely packed forest.
