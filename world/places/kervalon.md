# Khaervilon — contested border keep and Demon Vold approach

> **Canonical written name (D-044):** **Khaervilon**, owner-confirmed from the larger *Known Wastes* map. Earlier project/ASR variants `Kervalon`, `Kaer Valon`, `Caravalon`, `Caeravalon` and similar forms remain historical aliases rather than separate places; pronunciation has not been audio-verified. Discussed [E061](../../episodes/E061.md), reached physically [E067](../../episodes/E067.md). **Full spoilers through E081**.

The party approaches **Khaervilon**, a contested border keep above the buried **CHIDS** research complex, after Sira's abduction. Their exploration moves from the keep's defended ruins to the Traversal Drive chamber below. **By the played E081 cutoff**, Sira and Eddie escape the machine's beam, Chance remains missing, and the GM declares the four protagonists dead after their sacrifice. The next episode follows their divergent fates in **Sigil**, not a witnessed return to inspect Khaervilon's final condition. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md) · [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [E082](../../episodes/E082.md).

## Earlier warnings and route

In **E061**, a northern **Zark clan** envoy at Razak Val reports that **Khaervilon** is a defended area near the **Demon Vold** where wardens are losing fighters to demonic incursion. The envoy alleges a formidable enemy with a name reminiscent of **Dollo Poppio**. The party later learns guards attribute the tainted **Sun's Witnesses** delivery to the Zarks, prompting suspicion but **not proving every Zark deliberately caused the outbreak**. In **E064** Vorel and the group flee Razak Val amid transforming prisoners and six-armed lizardfolk, heading toward **Khaervilon** to seek explanations. **Omnom stays to fight**, taking the Necklace of Fireballs. [E061](../../episodes/E061.md) · [E064](../../episodes/E064.md).

## The changing purple sands (E065–E066)

A **violent, prolonged thunderstorm** over the purple sands appears unprecedented to several travelers. **Fate 4** bogs down in mud and is freed by Nash; near camp **a T. rex** prowls. In **E066**, it **crushes and later causes destruction of Fate 4**, while Vorel and Nash defeat it. A **mind flayer calling itself roughly Oubvayx/Ovaxiad**, claiming allegiance to **Dollo Poppio**, attempts to extract a deal from Queen Hope and **abducts Sira**, directing the party to find him at **Khaervilon**. It **kills Rorf**, fatally injures **Follow**, and sprouts **eyeball-bearing black trees** across the surrounding terrain. The darkness and storm continue long past an ordinary night's rest. These physical phenomena are witnessed; **the mind flayer's control of every lizardfolk, authority to cancel Hope's Soulbroker bargain, or identity as a fully manifested god are not established**. [E065](../../episodes/E065.md) · [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

A settlement of **skeletal-looking travelers** is encountered amid the strange trees. They **claim to be stranded space voyagers**, and a leader named something like **Sergeant T'Lupa/Tulupa** provides a **green spectral horse-and-carriage** that bends the tree trunks aside; the crew also gives the party a supposed **warp-drive cylinder** for a crashed ship. These are **their statements and observed objects**, not verified spaceflight, and no repair/installation occurs in E066. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

## Keep and underground ruins (E067)

On the magical carriage the travelers experience a **starfield encounter with a translucent dragon spirit** which **claims an ancient pre-Scorching human-built device lies beneath the keep**. According to it, the **machine was intended to relocate the entire planet** to evade an ancient dragon assault and is now being abused to help **Oubvayx bring his realm to Earth and incarnate Dollo Poppio through Sira**. The spirit claims its dead body lies beneath the castle and names a dragon associated with Nash's heart as **Kazdejira/Kazdeshira**. All are **important but attributed supernatural testimony**; the party **has not inspected the actual machine or corpse** at E067 cutoff.

At the keep the party sees **stone ruins, a bright beam dropping from above, approximately 30-foot walls and stationary armed lizardfolk on the ramparts**. Rather than carry out hypothetical frontal **T. rex-polymorph or invisible-dragon assault** schemes, **Nash tunnels with Mold Earth beneath the outer wall**; Hope scouts a disused underground chamber. The party seals the route behind them, defeats a **flesh-and-bone creature that continuously spawns animated skeletons**, and advances cautiously through vacant stone rooms and lowered corridors. **Hope observes an armed lizardfolk lying in wait farther down a passage.** E067 ends before they confront that guard or reach the machine, Sira, the mind flayer or its cabal. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md).

## Sealed rifle ambush and hidden elevator (E068)

The apparently waiting rifleman is part of **a larger force with bulky, energy-pulse firearms**. Vorel's shouted **Great Fang** challenge draws attackers into a broad basement battle. **Nash seals roughly eight lizardfolk melee pursuers into a stone passage**; their hammering later **stops suddenly**, but the party **does not open the sealed corridor or prove the occupants dead**. **Stanley Polymorphs Vorel into a T. rex** (executed now, unlike the previously discussed E067 tactic), and Vorel kills several guards. **Hope's shotgun blasts interrupt the mind flayer's Dominate Monster**, then a psychic Mind Blast leaves Hope and Vorel stunned, and rifle fire ends the polymorph. **Stanley's Diplomacy runs through the illithid-like foe**, whose visible body becomes **empty skin and black oil**. Enemy permanence, possession and relation to the E066 kidnapper **are unresolved**; **Sira is still missing**. All exposed riflemen are eventually killed, **not necessarily the entire garrison**. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md).

A **spiral-stair tower contains a searing, vertical shaft of exceptionally strong magic** extending from sky through floor. Objects and an already-dead lizardfolk placed directly in it **disintegrate**. **Hope's approaching leg spirals briefly but recovers** when withdrawn; **Nash's Mold Earth is actively resisted below the shaft**. The party ascends to the rainy roof, descends again and follows **little black worms to a hidden floor seam**. Removing masonry reveals **two solid steel elevator-like doors, a control panel and a round glass feature**, with dozens of worms retreating from light. **Hope reaches to press the panel; it becomes a biting mouth and narrowly misses her fleshy fingers**, leaving the doors **closed and purpose unknown**. The [August bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-08.md) has **GM testimony that the beam was intended to point toward a deeper, hidden room and the worms were clues**, but does not establish the ancient machine's operation. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md).

## Underground CHIDS complex and three routes (E069–E071)

In **E069**, a **sentient Door Authority panel** beneath Khaervilon refuses access and calls the party criminals, but **Nash's bargained Acid Splash actually causes it to open**. Beyond it lie a **purple fog-filled 45-degree funicular** and an ancient research complex: **[CHIDS — Center for Human-Demon Interdisciplinary Studies](chids.md)**. **Magical pink flowers/spores** put Hope and Nash to sleep during the funicular descent, but **Stanley's Feather Fall** prevents death in a catastrophic crash. Within the complex, **Julia's holographic welcome guide** explains a **pre-Scorching human/demon science center** and shows a multi-color **subway map**. The group recovers long-dead scientists' **employee ID cards**, meets automated guns at an ID checkpoint, and boards a functional **blue-line railcar**. It passes numerous collapsed stops, an open **Gordon Shuttle Bay**, and **Laboratory A with humming violet crystals**, before the group selects **executive offices**. No reconstructed map coordinates beyond the in-session diagram are independently checked. [E069](../../episodes/E069.md).

In **E070**, the executive office's console and wired conference glasses lead Nash and Hope into a **subjective illithid corporate “Void Realm”** where **Charles Dickens**, a disgruntled employee, claims a rival plans to use a **Traversal Drive** to import their realm and harvest Earth's souls. With Hope's avatar disguises, **Nash uploads fake reports** into a server. The resulting **real-world alarm** summons **flaming hellhounds and demonic ape security**, and the pair narrowly escapes headset membranes. **Stanley Polymorphs one ape into a living snail**, the other becomes invisible; the fight carries into E071. The virtual world's proposed political consequences are **Charles's interested claims**, not confirmed physical evidence of an operating drive. [E070](../../episodes/E070.md).

In **E071**, the party kills the active ape and remaining hellhounds, and meets **Alvert**, an imp claiming membership in the **Underworld Bureau of Investigation**. He identifies **engineering** as his best guess for the Traversal Drive but says the **face-like engineering door has no power**. This is **his report**. A later console message purporting to come from **Charles Dickens** confirms the data upload; the **promised months of delay are not independently verified**. The group launches a **still-live polymorphed-snail ape in an unattended railcar**, **without witnessing any crash or reversion**. They travel by foot to the **north transfer** and along the **green line**, enter the **Archives** with a credential and persuasion of another sentient door (which refers to the **Door Street Journal**), but find the archives' inner doors/terminals **unpowered**. Continuing without a long rest, they reach the **data center**, whose enormous steel gate is **sealed beneath ~20 feet of freezing, regenerating ice**. **Stanley's Heat Metal, the Eye of Groxl and a thinner overhead ventilation duct** make an indirect route. **All four crawl into the icy duct at E071 cutoff**; **Alvert remains outside**, and **data center/engineering are separate destinations**. [E071](../../episodes/E071.md).

## Freezing data center, cursed elf and stalled laboratory elevator (E072–E073)

From the **overhead data-center duct** reached E071, the party crawls through icy vents until **Stanley's Heat Metal destabilizes a passage**; **Feather Fall** rescues them during the fall into **CHIDS's huge frozen server hall**. The hall contains computer racks, immensely tall cooling fans and preserved bodies. A withered elf, **[Cerinthala](../characters/cerinthala.md)** (provisional ASR spelling), is camped behind a windbreak with four frozen human companions **Toby, Carl, Gordon, Benson**. After **Stanley promises best efforts to return her to Elhandar**, she **remotely unlocks engineering via data pad** and gives him an **amulet granting +1 AC**. The party takes a **long rest**, but giant **corpse-containing ice constructs** attack Stanley and his necklace chokes him. [E072](../../episodes/E072.md).

In E073 **Hope frees Stanley** by severing an ice giant's arm; Vorel's **Eye of Groxl** and Nash's **Careful Spell Fireball** defeat multiple icy forms and their **blue-eyed reanimated corpse occupants**. **Toby still speaks after the attack**. Cerinthala reveals she **once considered eating or using the party** and made Stanley's **unremovable, choking and lightning-discharging curse herself**; his **original best-effort promise still stands**, and the item is not removed. She travels with them and directs Vorel to excavate a **frozen ~25-foot maintenance passage** beneath her **Maltaweed garden**, exposing **red-lit service corridors** that lead to a **biological laboratory containing rows of vats and aggressive pink-purple flesh**. A tendril **snaps Nash's staff into two pieces**, while the nail-bearing short portion retains use as a focus. Cerinthala alleges old **lizardfolk/vampire experiments and infernal-wave reproduction**, but these are **her claims**, not independently verified; **Vorel refuses to manufacture a child**. They reach a **four-lift bank**: one lift falls and crashes; a second stalls between floors with fraying cable, and **a giant spider-shaped threat descends toward the party at the roof hatch**. **No machine, Sira rescue, spacecraft launch or engineering door visit by E073**. [E073](../../episodes/E073.md) · [CHIDS site](chids.md).

## CHIDS lower laboratory fighting and portal escape plan (E074–E075)

The party descends the **failed laboratory elevator shaft** through **brain-backed spider swarms**. **Hope kills the matriarch**, and Vorel turns its shell into shields. **Cerinthala dies after a selective pink flesh passage closes on her**, but **revives in E075**, confirming a return from death on this occasion. Lower-level **energy-rifle lizardfolk guards** are killed, then a CHIDS console **illithid summons a red memory-devouring organism**. **Vorel kills the organism and its two tethered clones**, and **Hope and Nash kill the illithid**. **Vorel, Nash and Stanley remain memory-impaired**. **Hope removes three black headworms** from the illithid and two guard heads. [E074](../../episodes/E074.md) · [E075](../../episodes/E075.md).

Behind the console a sealed room yields one **red/green paired-[portal device](../technology/portal-device.md)** after coordinating ancient controls. **Vorel first takes it, later transfers it to Hope**. **Cerinthala uses the recovered blue gem to apparently summon ice Toby, paralyzes Hope, and flees**; the heroes defeat Toby and **Stanley dispels a Wall of Ice**, but **do not recapture her**. **Nash destroys some bugbears with Fireball**, and E075 ends with **Vorel drawing a charge from around fifty bugbears and an illithid** while the others consider a portal escape. **The escape is still a plan, not an accomplished return to the surface**. [CHIDS](chids.md).

## Drive chamber and end-of-arc outcome (E076–E081)

After E075's unfinished bugbear charge, the party **actually escapes by the red/green linked portal device in E076**, enters **CHIDS Containment**, and discovers experimental **lizardfolk vats, weapons, the living hive council and alleged future Wall Nash** in E077. The council supplies cache codes and **physically transports the adventurers near engineering in E078**, where they rescue **Chance and Eddie**. **E079's illithid disguise and Stanley/Hope Dimension Door plus portal crossing bypass a crystal lock**. The team physically sees the **Traversal Drive's transformed engineering chamber, central column, dragon-worm Dolo Popio, large commander illithid and a captive Sira**. Hope's opening rockets destroy several illithids and Cerinthala's observed body. In **E080 Nash kills the large commander**.

The **starry-void battlefield in E080–E081** is strange and partly unexplained, but is **directly depicted**. **Chance drifts away and is not recovered**, **Hope kills Dolo Popio's manifested body with radiant darts E081**, and the group returns to engineering. **Sira physically leaves the central beam**, ending the prior abducted status. **Quizquaz** offers the heroes a chance to allow Earth to be destroyed, allegedly defeating the Principal, but they reject that offer. Instead **all four protagonists enter a high-frequency crimson beam together**, disappear, and **are explicitly declared dead by the GM at E081 cutoff**. **Eddie and Sira remain outside alive**; **Chance's ultimate destination, the heroes' permanent fate, and the planet/Drive's later condition are not shown**.

These are **source-cutoff observations and explicitly attributed claims**, not proof of either the spectral dragon's ancient planet-relocation theory or Quizquaz's Void mechanics. [E076](../../episodes/E076.md)–[E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [CHIDS](chids.md) · [Arc chronology](../CHRONOLOGY.md).

## Current status and limits of observation

**At the last witnessed Khaervilon/CHIDS site cutoff (E081)**, the four heroes have used the crimson beam and disappeared; the GM declares them dead. **Sira and Eddie are out of the beam alive**, while **Chance remains missing in the void**. The group did not perform a subsequent inspection of the ruined keep, the entire CHIDS facility or the final physical condition of the Traversal Drive. Quizquaz's claim about a planet-threatening energy source and the spectral dragon's account of an ancient planetary relocation device remain testimony; the actual intervention does not independently establish the machine's permanent shutdown or all of Earth's later history. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md).

**After the E081 cutoff**, [E082](../../episodes/E082.md) depicts the protagonists' different consequences in Sigil: Vorel returns in a living body, Nash persists in a mechanical heart, and Hope and Stanley are found motionless. That episode does not depict a return to Khaervilon or establish Chance's fate. The earlier statement that **Sira had not been rescued at E067** is a true historical episode cutoff, **superseded by her observed E081 exit**, not a description of her current captivity.

## Open issues

- **Name and geography:** **Khaervilon** is the owner-confirmed map spelling (D-044); the existing `kervalon.md` path is retained for compatibility. Pronunciation and **Demon Vold**, **Oubvayx/Ovaxiad**, **Kazdejira/Kazdeshira**, **Sergeant T'Lupa/Tulupa** need original-audio or creator-written verification. The keep and any Demon Vold portal must not be assumed to be the same structure.
- **Machine origins:** The dragon's supposed pre-Scorching planet-moving device, its alleged builder and Quizquaz's Void account are **attributed claims**. The skeletal travelers' purported warp coil and crashed ship are separate from the witnessed CHIDS Drive.
- **Defenders and earlier tactics:** The motionless rampart guards' mental condition is unknown; the E068 sealed-corridor lizardfolk were not inspected after hammering stopped. A T. rex attack plan proposed before entering the keep was not executed at E067, although Vorel later underwent a real polymorph in E068.
- **Travel and custody:** The spectral carriage disappears at the keep; Fate 4 is physically destroyed in E066, and the later Fang Fate Five cart is not shown being brought through the dense forest. Sira's earlier abduction ends with her E081 beam exit, but Chance remains unlocated.
