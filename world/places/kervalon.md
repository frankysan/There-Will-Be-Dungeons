# Kervalon / Kaer Valon — contested border keep and Demon Vold approach

> **Working name only:** `Kervalon`, `Kaer Valon`, `Caravalon`, `Caeravalon` and similar ASR variants. **Do not silently declare canon orthography from a processed transcript.** Discussed [E061](../../episodes/E061.md), reached physically [E067](../../episodes/E067.md). **Full spoilers through E067**.

## Earlier warnings and route

In **E061**, a northern **Zark clan** envoy at Razak Val reports that **Kervalon** is a defended area near the **Demon Vold** where wardens are losing fighters to demonic incursion. The envoy alleges a formidable enemy with a name reminiscent of **Dollo Poppio**. The party later learns guards attribute the tainted **Sun's Witnesses** delivery to the Zarks, prompting suspicion but **not proving every Zark deliberately caused the outbreak**. In **E064** Vorel and the group flee Razak Val amid transforming prisoners and six-armed lizardfolk, heading toward **Kervalon** to seek explanations. **Omnom stays to fight**, taking the Necklace of Fireballs. [E061](../../episodes/E061.md) · [E064](../../episodes/E064.md).

## The changing purple sands (E065–E066)

A **violent, prolonged thunderstorm** over the purple sands appears unprecedented to several travelers. **Fate 4** bogs down in mud and is freed by Nash; near camp **a T. rex** prowls. In **E066**, it **crushes and later causes destruction of Fate 4**, while Vorel and Nash defeat it. A **mind flayer calling itself roughly Oubvayx/Ovaxiad**, claiming allegiance to **Dollo Poppio**, attempts to extract a deal from Queen Hope and **abducts Sira**, directing the party to find him at **Kervalon**. It **kills Rorf**, fatally injures **Follow**, and sprouts **eyeball-bearing black trees** across the surrounding terrain. The darkness and storm continue long past an ordinary night's rest. These physical phenomena are witnessed; **the mind flayer's control of every lizardfolk, authority to cancel Hope's Soulbroker bargain, or identity as a fully manifested god are not established**. [E065](../../episodes/E065.md) · [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

A settlement of **skeletal-looking travelers** is encountered amid the strange trees. They **claim to be stranded space voyagers**, and a leader named something like **Sergeant T'Lupa/Tulupa** provides a **green spectral horse-and-carriage** that bends the tree trunks aside; the crew also gives the party a supposed **warp-drive cylinder** for a crashed ship. These are **their statements and observed objects**, not verified spaceflight, and no repair/installation occurs in E066. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

## Keep and underground ruins (E067)

On the magical carriage the travelers experience a **starfield encounter with a translucent dragon spirit** which **claims an ancient pre-Scorching human-built device lies beneath the keep**. According to it, the **machine was intended to relocate the entire planet** to evade an ancient dragon assault and is now being abused to help **Oubvayx bring his realm to Earth and incarnate Dollo Poppio through Sira**. The spirit claims its dead body lies beneath the castle and names a dragon associated with Nash's heart as **Kazdejira/Kazdeshira**. All are **important but attributed supernatural testimony**; the party **has not inspected the actual machine or corpse** at E067 cutoff.

At the keep the party sees **stone ruins, a bright beam dropping from above, approximately 30-foot walls and stationary armed lizardfolk on the ramparts**. Rather than carry out hypothetical frontal **T. rex-polymorph or invisible-dragon assault** schemes, **Nash tunnels with Mold Earth beneath the outer wall**; Hope scouts a disused underground chamber. The party seals the route behind them, defeats a **flesh-and-bone creature that continuously spawns animated skeletons**, and advances cautiously through vacant stone rooms and lowered corridors. **Hope observes an armed lizardfolk lying in wait farther down a passage.** E067 ends before they confront that guard or reach the machine, Sira, the mind flayer or its cabal. [E067](../../episodes/E067.md).

## Open issues

- Verify **Kervalon/Kaer Valon**, **Demon Vold**, **Oubvayx/Ovaxiad**, **Kazdejira/Kazdeshira**, and **Sergeant T'Lupa/Tulupa** against audio/creator-written sources.
- **Keep location / Demon Vold portal:** do not collapse these into one structure merely because both concern the Zarks and demon invasion.
- **Dragon's planet-moving-machine claim:** distinguish observed beam and supernatural conversation from verified engineering. Also keep alleged spectral travelers' **warp coil/ship** distinct from the supposed relocation machine.
- **Sira:** abducted alive E066, **not rescued E067**; Follow and Rorf **die E066**.
- **Ruin's defenders:** the previously motionless rampart lizardfolk **might be enthralled**, but the discovered underground guard's allegiance and consciousness are not yet established.
- **Travel:** the ghost carriage disappears at Kervalon E067; **Fate 4 is destroyed E066**, and **Fang Fate Five** is not physically carried through the densely packed forest.
