**Browse:** [Places](../PLACES.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Type:** Important origin setting in Nash Maggard's background. **Evidence:** Character statements, retrospective accounts and later research; **not** a fully played historical side adventure. **Spoilers:** Through E118.

**Nash** describes captivity, survival and resistance connected with the **Solar Mines** and **Blood Canyons**. His descriptions develop across the campaign and include memories that seem contradictory; they should be presented as the state of Nash's own recollections and others' testimony, not a reconstructed omniscient history. His implanted **dragon-metal heart** and later dragon forms are observed, but the exact circumstances of implantation require careful source distinction. He emphasizes the difference between **what Nash remembers as an exploited child and what the mines actually involved**, leaving unknown aspects to be discovered. Scott says Nash escaped with **one other individual** whose later whereabouts have not been revealed; Scott wonders whether the guards could have since upgraded **Slow Peters**, but **Beau does not confirm any specific upgrades, present garrison or ongoing operating procedures**. These are **out-of-game creator context and player conjecture**, not additional played travel, a newly revealed escapee identity or witnessed past footage. ## S2E03 — detailed recalled structure and proposed Earth-core invasion

The [Council of Earthly Defenders](../../episodes/S2E03.md) chooses Solar Mines over the Firelands and Black Mine as the **proposed deep route to Earth's core** for the incomplete Triangulosphere, based on strategic discussion and Hope's memory of Tungsten's notes. **No one travels there during S2E03.**

In his longest newly recorded **in-character recollection** (~00:42–01:05), Nash describes a mountain-enclosed complex with a **two-column fortified entrance**, wide **solar-powered concrete high-rises, libraries and classrooms** for children, and an internal policy of selecting people judged capable of reproduction. Children deemed unsuitable are forced into deep **crowded, disease-ridden labor quarters and mineral mines**, overseen by magical-mechanical **Slow Peters**. Nash exposes scars he attributes to this mining labor and says he escaped concealed inside one gutted Slow Peter, helped by fellow captives who died. He discusses immense solar-energy pipes feeding production of special material far underground, rockfalls, hazardous liquid/lava and possible air defenses. **The true goal of material production, exact reproductive policy, surviving populations, defenses today and rumored current allegiance remain partly Nash's remembered beliefs, not GM-presented modern visual reconnaissance.**

He remembers a hidden central dictator called **“Emperor With No Name”**, whose propaganda was announced through speakers but whose identity and continued rule are unverified. The council expects **the Principle** and various factions may have moved there after destruction of Principle City, **a strategic forecast, not a sighting**. Nash suggests rearward *Mold Earth* penetration to distract Slow Peters while forces attack through the mountain pass; **no tunnel constructed yet**. At S2E03 cutoff a projected coalition is mobilizing but no battle is underway. [Source comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).
