# Solar Mines and Blood Canyons

The **Solar Mines** are a fortified industrial and mining complex in the mountains of Earth's Wastes, adjoining the **Blood Canyons**. Their surface contains the northern metal gate, military batteries, worker facilities, and the glass-domed residential district called the **City of the Chosen**. Tunnels, labor cages, old classrooms and mine carts descend toward a geothermal chamber beneath the settlement. Nash Maggard recalls childhood captivity and forced labor here. Later, the four travelers actually enter the site during an allied assault and reach its deepest chamber, where the Principal has brought the planet's fate into dispute. [Nash](../characters/nash-maggard.md) · [S3E10](../../episodes/S3E10.md).

**The place has two distinct source histories.** Nash's descriptions of his childhood, the supposed Emperor With No Name, and the original purpose of the mines are **in-character memories and other speakers' claims**; the campaign does not play his childhood as a separate side adventure. The S3E05–S3E18 battle, occupation and Ruby deployment are **later witnessed events**, each with its own episode cutoff. The return of trees and rain in the finale is performed, but no complete reconstruction of the mines or universal freeing of prisoners is shown. [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) · [S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md).

## Origins, remembered childhood and uncertain history

Nash describes confinement, harsh labor and a rebellion from which he escaped, carrying scars he attributes to the mines. In his fuller S2E03 account he recalls solar-powered towers, a fortified two-column entrance, libraries and classrooms for privileged children, and crowded dangerous mine quarters for others deemed unsuitable under a reproductive-selection policy. Huge magical-mechanical **Slow Peters** enforced the work. He says other captives helped him flee inside the body of a gutted Slow Peter, and that they died during his escape. His account includes enormous solar pipes, hazardous subterranean material and a distant ruler called the **Emperor With No Name**. These are important recollections, not an independently witnessed census, source of Nash's implanted heart or proof that the same administration still held power years later. [E000](../../episodes/E000.md) · [E024](../../episodes/E024.md) · [S2E03](../../episodes/S2E03.md) · [S2E03 ASR comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E03_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

In **E117**, Nash recounts the uprising to Elhandari listeners; it is a performance of his memory, not a newly played flashback. In **E118**, the [Book of Adjacencies](../technology/book-of-adjacencies.md) has a globe apparently marking the Solar Mines. Delit Or'Nath's theories about N4SH, the Principal and time remain the author's claims rather than proof of the facility's history. In a September 2020 creator Q&A, Scott says he shortened Nash's story because his notes were unavailable; that does not supply the missing historical incidents. [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) · [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [September 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-09.md).

## The proposed route and war council — S2E03 to S3E04

The Council of Earthly Defenders chooses the Solar Mines over the Firelands and Black Mine as a **proposed route to Earth's core** for the unfinished [Rubistic Triangulosphere](../technology/rubistic-triangulosphere.md). Nash imagines approaching through the mountain pass or digging behind the defenders with *Mold Earth*. Neither an excavation nor a mine assault occurs in S2E03. The council also speculates that the Principal may have sought shelter there after Principal City's destruction; this is not an observed sighting. [S2E03](../../episodes/S2E03.md).

At the second Mars war council in **S3E04**, a scout provisionally named **Nobieri** reports a hot, waterless valley and a map containing **Workerberg**, the **City of the Chosen**, an **Information Library** and a central lava basin. He estimates four anti-aircraft batteries, seven or eight enormous Slow Peters, 1,000–2,000 Toyota Host personnel, and Kuromokishi and Umbilical Order forces. These are scouting reports, not an exact inventory or a demonstration of their readiness. The allies **plan**, but do not yet perform, a decoy attack at the northern gate while Diontalus, Monsoon, Vorel's lizardfolk and alternate Nashes target separate anti-aircraft positions. A later flight of [Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md) carrying the four heroes and completed Ruby into the main mine is still only a plan. [S3E04](../../episodes/S3E04.md) · [report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E04_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## The northern gate battle — S3E05 to S3E07

In **S3E05**, fifteen Elhandari vessels and Fate Six actually reach Earth. Liberators, Bokbok's goblin convoy and the four adventurers advance toward the **closed northern metal gate**, defended by two immense lava-and-metal Slow Peters. Nash repeatedly casts *Ice Storm* and Stanley kills one defender with *Eldritch Blast*; Nash destroys the second. Hope drives and organizes the approach, while Vorel's fire resistance protects companions from the heat. Goblins climb the adjacent cliffs, but **the gate is still closed** when an Umbilical Order Paladin and Kuromokishi rush to engage. Other anti-aircraft detachments are only reporting by radio: no battery is then confirmed disabled. **Ruby is aboard Fate Six, not in the mine.** [S3E05](../../episodes/S3E05.md) · [comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E05_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

In **S3E06**, the party defeats the Paladin and the eight Kuromokishi who attack it, yet still does not open the gate. Diontalus reports one battery destroyed, while other positions give ambiguous radio messages; a meteor swarm strikes the valley without a reliable whole-army casualty count. The Nameless One's vehicle crashes and explodes, but his death is reported as apparent. The immediate defenders' defeat should not be read backward as the gate's capture or Ruby's delivery. [S3E06](../../episodes/S3E06.md).

**S3E07** finally shows the **northern wall breached**. Bokbok is launched onto the battlements with explosives, and ape-form Stanley throws an armed Paladin suit against the defenses; allied troops enter. Later confirmation treats all four anti-aircraft positions as neutralized, although the southwest detachment's reported losses among alternate Nashes are not individually counted. Fate Six carries the heroes and Ruby toward the central lava basin, where a chrome dragon brings up a vast egg. Vorel kills the dragon, but the egg releases the enormous **Groxl**, with the building marking the original mine entrance attached to his back. Groxl **crushes Fate Six**, and dragon-form Nash **actually rescues Ruby** as it falls. The central entrance is no longer accessible and the main core has not yet been reached. [S3E07](../../episodes/S3E07.md) · [comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E07_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

## Lava battlefield and an alternative route — S3E08 to S3E09

An expanding lava pool cuts the valley into islands in **S3E08**. Groxl's giant body and attached weapons kill allied fighters, and the embedded entrance building cannot serve as an ordinary doorway. Hope identifies a **human-powered generator** on the giant; dragon Nash and Stanley use force and *Telekinesis* to tear it free and drop it into lava. The giant continues fighting until Vorel severs a heart/aorta cable with **[Chrome's Bane](../technology/chromes-bane.md)**, causing it to collapse. Diontalus is visibly disintegrated by an eye attack, and Union and Emenil are crushed; no resurrection of those casualties is played at that point. **The fallen giant's generator is not the separately completed Ruby**, and nothing yet activates planetary repair. [S3E08](../../episodes/S3E08.md).

Monsoon rescues the four heroes from the aftermath in **S3E09** and brings them to an allied wagon-circle camp. **Ruby is visibly in this camp** with Hope's remote, not inside the mine. Admiral Chrysorius reports that lava has flooded the central approach. Nash and White Nash recall a strange, comparatively comfortable **glass-domed graduate suburb** with stores and an apparent local internet. The party and several allies parachute into the dome and enter its **Walmart**, where armed Toyota Host soldiers and Kuromokishi stand under the half-mechanical **Dick Pendleton**, called General Motors. During the fight Bertrand kills White Nash; Unfortunate Nash kills Bertrand with *Meteor Swarm*, and Hope kills General Motors. The group secures a stockroom freight elevator. **The subterranean destination is not yet shown in S3E09**, and Ruby's physical later movement from the camp is not witnessed. [S3E09](../../episodes/S3E09.md) · [report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E09_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md).

## The mine below: captivity, conditioning and the core door — S3E10

In **S3E10**, the Walmart freight elevator descends to working mine carts, steam pipes and tunnels. The GM now places **completed Ruby with the group underground**, protected by *Otiluke's Resilient Sphere*, while Hope retains its remote. This is the later established state; the precise camp-to-elevator transfer is **not independently staged**. The heroes pass six cages containing the bodies of hundreds of decapitated laborers and rooms used for whipping. They do not witness the massacre occurring or identify every perpetrator. [S3E10](../../episodes/S3E10.md) · [comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E10_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md).

Nash recognizes a wing of restraint chairs, films and electrical conditioning from his childhood, and a masked teacher attacks him. Stanley kills the teacher with *Eldritch Blast*. Nash visits his former cell and an operating slab with clamps, blood and a dragon emblem where **he believes** his engineered heart was installed. The room is directly observed, but the actual childhood operation is not replayed. Beyond a molten-lava pipe the companions deceive and kill three Kuromokishi. They then enter a corridor of mounted chanting human heads, where **Hope recognizes and speaks to a head resembling her father** before killing it. The final metal doorway, surrounded by aware heads, calls itself **Door Authority**. It **does not open at this cutoff**: there is no meeting with the Principal, Ruby installation or core activation in S3E10. [Nash's heart](../technology/nash-dragon-heart.md) · [S3E10](../../episodes/S3E10.md).

## The Principal's geothermal chamber — S3E11 to S3E12

In **S3E11**, the Door Authority opens after saying Nash's father has paid his old fines. The four descend through a cavern of chanting heads and **actually enter the central lava-lake chamber**, with its stone bridge, vertical hot lava beam and four robed dragonsons. The **Principal is present at a desk**. He speaks about Nash's creation and his projected chrome destiny; his assertions about time, history and biological ownership remain interested testimony. **Ruby physically floats in its protective sphere in the chamber**, but the heroes do **not** put it into the lava or activate it. A temporary ceasefire, a real long rest and a feast follow; Hope obtains the charged [Hollow Ring](../technology/hollow-ring.md) and opens a *Gate* for all four heroes and the Principal to **Château Weir**, leaving Ruby behind. [S3E11](../../episodes/S3E11.md) · [Château Weir](chateau-weir.md).

**S3E12** does not depict a return to Solar Mines. The Soulbroker is physically defeated in the Weir, and the Principal opens an escape portal to **Hell**. Breaking the Weir–Earth connection does not remove the **Worldbreaker seed** already inside Earth. The travelers discuss returning to place Ruby in the lava, but that is **an unperformed plan**. The last observed Ruby status is still the protected device in the Principal's geothermal chamber, with Hope retaining the remote. [S3E12](../../episodes/S3E12.md) · [comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E12_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md).

## Allied occupation and guarded Ruby — S3E13 to S3E15

Brett/Asmodeus returns the heroes to Earth in **S3E13**; they do not use the Hollow Ring for this journey. Stanley summons a restored Fate Six. A flight reveals northern fissures, lava and purple geysers, which Amanthalodar attributes to the still-growing Worldbreaker. They land at **Solar Mines now physically controlled by allied Elhandari forces**. Some survivors of the residential dome are held in cages, without a shown individual trial or general emancipation. Amanthalodar discovers that Ruby has **not** been activated and criticizes the prior truce; Chrysorius urges delaying the mana-suppressing cure while a giant threat is still alive. Stanley **orders** elite troops to enter the deep chamber and secure Ruby. Their entry and retrieval are **not yet shown** in S3E13. [S3E13](../../episodes/S3E13.md) · [report](../../reports/WASTES_S3E13_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md).

In **S3E14**, the GM **confirms Elhandari guards actually stand beside the still-floating Ruby in the deep chamber**, on standby for Stanley's authorization. The earlier security order has now had an observed result, but the device remains unactivated. The party departs north; the Principal shows the heroes several powered [windboards](../technology/windboards.md) and joins preparations at Leaden Fingers, where Vorel arranges traps. The gray Worldbreaker carapace is visible at the lava's edge, not yet defeated. [S3E14](../../episodes/S3E14.md).

In **S3E15**, Hope and Stanley physically return to the allied armory. Hope acquires a +2 crossbow; Stanley obtains bowls for a later *Heroes' Feast*, which he actually casts at the northern camp. Elhandari troops still guard **Ruby in the core**; **no activation order is issued during S3E15**. The heroes fight the Worldbreaker and draw it toward Vorel's prepared canyon, but do not yet cure Earth or rebuild the mines. [S3E15](../../episodes/S3E15.md) · [comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E15_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md).

## Planetary restoration and aftermath — S3E18

By the **played S3E18 finale**, the Worldbreaker has been defeated separately and Elhandari personnel still protect Ruby underground. Stanley asks Nash, Vorel and Hope to decide whether to use it despite the expected end of ordinary Earthbound arcane spellcasting. They agree; Stanley **actually orders the device activated** and Amanthalodar acknowledges the order. **Its physical insertion into the core is not separately depicted**, but the GM explicitly presents the mechanism as having operated: **rain, forests, new growth and waterfalls return to Earth**, and attempts to cast ordinary spells on Earth fail. This is an observed planetary outcome, not a mere unexecuted proposal.[^solar-mines-s3e18]

A **stone memorial to Nash** is built at the Solar Mines during a four-day interval while he is believed dead; Nash's later fate occurs elsewhere, not at a witnessed visit to that memorial. A postwar council discusses handling the survivors, a possible mixed township and future work to clear the **Clench** facility. Those are **proposals or assignments**, not scenes in which the mines are completely rebuilt, every captive freed or the Clench conclusively cleared. [S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md).

## Current status

**At the latest played S3E18 cutoff**, Solar Mines is an **allied-held but battle-scarred site** with its deep Ruby position previously secured by Elhandari guards. Ruby's ordered use has visibly restored the planet; the exact moment of insertion is unshown. Former prisoners, residential-dome survivors, remaining industrial machinery, long-term government and the complete casualty list are **not fully inventoried**. The monument to Nash is completed, while settlement, reconstruction and Clench-clearing remain future work. The Earth of S3E18 has returned vegetation and lost ordinary mana-wave spellcasting; this is **not proof all hazards or political disputes have ended**. [S3E18](../../episodes/S3E18.md).

## Creator commentary and source boundaries

A **December 2021** creator Q&A describes Beau's intended connections between the Principal, the [dragon-metal heart](../technology/nash-dragon-heart.md) and the Solar Mines. He distinguishes Nash's incomplete childhood memory from the facility's hidden past; Scott mentions a fellow escapee whose fate was not yet told. Neither remarks supplies an additional played childhood scene, confirms specific newer Slow Peter upgrades or proves who originally operated the site. [December 2021 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2021-12.md).

The source material blends **Nash's recollection**, **scout estimates**, **directly played actions** and **later creator comments**. This page retains those distinctions rather than inferring an uninterrupted omniscient history or inventing a completed mass liberation.
