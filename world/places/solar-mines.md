# Solar Mines and Blood Canyons

**Browse:** [Places](../PLACES.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Type:** Important origin setting in Nash Maggard's background. **Evidence:** Character statements, retrospective accounts and later research; **not** a fully played historical side adventure. **Spoilers:** Through E118.

**Nash** describes captivity, survival and resistance connected with the **Solar Mines** and **Blood Canyons**. His descriptions develop across the campaign and include memories that seem contradictory; they should be presented as the state of Nash's own recollections and others' testimony, not a reconstructed omniscient history. His implanted **dragon-metal heart** and later dragon forms are observed, but the exact circumstances of implantation require careful source distinction. [E000](../../episodes/E000.md) · [E024](../../episodes/E024.md) · [Nash](../characters/nash-maggard.md).

The **E117** Elhandar ceremony sees Nash publicly recount the Solar Mines uprising. This is **an in-world retelling**, not a newly staged flashback. In **E118**, Stanley's father's private **Book of Adjacencies** has a globe marked at a location apparently corresponding to the Solar Mines on **Handsome Dan Dakota's map**. Dalit's theories about that correspondence, N4SH and future-Principle experiments are **unverified research claims**; no journey to the Mines occurs in E118. [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) · [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Book](../technology/book-of-adjacencies.md).

In the [September 2020 public-release Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-09.md), **~00:00:43–00:01:29**, **Scott says he abbreviated a much larger Solar Mines story “today” because he lacked his notes**. This is **retrospective creator commentary associated with the E117 retelling**, not an additional played revolt, proof of a recording day or a source for the untold details. It does not validate the E118 journal's theories. [Comparison](../../reports/BONUS_2020_09_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md), ASR-provisional, no listening.

**Open:** exact geography, chronology of captivity and revolt, historical perpetrators, Nash's lost/alternate memories and the journal's possible time connection. [Ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
