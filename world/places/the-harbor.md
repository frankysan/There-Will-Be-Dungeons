# The Harbor — Elhandar's tiefling survivor community

> **The Wastes · Arc Five · actually named in [E117 — The Fireside Stories](../../episodes/E117.md) (~02:11–02:15).** [Complete Parakeet/Qwen comparison](../../reports/E117_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). Source transcripts inspected fully; **no audio heard**, proper names/participant count provisional. **Do not conflate this place with Sigil's harbor or Tietkalla's original capital.**

## Location and founding

A **small cluster of approximately eight wooden cottages around a central well/clearing**, reached by about **ten minutes of forest walking** from the main party's lakeside cottage, **inside Elhandar's orbital forest biodome**. [E115](../../episodes/E115.md)'s **Nobieri report** identified tiefling refugees across the clearing; **E117 actually brings Hope, Nash, Stanley, Vorel and Chunks** to the residents and **physically confirms Nobieri and Emenil among them**. The GM estimates **roughly a dozen to two dozen inhabitants** and later narrates about **seventeen** participating survivors. These are **rough encounter counts**, not an official census, nor proof that all Tietkalla's missing people died.

At a public bonfire Hope **admits that bringing the Hand of the Voice into Tietkalla endangered her people** and considers releasing them from her leadership. **Nobieri asks the people to choose freely**. A man of the **Gretchel/Gretchil (?) family** supports her for improving family rights; **another man leaves with wife and son**, and the other survivors remain with her. Nobieri leads a communal **ash-and-breath rite**. A **ten-foot flaming female tiefling apparition identifying as Abegir/Abigrier (?)** appears, offers strong counsel about Hope's duty and leaves. **Its physical manifestation is played**, but it does not independently authenticate the being's godhood or the claims about afterlife.

The residents **share bereavement and pre-Night-Eaters histories**. One ranger says **his pregnant partner Zalna/Salma (?) died under collapsing masonry**; **Emenil** speaks of her faith in **Malhaya**, fear for Stanley and a hope that **unlimited water** could end scarcity. These are **attributed accounts**, not newly replayed events or achieved ecological fixes.

## The naming — actually completed

With the meeting finished, **Nobieri asks Hope to give a name to the community and its future purpose**. **Hope chooses “the Harbor”** for the home and **“Harborers”** for its inhabitants, explicitly wishing to shelter and help anyone who is lost and needs assistance, including lost people among the stars. **Nobieri publicly proclaims the name and intent**, and the residents disperse back into their work. **This is a completed naming and local founding declaration.** It is **not** an independent planet, restored government in old Tietkalla, established trade route, or operational rescue organization across multiple worlds. Hope remains queen **to those who continue to recognize her**, and one family has opted to leave.

## Status at E117 cutoff

Hope and the original protagonists are **physically reunited at Elhandar**, but **Stanley still mistrusts her and the demon Hand has not been surrendered**. **Nobieri and Emenil** remain alive at the Harbor. After the meeting, **Emenil privately confesses love to Stanley**, receives no romantic commitment, and destroys the Malhaya altar in her own cottage according to the GM's Perception adjudication. **No significant construction, new military alliance, planet-wide water project or departure from Elhandar is completed**. **Vorel later smells lizardfolk near the main cottage but the intended search is deferred**.
