## The first visit

Following the beholder's defeat beneath the **Black Mine**, a rift leads to an uncanny region and hospitable château whose extravagant meals and comfortable rooms mask serious supernatural risk. **Quizquaz** is present in the lead-up to the encounter, but **Quizquaz, the beholder and the Soulbroker are distinct actors**.

In **E023** the Soulbroker offers the protagonists a pact: **Nash, Stanley and Hope sign**, while **Vorel refuses**. The signed characters must destroy **the Principal** within the stipulated thousand-day/night period or face a soul-related penalty. The precise legal language, benefits and calendar should be checked against the performed contract or handout; hospitality waivers and a soul pact are **different documents**.

## Powers and limitations

In E024 Nash receives an intense private view of the **Solar Mines**, learns about his **dragon-metal heart** and changes into a black dragon. The Soulbroker also makes claims concerning **Fox and Scully** (eventually recovered in T'ietkalla), lost deeds, **Elhendar**, possible routes and political conditions. These claims do not replace in-world verification. **Keys to Château Weir** are given or used later, under a reported condition that entry be sufficiently far **underground**; the exact distance/mechanics remain for checking rather than assumed universal magic.

In **E048**, the freed old tiefling **Nobiery/Nobieri** steps through Nash's activated Château portal, greeted by **Baba**, to seek a hidden army. In **E054**, the elder **actually returns with hundreds of armed tieflings and crowns Hope**. The presence of those troops validates the return itself; it **does not prove** the elder's entire claimed story of a thousand soldiers and a historic bargain sacrificing twenty children.

## Interpretive boundaries

A subjective Weir **dream/vision of Hope's mother** and claims about the **afterlife, gods and fate** are not independent confirmation of the cosmology of the setting. The exact identity/name variants **Catoblepraithe/Catobelpraithe** remain disputed. The Year One Q&A adds that major **artifacts existed irrespective of whether a character took the Soulbroker's offered chrome-related path**. It is **not** a scene that retroactively awards refused powers to Vorel.
