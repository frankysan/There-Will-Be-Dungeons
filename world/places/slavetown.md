# Slavetown

**Variants:** `Slave Town` appears as a descriptive/source form; `Slavetown` is the established project display with wiki support. **Type:** Slaveholding city, trade hub and a major regional power nexus. **Year One coverage:** Mainly E010–E018, with later aftermath and reports. **Source status:** Processed SRTs and provisional dossiers, not fully audio-reviewed.

## Entry under coercion

After the collapse of the buried facility and appearance of a black dragon, armed forces serving **the Principle/Principal** capture the travelers and take them to Slavetown. The city makes its violent institutions plain through soldiers, courts, martial orders, underclass labor and the continued captivity of people taken from **Dusthill**. The four protagonists win a **trial by combat**, but freeing themselves does **not free all enslaved townspeople or the people they originally set out to rescue**.

The city contains hotels, a foundry, markets and a jewelry shop, competing patrons and bureaucracies, plus groups identified with **the Koromukishi** and other violent powers. Stanley meets **Talia**, Bruce Bane's mother, among enslaved people. **Celibus Stump**, the **Jeweler**, a magistrate and an elusive **mine deed** draw the group into political bargains that often appear mutually incompatible.

## Violence, mystery and fire

The party encounters the **Lactid Cartel** and begins investigating **Tylander Sullivan**, murders, ritual sites and a stolen ruby necklace. The cyclops **Orp** becomes involved, while apparently defeated enemies and claimed returnees complicate the story. These episodes establish visits and fights, **not a fully validated history of every faction**.

Nash takes **Pixie Blush**, and later causes a **real destructive foundry/explosives incident** while experiencing hallucinations. Fire spreads through much of Slavetown; the party leaves for the Black Mine. The possibility that an intervening **sandstorm** later saved the city was discussed but **not confirmed**. During later episodes the party learns of an **offscreen heist associated with Jolt** and Vorel receives gold, but that is not a new return visit witnessed in E037.

## How it connects outward

**Black Mine** is tied to the city's labor economy, old property claims and the alleged deed of **Catherine Speckles**. **Esophageal Pass** is a strategic route in the region. **Fate 4** is recovered here before the adventurers depart and is later stranded/abandoned again. The **Toyota Host** is mentioned as a dangerous organized force; the GM deliberately withholds whether a later anticipated wedding group survived/arrived in the Year One Q&A. Do not invent the outcome.
