# Elhandar — the forest among the stars

## Introduction

Elhandar was an inhabited sanctuary in the sky, a vast elven station where craft, magic and living nature existed side by side. From the approaching rescue ship its long technological cylinder rose beside a glass-enclosed sphere filled with forest. Within the dome lay lakes, brooks, tree dwellings and broad hunting grounds beneath a carefully ordered passage of day and night. Though magic nourished the vegetation, the trees and animals were real, not illusions. The station offered its guests the astonishing possibility of finding shelter, fresh air and growing things far above the ravaged surface of Earth.[^elhandar-first]

The travelers were welcomed by Admiral Chrysorius and guided along **Rainbow Road**, a luminous transport network reaching the heart of the complex. Elhandar's spiritual leader, known as the High Elf, presided over a society that described itself as cooperative rather than ruled by kings. Care for the wounded included assigned therapists, although the guests were not compelled to attend their sessions. The High Elf spoke of ancient ecological restoration and of the difficulty the elves believed their dead faced on Earth; these were his people's explanations of their history and faith, rather than memories shared by the newcomers.[^elhandar-society]

In the forest stood a furnished lakeside cottage, with a kitchen, bedrooms, a shed, a wharf and a firepit. It sheltered Nash and Angelashi, their four newborn children, Stanley, Vorel, Bok Bok, Chunks and Lyrielle. After their flight from a devastated homeland, the cottage offered a rare chance to rest together. A district devoted to science and magic was said to contain a permanent teleportation circle, although the visitors did not yet know the route by which to use it.[^elhandar-cottage]

## Development by arc

### Arc Five — the refuge, the mountain and the fall

#### The first days in the forest

The station's hospitality extended beyond the rescued household. Nobieri brought news that tiefling survivors from Tietkalla were nearby, that Omnom Cinderborn had been found wounded, and that Emenil was asking after Stanley. Stanley declined the High Elf's offer of shared memories, unwilling to surrender control of his mind again. Meanwhile the admiral promised to investigate the fate of Stanley's father. Vorel explored the forest and hunted; Lyrielle tended the house. For several days there was food, safety and space enough to recover.[^elhandar-first-days]

Even here, the troubles of the wider world intruded. Elhandar's weapons destroyed a pirate ship that had menaced the travelers' rescuers. Later the Principle appeared before the assembled guests, displaying formidable powers and declaring himself responsible for devastation on Earth and newly risen to divine stature. The display was real, but the truth of his claims was not established by his arrival. He left without transforming Elhandar into his kingdom or securing the allegiance he demanded.[^elhandar-principle]

#### The Harbor and the hidden study

Hope found Nash through a magical Gate and drew him briefly from a forest glade into Dr. Spider's infernal laboratory. She then returned beside him to Elhandar. Their arrival did not immediately place her before everyone in the cottage, but soon she was reunited with Stanley, Vorel and the surviving people of Tietkalla.[^elhandar-hope]

A short walk among the trees brought the companions to a clearing of modest cabins around a well. Nobieri, Emenil and other tieflings had made a home there. During a gathering of the refugees, a flaming apparition calling herself Abegir appeared at Nobieri's rite. Hope spoke to her people and gave the settlement its new name: **[the Harbor](the-harbor.md)**. Those who stayed became Harborers, bound by the hope of offering a place to others who had lost their way. Not everyone agreed with the new beginning; one family left the gathering. The forest had given the survivors a refuge, not restored the lost kingdom of Tietkalla.[^elhandar-harbor]

The uneasy peace soon brought old wounds into view. Vorel found [Omnom Cinderborn](../characters/omnom-cinderborn.md), terribly injured and embittered by the fate of Razak Val and Tietkalla. Omnom blamed Hope for his losses, but Vorel would not grant his request to be killed. An apprentice named Dilenoria sought Stanley's guidance. Around the same time an elven doctor delivered a key left to Stanley by his father. A remembered childhood poem revealed a hidden lock, and the cottage opened into an impossible third-floor study, far larger than the building's outer shape allowed. Stanley and Hope found star charts, an Earth globe, the **Book of Adjacencies** and the **Mask of Eltanfawr**. The father's writings suggested connections among Nash, the Weir and the Principle, but their remarkable theories remained his own.[^elhandar-study]

#### The hunt and the cavern beneath the mountain

A hunting party set out from the cottage with Omnom and Dilenoria, passing the Harbor, lakes and woodland paths toward the northern reaches of the dome. Lyrielle provided a map. The travelers met a berry gatherer named Fitz, talking squirrels and a fisherman. They were also warned of **Chester**, a huge beast said to have escaped long ago from an Elhandari experiment that mingled a bear with a dinosaur. Its supposed creation and earlier victims belonged to local accounts, not to anything the companions had seen.[^elhandar-hunt]

After a night beside the water, Chester appeared: enormous, antlered and partly reptilian. The company fought it, and Hope pulled the beast from a tree; its fatal fall nearly crushed her before Vorel lifted its body away. Six talking deer then approached as if to congratulate the hunters, but Stanley's magic exposed them as hostile elves in disguise. The mages attacked. Dilenoria revealed his complicity, took control of a magical sphere enclosing Hope and fled toward a mountain cave. The pursuers followed through groves of glowing fungi, where Nash discovered that eating one luminous mushroom made his open mouth shine like a lamp. The ordinary forest's enchantments had become the setting for a much more personal danger.[^elhandar-chester]

Deep in the cavern, **Diontalus** emerged as a separate spectral presence from Dilenoria's body. Hope remained trapped in the floating prison while her friends battled the archfey. Omnom died in the struggle, and Diontalus escaped through the rock after a scarred black dragon arrived to strike him. The dragon, grievously injured itself, claimed to recognize the magic in Nash's manufactured heart. Nash's chest opened and a golden-orange draconic apparition appeared, greeting the dying creature with tenderness. The apparition was witnessed, but the explanation of Nash's origins offered by the dragon was not thereby proven.[^elhandar-cavern]

Before dying, the black dragon spoke of ancient struggles against the Weir and proposed a means of protecting Earth: a **Rubistic Triangulosphere**, to be made around the dragon's own natural metal heart. The proposal depended on knowledge and craft the companions did not possess. Once the dragon died, Vorel extracted the heavy heart; the friends hid it in the cavern, while Stanley finally freed Hope from her prison. They left the mountain for the **Gatherers' Lodge**, where two ghostly elven custodians provided rooms and refreshments. One caretaker later reported that Chester had indeed been killed and speculated that the hostile mages belonged to an elven council. None of this proved that the entire station or its magical orders had been corrupted.[^elhandar-heart]

After resting at the lodge, the four returned toward the Harbor in a [noble speeder](../technology/noble-speeder.md), carrying mushrooms for Nobieri. Emenil's communion with Malhya took the companions into an extraordinary underwater encounter. Stanley and Vorel emerged relieved of the injuries Diontalus had inflicted on them. The settlement remained inhabited, and the party still hoped to warn Elhandar's leaders, recover the heart and find someone capable of building the proposed device.[^elhandar-return]

#### The audience and the siege

The four adventurers gathered Nash's family at the cottage and made their way back to the central district. Lyrielle and the household waited outside the High Elf's audience chamber. Inside, the High Elf told of earlier efforts to imprison Diontalus and summoned the archfey in spectral form. Stanley negotiated a fragile understanding: Diontalus would return to his own court, await a future meeting and might help against the Principle. The agreement came with threats against Elhandar should Stanley fail to keep his side. Diontalus's promise was not the same as proof he would honor it.[^elhandar-court]

The High Elf also named [Tungsten Wheel](../characters/tungsten-wheel.md), an engineer who might be able to construct the dragon's proposed device. An acquaintance of the missing engineer was said to be at a terraforming platform on Mars. Nash received magical coordinates for travel between that destination and the elven spire. It was a lead rather than a completed journey, and neither the engineer nor the promised device had yet been found.[^elhandar-engineer]

Then a castle and a fleet of airships attacked Elhandar. Mercenaries from Sigil's Hive poured into the High Elf's chamber under the command of the three-headed musician [Henatar Grape](../characters/henitar-grape.md). The High Elf answered with living defensive trees, powerful shields and volleys turned back upon the attackers. Diontalus fought among the intruding forces and the hostile ships. But the station was losing its position in the sky, and the High Elf ordered the survivors to flee. He sent Stanley toward his father's hidden room, believing it offered the household the best chance of surviving the fall.[^elhandar-siege]

The escape led through a failing elevator and along Rainbow Road while debris fell from above. Stanley slowed the descent of the broken lift, Vorel cut an exit through its roof, and Lyrielle raised a wall of force against the wreckage. Three speeders carried the household to [Villa Ornath](villa-ornath.md). There the family crowded into the concealed study, where seats and restraints appeared around them. The station struck Earth before anyone in the room could know whether the strange house would protect them.[^elhandar-flight]

#### Wreckage and departure

The hidden study did protect its occupants. Nash, Angelashi, their children and the companions survived inside the damaged cottage, now buried in the ruins of Elhandar. Wounded Harborers found shelter with them, reporting that elven rescuers had gathered survivors before the fall. Stanley discovered that the cottage was itself a disguised vessel, although he could not yet command it to fly. Vorel found a way through the debris into Earth's desert.[^elhandar-impact]

A second tremendous impact struck when the attacking castle fell amid the shattered station. The cottage spun through the wreckage; several tiefling refugees died, though the household and children survived. Outside, Diontalus fought Henatar and was thrown down by the mercenary's enchanted guitar. Nash took dragon form and eventually brought the musician's hovering disk crashing to the sand. Henatar was still alive when he surrendered, but Nash killed him afterward. Diontalus was restored and drew a pale, soul-like essence from the dead mercenary.[^elhandar-aftershock]

Elhandari troop ships landed among the fallen structure to recover those who remained alive. The High Elf and Admiral Chrysorius organized the search. They also sent a party into the ruined mountain to retrieve the dragon's natural heart, and the admiral later confirmed that it had been found. Four additional speeders were secured to the exterior of Villa Ornath. The surviving tieflings, Angelashi, Nash's four children, Lyrielle and Bok Bok boarded ships bound for the Mars terraforming platform. The original four companions stayed on Earth. They had escaped the fall, but the home that had sheltered them in the stars could no longer offer refuge there.[^elhandar-exodus]

Even the wreck was not left undisturbed. A vast purple rupture opened across the sky, and an enormous burrowing creature descended into the distant desert. Its passage unleashed a wave of sand that swept over the ruined landscape. Nash carried his companions above the storm in dragon form. When they returned, Villa Ornath was still standing, partly banked by windblown sand; the creature had vanished beneath the earth.[^elhandar-storm]

## Current status

Elhandar no longer exists as a functioning orbital sanctuary. Its forest dome, machinery and halls lie scattered across Earth's desert, with many of its people dead and others carried away by the surviving rescue vessels. The full number of the lost is unknown. The High Elf and Admiral Chrysorius were alive to oversee the recovery, but the lasting fate of the station and all its survivors remains uncertain.[^elhandar-final]

Ships carrying the refugees and the recovered natural dragon heart have departed toward Mars. Whether they have reached Mars safely remains unknown. Stanley, Nash, Hope and Vorel remain beside Villa Ornath on Earth. The Rubistic Triangulosphere is still unbuilt, Tungsten Wheel has not been found, and the enormous creature beneath the desert has left the world's future in question. Elhandar's work of sheltering the displaced survives, for now, in the people who escaped it.[^elhandar-status]
