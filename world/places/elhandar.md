# Elhandar — elven space station and forest biodome

**Spelling:** Elhandar is the established archive working form; E115 ASR also gives **Elhendar / Elendar / Elhanda**. **Continuity:** The Wastes, main-party Arc Five. **First researched physical arrival:** [E115](../../episodes/E115.md), ~00:30–00:39, following E111's rescue and E114's interrupted voyage. Earlier references do not constitute earlier visits. **Spoilers:** Through E120; **ASR-provisional, not audio-verified**.

## E120 — Chester slain, illusion-deer ambush, mountain fungus

At [E120](../../episodes/E120.md) the **reported bearsaurus Chester is actually seen**, standing upright as a giant antlered, partly reptilian bear. **Stanley turns into a T. rex**, the party mauls Chester and **Hope pulls it from a tree by its entrails**; the fall **kills Chester** but traps Hope alive until **Vorel lifts the carcass**. **Chester's existence and death are witnessed**, although Dilo's tale of his laboratory origins is still uncorroborated.

Afterward **six allegedly grateful talking deer prove to be six disguised hostile elves**, revealed by Stanley's ***Dispel Magic***; they attack and are **killed**, but **Dilo collaborates with them**, seizes a wand controlling **a floating sphere imprisoning Hope** and runs her to a **northern mountain cave**. The attack **does not establish that Elhandar's ordinary talking squirrels were also disguised**. The pursuers locate the cave, find glowing mushrooms, and **Nash eats a luminous sample, developing a mouth-beam of light**. Hope remains **alive captive elsewhere in the cave** at cutoff; no identified captor faction, station-wide attack, lab proof or settled fate of Diontalus. [Full comparison](../../reports/E120_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md).

## E119 — a real hunt, old biogenetics claims and unburnt forest

In [E119](../../episodes/E119.md), **Omnom and Dilenoria/Dilo** actually join the four original heroes on a **two-day hunt within the Elhandar forest**. **Lyrielle supplies a map** covering the route from **Villa Ornath (?)** past **the Harbor**, **Gatherers' Lodge**, lake/bridge crossings and **Grushen/Grousen (?) hunting grounds**; proper names on the spoken Roll20 map require source-map confirmation. **Fitz** gathers berries for the Harbor, recognizes Hope as queen and asks the party to seek unusual mushrooms for Nobieri (none are picked). Four squirrels actually speak and receive rations; an elf fisherman named **Tranquilar (?)** greets the travelers and gives Nash chewing grass. These are observed local encounters, not representative surveys of every animal or elf.

Dilo **reports** that Elhandari engineers combined a bear and dinosaur in an old genetic-diversity lab, producing a large **bearsaurus/barosaurus called Chester** that escaped centuries ago, threatens other wildlife and allegedly killed hunters. A fisherman echoes missing-hunter stories. **No laboratory evidence, living specimen or confirmed kills are shown**. The adventurers **actually camp by a lake**, catch **ten fish**, sleep in Stanley's *Tiny Hut*, and the following morning hear heavy steps in the northern forest. Vorel **throws a burning fish-head lure** into brush; **Stanley actually extinguishes the small resulting fire**. The episode cuts off **before a Chester sighting or attack**, and the station's biodome is **not destroyed by fire**. [E119 ASR report](../../reports/E119_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## E118 — Omnom found and a hidden father's study

[E118](../../episodes/E118.md) confirms **Omnom Cinderborn truly survives**, beyond Nobieri's E115 report and the E117 lizardfolk scent. Admiral Chrysorius says he is injured/struggling; **Vorel actually calls Omnom out of the forest near the cottage**, sees a **missing part of face and severely wounded limbs**, and after Omnom threatens Hope, **Stanley prevents assault with *Hold Person***. The group hears Omnom's claims about Razak Val, Hope's rocket, the Tietkalla station fire and his shameful retreat; **he asks Vorel to kill him, Vorel refuses**, and **a hunt is planned for the following morning but is not carried out here**. [Omnom profile](../characters/omnom-cinderborn.md).

**The four assigned Elhandari therapists visit the family cottage with food**; assignment to therapy remains mandatory while session attendance is optional. A woman doctor **actually hands Stanley a large key left for him by his father Dalit/Daliot Ornath (?)**, said to open an unknown door **in that same cottage**. Young **Dilenoria/Dilo (?)** proposes apprenticeship to Stanley, **not yet accepted pending a next-morning meeting**. At night **Stanley's recalled parents' poem causes the key to float into a magical invisible upstairs lock**, and he **turns it**, opening a **pocket-like third-floor study impossible in the outside geometry of this two-story house**. **Stanley and Hope enter**. The room contains **a huge Earth globe marked near the Solar Mines, star charts, the *Book of Adjacencies* and *Mask of Elton IV (?)***. **Stanley inventories both items and reads a message from his father, plus speculative notes about N4SH, time adjacency, the Weir and the Principle**. **Four-rest mask attunement and its psychic/d20 powers are not used**. [ASR comparison](../../reports/E118_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). This is real newly accessed architecture and custody, **not proof that the author's time theories are objectively correct**.

## E117 — the Harbor in Elhandar's forest

At [E117](../../episodes/E117.md), the Elhandar forest **really contains Tietkalla tiefling refugees, not just the reported survivors of E115**. After Hope and Nash return, Nash recalls Stanley and Vorel from hunting; **Stanley asks Lyrielle to prepare station shielding as a precaution**, but **no defensive barrier is activated and no demon attack occurs**. Lyrielle says the station's **magical-scientific shield and biodiversity could permit long resistance**, but these are **capability estimates**, not a proven battle or unlimited impenetrability. **Bok Bok declares he will stay at the main cottage guarding the family, while Vorel takes Chunks** on trips, and **Vorel actually carries Chunks toward the refugees**.

About **ten minutes through the trees**, the old adventurers find a **clearing of eight or so cabins surrounding a well**, with **Nobieri, Emenil and a small community of surviving Tietkalla tieflings**. Hope speaks publicly to her people, and a **flaming female tiefling apparition calling herself Abegir (?)** appears during Nobieri's ash ritual. Following shared survivor stories, **Hope physically proclaims their new community “the Harbor” and its members “Harborers,” with a mission to help people who have lost their way; Nobieri confirms the name**. **One dissenting family of three leaves**, others stay. [The Harbor profile](the-harbor.md) details the new local polity; **it is not a second planet or restored Tietkalla city**.

**Emenil and Stanley meet privately**; his inability to return her declared love leads to the **shrines/altar smashing event inferred by his Perception 29**, not a public miracle or battle. Back by the main cottage **Vorel senses lizardfolk in the woods but cannot find them**. He proposes an **evening search after supper**; **no expedition is carried out before E117 ends**. [Comparison](../../reports/E117_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md), not audio verified.

## E116 — Hope actually joins Nash in the biodome

[E116](../../episodes/E116.md) plays **Hope's first physical reunion with Nash since E101**. The copper-ring Gate first **draws Nash by his true name** from a **glade in Elhandar's living forest beside the cottage into Dr. Spider's Hell laboratory**. **Hope physically takes his arm and escorts him back to Elhandar through the same portal**, where the GM leaves **both Hope and Nash standing together in the biodome**. **Steerious Business, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider remain in Hell**; **Vorel, Stanley, Angelashi and the four babies are not shown meeting Hope at this E116 cutoff**. E115's day-five breakfast status remains the previous original-party cutoff; E116 does not advance several more days or show a full family reception. **No new teleport circle construction or sigil knowledge is required/observed for Hope's ring Gate.** [Full ASR comparison](../../reports/E116_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## Observed station and transport

The rescue ship **actually docks** at a large three-part orbital structure. The visible components include a long technological cylinder and a **glass-enclosed globe containing forest**. The visitors disembark, meet **Admiral Chrysorius/Chrysoerius (?)**, and use **Rainbow Road**, a rainbow-energy transport network. All three heroes complete the ride safely. The admiral's warning that violent movement can destabilize it is an instruction, not an accident observed here.

The science/magic district, named uncertainly **Cadulate/Cagealat/Cadgilet (?)**, is said to contain **an existing permanent Teleportation Circle**, confirmed by Lyrielle following the GM's clarification (~02:40–02:42). **No sigil sequence is learned and no teleportation is performed**. Discussion of building another circle over a year does not create one.

## A real forest and a shared cottage

The biodome is humid, wooded and animal-populated, with apparent sunlight, an ordered day/night cycle, lakes, a brook, elven tree dwellings and expansive terrain. **Stanley examines real living vegetation** with Nature and Detect Magic; growth/life magic runs through it, but the GM says it is **not an illusion or magical summoning**. Vorel explores and hunts over the completed four recovery days. Animals actually described among his catch include deer, a ferret, ducks and an oversized mother raccoon he wants preserved; other wildlife described as present is not automatically encountered or killed.

The heroes are given **a two-story lakeside cottage** furnished with rooms, a common kitchen/dining area, a shed, wharf and firepit. **Angelashi and four babies, Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Bok Bok, Chunks and Lyrielle** are accommodated there; Vorel sleeps in the old firepit ash rather than a bed. Lyrielle cooks, mends clothing and provides guidance. **Four days pass**, and the E115 cutoff is **breakfast on day five**. No months-long settlement is completed.

## Society, testimony and unresolved questions

The **High Elf**, an elderly spiritual leader in a water-supported chair, and the admiral describe a cooperative society without kings. **A therapist must be assigned, but attending sessions is voluntary**; **Elhandar first** is another stated principle. Stanley resists a memory-sharing trance and the High Elf **withdraws**. No successful memory transfer or compulsory attended therapy is shown.

Their accounts of pre-Scorching history, ecological restoration and **Earth's corrupted soil preventing elven souls from passing on** are **NPC testimony**, not witnessed historical events or tested universal mechanics. The admiral offers to search for Stanley's deceased father; **no body or spirit is recovered**. Nobieri reports surviving tieflings **across the clearing**, **Omnom recovering aboard**, and **Emenil asking after Stanley**; those people are not visited in E115.

The station **destroys the retreating pirate vessel** after the rescue ship requests aid. Later **the Principle physically portals into the public welcome**, claims demigodhood and responsibility for Principal City's explosion, demands support, then departs. His size change, telekinesis and portals are observed; **his causal and divine claims remain unverified**. No new attack on the cottage or completed political alliance follows in this episode.
