# Elhandar — elven space station and forest biodome

**Spelling:** Elhandar is the established archive working form; E115 ASR also gives **Elhendar / Elendar / Elhanda**. **Continuity:** The Wastes, main-party Arc Five. **First researched physical arrival:** [E115](../../episodes/E115.md), ~00:30–00:39, following E111's rescue and E114's interrupted voyage. Earlier references do not constitute earlier visits. **Spoilers:** Through E116; **ASR-provisional, not audio-verified**.

## E116 — Hope actually joins Nash in the biodome

[E116](../../episodes/E116.md) plays **Hope's first physical reunion with Nash since E101**. The copper-ring Gate first **draws Nash by his true name** from a **glade in Elhandar's living forest beside the cottage into Dr. Spider's Hell laboratory**. **Hope physically takes his arm and escorts him back to Elhandar through the same portal**, where the GM leaves **both Hope and Nash standing together in the biodome**. **Steerious Business, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider remain in Hell**; **Vorel, Stanley, Angelashi and the four babies are not shown meeting Hope at this E116 cutoff**. E115's day-five breakfast status remains the previous original-party cutoff; E116 does not advance several more days or show a full family reception. **No new teleport circle construction or sigil knowledge is required/observed for Hope's ring Gate.** [Full ASR comparison](../../reports/E116_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## Observed station and transport

The rescue ship **actually docks** at a large three-part orbital structure. The visible components include a long technological cylinder and a **glass-enclosed globe containing forest**. The visitors disembark, meet **Admiral Chrysorius/Chrysoerius (?)**, and use **Rainbow Road**, a rainbow-energy transport network. All three heroes complete the ride safely. The admiral's warning that violent movement can destabilize it is an instruction, not an accident observed here.

The science/magic district, named uncertainly **Cadulate/Cagealat/Cadgilet (?)**, is said to contain **an existing permanent Teleportation Circle**, confirmed by Lyrielle following the GM's clarification (~02:40–02:42). **No sigil sequence is learned and no teleportation is performed**. Discussion of building another circle over a year does not create one.

## A real forest and a shared cottage

The biodome is humid, wooded and animal-populated, with apparent sunlight, an ordered day/night cycle, lakes, a brook, elven tree dwellings and expansive terrain. **Stanley examines real living vegetation** with Nature and Detect Magic; growth/life magic runs through it, but the GM says it is **not an illusion or magical summoning**. Vorel explores and hunts over the completed four recovery days. Animals actually described among his catch include deer, a ferret, ducks and an oversized mother raccoon he wants preserved; other wildlife described as present is not automatically encountered or killed.

The heroes are given **a two-story lakeside cottage** furnished with rooms, a common kitchen/dining area, a shed, wharf and firepit. **Angelashi and four babies, Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Bok Bok, Chunks and Lyrielle** are accommodated there; Vorel sleeps in the old firepit ash rather than a bed. Lyrielle cooks, mends clothing and provides guidance. **Four days pass**, and the E115 cutoff is **breakfast on day five**. No months-long settlement is completed.

## Society, testimony and unresolved questions

The **High Elf**, an elderly spiritual leader in a water-supported chair, and the admiral describe a cooperative society without kings. **A therapist must be assigned, but attending sessions is voluntary**; **Elhandar first** is another stated principle. Stanley resists a memory-sharing trance and the High Elf **withdraws**. No successful memory transfer or compulsory attended therapy is shown.

Their accounts of pre-Scorching history, ecological restoration and **Earth's corrupted soil preventing elven souls from passing on** are **NPC testimony**, not witnessed historical events or tested universal mechanics. The admiral offers to search for Stanley's deceased father; **no body or spirit is recovered**. Nobieri reports surviving tieflings **across the clearing**, **Omnom recovering aboard**, and **Emenil asking after Stanley**; those people are not visited in E115.

The station **destroys the retreating pirate vessel** after the rescue ship requests aid. Later **the Principle physically portals into the public welcome**, claims demigodhood and responsibility for Principal City's explosion, demands support, then departs. His size change, telekinesis and portals are observed; **his causal and divine claims remain unverified**. No new attack on the cottage or completed political alliance follows in this episode.
