# Leaden Fingers and the Stormlands

Beyond the fractured lava fields north of ruined [Principal City](../characters/the-principal.md), the **Leaden Fingers** rise in branching mountain ridges and narrow, rocky passes. Rivers of lava run between the heights. Farther on, the **Stormlands** are wrapped in unnaturally persistent rain and lightning, their interior hidden even from an aircraft approaching the edge. The canyons offered a possible route for drawing the Worldbreaker away from the open lava field, but the storm itself was never proved a safe refuge.[^survey]

## The mountain approaches

Vorel first surveyed the ridges aboard [Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md), staying outside the storm's dangerous center. The natural chokepoints suggested a way to slow a creature too large and fast to confront on ordinary ground. He then traveled to the [Solar Mines](solar-mines.md), recruited roughly seventy-five lizardfolk tactical fighters and secured explosives and goblin volunteers from the living King Bokbok. Together they prepared three possible rockfall sites, with a three-button remote entrusted to Vorel. None was detonated during the initial preparations.[^survey]

## The prepared canyon

Before the Worldbreaker reached the mountains, Vorel and his allies concentrated their defense into **one principal canyon**, rather than staging three separate encounters. [Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md) delivered gunpowder and TNT to the high ridges, while goblins concealed themselves and lizardfolk prepared to descend with grappling gear once falling stone checked the monster's advance. Vorel kept the detonator attached to his shield. A nearby map label, provisionally rendered *Corvutican*, has not been confirmed from the original map.[^preparation]

The four riders eventually led the immense, burrowing Worldbreaker toward that canyon. Vorel and Bokbok called the waiting troops into position, but the encounter stopped just before the trap was sprung. The charges, the rockfall and the lizardfolk assault belong to the **subsequent battle**, not to this earlier approach.[^preparation]

## Battle in the pass

When the Worldbreaker entered the narrowest stretch, Vorel **pressed the detonator**. The explosives brought sections of the canyon walls down around the creature's lower body and briefly checked its burrowing. The fire itself was judged ineffective, but the fallen rock made a real obstruction. Many goblins fled in terror; Bokbok and some others remained, while lizardfolk descended onto the monster's armored body and Vorel joined the attack with [Chrome's Bane](../technology/chromes-bane.md).[^battle]

The victory was costly. The creature fought free of the rockfall, and a later burst of redirected force destroyed the lizardfolk still attached to it and goblins close to its body. That does **not** establish the fate of every one of the roughly seventy-five original recruits. Vorel survived, as did Bokbok, who fled the battlefield. The Worldbreaker was subsequently transformed and banished from Earth during the continuing confrontation; the rockfall alone neither killed nor banished it.[^battle][^aftermath]

## Current status

The ambush left a collapsed, battle-scarred canyon in the Leaden Fingers. The later healing of Earth's devastated landscape is witnessed, but no return survey establishes the canyon's exact new terrain or whether the Stormlands' continuous lightning persisted after that change. The inner storm was not explored during the reconnaissance or battle, and the map's provisional place names remain unresolved.[^aftermath]
