# Razak Val — lizardfolk clans, Great Fang and scaling

**Working name:** `Razak Val` (processed ASR also `Razak Fal`, `Razakvale`, `Razokval`). **Spelling not independently confirmed** by audio or an original map. First physically visited by the party in corrected [E059](../../episodes/E059.md), developed in [E060](../../episodes/E060.md). **Spoilers through E060**, based on **unverified processed Parakeet SRTs**.

## Landscape and settlement

A **mountain-walled sunken valley** in the lizardfolk lands west of the group's Tietkalla journey; entrances into rock dwellings, tents, hunters' stalls, bone/leatherwork and guards occupy it. Its chief public components are the **Razak Duar arena** (provisional spelling), **Cave Fengar** for influential clan business and feasts, and **Tailway**, an open-air shrine with pillars and dragon-priest officiants. The characters **ride velociraptor-like mounts** alongside **Fate 4** to reach this region and use a **Tiny Hut** to wait out a huge bloodsucking desert swarm on the way. [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [E060](../../episodes/E060.md).

## Political organization

The highest title is **Great Fang**, currently held by **Orp the cyclops** at E060. Orp once met the party in Slavetown but has been adopted into the lizardfolk **Fengar** ruling clan after defeating its previous Great Fang in a duel. The club of local clans—**478 claimed in dialogue**—follows **scaling**, a single-combat succession rite; a formal challenge is intended to settle legitimacy. **Mirion/Mirian Fengar**, a small-bodied but politically skilled adviser called **“the Morsel,”** says he is the prior Great Fang's son and has Orp's ear. This is demonstrably an organized polity, not just a mob respecting the strongest punch. [E060](../../episodes/E060.md).

**Orp's rule is lethal and predatory:** members bring **living captives** for him to eat, and **he murders one or more captives before the party**. **Vorel challenges him in E060.** Orp **kills Diptak with a single blow**, and the court removes his body. During the evening feast, **Orp accepts a duel for the next sunrise**. **Orp is alive and remains Great Fang by the E060 cutoff; the duel and captivity system are not yet resolved**. [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [E060](../../episodes/E060.md).

## Clans, allies and priesthood

**Diptak** is Vorel's earlier oath-bound lizardfolk ally from the Golden Lands; he brings Vorel here but is **killed E060**. **Mirion's brother Bramie/Braimie Fengar**, famed as a **Tyrant Slayer**, and sister/partner **Zercil/Zircal** are powerful hunters. **General Toxar** and **Skrot/Scrot** from another warrior clan (ASR `Mekatek`) voice distrust of powerful hierarchies while maintaining martial traditions. These names, kinship nuances and office titles await primary handout verification.

The **Council of Twelve** is the religious framework said to bless the city. The first Great Fang **Razak the Bronze**, **Fuxi/Fushi** the fire-father, **Rezar** and other named dragons are cited in tradition, with **one priestly emissary per god**. **Typalla** is said to speak for **Fuxi/Fushi**. The **Tailway** is an observable shrine, but the truth of each vision, creation myth and divine status is **NPC testimony**, not verified cosmology. [E060](../../episodes/E060.md).

## Food, monsters and local speech

Feasts serve **bloodberry drink**, **live snakes** that guests subdue and eat, and mostly raw **“chicken lord”** apparently hunted from the nearby **Demon Vold**. Hunting dangerous monsters there carries social prestige; **the Demon Vold itself has not been entered** by the party through E060. Vorel, having expected uncomplicated strength politics, expresses disillusionment at the place's patronage and plans to seek **Typalla**, but a completed meeting and prophetic judgment should not be assumed from E060 alone.
