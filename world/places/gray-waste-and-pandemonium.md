# Gray Waste, River Styx and Pandemonium — Hope's route

Seeking the **Eye of the Voice**, **Hope Akmenos** leaves [Sigil](sigil.md) with **Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider**. Their [E112](../../episodes/E112.md)–[E113](../../episodes/E113.md) journey takes them through the color-draining **Gray Waste**, across the **River Styx** and into the wind-scoured halls of **Pandemonium**. They actually meet the **Council of Clowns**, receive a possible destination and return to Sigil. They **do not recover the Eye** or reach the distant prison in these episodes. Nash, Stanley and Vorel are **not** part of this expedition.

## Gray Waste, from House of Delights to Pointless

In **E112**, the four eat portions of a carrot and grasp whips in a closet at Sigil's **House of Delights**. The ritual **actually transports them** to a bleak gray landscape. **Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider** lose color and become melancholy; after longer travel in **E113**, **Hope also turns gray**, while Steerious retains his color. Their disorientation is shown, rather than proof that every visitor to this plane must respond alike.

They meet an apathetic orc who directs them toward **Pointless**. Steerious **cuts off his head**, which remains **blinking and responsive** even after being shot by an arrow; the companions call him **Headsy/Hedzy (?)**. They carry his head and drag the body until, after hours of walking, they **reach Pointless**, a small settlement of roughly **ten huts** beside the black river. Headsy is later **left alive/responsive at its wharf**, not killed or taken into Pandemonium.

## The Styx ferryman and passage

The cloaked ferryman **Hesterbrook**, whose face has blue skeletal features, requires each passenger to give up **something personally valued**. Four payments are actually made:

| Traveler | Fare accepted by Hesterbrook |
| --- | --- |
| **Hope** | Her pistol **Fox**, **not** her distinct weapon *Scully* |
| **Steerious** | His large **greatsword** |
| **Blood Skunk** | His **left little toe**, voluntarily cut off; Dr. Spider heals the wound, but regrowth is not shown |
| **Dr. Spider** | His treasured **lab coat** |

Headsy cannot pay and is refused passage. Hesterbrook **claims** he was once a prince who chose ferrying after death and invokes an **ancient protection pact**. Neither his former rank nor the pact's universal terms are independently verified by the travelers.

During their **actual boat crossing**, a giant serpent called **Velithon/Velothan/Velathon (?)** rises from the Styx and recognizes something of its absent master in Hope's **Hand of the Voice**. The hooded Voice appears **to Hope alone**, not visibly to the serpent. Hope flatters the creature into allowing the ferry onward and **promises a future visit** beneath the water. No such visit or enduring alliance takes place here. The boat **does reach Pandemonium**, where the Gray Waste's visible color and despair effects **lift**.

## Pandemonium, Council and the phlogiston lead

The party lands among enormous **wind-carved rock formations** and follows the imp **Voxil/Varkso (?)** through a hidden stone doorway and a **fast mine-cart passage**. **Dario the Impresario** first offers an absurdly distant appointment, then **admits them immediately**. In a strange auditorium, **Bobo** and the **Council of Clowns** hear Hope's petition, mock her seriousness and become amused by her anger. Bobo **smells her altered hand**, identifies it with the Voice and **grants a request for information**, not possession of the Eye.

The Council **claims** the Eye lies in **Pentel Weiss**—also transcribed *Pentelvice/Pentalvise*—an ancient extraplanar prison in **phlogiston space**, near the **Corinthian Nebula**. Those are **the Council's directions and history**, not coordinates or a prison physically seen during E113. The clowns **actually hand Hope** a wind-up **jack-in-the-box compass** and say it points toward the prison **for her alone**. She accepts it; its accuracy and operation on the proposed journey **are not tested in E113**. The *Eye of the Voice* is distinct from [Vorel's Eye of Groxl](../technology/eye-of-groxl.md). See the [later Pentel Weiss history](pentel-weiss.md) for what is **actually visited in Season Two**, rather than backdating that discovery to this council.

Hope then **uses her copper Gate ring** to open a passage back to **Tobacho Fleur's tavern in Sigil**. **All four step through** before E113 ends. The **Gray Waste–Styx–Pandemonium excursion is complete**; the greater Eye search is not. Hope's **signed E112 agreement** to help Tobacho overthrow the **Lady of Pain** likewise remains **unfulfilled**.
