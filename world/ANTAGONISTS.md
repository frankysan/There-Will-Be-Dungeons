# Antagonists and contested power brokers

Rivals, rulers and supernatural claimants compete for power across The Wastes. Their place here reflects their role in the story, while their motives and claims remain open to judgment.

## Dangerous or ambiguous intermediaries

In Sigil and the Hive, **[Tobacho Fleur](characters/tobacho-fleur.md)** receives the separated Fist gauntlet and strikes a bargain with Hope. **[The Voice](characters/the-voice.md)** demands another Eye; its identity and agenda, and either figure's full power, remain unproven.[^intermediary-tobacho-voice] [E106](../episodes/E106.md) · [E112](../episodes/E112.md).

**Quizquaz** bargains and offers contradictory accounts across Dusthill, the Black Mine and CHIDS. He is not established to be the Soulbroker or an all-knowing witness.[^intermediary-quizquaz] See the [contested power broker](characters/quizquaz.md).

**Jolt Elegos** dies in E050, **Suck Master Din** is executed in E054, and **Blunt Goliver** dies in E038. The Council of Clowns, Tobacho Fleur, Lady of Pain, hooded Voice, the Weir and CHIDS hive have competing or uncertain roles.[^intermediary-arc-two-powers] [Characters](CHARACTERS.md) · [T'ietkalla](places/tietkalla.md) · [Factions and powers](FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md) · [Sigil](places/sigil.md) · [World](WORLD.md) · [Hope](characters/hope-akmenos.md).

The **Council of Clowns** is a distinct named group; do not merge them on the basis of the word *Council*.

[^antagonist-worldbreaker]: The Soulbroker visibly opens the E128 breach and the burrower enters Earth; the villain's larger Weir origin story remains attributed testimony. S3E12 severs the Weir–Earth link but leaves the creature there. In S3E15 it fully emerges as the campaign's Tarrasque; S3E16 captures the actual canyon ambush, the Principal's betrayal and **successful *Banishment* still awaiting its concentration minute**, which Nash completes in S3E17. The GM then **adjudicates permanent removal from Earth**, not on-screen killing or a surveyed destination plane. Two S3E15 effective *Fireballs* versus S3E16 immunity and the six/eight remaining-round discrepancy remain open source issues. Only **S3E18 Ruby/Triangulosphere actually restores rain, forests and waterfalls and suppresses Earthbound mana-wave magic**, so “Earth not yet healed” is true at S3E17's cutoff. No Tarrasque return is shown, and the finale's warning from an unidentified second Nash cannot be identified as the Tarrasque.
