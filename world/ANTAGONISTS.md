# Antagonists and contested power brokers

Rivals, rulers and supernatural claimants compete for power across The Wastes. Their place here reflects their role in the story, while their motives and claims remain open to judgment.

## Dangerous or ambiguous intermediaries

In Sigil and the Hive, **[Tobacho Fleur](characters/tobacho-fleur.md)** receives the separated Fist gauntlet and strikes a bargain with Hope. **[The hooded Voice](characters/the-voice.md)** demands another Eye; its identity and agenda, and either figure's full power, remain unproven.[^intermediary-tobacho-voice] [E106](../episodes/E106.md) · [E112](../episodes/E112.md).

**Quizquaz** bargains and offers contradictory accounts across Dusthill, the Black Mine and CHIDS. He is not established to be the Soulbroker or an all-knowing witness.[^intermediary-quizquaz] See the [contested power broker](characters/quizquaz.md).

**Jolt Elegos** dies in E050, **Suck Master Din** is executed in E054, and **Blunt Goliver** dies in E038. The Council of Clowns, Tobacho Fleur, Lady of Pain, hooded Voice, the Weir and CHIDS hive have competing or uncertain roles.[^intermediary-arc-two-powers] [Characters](CHARACTERS.md) · [T'ietkalla](places/tietkalla.md) · [Factions and powers](FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md) · [Sigil](places/sigil.md) · [World](WORLD.md) · [Hope](characters/hope-akmenos.md).
