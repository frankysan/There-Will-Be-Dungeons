# Campaign chronology — five Wastes arcs (Arc Four/Arc Five transition inside E101)

**Horizon:** Corrected **E000–E116** main-party (E107–E109 are a separate historical prequel) *The Wastes* storyline, plus the separately framed **E107–E109 Dunebreaker prequel** (set **long before E001**, despite its later release), and prior **E013.5 supplemental**, **E043 holiday one-shot** and bonus exceptions. **Spoilers:** Main Wastes through E116, separate historically placed **E107–E109** prequel, plus separately labeled 2019 and 2020 retrospective bonus discussions. **Research status:** The events below are consolidated from **unverified processed Parakeet episode dossiers**, not independent listening; times within individual episodes remain in their [source-first dossiers](../episodes/E000.md). The full **pre-Phase-C chronology**, including detailed recorded cue windows and historic uncertainties, is [preserved here](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/WORLD_CHRONOLOGY.md).

**Reading key:** Each row covers a **played episode or directly observed outcome** in narrative order. A **reported death, genealogy, prophecy, pact history or offscreen action** remains an attributed report unless independently played. The **episode's own cutoff** matters: E048's rescue is real even though Tedna dies E050; E048's report about Angelashi is not proof she died before her E054 rescue. For a connected story, use [Arc One](../narrative/ARC_ONE.md) and [Arc Two](../narrative/ARC_TWO.md) narratives; for subject facts use [world history](WORLD.md) and [character](CHARACTERS.md) / [place](PLACES.md) profiles.
