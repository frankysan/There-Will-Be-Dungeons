## Ancient setting chronology (prologue, not date-verified)

1. An earlier Earth supports flourishing human civilization. **E000 00:01:26–00:01:51.**
2. Beings called dragons arrive through gates and attack. **E000 00:01:51–00:02:09.**
3. Humanity fights back with battle machines and dark bargains; dragon engines are created. **E000 00:02:09–00:03:07.**
4. Prolonged war devastates the planet; dragons ultimately vanish and dragon engines survive. **E000 00:03:07–00:03:49.** Exact timescale pending verification.
