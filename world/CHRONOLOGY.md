# Campaign chronology — the complete Wastes story (eleven arcs; historic six-arc edition preserved)

**Horizon:** Main-party *The Wastes* from corrected **E000–E128** (Season One ending with new Arc 09) through played **Wastes S2E01–S2E12 and S3E01–S3E18** (new Arcs 10–11). These later sessions appear together in the preserved historic first-edition Arc Six. **E127** and **E043** are separate holiday one-offs; E107–E109 (*Dunebreaker*) is an older in-world prequel despite its publication placement. E013.5 supplemental scenes and retrospective bonus discussions are labeled separately. **Spoilers:** Main campaign through **Wastes S3E18**. **Research status:** The events below are consolidated from **unverified processed Parakeet episode dossiers**, not independent listening; times within individual episodes remain in their [source-first dossiers](../episodes/E000.md). The full **pre-Phase-C chronology**, including detailed recorded cue windows and historic uncertainties, is [preserved here](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/WORLD_CHRONOLOGY.md).

**Reading key:** This is the one detailed, episode-ordered *time-of-knowledge* timeline, intentionally more granular than the subject indexes. Each row covers a **played episode or directly observed outcome** in narrative order. A **reported death, genealogy, prophecy, pact history or offscreen action** remains an attributed report unless independently played. The **episode's own cutoff** matters: E048's rescue is real even though Tedna dies E050; E048's report about Angelashi is not proof she died before her E054 rescue. For the connected story, begin with [Arc 01 — Beginnings in Dusthill](../narrative/ARC_01.md) and follow the [eleven-arc reading guide](../narrative/README.md); the [historic six-arc edition](../narrative/README.md#historic-first-edition--six-arcs) retains its original paths. For subject facts use [world history](WORLD.md) and [character](CHARACTERS.md) / [place](PLACES.md) profiles.

**How to read this page:** The three latest played episodes are pinned first as a **reverse-order finale snapshot** (S3E18, S3E17, S3E16), **not** the beginning of the campaign. For the story in order, start with [the pre-campaign accounts](#before-the-main-campaign--accounts-not-dates) or [Arc One](#arc-one--becoming-the-heroes-of-the-wastes-e000e026), continue through [Season Two and S3E15](#season-two--return-to-the-wastes), then return to [S3E16](#prior-played-event--s3e16-arc-11-still-open), [S3E17](#prior-played-event--s3e17-arc-11-still-open-at-that-cutoff) and [S3E18](#final-played-episode--s3e18-the-wastes-and-arc-11-complete). ## Final played episode — S3E18: The Wastes and Arc 11 complete

**Wastes S3E18 — Chrome, Chrome on The Range:** [Episode](../episodes/S3E18.md) · [comparison](../reports/WASTES_S3E18_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md). The Rubistic Triangulosphere is authorized and Earth physically becomes green again under translucent dragonflight. Earth mana-wave spellcasting ends, with offworld spellcasting remaining. Monsoon Cabbage ends his long life in daylight and Hope retains his helmet. Nash visits Moon facility, Council of Faces forcibly turns him into a chrome successor to the Principal, and party demolishes facility; Nash expires and Stanley retains a vial of chrome. Four days later *Fate Six* is restored, and on Planet Nash Stanley uses ninth-level *True Resurrection* via expanded *Wish* to return Nash to life in his original body. Nash retires with Angelashi and their four children; Vorel travels to rebuild Razakval with a new “Friend” title, Hope inherits mask/Fate Six and rebuilds T'ietkalla, Stanley returns to Feywild. A final apparent second Nash issues unexplained warning. **GM explicitly ends campaign. S3E019 is an out-of-game cast Q&A, not another played session.** Original audio not heard.
