# The Wastes — world systems and setting overview

**Spoilers:** Main campaign through researched **E120**, plus separately labeled cast Q&As and historical Dunebreaker E107–E109 prequel. **Evidence:** Reference synthesis from processed ASR, **not independent audio verification**. This page describes *how the world works and what its inhabitants claim*, rather than duplicating every plot beat. Browse [places](PLACES.md), [characters](CHARACTERS.md), [artifacts](ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md), [factions](FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md) and [historical events](HISTORICAL_EVENTS.md) for focused treatments.

## A surviving far-future Earth

The GM's [E000 prologue](../episodes/E000.md) presents a **drought-stricken future Earth** after dragons entered through gates and immense war devastated earlier civilization. People survive through settlements, scavenging, old machinery, magic, and contested political bargains. “The Wastes” is a lived human geography, not proof the protagonists have surveyed the entire globe. Ancient dates, gate-builders and the precise nature/duration of the **Scorching** remain uncertain. [Ancient catastrophe](HISTORY_ANCIENT_CATASTROPHE.md).

The early campaign's mechanical problem is **water**: Dusthill relies on underground pumps and a dragon-engine complex and **actually tests public water flow in E006**. Tietkalla's old **Gem of Tides** is a **different device** associated with oil/water conversion, physically retrieved E050 but not independently proven to restore an entire municipal system. [Dusthill](places/dusthill.md) · [Gem of Tides](technology/gem-of-tides.md).

The **Solar Mines/Blood Canyons** figure in Nash's remembered history and later alternate-time stories, but a character's autobiographical account is not an independently played flashback. The moving **Dunebreaker** demonstrates an extraordinary glass-trailing landship in a **same-world historical prequel** set long before the original company leaves Dusthill. [Solar Mines](places/solar-mines.md) · [Dunebreaker](places/dunebreaker.md).

## Society, resources and political authority

The setting's city-states are starkly unequal. **Slavetown**, called **Pristine** in an E013 GM background briefing, uses forced labor and competing commercial/political networks including Oilbeard interests and agents of the **Principle**. The protagonists gain their own freedom there, but this is not a blanket abolition of slavery. **The Black Mine** combines exploitative work and older supernatural dangers. [Slavetown](places/slavetown.md) · [Black Mine](places/black-mine.md) · [E013](../episodes/E013.md).

**Tietkalla/Trashburg** has rival gangs, fortified rulers, pixie exploitation and remnants of an older tiefling kingdom. **Hope is actually crowned E054**, after Nobiery returns with soldiers; three months of in-story rebuilding and contested anti-servitude reforms follow. The **Principle proposes** trading links and rail infrastructure; real prisoners return E059, but promises are not already accomplished public works. Later the city suffers another devastating demonic attack. The refugee **Harbor** is named in an **Elhandar** biodome E117; it is not an accomplished rebuilding of the original city. [Tietkalla history](HISTORY_TIETKALLA.md) · [The Harbor](places/the-harbor.md).

At **Razak Val**, Vorel challenges the cyclops Orp, becomes **Great Fang** and confronts a violent outbreak that he does not fully solve. Omnom survives to accuse Hope of grave responsibility for losses; **his E118 accusations and Hope's rebuttals remain competing perspectives**, while his injuries and Stanley's restraint of his attack are played. [Razak Val](places/razak-val.md) · [Omnom](characters/omnom-cinderborn.md) · [E118](../episodes/E118.md).

The setting also contains other possible routes of life: Dusthill's mutual aid, Elhandari intervention, the White Tower's good-aligned aspirations and later Harbor refuge. In an April 2020 GM Q&A, Beau describes friendly Sigil residents as a deliberate contrast with the harsh social atmosphere of the Wastes. **That is an authorial explanation, not direct character knowledge of every Sigil resident**. [April Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2020-04.md) · [Sigil](places/sigil.md).

## Technology, organisms and magic

**Dragon engines**, old robotics, weapons, digital interfaces, unusual surgery, body prostheses and magic coexist. The **Leopold facility** and **CHIDS** are distinct ancient laboratories; the former's E008–E009 collapse is not the later CHIDS expedition. **Fate 4**, Hope's modified road vehicle, is actually lost to a dinosaur attack E066, not shown subsequently repaired. **Nash's implanted dragon-metal heart**, transformations and Hope's prosthetic limbs are physically consequential, not cosmetic flavor. [Leopold](places/leopold-facility.md) · [CHIDS](places/chids.md) · [Hope](characters/hope-akmenos.md) · [Nash](characters/nash-maggard.md).

CHIDS contains **witnessed human/creature experiments, lizardfolk bodies, neural-conditioning interfaces, living organic walls and a physically reached Traversal Drive**. These establish that *particular* experiments happened, but not the origins of every present-day species. A hive claims to preserve human minds; a spectral dragon, simulated corporate employee and Quizquaz tell different stories about planetary transport, ancient research and Void energy. Observed machinery **does not validate all their explanations**. [CHIDS crisis](HISTORY_CHIDS_DRIVE.md) · [Traversal Drive](technology/traversal-drive.md). **Separately**, Dilo in E119 **claims** Elhandari scientists bred an escaped bear/dinosaur hybrid called Chester. **E120 actually depicts the gigantic antlered specimen and its death in combat**, though its alleged laboratory origin and earlier history remain uncorroborated. Dilo **subsequently betrays the party** during a six-elf disguise ambush, and his supernatural identity remains unproved. [Elhandar](places/elhandar.md) · [E119](../episodes/E119.md)–[E120](../episodes/E120.md).

The [paired red/green portal device](technology/portal-device.md) recovered E075 is **portable**, while the **Traversal Drive** is an enormous installation physically reached E079 and activated in the crisis E081. A separate [Eye of Groxl](technology/eye-of-groxl.md) is **one owner-confirmed artifact** with changing physical/active states; it is neither an alleged extra entity “Grojil” nor the sought **Eye of the Voice**. [Artifacts](ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md).

The GM also describes **sand-fishing** for desert animals in a June 2019 Q&A as background design. That description is not proof the party met a particular prepared podcaster/fisherman, just as an unclaimed staff in Leopold should not be inserted into an invented inventory. [June Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md).

## Pacts, religions, planes and uncertain cosmology

At [Château Weir](places/chateau-weir.md), **Hope, Nash and Stanley sign the Soulbroker's compact E023** concerning the Principle; **Vorel refuses**. The terms, ultimate enforcement and nature of the Weir are distinct from the observable signatures. The **Principle/Vladimir** is directly encountered and performs extraordinary acts, but claims about his own age, species, human-processing research and demigodhood remain partly attributed. [Soulbroker](characters/soulbroker.md) · [Principle](characters/the-principle.md).

**The Weir**, the **Night Pasture**, the **Feywild**, **Sigil**, the **Gray Waste**, **Pandemonium**, **Hell** and several named gods are invoked or encountered in different ways. Nobiery, Kefeli, Monsoon, the hooded Voice, the Elhandari and the Principle have differing interests and accounts. **A vision, a cult rite or a being calling itself a deity is not by itself objective proof of metaphysics**. The E117 Harbor fire-figure's statements and E118 journal's claims of adjacency should likewise be attributed. [Factions and powers](FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md) · [Nobieri](characters/nobieri.md) · [Voice](characters/the-voice.md).

In the **March 2020 Q&A**, Beau distinguishes the **originally created Weir** and its inhabitants from his use of **published D&D/Planescape settings such as Sigil**. Later mentions of the Far Realm are allusions, **not a literal import of the whole published cosmology as established Wastes history**. The August Q&A discusses his unplanned need to rethink the aftermath of the party's four-person E081 sacrifice; alternate plot ideas are not canon events. [March Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2020-03.md) · [August Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2020-08.md).

The **E081** ending really declares the four dead after the beam sacrifice; **E082** reveals them again, alive in altered circumstances. **E101** separates Hope and the other three; **E116–E117** show their reunions at Elhandar. **E118** brings the [Book of Adjacencies](technology/book-of-adjacencies.md), *Mask of Elton IV (?)* and Stanley's father's speculative time theories into play, **not a demonstrated solution to cosmic history**. Those changing facts belong to [chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md) and individual profiles, rather than further world-essay appendices.

## Evidence rules and preservation

Use a single subject page for biography, ownership, local politics, mechanisms and open questions. Record **direct observations**, **GM exposition**, **retrospective authorial notes**, **NPC testimony**, **private visions**, **unverified ASR**, **owner-confirmed spellings** and **unplayed alternatives** as distinct kinds of evidence. A later revelation should not erase the earlier cutoff; the [consistency ledger](CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md) and [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md) track unresolved details.
