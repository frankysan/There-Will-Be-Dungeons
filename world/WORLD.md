# World setting and historical claims

## Present-day world

- **Earth, long after human civilization as we know it:** The setting is an Earth covered in wasteland; the GM describes no oceans, no winter and pervasive dust, with scarce resources and pockets of surviving civilization. **Source:** E001 00:04:17–00:05:12 (GM exposition).
- **Dust Hill as a surviving settlement:** One of the remaining centers of cooperative habitation, built into a rocky, defensible site. **Source:** E001 00:05:12–00:08:13 (GM exposition).
- **Scavenged technology and magic coexist:** Residents repair machines and use old mechanical technology. Magic is present, discussed in the introduction in connection with engines made from dragon remains. **Source:** E000 00:02:42–00:03:25 (GM prologue); E001 00:07:14–00:07:30 (GM exposition).

## Ancient history — GM's dramatic prologue

- Long ages ago, humanity inhabited a more flourishing Earth and reached for the skies/other realms; alien beings called **dragons** arrived through gates and devastated the world. **Source:** E000 00:01:26–00:02:09.
- Humans developed battle machines, and made dangerous pacts involving demons and monsters in their fight against dragons. **Source:** E000 00:02:09–00:02:42.
- Humans made enduring **dragon engines** using the remains of slain dragons. The narration associates the engines with mana waves and extraordinary magical power. **Source:** E000 00:02:42–00:03:07.
- A prolonged war between humanity and dragons further ruined the world, and the dragons eventually vanished after scorching the land. The engines survived as valuable power sources. **Source:** E000 00:03:07–00:03:49. **Text caution:** words around 00:03:07 are visibly mistranscribed; confirm exact length of war before standardizing it.

## Cultural and metaphysical concepts

- **Dragon engines:** Rare, ancient engines associated with the dragon war and with powerful magical effects. Hope seeks one; the introduction presents them as technologically and metaphysically significant. **Sources:** E000 00:02:42–00:03:49 (GM); E000 00:09:59–00:10:17 (character aspiration); E001 00:16:02–00:16:17 (character activity).
- **Tieflings and the dark pact:** Kristen's introductory narration describes her people as emerging from a dark pact connected to the ancient conflict. This is a **character's account**, not independently established universal history. **Source:** E000 00:08:46–00:09:44.
- **Scarcity and mutual aid:** Dust Hill's economy, water supply, agricultural facilities and governance demonstrate the importance of cooperation and protected resources. **Source:** E001 00:05:55–00:08:13.

## Statements not yet promoted to established fact

- Hope believes finding a dragon engine will let her return to the past and restore the world. Her objective is explicit, but **the feasibility of time travel is not yet verified**. **Source:** E000 00:09:44–00:10:17.
- The exact chronology of the ancient war and human history contains potential recognition errors and dramatic stylization; defer precise dates.
- The Hillmasters say an old dwarven pump system feeds Dust Hill's well, but in the first episode this is presented partly as transmitted local knowledge; avoid claiming the engineering history has been independently established. **Source:** E001 00:07:30–00:08:09.
