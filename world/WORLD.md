# World setting and historical claims

## Present-day world

- **Earth, long after human civilization as we know it:** The setting is an Earth covered in wasteland; the GM describes no oceans, no winter and pervasive dust, with scarce resources and pockets of surviving civilization. **Source:** E001 00:04:17–00:05:12 (GM exposition).
- **Dust Hill as a surviving settlement:** One of the remaining centers of cooperative habitation, built into a rocky, defensible site. **Source:** E001 00:05:12–00:08:13 (GM exposition).
- **Scavenged technology and magic coexist:** Residents repair machines and use old mechanical technology. Magic is present, discussed in the introduction in connection with engines made from dragon remains. **Source:** E000 00:02:42–00:03:25 (GM prologue); E001 00:07:14–00:07:30 (GM exposition).

## Ancient history — GM's dramatic prologue

- Long ages ago, humanity inhabited a more flourishing Earth and reached for the skies/other realms; alien beings called **dragons** arrived through gates and devastated the world. **Source:** E000 00:01:26–00:02:09.
- Humans developed battle machines, and made dangerous pacts involving demons and monsters in their fight against dragons. **Source:** E000 00:02:09–00:02:42.
- Humans made enduring **dragon engines** using the remains of slain dragons. The narration associates the engines with mana waves and extraordinary magical power. **Source:** E000 00:02:42–00:03:07.
- A prolonged war between humanity and dragons further ruined the world, and the dragons eventually vanished after scorching the land. The engines survived as valuable power sources. **Source:** E000 00:03:07–00:03:49. **Text caution:** words around 00:03:07 are visibly mistranscribed; confirm exact length of war before standardizing it.

## Cultural and metaphysical concepts

- **Dragon engines:** Rare, ancient engines associated with the dragon war and with powerful magical effects. Hope seeks one; the introduction presents them as technologically and metaphysically significant. **Sources:** E000 00:02:42–00:03:49 (GM); E000 00:09:59–00:10:17 (character aspiration); E001 00:16:02–00:16:17 (character activity).
- **Tieflings and the dark pact:** Kristen's introductory narration describes her people as emerging from a dark pact connected to the ancient conflict. This is a **character's account**, not independently established universal history. **Source:** E000 00:08:46–00:09:44.
- **Scarcity and mutual aid:** Dust Hill's economy, water supply, agricultural facilities and governance demonstrate the importance of cooperation and protected resources. **Source:** E001 00:05:55–00:08:13.

## Statements not yet promoted to established fact

- Hope believes finding a dragon engine will let her return to the past and restore the world. Her objective is explicit, but **the feasibility of time travel is not yet verified**. **Source:** E000 00:09:44–00:10:17.
- The exact chronology of the ancient war and human history contains potential recognition errors and dramatic stylization; defer precise dates.
- The Hillmasters say an old dwarven pump system feeds Dust Hill's well, but in the first episode this is presented partly as transmitted local knowledge; avoid claiming the engineering history has been independently established. **Source:** E001 00:07:30–00:08:09.

These events are documented in the [E002–E005 episode dossiers](../episodes/E002.md), [E003](../episodes/E003.md), [E004](../episodes/E004.md), and [E005](../episodes/E005.md). The added sources are **processed Parakeet SRTs, not listener-verified audio**.

In E005 ~00:04–00:08 the apparition identifies himself as **Peter** and claims that later Hillmasters were impostors. Record that claim and its speaker, rather than retroactively rewriting the previous rulers' documented status.
- **Dwarven history:** E003 ~02:45–03:08 and E004 ~00:45–00:54 expose a sealed dwarven site, **Queen Amelda/Emelda**, and **Quizquaz**, who appears to feed on the queen's dreams. ## E006–E008 developments (processed transcripts, not audio-verified)

- **Water supply verified at the outlet:** Nash opens Dusthill's public water valve and confirms flowing water in **E006 ~00:24–00:26**, after work on the subterranean intake and engine in E003–E005. **Do not retroactively describe E005's untested outlet as a direct observation**; E006's opening GM recap is retrospective. See [E006](../episodes/E006.md).
- **Governance and defense after the goblin raid:** E006 ~01:19–01:52 has a public meeting. Shun'ti assumes an interim *townmaster* role, while the half-orc **Tetsuo** retains a militia-defense leadership role with **Jimster** assisting; the armed confrontation with Wilma remains an unresolved civic conflict. **45 dead and 25 abducted** are the numbers reported at the meeting, not confirmed demographic records.
- **Scoutmasters and settlement isolation:** E006 ~00:03–00:05 confirms the GM's earlier description that Dusthill's Scoutmasters vet potential residents and trading contacts without broadly revealing the hidden town's location. **Zorenthelas**, an elven Scoutmaster, discusses security and exploration later in E006.
- **Elven beliefs about Elhandar and dragon engines:** In **E006 ~01:58–02:11**, Zorenthelas asserts dragon engines are remolded dragon hearts, and that elves seek to return to **Elhandar**, a place in the sky, bearing gifts or world-transforming resources. **Source role: NPC testimony/religious narrative**; never collapse his apocalyptic claims into objective prologue history. The SRT varies on `El Hondar`/`Elhandar`. The [fan wiki E006 recap](https://twbd.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_6) uses `Elhandar`.
- **Dwarven artifact responsiveness:** E006 ~02:12–02:17 shows Hope's shape-changing wrench responding in Dwarven with Shun'ti translating. This adds direct in-session evidence for a speaking artifact, whereas E005 only contained Nash's theory; its maker/identity is still not confirmed.
- **C4N-D13's data core:** In E006 ~02:20–02:28, Stanley entrusts a data core from the dwarven automaton to Zorenthelas for possible delivery to Elhandar. The core's purported ability/need to “save the planet” is **reported purpose**, not a demonstrated result.
- **Technology and travel in the wastes:** **Fate 4**, Hope's monster-wheeled RV (~E006 02:15–02:34), becomes the party vehicle. It suffers an overheat/disablement in **E007 ~01:00–01:45** after combat with goblin vehicles. Vehicles, altered buses/catapults, radios and powered equipment coexist with spells. E007's discussion of fuel/range/repair is partly out-of-game rules, not automatic engineering canon.
- **Wasteland fauna:** The three frightening lights at the end of E006 are revealed as large, luminous **beetles** in **E007 ~00:06–00:40**. This is a new identification at E007 time, not an E006-observed fact.
- **Uncontrolled magic and visions:** Nash has a fiery episode with solar-mine memories in **E006 ~01:42–01:45** and another, more extended visionary experience in **E008 ~00:06–00:10** (revenge/power voices and a light-shaped prisoner asking for release). Do not identify these entities, their motivations or the cause of the flames as objectively established.
- **Separate secret installation:** Nash and Stanley encounter an antenna/sand mechanism in E007 ~01:54–02:16; E008 explores an underground facility with a B7 elevator, containment/security claims, projection-like environments, **Leopold**, and hostile creatures. This is **not demonstrated to be the same complex** as Dusthill's dragon engine or Moag Ferhain. Leopold's account of the installation's purpose and his denial that he is a computer are **his claims**, not independent confirmation.
