# Tietkalla — ancient kingdom, reform and catastrophe

**Place:** [Tietkalla / Teet keep / Trashburg](places/tietkalla.md). **Spoilers:** Through E118. **Research:** Provisional ASR with contextual cast-written support for the *Tietkalla* spelling.

## Before the party

Ancient buildings, the water throne, tiefling remains and lost records give material evidence of a former polity. **Nobiery/Nobieri**, freed from a magic book in E047, reports a royal **Akmenos** lineage, lost houses, a concealed army and an old pact; **Kefeli** describes another version involving protection, vampires and Andreeste. **Monsoon** has his own interested account. None of these perspectives alone fixes an exact ancient date or establishes every sacrificial or supernatural clause. [E047](../episodes/E047.md) · [E048](../episodes/E048.md) · [E051](../episodes/E051.md).

## Contest for the keep (E031–E054)

The lower district suffers exploitation, gangs and **Pixie Blush** trafficking while the **Guzzle Gang**, **Twenty-Sixers** and other interests vie for power. Order 26 transforms **P0D** E049; **Vorel kills Buttons** there, **Diontalus kills P0D** and **Tedna dies** E050. **Gem of Tides recovered** E050, and **Kefeli destroyed** E051. A grotesque froghemoth splits: the **lower half dies** while its **upper part leaves** E053. At E054 the city is devastated, **Angelashi is freed alive**, **Monsoon escapes as mist**, and a genuine tiefling contingent returns to crown **Hope** queen. [E049](../episodes/E049.md)–[E054](../episodes/E054.md).

## Reforms and pressure (E055–E059)

After **three months of played downtime**, Hope's court attempts social reform, infrastructure and security amid severe resource limits. Civilian-killing **stonefall** occurs; the Principle offers rail trade and prisoner return. **Several Dusthill prisoners really return E059**; proposals for rail and reconciliation must not be retroactively described as completed earlier. [E055](../episodes/E055.md)–[E059](../episodes/E059.md).

## Arc Five devastation and rescue

Hope returns to a **populated** Tietkalla in E103 and burns a **temporarily closed, empty rail station**; then a demonic assault destroys swathes of the city. Her flight and the invaders' motives are disputed by participants. In E111 **the Elhandari intervene**, evacuate survivors and **capture General Carthax alive**, though he has killed **Squirts and Melgor**. The city remains badly damaged; its complete casualty count and eventual political settlement are not witnessed. [E103](../episodes/E103.md) · [E104](../episodes/E104.md) · [E111](../episodes/E111.md).

The survivors' later community is named [the Harbor](places/the-harbor.md) **in Elhandar E117**; that does not imply the original city was rebuilt. **Omnom's E118 allegations** against Hope are testimony alongside his observed physical injuries, while the party actually postpones a planned reconciliation hunt. [E117](../episodes/E117.md) · [E118](../episodes/E118.md).
