# Peoples and cultures of the Wastes

Beyond the companions and the rulers they encounter are communities with their own histories, loyalties and divisions. The Wastes' peoples cannot be understood through any single hero, settlement or faction.

## Peoples encountered

| People | Where their stories lead |
| --- | --- |
| [Lizardfolk](races/lizardfolk.md) | Hunters and priestesses of the Golden Land; the clans, rivalries and Great Fang of Razak Val; survivors seeking a future after the war. |
| [Tieflings](races/tieflings.md) | The old kingdom of T'ietkalla, Hope's contested coronation and reforms, the Harbor's refugees and the uncertain rebuilding of their city. |
| [Harlequinari](races/harlequinari.md) | Blood Skunk's estrangement, the comic cruelty of the Council of Clowns and a wider, only partly known Harlequinari world. |
| [Elves](races/elves.md) | The Elhandari's forest sanctuary and refuge for others, the fall of Elhandar, and the communities that survive on Mars, Earth and in the Feywild. |
| [Dwarves](races/dwarves.md) | Ancient underground halls, the Oilbeard-controlled Black Mine, and Burp's declining mithril town, alongside distinct craftsmen and ship crews. |
| [Orcs](races/orcs.md) | Differing loyalties among the Principal's Koromukishi martial order, Liberator dissidents, mercenary crews and former enemies offered a future with Vorel. |

## Communities and connections

For the fuller accounts of the places and people, visit [Razak Val](places/razak-val.md), [T'ietkalla](places/tietkalla.md), [the Harbor](places/the-harbor.md), [Elhandar](places/elhandar.md) and the dwarf-run [Black Mine](places/black-mine.md). The lives of [Vorel](characters/vorel.md) and [Hope](characters/hope-akmenos.md) belong to their own biographies; clan leadership, the city and individual choices do not describe every member of a people.

The [factions and powers](FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md) directory follows organizations and alliances, while [creatures and monsters](CREATURES_AND_MONSTERS.md) describes encountered creatures rather than treating intelligent societies as monsters.

## Sources and project notes

The first two source-backed population studies, Lizardfolk and Tieflings, were created under the approved [Races-domain decision](../project/DECISIONS.md); later Harlequinari, Elves, Dwarves and Orcs studies use narrower, differently evidenced community histories. Their accounts distinguish events witnessed in play from local history, testimony and uncertain origins. Primary episode coverage includes [the early lizardfolk encounters](../episodes/E016.md), [Razak Val's succession](../episodes/E060.md), [the T'ietkalla coronation](../episodes/E054.md), [the Harbor's founding](../episodes/E117.md) and [the played campaign finale](../episodes/S3E18.md).

**Lactids remain a research candidate, not a standalone population page.** The [Lactid Cartel](FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md) is an organization implicated in the played [Sapphire attack](../episodes/E013.md), not representative evidence for the culture or abilities of all Lactids. [Bertrand's account](../episodes/E026.md) of a Principal City underworld and possible mind control, and [Monsoon's claim](../episodes/E039.md) about an exile influencing Blunt Goliver, are interested testimony rather than independently witnessed people-level history. The [S3E019 roundtable](../episodes/S3E019.md) discusses an underground, cockroach-like society and pixie-blush trafficker in **unused Principal City preparation**, not a city visit or played cultural survey. The owner's `Laktid` spelling remains a [research alias](../project/OWNER_EPISODE_NOTES.md), not a verified replacement for the working written form. **Disposition:** retain the cartel under Factions; defer an independent culture article until population-level evidence is established.

The [Harlequinari profile](races/harlequinari.md) rests on [the Council audience and Blood Skunk's own words](../episodes/E113.md), including his self-identification, estrangement and the council's references to a wider Harlequinari world. It is **not** proof that every Council of Clowns member is Harlequinari, or that Blood Skunk's individual racial ability and appearance are universal. These sources are processed transcripts, episode dossiers and explicitly identified retrospective testimony, **not independently audio-verified**. The machine-readable [entity index](entities.json) still includes only the two original limited race/culture records under its [version 2 schema](ENTITY_SCHEMA.md); the new profile does not silently expand this partial index.
