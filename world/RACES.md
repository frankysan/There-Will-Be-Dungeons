# Peoples and cultures of the Wastes

Beyond the companions and the rulers they encounter are communities with their own histories, loyalties and divisions. The Wastes' peoples cannot be understood through any single hero, settlement or faction.

## Peoples encountered

| People | Where their stories lead |
| --- | --- |
| [Lizardfolk](races/lizardfolk.md) | Hunters and priestesses of the Golden Land; the clans, rivalries and Great Fang of Razak Val; survivors seeking a future after the war. |
| [Tieflings](races/tieflings.md) | The old kingdom of T'ietkalla, Hope's contested coronation and reforms, the Harbor's refugees and the uncertain rebuilding of their city. |

## Communities and connections

For the fuller accounts of the places and people, visit [Razak Val](places/razak-val.md), [T'ietkalla](places/tietkalla.md) and [the Harbor](places/the-harbor.md). The lives of [Vorel](characters/vorel.md) and [Hope](characters/hope-akmenos.md) belong to their own biographies; clan leadership, the city and individual choices do not describe every member of a people.

The [factions and powers](FACTIONS_AND_POWERS.md) directory follows organizations and alliances, while [creatures and monsters](CREATURES_AND_MONSTERS.md) describes encountered creatures rather than treating intelligent societies as monsters.

## Sources and project notes

These are the first two source-backed population studies under the approved [Races-domain decision](../project/DECISIONS.md). Their accounts distinguish events witnessed in play from local history, testimony and uncertain origins. Primary episode coverage includes [the early lizardfolk encounters](../episodes/E016.md), [Razak Val's succession](../episodes/E060.md), [the T'ietkalla coronation](../episodes/E054.md), [the Harbor's founding](../episodes/E117.md) and [the played campaign finale](../episodes/S3E18.md).

Lactids and Harlequinari remain research candidates, **not established standalone population pages**. The Lactid Cartel is an organization, not a reliable description of every Lactid; much wider Lactid society appears in unused creator preparation. Blood Skunk's Harlequinari associations and a custom ability do not establish a common culture for that people. Sources are processed transcripts and episode dossiers, **not independently audio-verified**. The machine-readable [entity index](entities.json) now includes two limited race/culture records under its [version 2 schema](ENTITY_SCHEMA.md); it remains a partial name index, not a complete catalogue of peoples or episodes.
