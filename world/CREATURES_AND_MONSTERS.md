# Creatures and monsters

The Wastes harbor desert wildlife, dangerous beasts and monstrous transformations, from sand squid used in everyday meals to the enlarged pixies unleashed against Tietkalla's keep.[^scope]

| Creature | Nature and place in the world |
| --- | --- |
| **Mind fluff — green face parasites** | Tentacled creatures physically attached to each protagonist aboard Fate Six, inducing linked dream; **Union frees Vorel, Vorel frees Stanley/Hope, Nash's waking fire burns remaining specimens**. Exact species and how they boarded unknown. [S3E03](../episodes/S3E03.md) |
| [Sand squid](creatures/sand-squid.md) | Schooling animals that travel through desert sand and feed on insects; their meat, dried bodies and creamy juice supply meals, rations and coffee ingredients. |
| [Chicken Lords](creatures/chicken-lords.md) | Huge chicken-like monsters used in arena trials and served at Razak Val's feasts; the same name is given to the creatures into which the city's captives transform. |
| [Pixie Hulks](creatures/pixie-hulks.md) | Enormous, muscular forms forced upon pixies under Monsoon Cabbage's command, combining destructive strength with pixie magic. |
| [Chester the bearsaurus](creatures/chester-the-bearsaurus.md) | Enormous antlered animal of Elhandar's hunting forest, with bear-like bulk, powerful hind legs and a partly reptilian head. |
| [Dunebreaker fruit ooze](creatures/dunebreaker-fruit-ooze.md) | Fruit-speckled jelly that escaped into the landship's drains and grew into a vast acidic mass by absorbing waste and debris. |
| [Tainted displacer beast](creatures/tainted-displacer-beast.md) | Wounded tentacled predator of the Black Mine, whose shifting appearance made it difficult to strike. |
| [Brain-backed spiders](creatures/brain-backed-spiders.md) | Giant spider with an exposed pulsing brain and accompanying smaller swarms; venom and mental control made the CHIDS lift shaft perilous. |
| [Skeleton-spawning flesh giant](creatures/skeleton-spawning-flesh-giant.md) | Malformed flesh-and-bone creature beneath Kervalon, producing animated skeletons from shoulder cavities and wounds. |
| [Black puddings](creatures/black-puddings.md) | Corrosive black cave oozes that divide under cutting attacks and dissolve weapons and wooden barriers. |
| [Basilisk of the under-Pit caves](creatures/basilisk-of-the-under-pit-caves.md) | Eight-legged reptile with a petrifying gaze, whose remains later helped restore Hope and Vorel to living flesh. |
| [Luminous desert beetles](creatures/luminous-desert-beetles.md) | Large insects with glowing fleshy appendages, encountered around Fate 4's desert camp at night. |
| [Tyrannosaurus of the purple sands](creatures/purple-sands-tyrannosaurus.md) | Huge predator that crushed Fate 4 during the rainstorm near Kervalon; its teeth and bones became tools and salvage. |
| [Outlands wyverns](creatures/outlands-wyverns.md) | Flying attackers of the Traveler's Twin, capable of ramming its hull and injuring defenders with bites and venomous stings. |
| [Tentacled purple fungi](creatures/tentacled-purple-fungi.md) | Large animate mushrooms of the Black Mine, using tentacles to grapple and strike travelers among dense fungal beds. |
| [Riding raptors](creatures/riding-raptors.md) | Velociraptor-like mounts used by lizardfolk hunters and the fugitives from Razak Val; their hides also served for repairs. |
| [Green-case mimic](creatures/green-case-mimic.md) | Toothy, tongue-bearing creature disguised as a military case in Leopold's laboratory. |
| [Eight-legged facility predator](creatures/eight-legged-facility-predator.md) | Large doglike animal that vanished and reappeared across the laboratory doorway, then fed on the dead mimic's tongue. |
| [Gibbering mouther](creatures/gibbering-mouther.md) | Many-eyed, many-mouthed mass whose chatter, sticky surroundings and blinding spit threatened the escape from Leopold's facility. |
| [Mushroom-pit spider](creatures/mushroom-pit-spider.md) | Gigantic blue-green spider concealed against a cavern ceiling; its severed legs later supplied food. |
| [Pale tentacled burrower](creatures/pale-tentacled-burrower.md) | Pale wormlike cavern animal with grey eye-like surfaces and mouth tendrils; warns intruders and burrows away. |
| [River undead](creatures/river-undead.md) | Blackened humanoid dead whose gaze, chanting and presence afflict travelers with despair and painful buzzing. |
| [Froghemoth gut worms](creatures/froghemoth-gut-worms.md) | Pink stomach worms flee light; farther intestinal worms ignore it and process waste. |
| [White filament hunter](creatures/white-filament-hunter.md) | White clawed gut predator that reels prey upward on a hanging line and replaces its severed filament. |
| [Froghemoth intestinal ropers](creatures/froghemoth-intestinal-ropers.md) | Tentacled, rocklike gut predators; the first is killed and a second survives the travelers' flight. |
| [Bloodsucking desert swarm](creatures/bloodsucking-desert-swarm.md) | Dark cloud of tiny bloodsuckers that drives travelers and mounts into shelter beneath a sand canopy. |
| [Black headworms](creatures/black-headworms.md) | Small black leechlike animals found in brain tissue, still crawling and seeking flesh after removal. |
| [Razak Val invasive worms](creatures/razak-val-invasive-worms.md) | Worms expelled by an altered captive that attach to skin and begin entering flesh before being burned away. |
| [Dolo Popio's spawned worms](creatures/dolo-popio-spawned-worms.md) | Large worms released as living weapons, including green explosive forms and later worms drifting through the floating battlefield. |
| [Translucent wall-passers](creatures/translucent-wall-passers.md) | Faintly glowing tentacled creatures that drift through CHIDS walls, gather to songs and share painless mental sensations. |
| [CHIDS hellhounds](creatures/chids-hellhounds.md) | Black flaming hounds with molten skin and scorching breath; the conference-room pack is defeated after a prolonged fight. |
| [Blue-black chasm flyers](creatures/blue-black-chasm-flyers.md) | Leathery-winged CHIDS predators with thorned, arrowhead tails and frightening cries; others remain after the first attacker dies. |
| [Crater wyvern](creatures/crater-wyvern.md) | Winged reptilian pet of the crater attackers, using a venomous sting and bites before Stanley kills it. |
