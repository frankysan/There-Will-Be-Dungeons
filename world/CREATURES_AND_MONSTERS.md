# Creatures and monsters

The Wastes harbor desert wildlife, dangerous beasts and monstrous transformations, from sand squid used in everyday meals to the enlarged pixies unleashed against T'ietkalla's keep.[^scope]

## Wildlife, mounts and hunted creatures

These animals inhabit deserts, forests, caverns and distant worlds. Some are food or mounts; others are dangerous encounters. A single observed animal does not establish an entire species' range.

| Creature | Nature and place in the world |
| --- | --- |
| [Sand squid](creatures/sand-squid.md) | Schooling animals that travel through desert sand and feed on insects; their meat, dried bodies and creamy juice supply meals, rations and coffee ingredients. |
| [Luminous desert beetles](creatures/luminous-desert-beetles.md) | Large insects with glowing fleshy appendages, encountered around Fate 4's desert camp at night. |
| [Riding raptors](creatures/riding-raptors.md) | Velociraptor-like mounts used by lizardfolk hunters and the fugitives from Razak Val; their hides also served for repairs. |
| [Bloodsucking desert swarm](creatures/bloodsucking-desert-swarm.md) | Dark cloud of tiny bloodsuckers that drives travelers and mounts into shelter beneath a sand canopy. |
| [Tyrannosaurus of the purple sands](creatures/purple-sands-tyrannosaurus.md) | Huge predator that crushed Fate 4 during the rainstorm near Khaervilon; its teeth and bones became tools and salvage. |
| [Chester the bearsaurus](creatures/chester-the-bearsaurus.md) | Enormous antlered animal of Elhandar's hunting forest, with bear-like bulk, powerful hind legs and a partly reptilian head. |
| [Crater wyvern](creatures/crater-wyvern.md) | Winged reptilian pet of the crater attackers, using a venomous sting and bites before Stanley kills it. |
| [Outlands wyverns](creatures/outlands-wyverns.md) | Flying attackers of the Traveler's Twin, capable of ramming its hull and injuring defenders with bites and venomous stings. |
| [Pale tentacled burrower](creatures/pale-tentacled-burrower.md) | Pale wormlike cavern animal with grey eye-like surfaces and mouth tendrils; warns intruders and burrows away. |

## Predators, oozes and other unusual encounters

The companions meet creatures in laboratories, mines, caves and other planes. Their origins, intelligence and wider populations are often uncertain.

| Creature | Nature and place in the world |
| --- | --- |
| [Green-case mimic](creatures/green-case-mimic.md) | Toothy, tongue-bearing creature disguised as a military case in Leopold's laboratory. |
| [Eight-legged facility predator](creatures/eight-legged-facility-predator.md) | Large doglike animal that vanished and reappeared across the laboratory doorway, then fed on the dead mimic's tongue. |
| [Gibbering mouther](creatures/gibbering-mouther.md) | Many-eyed, many-mouthed mass whose chatter, sticky surroundings and blinding spit threatened the escape from Leopold's facility. |
| [Tainted displacer beast](creatures/tainted-displacer-beast.md) | Wounded tentacled predator of the Black Mine, whose shifting appearance made it difficult to strike. |
| [Tentacled purple fungi](creatures/tentacled-purple-fungi.md) | Large animate mushrooms of the Black Mine, using tentacles to grapple and strike travelers among dense fungal beds. |
| [Brain-backed spiders](creatures/brain-backed-spiders.md) | Giant spider with an exposed pulsing brain and accompanying smaller swarms; venom and mental control made the CHIDS lift shaft perilous. |
| [Black puddings](creatures/black-puddings.md) | Corrosive black cave oozes that divide under cutting attacks and dissolve weapons and wooden barriers. |
| [Basilisk of the under-Pit caves](creatures/basilisk-of-the-under-pit-caves.md) | Eight-legged reptile with a petrifying gaze, whose remains later helped restore Hope and Vorel to living flesh. |

| [Blue-black chasm flyers](creatures/blue-black-chasm-flyers.md) | Leathery-winged CHIDS predators with thorned, arrowhead tails and frightening cries; others remain after the first attacker dies. |

## Transformations, parasites and constructed threats

These encounters include imposed forms, infesting creatures, the altered dead and engineered defenders. A transformed victim remains a person with their own history; neither a machine nor a monstrous body establishes a people or ancestry.

| Creature | Nature and place in the world |
| --- | --- |
| [Chicken Lords](creatures/chicken-lords.md) | Huge chicken-like monsters used in arena trials and served at Razak Val's feasts; the same name is given to the creatures into which the city's captives transform. |
| [Pixie Hulks](creatures/pixie-hulks.md) | Enormous, muscular forms forced upon pixies under Monsoon Cabbage's command, combining destructive strength with pixie magic. |
| [River undead](creatures/river-undead.md) | Blackened humanoid dead whose gaze, chanting and presence afflict travelers with despair and painful buzzing. |
| [Skeleton-spawning flesh giant](creatures/skeleton-spawning-flesh-giant.md) | Malformed flesh-and-bone creature beneath Khaervilon, producing animated skeletons from shoulder cavities and wounds. |
| [Black headworms](creatures/black-headworms.md) | Small black leechlike animals found in brain tissue, still crawling and seeking flesh after removal. |
| [Razak Val invasive worms](creatures/razak-val-invasive-worms.md) | Worms expelled by an altered captive that attach to skin and begin entering flesh before being burned away. |
| [Dolo Popio's spawned worms](creatures/dolo-popio-spawned-worms.md) | Large worms released as living weapons, including green explosive forms and later worms drifting through the floating battlefield. |
| **Mind fluff — green face parasites** | Tentacled parasites trap the companions in a shared dream aboard Fate Six. Union and the waking travelers remove them, while Nash's fire destroys those still attached. Their origin and how they boarded remain unknown.[^mind-fluff] |
| **Slow Peters — giant molten sentry machines** | Towering metal-and-lava sentries defend the Solar Mines. Two are destroyed in the north-gate assault after ice and magical attacks, but others are reported beyond the gate.[^slow-peters] |

**Boundary cases — not established creature species:** The [Pit of Despair's froghemoth](HISTORY_TIETKALLA.md) is witnessed as a three-eyed predator in E041 and later as an enlarged many-eyed form; its telepathic bargaining and the escape of its upper cocoon prevent treating it as a mindless monster that was wholly killed.[^pit-froghemoth] CHIDS's **red memory-eating growths** include one many-faced mass whose tethered copies dissolve with its E075 death and later red attackers in E080–E081; their common origin, independence and reasoning capacity remain unverified.[^chids-red] The E128 burrower is covered as the later-identified [Worldbreaker](ANTAGONISTS.md), **permanently banished from Earth in S3E17**, not slain or established as a new species.[^worldbreaker-routing]

## Sources and project notes

[^mind-fluff]: [Played S3E03](../episodes/S3E03.md) ~01:04–01:16: Union removes one parasite from Vorel; Vorel and Stanley help free the others. Nash's uncontrolled waking fire destroys the remaining attached creatures. The shared dream is played, but its apparent witnesses' historical claims are not independently verified. No species or boarding route established.
[^slow-peters]: [Played S3E05](../episodes/S3E05.md) ~01:31–02:23: two roughly 40–50-foot sentries fire lava beams and are actually destroyed by the heroes. The GM adjudicates doubled cold damage and halved bludgeoning against those machines; the dossier reports others still inside, not a verified full force or later destruction.

[^pit-froghemoth]: [E039](../episodes/E039.md) names a froghemoth in Monsoon's testimony; [E041](../episodes/E041.md) observes a three-eyed captive, [E046](../episodes/E046.md) confirms it remains alive, and [E052](../episodes/E052.md)–[E053](../episodes/E053.md) show a radically enlarged form with a private telepathic voice, a falling/dying lower half and a floating upper cocoon that escapes. Identity across earlier and later forms and the speaker's agency remain source-bound questions; do not describe the whole being as dead.

[^chids-red]: [E074](../episodes/E074.md) shows a many-faced memory-draining mass with Vorel/Nash tethered copies; [E075](../episodes/E075.md) records its destruction and the copies' disappearance. [E080](../episodes/E080.md)–[E081](../episodes/E081.md) describe additional red flesh/memory-eating threats, including one killed while burrowing into Hope. No observed continuity of a single specimen, universal shared mind, or independently speaking kind is established.

[^worldbreaker-routing]: [E128](../episodes/E128.md) observes the six-limbed burrower entering Earth; [S3E14](../episodes/S3E14.md)–[S3E17](../episodes/S3E17.md) identify the campaign's Worldbreaker/Tarrasque and document its completed Earth-only banishment. [Detailed event history](HISTORICAL_EVENTS.md#worldbreakers-arrival-and-earth-only-banishment--e128-to-s3e17) separates performed acts from the Soulbroker's claims. No original audio or universal creature taxonomy independently verified.
