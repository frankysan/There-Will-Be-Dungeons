## How to read status

| Status | Meaning and editorial action |
| --- | --- |
| **Resolved — owner/creator** | An explicit authorized **identity/spelling or editorial policy** decision. Use this in display/reference text; **not automatic verification of an audio phrase or story claim**. Link to [decisions](../project/DECISIONS.md). |
| **Resolved — source-cutoff** | The later **processed-episode source** provides a clear directly depicted outcome, correcting an earlier provisional report. No current narrative contradiction remains; **audio accuracy is still pending**. Preserve what characters knew earlier. |
| **Open — audio/orthography** | Important name, wording, narrator, timing, identity or mechanics require direct listening or a sufficiently authoritative written source. Do not normalize a source SRT without an explicit edit/review decision. |
| **Open — lore/claims** | NPC testimony, private vision, historical inference, planned route or unseen future outcome lacks independent resolution by the **reviewed source cutoff**. Additional listening may clarify exact **claim wording**, but cannot prove a world fact the GM intentionally leaves uncertain. |
| **Open — custody/mechanics** | Ownership, one-use patch, special weapon power, game-rule interaction or artifact history needs source/handout reconciliation. Never silently use final-campaign character sheet state as an earlier inventory. |

**“Resolved — source-cutoff” is not “audio verified.”** No new listening has occurred in this consolidation; the prior distinction between observed event and misrecognized ASR remains.

## K. Closure and new-entry procedure

1. Identify a **stable key** and classify evidence: **direct episode action, GM exposition, character testimony, private vision, out-of-character GM retrospective, official/cast-written name, owner decision or fan reference**.
2. Record **exact source and approx. time/cue**, optionally handout/document revision. If review was by audio, explicitly record **listener/editor and date**. “Compared transcripts” is **not** an audio-verification method.
3. Determine the **narrowest change**. A creator can settle **orthography** without establishing a god's existence; E054 can settle Angelashi's **survival** without authenticating an unrelated story about her past.
4. If actually resolved, record or update an accepted editorial policy/exception in [DECISIONS.md](../project/DECISIONS.md) **only when a project-level decision is needed**, not a new numbered decision for every scene. Keep status-cutoff resolution in this table and the [chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md).
5. Update the active [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md) by closing/reclassifying that **specific** task, while retaining its original record in the [archived queue](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/PROJECT_REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
6. Update the affected reference or transcript **only at the correct evidence tier and spoiler horizon**; preserve machine SRTs, historical alternate spellings, original episode-era knowledge and source history.

**This structural cleanup performed no new audio listening, transcript edits, handout verification or `world/entities.json` synchronization.** It does not claim that the hundreds of archival subquestions are all closed.
