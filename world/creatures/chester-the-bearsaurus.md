# Chester the bearsaurus

## Introduction

Chester was a large animal said to haunt Elhandar's northern hunting grounds. [Dilo](../characters/dilenoria.md) described him as a bear-and-dinosaur hybrid that had escaped from an Elhandari laboratory, where he had been created for druidic summoning experiments. According to Dilo, the animal had killed elves, disrupted the forest and left earlier hunting parties dead.[^reports]

## Arc Five — the Elhandar hunt

When the hunters finally found Chester, his body combined several striking forms: an upright, bear-like frame, massive hind legs, small forelimbs, a partly reptilian head and large branching deer antlers. He was comparable in size to the Tyrannosaurus form Stanley assumed to confront him. Despite his bulk, Chester could leap high into a tree, escaping Stanley's grasp even after suffering severe wounds.[^appearance]

Chester's size remained dangerous when his strength failed. Hope pulled at the injured animal's dangling entrails, bringing him and a tree down onto herself. The fall killed Chester, but his weight pinned Hope and left her struggling to breathe until Vorel tipped the carcass aside.[^fall]

## Current status

Chester is dead, his body left at the forest hunting site. Omnom began eating the flesh after the fight. Whether Dilo's account accurately explained the animal's creation and long history remains uncertain.[^status]
