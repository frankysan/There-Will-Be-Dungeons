# Chicken Lords

## Introduction

Chicken Lords are huge, grotesque chicken-like monsters. One brought to the Dunebreaker's guard-selection arena had an extending fleshy neck, a circular toothed opening and corrosive green bile. Kept in a battered metal box until the trial began, it was a formidable test for would-be caravan guards.[^arena]

## Encounters and transformations

### Before the journey from Dusthill — the arena beast

Long before Nash and his companions left Dusthill, a captive Chicken Lord was unleashed against the Dunebreaker's final guard candidates. Its bites and acid brought two competitors down before Hack Loman killed it. The creature's death secured employment for the surviving successful team; the two rejected candidates were stabilized and carried away alive.[^arena]

### Arc Three — food and danger at Razak Val

At [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md), Chicken Lord meat appeared on the feast table, mostly raw and seasoned with rock. The hosts described the animal as coming from the Demon Vold, making it part of the city's dangerous hunting culture as well as its food supply.[^feast]

Later, the Sun's Witnesses imprisoned in the city began transforming into creatures recognized as Chicken Lords during an outbreak of worms and bodily changes. Many survived the first fire in the prison pit and pursued the fleeing adventurers. Their numbers overwhelmed a narrow bridge, sending pursuers into the ravine. The creatures arising from the captives shared the name of the hunted beasts, but their transformation left their relationship to those animals uncertain.[^outbreak]

## Current status

The Dunebreaker arena specimen is dead. Many of the transformed pursuers at Razak Val were killed, but the city's wider outbreak was unresolved when the adventurers escaped. The full fate of the transformed captives remains unknown.[^status]
