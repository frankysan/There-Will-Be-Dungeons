# Razak Val invasive worms

## Introduction

During the catastrophe in [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md), a transformed captive expelled a shower of living worms. They fastened onto those struck, and some began sinking through skin and scales into their bodies.[^nature]

## Arc Three — the outbreak

The worms caught Vorel, Stanley, Nash and Omnom, while Hope avoided the spray. Those upon Vorel and Omnom began entering their flesh; others remained attached to the surface. Vorel unleashed a burst of flame that burned the worms off the nearby victims and set the continuing spray alight.[^fire]

The eruption accompanied a wider disaster: captives transformed into Chicken Lords, and altered lizardfolk turned upon one another. The travelers could destroy the worms within reach, but they could not bring the whole settlement's crisis under control.[^outbreak]

## Current status

The worms visibly attached to the immediate group were burned away. That escape did not resolve the outbreak throughout Razak Val or establish that every threatened person was free of infestation.[^status]
