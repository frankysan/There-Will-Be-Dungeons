# Tainted displacer beast

## Introduction

A wounded, tentacled cat-like beast prowled a deep cavern of the [Black Mine](../places/black-mine.md). Its body bore old injuries, and damage to its head left part of the brain exposed. Even in this condition, its deceptive shifting appearance made it a dangerous opponent.[^nature]

## Arc One — the Black Mine chasm

The beast occupied a chamber where mushrooms, glowing glyphs and a narrow ledge bordered a deep chasm. Its apparent position shifted during the confrontation, frustrating attempts to strike it. The fight carried down into the pit, where its exposed brain proved a fatal vulnerability: Vorel killed the creature by biting into it.[^fight]

## Current status

The animal is dead. A piece of its remains was used to trigger a nearby glyph, releasing magical darkness into the passage. The cause of its wounds and altered condition remains unknown.[^end]
