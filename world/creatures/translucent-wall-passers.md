# Translucent wall-passers

## Introduction

Small, translucent creatures drift through the walls of CHIDS. Their round bodies trail small tentacles and give off a faint light. They gather around singing travelers, making their presence felt as soft, painless touches within the mind.[^nature]

## Arc Three — songs in the corridors

Vorel's singing drew dozens from the walls. They circled him without visibly touching his body, bringing a sense of their happiness and contentment. His questions brought no clear answers; even when he asked for directions, the feelings remained much the same.[^contact]

Nash's songs also attracted them, but Hope's proximity made them agitated and fearful. They approached him from the side away from her. Stanley's beautiful melody drew them out only briefly before they recoiled from him as well. The company came to understand them as sensitive to the emotions of those nearby.[^emotion]

The squeak of Hope's illithid doll sent the creatures retreating into the walls. Renewed singing brought them back. Their departure ended the mental sensation, leaving Vorel briefly lethargic.[^squeak]

## Current status

The wall-passers remained free within the facility. None was killed or made into a lasting companion, and their later movements are unknown.[^status]
