# Eight-legged facility predator

## Introduction

A large doglike creature with eight legs approached the laboratory in [Leopold's underground facility](../places/leopold-facility.md). It scratched at a partly opened door and tried to push through the narrow gap, bringing another danger to the travelers already fighting a [case-shaped mimic](green-case-mimic.md).[^appearance]

## Arc One — movement, hunger and caution

The animal could vanish and reappear across an obstacle. After running at the door, it disappeared and emerged on a laboratory table in front of Nash, snapping at him. It later used the same startling movement to leave and return to the room. A sleep spell briefly subdued it, but shouting woke it again.[^movement]

Food offered a more lasting distraction. Chance gave it the dead mimic's tongue, which it sniffed, tore at and began eating. It still growled when the travelers approached the door, but Chance's cautious gestures eventually quieted it. An attempt to pet it made it recoil and vanish; it reappeared in a corner with the tongue, allowing the others to move the unconscious Nash toward the quarters.[^food]

## Current status

The animal was last seen alive in the laboratory, eating the mimic's tongue. Its fate after the facility's later collapse remains unknown.[^status]
