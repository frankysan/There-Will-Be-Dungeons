# Riding raptors

## Introduction

Velociraptor-like animals carried lizardfolk hunting parties across the Wastes. They accompanied Diptak's hunters to the travelers' desert camp, where the animals proved capable of grappling and attacking alongside their riders.[^nature]

## Arc Two — hunters' mounts and useful hides

During [Diptak's](../characters/diptak.md) challenge to Vorel, the raptors joined the fighting. One wounded animal continued attacking Hope after Diptak yielded and his followers stopped. Hope killed it with magical fire; the surviving hunters later departed.[^challenge]

The animals' bodies also supplied useful material. Bok Bok recovered raptor remains, and Vorel and Hope used scales and skin to patch Fate 4's damaged roof. The hide covered the opening, providing a practical repair after the desert battles.[^hide]

## Arc Three — escape from Razak Val

[Omnom](../characters/omnom-cinderborn.md) supplied four raptors for the flight from [Razak Val](../places/razak-val.md). Vorel named his new mount Mawstorm. The animals carried the fugitives through rocky passages while transformed Chicken Lords pursued them.[^escape]

The pursuit put both riders and mounts at risk. Stanley's raptor was seized, forcing him to transfer to Hope's. The company continued toward the purple sands after surviving a collapsing bridge, relying on the animals until they encountered Fate 4 again.[^ride]

## Current status

The travelers released their raptors when they boarded Fate 4. The animals' later whereabouts, including Mawstorm's, remain unknown.[^status]
