# River undead

## Introduction

Blackened humanoid dead haunt an underground river beneath [T'ietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md). Their rotten flesh hangs in loose folds, with hollow black spaces in their faces and a gaping opening extending down toward the chest. Their presence fills nearby minds with a painful buzzing; their gaze brings despair and a withering pain without any physical blow.[^appearance]

## Arc Two — the river chamber

The company first found one standing on a rock across the water. When illuminated, it turned toward them and advanced into the river. Chanting in a language none of the travelers understood echoed from its open face without movement of its mouth. Its approach intensified the pressure in their heads.[^chant]

Diontalus destroyed that first figure with two magical beams, breaking it into particles. The brief silence ended as more black hands broke through the chamber walls and similar figures emerged around the party.[^arrival]

The ensuing fight nearly overcame the travelers. One creature's gaze left Vorel unconscious. Nash burned another with retaliatory flames, then melted the final attacker with acid. As its body crumbled, the crushing pressure in the survivors' heads lifted.[^battle]

## Current status

The attackers encountered in the river chamber are destroyed. Vorel survived and was stabilized, but the company learned neither the dead figures' former identities nor the cause of their condition. Their defeat does not establish that the surrounding caves are free of others.[^status]
