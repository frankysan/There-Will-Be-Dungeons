# Blue-black chasm flyers

## Introduction

Large, leathery-winged creatures occupy a deep chasm within [CHIDS](../places/chids.md). The first to attack the company had slick blue-black skin, a gaping mouth and a long tail covered in thorns, ending in a jagged arrowhead. Its eerie cries could leave nearby travelers afraid to approach it.[^nature]

## Arc Three — predators above the pipes

One flyer descended behind Nash while he worked at a jammed containment door. It hovered near the pipework, snapping at him and striking with its thorned tail. Nash and Vorel briefly succumbed to its frightening presence, making the exposed crossing still more dangerous.[^attack]

Through the Eye of Groxl, Vorel perceived that the creature disliked bright light and that illuminating it offered a way to defeat it. Hope instead finished the attacker with a shot through its eyes, sending its ruined body tumbling into the darkness below.[^death]

More cries rose above the chasm. As the company escaped through their portal, another flyer dived after them; Nash's change of the portal endpoint severed its head. The fallen head continued snapping briefly.[^portal]

## Current status

The first attacker is dead, and a second lost its head during the crossing. Other flyers remained above the chasm; their number and later fate are unknown.[^status]
