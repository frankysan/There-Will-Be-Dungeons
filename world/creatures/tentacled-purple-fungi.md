# Tentacled purple fungi

## Introduction

Large, animate purple fungi with caps and tentacles occupied a cavern deep in the [Black Mine](../places/black-mine.md). Dense mushroom beds crowded the ground near a hazardous ledge, where the fungi could grip and strike passing travelers.[^nature]

## Arc One — living obstacles in the mine

The company reached the mushroom beds after making its way through magical darkness. Clearing the thick growth proved difficult, and several of the large purple fungi attacked. Their tentacles grappled and struck the adventurers, turning the crowded passage into a close physical struggle. The company destroyed the attackers before continuing through the mine.[^attack]

## Current status

The attacking fungi are destroyed. Their wider distribution through the Black Mine remains unknown.[^status]
