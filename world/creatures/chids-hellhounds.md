# CHIDS hellhounds

## Introduction

Black, flaming hounds attack intruders in the CHIDS executive offices. Their molten-looking skin vents fire and embers, and their breath can become a scorching jet. Even biting into a dying hound exposes its attacker to the burning substance of its body.[^nature]

## Arc Three — the conference-room pack

The hounds charged into the conference room after the facility's alarm sounded. They leaped over the furniture and surrounded the travelers, combining snapping bites with bursts of flame. Vorel's own fire failed to harm their molten skin.[^attack]

Other attacks proved effective. Hope's radiant ammunition burst one hound into fragments that dissipated as they scattered across Stanley. Nash's frost covered another's fiery vents, slowing and hurting it. Vorel killed one with a bite, but the creature burst into fire inside his mouth and burned him in return.[^defenses]

The pack continued fighting alongside the office's larger security guards. The company finally beat the last surviving hound to death, leaving the room quiet beneath the continuing alarm.[^end]

## Current status

The hellhounds encountered in the conference room are dead. Their defeat cleared that immediate fight, without ending the facility's alarm or accounting for every defender elsewhere in CHIDS.[^status]
