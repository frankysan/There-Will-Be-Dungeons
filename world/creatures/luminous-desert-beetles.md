# Luminous desert beetles

## Introduction

Large beetles with luminous, fleshy appendages roamed the desert along the travelers' route from Dusthill. At night, their moving lights were visible around the camp beside Fate 4, revealing little of the creatures carrying them until the company looked more closely.[^appearance]

## Arc One — lights around the camp

Three blinking lights approached the camp after the travelers took in a frightened old man who feared the lights of the wastes. He ran into the darkness, and the glow later resolved into the beetles moving around the vehicle.[^lights]

Vorel waited concealed while the company watched the insects. Noise, distractions and the vehicle helped the travelers avoid them. Bok Bok eventually returned with the battered old man, rejoining the group near Fate 4.[^encounter]

## Current status

The beetles' later movements and fate remain unknown after the night near the travelers' camp.[^status]
