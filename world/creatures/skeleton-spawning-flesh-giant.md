# Skeleton-spawning flesh giant

## Introduction

A huge, malformed creature of flesh and bone occupied an old stone chamber beneath [Khaervilon](../places/kervalon.md). Extra bones protruded from its body, and cavities in its shoulders opened onto a still more dangerous feature: animated skeletons could erupt from within it.[^nature]

## Arc Three — the chamber beneath Khaervilon

The giant was a source of enemies as well as a powerful attacker in its own right. When the adventurers broke into its chamber, it struck at Vorel while skeletons emerged from its shoulders. Further wounds became additional openings, producing more skeletons as the fight continued. Hurting the giant could therefore increase the number of foes around it.[^spawning]

Fire destroyed many of the skeletal attackers, but new ones continued to emerge until Stanley's animated blade, Diplomacy, killed the giant. Its body collapsed and the spawning stopped, leaving the adventurers to destroy the remaining skeletons.[^end]

## Current status

The giant and the skeletons it had produced are destroyed. Its creator and the means by which its body generated the undead remain unknown. Other dangers still lay in the passages beyond its chamber.[^status]
