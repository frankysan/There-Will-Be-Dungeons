# Crater wyvern

## Introduction

A large winged reptilian beast accompanied the bugbear band at the newly green impact crater in the Wastes. Its dragonlike body carried a scorpionlike stinging tail, with venom that burned painfully after entering a wound. It combined diving attacks with bites and clawing blows.[^nature]

## Arc Three — the Wormfather's pet

The band’s robed spellcaster presented the wyvern as his pet, while the bugbears called it the Wormfather's. It circled above the crater before diving at Hope, stinging her side and biting into her shoulder and chest.[^attack]

The wyvern later turned its sting against Nash in his young black-dragon form. His body resisted the venom, and the beast's following claw strike missed. Wounded by the company's attacks, it eventually landed, exhausted from flying.[^wounds]

Stanley thrust Diplomacy toward it with a thunderous magical strike. The blast tore through the wyvern, rupturing its body and throwing it to the ground in front of Nash.[^death]

## Current status

The wyvern is dead at the crater. Its fall left the bugbear attackers ready to continue the fight; it did not end the confrontation.[^status]
