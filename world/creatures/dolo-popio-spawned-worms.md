# Dolo Popio's spawned worms

## Introduction

Dolo Popio releases large worms from his monstrous body during the battle at CHIDS. The first group has little feet, a green glow and swollen growths upon their backs. When struck, they burst into green acidic fluid, making nearby worms liable to explode in turn.[^green]

## Arc Three — living weapons

Six glowing worms spilled from Dolo Popio's mouth into the chamber. Hope's gunfire triggered successive explosions among them, breaking several into pools of corrosive goo.[^explosions]

Later, in the strange battlefield of floating debris, Dolo Popio exhaled more large worms in a spreading wave. They drifted outward through the weightless space toward the company. Nash destroyed several with his dragon breath while the others fought through the surrounding threats.[^breath]

## Current status

Several spawned worms were destroyed, and Dolo Popio's physical form was eventually defeated. The fate of every remaining worm is unknown; his defeat does not account for all the creatures he released.[^status]
