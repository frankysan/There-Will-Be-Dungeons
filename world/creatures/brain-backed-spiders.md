# Brain-backed spiders

## Introduction

An enormous black spider with an exposed, pulsing brain inhabited the depths of [CHIDS](../places/chids.md), accompanied by swarms of smaller spiders bearing similar brain-like markings. The giant descended through a damaged lift shaft, its shining eyes visible above the stranded travelers, while the smaller spiders fell around them.[^appearance]

## Arc Three — the CHIDS lift shaft

The spiders threatened both body and mind. Repeated bites from the small swarms poisoned Hope and left her exhausted. The giant could impose its will on Vorel, briefly controlling him. Nash's burst of fire broke that hold, freeing him to fight the creature again.[^powers]

The giant's size also made the shaft a dangerous battlefield. Stanley transformed it into a sheep, but the fall that followed merely stripped away the borrowed shape: the spider returned to its own body and remained a threat. Hope's gunfire finally killed it.[^defeat]

## Current status

The giant spider is dead. Vorel used its carapace to fashion a shield for Cerinthala and a smaller one for Hope's little artificial arm. A pouch of small brain-marked spiders was later found among Cerinthala's belongings; their origin and her connection to them remain unexplained.[^aftermath]
