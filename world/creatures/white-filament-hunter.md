# White filament hunter

## Introduction

A white, clawed predator lurks above an opening inside the enlarged froghemoth. Its shape resembles a scorpion, but its face is a large hole filled with teeth. A hanging filament catches passing prey and reels it upward toward the waiting mouth.[^appearance]

## Arc Two — a line above the acid

Vorel walked into the suspended line after climbing out of the stomach fluid. It wrapped around him and lifted him toward the predator. Stanley sent Diplomacy against the filament, cutting it and dropping Vorel back beside him.[^rescue]

The hunter survived the severing of its line. It moved along the walls, clung to the ceiling and lowered a new filament from another angle. The travelers avoided the renewed trap as they moved onward.[^renewal]

## Current status

The hunter was left alive inside the froghemoth. Its fate after the beast's body separated is unknown.[^status]
