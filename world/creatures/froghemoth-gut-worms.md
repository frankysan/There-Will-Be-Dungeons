# Froghemoth gut worms

## Introduction

Pink worms occupy the enlarged froghemoth's digestive passages. Those swimming in its stomach acid have toothed openings and small projecting tubes, which they press into swallowed pixies as though injecting them. Farther along the intestines, long fleshy worms swallow waste, visible bulges traveling down their bodies.[^forms]

## Arc Two — life within the beast

The stomach worms scatter from Nash's lightstone. They follow at the edge of its illumination, leaving him a moving space through which the company can swim. He believes their injections help immobilize swallowed prey for digestion.[^stomach]

The waste-processing worms behave differently. They show no visible eyes and do not react to the light. They continue feeding as the travelers pass, moving around them rather than attacking. Even after the froghemoth's lower body separates and begins dying, these intestinal inhabitants remain active.[^intestines]

## Current status

The company passed the intestinal worms without killing them. Their fate within the collapsing lower carcass is unknown, as is that of the worms left in the stomach.[^status]
