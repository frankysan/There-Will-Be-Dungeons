# Outlands wyverns

## Introduction

Wyverns attacked the Traveler's Twin as it crossed the Outlands toward the [White Tower](../places/white-tower.md). Flying alongside grey, winged beastmen, the creatures threatened the vessel with repeated diving impacts as well as dangerous bites and venomous stings.[^attack]

## Arc Four — predators against an airship

The wyverns' weight and force could break the ship's wooden structure and throw people from the deck. Their collisions sent Hope sliding over the stern and cast crew members into the open sky. One damaged attacker crashed into the vessel after Nash struck it with dragon acid, while a wyvern later broke through into the ship's interior.[^impacts]

The confined passages belowdecks brought their other weapons into play. A wyvern stung and bit Vorel, leaving him poisoned and badly injured before he killed it with his borrowed greatsword. Above, Stanley transformed a wyvern into a tiny rat, removing its flying form and sending it falling out of view.[^close]

## Current status

The attack ended with at least one wyvern dead. The animal transformed into a rat fell beyond the travelers' sight, leaving its eventual fate unknown. The fighting left the Traveler's Twin badly damaged.[^status]
