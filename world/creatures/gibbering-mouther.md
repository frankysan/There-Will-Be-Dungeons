# Gibbering mouther

## Introduction

A gibbering mouther inhabited [Leopold's underground facility](../places/leopold-facility.md). A great mass of pink flesh covered in blinking eyes and mouths, it dropped from the ceiling and blocked the travelers' exit after they activated the facility's self-destruct system.[^appearance]

## Arc One — flesh, voices and engulfment

Teeth along the creature's lower body gripped the ground and hauled it forward. Around it, the floor became a gum-like substance that could hold a person's feet fast. Its many voices were unintelligible to the travelers, yet their overlapping chatter intruded on concentration. Nested mouths could also spit globules that burst in dazzling flashes, temporarily blinding Nash.[^powers]

The mouther's body threatened to consume those caught close to it. It bit Chance unconscious and began drawing him into its flesh. Stanley restored him to consciousness, but his upper body remained enclosed and he struggled to breathe. Nash's fire tore away enough of the creature for Chance to pull his head free.[^rescue]

## Current status

The mouther is dead. After Eddie's final arrow struck its eyes, the flesh turned grey and hardened, its movement stopped and its voices fell silent. Chance escaped its body alive.[^end]
