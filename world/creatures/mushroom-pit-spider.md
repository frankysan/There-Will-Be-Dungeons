# Mushroom-pit spider

## Introduction

A gigantic spider lay concealed above a mushroom-lined pit beneath [T'ietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md). At first its body looked like part of the moving ceiling; as it descended, its skin changed into dark blue and green. Its eight legs spread around the people below, obstructing their escape.[^appearance]

## Arc Two — an ambush among luminous fungi

The spider's size made the narrow pit entrance a trap. Its legs braced against the surrounding walls while its body blocked the passage, and it attempted to bite Nash. A foot disturbed the mushrooms beneath it, releasing green particles that set him sneezing.[^ambush]

Vorel cut through the legs with Mawstorm, opening a way out. Unable to support itself, the spider slid backward and grasped at the travelers as it fell. They avoided its reach, and its body crashed into the pit's mushrooms, filling the chamber with more irritating spores.[^death]

## Current status

The spider is dead, its curled body at the bottom of the pit. Vorel gathered severed legs as food for the company.[^remains]
