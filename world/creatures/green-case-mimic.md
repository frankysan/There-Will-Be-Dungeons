# Green-case mimic

## Introduction

A mimic in [Leopold's underground facility](../places/leopold-facility.md) resembled a large green military case. It rested on a shelf with straps, a handle, a combination lock and a hazard marking, offering the appearance of a sealed container worth investigating.[^disguise]

## Arc One — the living case

When Stanley reached to open it, the two halves spread apart and revealed teeth and a long tongue. The creature sprang from the shelf and clamped onto him. Its tongue could wrap around a victim while its toothed sides bit down, making the case's apparent opening into a trap.[^attack]

The creature bled when wounded, and the hinges joining its halves proved vulnerable to Stanley's blade. Breaking one hinge freed Chance, but the injured mimic continued fighting. It caught Nash by the leg and pulled him down hard enough to knock him unconscious. Only when Stanley broke the remaining hinge did the halves and tongue fall apart and stop moving.[^defeat]

## Current status

The mimic is dead. Chance offered its severed tongue to the [eight-legged creature](eight-legged-facility-predator.md) sharing the laboratory. A metal tube was later pried from a fleshy fold in the corpse.[^remains]
