# Froghemoth intestinal ropers

## Introduction

Large tentacled predators inhabit the enlarged froghemoth's intestinal passages. One first appeared to be a towering rock formation, then opened a central eye and spread long tendrils from a fleshy mound. A large mouth waited beneath them.[^appearance]

## Arc Two — predators in a dying body

The tendrils wrap around intruders, pinning their arms and drawing them toward the predator. The first roper caught members of the company as they searched the lower carcass. Hope's explosive shells tore its body apart, and Vorel's blade split what remained. Its tendrils fell lifeless.[^first]

A second roper guarded another passage. It seized Vorel, but the company freed him and fled. The creature made another unsuccessful grab at Stanley before encroaching intestinal ooze hid it from view.[^second]

## Current status

The first roper is dead. The second was still alive when the travelers escaped its reach; its fate within the collapsing carcass is unknown.[^status]
