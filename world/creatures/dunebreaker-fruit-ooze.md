# Dunebreaker fruit ooze

## Introduction

A red, translucent ooze flecked with fruit haunted the [Dunebreaker's](../places/dunebreaker.md) larder long before Nash and his companions left Dusthill. It could rise from a puddle, lash out at intruders and slip through the central drain, making the ship's food stores dangerous to enter.[^larder]

## From dessert to a threat to the ship

The cook, known as Dipshit, said he had bought gelatin and a dessert recipe at a market, then prepared fruit jelly against Ember's instructions to use only the ship's own ingredients. The dessert became the mobile creature the guards encountered in the kitchen. What first brought it to life remained unexplained.[^origin]

The drain offered the ooze both an escape and a route to growth. Beneath the landship, it absorbed sewage and other matter until enormous translucent tentacles could rise on either side of the vessel. Bodies, glass, metal and debris hung within its mass. Its acid and reach threatened the ship's guards and the marauders meeting them outside, turning a kitchen hazard into a danger to everyone around the hull.[^growth]

## Current status

The ooze is destroyed. Al opened the Dunebreaker's steam vents, breaking its vast body apart with superheated vapor. A final small globule containing a cherry landed beside Ramilda; she stamped it into inert liquid. The Dunebreaker survived the attack.[^end]
