# Tyrannosaurus of the purple sands

## Introduction

An enormous Tyrannosaurus approached the travelers' camp in the rain-soaked purple sands near [Khaervilon](../places/khaervilon.md). Lightning revealed its huge head and small forelimbs, while its approach sent tremors through the muddy ground. Its snout nudged Fate 4 as it sniffed around the parked vehicle.[^arrival]

## Arc Three — the storm and Fate 4

The animal's weight made the camp vulnerable even before a prolonged fight could begin. Vorel emerged from beneath Fate 4 and tried to draw it toward Nash's deep storm-drain pit. The predator crushed the vehicle as it followed him, then fell into the excavation. The fall alone did not kill it.[^attack]

Nash cast fire into the pit, and Vorel's returning flame shield finally brought the dinosaur down. Its remains became useful material amid the ruined camp: Vorel fashioned a tooth into a dagger and combined bones with recovered vehicle parts to build a rickshaw. The improvised cart offered transport after the loss of Fate 4, although the forest ahead soon made it impractical.[^remains]

## Current status

The Tyrannosaurus is dead. Its attack left Fate 4 crushed, and a subsequent explosion burned the wreck. Its teeth and bones were reused in a dagger and an improvised cart.[^status]
