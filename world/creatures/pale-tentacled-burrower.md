# Pale tentacled burrower

## Introduction

A pale, wormlike animal lives in the caverns beneath [T'ietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md). Its long body blends with the white and yellowish mucous coating on the walls. Two broad grey surfaces resemble eyes, while numerous slender tendrils surround its feeding opening.[^appearance]

## Arc Two — a warning in the dark

The travelers first recognized the animal when a length of the seemingly coated wall began to move. It raised its head toward them and made a soft, reverberating vibration from within its body, holding a threatened, territorial pose.[^warning]

As the company backed away, the burrower held its ground rather than pursuing. It then lowered its face to the dirt, scratched out a hole and disappeared underground. Vorel could still sense its digging through the earth.[^retreat]

Farther through the caves, the travelers found a stone form resembling the same kind of animal. Its familiar eye-like surfaces and trailing mouth tendrils remained visible, though the body appeared partly eaten. They could not establish what had turned it to stone or whether it was the animal they had spared.[^stone]

## Current status

The living burrower was last sensed digging beneath the cavern floor. The later stone body has no established connection to that individual's fate.[^status]
