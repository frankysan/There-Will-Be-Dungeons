# Black headworms

## Introduction

Small black, leechlike worms have been found among the brain tissue of several peoples of the Wastes. They remain alive after a host's death and can crawl toward nearby people or try to fasten onto exposed flesh. One removed from a bugbear was only a few inches long.[^nature]

## Arc Three — discoveries beneath the skin

Vorel found a living worm while examining a dead bugbear's brain. When dropped, it slowly followed him across the sand until Nash destroyed it with dragon acid. Vorel believed it whispered when held close, though the company learned no clear message from it.[^crater]

In Razak Val, Vorel saw dark shapes moving behind Zercil's eyes and worms pressing against them. He suspected an outside influence upon her. A separate black worm found burrowing through her bedding was caught in a bowl of sand, giving the company a living specimen.[^razak]

At CHIDS, Hope found another in a dead illithid's head. It crawled onto her metal arm and tried to attach to her flesh before she shook it off and crushed it. Worms also emerged from two dead lizardfolk guards' heads; Hope and Vorel destroyed those as well.[^chids]

## Current status

The worms recovered from the CHIDS heads are dead. The captured bedding worm's later fate is unknown. The discoveries leave the extent of infestation, the worms' origins and their influence upon living hosts unresolved.[^status]
