# Bloodsucking desert swarm

## Introduction

A vast dark swarm crosses the dunes between [T'ietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md) and Razak Val. From a distance it resembles a black distortion spreading across the horizon; as it approaches, its noise rises like a chorus of mosquitoes. Diptak recognizes the cloud as bloodsuckers and warns that their tiny bodies and overwhelming numbers make ordinary shelter unsafe.[^appearance]

## Arc Three — shelter beneath the sand

Diptak first sought refuge by digging into a dune. He feared the swarm would find gaps in a hut and overwhelm anyone exposed, though Stanley assured him that his magical shelter could bar the creatures.[^warning]

The travelers and their riding raptors entered Stanley's Tiny Hut. Nash drew a canopy of sand over it, giving the company a covered refuge in which to rest as the cloud approached.[^shelter]

## Current status

The company avoided an open confrontation with the swarm and continued toward Razak Val. The bloodsuckers were not destroyed, and their subsequent movement is unknown.[^status]
