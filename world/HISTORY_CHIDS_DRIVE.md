# CHIDS and the Traversal Drive crisis (E067–E082)

**Browse:** [Historical events](HISTORICAL_EVENTS.md) · [World portal](README.md)

**Historical layer:** The ancient [CHIDS](places/chids.md) installation beneath [Kervalon](places/kervalon.md). **Played crisis:** E067–E082. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional.

## Discovery of the ancient complex

Seeking abducted Sira and ancient technology, the original protagonists enter ruined **Kervalon** and descend toward **CHIDS**, which identifies itself as the **Center for Human-Demon Interdisciplinary Studies**. Their expedition encounters hostile illithids, simulated offices, labs, a dangerous red memory-eater, animal/human experimentation and living containment tissue. **Cerinthala** appears, dies, revives and betrays them; the laboratory physically contains lizardfolk subjects and programming controls. Such things demonstrate **specific abusive experiments**, not a complete explanation of the origin of all lizardfolk. [E067](../episodes/E067.md)–[E078](../episodes/E078.md).

The party recovers the **dual-color portal device** E075, later exploiting its paired gateways; it is **not the larger Traversal Drive**. The living hive claims to be former archived minds defending against illithids, and tells stories of divine anti-dragon projects including **GR0X1**. Physical features are witnessed but the hive's antiquarian interpretation remains testimony. [Portal device](technology/portal-device.md) · [Eye of Groxl](technology/eye-of-groxl.md).

## Entering and operating the Drive

The **Traversal Drive** is **physically entered E079**, following earlier speculation about relocating Earth and a false-progress upload in a simulated office. **Nash kills a giant commander E080**, and the party defeats the manifested **Dolo Popio E081**. **Quizquaz** presents dangerous interpretations of the machinery, pacts and Earth. The four heroes **reject destroying the planet** and accept a crimson-beam sacrifice; they vanish in white light and are **GM-declared dead at E081 cutoff**. **Sira escapes alive with Eddie; Chance's later location is unresolved.** [E079](../episodes/E079.md)–[E081](../episodes/E081.md).

## The later cutoff

In **E082**, the protagonists are again encountered in Sigil in altered conditions. Thus the E081 declaration was a real earlier scene/status, **not confirmation of their permanent deaths**. Their transition does not provide a complete Earth-side survey or prove every account of what the Drive did. [E082](../episodes/E082.md) · [Chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md).

**Open:** machine's ultimate condition, planetary damage, builders, time effects, Chance's fate, hive's historical reliability. [Drive](technology/traversal-drive.md) · [Ledger](CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
