# CHIDS — experiments and the Traversal Drive crisis

Beneath the ruined stronghold of Khaervilon lies CHIDS, an immense laboratory from Earth's earlier age. Once dedicated, by its own surviving institutional account, to human and demonic research, its automated corridors and living containment systems outlasted the people who built them. The companions' search for the abducted Sira brought them through the remains of an experimental program and into a struggle over a machine that others claimed could change the fate of the world.[^facility]

<a id="chids-and-the-traversal-drive-crisis-e067e082"></a>

## Discovery of the ancient complex

The descent reveals abandoned offices, transit routes, frozen data halls and laboratories where organic tissue has grown around the machinery. The facility's automated guides describe human-demon collaboration, but their institutional explanations cannot by themselves establish when each program began or what it ultimately achieved. The companions find surviving lizardfolk subjects, restrained organisms, weaponry and equipment apparently designed for training and control. In one chamber, dead lizardfolk lie in vessels beside screens and commands for altering behavior and teaching skills. Those discoveries show deliberate experimentation on particular subjects without establishing that CHIDS created every lizardfolk or other people of the Wastes.[^laboratories]

Cerinthala, a surviving elf connected to the complex, assists the travelers, dies in one containment passage, appears alive again and ultimately betrays them. Deeper within, a wall of living flesh speaks as a collective intelligence. It claims to preserve human minds, to have opposed an illithid incursion and to have participated in ancient anti-dragon projects. A broken vessel bears the designation GR0X1, to which Vorel's Eye of Groxl reacts strongly; the collective's account of that vessel and of a captive winged experiment remains a claim about origins, not a verified history of the Eye. Another flesh-made figure professes to be an older Nash who remembers a destroyed Earth, but neither his identity nor the supposed future is proven.[^hive]

The explorers also seize a small device that makes paired red and green passages and can be used as a dangerous beam weapon. It is **not** the vast Traversal Drive in the engineering complex. With the living intelligence's assistance they reach nearer the Drive, recover two fellow travelers, Chance and Eddie, and continue the attempt to free Sira.[^portal]

## Entering and operating the Drive

The machine's engineering chamber contains immense transformed structures, a central column of light and illithids guarding a captive Sira. A spectral dragon has spoken of a mechanism capable of moving Earth, while the facility intelligence and its enemies provide conflicting warnings about its purpose. The companions can see the apparatus and its active effects, but no complete account of its creation or true planetary mechanism is demonstrated. They fight through the defenders: Nash kills a powerful illithid commander, and Hope ultimately destroys the manifested body of the dragon-worm Dolo Popio in a shifting, space-like battlefield.[^drive]

When the surroundings return to the chamber, Stanley, Eddie and Sira are confined within the luminous column. Chance has drifted beyond reach during the preceding battle and does not reappear. Hope tests the controls, which change the light and cause alarming effects without providing an evident safe shutdown. Quizquaz then offers the company a terrible bargain: allow the world to be destroyed, supposedly defeating the Principal, or let a willing subject absorb the beam's power. His explanation of its source in the Void and of the supposed consequences belongs to an interested demon, though the subsequent manipulation of the controls has directly visible effects.[^bargain]

The heroes refuse to sacrifice Earth. Eddie and Sira are freed from the column, while Stanley, Hope, Vorel and Nash choose to bear its power together rather than leave Stanley alone. Eddie operates the controls from outside; the beam turns crimson, overwhelms the four and dissolves them into white light. Their apparent deaths are not merely a forecast: the storyteller declares them dead as the scene ends. The machinery's continued condition and the full consequences on Earth are not shown in that moment.[^sacrifice]

## The later cutoff

The next part of the story finds the four in Sigil under sharply different conditions. Vorel returns conscious in a living lizardfolk body. Nash is aware within a floating heart-like form, not restored to his familiar body. Hope is found motionless with limbs missing; Stanley is cold and still described as dead. Their presence beyond the beam does not erase the declaration made in the chamber or establish that all four returned alive at once. Eddie and Sira do not appear in that immediate aftermath, and Chance is still unlocated.[^aftermath]

## Lasting legacy

CHIDS leaves material proof of a civilization willing to combine advanced machinery, living tissue and coercive experiments, but the witnesses and surviving intelligences disagree about its original ambitions. Its supposed species-creation program, archive of human minds, relation to divinity and ability to relocate or destroy the planet remain unsettled. The companions survive into later parts of the campaign, and Earth itself is eventually restored to greenery and rain; neither outcome proves the Traversal Drive was physically dismantled, permanently shut down or understood.[^legacy]

The history of the people and choices behind the crisis is distinct from the [CHIDS site](places/chids.md), the [Traversal Drive](technology/traversal-drive.md) as a machine and the [portable portal device](technology/portal-device.md). The fate of the old engineering installation, the full history of its builders, the extent of its damage and the ultimate mechanism of the beam remain open questions.[^legacy]
