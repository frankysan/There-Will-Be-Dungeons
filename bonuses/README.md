# Cast conversations, Q&As and retrospectives

The cast discuss character choices, setting ideas, unexpected turns and possibilities left unexplored. These conversations offer another way into the stories while keeping a clear distinction between what happened in play and what someone later explained, imagined or planned.

## Campaign retrospectives

- **The Wastes:** [final cast roundtable](../episodes/S3E019.md), separate from the played finale.
- **Delvers:** [post-campaign conversation](../episodes/BONUS-2021-11-13.md), including design choices and unused possibilities.
- **Inverse:** [October 2019 discussion](../episodes/BONUS-2019-10.md) and [January-publication 2021 conversation](../episodes/BONUS-2021-01.md); possible future material is not a recorded continuation.

The labels below follow the archive filenames. They help locate a discussion; they are not an in-world timeline or a guarantee of the recording date. Each dossier identifies the campaigns and topics it actually discusses.

Creator intent, unused preparation, audience hypotheticals and character testimony do not become performed story events. Detailed transcript comparisons and uncertainty remain in the linked dossiers.
