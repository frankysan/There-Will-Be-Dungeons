# E099 — Now We Have a Moon

> **The Wastes · S1E099 · Arc Three · provisional full-episode ASR research, not audio-verified.** Sources: [Parakeet processed SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E099.%20Now%20We%20Have%20a%20Moon.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E099.%20Now%20We%20Have%20a%20Moon.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [full-export ASR comparison](../reports/E099_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). The source is a **played White Tower battle session**, not a bonus or one-shot. [E098](E098.md) ended with Nash's black dragon and the reappearing, formerly banished demon in the same square, the unopened **Zorander's Fist** chest beside **Knight Commander Alentris Vayne**, Vorel roped to a winged assailant, and Hope on her broom. Neither party nor GM had yet resolved the overlap. E099's introduction **recaps** E098; the action begins afterwards.

## Played sequence and source cutoff

1. **Nash survives the overlap, but the opponent is physically embedded** (~00:06–00:09). Beau describes **both bodies convulsing**, the green winged fiend now projecting **perpendicularly from Nash's black-dragon torso** like a conjoined being. It retains its hostile mind and can fight; neither dies on reappearance. Nash can still cast, fly, move and attack, but **moving carries the attached enemy with him**. This establishes E098's deferred immediate consequence, **not** a permanent mutation or confirmed safe separation.
2. **The enemy caster rains Fire Storm on the roof** (~00:09–00:16). A smaller, green-skinned, yellow-eyed winged squadron commander appears by Alentris and actually casts **Fire Storm**. The GM assigns **40 fire damage** to Alentris and half or resisted damage to others according to their saves/positions; Vorel beneath a creature is shielded. These **caster-like green figures** are distinguishable from the giant axe-bearing green winged attackers. The approaching **pegasi-mounted paladins actually enter the fight**. Their most ornate rider, a **second knight commander, named Thayle/Thale (?)**, physically **takes custody of the still-closed floating Fist chest from Alentris** while mounted. This is the first played transfer toward the celestial escort, **not delivery to Mount Celestia**.
3. **Vorel wounds his rope-caught flier but is not enlarged** (~00:16–00:24). The GM clarifies that Nash's **Enlarge effect on Vorel is no longer in force** because of broken concentration. Vorel holds the rope **in his teeth** to swing his two-handed, newly acquired **Flame Tongue greatsword** using the spoken command **“Fangs Inferno.”** He hits the winged fiend twice, including a critical blow that pulls out an organ. The surviving target produces **multiple apparent copies** around Vorel; the illusion/copy effect is later tested by Hope's shots. A second hostile aerial squadron arrives from the north. One celestial rider and its Pegasus suffer lethal or devastating attacks from the giant fliers; some nearby riders remain active.
4. **Hope tests copies; Stanley continues Telekinesis** (~00:25–00:38). From her broom, Hope shoots different apparent copies of Vorel's foe; **one disappears when hit** while another shot misses, supporting an illusion/copy rather than four independently confirmed enemies. Stanley **actually drags the first Fire Storm caster onto White Tower's roof with Telekinesis**, where it **explodes on impact**. Stanley uses **Mantle of Inspiration** for allies; spell arithmetic and Roll20 map adjustments are game mechanics, not separate in-world travel.
5. **Nash's first random consequence is a fatal-picture conviction, not a death** (~00:37–00:44). Beau has Nash roll four d10s to choose a result from a very large table while the demon remains conjoined. Nash becomes **absolutely convinced he will one day drown inside a picture/painting of a lake**. This is a **GM-imposed mental conviction** or prediction-like magical thought, **not an observed future death or established cosmological prophecy**. The GM explicitly says it might never affect his actions; the exact interpretation is still open. Nash's first attempted close-range Fire Bolt is replaced after they clarify he is **still a dragon**, and he **actually bites/claws the embedded attacker**. Those attacks do not obviously wound Nash himself.
6. **A second hostile commander forces a southern movement** (~00:49–01:03). Another green commander, riding a black flaming **Nightmare**-like mount, casts **Mass Suggestion** to **go south**. **Vorel and several celestial riders, including the chest-carrying Thayle (?)**, fail and start obeying, while Stanley resists and Nash is out of range. The table confirms this does **not require creatures to walk off the tower and knowingly kill themselves**. Under the compulsion and still raging, **Vorel leaps from his rope and kicks the chest-carrying rider off her Pegasus**, seizing the mount. **The closed Fist chest falls but lands on the rooftop**, rather than immediately falling from the whole tower. **Stanley actually casts Feather Fall** on the displaced commander, slowing her descent toward the ground; **she is not declared dead**. Later attacks by the caster's allies interrupt Vorel's compulsion under the table's reading of the spell; he regains agency.
7. **Fighting spreads, and the chest changes hands again** (~01:04–01:21). Vorel is battered while aboard his reluctant Pegasus; Hope's later bullet **actually kills the real rope-linked flier**, eliminating its copies. Hope also shoots the black flaming horse, without stopping it. A rooftop archer pair of tall, black-armored winged women appears and **wounds Hope with arrows and poison damage**, although her saving throws prevent an additional poison condition. The previously charmed celestial squad scatters south; some Pegasus and rider deaths are **depicted explicitly**, others merely fall or flee. **Alentris recovers the chest where it landed, finds an unoccupied Pegasus and mounts it**. The Fist remains **inside**; there is no successful opening or handoff beyond the temporary Thayle possession.
8. **Nash decapitates the embedded demon, but its attached body remains alive/active for a time** (~01:30–01:36). After the failed/ineffective smaller attacks, Nash **flies his dragon body hard past a battlement and shears off the attached demon's head** (Athletics check **22**). The GM describes the **head separating while the attached torso still moves and is sustained by Nash's life**, with the join burning and demonic blood beginning to spread through Nash's system. This resolves the enemy's immediate ability to bite/attack with its head, **not the ongoing physical attachment or infection-like magical risk**. A new large random-effect roll **doubles the size of a nearby Pegasus, its rider and two winged enemies**. This is actually played: later combat uses the giant sizes. The title does **not** refer to this enlargement.
9. **Another ring of fire and unsuccessful rescue maneuvers** (~01:36–02:09). A hostile mounted spellcaster **creates a Wall of Fire around Stanley**; it encloses him without automatically dealing damage while he stays inside. Vorel later **leaps through the wall to join Stanley**, taking resisted fire damage, and extends his own fire-resistance aura. Stanley uses **Dimension Door to transport himself and Vorel onto the Nightmare horse behind a green caster**; his offhand dagger strike misses and the caster subsequently **escapes/invisibly relocates**. Meanwhile Vorel and Stanley **attack the Nightmare**, and another very large demon attacks them. Several celestial mounts and paladins die amid the siege; one of the rooftop archers is **killed when Stanley presses her into the battlement using Telekinesis**. Hope's gunshots contribute damage and one actual flier/copy resolution but do **not** complete her proposed E098 dive-bomb.
10. **Nash continues casting, and further random effects affect the world** (~02:00–02:45). Nash **casts Banishment again**, temporarily dismissing a now-enlarged winged attacker; the earlier conjoined demon's **headless body remains attached**. On a random-effect roll Nash acquires **knowledge of where an addictive euphoric substance can be found**; this is knowledge from magic, **not an observed purchase, ingestion or addiction**. Later a hostile attack makes him fail concentration and the **banished enlarged enemy actually reappears**. Separately the remaining mounted attacker damages the Nightmare until a massive green flier **destroys the mount**, and Stanley uses **Feather Fall on himself and Vorel**, allowing a safe descent. This is a **second Feather Fall event**, separate from saving Thayle. **Neither Stanley nor Vorel dies in that fall.**
11. **The closed Fist chest and Alentris both fall off the tower** (~02:19–02:39). Alentris is **actually mounted on a Pegasus and flying away from the roof with the closed chest**, apparently toward the northern route; a hostile black-armored flying archer fires at the pair. **Arrows wound Alentris, and a critical hit kills her Pegasus**. Alentris **loses her grip on the chest**; the **commander, dead Pegasus and unopened chest are all falling from the outside of White Tower** at the end of their depicted motion. **Alentris is NOT declared dead**, the chest is **NOT recovered from its fall**, and Stanley cannot reach her with Feather Fall from his position. This **supersedes E098's chest-on-roof custody and the E099 earlier, temporary transfer to Thayle**.
12. **A sudden moon appears—final played cliffhanger** (~02:43–02:47). Following a d10,000-style roll due to Nash's unresolved demon-body magic, **day suddenly becomes night and a huge, unnervingly close moon appears**. **A city is visible on its surface.** Beau expressly calls the incident an **unplanned random wild-magic-table result**, not a secret prewritten plot beat. The party does **not** travel to the moon, identify its city, learn how it came to be or restore daylight. The group **chooses to stop the session to let Beau process the unexpected change**, rather than play a few more turns. The siege and cliffhanger continue beyond the end. No named celestial courier successfully leaves with Fist, and no Earth passage occurs.

## Custody, continuity and uncertainty at E099 cutoff

- **Zorander's Fist:** As far as the played scene shows, **still sealed in the closed chest**. It moves **Alentris → Thayle (on Pegasus) → roof after Vorel's kick → Alentris (on another Pegasus) → falling off the tower**. No one is shown opening it; **no confirmed impact location, new keeper, recovery, theft, Mount Celestia delivery, Tobacho return, or Earth transit**. The **separate real pendant key** was last documented with Stanley; no handover to Walnut occurs in E099.
- **Alentris / Thayle:** They are **distinct commanders**. **Thayle's fall is slowed by Stanley's first Feather Fall**, and the scene depicts her descending, not her eventual arrival or death. **Alentris later falls with her slain mount and relinquished chest**, and her fate is **unresolved**; do not transpose the earlier Feather Fall protection to this later fall.
- **Nash:** **Alive and in black dragon form**, conjoined with the **headless demon body**, whose residual join burns; actual lasting bodily consequences unknown. The drawing-lake fatality is a **belief/mental magic**, not an accomplished death. The enlargement event is observed, knowledge of euphoric substance learned but not acted upon, **new city-bearing moon exists in scene** but origin/fate unspecified. The second Banished foe has returned after concentration failed.
- **Vorel / Stanley / Hope:** **Vorel is no longer under Mass Suggestion**, and he and Stanley **Feather Fall safely after Nightmare destruction**; they remain exposed in an active siege. Hope continues **flying her broom**, is wounded by archers, and **has not carried out the hoped-for dive-bomb**. Multiple enemies and surviving defenders are still in play.
- **Proof and hazards:** The episode's regular **grounded combat effects, GM death declarations, enchanted compulsions, and wild-magic outcomes** must be separated from rules debate and joking about moon residents. The exact mechanics of the attachment and random table (including whether the lake conviction is a guaranteed fate) remain unverified beyond the GM's played words. **The moon, its city, Nash's body, the Fist's whereabouts and any future homecoming are not resolved by this recording.**
