## The Elhandar doctors and a key from the past (~00:02–01:02)

1. **Dinner at the cottage, not a forced therapy appointment (~00:02–00:21).** **Admiral Chrysorius/Chrysoerius (?)** comes with **five elves**—four therapists and a younger dark-clad apprentice candidate—carrying **food gifts for the cottage**. The party jokes that inviting them inside might expose them to vampires, but **the visitors all reflect in mirrors**; no vampiric combat or reveal occurs. Lyrielle prepares and later serves dinner. The elf visitors identify the four assigned doctors: **Dr. Zanzafir/Zanzapyer (?)**, **Dr. Tindalanthalas/Tyndalanthalas (?)**, **Dr. Mahasiabi (?)**, and **Dr. Joam Emantha (?)**. Other doctors are mentioned for **Angelashi**, **Bok Bok** and **Chunks**, one name heard **Brandy Lockripple (?)**. These names/assignments need audio confirmation. **Being assigned a therapist is required on Elhandar, attending sessions remains optional**; *no therapy session or psychological treatment is completed* in the social visit.
2. **An actual handed-over heirloom (~00:32–00:39).** During the introductions **one of the assigned female doctors hands Stanley a large ornate key** and says it belonged to his father **Delit Or'Nath**, who left it with the Elhandari many years before with instructions that Stanley receive it. The doctor says **the key opens something in this cottage**, but neither she nor the doctors know **where**; she urges Stanley to search when ready. The GM explicitly tells him to **add the cottage key to his inventory**. **The transfer is real**, not an offer to find the key later. More about the father and the origin of his key comes only afterward.
3. **The admiral tells Vorel Omnom is nearby (~00:24–00:30).** In a private aside the admiral says **Omnom survived**, reportedly felled a dozen or more demons before being incapacitated, and has been **released into Elhandar but is struggling to adjust**. The admiral does not know his exact whereabouts and asks **Vorel to seek his countryman**. That is first **NPC testimony**, not yet a physical encounter, but **the later E118 meeting confirms Omnom alive**. Elhandar's treatment plan may include returning Omnom to the planet if the biodome does not suit him; **no return transport is performed**.
4. **A possible apprentice, not yet a new party member (~00:45–00:57).** A young black-clad elf, **Dilenoria/Dylanoria (“Dilo”?)**, recognizes **Stanley's actions during E114's undead-pirate boarding**. He admires the half-elf's abilities and asks to **accompany him as apprentice/squire**. He says he has studied historic **human unarmed martial arts**, has little real combat practice and wants to learn. **Stanley does not accept him into a mission or formally make him a permanent companion**: he says Dilo should **return tomorrow morning**, after Stanley has discussed the proposition with the others. **No apprentice adventure occurs in E118**.
5. **Dinner and an unresolved question (~00:41–01:01).** Nash, Hope, Stanley and Vorel all attend the civilized feast with doctors; **Nash meets his own enthusiastically assigned therapist**. The group discusses their adventures and the elven approach to mental health, but does not sign contracts. After the elves leave **Stanley chooses to follow the key mystery**, while the others offer to help or seek Omnom. **All four ultimately go outside toward the lizardfolk first**, deferring the key's search until they return.

## Omnom returns and Hope is held to account (~01:02–01:31)

6. **A real reunion (~01:02–01:08).** In the simulated but living forest around the cottage, **Vorel smells and calls to Omnom**. **Omnom Cinderborn physically emerges**, severely injured: **part of his mouth/face missing, many scales gone, deep scars, arm/leg in casts, disheveled bandages**. **He has not died, been resurrected, or healed to full function**. He tells Vorel he fought **“chicken lords”** around **Razak Val** for **five nights** after Vorel left, watching wounded/infected lizardfolk die, before abandoning the fight rather than dying. His account is **his report**, not a new E118 enactment of the old war.
7. **Omnom attacks Hope but is stopped (~01:07–01:13).** Upon noticing **Hope** in the group, Omnom moves to attack her: **initiative is rolled and Stanley successfully casts *Hold Person***, **paralyzing him temporarily**. **No blow lands, Hope is not wounded or killed by Omnom**. Vorel physically **carries the restrained Omnom roughly 120 feet farther into the trees**, where he releases him as the spell expires. Stanley keeps Hope within sight while the group gathers. **This is a real attempted hostile encounter resolved without a combat casualty**.
8. **Omnom's allegations and Hope's actual answers (~01:12–01:26).** Omnom alleges **Hope's explosive rocket arm killed his family at Razak Val**, she set Tietkalla's **Principle-linked railway station on fire**, and **deliberately led the demon army into the city before escaping through a portal**. **Vorel had not known Hope's rocket propelled him against Orp**; Hope **admits firing it** but says Vorel wanted that attack. **She acknowledges the station burning**, but says **the Hand drew the demons because they sensed its power**, and **she fled through Gate hoping the army would follow her away from Tietkalla**. She **does not confess to intentionally inviting an invasion or ordering the demons**. Distinguish **Omnom's sincerely held accusatory account** from **Hope's own explanations and already-played E103–E105 outcomes**; the train fire itself is earlier play, not a new E118 fire. **Vorel remains wary and asks questions**, not issuing a criminal verdict or renewing unquestioning trust.
9. **Grief and a planned hunt (~01:24–01:31).** Omnom is overwhelmed by what he describes as **sadness and dishonor**, asking Vorel to kill him. **Vorel refuses**, says Omnom carries surviving **Razak Val memory**, persuades him to live, and proposes a **joint hunt the next morning** as a test and possible path to healing his rift with Hope. **Hope, Nash and Stanley express willingness to join**, and Omnom agrees to follow Vorel. **Omnom then heads off to find shelter for the night** rather than remaining in the party's cottage. **No animal is hunted, Vorel doesn't kill Omnom, and no complete reconciliation is observed**. The E117 planned evening search is thus resolved **as locating Omnom and speaking with him**, not as carrying out the proposed hunt.

## The hidden floor and Dalit's records (~01:31–02:19)

10. **The house search initially fails (~01:31–01:39).** Back at the family cottage, Stanley, Hope, Nash and Vorel inspect the interior for a place that could take the ornate key. **Even board-prying and a broad search find no ordinary door or visible keyhole**. The group suspects magic. **Vorel and Nash move outside**; Hope stays with Stanley. In conversation **Stanley recalls the Elven meaning of his birth name as “the song at the end of the woods,”** and a lullaby or bedtime poem from his parents. **He actually recites the old poem**, beginning with verdant fields and ending with the song at the end of the woods (the exact text is an audio review target, not a certified quotation).
11. **Magic key and real pocket study (~01:39–01:46).** **The key actually lifts from Stanley's hand** as he recites the poem, floating upstairs; it falls when he stops. Hope joins him reciting/humming, and **the key enters an invisible keyhole at the upstairs landing**. **Stanley physically turns it**, activating heavy gears and **a descending staircase to a third floor impossible within the cottage's apparent exterior**. Both **Stanley and Hope actually climb into the secret study**. Its fittings include **wall charts, constellation and planet diagrams, an enormous mechanical Earth globe, a desk, an elaborately bound book and a gem-studded black mask**. The pocket-space appearance is GM description, not an independently mapped extradimensional coordinate.
12. **Book of Adjacencies acquired (~01:46–01:56).** Stanley **opens the heavy/infinite-page book**, stops pages and reads Elvish, while **Hope says she does not read Elvish**; the first page is accessed privately. GM identifies it as **Book of Adjacencies**, the **personal journals of Delit Or'Nath**, with **between one and limitless pages** that react to authorized voices. **Stanley is explicitly authorized to add the book to his inventory**. Speaking his name and topic causes pages to turn to relevant notes; authorization is tied to name/voice. **It is not a universal encyclopedia** and unread/unqueried entries do not become party knowledge.
13. **His father's letter is personal testimony (~01:51–02:00).** Addressed to **Valiir Or'Nath**, the father **claims to know the exact time/nature of his own eventual death and Stanley's future name before Stanley was born**, predicts his pain, and urges him to resist the **Weir**. He writes of meeting an anomalous, Nash-like being named **N4SH (?)** who came looking for Stanley, and of excursions to Earth looking for the anomaly's origin. He mentions his future love **Krista (?)**, the eventual **Solar Mines**, and hoped-for reunion in the **Feywild**, advising Stanley to die at Elhandar or in the Feywild so his spirit may reach them. **The message is genuinely found and read; the predictions, extrapolations and afterlife counsel are the father's claims, not observed future events.** Stanley recognizes a possible connection to the **alternate Nashes**, but **does not find or summon that anomaly here**. Hope gently hugs him; **he does not reject her touch**, which is progress but not complete forgiveness.
14. **A private command by the Voice (~01:59–02:04).** As **Hope leaves the study**, she sees the **hooded Voice in the upstairs passage**, visible to her only in the immediate scene. **Her altered Hand burns and becomes heavy**, and the figure urges an immediate journey to **Pentelvice/Pentalvise (?)** prison to complete its objective. Hope asks for more time. **No Gate is opened or Eye retrieved**. Meanwhile **Vorel passes a Stealth 26 listening check**, so he and **Nash are informed of what was said in the study**; distinguish that from automatically sharing the **private Voice encounter**, for which the GM uses a separate private aside.
15. **What the journal argues (~02:05–02:14).** Stanley **experiments with the book through the night**. The GM reads summaries/entries of Dalit's **theory that “adjacency” and N4SH's “time barf” travel arise because the Weir is breaching formerly mundane Earth**, distorting the fabric of time. He speculates the **Principle in a future era** used **Earth science and Weir energy** to create nonmagical-engineered anomalous beings, perhaps connected to **N4SH**; he judges the **Principle a threat to creation**, against the caution of Elhandari scholars who dismissed him as a conspiracy theorist. **These are unfinished journal theories, not verified cosmology, a proven direct Principle experiment or present-time ability of Nash.** The huge globe has **a red X apparently corresponding to the Solar Mines area** on **Handsome Dan Dakota's map**; this is **observed map correspondence**, not actual travel.
16. **Mask of Elton IV (?) and serious cost (~02:13–02:18).** Stanley is **told to add the jeweled `Mask of Elton IV / Elton Four / Eltenvor (?)` to his inventory**; it is shown to have no straps, fit the face magically and require **four successful long rests while worn to attune**. Its described ***Peer into Plurality*** ability interacts with future **d20 number results** at increasing psychic cost (first selection **30 psychic damage**, additional selected numbers **double cumulatively**, per GM item description); a crushed herb **cashib (?)** or heart of a time-flexible entity before rest is described as a counter reset, and dying to the mask's psychic harm may prevent ordinary resurrection except ***Wish***. **No attunement, action use, damaged wearer, triggered altered d20 roll, reset or death actually occurs in E118**. Record rules as **GM read-out**, not tested mechanical effects or established general laws.
17. **Night ends with the study still open (~02:18–02:19).** **Stanley studies the book and mask** while **Nash returns to Angelashi and their four children** for bedtime; the company has **not set out on the hunt**, and Dilo has **not returned the following morning**. The GM closes on Stanley among his father's papers. The host's teaser about next week's hunt is **out-of-game future planning**, not a played scene.
