## Key continuity boundaries

- **The E027 challenge is resolved in E028**. Diptak **yields and makes a clan pledge**, but the **Typala consort arrangement remains alleged**; no wedding or legally binding marriage is shown. The pledge is real spoken diplomacy; fulfillment is not yet witnessed.
- **Buttons's first in-story appearance is E028**. He is **an ordinary-origin, magically awakened cat with a prosthetic leg**, per his testimony, **not inherently a cat-person species** merely because of the title. His Arcanum background, local guide material and patron wizard name require audio/period written confirmation.
- **Teet information is mainly hearsay and an older map**. The Teet's Guzzle Gang, Suckmaster, Trashburg, Andreeste and Pit of Despair are Buttons/Dan-Dakota source leads. The party **does not enter the city in E028**.
- **Fate 4 repair** is partial; roof covered by raptor hide. The magical blast causes a new small hole. Never mark vehicle fully undamaged.
- **Episode boundary:** Hope's compelled departure closes E028; E029's special-session investigation and E030's rescue/collapse are not played here. **Do not misdate an encounter under the oil sands to E028**.
- **Owner note / audio hierarchy:** Names such as **Diptak, Typala, Arcanum Universalis, Andreeste, Razhak Val, Milinar's** are working source candidates, not editor-verified canonical spellings.
