# E072 — Weekend at Toby's

> **Corrected main-campaign E072**, following [E071](E071.md), documented from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E072.%20Weekend%20at%20Toby's.parakeet.processed.srt). **Not audio-verified.** Approximate recording times include listener mail, recap, intermission and outro. **Spoilers through E072**, stopping during combat.

## At a glance

The party enters **CHIDS's frozen data center through the ventilation duct** found in E071. Stanley leads through a confusing network of turns, dodges grates and fans, and casts **Heat Metal** to clear an ice-blocked section. The loosened metal duct **partially collapses beneath Hope**, with Nash and Vorel at risk as well, but **Stanley casts Feather Fall** and everyone descends safely into a giant **server-farm chamber** filled with old computer racks, enormous cooling fans, thick ice, and bodies frozen in place. **No Traversal Drive has been inspected.**

After an unsuccessful attempt to use a computer panel buried beneath ice to control the fans, a voice calls from the darkness. An elderly, much-shrunken elf woman introduces herself with a name heard roughly as **Cerinthala / Saranthala / Ceranthalis** (source spelling unresolved). She wears many layers of clothing and speaks incessantly to **four frozen human bodies** she calls **Toby, Carl, Gordon and Benson**; she attributes answers and opinions to them, although the party does not initially hear them reply. She claims she has been trapped **for thousands of years**, has **withered rather than dying** because she cannot reach the elves' resting place **Elhandar / Elandar**, and wants to escape Earth. **Her biography, religious doctrine and account of the dead are testimony, not verified objective history.**

In exchange for Stanley's promise that he will do what he can to get her to Elhandar, she offers a wooden totem **necklace**. **Stanley actually puts it on**, receiving **+1 AC** as ruled by the GM. The elf then reveals she previously **sealed the route to the engineering bay for a mind flayer**, in return for a promise of release, but uses a **data pad to unlock the way**. She identifies the data center as a point from which she can control functioning facility systems, suggests **ships at Gordon Shuttle Bay** might reach Elhandar, and claims pre-Scorching research **created lizardfolk as instruments for the dragon war**. She says the volatile **purple crystals in Laboratory A** were an environmentally preferable **anti-dragon weapon**, **detonating in response to sound** and growing organically. She also recalls a past site official she calls **“Principal Dombrovski”**, which **could** relate to the present Principle but is not confirmed to be the same person.

Stanley casts **Tiny Hut**, and the party **completes a full long rest**. Hope receives a distressed **sending-stone message** from Tietkalla, but the ASR is unusually unclear, especially the claimed emergency and sender; she **sends a reply summarizing the party's situation**. Hope later shares a bowl of the woman's **Malta/Malterweed soup**, finding it unexpectedly tasty despite having seen metal scraps placed in the pot. The elf claims **tieflings were produced through human–demon gene manipulation** and hints that the **Eye of Groxl** has dangerous dark magic, yet neither claim is independently verified.

When Stanley emerges from the hut after resting, **four huge ice figures containing frozen corpses arise**. One **grabs Stanley and carries him away**, three assault the hut and company. **Vorel rages, uses the flaming Eye of Groxl shield and then receives Nash's Enlarge spell**; Hope attempts to divert the captor. As the attackers carry Stanley off, **the totem necklace from Cerinthala tightens around his neck and begins choking him**. E072 **ends during this attack**, not after a rescue, curse removal or confirmed betrayal.

## Consequences / uncertainties

**Promise and cursed item:** Stanley's promise is **best-effort assistance** in taking the elf to Elhandar, not a binding public pledge of immediate departure, abandonment of Sira or success beyond his abilities. The **necklace grants +1 AC and is worn** at E072 end; its **choking effect activates during the ambush**, with cause and precise mechanism only clarified in [E073](E073.md). **Do not call it a harmless amulet or silently remove the curse.**

**Ancient experiments and chronology:** Her accounts of **lizardfolk created for the dragon war, tieflings as demon-serving constructs, noisy organic anti-dragon crystals, Elhandar and Principal Dombrovski** are high-value **NPC testimony** from an apparently ancient participant, not automatic narrator-certified cosmology. The **Eye of Groxl** remains the one owner-confirmed artifact; any “Grojil” form is its ASR approximation.

**Items and positions:** **One flying underwear item** moves **Vorel → Nash** within E072, ending with **Nash wearing it**. **Stanley is grabbed/choked** but alive, Hope pursuing, Vorel **Enlarged**, and four ice figures remain active at cliffhanger. **Gordon Shuttle Bay has not been revisited; no spacecraft flight**.

**Audio-review priorities:** Exact **Cerinthala** spelling/name and **Toby/Benson/Carl/Gordon** distinction (~01:00–01:10); **Elhandar/withered** testimony and **+1 AC neckpiece** (~01:13–01:28); **engineering unlock and lab history** (~01:26–01:39); **sending-stone sender and crisis** (~02:06–02:10); **soup ingredients versus Hope's taste and tiefling/Eye statements** (~02:10–02:24); **ice construct identity, grabbing Stanley, necklace's timing** (~02:27–02:43). Source SRT remains untouched.
