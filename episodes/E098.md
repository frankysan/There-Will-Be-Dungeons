# E098 — Roll for Arousal

> **The Wastes · Season 1 · Arc Three · provisional processed-ASR research, not audio-verified.** [Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E098.%20Roll%20for%20Arousal.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E098.%20Roll%20for%20Arousal.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [dual-ASR report](../reports/E098_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). Both sources end at **~02:51:08**, after credits/outro. The host calls this the show's hundredth episode; that statement does not change the archive's corrected **S1E098** ID. E097 left **Stanley and Alentris in the chapel after their private future-family discussion**, the rooftop celestial handoff planned but not performed, and the Fist still closed.

## Played developments and actual episode cutoff

1. **Two simultaneous Sending Stone messages force an urgent timetable** (~00:07–00:11). While Stanley tells **Knight Commander Alentris Vayne** he must keep his promise to bring his companions home before settling with her, she receives a message from the celestial side and Stanley hears **Captain Waltz**. Waltz reports sighting pegasi riders and says his ship is **following them**; he urges the party to seize this chance. Alentris says the celestial riders should arrive **within the hour**, not merely by nightfall. **Waltz's pursuit is a message, not a witnessed airship arrival or successful recovery of the relic.**
2. **The rooftop plan is clarified and disputed** (~00:10–00:20). Alentris expects the main pegasi force to distract the surrounding host while a smaller party collects **Zorander's Fist** from the tower roof. Stanley admits Tobacho's associates on Waltz's ship want it. The companions consider **falsely reporting their deaths/capture** to divert Waltz, but **Alentris refuses the lie on moral grounds**; no such reply is sent. Vorel distrusts the conveniently timed escort and suspects possible possession or deception, **without proof**. Alentris identifies **Knight Commander Thayle** as honorable, but her assurance is still a character statement. Stanley's hope of protecting Earth conflicts with Alentris's refusal to use falsehood as a tactic.
3. **Plans to move defenders are debated, not all executed** (~00:17–00:36). Alentris directs the party toward the basement blacksmith and discusses scouts: **Gregor and Clotchup** are suggested, but their actual departure is not followed. She begins preparing defenses. Stanley initially estimates his **Dimension Door** uses and one scroll, and the party proposes **Reverse Gravity** along the outside wall; they discover the latter spell is not on Stanley's class list. These are **alternatives under discussion**, not spells cast to lift the tower's garrison. Nash mentions **Teleportation Circle**, prompting Alentris to disclose an old circle in the basement. The commander reacts to Nash's disrespect but does **not** attack him. She leaves the four alone to prepare. Vorel continues to question whether any promised heavenly destination is real; there is no evidence she is possessed.
4. **A material scroll transfer does occur** (~00:34–00:36). Stanley hands **eight Reverse Gravity scrolls** to **Nash** after learning that Stanley cannot use the spell as expected; the GM and Nash explicitly enter the eight into Nash's inventory. They agree to investigate the circle and then speak to the blacksmith. This is **actual custody change**, separate from the Reverse Gravity exterior-ascent plan, which they never execute.
5. **The group reaches White Tower's dwarf blacksmith** in the deep cavern workshop (~00:36–01:00). The unnamed red-haired dwarf offers arms from his stores rather than manufacturing new items. **Vorel receives a golden-hilted greatsword** with a red gem and flame affinity; the GM ultimately identifies it as a **Flame Tongue greatsword**. Vorel chooses **“Fangs Inferno” as its command phrase**, activates its flaming blade during the visit, and considers naming it later; do **not** mistake the activation phrase for a verified formal artifact name. **Stanley receives a Corpse Slayer dagger** with strange faces and glyphs and **actually gives the dwarf a coin** under his customary exchange rule; the exact coin denomination is not established. The dwarf gives **Nash a Skyblinder staff** with a white-winged bird ornament, described as assisting attacks and reacting against flying attackers; the cast has trouble finding this item in the digital inventory, but the GM provides its text. **Hope claims a Broom of Flying** after it hovers when the dwarf tosses it aside. **Each of the four receives one ordinary Potion of Healing**, not a greater or superior potion. A demonstration or inventory discussion does not prove later combat use.
6. **The ancient teleportation room is actually found** (~01:00–01:07). After asking the blacksmith, the party enters a concealed side passage and a dusty domed chamber with old paladin statues and a large permanent inscribed circle. **Nash rolls Arcana 25 and the GM confirms the circle still works.** Nash copies/learns **White Tower in the Outlands as a destination** and the GM retroactively provides the spell's two initial **Material Plane** sigil destinations: **Tietkalla** and a second **unidentified place on Earth**. This is knowledge acquired, **not a teleport cast, new portal to Earth, or proof the party can cross planes with this spell**. The GM requires **rare chalks and inks infused with precious gems worth 50 gp**, not simply 50 gp in cash; Nash has no established supply of those components here.
7. **The party spends its remaining preparation time studying/attuning** (~01:07–01:14, across the break). Hope tests the broom in the chamber and then attunes; she prefers its eventual command words in **Infernal**, but the precise words are not stated. The GM confirms the item attunements/preparation succeeded. Vorel's command phrase, Stanley's dagger and Nash's staff are reiterated. **Walnut rushes in claiming the riders have reached the tower** and brings the party to the mustered defenders; his urgency does not confirm all earlier proposed scouts or preparations were carried out.
8. **The White Tower gates are really opened; the ground battle starts** (~01:14–01:25). Before roughly a hundred defenders, Alentris initially expects **Dimension Door** rooftop transport. Stanley announces the **Reverse Gravity** concept instead, and she orders the **main gate opened** to make that possible. Outside, ranks of demons surround the tower; arrows fly as knights with shields push out and engage a first wave of piglike attackers. The players rethink the risky exterior-ascent idea during battle setup, eventually **abandoning it without casting Reverse Gravity**.
9. **All five reach the roof, but by different means** (~01:25–01:31). Stanley casts **Dimension Door with Alentris**, who **is visibly guiding the closed Fist chest floating beside her**. Nash uses the **last available Dimension Door scroll** to bring **Vorel**. **Hope actually flies up on her newly acquired broom**. The four protagonists and Alentris assemble atop the tower, not through the planned Reverse Gravity movement. The relic chest is there **unopened and still under Alentris's escort**; it has not been delivered to Waltz, Tobacho or the celestial riders. Hope notices that **standing on the roof no longer injures/repels her** as on the earlier visit and *assumes* her continuing good-alignment effect is why; the cause is not independently confirmed.
10. **Hostile winged forces approach before the celestial escort** (~01:31–01:43). The party sees apparent pegasi in the northeast but a closer squadron of large green bat-winged axe/claw creatures accompanied by an airborne commander and black flaming horse attacks from the south/west. Ground siege machines and a giant whip-bearing figure are visible below; those ground creatures are **not engaged directly by the party on the roof**. Hope **hits** one approaching flier with a bullet; Stanley **successfully Polymorphs the commander's mount into a mouse**, causing the mouse to fall while the rider keeps flying. **The GM later infers that the fallen mount likely died once Polymorph ends; the fall's final body/outcome is not individually inspected.** Vorel ignites his Flame Tongue, Nash **actually becomes a black dragon**, and Alentris manifests a brilliant holy-light sword.
11. **Dragon acid fails against two targets; melee and magic continue** (~01:43–02:15). Nash breathes acid at two fliers; they fail their saves but the GM says it **does no damage**. Nash later bites/claws one, which attacks with an enormous axe and poisonous/festering claw; he saves against the additional claw effect. The accompanying smaller spellcaster **casts magical Darkness**, temporarily concealing Stanley and Vorel, then **turns invisible**. **Pegasi knights become visibly nearer**, and another celestial squadron is described engaging the wider host, but **no relic courier or handoff arrives on the roof**. Vorel successfully **lassos a flier's foot** and is lifted dangling beneath it; his subsequent attempt to tie a tightening knot **fails**, though he remains attached. A catapult blast **actually hits Hope for 11 fire damage**, while later shots miss the party.
12. **Two winged attackers die on contact with the roof** (~02:13–02:45). Stanley escapes the Darkness and **uses Telekinesis** to drag a flying foe down; the creature **bursts on impact**, showering him with gore. In a later turn he **repeats Telekinesis and slams another attacker into the roof**, where it too bursts. These are observed battle outcomes, unlike proposed attacks or unverified monster lore. Nash also **Banishes one opponent successfully**, temporarily removing it to another plane while maintaining concentration. Nash's dragon tail **successfully blinds** another attacker at the GM's discretion. Alentris trades blows with a flier, is clawed but resists its further effect, and uses her luminous weapon to blind and deeply wound it; the **“roll for arousal” dialogue is table banter**, not evidence of a lasting story status or an independently tracked mechanic. Hope flies on her broom above the flier carrying Vorel, **preparing** a dive-bomb to interfere with its wings. **She has not yet made that impact.**
13. **The episode ends on a dangerous unresolved overlap** (~02:45–02:48 played scene). Nash takes an opportunity hit, moves toward more secure footing and **casts Enlarge on Vorel**. Because this is another concentration spell, **his successful Banishment ends**, bringing that banished creature back **exactly where Nash's dragon body is now located** (the creature had been hidden on the GM layer). Beau describes the two occupying the **same space / grotesquely overlapping**, then **explicitly defers the consequence to next episode**. Do **not** state Nash died, the creature was destroyed, or that the overlapping bodies were safely separated. The Fist chest remains shut and undelivered; celestial extraction, Tobacho confrontation, Hope's threatening voice, and return to Earth **are all unresolved**.

- **Travel:** The White Tower circle is **identified and usable as a destination**, not activated; neither an immediate route to Earth nor an outbound planar escape. Nash now has its sequence and the GM-assigned Tietkalla/unknown-Earth sequences. **Component supply unresolved.**
