## Major continuity consequences

- **The Catherine Speckles problem is not just an alias:** In the same episode Tylander **assumes Catherine's appearance** as a demonstration of impersonation (~02:12), while another woman later **identifies herself as Catherine** and gives the Jeweler a personal message (~03:07). Record an **actual prisoner claiming Catherine identity** and **Tylander's separate impersonation**, rather than asserting the real Catherine never existed. The prisoner is **apparently dying/dead** by episode's end; no independent body-confirmation beyond party observation is documented.
- **Tylander's history and family:** His claims about **Pristine's fall**, **Oilbeards**, the **Principle**, and deaths of his family gain details from his **own spoken confession**. Compare with earlier **E013 GM background exposition** and the **signed journal in E020**; concordance does not prove all particulars, nor date exact atrocities. The diary lists **Mariosa**, **Daisy**, **Tobyl/Tobel**, **Lily**, while the chapel speech gives family roles; attach claims to their speaker and date.
- **Undead origin:** Tylander **takes credit for turning enslaved miners into undead**, explaining some phenomena of E018–E019, but this remains **actor confession** supported by physical undead evidence, not verified evidence that every worker has been accounted for.
- **The Fingers:** Eight visible followers in chapel **transform** when attacking; they are **not** automatically the same zombies already killed in elevator, nor are they the Oilbeard guards.
- **Dorothy and voices:** Dorothy speaks as an apparently enchanted door; that **does not** establish what she fundamentally is. Hope's **“find me”** voice remains **her private experience**, not knowledge shared with all party members.
- **End monster vs named patron:** The ~20-foot winged oily black creature **appears and speaks** at the E021 ending; at this stage it is *possible* this relates to **Catoblepraithe** (named by Tylander), **not yet explicitly confirmed**. Its actual allegiance, powers and combat outcome belong to E022.
- **Episode split:** E020 ends with the group inside **magical darkness**; E021 contains mushrooms, Dorothy, chapel, **Catherine's apparent death**, and the **giant-figure cliffhanger**. Never backdate the later chapel into E020.
- **Visual provenance:** Wiki-sourced [Catoblepraithe artwork](../sources/images/Catoblepraithe_-_Audie_Norman.webp) gives a **fan illustration/spelling lead**; it does not independently identify the E021 final creature or clear media reproduction rights. See [image register](../project/IMAGE_ASSETS.md).
