**Played cutoff:** **Diontalus is directly revealed and physically separates from Dilo's body**, leaving the actual **Dilo apparently dead**, and battles the party. **Diontalus escapes into the rock**, not confirmed dead. **Omnom Cinderborn is killed**, leaving only feet/stumps, by Diontalus's chain lightning. **Hope remains trapped alive in the magical sphere** filled with empathy grenades; no completed release. A gravely wounded **black dragon from the earlier desert imprisonment** intervenes, drives Diontalus off and **actually heals Vorel, Stanley and Nash**. The dragon claims **Nash bears the signature of his mother through his artificial dragon heart**. When Nash addresses a name heard as **Kaz Deziree / Cas de Jurey (?)**, his dragon chest opens and **a golden-orange spectral dragon appears and comforts the injured dragon**, addressing him as **Yoshalar / Shallar (?)** and singing a lullaby. These **visible spectral events are played**; the exact spelling, heart's full metaphysics and dragon's promised planetary rescue remain unknown.

## Pursuit reaches Dilo and captive Hope (~00:03–00:41)

1. **Cave reconnaissance (~00:03–00:24).** Nash scouts the luminous mushroom grotto first, using the **E120 mushroom-induced mouth/nose light** as a narrow flashlight and attempting stealth with limited success. He walks past giant reactive blue mushrooms, thick soft moss and a side alcove; his **Perception** finds scrape marks suggesting a **large creature traveled up the ledge and deeper north**, but no attack. He returns to report it. **Vorel and Omnom climb**, Stanley follows by jumping; all four proceed through the cave. No new mushroom is eaten or identified here, and no extra attack occurs during the scouting.
2. **The captor is waiting (~00:24–00:32).** At a larger fungus-filled cavern, **Dilo** stands atop a ledge with the **wand/controller and Hope in a floating blue sphere**. Hope is alive, kneeling inside and **cannot hear those outside**. The young elf addresses Stanley by his **Elhandari birth-name form Valier Ornath (?)**, speaks like an old acquaintance and states that **Hope possesses something necessary**. He claims she would not survive what he wants; the threat is testimony from the captor, not a performed killing. **Stanley actually casts third-level *Dispel Magic* and rolls 18**, but the sphere **does not vanish**; a successful check cannot be equated with actual dispelling of this device.
3. **The empathy-grenade trap (~00:32–00:41).** Dilo activates a mechanism filling Hope's sphere with **numerous spherical empathy grenades**, threatening to render her **catatonic** if the others intervene. Vorel observes that both terms of the ultimatum seem to endanger Hope. The apparent apprentice admits Chester was a **pretext for drawing the party out** but says this cave confrontation **was not his original plan**. He claims he needs Hope's body, especially the **Hand of the Voice**, to accomplish a return to the **Feywild**. These are statements made by a deceptive adversary and **not yet a completed ritual**.
4. **Diontalus named and his demand (~00:37–00:42).** The adversary **directly acknowledges Stanley identifying him as Diontalus**. He offers to spare Hope if **Stanley gives up his own body completely**, with no shared control; Diontalus intends to **suppress Stanley's consciousness**. He claims that **the Principle helped him** since E059 and that bringing Stanley or Hope back to the Feywild would finish his **reconstruction**. His promise to bury Hope's evil artifact where gods cannot reach it is **an antagonist's assertion**, not demonstrated custodial power, benevolence or a verified deal with the Principle. The E119–E120 fear of Diontalus's involvement is now **confirmed through direct encounter**, while **details of how he escaped the Principle's crystal are still not observed**.

## Hope's resistance and Stanley's duel (~00:42–01:24)

5. **First grenade attempt (~00:42–00:52).** In a **private Hope/GM scene**, Hope scratches the sphere with the Hand without effect and examines a grenade's internals. **Engineering 17** reveals **red, white and green wires**; a follow-up 13 leaves **red versus white** unresolved. She considers using her **appearance-changing necklace** to become a stone/warforged-like being, but **does not establish or complete a protective transformation**. She **pulls white**; the **grenade actually detonates**, flashes and temporarily blinds her, without making her catatonic or igniting all the other grenades. She infers white is the wrong wire. The sphere remains intact.
6. **A one-on-one accord (~00:52–01:03).** Stanley persuades Diontalus (still showing Dilo's body) to accept a **duel** in lieu of immediate escape with Hope. Stanley orders **Vorel, Nash and Omnom not to help**, and they withdraw to watch, though Vorel openly says that an eventual possessed Stanley would not be immune from later attack. **The parties' terms are not a signed supernatural compact or proof that Hope will actually be released**. Dilo's body floats down and the opponent mocks Stanley with a rapier. Actual initiative and combat follow the table break.
7. **Dilo dies; Diontalus steps out (~01:03–01:09).** Stanley ***Polymorphs* into a giant ape** and strikes **Dilo's physical body for 22 damage**. The elf's body **flies away, drops its rapier, lies motionless**, and a distinct **blue spectral figure of Diontalus stands in its place**. The GM later confirms **Dilo's body has no breath or movement**, and Vorel puts Omnom's severed feet near it. **Diontalus is not just a frightened apprentice or merely a suspected host anymore**. Yet whether Dilo ever voluntarily consented, how long he was controlled or whether the apparent corpse can be revived remains unverified. Diontalus calls him another fool taken to slaughter.
8. **Scythe and lasting harm (~01:09–01:21).** Diontalus manifests a huge **ethereal wooden scythe**, evokes the Grim Reaper and repeatedly strikes Stanley with **necrotic damage** and GM-declared **Strength/Dexterity score reductions** after failed Wisdom saves. Stanley's physical ape punches **pass through Diontalus**, but ***Destructive Wave* (thunder/radiant)** hurts him even though he uses a declared **legendary resistance**. Stanley then resumes half-elf form and sends **Diplomacy**; the sword initially misses. The GM's score-reduction interpretation is **played ruling**, not independently verified rules permanence.
9. **Stanley breaks the duel (~01:19–01:24).** Seeing he is outmatched, Stanley ***Dimension Doors* up to Hope's ledge**, takes the wand/detonator device and shouts their **“lemon tree”** intervention phrase. He **does not push the unidentified detonator**, and later **physically snaps the wand**, but **neither action frees Hope or stops the sphere**. His friends interpret the signal as permission to join combat. **Stanley's attempted rescue is real; it does not work at this cutoff**.

## The chaotic expanded battle (~01:24–02:23)

10. **Companions break the one-on-one fight (~01:24–01:40).** Nash first proposes ***Enlarge*** for Vorel, **then declines before casting** when told a spectral foe cannot be effectively hit by bare weapon strikes. He instead ***Polymorphs* himself into an adult black dragon**. Vorel rushes in, **rages** and lands two flaming-sword hits on Diontalus, including a critical. **Omnom's bare-handed grapple fails**, and he crashes onto Vorel, leaving both prone. Hope manages to **defuse three more grenades after the first detonation**; more remain. Diontalus manifests **restraining animated roots** around Nash's dragon and both lizardfolk, though Stanley and Hope are not rooted.
11. **The entire Necklace of Fireballs is spent (~01:45–02:02).** While beneath Omnom, **Vorel actually retrieves the Necklace of Fireballs from Omnom's concealed pouch** and hurls **the entire necklace** at Diontalus. The players and GM revisit an initial failed-throw/attunement interpretation and **explicitly walk it back** before resolving the action. They settle on **approximately five remaining beads** (not inventory-verified), resolving **a seventh-level Fireball with 12d6** as a **table ruling**. The blast **actually occurs**, hurts Diontalus and allies, and **burns away nearby roots**, freeing Vorel/Omnom. **The necklace is no longer in Omnom's or Vorel's possession as an unused item**; do not treat the initial miss before the rewind as the played outcome. The numerical damage is disputed mid-calculation; avoid canonizing inconsistent offhand totals.
12. **Hope still cannot get out (~01:40–02:13).** Her disarming process is slow with dozens or an unspecified large number of devices. A later failed check ***sets off another grenade***, flashing and disorienting her; the others do not all chain-detonate, and **she remains alive in the sphere**. After seeing Stanley seize the controller she also **stabs the sphere with a dagger**, with **no effect**. No magical prison escape, successful disarm of the whole set or Hope rescue is observed.
13. **Omnom is actually killed (~02:04–02:14).** Diontalus casts a **necrotic/lightning-themed chain spell** hitting Stanley, Vorel and **Omnom**. The GM later unequivocally reports that **only Omnom's feet/stumps remain**: the blast **destroyed his body**. **This supersedes E120, when Omnom was alive**; do not treat his earlier E118 survival as proof of surviving E121. No resurrection or remains recovery beyond the two feet is performed.
14. **Vorel's Eye and battle persistence (~02:11–02:20).** Vorel uses his **Eye of Groxl** to inspect a weakness, and the GM identifies **radiant damage as Diontalus's vulnerability**. The Eye displays a separate fury and compels Vorel toward fighting. Vorel's **flaming sword** lands further hits that appear painful yet sometimes heal over; an opposing scythe reduces Vorel's **Strength/Dexterity** on a failed save. **The title “Great Fang” is not newly conferred**, and the Eye's confirmed revelation is a vulnerability in this fight, not proof all archfey are universally vulnerable.
15. **The black dragon intervenes (~02:16–02:23).** A large, badly injured **black dragon** rushes out from a deeper cavern, scatters Hope's **still-sealed** sphere into the chamber like a pinball, **actually grabs Diontalus despite his ghostly form** and savages him with breath/physical attacks. Diontalus severely wounds the dragon, then **withdraws through a rock wall**, saying the struggle with Stanley is not finished. **Diontalus retreats alive; no corpse, extraction, binding, permanent banishment or negotiated return of Hope**.

## The heart, the dragon and a spectral mother (~02:23–02:52)

16. **Nash's unintended fire and collapse (~02:23–02:28).** Following Diontalus's exit, Nash **cannot control his adult black-dragon body**, feels terror/adrenaline, **bursts into flames that hit his companions**, and involuntarily breathes **acid** toward Vorel. **Stanley drops to zero hit points** from the damage; Nash himself becomes unconscious in dragon form. In Nash's **private vision** he again floats as a heart among fire, remembering his earlier Tietkalla vision; an unseen voice cries **“Mother”** and demands her release. Neither the uncontrolled attack nor the message is a deliberately cast Nash spell/rhyme.
17. **First triage and physical losses (~02:28–02:36).** Vorel **actually uses up Stanley's whole healer's kit to stabilize Stanley**, who remains unconscious until later healing; **Stanley is not dead**. Vorel turns Nash's dragon head away from Stanley for safety. He recovers **Omnom's two feet** and places them by the apparently dead **Dilo**, not reconstructing or reviving either. Hope is **still bouncing and trapped** in the sphere. The newly arrived dragon remains severely injured and bleeding.
18. **A witnessed rescue spell (~02:35–02:40).** Vorel speaks to the dragon in Draconic, helps prop its head up, and sees **scars, missing scales and demonic-looking inscriptions** from captivity. The dragon **consciously casts restorative blue mist**, which the GM gives **73 hit points each to Vorel, Stanley and Nash**, rousing unconscious allies. **The dragon does not heal itself, Omnom, Dilo or Hope**, and the older lizardfolk companion remains dead. The GM's brief uncertainty about kit-stabilization hit points should not overwrite the subsequent clear healing.
19. **The dragon remembers Earth (~02:39–02:47).** The stranger identifies itself as the **same imprisoned black dragon the party previously encountered in the desert on Earth**. It followed the group for a time but could not always locate them; this **does not mean it followed them every instant or guided E120 events**. It says the **heart/dragon signature in Nash** belongs to **its mother**, and claims earlier involuntary fire outbursts were **attempts to reach Nash**. It reports human captors carved marks into it; its assertion of **what the humans did to his mother** is **not independently verified**. The dragon has come to Elhandar's Feywild-like environment to **die and nourish future generations**, and believes Diontalus's assault hastens death. It offers to explain **how Earth could be saved and why ancient dragonflight efforts failed** *if* Nash allows a final meeting. **This is an offer, not an explanation already delivered**.
20. **Spectral encounter and cutoff (~02:47–02:52).** Nash, still in black-dragon shape, calls into his chest to the dragon's mother, using a name heard inconsistently as **Kaz Desiree / Cas de Jurey (?)**. His body **actually rises**, chest opens, and the artificial heart appears as a **large mechanical device with gauges**. An **orange-gold spectral dragon emerges**, tenderly touches the dying dragon and addresses him with a name heard as **Yoshalar / Shallar (?)**, then **sings a lullaby**. The show ends on this **performed, visible apparition** before the mother answers questions, Nash regains control, Hope is released, the dragon dies or explains the ancient plan. Treat the spoken names as **ASR-provisional pending original audio/cast handout**.
