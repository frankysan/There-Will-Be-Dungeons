## Confirmed played outcomes and cutoff

The cleric **puts on the fake**; Stanley **keeps the genuine pendant** and checks it is still on him after escaping. **The pendant has not yet been tried in the White Tower's box; its fitness as the actual key remains bishop/Jelver testimony**.
- The exchange is **nearly exposed**: a portion of the real pendant protrudes from Stanley's sleeve; **Hope and Vorel create an exaggerated display of devotion/tears**, distracting the cleric. He reads Hope's apparent spiritual ecstasy as a sign that she should **stay behind for further examination**. She **actually is held temporarily**, but **Stanley returns and casts Modify Memory on Sister Anulak/Annulac (?)**, persuading her (in the played outcome) that **Hope/Candice must leave with the other acolytes**. **Hope is actually collected and exits unharmed with the party**. The spell's described saving-throw adjudication is confusing in ASR; prioritize played NPC behavior, and **audio-check the exact roll/result**, rather than asserting mechanically impeccable application.
- **The sealed Tobacho passage writ from E091 is actually shown to a Harmonium checkpoint and accepted**: party **leaves the Hive**, reversing E090's officer's threat in practice. Three bumbling armored guards get stuck in the carriage door; **Nash casts Reduce on their leader** to extricate them. He subsequently reverses the shrinking spell. The leader gives his name as **Sir Lipso Tuxis/Texas (?)**, explicitly threatens Nash if the magic violates his oath; **no guard killed, arrested or fought and no proven oath violation**.
- At Lohar's Great Bazaar stall **Vorel actually presents Tobacho's sealed authorization**, and **four new white priest/acolyte disguises are made and delivered to the coach**, *not merely requested*. No independent purchase cost/payment is shown. At the **Amber Cutlet** they drink mead; **Vorel pays a 5sp tab**, and **leaves a 1gp concealed gratuity** after his first offered coin was refused. The table agrees **to proceed homeward**, particularly Hope's desire to return to her kingdom, while Vorel drinks heavily.
- **Driver (six arms, eight red eyes)** carries group between Hive, Bazaar, drinking stop and a safehouse near the temple, later to the airship. A late name reads **Cadingus/Kadingus Renti/Ready (?)**, but the **GM and cast confuse it with an improvised guard name in the outro**; *do not treat orthography or drawing identification as verified*.
- **The changeling posing as kidnapped Bishop Stevius Rintley (?) actually meets them at the safehouse**, reveals his neutral olive/greenish natural appearance, helps with disguises, and devises a fifth exceptional blessing at the Unwavering House. They **take a full long rest** and eat a strange breakfast before the mission. He temporarily **reshapes Nash into a handsome young human “Horace Saddlesworth,” Vorel into a Goliath, and Hope into a human (“Candice/Candace”)**; **Stanley is deliberately left unchanged**, praised as already handsome. **These are temporary disguises, not permanent race/body transformations**. The changeling later departs the safehouse; the true bishop remains in Tobacho's custody unless later evidence shows otherwise.
- **Promised E091 equipment is ACTUALLY DELIVERED in E092**, in their coach after the temple: **8 Dimension Door scrolls + 4 Reverse Gravity scrolls**; **Nash's Ring of Spell Storing** (he **fills it with three first-level and one second-level Chromatic Orb**, using spell slots); **a +2 magic dagger explicitly assigned to Hope in the handover** (despite E091 sounding like Nash requested a magical dagger); **Fox AND Scully firearms plus a box of bullets**, with **Smokey's ammunition belt explicitly still missing**, and **12 rations, sleeping bag, rope and an adventuring pack**. Prior E089/E090 account of *Mulder* as Hope's other missing pistol conflicts with the E092 “Fox and Scully” wording; **do not silently equate Fox and Mulder or infer which item is historical without owner/audio/handout check**. **Scully is now back among the supplied guns; it was genuinely given away in E090 and wasn't in Hope's hands at E091 cutoff**. Some items are described as being **in the coach/group possession**, not explicitly assigned to individual inventories beyond the listed recipients. **Shatter remains a loaned +2 greatsword with Vorel**.
- **Airship procurement is also REAL by E092**: the carriage actually descends to an **underground hangar in Sigil's Dark Below**, where a large **gnome-worked airship** is being serviced, with an **enormous portal in the wall facing its departure path**. A **bipedal elephant-like captain** gives a tour; the GM temporarily loses their intended name and improvises **“Captain Meart/Miart/Meyart”** with several variants and ambiguous pronouns. **The ship's name isn't provided**. **The crew has not launched, traveled through the portal, flown over the Outlands or reached White Tower by episode end**. The captain announces impending departure; **no departure actually occurs**.
- **No Fist recovery, White Tower infiltration, opening of its chest, return to Earth, rescue of original Bishop Stevius or resolution of the Spatulo bounty**. E091's arc-three quest has advanced from **plan-only** to **first-phase pendant theft completed + equipment/ship delivered**, not to a completed heist.

## I. The Hive checkpoint and shrinking a paladin (~00:02–00:25)

Tobacho's orc staff escorts **Lady Despair and the Rambunctious Bunch/Rascals** out of the dragon's lair to a carriage. The driver is an eight-eyed, six-armed grey-skinned/furry being, who is commissioned to take them where necessary. Their first destinations are **tailor Lohar** and a **safehouse** near the temple of Euphemius. The driver soon encounters a Harmonium checkpoint **on the way out of the Hive**. Stanley shows the **Tobacho-sealed writ received in E091**. The soldiers struggle to read its language but **accept it and permit passage**; **E090's explicit no-exit policy is overcome here, not through a battle or secret escape**.

Three armored guards then inadvertently wedge themselves together in the coach entrance. **Nash asks permission and casts Reduce on their leader**, which frees all three. He immediately ends the reduction. The offended officer, **Sir Lipso Tuxis/Texas (?)**, warns that if the spell offended his order's vows he will punish Nash. The departure **actually continues** without combat or arrest. The driver offers Nash hypothetical future work as a bodyguard; **Nash does not take the job**.

## II. A second disguise order and drinks before duty (~00:25–01:08)

In the Great Bazaar Vorel, back to proudly wearing his recognizable first criminal costume, passes **Jelver's Tobacho-sealed note** to Lohar with Stanley's discreet help. The orc tailor is startled by his powerful connection but **immediately commissions four specific new garments**; they are **physically delivered to the carriage** while the group waits. **No confirmed fee is paid**. The original E090 spiked-leather disguises were real and remain historical; these are **a new priestly costume set**.

While waiting, the party eats/drinks at **the Amber Cutlet**, where elaborate exchanges about tipping accompany Vorel's first prolonged drinking. The waitress is a **dwarf**; a hostess is **an elf**. Vorel **pays five silver** for the mead and later **conceals a one-gold tip on the table** after failing to convince staff to take a generous payment directly. A minor conflict happens when Nash mistakenly calls the dwarven server an orc; she strikes at him and they topple together, but **no campaign-defining injury is declared**. At the table **Stanley notes he originally considered staying in Sigil** but was persuaded by the others' commitments, and **Hope explicitly reaffirms she must return home**. **No Earth portal opens**.

On the carriage ride to the safehouse, Vorel becomes tipsy and complains of hunger, thirst and headache. The driver stops at another alehouse, where **Vorel buys three large casks for 15gp and tips 3gp**; these are **actually purchased and carried**, while the driver retains his own previously handed coin purse. The group urges him to remain sober for the heist.

## III. Changeling, false acolytes and an overnight rest (~01:08–01:39)

In a clean temple district the group reaches a small concealed safehouse. **A changeling operative**, currently masquerading as **the genuine imprisoned Bishop Stevius Rintley (?)**, receives them, reveals his nonhuman form, and reminds them to wear the newly delivered clerical clothes long enough to make them look used. **Nash reduces the ale barrels** to fit through a narrow doorway, another temporary spell. The operative explains the plan: claim an exceptional **fifth yearly blessing** by High Cleric **Butar/Buntar Thrun**, who is busy with a war council. They will pose as acolytes of **Euphemius**, gain close access, make the sun-and-fist medallion fall, and **substitute a convincingly manufactured fake**. The changeling warns against bringing visible weapons. His explanation of **the Lady of Pain's indifference to personal moral drama** remains **his testimony**, not a measured universal law of Sigil.

The party **takes a full long rest** at the safehouse, with an odd egg-and-worm breakfast the next morning. The changeling reshapes the others' **visible outward forms**: **Nash** becomes a handsome human **Horace Saddlesworth**; **Vorel** becomes a large humanlike **Goliath**, rather than recognizably famous lizardfolk; **Hope** appears human and answers to **Candice/Candace**. **Stanley retains his own appearance**, because the changeling regards him as already suitably handsome. The GM explicitly restores everyone's normal appearance **after the temple visit**. No actual body-race change or lasting cure of scars/cybernetics is claimed.

## IV. Entry into the Unwavering House and the pendant theft (~01:39–02:09)

Early in the morning **the five in priestly clothes enter the Unwavering House**, the headquarters/church of Euphemius, through a brick courtyard. **Sister Anulak/Annulac (?)** recognizes “Stevius” and says the High Cleric is resting after many weeks of war council, so cannot accept the odd extra blessing. An attempted offer of coin from one party member is stopped. **Stanley gives a successful persuasive deception about their supposed home world “Gaetor” suffering under demons**, prompting her to take the case upstairs; **this invented religious mission is not an actual observed new Prime world**.

The very aged **High Cleric Thrun**, with ornate white robes, architectural headpiece and a prominent medallion, actually enters. He praises the handsome Nash alias **Horace** and the unchanged **Stanley**, frowns at Hope's awkward smile, and accepts the changeling's explanation that mute-looking Goliath Vorel is a devout recruit. He performs a **blessing**, moving among them. **Stanley disguises the verbal component of Telekinesis inside his prayers**, so when the cleric reaches toward his ear the real pendant's clasp **comes undone** and the ornament falls.

**Stanley physically swaps the real pendant for the prepared imitation** with an **18 Sleight of Hand roll**. The **real medallion initially sticks out of his sleeve**, a close call. **Hope loudly performs sudden religious fervor** while **Vorel cries and assists** as distraction, which distracts the room long enough for Stanley to hide the genuine piece. **The cleric explicitly accepts and wears the imitation**, without recognizing the theft; the party later confirms possession of the authentic-seeming pendant. However, the unexpected consequence is serious: the cleric believes **Hope/Candice experienced special divine inspiration** and demands **she remain in the Unwavering House for spiritual examination**. **Stanley, Nash, Vorel and the false Stevius leave**, while Hope is actually kept behind temporarily.

## V. Recovering Hope, not losing the key (~02:09–02:22)

Outside, the changeling worries that Hope cannot sustain her apparent mystical persona and will be exposed. The party jokes about various rescues, including windows, an angel impersonation or a blow to the sister, but **none of those are attempted**. **Stanley returns to the building** and **casts Modify Memory on Sister Anulak**, describing a false meeting outcome in which the group must immediately retrieve Candice and depart for “Gaetor.” The table's discussion of the spell and the saving throw deserves **careful audio review**: the spoken line about a “success” is ambiguous relative to the subsequent **explicit played memory rewrite behavior**. The sister **acts on the false memory despite momentary confusion and asks that Candice be fetched**. Hope **actually exits the temple**, meeting the other companions near the gate. The original medallion remains in the party's possession. **The high cleric has not confronted them, and no temple guards chase them by cutoff**.

Back at the safehouse the operative reverts to changeling form, **leaves through another door**, and the three altered appearances wear off. **The real Stevius remains the earlier bound captive**; the actor who participated in the theft is **his shapeshifting impersonator**, not the bishop himself.

## VI. Delivered kit and the ship that has yet to depart (~02:22–02:44)

The driver—whose spoken name sounds **Cadingus/Kadingus Renti/Ready (?)**—collects them. He reveals Jelver's requested equipment **is physically in their carriage**. The group opens containers and the GM explicitly allocates **8 Dimension Door scrolls, 4 Reverse Gravity scrolls, a Ring of Spell Storing for Nash, a +2 magical dagger to Hope, Fox and Scully in a box with bullets, 12 rations, ropes, sleeping kit and other adventuring gear**. **Smokey's belt is explicitly *not* present**. E091 only promised some of this. **The names “Fox and Scully” here conflict with E089–E091 accounts calling Hope's second missing pistol “Mulder”; preserve a tracked naming/custody issue, don't invent an additional third gun**. It is clear **both firearms are now physically present**, including Scully previously surrendered to Tobacho.

**Nash actually charges his delivered ring** with Chromatic Orbs: after an initial idea of five first-level casts, he finalizes **three level-one and one level-two**, filling **five total spell levels**. Charging **uses his spell slots**. **Vorel still has Shatter**, confirmed in the carriage, and its return obligation continues.

The carriage travels for hours and descends **beneath Sigil into the Dark Below**, reaching an immense **underground airship dock** with gnome workers, an elaborate multi-level flying ship and a **football-field-size portal aimed toward the ship's departure route**. An upright elephant-like captain approaches; the GM says they had a name prepared but misplaced it, makes up **Captain Meart/Miart/Meyart (?)**, and inconsistently uses gendered pronouns, eventually choosing **she**. The captain gives a **tour of the ship's rowing/energy section, cargo hold and upper deck** and says they will depart soon. The **ship's name is asked but not supplied**, deferred to a future episode. **No launch, transit through the enormous portal, journey across the Outlands, entry into White Tower or retrieval of Zorander's Fist occurs before outro.** Keep *airship obtained and visited* distinct from *mission flown*.
