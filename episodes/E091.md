# E091 — Going on a heist

[Dual-ASR comparison and review priorities](../reports/E091_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). **Neither the audio nor any original SRT has been changed or independently listened to.** E090 ended as the four followed Tobacho's intermediary into a private passage, after Hope gave Scully to the blue dragon. *Inverse* remains a distinct continuity.

## Consequential outcomes and limits at the cutoff

- **The private briefing actually occurs in E091**, not E090. **Jelver/Jalver (?)** leads the four toward a magic circle; on the way they briefly **see Gerbken Shartlinger / Mind Master** through another room's open door, which is shut by a guard. **No conversation with him, new demonstrated conspiracy or confrontation occurs**.
- **Jelver transports the four to an apparently real desert canyon and tests them against four giant trolls**, including two-headed **Bit and But**, **Barnold**, **Sicky** and **Gloria** (ASR spellings provisional). The fight is actually rolled and causes apparently real injuries: **Stanley kills Barnold with Booming Blade; Vorel kills two-headed Bit and But using flaming spiked boots; Sicky kills himself by spraying poisonous blood; Hope kills Gloria with a crossbow shot**. **Stanley falls unconscious and begins death saves**, and **Nash stabilizes him using Stanley's healer's kit**. At the end the canyon **dissolves into a conjured test space**, revealing robed conjurers; Jelver explicitly says they **passed his test**. Do not treat the troll attack as a lasting real-world journey to Earth/Outlands or four independently verified natural monsters.
- **Jelver actually heals Stanley for 22 hp**, and subsequently medics give **Stanley, Nash and Vorel** large medicinal drinks restoring their hit points **to full**. Hope had not sustained the same serious injury; do not leave the three wounded at episode end or call Stanley dead/resurrected.
- **Mission target finally named:** a powerful, visually uncertain gauntlet called **Zorander's Fist** (name provisional), originally from a Prime world sounding like **Drethiel/Dreftiel (?)** and contested by **demons of the Abyss** and paladins of **the Obsidian White** devoted to **Euphemius** (order's adjective sounds *Peladric/Pelagic*). Jelver says Tobacho wants the item but **does not know its actual power** and may be seeking to trade it. Initial guesses about which faction possesses it are superseded by later captive testimony.
- **Tobacho's people have actually kidnapped Bishop Stevius Ritley/Rintley (?)**, a Sigil Euphemius priest, and **placed a changeling impersonator in his public role** to hide his disappearance. **Stanley actually enters his cell and uses Enthralling Performance**; the bishop is **successfully charmed/credulous**, accepts Stanley's invented righteous mission, and discloses that the **paladins have Zorander's Fist in the besieged White Tower in the Outlands**, hoping to transfer it to **Mount Celestia**. This is **the coerced/charmed bishop's information**, not separately observed tower inventory.
- **The Fist is reportedly sealed inside a divinely made/arcane locked box**, whose **key is worn as a neck pendant by high cleric Butar Thrun/Butarthrun (?)**, at Euphemius's **temple in Sigil**. According to the bishop, **only pure-good souls may move through the doorless White Tower's walls**; demon forces outside batter it with siege weapons and winged creatures. **Neither the box, key, cleric, tower nor battle is physically visited in E091**. Stanley hears that the bishop's aunt **Eulonis/Julonis (?) Ritley** runs a children's bauble shop in the Market Ward; **no safety check or rescue is carried out**.
- **The agreed provisional heist sequence** is to **obtain the cleric's key without alerting him**, preferably use a replacement amulet, then **fly above the White Tower on a crewed airship and “tele-shoot” inside with Dimension Door**, extract the box/relic and return to Sigil. The team discusses tunnels, false paladin disguises, offering a rescue route and a dragon flight, but **does not undertake those alternatives**. **No temple infiltration, key theft, real airship flight, teleportation into the tower, Fist recovery or Earth-portal crossing occurs**.
- **Equipment statuses matter:** the team **requests** an airship/crew, **eight Dimension Door scrolls**, **four Reverse Gravity scrolls**, other supplies, magically supplied bullets and loaned firearms; **none is physically delivered on screen**. Jelver **actually gives a Tobacho-sealed authorization for Lohar** to supply disguises and **offers a separate sealed writ to ease travel past Hive checkpoints** (neither yet redeemed/tested). **Vorel actually takes custody of Jelver's +2 greatsword “Shatter,” loaned with an explicit return condition**, whose alleged earlier use to kill an angel is Jelver's story. A **ring of spell storing** and Nash's magic dagger are **requested, not awarded**. Hope's **Scully remains gifted to Tobacho**; his possible gun-loan offer is not the return of Scully.
- At the close **Jelver reiterates that Tobacho will grant actual *use* of an Earth portal only after successful delivery**, and asks for discretion and speed. The **GM announces a level-up to level 10** after the adventure, not a played in-world encounter. **No reward or new portal access is yet received**.

## I. Beneath Tobacho's cantina and the suspicious adjacent meeting (~00:04–00:10)

Jelver, a large eyepatched, dreadlocked man with a white gown and gold chains, escorts **Hope, Vorel, Stanley and Nash** from the blue dragon's underground audience chamber into passages guarded by orcs. Through a briefly open office door they recognize **Gerbken Shartlinger, the Mind Master**, seated across from another figure. An orc closes the door before the party can approach; **this is a sighting only**, not proof Tobacho ordered the E086 assassins or killed Kanja.

In a larger otherwise empty stone room a **rune circle** glows. Vorel initially hesitates to step on it and insists on the alias **Toyota Stump**, though Jelver has already heard his actual name. All four enter; Jelver's eyes glow and magic abruptly places them **in a hot desert canyon**. He challenges their competence before vanishing in apparent dust.

## II. Four trolls and a test with consequential damage (~00:10–01:36)

Four huge green trolls rise from the sand around the party, some discussing who will attack first. Three characters initially try **playing dead**. **Vorel alone stands and squares up**; the others begin defending themselves once initiative starts. The troll group includes a **two-headed troll (Bit and But)**, a large brute **Barnold**, a bleeding/poisonous **Sicky** and rock-throwing **Gloria**; exact pronunciation and which title applies to which form merit listening.

**Nash casts Haste on Stanley**, not the entire party: the player first proposes multi-target haste but the table corrects that assumption. **Stanley animates one huge boulder** to batter Barnold; **Hope fires a critical heavy-crossbow bolt**, while **Vorel grapples a troll and deploys his newly acquired retractable boot spike** with additional fire damage by explicit rolls. This is **the first documented combat use of the E090 toe-spike disguise gear**; E090 itself did not grant or demonstrate an attack bonus.

Nash is pinned by a thrown boulder and takes repeated hits. He casts **a carefully placed Fireball** (the table rules allied saves succeed but **half damage still applies**). Trolls recoil from fire. Barnold destroys Stanley's animated stone, then **Stanley kills Barnold with a Booming Blade rapier attack**, and uses Mantle of Inspiration to give allies temporary hit points and an opportunity to move.

**Sicky cuts himself open to spray poisonous blood** on Stanley and Nash, then pounds Nash into perilous low health. Stanley **Polymorphs Nash into a giant ape**: this supplies a large temporary form/hit point pool, **not a permanent Nash transformation**. In the fighting **Vorel kills the two-headed troll** with further flaming boot attacks. The ape-shaped Nash hits and throws a huge rock at Gloria. **Sicky cuts himself again and dies from his own inflicted injury**, with Vorel suffering temporary poison; **Stanley casts Lesser Restoration to remove Vorel's poisoning**.

A large rock thrown by **Gloria knocks Stanley unconscious**, ending his active concentration on Nash's polymorph; Nash resumes human form. The remaining foe continues to use boulders. **Nash casts a fire-damage Chromatic Orb**, **Hope kills Gloria with a later critical crossbow shot**, and the team scrambles to free the fallen **Stanley** beneath rock and corpse. **Vorel lifts the stone enough to help; Nash uses Mold Earth**, and **Hope pulls Stanley clear**. The group retrieves a **healer's kit from Stanley's inventory** and **Nash uses it to stabilize him without a rolled Medicine success**. This saves him from dying but **does not immediately restore consciousness**.

Just then the desert facade disappears. They stand back in a **brick/conjuration room with four injured robed figures**, suggesting the battle was an engineered magical scenario. **Jelver congratulates them on passing**, directly **restores Stanley by 22 hit points**, and then invites them to receive further treatment. The canyon is **not established as a true external journey to the Outlands**; however, the combat's effects and healing are played rather than merely narrated as a harmless vision. A break divides the episode at roughly 01:36.

## III. The real commission and a captive Sigil bishop (~01:36–01:51)

In Jelver's private office, attendants arrive with food and medicinal drinks. Stanley, Nash and Vorel **accept the offered restorative draughts and recover to full health**, after Stanley's earlier 22 hp healing. Jelver congratulates the team on the troll trial but warns that **Tobacho does not tolerate failure once they attempt the actual commission**.

Jelver names the **gauntlet Zorander's Fist**. In his account it originated with a fallen champion on a Prime world called **Drethiel/Dreftiel (?)**, is associated with followers of the radiant deity **Euphemius** and an **Obsidian White paladin order**, and has changed hands repeatedly between the order and demons campaigning across the **Outlands**. The gauntlet is described from rumor as ornamental metal fist armor, perhaps white-and-gold with solar symbols; **neither its visual form, supernatural properties, provenance or value are independently seen**. Tobacho wants this much-contested object, apparently as a valuable commercial opportunity, not because a specific magic effect is shown.

A hidden observation window exposes **a bound, hooded man in another cell**. Jelver names him **Bishop Stevius Ritley/Rintley (?)**, a Sigil priest of Euphemius. **An associate kidnapped him and a changeling took his place**; this is explicit participant testimony, not an assumption the bishop is simply missing. The party is invited to question him. The captive is **not released or killed** in this episode.

## IV. Stanley's charm, the White Tower and the second key (~01:51–02:13)

**Stanley goes alone into the cell**, lights candles with Prestidigitation, removes the hood and speaks gently. He initially claims that he might help the captive, but refuses immediate release. When questions about the Fist yield evasive answers, Stanley **performs the bardic Enthralling Performance feature for about one minute**. The bishop **fails to resist and becomes convinced Stanley shares his religious cause**. Stanley succeeds at **high Deception rolls**, persuading the bishop that the captivity is a covert necessary ruse, and that nobody else is listening. **The other party members and Jelver continue watching through the one-way window**; the captive is deceived.

The bishop divulges that paladins of the **Obsidian White now keep Zorander's Fist in their White Tower in the Outlands**, with **demonic forces besieging it**. Their stated goal is to arrange transfer to **Mount Celestia**, to put the relic beyond the demons' reach. His further account: the tower has **no conventional doors**; **only souls of pure good can pass through its walls**, and the attackers try to break it apart through siege and aerial assault. These are **the bishop's assertions**, and the party has not inspected either tower defenses or the relic.

The gauntlet is reportedly inside **a locked arcane/divine box**, requiring **a separate physical key worn around the neck of Sigil's High Cleric Butar Thrun/Butarthrun (?)**. This crucial distinction means a successful tower raid alone may not be enough. The key, key's visual design, cleric's residence and whether the box can be bypassed are **not verified**. The bishop asks Stanley to look after **Aunt Eulonis/Julonis (?)**, who sells minor trinkets for children in the market. Stanley **promises to try**, covers his head again and leaves **without freeing him**. Jelver expresses concern about Stanley's persuasive abilities when he returns.

## V. Selecting a two-stage heist and negotiating support (~02:13–02:44)

The group and Jelver discuss possible ways to get through the supposedly **doorless, good-soul-selective White Tower** while demons occupy its perimeter: disguising as paladins, persuading defenders to move the box to safety, digging beneath the siege, posing as rescuers, flying in dragon form or using ordinary teleportation. **These are proposals, not journeys**. Jelver suggests **“tele-shooting”: jumping from a high airship and casting Dimension Door into the tower just before impact**, then escaping using teleportation and a proposed **Reverse Gravity** return toward the ship. No mechanism has been field-tested, and the effects of the tower's protections on teleportation are **assumed rather than independently proved**. The team opts for **speed and minimal confrontation**, supporting the airship plan.

Jelver stresses that **the key should be stolen first**, preferably replaced with a look-alike medallion so the high cleric does not know the relic is threatened. **Butar Thrun (?) is not met or robbed**. The team wants a crewed **airship**, **eight scrolls of Dimension Door** and **four scrolls of Reverse Gravity**. These are **procurement requests recorded by Jelver**, not objects already in their hands. He offers to fund equipment, while threatening to charge the party for failure; this is **a commitment by a self-interested NPC**, not completed provision or an agreed itemized debt. He also indicates potential spell-key concerns on divine planes; the White Tower in the Outlands is not itself a god's inner realm, by his testimony.

**Jelver hands over a sealed note bearing Tobacho's dragon emblem for tailor Lohar**, permitting needed disguises without immediate charge; **no fresh outfit is made or collected in E091**. He says he can loan firearms from the dragon's collection and commission **gnomes to manufacture suitable bullets** for Hope; **there is no demonstration that ammunition exists for Scully or that any gun is physically delivered**. The party discusses rations, sleeping gear and four 50-foot ropes, plus potential rings and a magic dagger. **Nash's ring of spell storing and magical dagger are merely requests**.

When Vorel says he lacks a proven weapon, Jelver brings a huge **+2 two-handed greatsword called Shatter** from a wardrobe. Jelver claims he slew **an angel** with it as a former general and says the blade must **be returned**. **Vorel explicitly takes the weapon into his own custody**, a real equipment change distinct from the promised gun loans and scrolls. Jelver's history is **not independently corroborated**.

At the end Jelver recaps **key theft in Sigil → obtain airship/scrolls → drop into the White Tower → recover Fist and return → Tobacho provides the Earth portal for actual use**. He gives the party **a Tobacho-sealed writ intended for Harmonium posts** to address E090's threatened no-exit policy, warning that an uncooperative checkpoint should be avoided in favor of a different Hive gate, possibly held by his agents. **No officer has honored that writ yet, and the group has not exited the Hive**. **The group does not depart on the heist this episode**. In outro table talk the **GM grants level 10**, with no played level-ten encounter or evidence that all character sheets were updated.
