# E090 — The smell of strawberries and sadness

[Dual-ASR comparison](../reports/E090_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md). **No original subtitles modified or audio independently listened to.** E089 ended with the reunited four in the Dark Below debating a rat-sized route into the Hive. *Inverse* is an independent continuity.

## Consequential outcomes and what is *not* established

- **The party chooses the surface route to the Hive**, not the rat-sized Dark Below tunnels. Returning from the sewer, they commission matching threatening-looking leather outfits from orc tailor **Lohar**: spikes, ropes/belts, horned demon chest and triple-skull back emblems, and **steel-toed boots with retractable toe spikes**. **Quoted cost: 50 gp per character, four sets**, or **200 gp total if paid**. After a **two-day downtime skip**, all four **actually collect and wear** the outfits and Stanley soils/ages them with *Prestidigitation* to look less new. **No explicit on-air money transfer for the tailor is witnessed**, so do not insert a proved 200-gp purse debit.
- **The GM explicitly corrects/expands Nash's post-regrowth mechanics**: the **thermal underwear fused to his DNA during the beam**, leaving a small vent/switch **in his armpit**, passive **cooling**, and an ongoing **10-foot fly speed** despite the complete humanoid regrowth established in E089. Beau asks Scott to note this as an eventual feat; **no formally published feat/character-sheet edit is shown**. This is **direct GM adjudication**, not mere Nash speculation or a future shopping proposal. The reveal follows E088's separate correction of Sigil spellcasting suppression.
- In the two-day downtime, **Hope unsuccessfully tries to gather public rumors of Tobacho Fleur**; tavern patrons evade the name. **Stanley's successful library check** yields estimates that the **Hive's population may exceed the rest of Sigil tenfold**, with Harmonium border checkpoints controlling traffic; these are **in-world library/census claims**, not independently established statistical fact. **Vorel's planned illithid BMF pupil Fax/Faks fails to attend a scheduled lesson**; Tubbs 9K cannot locate him. **The reason for Fax's disappearance is unknown**. Nash simply relaxes at the Soggy Feather.
- The party **actually travels in a rickshaw to the Hive's Harmonium-controlled boundary** for **1 gp paid**. About a dozen armed officers build a **gallows** beside cages containing prisoners. The captain, name sounding **Sir Unindra/Unidra (?) of the Fifty-Seventh Legion**, first forbids passage, then is persuaded/intimidated to **let the party *enter* the Hive but explicitly bars their return**. He says a caged **furry prisoner is due to be hanged for trying to leave the Hive**. This is **Harmonium captain's stated justification and threat**, not evidence that the prisoner was actually executed in E090. The party **does not free the prisoner, attack the guard or attempt to leave through the checkpoint**.
- In the rundown, smoke-choked Hive, the group initially rejects a street tout inviting them to a club named **Daddy Long Legs**. **Hope eventually enters** to find information about Tobacho. The establishment presents a sexualized entertainment business, weapon-check and various patrons; **the actual working conditions/coercion of all dancers are not independently verified**, though one tiefling dancer tells Nash she **does not want to remain**. Nash calls her smell **“strawberries and sadness,”** providing the episode title. **He promises only to *try* to help her later**; she is **not rescued, hired or transported to Earth**. He also **chases a small passerby, tackles him, threatens with a flaming hand and returns to the group**; the passerby survives, no actual *Fire Bolt* impact and no bounty are played.
- **Hope obtains an actionable route to Tobacho** from a **hooded, heavily scarred dark-red tiefling with gold skull-engraved teeth**, name sounding **Spatulo/Spatula (?)**. He says to seek a **frog-on-a-stick vendor near the center of the Hive**, then a cantina entrance in a back alley, and to mention Spatulo *only if* the reception seems welcoming. **This is his guidance, not verified alliance**. His relationship with Tobacho is later shown to be **hostile**.
- The group actually **finds the vendor**, obtains directions to an unmarked **burgundy metal door**, and descends into **Tobacho's guarded underground cantina/lair**. Tobacho Fleur, the reputed E089 Earth-portal steward, is revealed as a **huge, lounging blue dragon**, wearing **monocle and beret** and smoking a pipe, surrounded by attendants, women, a **stone golem that translates very old Draconic** and heavily armed patrons. A man named **Jelver/Jolver (?)**, with dark skin, eyepatch and chains, offers to broker safe introduction. **Vorel actually pays 50 gp for an appointment**, via the barkeep; Jelver then presents the party to the dragon. The team adopted disguises **“Lady Despair and the Rambunctious Bunch/Rascals,” “Toyota Stump,” “Brildaro” and “Nuts Phenom”**, but Jelver calls out the false names and warns them not to deceive Tobacho.
- **Hope gives up *Scully*, the pistol recovered in E089.** She voluntarily passes the firearm to Stanley, who **presents it as a gift to Tobacho**. The dragon accepts and **a goblin servant physically carries the gun beneath the dragon's couch**. Thus **at E090 cutoff Hope no longer possesses Scully**. **Her other gun, *Mulder*, remains unrecovered**, its Fedor-associated sale only Java's E089 testimony. Do not list *Scully* as a retained weapon after the dragon's audience.
- In exchange for the party retrieving **an unspecified special object requiring capable fighters**, Tobacho **promises to reveal the Earth portal's location and means of access**. **The bargain is accepted provisionally; the item's identity, conditions, costs, assignment briefing and any actual retrieval are *not* played in E090.** The dragon's assistant summons the party down a **dark private passage** to discuss details in the next session. **No portal key, doorway coordinates, Earth crossing or final fulfillment occurs**.
- As the meeting ends, **Hope tells Tobacho that “Spatulo sends his regards.”** The dragon reacts with hostility and the golem calls Spatulo **an “infidel” who disrupts the dragon's business**, warning the party to stay clear. The dragon also offers **a reward for delivering Spatulo as a captive**. **The party does not accept or perform that bounty before the cutoff**, and Spatulo's wider identity/faction remains unknown. The false inference that he was a trusted referral should not survive E090.

## I. Sewer departure and a plan to look dangerous (~00:02–00:26)

The four stand in the sewer arrival chamber with **Java/Jarver the Carver (?)**, the ratfolk who gave them Tobacho and Fedor's names in E089. Nash mentions that the strange **purple portal seen with the umbrella Barbara** might reappear; the group recognizes its destination was dangerous and warns against casual experiments. Vorel suggests shrinking everyone to rat size for the subterranean tunnels, but the available animal-transformation magic affects **only one member for about an hour**. Java says **even rats might require many hours below the Hive**, and full-sized visitors face unpredictable blocked/dangerous routes. He corrects a joking **“Tabasco Joe”** alias toward the steward's name **Tobacho Fleur**. They **decide to travel by surface transport instead of crawling underground**.

Java offers an opportunity to **steal** clothing from a sleeping tailor, but the group declines. They climb back into the city and visit **Lohar**, the orc tailor who already supplied their formal suits and Vorel's cape. Their chosen disguise is a self-described rough, threatening gang, **“Lady Despair and the Rambunctious Bunch”** (also *Rascals*), led in their fiction by **Hope as “Lady Despair.”** Vorel roleplays the muscle **“Toyota Stump”**; Stanley/Nash later invent names at the cantina. Lohar proposes extensive **spikes**, decorative belt buckles, **ropes rather than obvious valuables/chains**, demonic skull motifs, light enough for movement, and **steel-toed boots with concealed extending spikes**. **Four outfits are commissioned at 50 gp each** and the tailor says **two days** for clothing plus metalwork. **No explicit advance cash payment is witnessed in this scene.**

## II. Two days' downtime, an embedded thermal vent and the missing student (~00:26–00:44)

The GM moves forward **two days**. During downtime **Hope approaches people in bars to learn about Tobacho Fleur**, but a poor social roll produces evasion and discomfort whenever his name is brought up—not reliable information about a precise address. **Stanley succeeds on a strong library History check** and learns a **census estimate**: the Hive may contain **ten times** the population of the city's other wards combined, with overcrowding, criminal power and deprivation. The books describe **Harmonium checkpoints, rioting and campaigns to keep disorder within the Hive**, providing **in-world reporting, not an omniscient or socially neutral census**. **Nash stays at the Soggy Feather drinking coffee and relaxing**. **Vorel leads BMF coaching sessions**, with plenty of patrons drawn to the professional cabinet he bought in E088; his former contender/potential pupil **Fax the illithid does not show for a scheduled lesson**, and **Tubbs 9K cannot find him**. There is **no proved death, imprisonment or disciplinary action** concerning Fax.

The GM gives **Nash a retroactive trait after physical regrowth**: the earlier **thermal underwear became integrated into his DNA** during the transport between realities. A vent/switch **under one armpit** provides **temperature control** and Nash retains **10 feet of flying/hovering movement**, despite being physically human-shaped by E089. Beau intends later to add this as a feat and asks Scott to note it; **the trait is actually stated/adjudicated**, while the formal sheet update remains unverified.

On the departure afternoon Vorel brings the newly **delivered four outfits** in carefully wrapped packages. Their brown/black leather, heavy spikes and belts, oversized demonic chest face with red-glinting eyes, triple skulls on the back and **retractable steel-toe spikes** make them look like a mismatched outlaw unit. Vorel chooses a **pillowcase eye patch** and smears **coal soot** onto his face; Stanley uses **Prestidigitation in reverse to grime all four outfits**, leaving no expensive new shine. The party **actually changes into this gang apparel**. Do not assume each suit is plate armor or invent damage bonuses for the boot spikes. **200 gp total retail quote** is **not documented as a paid amount**.

## III. The Harmonium border and prisoners forbidden to leave (~00:44–01:05)

The four hail a **rickshaw pulled by a strong woman**, choose it over a more ostentatious sedan and ride toward the Hive. At the border, smoke, soot and the smell of sulfur mixed with animal waste fill the streets. **One gold is handed to the driver**; she leaves the group near an ominous construction crew.

About **twelve men in official armor** are assembling a **hanging platform** beside **three metal cages**, some containing imprisoned people. A mounted officer with a halberd names himself **Sir Unindra/Unidra (?)**, **captain of the Harmonium's Fifty-Seventh Legion**, responsible for this entrance. The party attempts to walk past, but he demands names and claims authority. Nash struggles to intimidate him, then succeeds in unsettling his **subordinates** with a strong roll; **Stanley successfully persuades** the captain that letting these apparently unruly people **into** the Hive will remove a threat from the rest of Sigil. The officer agrees **on the explicit condition the four may *never come back out***. This is **a coercive admission and an announced intent to imprison them within the ward**, not a binding cosmological rule preventing all exits or proof the group accepts a permanent sentence.

From a cage, a **furry prisoner pleads to be rescued**, protesting innocence. The captain strikes the cage and says he will be hanged for **attempting to leave the Hive**, an offense he says **merits death because dead people cannot commit later crimes**. The heroes debate attacking the dozen officers and freeing those in custody, but **continue into the Hive without fighting, opening a cage or seeing an execution carried out**. Moral protests and plans to return disguised are **not completed actions**. They have **not yet returned to that checkpoint** by E090's end.

## IV. An unpleasant club, strawberry perfume and a new ally-or-enemy (~01:05–02:05)

The Hive has **badly broken paving, black thorny ivy, smoke, falling debris, shouting and explosions**. The characters decide against investigating every burning object, cries for help and possible crime because they need to find Tobacho before losing time. A slick-haired, fast-talking tout aggressively offers to escort them to the club **Daddy Long Legs**; he promises entertainment from women and eventually the possibility of information about Tobacho. **Nash initially pushes/hits him with a tiny extra hand**, causing **one point of damage by GM adjudication**; that little hand still exists as part of his regrown anatomy, not a separate active PC.

They first **refuse** the tout's invitation. **Hope then decides to enter Daddy Long Legs** to gather information. At its entrance he attempts to charge a gold piece for admission, but the party refuses and **is allowed in for free**. Bouncers require weapons be checked; **Stanley deposits numerous blades but keeps a concealed one**, while Hope leaves her bow and is allowed to retain **Scully**, because the staff do not recognize the pistol; **all checked weapons are retrieved when they leave**. The smoky upstairs club has dancers, customers and a crowded bar. **One dancer, a tiefling woman, asks Nash to buy her a drink.** His remark that she smells like **“strawberries and sadness”** becomes the title; later she tells him that her childhood *was* painful and that she dislikes her work. He discusses his wife, the Wastes and the possibility of finding her another life, but **does not commit to transporting her, learn her actual name or effect a rescue**. The group's discussion of prostitution/coercion includes their **inferences**; the dancer's expressed unhappiness is real dialogue, while assumptions about every worker or contract remain unverified.

**Hope** rejects the club's solicitations and investigates. A strange old patron performs a trick involving his eye, says a name resembling **Gapsafain (?)**, and repeatedly tries to meet Hope; she avoids him. A **scarred, deep-red hooded figure with engraved gold teeth** sits alone and does not join the general revelry. Hope offers him a drink and speaks of Earth. He calls himself **Spatulo/Spatula (?)** and says the **Tobacho Fleur** cantina is near the **center of the Hive**: find the **frog-on-a-stick seller** near an alley and ask for directions. He advises mentioning his name to Tobacho *if welcomed*, otherwise keeping silent. This is **useful directions but not a proved friendship with Tobacho**; the dragon's later anger confirms **Spatulo is a hostile rival/outcast in Tobacho's view**.

Vorel's alter ego **Toyota Stump** tries to withdraw from an orc dancer, loudly threatens an orc patron and almost starts a fight; the scene is **defused without combat** and he slips away. Nash stays behind briefly with the tiefling dancer, offers **only a tentative promise to try to return**, then leaves to rejoin the party. Later a hurried passerby accidentally angers him; **Nash chases him for several minutes, tackles him in an alley and threatens him with his glowing *Fire Bolt* hand**, then releases him and finds his way back. The man **survives** and no actual attack spell hits him. This side incident is **not a signed gang membership, jail offense or death**.

## V. The frog vendor, Tobacho the blue dragon and the earthward bargain (~02:05–02:49)

The group walks **for hours into the dark central Hive**, following intermittent signs. A small, dim **frog vendor stall** stands opposite another loud music venue; a short queue of drinkers buys fried frogs. The vendor serves the party; when **Hope mentions the cantina**, he subtly points into the adjacent alley, to a **burgundy metal door** under a lamp. The party knocks, and a cloudlike, oily guardian speaks through a sliding slot. Hope gives the invented gang name **“Lady Despair and the Rambunctious Rascals”**; the door **opens**, and the four descend stairs toward trumpet music and armed patrons.

Tobacho Fleur, earlier merely the **alleged steward of a recorded Earth portal in E089**, is here physically revealed as **a huge blue dragon** reclining in a specially built couch with **beret, monocle, pipe, attendants and women lounging around him**. A **stone golem** nearby interprets his speech in a **very old, only partly intelligible Draconic-like dialect**. Although Hope/Nash/Vorel may know ordinary Draconic, the GM rules that **they cannot fully understand this older dialect**. The cantina patrons are **heavily armed and tense**, and the underground establishment is a **dangerous meeting place**, not merely another entertainment club.

A powerful human intermediary **Jelver/Jolver (?)**, wearing an eyepatch, chains and a white tunic, approaches the group and offers pointers on an introduction. They invent false names: **Lady Despair (Hope), “Toyota Stump” (Vorel), “Brildaro” (Stanley) and “Nuts Phenom” (Nash)**. Jelver recognizes that they are lying and warns that Tobacho **hates deception**. He says any request should begin with a gift, preferably showing respect to the dragon's entourage. **Vorel pays an actual 50 gp** to secure an audience. After a short interval Jelver confers with Tobacho; the golem announces the visitors.

On the grated floor before the dragon, Stanley speaks politely and, after Hope's explicit nod/approval, takes **her actual reclaimed pistol *Scully*** and **presents it as a gift**. The dragon **accepts the object**, and a goblin servant **carries Scully under the dragon's enormous couch**. **Hope's possession of Scully ends here**; she has **not yet recovered *Mulder***, the second gun tied by Java to Tobacho's network. The weapon is **not returned on screen**, regardless of whether a later bargain might recover it.

The group requests access to a portal leading to **Earth**. Tobacho describes Earth as **a cursed destination** and says he can help, but **not without compensation**. Through his golem he offers a job: **recover a special unspecified item using their fighting abilities**, after which he will **provide the location and means of accessing the Earth portal**. He directs them to Jelver to hear the **sensitive details in private**; **the job target, custodian, exact risk, deadline, contract text and portal keys are *not* yet disclosed**. The offer is **a negotiated promise with an interested and dangerous merchant-dragon**, not a verified open portal ready for immediate travel, and no item retrieval or completed Earth journey occurs.

As they withdraw, **Hope says that Spatulo sends his regards**. The dragon reacts with visible displeasure; his golem labels Spatulo **an “infidel” who interferes with Tobacho's business** and advises the party to stay away. Tobacho then offers **additional payment for bringing Spatulo to him captive**. Hope does **not accept or perform the task before the episode closes**; this may be a future source of pressure. The group **follows Jelver into a dim passage** for the pending private mission briefing. **The episode ends there**, before they learn which artifact Tobacho wants.
