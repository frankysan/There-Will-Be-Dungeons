## Continuity decisions and cautions

- **E034 changes our evidentiary status for Stanley:** E033 only suggested an alternate identity; E034 provides **direct in-session self-identification as Diontalus**, including an admission of a **forced**, not consensual, arrangement. His *ancient history* remains unverified, as does the precise control mechanism.
- **Dion versus Stanley:** They share **one body** by the entity's admission. Do not index Dion as a separate physically present adventurer or call Stanley dead.
- **Vampirism remains unproven:** A reflected image is one table-side test. Hope's E033 max-HP reduction is real play data; whether it persists and what caused it remains a review item.
- **Blood Ball is practice, not Friday's game:** The **jerseys and training** are observed; **200 guzzbolts**, royal dinner, and keep access remain **prospective benefits**. No victory, team deaths in this match or invited royal dinner has yet occurred.
- **P0D's testimony about captive pixies** and the plan to help remain separate from any actually executed infiltration tactic.
