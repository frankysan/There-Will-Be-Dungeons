## Continuity boundaries

- **Do not infer bleach was administered** from the title; the plot discussed involves a **pixie-farm rescue** and **unresolved plans** to reach the wedding. No mass-casualty incident is shown.
- **Twenty-Sixers ≠ Toe Rippers:** Monsoon leads the first faction; assassination target belongs to the rival gang. **Bok Bok wearing a toe** is troubling but does not independently prove he murdered someone or secretly joined the Twenty-Sixers.
- **Diontalus and consent:** Buttons's firsthand eyewitness concern in E032 corroborates that **the E029 forced intrusion was observable to him**. **Stanley uses Suggestion on Buttons**, creating a new distinct act of magical coercion. Neither proves voluntary pact or definitive possession.
- **Access route alternatives:** Monsoon's conditional fortress direction, **Tedna's catering lead**, and the city's official/blood-ball routes are different **proposals**, not accomplished access to the keep.
- **Jedel:** The building's corpse and fanged figure at E032 end are not proof the dead man was **Jedel McCready**, and the attacker is called **vampire-like** on appearance/behavior, not independently identified by species.
