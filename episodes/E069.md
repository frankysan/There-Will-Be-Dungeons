# E069 — Crimes Against Doormanity

> **Corrected main-campaign E069**, after [E068](E068.md). Based on [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E069.%20Crimes%20Against%20Doormanity.parakeet.processed.srt); **not verified against audio**. Approximate recording offsets include listener mail, recaps, intermission and outro. Full spoilers through E069.

## What happens

The hidden **metal double doors under Kervalon** are protected by a speaking, biting **door-control creature**. It says the party is wanted by the **Door Authority**, which records crimes against **“doormanity”**, and that the party is banned from opening doors. After Hope fires her shotgun, Nash threatens the door with **Acid Splash**; the creature bargains to receive a small amount of acid so it can truthfully report being coerced. Nash actually splashes acid on the panel, and the doors **open**. This is a negotiation followed by a played spell, not merely a hypothetical threat.

Beyond lies an ancient, **purple-fogged transport tunnel** with many black worms heading toward the surface. A cable-driven **funicular** descends at roughly 45 degrees. Hope inspects the controls and Stanley uses *Mage Hand*; Vorel **breaks its stuck entrance door**, and the group boards. A telepathic voice threatens the travelers. **Magically charged pink-purple flowers and spores** along the tunnel put **Nash and Hope to sleep**, while **Stanley is protected against magical sleep by his elven ancestry** and Vorel holds his breath. The funicular **runs away downhill and crashes**, but Stanley casts **Feather Fall**, saving the passengers from the worst of the wreck. Vorel forcibly shakes Nash and Hope awake. The **funicular is wrecked**, not an operable vehicle at episode end.

At the foot of the line, they discover the ancient **Center for Human-Demon Interdisciplinary Studies**, lettered **CHIDS** according to the sign (working abbreviation, transcript `C H I D S`). The welcome desk contains long-dead human remains, old computers, and an interactive **hologram named Julia**, who describes partnerships between human research and demons, including alleged **infernal-wave technology** and a **“mega cooker.”** She shows a facility rail-map with several colored routes, offices, laboratories, containment and engineering, but requires identification to pass security. She refers to a **United Federation of Continents** identity-verification system. **Her pre-catastrophe institutional claims are an old automated briefing, not current evidence that facilities are operational.**

The group searches **living pods**, the **food court**, and an **oxygen bar** for credentials. Cards on dead employees include **Stefan Borghetty**, **Bobby Bees**, **Dr. Yxtlee/Yxtle** (a nonhuman/demonic scientist; letters spoken `Y X T L E`), **Ricky Julian**, and **Tara Mebat** (all phonetic/source spellings provisional). Nash finds/wears a pair of **self-cooling magically levitating briefs**, giving him **10 feet of flight speed** as the GM explains; a separate skeleton floated while wearing them, but falls apart when the underwear is removed. Stanley and Vorel collect a still-liquid bottle labeled **Coca-Cola** from an old McDonald's. Hope takes **four Jack Daniel's bottles** from the bar. Hope activates an old “oxygen” mask for Nash using Tara's card: **brown, foul fumes poison him and cause 6 damage**. The poisoning **is not expressly cured in this episode**.

At security, passing the barrier involves ID cards and automated biometric scans. **Vorel, Nash and Stanley pass**; Hope encounters failed scans, **turrets deploy**, Stanley's **Diplomacy** intervenes, and Vorel **punches/bends open the exit door** to reunite the party. There is **no proof that the employee names match the protagonists' real identities**. The party boards a powered **railcar**, travels the **blue route**, and bypasses multiple ruined/collapsed stations, notably **Laboratory C, storage, shipping and receiving, administration**. A functioning **Gordon Shuttle Bay** and **crystal-covered Laboratory A** are noticed but **not entered**. At **executive offices**, they disembark to search for clues. In the final scene, something **tentacled seizes toward Hope's head from behind**, but its nature is **not yet resolved in E069**.

## Source cautions

**Earth-before-Scorching history:** The university/administrative world of **CHIDS**, **United Federation of Continents**, *infernal wave*, and **Julia** arise from in-world machines/recordings. The facility is visibly old and partially powered; do not assume the automated spokesperson knows current events or is alive.

**Spells and status:** *Mold Earth* **loose-earth correction is an OOC rules retraction**, not a performed undoing of E068's trap. **Nash's oxygen-mask poison** requires follow-up; its removal/expiration is not yet sourced. The levitating **briefs belong in current Nash inventory E069**, not Hope's; different cards have specific custody and access utility.

**Open at cutoff:** **Fate 4 remains destroyed**, **Follow/Rorf remain dead**, **Sira is not rescued**, and **the planet-moving machine and mind-flayer commander remain inaccessible**. Tentacled presence near Hope is a cliffhanger, not already a confirmed new illithid attack.
