# E106 — I Took An Arrow Thru The Brain

> **The Wastes · corrected S1E106 · Arc Five · played campaign session · ASR-provisional.** [Processed Parakeet](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E106.%20I%20Took%20An%20Arrow%20Thru%20The%20Brain.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E106.%20I%20Took%20An%20Arrow%20Thru%20The%20Brain.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [full-export comparison](../reports/E106_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). Both SRTs run through **02:40:30**, including postgame and credits; not independently listened to. The owner-preserved publication catalogue identifies **TWBD 108, 8 June 2020**, distinct from corrected episode E106; its source metadata has not been independently verified. Host says **Sunday, 7 June** and apologizes for missing the previous week's show; neither automatically proves recording day. Unlike E105, **both separated party lines are played** here.

**Played cutoff:** Tobacho **examines and retains the separate Zorander's Fist gauntlet** after Stanley locates the key; the **Hand of the Voice is missing** and he notices. Despite the incomplete prize, his representative says the bargain will be honored by contracted guide **Badass T**—**not** a direct Sigil–Earth portal. The original travelers perform a Dwarvish poem/plant portal ritual, reach a strange **spaceport** and board a gnome-crewed ship; after an improbable jump they **dock on paired asteroids** outside a crooked tower, where a **giant carved likeness of Nash calls him “Junior”**. Separately, **Hope returns to Brett's Kaiser Casino**, meets **the Principle/Vladimir**, hears his claims about Earth and the **Lich Project**, accepts a proposed cooperation and is directed toward the **Eye of the Voice** and **Tobacho Fleur**. **Spatulo is killed by Dr. Spider's Mental Prison in the doctor's infernal laboratory**, his brain is removed and shot with an arrow; **Hope, Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider then physically arrive on Sigil streets**. The two rescued tieflings **do not accompany them**; their location is unconfirmed. **No one reaches Earth during E106; neither side meets the other in Sigil.**

## The Fist, a revised bargain and a bounty in Tobacho's club (~00:03–00:46)

1. **An audience with Tobacho (~00:03–00:18).** The party approaches the raised blue dragon amid music and attendants. **A stone golem acts as Tobacho's interpreter** while he speaks an unfamiliar ancient-sounding Draconic; don't treat the transcription of his syllables as translated dialogue. **Stanley performs Enthralling Performance**, deliberately targeting **Tobacho, Jalver** (visible with Perception 24) and selected spectators, under the ability's charm restrictions. The table explains the mechanic, but individual save outcomes and persistence should not be overstated. Stanley gives a formal account of the party's dangerous retrieval. **The case is still closed at this point**, in Vorel's possession.
2. **The lost key is actually found (~00:19–00:25).** The players hesitate about custody of the **medallion key** and check inventories; the GM adjudicates **Stanley finding it unusually tucked into his boot** after an Investigation check of 20. **The odd placement is not attributed to an observed thief or secret transfer. Stanley uses the recovered medallion to open the enchanted case** before the audience. The ornate **gold-white Fist gauntlet with purple corruption** is visibly present. This supersedes the uncertainty over key location, but does not restore the Hand.
3. **Tobacho takes the gauntlet and discovers the missing Hand (~00:25–00:32).** The golem carries **the actual gauntlet** to Tobacho, who holds it in his claws and examines it with a jeweler's loupe. He points to the **toothed/membranous inside** and says he expected **an item within the glove**. **The missing item is Hope's now-attached Hand of the Voice**; neither a second gauntlet nor a new unmentioned relic is seen. Stanley deceives him about their ignorance of the missing component: **Deception 32**, followed by **19**, with Tobacho's reaction becoming more suspicious. **Physical delivery of the gauntlet succeeds; delivery of the Hand does not.**
4. **No direct portal in Sigil; contracted onward transport (~00:32–00:39).** Tobacho, via the golem, says he **will honor the bargain** despite disappointment. He then admits there is **no known Sigil portal directly to Earth**, contrary to the party's expectation of a location and access key. He introduces **Badass T**, a bald, gas-masked human-looking man with a car-front breastplate, marked head and one wandering eye, as an outside contractor who claims he knows them. **The route is a multi-stage onward trip**; at this stage Tobacho does **not** give the characters a direct Earth portal or return them home. Nash objects privately that this is not the deal promised.
5. **Hope's price announced in public (~00:35–00:40).** Tobacho projects an illusion of **Hope**, then announces an **open bounty across creation**: **10 million gold for Hope without the right arm**, **another 10 million in the dead-with-arm description**, and **50 million for the right arm alone**. Exact additive interpretation of the middle payment and whether the first option means alive require listening; don't flatten to a confidently priced captured-alive tier. This is **a bounty advertised by Tobacho**, not a demonstrated capture or an independently identified buyer. He notes the companions' proximity to her and says he considers **debts associated with their taken items absolved by the delivered gauntlet**. Whether this also releases Nash from the **independent harbor's 100,000-gold uninsured-airship claim** is **not expressly adjudicated**. The golem reverts to stone, the nightclub resumes, and **Vorel leaves the chest/case with Tobacho's people**, so he is no longer transporting a sealed Fist after this.
6. **Returning a different borrowed weapon (~00:39–00:42).** **Vorel finds Jalver** in the club and **physically returns the borrowed Shatter/Angel Slayer sword** with a respectful exchange. Do not retain this as Vorel's current carried weapon or imply it was consumed. **Badass T** urges the party to leave quickly; his competence, motive, claimed history with them and master are not yet established.

## The route out of Sigil is paid for in blood (~00:44–01:20)

7. **The Mangler's fatal interruption (~00:44–00:49).** Outside the club, an armored swordsman calling himself **the Mangler** proposes teaming up to kill/capture Hope for the bounty. **Badass T repeatedly stabs him in the neck with a machete** before any deal is made. The narrated death is deliberate and witnessed by passersby, who react little in the Hive. **Neither Nash, Vorel nor Stanley ordered or performed the killing**. Badass T insists speed and secrecy matter. The group looks for a rickshaw.
8. **52 Pinocchio Street is the frog vendor's home (~00:49–01:03).** **Stanley casts Haste on Vorel**; Vorel races to a distant rickshaw, pays the driver and returns with transport. The guide takes them to **number 52 on Pinocchio Street** (working ASR street spelling) in a verdant residential area. The elderly owner **is the familiar frog-on-a-stick seller** normally seen near Tobacho; his E105 absence is explained as **his holiday**, rather than proof he had already been killed. **Stanley's Persuasion 27** wins permission to use a portal hidden in the man's basement in exchange for having revealed it. Frog preservation/slaughter equipment is present. At **this moment the owner is alive** and voluntarily grants access; he has not been described as an enemy.
9. **Ritual actually performed (~01:03–01:14).** **Badass T's notebook** describes two actions in order: **Stanley stands on his head and reads a long Dwarvish/phonetic poem**, using **Mage Hand** to hold the book, then **Nash eats a green bulb-tipped plant called a dizzle/dwizzle dink (?)**. Stanley succeeds in the handstand checks (natural 20 initially, then 18). Nash **really eats the plant**, and a **purple portal opens in the brick wall**. These are performed actions, not merely travel plans. **Stanley ends up carrying Badass T's ritual notebook through the portal**, with no observed return of it before episode cutoff.
10. **The vendor is left behind; the route is attacked (~01:14–01:20).** Vorel, Stanley, Nash, Squirts and Chunks, and their guide **cross to an alien-looking docking area** populated by uniform yellow blob-like beings; the GM refers to **six** entering the subsequent ship group. **Badass T crosses last with a bloody knife**. Asked whether he killed the frog seller, he **declines to give a direct yes/no**, defending the need to remove witnesses. The later **cast talks as though the vendor died**; separate **on-screen body/death verification is missing**, so record **strongly implied killing, not directly witnessed corpse**. They board a waiting gnome-crewed airship; **Badass T orders the crew to fire its cannons at the just-used portal** and the ship departs and makes an **improbability/hyperspace-style jump**. Do not describe an intact, usable return route or completed Earth arrival. **Sam and the earlier orc survivors are not re-enumerated aboard this second ship**; do not silently carry all ten onto it.

## Hope, the Principle and the cost of survival (~01:20–01:58)

11. **Hope's E104 gate led back to the Kaiser Casino (~01:20–01:32).** Hope, **Steerious Business**, **Blood Skunk Hendrickson**, **Dr. Spider** and **Spatulo** emerge in Brett's casino. **Steerious initially still carries the two Tietkalla tiefling refugees**, who are put down; their presence in the casino is **directly depicted**. Brett introduces his present guest: **the Principle**, personally called **Vladimir**, in a towering metal body. Hope confronts Brett for knowing that hostile forces could track her. Brett claims she has only been gone **about half a day**, a local relative-time statement, not a globally established chronology. The Principle says his **Principal City is under demonic attack** and claims an **existing peace treaty** with the tiefling kingdom; neither the exact battle outcome nor invasion motives are independently observed.
12. **An alliance proposed amid a narrated catastrophe (~01:32–01:45).** At a private table, Brett attempts to reconcile Hope and the Principle. The Principle says he sought the **Hand of the Voice** to defend Earth, but Hope now possesses it; he proposes they **work together**. He and Brett assert the demonic assault on Principal City is underway and that defending Earth matters to both. Brett creates **illusory/history scenes** showing a prosperous pre-Scorching Earth, **tieflings treated as demon property**, dragon incursions, aerial lizardfolk defenders and a younger human **Vladimir** entering a secret **CHIDS facility**. A security guard is shot in the reenactment, a large blood-fueled apparatus starts and purple devastation spreads near the old site. **These are presented by interested witnesses through illusion**, not time travel or independent objective observation of every historic detail. The Principle claims the Scorching happened **once**, killed **billions**, and that reports of **millions dying per day** reflect its duration, not repeated Scorchings.
13. **The Lich Project (~01:47–01:55).** Another **illustration shown by Brett/the Principle** depicts a vast facility of **rapidly grown, limbless, eyeless human “pod people.”** The Principle openly **claims to consume the souls of 100 living humans per day** to prolong his own life and to recycle their remains as nutrients for successors; his life-preserving system is called the **Lich Project**. The GM depicts one pod in the projected scene, but **this is testimony/vision**, not an unannounced physical party visit or independently audited census. Hope **nods her assent to cooperation after learning this**, a major character decision; no formal written contract or actual joint combat against demons follows.
14. **A different artifact and objective (~01:55–01:59).** The Principle identifies a sought **Eye of the Voice** as another artifact connected with the secret-bearing being that now owns Hope's arm, and sends her to **Tobacho Fleur** as someone who might help find it. **The Eye of the Voice is not the Eye of Groxl** restored to Vorel, and **neither is recovered in this episode**. The Principle warns of wider competition and suggests **Carvutican/Car Vatican (?)** on Earth as a future rendezvous via Hope's ring. The meeting's exact place-name pronunciation needs listening. Brett's claim to friendship and the Principle's assertions about the Voice remain **interested testimony**, not definitive authorial cosmology.

## Spatulo's death and the separate Sigil crossing (~01:58–02:32)

15. **Spatulo's challenge becomes a killing (~01:58–02:12).** Spatulo declares that claiming the Hand was **his destiny** and refuses Hope's authority. **Steerious Business twice attacks with his enormous sword** (11 and 10 damage), badly wounding but not yet killing him. Spatulo falls to his knees and **begs Hope for mercy**. **Blood Skunk threatens to shoot him**, while Dr. Spider suggests obtaining his secrets by preserving his brain. Spatulo says he knows **Tobacho's weakness**, but that is **unverified testimony** at this point, not a confirmed exploitable weakness. The company and Brett move to **Dr. Spider's laboratory in Hell**; Hope's **single-charge copper Gate ring was spent on the previous escape**, preventing an immediate second use before a new qualifying act.
16. **Dr. Spider kills Spatulo; the brain survives as a talking specimen (~02:09–02:23).** The doctor's infernal quarters contain tanks, an assistant **Nurse Wilkins/Milkins (?)** and a preserved infant-like **star child** in a container; no fresh birth is depicted here. The captured Spatulo is strapped to an examination table. **Dr. Spider casts Mental Prison**, and although the target passes the Intelligence save, the table assesses **41 psychic damage**. The GM **explicitly narrates Spatulo expiring**. **Dr. Spider removes his brain into a yellow-fluid jar fitted with a speaker** and uses a *Whispers of the Grave/Speak with Dead* effect to question the dead. **The speaker's answers are necromantic testimony, not a living resurrection**: the alleged Tobacho “secret” is **a lie**; he further claims Tobacho envies the Lady of Pain. **Blood Skunk physically fires an arrow through the glass and the preserved brain** (attack 18), and the speaker **continues to respond afterward**. Subsequent organ harvesting and payment of **five gold each** to companions are depicted. **The title's arrow strikes Spatulo's already dead, bottled brain**, rather than being the spell that initially killed him.
17. **Hope reaches Sigil, but not Tobacho yet (~02:23–02:32).** Brett declines transport **into Sigil**, citing its restriction on powers; the Principle departs using a jewel/ring effect. **Steerious admits he has lost track of the two refugees** somewhere in the infernal casino environs; **do not invent their death, departure, or appearance in Sigil**. Hope takes killing Spatulo as the evil deed needed to **recharge her copper ring**; it heats to the point of scorching her hand, and the GM **accepts the resulting charged use in play**. She creates a **visible violet-blue Gate to the streets of Sigil**, and **Hope, Steerious, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider** really cross. The doctor's star-child container is **taken through**. This **is not a reunion with Nash's group** and not proof Hope has met Tobacho or found the Eye of the Voice.

## Other travelers, another apparent history (~02:32–02:40)

18. **A second space destination, not Earth (~02:32–02:39).** The scene returns to **Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Squirts/Chunks and Badass T** as their ship arrives in an eerie green-yellow nebula. Two **connected asteroids** carry a **crooked medieval-style tower** and a floating docking ledge. There is a large **statue of Stanley** at the dock, whose origin **even Stanley cannot explain**; the group finds breathable air despite outer space. A nearby **hamburger factory** is identified by Badass T as a failed Sigil business retained to keep workers employed, **his report only**. The door to the tower resembles a **skull's mouth**; the interior immediately proves **larger than the outside**.
19. **The real cliffhanger (~02:38–02:40).** Inside are **twelve small pedestals, rear stair doors and a carved wall-face of Nash Maggard**. **The face actually speaks**, addresses Nash as **“Junior”** and says it is glad he has arrived. Nash asks who it is; **the scene cuts off before an answer**. **Do not assume a verified father, time-travel duplicate, dragon-heart origin or identity of Badass T's master**. The brief **“I've aged pretty well”** at the very end of both ASR exports is an orphaned cue outside the played cutoff/credits, potentially copied or displaced from the Principle's earlier self-commentary; audio must determine its true place. Cast sign-off and credits follow the played ending.
