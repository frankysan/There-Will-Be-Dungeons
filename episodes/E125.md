**Main-party cutoff (~02:21):** The four companions, **Lyrielle, Angelashi, four children, Bok Bok and Chunks** enter the hidden study/secure upper floor of **Villa Ornath** as Elhandar falls toward Earth. This changes Squirts's post-E111 status **for that later scene**; it does not undo her E111 death, place her aboard Elhandar, or mean her daughter Chunks has died.

## The engineer, Mars and a call on Diontalus (~00:03:33–00:29:35)

1. **E124 accord remains conditional (~00:03–00:08).** The GM restates the agreement: Diontalus returns to **his own court**, not immediately the royal court; he promises to wait for Stanley and provide help against the Principle. The party is still physically in the upper **Cadulet/Cadulate (?) spire audience chamber**, with High Elf **Amanthalodar/Amenthalodar (?)**, and the family waiting outside in reception. The High Elf's old proposed mind-sharing/introduction is later set aside; Stanley has already discovered his father's study, but the elf warns **more secrets remain**. None of the earlier negotiated terms proves future compliance.
2. **Tungsten Wheel (~00:07–00:12:45).** Stanley asks about a **Rubistic Triangular Sphere** (orthography/source meaning provisional), which the party believes is needed for Earth's recovery. Amanthalodar says a single gifted Elhandari engineer, **Tungsten Wheel**, might construct it. The engineer has been absent for decades, designed parts of the spire and the empathy-grenade shipyards, and reportedly disappeared during his pursuit of exotic minerals and dimensional engineering. **His fate and whereabouts are unknown**, and nobody actually contacts him here.
3. **A lover on Mars; coordinates actually supplied (~00:12:45–00:18).** The High Elf says Tungsten's lover is at an **Elhandari Mars terraforming platform**; the spoken name is exceptionally uncertain, approximately **Dungstil/Dungilsta Ravensbread (?)**. A planted tree has begun to improve the platform's air; the surrounding Mars environment remains lethally inhospitable according to the elf. He **actually supplies Nash with teleportation-circle glyphs** for both the Mars platform and Elhandar's spire. The party **does not cast Teleportation Circle, go to Mars, locate the lover, or fabricate the sphere** during E125. The natural dragon heart remains buried in its earlier location.
4. **A tangible summoning ocarina (~00:18–00:22).** Stanley again lists his obligations: the engineer and device, the hottest place on Earth, defeating the Principle, and finding his father. Diontalus insists he will strike the killing blow against the Principle and gives Stanley an **ethereal ocarina which actually becomes a polished dark-wood instrument in Stanley's hand**. Playing even a few notes is supposed to call him. This is a **real transfer of a physical artifact**, not a summons successfully tested yet; the promise is Diontalus's own claim.
5. **Attribution for earlier attacks (~00:23–00:27).** In answer to Vorel, Diontalus **admits forcing the deer-disguised mages to serve him**, and also **claims ownership of the ghostly pirates who haunted the party's ship during the pregnancy**: he says he released them while escaping and used them for passage back to Elhandar. This is antagonist confession, rather than new observation of those earlier scenes. Vorel challenges his treatment of mortals and the threat to the family; Diontalus responds with another threat. The accord's stability remains questionable.

## Fleet assault and parley (~00:29:35–00:42)

6. **Elhandar attacked in play (~00:29–00:33).** A **castle accompanied by approximately one hundred airships** appears outside the observation dome and fires blue projectiles. The station rocks, its shields fail, damage is reported below decks, and evacuation preparations are ordered. Amanthalodar visibly **intercepts an incoming projectile with magic**. Simultaneously about **one hundred armed figures resembling Sigil Hive mercenaries teleport into the audience chamber**, surrounding the companions, the elf and Diontalus.
7. **The three-headed fleet commander (~00:33–00:40).** A human with **three speaking heads**, purple robe and hovering disk introduces himself as approximately **Henitar/Henatar Grape (?)**, leader of the **Asphyxiant Army (?)**. He says his employer has issued a contract on **Hope and Vorel**, one dead and one capturable alive or dead, but does **not consistently identify which is which**. He attributes the commission to **Mind Master Gerbken Shartlinger** (project's earlier accepted name; E125 ASR says Gerbkin/Gerbken), who allegedly betrayed **Tobacho Fleur** to the Lady of Pain, causing the dragon to be imprisoned in a labyrinth. He also alleges that Vorel cheated at M Sports. These are **the commander's accusations and testimony**, not a witnessed Sigil power transfer or verified fate for Tobacho. Hope associates the interest in her with the Hand of the Voice; Vorel jokingly announces an intention to take the commander's castle, **not actual new ownership**.
8. **The berries activate (~00:35–00:36, effect confirmed ~01:13–01:14).** The berries supplied before E124's Diontalus audience warm in the companions' stomachs. Their **Haste-like effect becomes explicit during the battle**: extra action/speed, +2 AC and Dexterity-save advantage as ruled at the table. This was not shown to be a permanent property or an additional gained spell slot.

## The fight and the station's collapse (~00:42–01:40)

9. **Stanley initiates combat (~00:42–00:49).** Rejecting parley, Stanley **actually casts Destructive Wave**, dealing a narrated **42 damage to failed saves / 21 to successes**, knocking roughly ninety of the first hundred soldiers prone. The GM treats it as creature-focused energy that does not destroy the chamber's physical structure. Stanley then uses **Mantle of Inspiration** to give allies and Amanthalodar **11 temporary hit points** and allow reaction movement. Hope and Vorel close on the hovering commander; Nash rises above him.
10. **Hope's knife and the High Elf's defenses (~00:51–00:57).** Hope's attempt to kick the floating disk fails, but **two dagger strikes hit** with additional cold damage from her hand. Amanthalodar grows **vine armor**, animates **four giant trees** and produces a protective **wind wall/canopy**. Diontalus joins the fight by cutting through mercenaries with his spectral scythe and visibly **reaping their ghosts/souls**. These are performed acts, not merely hypothetical contributions to the fight.
11. **Nash's first lightning rhyme (~00:58–01:02).** Nash casts **Chain Lightning** from above, after reading and clarifying the four-target effect; the GM resolves **42 damage** and the death of all four struck mercenaries. Nash voices an improvised casting couplet approximating **'Here I float above your heads / May all these volts put you to bed'** (ASR-only, exact wording/listening pending). He retreats behind the defensive wall. The commander's disk is then **abandoned on the floor** as he teleports away; **it is not shown seized or owned by Vorel**.
12. **Mercenary counterattack (~01:03–01:12).** Ranged attackers shoot Vorel; his **Eye of Groxl** draws attacks and grants a noted resistance. Hope takes heavy melee damage and Vorel lesser damage. Vorel **rages** and kills two attackers with his flaming sword; the party's companions remain alive at this point. The first mercenary wave is scattered and repeatedly reaped by Diontalus and the animated trees.
13. **Reinforcements, haste and further attacks (~01:12–01:31).** Eight ogres join the fight; the berries' Haste benefit is made explicit. Hope **kills five mercenaries** in a hasted dagger sequence and moves back-to-back with Vorel. Stanley casts a **second Destructive Wave**, devastates surviving mercenaries, and then stabs an ogre with Diplomacy. Nash **casts another Haste on Vorel**, and says a second improvised rhyme approximately **'Hey Vorel, have a taste / It's time for a little bit more of the haste'** (ASR-only). The GM allows stacked effects as a **specific table ruling**, not verified general D&D rules. Nash proposes **Stinking Cloud** but **does not cast it** after a same-turn spell restriction is invoked; he instead attacks an ogre with a spiked boot. Another ogre unsuccessfully attempts to grapple him.
14. **Castle bombardment, ogres and assault escalation (~01:31–01:40).** Amanthalodar lets the wind canopy fall and produces a barrier in front of the dome. He **reflects an eight-projectile volley back toward the assaulting ships**. Vorel, moving at extraordinary Haste-granted speed, **kills an ogre** beset by Stanley; trees and remaining ogres fight. At the second-round cutoff **another hundred mercenaries teleport in**. Elhandar **begins an uncontrolled descent toward Earth**, and Amanthalodar orders immediate evacuation, telling Stanley to take his family to **Villa Ornath**, where his father's secrets should afford protection. The total number of combatants actually killed is **not an audited census**.

## The escape to Villa Ornath (~01:40–02:21)

15. **Abort a risky portal; Diontalus acts on the accord (~01:40–01:45).** The ship's violent shaking prevents Nash from confidently drawing usable teleportation sigils, so **he never completes a Teleportation Circle**. Vorel physically gathers the companions; Hope wants to leave with Stanley. As they depart the audience, **Diontalus says a farewell warning to Stanley, teleports away and is then visibly seen as a blue moving light attacking and exploding several ships in the hostile fleet**. This is **observed aid against the fleet**, distinct from his as-yet unfulfilled pledge to fight the Principle or return to his court. Amanthalodar heads for a separate evacuation pod. The party retrieves its family in reception; an initial mention of Squirts among the small companions is **explicitly corrected to Chunks** at ~01:45, with no resurrected Squirts on the station.
16. **Elevator cable snaps (~01:45–02:00).** The household rushes into the spire's descending elevator. Its cable **breaks**, leaving the car in lethal freefall. Stanley casts **Feather Fall** over the occupants, accounting for children carried/attached to adults and picking up Chunks; the descending metal car still pushes them against its ceiling. Vorel begins a dangerous **cut-the-ceiling skill challenge** with his flaming sword, aided by Stanley's bardic inspiration, Hope scoring the metal with a dagger, and **three distinct Mage Hands** from Nash, Lyrielle and Stanley. One cutting attempt **fails**, not a completed escape.
17. **The ceiling is finally opened (~02:00–02:04).** On a later roll, **Vorel succeeds** in cutting a full opening in the car roof. The car crashes apart below while the occupants **separate from the detached roof and reach the shaft floor under Feather Fall**. No member of the family is described as dying in the elevator. The episode-specific skill/ruling is not a general rule that Feather Fall alone safely separates someone from a falling elevator.
18. **Flight across the biodome (~02:04–02:18).** They pass through Rainbow Road and find several parked **noble speeders**. **Stanley, Hope and Lyrielle actually pilot three**, with Vorel riding Hope's, Nash and Angelashi carrying the four children on Lyrielle's, and Bok Bok and **Chunks** on Stanley's. The dome ruptures overhead and falling metal threatens the riders. Stanley prepares a **Diontalus-device Otiluke's Resilient Sphere** contingency but **does not activate it**. Lyrielle instead **casts Wall of Force**, creating a protective overhead surface that permits all three speeders to slip beneath a huge descending beam. They **reach Villa Ornath alive**, leaving the station's ultimate fate unresolved.
19. **Hidden shelter and main-party fadeout (~02:18–02:21).** Stanley leads the household up into his father's previously discovered **concealed study/upper room** using its key and closes the entry. Magical lights/candles illuminate it, and **wooden seats with restraints actually emerge** in response to the descent. Nash secures his children and the others buckle in. Violent tearing, fire and then a **major impact** are heard, after which the GM **fades to black**. Neither a witnessed rescue afterward nor a confirmed family death, total station loss, heart recovery, engineer visit or Mars arrival occurs before this cutoff.

## Played Hell/Sigil epilogue: a new captain (~02:22–02:31)

20. **Separate viewpoint, not the family after the crash.** The GM expressly switches sheets to **Steerious Business**, **Blood Skunk Hendrickson** and **Dr. Spider** in Hell after Hope departed their group. At Spider's medical ward, the three gather because **Brent has summoned them**. Blood Skunk still lacks his external ears; cosmetic recovery is not played. The group describes Hope's earlier escape through a portal with the 'bandage man', but this is their own remembered account, not something newly witnessed at this point.
21. **Brent's instruction and Mysteriosa (~02:25–02:29).** Brent arrives with **Mysteriosa**, who later displays a **snake-like tongue**. He criticizes the three for losing track of Hope and says they need a better finder. Their earlier assignment to watch Hope continues; this **does not imply any of them is now at Villa Ornath**. He takes the three through a portal to a technologically advanced, fluid-fed Hell medical chamber.
22. **Squirts reappears in a new body (~02:29–02:31).** Brent **claims** that he noticed **Squirts's soul in a soul stream**, struck bargains and made modifications. Mysteriosa operates the chamber; a pod **actually drains and opens** to reveal a small kobold whose **eyes open**, and Brent formally introduces **Squirts as the group's new captain tasked with tracking Hope**. This is the actual post-E111 on-screen return/reconstitution, **not a false E124 family roster resurrection**. The precise resurrection mechanics, what modifications were made, Squirts's agency, memory, continued loyalty and later actions are **not yet shown**. The scene ends at the introduction; credits/closing discussion continue through **02:34:08**.
