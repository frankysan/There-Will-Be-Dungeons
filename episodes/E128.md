**Played finale cutoff (~02:08–02:12 before discussion/outro at 02:14:47):** **All four main adventurers—Stanley, Nash, Hope and Vorel—remain alive on Earth's desert surface**, gathered near intact but battered **Villa Or'Nath** among wreckage of **Elhandar**. A colossal Weir-associated six-limbed, gear-scaled **burrowing creature** has entered Earth's ground from a violet tear and departed beneath the sand; the party **survives its tidal sandstorm by riding Nash's black-dragon form over the storm**. The surviving Harbor tieflings, Nash's family, Lyrielle and Bok Bok have **boarded Elhandari ships bound for the Mars terraforming platform**. The elves **actually recovered the dead dragon's natural metal heart** and carried it aboard, but **Ruby/Rubistic Triangulosphere is not built**, **Tungsten Wheel not located**, **the Mind Master not confronted**, **the Principle not killed**, **the Weir not defeated**, and **Stanley has not completed mask attunement/spaceflight**. **E128 closes this story arc and Season 1, not the campaign's ultimate conflict**. They **complete an uneventful overnight rest** and the GM awards **level 13**; proposed mission ordering is still a discussion, not a journey completed.

## After the guitar falls: the mercenary's fate (~00:00–00:21)

1. **A living prisoner, a mercenary's plea (~00:03–00:15).** Kristen recaps E126 in verse. Henatar/Henitar Grape is **conscious and alive** after the fall. Nash, still a **large black dragon**, confirms movement/breathing, holds him beneath a foot and demands answers. Henatar identifies **the Mind Master in Sigil** as his employer (a claim, not an independent meeting), calls the attack a paid job and complains of the unforeseen appearance of **Diontalus**. **Stanley still wears/carries the magical guitar**. Stanley **stabs the captive's leg nonlethally with Diplomacy**, not a lethal blow. The idea that a deal might spare him is discussed, but **the party makes no agreement granting him safety**.
2. **Nash kills Henatar (~00:15–00:19).** Nash attempts to tear off one of the three heads while Henatar pleads to live. The first bite partly lifts the weakened man by the neck; the GM asks for a Medicine/Survival check to separate the head without killing him. Nash **makes the check**, tears a head free, and the GM expressly narrates that **Henatar takes his last breath and dies**. Nash chews and swallows that head. This supersedes **E126's correct “limp but not established dead”** cutoff; do **not** backdate the death to the earlier acid/fall. Later, in front of the party, **Nash tears off the second side head**, leaving the middle for Diontalus. **Violent execution after surrender is played, not simply “defeated in fair combat.”**
3. **The orbs are hazardous (~00:19–00:21).** Hope searches the dead mercenary, finding a robe, the fallen disk and scattered spheres. **Trying to take one orb triggers a magical/electrical shock and hurls her approximately 30 feet**, destroying that orb; do not list the set as collected or safely possessed. A numerical damage phrase is ambiguous between a dice notation and number in ASR; **do not assert exactly 56 HP without audio/dice verification**. Stanley **retains the guitar**, whose permanent item mastery remains distinct from possession.

## Diontalus and the Elhandari survivors (~00:21–00:40)

4. **Diontalus revived (~00:21–00:25).** Stanley finds the blue spectral **Diontalus motionless in a crater**, casts **third-level *Healing Word*** and the GM narrates **12 HP recovered; Diontalus opens his eyes, rises, speaks and walks**. **This is a witnessed recovery from E126's defeat**, not a new physical possession of Stanley. The archfey complains about being denied Stanley's body and wants the fallen mercenary.
5. **Surviving elves land (~00:23–00:35).** Elhandari troop ships land in the desert; **Admiral Chrysorius (?)** and **High Elf Amanthalodar (?)** (names ASR-provisional) come down with elves who scour wreckage and recover other survivors. The High Elf says **all hostile fleet vessels were lost except the commander**—his account, not an independently audited ship count. Elhandar has suffered **catastrophic structural loss and crashes**, but do not claim every Elhandari perished. The High Elf is grieving and intends to withdraw the rescued community to a **Mars terraforming platform**. He accepts the surviving **Harbor tieflings and household members** as evacuees. Stanley and the other original four **decline boarding now**, intending to rest and pursue the proposed planetary-sealing device later.
6. **Recover the buried dragon heart (~00:28–00:33; confirmed ~01:05).** Stanley tells the elves the **large natural dragon-metal heart extracted in E122** was hidden in the mountain cave which has been wrecked by Elhandar's crash. The High Elf orders a platoon to locate it (drilling equipment may be needed). This is **initially a mission order, not a recovered artifact**. Later **Chrysorius explicitly confirms that the elves *found* the heart and are taking it to Mars**. The **separate engineered dragon heart remains implanted in Nash**. The High Elf gives **four noble speeders**, **actually clamped to the exterior of Villa Or'Nath**, and points the party to **Dunglestar Ravensbread (?) on Mars**, an intermediary believed to know the whereabouts of absent engineer **Tungsten Wheel**; **no construction of Ruby yet**.
7. **Soul-taking after death (~00:35–00:40).** Diontalus reappears at Henatar's corpse, draws forth a visible **blue soul-like energy** with his spectral scythe and audibly consumes it, leaving a gray desiccated husk. This is a **performed supernatural action**, though the metaphysical permanence of the victim's soul fate is still not independently tested. The archfey departs across the desert; Stanley says they have an accord to meet again and he is **not reintegrating with Stanley now**. The archfey remains alive at cutoff and is not shown to have fulfilled every term of the E124 court promise.

## The refugees leave; the companions stay (~00:40–01:09)

8. **Threats, uncertainty, exhaustion (~00:40–00:53).** The party discusses whether **Mind Master Gerbken Shartlinger** will keep hiring bounty hunters to pursue **Hope and Vorel**. **Stanley casts *Speak with Dead* on Henatar's corpse** (after Diontalus's draining, with a surviving mouth). Five corpse answers are performed: he was **told to go to Earth**, identifying the party aboard Elhandar was an **educated guess**, Mind Master has spread **Vorel-as-cheater accusations and M-sports Weekly publicity**, he is **in the Hive at Tobacho's chair** (the corpse's account), and Henatar intended to use a **castle-ship console and coordinates to get back to Sigil**. Do not infer reliable evidence that Mind Master has moved into an actual dragon body, or that the castle/console survives the crash. **Hope discusses her evil-act-fueled Gate ring as a way to reach him**, **not cast yet**. Vorel is angry and weary of repeated disasters, says he wants a rest in the desert, and voices a **drastic indiscriminate-killing fantasy**; he **does not enact that threat**.
9. **Hope sends her people to Mars (~00:53–01:05).** As queen, Hope **instructs the surviving Harbor tieflings to board the Elhandari transports** rather than remain exposed in Earth's desert. The GM reports they comply; do not claim every historic Tietkalla survivor is present. **Angelashi and all four living children**, **Lyrielle** and **Bok Bok** also actually board. **Nash has a private, played farewell** with Angelashi; she is anxious about their separation and about the children growing up without him. He explains that staying with him exposes them to mercenary danger and promises to return, with **no reunion yet enacted**. The GM also says **“Bok Bok has Squirts in tow”**, despite E111 Squirts death and E125 separate Hell resurrection; treat as a **serious spoken character-roster conflict**, **do not convert it silently to Chunks or claim Hell Squirts joined the Mars ship**.
10. **Hope's ring does not recharge (~00:54–01:08).** Hoping to power her evil-act-triggered Gate ring, Hope **carves infernal markings into dead Henatar's body**, then tries **desecrating an already-dead tiefling from the villa**. The GM explicitly **rules neither act sufficiently evil to charge the ring**: disturbing a corpse is not the morally harmful act required by this item; **no ring charge or Gate cast** occurs. Hope **buries the tiefling, cries and apologizes**, then begins burying the remaining dead. **No living tiefling is harmed in this attempt.**
11. **Departure actually occurs (~01:05–01:09).** Chrysorius confirms **the natural dragon heart is recovered and taken aboard the Elhandari ships**. He apologizes to Stanley for the part his people played in bringing **Dilenoria/Dilo** to him. The surviving troop ships **ascend and depart toward Mars carrying the refugees, family and heart**. The High Elf's hope for secrecy at Mars and available future aid are **plans**, not guaranteed security or completed help. The main quartet **remains by Villa Or'Nath on Earth**.

## The Soulbroker and the Burrow (~01:09–01:43)

12. **Guitar in Stanley's custody (~01:09–01:13).** Stanley withdraws alone, **actually strums the acquired electric guitar**, and the sound is **magically amplified but not a destructive spell effect in this controlled test**. No full attunement/proficiency, future ownership transfer or reliable recipe for recreating Henatar's attacks is given. The familiar goat-man **Soulbroker (Kitobol/Kitobelpraeth/Kitobol Prith (?)**, whose earlier domicile is **Château Weir**) manifests behind Stanley. **Only Stanley hears/participates in this private interview**, until he later voluntarily recounts it to the group.
13. **Soulbroker's accusations and admissions (~01:13–01:25).** He says **Stanley is the only remaining companion still bound to their Soulbroker bargain**, asserts **Hope had a god annul her own claim**, **Vorel never agreed**, and that **Nash's dragon heart prevented the Soulbroker from ever owning his soul**. These are **the speaker's revelations/claims**; no independent verification of soul registries occurs. He describes **the Principle as once an Earth man** who supposedly sought the Soulbroker's help during the dragons' devastation and then **reneged on a confidential price**. He also alleges he fought **Andreeste and her courts**, while the companions had earlier overcome Dolo Popio. Do not adopt the claimed extermination of those courts as directly witnessed battle.
14. **The Weir's declared project (~01:17–01:27).** The Soulbroker claims his people, the **Weir/Weir-kind (phonetic)**, once resembled mortals but came from/formed a realm **between creation and the Far Realm**, and **require living/soul energy as fuel**. He proposes replacing existing cosmic order with his own “just” rule while **feeding upon the world's soul energy**. He claims the Principle's old planetary protections inhibited entry, but the Principle's new drive toward **godhood** has weakened that barrier. Stanley resists the promised vision while acknowledging he still intends to oppose the Principle. The Soulbroker **reasserts contractual consequences** if Stanley does not fulfil his side. Treat every historical/cosmological claim, soul ownership rule and rationale as **interested testimony**, not independently established universal truth.
15. **The planet is breached (~01:25–01:37).** This is **not merely alleged**. The Soulbroker **raises his hands**, a vast **purple rift visibly opens across the sky**, and an **immense dark creature with six digging limbs, tooth/spike structures and gear-like scales** drops far away into the desert, then **burrows into the ground**. He declares the **“conversion” of Earth has begun**, then vanishes. A huge **sand tsunami with pink-purple lightning** tears toward the party. **Nash flies in his black-dragon form**, and **Hope, Stanley and Vorel climb aboard**, with Vorel joining last; they **fly above the moving storm**. The creature's moving back/tail disappears into earth, earth shakes and fractures, and after roughly **five minutes** the desert calms. **None of the four dies, and the creature is not killed, pursued or contained**. Villa Or'Nath remains **in its original place and intact**, only partially banked by sand.
16. **Stanley shares what he heard (~01:37–01:44).** On their return to ground, Stanley **actually recounts his private Soulbroker conversation** to the party; the GM explicitly confirms that they now know the substance. The group sees conflict among **Mind Master, the Principle and Soulbroker/Weir**. Do not transfer the interview's information to the party **before** this disclosure.

## Season One closing plans and actual rest (~01:43–02:14)

17. **Ruby's real state (~01:43–01:52).** The GM clarifies **Ruby / Rubistic Triangulosphere is NOT being made** and **no construction deadline is assigned**. The **two distinct metal hearts** must not be confused: the natural black dragon's heart **is now at/in transit to Mars**, while Nash's engineered heart stays in him. The party must **talk to Dunglestar Ravensbread at Mars** for a lead on **Tungsten Wheel**, then obtain the engineer's help to build the device. A **suggested installation at the hottest point on Earth** and its supposed planetary-blockade operation derive from **Yoshalar's testimony**, with a **late GM hint** supporting the dragon's advice; **no installation, world cure or measured portal shutoff occurs**.
18. **Hope discloses her parallel adventures (~01:50–02:00).** Hope **actually recounts to the other three** her solo path from White Tower: the living **Hand of the Voice**, meetings with **Brent/Asmodeus-associated powers**, Tobacho/Sigil leads, **the proposed Eye retrieval**, Pandemonium, Council of Clowns and the Pental Weiss pointer. The table clarifies **Brent offered help/items, not an unambiguous signed contractual “deal.”** Her **Gate ring still lacks the demanded harmful-act charge**. The group debates the risks of putting the Eye back together or empowering either side, and asks whether **Mind Master** should be confronted first; **no united execution plan or portal action is accomplished**.
19. **Vorel's supporters and the king-of-space idea (~01:47–02:05).** GM reminds the party Vorel **has fans/allies from M-sports**, despite the Mind Master's smear campaign. Talk of installing him as **“king of space” in Tobacho's Hive base** is playful **proposal**, not a coronation or transfer of political control; it repeats the previously recorded title, **no new Vorel-title register ID warranted**. Sigil gate access is uncertain; the GM judges Hope's high-level Gate **potentially viable**, but **none is cast**. Stanley's mask still needs **more days** before Villa Or'Nath is a controllable spelljammer.
20. **A Sending, not a shortcut (~02:01–02:08).** Stanley **casts *Sending* to the High Elf**, asking for information on Tungsten Wheel, with some conversational confusion as to whether he requested the engineer or Dunglestar. The reply states **Dunglestar is already on Mars**, but Stanley **must speak to him himself**. This neither locates Tungsten Wheel nor puts the elf in motion to fetch him. The party discusses staying **approximately three days** to finish the villa-mask process; **the future three-day stay is a proposal, not elapsed time at the cutoff**.
21. **Night falls, the arc ends (~02:08–02:14).** The GM adjudicates **the four retire, Nash drops back out of temporary dragon form, and a full peaceful overnight rest actually completes**; the party is formally **level 13** (some players had early advance notice, not earlier in-world level-ups). The players choose to defer detailed prioritization among the Mind Master, Ruby, Principle, Weir and Eye to future planning. Beau gives a late **out-of-character reminder** that dragonkind's sealing-device advice is probably the best lead for defending Earth, **not a newly built device or a witnessed cure**. Holiday show/news announcements and the outro are **out-of-game**, not a bonus played scene.
