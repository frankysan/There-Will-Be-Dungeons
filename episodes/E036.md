## Major interpretive consequences

- **Blunt Goliver survives this episode as far as the source shows.** E035's building fire killed various Toe Rippers, but not the leader demanded by **Monsoon Cabbage**. E036's battle against the leader's force ends with **withdrawal via Fango's escape route**, not a certified kill or a submitted proof of completed assassination.
- **Vorel's leadership claim is contested:** He is a Golden Land clan ally after E028, but simply proclaiming himself king does **not establish control over the Toe Rippers**, who continue fighting.
- **Toe consumption:** Gang members visibly consume toes and attribute strength to it. Record **their claim** separately from any demonstrated mechanical buff, magic or physiology.
- **Angel Ashi:** She is an actual in-world singer and later **accompanies Nash**; artistic lyrics, suggestive comments, Fango's anxieties and the party's guesses are not independent confirmation of romantic, political or demonic allegiances.
- **Fango's identity:** The private exit and his changed manner/voice suggest he knows much more than a card promoter would, but **do not establish a proven alias or employer** before later corroboration.
- **Nash transformation costs:** The GM applies **two exhaustion levels** by the end; do not erase them merely because **level 5** is awarded in the outro. GM rules and character-sheet snapshots should be kept at episode-era capability.
- **Performer versus character:** Stanley and Dion share the same character body during the game; the active interlocutor can shift, but they are **not two simultaneously present adventurers**.
