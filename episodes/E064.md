# E064 — Mount Up and Ride

> **Corrected Wastes S1E064**, after [E063](E063.md). Provisional notes from the [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E064.%20Mount%20Up%20and%20Ride.parakeet.processed.srt), **not audio-verified**. Times are approximate recording offsets, including recap, break and the **played post-credit scene**. Full spoilers through E064.

## At a glance

**Follow/Follo**, the calm legless prisoner, reveals **telepathic speech and sensitivity to nearby minds**, and says the child Stanley saw disappear in E063 is connected to him. Following his identification of a particular **Sun's Witness**, the party forcibly examines the prisoner, but ordinary inspection does not detect a worm. Vorel repeatedly invokes **Dollo Poppio**, whereupon the prisoner develops eye stalks and sprays live worms; some begin entering **Vorel and Omnom**. Vorel's fiery aura kills the attacking worms and he kills the transformed host. In an escalating catastrophe the other prisoners appear to **transform into “chicken lords,”** while outside **many lizardfolk sprout additional arms and turn on one another**. Nash's fireball kills many transforming prisoners, **not all**, and he temporarily closes access using *Mold Earth*. **Omnom stays to defend Razak Val** while the party escapes toward **Kervalon/Kaer Valon**, to question the Zark clan. The Zarks' involvement in delivering the captives is **guard testimony**, not an independently verified plot against their home city.

The fugitives flee on velociraptor mounts, pursue a narrow bridge under attack and survive when it collapses, while many pursuing chicken lords fall into the ravine. Camp and travel decisions lead the party into purple sands. They unexpectedly encounter **their own Fate 4**, now driven by a huge orc and occupied by Follow and his apparently disappearing young daughter. In the **played post-credit continuation**, the strangers offer the party passage but ask to **borrow the vehicle** afterward; **the party does not surrender ownership**. Also, **Mawstorm** refers both to Vorel's provisional-name sword and, during an improvised naming ritual, to **a newly assigned raptor**—two different things.

## Boundaries and downstream checks

**Mass transformation, not a proven universal infection route.** Vorel really sees the prisoner transform and worms strike several people; his flame removes the worms **then visible**, but no full-body examination demonstrates guaranteed immunity. **Sun's Witnesses become chicken lords**, and six-armed lizardfolk appear after others ate **Orp's flesh**. The GM's later clarification is evidence for intended causal storytelling, but **which specific hosts were already infested, the exact chain, and who orchestrated delivery** remain partly unsettled.

**Difficult ethical choices:** Stanley resists killing and intentionally infecting a captive to discover a parasite; the party nonetheless **forcibly examines** a frightened man, **Vorel kicks another into the pit**, **Nash's fireball kills many altered and altering captives**, and **numerous inhabitants remain endangered**. Do not rewrite these as a painless cure or a completed emancipation.

**Vehicle and inventory:** Omnom now physically has the **Necklace of Fireballs** (five beads as counted E063; no use shown before transfer). The **Eye of Groxl shield** is not **Mawstorm the sword**, which in turn is not **Mawstorm the named raptor**. **Fate 4** is back in the party's presence, but being driven/occupied by other people; return of **full control** awaits E065 and beyond.

**Open:** Razak Val's survivors and Omnom's fate, Zark complicity, any lasting infestation, Follow's nature and the girl's powers, Fate 4 custody, and precise phonetics of all new names.
