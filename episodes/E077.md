# E077 — The Wall Nash

> **Corrected main-campaign E077**, sourced from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E077.%20The%20Wall%20Nash.parakeet.processed.srt). **Not audio-verified**. Approximate offsets include recap/breaks. **Spoilers through E077.** The testimony of the alleged 'future Nash' is **explicitly a contested in-world claim**, not an independently established future timeline.

## Session overview

All four travelers safely cross the flesh passage at the [E076](E076.md) cutoff; **Nash is no longer trapped or crushed**. Now deeper in CHIDS **Containment**, they negotiate a security checkpoint with a **portal opened into a caged console area**. A terminal opens a door to a **deep pit with fans and green laser lines**; Hope uses the portal device again to cross an otherwise dangerous gap. The next large chamber contains rows of **green-fluid cylinders with dead lizardfolk subjects**.

These cylinders have working medical screens and a teaching interface. The party observes **subjects marked 'deceased'**, categories of instruction (mathematics, languages, weaponry, etc.), and **controls/macros apparently designed to modify lizardfolk brains**: terms resembling *neuter*, *remove apprehension*, and forced instruction. This is **direct CHIDS laboratory evidence of neurological programming of particular lizardfolk**, not proof of every universal genetic-history claim Cerinthala previously made. The cause of these subjects' death is not established.

Two special cylinders lie deeper against the flesh wall. One contains an extraordinarily muscular, black-winged male figure, **a subject whose name is spelled aloud 'L E T H A N P H I U S' (working: Lethanphius)**; the other is shattered and labelled **'G-R-zero-X-one'**. The **Eye of Groxl**, Vorel's chest-bound artifact, shows intense hatred for the intact figure and shame on sight of the shattered companion vessel. **The letters/numbers strongly implicate a prototype designation associated with the Eye**, but the Eye's earlier tale of capture by a warlock has not been verified. Vorel tears his new suit to let the Eye see.

The **living flesh** uses tool-shaped tentacles to open the intact vessel. The winged occupant wakes, distressed that he can no longer hear his 'father' and feels defiled, then manifests black visor-like glasses and an **electrified black blade** and attacks. The creature is powerful and **vulnerable to necrotic damage** according to the Eye's ability. Vorel uses his shoulder grappling harpoons to bind it, but it punches him, tears off **a pinky and thumb**, and later **cuts off a whole hand at the wrist** (different limbs after table clarification). Nash casts *Enlarge* on Vorel; Hope fights with her shotgun and Stanley with **Diplomacy**, *Vicious Mockery* and *Mantle of Inspiration*.

Hope discovers that the supposedly red/green-only apparatus has a **third 'neutralization' mode**. **Both triggers held together produce a sustained void/necrotic beam**, with an attack penalty and serious risk to anyone in its line. Her first critical beam wounds both the winged subject and **Vorel**; the vulnerable target receives about **130 damage**, while the beam deals **65 necrotic damage to Vorel**, knocking him unconscious. A continued beam severely bisects the opponent, revealing **black metal bones and spine**, yet its upper half keeps moving and fighting. **Stanley heals Vorel**, and Vorel drinks a **Monster Mender**, whose tooltip reads approximately **8d4+8 healing** for lizardfolk/dragonborn and a possible poisoning risk for other races. The opponent strikes Hope with the dark blade; she replies using *Hellish Rebuke*. **Nash's later Fire Bolt destroys the surviving upper body**. The opponent's blade disappears.

**Nash gathers Vorel's severed fingers and hand and preserves them frozen in his cooling flying underwear.** Vorel is alive, though injured. Late in the episode the GM summarizes **reattachment using another Monster Mender plus the recovered pieces**, and instructs all characters to take a **full rest and advance to level 9** before the next session. The restoration is **GM-narrated at the end**, not a separately played multi-hour medical operation. This supersedes the temporary handless combat status.

After the fight the flesh wall **constructs a humanoid avatar**, which calls itself the representative of **a 'hive mind' or council of thousands of stored human minds**, formerly a CHIDS knowledge system now protecting the facility. It says it purposely guided the party and seeks help against an **illithid incursion into the Carl Jim Baker (?) engineering area**, where the **Traversal/Transversal Drive** is housed. It claims the invaders began arriving only months ago; the council lacks full visual coverage and says **the living wall uses leadlike tissue to shield itself from telepathic detection**. The council acknowledges that internal members may disagree and it cannot reliably account for why Cerinthala was crushed. These remain **the council's own explanations** rather than omniscient narration.

An apparently separate flesh-made figure then announces **that it is 'Nash Maggard'**, having recognized Nash when the wall takes a face impression. It claims to be **an older/time-traveling Nash** trapped in the hive wall for **~800 years**. Its account alleges that in its timeline the heroes defeated major foes but failed to remove Sira from the Drive; a later Soulbroker/Quizquaz bargain persuaded Hope and Stanley to destroy the planet, Vorel was killed, and Nash was spared and sent traveling, later seeking to reverse that fate. It also claims the Drive could have been shut down by one person's self-sacrifice, and asserts Hope's family could be used as leverage. **Present Nash distrusts this narrator, and even the council says it is reassessing time travel.** The alleged older Nash admits confused memories and details inconsistent with current Vorel's wounds. **None of the prophesied deaths, deals, planetary destruction, or irreversible Sira entrapment has happened in this played timeline.**

The council offers to provide **locked supply codes** and devise a means to convey the party through its flesh passages to an assault landing near engineering. It specifically claims the defeated subject's other vessel **GR0X1** was an unfinished **divinity-derived lizardfolk dragon-fighting prototype** that escaped; it does **not** confirm the Eye's warlock-captivity narrative. It says Lethanphius was abducted from heaven and experimented on, claims it can help Vorel heal, and permits rest while it deliberates. This is a consequential **new account of the Eye's origin**, but it is still attributed to a fallible facility intelligence.

**E077 cutoff:** All four heroes survive and have an **announced full rest and advancement to level 9**. Vorel's lost hand/digits are reattached in a narrated closing resolution; **Sira remains captive**, the **Traversal Drive is neither inspected nor activated by the party**, and the Hive council's promised assault transport is **not yet performed**. The alleged Wall Nash's alternative history is **unverified testimony**, not settled canon.

## Consistency and review priorities

**Names:** *Lethanphius* is spelled at the table, but the processed transcript intermittently renders *Lathanphius/Lefanthius*; verify spoken spelling and any handout. The vessel reads **G-R-0-X-1**; owner-approved **Eye of Groxl** remains the single item, with *Grojil* an ASR-like spoken approximation, not automatically a separate deity. The engineering building sounds like **'Carl Jim Baker'**, spelling/official designation uncertain.

**Inventory, recovery and mechanics:** **Hope uses the portal device again in E077**, following Nash's E076 use; the precise intermediate handoff is **not confirmed by a distinct observed scene**; it can create red/green endpoints **and** sustain a dangerous necrotic beam in another mode. Count **Vorel's Monster Mender use in battle** and the **additional end-of-episode use** for limb repair; exact division of consumed bottles (Hope's cache versus Vorel's personal bottle) needs sheet review. The GM explicitly directs a **full rest and level 9**; do not retroactively treat the prior E075 short rest as long. The Eye's memory-drain penalties were still active **during E077 combat**, but post-long-rest reversal is **not explicitly confirmed**; track separately.

**Competing testimony:** Present GM narration supports a physically observed **living hive avatar** and a materially present **second Wall Nash-like avatar**. Whether the second contains an authentic time traveler, simulation, unreliable future self, or manipulated stored memory is **not yet proven**. The claimed alternate timeline should be indexed as **reported future**, never merged into main chronology or accepted as character deaths.
