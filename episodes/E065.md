# E065 — Under the Dome

> **Corrected Wastes S1E065**, continuing [E064](E064.md). Provisional source-first dossier based on [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E065.%20Under%20the%20Dome.parakeet.processed.srt), **not audio-verified**. Offsets include intro, recap, breaks and wrap. Full spoilers through E065.

## At a glance

Within **Fate 4**, the party learns that the telepathic legless **Follow/Follo** travels with a girl he calls **Sira/Sera** and a huge orc shapeshifter **Rorf/Rorf** (tentative orthography). Their **former refuge was a settlement literally called “Home”**, destroyed by attacking gobbears; a mixed lizardfolk hunting party then massacred their surviving group as supposed **“mutants.”** Follow says he can **influence minds**, the girl **turns invisible**, and the orc **changes into many creatures**. Crucially, **Rorf explicitly admits killing Bramie/Baymie Fengar**, as part of Follow's scheme to remove older clan heirs and engineer a challenge against Orp so Follow could escape. **This resolves the earlier mystery at the level of an in-story perpetrator's confession, not eyewitness/audio confirmation.** It **does not prove Zercil murdered Bramie**, as Vorel previously suspected. The party offers the three sanctuary in **Tietkalla**, which Follow conditionally accepts; he **refuses to accompany a demon-hunting expedition**.

Approaching Kervalon, the normally dry purple sands are drenched by an astonishing **electrical rainstorm**. Hope takes back the wheel when Rorf cannot drive in the rain; Fate 4 bogs in mud, and Nash raises earth to free it. The group camps beside **Stanley's Tiny Hut**, while Nash digs a large diversion trench and reservoir. At night they see a **gargantuan T. rex-like silhouette** in lightning. **Several lookalikes claiming to be different “future Nash” variants appear at the hut**, returning **two copies of the Fengar egg** and contradicting each other about whether the needed object was **an egg or a leg**. One later visitor takes **one** egg, while **one egg remains with the party** as last shown. They mention an exploding planet and a winged employer, but **these remain visitors' extraordinary claims, not observed future events**. The episode ends as an actual **T. rex approaches Fate 4**, while Vorel hides beneath it and the others shelter nearby. **There is no dinosaur battle or car destruction yet.**

## Evidence and thematic consequences

**Bramie's murderer:** E061 established the murder; E063 Vorel **speculated Zercil did it**; E065 provides the first **participant's confession**: **Rorf says he killed Bramie**, and Follow outlines the heir-removal conspiracy. This resolves the current *in-story admitted killer*, though direct audio confirmation of name, exact event reconstruction and whether the pair directed every other murder remain open. Neither the **future Nash egg theft** nor Zercil's alleged mind-worm provides a stronger observed account.

**Follow family and power:** Follow is **not merely an ordinary human Sun's Witness**. Telepathic abilities were depicted E064, and his E065 story attributes shapeshifting to Rorf and invisibility to Sira. Follow's assertion of parenthood/guardianship and claim the group was persecuted are not proof of biological relation, original home location or a universal taxonomy of mutants. **Sira is an elf-associated child**, with deliberately cut ears and apparent Elvish understanding; whether she is related to Nash's earlier dream-girl is **addressed cautiously by the GM in [July bonus](BONUS-2019-07.md)**, not proved by appearance alone.

**Fengar egg inventory:** The odd multi-Nash return is **played**, unlike a mere hypothetical discussion of time travel, but there is still **no trustworthy account of how multiple eggs exist** or whether one is the original. Later item history must record **one retained by the group** and **one taken away by a visitor**, pending listening. The requested **“leg”** has not been recovered here.

**Risk cutoffs:** The storm is observed and the dinosaur is physically approaching; its identity beyond T. rex appearance, relationship to the magic and fate in the next episode are unknown. Follow's Tietkalla refuge has been **offered and accepted, not completed**. The Principle has **not yet sent his trade/rail construction workers by the Tietkalla message**.
