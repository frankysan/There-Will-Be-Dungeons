# E055 — My Standards Are Very Lizard

> **Corrected main Wastes episode E055** (original publisher **TWBD 56**), **not** original **TWBD 55: Year One Q&A**. This is the **first played episode after E054's arc finale**, with a **three-month fictional-time jump** and level-seven party; the Year One Q&A itself did **not** enact that downtime. Based exclusively on [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E055.%20My%20Standards%20Are%20Very%20Lizard.parakeet.processed.srt), **not audio-verified**. Approximate recording offsets below include introduction, character recap and end-show talk. **Spoilers:** E054–E055.

## At a glance

The party resumes play after **three months of actual in-fiction rebuilding**. Hope is a busy queen, Stanley has taken up medical study and **Fishbird/Tidebearer** worship, Vorel runs a new **Golden Sands inn/adventurers' guild** with Bok Bok, and Nash lives with **Angelashi**, who announces their **pregnancy**. Political and ethical friction appears at court: a merit-versus-hereditary job dispute, depleted construction materials, suppression of surviving Twenty-Sixers and an extraordinary peace overture by **Softhead**, now calling himself an agent of the **Principle**. Hope orders Softhead's **tongue cut out**, but leaves him **alive** with instructions to send terms. An unknown assassin later invokes a demon name while attacking Hope in her bedroom; guards kill the assassin. At the Golden Sands a traveling dwarven merchant describes the world outside Tietkalla; a drunken, discriminatory fistfight gives way to an enormous **comet-like impact and falling stones** over the city. This ends **E055**, not a successful rescue or a settled negotiation.

## Active continuity and reference changes

**Time jump is real starting E055.** The three months and level-seven status are now in-session fact; they must **not be silently inserted into E054**. **Nash/Angelashi pregnancy is a current-character reveal**, not evidence of what biological mechanism makes it possible, and Nash's opening encounter with P0D and dynamite is **a nightmare**. **Hope is no longer merely planning reform**: some rebuilding/policy changes have taken effect; shortages, discrimination and house rivalry remain. **Softhead's survival** and claimed service to the Principle are new, but **his offer is not a completed exchange of slaves**. The **Dollo Poppio** syllables, merchandiser name, ceremonial tiefling titles and modified place names are **recognition hypotheses**, not canonical spellings. A captured **47 Carnage** is reportedly executed; **E056 introduces a similarly named reanimated guard**, whose precise identity/necromantic chronology should be checked rather than ignored.
