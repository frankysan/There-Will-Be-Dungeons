## Prologue: source-declared history, not new played action (~00:01–00:06)

Beau replaces the usual alliterative recap with a **GM-narrated history of Zorander's Fist**, attributing its original white/gold gauntlet and extraordinary strength to Zorander's plea to **Euphemius** during a failed defense against giant **Muglep** invaders on **Zygast IV (?)**. After defeat, a mysterious voice purportedly invited Zorander to the **Plains of Antipathy (?)**; he later returned with a purple-filled gem in his gauntlet, conquered the invaders and turned their army against his former holy order. The prologue says the Fist was discovered **71 planar centuries later** in a jungle chasm on **Yox (?)**, leading to multiplar warfare. **These are narrated in-world history/legend statements**, with several ASR-unstable proper names and disputed historical motives. Neither the prologue nor later NPC assertions proves what happened in the unknown intervening period, the voice's actual identity, or the glove's complete powers.

## Arc Three finale — the hand and departure (~00:06–01:59)

1. **The E100 danger remains live** (~00:06–00:13). Outside White Tower, defenders clash with demons. **Hope and Nash are at ground level**; **Vorel holds the opened Fist**, while **Stanley and Alentris are unconscious and descending by Feather Fall**. Hope **sends her flying broom to push Stanley and Alentris downward**, leaving them just above the ground. Stanley rolls a **natural 20 death saving throw**, returns to consciousness at **1 HP**, **Healing Words Alentris for 12 HP**, and **takes her safely inside White Tower**. E100's apparent fatal status is **resolved as surviving**, not a new resurrection.
2. **The Fist changes custody again** (~00:16–00:40). Nash's **cold Chromatic Orb hits the minotaur**; he and Alentris/Stanley seek safety. A mounted green demon **hypnotizes and stuns Vorel**, who **drops the Fist**. Hope commands her broom to **scoop it up and fly it through the White Tower entrance**, but an invisible **magical barrier stops the loose relic at the threshold**—evidence that merely possessing the gauntlet does not get it inside. Hope **slides under the minotaur, is wounded and picks the Fist up herself**. She privately asks the unseen voice what to do; the voice explicitly directs her to **discard the old hand and attach the Fist's severed stump to her arm**. **Only Hope hears this instruction**; observers do not receive the audio.
3. **Hope elects to make the irreversible-looking sacrifice** (~01:02–01:15). At the threshold, Hope puts the relic underfoot, **cuts off her own right hand/forearm with her dagger**, taking enough damage to reach **0 HP**, then reaches to attach the exposed stump. The moment is split by the show's mid-session break: after returning, an **Acrobatics 25** lets her connect the two. Flesh threads fuse, a powerful shockwave knocks surrounding combatants down, and **Hope recovers to full health**. She gains the **Hand of the Voice** on her right arm. This is **actually played**, not merely a contemplated self-injury or unsuccessful attempt.
4. **The hidden patron manifests to Hope, with selective visibility** (~01:15–01:21). In apparent suspended time, a **hooded, face-obscured figure** speaks privately to Hope: she is his **champion**, and when asked what she wants, she answers **to become a god**. A misty trace of his form is perceptible to **Vorel**, who witnesses him **restore the Eye of Groxl to the cavity in Vorel's chest**. The patron's words to Hope are not generally audible to Nash, Stanley or the paladins. **He dislodges the gauntlet from Hope**, telling her she will no longer need it; the **gauntlet and the attached Hand of the Voice are now separate artifacts**. Restoring Groxl is **actually witnessed**; it is not simply another offer from the patron.
5. **The Hand's transformation and first demonstrated power** (~01:18–01:28). Beau describes a **Strength score of 20**, extra cold damage, charges/spells including **Finger of Death**, altered physical form and a **Charisma penalty**, cold body temperature, pronounced horns, silver hair and scars suggesting previous bearers. These are **GM-adjudicated mechanics/appearance**, not player predictions. Hope **casts Finger of Death**, kills a green flying demon and watches it **rise as a zombie under her control**; she mounts this creature and flies. The monster army immediately begins **treating Hope differently**, avoiding rather than attacking her. The Hand's curse/patronage is established, but its **full scope, ultimate loyalty or ability to resist is not**.
6. **The others try to salvage their objective** (~01:28–01:43). Nash **pours his ordinary Potion of Healing into Vorel** to keep his ongoing bleed from dropping him, then withdraws; Vorel remains Hasted. Vorel picks up the separate **Zorander's Fist gauntlet**, observes fleshy teeth on the inside, and **returns it to the open floating chest**. His attempt to kick the complex mechanism closed **fails**—it springs open again. **Vorel summons the Eye of Groxl sword** back after the eye's restoration. Alentris, the surviving paladins and companions witness Hope's new state with alarm; this is not a peaceful celestial victory.
7. **Hope chooses solitude at the crossroads** (~01:43–01:49). The hooded patron, invisible to the others, **orders Hope away from White Tower toward the surrounding demon army**. The patron says she can take companions but will otherwise **not see them for a long time**. **Hope explicitly refuses to bring any of the party**, tells Vorel to go back, looks back at her friends and **walks alone through the path opening in the dretches**. This is the **decisive personal separation**. **Do not claim the party agreed with her choice, saw the patron or knows its private terms.** Both the choice and the farewell belong to the **end of Arc Three**.
8. **The enemy alliance collapses; the siege ends** (~01:49–01:56). At the commanders' raised camp, large demon **General Carthax/Akarthax (?)** addresses devil **Ulvert/Olvert (?)**. He declares their temporary demon–devil compact over: **Orcus has ordered the demons' retreat to the Abyss** after the Hand became attached to a mortal who can converse with the Voice; he says a **one-day armistice** will be honored but the wider **Blood War resumes**. The armies **actually begin withdrawing** from White Tower; the cessation is not a merely promised future ceasefire. The **devil** hands Hope an elegant written **invitation from Asmodeus to the Kaiser Casino**. Spatulo, the tiefling who recognized Hope, warns she owes Tobacho and should not go with the devils; **Hope invites him along and he agrees**. No evidence Spatulo was a trusted celestial emissary.
9. **Arc transition: Hope passes through the portal** (~01:56–01:59). A faceless devilish attendant **raises a stone gateway** framing a beautiful path and casino beyond a lake of fire. The hooded patron **enters first**, then **Hope walks in**, with **Spatulo following**. The portal **closes and sinks back into the ground**. The three companions at White Tower see the army withdraw and **Hope gone**. **This completed physical separation ends Arc Three and opens Arc Four**; neither Hope's independence nor a permanent inability to return is established, although her commitment and situation have changed radically.

## Arc Four opening — separate paths (~01:59–02:49)

10. **The remaining party and tower process a costly victory** (~01:59–02:23). **Stanley staunches Vorel's bleeding with a healer's kit**, and calls the paladins back inside; many are wounded or carrying fallen comrades. Nash interprets Hope's departure as **abandonment**, while Vorel views her ascent and Groxl's return as a **victory** and confidently predicts a reunion—**their opposing feelings are not proven future outcomes**. Walnut sees the aftermath, asks where Hope is and examines the gauntlet. The **moon with a lit city remains in the sky**, and the **three airships remain overhead**, with their purpose/owner unresolved. Alentris states that **the Hand was the true protected object**, the gauntlet secondary, and that its loss makes the mission **a failure**, regardless of the retreat.
11. **The box is actually re-sealed, and the key returned** (~02:15–02:23). Vorel **throws the loose gauntlet toward White Tower**, but **the same ward rejects it**, demonstrating even the separate gauntlet cannot enter without containment. Nash reasons through the locking mechanism with **Arcana 18**, closes the elaborate case around the gauntlet and **uses/removes the medallion key to re-lock it**. The **now-closed, locked chest can float inside the tower**. Nash **hands the recovered medallion/key back to Stanley**, who **puts it in his bag**. **This supersedes E100's key-in-socket custody**, and means **Vorel no longer holds the Fist**. The three remaining companions **enter the tower and close its gates**; a later Earth journey, rendezvous with Waltz or completed Tobacho transaction **is not shown**.
12. **In the new location the patron removes a second bond** (~02:23–02:28). In the **Nine Hells-like landscape reached through the portal**—fiery lake, gardens and a modern casino road—the hidden patron pulls a visible **Infernal-written piece of flesh/mark** from Hope's neck/shoulder, described as **Kitobal Prith's** prior claim on her soul, and throws it into the fire. This is **an enacted removal of the visible mark**; the line that she will never need Prith again is **the patron's claim**, not independently verified universal freedom from all contracts or consequences. A **black SUV chauffeur** transports Hope and Spatulo toward the casino. A fire serpent briefly leaps from the lake; this is scenery, not an attack on Hope.
13. **Hope actually meets a figure calling himself Asmodeus (“Brett”)** (~02:30–02:46). At **Kaiser Casino**, Hope passes a lobby with ordinary-seeming elderly patrons and slot machines and follows an invitation upstairs to a private dining lounge. A well-dressed bartender/host sends **Spatulo outside** and **introduces himself as Asmodeus, inviting her to call him “Brett.”** Treat the identification as **the figure's own claim and GM scene presentation**, not independently authenticated cosmology. He offers fire-based drinks and speaks congenially while warning that the Hand's **mysterious patron seeks to subjugate all creation**. These are **Brett's assertions and recruitment effort**, not proof of the patron's ultimate plan. He offers possible help seeing Hope's **kingdom Tietkalla** and **checking on her friends**, asking for information/friendship in return. Hope **says she will not make any formal deals**; **no binding infernal pact is concluded here**.
14. **The session ends with an open offer, not a return home** (~02:45–02:49 played). Brett stages a show tune with a demon big band and suggests Hope **drink and enjoy the casino**; Hope agrees and is shown beginning a drinking evening while Spatulo returns. She **does not actually visit Tietkalla, speak to the friends, sign a pact or accept a permanent assignment from Asmodeus**. Beau gives the dispersed cast a **long rest and a level-up** at the session end; the three tower companions also rest. This is the **opening status of Arc Four**, not a closure to Hope's independent storyline.

## Item custody, knowledge and continuity at cutoff

- **Hope:** Alive but radically altered, with **Hand of the Voice replacing her right hand**, strength/cold and curse features described by GM. **Zorander's Fist gauntlet is *not* on her arm** after the patron removes/dislodges it; she takes **no companions from the original party** through the portal, but Spatulo follows as a new companion. **Eye of Groxl restored to Vorel**. Patron's instructions and identity remain private/obscure to others; claims of soul ownership, destiny and freedom from Prith require caution.
- **Fist gauntlet / chest / medallion:** **Gauntlet resealed inside closed levitating chest**, now inside White Tower; **Nash returns the medallion key to Stanley**, who pockets it. Hand that was originally inside the glove **now integrated with Hope**, separated from gauntlet. Neither delivery to Tobacho nor the celestial order is performed.
- **Tower survivors:** **Stanley and Alentris alive** after natural-20 recovery and Healing Word, with the paladin garrison withdrawing after siege. **Nash and Vorel alive**; Vorel's Eye restored and bleeding tended. Their grief/confidence about Hope's return conflicts; don't turn either view into evidence of a certain reunion. No homecoming to Earth; moon and airships still visible.
- **Politics and new storyline:** **Demons actually retreat**, with Carthax's stated reason being Orcus's order and breakdown of compact with Ulvert's devils. **Invitation to Asmodeus** accepted as social meeting, **NOT a binding deal**. His alleged identity and plan to contain the Hand's patron are presented **through his own words**. Hope says she wishes to be **a god**, and requests eventually seeing **Tietkalla and her friends**, but accomplishes neither in E101.
- **Research boundaries:** Spellings **Zorander/Euphemius, Carthax/Akarthax, Ulvert, Spatulo, Kitobal Prith, Muglep, Zygast IV, Plains of Antipathy, Yox** need audio/written provenance checks.
