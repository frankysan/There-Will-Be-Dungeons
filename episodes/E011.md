## References and cautions

- **Trial outcome:** E010 closes with victory/level gain; **E011** stages the official acknowledgment and release of the *party*, not Dusthill's enslaved civilians.
- **Slavetown politics:** The Principle's martial order, official magistrate and local gambling/hospitality economy operate alongside slavery. Corruption, jurisdiction and historical origins remain partly hearsay and should be attributed individually.
- **Talia Bane:** The [fan wiki's Bruce Bane page](https://twbd.fandom.com/wiki/Bruce_Bane) lists **Talia Bane** as his mother. E011 ~02:00 has her identify herself as Talia and name Bruce. This supports the connection; it does *not* establish that she had been rescued or returned home.
- **Celibus Stump:** ASR varies `Celibus`, `Syllabus`, `Selylus` and `Sullivan`. Index variants, and do not merge him with **the Jeweler** or the **Oilbeards** merely because all have local influence.
- **Bok Bok and old man:** The party's companion goblin and elderly traveler are separate from the four Hill Guard PCs; track them as needed and check where they are left during the various town scenes.
- **Map and town names:** The E011 in-show **Slavetown town map** is a useful original asset to recover, separate from the **E005 regional Wastes map**. Buildings and street names recognized phonetically need visual/source-map confirmation before canonical spelling.
- **Spells and inventory:** Periapt effects and foundry work require character-era evidence, not the later D&D Beyond end-of-campaign sheet.
