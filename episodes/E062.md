# E062 — Orp Fight!

> **Corrected Wastes S1E062**, direct continuation of [E061](E061.md). Provisional episode dossier from the [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E062.%20Orp%20Fight!.parakeet.processed.srt), **not audio-verified**. Times include opening mail, recap, intermission and outro. **Full spoilers through E062**.

## At a glance

The murder investigation **does not solve Bramie/Baymie Fengar's death**. Nash acknowledges helping the apparent future copies steal the Fengar egg; the other lizardfolk dispute his extraordinary explanation. Vorel privately confronts **Zercil/Zershil**, who offers to join forces against **Orp** if Vorel will submit as her husband/right hand after the victory. He rejects her and addresses the court's complicity in Orp's human-eating regime. When the group openly threatens the cyclops, **Mirion and Omnom's followers attack**, and **Vorel begins an immediate scaling against Orp**, rather than waiting for sunrise. **Hope fires a single enormous rocket into the combat**, with a blast affecting much of the crowded Cave Fengar. The GM narrates **mass deaths**, notably **Mirion, priestess Typalla, Elder of Families, General Toxar, the winged Featherscale visitor and numerous others**. **Mola Janda Zantlar is killed**; **Omnom survives**, as does another Zantlar and some guards. Outside, Vorel fights Orp alone, **drives his flaming Eye of Groxl shield through the cyclops's eye**, and **Orp dies in E062**, witnessed by many Razak Val residents. **Omnom names Vorel the new Great Fang**. This is a **played, publicly recognized succession**, not merely Vorel's earlier self-proclaimed “Lizard King” title or an unplayed future duel.

## Continuity, character and ethics

**Great Fang transition:** **Orp dies E062; Vorel becomes the witnessed Great Fang at the episode end.** The initially scheduled sunrise duel is superseded by this immediate combat. **Zercil's earlier claim is not silently resolved** by Orp's death. **The missing Fengar egg and Bramie's killer remain unresolved.** The sorcery described by the apparent “future Nashes” remains testimony, not proven time travel.

**Civilian deaths matter.** Mirion was directing opposition, but **Typalla, Elder of Families, visiting nobles, musicians and others are caught in the same rocket**. Do not frame Hope's blast as only killing hostile guards or as a no-cost rescue; likewise do not infer a complete extermination of every clan from a still-populated city. **Omnom and some witnesses explicitly survive**. Orp's captives are **not yet freed in E062**.

**Artifact separation:** Vorel is wielding the **flaming shield provided by the Eye of Groxl**, a single owner-confirmed artifact. It is distinct from **Mawstorm**, his sword. The Eye's described redirection helps explain why the intended rocket travels into **Vorel's back** and sends both him and Orp outward; treat exact damage and range interpretation as **session-specific GM adjudication**, not certified official rules text.

**Title provenance:** **Great Fang** is now an actual position Vorel **wins before witnesses**, not self-awarded wordplay. Track it in [his title register](../world/characters/vorel.md) as **formally recognized**, separate from the E036 self-proclaimed **Lizard King**. The GM/Omnom's pronunciation `Vorel Rasvim Kurik` is still an ASR-based phonetic record, while the longer name has fan-source written support.
