## Continuity safeguards

- The **E051 giant pixies are multiple other subjects of transformation**, not resurrected P0D (killed E050). The enlarged froghemoth can **swallow them**; whether it was genetically the exact E041/E046 specimen is plausible but needs explicit source matching.
- **Nash's baby/extra hand** becomes a tangible anomaly; don't treat it as a separate sentient NPC or as a permanent feature until later corroborated.
- The **froghemoth is not confirmed slain in E052**. What happens to its body after Vorel's conversation appears in **E053**, not here.
- **Vorel's communication** is privileged private-roleplay information with later public reporting. This is not evidence every player heard or could use the whole mental dialogue.
- **Robe patch inventory**: earlier **24-foot ladder patch consumed E048**; remaining rope/pole/boat patches must be counted before claiming inexhaustible supplies.
- **Angelashi** remains missing as of **E052** despite her reported fate; being swallowed by this creature does not prove she survived, either.
