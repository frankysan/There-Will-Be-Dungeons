# E117 — The Fireside Stories

> **The Wastes · corrected S1E117 · publisher TWBD 119, September 7, 2020 (owner archive catalogue row 141) · played Arc Five.** [Parakeet source](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E117.%20The%20Fireside%20Stories.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen-labelled source](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E117.%20The%20Fireside%20Stories.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [complete ASR comparison](../reports/E117_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md) · [Arc Five narrative](../narrative/ARC_FIVE.md). Both **complete existing SRT texts through the ~02:48:20 outro** were inspected at clean workspace and pinned GitHub commit `d3648c6c4c6a38f72f46d3b90fbcfed48ac3e589`. **Not audio verified**; timestamps are recording clock, not in-world time.

**E117 cutoff:** **Hope, Nash, Stanley and Vorel really reunite and discuss what happened** at Elhandar, but **Stanley does not declare forgiveness or trust**. They visit surviving Tietkalla tieflings in their Elhandar forest clearing; **Nobieri and Emenil** are physically present. Hope admits her **own responsibility** for bringing the Hand back and for making the invasion possible, but direct independent evidence of **who intentionally ordered the demon army or all its causes remains incomplete**. A **flaming figure calling itself Abegir** appears during Nobieri's ash ritual, counsels Hope, and departs; **manifestation is witnessed but claims of godhood, its historical identity, and afterlife promises are not independently tested**. The refugees **choose whether to continue as her people**; **one man leaves with wife and child**, while **the other participating survivors remain**. Following their shared accounts, **Hope actually names the new local tiefling polity/community “the Harbor,” its people “Harborers,” and its purpose to help anyone who has lost their way**; Nobieri publicly ratifies the declaration. **This is not renaming all of Tietkalla or establishing an operating multi-world refuge network.** Stanley privately hears **Emenil's confession of love**, cannot reciprocate yet; her subsequent destruction of her shrine is inferred with high perception but **not seen by him in the room**. Finally **Vorel smells lizardfolk around the biodome**, fails his initial tracking attempt, and the companions **plan to search after supper at evening**. **They have not set out or found Omnom/a new clan by the cutoff**.

## Hope must face her friends (~00:02–01:08)

1. **A reunion beginning with disorientation (~00:02–00:15).** [E116](E116.md)'s Gate has brought Hope and Nash back to **Elhandar's living forest**. A small snake attacks **Nash's cloak**; he grabs and throws it off, then the same Gate event is recapped. Hope asks where they are and how long it has been. Nash explains the elven forest-biodome station, and announces **four newborn children with Angelashi**, naming **Fork, Boot, Pipe and Nash Junior Maggard**, though he momentarily forgets the names. Their estimates of separation time are **confused by different experiences**; the cast disputes a literal two-day common interval and acknowledges **four E115 recovery days**. **Do not fix a synchronized cross-party chronology from the confused remarks.**
2. **Vorel, Stanley and the hunting party recalled (~00:12–00:31).** Nash finds **Vorel, Stanley, Bok Bok and Chunks hunting** with an elven bird helping his search, and announces that Hope is back. **Bok Bok and Chunks are alive and hunting**, not aboard a separate spacecraft or resurrected. **Stanley tells Lyrielle to prepare the station's defensive shield**, and returns **armed with Diplomacy and other equipment**, fearing that Hope's new companions or enemies might be coming. **The shielding is a precaution/request, not a played successful activation or confirmed attack on Elhandar**. Elven claims that station defenses could withstand a siege for many years are **NPC estimates**, not combat-tested here.
3. **Tears and accusation in the cottage (~00:31–00:50).** Hope starts **sobbing** when Stanley and Vorel arrive. **Stanley confronts her** about the **Hand of the Voice**, the fate of Tietkalla, missing friends and the bloodshed after her departure. He does not accept a general claim that she took the Hand merely to keep them safe. Vorel disputes that the remaining heroes were helpless without her; Nash counsels against attacking during an emotional confrontation. **Stanley slides an appearance-changing ring back to Hope** using his rapier and withdraws. **The arm is still present**, and her motives remain complicated; **no executed punishment, group disbandment or restored trust**. Chunks imitates adult judgments by accusing Hope of killing “mommy”; **that is child/NPC speech, not proof she ordered Squirts's death**.
4. **Bok Bok's family intentions (~00:46–01:06).** The survivors eat at the cottage; **Chunks climbs into Hope's arms** and Bok Bok talks about wanting her to be a “mommy.” **Vorel clarifies that Hope has a living former lover in the person of alternate Tedna/Badass T**, a very different being from their original deceased Tedna; Hope is understandably bewildered. After an awkward private lesson and conversation on the porch, **Bok Bok announces he will remain at the cottage and protect the family**, while **Vorel should take Chunks on his next journey and teach him to be a warrior**. **Vorel does take Chunks for the ensuing survivor visit**. This is **a change in proposed travel/caretaker arrangement since E115**, not proof of a legal adoption, permanently severed father role or completed later expedition. Neither Hope-Bok Bok marriage nor a new Hope-Tedna romance is performed.
5. **Choosing to meet the survivors (~00:49–01:08).** Hope asks what happened to Tietkalla and learns some tieflings survived the demon attack. Nash and Vorel suggest she hear them personally; **Stanley goes despite his distrust**. The four protagonists and **Chunks carried by Vorel** walk about ten minutes to the Elhandar refugee cottages. Angelashi and the four newborns **remain at the main cottage**, not shown joining this mission. Hope chooses **not to disguise herself** before meeting the survivors.

## The circle and Abegir's flame (~01:08–01:47)

6. **Nobieri and Emenil actually reunited (~01:08–01:17).** The characters arrive at a clearing ringed by **roughly eight wooden houses and a well**, housing **approximately a dozen to two dozen surviving tieflings**. **Nobieri (often Riddle Nobieri in ASR)** and **Emenil/Emmanil (?)**, tidebearer of Malhaya, emerge; Nobieri **physically hugs Hope**. The group gathers around a central bonfire for counsel. **Hope rejects a throne-like chair** and sits in a regular one. Emenil quietly acknowledges Stanley.
7. **A queen admits a role in disaster (~01:15–01:21).** Hope interrupts Nobieri's denunciation of the demons to explain **she brought back the sought-after Hand**, thinking it would protect and rebuild Tietkalla, and that its presence drew catastrophe. She offers to release her subjects from obligation. **This is her sincere public admission of responsibility, not proof she intentionally called the invasion, commanded every demon or knew the outcome in advance.** **Nobieri says loyalty is a matter of choice** and asserts he still recognizes Hope's claim. A man of the **Gretchel/Gretchil (?) family** thanks Hope for ending old inequities and pledges continued support. **A dissenting tiefling leaves the clearing with his wife and child**; the **other survivors remain**. No arrest, forced exile or full census of Tietkalla results.
8. **Ash ritual and selective participation (~01:20–01:28).** Nobieri marks foreheads and cheeks, shares ash, and participants blow it toward the fire; **most remaining tieflings participate**. **Stanley explicitly refuses** on the ground he is not a tiefling and Hope is not his queen. **Nash, Vorel and Hope actually participate**, with Nash's unusually comic attempt initially missing the fire. **Hope receives ash into her left, biological hand**; her demonic right Hand is not physically severed or purified.
9. **An actually seen fire-figure (~01:28–01:40).** The fire changes color, dims surrounding daylight, and **a ten-foot armored female tiefling form appears within the flame**, presenting herself as **Abegir/Abigrier (?)**—figure revered by **Melgor's Order of Poxis/Paxus** for ending an old tiefling civil war. The GM explicitly distinguishes **an image made entirely from flame** from a flesh-and-blood body. It **addresses Hope** and counsels that leadership means protecting **the living** instead of seeking forgiveness or power from gods; it says even the power she carries cannot do that work for her. It offers an account of tiefling salvation and afterlife and **declares it cannot interfere directly in predestination**. **A performed supernatural manifestation is established; identification as the literal deity and claims of divine status are not independently verified.** Nash's query about alternate Nashes receives **no concrete answer**. **The figure vanishes back into the bonfire**. Emenil is visibly moved, but Stanley does not share her enthusiasm.
10. **Witness testimony, not replayed events (~01:43–02:07).** Hope asks the survivors to **tell their stories**. A tiefling ranger recounts seeing demons attack, a huge throne-like fiend, and finding his **pregnant companion Zalna/Salma (?) dead beneath a fallen building**; **his past observations and reported death are testimony**, not a newly played battlefield sequence or universally authenticated casualty inventory. Other survivors (the GM summarizes **approximately seventeen**) recount grief and pre-Night-Eaters experiences. **Emenil** describes her **Malhaya/Tidebearer faith**, hiding in prayer during the attack, wishing Stanley survived, and concludes that **Earth needs abundant water rather than wars over scarcity**. **Her suggested ecological solution is her belief/advocacy, not a completed planet restoration or verified miracle.**
11. **Stanley's identity and spiritual alienation (~01:51–02:00).** Stanley publicly introduces himself as **Valiir Or'Nath** with a father **Daliot/Dalit (?)** and a human mother with uncertain ASR name **Cristopher/Kristopher (?)**. This is a **character disclosure**, strengthening E115's birth-name evidence but not resolving every spelling. He recounts **poverty, failures of towns to protect the weak**, Emenil's teaching, his **E081 sacrifice**, being cast into an unexpected death realm, and feeling **deceived rather than comforted** by a deity that affirmed his sacrifice. **Do not insert those old scenes anew into E117 or claim Emenil personally caused his death.**
12. **Nash's substantive Solar Mines account (~02:00–02:08).** Nash says he **never met his parents**, grew up believing the Solar Mines' supervisors were benevolent substitute parents, and learned from overhearing a figure conversing with the **Slow Peters** that **the workers were enslaved and disposable**. He describes **illness, skin damage, starvation, an organized revolt he led, his early unexplained fire power, and killing the abusive foreman/father figure while setting the barracks afire**. **This is his first fully played-in-scene personal account, not a time-travel recreation of the Solar Mines.** Scott explicitly says **“Pedro” is a name he improvises out of character**, **not confirmed canonical foreman's name**.
13. **Vorel's witnessed recital (~02:07–02:09).** Vorel recites his long **self-awarded titles**, many first attested in prior episodes, including **Fang of Razak Val**, **Keeper of Groxl**, **Creator of the Fate Five**, **Hunter of the Elven Woods**, and a new framing **“Keeper of Stories.”** He vows to carry the refugees' accounts and renew the kingdom. The title register in [Vorel's profile](../world/characters/vorel-titles.md#source-indexed-titles-epithets-and-disputed-labels) already indexes individual E117 lines and variants; **self-proclamations are not a new official appointment or proof he regained subjects**. No Nash casting phrase is performed in this speech scene; Nash's autobiographical fire is a retrospective claim.

## The Harbor and an unsettled love (~02:09–02:48)

14. **A name formally proclaimed (~02:09–02:14).** Nobieri calls the assembly **a new family / polity** under Hope and asks her to name their home, the people and their common purpose. **Hope explicitly names it “the Harbor”**, assents to **“Harborers”** and declares their mission **to help anyone who needs it, especially those lost among the stars**. **Nobieri publicly announces it into their founding law/tradition**. **This is a completed communal naming, not a mere brainstorming suggestion**. The actual physical site is **the tiefling refugee clearing within Elhandar's biodome**; there is no implemented interplanetary shelter network or return to rebuilt Tietkalla yet. Survivors begin dispersing to their homes.
15. **Emenil confesses love (~02:17–02:36).** **Emenil** invites **Stanley** inside her cottage. She apologizes for how religious instruction and hope of a greater force failed to meet his needs, then **tells him she loves him** and has long wanted him to return. He says her survival brings him relief but **he cannot reciprocate or start a relationship while he remains unsure of his identity**. He **does not promise courtship or propose**. After he leaves, a crash is heard; on a **Perception 29**, the GM tells Stanley he can determine Emenil **has smashed the Malhaya altar/statue and bowls**. **He hears and infers this; no direct physical rescue or reconciliation is played, and it is not a witnessed repudiation by Malhaya herself**.
16. **A lizardfolk scent, not a rescue (~02:36–02:45).** Back toward the main Elhandar cottage, **Vorel smells lizardfolk** in the forest and excitedly suggests survivors or even **Omnom**. A private successful tracking estimate directs him north initially, but when the group follows **a subsequent low Survival result fails to identify the precise route**. **No lizardfolk, Omnom or new clan is seen**. Stanley returns from the Emenil meeting; the group remains wary of Hope, including Stanley's insistence that she still wears the demonic Hand and has **not demonstrated reform**. **Vorel plans to take the group searching after supper once it grows dark**. That future excursion **does not occur before this episode's cutoff**.
17. **End credits (~02:46–02:48).** The GM calls it an emotional session and closes before the planned lizardfolk search. The **out-of-game** title quips and upcoming bonus recording are not additional story events.
