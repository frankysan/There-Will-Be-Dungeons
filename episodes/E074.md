# E074 — The Big Red One

> **Main-campaign E074**, sourced from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E074.%20The%20Big%20Red%20One.parakeet.processed.srt). **Not audio-verified.** Scott's introduction calls the recording **Sunday, September 8, 2019**, not automatically the file's public publication date. Approximate timestamps include opening, recap, breaks and closing. **Spoilers through E074 only.**

## Session overview

The party, together with **[Cerinthala](../world/characters/cerinthala.md)**, begins atop a **stalled CHIDS laboratory elevator** while a huge **black spider with an exposed, pulsing brain** descends, shedding hundreds of smaller, similarly brain-marked spiders. The swarms poison several adventurers; **Hope suffers poison and one level of exhaustion** in the encounter. Vorel uses his flaming **Eye of Groxl** shield against the swarms. **Stanley casts Feather Fall** when party members leap or slip from the elevator; Nash's **one pair of flying underwear** helps him catch and lift Cerinthala. The big spider attempts to **mentally dominate Vorel**, briefly succeeding; **Nash's Fireball breaks its concentration**, freeing Vorel. Stanley **Polymorphs the giant spider into a sheep**, which falls to the shaft's bottom and **reverts to its spider form** after taking fall damage; **this does not kill it**. After further fighting, **Hope's two shotgun shots kill the large spider**.

During salvage, Vorel uses spider carapace to make **a medium shield for Cerinthala and a tiny shield for Hope's smaller artificial arm**, both accepted, **not a new Eye of Groxl item**. The adventurers force open an elevator landing into a **damaged, lower weapons-lab level**.

The laboratory corridor contains **responsive, pink organic membrane** resembling the growth that broke Nash's staff in E073. It allows **Hope, Stanley and Vorel** through but **closes in front of Nash and Cerinthala**. Nash's successful **singing/performance** persuades or calms the tissue to let him pass—**a played effect, not proof it is a magical song spell**. Cerinthala tries singing, fails, and the membrane **closes around and kills her**; **the group examines her and confirms she has no signs of life**. They carry her body on the shield Vorel made. Her earlier assertion that old elves **return after death remains unverified at the E074 cutoff**; **E075 subsequently resolves this specific prediction**. An oily, dark residue on her skin causes a **rash/itching when touched**; Nash **tastes it**, developing **an irritated/swollen tongue**. The magical cleaning of *Prestidigitation* removes some surface material but **does not cure that reaction**.

Beyond, **two green-scaled lizardfolk armed with energy rifles** fire on the party; Vorel's flame shield and Stanley's animated **Diplomacy** kill both. **Green scales and equipment resemble Zark troops**, but source evidence doesn't show their exact chain of command. Through an automatic door they find **an illithid operating six monitors**, who **conceals a disk inside his clothes**, notices Stanley, and tries to flee while summoning **a huge red, gelatinous, many-faced entity** from purple-black mist. **Hope and Vorel coordinate a launch** using Vorel's hands and Hope's mechanical leg; **Hope uses Action Surge to catch and grapple the fleeing illithid**, then keeps crushing its neck.

The red creature attacks with **bludgeoning, psychic harm and memory-draining tendrils**. It **drains Vorel's memories**, causing a **1d4 penalty to attack rolls and ability checks**, and **knocks him unconscious**. **Stanley heals Vorel**, but the illithid **Mind Blasts** him and others, knocking Vorel down again. **Vorel's special Eye of Groxl savagery temporarily restores him for a strike**, then he collapses; Stanley **heals him again**. Nash becomes a **black dragon** and attacks the red entity, but suffers **the same lasting memory-drain effect**. The blob extrudes **a Vorel duplicate**, physically attached to it by a red tether, and near the end a **second duplicate resembling Nash's dragon form**, also tethered. Damage to the replicas seems transmitted to the central mass.

**E074 ends in active combat**: the red creature and its **two connected copies remain alive**, **Hope holds the illithid captive**, Nash is in black-dragon form, and the group's memory loss has **not been cured**. **Cerinthala is physically dead and being carried** at this recording cutoff. **Sira, the actual Traversal Drive and any portal-device function are not yet recovered.**

**Enemies and spells:** **Large spider definitely dies** after temporary **sheep transformation**; little spider swarms and poison have their own effects. **The giant red construct is not killed here**, and its illithid summoner is **grappled but alive**. **Vorel and Nash's mind drain remains active** and is not ordinary temporary exhaustion. The **lizardfolk riflemen die**; exact Zark membership requires confirmation.
