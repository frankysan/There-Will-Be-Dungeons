# E111 — Squirts Into The Night

**Played cutoff:** From [E110](E110.md)'s keep overlook, **Nash, Stanley, Vorel, kobold cleric Squirts and infant Chunks** sneak across demon-patrolled Tietkalla to the **Golden Sands**. In a hidden basement **Vorel reunites with goblin Bok Bok** and **Nash physically reunites with pregnant Angelashi**, both **alive**, correcting E110's vision-only status. They prepare to leave using Nash's *Teleportation Circle*, but **Riddle Nobieri (?)** contacts Stanley saying he has brought **Elhandari elves** to intervene. **Squirts and Chunks, Stanley and Vorel** emerge and see elven soldiers **pacifying and trapping nycaloths**, backed by huge spacecraft; **Nobieri and Melgor of Paxus (?)** reunite with the party, although Melgor distrusts them over Hope. **Captain Tenmuth/Tamneth (?)** presents the elves' humane, advanced civilization as refuge; **Angelashi wants to go for their child's safety**. Before they depart, **General Carthax/Karthax** proves too dangerous for the elves alone. In a deliberately chosen fight, **Squirts heals the exhausted Omnom Cinderborn, then is actually killed/disintegrated by Carthax's force beams**, alongside **Melgor and several elves**. **Chunks survives**, dropped onto the ground. After Vorel, Nash, Stanley and allied elves weaken Carthax, **Vorel actually plants multiple elven empathy grenades into his eye**, **pacifying him instead of killing him**. **Bok Bok dives into the general's mouth with explosives**, but **Stanley's *Command* (“vomit”) actually brings him back out alive**, badly wounded and **missing a hand**. Vorel gathers **Squirts's ashes** in a small container Stanley supplies and **retains that container**. Elves **capture Carthax alive** in a tentacled shrinking trap. **Nash, Stanley and Vorel elect to board an elven spacecraft** with infant Chunks, injured Bok Bok and Squirts's remains; **Angelashi has already boarded**. They **ascend away from ruined Tietkalla toward Elhandar**, not back to First Nash's asteroid tower. **Squirts is dead at this episode cutoff; no resurrection is cast or confirmed. Hope's separate Sigil course remains unplayed here.**

## Escape from the keep to the Golden Sands (~00:03–00:50)

1. **Reconnaissance and urgency (~00:03–00:15).** The team resumes at the former keep, looking at broken courtyards, two moving **nycaloths** and more winged patrols above. **The Golden Sands** flies a **bronze flag**, interpreted as a survivor signal. The oil-filled moat, rampart hangars, noble and guest houses, shattered right drawbridge and open left bridge are debated as escape routes. **Stanley uses *Prestidigitation* to soil/cloak clothes in mud and grease**, while Vorel relies on his color-changing scales; **Nash and Squirts also adopt the disguise**. This is **performed camouflage**, not a real gate explosion. They discuss igniting the oil moat with *Fire Bolt*, illusions or distracting enemies but **cast no fire spell and do not burn the moat**.
2. **An actual stealth crossing (~00:15–00:40).** **Squirts's *Pass without Trace*** remains active and the party makes successful group Stealth rolls. They run past buildings and shadowed walls, **cross a splintered drawbridge** and leave the keep. A tremendous demonic voice summons forces to an **“ascension”** near the Church of the Fishbird; **a separate voice appears to answer it**, and fighting begins. The group chooses **to keep moving**, not to join the unknown combat. **Ruined homes, burned corpses and whole stretches of dead tieflings** are visually observed. No careful count of city casualties is established.
3. **An intact refuge with a hidden exit (~00:40–00:51).** At last they **actually enter the Golden Sands**—first time since E110's outside sighting—find its main rooms abandoned and smashed but the **kitchen stove recently active**. They descend to Vorel's old **basement resting room**, where a covered earth passage to an emergency escape route can be dug open; **nobody yet escapes through it**. Vorel calls softly for Bok Bok and then begins clearing sand from the concealed passage.

## Reunion, Nobieri's message and conflicting choices (~00:50–01:11)

4. **Bok Bok's joyous return (~00:50–00:54).** **Bok Bok really appears alive** from the secret route, recognizes his master, hugs Vorel and speaks some Common/English Vorel taught him. Squirts has used her remaining *Pass without Trace* support; **she cannot freely cast *Silence* again**, so Vorel muffles his excited shouting. Bok Bok shows an embarrassed **crush on Squirts** shortly afterward; this is a roleplayed attitude, **not a future partnership already formed**.
5. **Angelashi's physical reunion (~00:53–00:58).** **Angelashi**, visibly **heavily pregnant**, emerges from the same concealed approach and embraces/kisses **Nash**, who welcomes her. She and Bok Bok had been hiding together, hoping for escape; **Nash did not find her through an unverified tower-vision alone—this is now a performed meeting**. **She urgently asks to reach somewhere safe for the child**. Nash begins outlining the costly ***Teleportation Circle*** glyphs in the basement, planning to transport them to the already known **First Nash asteroid tower**, but **does not finish or cast that circle**.
6. **Nobieri asks them to remain (~00:57–01:11).** **Riddle Nobieri (?)** contacts **Stanley** through a *Message*-like magical channel, instructing them to hold position because **help has arrived**. Stanley replies and learns the rescuers are his kin, the **Elhandari elves**. **Vorel urges immediate teleportation**—he distrusts Nobieri and suspects more exploitation—while Stanley wants to see whether the incoming force can save other surviving people. The companions argue over whether Hope's demon associates caused the destruction, but **their accusations and defenses are opinions, not proof Hope personally ordered the attack**. **Nash pauses the circle** to protect Angelashi while **Vorel, Stanley and Squirts go above**. Bok Bok follows them, after a shy moment with Squirts. No one actually teleports away from Golden Sands here.

## The Elhandari intervention (~01:11–01:42)

7. **An unusual nonlethal campaign (~01:11–01:23).** From broken windows Stanley sees **an enormous gray metallic ship floating over Tietkalla**, with dark-armored elven troops descending almost invisibly. **Dr. Yvaine/Yvain (?)**, a fair elven woman wearing spectacles and holding a clipboard, approaches a nycaloth as a **therapist**, using a thrown anti-aggression/empathy device to make the demon speak nonviolently. **Small wheeled traps unfold tentacles and shrink/pull the subdued demon inside**. This is **directly shown elven technology**, though the alleged rehabilitation outcomes are not observed. **Stanley is spotted and greeted as elf-kin**; Vorel is suspicious of brainwashing and cages. The elves state that they came in response to a plea and are helping civilians.
8. **Nobieri and Melgor return (~01:23–01:36).** The sky fills with aircraft and elven rescue teams as **Riddle Nobieri** and **Melgor of Paxus (?)**, who have been searching for their missing queen, descend. **Nobieri reports** searching Razak Val and the Purple Desert, seeing the damaged Fate Four and the aftermath of a rift. He marvels at **Sigil** and the Hand concealed in the Fist, after hearing the group's account. **Melgor angrily confronts Vorel**, who explains Hope's departure; **Nobieri still wants to save Hope**. Their search histories are **NPC testimony**, not new scenes personally witnessed by the protagonists. **Omnom Cinderborn** is also shown on the battlefield later, **alive prior to Carthax's beams**, despite E104 uncertainty.
9. **Captain Tenmuth's claim (~01:34–01:41).** Elven captain **Tenmuth/Tamneth (?)** introduces his people as peaceful scholars seeking harmony, without prisons. **Vorel gives his established public title “Fang of Razak Val”**, but distrusts “re-education.” Tenmuth counters that they have a **large biodome/wilderness** and safe refuge at **Elhandar/Elhendari (?)**. These are **claims by an official**, not proof his people are harmless or that the destination is really a utopia. **Angelashi urges Nash to accept passage** for their baby's safety; Nash agrees based on trust in her judgment. **No child is born or delivered in E111**.

## The deadly Carthax fight (~01:42–02:22)

10. **Rescue request and immediate preparation (~01:42–01:57).** An elf is **violently hurled dead** near the meeting; radio reports that a much stronger demon has destroyed earlier therapy squads. **Nash actually carries pregnant Angelashi upward to safety in a hovering spacecraft before engaging the threat**; she is **not left on the combat ground**. **General Carthax**, **20–30 feet tall**, sits on a grotesque throne made from dead tieflings; **Omnom is alive but exhausted** before him. **Nobieri stays with/returns to the ship rather than charging**. An elven squad gives the party **empathy grenades** that trigger after squeezing and throwing, aiming to **pacify** rather than kill. It also provides fast-rest **pink pills**: **Stanley and Nash actually swallow one**, receiving *long-rest benefits immediately*, but with a promised **four exhaustion levels in two hours** and immediate discomfort; **Bok Bok takes a pill**, with fewer details about effects. **Vorel and Squirts decline**. Pills are **not ordinary potions or harmless long rests** and no exhaustion-delayed resolution is shown before boarding.
11. **Face-to-face with the general (~01:56–02:10).** Vorel and **Melgor** lead the group to **Carthax/Karthax**, a giant general who taunts them about **Hope** and displays his tiefling-corpse throne. Vorel **rages and attacks with fiery transforming shield/blade**; **Squirts actually casts *Mass Healing Word* on Omnom**, whose wounds mend but who slumps exhausted. Her cleric **concentration on *Pass without Trace* ends** once battle magic is used. **Nash casts *Haste* on Vorel** and **Stanley uses *Mantle of Inspiration*** (temporary hit points) and ***Heat Metal* on Carthax's armor**, burning him without an ordinary drop/disarm action. Elven roof squads fire weapons. **Omnom is alive immediately after Squirts's healing; his fate after the fight is not independently followed to the ship**.
12. **Squirts and Melgor killed (~02:15–02:19).** The general releases **red force/disintegration beams from his fingers** at heroes and elven troops. **Squirts fails the saving throw (16) and takes 66 force damage**, disintegrating into a **small pile of ash**. **Melgor and several elven soldiers are also disintegrated in the same attack**. **Chunks, who is carried by Squirts, falls to the ground but survives**. The death is **GM-adjudicated**, not just unconsciousness, presumed disappearance or a hypothetical spell effect. **The other three original traveling PCs survive the beams**; there is no Squirts resurrection or restoration before the cutoff. Vorel attacks in rage after her death; Beau offers the table a moment to absorb the result.
13. **The general is not killed (~02:20–02:38).** Vorel deals repeated significant fiery sword wounds; Stanley continues high-level ***Heat Metal***, and Nash temporarily assumes **black dragon form**, but **Beau allows an explicitly exceptional inspiration-consuming takeback** to revert immediately when Scott recognizes an otherwise lethal interaction with fast-rest pill exhaustion. **Do not generalize this as the rules allowing free dragon-form cancellation**. Stanley's ***Dissonant Whispers*** damages Carthax without causing the intended retreat. **Vorel makes a successful Athletics contest** and **punches an entire bandolier of empathy grenades into the general's eye**, the explosion removing an eye and leaving him **pacified/empathetic, not dead**. **Dr. Yvaine leads therapy**, while the elves **actually entrap the subdued general alive** in a shrinking tentacled device.
14. **Bok Bok's terrible sacrifice and rescue (~02:30–02:49).** Before the general is pacified, **Bok Bok charges inside Carthax's mouth carrying dynamite**. An explosion later goes off internally, yet the demon remains intact. Fearing the goblin's death, **Stanley activates *Mantle of Majesty* and casts *Command* (“vomit”)**, which the GM allows against the now-charmed general. Carthax **actually vomits Bok Bok back out alive**, badly burned and with **one hand blown off**. **Vorel gathers the goblin**, while the elven forces proceed to contain Carthax. The goblin **is not confirmed dead, resurrected, rearmed, healed to full strength or given a prosthetic**. **Squirts is not the goblin rescued by this spell**.

## Preserving Squirts and abandoning the ruined city (~02:49–02:57)

15. **The ashes are secured (~02:49–02:51).** A descending elven ship creates a wind threatening to scatter **Squirts's ash pile**. Nash shields the ash with his cape, and **Stanley offers a small clean container/vial from his component pouch**. **Vorel actually scoops all that the GM describes as her remains into the container**, which he keeps. This is **physical custody**, not a resurrection spell or symbolic burial; whether the ashes permit future resurrection is **unanswered**. **Stanley briefly holds both Chunks and injured Bok Bok** during the collection, then passes Bok Bok back to Vorel.
16. **A choice and an actual departure (~02:51–02:56).** **Carthax remains alive but locked in an elven trap**, the elves working through the dead city. **Angelashi is already aboard**. **Nash says he must go with his wife and unborn child**; **Vorel elects to go rather than remain**, and **Stanley accepts the departure**. **Stanley boards with Chunks, Vorel with wounded Bok Bok and Squirts's vial, and Nash levitates aboard**. A technologically advanced ship closes its door, **ascends into the sky and the void beyond**. **Destination Elhandar is proposed and accepted, not yet depicted as arrived at**. The devastated city, its remaining tieflings, **Nobieri and Omnom's eventual status** and Hope's separate mission remain for later episodes. No return to the asteroid tower is shown.
17. **Out-of-game closing (~02:56–02:57:08).** The cast expresses real surprise and sadness at **Squirts's death**; they joke about the dangers of attacking a demon general but treat her loss seriously. **Comments are player reaction**, not a secret revival or the first appearance of a successor character.
