## Critical interpretation / source hierarchy

- **Nash:** The GM explicitly describes his custom item and dragon transformation mechanics. The **heart’s name and dragon broodmother spelling** (working `Kas de Jira`), as well as role of **dragon sun-mages**, need the contemporary **D&D Beyond item/rules** if accessible; later end-of-campaign sheets may contain upgraded powers and must **not** be backdated. The GM's level-4 description is a contemporaneous in-game mechanic, not simply fan theory.
- **Contract:** The **three signatories** remain Nash, Stanley and Hope; **Vorel refuses**. Vorel also has new powers from prior level-up (fire aura), but **not the E023 pact feat**. Soulbroker offers leads conditional on binding promise and acts as a **self-interested NPC**, not a guaranteed accurate historian.
- **Private experience:** Nash's Solar Mines ordeal is a **Soulbroker-induced memory/vision**; GM offers explanation to the **player**, but the other characters only witness the metamorphosis and what Nash tells them. Hope's dream about her father remains another private/qualified lead.
- **Artifacts/places:** `Fox and Scully`, `Elhendar`, the Teet woman and **Celestium** are now **quest leads from Catoblepraithe**, not independently found objects. **Château Weir keys** and newly transformed Nash are actually granted/demonstrated.
- **Deed:** No recovered deed; the Soulbroker **says** Tylander's deed was spent in the ritual. Earlier **E015 evaporation** is directly seen, but the demon's reagent explanation is additional attributed testimony.
- **Earlier chest versus later chest:** E021's locked strongbox remained **unopened** then; **E024 opens it**, recovering money, gems and potions, **not the deed**. Keep first sighting and retrieval distinct.
- **Door and departure:** Burning Dorothy's door creates dangerous smoke and **does not establish her destruction or a viable escape** by cutoff.
- **Catherine:** Her corpse is encountered again, but her farewell **has not yet been delivered** to Bertrand.
