# E110 — Do what the big head tells you

> **The Wastes · corrected S1E110 · Arc Five · main-party return after the E107–E109 Dunebreaker prequel · ASR-provisional.** [Parakeet](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E110.%20Do%20what%20the%20big%20head%20tells%20you.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen-labelled](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E110.%20Do%20what%20the%20big%20head%20tells%20you.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [comparison](../reports/E110_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md) · [Arc Five](../narrative/ARC_FIVE.md). **Both full SRT exports read from connected GitHub at pinned commit `b4aff5d69f961fc54b6e0ce0a90dee590c02a1e7`** after workspace transcript access was denied; **no audio listened to**. Owner catalogue calls this **TWBD 112, 13 July 2020** (publisher date, not proven recording day). Corrected **E110** resumes Beau's main campaign, not Jon's prequel.

**Played cutoff:** The giant wall-face calls itself **First Nash**, an alternate Nash who has become part of the tower. He recounts lost versions of **Stanley, Vorel and Hope** in his own history—not deaths of today's travelers—and says saving Hope's soul matters to the wider future. Nash uses his unusual **right eye** to see **pregnant Angelashi** and **Bokbok** fighting a nycaloth in Tietkalla and escaping toward **the Golden Sands**. **Badass T unmasks as an alternate Tedna**, and an **alternate Pod is alive with a pixie family**, but **both stay at the tower**. Stanley obtains equivalents of **Diplomacy, his megaphone and dictionary**; Nash obtains his old nonmagical staff. **Vorel briefly takes Hope's guns and belt but hands them back to Badass T** before departure. Nash successfully casts **Teleportation Circle**, transporting **Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Squirts and Chunks** into Tietkalla's underground keep. A frightened tiefling is **killed by an invisible nycaloth**, which the companions **then kill** with coordinated magic and Vorel's burning-sword strike. Under **Pass without Trace**, they find the keep devastated and see **the Golden Sands displaying a survivor flag**. **They have not yet reached the inn, reunited with Angelashi or rescued survivors**. Hope is not shown in E110.

## The face in the asteroid tower (~00:03–00:26)

1. **Another Nash, not a father (~00:04–00:15).** The voice inside the E106 tower names itself **First Nash** and explicitly claims to be an older, alternate version of Nash. It discusses multiple histories, stone circles, an enormous Stanley statue, the partly living structure and **alternate versions of Badass T**. “Junior” is a familiar form of address, **not proof of parentage**. Other-dimensional mechanics remain partly the speaker's account, not settled universal rules.
2. **Memorials and Hope (~00:14–00:24).** First Nash explains that **his** Vorel, Stanley and Hope died and he erected memorials. These losses **do not retroactively kill the existing main-party characters**. His and Badass T's ambition is to **save Hope's soul**; the tower speaker also mentions preserving the galaxy, without a completed rescue or verified forecast. Present Stanley criticizes Hope's departure and Vorel challenges that judgment; these are **conflicting companion opinions**, not proof of her betrayal.
3. **Nash's eye and Angelashi (~00:24–00:40).** First Nash instructs Nash to use his damaged **right eye** to sense Angelashi, not physically remove it. Nash at first tries to push the eye out and suffers bloody/purulent discharge from an ear. Under repeated attempts and changed skill checks, the eye extends into an extraordinary **floating/vision state**. He sees **Angelashi pregnant**, with **blue-glowing handprints of the unborn child** at her belly, running with **Bokbok** in Tietkalla/Trashburg. She immobilizes a **nycaloth** with magic as Bokbok enters its mouth; the creature explodes and the pair flee **toward the Golden Sands**. **This is a GM-described magical vision, not Nash physically arriving or speaking to Angelashi.** First Nash orders urgent use of the teleport room.

## The equipment room and living alternate companions (~00:40–01:22)

4. **Objects recovered, with cross-history custody (~00:40–00:51).** Badass T's junk-room road case contains counterparts of **Stanley's floating sword Diplomacy, megaphone and English dictionary**, **Hope's firearms and holster belt**, and Nash's familiar **unenchanted staff**. **Stanley actually takes the first three**, checking his dictionary (**Investigation 22**); **Nash takes the staff** after successful persuasion (**29**). Because these were preserved in **a different history**, exact same-object identity with originals is not established. **Vorel initially accepts Hope's guns**, but does **not** depart with them; he later hands the guns/belt to **Badass T**.
5. **Alternate Pod stays home (~00:52–01:21).** A living **Pod** with a **male pixie partner and children** inhabits a miniature home beside the teleportation room. She is overjoyed at Nash's return. **He explicitly distinguishes himself from her Nash** and says the Pod he knew died. Although she begs to join the rescue, **Nash promises to return and she stays behind with her family**. Chunks is hungry but **does not eat the pixie**. No Pod accompanies Nash through the portal.
6. **The guide is an alternate Tedna (~00:57–01:21).** Badass T removes his mask, visibly revealing a **lipless, disfigured alternate Tedna**. He claims **Suck Master Din** tore away his lips, insists on being called **Badass T**, and hopes the travelers will return to help **save Hope**. **Original Tedna's E050 death is not overturned.** The guide stays at the asteroid tower. **Vorel gives him Hope's firearms and belt** for safekeeping/use rather than delivering them to Hope.
7. **The circle is performed (~01:02–01:28).** The group establishes known glyph codes for **Tietkalla, White Tower and this asteroid tower**. They discuss the uncertainty of same-plane ***Teleportation Circle*** travel across the tower's unusual reality; the GM avoids pretending the multiverse rules are fully settled. Badass T/Pod secure enough **gem-infused chalk/ink** for two potential castings. **Nash draws the circle, adds little smiley faces, says his incantation and actually opens a portal**. **Nash, Vorel, Stanley, Squirts and Chunks cross into Tietkalla**; **Tedna and Pod do not**. A return journey is planned, not yet demonstrated.

## From the keep basement to its ruined surface (~01:28–02:27)

8. **A known chamber, changed inhabitants (~01:28–01:41).** They land in the old **underground teleportation-circle room beneath Tietkalla**. A damaged, number-tattooed zombie recalled as **Carnage** shuffles and speaks, with inconsistent remembered numeric prefixes; the table debates **26/42/etc.**, so do not standardize an invented final number. Nash has sensed Angelashi heading toward the **Golden Sands**. The **froghemoth pit is filled and now called Tedna's playground**; the party remembers other older routes through the crypts and prison.
9. **Climbing the corpse shaft (~01:39–01:49).** They choose the **body-hoist/conveyor shaft** rather than a blocked underground exit. **Nash floats and casts Light on a vegetable peeling**; **Stanley and Vorel climb chains and traverse rollers**, Squirts riding Vorel. **Stanley slips and Nash helps him**, so nobody dies or becomes stranded. They all reach the old **broom closet** and move out stealthily. The group has **not recovered spell slots via a long rest**; their limited abilities affect later decisions.
10. **A terrified tiefling survivor (~01:50–02:22).** Stanley quietly encounters a man in armor, crying and hiding near the supply rooms. **Stanley uses Message through his restored megaphone** to offer help. The survivor recognizes the **Heroes of the Wastes**, says he was studying writings about growing crops attributed to **Carventia/Carvencia (?)**, and claims Tietkalla has been suddenly overwhelmed by **thousands of winged demons**. He remains devoted to **Queen Hope** and reports wounding a pursuing fiend. **Thousands** is an NPC's frightened estimate, not a directly counted force. A leader whose death might scatter the invaders is only his suggestion, not proven.
11. **He dies before escape (~02:21–02:28).** The frightened survivor steps into a hall; an **invisible nycaloth impales and kills him**, becoming visible on attack. The creature then savages his corpse. It bears scars, embedded weapons and damaged wing, matching his account of having wounded an attacker. **The death is played**, not mere hearsay; **this is not necessarily the same nycaloth Nash saw through the eye**.

## A silent ambush and the real ending (~02:28–02:48)

12. **Coordinated surprise (~02:28–02:38).** Hiding nearby, **Stanley drops restored Diplomacy onto the nycaloth** and casts ***Heat Metal*** on it. **Squirts uses *Silence*** to suppress combat noise. **Vorel pushes a heavy barrel into the demon** (Athletics **23**), hampering but not killing it. Nash's ***Chromatic Orb* acid** has **no useful effect** because the fiend is immune; **Squirts's subsequent *Guiding Bolt* natural one is adjudicated as comic self-injury**, not successful damage to the enemy. Stanley continues heating Diplomacy.
13. **Vorel's killing stroke (~02:38–02:42).** **Vorel jumps across the barrel**, grabs the heated sword, drives it down through the nycaloth, and the GM **explicitly narrates its death, split in half and burned**. **Vorel takes reduced fire damage from holding the heated weapon** and **hands it right back to Stanley**. **Diplomacy belongs with Stanley at episode end.**
14. **The Golden Sands is only sighted (~02:42–02:48).** **Squirts casts *Pass without Trace***. The company climbs toward the ruined throne room as **two other nycaloths depart the keep**, unengaged. Above the broken throne and walls, demon patrols circle and search the city. Across the rooftops the adventurers see **the Golden Sands with a flag that signals survivors inside**. **No arrival inside the inn, successful evacuation, reunion with Angelashi/Bokbok or return to the asteroid tower is depicted**. This is the main-party **E110 cutoff**; credits and a displaced closing cue follow.
