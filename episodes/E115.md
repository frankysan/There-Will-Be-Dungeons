## Cutoff and material changes

**The party actually reaches Elhandar**, an elven space station with a living forest biodome. After settling into a cottage, **four recovery days pass**, and play resumes at **breakfast on day five, with Nash's and Stanley's pill exhaustion cleared**. No two-month or year-long stay is completed. **Nash and Angelashi name their four children Fork, Boot, Pipe and Nash Junior Maggard**; Nash Junior is the boy born in baby form. The complete sex/form mapping of the other three remains unspecified.

**Stanley's birth name is disclosed as Valier Ornath / Valeer–Valere Ornoth (?)**, while he explicitly prefers **Stanley**. Admiral **Chrysorius/Chrysoerius (?)** reports a genetic family match and offers a search for his deceased father **Daliot/Dalit (?)**. **No father is located or returned.** The **Principle appears in person**, claims the city explosion and demigodhood, privately warns Stanley that an unnamed old friend survived the blast, then leaves through a portal. His growth and portal are observed; his explanation of the explosion, ascension and the friend's fate are **his claims**.

**Vorel reports Squirts's ashes lost**, a continuity tension with their earlier vial custody; the loss mechanism is not witnessed. A memorial is performed. **Bok Bok declares himself Chunks's father/caretaker**, and the ensuing days show him caring for the child. **The GM now treats Bok Bok as having two hands**, after uncertain recollection of the loss; no regrowth procedure is played. **Nobieri visits**, reports **Omnom recovering aboard** and **Emenil asking after Stanley**, and urges Vorel toward leadership. Both reports remain unvisited leads. **No new played Hope-party scene, reunion or proof Hope ordered Tietkalla's destruction**.

## From the pirate ship to Elhandar (~00:00–00:51)

1. **Recap versus renewed play (~00:00–00:04).** Kristen recaps E114, including the four children and a stronger assertion of Nash's changeling identity. In resumed play Nash still disputes what this means; **the recap alone does not grant him new abilities or settle his ancestry**. Captain **Toesalt/Towsalt (?)** is still transmitting threats after his ship disengaged. Nash remains with the family behind the medical barricade; **Stanley and Vorel**, not Nash, enter the bridge.
2. **Withdrawal, then destruction (~00:04–00:12).** Vorel's rocket bluff fails; the elves confirm they have no rockets aboard. **Stanley persuades Toesalt to withdraw**, though the pirate promises revenge for Fluffy. The crew says scanners place the ghost ship's origin on the surface, **probably Principal City**; the city-escape connection is an inference. The elves **report that the personnel swept overboard have suffocated**, resolving their previously open status by crew testimony, not a body-recovery scene. Elhandar station then offers fire support. **Vorel assents, Captain Solania (?) orders fire, and the station's shot destroys the departing pirate vessel**; shields deflect debris. **The vessel is visibly destroyed; the undead captain's permanent individual fate is not separately established.**
3. **Family revelation becomes shared information (~00:12–00:24).** The medical door opens and **Bok Bok helps dismantle the canister barricade**. Stanley and Vorel learn about the four children; Angelashi remains visibly in changeling form. **She says she hid her identity to avoid exploitation/mistrust in Trashburg**, and that Nash is partly a shifter, although she does not know exactly what he is. She attributes an earlier intimate transformation to **Nash's own body**, not her plant magic. These are **her recollections and interpretations**, not a newly reenacted scene. Nash **tries to force a man-sized frog transformation without a spell**, but **the GM says he has no new abilities and nothing changes**. His words about the children being lizards are his confused description, not a new species ruling. After further hurtful remarks about her appearance, **Angelashi asks him to leave**, and Lyrielle directs him to his friends.
4. **Stanley challenges Nash, then arrival (~00:24–00:39).** Nash vents in the hallway; **Stanley urges him to judge Angelashi with the acceptance she showed him and support his family**. Both receive inspiration. The ship approaches a **three-part station**, including a long technological cylinder and a huge glass-enclosed forest globe. **It docks, passengers disembark, and Bok Bok carries Chunks ashore**. Lyrielle and attendants prepare Angelashi and the babies for transfer on a floating bed. **Admiral Chrysorius/Chrysoerius (?)** receives Nash, Stanley and Vorel. All three actually ride **Rainbow Road**, a station transport of rainbow energy; no instability occurs. The fast-rest pill penalty is still impending at this point.
5. **A public welcome and a declined memory sharing (~00:40–00:51).** Thousands of elves gather in a candlelit, multilevel concourse. The admiral says they know the heroes and have a particular history relating to Nash and Stanley; **Vorel is welcomed without an equivalent revealed connection**. An elderly **High Elf in a chair supported by moving water** greets Stanley as homecoming kin. The group jokes about fatherhood, but **no literal paternity is established**. The High Elf takes Stanley's hands and begins a trance/memory-sharing interaction; **Stanley resists and expresses distrust, and the High Elf withdraws rather than completing the exchange**. He states two principles: **a therapist must be assigned**, and **Elhandar first**. Their pre-Scorching history, station construction and planetary-restoration mission are **his account**, not independently inspected ancient events.

## The Principle's message and the cottage (~00:51–01:34)

6. **An uninvited visitor (~00:51–01:02).** **The Principle portals into the assembly**, chrome body now showing blue energy, gemstone and rings. Vorel activates his shield and Stanley draws Diplomacy/megaphone, but **no battle occurs**. The Principle claims **he caused Principal City's explosion**, not demons, and has reached **demigodhood**, offering the elves a place in his future order. **He visibly grows to roughly three times his size, changes his face, and telekinetically draws Stanley close**, then returns to his usual large stature. **Only Stanley hears the whispered assertion that an unnamed old friend survived the blast and will visit soon**. The Principle tells everyone to choose and **leaves through a ring-activated portal**. No alliance is signed, no godhood independently measured, and no old friend appears here.
7. **Elven response and living forest (~01:02–01:19).** The High Elf criticizes **Vladimir/the Principle's** pursuit of power and refusal of therapy, explaining elven life/death beliefs and promising discussion of the threat. **These are his judgments and cosmological claims**, not a confirmed planetary cure. He assigns **Lyrielle** as the visitors' guide. The heroes take Rainbow Road to the biodome and enter a humid forest. **Stanley's Nature/Detect Magic examination establishes genuine living vegetation with pervasive growth/life magic, rather than summoned or illusory plants**. His later suggestion it is a trick does not supersede that finding. The admiral leads them to a lakeside **two-story cottage**, with Angelashi, the children, Bok Bok, Chunks and Lyrielle present. **Kristen names the white mink familiar Acorn**; Beau acknowledges he misidentified some of her attempted familiar noises as Chunks last episode. **Do not globally reassign every earlier child cue from this correction.**
8. **Name, family and a conditional search (~01:19–01:31).** The admiral clarifies: **assignment of a therapist is required; attending sessions is voluntary**. He addresses Stanley as **Valier Ornath (?)**, explaining that the boarding scan matched the **Ornath/Onath** family records. **Stanley acknowledges the birth name**, repeats it, and asks to keep being called Stanley. Nash's **Flingus Fartfeather** is a joke, not an established birth name. The admiral says the High Elf is a spiritual leader, **not a king whose orders cannot be refused**. He recalls Stanley's father **Daliot/Dalit (?)** as a valued but scientifically dissatisfied elf. Stanley says his father died on Earth and he does not know the burial site. The admiral says **dead elves cannot pass on properly in Earth's corrupted soil**, and offers to **search for/recover the father if Stanley rests and engages with elven life/therapy**. **No search result, father sighting, resurrection or completed therapy session is shown**. Stanley retires to bed as the pill cost catches up.

## Loss, remembrance and the first night (~01:34–02:10)

9. **Ashes no longer in custody (~01:34–01:41).** Bok Bok asks for Squirts's funeral. **Vorel says he forgot the ashes during the ship battle and now has none**, suggesting **vacuum or his fiery sword** as possible explanations. Both exports preserve this confession. **E111 placed the ashes in a Stanley-supplied vial**; the change from vial to hand/lost remains is an **unresolved custody discrepancy**, not proof of an observed spilling, destruction or recoverable trace. Saying Squirts lives on in Chunks refers to her child/legacy; **Chunks has not swallowed her ashes and is not to be buried**. Lyrielle lights a memorial fire with **Fire Bolt**. A **~01:37–01:40 streaming/video interruption** is out-of-game, not elapsed story time or a lost fictional encounter.
10. **A performed memorial and a new caretaker (~01:41–01:46).** Vorel gives a spoken remembrance; **Bok Bok grieves, says he would have made Squirts his wife**, and **throws a lit dynamite stick into the lake**. Its explosion rattles the cottage but the GM says **no one is hurt**. No marriage is backdated from his wish. Bok Bok declares he is **no longer merely Vorel's helper: he is a father who will care for Chunks**. Vorel accepts this, and Bok Bok takes Chunks inside, blankets him and settles to sleep. This is **an actual caretaker commitment with subsequent care shown**, not a formal legal adoption or biological paternity.
11. **Reconciliation and accepted children's names (~01:46–01:56).** Nash returns to the family bedroom with Lyrielle's help. He and Angelashi **apologize and agree to forgive each other**. She says he seems human as well as shifter, “almost as if” made, but **does not know his origin**. Nash recalls being told he was an orphan in the Solar Mines. The mystery remains open. They discuss their four children, **two boys/two girls**, and Nash proposes **Nash Junior** for the baby-form boy and **Fork, Boot and Pipe** for the others, with **Maggard** as surname. After a **natural twenty / corrected total 28 Persuasion**, **Angelashi explicitly accepts the names**. These are now agreed names, not merely object nicknames. Her lack of a known surname is her own statement. **Nash's delayed exhaustion strikes** and he goes to sleep beside her.
12. **Resting places and an unfulfilled nighttime summons (~01:56–02:05).** Vorel cannot find comfortable dry sand and **sleeps in the old firepit ash** after eating. Lyrielle prepares/mends clothes, cleans with magic and turns in. Nash dreams of surrounding flames and a voice saying **it is close, dying, and wants him to find it**; **the voice continues after he wakes**. **Angelashi hears nothing**. An Arcana check supports a **telepathic contact probably nearby in the forest**, not the speaker's identity or a proven exact location. Nash starts to consider leaving, **then chooses to return to sleep**. **No search, rescue or identification of the voice occurs**, and he still receives long-rest benefit.
13. **Proposed downtime versus completed recovery (~02:05–02:18).** The GM offers a longer Elhandar rest and discusses “year two/year three” as an editorial/show transition; **he expressly says more remains in this arc**. The players consider months, but **only four days are actually advanced**, with **breakfast on day five** as the resumed scene. **All relevant pill exhaustion clears**. Vorel hunts; Lyrielle cooks, and Bok Bok cares for Chunks. **The GM now says Bok Bok has two hands**, admits uncertainty remembering the loss and suggests offscreen elven treatment; **no regrowth procedure, specific spell or universal medical capability is demonstrated**. Vorel's hunting includes a deer, ferret, ducks and an **oversized mother raccoon** he wants preserved. Lyrielle agrees to prepare other meat and preserve the raccoon; Nash's claim to be eating it is questioned/corrected. **A completed taxidermy trophy is not shown**.

## Breakfast, available routes and a visitor (~02:18–03:04)

14. **Why continue? (~02:18–02:38).** The group debates the Soulbroker bargain, the Principle, lost communities and Hope. **Vorel says he is no longer Fang, or “Fang of nothing,”** a self-assessment of his lost constituency, not a newly granted office or independent proof every Razak Val resident is dead. Nash wants time with his family; Stanley wants information before fighting. **Lyrielle tells a mermaid/sea-witch/seafoam story about herself**, including a prince, lost voice and children's prayers. This is **her autobiographical account during conversation**, not a played prior adventure or certified general resurrection rule. No children have been moved to an alternate Nash's home, despite proposals about that option. A year to construct a new permanent circle is discussed; **a year's construction is not performed**. The GM clarifies the earlier marriage downtime was **three months**, estimates roughly a year of in-game time with uncertainty, and says show “years” are not literal story years. **No exact Soulbroker deadline remaining is computed.**
15. **A real existing circle, no transit yet (~02:38–02:50).** Angelashi, **present at breakfast throughout**, says she would prefer retirement but supports Nash doing what he believes necessary, with visits home where possible. She considers learning/building a circle if needed. **Lyrielle, following a GM clarification, confirms a permanent Teleportation Circle exists in the station's science/magic district**, provisionally **Cadulate/Cagealat/Cadgilet (?)**. **No sigil sequence is learned, spell taught to Angelashi, new circle built, or trip actually taken**. The party considers negotiating out of its pact or finding Hope. Their claims that Hope caused Tietkalla's fall remain **accusations/speculation**; the GM's reminder about her darkness is not a new attack scene or proof of her command over that invasion.
16. **Nobieri's report and the actual ending (~02:50–03:04).** Just as they propose consulting him, **Nobieri physically arrives and joins the table**. He says **Melgor's search party and the elves pursued the heroes after the northern explosion**, found ruined Razak Val, joined forces and reached Kervalon before turning back when Tietkalla was attacked. **This is retrospective testimony**, not new scenes inserted into E107–E114. He **reports Omnom Cinderborn alive, recovering aboard**, and **Emenil/Emanil (?) among the refugees, asking after Stanley**; neither is actually visited here. Stanley recounts Hope's Hand transformation and the demons; **Nobieri does not know the Hand's identity** and promises to consult spirits. He urges redemption rather than vengeance, says the surviving tieflings are **across the clearing**, invites a visit, and **exhorts Vorel to lead** before leaving. **No spirit consultation, quest completion, father recovery or Hope reunion occurs.** The hosts correct an announced level from **13 to 12**, saying the increase was handled off air in the prior episode; do not report a new E115 level 13. Farewell and network credits close the recording.
