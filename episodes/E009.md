## Reference significance

- **Leopold:** the presence of a `warden AI protocol` control makes a machine/intelligence interpretation plausible, but is not sufficient alone to establish exactly what Leopold is. His repeated denial of being a computer is a character statement; the controls and observed responses must be recorded separately.
- **The tree and the ancient war:** Its claimed elven identity, past imprisonment and anti-dragon origin are *its narrative*, not independent proof that every detail happened. The tree seems able to influence its structure and direct the party. Its request is to end its confinement, not a neutral technical report.
- **Portable hole / rods / staff:** Chance's cloth opens a **portable hole** as observed. The rods/spikes, immovable rod and tree-gift staff have separate acquisition and custody histories; avoid assigning permanent ownership solely from in-session arguments. Check E009 ~03:00–03:10 before fixing inventories.
- **Countdown/escape:** Treat 24-hour selection and later minutes-remaining calls as observations at distinct times. Do not infer the whole fight and escape occurred in only 20 minutes or that self-destruct remained set exactly as planned.
- **Episode boundaries:** E008 concludes on Leopold's threat; E009 resolves that confrontation and culminates with the dragon and approaching vehicles. E010 covers the vehicle encounter. The exact causal relationship between destruction of the facility and the dragon's appearance is not mechanically demonstrated.
- **Do not backdate:** The E009 GM's explicit **out-of-character** assurance that Eddie and Chance are alive does not mean the surviving PCs witnessed a rescue or learned where the gnomes went.
