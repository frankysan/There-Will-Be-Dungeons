# E105 — The Fright Elevator

> **The Wastes · corrected S1E105 · Arc Five · played campaign session · ASR-provisional.** [Parakeet processed](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E105.%20The%20Fright%20Elevator.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E105.%20The%20Fright%20Elevator.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [full-export ASR comparison](../reports/E105_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). Both exports cover opening speech through the postgame, credits and final network sign-off (~02:52:14); no audio was heard. Owner-preserved archive catalogue lists **publisher TWBD 107**, publication **25 May 2020**, corrected Wastes S1E105, but that catalogue's original release metadata has not been independently verified. This episode depicts **only the Sigil group**, not Hope's offscreen journey after E104.

**Played cutoff:** The corrected fruit portal actually brings **Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Squirts, Chunks, Sam and the surviving orc escort** to **Sigil**, coated with portal slime, aboard their small vessel. Nash inherits a claimed **100,000-gold uninsured airship liability** upon having accepted captaincy. The party survives an **engineered freight-elevator ambush**, with substantial attacker and bystander casualties. **Squirts helps save the badly injured Fambo Joe; Vorel and the rickshaw company escape together.** Their guide **Bungor/Bungo (?)** takes them through the Hive to **Tobacho's bar**, where the **blue dragon is directly seen**. **No delivery of the closed Fist case, agreed payment, granted Earth access, Earth crossing or meeting outcome** is played.

## Portal, dock and Nash's new liability (~00:03–00:38)

1. **The correct gate reaches Sigil (~00:03–00:12).** The small airship passes through a membrane-coated portal into an enormous dim industrial arrival/dock hall. **Slimy translucent residue covers the passengers and ship**. Stanley uses *Prestidigitation* to clean companions with imperfect cooperation: Nash's or Vorel's boots/feet remain a joke and nuisance. The approach is over a deep dark drop, **not a conventional water port**. The boat docks; the surviving group and orcs disembark. **This resolves E104's actual-arrival uncertainty**, not the Tobacho exchange or anyone's return to Earth.
2. **The missing admiral's ship (~00:12–00:16).** A harried **wet harbor official** expected **Admiral Walls/Wals** and a larger leased airship called **the Hidden Stinker** (working ship spelling). Instead a small support boat returns. Nash explains that the big ship was lost to the wrong portal and that the admiral was reportedly lost in the battle. **The admiral's body is still not recovered**. Do not infer the official witnessed the disaster or that the support boat is the original leased main ship.
3. **Vorel's former rickshaw acquaintance (~00:16–00:25).** The **bronzed human Fambo Joe / Famo Joe (?)**, apparently an exceptionally strong rickshaw driver, approaches Vorel with colleagues, upset about a missed **Friday appointment**. Vorel offers a graceless apology (**Performance 4**) and Fambo **slaps him for one damage**; insults and threats follow, but the party does **not** fight or kill him in this first confrontation. His grievance does not establish Vorel broke an independently verified in-world schedule: day-of-week discussion is confused. The group separates for now.
4. **Captaincy has consequences (~00:25–00:35).** Nash is summoned into the harbor office. Because **the previous captain/admiral did not insure the destroyed ship**, the official says **Nash's acceptance of captaincy and succession makes him responsible for roughly 100,000 gold**, regardless of whether he personally caused the first ship's loss. The official discusses billing, an address and orientation; Nash supplies the **Soggy Feather** as a temporary contact rather than a permanent home. This is **a formal claim made by the harbor administration**, not proof the party has paid or the obligation has been adjudicated by another authority. Nash and Stanley disagree about how seriously to treat the liability and whether leaving Sigil might avoid it. **No debt is settled or collected** in E105.

## Finding the route toward Tobacho (~00:36–01:00)

5. **An unreliable would-be guide (~00:38–00:43).** An older man rises from a garbage container, offers to locate Tobacho and changes his demanded fee. **Vorel slaps him unconscious with a deliberately nonlethal blow**. No death or accepted guide agreement is shown; the exchange is played as an escalation of Vorel's misunderstanding of Fambo's earlier slap.
6. **Squirts wins over the drivers (~00:43–00:52).** The party asks the rickshaw group for directions. **Squirts casts a charm effect on one driver** and then emerges noisily from hiding; the friendly lizardfolk rickshaw driver finds her adorable and carries her as a baby, with two female colleagues along. **Bungor/Bungo** is the working identification (the ASRs vary). His willingness to help and his talk of a kobold pageant occur during an active charm and **are not proof of permanent allegiance**. Fambo remains part of this group despite Vorel's earlier treatment. The destination is said to lie in **the Hive**, a dangerous Sigil district.
7. **The freight lift fills (~00:52–01:00).** They board a **large enclosed freight elevator** with their surviving orcs and drivers. More passengers load at successive floors: armed or armored thugs, dwarves, a figure with a **red-and-black tiger-striped bandana**, **two large ogres**, an aged gnome attendant and other travelers. **Nash is intensely claustrophobic**; the ogres' foul gas and repeated stops aggravate him. Keep Nash's panic separate from a deliberate plan to attack.

## The sealed shaft and the ambush (~01:00–01:57)

8. **No easy way out (~01:00–01:31).** The lift stalls. A small hamster-like messenger brings a cryptic warning along the lines of **“This is what you get when you cross me”**, with **no authenticated sender**. Nash **considers Banishment** of an ogre and tries bargaining to shrink the ogres, but **neither Banishment nor Reduce is actually cast in these exchanges**. GM rules discussion explicitly rejects inventing innocuous spell incantations on the spot to hide casting. Nash opens the ceiling hatch only to encounter a hard **concrete/cement barrier**; an ogre punches through the wooden hatch covering, exposing the sealed shaft. The concrete seems dry/long established. Claustrophobia becomes fear of entrapment and suffocation; no proven cosmic or citywide mechanism is identified.
9. **The trap turns violent (~01:30–01:47).** One ogre unintentionally reveals that someone intends a killing. The lights go out, initiative begins, and **gang-like passengers attack with knives** while the **orc escorts and rickshaw group resist**. The bandana-clad figure helps direct the ambush; **an elf vanishes** in the encounter. **Attacker employer and intended victim are not conclusively established** by the GM's observed description. Several orcs are slain, others injured. Stanley suffers roughly **56 piercing damage in an early knife round**, but retains concentration on his cast **Heat Metal**. Vorel uses his **Eye of Groxl flame shield and protective aura**, attacks, and shifts allies away from danger. Nash evades an ogre's grapple by floating inverted above the crowd. The lizardfolk guide shields Squirts and is injured; the two rickshaw women counterattack, including slashing an ogre's heels.
10. **Costly magic in the enclosed lift (~01:55–02:19).** **Squirts casts a 70-hit-point *Heal* on Stanley**, flavored visually as a ball of crumpled rubbish; it is a **spell, not a consumed scroll**. Nash eventually **casts a Fireball from near the ceiling**, attempting to protect selected companions with **Careful Spell** and Vorel's fire-resistance aura. The chosen placement **does not exempt every bystander**: the elderly gnome attendant **dies in the blast despite a successful save**, and many attackers are killed or burned. Vorel continues using fire attacks; Stanley uses **Mantle of Inspiration** and **Heat Metal**. The weakening elevator floor buckles into a widening central hole, sending bodies and one ogre downward into the shaft. **Do not label all elevator deaths as enemy combatants or claim this fight was bloodless.**
11. **Fambo's survival and the remaining attackers (~02:19–02:34).** A knife-wielding thug had badly wounded **Fambo Joe**, while he resisted instead of betraying the party. **Squirts casts *Spare the Dying*** to stabilize him, and follows with **Mass Healing Word** on selected injured people; the subsequent scene shows Fambo active again, so **he survives**, not an unrevived corpse. Vorel fights remaining hostiles; the rickshaw women press the wounded ogre. A **Hold Person**-type paralysis permits the ogre's defeat, while Nash's attempted **cold Chromatic Orb misses**. A second ogre or other attacker is dispatched; the exact tally of every combatant and passenger is not established.
12. **The floor gives way; descent is survived (~02:34–02:42).** The lift floor finally fails. **Stanley casts Feather Fall to protect the falling companions and allies**, prioritizing those who cannot fly; **Nash remains suspended by his flight ability**. Vorel and the rickshaw drivers help force open the lower shaft door, with **Vorel's successful Athletics 20** making the effective difference. They emerge safely into the oddly curving cityscape; **the elevator's destruction and the loss of noncombatants remain real**, while **the named party, Fambo and their guide survive**. Fambo and Vorel reconcile through a handshake/forearm clasp and mutual respect; a deeper lasting friendship is possible, not yet tested.

## The Hive and Tobacho's room (~02:42–02:52)

13. **A fast rickshaw ride (~02:42–02:49).** **Bungor/Bungo** offers to carry Squirts and some of the group onward toward **Tobacho in the Hive**. Fambo and his rickshaw colleagues part from Vorel in considerably better spirits. The ride passes the Hive gate into a rougher neighborhood marked by gang-associated clothing and insignia. The destination looks **unusually quiet outside**; the former **frog-on-a-stick seller is absent**, with no demonstrated cause or fate here. **No attack is depicted on this road**.
14. **The blue dragon in the bar (~02:49–02:52).** The group enters the downstairs bar with music and a dance floor. **Tobacho is physically present**, seated beyond the dancers, and **looks toward the arrivals**. The last garbled sentence/name is not secure between ASRs. **He does not yet take the Fist, give an Earth portal ticket or conclude a new bargain.** The cast ends play here. A postgame joke calls it a **“fart elevator”**, retrospectively punning on *Fright Elevator*, not an alternate played episode title.
