## Evidence and continuity limits

- **Buttons' E041/E049 mercy contrast:** Stanley previously opposed killing him in E041; **Vorel kills Buttons in E049** during the ambush. His actual **statements about Monsoon/Jolt/Principal** are attributable to Buttons, not confirmed objective world history.
- **P0D's agency:** Monsoon's radio command precedes transformation; enlarged P0D is **not proof of voluntary betrayal**. **Her final outcome occurs E050**, not E049.
- **Softhead:** A previous underground-river disappearance **was not a witnessed death**. E049 directly features a person identified as **Softhead**, though how he reached the keep and his allegiance need follow-up.
- **Tedna and Bok Bok:** **Both rescued alive E048** and remain present through E049. **Angelashi's reported fate still lacks witnessed confirmation**.
- **Gem inventory:** Gem's location/transfer is a main subject of Buttons' claim. The group **does not yet restore it to the water system in E049**.
- **Name handling:** `Dashington`, `Aladweir`, `Septwyn` and `Softhead` need spelling/identity work. **Diontalus** is accepted creator-written orthography; **Tietkalla** is source-supported in context.
