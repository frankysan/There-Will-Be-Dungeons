# E080 — What's with the fire truck

> **Provisional source-indexed main-campaign dossier** from [processed Parakeet ASR](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E080.%20What's%20with%20the%20fire%20truck.parakeet.processed.srt). **Not independently audio-verified.** All times are approximate recording offsets, including the introduction and closing. **Spoilers through E080.** The title and names are retained provisionally; inconsistent ASR such as *Uvexiad/Uvexiid*, *Dolo Popio/Dolla Popeo* and *Sira/Ciri* requires listening or written references.

## Session overview

The fight resumes immediately after [E079](E079.md)'s **two-rocket opening strike** in the transformed **CHIDS engineering / Traversal Drive chamber**. The explosions have filled the air with dust; several enemies and Cerinthala's visible body were destroyed, but a **much larger surviving illithid commander** remains aloft. The GM's opening recap explicitly describes **Sira within the luminous central area along with the dragon-and-worm composite**. This is stronger evidence for her presence than the garbled E079 ASR, but does **not** show her rescued or explain her exact condition.

**Stanley and Eddie** maneuver under cover. Stanley removes **two spare crimson laser swords at a time from his Bag of Holding** and lays them out to prepare an *Animate Objects* spell. **Eddie searches for valuables**, finding nothing immediately useful. **Chance advances** and throws a dart at the hovering alien, while **Vorel** rushes up the raised structure, announcing himself as Great Fang and attempting to **harpoon/grapple the flying commander** with his new suit. The first struggle does not hold the target.

**Hope advances** and attacks the commander with her shotgun, using specialist ammunition; her two rockets remain **spent from E079**. **Nash transforms into a young black dragon** during this fight; the GM allows him additional actions in dragon form under the table's homebrew features and the prior **Blue Bull** boost. He initially fires a broad **acid breath** through two images of the leader. These are **Mirror Image duplicates**: they disappear, leaving the actual enemy active, rather than two separate commanders killed. Some lizardfolk riflemen survive in the room and fire upward at Nash.

The real commander releases an enormous **Mind Blast**. The GM adjudicates a failed save as **27 psychic damage and Stunned**: **Chance, Eddie, Vorel and Nash** fail, while **Hope and Stanley resist**. **Stanley's Feather Fall** safely lowers dragon Nash and Eddie, who would otherwise tumble from dangerous heights. The **dampening helmet on Vorel does not generally prevent all Mind Blast stunning**. Hope exploits the battlefield, knocking down and firing more rounds at the commander, while Stanley works to counter the numbers.

In the glowing central pool, the enormous **Dolo Popio**—a **rotted dragon front fused to a many-mouthed worm body**—produces several green-glowing smaller worms. A second wave brings back **the large red memory-eating creatures** encountered deeper in CHIDS, now appearing on panels around the room. **Hope deliberately shoots the smaller green worms**, whose explosive acid causes **chain reactions** that clear multiple worms. **Stanley attempts Animate Objects on the recovered Vorpal Laser Swords, but the spell requires nonmagical objects and the GM classifies the swords as magical**. He instead animates **ten small loose metal pipe/scaffold pieces** and sends them to batter the floating commander. This distinction matters: **none of the stockpiled swords is established as spent or destroyed by the spell**.

Nash later **recharges acid breath**, incinerates additional lizardfolk riflemen, and **grapples one surviving guard** in dragon claws before ascending. The commander, already badly injured by **Stanley's swarm of animated pipes**, casts a powerful **black necrotic lightning attack** across the battlefield. **Chance and Eddie both fall unconscious**; the large red creatures continue attacking and eating memories. One red monster strikes unconscious **Eddie**, causes **two failed death saves** and a further **1d4 penalty to ability checks/attacks**; another small worm envelops unconscious **Chance** in a cocoon-like grip. The exact mechanism of that cocoon is not explained.

Amid this danger, **Eddie rolls a natural 20 on a death save**, returning **conscious with one hit point**. Chance remains near death for several rounds. Hope keeps shooting the little explosive worms and targets the commander using the **portal device's necrotic neutralization mode**, but the distant commander **evades her attacks**. Stanley's **animated pipes repeatedly hit the commander**, leaving him close to defeat; his attempt to interpret a machine terminal reveals no usable control instructions. Later, Stanley uses **a stored Healing Word from his Ring of Spell Storing** to restore **Chance to consciousness with eight hit points**, definitively averting the immediate death-save emergency.

Nash flies high with his captured lizardfolk, drops the guard to a fatal impact, then approaches the battered commander and lands a recharged **acid breath for the killing blow**. **The commander visibly crumbles to flakes and disintegrates**. This **does resolve the named illithid commander's defeat**, unlike E079's cutoff; it **does not** kill Dolo Popio or shut down the Drive.

Following the commander's destruction, the dragon-worm announces that it will deal with the intruders **personally** and invokes an **alleged contract with a name sounding like 'Andreas Star'**. The scene **visibly transforms**: the characters are now scattered across **floating rocky/crystalline platforms in a magical space-like environment**, with the dragon-worm, its pool and **Sira still at the center**. It is **not confirmed** whether the room was literally transported into outer space, the party entered a magical pocket realm, or the effects are illusory; the GM describes this as an actual new combat environment. A half-buried human head is observed on Nash's sandy platform, **identity unknown**.

**Dolo Popio's fear aura** initially frightens **Hope**; later another frightening pulse affects **Nash**, but **Vorel is immune to Frightened through his dampening helmet** at this stage. The room has peculiar low-gravity/leaping behavior. **Vorel's Stunned condition clears**, and he finally gets into the main battle, activating his **Sibilica/Sivilica power-jump boots** and flying toward the worm-dragon. He **uses the previously hinted Eye of Groxl flame-shield ability on-screen**, reshaping the shield into **a flaming great blade** and inflicting heavy blows. **Vorel embeds the blade in the creature and grapples onto it to avoid floating away**, despite being struck by its claws; this is a **newly demonstrated use**, not an effect that had already occurred when the November bonus merely hinted at it.

Stanley strikes Dolo Popio with *Dissonant Whispers* and **Diplomacy**, then **expends a stored Healing Word to revive Chance**, while Nash's **acid breath scorches the central dragon-worm**. Nash also kills a nearby red flesh monster with claws/bite. **Dolo Popio remains alive**. At the cliffhanger the creature **flies away from its central position, gathers luminous space-like particles, and unleashes a sweeping breath attack toward the party**. **The breath's damage, saves, casualties and result are deferred to E081.** The host says all six are alive at the end, but that production outro is **not evidence the pending attack is survived unscathed**.

## Consistency and audio review

**Character status:** **Hope, Stanley, Nash and Vorel survive**, as do guests **Chance and Eddie**. **Eddie returns on a natural-20 death save; Chance is later healed by Stanley's Ring of Spell Storing**. **Vorel's stun ends and he physically engages Dolo Popio**. Hope and Nash experience **Frightened** at different points, unlike Vorel under the dampening helmet. **An E081 breath attack is pending.**

**Enemy status:** **The large illithid commander dies during E080** by Nash's breath. The **dragon-worm Dolo Popio does not die**; its newly spawned worms and red memory-eaters inflict substantial harm, while several are shot or shredded. **Sira remains inside the central region**, not rescued. The central Drive is **not operated or safely shut down**, and no irreversible planetary outcome is established.

**Inventory and mechanics:** Both rockets were used in **E079**. The **one portal device** remains with Hope and is fired in neutralization mode without a confirmed hit here. Stanley has many spare crimson swords in **Bag of Holding**, but **animates ordinary pipes**, not enchanted swords. **At least one preloaded Healing Word** from Stanley's new ring is consumed healing Chance. E078's actual long rest is **not repeated**. The flame-shield-as-blade is **Vorel's played E080 application** of an artifact capability previously discussed but unrevealed in [November bonus](BONUS-2019-11.md).

**Names/setting:** **Uvexiad/Ovaxiad** (commander), **Dolo Popio** (dragon-worm), **Sira/Ciri** (captive girl), **Andreas Star?** (alleged contractual counterparty) and specific **power-boot brand spelling** all require original audio/visual confirmation. Do not normalize labels from ASR alone. The space-like transition is **observed as a scene change**, not independently proof the whole planet has moved or vanished. The **half-buried head** in Nash's platform is not identified.
