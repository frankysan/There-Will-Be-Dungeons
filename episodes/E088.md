# E088 — Shopping Day

> **The Wastes · Arc Three · source-first processed-ASR dossier, not audio-verified.** Two versions of the same roughly **2 h 50 m 31 s** recording: [Parakeet processed](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E088.%20Shopping%20Day.parakeet.processed.srt) and [Qwen Q8 / Canary CTC / Nemotron-labelled](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E088.%20Shopping%20Day.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt). All spoken timestamps are approximate; source SRTs unchanged. **This episode materially supersedes the E087 cutoff:** Hope and Stanley are **actually restored in play**, and Beau **explicitly corrects his earlier Sigil spellcasting restriction**. These are not backdated to E085–E087. *Inverse* is a separate fictional reality.

## High-priority outcomes and epistemic boundaries

- **Hope and Stanley are restored through Kidigree's mirror ritual in the moving Chapel of Laughter.** Their **consciousnesses are separately played** in GM-only scenes (Jon for Stanley, Kristen for Hope), each choosing to approach a light and leave a timeless void. Their new forms **emerge from the mirrors looking like their living selves**, while the **old bodies remain strapped to slabs** and will, according to Kidigree, **become disposable husks**. The priest **refuses to release the old corpses**, asserts they are worthless, and tells the party to leave; **the old remains are neither carried out nor independently destroyed**.
- **Hope's new form has both limbs and is completely organic, without the former mechanical arm/gun augmentations**, but retains pre-existing **scars**. Stanley appears fully embodied; both emerge practically unclothed, acquire borrowed clothing afterward. **Identity is played as Hope and Stanley**, but **Vorel remains suspicious they might be deceptive mirror people** and seeks further verification. Do not present their restoration as a simple mending of the original corpses, as all equipment/implants retrieved, or as something merely promised.
- **Kidigree's scheme is revealed to be part of the Council of Clowns' malicious trickster culture.** A mirror shows **Kanja pursued, seized and threatened by Mind Master with Crimson Moon escorts**. Mind Master identifies himself as a **board member/investor of the marionette fighting organization**, accuses Kanja of cheating for ten years and demands **100,000 gold within one week**, threatening to kill **one small inhabitant of Kanja's body per week** if unpaid. **Kidigree harvests Kanja's tears into vials through the mirror**, exploiting the suffering of an apparent **entire population** within Kanja. This **directly connects Mind Master to Crimson Moon personnel in E088**, but **does not prove the earlier E086 inn attack was commissioned by him** or that every apparent revelation is independently verified outside priest's mirror.
- Kidigree says he **considered keeping Hope and Stanley** and tricking trusting, grieving Vorel, then **chooses an inconsistent trick**—honoring the bargain. He warns Vorel that good faith with Sigil's dangerous powers could kill him. He tells Vorel not to return. **The two returnees are genuine played PCs; priest's advice and mirror contents still require source attribution.**
- **Critical GM retcon:** During the exit from the temple, Beau states **“this sort of negation field doesn't apply within Sigil”** and that his previous blanket casting restriction was **a DM mistake**. Nash then **successfully casts Fire Bolt** without purchasing a key. **E086's merchant and E087's 500gp gift remain historically played**, but **do not justify continuing to claim that Sigil requires spell keys for Nash's everyday casting** after E088. The full revised lore/limits on other planes are **not supplied**. The party had **not bought a spell key**.
- **Nothing and Squirts step aside as active PCs** following Hope/Stanley's return. Squirts remains with **Kidigree at the Chapel of Laughter** and could recur; Nothing escorts the reunited party back to the inn and is expected to pursue the **separate Pelvis/Abyss kidnapping commission**, whose original **seven-day deadline is discussed**, but **no kidnapping is played**. The party/GM discusses both as possible recurring home-base allies, **not permanently dead or retroactively erased**.
- The now-reunited party uses the **Soggy Feather** as its headquarters. **Bill reveals “the Cage”/Sigil is full of hidden portals**, which can reach many planes, including potentially the **Prime Material/Earth**; he suggests the **Clerk's Ward** may have records of portal ownership or transit. **No portal to Earth is located or crossed**, and characters' ideas of purgatory are **their speculation**. Bill is alarmed by the party's history involving the **Weir and Kintobal/Kentoval Bray (spelling unresolved)**, asserting Weirlords are terrifying Far Realm beings. **These are NPC statements**, not universal cosmology independently tested in E088.
- **An ethereal spectrometer test is played at an expensive magic emporium**: a shopkeeper adjusts and lets Vorel try a **Treverson (?) professional spectrometer**, initially showing **similar white soul outlines** for Hope, Stanley, Nash, the shopkeeper and others. Vorel notices **“Stanley Billings” and glowing Infernal script on Stanley's neck** through the device, **consistent with continuing Weir-linked petitioner markings**. The device does **not prove either returnee an impostor or establish that the Weir/Soulbroker pact ended**. The device is **not purchased**. Earlier models quoted **100gp consumer /700gp pro** at one bookshop, while the actual item **at the second shop is 1,400gp**; these **are distinct merchant offers**, not one guaranteed market rate.
- At **Large S's / Lodess (Largess?) Appleton's emporium** (names provisional), **Nash chooses a 50gp skull-shaped arcane focus** and **Vorel orders a professional sixteen-marionette BMF cabinet for 1,200gp**, delivery/installation included. **Vorel actually pays 1,250gp for both**, with Nash offering to reimburse his 50gp eventually. Later, **the machine actually arrives and is installed at the Soggy Feather with Bill's permission**; **a purchase and installation**, not an unrealized proposal. The commercial use/profits from it are **only discussed, not quantified or paid**.
- At familiar tailor **Lohar/Lorhar**, Vorel orders **three formal outfits** for **Stanley, Hope and himself**, **10gp each**. **15gp is paid immediately**, and **15gp is paid on delivery** a few days later. Nash **waits to outfit himself** until his body finishes regrowing. Exact fashion styles are illustrative; the purchase and delivery are played. **No firearm or ammunition is actually found for Hope** in the bazaar; armor/basic-item purchasing is mostly deferred to an **off-air shopping list**, so do **not invent specific weapons, armor or offscreen total spend**.
- The players discuss **approximately eight days of planned recuperation/equipment preparation** for Nash. A **couple of days** explicitly pass for the machine and outfits to arrive; the GM says they **can** time-skip eight days and invites players to plan the week's activities for the next session. **Do not claim an eight-day period was fully played out, Nash fully recovered, an Earth portal discovered or the Soggy Feather purchased by this cutoff.**
- **Prize-money account:** E087 ended with **3,800gp remaining from the 5,000gp prize** after payments to Nothing (600), Nash (500) and Bill (100). In E088 Vorel additionally **pays 1,250gp for cabinet/focus and 30gp for three outfits**, leaving **2,520gp from that prize** *if no other purchases are included*. This **arithmetic subtotal is an editorial calculation**, not an explicitly declared new canonical wallet count. Vorel speaks of **buying/renaming the inn to Golden Feather**, but **does not acquire title, sign terms or pay Bill a purchase price**.

## I. The Chapel of Laughter and the mirror room (~00:02–00:19)

**Squirts leads Vorel, Nash and Nothing** through half an hour of Sigil's apparently shifting streets to the **hidden trickster temple**, now approached from a **different alley**. The entrance is a featureless, sunken building with large stone doors. **Squirts opens them with a fart sound**. Inside the pew-lined **Chapel of Laughter**, a second priest **Carr/Karr (?)** startles awake and demonstrates his own “pull my finger” joke with fireworks erupting near his ears. He is clearly an **acolyte/cleric**, not Kidigree or a new player character.

Carr brings the group **down roughly three floors of stairs** past rats and winding corridors to a sacred chamber. Its semicircular tiered wall holds **many different mirrors**, each showing a different shape/identity within the same hooded figure—cat, orc, aristocrat, reptilian creature, elf and others. **Kidigree** sits among **hundreds of tiny vials**, attending a mirror that shows **a remote alley** rather than merely reflecting the present room. The **Hall of Reflections** is a new, physically visited temple interior, not another name for the Soggy Feather or Kanja's Colosseum.

Via the mirror the group watches **Kanja flee and be physically seized** by ninjalike escorts. **Mind Master**, the halfling Vorel defeated in E087, arrives accompanied by **two Order of the Crimson Moon-looking enforcers**. He tells Kanja that as **a Marionette Fighting Championship investor/director**, his ten years of cheated victories make him responsible for **100,000gp of repayment within one week**. If unpaid, his assassins will kill **one of Kanja's miniature internal occupants each week**. The confrontation is **shown through Kidigree's magic mirror**, not experienced by the party as direct street witnesses; nevertheless it is a concrete new **in-world mediated scene** joining Mind Master and a Crimson Moon escort. It gives evidence of **their operational relationship now**, not a signed contract proving they ordered the earlier attack on Vorel. Kanja and the occupants' fear is visible; the full economics, guild hierarchy and later consequences are unknown.

**Kidigree collects tears through the mirror** into his vials, thrilled by **“tears of a civilization.”** Vorel requests the payment promised for the prank, **Hope and Stanley's resurrection**. The priest seats him and warns that **he almost kept the bodies** because corrupt tricks against mourners are valued by the **Council of Clowns**, but instead, by being inconsistent, he has decided to **honor the exchange**. He tells Vorel not to trust Sigil's supernatural factions or priests simply because they offer bargains.

## II. Hope and Stanley return, while the bodies remain (~00:19–00:39)

**Carr retrieves the corpses**. Attendants wheel **mirrors and upright stone slabs** holding Hope and Stanley's inert original bodies into the Hall of Reflections. The bodies look **well preserved**. Kidigree draws fluid/tears from two vials on their bodies and chants a ritual. The GM **privately addresses Jon playing Stanley and Kristen playing Hope**, each in a timeless **formless void**. Both individually approach a round opening with torchlight, see their own strapped corpse beyond and **choose to come through the opening**. Their consent and return are **played, not narrated by an NPC as an expectation**.

Back with the others, the reflections show **Hope and Stanley stepping outward from inky darkness into the room**, appearing **physically indistinguishable from their prior living selves**, dressed scantily at first, then acknowledged in table discussion to be **naked**. The original bodies **do not disappear**. The returning Hope has **both natural arms/legs and no gun/metal augmentations**, though old scars persist. Stanley appears uninjured, aware and able to speak. **They are both now active returning player characters** (Kristen resumes Hope, Jon resumes Stanley). The **continuing existence of two bodies each does not establish two conscious copies**; the prior ones stay inert while the newly returned forms stand and converse.

Nash and Vorel question the separated corpses. Kidigree says the old ones will become **husk remains** disposed of in the crypt and **does not let the party take them**, providing an assurance but **not showing disposal**. He also states **he is not a god physically manifesting in Sigil**, citing the Lady of Pain's interdiction; his true status remains **his own testimony**, not independently settled. He tells Vorel he did it for **Kidigree**, not out of love for the heroes, urges them to leave and **never return**.

In leaving the dark corridors, Nash complains he cannot create light because ordinary spellcasting was suppressed for him in the previous episodes. **Beau explicitly intervenes out of character, saying the supposed Sigil-wide magic negation was his DM error**, and clarifies that it **doesn't apply within Sigil**. Nash angrily emits **an actual Fire Bolt**, momentarily illuminating the ceiling. Nothing also produces a mundane lantern. **Do not preserve an outdated claim that Nash must buy multiple Sigil school spell keys to cast after E088**, even though earlier failed casts, merchant encounter and 500gp gift really occurred in those recordings. Exactly where magical constraints do operate outside Sigil is **not systematically explained**.

**Squirts stays behind at the temple** with Kidigree and Chunks, potentially a recurring ally; **Nothing follows the five returning heroes out** and guides them back toward the Soggy Feather. **No new ritual cost or paid resurrection invoice is charged**, beyond the humiliation prank already done.

## III. The reunited five at the Soggy Feather (~00:39–01:03)

Outside, the **city physically arcs overhead**, bewildering Hope and Stanley, who ask where they are and what happened to Earth/Tietkalla. **Hope borrows ordinary clothing from Nothing**, while **Stanley wraps Vorel's red Soggy Feather cape as an improvised toga**; Hope tries unsuccessfully to tear the good cloth in half. The party returns to the inn, still crowded with fans chanting Vorel's name. **Bill provides Stanley with old lawn-bowling clothes**, and Stanley uses **Prestidigitation** to remove their smell; **no spell key is described for Stanley**. Bill/Pam provide soup and drinks to the hungry newly restored characters. Bill remarks that these were the two corpses Vorel brought on floating carts.

Hope and Stanley say the void was **timeless/without memories of passage**, more like waking from unremembered sleep than enduring known decades. Nash explains he has been **a floating heart rebuilding mouth, legs and hands** while Vorel emerged physically intact thanks, Vorel believes, to **Eye of Groxl's sacrifice**. **Hope's former technological augmentations have not returned**, while she retains older scars. They still do not know **why Vorel and Nash survived the beam differently**, or what happened to Earth/Drive/the Principle afterward.

The GM and players discuss the temporary PCs: **Squirts remains available around the church**, while **Nothing may retain a Soggy Feather association but has an accepted Pelvis/Abyss contract that must be attended to within about seven days** (the deadline is a **character/table plan**, not a successfully performed kidnapping). The party considers **a longer recuperation/shopping phase**, especially Nash's hoped-for full regrowth.

## IV. Family meeting, Earth portals and Weir history (~01:03–01:40)

The next day, **Bill and Pam arrange a family brunch** for the reunited group, and Bill advises them to share their plans. Vorel summarizes the **five days since arriving in Sigil**, the value of the Weir-bound corpses, the hidden church and the humiliating marionette championship that brought his friends back. He continues to suspect Hope/Stanley might be **false mirror people**; Nash and Stanley challenge that suspicion by invoking shared memories and their mutual sarcasm. Vorel believes **a borrowed ethereal spectrometer** might confirm Weir markings, though such a device cannot alone conclusively prove identity.

At the table, Nash recalls **Master Beltnotch** and a possible **purgatory between worlds**, but this is **his fallible remembered teaching and speculation**, not an identified location of Earth or proof they are dead. Vorel and Nash discuss the **Eye of Groxl**, the **Weir/Soulbroker marks**, the loss of almost all their equipment and plans to rebuild. Vorel talks about **purchasing the Soggy Feather** as a business/home base, but has **made no purchase**. Hope wants **guns, ammunition and armor**, and Stanley wants new clothing and arcane equipment. The GM notes that **firearms are very difficult/absent from the actual searched market**; the character's wishes are not a catalog of acquired guns.

**Bill explains Sigil as “the Cage”:** hidden **portals** throughout the city can lead to outer planes, elemental realms and potentially the **Prime Material Plane**. Portal keys/ownership/access may be controlled by different groups. He suggests the **Clerk's Ward** may have **records of portal ownership or registered travel**, potentially helpful for finding a route to the old **Earth/Wastes**. **No specific Earth portal, key or permission is located**. When the party mentions **Kintobal/Kentoval Bray**, Bill is alarmed and describes **Weirlords** as terrible Far Realm threats that even conventional gods/fiends fear. This is **Bill's planar account**, not proof that all factions share the same theory or that the Soulbroker contract was nullified in the mirror restoration.

Stanley recounts his childhood **family's murder and enslavement** because a half-elf was a rare curiosity; Nash describes the **Solar Mines' deceptive upbringing under Beltnotch** and an enduring desire to confront those responsible. Vorel calls his hatchling upbringing happy but without strong identification with biological parents. **These are PC recollections and character disclosures**, not verified ancient records. They improve group understanding but do not settle whether Nash is entirely human or how the family might escape Sigil.

## V. Shopping and the ethereal spectrometer test (~01:40–02:25)

At the bazaar the group mostly **discusses** armor, clothing and weapons. Stanley initially thinks of **separate charitable appeals**, while Vorel invites him to share party funds. **No separate completed begging transaction** occurs. **Hope cannot find the guns/ammunition she wants**, and actual armor/weapon purchases are **deferred to an off-air shopping list**. The played errand focuses first on Vorel's spectrometer.

An old **arcane bookkeeper** offers to order an ethereal spectrometer: **100gp for unreliable consumer equipment, 700gp for a professional one**, delivery uncertain. He refers them to a rival equipment dealer across the bazaar. At **Large S's / Largess Appleton's store**, the ground floor resembles a toyshop and the upper floor is a **dark, starfield-like magical showroom**. The owner, an extravagant wealthy woman (**shopkeeper/shop spellings provisional**), demonstrates a **Treverson (?) spectrometer made with a crystal from Pandemonium** and adjusts the frame to fit Vorel's face.

With the dial turned up, Vorel sees **similar luminous white soul outlines** on the returning Hope/Stanley, Nash and shopkeeper, **not evidence they lack souls or are impostors**. Inspecting **Stanley's neck**, he reads **“Stanley Billings” and glowing Infernal text**, consistent with earlier priestly petitioner/Weir markings. **The precise infernal wording is not transcribed or translated**. When he looks too closely, the merchant removes the borrowed device and quotes **1,400gp for this unit**, requiring purchase for further use. Vorel **does not purchase or rent it**, and the two merchants' professional offers **must remain distinct**. There is **no independent external identity certification**.

The merchant suggests Nash seek an advanced **Regeneration** healer for his anatomical predicament; **no such appointment or casting occurs**. She shows a jeweled magical scimitar for **6,000gp**, **not bought**. Nash purchases a **skull-shaped arcane focus priced 50gp**, and Vorel agrees to buy a **professional BMF cabinet with 16 fighters for 1,200gp**, installed at the **Soggy Feather**; Nash says he will repay his share later. **Vorel pays the entire 1,250gp at the register**. A professional game machine is **actually delivered and installed a couple of days later**, with Bill's consent and the idea that the inn could profit, though no sales revenue or student lessons are yet demonstrated.

The wealthy shopkeeper expresses **openly classist hostility** toward poor people in Sigil's Hive, saying she'd expel them through a portal to the Abyss if she ruled. **This is her ugly personal opinion**, not a law, policy enacted by the Lady of Pain or the party's endorsement. The group's discomfort contributes to departure. Stanley **declines a horn/instrument focus** and does not recover his lost **megaphone/Diplomacy sword**, while Hope finds **no firearms** here.

## VI. Fashion, delivery and a possible eight-day rest (~02:25–02:50)

Although directed east toward the **Clerk's Ward** and possible portal records, the group **does not travel there yet**. Vorel calls a huddle and suggests that to gather information in wealthy Sigil, they should **look like rich, respectable customers**. At the orc tailor **Lohar/Lorhar**, who previously made Vorel's Soggy Feather cape, he orders **three sophisticated outfits**: **Stanley** in a blue/silver/white or purple-accented **Witherford/Weatherford (?)** style with scarf and square hat; **Hope** in a daring **black-and-gold Manchester (?)** style with large bag and jewelry preferences; and **Vorel** in a fine **tuxedo, monocle and cufflinks** for his proposed innkeeper role. **The tailor refuses to outfit Nash until his changing body settles**. These are choices in tailoring, not evidence the PCs have acquired full armor or recovered past weapons.

Three suits at **10gp each** mean **30gp total**. **Vorel actually pays 15gp down**, measurements are taken, and **the remaining 15gp is paid upon delivery** several days later. The completed garments are **received** within the episode's closing montage. The **professional BMF machine is also installed in the Soggy Feather** with Bill's approval, and the 16 fighters are playable.

**Passage of time:** The GM narrates **one or two days passing** to fulfill orders, then discusses a potential **eight-day convalescence** for Nash so he can rebuild into his old form. The group is generally amenable to taking eight days, but the GM primarily assigns **next-session/off-air planning** of individual activities, basic equipment lists and potentially future expeditions. **Do not describe a completed eight-day full Nash regrowth, a finalized inn acquisition, future key/portal discovery, weapon refit or Nothing's performed kidnapping** at this cutoff.
