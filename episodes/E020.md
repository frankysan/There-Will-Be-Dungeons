## Provenance and interpretive safeguards

- **Tylander's journal is physically recovered**, and the GM explicitly identifies it by **his name written in it** (~01:06–01:07). Its **claims** about a voice, revenge, family names and seducing/convincing a woman do **not** prove the supernatural history. A **male skeleton in the same room is not Tylander**, merely because his journal lies there; it remains unidentified.
- **His lost family:** Journal names are **personal written first-person claims**, separate from the later **E021** Tylander speech explaining the downfall of **Pristine**. Retain variants `Mariosa`, `Daisy`, `Tobyl/Tobel`, `Lily` and their roles as journal-leads until audio/original prop text can be checked.
- **Two lever controls:** Their observed coordinated use resolves the E019 cliffhanger. Never assign the cave-in to a mythical giant that PCs speculate about; the source demonstrates a **mechanical rockfall**.
- **Tickles:** E019 concludes with him buried and unresponsive; E020 **confirms death** with dented helmet and absent reaction. Treat his death as **episode-verified-by-GM-narration within unverified ASR**, not listener-audio-confirmed.
- **Last glyph:** The E020 cliffhanger is **walking into impenetrable darkness**, not having crossed it or encountered a later speaking door. E021 contains those events.
- **Rules talk:** The correction about fiery aura and an object is an **out-of-game table ruling**, not an official rulebook citation.
