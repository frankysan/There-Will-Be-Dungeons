**Played cutoff:** At Elhandar's central audience chamber, **Stanley wins a negotiated accord with the summoned, ghost-form Diontalus**. Diontalus **agrees to return to his own Feywild court, await Stanley and help the party**, but threatens every elf on the station if Stanley fails to come or dies first. **No physical departure or delivered assistance is shown before the cut**. Earlier, **Malhya/Fishbird explicitly cured Stanley's and Vorel's Diontalus-inflicted debuffs**. Nash's family and Lyrielle have **actually left Villa Or'Nath and reached the central reception**, remaining outside the four companions' audience. **They have not left Elhandar**. Emenil remains alive and responsive beneath the Harbor lake; the noble speeder is left at the villa; the natural dragon heart remains buried and Ruby unbuilt.

## Fish flakes and the first rite (~00:02:46–00:28)

After the opening E123 recap, **all four companions join Emenil in the Harbor lake**, undressing before the rite. Vorel considers burning his clothing but does not. Emenil distributes fragile **brown/orange fish flakes** from a small gold jeweled locket. Stanley glimpses a male tiefling portrait, **without an explicit identification at that moment**. They dissolve the flakes on their tongues, swallow and hold hands beneath the water. **No identification as Pixie Blush, a poison or an ordinary narcotic is established**.

Each discovers that **breathing water works**. They descend to the lake bottom; Emenil releases the remaining flakes, chants and leads a **shared blackout/transition into apparently endless water**, with no visible floor. They remain together. This is a played experience, **not yet a verified planar map or a dream diagnosis**.

Emenil calls **Malhya, “great Shepherd of the tides.”** Fish scatter and she becomes afraid. She admits **she has never performed this rite**. Her late husband **Lelu / Leilu / Lelou / Lilu (?)**, a reverend of the Fishbird church, allegedly died under Tietkalla's vampire rulers after refusing to abandon his faith. She says he gave her the flakes on his deathbed and promised help when she needed it. She did not then share his beliefs. **This is her testimony about a past death**, not a new played killing, a fixed date or a proven identity for the portrait.

## Vorel's introduction and the visitors (~00:28–00:50)

**~00:28:04–00:28:38:** Vorel formally introduces himself and says he will **skip his earlier title list**. The spoken ending includes **Keeper of Stories**, **Butcher of the Barosaurus / “barrow saurus” (?)**, and **Deceiver of the Cheater Diontalus / “Cheetah, Dian Talos” (?)**. These are **self-recited titles**, not new grants, proof of defeating Diontalus or permission to reconstruct the omitted list. [Source-indexed title register](../world/characters/vorel-titles.md).

Vorel's natural-20 introduction is followed by enormous eyes and a creature Emenil identifies as a **kraken**. She tries to flee and helps Stanley; Hope and Vorel hold position. The kraken **turns and swims away without a fight**. The GM's vast size comparisons are descriptive analogies, not measured planetary dimensions or a Vorel victory.

A whale-sized **winged, rainbow-scaled fish** arrives, laughs and creates a **disc of solid water, explicitly not ice**, on which all five can stand. It answers to **Malhya/Malhaya/Fishbird**. The manifestation and its actions are witnessed; its divine identity and theological explanations remain **speaker-attributed**. It says the dragons' heat/blight prevents Earth's waters from returning and that the party must first cool the world or provide the means to do so. **No key, blueprint, restored ocean or changed planetary climate is delivered**.

## Banishment reversed; the drain actually cured (~00:50–01:10)

Stanley angrily invokes his sacrifice. **Malhya makes him disappear (~00:50:44)**. Nash demands his friend's return and **also disappears (~00:51:18)**. Each experiences isolated blackness, with no sense of elapsed time or awareness of the other's presence. **This is not an adjudicated death or resurrection**. Vorel imagines the Golden Sands air conditioner and a future ice/lightning aura; neither becomes a new acquired power or completed cooling project.

After Vorel intercedes and Malhya cites Emenil's faith, **both companions reappear (~00:53:55–00:54:25)**. Stanley and Nash did **not hear the intervening conversation**. Emenil explains their second chance; Stanley apologizes. The earlier phrase **“permanent banishment”** is the creature's statement, subsequently rescinded in play.

Emenil petitions for the injured companions. Malhya **takes a blue, fish-scaled version of Vorel's shape**, cannot reproduce his chest Eye, and requests permission to touch it. Vorel senses **the Eye of Groxl's anger and desire to kill the visitor**, tempered by an infinitesimal chance of success. He permits the touch. Malhya recognizes the being and calls it feared among gods and dangerous. **`Grojil / Gergil / Grosjean` remain ASR readings of Groxl, not additional artifacts**.

At **~01:01:47**, the warning moves from **“the one he slew”** to **“he is the very unmaker”**. **The pronoun's antecedent is unresolved**; do not identify an unnamed slain god, independently certify a cosmology or flatten the sentence into a certain Groxl title. Malhya also says the gods do not know the outcome.

**~01:02–01:04:06: actual restoration.** Malhya draws blue ethereal strands out of Vorel; the GM expressly says **he is cured of the debuffs Diontalus placed on him**. Stanley steps forward and receives **the same treatment**, and both are declared healed. **E123's persistent scythe-inflicted ability drain is resolved here**, rather than by that episode's long rest. The scene does not provide a newly audited full character-stat block.

Malhya says an **engineer of impossible works** is needed for the dragon's device. The engineer's present location is allegedly **hidden from the gods somewhere in the Prime Material**, last seen near a world rendered **`faerun` / `Favron` (?)**. It directs Stanley to **the Elhandari leader and royal court**. **No engineer name, confirmed location, construction or completed Ruby is supplied**. Malhya swims away, and a shared transition returns the five to the Harbor lake bottom.

## From the Harbor to the central chamber (~01:10–01:48)

The four emerge and dress. **Emenil remains underwater** but answers Stanley's **Message**, asking for time and saying she will be fine. **No death, romantic resolution or abandonment of her faith is shown**. Nobieri discusses the group's account, **gives Hope a cellstone**, and says the refugees can fight for themselves beside their queen. They **stay at the Harbor**; his E123 mushroom investigation is not performed here.

Hope pilots the **noble speeder to Villa Or'Nath**, with Stanley and Vorel aboard and Nash towed as before. Angelashi greets Nash affectionately, and he checks the four children. Stanley's Insight check does not make him suspect her behavior, **without proving a magical identity test**. The household packs; Lyrielle agrees to accompany them after **a limited, confidential warning about danger**, not a complete Diontalus briefing. Stanley **retrieves his father's key**, entered in inventory as **Villa Or'Nath cottage study key**; he assumes removing it closes the study doorway, without a separately described closure test.

**Roster conflict (~01:26–01:28; repeated at the lift/reception):** Both exports call the accompanying small companions **“Bok Bok and Squirts.”** **E111 explicitly disintegrated Squirts, and E115 performed her memorial; Chunks was the living child.** No resurrection occurs here. Preserve this as **an unresolved roster/name/ASR conflict**; do not silently revive Squirts or replace the word with Chunks. The outro's “baby Chunks” credit is not an in-scene identity correction. Omnom is expressly remembered as dead.

The group **leaves the speeder at the villa**, walks **about four story-hours** to the Rainbow Road terminal and **actually travels to the central core**, rendered **Cadulet/Cadulate/Cadgilet (?)**. Angelashi has made paired front/back carriers adaptable to object forms. **Nash carries Nash Junior and Boot as babies; Angelashi takes Fork and Pipe in their namesake forms**. At **~01:32:06–01:32:25**, the table explicitly retains the bonus-show decision: **Nash Junior is female and Nash's daughter**. This confirms the later individual assignment at E124; it **does not erase E114's two-girls/two-boys birth description or settle the other children's mapping**. **This differs from E115 ~01:51:19–01:51:35, which explicitly names the baby-form boy Nash Junior** (primary cues 1635–1639; secondary 2610–2624). Lyrielle guides the household through the station and calms Vorel's elevator anxiety with **Prestidigitation harp music**. The rapid lift causes vomiting; Stanley and Lyrielle clean it magically. The four enter the High Elf's chamber, while **Lyrielle, Angelashi, the children and accompanying small companions remain in reception**. Proposed secure chambers are later declined in favor of keeping them close. These household members **do not hear the private audience merely because they traveled there**.

## The High Elf's history and the summons (~01:48–02:15)

The High Elf is named approximately **Amanthalodar / Amantha Lodar / Amenthalodar (?)**; a promised Slack spelling is **not a handout examined in this research**. Stanley tells him about Diontalus, the dead mages and the group's intent to leave. The leader recognizes the danger and reports that council mages are missing, calling their grand title unofficial. **Their deaths are Stanley's report to him**, not a newly witnessed attack or proof every council member was possessed.

**The High Elf's testimony:** Elhandari elves once encouraged human activists against demon-influenced humans, then withdrew support; those activists found Diontalus, **archfey of carnage**, in the Feywild. Diontalus subsequently attacked demons, collaborators and Elhandar, allegedly killing thousands. Elves and humans imprisoned him together until he could be returned to the royal court. The leader says archfey are creation's true gods, must not be harmed, and that earthly decay has driven Diontalus toward madness. **These are historical, theological and causal claims**, not newly played history or independently proven universal rules. The claim that Stanley accidentally opened the prison is the High Elf's interpretation of Stanley's account.

The party consents to **a social encounter**, despite doubts. All four eat berries offered to strengthen resolve; **no separately quantified lasting buff is established**. The High Elf **actually summons a ghost-form Diontalus (~02:06:28–02:07:46)**. This is a witnessed appearance, **not proof that no other body or host exists**. Stanley appeals to home, purpose, forgiveness and Diontalus's interest in him, winning successive persuasion contests. No weapon duel or combat death replaces the negotiation.

## Hope's private refusal and Stanley's accord (~02:15–02:25:34)

**~02:15:13–02:17:52: Hope-only scene.** Nash's, Stanley's and Vorel's players remove their headphones. The hooded **Voice** orders Hope to **kill Diontalus and take his power**; **she refuses twice**. It nevertheless gives her a supposed archfey **undoing phrase**, rendered **`care laundry me he jaju my heistita` / `Care, laundry, meji, jajul, my histita` (?)**. **The Voice claims** every archfey has such a phrase. **Hope does not say it to Diontalus, kill him, absorb his essence or test that rule**. The other companions do not automatically know this conversation. This is **not a Nash spellcasting rhyme**.

Diontalus calls Stanley his **anchor to normalcy** and asks him to return to the Feywild as his chosen, speak to his father and rule beside him. Stanley initially offers help but asks to complete his obligations first. Diontalus says that accompanying him would mean **not returning**. The High Elf's assurances about archfey keeping their word remain **his testimony**, not immunity from future harm. At **~02:21:29**, he **privately warns only Stanley** that delay increases the chance Diontalus changes his mind.

Stanley cites the **Weir's claim on his soul** and his friends' goals. A final **Persuasion 29 (~02:23:50)** wins different terms: Diontalus says he will **return now to his own Feywild stead/court, not the royal court, and wait for Stanley**. He threatens to return and destroy Elhandar's elves if Stanley does not come or dies beforehand. **Stanley accepts the accord**, then asks him to help against the Principle. **Diontalus answers “yes” (~02:25:31)** and the GM cuts the scene, congratulating Stanley's social victory.

**The negotiated commitment is played; its future fulfillment is not.** There is no depicted departure, royal judgment, permanent Stanley relocation, soul-contract discharge, supplied anti-Principle aid or removal of the threat from every possible host. The closing schedule discussion, toilet-world joke and promotions through **02:28:52** are **out of game**.
