## Continuity and confidence boundaries

- **Actual events versus E039 intentions:** Monsoon's suggested path becomes a live **nighttime Teet/Guzzle search and fight**, not an immediate or successful keep entrance. The group begins this episode inside Twenty-Six, **not inside the Teet**.
- **Stanley and Dion:** The apparent guard/Guzzle Gang member is **Stanley's body under Diontalus's control/role and magical disguise**; allies do not automatically recognize him. No proof he has defected to either faction.
- **Hope's mobility and arm:** Her **mechanical left leg is slow** and Vorel carries her; her fitted arm and firearm remain in action. Do not treat the fresh prosthetics as an instantaneous full return to normal movement.
- **Two Phillips:** The guards' names, individual nicknames and outcomes are comic dialogue in an ASR track; the party does not examine their bodies. Their disappearance in the magical zone should not be converted into independently confirmed deaths.
- **Fight cutoff:** E040's battle reaches a temporary local lull, **but the keep's search and pursuit remain active**. E041 begins with the group still under spotlights near the pit, not after a safe rest.
