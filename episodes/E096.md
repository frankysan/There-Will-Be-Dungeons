# E096 — You hold the soup

> **The Wastes · Arc Four · processed-ASR first-pass dossier; not audio-verified.** Source texts: [Parakeet processed SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E096.%20You%20hold%20the%20soup.parakeet.processed.srt) and [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E096.%20You%20hold%20the%20soup.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt), both ending **~02:44:05** including outro. [Comparison](../reports/E096_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). E095 ended with **Vorel/Gregor preparing broth, Stanley/Nash looking for Walnut and Hope still in the underground library**, while a plague ravaged White Tower, the separate stolen pendant remained in Stanley's bag and the Fist chest was unopened.

## At the actual E096 cutoff

- **The cure succeeds and the remaining sick in White Tower are treated.** Gregor and Vorel **finish the chicken broth**, and the three upstairs **actually locate Walnut**, a nervous young apprentice of Euphemius. **Walnut prepares a phonetic transcription of the Celestial words** from Hordweld's **“shale disease”** journal page for **Stanley** to recite; the spell sequence requires **cradling/rocking the patient, chanting, and pouring broth onto their face**, rather than having them drink it. **Stanley actually cures critically ill Knight Commander Alentris/Elentris Vayne (?)**: she **expels a black cloud from her lungs which flashes blue-white and dissipates**. The commander **explicitly feels freed of the sickness**. **She then uses the ritual to cure Stanley**, also with an expelled cloud. **Walnut successfully cures Vorel and Nash** (the latter only after broth is added to the chant), likewise expelling and dissolving clouds. Cure is **played and visible**, unlike E095's incomplete rocking.
- **The party and Walnut spread the cure across the tower, with others assisting.** A montage/GM time skip shows **Walnut, Nash and Vorel continuing the treatment**, later **Stanley and Alentris** assisting; after several further hours the GM explicitly calls **the remaining White Tower residents cured/illness eradicated**. **At least some defenders had died beforehand**, with the commander later saying **a dozen knights lost** (E095's four was an earlier overnight report, not the final death count). **Do not say the dead are resurrected**, the siege ended, the entire Outlands is cured, or the plague's original agent was conclusively identified. **It is safe to say the acute outbreak within White Tower was cured by cutoff**. The **black clouds still inside the lower library/stairway after the cure are not individually proved dispelled**.
- **Stanley and Knight Commander Alentris Vayne develop a consensual intimate/romantic relationship** following her first recovery and Stanley's own cure. She **kisses him**, they retire privately and later clearly allude to **sex**. They discuss serious trust, faith, the Fist and an alternative escape route. This is **played**, not April-bonus conjecture or a mere unrealized attraction. **Neither marriage nor a permanent bond is established**.
- **Captain Waltz (provisional exact spelling) actually contacts Stanley through the sending stone**, saying he has been **commissioned by the same interested party behind the first ship** to replace lost **Captain Meart** and her damaged Traveler's Twin. **Waltz proposes to launch that evening and arrive near White Tower at noon the next day**, instructing a quick pickup of the “package,” i.e. **Zorander's Fist**. **The ship has not arrived at E096 cutoff** and its name/model, crew, survivability and honest intentions are **unknown**. Do not overwrite E094's **Meart alive with ~week of Traveler's Twin repairs**: Waltz only **claims the original ship went missing** from his information, not that Meart died.
- **Alentris and Stanley negotiate using this replacement ship for the paladins' own purpose**: take the sealed relic and an escort aboard, **seize the transport if needed**, and travel **toward Mount Celestia rather than return Fist to Tobacho**. This is a **conditional plan agreed in conversation, not an executed heist/hijack**. The commander intends **to take the Fist with them**, but to **give its separate key pendant to Walnut and send him in another direction**. **Neither the physical separation/hand-off to Walnut, departure nor Fist transfer happens in E096**. The key remains last witnessed with Stanley; the chest stays closed under the order's control.
- **Alentris offers a possible route home via the bridge to Mount Celestia**, where an **attendant may judge whether to assist the four in returning to Earth**; she can **vouch for them**, but **expressly cannot promise a result**. The formerly offered Tobacho bargain was conditional and remains unfulfilled. Stanley **agrees personally to help and to promise before Euphemius that the Fist will be returned to the god/kept safe**, but says **he must consult his three companions rather than bind them without consent**. **A formal oath before the full group has not yet been performed**.
- **Hope continues the lower-library investigation alone for much of the episode.** She looks for more disease accounts, deities and demon names, then **finds a gated restricted-tome section** with a promising **compendium** potentially related to demon identification. She **does not open the gate, obtain the compendium, identify a hidden demon, shoot the mechanism or find a legal exemption**. A law-book named **“Executus”** gives an extreme option of **renouncing other worship, stripping possessions and begging Euphemius for pardon to escape a death sentence**, but **Hope doesn't perform this**. **She safely crosses remaining black mist to return upstairs**, proving her actual rejoining is **E096**, not E095.
- **The unseen inner voice privately accuses Hope of ruining its plan by saving the infected defenders**, tells her **they could have met if she had let everyone die**, and warns **the magical lawful-good protection permitting her presence in White Tower will not last forever**. **Hope explicitly refuses to sacrifice her friends**. This supports a malicious intelligence and connection to the plague plot, but **does not establish the speaker's identity, location, full transmission mechanism or guaranteed future ejection**. **The voice's statement is an interested antagonist's claim**, not proof it is a particular demon or that the tower wards already have failed permanently.
- **Alentris and the defenders publicly thank Hope**, kneeling before her when she reappears, acknowledging that **her discovery of the journal enabled the rescue**. **Hope remains wary**, but **no subsequent arrest/trial occurs in E096**. In a later private party discussion she **finally tells Stanley, Nash and Vorel of the inner voice**, its instruction to allow the infected to die, the possible **evil presence still within White Tower**, and the **time-limited lawful-good alignment effect**. This **changes party knowledge**, but isn't yet a successful exorcism or demon sighting.
- **Vorel/Nash/Hope discuss going to the library after hours to identify the suspected demon.** They propose that **shrinking a character** might pass through the gate, but **do not cast such a spell**. **They visit Walnut in his room** and learn **the restricted books are barred by a lever, not a key**, and ordinarily require **permission from the High Cleric or Knight Commander**. After confusing/pressuring Walnut they **actually persuade him to accompany them down to the library**; **at episode cutoff the four—Hope, Nash, Vorel and Walnut—start descending**. **No gate opening, restricted-book retrieval, summoned demon, defeat, Fist custody change, or Earth portal** yet.
- **Stanley is separate from that library party** after a late private conversation with the other three and Alentris. His precise offscreen activity/romantic continuation at final second isn't documented; do not insert him with Walnut's descending group.

## Scene-by-scene record

### 1. Broth, phonetic words and the Knight Commander (~00:02–00:29)

Gregor and Vorel finish cooking broth; **about one hour** of simmering passes. By then **Stanley and Nash have found Walnut** in the main dining area. The GM initially places Hope at the table, **then later explicitly corrects the position: Hope is still searching the lower library during these early cures and returns upstairs later in E096**. Walnut is uncertain that he can channel Euphemius directly, so **he prepares a phonetic Celestial transcription for Stanley** (not a claim Stanley suddenly knows Celestial).

They choose **critically ill Alentris** as first patient. The commander is extremely weak, feverish and unable to hold herself up. **Stanley cradles her and chants from Walnut's guide; Vorel pours chicken broth across her face/nose** in accordance with the diagram after the characters correct an assumption the broth should be drunk. **A black cloud leaves her mouth, glows blue-white, dissipates**, and she says **she feels much better**. She kisses Stanley without his rejecting the gesture. Alentris then **cradles and chants over Stanley**; **the broth is similarly poured**, his own black cloud expelled and destroyed; **Stanley cured**. The two have a **clearly consensual romantic and sexual encounter in her room** while their companions leave with Walnut and soup. The player and GM treat it as actually happening, not a player imagining a future possibility.

**Walnut treats Vorel** outside, initially afraid he will get the incantation wrong. **Broth administered to Vorel produces the same black-cloud/electric dissipation and GM confirms he is cured**. Then **Walnut treats Nash**, with an almost-failed chant-only moment before Vorel **actually pours broth**, causing a confirming **black-cloud exhalation and cure**. This is **the previously uncertain ritual verified in play**; the page's earlier **“shale” versus “Incubatrix” name mismatch persists as textual evidence**, but the ritual visibly works on the current disease.

### 2. Recovery becomes a tower-wide mission (~00:29–01:13)

Walnut is initially frightened to repeat his success, worried he might fail Nash or other patients. **Nash bolsters his confidence**, and Vorel keeps the big pot of broth moving. The group begins **visiting each sick defender and performing the rite**. When Nash attempts to rouse Stanley from the commander's quarters to speed the treatment, Stanley **refuses to interrupt the intimate encounter**, and **Nash takes responsibility for administering the words himself despite pronunciation anxiety**. Nash **visibly cures one guard** by chanting and Vorel supplying soup. **The healing effort proceeds without waiting for Stanley**.

The GM then **compresses roughly eight hours** of walking floors, including efforts to help reluctant victims, not eight hours of individually transcribed cures. Walnut gains conviction that **Euphemius may have accepted his calling**; he starts imagining further training at the **House of Answers** in Sigil. This is **a future aspiration**, not a played appointment/promotion.

### 3. Hope's library research and new coercive warning (~01:13–01:29)

Hope is still in the **underground library early in that elapsed period**, searching after the party has departed. Books mostly document **religious observance, legal theory and battles**, not another plague cure. She wonders whether the **Principle**, **Kitobalpraith** and other powerful figures appear in a **hierarchy of deities**, but **fails to find a general reference**; **no confirmation they are gods** emerges.

The **same deep mental voice** returns as light seems to dim and redden around her. It says **she had everything** if she had **simply let the tower die and waited for the Fist**. **Hope refuses because the victims include her friends**. It warns **the spell that lets her pass as good within White Tower is temporary**. The voice offers no identity and no resolved next action.

Hope searches **locked/restricted tomes** that could identify demons and finds **a barred bookshelf/gate** with a visible **compendium**. Her attempt to locate a mechanism **fails**. She reads **“Executus”**, a book on Euphemian judgment that offers only **conversion, public supplication and abandonment of other worship** as a way of escaping a death sentence; **Hope does not carry it out**. She later **crosses the mist on the staircase unharmed and returns to the upper tower**, actually rejoining the others by the later curing procession.

### 4. Vayne, a substitute airship and the destination dispute (~01:29–02:07)

After their intimate time **Alentris/Elentris Vayne** voices renewed concern about **someone inserting the curse virus despite the ward**. **Stanley successfully argues (Persuasion 31)** that **Hope deserves recognition for finding the cure**, regardless of the earlier suspicious exposure. Alentris concedes the tower is **unsafe for the Fist to remain** and proposes to move it.

Stanley checks a **new message on the sending stone**. A man calling himself **Captain Waltz** says their **mutual employer is sending a second airship** because the first is missing, and expects to reach the tower **at noon tomorrow**, after launching tonight. **The recorded promise is not real-time confirmation of a future arrival**. Its sender wants the “package”; Stanley is transparent with Alentris that the employer is **Tobacho Fleur**. Alentris and Stanley agree on a **proposed hijack**, redirecting the ship to **Mount Celestia** with the Fist and the paladins. They discuss scroll/Reverse Gravity carrying capacity, **without actually casting spells or boarding**. Alentris intends to **send the pendant key away with Walnut**, separate from the Fist, but **does not physically transfer it during this private planning**.

For a possible passage back to Earth she says **the divine bridge/attendant at Mount Celestia may judge them and return them**, but **cannot guarantee it**. Stanley agrees to **promise to do the right thing** before Euphemius, provided he can **consult his friends** and not swear on their behalf. Alentris later dons armor and joins the remaining treatment. At that time about **one third of the tower is said to have been cured**, and the GM later compresses **the last several hours of successful work**. The commander is referred to as **Alentris Vayne (?)**, a stronger full-name basis than E094/E095's surname-unknown identity.

### 5. The recovered tower, and a different mission to the library (~02:01–02:39)

Hope returns and cautiously observes the treatment. **Alentris and the gathered order kneel to thank her**, recognizing her part in finding the cure. The group then **finishes treating the remaining sick**, with the GM explicitly saying the disease has been eradicated in the tower. This **does not resurrect those who died**; Alentris earlier gives a higher **roughly dozen-knight casualty statement**. The commander calls for **a meeting at dawn** to choose next actions.

Without Walnut listening, **Stanley tells the other three** about the **replacement airship** and asks whether they will join his plan **to deliver Fist to Mount Celestia**, rather than to Tobacho, hoping that divine judgment may offer a way home. He **apologizes to Vorel for unilaterally redirecting their mission in E094**, although they **do not reach a single clearly binding unanimous decision**. **Hope now tells the companions about the voice**, how it wanted them all to die and is tied to delivering the Fist, and the spell's impermanence. **Vorel and Nash favor finding the demon in the tower, potentially killing it, then working out Fist extraction**; neither demon's name, power nor location is verified.

Hope remembers **the restricted demon compendium in the library**. Nash might shrink someone to pass the bars; they **only discuss** casting **Reduce**, they do **not do it yet**. Before meeting the Knight Commander at dawn, **Vorel, Nash and Hope go to Walnut's room** and ask about the gate. Walnut explains **it is controlled by a lever and requires formal permission from a high cleric or Knight Commander**. They **do not have such permission in evidence**, but through persuasion and deliberate confusion **get Walnut to accompany them**. **The last played location is those four descending toward the library**, no compendium opened, no demon evoked or killed. **Stanley is not seen joining them**; his latest firm commitment was to speak with Alentris and his friends about a possible Mt. Celestia mission.
