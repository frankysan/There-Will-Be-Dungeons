## Source-first findings

1. **Scott abbreviated a larger Solar Mines account (~00:43–02:08).** He says the story he told “today” was a small, improvised version of a much larger account he would have needed notes to tell fully. The [E117 public retelling](E117.md) supplies a content association. **No missing history is actually supplied here**; an unpublished fuller version cannot fill the gaps in Nash's memories or validate the father's E118 journal. Scott contrasts this with Nash's foolish acts, including lethal consequences, which he does not regret as a player. These are retrospective remarks, not new deaths or newly performed spell phrases.
2. **Jon distinguishes player regret from Stanley's characterization (~02:15–05:39).** He recalls letting frustration during a **black-pudding fight** prompt a choice that nearly killed Hope, distinguishing this from intentional roleplay. The exact fight/action is not newly identified by episode here. Beau teases him about missed romantic opportunities near Talia and Lady Salt Bubble; Jon denies a hookup and describes occasionally wishing he had made a more conventional, disreputable bard. **No new romantic encounter is established.** Asked about the addict recognized **`brint` / `Brent`**, Jon describes losing sleep over the killing, whereas playing **Diontalus's cruelty** did not affect him the same way. This is **performer reflection on different character agency**, not proof Stanley killed Diontalus or a reversal of [E059's extraction into a crystal](E059.md). The victim's exact name/substance and episode mapping remain open.
3. **Squirts's inspiration went unused (~05:40–07:19).** Kyle jokes that using “lion's den” bothered him because he doubted Vorel would know lions; Beau's tentative reply does not settle Wastes zoology. Kristen says she generally accepts events as the story, then reveals **Squirts had inspiration and could have rerolled**, but she realized after the story had moved on and did not request a retroactive change. **No reroll, successful save, survival or resurrection occurs in this bonus.** [E111's disintegration](E111.md) remains the played outcome; a different roll's result and mechanical eligibility were not tested here. The intervening word recognized **`Flynn`** is uncertain and should not become another character.
4. **Lyrielle combines princess archetypes and books (~07:19–09:08; 34:09–34:40).** Kristen had wanted a **Disney-princess-style character** and developed the concept over a week as **a wizard who reads many books**; whether the princess stories were actually lived is part of the concept's ambiguity. Later Kristen attributes the name to a book series, recognized **`Sabriel / liriel`** in Parakeet and **`Sabriel, Lyriel, and Horison`** in Qwen, and to **Ariel / The Little Mermaid**. **Literary orthography, the character's full name, class sheet and the truth of her mermaid memories are not verified by this discussion.** Existing display **Lyrielle** remains provisional; a listener's use of a similar name supplies no creator spelling authority.
