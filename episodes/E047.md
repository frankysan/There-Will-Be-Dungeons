## Key evidence boundaries

- **E046 book → E047 rescue:** The moving book genuinely contains/mediates the escape of a **tiefling person** by E047; it is not simply inert literature, nor proof the captive is friendly or truthful.
- **The historical scroll:** Only **“M”** addressee and **“N”** signer, and a promise to finish a protective book, are public in this processed SRT; **do not fill in full names or conflate with E045 private papyrus**.
- **The house lineages book:** Hope receives information **privately**. Later statements about the Akmenos family can be cited to E048 *as testimony* but **must not be falsely quoted as an E047 public reading**.
- **Helmet:** Vorel wears **+1 resilience/protection helmet fashioned from a large lizardfolk skull**. Avoid assuming the skull originally belonged to the deity named in later oral/fan sources.
- **No prison rescue yet:** The party reaches a shaft/library/office and retrieves an old tiefling by E047's end. **Tedna and Bok Bok are not released until E048**.
- **Spelling:** **Diontalus** creator-approved; `Tietkalla` a contextually strong cast-written candidate; **Nobiery/Nobieri** and alleged `Nobiery` family need name-spelling validation from E048 and retrospective recaps.
