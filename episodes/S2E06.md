## Continuity, inventory and epistemic guardrails

**The Tungsten evidence has advanced:** The **signed employment agreement and desk ledger** corroborate he historically worked here, beyond S2E05's uncertain innkeeper recollection. Stanley holds the agreement; key from Tungsten's desk opens **box 724**, and Hope opens it to obtain a **rolled parchment with coordinates**. Exact destination, whether Tungsten is alive, Gluptwarp availability and next journey remain open. The **Rubistic Triangulosphere remains unfinished on Mars**.

**Different attribute effects:** Nash's baseline **INT 3 / DEX 3** after S2E05 survives the episode, even though adult dragon form uses its own **INT 14**. Stanley's newly **stolen WIS 3** is not repaired by killing the ooze or healing nine HP. No permanent-versus-curable ruling is established for any of these scores.

**Custody and combat:** The initial **13,365** estimate cannot be booked as net treasure: GM revises to **12,968 gold-equivalent**, and coins are dropped, shares discussed, not unambiguously audited. Three defeated raiders are accepted as crew; remaining attackers are not allies. Badass T was captive, **not fighting alongside orcs**; Hope actually frees him. Vorel's line of self-introduction gives **no newly conferred title**. Nash's *Reduce* rhyme **reuses NSP-013**, rather than creating a duplicate; the *Light* and dragon transformation do not yield separate new spoken rhymes.

**Listening priorities:** ~00:11 flea-rhyme (already indexed), ~00:35–01:08 Tungsten contract and three clue words, ~01:08–01:23 Stanley's WIS drop, ~01:40–01:56 deposit-box **724**, coordinates and two loot totals, ~02:00–02:44 Stink Eye/Green Vale/casualties/new crew, ~02:44–end the still-unhealed stats.
