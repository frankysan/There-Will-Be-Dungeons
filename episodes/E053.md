## Source and inventory boundaries

- **Two distinct surviving structures:** The **upper flying cocoon separated and departed E053**; the **lower carcass landed, contracted and rotted**. This supports the party's escape but **does not prove extinction of the entire froghemoth**.
- **Artifact identity resolved by archive owner (2026-10-21):** **Eye of Groxl** is the **canonical spelling for this single item**. E053's `Eye of Grojil`, E054's `Eye of Groxel`, and cast-written **The Eye of Groxl** refer to **that same artifact**, not three separate items. Retain `Grojil` and `Groxel` as source/search aliases. **This item confirmation alone does not settle whether any separately described deity or historical warrior has the same name/identity.** [Decision D-028](../project/DECISIONS.md).
- **Separate new abilities:** **Eye in chest = sentient artifact**. **Prismatic scale-change = separate physical alteration** connected by GM to Vorel's contact with froghemoth/pixie-blush magic.
- **Robe stock:** Rowboat patch **used E053**, ladder **used E048**, steel mirror **used E051**. **Nash's small third hand remains**.
- **Angelashi missing** at E053 cutoff; no proof she was in the internal roper area. Her fate changes in E054.
- **Hoard**: ~100,000 gold is an approximate GM count, not audited character inventory; most is **reburied** and no **gold has yet been scattered above the town** by E053 close.
