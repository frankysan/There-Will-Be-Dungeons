## Continuity and provenance

- **The sphere's change is an E042 event:** Nash takes a whole **unidentified froghemoth-adjacent sphere in E041**. **E042** cuts its membrane and preserves only its **black center**. Do not treat it as a live verified egg or leave a whole intact sphere in inventory after this scene.
- **Not every cave beast is the same:** **Froghemoth in the open pit E041**, **tentacled pale worm-like cavern organism E042**, and **two black puddings E042** are distinct encounters. The singing “king” remains an unseen voice.
- **Infernal writing is partially inaccessible:** Hope can read it but is given a **private message** by the GM. The ASR's brief spoken phrase does **not** establish the complete inscription; do not supply a fabricated translation.
- **Inventory and rest:** This is a **short rest** and a newly recovered cache, **not a long rest / restored spell slots**. The identity of the wooden stick and final ownership of loot remain unverified. A **first-aid kit is available**, but George's acute gunshot injury is unresolved when the episode ends.
- **Petrification and black pudding mechanics:** Stone boots invite an inference about earlier reported statues, not an established link; the GM's black-pudding rules are **session mechanics**, not necessarily a literal canonical monster stat block for external publication.
- **Still no completed Monsoon objective:** The group remains below the pit, **has not reached the keep**, freed Tedna or P0D's pixies, found the Gem of Tides, or resolved Buttons's status.
