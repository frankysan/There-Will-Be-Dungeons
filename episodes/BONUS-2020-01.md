## What this source can and cannot establish

This bonus is a series of fan questions answered by **Beau (GM)** and the players. GM answers can clarify **the intended explanation of played events** or supply a **future-facing expectation**, but do not add new character actions at E085's cutoff. A cast member's forecast, a hypothetical, a publisher-style product announcement and the GM declining to answer a secret question must stay separate from events the party witnessed. **The E085 source dossier** records the actual game actions; the [ongoing Arc Four narrative](../narrative/ARC_FOUR.md) ends at the **two intruders entering the Soggy Feather room**, not at an imagined outcome. *Inverse* is a different fictional reality: its mention here is production commentary only.

## Cross-source implications without rewriting chronology

### Sigil, the Lady, and M-fighting

In [E085](E085.md), players and spectators **witness a giant green woman-shaped manifestation** and Ventro's impossible removal; the figure **does not announce its identity**. Here, **Beau later explicitly names the Lady of Pain** as the force involved. These are **two compatible but different levels of evidence**: observed appearance, and **later creator identification**. The GM's remarks also support marionette fighting as a way around Sigil's dangerous restrictions on **uncontrolled physical violence**, but do **not** codify every law, guarantee a police-free city in all senses, or prove all punishment leads to death.

**Ventro** is the **Royce local champion** Vorel defeats in E085; the bigger **Kanja/Kenja (?)** opponent is still outstanding. Prize pool **5,000 gp advertised** is not a payout to Vorel or his companions. Nothing here is another round in the bracket.

### Nash's body and outside spell denial

The [E084](E084.md) and [E085](E085.md) observations still govern physical cutoffs: Nash remains a floating heart with new appendages; he **attempted Enlarge, Minor Illusion and Ray of Frost, only to have them suppressed externally**. This later Q&A indicates **rest-based reconstitution toward his previous body** and **no permanent reduction in inherent magical ability**. But **full recovery, a healed original defect, actual dragon form, spell-denial cause and a practical fix are not observed**. Scott's preferences do not become automatic biological mechanics.

### The unresolved dead, Diontalus and party knowledge

The [December 2019 Q&A](BONUS-2019-12.md) previously described the Hope/Stanley petitioners **interrupted en route to the Weir**. This January-publication bonus does **not** resurrect them or verify their experiences in an afterlife void. **Diontalus** was extracted by the Principle into a **crystal for study** in [E059](E059.md). Any alleged launch into space reflects **Vorel's old proposal, not the Principle's performed transfer**; the **current location** of Diontalus's soul is still unresolved.

The group has **not performed Nothing's Abyss kidnapping**. The party in E085 has **not been briefed in-scene on the secret target**; Beau's idea that she *might* involve them later is not a prediction to narrate as completed fact.
