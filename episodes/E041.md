## Key evidence boundaries

- **Monsoon's under-Pit lead is physically tested E041:** The party crosses the grate over the pit, sees a real froghemoth-like creature, finds and opens a **different, small concealed exit grate** in the side crevasse and descends a second ladder into oily caverns. This does **not** establish a successful connection to the Teet keep.
- **George versus wounded Guzzle pursuer:** **George is a Teet Guard who begs for his life and accompanies the party**. The injured Guzzle member is separate and does not become their cave guide. Verify nicknames, wounds and exact outcomes in audio.
- **Sphere is not a confirmed egg:** The GM explicitly allows it to be named an **“egg” provisionally** in Nash's notes but refuses to verify its nature. The sphere remains **carried by Nash at the E041 cutoff**; what he does with it follows in E042.
- **Illusions versus real bodies:** The extra Vorels exist only as **Phantasmal Force** in the monster's perception; no additional lizardfolk have materialized.
- **Ethics:** Stanley's defense of Buttons is **his argument**, not confirmation Buttons was actually responsible for the reported charges, nor a completed reconciliation.
