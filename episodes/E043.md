## Campaign separation and evidence boundaries

- **No direct Wastes continuation:** The narrative is intentionally a self-contained holiday alternate adventure with a **different DM and player-character premise**. **E044 resumes where E042's underground cave scene ended**; E043's ghostly device, Vorel doll, Santa death, magical necklaces and reality reversal are **not evidence about The Wastes, Jesus Chrome, the Teet or the main adventurers**.
- **Performers versus fictionalized selves:** Scott is announced **absent from playing the one-shot** but appears as a **character inside the holiday fiction**. Beau is a **player**, not the session's DM; Jon DMs and is also represented in-world. Speaker-number assignments in the machine SRT require separate validation.
- **Spelling and effect caution:** `Horatio`, `House of Holiday Spirit`, the dog/familiar names, the **Hillgard/Champions** necklace, song selection sequence and exact workings of the **reverse** machine need audio verification; the finale should not be explained with invented canon.
- **Metafiction:** References to other campaign names and monsters are jokes, props or player analogies; no automatic crossover.
