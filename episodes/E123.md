**Played cutoff:** All four **complete a long rest**, but **Stanley's and Vorel's scythe-inflicted ability losses remain**. Hope pilots the lodge's **noble speeder** to **the Harbor**, carrying Stanley and Vorel and towing Nash in a harness. **Mushrooms are delivered to Nobieri**. The XP remarks are comic table discussion, not an independently audited progression ledger. Tom's return is awaited; Nash sulks, and the four remain at the lodge.

## The Harbor reached; an investigation authorized (~02:00–02:35)

After breakfast, **no speeder fuel is found**. Weight distribution forces an adjustment: **Hope pilots, Stanley sits behind her, Vorel crouches on the forward poles facing back**, and Nash uses his existing floating garment while **towed in a chest/upper-body harness**, with about **seven feet of slack**. Stanley's **Sleight of Hand 25** secures the knots. Foot-only tying and a three-foot line are discussed but **not the final arrangement**. The group **does not establish that Nash could never fit on the speeder**; they choose towing without testing all four aboard.

Hope starts gradually, then follows the roads; Nash first slides over grass and then flies behind. They **actually travel to the Harbor in about fifty story-minutes (~02:22–02:26)**, without the proposed crash or a new fight. The GM's casual hex/mile/minute estimates are not a calibrated vehicle specification. No flight to Earth or Planet Nash occurs.

**Hope hands Nobieri the mushroom bag (~02:26:40)**. Stanley and Vorel initially go into Emenil's house, leaving Nash tied outside. Hope returns to release him, fails the knot check and **breaks a finger for 5 damage**. Nobieri **magically loosens the ropes**, then utters an **Infernal mending prayer** and **sets the finger back into place**; **the GM explicitly does not restore those hit points**. His magic does not cure Vorel's or Stanley's ability losses. The priest's prayer is **not Nash's spellcasting**.

Nobieri says **one specimen of each mushroom may be insufficient**. Hope gives landmarks toward the lodge, hunting grounds and northern cave, and warns of the **possessing spirit and veined eyes**. Nobieri requests and receives **permission to learn more**, promising to keep numbers low so the Harbor can survive a bad outcome. **No expedition departs onscreen, extra mushrooms return, preparation is consumed or heart location knowingly disclosed as such**. Vorel's attempt to interject that the site is secret is stopped because **he is not present in this outside conversation**.

## Emenil's objection and the lake cutoff (~02:35–02:45:55)

All four finally meet Emenil. She initially avoids Stanley's eyes. Stanley **warns that Diontalus has returned and may target her/the community**, but acknowledges he cannot prove he is not already speaking to a possessed person. **Emenil is not revealed as Diontalus**. She objects to abandoning Elhandar, which sheltered them, and insists **Stanley should warn his people's leader**. Hope and Stanley explain their wish to draw the pursuer away; control of multiple mages or exclusively elven hosts remains **speculation**.

Emenil listens and offers **Fishbird/Malhaya guidance**. She would obey Hope if ordered to evacuate, but **Stanley proposes leaving the refugees their home and taking danger away with the party; Hope agrees (~02:41:29–02:42:05)**. This supersedes the earlier broad evacuation plan. **The elven leader is not yet personally briefed**, although Tom reports sending word. Nash's family is **not collected or relocated**.

Emenil says planar connection is strong and leads them toward a **lake communion**, explaining the old church baths. **She disrobes and enters the lake**; Stanley accepts the invitation and begins removing his clothing while following. The GM clarifies **everyone is invited**, but the final image specifically follows **Emenil and Stanley**; Vorel remains watching from the doorway, asking about his curse. **No Fishbird appears or speaks, no communion result or ability restoration is shown, and no romantic commitment is declared**. Stanley suggests asking Nobieri, who has **returned to his own house**; this inquiry is not undertaken before fade-out.
