**Played cutoff:** **Chester the bearsaurus is physically revealed and actually dies** during the party's brief fight. The apparently grateful talking deer are **six hostile Elhandari-looking spellcasters under illusory disguises**, exposed by Stanley. **Dilenoria/Dilo turns on the party, takes a magic control wand, and actually abducts Hope inside a moving sealed sphere**, fleeing toward a mountain cave. **Hope's attempted *Teleport* out fails**, using its spell slot; she remains alive and imprisoned. Stanley, Vorel, Nash and Omnom **pursue to a glowing-mushroom cave** but **do not recover Hope or capture Dilo before the episode ends**. ## Hunting Chester: a bear versus a Tyrannosaurus rex (~00:03–01:06)

1. **Exact E119 cliffhanger resumed (~00:03–00:20).** The morning is around 7 a.m. in the Elhandar forest, the flaming fish bait **already extinguished by Stanley**. The group remains concealed; **Stanley glimpses a patch of brown fur about 100 feet away** and warns Vorel through *Message*. Nash moves into a protected position and **readies *Haste* on Vorel for the start of combat**, without wasting its one-minute duration beforehand. Hope, Omnom and Dilo reposition. Omnom and Dilo's clumsy scramble makes noise, and the large creature approaches the party.
2. **Chester actually seen (~00:20–00:27).** Stanley **casts *Polymorph* on himself to become a Tyrannosaurus rex**, begins the battle and frightens Dilo with his audacity. The GM reveals **Chester**, an enormous **upright bear-like creature with massive hind legs, small forelimbs, a partially reptilian head and large branching deer antlers**, comparable to Stanley's dinosaur in size. **This establishes the specimen's played appearance and existence**, not the laboratory creation date or its claimed centuries of activity. Nash **actually triggers the readied *Haste*** on Vorel, uttering a short improvised rhyme; the spell succeeds.
3. **A very brief violent fight (~00:27–00:48).** Vorel **charges with *Haste* and Rage**, cutting Chester's leg with his **flaming sword associated with the Eye of Groxl**. Nash **casts *Ray of Frost***; he considers *Reduce* but **does not cast it**, remembering concentration is occupied by *Haste*. As a Tyrannosaurus rex, **Stanley lands a critical bite and holds Chester**, the animal breaks free, **leaps about 50 feet up a tree**, and Stanley/Vorel's opportunity attacks **tear its body open**, leaving a trail of entrails. Omnom climbs the tree; Dilo remains out of his depth rather than landing a significant hit.
4. **The falling animal and rescue (~00:48–01:01).** **Hope runs to the dangling intestines and pulls**. The badly injured Chester and a tree **fall onto Hope**; **Chester actually dies from the fall** according to the GM. Hope is **alive but pinned and suffocating**; Nash and Omnom take some damage from the tree, rather than being killed. Vorel **physically tips the massive carcass on its side**, allowing Hope to crawl out alive, then lowers it. Stanley relinquishes dinosaur form. **No living Chester remains after this played kill**; the earlier E119 no-sighting/no-kill status is superseded, not an error at that cutoff.
5. **Omnom's reaction, uneasy Dilo (~01:00–01:12).** Omnom **expressly reaffirms Vorel as the legitimate “Great Fang”**, saying doubts that he might deserve the title himself are gone; he calls the victory one for future stories. It is **renewed personal recognition**, not a separate political coronation. He calls Dilo cowardly, though Dilo had not been offered a useful attacking opportunity. Vorel and Omnom discuss eating/using parts of the body; **Omnom actually begins eating the flesh**, despite warning about worms. A proposed **lethal sparring match** between Dilo and Omnom is **rejected**, not performed. Dilo asks Stanley about keeping his own mind under *Polymorph*; Stanley explains concentration and animal instincts. This exchange **does not establish a magical identity test for Dilo**.

## The grateful deer are not deer (~01:12–02:10)

6. **Apparent forest tribute (~01:12–01:31).** Stanley hears cautious footsteps; **six seemingly articulate deer** (three stags and three does in the GM's description) approach. A stag **thanks Vorel for killing Chester** and claims the predator terrified the local forest. The others beg Nash and Stanley for dried fruit. The party initially treats them as further talking Elhandar wildlife. One apparent doe says **unusual mushrooms grow in a cave north of the hunting grounds**, making a possible link to Fitz's **unfulfilled mushroom request for Nobieri**. Dilo claims fey-soil wildlife commonly speaks. **These are statements by disguised hostiles**, not trustworthy deer testimony or independently verified Feywild biology.
7. **Stanley exposes the disguise (~01:31–01:38).** In a private GM aside, **Stanley's high passive perception gives him an uneasiness that the deer may be magically altered**. He offers fruit and **actually casts *Dispel Magic* on one animal**. One transforms into a startled **fully clothed elf with dark veins around red-glowing eyes**. The remaining five cease their deer act and **also become hostile elf spellcasters**, all six attacking the heroes. **They are observed elves rather than ordinary talking deer, but exact controlling faction/motive is not identified**.
8. **Hostile spells and divided control (~01:38–02:05).** Hope **slashes an elf's throat**, interrupts that caster's verbal spells, but **blood blinds her**; she also grapples another. Dilo uses **monk-like *Flurry of Blows*** to strike the target Hope holds, creating the appearance of assistance. An elf casts ***Mass Suggestion*** to make **Hope and Omnom behave like squirrels**; **Stanley, Vorel, Nash and Dilo resist**. Another enemy ***Cone of Cold*** hits friends and foes, damaging the group and **freeing Hope from the suggestion**; one injured elf is left **frozen/dead**. Another elf casts ***Entangle***, **restraining Stanley and Hope**. Vorel grapples and bites the stag-form mage. Nash **actually casts sixth-level *Chain Lightning*** against **four distinct hostile targets**, not his allies. The apparent deer plot is now a real ambush, not woodland comedy.
9. **Dilo's betrayal amid the fight (~02:05–02:12).** As Stanley begins ***Destructive Wave***, **Dilo calls a cue to one of the mages**, who pulls a **wand/device** and **encloses Hope in an impermeable floating magical sphere**. The caster **throws the controller to Dilo**, who **catches it and flees west with the imprisoned Hope**. This **directly observed collusion supersedes E119's wholly speculative concern that Dilo is suspicious**. Stanley's ***Destructive Wave*** resolves **after Dilo leaves the blast zone**; the GM says **all six attacking elves are killed**, leaving smoking corpses. **Dilo is not slain or proven to be one of those six**.
10. **Hope's failed escape (~02:10–02:15).** In a **private Hope/GM passage**, Dilo runs with **unnaturally long, fast strides**, intentionally changes direction from west to north, and **takes Hope inside a cave in the mountains**. Hope is **conscious, breathing and physically contained**. She **actually attempts *Teleport* through the Hand's magic**, specifying the friends as her destination; **the spell fails while consuming its slot**, without breaking the sphere. She wipes blood from her eyes, but **does not escape, die, arrive elsewhere, or return to the group** in E120. The device's anti-teleport mechanism and its creator are unverified; only the outcome is played.

## Into the mountains, then a luminous cave (~02:12–02:45)

11. **Pursuit, not recovery (~02:13–02:27).** Stanley ***Polymorphs* into a giant eagle** to chase and locate the fleeing blue **sphere**, which he observes traveling into the mountains. He spots a **large, narrow cavern mouth** in a high rock wall. He circles back to guide **Vorel**, with **Nash slower on his flying underwear**. **Omnom remains briefly compelled to act as a squirrel**, then eventually catches up and expresses shame that a spell overcame him; he is **alive and accompanies the rescue party**. **No direct physical confrontation with Dilo occurs yet**.
12. **Clues at the entrance (~02:22–02:31).** Vorel examines the mountain and **finds Dilo's distinct, enormous running strides** and traces of a **very large beast having used the cave perhaps months before**. He finds no **other nearby open entrance**, but does not rule out concealed tunnels. Stanley resumes his normal form and the **four pursuers enter the cave** together. Signs of old large-animal passage are **not evidence Chester was alive again**; the corpse remains at the fight site.
13. **The mushroom grotto (~02:29–02:40).** The cave has **self-glowing multicolored fungi**, damp air, moss and huge caps; a cliff and winding path continue inward. Vorel remembers dangerous fungi in the earlier mine and urges caution. Nash's **Arcana check** suggests genuine magic flowing through mushrooms, but **cannot identify their species or specific effect**. Tracks are uncertain among disturbed vegetation; **the location of Hope and Dilo beyond the entrance remains unknown to the rescuers**.
14. **Nash eats the mushroom (~02:34–02:45).** Ignoring caution, Nash **cuts a small piece of a glowing blue mushroom**, releasing luminous particles. A failed Constitution saving throw triggers a **very loud sneeze**. He **actually eats the sample**, makes another saving throw, and for a moment merely feels internal warmth. As he talks, **bright white/blue light shines out of his mouth**, temporarily dazzling or blinding companions who look straight into it, including a startled Omnom. Nash turns his face toward a wall, then **opens his mouth to illuminate the cave as the others advance**. **No permanent blindness, further transformation, poison cure, death, mushroom gathering for Nobieri or Hope rescue occurs** before the episode ends. “Fly Like An Eagle” corresponds to **Stanley's performed eagle transformation**, not the return flight of Hope.
