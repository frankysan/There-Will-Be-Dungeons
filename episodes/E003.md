## Reference candidates and caution

- The medical automaton is a potentially important **long-lived technological witness**, but its own statements and any damaged records should be attributed, not treated as universal world history.
- **Mog/ Moag Forain/Ferhain:** Multiple machine spellings occur. A later fan-wiki Episode 6 summary uses *Moag Ferhain*; defer final orthography until more reliable source evidence is checked.
- **Softhead's return** is distinct from the Hillmaster's death. Avoid claiming the river killed Softhead; the apparent outcome is unresolved here.
- The strange skeleton behavior should be described as what the party observes, not proof of exactly which animation magic is operating.
