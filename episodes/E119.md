**Played cutoff:** The **four original protagonists**, **Omnom Cinderborn** and elven apprentice candidate **Dilenoria/Dilo (?)** undertake the promised Elhandar hunt. Their quarry is **Chester**, an allegedly escaped engineered bear/dinosaur hybrid called a **bearsaurus/barosaurus (?)**. **The party camps, rests, then enters the northern hunting forest**. Vorel throws a **lit fish-head-and-eggs lure** about 150 feet into trees; **the bait begins to ignite vegetation, but Stanley actually extinguishes the small fire with *Prestidigitation***. Heavy nearby steps and cries approach, **but no creature is visibly identified and no combat or kill is played**. Stanley privately fears that **Dilo might be hosting Diontalus** after the Principle's **earlier warning that the extracted entity might be loose**—a concern, *not* confirmed possession, escape or identity. The hunt is underway; its final outcome is deferred.

## Morning in the Elhandar cottage (~00:02–00:35)

1. **Preparation, not an immediate battle (~00:03–00:10).** Day breaks after E118's night in Stanley's newly opened study. **Vorel** rises from sleeping outside and uses **Hunting Artisan** and available animal remains/tools to make a **spear/javelin for Stanley**, **raccoon-and-deer-rib shield and an ordinary woodcutting axe for Nash**, **blowgun and needles for Hope**, and a **deer-leg club with an inserted dagger** for Dilo. These are **actually handed out** around breakfast, but they are improvised mundane equipment, **not replacements for the party's magical weapons**. The group later explicitly relaxes Vorel's proposed restriction to hunt with these alone.
2. **Pipe is missing and found (~00:09–00:24).** With Angelashi and their four infant changelings in the cottage, **Nash notices that infant Pipe is absent**. He searches, makes use of the **unusual right-eye loved-one locator first demonstrated at the alternate Nash tower** and sees Pipe-like surroundings in a dark cloth compartment. **Nash subsequently checks his own pocket and physically retrieves Pipe as a pipe-shaped object**. The child **transforms back into a baby and laughs**. **Pipe was never abducted, killed or lost in the forest**, and no other baby is newly missing. This confirms a played changeling transformation, not a new universal ability for every child. [Nash](../world/characters/nash-maggard.md).
3. **The proposed apprentice actually joins the hunt (~00:22–00:31).** **Dilenoria/Dylanoria (?)**, who had only been asked to return in E118, arrives in hunting clothes, seeks **Stanley's** approval and is allowed to **come on this specific outing**. He explains that he wants to study **what makes a hero and how to outthink intelligent opponents**, not simply learn Vorel's fighting style. Stanley agrees to the experience/observation and teaches the shouted **“lemon tree”** signal for fighting; this is **not a permanent oath, supernatural command or proof of indefinite party membership**. Nash repeatedly calls him a rude nickname despite Dilo's correction. The Dilo nickname itself is supported in both ASR exports; his full name requires listening.
4. **Omnom chooses the trial (~00:22–00:35).** **Omnom** comes out of the trees, still injured from Razak Val, **physically agrees to accompany Vorel** and speaks about hunting as a way to overcome his grief. Vorel gives Dilo a makeshift club. Omnom says he will fight with his hands rather than accepting a new crafted weapon. His involvement **does not settle Omnom's allegations against Hope or complete their reconciliation**.

## The quarry, private suspicions and expedition preparations (~00:35–01:08)

5. **Chester is reported, not yet seen (~00:36–00:48).** Dilo tells the group that an **Elhandari genetic-diversity laboratory** created a bear/dinosaur hybrid **several hundred years ago** for druidic summoning experiments. It **escaped**, supposedly killed elves, disrupted the biodome ecology and left other hunting parties dead. He calls it **Chester the bearsaurus/barosaurus** and says that eliminating the animal would protect the forest. **The experiment, casualty totals, century-long survival, reproduction risk and ecological scale are Dilo's secondhand account**, not newly toured laboratory evidence. The heroes **actually decide to seek Chester**; **no den, animal or eggs are found in E119**.
6. **An important but still unproved Diontalus warning (~00:38–00:44).** **Stanley privately tells Vorel** that, during the **Principle's visit aboard the Elhandari ship in E115**, the Principle said the dangerous former companion once removed from Stanley was **on the loose and might return**. Stanley fears a surprise interested-in-him apprentice could **be hosting Diontalus**, since the entity once possessed Stanley. **Vorel agrees to watch Dilo**. Stanley explicitly acknowledges that the Principle **might have been bluffing**. This is **Stanley's played report of the Principle's earlier assertion**, not a witnessed escape from the E059 crystal, direct Diontalus appearance, proof Dilo is possessed or a new identification of the pupil. Dilo notices the suspicion and privately asks **Nash**, who reassures him without receiving proof of his innocence.
7. **Old necklace stays with Omnom (~00:51–00:55).** Vorel asks about the **Necklace of Fireballs Stanley previously passed to Omnom**; Omnom **physically produces it from a concealed skin/scales pouch**. He offers it to Vorel, who **refuses the transfer**. **Omnom retains the necklace at this cutoff**; the total remaining charges are not verified by merely showing it. [E064](E064.md) · [Omnom](../world/characters/omnom-cinderborn.md).
8. **Nash actually bathes (~00:55–01:03).** Vorel and Omnom insist the hunt requires removing Nash's strong scent. **Omnom physically relocates a heavy stone basin outside the cottage**, Dilo helps fill it and Angelashi approves. Nash **gets into the water and washes with fragrant soap**. Omnom drinks some bathwater in the ensuing grotesque conversation. The group later confirms Nash **still has his ordinary bodily/tumor smells**; bath and soap are not a magical stealth enchantment or an immunity to Chester's scent.
9. **Map and departure (~01:04–01:08).** **Lyrielle provides a physical map** of the Elhandar biodome and Dilo describes a route from **Villa Ornath (?)** by **the Harbor**, **Gatherers' Lodge**, waters/bridges and the **Grushen/Grousen (?) Hunting Grounds**, with other named features possibly rendered by Roll20. Hex distance and pace are **GM table-mapping conventions** (about fifteen minutes per hex / about one hour per four hexes), not proof of measured real-world geography. The group loads ordinary provisions for free from the cottage and **sets out together**; **Angelashi and the newborns stay behind**.

## Across Elhandar's living landscape (~01:09–01:20)

10. **Fitz of the Harbor (~01:11–01:22).** After several travel checks, **Vorel hears berry-picking near the road**. A tiefling named **Fitz**, who attended the E117 Harbor bonfire, **physically emerges alive**, greets **Hope as queen** and shares large berries, including with Dilo. Fitz says the new settlement is gathering food and asks the travelers to **bring unusual northern mushrooms to Nobieri for potential visionary preparation**. The party **agrees to look**, but **collects no mushrooms or returns to the Harbor** during E119. Claims that the mushrooms provide useful visions are a local's assertion, not tested properties.
11. **Talking squirrels (~01:23–01:37).** Nash pokes a moving bush, startling squirrels who then **actually speak to the company**. **Four squirrels ask for nuts**, one climbing onto Nash. Hope provides **two portions of rations/nuts**, after which they leave to store food. They do **not** lead the party to Chester or verify the quarry's precise position; Nash does **not** cast *Speak with Animals* in order to hear them. Their talk is a played setting feature, not proof all Elhandari wildlife speaks.
12. **The fisherman (~01:44–01:56).** At a later bridge, the group meets an elf fisher heard as **Tranquilar/Tranquila (?)**, acquainted with Dilo. He greets the travelers, discusses his luck at fishing and says **earlier Chester hunters failed to return**—more **NPC testimony**, not a witnessed casualty. He **actually gives Nash a piece of chewing grass**. The party does not join his fishing or hunt Chester on this same day.

## The lakeside camp and the approaching threat (~01:57–02:47)

13. **Night instead of dusk pursuit (~01:55–02:09).** With darkness approaching, the group debates continuing into the hunting grounds versus going to **Gatherers' Lodge**. They **actually choose an overnight camp beside a lake**, not the lodge or a dusk fight. **Vorel and Stanley swim and catch ten fish for supper with Survival assistance**. Dilo fillets and cooks them while Stanley lights the campfire with *Prestidigitation*. **Nash takes ten separated fish heads (one missing an eye) and about 250 fish eggs** as explicitly inventoried bait. This is **fish gathered at camp**, not a fish fire yet. They complete a **long rest inside Stanley's *Tiny Hut***; there is no midnight Chester fight.
14. **The next morning's sound (~02:12–02:22).** An unfamiliar bellow is **actually heard from the northern woods**, sounding vaguely ursine to the party; **its source is not seen or positively identified**. Dilo marks **nine broad investigation regions**, not nine confirmed bearsaurus positions. Vorel rejects splitting up and leads **all six hunters** north across a bridge into the forest. The DM advances the travel/clock to the second morning.
15. **Stealth and first disturbance (~02:21–02:35).** Vorel uses his **color-shifting scales for concealment** and he and Omnom approach low cliffs among the trees; the other four hide nearby. **Heavy steps, snapping branches and a distant call** are heard. Omnom, whose injuries still matter, **scrapes the ground and makes a noise**, putting the group on alert. The quarry is *suspected*, not yet verified. Stanley climbs to higher ground; characters make repeated Stealth/Perception checks with mixed results, not automatic perfect concealment.
16. **Improvised flaming fish bait (~02:35–02:44).** Nash **silently levitates using the established flying/cooling underwear** to hand Vorel a **wax-wrapped fish head smeared with fish eggs**. Using his **Storm Soul** fire feature, **Vorel actually ignites the wax**, then **throws the lure backward** roughly **150 feet** into the forest. It lands in brush beneath a dead tree. Neither the nature of the approaching creature nor its reaction to the bait is yet established; **this is one improvised trap, not an accidental explosion of all his fish supplies**.
17. **The titular fire is extinguished, and the cliffhanger remains (~02:42–02:47).** Stanley moves nearer when he sees **the bait has begun a small vegetation fire**, and **casts *Prestidigitation*** within ten feet to **put the flame out**. Smoke remains; he has **not burned down the forest**. Heavy steps and animal-like cries grow closer to Stanley and the hidden group. The GM **stops before revealing or starting combat**, asking players to decide their positions before the next session. The later credits are **out of game**, not evidence Chester was killed, the bait recovered or a fire restarted.
