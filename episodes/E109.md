# E109 — Sand Jell-O

> **The Wastes · corrected S1E109 · Dunebreaker prequel, part 3/3 · same Wastes, long before Dusthill's main-party departure · ASR-provisional.** [Processed Parakeet](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E109.%20Sand%20Jell-O.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E109.%20Sand%20Jell-O.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [full-export text comparison](../reports/E109_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md) · [Dunebreaker profile](../world/places/dunebreaker.md). Both transcript exports extend to ~**02:43:00** including cast talk and network sign-off. **No audio has been listened to.** Owner's archive crosswalk: publisher **TWBD 111**, published **6 July 2020**, corrected **E109**; spoken introduction describes **Sunday 5 July 2020**. These are different date types, and publication metadata is not independently verified. Guest GM **Jon Jagger** explicitly closes the three-part prequel after this session, and the cast says **Beau Schwartz returns next week**.

**Played ending:** **Hack Loman, Ramilda Gloryfaded (?) and Al** complete their **second day** as guards on the Dunebreaker. A neglected gnome cook's experimental **fruit gelatin**, previously purchased against **Ember's** instructions, has become a mobile, absorbing **ooze**. The trio initially drives it through the larder drain without killing it. **Al investigates the sewage machinery and casts Thunderwave inside a containment tank, damaging the plumbing and contributing to foul flooding**. During a secret guarded desert exchange between Ember's party and a force of marauders, the ooze rises outside as an **enormous acidic creature that has absorbed sewage, debris and bodies**. **Hack and Ramilda fire mounted deck miniguns** (with other guards and marauders fighting below); **Al reaches the bridge and activates the ship's “vent” control**, pouring superheated steam on the monster. **Ramilda stamps out the last cherry-bearing globule**, definitively ending this **individual ooze**. Several marauders and **at least two Dunebreaker guards are killed**; Ember, Lotho, Fawkes/Fox and the unknown tall negotiator survive. The PCs are **alive and advanced to level four** in Jon's end-of-session ruling. **The secret trade's nature, Lamulkin Farthing's continuing survival, the two daughters' safe departure, the meaning of Hack's scars and Fleece's employer remain unresolved.** No Nash/Stanley/Vorel/Hope scenes occur.

## A guard sash and the suspicious murder report (~00:00–00:23)

1. **Morning after E108 (~00:00–00:12).** Cast recap affirms that Lamulkin **took the blame for his older daughter's murder of the engineer**, that he was sent to vent work, and that both daughters remained concealed and were fed. A new guard **Fawkes/Fox (?)**, a dark-haired, bearded man, escorts the three PCs to the **segment-one guard office**. Each receives a diagonal **guard sash whose emblem consists of three welded platinum pieces**, a striking contrast with the alleged lack of cash wages. This is a **literal item given to each character**, not a magical power or payment. A small barred cell is empty. They report to **Captain Lotho**.
2. **The lie is not fully accepted (~00:12–00:20).** Lotho asks **Hack, Al and Ramilda** to revisit why they spared Lamulkin. After medical examination, he knows the engineer had **four stab wounds in his back**, incompatible with a straightforward claim that he lunged at the old man and was killed while attacking. **Hack repeats the misleading story**, rolling **Deception 12**; **Lotho explicitly says details do not add up** and **orders them to reinvestigate later that evening**, while noting other tasks take priority. This means **E108's concealed daughters have NOT been permanently cleared or absolved**, though nobody immediately arrests Hack or discovers the girls in E109.
3. **Two assignments (~00:20–00:23).** Lotho sends them to help the gnome kitchen worker, nicknamed **Dipshit**, who has complained about impossible staffing and has been punished by Ember. They must also attend a **midday lockdown briefing/upper-deck duty**. New guards are to serve practical ship needs, not only fight monsters.

## The thing in the larder (~00:23–01:13)

4. **Overworked gnome (~00:23–00:35).** All three **actually help** with breakfast: **Al collects dishes** by recorded/automatic announcements, **Ramilda scrubs aggressively**, and the food service briefly improves. Dipshit has been alone serving hundreds and asks for reliable help. He explains that workers sent to the larder have fled or suffered danger. A past employee **Lance (?)** reportedly died there, but **his exact manner of death was not independently witnessed**. It is reasonable to investigate, not yet evidence of a supernatural fiend.
5. **A suspicious impression, not an identified demon (~00:34–00:39).** Hack spends a ranger spell slot using **Primeval Awareness** and senses **a faint fiend-like trace** somewhere within its non-directional range. The GM compares the trace to an earlier **faint elemental impression associated with Ember**, without proving either is an actual fiend/elemental of full type. **The ability does not provide the creature's location or number.** Al **prints an official investigation/removal request**, and Dipshit signs it; **no monetary reward is offered or received**.
6. **First manifestation (~00:39–00:50).** Inside the dim **larder** are shelves, cabinets, metal cold-storage doors, a wet floor and central drain. **Al deploys an Eldritch Cannon** near the entrance; Hack hears sloshing, then **a red translucent, fruit-speckled ooze rises from a puddle** and attacks. Hack initially misses with his machete-like paired weapons. **Al uses *Catapult* to launch an apple**, which damages the mass, while Ramilda's attempted **Guiding Bolt misses**. Hack slices at it as it **retreats down the drain**. This **does not kill the creature**, and the trio's first declaration of partial victory is correct.
7. **The forbidden gelatin explained (~00:57–01:08).** Dipshit reveals he **bought gelatin and a dessert recipe** from a market seller, made fruit Jell-O and was reprimanded by **Ember for using ingredients not supplied by the ship**. The substance was not initially imported as a known monster. **The gnome asks the party not to blame his experiment**, suggesting they report “rats” instead. **This plea is an NPC's self-protective request, not the party's established final official report or proof of a demonic origin.** The GM later confirms the gelatin escaped through the sewage and grew by absorbing matter.
8. **Al causes a plumbing emergency (~01:08–01:35).** **Al decides to hunt the ooze through the pipes**, asking engineers where waste flows. He finds a covered **collection tank/vat** associated with gravity/pressure and waste filtering, climbs it, creates a small light and **actually drops a Thunderwave-like sonic grenade into the closed vessel**. The blast deforms equipment, breaks pipes and pushes **sewage/wastewater back into the larder**, making lunch service worse. **No ooze is visible to Al in the tank afterward.** Do not record this as having killed or trapped the monster, or as a successful controlled repair. The trio's original kitchen assignment is **not fully solved** by the time the alarm sounds.

## Guarded desert rendezvous and unholy expansion (~01:35–02:03)

9. **Emergency lockdown and mounted guns (~01:35–01:45).** A shipwide PA announces passage into dangerous territory and **mandatory passenger lockdown**, while guards report to the **top deck**, lined with **mounted miniguns** and a control tower. **Hack and Ramilda go straight there**. **Al briefly returns to the larder before joining them**. Lotho orders absolute confidentiality: the guards are to **observe without firing unless explicitly ordered**, under a death threat for betraying what they witness. Their **later gunfire is a response to an actual lethal attack**, not authorization to interfere in the preceding trade.
10. **Ember trades with the marauders (~01:42–01:51).** The Dunebreaker **actually stops** in the desert and lowers a ramp. **Ember, Lotho, half-orc bodyguards Argul and Gasper (?), Fawkes/Fox and other guards** travel out with a trailer to meet approximately **six or seven armed marauder vehicles** and roughly fifteen people. A **very tall thin person with an illuminated pad** participates. **Goods, including metal, boxes and water, change hands, with paper documents exchanged**, then some goods move back amid animated disagreement. **The cargo, documents, governing agreement, identities, and whether the apparent negotiation buys safe passage are not established** from what the PCs can see from the deck; neither side is demonstrated to have attacked first.
11. **The same ooze has become vast (~01:51–02:03).** As the visitors prepare to separate, **giant translucent tentacles surge up on both sides of the ship**; suspended **bodies, debris, glass and metal** are visible inside. GM identifies this as the **same gelatinous larder creature**, enlarged by absorbing sewage and material under the landship. The **marauders and ship guards alike shoot and fight it**. **Do not claim all suspended people were newly killed by it on screen**, and distinguish guards' and marauders' losses from the distinct E107 engineer murder.

## Defeat, casualties and definitive prequel cutoff (~02:03–02:43)

12. **Battle on two levels (~02:03–02:27).** **Hack** uses deck miniguns repeatedly, dealing immense damage despite repeated overheating/cooldown; a dramatic **natural 20 and initial 6-6-6 damage dice** occur near the climax. **Ramilda** fires additional guns, endures acid and a blow from falling metal, and keeps attacking. **Al** runs to the ship's bridge despite a damaging tentacle swipe and **searches for the system controlling steam vents**. Some side exchanges speculate about *Feather Fall* escapes or spells, but **no ship jump occurs**, and the mini-guns are mechanical mounted weapons, not Al's earlier Eldritch Cannon.
13. **Venting and the final cherry (~02:27–02:36).** **Al presses a bridge button labeled “vent”**. **Superheated steam pours from the ship's sides, burning and breaking apart the enormous ooze**, despite its successful saving throw against part of the effect. The remaining **foot-sized gelatin glob, with a cherry inside, lands beside Ramilda**. **She stomps it** (GM accepts hit and two damage, more than required); it **liquefies and becomes inert**. This is a **confirmed kill of the episode's gelatin monster**, not just an escape into a drain.
14. **Survivors and losses (~02:36–02:40).** From the deck **Hack and Ramilda see fewer marauders leaving than arrived**; **at least two guards lie dead** below. **Ember, Lotho, Fawkes/Fox and the slender negotiator** are shown alive. Ember's group returns to the vessel **with a trailer holding additional supplies**, contents uncertain. **Al remains at the bridge studying controls** after his successful action. No exact death count or cargo manifest is given. The **Dunebreaker survives** and remains operable at cutoff; no direct demonstration that all plumbing is repaired.
15. **GM's formal final scene (~02:39–02:43).** Lotho commends the guards and **Jon ends their second working day**, grants the three PCs **level four**, and states the **three-part side adventure is over**. In an **out-of-game explanation**, Jon says Dipshit's market gelatin leaked into the sewer, absorbed waste and grew—**not that a parent jelly sent out a spawn or that a verified actual fiend was summoned**. Cast speculation about whether Nash would taste the jelly, a future Dunebreaker appearance, and **Beau's promised return** are not played crossover events.
