## Interpretation / open boundaries

- **What caused the fall:** The ascent and descending orders are distinct. The guards **reverse the elevator**, and a later powerful spell/combat event damages the support. E020's GM recap attributes the chain failure to **Nash's Thunderwave**. Track **played physical damage** versus **retrospective explanation**, avoiding an invented mechanical chain if the SRT is ambiguous.
- **Tickles:** An enslaved **human** abused as a “dog” by the upper-mine guards in E018; **jumps onto the lift** in E019. Do not count him as a new player character or literally a dog. E019 ends with him buried/unresponsive, while **E020 ~00:14** reports no response and a dented helmet; later speakers call him dead.
- **Depth:** The elevator passes sign **78** during a long fall, but no reliable floor number at the bottom. The **Catherine Speckles/999th-floor** record remains *magistrate testimony* from E016, not an E019 arrival sign.
- **Combat methodology:** This recording contains extensive rules adjudication, spoken descriptions of maps/tokens and performance chatter; those do not automatically constitute official D&D mechanics or fixed creature counts. Out-of-game fan-art montage must not be used as contemporaneous in-story visual evidence.
- **Backdating:** E018 ends as undead converge; E019 resolves the elevator fight and ends with the **rockfall**. E020—not this episode—shows the actual rescue from the rock trap.
