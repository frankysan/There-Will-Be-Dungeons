# The Wastes S2E03 — Enemy Mine

**Status:** ASR-provisional. Played Season Two session continuing the Mars war council from S2E02. Sources: processed Parakeet and Qwen Q8/Canary/Nemotron-labelled SRTs. No original audio reviewed.

## Episode summary

The Council of Earthly Defenders chooses the Solar Mines as the strategic route for a future Triangulosphere mission. Nash provides a detailed childhood account of the mines. The party plans recruitment and a possible three-pronged attack, while distinguishing speculative allies from secured forces. The World Breaker is identified as a Weir-altered Tarrasque.

Stanley accepts appointment as the Elhandari's new Selle Prime (spelling provisional), undergoes a public transformation and receives Unbreakable Majesty early. During a private communion the elder tree makes a disturbing prophecy concerning Nash. A new sworn guardian says Tungsten Wheel is her uncle. The council adjourns; the party remains on Mars.
