## Continuity gates

- **E045 headache battle continues:** E045 ends with one foe killed; **E046 immediately introduces more**. The group defeats those they fight, but cannot infer global extinction.
- **Robe and patch inventory:** Found **E046**, held by **Nash**, and **not** the earlier **Wand of Scowls**. The **24-foot ladder remains available at E046 close**; see E048 for use.
- **Seven enchanted beans + etched key (E044):** Become the **beanstalk and hatch-opening key E046**. They are **not** the E042 cache or Hope's unrelated keys. At the time of E046 the party now has a **new possible way upward**, not confirmation they have traversed every level of the keep.
- **The E045 skeleton earrings:** Identified E046 as **ear-enlarging**; this resolves a particular item's property, **not the contents of the privately delivered E045 papyrus**.
- **Froghemoth and spheres:** Still dangerous at the mouth of the Pit; the party backs off **without destroying eggs** or verifying what the spheres actually are biologically.
- **Book unverified:** E046's changing illustrations and resistance to Identify are directly narrated; that it contains a physical prisoner is not confirmed **until E047**.
