## Continuity safeguards

- **Do not call the title a name correction:** `Genitals McReady` is the episode title; the arms dealer's **working spoken name is Jedel/Jetel McCready**. Charlemagne's grief supports his *belief* that the dead body is his friend; this is stronger testimony than E033 but is not independent visual identification.
- **Toe Ripper violence is not the contracted kill:** The players attack rank-and-file Toe Rippers and burn part of their territory; **Monsoon requested their leader**, who is neither positively identified nor confirmed slain by E035. A later E036 confrontation introduces a more specific leader.
- **Weapons source unproven:** The Twenty-Sixers' large arsenal and looted arms-dealer property invite suspicion, but the party does **not observe the theft** or verify who killed McCready.
- **Nash's acid breath** is an **observed dragon-heart ability used without full dragon transformation**, supported by a GM table ruling. Keep it separate from E024/E025/E036 full-body dragon form and its exhaustion.
- **Fango's evening invitation** is just an invitation; no earlier signed gang bargain or guarantee that the Gargoyle will admit the party to the keep.
- **No completed Pixie Blush rescue, Blood Ball match, or royal wedding** within this session.
