# E086 — Don't be such a Berk

> **The Wastes, Arc Four, Sigil — processed-ASR research only.** Two full exports: [Parakeet](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E086.%20Don%27t%20be%20such%20a%20Berk.parakeet.processed.srt) and [Qwen Q8 / Canary CTC / Nemotron-labelled](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E086.%20Don%27t%20be%20such%20a%20Berk.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt). Both cover roughly **2 h 55 m including recap/break/outro**. **No audio or source handout was independently verified, no original SRT changed.** [E085](E085.md) stops when two armed intruders enter the Soggy Feather room. The [January 2020 public-release bonus](BONUS-2020-01.md) is **GM retrospective**, not another played session. *Inverse* is a separate reality.

## Actual E086 outcomes and cutoff

- **A large wave of masked killers attacks the party in their Soggy Feather suite.** Most disappear into dust, leaving clothes, upon being defeated. **Nothing privately recognizes them as the Order of the Crimson Moon**, an extremely costly and elusive assassins' guild. The others painstakingly reconstruct the name from Nothing's mimicry and gestures. **No commissioner, assassination motive or mechanism of vanishing is identified.**
- Nash asks Squirts how to restore his spellcasting. **She says he needs a “key.”** He cannot obtain one during the battle; instead **his retained dragon-metal heart enables a breath attack** and he repeatedly blasts ninjas, finishing the specially dressed female attacker **before Vorel can question her**.
- After a further rest, Nash grows **a calf and thigh attached to the previously grown foot**, making an increasingly coherent **single leg**, while remaining a floating heart with other incomplete appendages.
- **Kidigree/Kidagri (?) and Squirts make a performance-enhancing soup**, using mushrooms, biting plants, Squirts' trickster ability and squirrel eyes. **Vorel actually drinks a jug** meant to enhance his M-fighting decisions. The mixture's claimed benefits **have not been tested in a match**. The upcoming finals **ban doping and magic aids**.
- A young lizardfolk admirer confronts Squirts after Vorel refuses to adopt him. **Squirts attacks with Sacred Flame; the boy collapses and appears dead, and she hides him in an alley. She later returns, casts Spare the Dying, and the boy revives enough to speak and run away. He survives, badly burned; future consequences unknown.** Onlookers believe Squirts murdered him, causing Vorel's **Arcane Spice sponsorship to collapse before payment**.
- Vorel briefly arranges **a 200-gold, one-day Arcane Spice endorsement** and dons its cape, but the representative rescinds the deal and takes the cape back after witnessing the child incident. Bill separately agreed to promote Arcane Spice at the inn but expects that arrangement to be endangered. **Neither Vorel's 200 gold nor other sponsor cash is paid.**
- A BMF official called **Pentheon/Pantheon (?)** schedules **escort around 1 p.m. for a 3 p.m. start** of the larger finals and lists disqualifications, especially **doping, magical aids, cheating and violence against rivals**. **No finals match is played.**
- At the market, the orc rug-seller **Zarfur/Zarfour (?)** refers Vorel to tailor brother **Lohar/Lorhar (?)**. Vorel obtains an impressive **red Soggy Feather cape with white lettering**; it is **ultimately free** because his presence attracts customers, despite initially discussing 20 gold. **No 20-gold purchase is shown.**
- Nothing guides Nash to a seller of **spell keys**. The vendor explains **school-specific unlocks** for spellcasting in Sigil, charging **500 gold each**, or **twelve payments of 50 gold** (600 total) **subject to identification, faction-issued ID and credit approval**. Nash **cannot afford a key or qualify for credit** and leaves without one. Later **out-of-character GM explanation** says the Lady of Pain's damping field imposes the restriction, and stronger **power keys** exist; neither is a device obtained by the party.
- The GM clarifies **Nash's 25,000 gold is stored in a Tietkalla vault**, rather than lost with his carried equipment; **Stanley's personal carried gold was lost in the beam**. This is a **retrospective financial note**, not gold available to Nash in Sigil. Nash's actual **inn wages and tips are reconciled to 21 gold** in this episode.

## I. Assassins in the executive suite (~00:02–01:07)

The session opens with a production interruption: **D&D Beyond is down**, so players are working partly from memory and later locate reference material. These difficulties explain some repeated or revised rulings; they are **not fictional interference with the characters**.

The **two E085 masked intruders** wear black clothing with white headbands and an insignia of a **howling wolf facing a red moon**. They attack **Nothing** first because Vorel is hidden under a bed. Both sword blows land; Nothing uses **Uncanny Dodge** against the stronger hit, then **kills one attacker in a two-weapon counterattack**. His body dissolves to dust within the costume, leaving garments behind. Nothing also attempts Action Surge against the other without landing that blow.

Vorel rises from the bed and **calls to the Lady of Pain** to intervene against violence, but his shouted appeal and roll **have no observed effect**. Squirts tries unsuccessfully to tie a living assailant's legs with clothes from the first defeated killer. Nash stays in bed and urgently asks why his new mouth and hands still cannot make his sorcerer's spells work. More ninjas enter **through the door**, attacking Vorel, Squirts and Nothing with **swords, nunchucks and thrown kunai**. Vorel grapples and unmasks one: the humanoid face has a **distorted, jawless mouth/tongue**, with apparently no ability to answer normal questions. Nothing continues using two swords and trips and kills another.

Squirts responds to Nash's plea that he **needs a “key.”** The characters **do not possess one**, and even her player explicitly says it cannot be acquired in the middle of combat. She also uses a cowering/groveling party-assistance ability; because the online sheets are down, the GM temporarily accepts the asserted advantage without fully auditing the printed mechanics. **Do not infer a universal rules ruling**.

Nash can, however, use a surviving feature of his **dragon-metal heart**, described as the **Heart of Kazdin/Kaz Desiray (?)**. The GM finds that it can channel a **Dragon's Breath-like effect**, independent of Nash's presently blocked personal spells. The table negotiates a **15-foot cone, 3d6 damage and a one-minute duration**, with confusion over **acid-specific wording versus a chosen damage type**; the played narration repeatedly calls the effect **fire** and shows ninjas set alight. **Record the physical attacks and their consequences, not a perfectly resolved item stat block**. Nash burns multiple attackers, including a captive Vorel was trying to keep alive.

As more attackers crowd the room, Vorel holds one ninja down to ask questions and **tries to extinguish the fire on him**. Other ninjas aim at Vorel's exposed neck. Nothing flips across the battlefield and kills two assailants who were about to attack him. Vorel kills the pinned ninja with a stomp, despite previously expressing a wish for a prisoner. A more elaborately dressed **female-looking leader** appears, casts an apparently paralyzing dark effect that **Vorel resists**, and throws darts. She also performs a very effective **martial counterattack**: when Nothing attempts to strike, she catches the wrist, kicks and **stuns Nothing**; when Vorel charges behind a mattress in an attempt to subdue her, she sidesteps and briefly stuns him too. Vorel later shakes it off.

Squirts' first **Command** attempt against this leader fails, but a second succeeds, leaving her frozen in place. Nash breathes fire on her. Vorel **pins her against the floor using the mattress nonlethally**, intending to secure a live prisoner; Nash **lifts an edge and deliberately breathes fire again**, killing/causing the leader to **disappear into dust**. This destroys the possibility of interviewing her. Vorel, enraged at the lost prisoner, **tears open Bill's expensive mattress** looking for her. He finds only fabric, springs and the assassin's clothing. Most ninjas similarly leave behind dust or vanished bodies; **no resurrection, portal return or magic mechanism is demonstrated**.

The last ranged kunai attacker is first **clubbed by innkeeper Bill** from the hallway and then **killed by Nothing's thrown sword**. The action ends with many empty costumes, broken windows, a ruined mattress, and damage to the Soggy Feather. **No PC dies in this attack.** Bill is angry at the destruction and expects recompense.

The GM pauses to tell **Jon alone**, with the other players temporarily out of audio contact, that Nothing recognizes the crest as belonging to the **Order of the Crimson Moon**: an **expensive, secretive and nearly legendary assassins' guild**, known to a person with Nothing's underworld/basher contacts. An attack by professionals of this caliber on an apparently unknown newcomer is **disproportionate**. Jon/Nothing alone has this full account initially; **the guild did not announce its name to Vorel or Nash**.

After the break, Nothing marks a **red crescent moon** and shows Vorel the attackers' headbands, communicating with imitation and gestures. Vorel and Bill make several wrong guesses before **“Order of the Crimson Moon”** is assembled. The group now has a **name**, but still **no proof who paid the killers** or whether the incident relates to Vorel's local sporting title, Pelvis's side assignment, Soulbroker plots or any other antagonist. Bill initially assumes the tournament celebrity provoked trouble, but **that is his inference, not an observed commission**.

### The Lady is not a personal guard (~01:11–01:28)

Bill explains that Sigil is **not free of violence** and that Vorel was mistaken to infer **the Lady of Pain always appears to punish it** after witnessing Ventro's disappearance. Her actions and motives are not reliably deducible; Bill says **open faction war** is one thing she does not tolerate, but he **cannot know** why she appeared at Royce and not at the inn. Nash worries whether she watches everyone constantly; Bill speculates but **does not claim omniscience as demonstrated fact**. Vorel imagines becoming **her champion** by exterminating dangerous organizations, or that being factionless makes him immune, while Bill says **no immunity exists** and advises him to concentrate on the tournament, his friends and finding passage home. The **Lady is not seen in E086**, and Vorel's attempts to summon her **fail**.

## II. Rest, sponsorship and the trickster's breakfast (~01:28–01:59)

After the staff and PCs extinguish a **smoldering mattress fire**, the group finally gets another long rest. Nash spends **two regeneration choices** to extend his earlier-grown foot into **calf and thigh**, forming a more complete leg growing from the heart. He still has only **an eye, mouth, two differently formed hand/arm protrusions and one increasingly complete leg**, and continues floating. His apparent ability to hear without ears is described by the GM as vibration or tremor sense; **not an externally verified anatomy rule**.

The Soggy Feather downstairs is **crowded with returning admirers and prospective sponsors**, including the **Arcane Spice** and **Angry Dawgz** representatives, a couple seeking dinner with Vorel, and the little lizardfolk boy who has asked Vorel to become his adoptive father. Vorel initially scowls at the fans, then seeks out Arcane Spice and says he might be willing to deal now. The representative proposes a regular monthly endorsement requiring tournament participation and performance, but Vorel asks for **a single-day exception**. The representative **agrees to 200 gold for wearing branding that day** and fits him with an attractive blue animated **Arcane Spice cape**. The contract is **not paid or finalized beyond the in-scene arrangement**, and is **rescinded before any demonstrated money transfer**. Bill separately has agreed to advertise Arcane Spice as an official inn vendor and promote Vorel, expecting proceeds that the morning's later events endanger.

The birdlike **trickster priest (Kidigree/Kidagri, name uncertain)** arrives **disguised as an older human man**. He cannot repeat the earlier injections or bracket tricks at the larger finals under scrutiny, and persuades Bill to let the group use the **Soggy Feather kitchen**. Nothing dons a **chef hat**, Nash carries a heavy cauldron, Squirts and the priest harvest a **pink, tumorous mushroom** and **biting plants** from the priest's soil-lined coat, and they add onion and a trickster **Blessing of the Trickster-like ability**. This includes **Squirts entering a hot cauldron**, then releasing the blessing into the broth. The priest kills a squirrel and drops **both eyeballs** into a jug of the mixture, discarding the carcass into the pot. This is the played grotesque preparation, not a general potion-crafting recipe.

Vorel **drinks the jug, including the eyeballs**. The priest says it will make him extraordinarily perceptive of **marionette opponents' moves and patterns**, granting an unfair advantage in the **3 p.m. finals**. **No immediate effect is observed** and **no match occurs this episode**. The priest warns the **tournament strictly prohibits doping**, and urges the group **not to admit using a performance-enhancing substance**. Squirts agrees that the deceit is for a “greater tricking good,” an **in-character rationalization**, not proof it will be fair or that resurrection is assured.

## III. The lizardfolk child and a broken endorsement (~02:00–02:17)

Before the party leaves, the small lizardfolk boy **again asks Vorel to be his father**. Vorel advises him to become his own hero. **Squirts bites the child's tail**, then tries unsuccessfully to order him away with **Command**. The boy pretends to rage and attempts an ineffectual physical attack. Squirts retaliates with **Sacred Flame**, for **seven damage**. The GM describes him **burned, falling motionless and apparently dead**. Squirts drags him from the common room through the kitchen to an alley and **covers him with rubbish**. Bill is busy and does not notice what she carries; patrons and representatives **do witness enough to believe she killed a child**.

The **Arcane Spice representative publicly condemns what appears to be a child killing** and Vorel's indifference, **tears up the unfinalized contract and takes his blue cape back**. The Angry Dawgz man and numerous fans leave. **No sponsorship payment is made**. The event also jeopardizes **Bill's independent Arcane Spice inn-vendor sponsorship**. The **boy's actual survival must be kept distinct from the spectators' reasonable belief that he was murdered**.

An out-of-character discussion about death saves and Squirts' available cleric cantrip prompts her to **return to the alley**, recover the child and cast **Spare the Dying**. The GM allows the child to **speak, recognize the situation and run home**, severely burned but **alive**. Squirts tells him falsely that **his actual father**, rather than Squirts, saved him, and tells him not to return. **No future medical prognosis, reunion, arrest or formal exoneration takes place**. Bill still hears that someone killed a child, and the **public business damage is not shown to reverse**, even though the boy has survived. Neither **an actual completed homicide** nor an **uninjured child** accurately captures this sequence.

After the crowd disperses, Bill and Pam serve breakfast to the party. Nash is reminded that he **can and should eat** to sustain his odd regrowing body, even if its stomach is not where he expects.

## IV. The bigger finals, a handler and a market cape (~02:17–02:41)

A well-dressed young official named **Pentheon/Pantheon (?)** arrives from the **Big Time Smackeroo M Sports Gaming Commission** as Vorel's **assigned handler**. He gives **3 p.m. showtime at the larger Colosseum** and says he will **collect the party at approximately 1 p.m.** for registration, allowing four hours of free time. He outlines rules: **no performance-enhancing magic/potions or magic gear; cheating and physical attacks on rivals can disqualify**. He bizarrely says **a past crime such as infanticide is not within this particular sporting commission's disqualification remit**; this is **not a universal moral or legal permission to harm children in Sigil**. The handler notices Nothing, becomes visibly nervous or transfixed, and excuses himself. **No reason or earlier acquaintance is disclosed**. Nothing offers to provide the party's own security.

Vorel decides to make his own sponsorship: a cape promoting **the Soggy Feather**. He initially lacks cash, but Nash confirms **21 gold on hand** from his waiter earnings (a disputed E085 figure, now directly reconciled). Bill enthusiastically approves using his establishment's name, but **does not pay Vorel** and notes the damaged bed remains an expense.

At Sigil's sprawling **market bazaar**, Vorel passes a propeller-hat merchant and speaks to an orc rug-seller **Zarfur/Zarfour (?)**, who recognizes the newfound celebrity and directs him to his capemaking brother **Lohar/Lorhar (?)**. Vorel requests a **bright red cape with “the Soggy Feather” in white lettering**, rather than the expensive animated magical attire of his earlier sponsor. The tailor initially quotes a workable arrangement for **20 gold**, with completion in an hour. The team browses nearby stalls; traders repeatedly advertise Vorel's presence to attract customers, and a small floating winged eye observes him without any defined later significance.

When Vorel returns, the finished red cape has **white lettering and decorative gold detailing**. The orc tailor **waives the price entirely**, grateful for business brought by Vorel's celebrity. **Vorel obtains the cape free**, keeping his and Nash's cash; **it is not an enchanted combat item**. This is **inn promotion/merchandise**, not reinstatement of the torn Arcane Spice contract, acceptance of a new paid sponsor or proof the Soggy Feather paid anything.

## V. The actual source of Nash's spell problem (~02:41–02:54)

With limited time before the handler returns, **Nothing finally guides Nash to a spell-key vendor**, an older cloaked woman selling small differently shaped trinkets. She recognizes him as **clueless** about Sigil's magic system, and explains that **spell keys unlock schools of magic** in the city. A sorcerer like Nash may need **separate keys for evocation, illusion, conjuration and other schools**, with **higher-powered spells constrained by the quality of keys**. The **immediately quoted price is 500 gold for one key**. The vendor offers financing at **50 gold monthly over twelve months** (500 principal + 100 interest), but requires a **credit check, faction-issued identification and the first payment at approval**. Nash has neither the money nor that ID, and the vendor mentions **Frobisher Bankers** as a possible lender. **Nash purchases nothing, secures no loan and casts no ordinary spell successfully here**.

In the **out-of-character epilogue**, Beau specifies that the **Lady of Pain's dampening field** is the reason ordinary spellcasting is blocked in Sigil. School keys counter that for their associated schools and certain strength limits; he also mentions **rarer multipurpose “power keys”**. This is **GM system explanation after the played scene**, not a rule that Nash instantly gains access to every spell, nor proof Squirts' apparently functioning magic is subject to an identical restriction. The prior [January 2020 Q&A](BONUS-2020-01.md) had already said Nash's **innate capacity remained intact and the cause was external**, without naming the solution. **Now the local vendor makes keys concrete**, but Nash still has no usable one.

The same epilogue resolves a money inconsistency: Nash's previously listed **25,000 gold is not cash currently carried into Sigil**. The GM states it was deposited in a **Tietkalla vault by Hope**, while **Stanley personally carried and lost his own gold** during the E081 beam. The larger bank-heist pool is recalled, but it is **not money retrieved or transferable to Nash in this episode**. This is a **GM bookkeeping correction**, not a new in-world vault transaction.

**The episode ends as a disappointed Nash steps back into the bazaar crowd.** Vorel has his Soggy Feather cape, the priest's alleged advantage potion is in his body, the finals have **not begun**, and **Hope and Stanley remain unrevived in crypt custody**. The Order of the Crimson Moon's **employer is unknown**, and **Nothing's separately accepted Pelvis/Abyss kidnapping is not started**.
