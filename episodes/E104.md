# E104 — I Blew A Dragon For THIS

The archive title/ID are established by the exact source basenames; **original publisher number/title and recording/publication day were not independently established**. No date is supplied by the opening. The introductory alliterative recap describes **E103**, not additional E104 events.

**Played cutoff:** Hope escapes **Carthax's attack on Tietkalla** through a gate addressed to Asmodeus; her destination is not shown. Separately, **Squirts's successful Divine Intervention removes the moon and surviving Tungan forces**, while prior wreckage remains. Orcs acclaim **Nash as Captain Dragon Lord** and rename their main airship **The Great Angelashi**. Nash's **wrong-order fruit ritual** opens a portal that destroys that ship; the party saves itself and the **still-sealed Fist case** aboard a smaller support craft. With **Farco, renamed Samuel/Sam**, they perform the corrected ritual and **enter another portal**. **The destination is deliberately deferred; there is no demonstrated Sigil arrival, Tobacho delivery or Earth return.**

## Tietkalla: the price, the invasion and the escape (~00:03–00:37)

1. **The Voice accepts the station fire (~00:03–00:07).** Hope is still disguised as **Greg**, watching the railway building turn to **ash and rubble** with Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider. The hooded patron calls the deed **sloppy but acceptable**, apologizes for treating Hope like the previous, more willful bearer, and says she may rest at home before they must leave. **Acceptance is now explicit**, resolving E103's uncertainty about his response. **No particular Hand-granted power is tested afterward**, and the earlier bearer is not identified. Hope starts looking for the **Steel God/Toyota church** she had agreed to permit; she does not locate or enter it before the attack.
2. **Omnom is alive; Hope rejects the Principle publicly (~00:07–00:18).** **Omnom Cinderborn** (Qwen “Omnium Cinderborn”; later Omnom/Amnom/Nam Nam variants) recognizes arson and spots the conspicuous newcomers. Hope drops Greg's appearance and reveals herself to the crowd. **Omnom says Razak Val has fallen and his attempt to retake it failed**; his presence establishes his survival after E064, while the city's present condition remains **his testimony**, not a new survey. Hope declares that **Tietkalla will no longer deal with the Principle** and calls the earlier railway agreement a mistake. Residents bow and welcome her, while questioning the loss of supplies. A supportive craftsman's name is unclear (**Parakeet “better” / Qwen “Battered”**); do not assign an established house identity from that reading.
3. **An invasion interrupts her return (~00:17–00:25).** Alarm bells sound; a **floating castle** appears overhead and winged attackers descend with fiery trails. The hooded patron says **“So it begins”** and smiles; this **does not establish that he commanded the attack**. An impact knocks most of the company down. Hope uses **Thaumaturgy** to call residents to the keep, but a **Performance 5** reaches the nearby crowd rather than the whole city. She orders the henchmen to split up and help. **Steerious physically carries two tieflings**, with an expressed intention to use them as shields if attacked; no actual shield injury is shown. **Blood Skunk follows him**, explicitly declining to carry others. **Dr. Spider climbs the walls** and persuades/intimidates three of four guard groups into retreating; one group nearly attacks him. Hope continues warning residents.
4. **Celibus's corpse walks (~00:25–00:28).** **Celibus Stump**, killed by Hope's *Finger of Death* in E103, appears as a **limping animated corpse/zombie** and attempts to hug her. A simple “stop” does not halt him in the depicted exchange, despite table discussion of verbal control. Hope leaves to continue the evacuation. This is **undead animation following the earlier killing**, not living resurrection and not a demonstrated effect of *Speak with Dead*. **His subsequent location and fate are not shown.**
5. **Carthax breaches the keep; Hope leaves (~00:28–00:37).** Meteors hit buildings; a strike brings down **a tower of the keep** while refugees are gathering there. Winged beastmen torch the streets. **General Carthax**, recognizable by green eyes and rock armor, advances with roughly twelve to fourteen warriors. Hope uses Brett's **copper ring to cast Gate**, naming **Asmodeus**. The skull-framed blue-purple portal opens, but **Asmodeus does not appear or come to her aid**; discussion of deities and *Gate* is a table ruling, not a fully established cosmological explanation. **Hope crosses**, followed by **Steerious with both tieflings**, Blood Skunk, Dr. Spider using his web, and finally **Spatulo**. The last view shows Carthax breaking into the courtyard and demanding Hope. The portal closes. **Where Hope arrives, the refugees' eventual survival, Omnom's later fate, the floating castle's ownership and Tietkalla's full death toll remain unknown.** No reunion with the original trio occurs.

## Outlands: boarding the enemy and the returning trickster (~00:38–01:34)

6. **Three friendly ships face the moon's forces (~00:38–00:49).** Nash, Stanley and Vorel occupy the main airship, with **two smaller escort/support boats** nearby. The larger hostile craft consists of **three linked saucers with heavy guns/turrets**, accompanied by many small discs. The defenders have ordinary broadside cannons and poorly effective bows. **Stanley casts Dimension Door**, taking **Vorel and the closed relic chest** into the enemy ship's forward circular control room. The GM explicitly permits the carried box after checking carrying capacity. **Nash stays on the airship**; he feels abandoned, though the others believed they had agreed on the plan.
7. **The bridge is cleared; Squirts is aboard (~00:50–01:18).** Vorel rages and kills Tungan operators using the **floating chest as an improvised weapon**, his **Eye of Groxl fire shield**, bare hands and later a crushing foot. **Stanley uses Telekinesis to eject operators through the broken window**. Most operators continue working or later cower rather than mounting an effective personal defense. The craft lists and its guns stop after the bridge is cleared; it is **not successfully commandeered**. Stanley also **redirects a heavy gun into three small saucers** (~01:07–01:10). Nash spends turns investigating an orange crate: **Squirts has stowed away with Chunks**, laughs, licks his hand and hides among the fruit. The audience knows who she is before Nash identifies her. Her attempted **orange-juice squirt misses** him. **No route or time of her boarding is revealed.**
8. **Moon rockets carry three mechs (~01:01–01:27).** A flare from the enemy ship precedes warning lights and **three launches from the moon**. The rockets shed their shells, delivering piloted giant machines onto the main airship: a sword-wielding flying mech, a machine-gun mech and a squat charging-cannon mech. Their names/calls are provisionally **Sunga, Fina and Blazar**; the early call sounds like one compound word in the ASRs. They project **a giant image of Nash with a flashing X**. This supports targeting Nash, **not an independently verified interstellar warrant**. Orcs are shot; one smaller boat appears to go down, while the other is explicitly present later. **The changing battle description does not support counting both escorts permanently destroyed.**
9. **Nash becomes a dragon; the bridge mission ends (~01:19–01:34).** Nash transforms into a **young black dragon**, breaking the orange crate. **Squirts narrowly stops herself sliding off the deck**; Chunks is still present afterward. Nash flies to the mechs and uses **acid breath**, damaging the sword mech and destroying **two saucers**. The GM briefly calls the breath “lightning” while the action and damage discussion identify acid; **do not infer a second breath attack or damage type**. Vorel kills the last bridge operator, **smashes a warning-marked console and bites off its projecting keypad**; no self-destruct is confirmed. **Stanley Dimension Doors himself, Vorel and the case onto the machine-gun mech**, leaving the hostile ship falling without its crew.

## Squirts ends the moon crisis (~01:34–02:09)

10. **A real transported moon, not a dispellable illusion (~01:35–01:48).** Squirts's **Arcana 17** receives the GM's explanation that this is **a real moon brought from elsewhere**; *Dispel Magic* will not simply remove it. That spell is **not cast**. Vorel attempts to drop through the machine-gun mech's cockpit, gets **his backside stuck in reinforced glass**, then **Flame Bursts the pilot through its cracks**, filling it with smoke and also scorching Stanley. Nash **successfully reduces the sword mech to half size**, then flies away; its opportunity attack misses. Stanley **Telekinetically crashes two saucers into each other**. The heavy cannon continues charging but **never resolves its promised shot before the intervention**.
11. **Divine Intervention actually succeeds (~01:48–01:54).** Squirts grips a **Toyota-symbol necklace** and requests divine help, rolling **9** on percentile dice. Kristen identifies her trickster god with the spelling **O-L-I-D-A-M-M-A-R-A** (ASR Oldemara/Aldemara/Olidmara); **Olidammara** is therefore a **transcript-supported spelled form**, still awaiting audio confirmation. Kidigree is discussed separately; do not merge priest and god. Squirts asks for the moon to be put back or otherwise removed to safety. The GM declares **the moon, mechs, Tungan occupants and surviving enemy ships gone**, pauses to check the feature, then **reaffirms that result** after the break. **Existing damage and wreckage remain**; a falling orc escort crashes below. There is **no played mecha-piloting solution**, and the moon's new destination, inhabitants' later lives and whether it physically returned home are not observed. Stanley **casts Feather Fall** so he and Vorel descend safely back to the airship; **Hope is elsewhere**, despite a stray “Hope” in the GM's party list.
12. **Nash gains followers for someone else's miracle (~01:55–02:09).** Nash lands and returns to humanoid form. An orc says **Admiral Walls went overboard and is missing**, later reports him dead; **no body is recovered**. The crew wrongly credits Nash with removing the enemies. **Farco** leads their pledge. Nash **chooses “Dragon Lord”**, the crew adds the required **“Captain”**, and **Captain Dragon Lord** is locally acclaimed, not a universal title or proof Nash performed the intervention. Nash names the main vessel **The Great Angelashi**, after his wife, and orcs begin painting it. Farco introduces **Orc Airship Worker Union** obligations, whose wider enforceability remains his account. The crew recognizes Vorel's **M-sports championship**. **Stanley saves an overexcited falling orc with Telekinesis**; that rescued individual is not automatically the admiral or the orc who fell during combat. The party resumes its **intended journey to Sigil** with Squirts and the relic.

## Fruit, two portals and the actual ending (~02:09–02:57)

13. **A missing captain and a witness to the ritual (~02:09–02:26).** After several hours flying near the apparently unreachable spire, Farco says the captain must **open the air route to Sigil**. **Stanley sends a stone-message to Captain Meart/Myard** asking for help; **no reply arrives in E104**. Farco privately reveals that the former admiral's **monkey-faced servant Snatch** saw the protected ritual. Nash reassures Snatch with **Persuasion 22**. Snatch says to **lay out orange, banana, apple**, then **smash the banana, throw the orange out the window, eat the apple, and say “Calabazoom”** (working spelling). Farco hears this despite professed union restrictions. Nash spares him and later **actually renames him Samuel/Sam**, after a loyal dog from his Solar Mines past. **Farco and Sam are the same orc**, not two officers; “Farquad” is Nash's repeated mistake, not a confirmed separate crew member.
14. **Wrong order opens the first portal (~02:26–02:34).** Nash instead **throws the orange out first, then smashes the banana, eats the apple and speaks the word**. A portal appears. Snatch points out the reversed actions. Nash initially gives the others a confident account; **Stanley questions him and gets Snatch to admit the mistake**. The crew has already set the ship moving into the gate. **A visible portal is not proof of the intended destination or a correctly performed ritual.**
15. **Escape saves the party and case, not the whole crew (~02:34–02:43).** With roughly thirty seconds available, **Nash carries Sam using his integrated flying underwear**; Sam becomes rigid/catatonic with fear. **Stanley Dimension Doors Vorel, the sealed box and grabbed fruit onto the remaining small boat**. **Squirts uses her own Dimension Door**, bringing Chunks. Nash had intended to take Snatch, but after learning Sam knows the sequence, **leaves without a depicted rescue of the monkey**. **The Great Angelashi enters the wrong portal, is seized and crushed by giant tentacles, and is dragged through in pieces**. This destruction is directly narrated; the later cast calls it an airship full of dead orcs. **Exact casualties, Snatch's individual fate and any unseen survivors are not independently checked.** Fruit is retconned/adjudicated through luck rolls after several people say they gathered all three: they have **many apples, a banana in Nash's bandages and an orange in Sam's pocket**. Use that final available inventory, rather than inventing additional missing fruit or a second expedition.
16. **The corrected ritual reaches only another cliffhanger (~02:44–02:54).** **Stanley casts Lesser Restoration on Sam**, with the GM allowing it to clear this nonstandard catatonia. A proposed memory alteration is **not cast**. Sam recalls the **banana–orange–apple action order**. The small ship has no window, so they go below; **Stanley cleans the waste bucket with Prestidigitation**, and **Sam actually saws a square hole and builds a wooden window frame** over about five to ten minutes. Nash lays out **orange–banana–apple**, then **smashes banana, throws orange through the new window, eats apple and says the word**. The new portal has **red/yellow/purple** swirls; Sam says its colors look correct. The GM counts **ten orcs aboard the surviving boat**. **The craft enters and the portal closes; the episode explicitly withholds where it went.** Ten is a current onboard count, not the campaign's final orc survivor total.
17. **Postgame and outro (~02:55–02:57:02).** The cast jokes about Nash's accidental body count and praises the fruit sequence; Beau says only one detail had been reversed. These are **postgame remarks**, not additional deaths played after the crossing. Membership credits, network sign-off and a final **“So we did it”** follow in both exports.
