## High-priority distinctions

- **Death/resurrection versus witness:** E015 stages **Tylander's apparent death**. E016 stages the **arm attack and dead Toyota Host** and gives a **magistrate's report** Tylander returned; the heroes **do not themselves verify a reanimated Tylander body**. The magistrate's explanation of a *demonic pact* is attributable and potentially self-serving. The Black Mine is a **new lead**, not yet explored.
- **Nash's dragon symptoms:** The black dragon is **externally observed** flying near Slavetown, and Nash's burning/scaling is depicted. The repeated word **“Mother”** is heard by **Nash**, not by everyone, and the caller's identity/recipient is unsettled. Do not reinterpret E000 solar-mine visions or the E009 dragon emergence as confirmation of a particular parentage.
- **Vorel's faith/rage:** The priestess's words about **Fuxi, Father of Fire** and the `Tepala` title are source claims. The vision and fiery rage are a **meaningful character experience**; their literal plane, divine authenticity and any formal character-build mechanics require audio and episode-era rules evidence. **Bok Bok's presence afterward** is a separate observable event.
- **Slavetown and exploitation:** The **Salivating Fields** is the reported evacuation/refuge location for captives, under Principle force. This changes their **location**, *not ownership/free status*. A magistrate's promise is not a rescue.
- **Orp's new danger:** Sending a man-eating giant toward the **Esophageal Pass** is an ethically consequential, enacted deception by **Nash and Vorel**. **No attack on specific traders is observed by the E016 ending.** They deliberately give him a **nonmagical dagger** presented as a communications channel.
- **Catherine and mines:** The magistrate's **999th level at the Black Mine** is a **reported land-record claim**. Keep it distinct from Tylander's evaporated purported deed in E015, the E012 copper/gold discrepancy, and the Oilbeards' many mining interests.
- **Time/location:** Vorel undergoes a private ritual and returns toward town near the end; don't put him physically at the officials' negotiations or Nash's dragon symptoms.
