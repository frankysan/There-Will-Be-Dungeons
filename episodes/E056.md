# E056 — Welcome back, George

> **Corrected Wastes E056** = original publisher **TWBD 57**. Direct sequel to [E055](E055.md), continuing the **stone storm over Tietkalla**. Provisional research from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E056.%20Welcome%20back%2C%20George.parakeet.processed.srt), **not independently audio-verified**. Episode-time offsets include show introductions and outro. The [April 2019 bonus](BONUS-2019-04.md) is **out-of-character discussion**, not an extra battle scene.

## Synopsis and evidence boundaries

The city is struck by falling stones and dangerous winds. Vorel, Nash and Stanley direct endangered residents toward **sewer/manhole shelters**, while Hope returns to the keep. Some civilians are **directly killed** by the catastrophe; the evacuation is neither instantaneous nor guaranteed successful. Nobiery convenes the four beneath the keep to send **Hope into an ethereal realm** to consult Akmenos ancestors. Vorel/Nash/Stanley defend the ritual from hostile spirits, including **the ghost of George Miller**, whose murder occurred in E045; a later apparition identified as **Martha Miller** appears, and **P0D's spirit talks to Nash**, without bodily resurrection. Hope receives an ethically charged test from four ancestral spirits, then encounters the oil-demon **Quizquaz**, who offers another bargain and makes startling **claims** about **Andreeste**, a name approximately `Dollo Poppio`, the Soulbroker's legal defense, and a **dragon destroyed on entry to the world**. These remain **Quizquaz's testimony**, not a witnessed verification of every cause.

The hostile ghost **prematurely ages Vorel**, but the **Tidebearer priestess Emenil/Eminel** performs **Greater Restoration**, returning his body to normal during this very episode. Stanley demonstrates newly practiced **Fishbird/Malhya water creation**. Then Nobiery lets slip that the royal entourage has made deals with demonic powers. Emenil recoils and says citizens deserve to know; while Stanley tries to reason with her, **Nobiery orders an animated corpse called Carnage to bite/attack her**. **The session ends mid-assault—her death is NOT confirmed.**

## Character, cosmology and political cautions

- **George and P0D remain deceased**: ghostly appearances are not resurrection. **Martha**'s appearance is further evidence of a connection to George, but whether her spirit's apparent identity and personal history are accurate is **not yet independently proven**. Their appearance also does **not** prove every NPC's claimed afterlife doctrine.
- **Vorel restores during E056**. He should not appear permanently aged in current character references because of the mid-episode ghost encounter.
- **Quizquaz speaks on behalf of his claimed lord** and makes an offer tied to **Melgor and fifty souls**. Neither **legal proceeding**, **dragon death**, **contractor's exact name**, nor supposed heavenly/ethereal status becomes objective historical fact solely from his speech.
- **Nobiery, Tidebearers and Hope** now face a real, newly played **legitimacy/secret-pacts dispute**, and **Nobiery's ordered assault on Emenil is directly witnessed**. The priestess survives at least to the E056 cutoff; death/dismemberment not decided at the end.
- **The city storm causes direct civilian deaths**; resettlement is incomplete. No confirmed population total, strike mechanism, or external battle-site survey.
- **Name uncertainty:** `Emenil/Eminel`, `Fibel/Fibble`, `Malhya/Malhaia`, `Dollo Poppio/Doro Popio`, `Theresa` and the `Carnage` tattoo/body number require review. **Eye of Groxl** remains the **owner-confirmed canonical single item** despite in-session `Grojil` ASR. This episode's content does **not** change that decision.
