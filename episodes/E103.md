# E103 — When in doubt, burn it

> **The Wastes · corrected S1E103 · Arc Five · ASR-provisional played-episode dossier.** [Parakeet processed source](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E103.%20When%20in%20doubt%2C%20burn%20it.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled source](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E103.%20When%20in%20doubt%2C%20burn%20it.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [full-text ASR comparison](../reports/E103_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). The host says **Sunday, 10 May 2020** (spoken date, no independent podcast-release audit). Both processed SRTs reach **~02:44:13**, including postgame/network outro. No original audio heard. **Steerious Business** is the **user-confirmed canonical spelling** of the minotaur's steer/serious joke, even when an ASR writes “Serious,” “Stereus” or “Mysterious Business.” **Celibus Stump** is the existing [E011 dossier](E011.md)'s *working* spelling; this episode's outputs vary “Celivus/Sellevus/Syllabus,” requiring listening, not a new character.

**Played E103 cutoff:** In **Tietkalla**, Hope returns to an inhabited but politically fractured city, **kills acting chancellor Celibus Stump with Finger of Death**, and defeats his guard contingent with Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider. She learns **three months have passed in Tietkalla**, consults old allies and hears reports (not independent proof) of the Principle's influence, alleged Liberator train raids and vanished political opponents. The hooded **Hand of the Voice** patron demands a new evil act to retain her power; Hope **refuses to murder priestess Emenil or a random resident**, but participates in deliberately **burning an unoccupied railway station** with Blood Skunk's burning arrow and Dr. Spider's *Wall of Fire*. Separately, **Nash, Stanley and Vorel remain aboard a chartered airship heading to Sigil with the still-sealed Fist gauntlet**; Nash's huge moon appears to be **approaching, not merely growing**, and **a saucer squadron plus an armed larger craft open fire around their airship at the cliffhanger**. **No crash, collision, airship loss, completed moon solution, Tobacho delivery or Earth return is resolved in this episode.**

## Hope returns to Tietkalla (~00:04–00:28)

1. **The completed E102 gate leads to a fuller group (~00:04–00:11).** Hope, **Steerious Business** (Jon's suited minotaur), **Blood Skunk Hendrickson** (Scott's demonic harlequin-like archer) and **Dr. Spider** (Kyle's spider-bodied physician) emerge in **Hope's familiar royal bedroom**. **Spatulo also emerges immediately afterward** from the dissipating portal mist; he is **alive and physically in Tietkalla**, superseding the E102 trio's false report that he died. Spatulo grasps Hope's transformed arm and bitterly protests she nearly abandoned him. Hope **wins the contested Intimidation check** (reported **15**), causing him to release her, but **does not kill, release, reconcile with or dismiss him**. He trails the entourage later. The **Hand remains attached** under the appearance choker; no new pact negotiated.
2. **The new helpers reveal methods but not loyalty (~00:07–00:17).** Hope learns that the men technically follow Brett's orders and are here to help. Blood Skunk speaks in a rough, eager voice, often breathing raspingly; Steerious is physically imposing and carries an enormous flattened-coffin-like greatsword; **Dr. Spider has eight walking spider legs, two arms, a physician's coat and a baby-linked device/tank on his equipment cart**, finally visible after the mic issue in E102. The infant in the glass container communicates or vocalizes through equipment; **its nature, welfare and degree of independent agency are not established**. No claim that the companions have stopped reporting to Brett is made.
3. **Secret passage reveals the throne-room crisis (~00:11–00:23).** The halls look familiar but strangely quiet. Hope listens from the royal passage into the throne room. **Tiefling residents are alive, gathered and able to petition**, with the partly restored ceiling/guard posts still recognizable. Her throne is occupied by the Slavetown businessman **Celibus Stump**—previously associated with the **Buxom Confederate**, now acting as chancellor. The guards include **Toyota Host** gang-like enforcers and two **Kuromakishi (?) orc warriors** with firearms/blades. A craft/armorer-house speaker (**Keith Ickshil (?)**, name very uncertain) complains that **steel/textile shipments from Principle-associated rail links have been interrupted** despite outgoing water. Celibus attributes the attacks to **Liberators**, voices suspicion of local sympathizers, and tries to mediate the houses. These are **character reports and accusations**; no scene yet proves who actually raids the trains or ordered disappearances.
4. **Immediate execution of the chancellor (~00:23–00:29).** Hope walks into the public gallery, the three new companions flank her, and **Celibus greets her warmly, offering an embrace**. Hope **physically seizes his neck with her hidden altered hand and casts *Finger of Death***; the GM reports **54 necrotic damage and immediate death**. His last reaction is to ask why. **Celibus Stump dies in play**; it is **not merely a threatened attack, illusion or an outcome inferred from later Q&A**. Hope then directs her followers to fight the **non-tiefling guards in the room**. **This is a specific throne-room order, not a public policy to kill all non-tieflings everywhere**; she explicitly clarifies its local scope later. The civilian gallery flees; the armed men attack.

## First battle with Hope's new companions (~00:29–01:50)

5. **Opening barrage and unfamiliar abilities (~00:29–00:52).** Hope fires **Scully**, explicitly receiving **three attacks**, killing **Sir Pork Napkin (?)** (one of the Kuromakishi) and injuring **Sir Lamb Puncher (?)**, who attempts escape through the crowd. Dr. Spider channels ***Guardian of Faith*** through his baby-linked hose as a **spectral ring/cloud of golden giggling infants**; its **20 radiant damage on failed saves** destroys several advancing Toyota Host guards before the manifestation vanishes. **Steerious** cleaves attackers with a **greatsword**, enters **Rage**, and displays a **scorpion-like tail** (his beast-form trait) and later **an echo/double**. The Echo and tail are actual adjudicated combat features, though class-sheet configuration is not independently verified. Hope uses ***Slow***, affecting **four guards** (two reported saves pass), and her **Hand-enhanced cold dagger attacks** later kill others.
6. **Blood Skunk's luck and temporary summoned creature (~00:39–01:33).** Blood Skunk first struggles with a bow in melee and dual swords, then suffers critical failures: his **harlequin racial “Critical Fortunes”** result sends his two held short swords **120 feet away**. No sword recovery is shown at that time. The cast identifies **custom demon-clown/Harlequinari** and demonic-spider race concepts, **not standard D&D rulebooks**. Dr. Spider uses a separate **healing beam** to restore Blood Skunk's health and later a ***Sacred Flame*** attempt misses; the exact prepared class abilities are still being finalized during play. Blood Skunk finally ***Conjures Animals***, selecting a **giant constrictor snake**, whose initial natural-one bite **strikes its own tail** under the GM's ruling; it later consumes defeated guard corpses. A proposed ***Hunter's Mark*** is **not cast** because it would replace the snake spell's concentration. Blood Skunk **does kill the last escaping Toyota Host with a bow shot** (~01:40); do not reduce him to only failed attacks.
7. **One guard captured then killed; dead chancellor questioned (~01:17–01:50).** Hope uses **Action Surge** to seize the fleeing Kuromakishi warrior, later uses **nonlethal cold dagger damage** to leave him unconscious. She orders tieflings out of danger and to **spread word the queen has returned**. When Hope attempts questioning, **Steerious stomps on the captive and kills him immediately**, inadvertently ending the live interrogation. **Dr. Spider then casts *Speak with Dead* on Celibus Stump**, dramatizing the spell with a **stethoscope/scalpel** and up to **five questions**. The animated corpse says Hope/the queen has been gone **three months**, claims **Celibus himself has been in charge**, condemns the **Liberators/Jeweler**, and says **the Principle owns everything**. As ***Speak with Dead*** explicitly does **not compel truthful answers**, these are **attributed corpse utterances**, not verified descriptions of legal sovereignty, conspirators or the Jeweler's current role. **Celibus is still dead afterward**, not resurrected.

## Looking for her city and the patron's price (~01:50–02:24)

8. **Sky phenomenon, rains and Jimster (~01:50–01:58).** On leaving the throne room, Hope sees the repairs to the keep still unfinished, a **blue-white crack in the northwest sky** apparently toward/beyond **Razak Val / Daemon Vold**, and an unfamiliar bank of **purple-bottomed storm clouds**. **Jimster** from Hope's Dusthill past greets her by the garage; he says **rain has fallen roughly every two days since the crack appeared**. Hope actually **feels heat on her skin again for the first time since attaching the Hand**—a bodily sensation under local sunlight, not proof the Hand has been removed or its cold effect permanently cured. Jimster says Celibus became a **Principle-backed interim chancellor after disputes among the houses**, with **Nobiery/Nobieri** unable to hold the groups together. These are **Jimster's reports**, not witness to the private appointment ceremony. He invites Hope and her entourage to a **future dinner with Edna**, his new companion, after preparations; **the dinner has not happened by cutoff**.
9. **Golden Sands is still operating (~01:57–02:00).** Hope transforms using the **Necklace of Infinite Appearances**, becoming an **older human beggar “Greg”**, and discreetly checks the **Golden Sands inn**. She **sees Bok Bok serving from behind the counter** and a waiter whose name may be **Fibble/Fiddles (?)** working amid patrons. The inn is operating, although nearby housing is worn. **This directly disproves any claim Tietkalla is wholly destroyed or its inhabitants all gone**, without demonstrating that every district and citizen is safe.
10. **Emenil's separate testimony (~02:00–02:09).** Hope visits the newly built **Fishbird/Tidebearer church**, initially disguised, then reveals herself privately to **priestess Emenil**. Emenil bows and welcomes her queen. She says **she and Nobiery disagreed about spiritual priorities versus governing**, after which **Nobiery disappeared from city politics**; Celibus, an envoy from the **Principle's cities**, seemed capable of solving problems and was raised as acting chancellor until Hope's return. **Rail service to Tietkalla had begun**, but incoming supplies have been **raided**, reportedly by Liberators, while **outgoing water trains go untouched**. A city investigation party has **not returned**. Emenil warns that some royal **houses** allied with Celibus (including **Grimoire Macab/Makab (?)**, banker/head of one house), and she **reports rumors of an “inquisition” and three opponents disappearing during the night**. These **are secondhand claims and rumors, not witnessed executions, proven perpetrators or an authenticated missing-person list**. Hope asks to be notified when investigators return; **none return here**.
11. **The Voice demands a new evil act (~02:09–02:14).** The **hooded patron physically appears in the church to Hope** and asks how killing the chancellor felt. He demands she kill **Emenil, the Fishbird priestess**, as a display of evil in exchange for continued enjoyment of his **Hand powers**. **Hope refuses to kill Emenil**; he next tells her to kill the first person seen outside. **Hope does not comply with either murder instruction**. The **Hand becomes heavy** and the GM clarifies that **she will be locked out of its granted powers until a qualifying evil act is committed**. This is a **conditional patron-imposed loss/withholding**, not proof that all her mundane strength, ordinary spells or independent equipped gifts vanished. No specific on-screen combat spell is tried to test the precise subset disabled.
12. **Destruction of the railway station (~02:14–02:23).** Outside the church, Hope asks the new helpers what evil act to perform. Blood Skunk proposes **burning the church**, Steerious suggests **sowing disorder**, and they agree instead to destroy **Tietkalla's railway station**. It is **temporarily shut/empty** amid the raids, with **no train, staff or victim observed on the platform**. **Blood Skunk fires an actual magically flaming arrow** into the station building, hitting and **starting a fire**. **Dr. Spider then casts *Wall of Fire* to surround the building**, creating a destructive pillar of flames; both acts are played. A passerby cries out, and residents **begin fetching water buckets to put it out**. Hope, still appearing as **Greg**, falsely tells an onlooker she saw unidentified figures flee; he notices the conspicuous minotaur, clown and spider. **Blood Skunk intimidates him into silence**, or at least makes the man less likely to report. Hope and her companions watch the building burn. **The narrator treats the station as engulfed and made inoperable, but no later repair, casualties, citywide war, completed railroad loss or demonstrated reactivation of the patron's powers is shown before the episode cuts away.** Calling the fire an “evil act” is the characters' purpose, not an automatic verified fulfillment of every patron condition.

## The returning airship and the moon (~02:23–02:44)

13. **Crew hierarchy and growing threat (~02:23–02:31).** The original three heroes are still **on the airborne vessel bound for Sigil**, carrying the **locked enchanted chest with gauntlet, not Hope's Hand**. The elf charter captain/admiral **Bubbin Walls (?)** (rendered *Bubbinwals/Bubbinwols/Balinwols* in the ASRs) commands a mixed **non-orc contracted operating crew** of **halflings and goblins**, whereas the armed **orcs are mercenaries associated with Spatulo's mission**. The party had incorrectly reported Spatulo dead in E102; the audience now knows he is **physically with Hope**. The captain says the **city-covered Tunga moon has become drastically larger in the sky**, with inhabitants displaying flashing emergency signals. He fears collision would destroy them and that an airship cannot simply outrun it.
14. **Unresolved cause and trust after *Modify Memory* (~02:30–02:39).** Vorel challenges Stanley about **altering Alentris's memory** in E102 and worries Stanley might eventually modify theirs. **Stanley admits the act toward Alentris but says he has not erased his companions' memories**; neither an independent test of his statement nor a new cast is performed. The players joke about writing notes and checking spell exhaustion; **the argument does not itself establish hidden additional memory edits**. Nash says the moon was spawned by **uncontrolled wild magic**, that his attempted visit with Walnut yielded no useful explanation, and that the crew should perhaps avoid its path. The captain questions whether the moon is **growing** or **approaching**; after **Arcana 16** Nash has a **gut impression it is falling**, **not a mathematically measured orbit, precise collision time or successful solution**.
15. **An actual UFO attack, cliffhanger (~02:39–02:41).** A **squadron of silver flying saucers appears from the moon** and surrounds the ships. A **larger armed craft**, apparently constructed from linked saucers, **decloaks astern with two large guns and fires bright laser-like shots around/toward the airship** as those aboard take cover. The captain calls battle stations. **This is a real hostile contact/opening attack**, not merely the captain's fear or the GM's abandoned idea. **The battle map and its result are for E104**: no damage total, casualty, successful defense, destroyed UFO, landing, moon impact, or outcome of the **Tobacho bargain** is resolved before E103 ends. The GM says in postgame there is a possible way through the forthcoming encounter to save the wider situation, but this is **out-of-character teasing, not a deployed plan or foreshadowed guaranteed rescue**.
