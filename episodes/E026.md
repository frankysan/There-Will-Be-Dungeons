## Major identity and source checks

- **Bertrand, Trigger and Bok Bok** are confirmed in the prison. E025 only establishes the lockup and Bok Bok's recognizable voice. The restored **Fate 4** is present and driven out **E026**; do not silently restore it in E017–E025.
- **Scrolly's violent treatment** is an E025 action, corroborated as troubling in E026's **listener email** and NPC response. Audience reactions and character ethics are not automatic alignment changes, yet the *physical actions* are directly in the previous SRT.
- **Lactids** (Bertrand's spoken name or ASR) are described as a **Principle City underworld network** and alleged controllers of minds, but do not declare all E013 Sapphire attackers psychically controlled or Slavetown definitely captured.
- **Tylander's prior execution** (~10 years ago) is a **Bertrand claim** aligning with E021's reported history of death and return. The **deed/dragon metal** connection is his theory, and Soulbroker E024's **ritual reagent** explanation is a *different interested source*.
- **Brent:** Separately index owner working spelling `Slippery Brent` and transcript `Brint` variants, effects of Pixie Blush, and the party's *coercion/assault*, rather than turning him into a faceless monster or proven cartel spy.
- **Sightless orc:** No given name is a **chosen refusal of former Koromukishi identity**, not missing metadata to invent. His anti-Principle view and knowledge of past actions are first-person claims.
- **Nash's decision-making tension:** Concern that harming people and revealing the camp compromises their goals is **played character disagreement**, not an editorial judgment of player intent.
