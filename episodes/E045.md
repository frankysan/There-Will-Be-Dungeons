## Cross-episode facts and open interpretations

- **George: live E041–E044 → dead E045.** E042's self-inflicted foot wound is treated E044; E045's death is a **deliberate attack by Dion**, **not another accident or an outcome of the black-pudding battle**. Stanley's **E044 plea for better morality** directly contrasts with Dion's first action after waking.
- **Sphere/core inventory:** E041 takes **intact unknown sphere**; E042 ruptures it and keeps **black core**; **E045 reveals a biting mobile creature and Nash destroys it**. Do not retroactively label it a confirmed froghemoth egg or continue to list it as a carried specimen.
- **Basilisk vs shadow vs headache figure:** A **basilisk is killed**, a **separate wall-shadow encounter disperses into smaller shadows**, and a **dark, head-hurting river creature is slain**. These are separate sources of danger, and E042's stone boots and E045's stone-like worm remain speculative links to basilisk petrification.
- **Private in-world letter:** The writing taken from the **horned tiefling skeleton** was delivered privately to Dion/player via GM message. Exact contents are **not available from the SRT**; document the item, not a fabricated transcription.
- **River bargain still pending:** **E044 Hungui** accepted some rent/gems but made crossing conditional on addressing headache man. **E045 kills a matching river entity**, yet the party has not reported back or crossed by episode close.
- **Show-era effects:** Hope's worn armor changes and gun jam, petrification saves, shadow-drain condition, basilisk-derived javelin and magic use are provisional **table adjudications**; do not infer the end-campaign character sheet.
