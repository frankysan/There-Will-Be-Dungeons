## Key continuity findings

- **Stanley's archfey incident is not resolved as a benign consensual pact.** E029 shows his explicit refusal followed by forced blue light; E030 does **not establish that every party member knows what happened**. A dream experienced by the others does not retroactively confer knowledge of the private scene.
- **Immobilized Nash** was a deliberately restrained sleepwalker, freed in E030 by **Reduce**. He is **not** among the victims dragged into Blort's den and is reunited at the surface.
- **Immortality/necromancy and other lore:** The hag's claim that elves “rot” and an archfey prison existed is **antagonist testimony**, not proven history. Cat Buttons's Arcanum background remains **his personal account**.
- **Loss and acquisition:** **Immovable rod submerged/lost** near underground exit. **Blink spell text** offered to Stanley is a separate, newly acquired potential resource. The original **Fate 4** and companions survive escape; do not infer the RV has already entered the Teet.
- **Teet scope:** The party **approaches/parks outside the town** and plans reconnaissance. A later confrontation with **Guzzle Gang or Acrimonicar** has **not happened** by E030 cutoff.
- **Two source categories for later Teet map:** Buttons's oral lore and older **Handsome Dan Dakota** map; a fan-wiki map dated later is not independent validation of episode-era geometry. [Image provenance](../project/IMAGE_ASSETS.md).
