# E066 — The Eyes Have It

> **Corrected Wastes E066**, continuing the [E065](E065.md) Tyrannosaurus rex cliffhanger. Provisional source-first dossier from the [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E066.%20The%20Eyes%20Have%20It.parakeet.processed.srt), **not verified by listening**. Recording offsets are approximate; opening email, recap, intermission and outro are not played events. **Spoilers through E066**.

## At a glance

**Fate 4 is crushed by the T. rex and subsequently explodes.** Vorel lures the predator away, it falls into **Nash's deep storm-drain pit**, and Vorel kills it with the **Eye of Groxl's flaming returning shield** after Nash's Fireball. Vorel crafts a **T. rex-tooth dagger** and salvages wreckage into a bone-and-vehicle-parts **rickshaw**, provisionally called the **Fang Fate Five**; the dense forest soon makes it impractical for travel.

A telepathic, floating **mind flayer** introduces himself with a name like **Uvexiad / Oubvayx / Ovaxiad** (ASR highly unstable) and professes service to **Dollo Poppio**, the **“wizened wyrm”**, while menacing the party with apparently enthralled lizardfolk. He attempts to bargain with **Hope**, claiming **Andreeste** covets Tietkalla's souls and that killing the **Principle** would discharge the Soulbroker/Catoblepraithe deal while surrendering Tietkalla. **Hope rejects another demonic bargain.** The mind flayer holds **Sira/Sera** aloft, kills **Rorf** by horribly folding his shapeshifted bird body, grievously injures **Follow**, and departs with the girl. After charging the group to retrieve Sira at **Kervalon/Kaer Valon**, he conjures a vast **eyeball-tree forest** in the purple sands. **Follow dies, after Stanley promises to save Sira.** The group **buries him**. This is **an observed character death**, not merely a disappearance.

The party then encounters **a settlement of skeletal-looking people** who believe themselves to be space travelers. Their leader, **Sergeant T'Lupa/Tulupa** (provisional), identifies a crew from another planet and offers a **glowing, apparently spectral green horse and carriage** that can bend the forest's trees aside. He also hands over a glowing **cylinder/coil** described as a means of powering a nearby buried/crashed ship. Their extraterrestrial history and interpretation of the surrounding realm are **their testimony**, not independently proven cosmology. The group leaves on the carriage toward Kervalon, **without reaching the keep in E066**.

## Continuity and verification boundaries

**Deaths and promises:** **Follow and Rorf die E066**, Sira is **abducted alive as last depicted** but not yet rescued. Hope **does not surrender Tietkalla** or conclude the offered demonic deal. Stanley promises Follow to rescue the child. The mind flayer's **Oubvayx/Uvexiad** name, master titles and Kervalon spelling are provisional. **Don't treat a subsequent glimpse of a floating projection as proof a body was already killed or that the apparent omnipotence is literal.**

**Equipment:** **Fate 4's destruction is explicit and final for the vehicle observed**, absent future repair/replacement evidence. **Fang Fate Five** is a **handmade wheeled rickshaw** with recovered truck parts, not a numbered model of the RV; **Vorel's new tooth dagger** is a separate crafted item. A **glowing cylinder** is accepted from the skeleton traveler; its exact custody, composition and operation are not confirmed. The **Eye of Groxl shield** and **Mawstorm sword** remain distinct.

**World testimony:** The stranger's stars/ship journey, the mind flayer's claims of demonic legal supremacy, and the interpretation of the forest as another world are **statements**, not yet settled facts. The **persistent night and rain** and **eyeball trees** are visibly real to the party. The skeletal travelers may be **misperceiving their own appearance or environment**; do not classify their claimed species without more sources.

**Outstanding listening:** Check destruction and explosion times (~00:18–00:31), **rickshaw name** (~00:41), mind flayer's **spoken name/master** (~00:53–01:00), identity/cause of **Rorf's death and Sira's kidnapping** (~01:13–01:17), **Follow's explicit last breath** (~01:21–01:23), cosmic crew names/coil transfer (~02:10–02:25), and glowing carriage properties (~02:27–02:31).
