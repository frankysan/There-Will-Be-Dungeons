# E084 — Select Your Champion

> **The Wastes, Arc 3, Sigil.** First-pass, **processed-ASR-only dossier** from [Parakeet](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E084.%20Select%20Your%20Champion.parakeet.processed.srt) and [Qwen Q8 / Canary CTC / Nemotron-labelled export](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E084.%20Select%20Your%20Champion.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt). Approximate recording length **2 h 52 m**, including recap, break and outro. **Neither model was verified against the audio**, and the files' speaker IDs are not independently matched to performers. Source spellings and contested rules are identified below. The [E083 dossier](E083.md) ends with the party looking for a tournament strategy book; [December 2019 Q&A](BONUS-2019-12.md) is **retrospective commentary**, not a played scene. **Inverse is a separate reality.**

## E084 in brief — outcomes, not plans

- **Vorel, Nash, Squirts and Nothing take a strategy book** from an M-sports library reader by deception and leave with it. **Vorel knocks the pursuing halfling unconscious, nonlethally** (the party checks a heartbeat). A trickster cleric supplies a **forgetfulness preparation**, administered by Squirts; its exact memory effects are **never tested**. They leave the halfling near the library.
- **The trickster priest identifies his intended humiliation target** as the young **unbeaten BMF champion and strategy author** (rendered **Kenja/Kanja/Kanja-like**, canonical spelling uncertain). He proposes **rigging Vorel's bracket and using sickness-causing needles on Vorel's opponents** in exchange for a later attempt to restore Hope and Stanley. **This is a planned fraud with one executed intervention, not an honorable tournament victory plan**. There is **no resurrection**.
- **Nash regrows an additional arm/hand and one foot** after another overnight rest. He still **floats as a heart** with an eye, mouth and other small protrusions, **not** as a restored human.
- **Nothing privately receives a dangerous kidnapping assignment** from a fixer called **Pelvis**, contacts an anonymous client through a magic shop and portal device, and is tasked with abducting **Stevius Ronkly (?)** to an Abyss-linked address. **No kidnapping is begun or completed by episode end**; the other companions are not briefed on the details.
- **Qualifier matches actually occur.** Vorel wins with **Kung/Kong** against a sickened gnome using **Rubles**; Nash wins as **Rubles** against an elderly female orc playing **Bland**; Nothing and a second kenku wait each other out, causing a **double forfeit**. There is **no prize payout, finals match against the champion or resurrection**.

## The book, the halfling and the trickster's new plan (~00:02–00:47)

The session resumes immediately where [E083](E083.md) ended: Nash has spotted the sought-after **champion-authored S-tier guide to Big Time Smack'em Brothers** in the hands of a **halfling reader** at the small M-sports library. The reader refuses to share. Nash **threatens him**, then persuades him to step outside for a confrontation, leaving the book behind. Vorel **stealthily takes the book** from the desk and passes it to Nothing for concealment. There is a rear **“Authorized Personnel Only”** door, but the party ultimately leaves through the front. The halfling notices that the book is missing and chases them into the streets.

Squirts leads them along alleys. Vorel uses his **color-changing camouflage** to hide and lands a **nonlethal blow for nine damage**, rendering the pursuing halfling unconscious. He **carries the halfling** with them; back at Squirts' church, Nash urges checking that the victim is alive, and Vorel's **Medicine check confirms a pulse**. An unconscious victim **is not a corpse**, and any broken-neck description should not be promoted to a lethal outcome.

The **hidden trickster church** appears again **in a different district** than the first visit: Squirts thinks of the destination, finds a familiar alley and opens its heavy door with her characteristic comic sound. Its entrance disappears when the group later looks back. Squirts asks for **forget-me powder** to erase the halfling's memory of the book and tournament. The birdlike priest **Kidigree/Kidagri (?)**, with the church's human and goblin staff, provides **petals to crush**. Squirts mixes the powder, floor dirt and saliva into a **salt shaker** and administers it to the unconscious halfling by massaging his throat, giving him some superficial scratches. The cleric does **not specify the amount of memory removed**; the party has **no later evidence that the dose erased any particular event**. They place him on the steps near the library after leaving the church. Some passersby see the deposit but do not intervene.

The priest **now names the target for the promised cosmic humiliation**: the **young, undefeated BMF champion** whose guide the group stole. He has allegedly won **ten annual tournaments**. The cleric wants a **newcomer, Vorel**, to defeat him publicly and discredit his expertise. He promises to **manipulate tournament pairings** so Vorel faces easier opponents, while **Squirts will impair those opponents** with supplied **small sickness-causing “six-sticks”/syringe-like devices** (the quantity is described inconsistently as about sixteen and punning “six sticks”; do not assert a verified inventory count). He warns not to get caught. Vorel initially questions whether this is cheating, but the group agrees to pursue the plan because they hope to revive Hope and Stanley. **The cleric's own conflation of tricks and fair competition is not an editorial endorsement of honesty**.

When the group checks the **76 Mulberry Lane registration office**, Nash sees paperwork but **no posted bracket yet**. They return to the **Soggy Feather** with the stolen guide. Over **about six hours of in-world reading**, the party learns enough to **add a marionette-fighting skill notation and use Dexterity on attack rolls**, but Beau says **reading does not itself grant full proficiency/practice**. The new mini-game uses directional “happy stick” controls, fast/heavy punches and kicks, blocking/rush, hit points, stagger, daze, combo breakers and a possible **Big Time Smackeroo** knockout; **the actual match adjudications below supersede any perfectly tidy reconstruction of rules from the spoken tutorial**, which Beau revises during play.

## Dinner, Nothing's fixer and contestants' choices (~00:53–01:19)

At dinner Bill suggests that an M-fighting title is unlikely to replace regular employment; Vorel remains set on the tournament. **Bill and Pam** serve **chicken-and-pork “super stew.”** Vorel and Nash spend their newcomer vouchers; Squirts produces real copper after a failed bottle-cap offer, while **Nothing requests the cost on a tab**, not documented as paying the whole party. This is everyday innkeeping, not a money prize or bounty.

Nothing senses the approach of someone familiar and moves into a dark corner. A human man in a **bowler-like hat, vest, striped trousers and striking gold/diamond teeth** enters, asks Bill about a bird and finds Nothing. He is called **Pelvis** (repeatedly recognized in both ASR streams), an underworld **fixer who has previously supplied Nothing with jobs**. He calls the kenku **“Ebon/Eben Skulker,”** an apparent alias or underworld epithet, **not established as Nothing's legal birth name**. Pelvis offers **triple the usual fee** for an important assignment and gives Nothing an address to obtain a key and meet an anonymous client. The **actual fee amount, payer identity and job details are not yet told**. Nothing privately accepts; **Vorel, Nash and Squirts do not hear this conversation**. Pelvis departs with an audible escort or retinue.

At the dinner table the three registered competitors discuss characters. Vorel wants **Kung/Kong**, a lizardfolk fighter derided as a beginner choice; Nash wants **Buster Balls McBuster (“Balls”)**; Nothing looks at **Shadzy** and then **Scrumptious**, an avian chef fighter, and briefly produces a chef hat from cooking implements. **These are initial preferences**, not all characters later available in the qualifiers. Squirts reiterates her intention to interfere using the priest's needles; **she is not registered as a competitor**. Nothing's concealed note names a destination sounding like **47 Castamere/Castlemere Lane**; because it is late, she postpones the visit until morning.

## Next morning — Nash's regrowth and Nothing's secret client (~01:20–01:40)

Nash tests whether a heart-form body can urinate; he cannot, and a little blood leaks from an opening. **This does not demonstrate ordinary excretion or a new urethra.** During a further long rest, Beau grants **two regrowth choices**: Nash gains **a second functioning arm/hand and a foot**, retaining his earlier eye, mouth and other protrusions, and **continues to float**. Having hands may help operate the game controller; there is **no completed human-body restoration or witnessed spellcasting** here.

Nothing leaves the suite early and visits an **unnamed magic shop at the 47 Castamere/Castlemere Lane address** in the merchant district. An aged green-skinned **githyanki shopkeeper** recognizes the contact protocol and gives her a **small box containing a pyramid-shaped marble device with a button**. Using it inside a small private room moves Nothing to a **wooden space with an apparently endless ceiling** and a screen hiding an anonymous, **voice-distorted client**. This portal-like meeting **actually occurs**, unlike the yet-unperformed abduction.

The client requires **Stevius Ronkly (?)**, an important member of Sigil's **“Godsmen” (Parakeet) or “Guardsmen” (Qwen)**, to be **kidnapped from the high quarters**, transported to **10 Belshamer Lane (?) in the Hive**, and taken through a portal **into the Abyss**. The name and faction are **material ASR disagreements**; **do not silently identify a particular Planescape faction**. The client imposes **no more than seven days** to finish the job and says the **return portal stays available for only one hour** after use; otherwise Nothing risks remaining in the Abyss. Nothing is told to leave no witnesses, avoid capture, and contact Pelvis if equipment is needed. **The captive has not been located, seized, moved, or delivered**. Nothing returns to the Soggy Feather without giving the party these specifics.

## Royce venue: security, infiltration and tournament announcement (~01:40–02:12)

The group heads to the **Royce Colosseum/Gymnasium venue** in the athletic district for the next morning's **9 a.m. qualifying round**. Earlier E083 registration describes **Royce Gymnasium**; E084 sometimes says **Royce Colosseum**. Their identity or exact official venue name remains **unverified by audio**. A crowd lines up to enter. **Magic items, spells, auras and potions are barred**, with orc guards, an elven magical scanner and a disorganized confiscation bin. **Vorel passes**. Squirts' **forehead stone** is identified as magical; a narrated swallowing trick does not fool guards, and **Squirts is refused entry**. Nash's body looks like a magical heart, but the **scanner finds nothing and lets him in**; this is a **specific screening result**, not proof the heart is nonmagical.

Nothing steps aside instead of submitting to the scan. She **steals a small doll from the confiscation bin**, slips it onto another entrant and provokes a **fight at the gate** involving an orc, tabaxi and guards, then uses the diversion to **slip inside unnoticed**. The precise item and injuries are not yet tied to a larger magic plot.

A human-presenting worker quietly opens a side door for Squirts, then **reveals himself as Kidigree in disguise**; he intends to manipulate the bracket. Squirts invokes **Cloak of Shadows**, her trickster **Channel Divinity invisibility ability**. Beau debates its out-of-combat duration and provisionally rules that it can last indefinitely out of combat until a revealing action, while acknowledging this may be **incorrect and subject to later correction**. **Do not promote this improvised ruling to universal 5e mechanics.** The revelation supports Squirts as a trickster cleric at the played table; the deity name heard as **Olidammara / Old Mara** remains uncertain.

Announcers whose names sound like **Gully Plant** and **Truxlayer** tell the **twentieth annual tournament** crowd that a modron production problem limits qualifiers to just **three selectable characters: Bland, Kung/Kong and Rubles/Rubbles**. They also announce a **mysterious additional 4,000-gold donation** for the whole tournament and claim **10,000 entrants across five qualifier sites**. The E083 advertisement had offered **1,000 gold**; the new donation is an **announcement**, not prize money awarded, and **the total prize pool/actual payout should not be derived without a verified baseline and organizer terms**. No connection of the anonymous benefactor to Kidigree is established.

## The three played matches (~02:07–02:49)

**Vorel vs Randorf (~02:07–02:24).** Before the opposing gnome meets Vorel, **Squirts briefly becomes invisible and pricks him with one of the sickness syringes**, reappearing as the action resolves. The gnome notices a strange jab, rubs his leg, and becomes increasingly **ill or unable to complete input actions** as the match progresses; **one actual needle use is shown**. Vorel and the gnome shake hands sportsmanlike. Vorel chooses **Kung**; Randorf chooses **Rubles**. The older gnome initially lands combos and nearly wins via stagger. Vorel uses **Face Bite** to heal, plus kicks and punches, and his final blow **defeats Rubles by HP**. The opponent says afterward he feels unwell and suspects a kobold. **Vorel's first-round win is real; calling it clean or unaffected by deliberate sabotage would be misleading**. No final victory over the champion.

**Nash vs Farfrid (~02:24–02:43).** Nash, though initially preferring **Balls**, must choose among three enabled marionettes and selects **Rubles** against the elderly female orc's **Bland**. The opponent is initially kindly, then increasingly frustrated. Both players use blocks, rush, dazing, combo-breakers and stagger. Nash **misses an early Big Time Smackeroo**, is damaged by a strong counter, then builds Bland above **100 stagger** and succeeds with a later **Big Time Smackeroo**, sending the opponent's marionette off the screen. **Nash advances**; she **smashes her controller in frustration**. **No injection or other sabotage of Nash's opponent is observed**.

**Nothing vs Bockip (~02:43–02:49).** Nothing meets **another kenku**. He locks in **Bland**, forcing Nothing to select **Kung** because duplicate hero choices are not allowed at that booth. Both competitors **wait to mimic the other's button presses**; neither chooses an initiating move. A match timer reaches zero, declares **forfeit**, and both marionettes explode. The DM describes **“two matches and one forfeit,” explicitly a double timeout**. **Nothing does not win, does not visibly progress and is not awarded a bye**. Her *separate secret kidnapping assignment* remains open.

The rest of the episode consists of **out-of-character commentary** on Beau's custom marionette mini-game, limited character preparation and plans not to narrate every tournament round. These are **GM/table design comments**, not confirmation of twenty-five future matches or new in-world outcomes.
