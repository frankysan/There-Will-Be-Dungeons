# E094 — Where did my ball go

> **The Wastes · Arc Three · provisional, processed-ASR-only dossier; not audio-verified.** Source exports: [Parakeet processed SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E094.%20Where%20did%20my%20ball%20go.parakeet.processed.srt) and [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E094.%20Where%20did%20my%20ball%20go.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt). Both finish at ~**02:42:42** including outro. [ASR comparison](../reports/E094_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). E093 ended aboard the **smoking, descending Traveler's Twin** in the Outlands, with Hope just rescued by Nash's black dragon form, Vorel poisoned after killing a wyvern, Stanley wearing Mind Master's Amulet of Health and holding the stolen high-cleric pendant, **no confirmed landing/crash yet**.

## Actual episode outcomes and cutoff

- **The first infiltration into White Tower really takes place**, but **not as planned**. As Traveler's Twin drops toward the distant army, **Nash returns from young black dragon to humanoid form**. The four **actually leap from the failing airship**, paired **Stanley/Hope** and **Nash/Vorel**, with **Dimension Door scrolls** read in freefall. **Stanley, Nash and Vorel actually arrive inside the tower, in a cleaning supply closet**. **Hope is rejected by the ward**, takes **4 necrotic damage** against exterior wall, attempts to cling with her **new +2 dagger**, fails, then **takes 49 bludgeoning damage from an ~80-foot fall**, and **survives**. Thus neither “four together teleport successfully” nor “Hope dies from tower rejection” is correct.
- **Traveler's Twin crashes out of sight beyond a mountain according to narration from the falling PCs:** smoke rises behind the ridge; they do not directly inspect a wreck. **Later the living captain speaks over the sending stone**, reporting **bad landing and approximately one week needed for repairs**. The earlier conditional four-hour signal arrangement is superseded by actual message. Do not call vessel irrecoverably destroyed or captain/entire crew dead. The ship is **not available for an immediate pickup**.
- **Hope is actually captured by trident-carrying winged doglike troops** of the besieging army and brought to **General Carthax/Acarthax (?)**, a giant green-and-pale military leader. **Ulvert/Ullvert (?)**, a small winged infant-bodied, elderly-faced being who **recognizes Hope from the UBI/Solar Mines scientific facility**, intervenes and says **devils from Nine Hells and demons from Abyss are temporarily allied** against the tower. Their **claims** about command, Heaven and future Earth travel remain claims, not completed transport.
- Hope **admits she seeks Zorander's Fist on Tobacho Fleur's behalf**, and bargains with Ulvert and the general to retrieve it in exchange for the return of **herself and her three companions to Earth in their own time**. A strange **faceless/mouth-at-neck female-looking caster actually alters Hope's alignment to lawful good**, explicitly declared by GM: **she must behave lawfully good to sustain it**, but is **not otherwise mind controlled**. This change **permits passage through White Tower's ward**, whereas Hope's earlier alignment had caused rejection. Ulvert **instructs her to make an evil act after stealing Fist so ward expels her to them**, and **warns no plan has been provided to get all companions out**. **This is a coerced attempted double-cross, not proof Hope successfully steals Fist or accepts demonic ideology permanently.**
- Inside, **Sir Samson**, **Sir Clotchup/Clarchup (?)**, and a small robed acolyte **Walnut** encounter Stanley, Nash, and Vorel. **Stanley claims they are reinforcements**, talks his way into initial acceptance, admits one companion missing; Hope **actually arrives magically** after the alignment change. Paladins nearly attack her for being a tiefling; **Vorel drunkenly claims she is pregnant with a paladin's divine child**, a conspicuously **false deception, not confirmed pregnancy or romance**. Stanley **persuades them to stay their swords**. Hope **honestly reveals the demonic bargain**, making the paladins insist all four relinquish weapons.
- **Nash actually surrenders his magical spellcasting ball to Walnut**, inspiring the episode title. **Vorel refuses to surrender Shatter**, attacks Samson with a headbutt, and another paladin's wild sword blow **accidentally wounds Samson**. **Stanley heals Samson with Healing Word** and stands between Vorel and the knights, halting an immediate death spiral. **Nash grapples Walnut trying to recover his ball**, but Walnut escapes; he **is not permanently deprived of it**. A larger contingent of paladins surrounds them and escorts all four, now disarmed, to the commander. The initial fighting ends **without any party member or Samson being killed**.
- **The Knight Commander** (woman in armor with long strawberry hair; personal name not supplied with confidence) sits by a **hexagonal chest**, which **she identifies as containing Zorander's Fist**. The chest is **actually visible**, but **not opened**, and the relic inside **is not directly seen**. She recognizes their attempted theft, sentences them all to death **then grants an explicit stay of execution** after Stanley proposes helping **move the Fist safely to Mount Celestia instead of delivering it to Tobacho**, possibly accompanied by her knights.
- **Walnut actually returns confiscated belongings including Nash's ball, Stanley's bladed weapons, Hope's weapons, and Vorel's Shatter** under her instructions. **The Knight Commander physically inspects the stolen sun-and-fist medallion (“key”)**, then **returns it to Stanley**, saying it **must stay separate from the chest** until transfer; she does not hand them the relic. Stanley **still has both pendant and Mind Master's Amulet of Health** (the latter's wearer status unchanged), whereas the **chest remains under the Knight Commander's custody**.
- The Knight Commander offers a **separate Euphemius sun-and-fist devotional totem**, apparently a golden object in a belt case, to be taken to the highest point in Earth to call divine guidance. **Distinguish this from Zorander's Fist and from the pendant key**; the narration shows her displaying/offering it, but its exact physical transfer/inventory is unclear from ASR and needs review. Her account of **Zorander's tragic fall and the evil hand inside a glove being able to tear planes apart** is **NPC testimony**, not directly demonstrated properties.
- **Captain Meart (?) actually contacts the party by sending stone**, says the crash/bad landing requires **around a week for repairs**, and recommends independent escape. **The party receives quarters and food inside the besieged tower** through Walnut and the cook **Gregor (?)**, which are actually visited. A violent dispute follows between Vorel and Stanley: **Vorel wants immediate force/theft for the homeward bargain**, **Stanley refuses to kill paladins and proposes aiding them instead**, **Nash pledges to follow Hope**, and **Hope feels compelled toward “doing good” under the temporary lawful-good alteration**. **Stanley goes to sleep and leaves the pendant key in his bag in his quarters, explicitly inviting companions to choose**; he is not shown physically giving it to anybody.
- **The final private cliffhanger is Hope hearing a voice in her mind demanding “bring us the Fist”**. Hope attempts a silent response but receives **no answer, only pressure and renewed words**. **Source or identity of voice is not established**; it may relate to the coercive bargain, but never state an observed magical compulsion beyond the spoken GM action. **No Fist theft, Heaven delivery, Earth portal, party death or completed betrayal in E094.**

## I. Abandoning a damaged ship, not arriving by it (~00:03–00:22)

The **Traveler's Twin** is about twenty degrees below horizontal, its **engine pouring black smoke**. Its loxodon **Captain Meart (?)** tells the party to leave while gnomes try to save it. She states she'll **call on the sending stone** when/if ship repaired, predicts **perhaps four hours**, and warns they may have to make their own way. **The mast cracks and falls with several gnomes**. Nash is **still a young black dragon from E093**, but **changes back into humanoid form** to avoid an conspicuous and impractical infiltration. The group pair themselves with one scroll reader and one accompanying traveler each: **Stanley takes Hope**, **Nash takes Vorel**. The four **jump into freefall toward the White Tower**, initially each keeping a hold of their partner.

Both readers successfully hold the scrolls in the wind and cast **Dimension Door** as they near the ~500-foot limit. **The Traveler's Twin is seen smoking and disappearing behind a distant ridge**, with a cloud rising from behind it, prompting the reasonable fear of a crash. This early visual **does not prove everyone aboard died**; the later sending-stone contact changes the available evidence. The two teleportation spells **actually activate**, and the pairs disappear. The action then separates into distinct groups.

## II. Hope outside, then among the besiegers (~00:22–00:40)

Although Stanley's teleport brought **him** inside the tower, **Hope is violently expelled by the tower's magical ward**, scraped along the exterior for **4 necrotic damage**. She tries to catch herself with **the +2 dagger delivered in E092** and her boot spikes but is repelled. She falls to the ground for **49 bludgeoning damage**, survives, and sees the huge tower under constant bombardment.

**Winged doglike soldiers with tridents** surround her and take her to a raised command platform. There **General Carthax (?)** questions her, and **Ulvert/Ullvert (?)**, a small hovering infantlike creature with old man's head, **claims a prior connection at the UBI/Solar Mines facility**. Hope reveals her Earth kingship and the **Tobacho Fleur → Zorander's Fist** contract. The devils and demons **say they cooperate** in the siege, despite usually opposing each other.

Ulvert decides Hope can become a vehicle into the tower if her alignment is changed. A mouth-faced caster **actually changes Hope to lawful good**, according to the GM's explicit ruling. This is an **in-world temporary magical alteration**: she still makes decisions, but **must act according to the new alignment or the effect lapses**. Hope negotiates the promised trip home **for all four** if she brings them Fist; the general threatens terrible injury otherwise. Asked about fighting **Kitobalpraith**, the general's reaction and Ulvert's warning reflect fear, not proof that Kitobalpraith arrives. **Hope is actually teleported into the tower** after this change, with orders **to behave well until she acquires the Fist, then act evil to trigger ejection by ward**. Ulvert admits **no confirmed way to retrieve her three companions**.

## III. Inside the White Tower: a ball, a mistake and a healer (~00:40–01:35)

**Stanley, Nash and Vorel** emerge in a **20-by-20-foot janitorial room**, hearing two paladins playing a board/card game and staff praying nearby. **Hope is missing**, despite holding Stanley's hand. Stanley makes the brave choice to **open the door and pretend they are reinforcements**. **Sir Samson**, **Sir Clotchup (?)** and robed attendant **Walnut** become their first contacts. The religious troops of Euphemius accept reinforcements reluctantly, then are alarmed by the reported missing tiefling. They say the tower ward **repels those with evil hearts**. **Hope suddenly actually appears in the room**, confirming she lived.

The paladins prepare to kill the tiefling for assumed innate evil. **Vorel, drunk, declares Hope bears a divine child** (he is **deceiving** them; this is **not confirmed pregnancy**). **Stanley describes Vorel's courage, previous M-sports championship and defiance of a Weirlord**, winning a temporary stand-down. But Hope **states truthfully that the demons altered her alignment and want her to retrieve the Fist**. This destroys fragile trust. The paladins request **complete disarmament**, including **Nash's unusual magic ball**; **Walnut takes it**, prompting jokes about how a level-ten spellcaster's only weapon is a ball.

**Vorel refuses to relinquish Shatter**, headbutts Samson, and a paladin **fumbles a sword swing into his brother, severely injuring Samson**. **Stanley immediately casts Healing Word (eight HP restored)**, establishes he means to prevent needless deaths and stands against Vorel's plan to kill the paladins. **Nash tries to grapple Walnut for the ball**, but Walnut escapes into other defenders. No paladin is deliberately executed by the party. Many more armored defenders arrive, and **all four eventually relinquish weapons** and descend to face the **Knight Commander**, with Sir Samson vouching for Stanley's healing act. **The ball is returned under the commander's later instruction**, so keep the custody transition separate from the initial confiscation.

## IV. The Knight Commander and a death sentence delayed (~01:35–~02:05)

The knights escort the party several floors down past a level sounding like **a prison ward**, into a great hall with **several hundred defenders in the tower overall** and knights dining. At its far end a female **Knight Commander** sits beside a distinct **hexagonal chest**. The commander questions their attempts to infiltrate and their bargains with both the **Sigil dragon and the demons**, concluding they intended to steal a sacred item. Stanley describes the companions' **reasons for wanting to return to Earth**—Hope's kingdom, Vorel's people and Nash's family—and acknowledges they chose a compromised path.

Despite Stanley's argument, the Knight Commander **sentences all four to death** for trespass and threatened removal of the Fist. **This is an actual pronounced sentence, but it is NOT executed**. As they kneel, **Stanley proposes that rather than steal the relic for Tobacho, he and the others help move it to Mount Celestia** as the defenders wish, with an escort if required. He argues they had scrolls and an airship planned for removal; the latter's fate is not yet clear. His persuasion **wins a stay of execution** pending a plan. **All confiscated equipment is actually returned** at her order, Nash reclaiming his ball and Vorel his blade.

The commander **confirms that the hexagonal chest she guards contains the Fist**, but **does not open it**. The men show her the **stolen key pendant from Thrun**. She takes it momentarily, then after the later airship message **gives it back to Stanley** on the explicit principle that **key and Fist must remain separate**. Her narration describes **Zorander's faith, fall from grace and an evil hand that survives inside a glove, purportedly able to rip planes apart**. These are **character claims**, not power demonstrations. She also shows/offers a **gold sun-and-fist totem for possible use on Earth**, separate from the mission artifacts; **physical acceptance/ownership unclear**.

## V. No airship rescue for a week; disagreement inside (~02:05–02:38)

The **sending stone activates**, and **the captain is alive**. She reports **a bad landing**, says **about one week to repair Traveler's Twin**, and advises the party to seek other transport rather than wait. This converts E093's “descent” into **crash/bad landing with surviving captain**, but the exact hull losses and final rescue remain unknown.

**Walnut escorts all four to quarters and the mess hall**, where cook **Gregor (?)** serves a modest ration amid an extended siege and offers kitchen work. The characters debate options. **Vorel is enraged** that a heist intended to get him home has become a pledge to help the paladins, suspects Tobacho and the Mind Master will retaliate, and believes **violence is the shortest path to acquire the Fist**. **Stanley** argues they cannot hold the tower for a week amid endless siege, and won't slaughter decent defenders for a chance to return to a world where he feels he no longer belongs. **Nash** is frustrated but chooses **Hope's leadership**, stressing he has a wife and potentially a child at home. **Hope**, under the **temporary lawful-good alignment effect**, feels unable to adopt simple villainous tactics and tries to save as many people as possible. Their discussion produces **no completed heist**.

The **Knight Commander has returned the pendant key to Stanley** after explicitly refusing to keep key and chest together. **Stanley leaves it in his bag in his room and retires to sleep**, allowing friends to choose what to do without endorsing another murder. **Nash also goes to bed**, and Vorel tends the kitchen. **Hope, alone and angry, hears a private mental voice demanding she bring “us” the Fist**. Her attempted mental reply gets no dialogue—only pressure and the phrase again. **Neither the source nor the mechanism of that voice is confirmed.** The episode ends here.
