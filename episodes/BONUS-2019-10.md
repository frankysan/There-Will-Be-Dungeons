## Editorial separations and checks

**October bonus timing:** The month in the basename indicates **public release**, not an audio-confirmed recording day. Scott's early-access remark should not be used to calculate a precise date. **Speaker diarization clusters in this recording are contextual and must not be ported to E076/E077**; especially Jon as *Inverse* DM and Beau as Wastes DM.

**Wastes interpretations, not retcons:** The dragon-form baby hand is Scott's **preferred visual imagination**, explicitly flexible under Beau; its fate is not a mechanical power increase or P0D resurrection. Hope's mistrust, Nash's trauma and the theme of family provide **creative intent**, not alternate played events. **Vorel's anti-dragon suit is physically acquired E076**, independent of hypothetical costume talk here.

**Other series:** *Inverse* library, Mirgut, Zelganeth, Spores and the originally planned library encounter belong to **the Jon-run *Inverse* adventure**. Their [separate Inverse campaign portal](../inverse/README.md) and [S1E01 dossier](../inverse/episodes/S1E01.md) now exist. Later library events discussed in the bonus are **not backdated to Inverse S1E01** or inserted into the Wastes CHIDS timeline.
