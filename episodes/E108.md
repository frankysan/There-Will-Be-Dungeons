# E108 — Save The Two

> **The Wastes · corrected S1E108 · Dunebreaker prequel, part 2 of 3 · played in the SAME Wastes, long before the Dusthill departure · ASR-provisional.** [Parakeet processed](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E108.%20Save%20The%20Two.parakeet.processed.srt) · [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E108.%20Save%20The%20Two.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt) · [full-export comparison](../reports/E108_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md) · [Dunebreaker](../world/places/dunebreaker.md). Owner's normalized catalogue: publisher **TWBD 110**, **29 June 2020**, corrected **E108**; host opens by calling it **Sunday 28 June**. Publication date is catalogue metadata, not an independently checked recording timestamp. The [E107](E107.md) and [E109 — Sand Jell-O](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E109.%20Sand%20Jell-O.parakeet.processed.srt) sessions surround it in the release-order **three-part Dunebreaker prequel**, not Arc Five's present.

**Played cutoff:** The guards follow the earlier murder's tracks into a darker engine compartment and find a gaunt **stowaway, Lamulkin Farthing (?)**. **Ramilda attacks even after he calls out his surrender**; the man survives and claims to have stabbed the engineer in self-defense. Hack determines he is hiding others. **Two daughters** emerge, the elder about 16–17, the younger about 10. The elder **confesses that she, not Lamulkin, stabbed the engineer**; the father knowingly assumes responsibility to save them. After rejecting or abandoning several dangerous alternatives (an amputated limb as evidence, three people jumping off the ship under *Feather Fall*), the guards **actually escort Lamulkin to Ember and Lotho**, knowingly conceal both daughters and have him falsely admit sole guilt. **Hack's Deception 17 succeeds** sufficiently to avoid further questioning. **Ember spares Lamulkin and assigns him to hazardous under-ship “tumbler vents” as a “rat” worker**. **Strum is reassigned from guards to engineering**, alive. **Hack and Ramilda later accompany Strum to bring the two hidden girls real food**, while Al declines that excursion. The guards spend the remaining day settling into assigned rooms and private pastimes. **No girls are handed in, released from the ship or reunited with their father; the parentage and survival of all three are distinct from a full rescue.** The group's pre-existing contract with Fleece remains unfulfilled.

## Continuing the E107 murder investigation (~00:03–00:31)

1. **Recap and pursuit (~00:03–00:18).** Jon reminds the players that **Hack Loman, Ramilda Gloryfaded (?) and Al**, accompanied by **dwarf NPC Strum**, are **first-day guards aboard the Dunebreaker**. The previous [E107 cutoff](E107.md) left an unnamed engineer dead with **four punctures in his back**, and a witness pointed deeper into **segment 2 engineering**. Al asks that the body be secured for medical observation; that is **a request**, not a performed autopsy or confirmed permanent corpse transfer. The narrow, steam-filled lower passage forks around machinery. Ramilda charges ahead; Hack and Al attempt investigation before choosing a path. **Al's Investigation 10** sees more liquid to the right; **Ramilda's Investigation 19** distinguishes **blood mixed with condensation**, providing a concrete trail. The party proceeds through a tighter access opening.
2. **A voice says surrender (~00:25–00:37).** In a flashing red-lit chamber **a gaunt elderly man says “I surrender” and raises his hands**. **Ramilda starts to cast *Guiding Bolt*** regardless. Initiative allows the others to act first; Hack holds his fire. **Al uses *Mage Hand* to open a steam valve behind the man** in an attempt to flush him from cover, while Strum is temporarily stuck in the narrow entrance. **Ramilda's *Guiding Bolt* lands for 15 radiant damage**, badly burning his chest; a subsequent **morningstar swing does not finish him**. He repeatedly confesses to stabbing the dead engineer and begs them to stop. **He survives this assault**. The threat of further force is not proof he attacked the team.
3. **Initial confession and contradictions (~00:37–00:45).** The man, later named **Lamulkin Farthing (?)**, admits he hid as a **stowaway** and **stabbed the engineer with a dagger**, which he places at their feet. He says the engineer surprised him, carried **a piece of metal** and threatened to turn him in. **The four wounds were in the engineer's back** (reaffirmed by GM), an obvious reason the group questions how much of his claimed self-defense is true. Al checks his injuries rather than declaring him dead; **Strum says he is under arrest**. Hack's **Insight 18** finds his rapid denial of other hidden people suspicious. Neither the engineer's actual attack nor the dagger's precise sequence was witnessed by PCs; the man's story is testimony.

## The two daughters and competing rescue proposals (~00:43–01:17)

4. **Truth coaxed out, family revealed (~00:43–00:54).** After an empty-room search and ineffective threats to burn the hiding place, Hack sincerely promises to try to protect **whoever else is hiding**. **Persuasion 16** moves the man to call out his **two daughters**, one **about 16–17** with dark hair and one **about 10**, both starving and wearing ragged gray clothing. They came from **Slavetown**, secretly boarded while spectators celebrated the Dunebreaker tournament and wanted to escape their former life at the **next settlement**, not take over the ship. They have **no food or water**. The father's reassurance and intended departure route are not proof the vessel would permit them to disembark undetected.
5. **Hack's compassion meets Ramilda's legalism (~00:54–01:03).** Hack argues against returning all three to the authorities; **Ramilda objects that lying conceals a murder** and wants accountability, though she is willing to help feed the girls. Al explores calculated alternatives. **The group discusses cutting off one of the father's limbs as counterfeit evidence of a killer's death**, with later magical care. **No limb is severed** and no staged death presented. Al also proposes **casting *Feather Fall* for a jump from the landship into the wasteland** to escape all together; **the spell is not cast and nobody jumps**. The father reasonably fears losing the children in the desert. A workable way out through **small automatic undercarriage vents** is described as dangerous due to **unpredictable superheated steam**, and **not feasible while the vehicle moves**. This is information told by stowaways and a real ship design, not proof anyone safely escaped through them during this episode.
6. **Who really stabbed the engineer (~01:06–01:10).** **The older daughter interrupts that it is not right for her father to pay for “what I did.” Lamulkin confirms that the older daughter did the stabbing** after he knocked something over while trying to fetch food and the engineer spotted them. Jon's later recap affirms this revelation. These events occurred **offscreen before the PCs' arrival**, so preserve the GM-backed confession without inventing a flashback in which the party actually observed the attack or resolving whether the engineer threatened them first. The older daughter and father **must not be merged into a single killer**. **Neither child is given a verified personal name here**.
7. **A father deliberately takes the blame (~01:10–01:18).** Lamulkin chooses **to be turned in alone**, regardless of punishment, rather than risk harm to his daughters through amputation, jumping or exposure. The children remain in the hidden engine passage; the elder worries the guards might betray them. **Hack hears his private farewell on a Perception 24** and promises he will do his best to keep the father **alive through hard labor** and feed the girls. His promise is real, **not a guarantee he controls Ember's decision**. **Al retains the surrendered dagger as physical evidence**, a handoff clarified in table dialogue after the break.

## A false confession accepted, and a dangerous life spared (~01:18–01:39)

8. **The guards bring the wrong confessor (~01:18–01:26).** **Hack, Ramilda, Al and Strum physically escort Lamulkin** from segment 2 to the head offices. Engineering workers congratulate them; two guards question the prisoner's useless, starving appearance. The group reports **the man committed the murder in purported self-defense** and offers the dagger. In a private meeting with **Ember Shalisper (?) and lionlike guard captain Lotho (?)**, **Lamulkin himself knowingly confesses falsely**, keeping his daughters' existence secret. Lotho asks if there were other people, and **Hack lies that Lamulkin acted alone; Deception 17** earns acceptance or at least no immediate challenge. **This is a confirmed deception by the characters, not a source narrator's assertion that the father really stabbed the engineer.**
9. **Ember chooses useful labor over execution (~01:26–01:35).** Ember looks at Lamulkin's **fresh radiant burn** and asks about it. Hack admits he fired preemptively (in play **Ramilda was the *Guiding Bolt* caster**; the group's report is imprecise), then proposes the prisoner should earn his keep instead of being killed. Hack's initial **Persuasion 12** is unconvincing; **Ramilda later rolls Persuasion 23** while suggesting that a man who entered the ship undetected might be useful. Ember recognizes the access route as **“tumbler vents”**, narrow undercarriage cleaning shafts where **small workers nicknamed “rats”** are sent. He **actually assigns Lamulkin to that job**, and **Argul (?) takes him away alive**. The work is dangerous and socially degrading, not a guaranteed humane release, but it is **not the execution the guards feared**. The two daughters are **not disclosed** to Ember or Lotho.
10. **Strum changes jobs, and the first test ends (~01:34–01:41).** With a dead engineer, Ember seeks replacement labor. **Strum says he knows something about machines**, so the GM **actually transfers the dwarf from the guard party to engineering**; Jon makes an out-of-game remark about removing the NPC from the active party. **Strum is not dead, expelled from the Dunebreaker, or a playable fourth PC**. Lotho praises the other **three** and releases them to passenger quarters. Their jobs have been judged successful **on a knowingly incomplete report**.

## Rooms, a scarred ranger and feeding the children (~01:39–02:37)

11. **First rooms and the ship's food counter (~01:39–01:55).** A steward assigns **Hack room 3A, Al 3B and Ramilda 3C**, on the third floor of **segment 1**. In the passenger dining room they receive **stew and water without cash payment** from **the same gnome called “Dipshit”** (an insulting in-world nickname, **not confirmed birth name**). The counter and serving mechanism are **mundane, not magically materializing food**. Ramilda is frustrated to receive water rather than mead, even throws her cup back, but she is **not refused food**. Each room has a comparatively luxurious private bed/bathroom and stocked provisions. The ship's endless-supply claim remains unproved; the visible generosity is real.
12. **Hack's hidden facial scars (~01:56–02:06).** Alone at a mirror, **Hack removes his see-through blindfold**. **An old, poorly healed ring-like scar runs around his face over temples, forehead, bridge of nose and eyes**; he quietly meditates and replaces the veil. This is **direct character description**, but the cause, perpetrator, surgery and related personal history **are not revealed**. He walks the corridors to learn the layout. **Al rearranges his room for a mechanical workbench**, shifting the bed into the bathroom; **Ramilda wanders looking for games**. No evidence is given of hidden cameras or a public conspiracy simply from Al's concern about surveillance.
13. **Strum's concern and a real food delivery (~02:06–02:26).** **Strum encounters Hack and Ramilda** and worries that his new engineering duties place him near the still-hidden girls who killed a worker. They agree the daughters will need food. **Hack asks Al to join the escort, but Al declines**, reasoning that another visit to the crime scene would draw attention. **Strum obtains two servings of stew and drinks; Hack and Ramilda actually accompany him** to the hiding passage. The daughters cautiously emerge, ask whether their father was killed and are told that **he was reassigned to tumbler-vent labor**. **They receive the food and begin eating voraciously**. **This fulfills an immediate promise to feed them, not a durable food schedule or a completed escape.** Strum, still frightened, requests continuing help; Hack and Ramilda express an intent to return or bring rations **tomorrow**, not demonstrated future deliveries.
14. **Evening and cutoff (~02:26–02:37).** The companions separate again. **Hack whittles wood**, including a little depiction of Dipshit. **Ramilda fails to find a fight club, then actually plays cards** with two older people and roughly **breaks even**. The gnome provides her a **small bottle of mead** after asking Ember; he mentions a **special-reward points system**, which remains **NPC explanation, not demonstrated complete currency policy**. **Al tinkers in his room**. The GM **ends their first day aboard**. **No new attack, ship departure, revelation of Fleece's employer, further corpse investigation or rescue from the Dunebreaker** occurs. Credits and cast discussion follow the played cutoff.
