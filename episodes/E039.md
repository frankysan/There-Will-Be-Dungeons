## Continuity and evidence hierarchy

- **Hope's prosthetics:** Directly demonstrated in E039 after E038 trauma and operations: **left mechanical arm and leg**, enhanced grab, concealed rocket/launcher functionality. **The explosive is not fired during the recovery demonstration**, and custom game mechanics may not equal final published sheet abilities.
- **Stanley/Buttons:** **A public warrant and a mage's allegation** are official announcements, not proof of a deliberate murder attempt. Buttons's earlier E032 coercion is directly in the record; **Monsoon says Buttons betrayed the party** and that Tedna is captive, still requiring corroboration about methods and current custody.
- **Monsoon's deal:** E038 delivers **Blunt Goliver's head**; E039 obtains **weapons and possible fortress access intelligence** plus his broader political plan. Any declared true name and tiefling backstory should remain **NPC testimony**, not automatically canonical historical fact.
- **Monsoon's killings:** Hope sees fragments while sedated; **Monsoon explicitly says he had the caregivers killed**. Record **admission of responsibility** separately from independent postmortem confirmation and do not sanitize it as an ordinary medical discharge.
- **E039 still lacks completed end goals:** No confirmed Jolt robbery, royal wedding, Blood Ball result, Principle death, pixie-farm liberation or completed Teet infiltration.
