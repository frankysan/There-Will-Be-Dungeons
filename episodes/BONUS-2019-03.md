## Curated reference implications

1. **Supplementary source tier:** Here **Beau's GM explanations** carry value as retrospective intended-world/planning testimony, and **players' statements** carry value for motivation, authorship and hypothetical agency. Neither is a recorded **new Wastes session event**, and the machine transcript itself may misrecognize names.
2. **Buttons motive needs an annotation, not a rewritten scene:** [E049](E049.md) and [E050](E050.md) document direct violent acts and death. This Q&A, ~02:35–02:40, adds **Beau's later revelation of love for Stanley and attempted Arcanum rescue**, unknown to much of the group at the time.
3. **Order 26 and P0D agency:** The party's unchosen **pixie-rescue route** and GM-designed **super-soldier rebellion** clarify the force behind P0D's transformation; they do not retroactively create a played liberation mission or show P0D deliberately switched sides.
4. **Nash's heart and Solar Mines:** GM created the dragon-metal mechanism partly in reaction to early character play. Nash's contradictory memories may be deliberate **world mystery**, not reasons to erase first-person accounts.
5. **Steel heaven / gods:** The GM deliberately separates possible theology from objective setting exposition. **Hope's hallucination is not confirmed cosmology**, and the “savior gods” are discussed as a religious idea/future exploration, not a new observed god.
6. **World continuity:** **Toyota host** still undetermined; **Fate Four** still somewhere left behind; **George's radio clue** only counterfactual; **Nash's forgotten Blink scroll** is a reported inventory discrepancy; **Bok Bok's spontaneous invention** is production history, not an in-world spontaneous birth.
7. **Canonical spelling:** **Diontalus** and the **Eye of Groxl** follow owner-confirmed decisions, even where the Q&A SRT says `Dian Tallis` or other aliases. Spellings such as `Kuro-makishi`, `Toyota host`, `Arcanum`, and one-shot names need listening/source checking.
