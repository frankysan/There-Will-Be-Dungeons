# E057 — The Royal Truckery

> **Corrected Wastes S1E057**, [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E057.%20The%20Royal%20Truckery.parakeet.processed.srt). Approximate recording times, **not audio-verified**. Direct continuation of [E056](E056.md); the [April bonus](BONUS-2019-04.md) is separate out-of-character discussion. **Full spoilers.**

## Episode outcome

**Emenil survives Nobiery's animated-corpses attack.** The E056 cliffhanger's threatened bite is re-staged as an opportunity for the PCs to intervene; **47 Carnage actually bites her neck**, while **Theresa** approaches. Stanley attacks the zombie with **Diplomacy** and heals Emenil with **Healing Word**; at Hope's command Nobiery stops both corpses. Emenil and the party confront their hidden Weir bargains. **Nobiery himself admits** he told the tieflings their timeless refuge was the work of **Matamaru** while he really dealt with the **Soulbroker**, and acknowledges deaths sacrificed for it. Emenil, initially outraged, reluctantly agrees to hear the whole story in the interest of protecting the city; this is not automatic public approval or proof she becomes a willing conspirator forever.

The party investigates the **skyfall site** in a **repaired royal monster truck**, deliberately leaving **Fate 4** behind. In and around a steaming, suddenly green impact crater they find displaced rock, vivid growth, banana-like fruit, signs of an unknown creature **dragged onto a vehicle** by roughly fifty **bugbear-sized figures**, and an **unlucky leprechaun** buried beneath a giant clover. **Diontalus temporarily takes control of Stanley's body**, kills the leprechaun **Coopen/Coupon**, and consumes his magical essence; a **lucky coin** remains and **Hope acquires it**. A **bugbear party** loyal to a **Wormfather** encloses the crater, backed by a robed spellcaster and a winged, dragon-like stinging creature. **Stanley destroys the winged creature** with a magical attack; the remaining bugbears close in as the episode finishes. The details of any dragon's fate are **not** demonstrated by the crater alone.

## Continuity and open questions

**Emenil:** The attack that appeared to end E056 **actually bites her in E057** but **does not kill her**; Stanley's healing and Hope's order end the immediate threat. Her later theological position and public disclosures remain open.

**Nobiery:** His admitted **Soulbroker deal and deliberate cover story** are much stronger evidence of his own action than his prior disputed description of another family's pact. Exact newborn/soul sacrifice counts still require primary review.

**Diontalus:** Not separated in E057. He actively takes the shared body and kills **Coopen/Coupon**. The leprechaun's **luck coin ends with Hope**, but its exact refresh and limitations are narrated from an in-game item entry without original sheet authentication.

**Skyfall:** Crater greenery, unusual radiation, wagon/vehicle tracks and apparent bugbear retrieval are observed/investigated. Quizquaz's claim that **a particular dragon was killed on atmospheric entry** remains an interested report; a winged pet is a different beast. **Wormfather** connection and spellcaster identity remain unresolved as of E057.

**Orthography:** `Coopen/Coupon`, `Nobiery/Nobieri`, `Emenil/Eminel`, `Wormfather`, the original statue/place names and special-item text are still ASR-derived hypotheses. **Diontalus** and the **Eye of Groxl** remain established spelling decisions.
