## Boundaries and key research contributions

- **Diontalus's three-month silence after Tietkalla is explained by Jon as a deliberate player decision:** the extracted companion had warned Stanley that trying to do good in the Wastes would never be rewarded; Jon preferred to let subsequent events prove the warning, rather than have Diontalus interrupt Stanley's temporary victory. Jon imagines an eventual **“told you so,”** but says Diontalus was removed **before he could deliver it**. This is **player retrospective characterization**, **not a hidden on-screen visit, previously unheard speech or independent evidence of the disembodied soul's present location**. [Diontalus's biography](../world/characters/diontalus.md) and [Stanley's biography](../world/characters/stanley-billings.md).
- **Nash's Solar Mines origin receives production-side elaboration.** Scott says his new revelations concern **forced construction of a “micro sun” or artificial source of solar-like energy**, and coerced miners suffered terribly; he associates it with **Master Beltnotch** and mentions **“Slow Peters,”** previously established in [E073](E073.md) as a nickname for dangerous **fire elementals** employed in the mines; their exact role in the artificial-sun explanation still needs cross-source confirmation. This is compatible with Nash's **E089 played account of deadly radioactive material being pooled to generate energy**. **Do not convert colorful “micro sun” shorthand into independent proof of the engineering, claim a literal star was actually built, or invent a new identity for the already-mentioned Slow Peters.** Scott says he has a story he wants to tell about the mines, but **no return expedition, cathartic confrontation or exact source of the energy is played in this bonus**. Beau indicates **he has prepared information about Nash's parentage**, which Scott/the party **do not know or receive here**. [Nash dossier](../world/characters/nash-maggard.md).
- **The Eye of Groxl/Groxl's broader legend remains unresolved.** Kyle is interested in exploring more of the character's relationship with **Groxl**, including the previously shown **shield-transforming-into-flaming-sword ability** and the loss of the Eye in the E081 sacrifice. The GM likes the ambiguity of a possible **“God Killer”** legend. **No divine status, confirmed god-killing, new artefact acquisition or resurrection of the Eye is established** by this Q&A. **Groxl** is the user-confirmed canon spelling; ASR variants **“Grojil/Grozil/Grogil”** are recognition noise, not separate figures.
- **BMF championship contingency plans:** Asked about alternatives if Vorel **had not defeated Kanja**, Jon says **Nothing could not independently formulate a new prank**, a deliberate constraint of her mimicking, largely reactive character. Another player jokes about **pulling down Kanja's trousers**, and Nash's player says **he had no real plan**, being a floating, magically constrained heart at the time. **No backup trick was actually performed; the played E087 outcome is Vorel's win and Kidigree's revealing disc.** This Q&A also confirms the roleplaying experiment behind **Jon's kenku Nothing**, who relinquished Stanley's ability to dominate conversation and allowed Vorel to take charge more often. [E087 played tournament](E087.md).
- **M-fighting roster/development materials:** Beau says he had **sixteen fighter concepts**, with detailed background/catchphrases for only some; he reads examples from his **unpublished notes**: **Archibald** serves **Lord Abaddon** on the **666th Layer of the Abyss** (as a character bio concept), **Scrumptious** is a chef-like **arakocra / “laughing fowl”**, an arrogant **high-elf fencer** sounds like **Fochirolass/Forchiralas (?)**, and **Buster Balls McBuster** has a brief drinking line. These are **production-side unused/partially written marionette fighter concepts, NOT new NPC arrivals, canon Abyss theology or bouts the players fought**. Spelling, exact roster status and whether any particular fighter was fully implemented require Beau's original files. [E087](E087.md).
- **Hope's, Nash's and other PCs' possible futures:** Kristen says she **always expected Hope to be resurrected** and therefore created Squirts as a very different temporary character; this is **her confidence as a player**, not advance proof that a ritual was guaranteed. Scott has a backup human PC name sounding **Hack Loman/Hackloman**, and Kyle also considered a substitute for Vorel; **neither enters the played campaign**. The cast discusses glorious deaths versus retirement and Stanley's E089 suggestion to remain in Sigil. These are **hypotheticals**.
- **Nash's child:** A listener asks whether his **dragon-metal heart**, supposed dragon blood and the earlier interactions with **Angolashi/Angelashi (?)** might affect an elf-baby's lineage. **Beau refuses to disclose the answer.** **Do not turn this into proof that the child is dragonborn, born, alive, or even that the precise mechanics are settled in-world**. Questions reflect fan inference.
- **GM craft vs canon:** Beau advises against being a **simultaneous full player character and DM** because of impartiality and spotlight; he explains **inspiration points** as a tool for encouraging timid players rather than something the already confident cast needs routinely. Character advice stresses organic growth, stats second, and avoiding forced comedy. Beau credits **voice/cultural inspiration from films/TV** and says the **Lizardfolk family system existed independently of the week's Game of Thrones references**, although topical allusions affected naming. **The Mind Master's manner was inspired by a recently watched episode**, not evidence that NPC is literally a real person. These are **production statements, not extra Sigil history**.
