## Key interpretation boundaries

- **Written evidence versus inference:** E014's in-house note appears addressed to **Timber**, the Jeweler's missing agent. Finding a marked corpse in the same house **does not demonstrate the corpse is Timber** without another identification. The note's `Red Scarlet` sign-off may refer to a person, gang, signature or threat; do not equate it with the **Lactid Cartel**.
- **Cyclops and necklace:** It seeks a particular `shiny`, treats a man called `Thailander` as its friend/thief, and has an eye catching clues. A retrieved necklace has **five dangling red beads** by narrated design. If some dialogue says **six**, record as an inconsistency to check, rather than normalizing numbers invisibly. Its true name and the magician's alias remain unsettled.
- **Who died:** The **Jeweler** survived E013's assault; **Donk** did not. In **E014**, the identified dead **Randall Owen** is a *new* victim. The demon/soulbroker is **invoked by name, not yet brought onto the scene**, and E014 finishes with **reanimated bodies**, not their defeat.
- **Faction map:** **Liberators** brochure, **Red Scarlet** note, **Cartel**, **Oilbeards**, **Stump**, **Jeweler**, **Principle** and **Koromukishi** have distinct evidentiary roles. A single scene's co-occurrence does not establish who commands whom.
