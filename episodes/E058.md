# E058 — The Magical Banana Trail

> **Corrected Wastes S1E058**; source [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E058.%20The%20Magical%20Banana%20Trail.parakeet.processed.srt), **not audio-verified**. Follows the [E057 skyfall-crater battle](E057.md). Recording times are approximate and include non-story podcast material. **Full spoilers.**

## Episode outcome

The remaining **Wormfather-aligned bugbears** attack after the winged creature's destruction. The party defeats them, and **Vorel finds a live black leech/worm in a bugbear's brain**, lending direct evidence to the earlier claim of **mental parasites**. Nash destroys at least one with dragon acid; the whole network, who implanted them and its relationship to **Dollo Poppio** are **not established**. A spellcasting opponent's unusual disappearance leaves **clothing/effects** behind rather than a clearly recovered corpse; its precise method and fate need reviewing. The party retreats.

The drive home turns into **a literal trail of bananas**: Nash flies to divert or evade danger, becomes separated, and companions drop **bananas** from the moving royal truck at intervals to guide him. Nash follows them but his dragon form ends short of the capital; a **royal search party finds him and brings him home alive**. The group resumes personal life briefly. Several days later, after Hope prepares court, the **Principle arrives personally**—a towering, dark metallic, blue-etched figure decorated with chains and **ten red rings**. He speaks about Dusthill, the mine and the Weirlords, **promises the Dusthill prisoners' return by nightfall**, argues for mutually profitable trade and proposes a **rail link**. The meeting **continues into E059**; no completed captive transfer is shown in E058.

Near the end, **Diptak** enters court and urgently asks **Vorel** to return to the lizardfolk lands, asserting a leadership crisis. The claim is his; the state of that land is not observed until the journey in E059 and the encounter in E060.

## Key continuity transitions

- **Emenil survives** E056/E057 and attends E058 court.
- **Nash's safe return occurs through an actual search-party pickup**, not the proposed multiday walk, permanent loss or death.
- **Worm parasite** is physically observed inside a bugbear, strengthening but not fully authenticating **Quizquaz's contractor narrative**.
- **Principle's prisoner return is still a *promise in E058***, even though the deal later changes in E059.
- **Diptak's urgent invitation is witnessed; his interpretation of a “usurper” is a report** until Vorel reaches the territory.
- **Items and names:** `Relium Vanch`, `Fibel`, `Wormfather`, `Diamond Stroke`, `Mogen Frogan`, and the Principle's age are not independently audio/text verified.
