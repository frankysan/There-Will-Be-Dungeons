# E075 — Fly me to the moon

> **Main-campaign E075**, sourced from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E075.%20Fly%20me%20to%20the%20moon.parakeet.processed.srt). **Provisional, not audio-verified.** Approximate recording offsets include mail, recap and breaks. **Spoilers through E075 only.**

## Session overview

Combat resumes in a lower **CHIDS** control room. The enormous **red, face-filled, memory-eating organism** has produced **two attached simulacra**, Vorel and a copy of **Nash's black dragon**; its duplicate dragon breathes acid, to which real dragon-form Nash is **immune**. The organism **strikes Stanley, knocks him unconscious and drains his memory as well**. **Vorel destroys the main red creature with the flaming Eye of Groxl shield**, and its **tethered Vorel and dragon-Nash replicas dissolve immediately**—**not** a separately killed future Nash. Meanwhile **Hope maintains a crushing metal-arm grapple on the fleeing illithid**, eventually aided by **Nash's dragon bite, which tears away the body and leaves the head in Hope's grasp**. This illithid **dies**, distinct from the campaign's intended ultimate adversary, whose identity has **not been established as this office operator**. By E075's end **Vorel, Nash and Stanley each have the red creature's ongoing 1d4 penalty** to attacks/ability checks; **no reversal has been observed**.

Stanley makes **three successful death saving throws** and stabilizes at **0 HP**, rather than being healed instantly or dying. He is moved to a chair; the team searches bodies, desks and the **corpse of Cerinthala**, who **died in E074**. Inside her many coats Vorel recovers **a translucent blue gemstone** and eventually **an employee identification card explicitly labeled Cerinthala/Cerenthala** with a youthful red-haired photograph. They find **a written alphanumeric code** (the processed SRT sounds like **`A1275Z!2`**, audio review needed), but entering it alone or with the elf's name **does not unlock the computer**. The illithid's **concealed disk** is **not found**, despite searching the body and severed head. **Hope discovers a black worm in its brain and crushes it**, then similarly extracts and destroys **worms from both green-scaled lizardfolk guards' heads**. These are **observed parasitic headworms**, not automatically proof of identical allegiance to Dollo Poppio or confirmed origins.

The group shelters for **several hours** to allow Stanley to regain consciousness; after the initial recovery, he also takes a **short rest, uses Song of Rest and later heals himself**, rather than completing a new full long rest. **Vorel, Nash and Stanley's mind-eating penalties still apply**.

Investigating a **secondary, highly secured inner chamber** behind the illithid control room, Hope operates a computer while **Vorel, Stanley and Nash** stand at distributed controls. After the main door is closed and the sealed anechoic chamber's other buttons and valves are operated in sequence, an inner cradle dispenses a **white/gray, sleeve-mounted weapon with two triggers**. **Vorel first takes it**. Shots produce distinct **red and green projectiles** and seem harmless when fired at the ice construct or inert corpses. It is **not a normal energy gun**, despite initial impressions.

**Cerinthala visibly revives in the control area** during the recovery/armament operation, establishing that **her E074 death was not permanent** at least on this occasion. She sees the sealed chamber and **openly betrays the group**, calling for **Toby** and saying an **“Uvex” / illithid employer wants the adventurers kept alive**. The **blue gem Hope has recovered** becomes or summons **Toby as an ice construct** inside the secured chamber; the mechanism is visually observed but **not fully explained**. Cerinthala casts **Hold Person on Hope** (temporarily paralyzing her) and flees. **Stanley uses Dissonant Whispers, Diplomacy, Phantasmal Force and later Dispel Magic**; **Vorel's Eye shield and Nash's Fire Bolt** shatter Toby's icy casing and then destroy the reanimated corpse called Toby, apparently **killing him at this cutoff**. The **secured weapon** fails to damage Toby despite firing projectiles. Cerinthala **conjures a Wall of Ice** across the route as she escapes; **Stanley's Dispel Magic removes it**. The party pursues but **loses her at the earlier flesh-lined passage**, finding **greasy tracks and ice residue**. Her further fate and route remain **unobserved**.

The supposed gun's true use is finally discovered when **Vorel fires its red and green modes into compatible walls**: the two marks form **a traversable, bidirectional portal pair**. A shot into a body produces **no aperture** and can remove one active endpoint, leaving the other **inert/closed until paired again**. **Nash recognizes a similar ancient entertainment/game concept** and reasons about its travel and escape potential; these are partly **hypotheses**, though the **two connected openings are actually seen**. **Vorel hands the single portal device to Hope**, who holds it at episode cutoff.

A large **bugbear force** arrives in the hall, with hints of flamethrowers used to clear flesh growths. **Nash's fourth-level Fireball wipes out the front ranks**, while remaining bugbears fall back. **Vorel** ventures forward as bait to lure them toward the party's **planned portal escape**, leaving Hope with the device and the others by the secured room. He sees **roughly fifty bugbears and an illithid behind them** and calls out in **Goblin**. **They begin charging at him**. In the back room, the other adventurers spot **an air-blowing vent with a potentially traversable exit**, but do not investigate it. **E075 ends after the charge begins but before contact, a portal evacuation or the fate of the device is resolved. Sira remains missing.**

## Continuity and unsettled questions

**Live cast/status:** Four heroes alive; **Stanley returned to consciousness**, with **a cursed +1 AC Cerinthala amulet still previously worn** (no removal observed); **Nash returns from dragon form**; **Vorel/Nash/Stanley retain memory-devouring 1d4 attack/check penalties** until counter-evidence; **Hope temporarily paralyzed then freed**. **Cerinthala revived but escaped**; **Toby's corporeal ice-and-corpse form is destroyed**. Check whether a soul/mental version survives; do **not** simply keep him alive from E074 or extrapolate all frozen corpses' fates.

**Specific inventory:** **One dual-color portal device** is dispensed from secure vault **to Vorel, then to Hope**; **blue gem** retrieved from Cerinthala's clothing and **appears to trigger Toby's emergence**; **Cerinthala ID card** retrieved; **failed alphanumeric computer code** recorded; **illithid's pocketed disk not found**. **Broken Nash staff** remains only partly usable (nail-bearing piece), not miraculously repaired. **Flying underwear remains with Nash**. All three memories **drained by red organism**, not healed by ordinary short rest.

**Naming:** GM at the table revisits spelling **Cerinthala/Cerenthala/Saranthala**, but still inconsistent in SRT—**do not canonize**. The **Eye of Groxl** owner-confirmed spelling takes precedence over audio approximation “Grojil”. Name **Ovaxiad/Uvex** and relation to the captured/control-room illithid require audio and authoritative handouts.

**Unexplored route:** Near the secured back room, an **air-blowing vent with a possible traversable opening** is noticed at ~02:48. It is an observed alternative, **not a confirmed usable exit or an attempted escape**.

**World data:** Observed portals and gene/brain worms are **strong evidence of working ancient technology and infestation**, but **not proof of every claim Cerinthala made about elf afterlives, lizardfolk origins, or her employers**. Hope's E074 spider poison/exhaustion and Nash's E069 bar poisoning require precise condition accounting.
