E096 ended with **Hope, Nash, Vorel and Walnut approaching the restricted library; Stanley asleep separately with Knight Commander Alentris Vayne**, the plague cured, Fist still sealed and the stolen pendant/key last with Stanley.

## Played E097 outcomes and precise cutoff

1. **Hope, Nash and Vorel actually reach the restricted basement library without Walnut.** Walnut had agreed to accompany them but **is not present** during their first entry and must later be fetched from his room by Vorel (~00:39–00:47); preserve the corrected position rather than placing him at the gate from the start. The **black mist obstructing the lower stair has actually dissipated** since E096's successful cures, establishing this local disappearance rather than proving every cloud everywhere gone. The besieging army still bombards the tower intermittently.
2. The restricted section's **metal portcullis is actually lifted**, after Vorel first fails unaided (11 then 15), then the **three combine their strength (25)** and break its holding **lock/wooden mechanism**. **No Enlarge/Reduce is cast**, no guard intervenes, and the gate becomes accessible, probably unable to close normally. **Hope actually obtains and reads the handwritten *Monstrous Compendium*.** It has **general categories** of imps, devils, pit fiends and other creatures; **no indexed individual entries for General Carthax or Ulvert**, and no name for the voice. It discusses **Asmodeus and Orcus** in general, but these entries **do not identify her tormentor or prove that either is physically inside White Tower**. The book is **left behind** when the group later departs the library.
3. **Vorel finds and reads a manuscript of church relics** and checks entries under Fist, Euphemius, Zorander and other headings **without locating a definite entry for Zorander's Fist**. A few other purported Euphemian artifacts are described (a **golden apple said to restore sight**, and an **apron whose substance was lost/destroyed**)—these are **stories in a handwritten religious book**, not objects obtained by the party or independently verified powers. **Nash looks through the daily journal of an earlier Knight Commander**, finding mundane disciplinary notes rather than a reliable Fist origin.
4. **Hope remains at the books late into the night** looking for the Fist's arrival year/hostile demon identity, at the cost of a normal long rest. **Nash takes a short rest** while she reads. **Vorel fetches the reluctant Walnut from a modest room and brings him down**. Walnut says the paladins only **brought Fist to White Tower about a week ago**, fleeing across Outlands; this is his **NPC report** and does not become a verified calendar date. He states **great fiendish factions including Asmodeus want the relic**, then relays **unverified hearsay** that wearing it confers near-divine strength and lethal touch, and that it may be **haunted**. These powers **are not demonstrated**. Walnut likewise speculates about **the Great Road and judgment at Mount Celestia**, and talks about **Kidigree as a clown god from Limbo**. These claims reflect Walnut's uncertain education, not independent verification of Kidigree's status or a new visit to Limbo.
5. After an unsuccessful attempt to pin down the demon or a better plan, **the party returns from the library and wakes Stanley outside Alentris's chamber**, then discusses the choices **in the kitchen with Walnut** (~01:09–01:29). **Vorel is still determined to fulfill the original Tobacho bargain and reach Earth**, proposing **open the Fist's chest in prepared conditions and confront the apparent spirit** before delivery. **Stanley objects** to handing the dangerous relic to Tobacho or allowing a demon to take it, and wants to **consult Alentris at the following morning's council**. **Hope insists no one let her touch the Fist/glove**, stating a touch would be connected to her return to the enemy. The group **does not open the chest, force Hope to hold it, identify a demon or agree to a final mutually binding plan**.
6. **Hope, Nash and Vorel retire to their quarters** while Stanley returns to sleep. During a private awakening **Hope actually hears the familiar unidentified mental voice** announce **“today is the day” and “today ... we meet,”** and tell her to have faith and follow the plan (~01:32). This is **in-world private voice contact**, not proof the antagonist has manifested, been identified or entered an opened chest.
7. **Hope and Stanley actually begin preparing breakfast together**, later joined by Nash's assistance, with **Vorel observing/evaluating Hope's cooking**. **Hope's stew is eaten by the four and many defenders**; nothing here cures a renewed disease. Walnut joins them, repeatedly derails conversations and makes the title appropriate. A guard/paladin notes **Alentris Vayne was recently appointed around when Fist arrived with her**, another **NPC report**; do not take timing alone as proof she is a demon or impersonator. **Walnut claims Kidigree heads a “Council of Clowns” in Limbo**; none of this turns a cast joke into definitive cosmology. The party warns Walnut not to touch/approach Nash's **separately locked latrine chest**, an artifact distinct from the Fist container.
8. **Before the morning council the group sends Walnut on an errand** and privately notes the suspicious timing of Alentris's appointment, her intended **key-to-Walnut handoff** and Hope's latest warning. **Stanley says he did not notice anything obviously false about Alentris** during their intimate time. **Hope asks the party not to let her touch the glove**. Suspicions are **character conjecture**, not evidence of a revealed changeling, demon or deceit. **The key has still not been given to Walnut**, and no unauthorized ritual is performed.
9. **At the council in the fourth-floor chapel, Alentris publicly rescinds the previous execution order against all four companions** and commands the defenders to protect them as guests. This is a concrete status change from **E094's stayed death sentence**, not a new temporary reprieve. She recognizes **Hope for finding the cure** and acknowledges that **who caused the plague remains unknown**. Present include **High Cleric Crudmund Hordweld, Sir Samson, Sir Clotchup and Walnut**, along with a small selection of paladins.
10. **Alentris says she received a message carried from Knight Commander Thayle (?) stationed near the Great Road/Mount Celestia border**: **a battalion of mounted celestial paladins on pegasi is riding/flying toward White Tower and expected by nightfall**. The plan she announces is to **open the fortress gate and fight through the siege to let a messenger take Fist away**, after which defenders hope the siege stops. **This message, expected schedule and likely enemy withdrawal are beliefs/projections; no pegasi appear, gates open or handoff occurs in E097**. The arrival report **supersedes her earlier preferred use of Captain Waltz's Tobacho-backed replacement ship as the public plan**, but Waltz's promised ship is **not shown canceled, arrived or destroyed**. His E096 message remains an unresolved alternative, not magically erased.
11. **Stanley raises a second concern about the hostile voice promising to arrive that day**, suggesting the **opening of the Fist chest might trigger a confrontation**; **this remains a suggested ambush, not performed or endorsed**. He proposes a **safer extraction**: have the flying messenger **meet him at the tower roof**, use the party's teleportation to bring the messenger inside to collect the relic and back up, instead of fighting through the besieging army at the gates. **Alentris accepts this revision in front of the council** and says she **will send word changing the rendezvous to the roof**. **No message dispatch is directly followed to delivery, no teleport casts, and no celestial escort arrives yet**.
12. **Closing cliffhanger:** Alentris warmly urges Stanley not to succumb to the voice's temptations, speaks of a possible farm hamlet's recovery once the siege lifts, and **suggests the area would be a beautiful place to raise children** (~02:33). This is **romantic/future-family talk**, **not confirmation Stanley has fathered children or chosen to remain in the Outlands**. E097 ends with the party still **inside White Tower, its residents cured, siege ongoing, Fist chest unopened and key not handed off**. They **haven't entered a celestial portal, killed an unseen demon, boarded Waltz's ship, reached Mount Celestia or returned to Earth**.
