# E089 — It has an umbrella in it

> **The Wastes · Arc Three · source-first, processed-ASR dossier, not audio-verified.** Two exports: [Parakeet processed](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E089.%20It%20has%20an%20umbrella%20in%20it.parakeet.processed.srt) and [Qwen Q8 / Canary CTC / Nemotron-labelled](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E089.%20It%20has%20an%20umbrella%20in%20it.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt), about **2 h 51 m 40 s** including recap/break/outro. [ASR comparison](../reports/E089_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-22.md). **No direct listening, source handout verification, or original SRT edits.** E088 ended with an **eight-day recuperation *proposed* but not completed**; E089 opens **after that time has explicitly passed**. *Inverse* remains in its separate reality.

## Latest cutoff and major discoveries

- **The eight-day period actually elapses between E088 and E089. Nash has fully regrown his normal humanoid limbs/body**, with his distinctive pox, scars/red eye and other pre-existing physical features; he fights a bird while naked, can stand and wear clothes. His new clothes resemble **an unfashionable schoolboy/pilgrim outfit**; Hope, Stanley and Vorel have their previously ordered formal clothes, while Hope also has **studded leather and practical wear**. **Do not extend E088's incomplete-heart cutoff into E089**, or conversely backdate this completion into E088.
- **Kanja is reported found murdered** in **M Sports Weekly**: the article says he was **hanged and burned during a home invasion**, with some of the tiny inhabitants making up his body found dead around his residence. **The Harmonium is investigating**, and **no perpetrator/causal link is proved**. This follows E088's observed **Mind Master/Crimson Moon enforcers threatening Kanja over 100,000gp**; both facts support a **serious suspicion**, not an established guilty verdict.
- **The party actually reaches Sigil's Clerk's Ward**, via a hired rickshaw, and has a meeting with **Duerto/Dueto (?)** at the **Portal Regulatory Agency of Sigil**. He and his assistant, **Fax/Faks**, the illithid contender Vorel met during BMF, search a device/database called the **Echo (?)** and identify **one *recorded stable* portal to “Earth.”** The reputed steward is named **Tobacho Fleur/Tobacco Fleur (?)** and associated with the **Hive**. **Neither the portal itself, its key, exact door location nor the route to Earth is obtained**; the party gets the **steward's name and ward**. Duerto also mentions another world **“on the same shard”** accessible only with a **spelljammer** or other vessel; **no vessel is obtained**, and it does not itself prove the destination is the correct Earth.
- **The Portal Regulatory Agency's halfling clerk demands that Hope, as a tiefling, be accompanied by two armed guards** during the visit, claiming tieflings are innately dangerous. **That is institutional prejudice in the scene**, not verified tiefling biology or party consent. Duerto apologizes and treats the group respectfully. **Hope is not arrested**.
- **Nash reveals further personal history of the Solar Mines**: workers were forced to collect dangerous **radioactive material** to create heat/energy sold as **“solar”**; many laborers suffered radiation sickness and died, and Nash **suspects without proof** the Principle bought the power. This is **Nash's firsthand/claimed memory and inference**, not independent confirmation of the mine's buyer.
- **One of Hope's two beloved firearms, *Scully*, is actually recovered** by Stanley from the Sigil sewer room where Hope and Stanley's bodies were first found. It survived apparently intact. The party also recovers **real working ethereal spectrometer goggles** previously used by corpse scavengers; they are **not the 1,400gp store display and are not purchased**. Hope uses them to read **Stanley's glowing Infernal/Abyssal “soul destined for the Weir, proprietor ... reward if found”** neck inscription, including an **additional name sounding Valier Or Noth (?)**. **Exact Infernal proprietor and secondary-name spelling require audio and primary-character clarification**; the **Weir claim plainly remains after resurrection**, but no official metaphysical adjudication is performed.
- **Nash finds and retains a metal adult novelty object that unfolds into an umbrella**, then **names it “Barbara”**. As he repeatedly opens and closes it, **a purple temporary portal appears in the brick wall**. It permits passing objects through and Nash putting his head through; the other side is **hot, dark and enclosed**, with **many black, oily, many-legged crawling creatures** revealed by Fire Bolt. **No one crosses bodily**, and no origin/destination is identified. The portal **closes spontaneously after a short interval**, Nash retrieving Barbara at the last moment. **Cause of the portal is temporally associated with the umbrella, not conclusively demonstrated as a reliable use-trigger or a portal to Earth.**
- In the original sewer arrival room, the group finds **several corpses and shattered equipment**, and meets **Java/Jarva the Carver (?)**, a **rat-man contact from E082**. Java says **Rat King's people killed the scavenger gang** that had bought location rights from Vorel; he claims the arrival portal is **gone**, and nothing else of the original party's kit remains in that room. **His claims are NPC testimony**; the broken site and corpses are observed. For a total **9gp bribe (7 then 2)**, Java says **Hope's other pistol was sold to an intermediary called Fedor/Fedor (?)**, who works for the **same Tobacho Fleur** in the Hive. **The second pistol is not recovered, and no meeting with Fedor or Tobacho takes place**.
- The episode ends with the party **debating how to approach the Hive**: via surface streets/rickshaw, or covertly through the dangerous **Dark Below**. Java will **not guide full-size characters through rat-sized tunnels**, and the party's enlargement/reduction/polymorph options do not achieve a demonstrated workable group infiltration. **No Hive visit, Earth-return portal traversal, restoration of Hope's second pistol, reappearance of Chance/Sira/Eddie, or new action involving the Drive/Principle occurs.**

## I. Nash's eight-day regrowth and the bird (~00:02–00:24)

The GM introduces Sigil as an **inverted torus** and has a bird land on the window sill of the **Soggy Feather executive suite**. Nash lies in bed **with two arms, two legs and his glowing red eye**, having apparently **regrown his full body over the eight days** discussed in E088. A small white bird attacks his eye; it fumbles, defecates on him, and then attacks **Stanley**. Nash gets up and grapples unsuccessfully, while **Vorel throws a water pitcher at the bird** and apparently knocks it unconscious. Vorel conceals it beneath a pillowcase so its movements will be obvious later.

A recurring gag grows from Vorel's suspicion that **birds may be spies or journalists**, inspired by previously seen Sigil floaties and Nothing's old presence. **The bird later escapes**, leaving the covering disarranged; Vorel sees birds on the rooftops but **no direct indication one is an assassin, journalist, familiar or connected to the original victim**. Do not assert a proved avian conspiracy.

At breakfast, Nash cheerfully **displays his restored full humanoid anatomy**, while Stanley and Hope find the spectacle embarrassing. He still has **pox/scars, red eye and history of bodily trauma**; this is **regrowth of his previous physical form**, not evidence every old injury or magical item is reversed. The GM plainly says **“since eight days passed”** and that Nash has all limbs back. Nash has **a new set of trousers, shirt, stockings, buckle shoes and jacket** that he dislikes, styled rather like a school uniform or pilgrim outfit; Stanley, Hope and Vorel have the fine clothes arranged by tailor Lohar in E088. **Hope also has a practical outfit and studded leather armor**, acquired in the intervening shopping/re-equipment interval; **no detailed invoice or full rearmament inventory is given**. Nash's spells remain functional following E088's explicit ruling correction.

A newspaper boy delivers the **M Sports Weekly**. Its front illustration and headline state **“Kanja Found Dead.”** According to the *article*, the champion was **found hanged and burned**, and some **members of the many tiny beings formerly hidden inside his appearance were also discovered dead around his home**; authorities do not know the sequence of wounds. The **Harmonium** is investigating as a faction that functions as a **self-appointed or de facto constabulary**, not as directly appointed Lady of Pain police. The group naturally wonders if **Mind Master carried out E088's threats via Crimson Moon enforcers**, but **neither newspaper nor play proves the killer's identity**. Vorel checks the supposedly dead bird afterward and finds **it escaped**.

## II. The rickshaw and the Clerk's Ward (~00:30–01:20)

The reunited five decide to search the **Clerk's Ward** for records of a way back to **Earth**. Hope still wishes to recover **her pistols**, and Vorel/Nash remember their landing point in the **Dark Below** near the Welcome Center. Vorel previously **sold the arrival site's location to a corpse-scavenger crew**; re-entering could involve rival ownership claims. They prioritize the records office before returning to the sewers.

The party hires a very muscular human **rickshaw puller** from the Merchant's Ward, name sounding **Fanbo Joe/Fambo Joe (?)**. He tells them his ambition is **urban performance poetry (“wham poetry”) at the Dangly Dozen**, typically **Friday at about 7:30 p.m.**, and hands Vorel a card for **William's Bronze Gymnasium**. Vorel praises his physique, **helps run the rickshaw for part of the route** while still wearing part of his tuxedo, and makes a tentative plan to see a performance. **Neither poetry event nor gym appointment occurs this episode**, nor is a romance or lasting partnership established.

On arrival, **Vorel pays a fare of 1gp and an additional 20 silver pieces** as a tip; the latter is **2gp in standard D&D currency**, unusually large compared with the 1gp fare, but **the spoken transfer should be preserved** rather than silently rewriting the tip to 20 copper. The group enters the quieter, bureaucratic **Clerk's Ward**, whose buildings look alike and lack helpful labels.

The friends buy **two large deep-fried frogs on sticks** from a market vendor, paying **50 silver pieces** for the pair. He explains an important distinction: **the Lady forbids exclusive ownership of portals**, he claims, but **knowledge of their locations, practical stewardship or control of access** can be traded and defended. This is **vendor's account of how trade works**, not yet independently sourced universal legal code. The party discusses possible Earth exports; **Nash recounts that the Solar Mines' apparent “solar energy” was often derived from accumulated radioactive sources rather than the sun**, at great human cost. The claim that the **Principle** bought the disguised energy is **Nash's speculation**, not something he saw on sale receipts.

Initially they enter the **wrong office**, a horse-drawn-carriage regulatory department. Its receptionist draws a map directing them a few blocks away to the **Portal Regulatory Agency of Sigil**. This is **a concrete visited institution**, not just a contemplated search.

## III. The Portal Regulatory Agency and the Hive lead (~01:10–01:40)

The agency has an ornate gilded lobby and unfamiliar **revolving doors**. A staff member arranges a meeting with portal regulations expert **Duerto/Dueto (?)**. Before the meeting, a halfling attendant says **Hope must be escorted by two armored guards because she is a tiefling**, expressing a prejudiced stereotype about infernal ancestry and violence. **This is discriminatory treatment by officials**, not proof Hope has threatened anyone; she **plays along as though the guards are her entourage**. The party is taken to an upper-floor boardroom.

Duerto is a tall, unusual humanoid with **bulbous head, lateral eyes and small mouth**. His assistant is **Fax/Faks**, the **illithid** who competed in the BMF championship, with a distinctive metal fitting in his head. Hope and Stanley tense at the sight because of their earlier encounters with illithids in the Wastes. **Fax recognizes Vorel** and seems interested in his M-fighting coaching machine, but is told by his supervisor not to discuss sport at work.

The agency searches an information system referred to as the **Echo (?)**, using a headpiece and retractable lenses, for the planet **“Earth,” not the Elemental Plane of Earth**. Duerto reports **one *registered stable* portal to that destination**. He warns that the reputed **steward**, **Tobacho Fleur/Tobacco Fleur (?)**, operates in **the Hive**, a dangerous, deprived ward. He **does not supply the actual door's coordinates, operative portal key, cost, access permissions or details of a safe route**. He also mentions a **world on the same “shard”** reachable by a **spelljammer** or other craft, but no vessel or proof of connection to the Wastes is shown. **Do not inflate “one record” into proof only one portal in all existence could lead to Earth**, especially after the street vendor emphasizes secret access.

Duerto expresses regret over Hope's escort and disavows the attendant's fear; the group leaves with **a name and a ward**, not an appointment with the steward. They also learn the **Hive is viewed as Sigil's largest and most dangerous ward**, though such generalizations partly reflect local prejudices and bureaucratic class division.

## IV. Stay in Sigil, or go home? (~01:40–02:05)

Now outside, **Stanley asks whether they should even return to the Wastes**. Sigil has offered food, comfort and comparatively little overt danger during his brief stay; Earth repeatedly nearly killed them and actually killed two. **This is a serious philosophical proposal by Stanley**, not a decision that the group remains permanently. Hope speaks for returning as a **queen with responsibilities**. Nash says he has **a wife and child back home**, Vorel remembers people and promises left behind, and the group discusses **Chance drifting, Sira and Eddie's precarious situation, unfinished kingdoms, Diptak, Solar Mines survivors, promises, old enemies and the Weir**. These are **recalled obligations, not fresh in-world sightings**. The uncertain **time elapsed on Earth** further complicates their plans.

The group eats at an elegant Clerks' Ward restaurant, the **Pig's Foot/Big Foot (?)**, where Vorel's fame moves them from a cramped table to an expansive private-feeling seat. They eat a lavish version of **poutine** (potatoes, cheese, gravy and beef) and try **cola soda pop**. **The meal costs 20gp including tip**; **this is a paid purchase**, not evidence an inn partnership was completed. They take **another rickshaw back** toward the Soggy Feather for **1.5gp**, then change out of expensive finery for the sewer search. The old wardrobe/armor loadout is still not fully source-inventoried.

## V. Hope's pistol, Weir-inscribed spectrometer and Barbara (~02:05–02:36)

Near the Welcome Center the five descend into the **Dark Below** where the original party arrived in E082. In the chamber where **Hope and Stanley's earlier corpses were recovered**, they find **a severely decomposed small-bodied person** and refuse. Vorel recalls he violently attacked two corpse scavengers there, but **cannot conclusively identify the body by its destroyed face**, and the body has already been looted.

After group investigation, **Stanley fishes a handgun from the dirty water**. Hope recognizes **Scully**, one of her two personal pistols, by the grip marks; it is apparently **fully preserved**. She **physically takes possession** of it. **No ammunition count, full operational firing test or recovery of its counterpart is shown yet**.

A second object resembles an explicit adult novelty. **Nash briefly tries to use his remaining anatomical storage pocket and then discards it**, while Stanley examines the device and finds a switch: it is **a mechanical umbrella with a suggestive handle**. Nash retrieves it and experiments with opening and closing it. He later names it **“Barbara.”** It is **a found object, not a literal recovered gun, confirmed spell key or intentionally prescribed portal tool**.

A further search uncovers **ethereal spectrometer goggles**, the small crystal and metal extensions recognizable from the E088 shop display, though lighter. **Stanley cleans and activates them** and sees **blue-white auras/trails around other living party members**. They examine **Hope and Stanley's necks** and find **their names with Infernal/Abyssal script**, indicating their souls remain **destined for the Weir**, including a **proprietor and “reward if found”** marking. A secondary personal name sounds like **Valier Or Noth (?)**, and the proprietor's spoken name is highly unstable across ASR; **do not normalize spellings without audio verification**. **Hope speaks Infernal**, allowing a partial translation; Stanley is momentarily **dizzy for a minute on abruptly removing the active goggles**. This is **a newly recovered actual device**, not the premium glasses purchased in E088 (the party **declined** that offer); it strengthens evidence **the soul claim persists after embodied resurrection**, without proving the legal enforceability of the claim or who ultimately owns either soul.

While the others inspect auras, **Nash repeatedly flicks the umbrella open in mock dueling gestures**, and an **apparently new purple portal/door materializes in the nearby brick wall**. Nash throws a stone/trash through it, then **throws Barbara through as a test**: both objects disappear across the membrane. He **puts only his head across**, keeping hold of the masonry with his body still in the sewer. The far side is **hot, muggy, brown ground, pitch-dark and apparently bounded by distant walls**. He uses a **Light spell and Fire Bolt** to probe it and glimpses **many black-oily many-legged crawlers** on the walls. **The door is temporary and begins fading**. Nash **retrieves Barbara as it closes**, succeeding on a very high Dexterity check; no party member travels completely through. **Do not infer the umbrella reproducibly opens all portals or that the destination is the Hive, Abyss, Earth or any named plane.** The exact causal link and safe repeatability are **unverified**.

Stanley quietly attempts to call **“Parley”**, the word associated with his old megaphone/sword **Diplomacy**, but **nothing responds**. This is **a failed retrieval attempt**, not resurrection or successful recall of the weapon.

## VI. Rat King aftermath and the two Hive leads (~02:36–02:50)

In the original **sewer arrival room** Vorel/Nash find **six or seven bodies**, debris and shattered gear belonging to the **scavenger crew to whom Vorel earlier sold the landing site's location rights**. The party meets **Java/Jarva the Carver**, a little ratfolk contact whom Vorel knew earlier in Arc Three. Java says his **Rat King's minions killed the intruding scavengers, confiscated their belongings and searched for the original portal**, but **could not find the landing portal**. The bodies and broken equipment are **directly observed**; the Rat King's exact orders and whether a portal can ever recur remain **Java's testimony**. Java boasts about rats one day displacing all Sigil folk, an **NPC ideological threat**, not a revolution already in progress.

As Hope holds **Scully**, Java admits that he found **a second similar gun** earlier and **sold it**, misleading the buyer by claiming it was a virility-enhancing magical artefact. Vorel **pays Java 7gp**, then **another 2gp**, to extract the broker's name and employer. Java names **Fedor/Fedor (?)**, a supposed servant/agent of **Tobacho Fleur**, an influential **Hive/Dark Below trader**. This is **the same provisional name the portal agency gave as steward of Earth's one known stable portal**. The **double lead** is significant: locating Tobacho could bring both a **way home** and **Hope's second pistol**, but **neither is yet achieved**. Java also warns that **Dark Below tunnels toward the Hive are dangerous and narrow enough only for rat-sized travelers**. Reducing the whole party to that size is considered **out of character**, but no suitable sustained multicharacter spell solution is implemented.

The episode cuts off with the **five companions together in the sewers considering open surface entry into the Hive versus a covert rat-sized route**. **No Hive contact, second gun recovery, portal key purchase, departure from Sigil, inn sale or assassination-suspect confrontation occurs.**
