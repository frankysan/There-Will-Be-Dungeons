# E122 — That's Ghost Water

> **The Wastes · corrected S1E122 · Arc Five · played campaign session.** Original listing **TWBD 124: That's Ghost Water**, catalogue date **19 October 2020**, from the retained owner-spreadsheet snapshot; recording date not verified. Continues [E121](E121.md) in Elhandar's mountain mushroom cave. [Parakeet][p] · [Qwen-labelled export][q] · [ASR comparison](../reports/E122_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md) · [Arc Five](../narrative/ARC_FIVE.md). **ASR-provisional, no audio listening.** The entire primary export was read through the **02:40:10** network outro; the secondary was fully parsed for measurement and read in selected high-risk windows, not interpreted throughout. No original transcripts or media edited.

**Played cutoff:** The formerly imprisoned black dragon **dies**, Vorel **extracts its dragon-metal heart**, and Stanley/Nash **hide that heart beneath a headache-inducing metal spike in the cave**. The rest of the dragon's body **dissipates, bones included**. **Hope is freed alive** when Stanley presses the detonator. All four subsequently reach the **Gatherers' Lodge**, whose two elven ghost custodians offer hospitality. They end in a **play fight**: Hope holds Nash, Vorel opens his mouth and Stanley pushes bonbons into his face/mouth. **Swallowing and its outcome are deferred**. No completed sealing device, return to the cottage, departure for Earth, resurrection or new Diontalus confrontation occurs.

## The black dragon's last account (~00:00–00:31)

- **Intro/recap (~00:00–00:05)** revisits E121, including Dilo's body falling and Diontalus's manifestation. It is not another battle or another death. Beau resumes as GM with Scott/Nash, Jon/Stanley, Kristen/Hope and Kyle/Vorel; numeric diarizer labels remain export-local.
- **The apparition continues (~00:05–00:10).** Nash remains in dragon form, suspended with his chest and mechanical heart exposed. A golden-white spectral mother caresses the wounded black dragon. Nash can speak and move his head, is not in pain, and receives no automatic emotional calming. **Hope still cannot hear through her sphere**. Dilenoria's body and Omnom's feet remain nearby.
- **Interrupted communication (~00:10–00:17).** The wounded dragon says Nash shares an existence with its mother. It says it watched the party near **Slavetown/Tylander Sullivan** and attempted thought-messages; Nash describes dreams and destructive outbursts instead. The dragon blames its captors' experimentation for corrupting the contact. It calls Nash part of the **black dragonflight** and its prospective last survivor. **This is the dragon's account**, not a verified census, proof that Nash is its biological sibling, or proof that his children are dragons.
- **Earth, the Weir and a proposed remedy (~00:17–00:27).** The dragon says ancient human colonists entered a black hole that was a portal, bargained with the **Soulbroker** for lordship, and exposed Earth to the **Weir/Far Realm**. It describes Elhandar's elves establishing their station and seeking dragon intervention; the dragonflights ultimately lost, although the Weir were delayed. It calls the **Principle their remaining agent/pawn**. Stanley asks why the Weir then want the Principle destroyed; **the dragon does not resolve that contradiction**. Its estimates of former-human demon lords are not a counted population.
- **The heart mission (~00:22:51–00:25).** The dragon offers its own heart after death. It instructs the party to have Elhandar's elves construct a device rendered **`rubistic triangulosphere` / `robustic trianglosphere` (?)**, using impossible geometry, then **melt it at Earth's hottest point so it reaches the planetary center and seals the decay**. It predicts normal time and exclusion of Weir influence. **Instructions and predictions only**: no blueprint, knowledgeable smith, construction, sealing or outcome is demonstrated.
- **Alternate selves (~00:28–00:31).** The dragon attributes time's misbehavior to Weir corruption but expressly **does not know what caused Nash's particular alternate selves**. Its advice cannot establish that all copies share one heart, that time travel is solved, or that previously helpful Nashes are enemies.

## Nash's ancestry and the dragon's death (~00:31–00:43)

Nash addresses the spectral mother and receives **bodily purring and emotional impressions**, rather than an ordinary spoken explanation. She conveys that frequent communication is unavailable and this manifestation is bound to a mother's love for her son. Nash asks whether she is his mother too; he receives **a negative answer with pity/sympathy**. The surrounding ASR clause **“that she's you”** remains unclear and must not become an established metaphysical identity.

The black dragon then offers an account of Nash's manufacture (**~00:34:37–00:39:22**). It describes robed and bandaged people in a hot place, called **Dragon Sons** in Qwen (Parakeet fragments include **`dragons s ou l` / `dragonsons`**), who value dragon metal and seek immortality. It claims the **Principle commissioned an invulnerable body**, that their experiments lasted hundreds of years, and that Nash was **cast in a mold from human and other-species material from Clench's flesh pits**. It estimates **72 fathers and 83 mothers** among the donors. These are **NPC assertions about unplayed history**, not laboratory records, identified parents or independently confirmed genealogy. Scott explicitly describes Nash as **devastated**, his long search for family apparently frustrated; the dragon counsels him to value his children and chosen connections.

At **~00:40–00:42**, the black dragon sighs, goes limp and **stops breathing**. The mother's projection **withdraws into Nash's pacemaker**, his exposed body closes and heals, and he lands before returning to human form. Names remain provisional: son **Yoshular / Yoshalar / Yshilar (?)**, mother **Kaz de Jere / Cas de Gurey (?)**, alongside E121's variants. This is **an actual death**, superseding the living E121 cutoff; **the mother has withdrawn, not been transferred into a newly harvested heart**.

## Hope released; two different hearts and a recovered wand (~00:42–01:13)

1. **Release (~00:42:52–00:43:44).** Stanley finally **presses the detonator**. Hope's sphere disappears; her Dexterity save of **20** lets her land unharmed. **About forty grenades** fall without exploding. His explanation that Diontalus exploited his reluctance to press the button is **Stanley's inference**. This resolves captivity, not Diontalus's escape or the Hand's obligations.
2. **Knowledge becomes shared (~00:47:21–00:47:35).** Hope asks why the dragon must be cut apart. **Nash gives her a quick account of everything learned**, so she now receives the testimony secondhand; she did not hear it while sealed away. His private bodily impressions need not become word-for-word public knowledge merely because a recap occurs.
3. **Recovered equipment (~00:47–00:56).** Stanley finds Dilo's **apparently mundane rapier**. The two pieces of the snapped wand **magically rejoin** as he brings them together. Study establishes **three charges, recharging at dawn**, and a **one-minute resilient sphere**, movable by pointing the wand, **without concentration for this item**. The GM attributes Hope's much longer confinement to **Diontalus's additional effects**. These are the episode's item rulings, not an external rules verification. Stanley later actually uses it, although no separate formal attunement scene is narrated.
4. **Extraction (~00:48–00:54).** Vorel uses **Cunning Artisan** and a respectful, careful approach; the GM does not require a roll. The extraction **takes about four story-hours**. Nash provides mushroom mouth-light; the others may benefit from a **short rest**, not a performed long rest. The extracted black, glassy **natural dragon-metal heart is roughly five feet tall and initially “a couple of tons,” later discussed as five tons**. The inconsistent estimates are not a weighed specification. Nash's **smaller manufactured pacemaker remains inside him**; these are separate objects.
5. **Rejected transport branches (~00:54–01:13).** A sphere lasts too briefly for the return journey. Teleporting the heart to Planet Nash, shrinking it and carrying it as a dragon, and Hope's cross-plane travel are discussed. The GM clarifies **Planet Nash is on the same Prime Material plane**, and corrects Nash's exhaustion to **one level under the current transformation rule**, not the initially claimed four. **No teleportation circle is cast, no heart transported offsite, no second dragon transformation performed**, and no Elhandar circle coordinates are acquired.

## Concealment rather than delivery (~01:13–01:48)

The party debates splitting up to fetch help, burial and an earth-rail transport scheme. **Stanley argues they should stay together**, fearing Diontalus might control other elves or exploit their families. He proposes **taking a ship to Earth** to draw pursuit away. **His departure is a plan**; the group first deals with the heart and returns toward inhabited areas. Widespread possession of Elhandar is **a fear, not a discovered fact**.

**Vorel explores the rear cave (~01:20–01:27)** and finds the dragon's resting place, discarded scales, dried blood and a **roughly seven-foot black metal spike/stick**. It creates worsening **head pressure/headache** as Vorel, Stanley and Nash approach. Stanley reaches a zone imposing **disadvantage on everything while there**; no damage is yet assigned. ***Mage Hand* cannot lift it**. The GM supports a similarity to the psychic creatures previously encountered below Tietkalla, but **no origin, maker, official item name or faction is identified**. **“Staff of disruption” is party shorthand**, not a confirmed item identification. The suggestion that the dragon pulled it from its body is an interpretation of the scene.

**Actual burial (~01:35:49–01:38:40):** Nash uses ***Mold Earth*** at range to form an inclined cavity beneath/near the spike. Stanley **spends a wand use**, encloses the heart in a nearly weightless sphere, guides it into the cavity and releases it. Nash **closes and disguises the earth**. The spike stays in place. The heart is **hidden and left behind**, not given to smiths or protected by a tested ward. The dragon's remaining body then **floats up and dissipates completely, bones included**, filling the chamber with sparkling light. **No similar disappearance, burial or resurrection of Dilo or Omnom is stated**.

Before leaving, **Hope collects one of each mushroom type that fits in her bag**, intending them for the tieflings rather than eating them. Stanley pockets **eight empathy grenades**. Nash settles on **fifty slices of the light mushroom**; the table folds collection into the extraction time using his little additional hand. The light fades; duration is variously rounded to **four/five hours**, not a controlled measurement. At the lodge Nash explicitly says he **has no active new slice**. No new improvised casting phrase accompanies the actual *Mold Earth* here.

The four return to **Chester's kill site (~01:43–01:47)**. Stanley finds the dead elven mages badly charred and with **no useful surviving equipment or papers identified**. Their remaining **fine, intricately made robes** differ from the military/station/country clothing seen before. Vorel takes a **cloth sample/wrapping and bearsaurus tooth**. The robes are **a clue**, not proof of an organization or extent of mind control. He and Stanley navigate toward the lodge, arriving after **about two more story-hours**.

## The Gatherers' Lodge and the candy fight (~01:48–02:40)

The lodge stands on land surrounded by water, with elaborate hunting carvings, shuttered/draped windows and a messy but used mudroom. Vorel announces the party, senses danger and scouts a window. An initial **headphones-off scene (~01:57)** transcribes little beyond **“you hear”**; later Vorel explicitly reports **giggling**. Do not reconstruct missing sound or dialogue from that short ASR passage.

Vorel **smashes the window with the tooth-filled wrapping** and leaps inside. Stanley and Hope enter after him; Nash hovers above to survey **four connected buildings around an enclosed courtyard**, targets, a firepit and a **boat at a wharf**. The boat is **seen, not taken or used as faster transport**. Stanley opens curtains and lights the fireplace with ***Prestidigitation***. Rhythmic banging comes from upstairs until his **megaphone announcement** brings the custodians down.

Two **elven ghosts** welcome them: a male whose name appears as **Tomsilar Bear / Tom Cilabert (?)**, and a female **Aluneva / Eluneva (?)**. Their flustered furniture-moving explanation and the party's sexual jokes do not establish a sinister attack. The male **goes to verify Chester's death**, without returning with confirmation before the cutoff. The woman actually **brings water, readies rooms, cleans the lodge and provides colored bonbons**. Stanley drinks **real water**; Hope and Stanley enjoy sweets, and Vorel swallows a red one without chewing. **No completed overnight stay or long rest is played**. Vorel's claim that bodiless ghosts cannot be possessed by Diontalus remains **his safety inference**.

Nash distrusts **“ghost water” and “ghost candy.”** Hope uses her **appearance-changing necklace** to imitate an elf, pretending the sweets changed her. The GM retains **Nash's volunteered failed Insight**, despite Hope's poor Deception; the other two understand the trick. They begin explicitly **nonlethal play combat with a separate play-HP convention (~02:26–02:38)**. Hope grapples Nash, Stanley helps seat him, and Vorel positions the chair. Nash attempts ***Mage Hand***, initially described as appearing, but the GM **reverses that success because Nash cannot make the gestures while held**. **Record the final ruling, not a functioning defensive hand**; this is a table ruling, not a general statement about official grapple mechanics.

During that failed attempt the **GM supplies a rhyme**, rather than Scott delivering one: Parakeet **`you think i only know said but i actually know mage hand`**, Qwen **`You think I only know sand? But I actually know mage hand`** (**~02:30:58–02:31:24**). Scott remarks that he normally objects to Beau making up his rhymes but this one is about what he would have used. It belongs in [Nash's register](../world/characters/nash-spellcasting-phrases.md) as **GM-supplied wording for a failed attempt**, not a successfully performed spell or repaired quotation.

Hope next **uses the necklace to look like Nash**, with Stanley helping sell the deception. Nash fails Insight and believes a **future Nash** is involved; **no actual alternate Nash arrives**. Vorel wins the jaw-opening contest and Stanley presses the candy into Nash's face/mouth. Beau **ends play around 02:38:14**. Scott explicitly leaves **whether Nash actually eats it** for next time. The following discussion of escaping in dragon form is **an unperformed alternative**, followed by the show outro through **02:40:10**.
