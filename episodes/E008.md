## Reference significance

- **Guest-character distinction:** both guest adventurers are gnomes. `Edison Manchester III` is self-introduced; nickname orthography `Eddie` (ASR) versus `Eddy` (later wiki) is a deliberate alias pending verification.
- **Nash's voices:** a call toward vengeance and an appeal by a captive luminous figure should not be conflated into a single verified narrator; distinguish vision, source claims, physical threat and inferred magic.
- **Two underground complexes:** The E008 facility is reached from the **desert antenna**, not via the earlier Dusthill well/dragon-engine tunnels. No connecting tunnel is proven.
- **Leopold and security:** his name and claims are useful search anchors; “not a computer”, the complex's classification and imprisonment policy remain **character claims**, not neutral setting facts.
- **Mimic versus dog:** the [biting case](../world/creatures/green-case-mimic.md) and [eight-legged animal](../world/creatures/eight-legged-facility-predator.md) are separate threats. The floating rods/metal, locked containers and shifting projection environment are additional objects/phenomena, not names of confirmed technologies.
