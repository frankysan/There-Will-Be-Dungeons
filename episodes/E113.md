## A lifeless guide in the Gray Waste (~00:04–00:41)

1. **E112's color-changing aftermath (~00:04–00:15).** On arrival the landscape is uniformly gray, silent and apparently sunless. **Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider are already grayscale/melancholy from E112**. **Steerious** looks like himself, with surviving **gold accents**; Hope is **initially not discolored**, but **later becomes gray after further saves/travel**. Blood Skunk's ranger **Natural Explorer** is discussed as a possible desert skill, but the GM explicitly warns that the **Outer Planes can defeat mundane navigation**; a low assisted Survival roll leaves the party unable to orient by landmarks.
2. **The orc who knows the wrong way (~00:14–00:24).** A motionless **gray orc wearing a loincloth** lies almost beneath the group's feet, saying **“you're going the wrong way”**; he reluctantly identifies **Pointless**, the settlement by the Styx. **Steerious really punches his nose**; ***Detect Magic* yields no simple nearby enchantment to dispel**, although the plane's magic overwhelms the reading. The orc is **profoundly apathetic**, not a willing reliable escort or freely given map. Hope tries several directions until the orc does not object. Steerious drags the body **by the wrist** as they walk.
3. **An actual decapitation without observed death (~00:24–00:34).** Frustrated by his indifference, **Steerious repeatedly strikes the orc's neck with his greatsword until the head separates**. **The head is explicitly still blinking, looking around and answering**, so **do not record a dead orc**. **Blood Skunk also fires an arrow through the separated head**, leaving it responsive. Steerious affectionately calls this helpless passenger **Headsy/Hedzy (?)**, carries the head while dragging the body, and later attempts to board a ferry with him. Any asserted biological invulnerability or mechanics of Gray Waste petitioners is inferred from this one example, not yet a universal rule.
4. **Pointless at last (~00:32–00:43).** Blood Skunk finally spots a tiny shape on the horizon, and the party **actually marches roughly eight hours** to a **ten-hut settlement called Pointless**, bordered by **fog and the black River Styx**. During renewed **Charisma saves**, **Dr. Spider experiences overwhelming despair** and **Hope herself loses her natural color**. The others notice **Steerious remains colored**, including his gold nose ring, despite the surrounding bleakness. No other inhabited building or resident apart from the orc is clearly encountered; the company walks through to the **wharf**, where a cloaked ferryman approaches.

## What the Styx ferry really costs (~00:43–01:10)

5. **Hesterbrook's rules (~00:43–00:49).** The ferryman reveals a **shining blue skeletal face** and introduces himself as **Hesterbrook**. He demands each passenger **introduce themselves** and tender **something genuinely cared about**, not necessarily gold. A magical barrier prevents boarding without acceptable payment. This is **observed at this crossing**, not proof of the prices or all ferrymen's laws everywhere.
6. **Four completed payments, and a rejected fifth passenger (~00:48–01:03).** **Hope gives Hesterbrook her pistol *Fox***, which **the GM explicitly distinguishes from *Scully*** (previous Scully custody is separate); the ferryman **takes Fox into his cloak**. **Steerious sacrifices his large greatsword**, which also vanishes into the ferryman's cloak. He tries to bring **Headsy** aboard without payment, but **Headsy cannot pay**, as a Gray Waste denizen who says everything was taken from him. **Steerious really leaves him at the wharf**, with the previously shot arrow, **alive/responsive**, no crossing. **Blood Skunk refuses to part with his bow**, instead **cuts off his own left little toe** and tenders it; **Dr. Spider heals the immediate wound**, but **no toe regrowth is shown**. **Dr. Spider relinquishes his treasured lab coat**, which Hesterbrook takes; he still has his other equipment/device. **All four paid passengers physically board**. No payment is refunded, no sword/Fox/lab coat recovered in E113, and no transported Headsy.
7. **Across the river (~01:03–01:13).** Hesterbrook claims he was a prince who voluntarily chose ferrying after death and hints at special oaths/protection. The boat **moves along the Styx**, the ferryman paddling calmly. Hesterbrook says **an imp called Voxil/Varkso (?)** will meet them on the Pandemonium shore and guide them to the Council, whose proceedings require being **amusing rather than merely threatening**. He calls Blood Skunk a **Harlequinari exile**, an inference the clown resents; this **NPC characterization** does not reveal exactly how, why or when the exile occurred.

## The Voice's former serpent servant (~01:12–01:30)

8. **A giant serpent almost breaks the old pact (~01:12–01:19).** The Styx suddenly roils as a huge black-scaled leviathan emerges. **Hesterbrook invokes the ancient pact protecting ferrymen and their boats**, but the creature says it hears the **song of its absent master** among the passengers. It is provisionally named **Velithon / Velothan / Velathon (?)**, **“Lord of the Lost”**. Its stories about quilts of stolen souls are **its own supernatural testimony**; its full history is not independently witnessed. The ferryman warns against reaching into the water.
9. **Hope, the hidden Voice and a future promise (~01:18–01:29).** The leviathan singles out **Hope**. She **reveals her Hand-bearing altered appearance**, then briefly **uses her appearance power to imitate the hooded Voice**; the monster says she resembles but **is not** its old master. The **hooded figure appears at Hope's side**, visible to **Hope alone**; the leviathan **cannot see it** and begs her to make it speak. The figure suggests **flattering and redirecting** the monster, dismissing it as a former servant. Hope actually **relays exaggerated praise**, making Deception/Persuasion checks; the leviathan accepts sufficient flattery and **does not attack the boat**. She **promises a future visit to its undersea domain after her business**, which it says it will remember. **No visit, permanent alliance or literal command from the Voice to the serpent is performed**. They leave it in the Styx unharmed.
10. **A completed passage to Pandemonium (~01:29–01:36).** Hesterbrook safely pilots amid enormous waves to the **rocky wharf beneath titanic wind-bored stair-like stone**. The GM explicitly says **the Gray Waste color/melancholy effects end and color is restored to all affected PCs**, including Hope. Hesterbrook then disappears; **he has delivered the party**. Arrival is **played**, not merely forecast in E112.

## A mad council that eventually answers (~01:34–02:37)

11. **Voxil, mine cart, and Dario (~01:34–01:51).** The party meets a small bluish imp, name heard **Voxil/Varkso/Voxilb (?)**, who opens a magical stone panel and leads them into **wind-scoured caves**. All four **ride an actual mine cart** at extreme speed and stop near the **Council of Clowns** entrance. A spring-loaded clown head requires a password; **the imp supplies “dingleberries”**, and the door opens. The guide **leaves them**. Inside they meet **Dario the Impresario**, writing at a desk. He first proposes **an absurd appointment more than 4,500 years away**, then **a successful Persuasion roll wins an immediate audience**. No multi-millennial wait occurs.
12. **Audience and identity test (~01:51–02:13).** **Dario** changes to a formal ringmaster outfit and ushers them through a laughing doorway into an enormous magical **clown auditorium**, where **thirteen seats** face an unpredictable, heckling crowd. **Bobo the Clown** leads; **Princess Cecilia**, **Godfrey**, and other clowns trade cruel jokes and break their own proceedings. **Hope petitions for the Eye associated with her Hand**. Bobo **literally smells her exposed altered hand**, recognizes its owner as **the Voice**, and claims to know the secret. His description of the Voice as an entity bent on **controlling or displacing the gods** is the **Council's interested testimony**, not independent proof of every cosmological motive.
13. **Hope's character under examination (~02:13–02:29).** The Council demands evidence of **“character”** and humor. Hope discusses **leaving her former friends, guilt about Tietkalla's devastation, building/driving the Fate 4, guns, violence and a remembered dwarf mistreatment**. **Some of these are retrospective character statements with a Deception check, not fresh played deaths or independently established causation.** She also says **she wants godlike power and revenge**. The clowns find her serious responses inadequate, mock her and criticize **Blood Skunk as a Harlequinari outsider**. She responds with open disgust and a poor Intimidation roll. **Her anger itself amuses them**, and they reverse their posture.
14. **Approval and destination clue (~02:29–02:35).** Bobo announces the Council **grants the petition** (after a deliberately sham recess) because Hope's unintended humor entertains them. **The Council says** the Eye of the Voice is within **Pentel Weiss/Pentelvice/Pentalvise (?)**, an **ancient prison** a long-dead wizard created to hold dangerous entities. They locate it somewhere in the **phlogiston**, the space **between Prime Material worlds/disks**, **near the Corinthian Nebula**, and say **only wardens and council members know its precise position**; not an observed map/coordinates. They warn her not to broadcast the location.
15. **A compass actually handed over (~02:31–02:37).** A tiny clown car presents Hope with a **small wind-up box**; a plush spring-head pops out and tilts, initially apparently pointing left. **Bobo calls this a keyed compass** that will point **Hope** toward the prison and **will not work for others**. **Hope actually takes the box**, unlike a proposed future gift, but **has not tested it across planes or found the target**. The Council suggests returning to the Prime Material Plane and **obtaining passage aboard a ship** to reach phlogiston space. Hope **does not book a ship here**.
16. **Gate back to Tobacho (~02:37–02:40).** After the Council's absurd farewell song and remarks, **Hope actually opens a portal with her charged copper ring back to Tobacho's Hive bar in Sigil**. The **four walk through**, and the GM says **they have returned to Sigil**, resolving both the E112 Gray Waste cliffhanger and the full Styx/Pandemonium quest. **The signed Tobacho compact from E112 remains unfulfilled**, no Eye returned and no confrontation with the dragon is yet played after their arrival. **Out-of-game:** the host says the next show will return to **Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Angelashi, Omnom and the elves**, possibly a new character. **That is a preview, not new E113 play or an actual E113 reunion.**
