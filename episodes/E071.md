# E071 — The Door Street Journal

> **Corrected main-campaign E071**, continuing [E070](E070.md), derived from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E071.%20The%20Door%20Street%20Journal.parakeet.processed.srt). **ASR-based provisional episode dossier; no audio listening performed.** Approximate recording offsets include recap, intermission, tactical rules dialogue and outro. **Full spoilers through E071.**

## What happens

**E070's conference-room fight concludes:** Enlarged Vorel holds and bashes flaming **hellhounds**, while **Stanley's Booming Blade and animated Diplomacy**, **Hope's radiant ammunition**, and **Nash's cold spells** bring the remaining defenders down. The **large demonic ape that remained active** dies from damage and Vorel's dog-as-club strike; Nash avoids being crushed by its falling body but takes some damage. **The other security ape remains Polymorphed into a snail**. No active attackers remain when the party departs, **but the snail is not killed**, and alarms continue.

A small, leathery, winged being hiding nearby introduces himself as **Alvert the imp** (ASR also `Albert`; first name clearly contrasted by speaker). He claims to be an investigator for the **Underworld Bureau of Investigation (UBI)**, charged with stopping **demons' diversion of mortal souls from conventional afterlives into the Weir**. He says **devils/hell and demons/the Weir are rivals**, not interchangeable factions. He is **alarmed by the mind flayers' activity** and knows of the **Traversal Drive**, although most of his account is **interested supernatural testimony**, not objective confirmed theology. He says **engineering** probably holds the relevant device, but its **face-like door has no power**, making him unable to reach it.

Alvert offers startling background on the **Principle**: he claims the Principle once was a **mortal scientist in this same old facility**, who conducted a **last experiment upon himself** amid the dragon assault and became the **immortal champion/avatar or foremost cleric of the Steel God**. The imp further alleges the Principle needs human **“gene farm” biomass** to sustain and repair his structure and uses living people by rendering them to a liquid/soup and repurposing them into biological material. He **describes this part as rumor**. These are **claims made by Alvert**, not a narrated flashback, observed victim-processing or established reason to endorse the Steel God's alignment. **The Principle is not encountered here** and the player characters have not verified his origin or biomass use.

The travelers board their railcar with Alvert, consider the **south transfer station** and a large group of **unidentified silhouettes (which Alvert guesses may be bugbears)**. Concerned about limited health/spell slots and uncertain whether Charles Dickens's upload has bought enough time, they turn back toward the **executive offices**. Nash/Hope question the old computer: it **turns off an alarm after an unexplained password enters itself**, and a message claiming to be **Charles Dickens** says the **false reporting data was successfully uploaded**, then warns against further communications. A **computer disk/drive casing ejects burning from the wall**; Nash cools it with *Ray of Frost* and **keeps the damaged component**. **This confirms Charles's report of upload success**, not yet that the intended months-long setback has materialized. Stanley/Vorel find the **Polymorphed snail** and carry it back to the train.

They devise a **decoy**: place the snail in the railcar and send it **unoccupied toward the security forces**. Stanley/Hope operate the switch/lever with *Mage Hand*; **the train departs with the still-transformed security ape snail aboard**. **The crash and reversion into an ape are only intended consequences**, never witnessed in E071. The party and Alvert travel **on foot along the tracks** to the cold **north transfer station**, then down the **green line**. One patch of **ice trips Hope**, causing a small injury; Nash can levitate using the magically cooling briefs. At the **Archives** stop, a second Door Authority panel has read **the “Door Street Journal”** and fears acid, but **opens after being shown a credential and persuaded**; Vorel/Nash dub it **“So Dory”** (a joke, not confirmed self-given formal door name). Inside, the immaculate archive reception's inner doors and terminals **lack power**. **The party chooses not to take a long rest** despite discussing safety, and moves on.

At a later station by the **data center**, the enormous steel entrance is **encased in roughly twenty feet of exceptionally cold, regenerating ice**. Stanley's *Heat Metal* melts **a portion** and sends falling ice/water at the group; Vorel's **Eye of Groxl flaming shield** carves out more but not enough for passage. Nash and the others debate fire and a portable ram, but **no successful main-door entry occurs**. Hope spots a **high ventilation duct with thinner ice**, and the group breaches its ice with **Nash's Fire Bolt and Stanley's heated metal vent**, using **Vorel's shoulders and the enchanted flying underwear** to reach the opening.

The levitating briefs pass **from Nash to Stanley, then Hope, then Vorel**. **Vorel eventually wears them high across his shoulders/back in an improvised harness**, enabling him to **carry the injured Nash to the vent**. All four heroes **crawl into the freezing duct**. **Alvert refuses to accompany them**, saying the cold is too much, and stays behind. **The episode ends inside the vent**: neither the data center's interior, the engineering door nor the Traversal Drive has been reached. Sira remains missing.

## Continuity and editorial decisions

**Alvert and the Principle:** Record Alvert as a **self-identified imp from the UBI**, rather than labeling him a demon servant of Dollo Poppio. His claimed **Hell vs Weir soul conflict**, Principle biography and **human biomass use** are historically important, but must remain **attributed** until corroborated by site evidence/handouts. The Principle's reported age/history and claims of anti-demon work are **not an absolution for unverified human exploitation**.

**Effect of false data:** Charles **confirms that data reached the reporting system**. The hypothesis that this **delays the Traversal Drive by months** is still **unverified in the physical plane**; the party has not inspected engineering nor the device. **Sira remains missing**.

**Snail payload:** **One security monkey died in E071; one stays alive as a polymorphed snail**. Its abandonment on a deliberately dispatched railcar does **not** establish a crash, reversion, death or combat with pursuing forces. Record custody and prospective spell duration; follow up before asserting results.

**Item tracking:** Hope's **shotgun arm was repaired/un-jammed** at opening battle after E070, **Scully's radiant stock was replenished during fight**, Nash temporarily carries a **burned disk-drive case**, and **magically cooling flight underwear is most recently worn by Vorel** in the icy vent. This is an **unusual shared item**, not a new permanent flying ability for every character. The **Eye of Groxl** remains one owner-confirmed artifact regardless of `Grojil` in processed ASR.

**Next cutoff:** All four heroes are **inside the ventilation duct at the frozen data center**, **Alvert is outside**, and **neither the massive powered-off engineering door nor the Traversal Drive has been accessed**. No successful Sira rescue or world-transfer attempt on-screen.
