## Context and interpretation

- **First strong geopolitical exposition:** The GM explicitly distinguishes the **Principle's sovereignty**, **Toyota Host** troops, **Koromukishi** martial elite, **Slavetown/Pristine** local government, **Oilbeard businesses**, and local economic figures. This qualifies earlier E012 **NPC claims** but does not prove every character knows every historic detail. Keep the sheriff and magistrate distinct.
- **The Jeweler's name:** `Bertrand` is said by a cartel visitor (~00:19). The E014 opening recap supplies an additional surname-like recognition `Mulg`. Both need audio confirmation; `Bertrand` is a stronger **spoken** lead than a speculative visual identity.
- **Cartel encounter:** The Cartel assault is a present-tense episode event; **its contractual object** is unnamed. Do not conclude it is the mine deed or an arrangement with the **Red Scarlet** without evidence.
- **Multiple manifestations of Quizquaz:** E013 presents a deliberately conjured **phantasmal illusion** of the oil demon, not evidence E004's actual demon survived.
- **Campaign goals unresolved:** Gold/copper title and holder, the Dusthill captives, Talia's freedom, the Jeweler's ultimate recovery and the Red Scarlet's role remain open. **Donk dies**; **the Jeweler does not** in the described scene.
- **First disclosure boundary:** The creature is seen at E013 end; E014 provides its motives/name details. Record *Old One Eye* as **episode title**, not an automatically established personal name.
