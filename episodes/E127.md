## Premise and new characters (~00:00–00:10)

- The host explicitly announces **no regular Wastes recap**; Kyle is running a **one-off** in Beau's absence, using new characters. Scott identifies **Darnell Clovis** as a solitary, elderly, wary marksman/ranger on a poorly yielding hill farm who shelters an **unnamed strange person in his cellar**. Neither this person's identity nor a rescue of them is revealed here. Jon introduces **Davanos** (ASR also gives Devanos/D'Vanos), a wandering, publicity-conscious folk hero who seeks settlements in need and relies on a projected **spectral double / echo**, all while concealing his real body in danger. Kristen introduces **Riley**, a tough, kindly kitchen maid/cook and **mother of six** with extraordinary strength and **wild magic**; her family appears only in recollection.
- Without knowing how they were transported, the three awaken in a rusty, windowless facility with **cameras, speakers, a voting pillar and locked gates**. A voice addresses an apparent scientific audience, **the Spinner's Council** (word division/spelling provisional), and announces a **“Dr. Spider Christmas”** experiment measuring **holiday spirit under combat/stress**. The speakers promise freedom after **three themed challenges**, threatening punishment if subjects fail. **The named Dr. Spider in the speakers and later Professor Spider in the corridor are separately addressed actors**, not automatically aliases.

## Test one: decorating Christmas, fighting pudding (~00:06–00:42)

1. **Dessert vote (~00:06–00:11).** Options are **fruitcake, figgy pudding and Jell-O salad**. Riley chooses figgy pudding and Davanos seconds it, activating the room. Darnell's attempted later fruitcake vote is inert; **no separate second selection** changes the test.
2. **Decorating a fabricated living room (~00:11–00:27).** The next room is all steel and painted furnishing illusions with a real tree, mantle and couch. A mechanical box provides string lights, a **dead imp with sewn-shut eyes/mouth dressed in Christmas clothes**, a wreath, snowman pillow, star, welcome mat and decayed log. Davanos wraps the tree, Darnell hangs the wreath and places the pillow/mat/log, Riley puts the corpse **on the mantle like an elf on a shelf**. They finally plug in the lights. The walls include a **painted false door**, not a valid escape route.
3. **Failure and actual combat (~00:27–00:41).** The scientists grade them as insufficiently festive for omitting fireplace fire, socks/stockings and moving the couch. A **sugar-decorated figgy-pudding ooze** emerges and fights the three. Darnell shoots/stabs and retreats, Davanos' ghostly double repeatedly stabs while the man hides, and Riley casts **Frostbite**, then **actually cuts the ooze apart with her butcher knife**. The ooze dies and the exit opens. **No PC death or permanent splitting of this pudding is established.** Davanos remains evasive about how his duplicate operates.

## Test two: snow holiday activities (~00:42–01:00)

4. **Second vote (~00:42–00:45).** Choices are **Frosty the Snowman, Bumble and Snow Miser**. They vote **Frosty**; the next room contains manufactured cold wind and snow, painted village scenery and an exit gate.
5. **Enjoy the weather (~00:45–00:54).** For a short timed task they improvise snow angels, a snowball fight, snowman construction, a small fort and tasting the snow. At the end Davanos and Riley **take cold damage**, Darnell resists. The task is adjudicated a **success with extra credit**, not a combat victory over Frosty.
6. **Reward and final vote (~00:54–01:00).** They receive handmade **toilet-paper-tube “Santa seeker” goggles**, used later to resist the glare of Rudolph's magical/technological red nose. The third options are **Santa Claus, the Grinch and Krampus**. Davanos **writes the group's names on the Good/Nice list** and all choose **Santa**, hoping the list will protect them. There is no actual Krampus/Grinch combat.

## Test three: a rooftop, diseased reindeer and fake Santa (~01:00–01:36)

7. **A fake rooftop (~01:00–01:10).** The trio enters a constructed snowy roof with a narrow ridge, slippery slopes and deadly transparent spikes below. At first the silhouettes of **eight flying reindeer** appear; after landing, the animals are revealed to be **sickly, partly mutilated undead**. The characters fight, using Darnell's **Hunter's Mark/longbow**, Davanos' echo and Riley's fire.
8. **A powered Rudolph (~01:15–01:32).** Another reindeer arrives with a glowing red nose, **large battery packs and cables grafted onto its body**. Its beam deals **radiant damage**; it **reduces Riley to zero HP** and wounds/blinds Darnell after his improvised goggles slip. **Darnell immediately casts Cure Wounds, bringing Riley conscious again**, so a temporary fall is **not her death**. Riley uses **Maximilian's Earthen Grasp** to restrain and damage Rudolph; **Davanos' spectral double stabs it through the nose**, destroying the constructed attacker. This is a separate enemy from the original group of undead deer.
9. **Ogre Santa yields passage (~01:31–01:36).** A huge **ogre in Santa clothing** drops onto the rooftop. Davanos sends his echo to announce that they belong to the Nice list; the ogre **kneels, opens its bag and the exit door opens**. The trio **runs past**. **They do not kill the ogre, rescue the reindeer, or obtain a sack of gifts.** Dr. Spider's speakers praise successful data collection; **Professor Spider complains they cheated by writing the names** on the Nice list, but is silenced by another voice.

## “Gift,” crusher escape and the spiders' storehouse (~01:36–02:50)

10. **The trap disguised as the final reward (~01:36–01:48).** Their apparent prize sits in a room with a **segmented metal floor**. Riley notices seams and hinges that suggest a fall. They attach **50-foot rope to a sturdy camera** before Darnell lifts the wrapped gift. Coal falls out; the floor opens onto a steep **metal slide leading to a powered crushing machine**. **All three successfully hang from the rope instead of falling into it.** This is **not** a completed murder despite the host's congratulatory language.
11. **Clever vent escape (~01:48–02:12).** Riley finds an upper air vent, uses **Mage Hand** to unfasten its grate and puts **Light** on a Darnell arrow to inspect it. Davanos **summons his echo inside the vent and magically exchanges positions with it** after nearly slipping from the rope and being caught by Riley. To bring up the others, Riley carries slack with Mage Hand and Davanos anchors the rope with his handaxe; **Darnell climbs across and Riley follows**. Riley invokes **Tides of Chaos** for her attempt and subsequently rolls a **wild-magic surge granting temporary Fly**, *after* the dangerous traverse succeeds, **not before**. Everyone makes it into the vent alive.
12. **Backstage feeder corridor (~02:12–02:30).** The vent leads past the fake Santa room to overhead grating populated by **small ice-producing imps**. The group climbs to the **creature-storage/handling hall**, seeing enclosed specimens: **green material in a sack, a pipe-smoking goat-like figure, a magically animated hat and an elongated skeletal winter figure**, plus empty pens and tracks that feed creatures into test rooms. These are observed **captive/contained exhibits**, not all fought or identified with certainty. The group decides to **avoid releasing them** to prioritize escape.
13. **Professor Spider is slain (~02:30–02:44).** A **spider-like professor wearing an academic cap** opens the far door, aims an oversized **minigun** and attacks. This is identified in play as **Professor Spider**, not proved to be the voice that earlier advertised **Dr. Spider's Christmas**. Davanos hides behind a dolly and projects his echo; Darnell lands repeated **Hunter's Mark longbow hits**; Riley conjures **Maximilian's Earthen Grasp** and restrains the professor. The professor **uses Ethereal Jaunt to escape once** but reappears and is struck by more arrows. **The professor dies**, leaving **keys and a black electronic device**. **Do not backdate that death to Dr. Spider**.
14. **The Earth code (~02:44–02:50).** Riley reads the fallen professor's device because the interface is **in Infernal**; the screen lists **Material Plane destinations**. Selecting **Earth** displays **47829873**. The party leaves the pen keys unused, **does not free the captive creatures**, and moves onward to a chamber over the first test room, where a blue crystal and keypad await. **Riley enters the code**, blue light/mist overwhelms them and all sleep. They **awaken back in their separate lodgings** some hours later. The cast refers to this as the end of the **Dr. Spider Christmas** one-shot; final credits continue through **~02:54**.
