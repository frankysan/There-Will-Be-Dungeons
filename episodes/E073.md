# E073 — Slow Peter and Ice Boyfriends

> **Corrected main-campaign E073**, following [E072](E072.md), sourced from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E073.%20Slow%20Peter%20and%20Ice%20Boyfriends.parakeet.processed.srt). **Not listened to or audio-verified.** Recording offsets approximate; includes listener mail, recap, intermission, lengthy tactical deliberation and outro. **Spoilers through E073**.

## At a glance

The fight begun at the end of E072 concludes. **Stanley's borrowed +1 AC necklace actively strangles him and electrically shocks him when he tries to cut it**, while a giant ice elemental containing a frozen corpse carries him toward the aged elf **Cerinthala/Saranthala**. **Nash's Enlarge spell** keeps Vorel roughly the size of the ice creatures; **Vorel's flaming Eye of Groxl shield** severely damages and shatters several icy forms, while **Nash uses Fireball with Careful Spell** to spare Vorel from the worst of the blast. The broken constructs leave **previously frozen corpses that rise as clawing, blue-eyed “ice boyfriends”**; they appear capable of necrotic touch, but **Vorel resists the worst effect**. Nash supplies a new **Solar Mines** name: dangerous fire elementals used by mine workers were called **“Slow Peters.”** The character's past observations are **his own story**, not a previously recorded historical source.

**Hope's explosive Scully shot severs the captor's arm**, freeing Stanley; she helps him to safety. The captor retreats into the ice rather than being definitively destroyed. The other visible ice constructs and the reanimated corpse attackers are defeated; **the spirit/entity called Toby remains able to speak from the ice**. When Cerinthala arrives and asks why the party killed her companions, **Stanley's necklace relaxes**. Toby emerges as **a mouth/head formed from ice** and acknowledges that **the ice-companions attacked because they feared she would leave them**. Cerinthala apologizes and admits she **originally considered hunting/eating or using the party's bodies** before deciding they could help her escape. The necklace is **a cursed binding of her own creation**, not an ordinary elven vow relic: it painfully tightens when Stanley entertains mistrust or defiance toward her, and she claims he **cannot remove or break it**. **It remains on Stanley at the E073 cutoff**, still with the +1 AC boon; no safe cure or proof of its full trigger logic has been demonstrated.

Cerinthala claims **an ancient lizardfolk-soldier program** led to failed **blood-drinking, uncontrolled flesh-hungry variants (“vampires”)**. This adds to her E072 claims about **lizardfolk and tiefling engineered origins**, but remains **her testimony**. She pleads for safe return to **Elhandar**, and Stanley insists **Sira's rescue and halting the mind flayer take precedence**. The group chooses Cerinthala's proposed **maintenance-service tunnel route**. Under her greenhouse/locker rooms they **spend several hours melting and excavating roughly 25 feet of ice** with Vorel's **Eye of Groxl**, rather than using *Mold Earth* illegally on ice, until they reach an **open service corridor**. **She accompanies them**.

Beyond is an apparently ancient **biological laboratory**: many tall cylinders, pink-purple fleshlike growths, and security doors opened using a previously recovered **Tara Mebat/Mebatt employee card**. Cerinthala identifies it as part of the research that **made lizardfolk**, though this interpretation is hers; **the cylinders and growths are physically observed**. **Nash pokes the growth with his staff**, and a tentacle **snatches and snaps it into two pieces**. The GM says the **nail-bearing short section remains a valid arcane focus**, while the other half is just a stick. **Nash cannot repair it with Mending**, which he no longer has on his sheet.

Cerinthala explains an old **“infernal wave”** machine, claiming it can dramatically accelerate gestation, and **offers to fertilize a lizardfolk egg for Vorel**; **Vorel refuses**, asserting his existing **Great Fang of Razak Val** identity, and **no egg, pregnancy or new offspring is created**. She says ancient weapons and perhaps dragon-metal materials remain elsewhere in the labs. At an elevator bank the first attempted lift **crashes down its shaft**; another arrives, but the lift they enter **halts between floors**, its cables fraying. **Vorel forces its doors partly open only to find a concrete wall**, then tears an opening in the ceiling for the group to climb onto its roof. **Hope sees a gigantic spider-like creature with multiple shining eyes descending toward them in the shaft**, and the episode ends **before an attack or escape is played**.

## Current state and high-value source questions

**Cursed object:** **Stanley's Cerinthala necklace remains worn** and gives +1 AC per original GM description, but it can **suffocate and shock him** and may be impossible to remove normally. **He has not transferred it, broken it, dispelled it or consented to a new wider promise**. The elf's exact intentions and the curse's triggers require source checking.

**Who survives:** **Toby continues to exist and speak in ice**, despite ice construct/corpse casualties. Specific assignments of **Toby, Carl, Gordon, Benson** to destroyed or surviving forms must not be guessed. **Cerinthala now accompanies the group**, not left in server room. **Alvert** remained outside the data-center vent in E071 and has **not rejoined**.

**Ancient origins:** Creation of lizardfolk, tieflings, vampires and **infernal-wave machines** is **Cerinthala's claimed participation/recollection**, with some physical laboratory evidence but no complete verified records. **Principal Dombrovski** remains a provisional name from E072, not proven to be the contemporary **Principle**.
