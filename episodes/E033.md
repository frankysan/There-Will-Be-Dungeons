## Interpretive boundaries

- **Missing performer versus missing character:** Scott is absent from the E033 recording; this does not mean Nash died or permanently left the campaign.
- **Jedel's whereabouts unknown:** The building's contents and the stranger are observed. Neither proves the identified body belongs to the named arms dealer or that Charlemagne's parcel was successfully delivered.
- **Monster ontology unknown:** Its fangs, resistance, restoration and disappearance are observed. Whether it is undead or a particular monster type needs independent confirmation.
- **Hope's ongoing condition:** Record the **mechanical reduction of hit-point maximum** without extrapolating a new curse or transformation.
- **No completed access:** The **Blood Ball recruitment**, **Toe Ripper assassination bargain**, and **wedding service lead** are separate, unfinished possibilities for approaching the Teet keep.
- **Stanley's secret unresolved:** The E029 archfey figure's intervention was **not consensual**. E033 gives further character behavior and an ambiguous statement, not a definitive diagnosis or voluntary agreement.
