## Continuity and classification

- E006's **three unexplained night lights** resolve here as the luminous beetles; separate player fears/speculation (e.g. motorcycles) from the GM description.
- **Bok Bok** appears as a character with agency and recurring catchword `fresh`; his brutal recovery of the old man is different from rescue by the four original heroes. He remains associated with Vorel.
- The warband pursued from Dusthill is still ahead after the bus/truck fight. The assumption that the vehicles were a **sacrificial delaying force** is a character's provisional strategic reading.
- **Fate 4 fuel, overheating and repairs** are important travel constraints; do not treat discussed tabletop numbers as independently validated engineering specs or permanent stat block.
- The subterranean **antenna/sand-entry mechanism** is a *new* location separate from the earlier Dusthill dragon-engine shaft and dwarven ruins.
- The identity of the voice, underground installation and what actually happens to Nash and Stanley are open at the **end of E007**.
