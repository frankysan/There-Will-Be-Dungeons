## High-value cross-episode references

- **Peter** provides a new time-qualified identity for the first Hillmaster; earlier E002 only describes a skeleton by role. In E005 Peter also claims subsequent leaders were impostors. Those are his claims.
- **Water supply:** distinguish repair of the lower intake, operation of the dragon engine, and direct confirmation that water reaches Dusthill's public outlet. The E005 ending still leaves final surface verification pending.
- **Goblin raid:** E005 establishes the immediate attack and missing civilians, setting up rescue discussions. The subsequent chase, **Bok Bok**, and the Fate 4's departure belong to E006 per the [fan wiki Episode 6 summary](https://twbd.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_6), **not** this episode.
- **Post-apocalyptic geography:** The map is rich potential reference data, but the SRT is an especially poor authority for exotic place-name spelling. Prefer a licensed/authorized image of the actual map or a creator statement before standardizing entries.
- **Bruce:** His mother is absent after the raid and Stanley promises to look for her. Do not presume her fate has been settled by E005.
