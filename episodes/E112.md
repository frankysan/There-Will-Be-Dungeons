**Played cutoff:** **Hope Akmenos**, **Steerious Business** (minotaur), **Blood Skunk Hendrickson** (Harlequinari clown) and **Dr. Spider** enter **Sigil** from the E106 Gate and, after assaulting several civilians, hire a rickshaw into the **Hive** and see **Tobacho Fleur**, a blue dragon playing an electric guitar. A bounty hunter identified in ASR as **Pelvis/Helvis (?)** and his gang threaten them over the reward on Hope; **Steerious actually kills the leader**, after which Tobacho clears the tavern and holds a private audience. Hope demands help finding **the Eye of the Voice** (not **Vorel's Eye of Groxl**) and admits godhood/vengeance ambitions. Tobacho **actually offers a written compact**, promising a lead to the Eye and withdrawal of **his bounty**; in return Hope must help **depose the Lady of Pain and install Tobacho as ruler of Sigil**, keeping influence away from that city. **Hope signs** after reading the compact; **Tobacho signs and Hope takes a physical copy**. This is **a real agreement, not a suggested bargain or completed overthrow**. He then identifies **the Council of Clowns in Pandemonium** as possible holders of information (his claim, not confirmed location of the Eye). Direct passage is unreliable, so he offers an indirect itinerary: portal from Sigil's **House of Delights** to **the Gray Waste**, reach a **settlement by the River Styx**, then **ferry to Pandemonium**. After leaving Tobacho, the company rejects lodging at an inn, enters the home of a dead man **Davies Reese (?)**, spends a night there and actually rests, then disguises itself and **Dr. Spider sets the house ablaze with *Wall of Fire***. At the **House of Delights**, the proprietor admits them to a **closet full of whips**: all four eat portions of a **carrot**, grasp whips during the ritual and **actually arrive in the featureless gray landscape**. **Dr. Spider and Blood Skunk fail their Constitution saves and visibly become gray, melancholy and depressed**. **No ferry, Council audience, Eye retrieval, overthrow of the Lady of Pain, or actual return to the Wastes occurs**. Hope and Steerious do not appear to suffer the same initial adverse effect.

## Following the sound of the forge (~00:03–00:30)

1. **The new company in Sigil (~00:03–00:13).** The four emerge together from the violet Gate into a quiet cobbled residential street with distant forge sounds. **Hope says she does not know the route through Sigil**, despite her **earlier, directly played visits to Sigil in E082–E093**; this is navigational uncertainty or imperfect recollection, **not proof she has never been there**, and she wants **Tobacho Fleur**, whose contact was recommended by the Principle in [E106](E106.md). **Steerious** walks nearby in his suit, sometimes projected as a shadow double; **Dr. Spider** carries an unusual luminous infant/golden celestial device; **Blood Skunk** has an immense cache of arrows. The other main-party members are **not** here.
2. **Violence against neighbors (~00:13–00:24).** Searching for directions, **Steerious physically grabs an older man** with a rake, strikes him for **5 damage**, shakes him and refuses to release him while a neighbor threatens to call the **Harmonium**. The man's name is muddled as **Charles/Bill** in players' dialogue; do **not** stabilize one name as fact. **Steerious's shadow intimidation makes another resident retreat indoors**, and when an elderly woman with a cane intervenes, Steerious **hits her in the face for 5 damage**, crushing her nose; she is **alive when the party departs**, with the neighborhood rushing to aid the victims. **Dr. Spider proposes leaving no witnesses but Hope refuses**. The party **does not kill either neighbor**.
3. **Blacksmith, driver and Hive checkpoint (~00:24–00:49).** A smith advises a **rickshaw** rather than giving street directions. The cast remarks on Blood Skunk's **several hundred arrows** as a visible load, not an item newly awarded. The company obtains a driver identified as **Bungor/Bungo (?)**; the driver charges an initial **15 gold** and negotiates an additional **25 gold** to present his authorizing **writ** at the Harmonium Hive checkpoint. The party proceeds **through the checkpoint** and into the rundown Hive, **not by defeating the guards or magically bypassing them**. Driver identity/prices remain transcription-dependent and are not evidence the same Bungor personally survived the earlier E105 route without independent confirmation.

## A public killing and Tobacho's bargain (~00:49–01:40)

4. **Blue-dragon nightclub and the bounty (~00:49–01:03).** Tobacho performs on an **electric guitar** while seated in full dragon form. **Hope and companions are admitted**. A knife-wielding red-haired human whose spoken name fluctuates **Pelvis/Helvis (?)**, backed by several accomplices, boasts about the **large bounty on Hope**. **Steerious rages, manifests a scorpion-like tail and actually cuts the leader in half with two sword strikes**; the others retreat. Tobacho does not participate in this killing. **This murder is played**, unlike the elderly woman who was injured and left alive earlier. Keep both distinct.
5. **A private translated conversation (~01:03–01:15).** Tobacho orders the bar emptied, dismissing even **Jalver**, and encloses Hope's group in a magical privacy/language field. **The rock golem translator withdraws**, and the four can now understand him directly. He confronts Hope over the **Fist/Hand transaction**: **the gauntlet** was meant for his customer, but **Hope kept the true Hand for herself**, whereas **Stanley actually delivered the separate glove in E106**. **Do not give her the glove or erase the E106 custody transfer.** Asked why she took the Hand, Hope says power and survival matter more than the old Earth journey; their longer aims and claims of ascension remain personal ambitions, not accomplished state.
6. **Blood Skunk conjures a menagerie (~01:07–01:15).** **Blood Skunk**, not Nash, uses ***Conjure Animals*** to summon **an ape, goat, crocodile and giant eagle**, supplying improvised rhymes about grapes, boats, smiles and a beagle. The intimidation attempt against Tobacho **fails (18)**. **Four animals are actually present temporarily** and later released in the opulent quarter; this is **not a Nash phrase-register entry** or evidence of permanent familiars.
7. **The price of information (~01:15–01:29).** Tobacho describes his dislike of conventional dragon hoarding and desire to rule **Sigil**—but says **the Lady of Pain** stands above even him. He proposes to **supply a starting lead toward the Eye of the Voice and retract the bounty he placed on Hope** (with limits beyond his jurisdiction), in exchange for **Hope's future help deposing the Lady of Pain and making him ruler of Sigil**. He insists **Hope have no independent power base or sway here** as part of that bargain. These are **Tobacho's promises, claims and proposed future actions**, not proof the Lady can be deposed or that the entire multiverse would accept the contract. **The hooded Voice appears to Hope alone and warns against contracts**; she considers it but **ultimately signs anyway**. The writing appears visibly on parchment; **Hope reads the simple, roughly six-sentence text, signs; Tobacho also signs; two copies form and Hope physically receives one**. The exact formal obligations are **ASR-provisional**, but the completed signatures and custody are explicit.
8. **Tobacho's clue to the Council (~01:29–01:40).** After signing, Tobacho says he does **not** know the Eye's immediate location, but suggests **the Council of Clowns** in **Pandemonium** might reveal what she needs, via a petition (an **unfulfilled possibility**, not confirmed agreement by the Council). A direct portal into that chaotic plane cannot reliably reach the right place; Tobacho gives the route **Gray Waste → settlement near River Styx → ferry → Pandemonium**. He points to a working portal at **the House of Delights in Sigil's opulent quarter**, through its **impresario/proprietor**. **No trip is completed yet** at this scene's cutoff, and the Eye is not handed over. Blood Skunk's clown-origin/car-packing lore is joking/out-of-character and **not newly proven cosmology**.

## Squatting, disguise and a burned house (~01:40–02:37)

9. **Failed respectable inn (~01:40–01:57).** The four ride to a **wealthy/opulent district**, encounter a local official demanding visitor papers and an unwilling innkeeper, and **are denied practical lodgings** after threats, evasions and the release of Blood Skunk's conjured animals. Blood Skunk explicitly commands the animals to disperse—**the “fae wildlings” in the title**—rather than carrying them to the portal or claiming them as permanent pets. A proposed false “two couples” explanation for rooms and Steerious's saber intimidation **do not secure an uneventful night's stay**. The party opts instead for a vacant-seeming mansion.
10. **An entry and macabre dinner (~01:57–02:21).** **Dr. Spider**, via climbing and an ***Unseen Servant*** called **Miss Wilkins**, lets the group into a locked mansion; Steerious forcefully breaches its gate, damaging his suit. They find on the third floor **the body of a man hanging by a rope**, shoot the rope down and investigate. The GM later names the deceased **Davies Reese (?)**, an accountant mourning his family and **apparently dead by suicide**. There is **no newly discovered conspiracy or murder evidence**. Dr. Spider **actually uses *Speak with Dead*** to make the corpse talk during a bizarre hosted dinner, then the party **completes a long rest** inside the house. The spell **does not resurrect** the man; he remains dead.
11. **Morning costume and arson (~02:21–02:37).** Hope uses an ***Alter Self* appearance change** to resemble an **elderly human woman** and dresses in the deceased family's clothing (a modest expensive frock). The spell's treatment of clothing is openly debated; the **garments are actual clothes found and worn**, not magically supplied as an automatic effect. The companions take further clothes. **Dr. Spider leaves a written fabricated suicide note** and **actually casts *Wall of Fire* inside the house**, setting it burning before they depart. The **fire is played**, but **no surviving occupant, full collapse or eventual casualty count is observed**; the preexisting corpse is not evidence the fire killed him. The group heads towards the portal using directions from Tobacho.

## House of Delights and the Gray Waste cutoff (~02:37–02:56)

12. **The right building (~02:37–02:47).** After confusing directions and another evasive local, the party reaches an imposing house with a suited **human host/impresario**. He correctly recognizes the information **Tobacho sent Hope**, asks her name and accepts the group, despite Hope's disguise. **Blood Skunk negotiates bizarre side amusements**, but no torture-mime fight, dog mistreatment, 50,000-gold purchase, or sheep-intestine balloon service is actually contracted, delivered or paid for. A **proposed future appointment** is not a played encounter.
13. **Performed portal ritual (~02:47–02:53).** Host leads them through a basement playroom to a **closet full of whips**. **All four enter**, divide and **eat one carrot** (one portion each) as instructed, then grip the whips. The host shuts the door; a **huge mouth descends**, whips whirl, and the chamber resolves into another landscape. This is **the portal actually functioning**, not a mere threat, proposed escape, or return to the Hells' Kaiser Casino.
14. **First moments on another plane (~02:53–02:56:01).** The four now stand on **dry gray terrain beneath a uniformly gray sky**, without visible inhabitants. **All four roll Constitution saves** (approximately **19, 25, 13, 10** in the first ASR; speaker assignment needs listening). **Dr. Spider and Blood Skunk visibly lose color and become depressed/melancholic**, apparently affected by the plane; **Hope and Steerious are not described as suffering that first effect**. The destination is **the Gray Waste**, **not yet Pandemonium**. They have not found the **Styx riverbank village, ferry, Council of Clowns or Eye of the Voice**. The after-show uncertainty about whether the next recording will continue this branch is **out-of-game** and not an in-story departure.
