## Continuity and ethical boundaries

- **Rocket-launcher consequence:** E033 establishes acquisition; **E037 ends with Hope firing the launcher and the monk redirecting the rocket into her**, with catastrophic blowback/cave damage. **E038 begins with those consequences**. Hope survives this episode's events but suffers permanent-appearing loss of her left limbs **before later prosthetic installation**.
- **Blunt Goliver first shown alive E036, killed E038:** The assassination is **not completed** when the Golden Gargoyle is escaped; **head delivered to Monsoon** here and is examined, unlocking his assistance with medical treatment.
- **Treatment status:** **Amputations/urgent treatment are played in E038**; E039 demonstrates operating prosthetic limbs and their functions. Do not give her the mechanical arm's combat abilities during E038's cave fight.
- **No surgical “miracle” simplification:** Extensive trauma, time, resources and help from Dr Dickles/Stanley are narrated. The safety, ethics and final fate of caregivers are not resolved by the initial operation.
- **Pursuers:** Ordinary gang patrols and organized armed forces should not be identified as specific factions without narrator confirmation.
