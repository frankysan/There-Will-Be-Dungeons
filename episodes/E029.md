## Important evidence distinctions

- **Special episode, not alternate chronology:** Released E029 between E028 and E030, depicting a **direct next-night action** and a temporarily reduced cast; this differs from the **E013.5** retrospective insertion.
- **First refusal matters:** The title *Stanley's Choice* must **not** be read as Stanley choosing to accept the archfey: he **refuses** and then undergoes magical coercion. **Soulbroker E023's three signed pact** and **Diontalus E029 intrusion** are distinct obligations/phenomena.
- **Compulsion and perception:** Hope, Vorel, Bok Bok and Tedna follow a **siren-like attraction**; Nash is **tied at the RV**. The other PCs' later reported dream/hypnotic experience is in E030, and the other actors did not play them in this solo episode.
- **Enemy deaths:** Blort/worm is visibly killed or deflates; Stanley **later says** he killed hag too. Avoid deducing precise final wound to the hag from that later character recap alone.
- **Buttons participates actively** and accompanies Stanley, not just a map-giving hitchhiker from E028. His usefulness and magical assertions remain subject to source-role checks.
- **Archfey:** Calling himself an **archfey of carnage** does not independently prove his ancient title, race-wide ideology, cosmology or ability to fulfill a promise. Mechanism and permanence of blue light **unknown at E029 cutoff**.
