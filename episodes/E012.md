## High-value continuity

- **Party versus captives:** E011 legally frees the victorious adventurers. E012's negotiations seek release of Dusthill civilians, who are **still captive**. A one-night room for Talia is not the same as manumission.
- **Slavetown power structure:** The Jeweler says an earlier settlement called **Pristine** changed following the **Oilbeards' mining** and the Principle's expansion into slave trading. This is his *local historical account*, to compare against other witnesses/primary sources. The Principle, the **Koromukishi**, Stump, the Oilbeards, the Jeweler, and a sheriff are **not one undifferentiated faction**.
- **Mine deed uncertainty:** In the same narrative the item is called **copper mine deed**, **gold mine deed** and associated with another mine. Without a visual document or listening, maintain `mine deed` as neutral working title. Report rumored intermediary `Catherine Speckles`, missing `Timber McClellan`, and the Oilbeards separately with claimed source.
- **Backstory allegation:** Softhead's suspected connection to Dusthill's exposure does not establish **when or how** it was compromised. Compare the Jeweler's words with E001–E006 primary events before changing town history.
- **E011/E012 duel:** E011 ends at the challenge. E012 depicts the fight, Nash's lost possessions and recovery. Be precise about whether his gear is *offered back*, *handed back*, and which items remain missing.
- **Woman in red:** The SRT refers to a red-clad bandolier/bullet-belt woman with a gang, distinct in the Jeweler's speech from **Catherine Speckles**; don't merge identities from a visual guess.
- **Cast:** The five regular contributors returned in E010 and continue here; the guest gnomes' fates are not resolved by their lack of appearance.
