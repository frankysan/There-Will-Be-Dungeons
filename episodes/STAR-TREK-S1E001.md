## Cast, setting and initial state

The **new USS Ottawa**, a **Galaxy-class Starfleet ship**, is on Captain **Greg Spacer's first command assignment**, somewhere in the broad **post-TNG / Voyager era** (GM deliberately avoids an exact canonical year). Spacer is a human–Betazoid hybrid, self-confident and ambitious, with transporter-accident history, a daughter studying at Starfleet Academy and a deceased wife. Player Scott deliberately designs a showy captain willing to visit the planet himself. **Chief engineer Mark Maros** (Kyle; formal title/name **Antivera/Antivero(?)**, prefers “Maros”) is a physically imposing human engineering/transport specialist who jokes about ships **Fate 4/5 and USS Principal** exploding; **these are jokes/allusions in a standalone reality, not continuity evidence linking Wastes vehicles to Starfleet**. **Dr. Dolores/Delara Stadi (“Spatty/Spotty”)** (Kristen) is a full Betazoid Starfleet chief medical officer, newly seeking adventure, blunt in bedside manner and capable of sensing emotions/telepathy.

Other GM-run Starfleet crew: **Commander Ikoyo Adoyo(?)** first officer; **Lieutenant Cobot/Kobot(?)** Romulan communications officer; **Lieutenant Commander Tome/Toome** android science officer; **Lieutenant Commander Flex Reynolds** security chief; tactical **Gunther Schubert**; pilot **Lieutenant Young Sang(?)**; counselor **Dr. Penrose Lafferty**; transporter **Ensign Ben Kenobi**. Scientist **Dr. Tilda McGovern** brings assistants **Dr. David Stahl/Still** and **Dr. Arjen/Arian Sandoval**. Specific written orthography of minor supporting characters requires an original roster/audio.

## Cutoff and continuity / ethics ledger

**Performed and GM-confirmed by the end:** successful first-contact **Kulan rescue and return home**, engineering repair of alien ship, delivery of **Baculum crystal** to planetary organism/gods as represented by Kulan, **Ottawa's infected crew cured**, plant-infection crisis contained on Starfleet side, **Klingon ship infected and temporarily stopped** via intentionally deployed spores. **Off-screen but agreed in epilogue:** deferred Klingon decontamination/recovery after Ottawa withdraws. **Not established:** Klingon criminal culpability or eventual trial, lasting diplomatic relations, detailed true nature of Emfari sentience, the identity of a possible stolen owner, future fate of Klingon Captain Vang, canonical *Star Trek* franchise integration, or another actual one-shot installment.

**Narrative ethical tension:** Captain uses secrecy, threatened destruction of the Kulan craft and **transporting a contagious agent into a hostile ship**; GM **explicitly raises Starfleet conduct/biological warfare** as an objection. The crew's nonlethal intent and later cure do **not erase** the method or prove their plan was ethically endorsed by Starfleet. This is roleplayed fiction, not a recommendation.

**Cross-campaign isolation:** Engineer's line about **Fate 4/5 and USS Principal** are playful naming allusions, **not** evidence the Wastes' Fate Six or Principle literally exist in this universe. No Wastes character time travels into Star Trek in played scenes; distinct fictional continuities must remain separate.
