## Major continuity corrections

- **Holiday special skipped in Wastes chronology:** **E043 is an unrelated one-shot**. The consequences of E042 (George wounded, cave king unseen) are handled in **E044**, not in E043.
- **Singing king now visible:** The self-styled ruler **is directly encountered in E044** and receives a **payment of items/stones**. **River crossing is only promised conditionally on dealing with the headache man**. No successful keep entry or gem recovery results.
- **Loot continuity:** The **preserved stick from E042 becomes “Wand of Scowls” in E044** and is offered to **Nash**. **Seven beans + skeleton-etched key** are found inside a **different small bag from the E044 mushroom/spider area**, not the original E042 rucksack. Check transferred stones/ruby and item ownership separately.
- **George survives through E044:** His foot is dressed/healed as far as possible; he accompanies the party and is alive, participating in the camp conversation. His fate after the rest belongs to **E045**, not this episode.
- **Stanley identity:** He personally states **Billings is an adopted name**. This is a **direct character confession**, distinct from independently knowing his original name or the origin of Dion's control.
- **Long rest timing:** The party plans and seals the camp in E044; **actual refreshed abilities/waking are established at E045 opening**, not immediately after E042's brief short rest.
