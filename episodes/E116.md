# E116 — Large Spectral Pile Of Babies

**E116 cutoff:** **Hope actually uses her Gate ring to bring Nash Maggard from his Elhandar biodome to Dr. Spider's laboratory in Hell, then physically escorts him back through the still-open portal to the Elhandar biodome; the two arrive there together.** **Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider remain at the Hell lab.** This is **a real first direct reunion after Hope departed in E101**, but **no conversation with Stanley, Vorel, Angelashi, the four children, Bok Bok or Chunks is yet played**. No Eye of the Voice, spacecraft or prison has been acquired. **Elhandar is actually reached by Hope and Nash, but the three henchmen are not taken along.**

## A reversal at the Council's doorway and a charged ring (~00:03–00:22)

1. **The E113 choice is revised at the immediate opening (~00:03–00:10).** The host reprises E115's Elhandar events, then the GM places the Hell Squad back in **the now-empty Council of Clowns cavern in Pandemonium**. **Although E113's played finale said the quartet had actually crossed to Tobacho's Sigil bar, E116 immediately resumes as though Hope did NOT enter that Gate**; she changes the destination instead. The GM later says *out of game* that he had prepared Tobacho's encounter and Hope unexpectedly turned away. **Record a real E113/E116 scene-boundary/rewind discontinuity rather than invent an intervening trip back from Sigil or erase E113's original cutoff.**
2. **Hope tests whether injury will recharge Brett's copper Gate ring (~00:04–00:17).** She slaps and stabs **Dr. Spider** for **11 damage**, which he says is taken by his temporary-hit-point shield; **that attempt is NOT enough to recharge the ring**. Dr. Spider sprays a defensive web, restraining Hope and Steerious temporarily, before Steerious escapes via his echo and Hope breaks free. **Blood Skunk voluntarily allows Hope to cut off his FIRST outer ear**, giving explicit consent. This succeeds as an *evil deed* for ring recharge; **Dr. Spider closes the ear wound, but the lost outer ear is not shown to grow back**. Do not count stabbing Spider as successful recharge or a newly dead PC.
3. **A successful Gate to Sigil's original departure dock (~00:15–00:22).** Hope uses the now-charged ring to open a portal to **the Sigil dock from which they had departed for White Tower in E092–E093**, **not the dock from their later return in E105**. The group actually walks through into the great hangar dock where air/space-going cargo ships are moored. Hope wants **a captain and ship** capable of reaching the Council-described **Pentelvice/Pentalvise (?) prison** in phlogiston near the Corinthian Nebula; she lacks proper coordinates and a qualified pilot. **The Council's jack-in-the-box compass and the E112 Tobacho compact are not visibly disposed of**, though neither is independently tested here.

## Dockmaster's death, failed navigation and a disastrous ship attack (~00:22–02:07)

4. **Dockmaster murdered (~00:22–00:36).** At the busy dockmaster's house **Dr. Spider casts *Bestow Curse* (or a spell described as a curse) and *Mental Prison* on the human dockmaster** to interrogate him, but the GM says psychic damage **kills him outright** before questioning. Spider poses the body and casts ***Speak with Dead***; the corpse provides terse answers and knows **phlogiston** exists but does **not** know **Pentelvice** or the **Corinthian Nebula**. This is a **played homicide** followed by corpse questioning, not a live prisoner interrogation. **Steerious turns a visiting dwarf away**; the body is later discovered and reported.
5. **Failed shortcut to Earth (~00:36–00:41).** Hope attempts a Gate directly to **Kervalon/Caravalon (?)**, imagining the route to spacecraft on Earth. **The portal visibly opens onto packed purple earth rather than a usable ship facility**, so **Hope closes it without crossing**. It is a **performed but ineffective Gate**, not travel to Earth. They turn to the two visible cargo vessels at Sigil's dock.
6. **Dwarf ship instead of gnome frigate (~00:41–00:50).** The group considers **a northern gnome ship** but approaches **an eastern dwarven trade frigate** after Blood Skunk comments on ship access. A taunting dockworker and an angry taskmaster refuse to cooperate. **Steerious kills an armed/defiant dwarf**, and the others immediately ready for battle. This is **not a duel that confers captaincy**. Hope charges across the gangway, **kills several dwarves** with the Hand's enhanced dagger attacks; Steerious kills others with trident/pitchfork and claws; **Blood Skunk conjures an ape and giant wasps**, which actually fight or poison defenders; and **Dr. Spider employs *Sickening Radiance*, *Flame Strike* and other attacks**. The defenders arm themselves with crossbows. **Numerous dwarves die**, but not all are killed or pacified.
7. **Steerious falls, then climbs out (~01:16–01:42).** The minotaur **breaks a boarding plank and falls into the abyss beside the suspended ship**; after damage and failed attempts to exploit his shadow echo as an immediate rescue, he **actually reaches a ladder on the ship/ledge infrastructure and climbs back up**. No death, return to Hell or instantaneous full recovery occurs at the fall. The GM adjudicates several ability interactions; do not generalize a fall-survival rule.
8. **A Guardian made of spectral babies (~01:38–01:55).** Dr. Spider casts ***Guardian of Faith***, flavoring the spell as a **large spiraling/giggling pile of spectral babies** to defend the ladder approach. These are **magical figures, not Nash and Angelashi's four children nor real infants**. The guardian does **not stop crossbow bolts in flight** (the GM clarifies it affects approaching creatures), and is **not a resurrected child or literal infant army**. The dwarves maintain heavy crossbow volleys and deny the ship.
9. **Blood Skunk nearly dies, but survives (~01:46–02:06).** Blood Skunk stands exposed while attacking; the dwarf crew fires **a very large volley (up to approximately sixty bolts)**. His conjured ape initially absorbs hits and falls, then Blood Skunk is struck by **137 piercing damage** in one adjudication. He is **incapacitated at zero HP**, not finally killed. The discussion of *Barkskin* and alternate defenses is **after-the-fact mechanics talk**, not a retroactively cast spell. **Dr. Spider later uses Healing Light to restore about 25 HP**, so Blood Skunk **wakes alive**. The ship is **never captured**, its taskmaster remains in command as far as played, and Steerious is still climbing when escape begins.
10. **Real retreat via the Gate (~01:57–02:07).** Hope moves to cover, **opens a Gate beneath Blood Skunk's body** to **Dr. Spider's laboratory in Hell**, and crosses herself. **Dr. Spider escapes through the same portal and heals Blood Skunk there; Steerious reaches the ladder, runs and dives through last**. Hope **closes the portal** after everyone has crossed. **All four survive and are physically together in Spider's lab**, with **no dwarven ship, navigator or Eye**. The elves/Principle have no played role in this Sigil battle; no transfer of dockside treasure is recorded.

## Hell's counsel, an angry Principle and a real Nash reunion (~02:07–02:48)

11. **Spider Ward to Kaiser Casino (~02:07–02:17).** Dr. Spider leads the others through a vast **Arachnoidica Spider Ward** past working spider-people and cocooned figures to a **hellish rail station**. Its attendant initially requests **ten soul coins**, then **waives the fare for Dr. Spider and his guests**. They **really take the train** to Brett's **Kaiser Casino** and obtain an audience. This does **not** show coin payment or a general free-transit rule for outsiders.
12. **Brett restores sword and addresses the prison lead (~02:17–02:32).** **Brett**, presented as Asmodeus's local persona, **physically returns Steerious's previously forfeited greatsword** without explaining how he retrieved it from the Styx ferryman, reversing **E113's last-known sword loss**. Brett also **heals Blood Skunk's injuries** (the precise status of removed ears/toe needs listening; complete limb regeneration is **not established solely from “fully healed”**). He says **he has not heard of the prison**, explains *phlogiston* between Prime Material “discs” as **his account**, calls a wizard or lich a plausible source, and offers to investigate by interrogating damned souls. **No definite prison map, navigator or ship is provided**. Hope again mentions that she dislikes being indebted to Tobacho and has not visited him since the previous result; Brett's willingness to help and suggestions are **statements and possible future deeds**, not a completed solution.
13. **Unwelcome summoning of the Principle (~02:31–02:41).** At Spider's lab, Hope considers that **Gate can summon a named individual from another plane**. She calls **the Principle/Vladimir**; he **actually arrives through a portal**, immediately **grabs her by the throat** and demands never to be summoned this way again. He **does not injure her in that grip** but is furious. He admits he **does not know the prison's location**, says it may be hidden from the powerful, and speculates that **Nash's rumored time traversal** may help; this is **the Principle's conjecture, not proof Nash can currently locate that prison**. The Principle threatens their alliance if summoned again and **actually departs by a portal**. No new pact or combat defeat is performed.
14. **Second sacrificed ear and the intended target (~02:40–02:44).** To recharge the ring after summoning the Principle, **Hope asks Blood Skunk to kneel and cuts off his OTHER outer ear** (8 damage, he agrees). He already lost the first outer ear earlier, and **his left little toe in E113**. The ring responds as recharged. **Do not conflate this second ear with the first, or infer their reattachment**. Hope cannot locate **Elhandar** by description but knows **Nash Maggard of the Solar Mines** by his full true name.
15. **Nash really crosses both ways (~02:42–02:45).** Hope names Nash Maggard in ***Gate***, and the spell actually produces an opening to the **forested Elhandar biodome**, with **a cottage visible**. **Nash is literally pulled from where he was tending bandages in the glade into Dr. Spider's Hell laboratory** and reacts to the unexpected surroundings and Hope. Hope asks him to come back rapidly, enters the Gate toward **Elhandar**, then **takes Nash's arm and physically brings him back through**, while **Steerious, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider stay at the laboratory**. **Nash does not resist**. The GM explicitly ends on **Hope and Nash standing together in the Elhandar biodome**. Their discussion of motives, Vorel/Stanley's reaction, Angelashi's reaction and the new party's future are **not played at this cutoff**.
16. **Out-of-game ending (~02:45–02:48).** The GM says he prepared a Tobacho battle map but Hope chose not to enter the E113 portal, and explains the spontaneous branching of the session. The host announces **a one-week break** before the next episode. That break is **a publication/production note, not in-story time**. No additional fictional events follow the goodbye/network credits.
