## Source-role and continuity cautions

- **Substance and timeline:** The owner independently notes **Pixie Blush mentioned** in E011 and **obtained/used** in E017. Treat these as *different events*. The E017 SRT supplies a present-tense ingestion and prolonged effects, but **dosage, legal status and physical mechanism** should be verified from actual audio before precision claims.
- **Fan theory ≠ canon:** The opening listener email about **dragon-engine imprisonment and Nash's ancestry** is an **out-of-game speculation**, despite overlapping with genuine E009/E016 events.
- **“Jolt” and the posse:** The woman in the **red bandolier** is recognizable as the local figure foreshadowed in E012. Her personal/faction spelling, alias/title and posse composition are **ASR-uncertain**. She **offers** Vorel a role in a bank robbery; neither he nor the party is documented robbing the bank in E017. Do not conflate her with **Red Scarlet**, **Catherine Speckles** or the **Lactid Cartel** on clothing alone.
- **Maripol Bania:** An apothecary character offering treatments is explicitly present, and Nash's impaired visions follow. Her exact name and whether she **knowingly drugged** Nash are questions requiring precise dialogue and audio. Her predictions to Hope are **NPC claims**, not guaranteed outcomes.
- **Reality status:** Nash's **green-elf** experience is **hallucinatory or otherwise unverified**. The *explosion, fire and escape*, by contrast, are **GM-narrated external events** with impacts on other characters. Separate intent, observation and consequences.
- **Casualties:** The **foundry owner's death** and **Stanley's injury** are staged. Do not infer Maripol, Stump, Jolt, Talia, Bok Bok or any Dusthill captive died in the blaze; nor declare the whole town destroyed. **Slavetown was partly evacuated before the fire.**
- **Vehicle and location:** The **Fate 4 is not restored** here; the replacement is a **station wagon**. The party **sets off** for the Black Mine, whose actual approach and shaft are in E018.
- **Owner's personal spelling:** `Kuromo Kishi`, `Laktid` etc. from [owner notes](../project/OWNER_EPISODE_NOTES.md) remain aliases; do not copy the document's style into purportedly canonical transcribed dialogue.
