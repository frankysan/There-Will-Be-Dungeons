# E076 — Potable Portable Portal

> **Corrected main-campaign E076**, from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E076.%20Potable%20Portable%20Portal.parakeet.processed.srt), **not independently listened to or audio-verified**. Approximate offsets include the introduction, breaks and closing. **Spoilers through E076.** The opening explicitly says the main cast is back following **two weeks of Jon's separate *Inverse* campaign**, not two missing Wastes adventures.

## Session overview

Vorel's bait plan from [E075](E075.md) actually works. Pursued by **roughly fifty bugbears with an illithid behind them**, he runs back to the secure laboratory. Hope fires the red half of the **paired portal device** to reactivate the exit behind the pursuers. **Stanley crosses through**, Vorel rages and **grapples the isolated illithid**, and the others join him. The illithid telepathically orders its forces to **return**, but Stanley's *Diplomacy* and *Booming Blade*, Vorel's biting attacks, and Nash's *Fire Bolt* weaken it. **Hope kills this second illithid with her shotgun**; a worm seems to fly from the exploding head, **but its actual destruction is not shown**. The party flees rather than fighting the full column.

They return by the **flesh-controlled corridor** near the old elevator. The membrane **opens for Vorel, Hope and Stanley but closes against Nash**. Nash's attempted song no longer persuades it; a portal shot cannot pass a glass window. He eventually uses **Dimension Door** to reunite with the party before the bugbears arrive. They follow the alternative route into **CHIDS containment**, where a broken **catwalk spans a seemingly bottomless chasm** beside a flickering *Containment* sign. A bridge-control terminal reports that the bridge is already extended, despite its physical destruction.

Hope gives **the one red/green portal device to Nash**. Using the single pair of **magical flying underwear**, Nash levitates across the gap to inspect a mechanically jammed containment door. A crank opens the door only slightly before malfunctioning. A **large blue-black winged, leathery creature with a hooked tail** attacks him above the chasm. Stanley and Vorel emerge through a portal onto the pipes while Hope shoots from behind the portal. Nash's *Sleep* fails; the beast temporarily **frightens Nash and Vorel**, on top of their earlier memory-drain penalties. **Hope's shotgun ultimately kills it**; cries from additional flying creatures echo through the shaft. The portal pair is then used to cross safely, with one diving creature seemingly decapitated by an endpoint change—**a particular adjudicated effect, not yet a general established weapon rule**.

Beyond the crossing the team finds **pipes, responsive flesh growth and ghostlike blue laboratory workers**. The figures replay research conversations and typing that does not move the real keyboard, yet **they react to sounds and to Nash touching one figure**. A lab computer shows a **lizardfolk body with modification/equipment readouts**, including uncertain written/ASR terms resembling *psy blade* and *nanoweave integrity*. These observations strengthen the evidence that CHIDS experimented on lizardfolk; they do not establish that **all** lizardfolk everywhere share that origin.

In a preparation storeroom, **Hope finds two rockets that fit her existing launcher**—one loaded, one spare. She takes **five blue 'Blue Bull' bottles and five red 'Monster Mender' bottles**, chilled in separate marked containers. Warnings visually favor **lizardfolk** and caution against **elves**; exact chemistry and all dosage limits are not yet known. Nash tastes only a drop of blue liquid and experiences brief **stimulation**, not a demonstrated lasting character enhancement. Locked **white supply boxes** remain unopened; another military box bears a *C-Disk containment* designation without an actual disk recovery.

**Vorel dons CHIDS's full lizardfolk anti-dragon ensemble**: a white-silver nanoweave bodysuit, linked shoulder grappling-harpoon harness, heavy rocket-jump boots, and later a fitted helmet. The equipment feeds its purpose directly into his mind. The harness can tether and reel toward dragons, boots allow enormous leaps and safer falls, the suit protects against dragon elemental attacks, and the helmet resists telepathy/mind control. **The bodysuit still counts as armor, reducing his otherwise unarmored-defense AC; it is not an AC bonus.** His former skull helmet is placed with Stanley. Vorel additionally picks up **one of each potion**. The **Eye of Groxl** reacts angrily to the deeper containment area, but the object of its hostility is unknown at this cutoff.

**E076 ends with all four protagonists alive, moving into another narrow flesh-lined passage.** The tissue still tries to close against Nash as he sings. The party has **escaped the initial bugbear pursuit**, but has neither reached nor operated the Traversal Drive, rescued Sira, or fully explained the flesh wall and the Eye's reaction.
