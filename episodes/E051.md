## Continuity and evidence gates

- **Kefeli is genuinely confronted E051**; she presents **her version** of founding history, Andreeste's “gift,” royal succession and Monsoon's schemes. Neither **Akmenos genealogy** nor her political legitimacy becomes certain just because she exhibits **petrifying powers**.
- **Vorel and Hope are temporarily petrified and then restored *within E051*.** No permanent player-character death should be indexed. **Kefeli is physically destroyed** after petrification; no magical mass restoration follows that act.
- **Two basilisk-oil/eyeball preparations** are consumed to free companions. Do not falsely report the **E045 basilisk was processed back then**, or imply permanent stock remains after E051.
- **Gem of Tides is carried into Kefeli's room E051**, and the confrontation concerns restoring the mechanism. Preserve a careful distinction between **party custody E050** and later Gem emplacement/control; the exact hand-off/placement should be checked against audio, though flowing water/pumping infrastructure is narrated later.
- **Robe patches are finite**: E048 ladder consumed, **E051 steel mirror patch materialized**; ammunition used against snake hair reduces **Hope's specialty-round inventory**.
- **E050 P0D is dead**; the enormous pixies invading at the end of E051 are **other transformed pixies**, not evidence that P0D survived.
- **Names**: `Kefeli/Kepheli`, `Andreeste/Andriesta`, `Septwyn/Septwin`, `Tietkalla/Tiat Cala` and `Grojil/Groxl` remain source-bound; creator-approved **Diontalus** does not settle other variants.
