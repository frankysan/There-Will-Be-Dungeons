## Interpretive safeguards

- **Wedding schedule ≠ wedding:** Alidwyr and Jolt's marriage is **announced for Saturday**. It has *not* happened in E031. The aristocrats' names are ASR-sensitive and require creator-era orthography checks.
- **Pixie drug provenance:** P0D **says living pixies are harvested/crushed** to produce Pixie Blush. The group has not found the farm, independently inspected the production process, freed all pixies, or given a definitive, consensual ethical endorsement of any drug-spiking plan.
- **Teet locality:** E030 is the *approach*; **E031 is the first played exploration of Trashburg**, meeting Charlemagne and witnessing announcements/violence. Some maps were copied from older Dan Dakota work; Charlemagne annotates one now. **Direct observations** and **older/claimed map labels** remain separate.
- **P0D spelling:** Label includes a **zero**, not the letter O. The name “Pod” is an accepted **spoken nickname** but do not silently normalize her aquarium designation to a botanical or ordinary word.
- **Identity caution:** The woman called `Jolt Elegos / Bellicose` and title `Acrimonicar` connect to E017 recruitment and the earlier Soulbroker lead **as discussed by the cast**, but original spelling/real name remains unverified.
