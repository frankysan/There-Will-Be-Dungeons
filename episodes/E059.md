# E059 — Paradise of Mans

> **Corrected Wastes S1E059**; [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E059.%20Paradise%20of%20Mans.parakeet.processed.srt), **not independently audio-verified**. Follows the [Principle's arrival in E058](E058.md). Times below are approximate recording offsets including show introduction. **Full spoilers**.

## Episode outcome

The party debates whether to fight the **Principle**, then **accepts a conditional diplomatic arrangement**: railroad construction, trade, limited embassy and a Steel God prayer building. They ask for **paid voluntary construction labor**, security controls and restrictions; the Principle discusses his opposition to Weirlords and justifies his own slavery, an ethical disagreement not settled by a peace agreement. Most consequentially, the Principle **demonstrably pulls Diontalus out of Stanley's body and into a crystal** using his red-ringed metallic form. Stanley is visibly freed of that active presence in the scene, and the Principle **takes custody of the crystal**. **This is an observed extraction, not proof the Principle is trustworthy, that custody cannot be reversed, or that Diontalus is permanently destroyed.** The Principle promises future confinement and perhaps shooting the captive into space; this **has not happened**.

The **Dusthill captives actually arrive alive in Tietkalla** later in this episode, fulfilling part of the earlier promise: **Talia, Bruce/Bruce Bane, Jimster, Shun'ti and other familiar people** are reunited. The group wants them checked for parasitic magic and other tampering. Reunited prisoners describe working in the **Salivating Fields**, including farming, water and slavery's punishments; the apparent comfort of a farm does not make forced labor voluntary. **Not every earlier missing Dusthill person is confirmed among the arrivals**.

Vorel accepts **Diptak's invitation** to confront a leader in the lizardfolk lands. He rides a **velociraptor-like mount** and the others take **Fate 4** for the overland trip. A **vast bloodsucking swarm** forces them to take refuge inside **Stanley's magical Tiny Hut**, protected by Nash's surrounding sand. At **Razak Val** the party enters the lizardfolk settlement and discovers its **Great Fang is Orp the cyclops**, familiar from Slavetown, **with captives tethered to his body to be eaten**. Vorel's expected honorable leadership challenge is not yet fought by the episode end.

## Continuity cutoffs

- **Diontalus:** E057 he controlled Stanley; **E059 he is visibly extracted into Principle-owned crystal**. Treat **apparently separated/imprisoned, not destroyed**, and allow later episodes to test the permanence. Stanley's consent and Hope's agreement do not validate the Principle's promises about harmless custody.
- **Dusthill captives:** E055/E058 offers become **actual partial return E059**. Several named people reunite alive; fate of every historical missing citizen and internal security screening remains open.
- **Principle and Weirlords:** Railroad and diplomatic arrangements are **initiated**, not yet built. Religious buildings and embassy access invite questions about sovereignty. Do not turn the ruler's thousand-year autobiographical claims into impartial world chronology.
- **Travel and Orp:** Party uses **Fate 4** plus two ridden mounts; Orp controls a lizardfolk polity and holds humans as food. **The challenge is pending by the E059 end**.
- **Aliases:** `Razak Val/Razak Fal/Razakvale`, `Salivating Fields`, `Boxto`, `Shun'ti`, `Diptak`, `Diontalus` (owner-confirmed), `Eye of Groxl` (owner-confirmed singular item).
