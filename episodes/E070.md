# E070 — Polymorph The Security Monkey

> **Corrected main-campaign E070**, following [E069](E069.md), provisional dossier from [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E070.%20Polymorph%20The%20Security%20Monkey.parakeet.processed.srt). **Not audio-verified.** Approximate recording offsets include episode recap, OOC rules debates, intermission and outro. **Spoilers through E070**, ending during an active fight.

## Summary

The tentacled thing behind **Hope** at E069's ending proves to be **a weathered, squeezable plush doll resembling the illithid Ovaxiad/Oubvayx**, not a real attacking mind flayer. Hope actually **keeps the doll**. Squeezing it produces a squeak, momentary unease, and a **distant noise/skittering**; the noise is never shown to be caused by a separate monster. The party locates an old working **green-on-black computer** which responds to a typed version of **the enemy's name** by asking whether they want to play a game and directing them to **put on glasses in a conference room**.

While searching, Vorel walks ahead **singing loudly** and draws numerous **little translucent glowing tentacled creatures** through the walls. They touch or **nibble at the party's minds**, conveying an apparent sense of the creatures' satisfaction/contentment to Vorel and Nash without establishing induced euphoria in both, while **reacting with fear/disgust near Hope and Stanley**. The GM suggests these beings avoid negative emotions such as guilt or anger; **their precise species, allegiance, safety and motives remain unknown**. The mental nibbling is explicitly painless without visible contact; questions receive no intelligible answer. The emotional interpretation is not a reliable moral test, brain consumption or proof of mind control. Squeezing the mind-flayer plush makes them flee, and repeated songs invite them back. **Vorel uses the songs as bait and guidance, not evidence he has received a permanent spirit familiar.** His sung line “**I am the Lizard King from the Wastes**” reiterates his earlier self-assumed **Lizard King** epithet, rather than inaugurating a new office.

The **conference room** contains **eight seats and thick wired “glasses”** connected to workstations. **Nash puts a pair on** and experiences a fully restored corporate office, finding himself in an **illithid body/avatar**, while his physical body remains sitting with the party. In that **projected/virtual realm**, an illithid introduces himself as **Charles Dickens**, a disgruntled office employee who has not been promoted in 50 years. He claims these machines project a human's **ethereal self** into another plane that he calls the **Void Realm**, where the demon-lord alliance is preparing its invasion. **Hope joins Nash**; both can temporarily remove the headsets and talk to their guardians in the real basement.

Charles says his rival **Ovaxiad/Oubvayx** uses an ancient human **Traversal Drive**, originally meant to transport Earth away from dragons, to **bring the illithids' realm into Earth's plane** and harvest souls. This broadly corroborates the spectral dragon's E067 testimony but is **an interested character's exposition**, not physical inspection of the machine. Charles offers to impede the plan out of **office rivalry and desire to humiliate his superior**, not altruism. He gives Nash/Hope an apparent **memory stick containing false reporting figures** and instructs them to upload it to an unattended corporate computer to delay the project by **months**. They assume illithid appearances that can be altered through an avatar menu; Hope becomes briefly **a cockroach** to investigate an office, then **a coffee mug** as lookout, and eventually **a car** to create a distraction. **Nash uploads files to a “reporting” folder** and sees completion, but an employee challenges him and **alerts security**.

The **alarm also sounds in the physical CHIDS executive offices**, demonstrating a link between the two settings. **Both headsets transform into clinging membranes when the users try to remove them**, but **Nash and Hope succeed on Dexterity saves and escape**; they are **not permanently trapped in the Void Realm**. Flaming **hellhounds** charge into the real conference room, followed by huge **orange ape-like security guards**. **Stanley Polymorphs the first ape into a snail**, an active continuing condition (not death), while a **second ape turns invisible** and later attacks. **Nash casts Enlarge on Vorel**, who **grabs two hellhounds and uses them as improvised clubs against others**. Stanley grants temporary hit points through **Mantle of Inspiration**. **Hope's shotgun arm jams on a critical failure**, and she later uses **Scully radiant ammunition** against fiendish hounds; at least some hounds are killed. Nash uses **Absorb Elements** against fire and ultimately **Dimension Door** to escape being surrounded; **Nash is badly injured**. The episode **ends mid-combat** with the still-invisible-then-reappearing ape attacking Vorel; **no general victory or snail disposal has occurred**.

## Provenance and consequences

**VR vs physical:** The illithid workplace and **Charles Dickens** are scenes experienced by the projected player avatars, **not a claim that the ancient physical office has become fully restored**. What *can* be independently seen is the headsets, their dangerous **membrane transformation**, the real alarm and real hostile security response. The **Traversal Drive / Void Realm mapping** remains Charles's interested testimony. The upload was reported **complete within the simulated interface**; its causal effect is **not physically demonstrated in E070**.

**Statuses:** Vorel is **Enlarged and fighting**, not permanently a T. rex. **First guard is a live snail under Polymorph**, second guard attacks invisibly and then reappears. **Nash is alive but badly wounded after teleporting**. **Hope's arm shotgun is jammed**, while Scully remains usable. Hope carries **the plush mind-flayer toy**; Nash still has **magical cooling/levitating underwear**. The episode ends **in a fight**, without a long rest or Sira rescue.
