# E081 — There's Always A Chance In Space

> **Provisional, timestamped main-campaign dossier** based on [processed Parakeet ASR](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E081.%20There's%20Always%20A%20Chance%20In%20Space.parakeet.processed.srt), **not independently verified against the recording**. This is the **closing episode of the current CHIDS / Traversal Drive story arc**, but the broadcast explicitly ends on an unresolved post-sacrifice transition. Timestamps are approximate recording offsets, including recap, breaks, and outro. **Spoilers through E081.**

## Arc-ending overview

The encounter resumes at [E080](E080.md)'s precise cliffhanger in the **space-like battlefield** surrounding the **demon dragon-worm Dolo Popio**. Its ominous inhalation does not deliver an ordinary damaging breath: instead, **many large worms are exhaled and drift across the strange arena**. **Vorel remains attached to the dragon-worm** after his previous flaming-sword attack, **Sira is inside the cloudy pool/light enclosure**, and the six adventurers and guests are scattered across floating debris.

**Chance's fate diverges immediately.** He was **restored to eight hit points by Stanley at the E080 cutoff**, but on his first E081 turn the GM narrates him **slipping from a platform and floating away into the void**, out of view. No retrieval, body, death save, or arrival back in the laboratory is played for Chance. **The characters cannot locate him when the scene later returns to engineering**, and the outro jokes about Ben's character being lost in space; this is **missing/unlocated**, not an audio-proven death or successful rescue.

Stanley jumps into the central cloudy region near **Sira** and confirms she appears to stand with a hand against an unseen barrier. Elsewhere **Nash in young-black-dragon form** burns multiple drifting worms with a recharged breath and clears his **Frightened** condition. Hope continues firing at surrounding red memory-eating growths and worms, despite moments of fear.

**Vorel climbs up the enormous dragon-worm's body, removes his new CHIDS nanoweave suit, twists it into a makeshift binding and successfully wraps it around Dolo Popio's jaws**. The GM adjudicates a contested grapple; Vorel wins repeated checks, **holding the giant mouth shut and preventing further worm-breath releases** for several rounds. This is an **actual removal and improvised use of the suit**, not simply wearing it throughout the whole finale. While restraining the creature he attacks it furiously, including repeated bites; the Eye of Groxl remains **one sentient artifact** whose powers are used in different ways.

The smaller threats are dangerous. One large red flesh/memory-eater **burrows partway into Hope's chest**, causing a serious wound. **Hope skewers and kills it with her detachable metal horn**, leaves the horn at the obstruction, then propels herself away to reload. **Eddie also falls unconscious during the struggle**, but Stanley **expends another Ring of Spell Storing Healing Word and brings Eddie back with eight hit points**. He hands Eddie a **Monster Mender** and subsequently drinks one himself, citing a **Blue Bull interaction that prevents ordinary poisoning**. The exact number remaining in all caches and individual inventories still needs a character-sheet audit. The previously animated swords remain distinct from Stanley's E080 summoned pipes.

Nash and Vorel keep tearing at Dolo Popio. Floating debris includes **a modern-looking fire truck**, which Hope recognizes as a strange object she has never encountered; this is evidence that **unfamiliar real-world objects appear in the depicted space-like arena**, not proof the protagonists are now literally standing on present-day Earth. **Hope launches herself toward and grabs the truck's exterior ladder**, then loads *Scully* with **radiant darts**. Dolo Popio is stated to be **vulnerable to radiant damage**. Hope fires several rounds; a successful radiant shot produces enough damage that the GM asks her to narrate the kill. **She shoots the demon dragon-worm through the head, and it is physically destroyed**. The GM confirms a meaningful defeat, while cautioning that **demons do not die or persist in precisely the same way as mortals**; do not overstate that its essence is irreversibly erased.

The void battlefield **breaks down and the characters snap back into the altered CHIDS engineering chamber**. **Stanley, Eddie, and Sira are trapped together inside the central light**, unable to cross the invisible boundary; Hope, Vorel, and **dragon-form Nash** stand outside. **Chance does not reappear in the room**. Stanley tests the invisible border with an object and it fails to pass. Hope examines a complex **unlabelled console**. She discovers a **frequency dial** controlling the hum and waveform, a fader that changes the beam from bluish white to **crimson**, and another fader whose alteration produces alarming sensations in the people inside—**molecular tearing** at one setting and **rapidly growing nails for Eddie** at another. She restores the dangerous controls and searches for a safer way. At this point **the Drive has not simply been switched off**.

A new figure approaches after the lights briefly dim: **Quizquaz** (ASR variants), an imposing **winged, oil-black, white-eyed dream-drinker** speaking for **Kitobol Prith**. He congratulates them for defeating Dolo Popio but claims **the Traversal Drive cannot be switched off by ordinary controls**. In his explanation, its energy originates in the Void and is merely guided to Earth. He **offers a bargain**: let the beam destroy the world, thereby killing **the Principle** and purportedly fulfilling their demon pact, while Quizquaz arranges for the heroes to escape into other worlds. **All this is an interested demon's testimony**, not an achieved outcome or independently confirmed engineering model. He also claims **Andreeste** hired Dolo Popio, and hints at ongoing legal competition in the Weir; these relationships remain attributed allegations even as the creature itself was visibly present.

**Stanley refuses to allow the planet to die**, arguing that Quizquaz's urgency implies an alternative. The dream-drinker says **a sufficiently powerful willing subject can absorb the beam**, then be displaced with it, stopping the incoming energy but sacrificing the subject. Stanley initially **volunteers to remain in the light alone**. **Quizquaz uses the console to let Eddie exit**, while **Sira refuses to abandon Stanley** until he insists she join Vorel for protection. When Quizquaz permits it, **Sira becomes invisible and moves out of the beam**. Her eventual revealed position beside Eddie at the panel is shown later. This is the **played rescue from the beam**, not the earlier E077–E080 unrealized rescue.

The company resists Quizquaz's effort to push them toward planetary destruction. **Vorel** suggests a third path: **all four protagonists might share the energy burden**, rather than kill Stanley alone. He also challenges the demon to volunteer as the strong absorber; **Quizquaz reacts angrily and refuses**. Quizquaz eventually explains the controls: **flatten/lower the waveform frequency to permit entry or exit; raise the frequency and turn the beam crimson to engage its destructive/absorptive mode**. The instruction is **given by a manipulative witness**, but later **the device is physically operated as described**. He departs in anger after trying to persuade the party with threats about their souls and alternative worlds.

A particularly important decision precedes the finale: **Stanley casts *Augury* in prayer to Moha** about whether remaining in the beam to save the world is right. The GM grants a **favorable 'weal' omen but warns this will be Stanley's final result**. The omen is observed table magic; it is **not a guarantee the characters survive**, nor is it proof every claim about the Drive is objectively true.

Finally **all four main heroes choose to enter together**: Stanley is already inside; **Hope, Vorel, and dragon-form Nash** join him. **Eddie agrees to operate the console from outside**. While Hope shows him the controls, **Eddie secretly steals Hope's Bowie knife** with a successful sleight-of-hand contest; this is a **played inventory transfer**, not a hypothetical joke. Stanley instructs the now-freed **Sira to stay outside with Eddie**, extracting a promise that the gnome will protect her. **Sira becomes visible beside Eddie**, then **thanks Stanley and says goodbye**. **Chance remains missing**, so he is not one of the four people inside or the two outside.

**Eddie lowers the frequency**, enabling entry, then, once the four clasp hands in the beam, **raises it and sets the light crimson**. The group is filled with an overwhelming feeling of love and unity and begins to glow; **Hope's metal limbs do not glow like her organic body**. The GM calls for **Constitution saves and enormous damage**. **Hope falls unconscious**, **Vorel remains standing with ten hit points at the damage instant**, **Nash is forced from dragon into human form and takes additional damage**, and **Stanley's exact survivable HP is not resolved from the ASR alone**. **All four turn into white light and disperse into mist**, leaving the scene black.

The GM **explicitly tells the players the four are dead at this episode's cutoff**, although the cast also discusses discovering what follows in the next episode, and the exact spiritual/cosmic aftermath is unseen. Editorially, record both levels: **the GM states their deaths and shows their disappearance**, while **permanent nonreturn, individual afterlives, precise point of emergence, and final resolution of the soul pact remain beyond E081**. **Eddie and Sira are outside the beam and survive the activated sequence as observed**. The machine's beam is visibly consumed/ended with the four, and the scene cuts before the lasting planetary condition is explicitly surveyed.

## Arc-end continuity and open issues

**Known performed outcomes:** **Dolo Popio is defeated in visible physical form by Hope**; the space scene ends. **Sira physically leaves the central beam and stands with Eddie**. **Quizquaz arrives and later departs**, after explaining a procedure the players operate. **Four protagonists deliberately absorb the beam together**, **visibly disperse**, and the GM explicitly **declares them dead at this cutoff**. A surviving **Eddie** is charged to care for Sira. **Chance drifts away** and remains unlocated.

**Unresolved:** Are the four irreversibly dead? Has the Void stream been completely disabled permanently or redirected? What is the longer-term condition of Earth, the Principle, demon contracts and the captured identities? Does Chance land in another world or die in the Void? Has Dolo Popio's essence permanently died? **Do not fill in later-episode answers from a final-era wiki or player sheet.**

**Evidence tiers and names:** Quizquaz's assertions about Kitobol Prith, **Andreeste** contracting Dolo, the energy's Void origin, the Principle's death as a side effect of planetary destruction, and the need for a powerful absorber are **motivated NPC claims**; the controls' physical responses and white-light disappearance are **direct GM-narrated events**. **Chance, Eddie, Sira, Vorel, Nash, Stanley, Hope, Eye of Groxl** use established project spellings; **Dolo Popio / Dollopopio**, **Quizquaz**, **Kitobol Prith**, **Moha**, **Andreeste** are still ASR spelling candidates pending creator/handout checks.

**Inventory/status:** **Hope's Bowie knife transfers to Eddie**, while her detachable metal horn is **left in the worm/Hope injury scene**. **Stanley expends a Healing Word stored in his ring to revive Eddie**, and consumes **Monster Mender himself**, with a separate bottle handed to Eddie; exact remaining count unknown. **Vorel's nanoweave bodysuit is used as mouth-binding** rather than worn throughout the fight, and its fate after the void collapse is unrecorded. The **two rockets were already spent E079**. Avoid treating gear as automatically transferred to Eddie or Sira when four protagonists vanish.
