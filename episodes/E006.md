## New cross-references

- **Dusthill water chronology:** E005 records attempted/reported restart; **E006 ~00:24–00:26** is direct **surface outlet test**. The GM's E006 opening recap says water flowed to the surface but is a retrospective narration.
- **Local governance and accountability:** Shun'ti's temporary role, Tetsuo's continuing defense authority and Jimster's assistance are **different assignments**. Wilma/Aaron dispute and Jenjen/Thomas grief carry the earlier Hill Guard conflict into the present, but no court judgment is established.
- **Elven lore:** `Zorenthelas` is the form in the [wiki's E006 recap](https://twbd.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_6), against noisy SRT approximations `Xoranthulus` etc. `Elhandar` and alternate `El Hondar` should be indexed. The elf's religious account of dragon-engine purpose is **speaker claim**, not a GM-verified fact.
- **In-show source opportunity:** Fate 4 drawings, its rules sheet, and the **Wastes map** are discussed in E006 and the E007 opening. Seek contemporary creator-posted copies; do not reconstruct exact map spelling, vehicle dimensions or stats from uncertain ASR or later builds.
- **Episode boundary:** Unidentified lights end E006; the E007 beetle identification must not be backdated into E006 character knowledge.

## Newly archived contemporary-design reference (wiki image copy, reviewed 2026-10-21)

The repository now holds a [Fate 4 design/stat sheet](../sources/images/Fate_4_Character_Sheet.webp) and its [continued page](../sources/images/Fate_4_Character_Sheet_Continued.webp). The continuation visibly credits **Kristen Ashton** for the **Fate 4 images**; the first page explicitly identifies Hope and Jimster, calls the machine **"Fate 4, Hope's Vehicle of Destiny,"** and illustrates a modified RV on monster-truck wheels. The sheet describes its intended **10-foot width / 30-foot length**, **CB radio**, **roof access**, passenger facilities and other details. These are **creator-design/document claims**, corroborating that a real vehicle specification existed beyond the SRT's conversational presentation; they are not proof that each option was used or spoken in E006. A [separate floorplan](../sources/images/Fate_4_Floorplan.webp) provides another labeled interior schematic, but its creator and exact correspondence to Kristen's drawings remain unverified. Do not treat any sheet as an unchanged rules stat block for all later sessions.
