## Interpretation and continuity

- **Two “Freedom” objects:** In E014, the party finds a firearm named **Freedom** with three silver bullets (exact property/custody pending). E018 establishes **Nash's own name for the nail fixed in his staff**, also **Freedom**. They are **different weapons/objects** with different origin stories. Do not merge their inventories, owners or first appearances.
- **Nash's backstory source role:** The Solar Mines nail story is **Nash's first-person account**, including violence and remorse, *not* direct observed history. It may contain details that qualify his earlier E001–E005 past, but preserve the exact speaker role and time.
- **Survivors and casualties:** E017's fire occurred, but the E018 **sandstorm's effect on the fire is a supposition**. No verified death roll for the town is provided. The earlier Dusthill captives are **not yet rescued**.
- **Slaves at Black Mine:** The guards claim a **slave uprising** and deliberate starvation of **100–200 miners**. Their accounts of command, blocked alternative passages, absence of guards below and presumed dead sentries are **interested testimony**. The first undead and end-of-episode swarm are **played/narrated events**; this does not demonstrate every enslaved miner is dead or a zombie.
- **Catherine Speckles / Tylander:** The reason for visiting remains a **magistrate's lead** associating Catherine with the **999th floor**. E018 does not find Catherine, recover the deed or confirm Tylander's location. The site is now **physically entered**, unlike in E016.
- **The storm, shelter and wildlife:** The enormous carcass is physically entered; the **species of the eight attackers is not established** by their small size and speech. They are driven away, not slain. The vehicle's abandonment is an equipment/travel milestone.
- **The episode boundary:** E018 ends when **undead attack a slowly moving elevator**. E019 titled **Elevator Action** picks up from that situation; do not retroactively document the E019 outcome as E018 victory.
