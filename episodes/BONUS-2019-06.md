## Reference and continuity implications

**Character intentions, not retroactive events.** Jon's analysis deepens Stanley's crisis with oppressive order, continuing guilt for Diontalus's violence and desire for redemption. Scott confirms Nash's enduring **“of the Solar Mines”** self-identification. Kyle's comments explain Vorel's roleplaying choices; Kristen's godhood/destruction-of-gods ambition is explicitly an **unfinished character concept**. None of these statements retroactively change the contemporaneous E000–E060 played sequence or prove every other PC knows a private motive.

**GM-setting revelations versus unused preparation.** Beau's **Principle age/pre-Scorching knowledge** is an attributed creator statement, more direct than a villain's boast but still ASR-based and incomplete. The **druidic staff** is unidentified. **Sand-fishing creatures** are discussed as world design, while the **John Scots parody and prepared car chase remain unplayed**. In particular do not place a sand-fisher meeting, magically generated car barrier or destroyed vehicle into the official chronology.

Do not imply this June Q&A independently supports the spelling.
