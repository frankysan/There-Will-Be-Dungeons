# E078 — Who will flay the Mind Flayers

> **Provisional main-campaign dossier** from [processed Parakeet ASR](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E078.%20Who%20will%20flay%20the%20Mind%20Flayers.parakeet.processed.srt), **not independently audio-verified**. Approximate timestamps refer to the episode recording, including intro, break and closing. **Spoilers through E078.**

## Played sequence

The story resumes **before** the rushed rest announced at the close of [E077](E077.md): the council's flesh-made spokesperson has gone dormant in the CHIDS containment room. Vorel challenges the council over its historic anti-dragon program. Its representative **claims CHIDS did not create the lizardfolk species from nothing**: it took genetic material from existing lizardfolk in another world and bred **its own engineered soldiers**. The council says CHIDS was developing weapons against an invasion in cooperation with demon-influenced humanity, but lacked the resources to raise an army now; the organic infrastructure **cannot disconnect from the facility** without fragmenting its distributed consciousness. These are **the council's explanations**, not an independent origin record.

The council supplies Stanley with **codes to previously locked weapons caches**, acknowledges **Hope's 'displacement gun'**, and gives **one Ring of Spell Storing each to Stanley and Nash**. It says their operation is designed to store spells for later use. Vorel and Stanley privately discuss Wall Nash's warning: **Stanley says he would volunteer to take Sira's place and die, if it really were the only way**; Vorel is asked to help the others escape. This is a **conditional plan** based on **unconfirmed future-Nash testimony**, **not an executed sacrifice**.

Back in the preparation room, the group **actually performs Vorel's limb-repair procedure on-screen**. Nash retrieves the frozen severed fingers and hand from his flying underwear; companions position them against the wounds while Vorel drinks **Monster Mender**. The GM describes tissue knitting and movement returning, with scars remaining. **The repair was suggested/narratively anticipated at E077 close but is directly played around E078 ~00:27–00:31**. This later direct scene is the best source for its timing and success. An echo-like scientist briefly reenacts typing without moving the real keyboard.

**All three locked supply boxes are now opened** with the provided codes. They contain **20 crimson 'Vorpal Laser Swords'** (small power hilts projecting katana-like blades; **strength-based**, with a versatile one-/two-hand profile), plus **two pairs of suit-mounted forearm blade bracers**. **Hope, Stanley and Nash each take one sword**, leaving **17 swords**; Vorel mounts **one pair of wrist blades**, and the other pair is kept as spare. The party **packs the remaining 17 swords, spare bracers and approximately 11 complete anti-dragon equipment sets** into **Stanley's Bag of Holding**, estimating the load under its 500-pound limit. The ensemble comprises nanoweave suit, jump boots, shoulder harpoons and dampening helm. The GM specifically clarifies that the **helmet blocks telepathy but not Vorel's empathetic bond with the Eye of Groxl**. The exact custom item names, weight arithmetic and later distribution need sheet/handout checks.

The party returns to the chamber where Lethanphius died. **Stanley creates Tiny Hut and everyone takes a real eight-hour long rest in E078**, with watch order. Before sleep **Stanley stores two Healing Words in his Ring of Spell Storing**, and **Nash stores three Chromatic Orbs in his ring** using available spell slots. **Hope receives another nonurgent sending-stone message asking her to check in; she replies to Nobiery/Nobieri with a situation summary, without requesting reinforcements.** After the rest, **Nash feels aching and as though his memories/magic have shifted**, but the exact cause and spell-list consequences are uncertain; do **not** automatically describe this as restoration of the older red-creature memory-drain effect.

The council finalizes a transport route into the **recreation area adjoining the engineering complex**. It warns that **destroying the Traversal/Transversal Drive outright might destroy Earth** through an uncontrolled energy cascade; the goal should be to **power down or dismantle the illithids' operation without detonating the device**. It says the original project aimed to **move Earth to evade dragons**, but was never completed. **Stanley's successful Insight check suggests the spokesperson is genuinely telling what it believes**, not that every historical/machine claim is true. The two flesh avatars still exist separately; the figure claiming to be Wall Nash can speak, but its story remains contested.

The party **uses the hive's physical transport**: all four lie on organic beds and travel for several minutes inside cushioned tunnels, emerging quietly in a small engineering/recreation-side room beside **an unaware illithid console operator**. **Hope and Vorel attack**, Nash first uses **Chromatic Orb** and a diamond associated with his **wedding ring** (which Scott chooses to fit into his orb rather than merely hold), and the team kills the **first illithid** plus nearby **lizardfolk and bugbear defenders**. Screaming leads them to a second room.

In the second room, they unexpectedly find the old gnome acquaintances **Chance and Eddie Manchester III** **hanging bound and gagged above a pool full of small tentacled creatures**, threatened by **two more illithids**, a **robed human spellcaster** and a **security ape**. Their players are guest participants (names/hearing should be checked against the intro). The battle is dangerous: **mind blasts stun multiple party members**, Vorel's dampening helm prevents at least one control/stun-type interaction but **does not make him invulnerable to all psychic attacks**, and a mind flayer **grapples and begins eating Vorel's head** while he is helpless. **Nash uses Twinned Haste on Hope and Vorel**, and later attempts **Banishment**, which the illithid resists. **Stanley Polymorphs into a giant ape**, breaking the pool structure, and creates an opening for rescue. **Eddie's knife attack kills the illithid grappling Vorel, saving him**. **Nash kills the remaining illithid with Chromatic Orb**; **ape-form Stanley destroys the security monkey**. The robed caster also dies during the fight. **Chance and Eddie survive**, reunited with Nash and Stanley after their much earlier separation at the sand pit. The party ends the episode in this messy chamber, **not yet at the Drive**.

## Continuity and pending verification

**New knowledge tiers:** CHIDS's ancestry story and catastrophic Drive risk are **attributed council testimony**. The observed cache, repaired Vorel, real eight-hour rest, tunnel ride, enemy deaths and rescue of Chance/Eddie are **played events**. Stanley's sacrifice is a **private conditional intention** only. Do not turn the alleged other-timeline calamity into the actual chronology.

**Custody:** **Hope retains the one portal/displacement device**. **Two Rings of Spell Storing** belong to **Stanley/Nash**. Three laser swords are individually carried, **17 additional swords** and one **spare bracer pair** stored in **Stanley's Bag of Holding**, along with approximately **11 spare suit kits**; Vorel has two arm blades active. Confirm actual D&D Beyond item assignments. Consumable **Monster Mender** counts must be reconciled with E077's narrated anticipated use and the **actual E078 repair**—do not automatically deduct both as separate real applications.
