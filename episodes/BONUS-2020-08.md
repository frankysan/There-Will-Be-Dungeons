## Source-first findings

1. **Dungeons and the prison lead (~00:48–04:20).** Asked why a show with this title does not feature more dungeons, **Beau references a prison in phlogiston space**, recognized as **`pento vice` / `Pentel Vice`** in these exports. This is a retrospective reference to the [E113 Council lead](E113.md), **not a visit to the prison, Eye acquisition, or verified spelling**. The cast points to the long 2019 underground storyline and discusses megadungeons, outdoor encounters and variable adventure spaces. Portal-jumping and Mega Man jokes do not establish new Vorel actions.
2. **Scott still performs the sound live (~04:20–07:35).** Scott says **music beds and sound effects are still selected in real time while they play**, using a collection built over the show's run and a better sense of likely environments from the previous session. He says **Beau does not give him advance plot information**. He would have liked a mine-cart effect for “today's” sequence but lacked one, while water/waves and boat creaks were available for the Styx. He keeps the mix unobtrusive for the cast and geared toward the audience. **No specific soundboard application, automation workflow or detailed post-production process is identified**; his answer concerns the live effects.
3. **A reunion battle is speculation (~07:35–10:38).** A listener proposes an Endgame-like confrontation involving returning people, lost items and Hope's followers. **Beau says he does not know the ending and plans locations rather than a fixed final battle**. Scott enjoys encountering **a version of Pod and alternate Tedna**, not proof that the original dead characters have been resurrected. **Kyle recalls Vorel's earlier hope of becoming “king of the desert” or organizing a formal goblin force**, then jokes that space adventures could take him anywhere. This is **retrospective aspiration, not a newly claimed/awarded title, formed army or Star Fox identity**. **Jon says Stanley's original goals have changed**, without announcing a replacement ending.
4. **The shared sacrifice redirected the campaign (~10:38–12:30).** **Beau says the four heroes sacrificing themselves together in Kervalon was unexpected**. He had anticipated one sacrifice or a clever alternative, giving **getting Quizquaz into the beam** as an example of an unchosen route. He says he had to rethink the aftermath and the galactic/multiverse direction, while **Nash's time-related material was already in development**. These are **later GM accounts of preparation and counterfactual possibilities**, not extra E081 events, a promise that the alternative would succeed, or permission to erase the actual four-person sacrifice. His preference is to preserve player agency rather than steer toward a scripted finale.
5. **Scott's Nash ending is conditional and undisclosed (~10:38–13:35).** Scott initially sounds certain he knows Nash's final scene, then **explicitly qualifies it: if Nash survives until they finish with these characters**. An encounter death would change that. He may discuss the idea with Beau to stage it dramatically, but **does not reveal the scene**. No retirement, death, family destination, world destruction or guaranteed survival is established here. The recognizers differ on a nearby negation, so the surrounding qualification matters more than an isolated garbled sentence.
