# E067 — Frigid Fingers of Failure

> **Corrected Wastes E067**, following [E066](E066.md), drawn from the [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E067.%20Frigid%20Fingers%20of%20Failure.parakeet.processed.srt). **Source-based draft, not listened to or audio-verified.** Approximate recording offsets include reader email, recap, rules discussion, intermission and outro. **Full spoilers through E067.**

## At a glance

En route to **Kervalon/Kaer Valon** in the spectral carriage, **Hope experiences a scene/memory of early Dusthill**: Softhead introduces her to **Jimster** and the derelict **Fate 4**, which he hopes she can repair. She hears a song, *Long May You Run*, before returning to the present. This is **a personal subjective episode sequence**, not a second repair of the E066-destroyed vehicle.

The carriage rises above the eyeball forest into an extraordinary star-filled setting where **an ephemeral dragon spirit** addresses the party. It calls **Nash a bearer of the scent/mark of a great dragon**, named something like **Kazdejira / Kazdeshira** (spelling uncertain, the name is said to be written on an item/arm). The spirit says it was killed while trying to stop **Oubvayx/Ovaxiad** and **Dollo Poppio**. It claims an **ancient human-built, pre-Scorching machine beneath Kervalon** can **move the entire Earth elsewhere in space**, and that the demons are bringing their own realm into this world—manifest as **endless rain, darkness and eyeball trees**—to use the machine or a related portal and incarnate Dollo Poppio through the **captured mutant child Sira**. The dragon urges the party to destroy/stop the device and rescue the child. **These are a supernatural NPC's direct explanations, important but not yet mechanically verified by inspecting the machine.** The dragon refers to the **Eye of Groxl itself** as a **“god killer”** in Vorel's possession. The processed ASR renders the name as **`Grojil`**, a pronunciation approximation; the archive owner has explicitly confirmed that the referent is **the Eye of Groxl**, **not** a separate legendary figure or artifact. The Eye's claimed history as a god-killer is still **the spirit's testimony**, not independently verified. Its spirit dissipates and the carriage leaves them **outside the ruined Kervalon keep**.

After extended planning over an **open, guarded approach**, Nash uses *Mold Earth* to excavate a **20-foot-deep tunnel** below the perimeter; Hope scouts a deserted **underground stone chamber** through a temporary opening and hears heavy steps. They return to Vorel and Stanley, seal the external entrance and regroup. Breaking through the chamber wall together, they confront a **large flesh-and-bone creature that produces skeleton minions through bodily openings**. In a pitched fight **Stanley's animate weapon Diplomacy kills the spawning creature**; the remaining skeletons are destroyed by **Hope's Scully shots, Nash's Fireball, and Vorel's fire burst**. The adventurers explore side rooms and corridors; **Hope spots an armed, motionless lizardfolk guard lying in ambush** farther down a passage. E067 ends **before the guard is attacked or the machine found**. This is a **real subterranean assault**, not the proposed **T. rex polymorph / dragon-teleport breach**, which remains merely a debated alternative.

## Editorial boundaries and consequences

**Mystery of the device:** The dragon's explanation may be crucial, but preserve both levels: **the party observes a supernatural dragon-spirit encounter and a bright column over the keep**, while the **planet-moving machine, another world arriving and using the girl's body to incarnate Dollo Poppio** are **its testimony**. No working schematic or proof of its mechanism has yet been found. The alleged spectral dragon death, claimed **Kazdejira** name and the Eye's purported **god-killing history** merit listening and creator-written reference checks; **the E067 Groxl referent itself is resolved by the owner**.

**Time and knowledge:** Hope's Dusthill/Fate 4 memory is not a present-day repair, and neither the E066 skeletons' **warp-coil** explanation nor the E067 dragon's **Earth-transport machine** establish that the objects are identical. The group receives the supposed coil in E066, but **does not install or operate it in E067**.

**Story and tactical separation:** The only actual breach is **Nash's *Mold Earth* tunnel**, not the imagined polymorphed dinosaur or invisibility plan. The flesh giant **dies E067**, but the party is **still beneath the keep**, with armed lizardfolk farther ahead. No live encounter with the actual demon commander occurs this episode. **Sira remains abducted, Follow and Rorf dead**.

**Inventory and spells:** Vorel's fatal attacks against the pit T. rex in E066 and undead in E067 use the **Eye of Groxl shield**, **not Mawstorm**. **Stanley's Diplomacy** is the animated blade that kills the skeleton-spawner. Nash's *Minor Illusion* and *Ray of Frost* are tested during this battle; OOC feat/spell-sheet conversation requires independent reconciliation before treating it as canonical character-sheet progression.
