## White Tower — the three remaining original companions (~00:04–01:49)

1. **Unexpected Euphemian command arrives (~00:04–00:12).** About a dozen **Pegasus-mounted paladins**, led by a burly Captain whose name sounds like **Skrim/Skerim/Skirim (?)**, arrive after the battle. He says he has led a decade-long campaign for the Fist and angrily questions why the unarmored **Vorel, Nash and Stanley** are in White Tower. None claims to be a Euphemian knight. He orders his men to watch them and goes to speak with **Knight Commander Alentris Vayne**; no new execution order is passed on the party at this time. The **three airships observed in E100–E101 remain nearby**. Walnut confirms Hope is alive though gone, protects the exhausted guests and leads them toward their chambers.
2. **Rest/custody continuity clarified (~00:11–00:18).** The cast briefly debate whether the group has only 1 HP/no spells and needs to rest; Beau and the players explicitly reconcile this with **E101's already-announced long rest/level-up** and now **play out that same night's transition**. **Do not count an extra independent full rest.** Stanley goes to the room associated with Alentris; **Vorel takes charge of the locked floating chest**, with the **gauntlet inside, not the Hand attached to Hope**. They discuss hiding/removing the gauntlet but **do not actually open the case or remove the gauntlet**. It is brought to a closet in their fifth-floor guest quarters. An unsanitary alternate hiding spot and grotesque mutation fears are **jokes/plans**, not a realized new artifact or monster.
3. **Tobacho's signal and response (~00:19–00:26).** Stanley uses the **sending/communication stone** to report that a bloody battle occurred, their party has secured the requested item and they intend to return it **the following day**. The reply thanks them and says ships will **descend and meet them**, asking specifically **whether Spatulo is all right**. Stanley does **not answer that question** within the exchange; a one-use communication constraint is discussed. The original party considers presenting the **separate, now apparently lesser gauntlet** as fulfillment even though Tobacho may have wanted the Hand. **The item is neither delivered nor demonstrated to satisfy the dragon's bargain**. The three agree to keep **the whole locked case and key** together for plausible compliance, instead of hiding a loose glove elsewhere.
4. **Tungans from Nash's impossible moon (~00:26–00:46).** At dawn Walnut wakes Nash because **two living, yellow-furred, six-legged, eye-shifting spheroids** have come from the city-bearing **Tunga/Tungatung moon** in a **silver-domed flying saucer**. Their repeated **“Tunga”** is not translated. Nash walks them outside, elicits interest by repeating the sound and confirming by gesture that their origin seems to be the moon. Neither a mutually intelligible message nor proof of their motives results. The **three other airships have landed near White Tower**, and the city moon remains visible in daylight. Nash persuades Walnut to accompany him into the saucer as a self-styled “buddy system”; both get inside, and the two beings begin to operate its controls.
5. **Actual aborted flight, crash and rescue (~00:43–00:57).** Nash **chooses to stay aboard as the saucer launches toward the moon**; he speculates that his learned White Tower teleportation-circle sigils might provide a route home, although required chalk/materials and actual interplanar range are uncertain and **no teleport spell is cast**. As they rise high enough to see space, the craft malfunctions and falls earthward. Nash **opens a hatch, carries Walnut out and uses his actual integrated flying/hovering underwear** to save them. Because ten-foot-per-round descent would take hours, he **temporarily cancels flight, free-falls, and succeeds on a last-moment Dexterity-related check (14) to resume flight around eighty feet from the ground**, stopping with feet touching earth. Walnut survives but is deeply shaken. The **saucer crashes and explodes against White Tower, yet the tower is explicitly undamaged**. The two Tungan visitors' fate is **not observed**; never assert they were killed or escaped.
6. **Moon danger and a manufactured divine appearance (~00:56–01:03).** Paladins confront Nash about the moon, which they say **appears closer** than the preceding day. They **do not remove it, verify its future orbit or witness a collision**. Nash tries to reassure the distressed Walnut by making an illusion of **Euphemius** (small, robed, floating meditative figure). An **approximately 19 Deception** produces the desired effect: Walnut falls to worship, while other knights observe the spell and laugh. **The god did not truly manifest**; this was Nash's illusion and concealment from Walnut. Nash rejoins Stanley and Vorel and recounts his expedition; the trio resume their plan to leave with the chest.
7. **Alentris faces judgment and offers Stanley a different life (~01:04–01:19).** Before departure, **Alentris embraces and kisses Stanley** and says she has been **summoned to the high temple of Euphemius for a trial/review of her leadership and loss of the Hand**. She *expects* a loss of command or other less severe sanction and wants to leave her lengthy front-line service; **no trial, sentence, journey to the temple or execution is performed in E102**. She asks Stanley to **retire with her and live peacefully in the Outlands**. Stanley acknowledges the attraction but **explicitly refuses because he intends to keep his promise to return Nash and Vorel home**. Their affection survives the refusal for the moment.
8. **Stanley uses Modify Memory against Alentris (~01:19–01:28).** Alentris **demands the Fist gauntlet/case** be returned to her vault and forbids taking the evil relic away. Stanley becomes furious about divine failures and losing Hope, then **actually casts *Modify Memory***. Her Wisdom save **fails against DC 17**. The GM adjudicates the edit as **successful**: Stanley replaces the recent argument and her demand with a false recollection that the **gauntlet was left elsewhere in the tower, while they carry only an empty case as evidence for Tobacho**; he also removes her knowledge of the spell and keeps a remembered kiss/farewell. **She has not genuinely been given the Fist, the magical effects are not just a proposal, and this is a deception/agency violation against a romantic partner.** Alentris **turns and walks away**; whether she later detects the deception or faces repercussions is unshown. Stanley immediately urges the others to leave.
9. **Escape and actual airship takeoff (~01:28–01:49).** The three attempt to exit without arousing suspicion. **Stanley uses Dimension Door to move himself and Vorel, carrying the still-locked chest, outside and away from the entrance**; Nash separately passes the guards on foot. A startled off-duty knight and Walnut provide distractions, but **no party capture or battle occurs**. Walton asks Nash to return because he has something important to tell him privately; **the information is not disclosed**. Nash reunites with Stanley and Vorel via a **successful Perception 21**. At the **three grounded airships**, they find an **orc-only crew** carrying insignia resembling those at **Daddy Long Legs**. They tell a large horn-helmeted orc foreman that **Spatulo and another named escort did not survive**; this is **the party's false/inaccurate report**, since **Spatulo actually accompanied Hope through the E101 portal**. The orc commander says **Tobacho awaits**, orders an early departure, and points them to guest quarters. **All three climb aboard with the sealed chest, and the ship physically lifts off**. Its destination is intended to be Tobacho; **it has not arrived**. The **Tunga moon seems noticeably closer/larger and potentially collision-bound**, but no impact has occurred when the airship departs.

## Hope's separate path — an apparently generous devil's gifts (~01:49–02:46)

10. **Casino conversation after E101 (~01:49–02:00).** Hope drinks with the figure calling himself **Asmodeus, nicknamed Brett**, who asks about her becoming queen and the **hooded patron/Voice**. He **claims to know that being** but avoids saying its true identity, apparently wary of its reaction. Hope says she wishes to become a **god of revenge** and help her people and companions. She describes an earlier killing of a desert camp from anger after family loss; this is **character testimony about past action**, not newly witnessed E102 play. Brett offers his own monstrous parable of destroying a floating settlement over a trivial offense, claiming he regrets it. **Do not treat either interested/confessional account as externally corroborated historical fact or proof Brett's claimed Asmodeus identity is genuine**.
11. **Brett tests Hope with Pumpo (~01:59–02:18).** A loxodon messenger interrupts, and Brett takes Hope through a casino elevator and corridors to a walk-in freezer, where a **goblin called Pumpo** is restrained and being beaten. Brett says he supplies food to **Pumpo's impoverished village on Dorgorth VII (?)** in exchange for its **strongest warrior each year**. This is **Brett's account of the agreement and its purpose**, not independent census, provisioning or soul-ownership proof. Hope initially suggests harsh reprisals, including withholding food, killing Pumpo or taking substitutes. Brett explicitly probes whether she can find an alternative to killing a valuable client. He orders Pumpo's arm **violently torn off, then snaps it reattached**, coercing a confession that **Pumpo himself won the strength competition this year and was withholding himself to avoid death**. Brett **magically weakens Pumpo**, enabling another village contestant to be designated strongest, and **allows Pumpo to leave under his promise to supply the next strongest**. **Pumpo is not killed, no alternate warrior arrives on screen, and no proof all villagers are thereafter fed or saved**. The cruelty is played; the deal's future fulfillment isn't. Hope returns to the casino with Brett.
12. **Necklace of Infinite Appearances actually equipped (~02:18–02:24).** In an intimate private lounge, Brett says he will present gifts **without a formal pact**, while the hooded Voice becomes visible to Hope behind him and encourages acceptance. Hope accepts a gold **necklace/choker with a clear blue-white gem**, named by Brett the **“Necklace of Infinite Appearances.”** Upon equipping it, **her disfigured appearance is concealed and a beautiful/unscarred version is visible in a conjured mirror**; she successfully **changes apparent form to a kenku-like bird and back** at will. **The effects are visual/appearance changes, not the actual loss or healing of the attached Hand of the Voice**, restoration of original amputations, or removal of patronage. The item and its working effect are now actually obtained; detailed limits require the item sheet.
13. **A first travel ring is offered and rejected; a stronger second ring is accepted (~02:24–02:29).** Brett opens a second gift box containing an ornate **preprogrammed travel ring** with known destinations including **Tietkalla and Kaiser Casino**. Hope tries/handles it, but the hooded Voice objects and tells her to demand better; **she returns this first ring to the box, not keeping it**. Brett then gives Hope a **rough, jagged, rusty copper ring** described as much more powerful. **Hope puts the second ring on**, feels an unusual arcane sensation in her forearm, and accepts it. Brett demonstrates: when Hope pictures an image of the casino employee lounge conjured for her by Brett, **she successfully teleports there with Brett holding her hand**. This is **actual use**, not only a promise. The ring's full cost, travel limits and source of power are **not provided**. The hooded Voice claims it will take her **anywhere in the cosmos**; that universal scope remains **a claim**, distinct from the demonstrated local teleport and later gate.
14. **Three new companions enter Hope's played story (~02:29–02:42).** Brett leads Hope to a rough back-room bar and introduces **three paid henchmen**, frankly admitting they **report to him and serve as his “eyes and ears.”** The new characters are:
   - **Steerious Business** — **Jon's** tall black-furred minotaur with orange/red accents, horn jewelry and a tailored business suit. **Canonical spelling confirmed by the user; the name is a steer/serious pun that began as a joke**, as discussed in the [June 2020 bonus](BONUS-2020-06.md).
   - **Blood Skunk Hendrickson** — **Scott's** tall muscular, pale clownlike figure with naturally red features and tightly strapped black leather; his exact nonhuman **species is deliberately unspecified**. His temporary evil-ranger concept is discussed separately in the [June 2020 bonus](BONUS-2020-06.md), but this episode establishes his **in-world arrival**, not the bonus.
   - **Dr. Spider** — **Kyle's** arachnid-humanoid figure in a doctor's coat, muttering at the bar; **audio/mic problems interrupt his full player description**, so do not invent physical specifics, a complete backstory or in-play feats. The bonus gives separate retrospective design commentary.
   **No assumption that the original Wastes party has acquired these henchmen is warranted**: they are specifically introduced **to Hope**, are on Brett's payroll and accompany her separate plotline. No demonstrated betrayal, obedience or individual combat is shown before cutoff.
15. **A real gate to Tietkalla and completed crossing (~02:42–02:45).** The hidden Voice tells Hope to use the **second copper ring** to return to **Tietkalla**. Unlike the earlier local blink, a **large skull-adorned gate with violet/blue mist actually appears**, showing **Hope's private royal chambers inside Tietkalla keep** beyond it. Hope **walks through**; Brett gestures, and **Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider physically follow**. Brett stays behind, wishing them well and laughing as the **portal closes**. The visible destination is the interior of Hope's chamber; **no view of the city's condition, any massacre, uprising, household greeting or new pact occurs** before the episode ends. The [June Q&A](BONUS-2020-06.md) later discusses devastation of Tietkalla, but **must not be imported into this cutoff**.
