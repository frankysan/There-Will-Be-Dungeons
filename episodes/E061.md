# E061 — The Many Maggards

> **Corrected Wastes S1E061**, directly following [E060](E060.md). Provisional source-first dossier based on [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E061.%20The%20Many%20Maggards.parakeet.processed.srt), **not audio-verified**. Times approximate recording offsets including introduction, recap, break and outro. **Full spoilers through E061**. The previous episode's scarless, red-eyed visitor **claims to be a future Nash**; this episode depicts more apparent counterparts, but **does not independently prove their explanation of time travel**.

## At a glance

The figure who approached Nash privately in E060 is joined by **a second Nash lookalike**, wearing glasses and apparently lacking the first visitor's red eye. The pair solicit help **stealing an ancient preserved Fengar egg** and offer a grotesque, unverified explanation of **time as accumulating “time barf.”** Nash puts two hall guards to sleep, accompanies them to the Fengar heirloom chamber and distracts **Bramie/Baymie the Tyrant Slayer** while the purported future versions take the egg and vanish. Crucially, Nash **does not witness or perform Bramie's murder**. Later **Zercil/Zershil** announces her brother has been found **stabbed from behind with his own enormous dragon-tooth polearm, Tyrant's Bane**. Orp appoints **Vorel “sheriff”/investigator**, and Zercil **joins the next morning's scaling**, making the challenge **three-way** (Orp, Vorel, Zercil). Footprint evidence implicates **three identical human-sized visitors** but **does not establish who killed Bramie**. In the course of the investigation Nash reveals his encounter and involvement in the theft to his companions **and, because they can understand much of it, to nearby lizardfolk**. A sinister chill immediately follows the broken secrecy warning at the **E061 cliffhanger**; its consequences are **not yet established**.

Meanwhile Vorel seeks the **Elder of Families** for clan history and the legend of **the Eye of Groxl, called “the God Killer”** (`Grojil` is an ASR pronunciation approximation), with a young assistant called **Boy** to help after the feast. **Typalla/Typala** prepares a fire-illusion story of **Closthi/Closh-Ti the Hidden Claw** and the blue dragon **Telestar**, interrupted before completion by the murder report. A Zark clan representative, **Geshotl/Geshatl Zark**, warns that the **Demon Vold** is a growing extraplanar breach with a general's name pronounced like **Dollo Poppio**; he asks Vorel and Hope to help defend **Kervalon/Kaer Valon**. These are **urgent NPC reports and requests**, not proof the invasion has arrived at Razak Val.

## Interpretation and editorial controls

**One murder, one theft — connections unresolved.** Nash's actions **include trespass, putting guards to sleep and knowingly helping steal the egg**. This does **not** justify calling him **Bramie's murderer**, as the last direct Nash-perspective scene shows Bramie alive and being distracted. Conversely, the coincident identical footprints and Nash's confession mean he is **not an uninvolved bystander**. Vorel's proposed **two-plot hypothesis**, Zercil's motive and the unseen larger-lizard footprints are **investigative leads only**. A later episode may settle them; do not backdate conclusions.

**Future claims are still claims.** Two lookalikes **act with apparent spellcasting and vanish**, but their mechanisms, “time barf” theory, unknown “they” who compelled the theft, and claim of recursive future memories are their **statements**. Do not certify the existence of a stable Nash time loop, erase the current Nash's injuries or announce Angelashi's future from their silence. The episode's cold-chill cliffhanger **warns of danger**, but shows **no completed time calamity**.

**Culture and authority:** Orp's “sheriff” appointment is **externally conferred, informal investigative authority**; Vorel's ongoing **self-bestowed epithets** need their own register. The Elder of Families' promised records, **Fuxi/Fushi blessing**, Closthi legend, preserved-egg prophecy, Zark warnings and Demon Vold breach story remain **separately attributed testimony and incomplete performances**, not new independently verified world history. The GM's **Eye of Groxl** spelling and the owner's **provisional Mawstorm** name should not be confused with a **separate Grojil god-killer figure**: according to the archive owner, the phrase **“god killer Grojil”** refers directly to the **Eye of Groxl**. Ancient deeds attributed to the Eye remain characters' lore, not independently proven.
