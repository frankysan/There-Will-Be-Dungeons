# E079 — We go boom boom

> **Provisional main-campaign dossier**, [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E079.%20We%20go%20boom%20boom.parakeet.processed.srt), **not independently audio-verified**. Approximate timestamps include recap and closing. **Spoilers through E079**. This episode **ends immediately after the opening assault volley**, before any announced resolution to the main boss fight.

## Played sequence

The team is still in **CHIDS's engineering-adjacent recreation laboratory**, immediately after [E078](E078.md). **Chance and Eddie**, played by visiting participants, are free; **two illithids, a robed human caster and a security ape** lie dead. GM-only material read to the guest players explains that after the gnomes' earlier disappearance they travelled through underground tunnels/railways, were **captured by bugbears** and lodged for a time in surprisingly comfortable quarters by illithids before waking bound over the pool. **That is GM-provided guest backstory**, not an additional rescue scene or proof the illithids' motives were benign.

Instead of taking an immediate long rest, the group distributes supplies. The table explicitly reconciles the preparation-room stock as **20 total Monster Menders and 20 Blue Bulls**, with the E079 inventory discussion placing **five of each with Hope**, **14 Monster Menders and 15 Blue Bulls into Stanley's Bag of Holding** after Vorel's E078 repair, while Vorel's prior consumptions and one-bottle custody create a small **ledger ambiguity requiring sheet review**. **Stanley and Vorel drink Monster Mender** for healing; Vorel can regrow only as previously established, while non-lizardfolk risk poisoning. Nash is healed separately by Stanley. **The party does not complete another full rest here.**

The gnomes describe nearby **recreation and cafeteria** spaces and apparently indifferent automated **bugbear/lizardfolk guards**. The team elects to sneak past **two security apes** at a four-way checkpoint rather than fight. During a **short rest**, Vorel fashions **a hollow mind-flayer face mask for Nash from a slain illithid's head**; Nash uses the head/robes, his levitating underwear and **Minor Illusion voice projection** to impersonate a mind flayer. **Stanley dresses as the dead red-robed human with cosmetic tattoos**; Chance/Eddie play prisoners, and Vorel/ Hope disguise or obscure their posture. They settle on a cover story that **Ovaxiad/Ouvexiad (spoken uncertain)** wants the captives. The checkpoint apes challenge them, but **Nash's deception works and they pass**. The mask is **perishable**, not a permanent magical artifact; do not assign it unlimited telepathic powers. The **short rest** is real; no new eight-hour rest occurs.

Past the checkpoint, a blue-purple mist gives way to **natural-looking cavern rock and blue-purple crystalline growth**. A sealed **crystal door has unfamiliar glyphs and a four-finger hand motif**. Chance's hand, despite folding a finger, does **not** open it. Nash **returns alone to the prior battle room and carefully cuts off a dead illithid's hand**; its application also **fails**, and the GM indicates that **a living/magically viable hand** may be necessary. The group instead has **Stanley teleport through the door using Dimension Door**; Hope shoots a **paired portal endpoint** through once access is gained, and the party **actually passes through the linked portals**. This is **not evidence the biometric crystal lock was legitimately opened**. They **leave a portal escape route** available.

For the **first time**, the heroes see a cavernous **crystal-transformed engineering space** associated with the **Traversal/Transversal Drive**: enormous machinery half embedded in blue-purple crystal, a bright vertical beam resembling the one witnessed high at Kervalon, and a **rotting dragon-like front half fused to a vast worm/fleshy rear body** in rainbow-bright liquid. Three **chanting illithids** and **Cerinthala** stand above/near the machine, along with a larger illithid form consistent with **Ovaxiad**. **Lizardfolk with ranged weapons occupy scaffolding high above**. The ASR contains a brief unclear reference to **Sira near the beam**; her exact position/condition needs direct listening and **no rescue is observed**. The dragon-worm's identity and whether the machine has been altered by outsiders are not established by visual appearance alone.

The party makes a prolonged **stealth and first-strike plan** rather than opening the beam, attacking the dragon, or touching control consoles. They keep the machine's possible fragility in mind. Some allies take cover, while **Hope's two previously recovered compatible rockets** become the central tactic. They discuss **Haste** and use **Blue Bull** beforehand. The GM explains a blue stimulant granting increased speed/actions but that **non-lizardfolk recipients can vomit violently on taking it** and **excess consumption risks psychosis or attacks on friends**; **Vorel is exempt from the immediate vomiting**. Other characters **actually drink it and several vomit**, then prepare for combat. **Do not confuse its game-specific effect with standard Haste spell**.

**Hope fires both rockets** across opposite raised banks/scaffolds as her opening attack, the second following a reload. Their blasts **kill multiple lizardfolk guards**, and knock **three visible illithids and Cerinthala into the central beam**, where their **visible bodies disintegrate into ribbons**. **This is observed destruction of Cerinthala's current body, not established permanent death**: she has previously returned from a lethal crushing. The enormous **Ovaxiad-like leader survives the initial blast**, appears badly agitated and **begins levitating upward**. The **dragon-worm, Sira and the Traversal Drive are not confirmed destroyed, disabled or rescued**. The episode ends on the explosive **opening salvo**, not the completion of the battle.

## Editorial reconciliation

**Death versus final fate:** Three illithids **visibly destroyed** by beam after rockets; **Cerinthala's body likewise disintegrates**, but **permanent death is not demonstrated**, because an earlier apparent death was reversible. The leader **survives the first strike** and **the beam continues**; do not mark final victory.

**Observed machine versus source testimony:** The characters now **physically see a transformed engineering chamber**, unlike the E077 cutoff. They **do not activate/dismantle the Traversal Drive**. Council claims that destroying it would destroy Earth remain **unproven warnings**. The **dragon-worm, Sira's precise location, four-finger lock and Ovaxiad nomenclature** require audio/map/handout review.

**Inventories and rests:** E078's **real eight-hour rest** and E079's **short rest** are different. **Both compatible rockets have now been fired**, not still loaded/spare. The one portal device stays with **Hope** and is used again. Nash has a **perishable illithid disguise**, Stanley a red robe/cosmetic disguise, and **Chance/Eddie are alive**. The spoken tally for **remaining Blue Bull and Monster Mender** is approximate and may omit prior individual bottles/consumption; avoid asserting an exact final tally after the Blue Bull pre-fight drinks.
