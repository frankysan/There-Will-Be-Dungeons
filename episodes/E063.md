# E063 — Two Royals

> **Corrected Wastes S1E063**; follows [E062](E062.md)'s **death of Orp and Vorel's public recognition as Great Fang**. Based on [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E063.%20Two%20Royals.parakeet.processed.srt), **not audio-verified**. Approximate offsets include introduction, recap and closing. **Full spoilers through E063**.

## At a glance

**Zercil/Zershil Fengar survives E062's blast** and confronts Vorel while he has **only one hit point** remaining. She tries to make him **yield the Great Fang title to her**, but the assembled lizardfolk acclaim Vorel instead; she attacks. **Stanley heals Vorel without being noticed during the battle**, and **Nash transforms into a young black dragon**, prompting the crowd to bow. Vorel observes **worms moving behind Zercil's eyes**, and attempts to subdue rather than kill her. **Hope nevertheless fires an Obsidian Nail round from Scully**: Zercil fails the GM's saving throw and is **destroyed into fine particulate**. The shot is **audible and witnessed**, including as possible dishonorable interference in Vorel's scaling, though the crowd subsequently acclaims his authority. Zercil's exact murder of Bramie is **not directly witnessed or confessed**; Vorel later accuses her, but her observed infestation and death do **not independently solve the earlier crime**.

Vorel begins ruling in earnest: he rejects Orp's **human-feeding system**, calls for investigation of demonic parasites, and establishes a cautious **medical-screening process for roughly 200–300 captives** in the **“man's pit.”** Captive freedom is **announced, screening begins**, but **not all prisoners are shown released from Razak Val by the episode cutoff**. In Zercil's bed, Vorel and Hope **capture a live black worm** and encounter the dead Fengar siblings' children, including **Felcha**. Meanwhile **Stanley meets an unusually composed, legless prisoner calling himself “Follow”** (ASR spelling uncertain), senses something magical, and has him held separately for further questioning. **No parasite is visibly extracted from Follow in E063.**

## Source boundaries and developments

**Both royal positions have different histories.** **Hope's Queen of Tietkalla** title was established by her E054 coronation; **Vorel becomes Great Fang of Razak Val E062**, affirmed and exercised in E063. Zercil's requested gift of leadership **does not take effect**. Vorel is formally acclaimed, not exclusively a self-titled “Lizard King.” The party's victory carries a serious **mass-casualty and legitimacy cost**. The court/festival survivors (including **Omnom**) are not the whole population.

**Infestation:** Vorel observes apparent live black worms behind Zercil's eyes; a **separate worm is captured alive from her bedding**, and the earlier E058 enemy brain leech is additional evidence of invasive organisms. **Which person implanted them, whether they caused Bramie's murder, and whether every captive or household member is infected are still unknown.** Vorel's statement to the court that **Zercil murdered Bramie is a conclusion/accusation**, not a scene depicting her doing so. Likewise Stanley's **unnamed vanishing girl** and **Follow's mysterious composure** remain open leads.

**Human emancipation is unfinished.** Stanley **states the captives are free** and initiates triage under Vorel's authority. But the account stops after only the first few examinations; **some are isolated rather than sent away**, and conditions for food, onward transport, safety and lawful protection still require later source evidence.

**Inventory:** **Necklace of Fireballs — five beads/charges** actually removed from **dead Orp** by Stanley. Hope spends a **reported Lucky Coin charge** before using **Scully's Obsidian Nail**; the exact remaining coin availability requires an item-sheet check. Vorel's **Eye of Groxl shield** and his **Mawstorm sword** remain separate. The rocket used E062 is **not fired anew in E063**.

**Title register:** **Great Fang** is an office recognized by witnesses and city; Vorel's claim **“I am the force here”** while changing colors (~00:24–00:26) is a boast/statement of power, **not automatically another formal epithet**. **“Sheriff”** was earlier appointed by Orp, and **“Lizard King”** is self-proclaimed E036.
