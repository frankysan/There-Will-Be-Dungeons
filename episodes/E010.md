## Interpretation and cross-episode links

- **The Principle** is treated as an authority or ideological/political source by its followers, while the warriors and magistrate are its enforcers; the SRT fluctuates between `principal`, `principle`, and `principals`. Do not make a definitive personal-name or theological assertion from ASR alone.
- **`Koromukishi` / `Kuromukishi`** refers to an elite/initiating martial order. `Toyota` may be the recurring chant/faction label rather than the automotive company; written orthography needs audio or contemporary source confirmation. Keep *warriors*, *followers*, *magistrate* and *slavers* distinct until affiliations can be mapped accurately.
- **Rival pursuers:** the Fate 4's previous **goblin** intercept (E007) and E010's orc-led security force are **different encounters**. E010's force takes the party alive and moves them to Slavetown.
- **Dusthill rescue arc:** encountering captives inside the slave holding compound proves some reached Slavetown; it does not prove all 25 abductees survived, remain together, or have been emancipated.
- **Old man:** In E010 he is still traveling with the four in the Fate 4 and participates/appears in the arena. His exact contributions and welfare require listening; he should not be erased from the five-person combat group simply because the original heroes are four.
- **Gnomes and dragon:** E009's unresolved Eddie/Chance fate is not fixed by the regular cast's return; the dragon is no longer immediately present, not proven captured or killed.
