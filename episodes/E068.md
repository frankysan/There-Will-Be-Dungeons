# E068 — Stay Out The Shaft

> **Corrected main-campaign E068**, following [E067](E067.md). Source-first provisional dossier based on [processed Parakeet SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E068.%20Stay%20Out%20The%20Shaft.parakeet.processed.srt), **not audio-verified**. Approximate recording offsets include recap, lengthy out-of-character tactical/rules discussion, break and outro. **Full spoilers through E068.** The separately archived [August public-release bonus](BONUS-2019-08.md) is a Q&A recorded after this session, not another played episode.

## At a glance

The company continues inside **Kervalon/Kaer Valon**. A laborious plan to trap rifle-bearing lizardfolk in an illusory cul-de-sac **partly succeeds**: Nash uses *Mold Earth* to close a passage around **approximately eight melee pursuers**, who pound on the walls. Vorel's speech draws a larger contingent into battle; the guards carry **bright, pulse-like energy rifles**, unlike presumed ordinary gunpowder weapons, and appear strangely emotionless. **Stanley actually casts Polymorph on Vorel, making him a T. rex**—a deliberate change from the **merely discussed** dinosaur tactic in E067. The dinosaur crushes several guards, but an **illithid resembling the E066 abducting mind flayer** briefly **dominates** it. **Hope's two shotgun blasts interrupt the enemy's concentration**, freeing Vorel, before a psychic **Mind Blast stuns both Hope and Vorel**. Heavy pulse-rifle fire destroys Vorel's borrowed dinosaur form; he reverts to lizardfolk and suffers substantial additional damage. **Stanley's animated sword Diplomacy stabs through the mind flayer's head**, causing its visible body to **collapse into a sack of skin and black oil**. The party wipes out the remaining exposed lizardfolk with Fireball, Ice Storm, weapon attacks and spells. **Whether the deflated body is the same enduring mind flayer, a manifestation, or a final death is not established.**

A short rest follows in **Stanley's Tiny Hut**, with **Song of Rest** used. The sounds from the **sealed prisoner corridor stop all at once**, but nobody opens it, so **the trapped lizardfolk's fate is unknown**. The company then studies a **brilliant vertical magical shaft** descending from sky through a spiral-stair tower into the floor. **A thrown rock disintegrates, a lizardfolk corpse twists and disintegrates, and Hope's approaching leg briefly spirals but returns to normal when withdrawn**. Nash's *Mold Earth* cannot excavate directly beneath it. The group ascends to the rainy exterior tower, comes back down, and traces **small black worms emerging from a floor seam**. Nash opens a concealed alcove containing **two steel elevator-like doors and an electronic panel**. **When Hope reaches to press the button, it turns into a snapping mouth; she succeeds on a Dexterity save and retains her fingers.** The episode ends **without opening the doors, discovering the machine or rescuing Sira**.

## Source, character and continuity implications

**Opponent status:** A visible **mind flayer-form collapses** in E068, and the GM congratulates the party on a mind flayer kill. The resulting **empty skin and oily black substance** makes it prudent to distinguish **defeating this body** from permanent death of the E066 abducting entity. **Sira remains missing**. The **seven/eight trapped lizardfolk** are not confirmed dead or cured merely because their knocking ceases. The lizardfolk **energy rifles** have not been inventoried or adopted; the [August bonus](BONUS-2019-08.md) separately clarifies why the party often ignores loot.

**Magic and equipment:** **Stanley really casts Polymorph on Vorel into a T. rex here**, unlike the previous episode. The T. rex is destroyed by shots but **Vorel lives**, later healed. **Eye of Groxl** is a single owner-confirmed artifact; `Grojil` is a speech-recognition variant and this does not split its identity. **Bardic Inspiration and Song of Rest** are used. **Mold Earth** succeeds on masonry near the concealed doors but **fails to remove earth under the shaft**. The light's enormous energy and disintegration effects are real; a **planet-moving machine** remains the **spectral dragon's E067 account**, not visually established here.

**Vorel's epithets:** He repeatedly calls himself the **Fang**, asserting his **recognized Great Fang office**. During T. rex combat, the group jokes **“Rex means king”**, to which Vorel says **“I accept the title”** (around 01:32); record this as a **brief self-accepted joking epithet, “Rex/King Rex”**, not an appointment by lizardfolk, a distinct crown or an accepted legal title. Separate from **Mawstorm** the sword/raptor and **Fang Fate Five** the abandoned vehicle.

**Next cutoffs:** The party remains **inside the Kervalon basement**, at **sealed metal double doors** with an attacking panel. **Hope still has her flesh fingers**, the **shaft is unsafe**, the **mind flayer's original nature is unresolved**, the **machine has not been inspected**, and **Follow and Rorf remain dead from E066**.
