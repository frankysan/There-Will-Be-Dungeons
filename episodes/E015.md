## Important cross-episode corrections

- **Named participants, not generic types:** The giant's name is presented as **Orp**, with frequent `Gorp`/`Gorb` slips and SRT variation. **Tylander Sullivan** is supplied by the **E015 GM recap** and contextual dialogue; `Robert` was a name under which the suspected magician was held/known in town in E014. Treat alias linkage as **GM-sourced**, exact spelling **audio-pending**.
- **Death versus resurrection:** E015 explicitly calls Tylander a **corpse** after Hope/Vorel's killing. E016 later provides **new evidence he appears active again**. Report the observed E015 killing *and* separately the E016 aftermath; never silently erase either.
- **Deed:** Tylander's purported deed **disappears as smoke**. A candidate prop appearing in a scene is not legal proof or a recoverable inventory item.
- **Tylander and the slave trade:** **Lady Saltbubble** says he fought/opposed slavery and Principle interests. This does not exonerate his apparent murder/ritual offenses, nor establish the true allegiance of the **Liberators**, **Red Scarlet**, or **Lactid Cartel**.
- **Cyclops:** Orp is **not killed**, is briefly incapacitated, and leaves as companion/danger in E015. His presence does not confirm he'll remain loyal, safe, or controllable.
- **Sheriff Randy:** Opening GM recap explicitly says he was **killed by Orp** after firing upon him during **E014**; distinguish **E014 acted event**, **E015 retrospective clarification** and earlier provisional notes.
- **Episode boundary:** E016 begins by revisiting the necklace and explaining the separated party missions. E015 ends *before* any trade-pass interception and *before* any reported resurrection.
