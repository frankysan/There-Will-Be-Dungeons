## Key findings and caveats

- **Offscreen Slavetown robbery:** The stranger *says* Jolt's crew robbed an Oilbeard safe using the confusion of the **earlier Slavetown fire**. Vorel **receives valuables** in E037, but other heist claims remain the contact's testimony; do not reinterpret the E035 Trashburg fire as the Slavetown event.
- **Secret meeting and stash:** The loot is **Vorel's personal hidden trunk** (not necessarily a fully accessible party purse), and **Bok Bok guards nearby**. The contact's offer of future cooperation is **not a new signed pact** or a completed mission.
- **Angel Ashi:** Her family/Teet history and theory that dragon metal enables elven transport remain **her statements**. Creation of a flower is physically performed in play. Her temporary influence on Nash's body does **not** create a permanent new dragon-heart rule.
- **No Teet victory:** This episode is aftermath/secret-character scenes; the **Blood Ball game**, **royal wedding**, **Toe Ripper leadership assassination** and **pixie rescue** are not completed.
- **Chronology:** E036 finishes with Angel Ashi following Nash upstairs; **their private conversation and next morning occur E037**, not E036.
