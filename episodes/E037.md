## Evidence and continuity rules

- **The full E037 continues long after the Angel Ashi morning:** Charlemagne's evacuation, **Tedna's letter**, Monsoon's first briefing, the Twenty-Sixers' armory and the **rocket attack** all happen here. Do not misdate these to E038.
- **Stashed loot:** Vorel actually **buries** the Jolt-related trunk far outside Trashburg, accompanied by Bok Bok. Its exact location, contents/value and disclosure to the others remain uncertain. The **Slavetown safe raid** is described by Jolt's contact, not personally witnessed.
- **Letter versus direct knowledge:** Tedna's handwritten pages are an **in-world document**; his access is direct to certain keep corridors but Softhead/Andreeste/official plans contain **further speakers' claims**. Hope does not give the others the full letter.
- **Monsoon visit, bargain still pending:** Before the cave fight, he **marks the Toe Ripper hideout**, shares weapons and requests **Blunt Goliver's head**; the leader is **alive through E037**. His longer political/intelligence briefing after head delivery belongs to E039.
- **Rocket timeline:** The **launch, reversal, direct hit and cave-in are E037**. **E038** starts with the aftermath, eventually **kills Blunt Goliver**, transports Hope for surgery and amputations. Her cybernetic recovery begins E039.
- **Show-era item authority:** GM describes the item provisionally indexed as **Mawstorm** (ASR `Ma Storm`) as a homebrew chainsaw/greatsword needing a custom item; **not official D&D inventory text**. Elven portal lore and Monsoon's historical claims remain attributed.
