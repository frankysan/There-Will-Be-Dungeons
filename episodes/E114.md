## Source cutoff

**Angelashi survives childbirth and has four living changeling children, two girls and two boys.** Three initially resemble **a fork, a smoking pipe and a boot**, then visibly change into babies during a family embrace. The fourth is born in baby form. **Angelashi drops her elven appearance and reveals a gray-skinned changeling form.** She tells Nash he is a changeling too; **he protests that his dragon heart explains his only known transformation**. Her assertion is not a demonstrated new shapechanging power for Nash or a settled account of his ancestry.

**Stanley, Vorel and the elves repel an undead boarding attack and the ship actually disengages.** Stanley kills **Fluffy**, Vorel forces a giant skeleton back into a boarding tube, and the giant is subsequently swept into space. **Sensate Philanthalar (?) is killed**, and multiple elves are lost through hull breaches; individual survival beyond the ship is not established. **Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Angelashi, her four children, Chunks, injured Bok Bok and Kristen's new elf doctor Lyrielle/Liriel (?) remain aboard, headed for Elhandar, without arriving.** Squirts is not revived. Hope's separate Sigil party is not played.

## Boarding, screening and a new emergency (~00:00–00:35)

1. **Two different parties, not a reunion (~00:00–00:10).** Kristen's opening recap summarizes Hope's E113 journey; it does not replay that journey or place her aboard this ship. Beau resumes E111's departure with **Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Angelashi, Bok Bok and Chunks**. They experience launch, brief weightlessness and artificial gravity. The GM initially says exhaustion level five, then **corrects it to four levels** from the fast-rest pills; about **ninety minutes of the pills' benefit have elapsed**. Vorel mourns Squirts and displays her ashes, hoping the elves can revive her. **No resurrection is offered or performed.** Their reasons for accepting refuge are discussed, including Angelashi's urging; a return to Planet Nash remains a possible route, not an action taken here.
2. **Sensate Philanthalar's welcome (~00:10–00:18).** An olive-skinned elf, provisionally **Sensate Philanthalar**, says Elhandar has **a council rather than a single leader** and asks the visitors to pass a blue-light medical scanner. **All are admitted**. Bok Bok is unconscious and badly burned, prompting a call for **Doctor Evany/Vani/Avigne (?)** and medical attention. There is no finding that Nash is contagious. Docking is said to be roughly thirty minutes away while the council prepares; **that promised timetable does not establish arrival**. The red wall canisters are identified as **foam fire-retardant devices**, with Elvish instructions.
3. **A surface explosion and private labor disclosure (~00:18–00:25).** The ship rocks, and Stanley sees a screen showing a huge **purple/gray mushroom cloud rising from Earth**. Red emergency lighting comes on. **Angelashi privately tells Nash her water has broken**; only afterward does he announce the birth emergency to the crew. Another shock knocks Nash down. A female elf pages the doctor, whose surname is garbled in both exports. **Kristen introduces a new elf character**, first name **Lyrielle/Liriel (?)**, with golden hair, green eyes, pale skin, an off-white dress, a white mink companion and a rose-shaped crystal necklace. This is a newly played doctor, not Squirts restored to life.
4. **Medical bay and an extraordinary scan (~00:25–00:35).** Lyrielle escorts Nash and Angelashi to the medical bay; Bok Bok is also there, and Chunks later wanders in. A further tremor knocks Nash down. **Vorel and Stanley go toward the bridge**, while Nash stays with Angelashi. An attendant privately shows Lyrielle a scan containing **a baby, a boot, a fork and a smoking pipe**. Lyrielle reports four expected deliveries and concern about the fork. The proposed genetic/pathology scan **is not subsequently shown being performed**. At this point their true nature is unknown to the doctor and Nash.

## The bridge and the breached hull (~00:35–01:06)

5. **Access won with magic, not command authority (~00:35–00:41).** Vorel asserts the party's **Heroes of the Wastes** standing but fails to persuade the guards. Stanley uses **Enthralling Performance**; both guards fail their saves and admit them to **Captain Solania (?)**. A blue-glowing unfamiliar spaceship has appeared and does not initially answer hails. Elves locate the surface explosion near **the Principle's city** and offer speculative casualty estimates, including **half a million to a million** and a broader remark about millions. These are **crew estimates**, not an inspected death toll, proof the Principle is dead, or proof the pirate ship caused the blast. A glowing skeleton in pirate dress finally hails them as **Captain Toesalt/To Salt/Towsalt “the Tremendous” (?)**, declaring he has been sent to take their ship. **His employer is not identified.**
6. **Angelashi's private revelation; a failed spell (~00:41–00:45).** Angelashi privately tells Nash **she is not an elf but a changeling, “like you.”** That exchange does not automatically become Stanley's or Vorel's knowledge. Separately, an attendant gives Lyrielle a magical view inside the womb. **Lyrielle actually spends a spell slot on Animate Objects**, trying to move the apparent objects clear of the baby. **The GM privately says it fails because they are not objects.** This is not a successful animation, extraction, or confirmed ordinary debris inside Angelashi.
7. **Boarding attack and decompression (~00:45–00:53).** The pirate captain orders **Fluffy** forward, and impacts tear open the ship. Elves are drawn toward space; Lyrielle catches a corner, **Angelashi is pulled off the bed but hangs on**, and unconscious **Bok Bok is swept out of the medical bay into the danger area**, not declared dead. **The GM explicitly corrects “lifeless” to “unconscious.” Chunks shelters safely in a cabinet.** Nash approaches Angelashi and casts **Shield**; the GM describes attack protection, not a vacuum-proof enclosure. **Lyrielle casts Wall of Force over one breach**, stopping the immediate pull there. The computer initially reports **blast-door failure**.
8. **Tubes attach; Bok Bok is healed (~00:53–01:06).** Two boarding tubes connect. The captain warns that their usual psychological weapons are ill suited to **ethereal enemies**, asking the heroes to help. Vorel takes a **fire-retardant canister** and summons a fiery sword. **Stanley restores eight hit points to Bok Bok with Healing Word**, bringing him to responsiveness; **no missing hand regrows**. Vorel gives him the canister and tells him to protect the medical bay. **Bok Bok piles canisters into a ceiling-high barricade**, obstructing the door rather than operating the device. **Fluffy**, a huge tentacled, many-toothed creature with eyes on a rear appendage, breaks through the other entrance.

## Combat outside, labor inside (~01:06–02:16)

9. **Fluffy's victims and Vorel's resistance (~01:06–01:33).** Fluffy seizes and tears an elf in two; the upper half initially still breathes, and **the GM later identifies this elf as dead (~01:17)**. Stanley's **Polymorph** attempt fails against Fluffy's save; he gives Vorel bardic inspiration instead. **Vorel rages and attacks with his fiery sword and flame bursts**. The elves' lasers prove ineffective. Nash stays with Angelashi, whose hand-squeezing causes small amounts of damage as part of the scene's comic mechanics. An attendant **moves enough canisters to close the medical blast doors (~01:16–01:17)**, superseding the earlier door-failure state. Vorel resists Fluffy’s Hold Person attempt; Fluffy then **grapples and restrains him**, although its first bite fails.
10. **Undead swarm; a pill offered but refused (~01:18–01:44).** Ghostly cutlass-wielding pirates and crossbowmen pour through the tube. Stanley fights with **animated Diplomacy**, **Corpse Slayer dagger**, **Booming Blade** and a failed **Vicious Mockery** opportunity spell. He **Mage Hands a fast-rest pill toward Vorel**, but **Vorel blows it away and does not consume it**. The whole remaining supply's final custody is not resolved. A declared move to attack an archer is **rewound because Vorel is grappled**; his attacks are applied to Fluffy instead. Meanwhile Nash privately admits to Angelashi that **he respects Stanley**, despite his outward hostility. Stanley is not shown hearing that confession.
11. **A fork delivered; the greeter killed (~01:45–01:58).** Vorel spends inspiration to resist Fluffy's wall-smashing attack. **Angelashi delivers the fork (~01:47)**, caught and placed in a warming bed. Soon afterward **the pirates kill Sensate Philanthalar (~01:49)**. More boarders arrive, including a giant unarmed skeleton. Nash tries a bawdy limerick to encourage Angelashi; **a Performance check makes her laugh**, rather than casting a spell or naming an actual child. Elven defenders kill several smaller pirates and continue assisting Vorel.
12. **Pipe, telepathic threats and the killing blow (~01:59–02:16).** Fluffy bites Vorel, who saves against its additional effect. **A smoking pipe is delivered (~02:00)** and placed with the other newborn-shaped forms. Vorel's flame burst destroys the clustered archers; further strikes critically injure Fluffy, which **releases its grapple (~02:06)**. Nash's short **toot/boot poem (~02:08)** earns a successful Performance result and helps distract Angelashi from pain. Fluffy then **successfully Commands Vorel to “enter” its mouth**. The table debates the harmful-command rule and permits the command, but **Vorel is not eaten**: **Stanley sends Diplomacy into the mouth and uses fourth-level Heat Metal**, melting the jaws shut and **killing Fluffy (~02:15–02:16)** before entry is completed. This is the table's ruling, not an editorial statement of general spell rules.

## Disengagement and the family reveal (~02:16–02:42)

13. **Holding the tube and delivering the boot (~02:16–02:25).** Vorel **grapples the giant skeleton and pushes it back into the boarding tube**, holding the entrance. Nash tries to pull the partly delivered boot out; **Lyrielle wins the contested check and stops his hand**. Angelashi orders him out in frustration, but the barricade and Bok Bok interfere; **Nash remains**, redirected to helping catch the baby. **The boot is delivered (~02:23)** and Nash places it beside the fork. No sedative proposed for Nash is shown being administered.
14. **Cloudkill, Telekinesis and an actual breakaway (~02:25–02:34).** Laughter comes from farther down the tube and **Cloudkill** rolls into its entrance; **the caster is not visually identified**. Hearing the elves' disengagement plan, **Stanley secures his arm in a handhold and Telekinetically pulls Vorel back about thirty feet**. Vorel grabs a hold. **The elves actually tear the ship away**, exposing the hull again; the giant skeleton is **swept into space**, not explicitly killed by a finishing blow. During renewed decompression an elf collides with Stanley; **he loses Telekinesis concentration and cannot complete the attempted rescue**. Several elves, including **Doctor Evany/Vani/Avigne (?)**, are swept out. **Stanley catches a canister and sprays foam over the breach** as instructed; other elves seal the rear opening, replacing the need for Lyrielle's temporary force barrier. **The ship repressurizes and escapes the boarding contact.** No pirate-captain death, captured enemy ship or conclusive off-ship death tally is given.
15. **Four babies, not discarded objects (~02:34–02:39).** Nash **catches a gray-skinned newborn**, described with yellow/catlike eyes, one reddish eye, a flattened nose and little fangs. Lyrielle identifies this baby as a boy. Attendants take him to Angelashi, then place the fork, boot and pipe in her arms too. **The pipe hiccups**. During a family embrace **all three apparent objects change into babies**, and the GM explicitly establishes **four changeling children, two girls and two boys**. **Angelashi's elven form visibly falls away**, revealing gray skin, white hair and small pointed ears. She calls it a miracle they found each other and implies Nash is also a changeling; **Nash disputes this**, explaining his dragon heart. **The children's names, individual assignment of sex to each initial form, and Nash's unresolved identity must not be invented.**
16. **Actual ending and outro (~02:39–02:42).** The family embraces aboard the ship **still headed for Elhandar**. Out of character, Beau says their lives remain undefined and teases the possibility Nash is different from what he thought; this is **not a demonstrated ancestry mechanism**. Naming children and continuing the voyage are previews for the next session. Membership promotion and the **FrogPants network outro** follow; both exports reach the end, with slightly different cue timing. **No Elhandar docking or original/separate-party reunion is played.**
