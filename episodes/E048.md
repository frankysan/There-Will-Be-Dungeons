## Source-sensitive updates

- **E045→E048 George:** E045 **Diontalus kills guard George**; E048 **Stanley personally says he saw the killing** and believes Dion wanted to punish *him*. **“George Style”** becomes a joking phrase during party banter; not the name of a formal ability. A **future gift to George's family is proposed, not delivered**.
- **Hope's royal lineage:** The old tiefling initially mistakes her for his queen but then calls her an **Akmenos descendant** using horn/reagent examination. **Neither founding genealogy, title nor army is independently established**. An E047 private **house-lineages book** may contain more, but it was **not publicly transcribed**.
- **The old tiefling's identity:** The elder/“Dead Tongue,” here provisionally **Nobiery/Nobieri**, may refer to a **house/order name** as well as himself; **do not settle person/house spelling without audio or an authored source**.
- **Kefeli and petrification:** The elder's **Medusa hypothesis** is an inference upon hearing others' account of Andreeste, not proof that Kefeli is a Medusa or that all the stone figures are vampire victims.
- **Soulbroker connection:** Party previously met a Soulbroker in E023–E024. Elder **claims a separate historic bargain involving twenty newborns and an army**, not verified performance or fact that the Soulbroker has kept its promise. **Catoblepraithe/Catobelpraithe spelling remains unresolved**.
- **Prison achieved / partial rescue:** E048 establishes **actual entrance into the keep's prison level** and **Tedna/Bok Bok liberated**. **No confirmed Angelashi corpse, Gem of Tides, Monsoon assault, Jolt confrontation, wedding or mass liberation**.
- **Robe ladder consumed:** The E046 **24-foot ladder patch is now used**; avoid inventory suggesting it remains stitched to Nash's robe.
- **Time / levels:** The party levels up after resting in the old tiefling site, not at the beginning of E046, and the elder's alleged hundreds of years are testimony/inferred physical antiquity.
