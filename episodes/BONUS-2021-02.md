## Cross-continuity interpretation

### Delvers — character creation versus actions actually played

The cast's interest in different RPG systems says little about Delvers's current world, guild structure or next adventure. Comments about potential guests, Beau returning, a guild-assigned wizard and a possible guest voice for Veggie Sticks express **production options**, not future confirmed companions or casting. They explicitly recognize that the newly formed three-person party still needs time to develop its shared rhythm.[^delvers-production]

Scott's deliberate turn-by-turn preparation supports understanding **Krandal's thoughtful conduct** as a performance intention, even though Krandal sometimes misses social cues or forgets rules. Kristen sees Evelyn as an active front-line figure who seeks to shape space. Jon weighs tactical optimization against what a character can reasonably know. These statements add **interpretive context** without declaring extra skills, feats or new performed tactics. Jon's Tasha's subclass idea for **Koh** precedes later revealed psychic abilities; his remarks here alone should **not** assign a specific subclass or retroactively prove the identity of the Soul Knife. See [S1E05](../delvers/episodes/S1E05.md) for actually played level-three progression.[^delvers-build]

Scott's positive reception of the **Pelor** setting is an actor's developing relationship to Krandal's faith, distinct from confirmed communication with the god. Compare the [Delvers Krandal biography](../delvers/world/characters/krandal-splenderbelt.md).[^delvers-faith]

### Wastes — retrospective memory, not new canon

Jon describes Stanley's simple gratitude for scarce clean water and any reliably edible meal against the Wastes' deprivation. Nash's indiscriminate eating and Vorel's imagined gravy boat are part of a **hypothetical dinner question**; only the broader character motives can be used as retrospective interpretive leads. Neither the characters nor a Thanksgiving gathering are performed in this bonus.[^wastes-food]

Scott's concern about Nash becoming a privileged plot center illuminates the player's feelings toward clues surrounding the **Solar Mines, Time Bar and multiple Nashes**, but cannot supply an unpublished explanation or establish that Beau intended Nash to become the sole protagonist. Similarly, Jon's sadness about Stanley's Prince-of-Deals bond is **player characterization** without a new in-world bargain.[^wastes-roles]

Kyle discusses **Vorel's doubts about Groxl** and the unsettling implication of a voice calling Groxl an old friend. He distinguishes Vorel's appetite for glorious combat from any understanding of the cosmic relationship. His potential willingness to let **Mastermind's assassins** come to him is a prospective way to portray the character, not an event during the show's hiatus. **Groxl's ultimate nature, the speaker's intentions and Vorel's eventual choice remain unresolved.** Do not treat this as a new Eye of Groxl title, a revised item custody state or a later performed confrontation.[^wastes-groxl]
