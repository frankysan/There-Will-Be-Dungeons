# E093 — We're on a boat

> **The Wastes · Arc Three · processed-ASR provisional dossier, not audio-verified.** Two unchanged primary sources: [Parakeet processed SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E093.%20We're%20on%20a%20boat.parakeet.processed.srt) and [Qwen Q8/Canary CTC/Nemotron-labelled SRT](../sources/The%20Wastes%20S1E093.%20We're%20on%20a%20boat.qwen-q8.canary-ctc.nemotron.srt), both ending at ~**02:54:05** including outro. [Two-ASR comparison and listening priorities](../reports/E093_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). E092 ended **in Sigil's Dark Below hangar**, with the first theft completed (Stanley carrying Thrun's real-looking pendant, high cleric wearing fake), the crewed ship toured, and mission gear supplied. **No E093 action belongs at E092's cutoff.**

## Most important changes at this episode's actual cutoff

- **The airship launches and really reaches the Outlands.** The vessel is now **named Traveler's Twin** (apostrophe/spelling provisional); its large portal **opens pink/purple at the end of the Dark Below launch rails**. The ship passes through into real blue sky, temporarily falls, then **stabilizes under its engines and flies across the Outlands**. The characters see the **immense central spire with Sigil apparently suspended at its summit** and the sprawling colored terrain below. This is **played transit**, not E091's proposed flight or E092's dock tour.
- **Captain Meart/Miart/Captain Bjart (?)**, an imposing **loxodon (upright elephant-person) pirate captain**, commands the ship and its **gnome crew**. She shows **Stanley the actual distant White Tower under bombardment by trebuchets and a huge dark army through her telescope**. This **visually corroborates a tower and siege**, but **does not prove the bishop's claims about the Fist, locked chest, divine defenses or Thrun's pendant opening it**. The captain **actually places a sending stone on the table** for the party to contact her when they need extraction; the tower drop/infiltration **has not happened**.
- **Mind Master Gerbken Shartlinger physically visits the airship at the hangar before departure**, accompanied by **four Crimson Moon-uniformed attendants**, and says **he procured the airship for Tobacho** through his contacts. One attendant **actually offers Vorel an elaborate emerald-eyed dragon-head necklace**. **Nash casts Detect Magic** and the GM identifies it as an **Amulet of Health (Constitution set to 19 while worn)**; a note on its reverse is signed **Mind Master**, addressing Vorel and future friendship. Vorel **does not put it on**; **Stanley actually takes it, puts it on and experiences the healthier effect**. This is **Stanley's current equipment**, not Vorel's; potential ulterior motive for gift **unproven**. Mind Master refuses to explain how Crimson Moon agents work and leaves with his entourage; his claimed involvement **does not confirm he hired E086 assassins or killed Kanja**.
- **Aerial attack is PLAYED and serious.** Near the White Tower the Traveler's Twin is attacked by **numerous grey winged beastmen (“gargoyles” in subsequent battle shorthand) and two wyverns**, attacking both deck and belowdecks. **Several gnome crew are explicitly killed** (some tumble from ship). The attackers are characterized by Captain as **an army of the damned**; their exact species/allegiance beyond their appearance and her report remain uncertain. The wyverns **ram the ship repeatedly**, breaking wood, throwing crew from deck and eventually causing internal smoke and damage.
- **Nash actually assumes his young black dragon form** (an available in-character transformation/action, distinct from E091's Stanley-induced giant-ape Polymorph and E090's GM-ruled cooling vent/10-foot fly speed). He flies around and **breathes acid**, killing airborne enemies and one wyvern; later **successfully recharges and fires acid across the deck**, killing two more beastmen. **The table argues about whether acid damages wooden planks; don't claim the ship's final failure was caused by his breath**, as the actual cause isn't established by cutoff.
- **Hope is thrown off and saved**: a wyvern's impact sends her hanging by one hand from the stern; a winged enemy **slashes her fingers, causing her to fall freely**. **Dragon-form Nash flies to catch/recover Hope**, and she thereafter **rides him like a flying mount**, firing on attackers with her recovered **Scully**. Their coordination is actually played, not a future proposed tactic. During a later shot **Hope rolls a natural 1 and hits Nash in the back of the head for 18 damage**, then **a critical shot with Scully kills a flyer for 31**, triggering discussion/marking of Scully's **True Strike-on-critical** rule. **Nash survives the friendly fire**; Hope is no longer falling at cutoff.
- **Stanley actually casts Polymorph on a wyvern and turns it into a tiny rat**, which falls from the side of the ship; its subsequent landing, death or reversion is **not witnessed**. He uses **Mantle of Inspiration**, **Vicious Mockery**, **Booming Blade** and attacks with a rapier and shortsword, killing some enemy flyers. He also **maintains concentration despite taking a hit**. **The true pendant stolen in E092 remains the stated mission key; no played loss/opening**.
- **Vorel actually uses Shatter**, the **+2 greatsword loaned in E091**, against airborne attackers. He uses **rage/fire burst**, decapitates foes, and **breaks through the deck** to confront a wyvern attacking sailors below. **The wyvern stings and bites Vorel, inflicting serious injury and poison**, but **Vorel kills this wyvern** with Shatter while still suffering the effects. **No subsequent full healing, return of the sword or death is shown**.
- **At ~02:50 the attackers' immediate wave is cleared, but the ship is STILL damaged, smoking and beginning a dangerous nose-down descent.** Captain sees **thick grey smoke behind the ship**, it **keeps shaking despite the enemy wyverns being neutralized**, and begins to **tilt and descend**. **This is a cliffhanger, not a confirmed crash, landing, rescue, repair or arrival at the tower**. Neither the gauntlet nor an Earth portal is reached.

## I. Traveler's Twin, an old rival and the health amulet (~00:02–00:28)

The four remain in the **Dark Below airship dock**, where the vessel sits on **heavy tracks leading to an empty ring** that looks intended to form a portal. The captain is a **roughly seven-foot upright elephant-person (loxodon)** dressed in ornate pirate regalia, with cutlass, feathered hat, scarf and travel mementos. The GM **provides a usable ship name at E093: “Traveler's Twin”**; unlike E092, the ship isn't nameless by research cutoff. The captain says it is a very fast craft, though her boast about its speed is **not an independently measured journey time**.

Before departure **Mind Master Gerbken Shartlinger** approaches, bringing **four masked figures in the distinctive Crimson Moon wolf-and-moon garb**. **He claims to have personally arranged a ship for Tobacho** on short notice, which surprises and alarms Vorel, still resentful of their BMF rivalry and the earlier assassin attack. The factual interaction here establishes **business participation and direct contact with Crimson Moon-dressed people**, but Mind Master's **motives, the E086 contract and the source of the Kanja threat remain unverified**.

One attendant offers **a heavy, jeweled dragon-head amulet with emerald eyes**. Mind Master claims it will strengthen Vorel's defenses and bears **an inscription addressed to Vorel, with wishes for future friendship**. He will not explain the Crimson Moon's apparent dissolution trick. Vorel accepts the case but is suspicious and asks Nash to inspect the object. An **Arcana check detects enchantment without identifying the precise effect**, so **Nash actually casts Detect Magic**, and the GM calls it an **Amulet of Health**, raising the wearer's Constitution **to 19, rather than adding 19 or granting 19 hit points**. The party considers who would benefit; **Vorel does not want to equip it**, and **Stanley accepts and wears it**, immediately feeling stronger and healthier. This is a real equipment change, not a threat carried out by Mind Master. The halfling leaves after a parting look Vorel interprets as untrustworthy.

## II. A real portal transit into the Outlands (~00:28–00:57)

On board, the crew releases the ship from its rails. **The enormous ring at the end of the launchway activates as a pink-and-purple portal**, and the hull passes through. Suddenly the companions emerge into genuine blue sky, with **no surface beneath the ship for a moment**. The vessel **drops briefly and its engines stabilize it**; it begins sustained flight over **the Outlands**. A giant central spire rises beyond sight with a ring-like **Sigil** at its tip; the terrain below spans mountains, strange colored forests, water and deserts. The captain describes the Outlands as a meeting ground and battlefield for different planes, and discusses trading powers such as law-aligned Modrons and heavenly beings. **Her cosmological claims remain character testimony; the landscape, air and giant spire are witnessed**.

The captain entertains the party with **tea and “sky biscuits”** in her quarters. She asks about why the companions trust one another, their earlier deaths and Nash's time as a floating heart, while challenging Vorel about deceit and the life of a spy. Her friendly interest in Stanley and the party's personal lives does **not establish a romantic relationship or secret alliance**.

The plan remains **drop from high altitude, use Dimension Door to enter White Tower and retrieve the Fist, then escape with Reverse Gravity/teleportation**, still unexecuted. The captain points out that holding the ship stationary above the armies may be perilous and **hands over a sending stone for pickup coordination**. She then shows **Stanley the besieged White Tower through a telescope**: a tall, square white structure above a sea of blackened enemy columns, trebuchets striking it, and distant creatures climbing its walls. **This establishes a real siege**, not actual confirmation that the relic is inside.

## III. Raid upon the Traveler's Twin (~00:57–01:21)

A heavy bang throws the ship and some companions to the deck. The captain braces Stanley. A gnome rushes in to report an assault: **many grey winged beastmen and two wyverns** surround the vessel, and the gnomes begin firing **ship cannons**. The crew take up weapons against boarders.

**Nash voluntarily changes into a young black dragon**, flying over the deck. This is **not a permanent change to his base body and not the E091 giant ape**, and his base humanoid flight remains an independent earlier GM ruling. **Vorel shoves a cask of drink toward the captain**, who does not take it; it breaks and leaks as the airship rocks. Vorel uses a large ship-mounted rotating contraption to entangle a beastman, then bites the target. **Stanley grants companions temporary hit points and movement through Mantle of Inspiration**, and uses an effective Vicious Mockery insult. **Hope fires Scully** into boarders, despite being close enough for disadvantage.

The **wyverns repeatedly dive into the vessel**, destabilizing its motion. Several gnomes fall to their deaths during impact. **Hope slides off the stern and hangs by one hand on the railing**, holding on through repeated violent rocking. One of the wyverns is burned by **Nash's 30-foot line of black-dragon acid breath**, an enemy flyer dies immediately, and the severely damaged wyvern **crashes into the ship**, causing another impact.

## IV. A rat in the sky, Hope's fall, and a dragon rescuer (~01:21–02:13)

On the deck **Vorel enters rage**, spreads some fire in his immediate vicinity and uses **Shatter** to kill a winged boarder, with **Brutal Critical** discussed at the table. **Stanley casts Polymorph on one wyvern (DC 17 Wisdom save fails)**, reducing it to **a tiny rat in the air**, which falls out of view. **Nobody sees it land or verifies death/reversion**, and concentration continues on the spell when Stanley is struck later.

One gargoyle-like beastman attacks **Hope's supporting hand**, drawing blood. She **loses her grip and falls from the ship**. She has no demonstrated permanent demon wings and makes no successful self-rescue at this moment. **Nash, who sees her falling, flies after her in dragon form** and catches/retrieves her; the table then adopts the coordinated tactic **Hope riding his back**, shooting while he flies. The distinction matters: **she is rescued during this episode**, not left falling or killed. A nearby gnome avenges the attack on her fingers by killing her assailant.

Another wyvern **breaks through the deck into the ship's interior**, bringing more screams from crew. **Vorel forcefully breaches a hatch/plank section and descends to help**, rather than remaining only on the surface. Captain Meart fights effectively, cutting down boarders while gnome defenders take additional losses.

## V. Belowdecks poison and the failing vessel (~02:13–02:51)

While Hope fires from **dragon-form Nash**, **Vorel confronts the escaped wyvern** in smoky broken corridors belowdecks, using **Shatter** for severe damage. The wounded creature **stings Vorel, poisoning him after a Constitution saving throw and causing further poison damage**, then bites him hard. **Vorel does not die**; still in rage and able to wield his sword, he **kills the wyvern**. The gnomes rush to support the badly injured fighter. **No confirmed full recovery/clearing of poison occurs by episode end**.

On deck **Nash's dragon acid breath recharges**, then he fires at remaining beastmen, **killing two**. The discussion of whether acid erodes wood is ambiguous and partly table banter; **the ship is already physically damaged by crashing wyverns and belowdecks attack**, so **the final mechanical failure cannot be uniquely attributed to Nash's breath**. Hope's rifle/gunfire proves dangerous to friend and foe: **a natural 1 misfire with Scully hits Nash in the back of the head for 18 damage**, followed by a **natural 20 hit for 31 damage that kills a flying enemy**. The table refers to **Scully's critical-hit True Strike benefit**. Nash remains alive, and Hope remains safely on his dragon back in the battle.

The attackers' immediate wave is finally **cleared from the decks**, but the ship continues **to tremble without a wyvern actually striking it again**. A thick **grey smoke plume** rises from its rear; the captain reacts with alarm. The Traveler's Twin **tips forward and starts descending**. The episode cuts away on that impending disaster, **before any confirmed crash, successful repair or arrival at White Tower**. The **key theft is complete** from E092; **the Fist recovery has not begun**.
