## Played developments and actual cutoff

1. **The new moon remains in the sky; the fight continues** (~00:04–00:10). Beau resumes at **White Tower** beneath the unexpectedly close city-bearing moon and unnatural night that appeared at the E099 cutoff. **Alentris Vayne and the still-closed Zorander's Fist chest are falling outside the tower**; two enormous winged demons and other attackers remain. **Vorel leaps for one enlarged demon's leg** and bites it with *Hungry Jaws*, but the creature teleports out of his grasp, sending him back to the roof for a small fall. Another giant demon **grapples Hope and lifts her off her flying broom**. The moon's city and origin remain unexplained; there is no moon travel.
2. **Hope tries to send her broom to Alentris** (~00:15–00:25). Trapped in the demon's hand, Hope **commands her broom toward the falling commander**, rather than boarding it herself. The GM rules that it moves a limited distance and **cannot catch Alentris in time as shown**; Hope cannot see through the broom. Her dagger strike against the giant hand does not break the grapple. **Three airships then visibly emerge through the clouds**, one large with two smaller escorts; armed figures dive from them. **Do not yet attribute the ships to Waltz, Tobacho or the celestials** without evidence of who commands them.
3. **Stanley genuinely saves Alentris from the original E099 fall** (~00:24–00:31). Stanley uses **Dimension Door** to appear outside White Tower near its base; he locates Alentris roughly **ten feet above the ground** and casts **Feather Fall on her and himself**. She **lands safely alive**. A demon archer fires on Nash's dragon form; accumulated damage breaks his transformed form. **Nash reverts to humanoid form, physically separating from the decapitated demon body**. He avoids the short fall using his magically integrated **flying/cooling underwear**, and the lifeless enemy husk subsequently collapses and disintegrates. The GM temporarily rules Nash shares poison immunity **while the bodies share blood**; that is a battle-specific adjudication, not a general permanent immunity.
4. **Nash hides; Alentris recovers the chest** (~00:30–00:42). Still surrounded by flying demons, Nash **actually casts Greater Invisibility** and moves away. **Alentris finds the unopened Fist chest intact and hovering near ground level**; the fall did not crush it. She grasps it and moves toward the tower entrance, urging Stanley to retreat. Vorel, unaware Stanley escaped, attacks a demon while shouting over his presumed death; the creature's wound/counterstrike leaves **Vorel bleeding for five damage per turn until treated**. The bleeding persists across subsequent turns. The giant gripping Hope tries to crush her, but she initially withstands it.
5. **Hope escapes the giant's grip, wounded** (~00:43–00:50). Hope wins an **Acrobatics contest on her second attempt**, slips out and **falls about forty feet**, taking **21 bludgeoning damage**. She gets back on her feet alive. **Stanley drinks the ordinary Potion of Healing acquired in E098 and regains nine HP**, while continuing to escort Alentris; that potion has now been **consumed**, not available to him at the cutoff. The dropping airship party uses a scroll to teleport near them.
6. **The arrivals demand the Fist chest** (~00:50–01:05). The new landing party includes **two orcs, a hooded spellcaster and a massive four-armed, four-sword minotaur**. The hooded figure orders surrender of the chest in exchange for life. Their action is hostile, **not a verified rescue**. Nash casts **Chromatic Orb (cold)** at a giant enemy and moves while invisible. Meanwhile a green flying caster's **Fear** affects several combatants on the ground, including Alentris, causing her **to drop the closed chest** momentarily. **Stanley resists**, and her compulsion blocks immediate escape into the tower. The unknown airship operators and the already-present demons may not be one allied force; do **not** collapse them into one faction.
7. **Stanley returns the chest and Alentris to the rooftop** (~01:18–01:24). While enemies remain below, Stanley **takes the chest**, touches Alentris, and casts **Dimension Door (upcast)** to bring both to the top of White Tower rather than retreat inside. He reapplies **Mantle of Inspiration** to nearby allies. Alentris and the chest are on the roof, not yet securely evacuated. The last black-armored winged archer attacks Stanley. Nash's **fifth-level Fireball** destroys apparent *Mirror Image* copies and burns two actual fliers, without killing those targets on that cast.
8. **The defenders score kills, but the assault continues** (~01:25–02:12). Vorel kills an enlarged winged assailant he had been fighting, after repeated Flame Tongue attacks; **Stanley kills the remaining demonic female archer by slamming her into the roof with Telekinesis** and **heals Hope using Healing Word**. Nash casts a **second fifth-level Fireball**, killing one real attacker and damaging others. **Hope shoots a giant flier through the head, creating the “misty brain mist” of the episode's title, then kills it with a second shot**; this is an observed kill, not just visual description. Stanley wants to fight rather than withdraw; Alentris insists the celestial extraction has failed and they should retreat.
9. **The airship force takes the chest** (~01:50–02:20). The four-armed minotaur **reaches the roof and gores Alentris**, with a hooded caster and an orc following. Nash **ends Greater Invisibility voluntarily by concentrating on Haste for Vorel**. Vorel uses the additional attack and kills another flier, but his **bleeding is still ongoing**. Hope **shoots and kills one of the arriving orcs**. Stanley **successfully Dispels a hostile Reverse Gravity effect**, causing the levitating spellcaster to fall, but the minotaur's sword attack **knocks Alentris unconscious** before she can leap clear. **The minotaur seizes the *closed* chest and jumps over the tower edge with it.** He has the chest at this point, but **not at the eventual cutoff**.
10. **The group's own escape attempt is disrupted** (~02:21–02:39). Another hostile green commander casts **Mass Suggestion**; **Hope and Nash** obey an instruction to **hold their companions close**, while Stanley resists. **Vorel, Hasted by Nash, gathers Hope and Nash and jumps over the battlement** with them; Stanley casts **Feather Fall** to slow their descent. Stanley then **uses Telekinesis to pull the unconscious Alentris into his arms**, deliberately falling off the roof too, and casts another **Feather Fall** on them. **Nash/Hope's suggestion is later fulfilled and wears off**. The GM does **not** show any character crossing to Earth or arriving safely inside the tower in this sequence.
11. **An airborne spell duel reveals an old contact** (~02:30–03:02). The hooded mage has **Feather Fall** operating on his party and uses a scroll of **Reverse Gravity**, launching the descending participants upward again. After table discussion the GM **corrects an attempted removal of Feather Fall** (which is not concentration) and adjusts the floating positions. A new demon commander attempts **Dispel Magic** on Reverse Gravity and fails. **Vorel attacks the robed mage** while Hope and Nash cling to him. The hood falls, exposing the scarred red tiefling with **gold, skull-marked teeth**, identified as **Spatulo (?)**, **Hope's E090 informant at Daddy Long Legs** who had provided Tobacho directions and whom Tobacho's circle called an infidel. **Only Hope had met and recognized him firsthand**; his current alliance/motive is **not established by his appearance alone**. Vorel **grapples Spatulo by the mouth to prevent verbal spellcasting**. His attacks break concentration, ending that Reverse Gravity; the group returns to **slow descent**. Many hostile fliers with apparent duplicates close around the falling group.
12. **The commander repeatedly loses consciousness but is not declared dead** (~03:02–03:19). Stanley uses **Healing Word** to restore Alentris after the minotaur's sword injury; she briefly opens her eyes. The minotaur still has the chest initially, and his group and the PCs are **both under Feather Fall** while descending. Nash rejects **Teleportation Circle** as an immediate option: it needs a full minute, a surface and components. He instead **casts Thunder Step, taking Hope down to the tower's ground-level entrance**, with the blast hurting several enemies and allies despite **Careful Spell**; Stanley and Alentris remain in the falling group. Another winged caster then **casts Fire Storm**, and **both Stanley and Alentris are explicitly unconscious** after the damage. **This supersedes the earlier revival.** Vorel survives and remains active, still bleeding; the wider ground defenders shout to retreat inside.
13. **Vorel actually opens the Fist chest and removes its contents** (~03:19–03:23). With the minotaur fighting surrounding demons, **the closed levitating chest is now floating near him**, no longer physically held. Vorel, still **Hasted**, releases Spatulo and **searches unconscious Stanley's belongings**, **takes the stolen medallion/key**, reaches the chest and **fits that key into its receptacle**. The key **snaps into position**, luminous panels unfold, and the **Fist is exposed for the first time**. **Inside the gauntlet is a severed hand.** Vorel **picks up the entire Fist with the hand still inside**, but **does not put the gauntlet on, remove the hand, strike with it, return it to the minotaur or hand it to Tobacho**. The medallion is last **socketed in the opened container**; no later removal is shown.
14. **Private summons for Hope; uncertain effect on Vorel** (~03:22–03:24). The GM asks everyone **except Hope** to remove headphones and privately narrates **Hope** becoming focused on Vorel carrying the Fist and hearing a voice invite an encounter (“now is the time that we meet”). This is **private information for Hope**, consistent with but not proof of the identity of the hostile voice she previously heard; it must **not** be written as shared party knowledge. After recalling the cast, the GM describes **Vorel** holding the Fist and experiencing **dark whispers attempting to corrupt him**. **That is a separate stated effect**, not license to assign Hope's exact private message to Vorel or declare corruption successful. The episode **cuts off immediately here**, mid-air amid competing forces; do not import any outcome from E101 or later bonuses.
