## Two distinct chronologies

1. **Release/disclosure chronology:** List as **E013.5**, after E013 and before E014, because this is when listeners receive the extra scene and its explicit statements about Hope's objective and Vorel's traditions.
2. **Story chronology:** The Hope/Vorel dialogue is set during **E008–E009** while the other two PCs were underground, not after the E013 hotel/cyclops cliffhanger. This is a **separately published reconstruction/additional scene**; avoid pretending it appeared in the original E008/E009 episode SRT.
3. **Retrospective recap:** Stanley's summary is *spoken later* and can contain compressed casualty numbers (`24` versus earlier reported `25`), judgments about identities, story simplifications and character misunderstandings. Compare with direct episode material before correcting an earlier claim.

## Editorial priorities

- **Time-travel quest:** Explicit **Hope character claim/goal**, not proof dragons actually entered through the shown constellation, that the pact was historically real/unmade, or that a dragon engine can transport people to the past. This segment **adds a major motivation** absent from prior early-episode notes.
- **Vorel/dragon stories:** Preserve reported beliefs, including `Exantos` constellation and alleged lizardfolk breeding, with **character-claim** label. Do not add as objective setting truth.
- **Old man:** Stanley says he died during the **E010 Rusty Cage**; verify the original match audio and determine who directly witnessed the death. Do not treat his appearance in E010 as survival through the match.
- **Eddy and Chance:** The recap's `didn't make it` means **did not emerge with Nash and Stanley**; E009's out-of-character assurance rules out unqualified death entries.
- **Jets/backpacks:** Note the jetpack as introduced in this **extra scene**; do not silently backdate a tested flight or inventory item to E008 regular-recording knowledge.
