## Continuity and provenance

- **Mine exit achieved**, Dorothy's destruction **not** proven; her still-speaking voice makes “door burned down and killed” unsupported. **A pit fall is rescued**, not an E025 character death.
- **Nash's dragon power** is demonstrated a second time, but he **suffers serious mechanical costs**. Do not backport a later higher-level black-dragon form or casually treat his carrying capacity as settled rules.
- **Scrolly's treatment** deserves an explicit ethical/action record: **interrogation, healing and renewed violence before throwing him into the shaft**. No euphemistic “questioned and dispatched” or unstated moral justification.
- **The locked captives** include recognizable **Bok Bok**; identities of the other occupants and why they arrived here are explored **next episode**.
- **Fate 4** remains offscreen during E025 (its return is explained E026). The escape route is **dragon flight**, not a functioning elevator or the replacement wagon.
- The dragon-fire attack does not prove the **Oilbeard organization** destroyed; at least **one armed dwarf flees**. Precisely tallying the upper mine fatalities needs original combat checks.
