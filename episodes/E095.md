# E095 — A Cloudy Day

This episode follows **E094's actual White Tower entry, reprieve, returned weapons and separated Fist/chest key**. **No numbered episode later than E095 is assumed** from bonus teasers.

## Played changes and endpoint

- **A lethal, rapidly spreading illness actually develops overnight inside White Tower.** All four PCs sleep in their assigned quarters. The GM has **each roll eight Constitution saves privately**; **Hope wakes perfectly rested and symptom-free**, while **Stanley, Nash and Vorel do not benefit from a long rest**, wake feverish, burning in nose/mouth and aching, and suffer increasingly strange bodily and bowel symptoms. **Nash actually defecates a painfully hot substance into a spare storage chest, taking 6 fire damage, closes/locks it and pockets its key**. That locked dirty chest is **not** the hexagonal Fist chest and **not** Stanley's pendant bag. **Vorel, despite fire resistance, is still afflicted** and fights urgent bowel symptoms. **Stanley actually uses Lesser Restoration repeatedly**, which **reduces disease stages or symptoms but does not cure the source permanently**; later he casts it at a higher slot level. **Hope remains apparently unaffected throughout**; no proof of universal lifetime immunity.
- **Knight Commander Alentris/Alentris (?) finally named on-air in E095**, previously a personal-name-uncertain female commander with strawberry hair. She is now seriously ill and **reports four deaths among White Tower defenders overnight** and multiple battalions affected. **She believes that Hope may have carried the plague from the besiegers**. **Hope truthfully discloses** that the mouth-faced sorceress in the demon/devil camp **blew magical dust into her face while changing her alignment in E094**. **The commander orders Hope arrested** as a suspected carrier; **Stanley successfully persuades her to release Hope into his custody for cure-finding** (22 Persuasion roll; the commander explicitly reserves **trial/reckoning later**). The commander asks them to **find a cure**, and threatens to make a last stand and prevent defenders escaping if no cure can be found. **This is reported suspicion of delivery through Hope, not independently observed etiology or proof she intended the deaths**.
- The party consults **Archbishop/High Cleric Crudmund Hordweld/Hordveld (?)**, a very elderly, ill, nearly blind elf within White Tower. After Stanley offers a prayer, his memory produces an **old precedent from ~500 years ago at the Battle of Rabidal/Rabadol Ridge**: tiefling warlocks allegedly made an **undead, curse-bound microscopic virus**, whose **tiefling carriers were not themselves symptomatic**. He conjectures **the current plague resembles that old weapon**, and **directs them to his own journal in the library beneath the tower**, looking for an entry he names **“Incubatrix”**. **This is the cleric's historical testimony and clinical hypothesis**, not proof that the present disease has been identified or that Hope consciously introduced it.
- **Sir Daniel**, a blonde paladin, actually **intercepts them on the staircase** and begs to escape with them to reach **his wife and child Clarice (?)**. Stanley refuses because an infected person escaping could infect others, saying **cure everyone first**. There is **no played execution of Daniel, actual desertion, or reunion with his family**. The party hears **sword fighting on a level above the library** but **does not intervene or determine who fought or died**; Stanley and Vorel argue about violence versus triage.
- In the vast **lower library** they encounter **several black, opaque clouds**. **Nash's Arcana suggests their nature is strange/not conventionally natural**, **Stanley's Dispel Magic has no visible effect**, **Vorel lights a brazier and throws a burning chair into a cloud** (it vanishes from sight), and Stanley squeezes between patches. **An emaciated, disoriented man with bleeding eyes emerges from a cloud**, babbles about “Denise,” **vomits blood and dies**, and his body **turns into a new black cloud**. This transformation is **actually observed**: **the black clouds can be a manifestation of the deadly disease or its dead**, though there is no verified mechanism, no inventory of their victims and no demonstration that every cloud is an individual person.
- **Hope actually enters the clouds without apparent illness**; they block vision, but she feels no immediate harm. **Privately she hears the unidentified deep voice from the E094 mental command again**, urging her to **bring the Fist to her “new master”** and saying **all inside White Tower will die soon**. The voice tells her friends are unimportant. **This supports malicious orchestration as an inference**, but **the speaker and specific controller are not identified**, and she **does not share the private conversation with the others**. She continues searching the library.
- **Hope searches the personal-journal section behind the clouds**, throwing many books into the illuminated area. **Stanley uses a Dimension Door scroll to rejoin her**; the two recover the slim volume **“Musings of Hordweld,”** associated with the cleric. They encounter a diagram titled **“cure to shale disease”** showing **rocking/cradling a sufferer while a Celestial-language incantation is spoken and broth administered**. **The cleric earlier said to look for “Incubatrix,” a DIFFERENT name**. **Stanley and Hope notice that mismatch.** They **try the cradling/rocking motion on Stanley**, without the required translated incantation or broth, and **it does not cure the illness**. Do not record the cure as proven, completed, or correctly matched to the present plague. Hope's attempts to find a “Denise” journal fail.
- **Stanley takes the journal/page and stolen pendant (distinct objects) back to the cleric, while Hope remains searching below**. The **library exit stairwell becomes blocked by similar clouds**. The three **Stanley, Nash and Vorel actually use Dimension Door scrolls to bypass the blockage**—Stanley on his own, Nash with Vorel—**leaving Hope below**, because she has already demonstrated she can traverse the cloud with no immediate illness. The spell-scroll inventory decreases, but the exact distribution of all 8 supplied E092 scrolls over E094 and E095 needs an inventory audit; do not assume none remain.
- **Crudmund Hordweld is now much worse, his eyes bleeding.** He tells Stanley the **Celestial incantation needs someone who can read/speak that script**, and specifically names junior acolyte **Walnut** as a possible reader. **The text's “shale disease” discrepancy is still unresolved** when Stanley returns to him. He says **animal broth of various sorts can serve (lamb preferred, but chicken viable)**. **Nash and Stanley seek Walnut**, asking guards about him; **Vorel physically reaches cook Gregor and begins helping him prepare broth** with chicken scraps, celery, radishes and water. The episode **ends during broth preparation**. **Walnut has not yet translated/spoken the incantation, and no infected person is cured**. **Hope is still elsewhere in the library**; no confirmed rejoining or successful ascent at cutoff.
- **White Tower remains under siege; Zorander's Fist is still inside the unopened Knight Commander-guarded hexagonal chest according to her earlier testimony.** Stanley explicitly **checks his bag and confirms Thrun's key medallion is still there** before the investigation; no later theft/transfer is shown. **No relic extraction, Mount Celestia transfer, Earth portal, permanent cure, source-of-plague confirmation or actual kill order carried out**.

## I. A night without rest (~00:03–00:29)

At first the four take their beds in the tower's barracks. Hope has heard a mysterious mental demand to bring the Fist, but **goes to sleep instead of stealing the key**. Each player is privately asked for eight Constitution saves. **Hope wakes from a peaceful sleep**, while **the other three wake unrested, fevered and burning**. Nash suffers digestive distress so severe he uses an **empty storage chest as a makeshift toilet, takes six fire damage from the discharge, locks it and keeps the key**—a separate piece of scatological improvisation unrelated to the sacred relic's box. Vorel develops similar distress, but tries to hold it in.

**Stanley uses Lesser Restoration on himself**, partially relieving the feeling without total cure; he later casts it on **Vorel**, easing the immediate pressure, again without stopping progression. **Nash also feels very ill**, and is not fully cured at this stage. Stanley recognizes the simultaneous outbreak among companions but has no evidence that a normal cold or flu accounts for it. Hope is explicitly **the exception**.

## II. Alentris, the deaths and the suspected carrier (~00:30–00:52)

Hope brings a guard to the sick companions. He, too, coughs and reports **the Knight Commander wants to see them**. In **Alentris's quarters** they find the strong armored woman from E094 now with **sunken eyes and terrible weakness**. She says **four defenders died in the night**; clerics have only **temporarily lessened symptoms**, and she fears a malign plague may wipe out the order. She begs Stanley to find a cure, though if there is none, she would rather die holding the tower than let any oathbreakers escape.

Hope volunteers information: **a mouth-faced sorceress blew strange dust at her face while the enemy commanders changed her alignment in E094**. Alentris **suspects she was the disease vector**, has guards draw weapons and orders her under arrest. **Stanley makes a successful argument that jailing the one symptom-free potential carrier will hamper a cure**, and on **22 Persuasion** the commander **places Hope under his custody** while reserving **trial/reckoning afterward**. **Hope is actually free to help investigate**, though not exonerated. The villainous purpose of the dust is plausible **but not yet verified by an independent source**. The party remains authorized to move in the tower.

## III. An elderly cleric remembers an earlier curse (~00:52–01:08)

A guard leads the companions to a bedridden and almost blind elf, **Archbishop/High Cleric Crudmund Hordweld**. He doesn't immediately recall his own name and mistakes **Nash for Hope** because of his failing sight. He says this disease has a hateful presence and **seems unlike conventional illness**. **Stanley offers a halting prayer to Euphemius**. On a **15 Religion result** the cleric remembers an event **five centuries ago at Rabidal Ridge**, where tiefling warlocks allegedly **killed and raised a plague into a curse-driven “undead virus”**, while **tieflings could carry it without symptoms**.

He cannot recall the details of treatment but says his **library journal** contains instructions. He eventually remembers, with a paladin's help, his own name **Crudmund Hordweld (?)** and the disease name **Incubatrix (?)**. These spellings and purported 500-year history require audio or handout validation. **No one casts a completed cure** during this consultation.

## IV. An unchosen fight and a desperate paladin (~01:08–01:19)

On the staircase a blonde **Sir Daniel** privately asks whether the newcomers have an escape route after the mission. He desperately wants to see his **wife and child Clarice (?)**. **Stanley refuses**, insisting that letting a sick paladin leave risks spreading the plague, and reiterates that cure must precede escape. Daniel goes back down the stairs, alive and upset. On another level the group hears **swords clashing**, but the cause is unseen. **Stanley pushes for investigation of the sickness** while Vorel objects to perceived passivity; Nash tries to ease their continuing quarrel.

## V. A library full of clouds (~01:19–02:12)

The four enter a **vast, multi-tiered underground library** with hymn books, law volumes, religious epics, battle histories, staff diaries and a number of **inky opaque clouds**. **Nash's Arcana** suggests something artificial or unnatural; **Stanley tries Dispel Magic**, which has no observed effect. **Vorel lights a brazier and throws a burning chair into an opaque patch**, learning only that it blocks view. The clouds continue to obstruct the corridors and separate the adventurers; the illness worsens periodically, despite further **Lesser Restoration**.

A disheveled man with **blood running from his eyes** walks out of one patch and addresses Stanley. He seems confused about a way out and someone called **Denise**. He begins to cough/vomit blood, goes still, and his **body dissolves into another cloud**. Stanley directly witnesses it. It strongly suggests **the black patches relate to diseased dead**, though the actual causality and total number of dead are not known.

**Hope can enter a cloud unharmed in the moment**. She uses tactile contact with furniture and shelves to navigate despite near-total blindness. Inside, the **mental voice returns**: it says the sick defenders will soon die, insists she bring the Fist to a **“new master,”** and dismisses her companions. This is **only told to Hope/player in a private exchange**, not overheard by Stanley, Nash and Vorel, and no speaker's face or identity is seen. Hope deliberately keeps searching.

## VI. The wrong disease title and a recipe not yet performed (~02:12–02:38)

Hope finds the **personal-journal shelves**, flinging books out to inspect them. **Stanley uses a Dimension Door scroll to get to her** and they locate **“The Musings of Hordweld”**. In its pages is a **cure for “shale disease”**, a picture of **cradling/rocking a patient**, and **Celestial words** neither can read, while administering **broth**. The older cleric said **“Incubatrix”**, so this **is not confidently the right ritual**. Hope **tries the cradling/rocking with Stanley** but does **not complete the Celestial words and broth administration**; the attempt doesn't cure him. The two also look for **Denise** without success.

The black clouds now block the stair route upward. **Stanley and Nash/Vorel expend Dimension Door scrolls to cross the blockage**; Hope **continues searching down in the library**. On returning to **Crudmund Hordweld**, Stanley finds the priest **bleeding from his eyes and near death**. The priest says **Walnut can read/speak the required Celestial incantation**, and that **chicken broth can work though lamb is preferred**, on the assumption they found the correct cure. **Stanley and Nash seek Walnut** while **Vorel joins cook Gregor in making broth** from scraps and vegetables. They **do not yet sing the incantation, administer broth while cradling, or demonstrate reversal of the disease**.
