## Key evidence / continuity decisions

- **Catoblepraithe's identity as Soulbroker** is directly declared in this episode by the being itself; his true rank, intentions and whether he can be trusted remain uncertain. **Quizquaz acts as a subordinate**, separate from the beholder slain E022.
- **Host/realm:** An apparently airless black city and whirlpool of struggling souls are *GM-narrated scenes*. Statements about contracts, how souls are collected, hospitality safety and omniscience are **Catoblepraithe or staff assertions**.
- **Hope's mother and father:** Dream conversation is **private first-person experience**, not confirmed proof of her father's survival, a specific culprit's identity, or the mother having truly left the afterlife. E024 contains a further **qualified Soulbroker report**.
- **Vorel refuses the contract** despite receiving treatment/guest rest; it is inaccurate to attribute the three signed characters' **extra contracted boon or soul-forfeit condition** to him. **All four** separately reached character **level 4 at E022**, which is *not identical* to the contract bonus.
- **E023 cliffhanger:** The start of a reveal for Nash only. His precise metal-heart mechanics, dragon appearance and personal memory sequence are **E024**, not established by the signing alone.
- **Consent paperwork:** Hotel paperwork for food/services is **not the soul compact** later presented at the throne. Never merge the documents.
