## World/campaign implications

**Editorial arc opening:** This episode begins [Arc Two — The Road to the Crown](../narrative/ARC_TWO.md), following [Arc One's E026 close](../narrative/ARC_ONE.md). The Diptak cliffhanger remains joined to its E028 resolution within Arc Two.

- The party's effort to cooperate with the **Liberators** remains **tentative**; the camp's reported compromise triggers movement. Vorel's conversation with the ex-Koromukishi orc shows agreement on opposing the Principle without verified military capacity or a legally binding pact.
- The **Umbilical Order** is a newly named armed faction with advanced armor and alleged demonic detection. **Sir Ranko says** the order is *not* the Principle's servant; distinguish **self-report**, **actual aggression**, **signal fired**, and **an unknown wider organization**. An encounter with one paladin does not prove all members share his exact tactics.
- **Tedna/Ted** is a **young would-be adventurer**, alive but injured/unconscious when examined after the blast. Do not declare him killed by the vehicle accident.
- **Vorel and Typalla/Tepala:** The E016 priestess/leader and E027 challenge may relate, but the **name, whether marriage or consort status was truly arranged, and whether Vorel ever knowingly accepted** remain open. Diptak's demand is **his claim**; E027 has **not resolved the duel**.
- **“Rats”** in machine text refers to apparent **mechanical combat devices**, not necessarily ordinary rodents. The spectacular rocket/falling components and blast require careful listening/visual-map inspection for cause and chronology.
- **Source correction:** No ordinary episode named 27 in the historical podcast feed can override the repository's **owner-corrected E027**. Fan-art discussion at intro is period-show metadata, not in-world visual proof.
