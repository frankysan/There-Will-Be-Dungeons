## Especially important distinctions

- The **winged black figure seen at the end of E021** is discussed as **Quizquaz** in E022, not conclusively as Catoblepraithe. **Quizquaz, the enormous beholder concealed beneath the platform, Tylander, and the incoming goat-headed Soulbroker are separate figures.** The later Soulbroker's arrival must be placed at E022's end/E023's opening, not retroactively at E014.
- **Vorel's aggression** occurs under magical influence; a player/GM account of unwilling charm must not be filed as a character's deliberate allegiance. He is **incapacitated, not declared dead**.
- **Barrier and glyphs** vary from E014/E020 traps: the tail activation **seals the entrance**; the platform barrier and beams are separate phenomena.
- **Party outcome:** Beholder defeated; the Soulbroker's deal and hospitality **have not yet happened** within E022. The party's level-4 table progression is announced, but later extraordinary boons are a separate episode.
- Working spellings `Quizquaz/Quizquas`, `Catoblepraithe/Catobulpreith`, `Tylander` and personal-note variants are **search leads** until checked by audio/period sources.
