## Continuity and authority notes

- **Actual death vs hallucination:** **P0D, Tedna, Buttons (E049), Dashington and Jolt** are physically treated as dead by GM and later actions. Hope's **steel-heaven ascent** of Tedna and surfing blood are **drug-induced perceptions**, not established literal otherworld travel.
- **Hope's weapons:** **Fox and Scully** and **Smokey's ammunition belt** are *acquired E050*. Verify individual ammunition types and precise per-day limits before transcribing exact mechanics.
- **Gem custody:** Obtained **by E050**; **not already placed in pedestal until E051**. Earlier unresolved “still looking for Gem” claims must become time-qualified.
- **P0D's role:** Monsoon command E049, enlargement, then **Diontalus kills P0D E050**. Her distress, resistance and Nash's pleas contradict characterization as a willing enemy.
- **Party trust:** Vorel's **Dashington-is-honorable** belief may be flawed; Stanley's return and **Pillars of Decency** are proposals/played discussion, not magical protections.
- **Soulbroker / ancient world:** **The Key to Château Weir**, Soulbroker's firearm pledge, old Gem-dependent water system, secret tiefling crypt and alleged Medusa status have different source types; **Kefeli's actual petrifying capabilities tested E051**.
