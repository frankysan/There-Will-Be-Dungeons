## Canon, claims, and political-status gates

- **Angelashi alive and freed E054:** Tedna E048 said she was fed to a monster; the E054 hostage scene and rescue supersede that reported presumed death. **Not a resurrection.**
- **Nobiery's alleged soldiers materialize:** An actual **large, organized armed tiefling contingent enters** and **recognizes/crowns Hope as queen**. This is a meaningful political/status transition beyond the uncorroborated E048 offer. Specific army headcount and old lore remain uncertain.
- **Monsoon escaped:** Dissolves into mist following Vorel's strikes. Neither final death nor sincerely renounced ambition can be asserted. His warning about **Andreeste** is attributed to him.
- **Din / royal family:** **Suck Master Din directly executed** by Vorel under Hope's approving signal; **prince son already dead at E054 opening**. Exact prince/name variants require checking.
- **Governmental structure unresolved:** Melgor/Fula/Relium have distinct provisional portfolios and openly prejudiced assumptions; Hope's willingness to include **non-tiefling companions and surviving humans** is tested. **Do not claim functioning modern democratic governance or a completed census**.
- **Vorel's eye:** **Eye of Groxl** is the **owner-confirmed canonical spelling for the one artifact Vorel acquired in E053**. `Eye of Groxel` (E054 ASR) and `Eye of Grojil` (E053 ASR) are **aliases of this same item**; not evidence of separate artifacts. A differently sourced deity/legendary warrior's identity remains separately unverified. Never turn the eye's pro-combat urging into confirmed moral truth. [Decision D-028](../project/DECISIONS.md).
- **Closing meta discussion:** A **future time skip** and **level 7** are prospective production/game plans, not scenes already transpired by the E054 stop.
