## Reading guide: what kind of evidence this is

Several statements from **Beau as GM** explain the setting's intended mechanics and hidden dilemmas. Treat them as **later creator commentary**, not words uttered in E081–E083 or proof the characters understood them then. **Player interpretations and after-the-fact recollections** are a second category. **What-ifs about alternate choices**, future episodes, rejected character plans and possible kingdoms do not become events simply because the cast discussed them.

**Crucial cutoff:** the E081 GM-declared deaths, E082 discovery of **Vorel alive/Nash in floating heart form/Hope and Stanley's inert bodies**, and E083 **petitioner examination/crypt custody with no completed resurrection** are all different, source-supported moments. This Q&A discusses the presentation of those moments; it **does not play out a revival, a return to Earth or a later political outcome**.

## Cross-cutting corrections, not retroactive scenes

### Afterlife, soul-contract and possessions

**Beau's creator explanation** connects [E023's Soulbroker contract](E023.md) to **Hope/Stanley's petitioner condition in E082–E083**: their souls are **due to the Weir**, yet have not reached it because of the interruption and their physical manifestations in Sigil. This **supports** a theological reading of the priest's crystal diagnosis, but not a generalized rule that each undead-looking body is alive, nor that resurrection will succeed. When drafting from **E082's cutoff alone**, retain what had actually been observed then; make the Q&A's stronger knowledge available as a **later spoiler-tagged explanation**.

**The Eye of Groxl is one owner-confirmed artifact**, written **Groxl** even where the Q&A's machine transcripts render `Grojil/Grozil/Groguil`. Its earlier protective sacrifice and “God Killer” hint **do not identify the killed god**. **Nash's dragon-metal heart** remains, but whether that makes Nash immortal or replaces the person with an object remains deliberately unanswered. **The absence of old equipment on the petitioners is observed and later explained as a consequence of the beam**; do not conjure back missing guns, armor, key or prostheses.

### Retrospective plots versus actual game outcomes

- **No visible fall of Tietkalla.** Beau describes the **Dolo Popio–Andreeste–Weir contract conflict**; the kingdom's actual present status, regency, Hope's mother's fate and elapsed Earth time are **not observed**.
- **No alternate beam resolution.** Beau could imagine Quizquaz as the absorber or the party avoiding casualties, but **the played four-person sacrifice stands**.
- **No new character class, pregnancy outcome, elven rebirth or returned vehicle.** Cast preferences and possibilities remain exactly that.
