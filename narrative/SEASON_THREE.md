# Season Three — The Wastes finale

Season Three begins with the continuing voyages of [Arc 10 — Across the Stars](ARC_10.md), covering **S3E01–S3E03**. From **S3E04–S3E18**, follow [Arc 11 — The Last War](ARC_11.md) for the Solar Mines, the Worldbreaker and Earth's restoration. The played campaign concludes in [S3E18](../episodes/S3E18.md); [S3E019 — The Wastes Round Up](../episodes/S3E019.md) is a cast roundtable, not an additional story episode.

This page remains a season-oriented compatibility route. The entire cross-season story also remains readable as the unchanged [historic first-edition Arc Six](ARC_SIX.md). The [story guide](README.md) lists all eleven new arcs and the preserved six-arc edition.
