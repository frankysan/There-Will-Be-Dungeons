# Arc Five — No Place Like Home

**The Wastes · fifth story arc · Full spoilers.**[^five-balance][^five-archive][^five-1]

## I. The door between friends

**Hope leaves White Tower** through a portal with the hooded **Voice** and **Spatulo**. The siege has ended, but Nash, Stanley and Vorel are left with the empty **Fist gauntlet** while its living Hand goes with her. Nash feels abandoned with his family still on Earth; Vorel hopes she may return. Their own road home still depends on delivering the case to **Tobacho Fleur**.

Hope reaches the **Kaiser Casino**, where infernal fire gives way to slot machines and hotel hospitality. The Voice cuts away a marked piece of flesh and burns it, saying the Soulbroker's old claim can no longer hold her. A smiling host introduces himself as **Asmodeus**, offering the name **Brett**. He speaks of cooperation and the Voice's ambitions. Hope accepts help, but signs no pact with him. His gifts include a necklace of disguises and a copper ring capable of opening gateways.

At White Tower, the **Tunga moon** Nash accidentally summoned still fills the sky. An attempted visit ends with a crashed saucer and Walnut rescued from the wreck. **Alentris** asks Stanley to stay. He refuses, then alters her memory so she believes the real Fist remains safely inside the tower. The true case goes with the companions as they escape the wards and board waiting airships. Vorel is troubled by the deception; leaving does not settle what Stanley has done to someone who trusted him.

Brett sends three employees with Hope: **Steerious Business**, **Blood Skunk Hendrickson** and **Dr. Spider**. With Spatulo also following, she gates into her chambers at **Tietkalla**. **Celibus Stump** now sits on her throne as a chancellor backed by the Principal. Hope kills him with **Finger of Death**, and the newcomers' brutal fight with the guards makes clear that this homecoming will be no peaceful restoration.

Among the citizens, Emenil and others describe missing opponents, a disturbed government and an unpopular railway arrangement. The Voice privately demands evil from Hope. She refuses to kill Emenil or a passerby; Blood Skunk and Spider instead burn the closed railway station. Citizens rush to save it while Hope struggles with the power she has accepted. Far above the Outlands, the other companions see the strange moon growing and saucers gathering around their ship. Both halves of the divided company are bringing fresh danger toward places they hoped would shelter them.[^five-2]

## II. A moon, a false route home, and the price of a Hand

For a moment, Hope tries to reclaim her place in **Tietkalla**. **Omnom Cinderborn**, alive after the disaster at Razak Val, tells her of his failed struggle to recover his home. She rejects the Principal's railway arrangement and announces her return. Then a floating castle appears, **General Carthax** leads demons into the streets and the city burns. Hope gates away with her infernal companions, Spatulo and two rescued tieflings, leaving the people behind to an uncertain fate.

Above the Outlands, Nash, Stanley and Vorel fight the **Tungan** invasion. **Squirts and her infant Chunks** emerge from their hiding place among the ship's cargo. When the extraordinary moon and its machines threaten to overwhelm them, Squirts calls on **Olidammara**. The moon, saucers and advancing machines vanish. The damage remains, but the surviving orcs acclaim Nash as captain. He names the vessel **The Great Angelashi**.

Reaching Sigil requires a peculiar ritual involving fruit and a phrase. Nash's first attempt opens the way to a monster whose tentacles destroy the ship. The companions, Squirts, Chunks and other survivors escape in a smaller boat. With the servant **Snatch** explaining the sequence, the second attempt reaches a Sigil dock. Nash finds that his captaincy may carry an enormous debt. A freight-elevator ambush follows, killing the innocent operator as well as attackers; Squirts's healing and Stanley's **Feather Fall** help save the company.

At last they deliver the Fist to **Tobacho**. The dragon notices its living Hand is missing, accepts the diminished prize and advertises a bounty for Hope. His promised route home becomes a masked guide, **Badass T**, who takes them through a portal to a spaceport and onward to a distant tower. A gigantic stone face resembling Nash greets him as **Junior**. They are farther from Earth, with no explanation yet for the detour.

At the casino, Hope meets **the Principal**, whose projected history depicts dragons devastating Earth, CHIDS and his own **Lich Project**, sustained by consuming souls. Appalled but seeking allies, she agrees to consider cooperation. He points her toward a second **Eye of the Voice**. Spatulo's insistence that the Hand belongs to him ends in his death under Spider's magic and interrogation of his preserved brain. Hope's group enters Sigil to seek Tobacho, arriving as her former companions are carried away.[^five-3]

## III. Alternate lives, lost lives

The tower belongs to **First Nash**, an older counterpart who says his own Hope, Stanley and Vorel died. Memorials preserve their names, while other impossible lives continue nearby: **Pod** has a family, and **Tedna** lives behind Badass T's mask. These counterparts cannot restore the friends lost by the travelers. First Nash instead teaches Nash to look toward **Angelashi**, revealing her pregnant and fleeing demons with Bok Bok. Nash gathers familiar equipment and teleports Stanley, Vorel, Squirts and Chunks to the passages beneath Tietkalla.

The ruined city holds a real reunion. At the **Golden Sands**, they find **Bok Bok and Angelashi**, along with an **Elhandari rescue mission**. Escape is contested by **Carthax**, enthroned above the dead. Squirts heals the wounded Omnom, then the general's beams **disintegrate her, Melgor and other elves**. Chunks falls alive from his mother's arms. Vorel attacks in grief; Bok Bok carries dynamite into the monster's mouth, and empathy grenades finally leave Carthax pacified and captured. Stanley helps recover Bok Bok. The survivors depart on the elven ship, carrying Squirts's ashes and leaving Hope still absent.

In Sigil, Hope reaches **Tobacho**. He offers a lead to the other Eye in exchange for a future coup: if she gains the power, she must remove the **Lady of Pain** and give him rule. The Voice warns against signing. **Hope signs anyway**, and learns of the **Council of Clowns** in Pandemonium. Another promise now stands between her and the freedom she seeks.

The journey passes through Spider's mansion, the draining apathy of the **Gray Waste** and the ferryman **Hesterbrook**. Passage across the **River Styx** costs cherished possessions: Hope gives up **Fox**, Steerious his greatsword, Blood Skunk part of a toe and Spider his coat. A leviathan threatens the travelers, but Hope diverts it by flattering it on the Voice's behalf.

At the Council, mockery of her grief and ambitions finally gives way to an audience. The clowns say the Eye lies in a hidden prison drifting in **phlogiston near the Corinthian Nebula**. A toy-box compass offers direction, not arrival. Hope has neither the Eye nor a vessel to reach it. Her old companions have escaped Earth with survivors and a bereaved infant; she has traded a weapon and another piece of her future for a lead they know nothing about.[^five-4][^five-5]

## IV. A forest in the stars

The Elhandari ship carries the survivors away from Earth, but pirates breach its hull. **Captain Toesalt's** attackers force their way through the corridors; Stanley kills the tentacled **Fluffy**, and **Lyrielle** protects the medical bay from the vacuum. Elves die before the breach is sealed. Amid the battle **Angelashi gives birth**, revealing that she is a **changeling**. Four shapechanging infants survive: **Fork, Boot, Pipe and Nash Junior**. Nash faces fatherhood and his wife's hidden identity together.

The ship reaches **Elhandar**, a forest enclosed beneath an orbital dome. **Admiral Chrysorius** conducts the company to the **High Elf**, who recognizes Stanley as **Valiir Or'Nath**, son of a scientist who went to Earth. Stanley refuses an offered intrusion into his memories. The Principal then appears, claiming responsibility for a vast explosion and his own rise to demigodhood. Privately, he warns Stanley that an old enemy may have survived.

At **Villa Or'Nath**, Stanley's father's cottage, the company begins recovering. Nash and Angelashi reconcile, and Bok Bok agrees to help raise Chunks. Four days relieve the delayed cost of the restorative pills taken during the rescue. News that **Omnom and Emenil** survived offers another connection to home, though Hope remains missing.

Her search has stalled at the Council cavern. Blood Skunk sacrifices an ear to recharge her ring, but an attempt to seize a ship at the Sigil docks turns lethal and fails. Hope retreats to Spider's infernal laboratory. Neither Brett nor the summoned Principal can locate the prison. After Blood Skunk gives his other ear, Hope calls Nash through a Gate. He comes to her in Hell, then brings her into Elhandar. Her three infernal companions remain behind.

**Stanley resents her departure and fears the Hand**, while Nash and Vorel insist on hearing her. Among Tietkalla's refugees, Hope admits that pursuit of her power endangered them. She declines a queen's chair and names their new settlement **the Harbor**. A fiery figure calling itself **Abegir** offers encouragement during an ash rite. Reunion begins without erasing blame: Emenil's declaration of love leaves Stanley unable to promise a relationship, and Omnom confronts Hope over their ruined homes. Vorel refuses his wish to die and invites him hunting.

Meanwhile, the young elf **Dilo** seeks Stanley as a teacher. A key from Stanley's father opens the cottage's hidden study, yielding the **Book of Adjacencies**, a dark mask and a letter linking Nash's heart to older mysteries. Answers and danger have arrived together.[^five-birth][^five-6][^five-7]

## V. The stranger in the mountain

A hunt offers the companions a brief chance at ordinary life. Baby **Pipe**, feared missing, is found laughing in Nash's pocket. **Dilo** joins Stanley as a hopeful student, and **Omnom** accompanies Vorel. Yet Stanley remembers the Principal's warning and fears **Diontalus** may have survived. He shares that suspicion with Vorel before the group follows Lyrielle's map into the forest.

The quarry is **Chester**, an antlered bear–dinosaur creature. Stanley becomes a tyrannosaur, Nash hastens Vorel, and the fight drives the beast into a tree. Hope pulls it down by its entrails; its death pins her until Vorel lifts it clear. Omnom praises the Great Fang. For a moment, the hunt seems capable of healing old anger.

Talking deer then offer a lead toward glowing mushrooms. Stanley breaks their disguise, exposing **six armed mages**. During the battle Dilo appears to help, then signals the attackers. Hope is shut inside a floating blue sphere, and **Dilo escapes with her prison wand** while Stanley kills the mages. The others follow the glowing captive to a mountain cave, where the supposed pupil demands Stanley's body or Hope and the Hand.

**Diontalus** has returned. He claims the Principal helped him escape, then emerges as a separate blue apparition when Stanley attacks Dilo's body. His scythe drains strength, roots bind the company, and Vorel spends Omnom's fireball necklace in a blast that burns friends as well as enemies. **Chain lightning kills Omnom**, leaving only his feet. A huge scarred **black dragon** bursts from the cave and drives Diontalus away through the rock. Stanley and Nash collapse; Vorel stabilizes Stanley, while Hope remains helpless in the sphere.

The dying dragon heals the survivors and recognizes his mother's signature in **Nash's manufactured heart**. At Nash's invocation, the engine opens and a luminous mother dragon appears to greet her son. The stranger speaks of ancient resistance, the Principal and the Weir. He proposes a **Rubistic Triangulosphere**, built around **his own natural dragon-metal heart** and placed at Earth's hottest point, as a way to seal the world.

When the dragon dies, Stanley finally releases Hope with the wand. Vorel extracts the enormous heart, and the companions hide it under earth near a black spike. They emerge together and find shelter at the **Gatherers' Lodge**, grieving Omnom and carrying a plan whose maker they do not yet know how to find.[^five-8][^five-9][^five-10][^five-11]

## VI. The price of restoring a world

At the **Gatherers' Lodge**, the dragon's proposed machine acquires a smaller name: **Ruby**. Its natural heart remains hidden in the mountain, far too heavy to carry safely. The companions can name what they hope to build without knowing whether it will work or who can construct it. The lodge offers food, beds and a speeder, but no simple answer to the war beyond its doors.

Beside the vehicle, **Hope and Stanley share beers and an overdue conversation**. Stanley admits that his own bargain to save friends leaves him little ground for condemning hers. He apologizes and welcomes her back. Hope believes removing the Hand could kill her; accepting her company does not mean either of them trusts its patron. Her offer to search magically for Stanley's father remains untried, while the book he inherited leaves them wondering about other possible histories.

Rest does not heal the strength Diontalus stole from **Stanley and Vorel**. The company returns to **the Harbor**, delivers mushroom samples to Nobieri and warns the refugees that the archfey may come again. They resolve to draw danger away rather than bring another enemy to the settlement. **Emenil** leads them into the lake for a rite of **Malhya, the Fishbird**.

Fish flakes let them breathe beneath an ocean without visible end. After a kraken passes, a joyous winged fish answers the prayers. Stanley and Nash briefly vanish into darkness, then return alive. At Emenil's appeal, **Malhya heals Stanley and Vorel's injuries**. The visitor recognizes the danger of the **Eye of Groxl** and suggests an engineer beyond the gods' easy reach, perhaps known to Elhandar's High Elf. The route to Ruby begins to look like a search they can undertake together.

Back at **Villa Or'Nath**, Nash embraces Angelashi and the children, and the household prepares to travel. Lyrielle guides them along **Rainbow Road** to the station's core. While the family waits at reception, the High Elf summons **Diontalus**. The Voice privately orders Hope to kill him and seize his power; she refuses twice.

Stanley instead wins a dangerous agreement. Diontalus promises to return to his **Feywild court**, wait for Stanley and help against the Principal, threatening the elves if Stanley fails to come. He gives Stanley a wooden ocarina said to summon him. The High Elf names the missing engineer **Tungsten Wheel** and supplies teleportation glyphs for Elhandar and the **Mars terraforming platform**, where a contact may know his whereabouts. Before the party can pursue that lead, war reaches the station.[^five-12][^five-13]

## VII. The fall of Elhandar

A **castle and airship fleet** attack Elhandar. A purple-robed **three-headed mercenary** brings troops into the High Elf's chamber, demanding **Hope and Vorel**. He names the **Mind Master** as employer and says Tobacho has been betrayed to the Lady of Pain. Stanley answers with magic; the High Elf turns trees and cannonfire against the invaders. **Diontalus joins the defense**, making good on his promise of help as the station begins to fall.

The companions race to **Villa Or'Nath**. A failing elevator, falling dome and the exposed **Rainbow Road** threaten the household's escape; Vorel cuts them free of the lift and Lyrielle shields their route. Family, friends and children crowd into the hidden study, where restraints hold them through impact and darkness.

Far away in **Hell**, Brett's employees answer for losing Hope. **Brent** brings Mysteriosa to a laboratory and claims to have recovered **Squirts's soul**. A pod opens and Squirts awakens in a new body, assigned to find Hope. Her return occurs far from the child and companions who mourn her.

On Earth, the cottage has survived. Injured Harbor refugees have also reached its shelter, but rubble blocks escape. Stanley's father's instructions reveal **Villa Or'Nath as a disguised spelljammer**. He dons the dark mask; the house strains but cannot fly before further attunement. The mercenary's burning castle crashes into the wreckage, killing several refugees in a second impact.

Outside, the hunter's **electric guitar** batters Diontalus into a crater and compels Hope and Vorel to fight each other. Nash becomes a black dragon, whose acid bypasses the orbiting spheres intercepting spells. Stanley takes the instrument with **Telekinesis**, breaking its hold, and Nash destroys the mercenary's hovering disc.

The fallen enemy **surrenders alive**. Stanley strikes without trying to kill him; Nash tears off a side head, ending his life. Stanley then revives **Diontalus**, who drains blue soul-like energy from the corpse and leaves. Through **Speak with Dead**, the mercenary supplies further claims about the Mind Master's campaign against them.

**Elhandari rescue ships** recover survivors and the **natural dragon heart** from the mountain. Hope directs the Harbor tieflings aboard; **Angelashi, all four children, Lyrielle and Bok Bok** leave toward Mars. The four companions remain on Earth. Hope tries to charge her ring by desecrating the mercenary's corpse and a dead refugee, but neither act opens a Gate. She buries the tiefling in remorse, beside a refuge that survived while its world fell.[^five-names][^five-14][^five-15][^five-16][^five-17][^five-18][^five-19][^five-20]

## VIII. The Burrow and the night after

After the rescue ships depart, **Nash, Hope, Stanley and Vorel** are left beside the shattered station and the surviving **Villa Or'Nath**. Vorel storms away to sleep in the desert, Hope buries the dead and Nash remains a black dragon. Stanley sits apart with the mercenary's electric guitar. This time its clear notes bring music rather than destruction.

The **Soulbroker** appears behind him. Speaking to Stanley alone, he claims that Hope's bargain has been annulled, Vorel never signed, and Nash's engineered heart meant his soul was never theirs to take. **Stanley, he says, remains bound**. He recounts an old pact with the man who became **the Principal**, accusing him of exploiting its power and evading its price. Now, the Soulbroker alleges, the Principal's pursuit of godhood has weakened protections against the Weir.

His promised salvation is another form of consumption. The **Weir**, he says, learned to feed on life and soul energy after entering a realm beyond ordinary creation. He offers to remake Earth under his wisdom. Stanley still means to oppose the Principal, but sees no reason that victory should deliver humanity to a different master.

Then the others witness the threat for themselves. A **violet tear opens across the sky**, and an enormous black creature armored in moving scales drops into the far desert. Six digging limbs drive it beneath the sand. The Soulbroker announces Earth's conversion and vanishes. A vast wave of sand spreads under purple lightning; **Nash carries his three friends above it**. When the storm passes, all four and the cottage have survived, but the burrower remains somewhere below.

Stanley tells the others what he heard. Hope recounts her own separated journey: the Voice, infernal hospitality, Tobacho's bargain and the prison said to hold the other Eye. They can finally weigh these dangers together. Her ring is uncharged, the Eye remains beyond reach, and ideas for turning Vorel's fame against the Mind Master are still only possibilities.

**Ruby remains unbuilt**. The natural dragon heart travels toward Mars with their families and the refugees; Nash's manufactured heart stays within him. They must speak to **Dunglestar** there to find **Tungsten Wheel**, while the cottage's mask needs more nights of attunement. For now, Mars and the next confrontation must wait.

Nash resumes his ordinary shape, and the friends sleep through an uneventful night inside the battered cottage. Together again on Earth, alive and grieving, they face a world threatened from above and below—and the choice of how to defend it.[^five-21][^five-22][^five-23][^five-24][^five-25]
