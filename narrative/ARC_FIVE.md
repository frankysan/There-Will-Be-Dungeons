# Arc Five — Separate Paths

**The Wastes · fifth researched story arc · begins within corrected E101 (*A New Hope*), after Hope's departure from the party · ongoing**

**Full spoilers through corrected E105.** This remains **ASR-provisional, not an audio-verified narrative**. The arc now follows two separated storylines rather than assuming the same party composition throughout. The **same recording [E101](../episodes/E101.md)** contains the end of [Arc Four](ARC_FOUR.md) **and** the first played scenes here. **Hope's choice to go alone occurs around 01:44–01:48; the stone portal closes behind her at ~01:57–01:59**. The narrative begins at that crossing rather than assigning a fictional E102 transition. [E101 dual-ASR report](../reports/E101_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## The road that divides

Hope has left White Tower through a portal raised by the devils, following the hidden Voice's hooded figure with **Spatulo**, the scarred tiefling who once offered her directions to Tobacho. The dretch ranks close behind her and the gate sinks back into the earth. The three companions who survived the siege cannot follow. **The demon–devil compact has collapsed**; under **Orcus's order**, the demons leave White Tower for the Abyss, ending the attack, while the wider Blood War resumes. The sky is still dark. Nash's impossible **huge moon with an illuminated city** and the **three airships** still hang overhead. The enemy has gone for now, but none of the questions that brought them here has a simple answer.

Stanley tends to **Vorel's bleeding**, shouting for the remaining paladins to get inside. They carry the wounded and the dead back into the tower. **Nash feels abandoned**, saying the woman who was effectively their queen has gone and left them stranded; he worries about the life and child he hopes to return to on Earth. **Vorel insists that Hope has won and may someday return**, not least because the hooded patron restored the **Eye of Groxl** to his chest. These opposing judgments reveal their different responses to loss and power, not a promise about what will happen next. Alentris is shaken by the cost and says they **failed to protect what mattered**: **the Hand**, rather than merely the ornate Fist gauntlet, was the real object of the mission.

The still-glowing gauntlet lies in the opened ivory chest. Vorel tries to throw it inside White Tower, but the **barrier again turns the exposed relic away**. Nash examines the elaborate box, works out its mechanical/magical lock and, with help closing its folding panels, **seals the gauntlet safely inside**. He removes the **medallion key** and gives it back to Stanley, who puts it away. Only the **closed chest** can float through White Tower's protected entrance. Vorel finally comes inside, Stanley shuts the great gate, and the depleted party accepts a rest. The Fist is secure again, but its **Hand is gone with Hope**. No one has returned to Earth, made a successful celestial delivery or resolved what Tobacho will demand.

## A guest at the Kaiser Casino

On the other side of the gate, Hope enters a realm of fire and improbable comforts. A walkway passes near a lake of burning liquid and leads towards the modern **Kaiser Casino**. The hooded Voice stops her and pulls away a scrap of marked flesh bearing an **Infernal sign associated with Kitobal Prith**, who had claimed her soul. He throws the mark into the lake, saying she will not need Prith any longer. The act is visible and physical; **whether all consequences of that earlier claim have ended is not yet proven**.

A black **SUV** arrives. Hope and the perplexed Spatulo ride towards the casino with a cheerful chauffeur who talks of exercise, gambling and fire serpents that leap from the lake. Inside are brightly lit slot machines, elderly gamblers, stairs, and the everyday habits of a resort. A concierge directs Hope to a dining lounge upstairs, where an elegant host in a white jacket invites her to sit. The host asks **Spatulo to leave them alone**. With the doors shut, he identifies himself as **Asmodeus** and invites Hope to use the familiar name **Brett**.

Brett speaks warmly but with obvious interests. He claims long familiarity with the being now bound to Hope's arm, says that being ultimately wants **all creation subjugated**, and contrasts his own preference for negotiation with the demons' appetite for destruction. He asks whether he and Hope might be friends, offering help with a life she actually wants in exchange for occasional information and cooperation. **Hope does not sign a pact or agree to a formal infernal bargain.** She says she wants to see **her kingdom Tietkalla** and **check on the companions she left behind**; Brett offers possibilities but performs neither journey. His description of the Voice is **interested testimony**, not independent cosmic fact.

To win her trust, he proposes an evening of hospitality and drink. A band appears, Brett sings on a little stage, and the lounge fills with patrons. Hope agrees to the evening, with Spatulo eventually returning. She has exchanged a besieged holy tower for a casino in the Hells and a smiling host who may have plans of his own. The question now is not only what power Hope has obtained, but **what her new patron, the devils and her own choices will demand of her—and whether her former companions can ever follow**.

## The moon and the failing truce (E102)

The siege has ended, but White Tower's defenders have scarcely begun to mourn when another company of Pegasus riders arrives. Their stern Captain Skrim/Skerim (?) demands the missing Hand and resents the strangers left in the fortress. Walnut defends Nash, Stanley and Vorel and leads them back to their rooms. The three still have the **locked chest and its gauntlet**, though the living Hand is now with Hope. They decide to take the remainder to Tobacho and try to secure their return home. Stanley sends the successful-retrieval signal; the orc ships reply that they will land, and ask after Spatulo.

At dawn, Nash meets two little yellow, six-legged visitors from the impossible **Tunga moon**, which is still hanging over the charred plain. Their saucer offers a route to that strange new city. Nash takes Walnut with him, but the ship fails during its ascent and falls back to White Tower. The saucer explodes against the building, leaving the tower intact. Nash **actually saves Walnut** by escaping through a hatch and using his enchanted underwear to float, interrupting his descent with a dangerous fall and last-moment recovery. To comfort the shocked acolyte, Nash conjures an image of Euphemius. Walnut kneels; the other knights understand that it is an **illusion**, not a god come to bless the tower. The moon remains overhead and appears to be drawing nearer.

There is an unhappy parting upstairs. **Alentris** is called to the Euphemian high temple to answer for losing the Hand. She expects she may lose her command, and offers Stanley a different life: they might leave the fighting and settle peacefully together. He loves her but refuses to abandon his promise to get Nash and Vorel home. When she demands the Fist be returned to the tower, his anger breaks through. **Stanley casts Modify Memory**, and her failed save leaves her believing that the *real* gauntlet was hidden inside White Tower and the travelers are carrying only an empty box. It is a deliberate betrayal of her trust, not a misunderstanding; the actual case is still sealed around the gauntlet.

The three leave before the order can stop them. **Stanley uses Dimension Door to carry Vorel and the case outside**, Nash passes the guards separately, and they reunite beside the three landed airships. They tell the orc crew that Spatulo did not survive, though the audience last saw him following Hope. The crew admits them aboard. **The ship actually lifts off**, carrying the three away from White Tower with the relic case. No deal with Tobacho has yet been fulfilled, and neither the strange moon nor Alentris's pending judgment is resolved.

## A new company for Hope (E102)

At the Kaiser Casino, Brett—the figure who calls himself Asmodeus—talks with Hope about power, her ambition to become a god and the hooded Voice whose identity he refuses to disclose. He takes her to meet **Pumpo**, a goblin whose village receives food under a claimed bargain to supply its strongest warrior. When Pumpo admits he has withheld himself despite winning the contest, Brett **magically weakens him**, allowing another candidate to be selected. Pumpo remains alive; the exchange's longer consequences are unshown.

Brett offers gifts. Hope **actually puts on a Necklace of Infinite Appearances**, masking her transformed features without removing the Hand of the Voice. She rejects his first destination-programmed ring on the hooded patron's instruction, then accepts a stronger **copper ring** and demonstrates its power by transporting herself and Brett to another casino room.

Three strangers await them: **Steerious Business**, a black-furred minotaur in formal clothes; **Blood Skunk Hendrickson**, a pale, leather-clad clownlike warrior; and **Dr. Spider**, an arachnid in a doctor's coat. Brett admits they are **paid by him and will report to him**. Hope uses the copper ring to create a skull-framed violet-blue gateway showing **her own chambers in Tietkalla**. She **crosses with all three**, and the gate closes. The city's condition is still unseen at this cutoff.

## Returning to a divided court (E103)

The first sight after Hope's return is familiar: her bedroom, the corridors of the keep, and **Spatulo**, who manages to emerge from the fading portal just after her and the new helpers. He grabs her arm and demands not to be abandoned again, but she wins the contest of wills and makes him back away. In the throne room, **the tiefling citizens are very much alive**. Her throne is occupied by **Celibus Stump**, the former Slavetown saloon proprietor, who says he is trying to reconcile houses troubled by missing steel and textiles and alleged **Liberator raids on the railway**. He greets Hope eagerly. She seizes his neck with her altered hand and **kills him with Finger of Death**. The guards attack, and Hope's newly introduced entourage fights beside her for the first time.

**Steerious Business** swings his gigantic sword, sprouts a scorpion-like tail when enraged and fights beside his summoned **echo**. **Dr. Spider** heals allies and sends forth golden infant-like guardians whose light batters the charging guards. **Blood Skunk Hendrickson** fights through misfortune, summons a giant constrictor snake and eventually kills a fleeing enemy. Hope uses Scully, *Slow* and the supernatural cold on her dagger. She subdues a surviving orc warrior for questioning, but **Steerious kills the captive by stomping on him**. Dr. Spider instead questions **Celibus's corpse with Speak with Dead**. The dead man claims **Hope has been gone three months**, identifies himself as chancellor and attributes power to the **Principle**. His compelled return to speech **is not a resurrection or guarantee of truthful answers**. Hope tells the citizens to spread word of her return.

Outdoors, Tietkalla remains an inhabited city. A strange **blue-white fissure in the sky** and heavy new rains darken its prospects; Jimster greets Hope and describes the chancellor's rise during her absence. **Golden Sands is still open**, with Bok Bok working. Disguising herself as an elderly human, Hope visits **Emenil** at the Fishbird church. Emenil reports her quarrels with Nobiery, the appointment of a **Principle-backed interim ruler**, disrupted inbound rail shipments, and rumours of a local inquisition and three missing opponents. These are **her reports**, not witnessed accounts of each disappearance.

The hooded **Voice** then appears and demands an evil act in return for continued use of the Hand's gifts: first the priestess's death, then that of a random bystander. **Hope refuses both murders**. Under threat of losing access to the power, she asks her new helpers for a different evil. Blood Skunk ignites the **empty, temporarily closed railway station** with a flaming arrow and Dr. Spider surrounds it with **Wall of Fire**. Hope, still disguised, watches neighbours rush out with buckets and tries to deflect a witness's suspicions. The fire is real; whether this fully satisfies the Voice's condition is **not tested in play**. Tietkalla is neither untouched by political troubles nor the lifeless ruin later commentary might imply.

## The moon closes on the airship (E103)

Elsewhere, Nash, Stanley and Vorel travel aboard an **orc-guarded but mixed-crew charter airship** still carrying the **locked Fist gauntlet**, not Hope's Hand. The elf master **Bubbin Walls (?)** summons them to discuss Nash's monstrous creation: the **Tunga moon**, with its inhabited city now plainly visible and flashing alarms. It appears to be falling or growing toward them, and the master insists they cannot outrun it. Nash admits the result was uncontrolled **wild magic**, and his earlier ride with Walnut taught him nothing of how to end it. Meanwhile **Vorel distrusts Stanley after his memory alteration of Alentris**, though Stanley says he has not altered either companion's recollections.

There is no time to settle their argument. **A squadron of silver saucers flies out of the moon**, swarming the airships. A larger gun-equipped craft **decloaks behind them and begins firing**, and the crew seeks cover. The episode ends as the attack begins. The party has **not reached Sigil or Tobacho, delivered the Fist, returned to Earth, or stopped the moon**.

## A kingdom under fire (E104)

The hooded Voice returns to the burning station and accepts the destruction, apologizing for treating Hope like his previous bearer. For a moment she can believe there will be time to put her kingdom in order. **Omnom Cinderborn** appears in the crowd, alive after the disaster at Razak Val. He says his city has fallen and his attempt to reclaim it failed. Hope reveals herself, rejects further dealings with the Principle and tells her bewildered people that the railway agreement was a mistake.

Then the bells ring. A floating castle appears overhead, winged attackers descend, and fire strikes the city. Hope calls residents to the keep and sends her companions to help. **Dr. Spider** runs along the walls to turn guards inward; **Steerious** carries two tieflings while **Blood Skunk** follows. The dead **Celibus Stump** lurches after Hope as a zombie, still reaching for an embrace. She leaves him behind to warn more people. Streets burn, a meteor strikes the keep, and **General Carthax** advances through the devastation.

Hope turns Brett's ring and names **Asmodeus**, hoping to bring help. A gate opens, but he does not come through. With Carthax breaking into the courtyard, she crosses it herself. The three henchmen follow, Steerious still holding both residents; **Spatulo slips through last**. The gate shuts on Carthax's demand for Hope. Their destination is unseen. The kingdom has been attacked, but the last view cannot tell us every resident's fate.

## A trick greater than the moon (E104)

Over the Outlands, Stanley takes **Vorel and the sealed gauntlet case** into the three-disc enemy ship. The lizardfolk tears through its small operators, swinging the floating chest, throwing his fiery shield and crushing those who remain. Stanley hurls others through the broken window and uses their own heavy gun against the surrounding saucers. The bridge falls silent; the ship begins to list. They have disabled it rather than learned to fly it.

Nash, still aboard the wooden airship, is distracted by laughter from an orange crate. **Squirts and Chunks** have stowed away. While Squirts hides and squirts fruit juice at him, rockets from the moon deliver three giant piloted machines onto the deck. A towering hologram marks Nash as their target. He becomes a **black dragon**, shattering the crate and nearly sending Squirts over the edge, then flies to meet them. Acid eats into the sword machine and destroys two saucers. Stanley and Vorel teleport onto the machine-gun mech, where Vorel gets stuck in its cockpit glass and burns the pilot through the cracks. Nash shrinks the swordsman; Stanley crashes two discs together. The third machine is still charging its cannon.

Squirts has looked at the sky and recognized a real moon transported from elsewhere, beyond a simple dispelling. She asks her trickster god **Olidammara** for something greater. Her **Divine Intervention succeeds**. The moon, the machines and the remaining Tungan ships disappear. Their wreckage and the damage already done do not. Stanley catches his and Vorel's descent with **Feather Fall**, and they return to the deck where Squirts is calmly eating fruit.

The orcs see Nash the dragon and credit him with the miracle. With their admiral reported lost overboard, **Farco** leads them in accepting Nash as captain. Nash chooses **Dragon Lord**, receives the crew's insistence on **Captain**, and names his vessel **The Great Angelashi** after his wife. They begin painting the name. Stanley rescues an orc who falls while celebrating, and the surviving escort draws alongside. Nash has followers, a ship and union rules to learn. The relic is still with Vorel, and Sigil remains their intended destination.

## A captain's fruit ritual (E104)

The spire does not grow nearer, however long they fly. Farco explains that the route requires a portal ritual known to the missing admiral. Stanley sends a request for help to their earlier captain, but no reply comes. A frightened monkey-faced servant, **Snatch**, has secretly watched the admiral's practice. Reassured by Nash, he explains the fruit, their arrangement, the sequence of actions and the final word. Farco hears the instructions despite his professed union prohibition. Nash spares him and eventually renames him **Samuel**, or **Sam**, after a faithful dog from the mines.

Nash lays out the fruit and performs the actions, but **throws the orange before smashing the banana**. A portal opens. Snatch tells him he reversed the order. Nash tries to reassure his companions, and Stanley presses until the mistake comes out. By then the crew has already begun sailing into the opening.

They flee to the remaining small boat. **Stanley takes Vorel and the relic case through Dimension Door; Squirts follows with Chunks using her own spell. Nash carries the terrified Sam through the air**. Snatch had been meant to come too, but no rescue is shown. Behind them, giant tentacles reach through the wrong portal, crush **The Great Angelashi** and pull its broken timbers inside.

On the crowded survivor, the party gathers enough fruit for another attempt. Stanley restores Sam from his frozen panic. There is no window through which to throw the orange, so they go below and Sam saws one into the hull, finishing it with a wooden frame. This time Nash **smashes the banana, throws the orange through the new window, eats the apple and says the word**. Another portal opens, in colors Sam recognizes. **Ten orcs** remain aboard this boat. With the gauntlet case still sealed and their destination still uncertain, the ship crosses and the gate closes behind them.

## The freight elevator to the Hive (E105)

The small craft slips through the correctly prepared fruit gate and reaches **Sigil** after all. The portal leaves a transparent slime on the travelers and their vessel as they arrive at a dim industrial dock perched over an abyss. The harbor official had expected the grander **Hidden Stinker** and its missing admiral, not this crowded survivor. Nash, however, has accepted a captain's title. An official tells him that title also carries the uninsured wreck's claimed **100,000-gold debt**. He gives the **Soggy Feather** as a temporary address, and leaves with a problem his new authority has made worse.

Vorel encounters **Fambo Joe**, the imposing rickshaw driver angered by the Friday meeting Vorel missed. An awkward apology earns him a slap; the insult and the driver's departure become the day's running quarrel. Looking for Tobacho, Vorel next knocks out an elderly would-be guide who emerges from a rubbish bin and demands ever more payment. The travelers eventually return to the rickshaw group. **Squirts charms a lizardfolk driver, Bungor/Bungo (?)**, who is so delighted by her that he carries her as a baby and agrees to guide them into the dangerous **Hive**.

Their route leads to an enormous freight elevator already far too crowded. Dwarves, a gnome lift operator, armored strangers, a red-and-black-bandana wearer, two towering ogres, the rickshaw drivers and the survivors' orcs squeeze together. **Nash cannot bear the enclosure**. The ogres fill the lift with an unbearable stench. When the car stops, a tiny messenger brings an unsigned threat, and Nash finds the roof hatch blocked by old-looking concrete. The passengers have been shut inside a trap. Before they can decide how to escape, one ogre betrays a plan to kill somebody. The lights go out.

The disguised killers draw knives. Some of Nash's orcs fall immediately; Stanley is slashed repeatedly while maintaining **Heat Metal**. Vorel's burning shield lights the crowded car, his protective aura shelters nearby allies, and Nash floats upside down above grasping hands. **Squirts restores Stanley with a powerful Heal**, while the rickshaw women cut an ogre's legs and their lizardfolk colleague shields her. Nash's **Fireball** incinerates several enemies, but its blast also kills the old gnome attendant; not everyone trapped in the elevator is a combatant. Flame, broken metal and the ogres' weight tear open the floor. An ogre falls into the black shaft.

**Fambo is badly injured protecting himself and the others**, but Squirts stabilizes him and heals companions. The survivors eventually overpower the remaining attackers. When the damaged elevator gives way completely, **Stanley uses Feather Fall** to slow those who would plummet while Nash flies. Vorel joins the rickshaw men at a lower shaft door and pulls it open with overwhelming strength. The party escapes into the strange open city; **Fambo and Vorel reconcile**, clasping each other's forearms as friends or at least allies after the ordeal.

Bungor takes them swiftly through the Hive's marked and uneasy streets. Outside Tobacho's establishment the usual frog-on-a-stick vendor is missing, but the guide does not establish why. Downstairs the bar is full of music and dancers. **The large blue dragon Tobacho** sits on the other side of the floor and looks toward the arrivals. They have finally reached the being who promised them a way home, still bearing the **closed Fist case**, but there has been **no handover or Earth crossing**.

## At the E105 cutoff

**Hope:** Not depicted in E105. Her last observed location remains the exit through an **Asmodeus-addressed gate** in E104 with **Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson, Dr. Spider, Spatulo and two carried tieflings**. Tietkalla's siege and her destination remain unobserved.

**Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Squirts and Chunks:** Have arrived in **Sigil**, survived the freight-elevator ambush and reached **Tobacho's bar in the Hive**, with the **case still closed and held by Vorel**. Some orcs and bystanders died during the trap; **Fambo Joe survived**. Nash faces a claimed captain's debt of **100,000 gold** and the status of his remaining crew is not completely counted. **Tobacho has not yet received the Fist or supplied an Earth route**.
