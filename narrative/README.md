# The Wastes — story guide

This directory holds **narrative histories**: readable retellings of the actual played campaign, grouped into meaningful arcs. For factual investigation, editorial review or direct time-coded evidence, use the linked [episode dossiers](../episodes/) and [world-reference portal](../world/README.md).

## Available narrative

- **[Year One: From Dusthill to the Crown](YEAR_ONE.md)** — corrected [E000–E054](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md), concluding with Hope's coronation and the first major arc finale. **Full E000–E054 spoilers**.
- **[Year Two: Stones, Souls and the Great Fang](YEAR_TWO.md)** — the **consolidated second researched arc, E055–E081**: Tietkalla's rebuilding, Razak Val's disastrous succession, Sira's abduction, the road through CHIDS, Dolo Popio's defeat and **E081's shared-beam sacrifice**. **Sira is freed, Chance missing and all four protagonists GM-declared dead**; the Principle/pact and permanent aftermath remain unresolved. The [Sigil reference](../world/places/sigil.md) and [chronology](../world/CHRONOLOGY.md) record the earliest cutoff; later episodes should be researched before crafting a flowing arc-wide narrative. *Inverse* is separate.

## Reading rules

**Source status:** These are editorial syntheses from **processed Parakeet transcripts and existing provisional episode dossiers**, **not audio-verified authoritative transcripts**. No remembered or reconstructed dialogue is presented as a verbatim quote. Later clarifications are dated to when they were revealed; future production teasers and unchosen story routes are not smuggled into the account of what happened.

**Story chronology:** The unnumbered supplemental E013.5 has an earlier-in-story Hope/Vorel scene; the separate [E043 holiday special](../episodes/E043.md) is not part of the Wastes story. Episode numbering refers to the [archive's corrected IDs](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md). Each narrative chapter links to its primary dossier range.

**Scope:** Year One covers the first arc, **Year Two the completed CHIDS/Drive second arc through E081**, and **E082 begins a third researched arc in Sigil with only an episode dossier, not a completed narrative**. Their previous draft history is retained in the [research archive](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/README.md) and documented in the [consolidation report](../reports/SECOND_ARC_CONSOLIDATION_2026-10-22.md). To experience events without future spoilers, read individual dossiers or source recordings in order instead.

**Migration and governance:** [Documentation architecture](../project/DOCUMENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md), [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md) and [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
