# The Wastes — story guide

The five completed Season One arcs follow Nash, Hope, Stanley and Vorel from Dusthill through E128. **[Arc Six](ARC_SIX.md) is one ongoing arc across Seasons Two and Three**, currently researched through S3E04 only. **Full spoilers for reviewed episodes.**[^guide]

## Five completed Season One arcs

- **[Arc One — Becoming the Heroes of the Wastes](ARC_ONE.md)** — Four travelers meet in Dusthill and follow a trail of captives into Slavetown and the Black Mine. They emerge with a new name, a troubling bargain and little agreement on what their growing power is for.[^arc-1]

- **[Arc Two — The Road to the Crown](ARC_TWO.md)** — The road to Tietkalla draws the companions into a struggle for a lost kingdom. Betrayal, grief and the archfey within Stanley shadow Hope's ascent to the throne.[^arc-2]

- **[Arc Three — A World Worth Saving](ARC_THREE.md)** — Rebuilding gives way to catastrophe, and a promise to rescue a stolen child leads beneath the world. At the heart of an ancient machine, the four must decide who should bear the price of saving Earth.[^arc-3]

- **[Arc Four — The Price of a Way Home](ARC_FOUR.md)** — Lost in the City of Doors, the companions fight to reclaim one another and find a road home. Their search carries them to a besieged tower, where Hope takes hold of a power that divides them.[^arc-4]

- **[Arc Five — No Place Like Home](ARC_FIVE.md)** — Hope pursues the Voice's promises while her friends struggle to return to Earth. Reunion brings a fragile refuge among the stars, but the fall of Elhandar leaves them together in the Wastes as a monstrous new threat burrows into their world.[^arc-5]

## Arc Six — Seasons Two and Three (ongoing)

**[Arc Six — The Wastes (Seasons Two–Three)](ARC_SIX.md)** is the **single canonical narrative** for the renewed fellowship after Elhandar, the transformation of Villa Ornath into Fate Six, the search for Tungsten Wheel and the Rubistic Triangulosphere, the escape from Pentel Weiss and Nash's death/regeneration. **Season Two covers played S2E01–S2E12 and ends at S2E12, but the arc does NOT end there.** The continuous story follows **played S3E01–S3E04**: Stanley's *Regenerate*, Tungsten's claim to have concealed the missing reagent, Hell's harpoon ambush, the Principle **physically stealing Hope's Hand and escaping with it**, **Sprout emerging from Nash's regenerated shoulder**, **Union's life-saving surgery**, Hope's new ordinary arm, then the S3E03 shared parasite-induced dream, **actual landing on Mars**, the arrival of **99 alternate Nashes**, reunions, Stanley's **gift of Diplomacy to Hope**, and Elhandari pre-war augmentations **offered but not yet performed in S3E03**. **S3E04 ACTUALLY completes those operations for Nash, Hope and Stanley, not Vorel; Stanley casts *Heroes' Feast*; Tungsten demonstrates the FINISHED Triangulosphere; and the second war council adopts an unexecuted Solar Mines assault plan**. **Arc Six remains unresolved, in keeping with owner decision [D-042](../project/DECISIONS.md), and is intended to continue through the end of Season Three.** The **current research cutoff is S3E04**; **S3E05 and later are not yet incorporated**. No original audio was reviewed; the prose is ASR-provisional.     ## Season One ending — five arcs, unresolved world

    **The Wastes Season One ends in [E128 — Burrow](../episodes/E128.md)**, and that episode also closes **Arc Five — No Place Like Home**. The five arc boundaries are narrative divisions, not five seasons. **Arc One** establishes the companions, their dealings with oppressive settlements and the first Soulbroker bargain; **Arc Two** leads to Hope's Tietkalla coronation and Diontalus's visible extraction; **Arc Three** ends in CHIDS' shared-beam sacrifice; **Arc Four** restores the group in Sigil and concludes when Hope leaves at White Tower; **Arc Five** follows the separated adventures, reunion, discovery of Elhandar and the crash on Earth, finally revealing the **Soulbroker's planetary incursion**. The separate **E043/E127 Christmas sessions** and historical **E107–E109 prequel** do not advance the current main-party scene clock.

    At the Season One cutoff the **four living companions have completed a peaceful long rest and reach level 13 by Villa Ornath on Earth**. **Diontalus lives; Henatar is dead**; the elven refugee transports have **left toward Mars with the natural dragon heart and Nash's family**. The **Rubistic Triangulosphere** is unbuilt, Stanley's ship-mask not fully attuned, the **Mind Master still at large** and the **Weir's burrower underground**. **Hope has shared her other-planes history with the group** but has not assembled the second Eye or charged her Gate ring. This is a **dramatic ending to a season**, not completion of the wider Wastes mission.     ## Reading rules

    **Story chronology:** The unnumbered supplemental E013.5 has an earlier-in-story Hope/Vorel scene; [E043](../episodes/E043.md) and [E127](../episodes/E127.md) are separate holiday one-offs. Retrospective Q&A and *Inverse* remain separate. The earlier research drafts are preserved in the [research archive](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/README.md), while narrative consolidation does not establish audio verification or an exhaustive link/research audit.
