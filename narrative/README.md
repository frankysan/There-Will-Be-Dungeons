# The Wastes — story guide

This directory holds **narrative histories**: readable retellings of the actual played campaign, grouped into meaningful arcs. For factual investigation, editorial review or direct time-coded evidence, use the linked [episode dossiers](../episodes/) and [world-reference portal](../world/README.md).

## Reading rules

**Source status:** These are editorial syntheses from **processed ASR transcripts (including multiple-model E084–E097) and existing provisional episode dossiers**, **not audio-verified authoritative transcripts**. No remembered or reconstructed dialogue is presented as a verbatim quote. Later clarifications are dated to when they were revealed; future production teasers and unchosen story routes are not smuggled into the account of what happened.

**Story chronology:** The unnumbered supplemental E013.5 has an earlier-in-story Hope/Vorel scene; the separate [E043 holiday special](../episodes/E043.md) is not part of the Wastes story. Episode numbering refers to the [archive's corrected IDs](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md). Each narrative chapter links to its primary dossier range.

**Scope:** Arc One covers the first arc; **Arc Two covers the completed CHIDS/Drive second arc through E081**; and **Arc Three's initial narrative** covers the opening E082–E096 material in Sigil, **without claiming the arc is finished**. Their previous draft history is retained in the [research archive](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/README.md) and documented in the [consolidation report](../reports/SECOND_ARC_CONSOLIDATION_2026-10-22.md). To experience events without future spoilers, read individual dossiers or source recordings in order instead.

**Migration and governance:** [Documentation architecture](../project/DOCUMENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md), [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md) and [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
