# The Wastes — story guide

This directory holds **narrative histories**: readable retellings of the actual played campaign, grouped into meaningful arcs. For factual investigation, editorial review or direct time-coded evidence, use the linked [episode dossiers](../episodes/) and [world-reference portal](../world/README.md).

## Available narrative

- **[Arc One — From Dusthill to the Crown](ARC_ONE.md)** — corrected [E000–E054](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md), concluding with Hope's coronation and the first major arc finale. **Full E000–E054 spoilers**.
- **[Arc Two — Stones, Souls and the Great Fang](ARC_TWO.md)** — the **consolidated second researched arc, E055–E081**: Tietkalla's rebuilding, Razak Val's disastrous succession, Sira's abduction, the road through CHIDS, Dolo Popio's defeat and **E081's shared-beam sacrifice**. **Sira is freed, Chance missing and all four protagonists GM-declared dead**; the Principle/pact and permanent aftermath remain unresolved. Eight connected chapters replace the old 26-part draft.

Publication-month/host-introduction conflict requires review.

**Current Arc 3 reading:** [Strangers in the City of Doors](ARC_THREE.md) is the **ongoing continuous narrative through E087**. Supporting dossiers [E082](../episodes/E082.md)–[E087](../episodes/E087.md) preserve scene-by-scene evidence and disputed proper names. **E085 Vorel wins Royce's local qualifier; E087 he wins the overall Big Time Smackeroo championship and actually receives 5,000gp and a trophy.** His **E086 forbidden potion grants opponent-move prediction** and **Kidigree replaces his urine sample during doping testing**; a priestly disc publicly humiliates Kanja. **The Crimson Moon's employer remains unidentified.** Nash **receives 500gp toward a spell key but has not bought one**; Squirts **cures Vorel's persistent assassin poison** after the tournament. The group **sets out toward the temple to seek Hope/Stanley's resurrection, but the ritual has not occurred**; Nothing's Abyss commission remains unperformed. The [chronology](../world/CHRONOLOGY.md) and [Sigil](../world/places/sigil.md) retain episode-specific cutoff facts. *Inverse* is another reality.

## Reading rules

**Source status:** These are editorial syntheses from **processed ASR transcripts (including multiple-model E084–E087) and existing provisional episode dossiers**, **not audio-verified authoritative transcripts**. No remembered or reconstructed dialogue is presented as a verbatim quote. Later clarifications are dated to when they were revealed; future production teasers and unchosen story routes are not smuggled into the account of what happened.

**Story chronology:** The unnumbered supplemental E013.5 has an earlier-in-story Hope/Vorel scene; the separate [E043 holiday special](../episodes/E043.md) is not part of the Wastes story. Episode numbering refers to the [archive's corrected IDs](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md). Each narrative chapter links to its primary dossier range.

**Scope:** Arc One covers the first arc; **Arc Two covers the completed CHIDS/Drive second arc through E081**; and **Arc Three's initial narrative** covers the opening E082–E087 material in Sigil, **without claiming the arc is finished**. Their previous draft history is retained in the [research archive](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/README.md) and documented in the [consolidation report](../reports/SECOND_ARC_CONSOLIDATION_2026-10-22.md). To experience events without future spoilers, read individual dossiers or source recordings in order instead.

**Migration and governance:** [Documentation architecture](../project/DOCUMENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md), [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md) and [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
