# The Wastes — story guide

This directory holds **narrative histories**: readable retellings of the actual played campaign, grouped into meaningful arcs. For factual investigation, editorial review or direct time-coded evidence, use the linked [episode dossiers](../episodes/) and [world-reference portal](../world/README.md).

## Available narrative

- **[Arc One — Becoming the Heroes of the Wastes](ARC_ONE.md)** — corrected **E000–E026**, plus the **E013.5 supplemental**: Dusthill, Slavetown, the Black Mine and the Soulbroker bargain, ending with the party adopting its new name at the Liberators' camp. **Full E000–E026 spoilers**.
- **[Arc Two — The Road to the Crown](ARC_TWO.md)** — corrected **E027–E054**, excluding the separate **E043 holiday one-shot**: the road to Tietkalla, Diontalus, the struggle beneath the keep and Hope's coronation. **Full E027–E054 spoilers**.
- **[Arc Three — A World Worth Saving](ARC_THREE.md)** — the **consolidated third researched arc, E055–E081**: Tietkalla's rebuilding, Razak Val's disastrous succession, Sira's abduction, the road through CHIDS, Dolo Popio's defeat and **E081's shared-beam sacrifice**. **Sira is freed, Chance missing and all four protagonists GM-declared dead**; the Principle/pact and permanent aftermath remain unresolved. Eight connected chapters replace the old 26-part draft.
- **[Arc Four — The Price of a Way Home](ARC_FOUR.md)** — **completed E082 through E101's Hope departure**: Sigil, Tobacho's Fist assignment, White Tower's defense and Hope's fateful bond with the Hand of the Voice. The three companions remain behind when she leaves. Eight connected chapters replace the accumulated scene-by-scene draft. The same E101 also starts Arc Five.
- **[Arc Five — Separate Paths](ARC_FIVE.md)** — begins **within E101 after Hope's portal departure**, ongoing through **[E124](../episodes/E124.md)**. Diontalus escapes and Omnom dies E121; E122 frees Hope and hides the dragon's heart. E123 privately reconciles Hope/Stanley and brings all four to the Harbor. **E124's Fishbird encounter cures their lingering debuffs**; household moves to central reception, refugees stay. **Stanley wins a conditional Diontalus accord** for own-court return and aid, **not yet depicted as fulfilled**. Historical **E107–E109 Dunebreaker** is far earlier in the same setting.

## Reading rules

**Source status:** Arc narratives are editorial syntheses of processed ASR research (multiple models where available), **not audio-verified authoritative transcripts**. Do not silently turn character claims, speculative future routes or retrospective Q&A commentary into played outcomes.

**Story chronology:** The unnumbered supplemental E013.5 has an earlier-in-story Hope/Vorel scene; the separate [E043 holiday special](../episodes/E043.md) is not part of the Wastes story. Episode numbering refers to the [archive's corrected IDs](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md). Each narrative chapter links to its primary dossier range.

**Scope:** Arc One covers corrected E000–E026 plus E013.5; Arc Two covers E027–E054 excluding standalone E043; Arc Three covers E055–E081; Arc Four is **completed at Hope's E101 departure** (E082 through the portal); and Arc Five **starts inside that same E101**. Retrospective Q&A and *Inverse* remain separate. The earlier research drafts are preserved in the [research archive](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/README.md), while narrative completion does not establish audio verification or complete post-arc consolidation.

**Migration and governance:** [Documentation architecture](../project/DOCUMENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md), [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md) and [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
