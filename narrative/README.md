# The Wastes — story guide

This directory holds **narrative histories**: readable retellings of the actual played campaign, grouped into meaningful arcs. For factual investigation, editorial review or direct time-coded evidence, use the linked [episode dossiers](../episodes/) and [world-reference portal](../world/README.md).

## Available narrative

- **[Year One: From Dusthill to the Crown](YEAR_ONE.md)** — corrected [E000–E054](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md), concluding with Hope's coronation and the first major arc finale. **Full E000–E054 spoilers**.
- **[Year Two: Stones, Souls and the Great Fang](YEAR_TWO.md)** — the **consolidated second researched arc, E055–E081**: Tietkalla's rebuilding, Razak Val's disastrous succession, Sira's abduction, the road through CHIDS, Dolo Popio's defeat and **E081's shared-beam sacrifice**. **Sira is freed, Chance missing and all four protagonists GM-declared dead**; the Principle/pact and permanent aftermath remain unresolved. Eight connected chapters replace the old 26-part draft.
- **[Arc Three: Strangers in the City of Doors](ARC_THREE.md)** — **initial, ongoing story E082–E084**: the aftermath of the beam; Vorel and Nash in Sigil; Hope and Stanley's petitioners; Squirts, Nothing and the hidden trickster church; the first marionette qualifiers and Nothing's secret Abyss-linked assignment. **Not a complete arc, a new calendar year, or an audio-verified account**.
- **[March 2019 Year One Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md)** — GM/cast retrospective after first arc, **not corrected E055**.
- **[April 2019 Bonus Show 01: The First and Last](../episodes/BONUS-2019-04.md)** — cast Q&A; **no new in-world scenes**.
- **[October 2019 public-release Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2019-10.md)** — cast/GM creative commentary and separate *Inverse* material, **not a played Wastes session**.
- **[November 2019 public-release Q&A](../episodes/BONUS-2019-11.md)** — GM intent behind Wall Nash, item-rule clarifications and player reaction to E079; **not another scene**. Publication-month/host-introduction conflict requires review.

**Current Arc 3 reading:** [Strangers in the City of Doors](ARC_THREE.md) is the **first continuous narrative through E084**. The supporting [E082](../episodes/E082.md), [E083](../episodes/E083.md) and [E084](../episodes/E084.md) dossiers retain scene-by-scene evidence and unresolved proper names. Vorel survives, Nash gradually regrows parts of his floating heart-body, and **Hope and Stanley remain petitioners in crypt custody without resurrection**. The first qualifiers yield **wins for Vorel and Nash and a double forfeit for Nothing**; Nothing's separate abduction contract is **accepted but unperformed**. The [chronology](../world/CHRONOLOGY.md) and [Sigil reference](../world/places/sigil.md) provide factual cutoff tracking. *Inverse* remains an entirely separate fictional reality.

## Reading rules

**Source status:** These are editorial syntheses from **processed ASR transcripts (including multiple-model E084) and existing provisional episode dossiers**, **not audio-verified authoritative transcripts**. No remembered or reconstructed dialogue is presented as a verbatim quote. Later clarifications are dated to when they were revealed; future production teasers and unchosen story routes are not smuggled into the account of what happened.

**Story chronology:** The unnumbered supplemental E013.5 has an earlier-in-story Hope/Vorel scene; the separate [E043 holiday special](../episodes/E043.md) is not part of the Wastes story. Episode numbering refers to the [archive's corrected IDs](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md). Each narrative chapter links to its primary dossier range.

**Scope:** Year One covers the first arc; **Year Two covers the completed CHIDS/Drive second arc through E081**; and **Arc Three's initial narrative** covers the opening E082–E084 material in Sigil, **without claiming the arc is finished**. Their previous draft history is retained in the [research archive](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/README.md) and documented in the [consolidation report](../reports/SECOND_ARC_CONSOLIDATION_2026-10-22.md). To experience events without future spoilers, read individual dossiers or source recordings in order instead.

**Migration and governance:** [Documentation architecture](../project/DOCUMENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md), [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md) and [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
