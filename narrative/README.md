# The Wastes — story guide

Four travelers meet in Dusthill and set out into a world of ancient engines, lost kingdoms, uneasy bargains and distant stars. The story of Nash, Hope, Stanley and Vorel unfolds across eleven arcs, from the first broken waterworks to the last struggle for Earth. **Full campaign spoilers follow.**

    ## Season One ending — five arcs, unresolved world

    **The Wastes Season One ends in [E128 — Burrow](../episodes/E128.md)**, and that episode also closes **Arc Five — No Place Like Home**. The five arc boundaries are narrative divisions, not five seasons. **Arc One** establishes the companions, their dealings with oppressive settlements and the first Soulbroker bargain; **Arc Two** leads to Hope's T'ietkalla coronation and Diontalus's visible extraction; **Arc Three** ends in CHIDS' shared-beam sacrifice; **Arc Four** restores the group in Sigil and concludes when Hope leaves at White Tower; **Arc Five** follows the separated adventures, reunion, discovery of Elhandar and the crash on Earth, finally revealing the **Soulbroker's planetary incursion**. The separate **E043/E127 Christmas sessions** and historical **E107–E109 prequel** do not advance the current main-party scene clock.

    At the Season One cutoff the **four living companions have completed a peaceful long rest and reach level 13 by Villa Or'Nath on Earth**. **Diontalus lives; Henatar is dead**; the elven refugee transports have **left toward Mars with the natural dragon heart and Nash's family**. The **Rubistic Triangulosphere** is unbuilt, Stanley's ship-mask not fully attuned, the **Mind Master still at large** and the **Weir's burrower underground**. **Hope has shared her other-planes history with the group** but has not assembled the second Eye or charged her Gate ring. This is a **dramatic ending to a season**, not completion of the wider Wastes mission. [Full finale dossier](../episodes/E128.md) · [E128 textual comparison](../reports/E128_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md).

    **Editorial structure (2026-10-24):** [Arc Five](ARC_FIVE.md) has been rewritten as **eight connected main chapters** spanning E101–E128, rather than an episode-by-episode collection. The previous 62 narrative subheadings have been reduced to eight numbered chapters; source-rich episode cutoffs, transcript disagreements and detailed mechanics remain available in their [episode dossiers](../episodes/) and [continuity ledger](../world/CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).     ## Reading rules

    **Source status:** Arc narratives are editorial syntheses of processed ASR research (multiple models where available), **not audio-verified authoritative transcripts**. Do not silently turn character claims, speculative future routes or retrospective Q&A commentary into played outcomes.

    **Story chronology:** The unnumbered supplemental E013.5 has an earlier-in-story Hope/Vorel scene; [E043](../episodes/E043.md) and [E127](../episodes/E127.md) are separate holiday one-offs. The historical [E107–E109 Dunebreaker](../episodes/E107.md) predates the main party's present. Episode numbering follows the [archive's corrected IDs](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md). Each narrative chapter links to its supporting episode dossiers.

    **Scope:** **The Wastes Season One is documented through E128, the owner-confirmed season finale.** Arc One covers corrected E000–E026 plus E013.5; Arc Two covers E027–E054 excluding standalone E043; Arc Three covers E055–E081; Arc Four is **completed at Hope's E101 departure** (E082 through the portal); and Arc Five **starts inside E101 and completes at E128**, excluding the historical E107–E109 prequel and separate E127 Christmas one-off. Retrospective Q&A and *Inverse* remain separate. The earlier research drafts are preserved in the [research archive](../research/legacy/2026-10-22/README.md), while narrative consolidation does not establish audio verification or an exhaustive link/research audit.

    **Migration and governance:** [Documentation architecture](../project/DOCUMENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md), [editorial guide](../project/EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md) and [review queue](../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md).
