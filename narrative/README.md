# The Wastes — story guide

This directory holds **narrative histories**: readable retellings of the actual played campaign, grouped into meaningful arcs. For factual investigation, editorial review or direct time-coded evidence, use the linked [episode dossiers](../episodes/) and [world-reference portal](../world/README.md).

## Reading rules

**Source status:** These are editorial syntheses from **processed Parakeet transcripts and existing provisional episode dossiers**, **not audio-verified authoritative transcripts**. No remembered or reconstructed dialogue is presented as a verbatim quote. Later clarifications are dated to when they were revealed; future production teasers and unchosen story routes are not smuggled into the account of what happened.

**Story chronology:** The unnumbered supplemental E013.5 has an earlier-in-story Hope/Vorel scene; the separate [E043 holiday special](../episodes/E043.md) is not part of the Wastes story. Episode numbering refers to the [archive's corrected IDs](../project/EPISODE_NUMBERING.md). Each narrative chapter links to its primary dossier range.

**Scope:** The Year One narrative reveals how the first arc ends; Year Two records newly played E055–E056 events even when they clarify earlier story. To experience events without future spoilers, read individual dossiers or source recordings in order instead.
