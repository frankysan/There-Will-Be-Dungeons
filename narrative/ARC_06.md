# Arc 06 — Beneath the World

## I. The dragon's warning and the road below

**Hope** remembered the first time she had seen **Fate 4**. In the half-familiar scene of old **Dusthill**, **Softhead** had led her to **Jimster** and the battered vehicle that needed her hands to put it back on the road. The music playing there recalled a world before the long desert crossings and the wreck under a tyrannosaur's foot. Then the memory passed. Hope was not back in Dusthill, and the vehicle had not been restored. She was riding with **Nash, Stanley and Vorel** in the spectral green carriage, moving through a black forest toward the place where **Sira** had been taken.[^arc06-1]

The carriage rose above the eye-bearing trees into a darkness crowded with stars. A translucent **dragon spirit** appeared before the travelers and spoke of a danger older than the Wastes. It recognized in Nash the mark of a great dragon whose name he did not at first know, and said that it had died opposing the mind flayer responsible for Sira's capture and a being it called **Dollo Poppio**. The spirit claimed that humans before the Scorching had built a machine beneath **Khaervilon** capable of moving **Earth itself** to escape dragons. Now, it warned, the enemies were turning that ancient power toward a different purpose: bringing their own realm into the world, its rain and watching trees already advancing, and using Sira to give their master a form. It called the **Eye of Groxl** in Vorel's chest a **god-killer**. The party could hear the warning, but had not yet seen the machine or proved the spirit's history. The apparition faded, leaving them to decide what to do with the story.[^arc06-2]

The spectral carriage carried them back beneath the night and stopped outside a ruined keep dominated by a towering **shaft of light**. Armed lizardfolk guarded the open approaches. Vorel, newly named **Great Fang**, wanted to meet the danger head-on; the others weighed spells and schemes that might place a dinosaur or a dragon upon the ramparts. They chose something less conspicuous. **Nash dug an underground approach**, shaping a passage beneath the perimeter while Hope scouted ahead. She glimpsed an abandoned chamber of stone and heard something heavy moving within. The two withdrew to bring **Stanley and Vorel** with them rather than face the noise alone.[^arc06-3]

When they opened the wall again, the room answered with a giant mass of **bone and flesh**, its body producing armed skeletons from openings that multiplied as it fought. **Vorel's flaming shield**, Hope's shots from **Scully**, and Nash's fire kept the creatures back. It was **Stanley's flying sword Diplomacy** that delivered the final blow to the great spawning body; the others destroyed the remaining skeletons. The keep was not secured. Beyond empty chambers and dark stairs, **Hope glimpsed a lizardfolk guard waiting with a rifle**. The companions still had no sign of Sira or the workings of the beam above them.[^arc06-4]

They answered the hidden guard with a trap. Nash shaped passages and prepared a false route while Vorel called out to the lizardfolk in the name of the Great Fang. The defenders answered with **brilliant pulses from unfamiliar rifles** and sent fighters after him. **About eight melee pursuers** were sealed behind raised earth, pounding from the other side, while more riflemen remained outside. **Stanley transformed Vorel into a Tyrannosaurus rex**, this time in deed rather than merely in the planning they had abandoned outside. The enormous lizardfolk crushed and bit his enemies until an **illithid resembling Sira's abductor** seized his will. **Hope's two shotgun blasts broke that control**. A psychic shock then stunned both Hope and Vorel, and rifle fire tore away Vorel's borrowed dinosaur form. He survived the change back to himself.[^arc06-5]

**Diplomacy pierced the illithid's head**. Instead of a familiar corpse, the visible figure collapsed into **black oil and emptied skin**. The exposed riflemen fell to the companions' combined assault, but they did not open Nash's sealed corridor. The pounding from within ceased; no one learned whether those prisoners had died, escaped, or simply fallen silent. In the shelter of **Stanley's Tiny Hut**, the friends recovered enough strength to continue. The figure's collapse offered no certainty that the original abductor had died or that the girl was free.[^arc06-6]

At the keep's heart, the **vertical beam** ran from a spiral tower down through the floor. A thrown stone vanished inside it; a dead lizardfolk twisted and disintegrated. **Hope's leg began to spiral as she moved too close**, then returned to its shape when she withdrew. Nothing about these tests revealed a safe passage into the light. Small **black worms** moved through a crack in the floor toward it, leading the companions to a hidden pair of metal doors and a panel. When Hope reached for the button, **it snapped at her like a mouth**. She kept her fingers and stepped back. A machine lay somewhere below, if the dragon's warning could be trusted, but the passage had teeth of its own.[^arc06-7]

The panel spoke. It claimed that the **Door Authority** had a record of the companions' offenses against doors and had forbidden them passage. Hope answered with her weapon. **Nash bargained with the creature**, offering a small taste of acid so that it could describe its obedience as coerced. He **cast Acid Splash on the panel**, and the doors opened. Beyond waited an old descending railway, purple mist and more worms winding toward the surface. The party boarded a **funicular** after Vorel broke its stubborn entrance. Strange glowing flowers sent **Hope and Nash into magical sleep**, while Stanley remained awake and Vorel held his breath. The carriage accelerated out of control and **crashed**. Stanley's **Feather Fall** saved them from the worst of the wreck, and Vorel shook the sleepers awake. The vehicle they had taken downward would carry them no farther.[^arc06-8]

At the bottom was not an ordinary dungeon but the immense ruined complex of the **Center for Human-Demon Interdisciplinary Studies**, its old initials preserved on a sign: **CHIDS**. Human remains lay behind its welcome desk. A bright projected receptionist called **Julia** cheerfully explained laboratories, transit lines and an age of cooperation between humans and demons. Her words came from an ancient automated system; the dust and dead employees offered a different testimony about the institution's present. Searching deserted living quarters and a food court, the travelers collected employee access cards and remnants of impossible commerce. **Nash took a pair of cooling, levitating underwear** from a skeleton and discovered that they could lift him a little above the ground. An old oxygen station instead filled his lungs with foul fumes and **poisoned him**. The welcome had a cruel sense of humor even when no enemy stood before them.[^arc06-9]

The credentials carried three companions through a security checkpoint, but **Hope's scan failed** and ceiling turrets opened fire. **Vorel forced the exit**, bringing her back to the others. They boarded a working railcar along the **blue route** and passed abandoned stations, broken connections and distant laboratory entrances that they did not explore. At the **executive offices**, they disembarked to seek the secret that could lead them to Sira. Among the dead desks and old machines something **tentacled moved toward Hope's head from behind**. For an instant, it seemed that the enemy from the keep had found them again.[^arc06-10]

## II. The office behind the world

The tentacles behind Hope belonged to something smaller and stranger than a lurking mind flayer: **an old plush doll**, made in the likeness of the creature they feared. Its fabric squeaked when squeezed. Hope kept it. A distant skitter answered the sound, although no one could prove that the toy had summoned anything. In the old offices they found a working green-lettered computer. When the companions entered their enemy's name, it offered them a game and directed them to a room furnished with **wired viewing glasses**. Whatever the buried institution had once been, some of its machinery still waited for willing participants.[^arc06-11]

As they explored, **Vorel sang down the deserted corridors**. Little translucent creatures drifted through the walls toward his voice, their tendrils brushing minds without pain. They seemed content near Vorel and Nash, yet shied away from **Hope and Stanley**, and the toy's squeak drove them off. The travelers could guess at fear, pleasure or an attraction to different feelings, but the creatures offered no explanation that could be trusted. Vorel's familiar claim to be **the Lizard King** gained him no new crown among them. The moving lights simply made the passage toward the conference room feel less abandoned.[^arc06-12]

**Nash put on the glasses** and found himself in an office that appeared new, populated by people whose faces were those of illithids. His own body had an alien form, yet his physical body remained seated among his companions in ruined CHIDS. **Hope entered the same projected world**. A disgruntled employee introduced himself as **Charles Dickens**, resentful at being passed over by his superiors for decades. He called the place the **Void Realm** and asserted that the glasses projected their ethereal selves into it. The travelers from the Wastes were suddenly visitors in another workplace, where an ancient catastrophe could be discussed as a dispute over promotion.[^arc06-13]

Charles said that his rival was using a **Traversal Drive**, originally made to move Earth away from dragons, to bring an illithid realm into the planet's place and harvest its souls. The tale resembled the spectral dragon's warning, but Charles knew the project from his own uncertain position and wished to embarrass a rival, not save strangers out of compassion. He gave the visitors a **device holding false reporting figures** and told them that uploading its contents might delay the scheme for months. **Nash made the upload** while Hope changed her projected appearance to distract office workers—first a small insect, then an ordinary drinking vessel, and finally a vehicle that caused a commotion. The interface said the files had arrived. A suspicious employee sounded an alarm; whether the deception would truly delay the machine remained unknown.[^arc06-14]

The alarm sounded **in the physical ruins as well**. As Hope and Nash tried to remove their glasses, the devices clung to their heads like living membranes. Both tore free. **Flaming hounds** and enormous **ape-like security guards** entered the real conference room, proving that at least the danger crossed the boundary between the two places. **Stanley turned one ape into a snail**, leaving it alive in that form. **Nash enlarged Vorel**, who seized burning hounds and wielded them against their fellows. Hope fought with her radiant ammunition when her mechanical shotgun arm jammed. The other great ape slipped from sight and struck back. Nash escaped encirclement by magic, badly injured, while the struggle continued around him.[^arc06-15]

At last the surviving **hellhounds and the active ape were defeated** through steel, song, cold and Vorel's furious blows. The **snail was still a snail**, not a slain ape. Alarms continued to ring. From hiding emerged a small winged creature who called himself **Alvert**, an investigator for an **Underworld Bureau of Investigation**. He said that hell and the demons of the Weir were rivals and that someone was diverting souls that ought to follow other paths. He also knew the name of the Traversal Drive and believed its works lay farther down in **engineering**, beyond a door he could not open. The party had met a possible guide, not a neutral judge of how the afterlife worked.[^arc06-16]

Alvert offered a more dangerous story about **the Principal**. According to him, the metallic ruler had once been a **scientist in this very facility**, transformed into a champion of the **Steel God** during the ancient war. He went further, repeating rumors that the Principal consumed human bodies to maintain his unnatural structure. **The party had not witnessed this processing or inspected a record proving the account**. They remembered the returned Dusthill captives and the ruler's own defenses of his empire; neither version could turn the imp's accusations into a verified biography. His knowledge of CHIDS was worth following, but trust remained a separate matter.[^arc06-17]

The group briefly considered a southern transfer route guarded by uncertain silhouettes, then returned to the executive offices while the alarm still ran. The old computer offered an apparent message from **Charles Dickens** saying the false figures had been uploaded successfully. **That was confirmation of his report, not proof that months had actually been gained**. A hot, damaged computer component spat from the wall; Nash cooled and kept it. The companions also recovered the **polymorphed snail** and placed it in a railcar they sent away **without passengers**, hoping their enemies would pursue it. Whether it crashed and whether the ape returned to its old shape went unwitnessed. With their decoy on its way, the friends continued **on foot** through the rail tunnels.[^arc06-18]

Cold thickened along the **green line**. At an archive stop they confronted another of the Door Authority's wary attendants, which had heard of the party's earlier violence against doors. This one opened after credentials and persuasion, but the inner archive rooms lacked power. **The companions did not settle there for a long rest**. Farther on, the data center's great steel entrance lay buried in **deep ice that rebuilt itself when melted**. Stanley heated metal and **Vorel cut into the frost with the Eye of Groxl**, but neither opened a safe path through the main doors. The only promising way forward was **a ventilation duct high above the blocked entrance**.[^arc06-19]

The party melted a thinner ice barrier and made an awkward ladder of shoulders and magic to reach the vent. Their **single pair of enchanted flying briefs** passed from one companion to the next; Vorel finally arranged them across his broad shoulders so he could help lift **injured Nash** to the opening. **Alvert refused to follow into the freezing duct**, unable or unwilling to endure the cold. **All four companions crawled inside** and disappeared into the passage above the data center. The old complex still had rooms, secrets and enemies beneath them, and **Sira was still missing**. Their descent into the frozen machinery beyond the vent had not yet begun.[^arc06-20]

## III. The elf and the living laboratories

The **frozen ventilation duct** groaned beneath the four travelers. **Stanley** led through grates, bends and whirling fans, melting an obstruction with **Heat Metal**. The thawed metal shifted and gave way beneath **Hope**, threatening to drag Nash and Vorel down after her. Stanley cast **Feather Fall** before the descent could become another fatal plunge. They landed among towers of old servers and vast cooling machinery, where ice held human bodies motionless beside consoles that sometimes still glowed. Their guide **Alvert** had remained behind; the companions were alone with the frozen machinery and the unknown path toward **Sira**.[^arc06-21]

A voice emerged from the dark, addressing someone named **Toby** as though in ordinary conversation. It belonged to **Cerinthala**, an elderly elf wrapped in many garments, keeping company with four human figures entombed in ice. She called them **Toby, Carl, Gordon and Benson** and treated their silence as companionship. She said that she had lived here for ages, withering because the elves' refuge of **Elhandar** was beyond her reach. **Stanley promised to try to help her return**, while refusing to put her need ahead of the child's rescue. Cerinthala offered him a carved wooden **necklace** that seemed to protect its wearer. He put it on. Her hospitality came with a roomful of frozen bodies whose part in her life remained uncertain.[^arc06-22]

Cerinthala knew more of the old complex than its welcoming hologram had revealed. She admitted that a mind flayer had persuaded her to **seal the engineering route**, promising escape in return, but she now used an old data pad to **unlock it**. She spoke of vessels at **Gordon Shuttle Bay**, weapons made from responsive purple crystals, and human laboratories where **lizardfolk** had supposedly been designed for a war against dragons. She also recalled a former official whose name might resemble that of **the Principal**. Her familiarity with the controls was demonstrated; the histories were her own testimony. She spoke dismissively of Vorel as if he were a lesser creature, and Stanley defended his friend against the insult. At the elf's camp, even an offered bowl of soup seemed to come from a kitchen in which metal scraps were ordinary ingredients.[^arc06-23]

They sheltered in **Tiny Hut** and **completed a long rest**. Hope received a troubled message from **T'ietkalla** through her sending stone, but its details remained unclear; she sent word back rather than claiming to have resolved the trouble at home. Later she alone tasted Cerinthala's stew and listened to more stories about ancient human–demon experiments, the origin of tieflings and the dark power of **the Eye of Groxl**. Those explanations were troubling but not yet proved. The rest ended with an attack. **Four towering masses of ice containing old corpses rose** around the hut, and one seized **Stanley** and carried him away. The necklace Cerinthala had given him tightened around his throat. Vorel swelled under Nash's **Enlarge** spell, and the others rushed to keep the captive from disappearing into the ice.[^arc06-24]

Stanley's attempt to cut the necklace brought a **violent electric shock**. **Diplomacy**, his animated sword, struck at the arm that held him; **Hope's explosive shot severed that icy limb**, dropping Stanley so she could lead him away. **Vorel's flaming Eye of Groxl shield** shattered several constructs while Nash's fire damaged the others, but the broken ice revealed **blue-eyed human corpses that still fought**. The captor withdrew into the frozen floor rather than dying in front of them. The companions defeated the exposed corpse attackers, then faced Cerinthala again. At her approach, the necklace loosened. From the ice, **Toby formed a speaking mouth** and said that the companions had attacked for fear that she would abandon them.[^arc06-25]

Cerinthala apologized, but admitted that she had first considered **using or consuming the strangers' bodies**. She also acknowledged that **she had made Stanley's necklace as a curse**, binding him painfully when he resisted her will. She claimed he could not remove it. Stanley did not accept that a promise to try to help her meant forsaking Sira. The party nevertheless took her as a guide through maintenance tunnels, spending hours melting a path through solid ice rather than treating the frozen barrier as loose earth. They found service corridors lit by old emergency lamps and descended into a **biological laboratory** where cylinders stood among **pink, responsive growths**. When Nash tested the tissue with his staff, it **snatched and broke the wood**. He retained a smaller usable focus, not a repaired staff. Cerinthala offered to create a lizardfolk child for Vorel through ancient methods; **he refused**. He was already Great Fang, and no new egg or heir resulted from her offer.[^arc06-26]

A bank of old elevators promised a route farther down. One lift fell to the bottom of its shaft before they could board it; a second carried them until it stopped **between floors**. Vorel forced its doors only to find solid concrete beyond them, then tore open the roof so the others could climb. Above them **Hope saw a giant spider-like shape descending**, its many eyes glimmering while smaller spiders fell around it. Poisonous swarms struck as they fought to leave the stalled machine. **Stanley turned the huge spider into a sheep**, which dropped to the shaft's bottom and changed back when it was hurt. The transformation had not killed it. After the beast briefly seized Vorel's mind, **Nash's fire broke its control**, and **Hope shot the restored spider dead**. Vorel cut shields from its carapace, one large enough for Cerinthala and one sized for Hope's smaller mechanical arm.[^arc06-27]

The party reached a damaged lower laboratory where **pink membrane** filled the passage like living flesh. It admitted **Hope, Stanley and Vorel** but closed against **Nash and Cerinthala**. Nash sang, and the tissue opened for him. Cerinthala tried the same, failed, and was **crushed inside the closing wall**. When the others retrieved her, **she showed no signs of life**. They carried her body on the shield Vorel had made. A black residue on her skin irritated anyone who touched it; Nash tasted some and suffered a swollen tongue. Cleaning away the surface grime did not undo the reaction. Whatever Cerinthala had claimed about the deaths of ancient elves, **her body was dead at that moment**, and no return had yet been witnessed.[^arc06-28]

Farther through the wet corridors, two armed **green-scaled lizardfolk** died under Vorel's flames and Stanley's sword. In a control room beyond, an **illithid** worked at monitors. It concealed **a disk inside its clothing** before noticing the intruders, then called forth a monstrous **red mass with many faces**. Vorel propelled Hope across the room; she seized the fleeing illithid in her mechanical arm and **held him fast**. The red creature struck with crushing force and stole fragments of memory. It weakened **Vorel**, knocking him unconscious until Stanley healed him; then its attack reached **Nash**, who had taken his black dragon form. From the red body grew a **tethered duplicate of Vorel**, and then another of the dragon. The copies fought alongside their source, neither independent future travelers nor harmless illusions.[^arc06-29]

The friends were still struggling when the red creature reached Stanley's companions again. **Hope had not released the illithid**, the concealed disk was missing, and **the red mass and both copies were still active**. Vorel and Nash were suffering lasting memory damage, not a passing fright cured by the previous rest. Cerinthala's lifeless body remained with the party. **The battle was unfinished**, and the way to the Traversal Drive was still buried deeper in the complex.[^arc06-30]

## IV. A device with two doors

The memory-eater's **dragon-shaped double** breathed acid at Nash, but his own borrowed dragon body resisted it. Its central mass turned on **Stanley**, striking him down and draining his memory as well. **Vorel** battered the many-faced creature with the **Eye of Groxl's flaming shield** until the red body broke apart; both tethered copies dissolved with it. That victory did not restore what had been taken from their minds. **Hope kept the illithid pinned in her mechanical grip**, and **Nash's dragon bite killed it**, leaving its head in her hands. Stanley made his death saves and lay **stable but unconscious**, not healed to his feet by the end of the fight.[^arc06-31]

They searched the dead while waiting for Stanley to return to consciousness. In Cerinthala's robes they found **a translucent blue gemstone** and a photographic **employee card** bearing her name, evidence that she had once belonged to the institution she described. A recovered code failed to unlock the controls, and the disk the illithid had hidden **could not be found**. Hope discovered **a black worm inside its head** and crushed it, then removed similar worms from the two fallen riflemen. These were real parasites, but they did not reveal every host in the facility or their ultimate master. After several hours, **Stanley regained consciousness** and took a **short rest**, singing to help the company recover. **Nash, Vorel and Stanley still bore the memory-eater's penalties**. They had not taken another full night's rest.[^arc06-32]

Beyond the control room waited a sealed inner chamber, insulated against sound and guarded by a sequence of mechanisms. **Hope worked the terminal** while the others took positions at valves and switches; together they opened an inner cradle. It offered a strange **white sleeve-mounted device with red and green triggers**. **Vorel took it first**. The colored projectiles caused no ordinary gunshot wounds, even when aimed at bodies. The company could not tell what the instrument was meant to accomplish. They had acquired a machine without an instruction manual, and the people who might have understood it had become enemies or corpses.[^arc06-33]

Then **Cerinthala stood alive again**. The dead elf from the membrane passage appeared in the control area and saw what they had opened. Rather than thanking them, she called for **Toby** and declared that an illithid employer wanted the travelers captured alive. The **blue gemstone** became or summoned a hulking **ice Toby** inside the chamber. Cerinthala **paralyzed Hope with magic** and fled behind a **wall of ice**. Stanley fought Toby with spells and Diplomacy while **Vorel and Nash** broke through the frozen casing and destroyed the blue-eyed corpse inside. Stanley dispelled the barrier, and the group pursued the elf as far as the living corridors. **Greasy traces and ice residue** marked where she had been, but she was gone. Her return had proved that the earlier death was not permanent; **her latest escape was not a second death**.[^arc06-34]

Back at the secured chamber, Vorel tried the colored instrument against solid walls. **Red and green marks opened two connected passages**, allowing a traveler to step through one and emerge from the other. The device had **one paired set of portals**, not a stock of independent transport guns. A shot into a body failed to make a doorway; disturbing one end could close the connection. Nash recognized a resemblance to an ancient game he remembered, though his comparison could not explain all the machinery's limits. **Vorel gave the device to Hope**. With a route around ordinary barriers finally within reach, they looked for a way past the enemies closing in.[^arc06-35]

A **large company of bugbears** came through the lower laboratories, an **illithid** directing them from behind. Nash's fire wiped out their front ranks, but many remained. Vorel went forward as bait, shouting a challenge in **Goblin** and drawing the company toward him. In the room behind, the other three prepared the device and noticed a vent that might have offered another exit. **The pursuing force had begun its charge** when their plan took shape. **No portal evacuation had yet been completed** at that moment, and no one had rescued Sira or reached the Drive.[^arc06-36]

When the bugbears pursued, **Hope opened the paired route**. Stanley passed through, and Vorel grappled an **isolated illithid** while its soldiers were ordered to turn back. **Diplomacy**, spells and Vorel's attacks weakened the captive, and **Hope killed it with her shotgun**. Something wormlike flew from its shattered head, but no one demonstrated that it had been destroyed. The company **escaped the bulk of the bugbear force** rather than slaughtering everyone in the corridor. The responsive pink passage let **Hope, Vorel and Stanley** through but barred Nash. His song failed this time; **Dimension Door carried him back to his friends** before the soldiers could catch up. The retreat was real, and the way behind them was no longer safe.[^arc06-37]

They reached **Containment**, where a broken catwalk spanned a dark shaft. A control terminal insisted the bridge was already extended, indifferent to the gap beneath their feet. **Hope passed the single portal device to Nash**, who used the **enchanted flying briefs** to cross and inspect a jammed door. A huge **blue-black winged creature with a barbed tail** attacked him over the void. Stanley and Vorel crossed through a portal to fight from the pipes, while Hope fired from safer ground. Nash's attempted sleep spell failed, and the beast's presence frightened the two men briefly; their earlier memory injuries remained separate. **Hope shot it dead**. Other creatures cried somewhere below, but the party used the portal pair to cross before another could catch them.[^arc06-38]

Beyond lay warm pipes, flickering light and **ghostlike blue workers** repeating tasks at desks whose physical keyboards did not move. Some of the figures reacted to sound or touch, yet the companions could not tell whether they were preserved minds, recordings or something stranger. A nearby screen displayed **lizardfolk body modifications and equipment readings**. That was firmer evidence of experimentation on particular subjects than Cerinthala's sweeping claims about an entire people's origin. In a storeroom Hope found **two rockets** fitting her launcher and bottles marked **Blue Bull** and **Monster Mender**—**five of each kind** for herself. Their warnings suggested different effects upon lizardfolk and elves, not a universal cure. Nash tasted only a drop of blue liquid, feeling a brief surge; nothing showed that his abilities had permanently changed.[^arc06-39]

For **Vorel** the armory held an outfit made for fighting dragons: a **nanoweave suit**, a linked **harpoon harness**, great jumping **boots** and a helmet that helped resist hostile thoughts. The equipment spoke its functions into his mind as he put it on, but the suit's protection came with a cost to the defenses he had relied upon while unarmored. He gave his former skull helm to Stanley and took **one Blue Bull and one Monster Mender** for himself. **The Eye of Groxl grew angry** as they approached the deeper flesh-lined passages, offering no clear explanation of what it sensed. The companions passed onward together. **They had not reached the Traversal Drive, operated it, or freed Sira**, and the laboratory mysteries that might illuminate the Eye were still waiting ahead.[^arc06-40]

## V. The hive and the disputed futures

The passage of **living flesh** did not crush or imprison **Nash** as the four companions pushed into the deeper reaches of CHIDS. Past a security barrier they found a control station behind bars, then a dangerous shaft lined with fans and green beams. The **paired portal device** gave **Hope** a way around the gap, carrying them toward rows of cylinders filled with green liquid. Inside were **dead lizardfolk**, still attached to functioning monitors. The screens recorded their status and offered lessons in language, mathematics and war. Other controls spoke in the colder vocabulary of **altering behavior and suppressing apprehension**. Whatever Cerinthala had claimed about the origin of an entire people, here the travelers could witness a laboratory prepared to **change the minds of particular living subjects**. No display explained why those in the tanks had died.[^arc06-41]

Two vessels stood apart from the others. One, broken open, bore the label **G-R-0-X-1**. The other held **Lethanphius**, an immense winged figure whose body seemed built for violence. Vorel felt the **Eye of Groxl** grow furious at the intact prisoner and ashamed before the shattered vessel. The markings and the Eye's reaction suggested a connection, but neither proved that the being in Vorel's chest had been made there or that the Eye's earlier stories were false. The flesh of the wall formed tools, opened the tank and released Lethanphius. He spoke as if a father had abandoned him, then summoned a black blade and fell upon the intruders. **Vorel's new grappling harness** caught at him, but the prisoner tore himself free and ripped away **two of Vorel's fingers**.[^arc06-42]

The fight carried them through the chamber in a storm of metal, spells and black wings. **Nash enlarged Vorel**, who fought with the Eye's fiery shield despite his wounds. **Hope**, using the same red-and-green portal device, discovered that **pressing both triggers** could produce something far worse than a doorway: a sustained **necrotic beam**. It struck Lethanphius with devastating force, but **Vorel was in its path**. The blast dropped the lizardfolk unconscious as it tore much of the winged creature apart, revealing **black metal bones** beneath the flesh. **Stanley healed Vorel**, and a dose of **Monster Mender** helped him rise. The severed upper body of Lethanphius continued to fight; its apparent destruction was not yet its death.[^arc06-43]

Lethanphius struck again and **cut off Vorel's hand at the wrist**, a separate injury from the fingers already lost. Hope answered the creature's dark weapon with her own hellish fire. At last **Nash's Fire Bolt destroyed the surviving torso**. The foe's blade vanished with him. Nash retrieved the hand and fingers, **freezing them inside his cooling, flying underwear** so that they might be restored. Vorel remained alive but mutilated. By the close of that encounter, an eight-hour rest and repairs were discussed as the next necessary actions; **no completed reattachment had yet been witnessed**. The survivors still stood among corpses, machines and a vessel that seemed to know something of the Eye.[^arc06-44]

Then the **pink wall made a person**. A humanoid figure emerged and claimed to speak for a **council of minds** preserved from CHIDS, grown into a single living structure that could no longer leave the facility without destroying itself. The spokesperson said it had guided the group through its passages and wanted help against illithids who had entered engineering, where the **Traversal Drive** lay. Its account of the ancient institution, the timing of the invasion and the purpose of the responsive tissue sounded informed, but it was still the account of an intelligence whose reach and motives the adventurers did not know. It could not give them a reliable explanation of why **Cerinthala had been crushed** by the walls. Vorel listened closely, with his lost hand still waiting in Nash's improvised ice chest.[^arc06-45]

The wall raised a **second figure with Nash's face**. It declared that it was **Nash Maggard**, older by centuries and trapped in the hive after traveling through time. According to this apparition, the party had once defeated their enemies yet failed to free Sira; later deals with **Quizquaz and the Soulbroker** had brought ruin, and the world itself had been destroyed. It warned that saving Sira or stopping the Drive might demand a willing life. These were **the figure's recollections and predictions**, not events witnessed by the companions in their present course. Even the council questioned whether time travel could account for its story. **Nash refused to treat the alleged future as inevitable**, while Stanley and Vorel heard its warning with a different, private dread.[^arc06-46]

The council claimed that **G-R-0-X-1** had been a prototype made to fight dragons and that **Lethanphius** had been taken from another realm for the experiments. It also said CHIDS had bred soldiers from **lizardfolk that already existed elsewhere**, contradicting the simplest version of Cerinthala's origin tale without settling it. It could offer codes, equipment and passage nearer engineering, but not an army able to leave its own walls. When the company questioned whether the Drive might be destroyed, the spokesperson warned that a careless collapse **could destroy Earth itself**. **Stanley thought it believed what it said**; belief was not proof of its engineering calculations. In private, Stanley told Vorel that **he might give his life to free Sira if there truly were no other choice**. He made no sacrifice then, and the others were not present for that exchange.[^arc06-47]

The help became tangible. The council handed **Stanley and Nash each a Ring of Spell Storing** and supplied codes for locked CHIDS armories. Only now, **in the next stage of their journey**, did the group retrieve Vorel's frozen hand and fingers, fit them to their wounds and use **Monster Mender** to knit them back together. **The hand moved again**, though the scars remained. In opened boxes they found **crimson laser swords**, pairs of forearm blades and spare anti-dragon suits. Hope, Nash and Stanley each kept a sword; Vorel fitted a pair of blades to his arms, and the remaining equipment went into **Stanley's Bag of Holding**. The weapons were real prizes, not a promise of invincibility. Vorel's helmet protected against some hostile thoughts but did not sever his uneasy bond with the Eye of Groxl.[^arc06-48]

Back in the room where Lethanphius had died, **Stanley raised Tiny Hut** and the company **completed an actual eight-hour long rest**. Before sleeping, Stanley placed **Healing Word** magic into his new ring, and Nash stored **Chromatic Orbs** in his. Hope sent a brief reply to a message from T'ietkalla. When they awoke, Nash felt an unfamiliar disturbance in his memory and spellwork; no one could say that rest had erased all the red creature's earlier damage. The hive had meanwhile prepared a route. The four lay upon **soft beds of flesh**, which bore them for minutes through enclosed organic tunnels and deposited them quietly beside an **unaware illithid operator** near engineering. Their hosts had truly transported them, even if the larger warnings remained disputed.[^arc06-49]

The companions killed the operator and nearby guards, only to hear cries from another room. There, **Chance and Eddie Manchester III**—friends lost to the Wastes long ago—hung **bound above a tank of small tentacled creatures**. Two more illithids, a robed caster and a security ape guarded them. **Vorel** was seized and nearly lost his head to a mind flayer's hunger. **Eddie's knife struck that attacker dead**, saving him. **Nash killed the other illithid with Chromatic Orb**, while **Stanley became a giant ape** and smashed the security beast; the caster also fell. Chance and Eddie survived and were freed. The reunion was real, but they had not yet reached the Drive, and **Sira remained a captive somewhere ahead**. They ended the struggle together in the broken recreation laboratory, with an old friendship restored and a much larger danger still waiting beyond the next doors.[^arc06-50]

## VI. Inside the Traversal Drive

The gnomes' reunion left the companions no time to forget why they were beneath the earth. **Chance and Eddie** told what they knew of their capture by bugbears and their time in illithid custody, but none of it supplied a safe passage to **Sira**. The party checked their healing bottles and spare equipment, making use of **Monster Mender** where they could. They did **not** take another full night's rest. Instead, with guards still stationed near the engineering approach, they paused for a **short rest** and prepared a deceitful entrance rather than a noisy battle at every door. **Vorel made a mask from a dead mind flayer's face**, and Nash agreed to wear it. Stanley dressed in a dead caster's robes, while the gnomes played prisoners.[^arc06-51]

The disguise took them past **two security apes** at a checkpoint. Nash floated in his enchanted underwear, using an illithid appearance and **Minor Illusion** to speak as a commander; the guards accepted the performance and stood aside. Beyond them the old concrete of CHIDS gave way to **crystal and purple mist**. A sealed door bore a strange **four-fingered handprint**. Chance folded his fingers to fit it, without success. Even a real **severed illithid hand**, fetched by Nash from the previous battle, failed to open it. **Stanley carried Hope through the barrier by Dimension Door**, and she placed the second colored portal on the far side so the others could follow. They had bypassed the lock; they had **not learned its proper key**.[^arc06-52]

They emerged into a cavern that dwarfed the corridors behind them. Vast machinery ran beneath and through **blue-purple crystal**, its central beam climbing from a brilliant reservoir. At the heart of it hung the shape of a **decaying dragon fused to a monstrous worm**, half drowned in radiant fluid. The companions could finally see the **Traversal Drive's engineering chamber** that so many doubtful witnesses had described. **Cerinthala** stood near the machinery with chanting illithids and a larger commander, while armed lizardfolk watched from high platforms. The proximity of **Sira** to the central light was difficult to determine through the machinery and glare, and no rescue had occurred. Seeing the device did not certify every account of what it could do or how it had been built.[^arc06-53]

The group chose surprise. They took cover, discussed spells and drank **Blue Bull**, whose rush of energy came with nausea for those it did not suit. Hope had **two rockets** from the armory, and she aimed them high, away from the fragile-looking center. She fired **both** into opposite raised banks. Explosions swept through the scaffolding, killing several lizardfolk and throwing **three illithids and Cerinthala's visible body into the beam**, where they broke apart. The elf had returned once before; the company could not claim this newest destruction proved a permanent end. The **larger illithid commander survived the bombardment**, rose into the air and prepared to fight. The dragon-worm and Sira were not destroyed or freed, and the Drive was not shut down. The opening strike was the close of that day's battle, not its conclusion.[^arc06-54]

The commander was still aloft amid rocket dust when the fight resumed. **Sira could now be seen within the central luminous region**, beside the dragon-worm. **Stanley and Eddie** moved under cover, Chance advanced with a dart, and Vorel climbed toward the enemy with the grappling harpoons of his new suit. Hope fought with **Scully**, her rockets already spent. Nash took the shape of a **young black dragon** and sent a sheet of acid through what seemed to be several copies of the commander. **Two figures were Mirror Images**, which vanished; the real commander remained alive. Rifle-bearing lizardfolk on the surviving platforms shot back as the dragon rose above them.[^arc06-55]

The commander answered with **Mind Blast**. **Nash, Vorel, Chance and Eddie were stunned**, while **Hope and Stanley** resisted. Vorel's telepathy-dampening helmet had not made him invulnerable to that assault. Stanley cast **Feather Fall** to save allies from dangerous drops, as Hope continued to fire. Then **Dolo Popio**, the dragon-worm in the pool, released a cluster of **green glowing worms**. Hope shot them and used their explosive acid to destroy others nearby. Larger **red memory-eating creatures** appeared around the chamber, familiar from the lower laboratory. The companions were fighting not only armed soldiers but the living products of the same cruel machinery they had passed on the way down.[^arc06-56]

Stanley turned to the laser swords brought from the armory, intending to animate them into an attacking swarm. The spell **could not animate those magical weapons**. Instead he raised **ten ordinary metal pipes and scaffold pieces**, which battered the floating commander whenever they could reach him. The distinction mattered: the prized swords remained weapons in the bag, not sacrificed ammunition. Elsewhere the commander struck with **black necrotic lightning**. **Both gnomes fell unconscious**; one red creature threatened Eddie with a further memory wound, while a green worm wrapped itself around Chance. The party had recovered their friends only to risk losing them again before reaching the trapped girl.[^arc06-57]

**Eddie** rallied on his own, opening his eyes with barely enough strength to move. Stanley used **one stored Healing Word** from his ring to bring **Chance back to consciousness**, saving him from the immediate danger. Hope kept fighting worms and tried the **necrotic mode of the portal device** against the distant commander without landing the decisive blow. Stanley's animated pipes battered the enemy until it was near collapse. Nash rose high with a surviving lizardfolk in his claws, **dropped the guard to a fatal fall**, then turned his charged breath upon the commander. **His acid killed the illithid**, dissolving it into flakes. This was an actual victory over the leader who had survived Hope's rockets, **not the destruction of the dragon-worm or the machine**.[^arc06-58]

The creature at the center at last addressed the company itself. **Dolo Popio** spoke of a contract with a higher power, but its words were a threat and claim, not independently verified cosmic law. The chamber **changed around them**: the companions now stood or floated upon **separate rocky and crystalline platforms in a darkness crowded with stars**, with the worm-dragon and **Sira still at the center**. They could feel and fight upon the platforms, yet could not know whether they had literally left Earth or entered a magic-made battlefield. A half-buried human head lay in the sand near Nash, its owner unknown. **Hope felt the creature's fear**, and Nash would face that influence as well. The old facility's walls were no longer a dependable guide to distance or direction.[^arc06-59]

**Vorel's stun finally cleared**. He activated his great jumping boots and launched himself toward Dolo Popio, changing the **Eye of Groxl's flaming shield into a blazing great blade** as he flew. It was a new use of the same artifact, not a second weapon or proof of the hive's account of its origins. He struck the dragon-worm and clung to it rather than drift away. **Stanley** pressed the creature with spells and **Diplomacy**, while Nash scorched it with dragon acid and killed another red attacker. The monster was badly hurt but **still alive**. It pulled away, gathered luminous particles around its mouth and **unleashed a vast breath attack** across the strange arena. **The breath's contents, harm and consequences were not yet known**; the companions had no way to see what the next moment would take from them.[^arc06-60]

## VII. The four in the light

**Dolo Popio's breath was not fire.** The dragon-worm released a cloud of great living worms that drifted between the rocky platforms of the star-filled battlefield. **Vorel** was still clinging to its body, his **Eye of Groxl** burning in the shape of a great blade, while **Nash** fought from the air in his young black dragon form. **Stanley** struggled toward the bright enclosure where **Sira** stood behind an unseen wall. They had fought their way beneath Khaervilon to reach her, but the creature still controlled the space around the light. **Chance**, only just restored by Stanley's magic during the previous fight, **slipped from his platform and drifted away into the void**. No companion recovered him or saw where he came to rest. His disappearance was real; **his death was not established**.[^arc06-61]

Nash burned through several floating worms and shook off the fear that had impeded his flight. **Hope** shot at the red fleshy things that threatened her friends, while Vorel climbed farther up the giant creature. He tore off **the nanoweave suit he had obtained in CHIDS**, twisted it into a binding and **fastened it across Dolo Popio's jaws**. The monster thrashed, but the lizardfolk held on and kept its mouth closed through repeated struggles. It could no longer loose another full worm-breath while he held it. The suit had ceased to be armor and become a desperate restraint. Vorel continued striking and biting the creature at close range, staking his own life on holding it long enough for the others to fight.[^arc06-62]

The smaller horrors did not stop. One of the red **memory-eating creatures** burrowed into **Hope's chest**, tearing her badly. She pulled free the detachable metal horn she sometimes used as a weapon and **skewered the intruder with it**, leaving the horn behind in the monster's flesh as she moved away. **Eddie** fell unconscious again in the chaos. Stanley spent **another Healing Word stored in his ring**, bringing the gnome back to consciousness, and passed him a **Monster Mender**. Stanley drank one himself, taking care over how the strange mixture might affect a non-lizardfolk. There was no newly completed rest, and the injuries of the fight were not erased merely because the friends could rise again.[^arc06-63]

Debris drifted in the false—or perhaps impossible—sky. Among the rocks was **a fire truck**, an unfamiliar machine to Hope, floating where no road existed. She caught its outer ladder and used it as a firing position. Loading **Scully** with **radiant darts**, she aimed at Dolo Popio's head while Vorel held the jaws shut and Nash tore at its flesh. The radiant shots found a weakness. **Hope's final round destroyed the dragon-worm's manifested head and body**, a victory the company had actually witnessed. The demon had been defeated here; **nothing they saw proved that its essence could never return**. The stars and platforms collapsed from sight, and the survivors were thrown back into **CHIDS engineering**.[^arc06-64]

The machine was still running. **Stanley, Eddie and Sira were trapped together inside the central light**, held behind an invisible barrier; **Hope, Vorel and dragon-form Nash** stood outside. **Chance did not reappear** when the room returned. Stanley tried to pass an object through the boundary and failed. Hope searched a console with controls whose labels gave little guidance. One changed the light's pitch and frequency; another turned its color toward **crimson**. A third produced dreadful physical changes in those within, including sensations of bodies being torn apart and **Eddie's nails growing suddenly**. Hope restored the dangerous settings rather than claim she had found a safe way to switch the entire apparatus off. **Sira was within reach of her friends, but not yet free**.[^arc06-65]

The lights dimmed, and **Quizquaz** appeared: a towering, oil-black, winged visitor whose white eyes seemed to shine through the dark. Speaking as an agent of **Kitobol Prith**, he congratulated the companions for defeating the dragon-worm and offered them a bargain. He claimed the **Traversal Drive** was drawing power from the **Void**, that it could not be stopped by an ordinary switch, and that letting the beam destroy **Earth** would also destroy **the Principal** and satisfy obligations under the party's **Soulbroker pact**. He promised the adventurers escape to other worlds, leaving their people to die. He also alleged that **Andreeste** had sponsored Dolo Popio. These were the self-serving explanations of a being who wanted a decision from them, **not proof that a contract had already been fulfilled or a planet destroyed**.[^arc06-66]

**Stanley refused to bargain away the world**. Quizquaz admitted another possibility: a willing being strong enough to take the energy might absorb it and be carried away, leaving the planet unharmed. **Stanley offered to do it alone**. First, he insisted that the gnomes and Sira should be released. The demon operated the controls and **Eddie walked out of the light**. **Sira hesitated**, unwilling to leave the man who had come to find her, until Stanley persuaded her that Vorel and the others would protect her. She slipped through the barrier **invisibly**, then joined Eddie outside. **Her rescue from the light had finally happened in E081**, not when the party first found the Drive. The sacrifice Stanley contemplated was still only his proposal.[^arc06-67]

**Vorel proposed a different choice**. If the energy could be taken by one of them, perhaps the **four friends could share its burden**, rather than let Stanley disappear alone. He challenged Quizquaz to offer himself instead; the dream-drinker reacted with anger and refused. Under pressure, Quizquaz explained how the controls could be used: **lower the frequency to let people pass into or out of the beam**, then raise it and set the light **crimson** to concentrate the energy upon those within. The account came from a manipulative enemy, but the group now had instructions they could attempt. Quizquaz departed without accepting Vorel's challenge. **Stanley prayed to Moha and cast Augury** about staying in the beam to save the world. He received **a favorable omen**, with a warning that it would be his final result. It was not a promise that anyone would live.[^arc06-68]

**Eddie agreed to work the console** while the four companions stood together. As Hope showed him the settings, **he secretly took her Bowie knife**; the theft succeeded without changing his agreement to help. Stanley told **Sira**, now visible beside Eddie, to remain outside and made the gnome promise to watch over her. She thanked Stanley and said goodbye. **Chance was still missing** and could not be counted among the people awaiting the outcome. **Stanley remained inside the light** as **Hope, Vorel and dragon-form Nash** came through the lowered barrier to join him. The four clasped hands and claws, making their choice together. They had declined to let Stanley pay alone or to trade everyone else for a possible escape.[^arc06-69]

Eddie raised the frequency and turned the beam crimson. A feeling of **love and unity** passed through the companions as the light took hold; **Hope's metal arm and leg did not shine as her living body did**. The force struck them with terrible violence. **Hope fell unconscious**. **Vorel was still standing with a trace of strength at the instant of the blow**, while **Nash was torn from his dragon shape into his human body** and suffered more damage. Stanley's precise remaining strength was not clear from the moment. Then **all four became white light and dispersed into mist**. The scene went dark. **The GM explicitly declared Hope, Nash, Stanley and Vorel dead at the end of E081**. **Eddie and Sira had remained outside the beam alive**; **Chance remained unlocated**. What had become of the four after their disappearance, whether the Drive's danger had ended for good, and how the Soulbroker would reckon their choice were **not shown before the episode ended**.[^arc06-70]
