# Arc Two — The Road to the Crown

**The Wastes · second story arc · Full spoilers.**[^two-balance][^two-archive][^two-1]

## I. The road from the Liberators — A challenge and a pledge

The name **Heroes of the Wastes** cannot hold the company together by itself. Disturbed by their brutality, **Stanley withdraws from the Liberators' camp**, and the others must find him before setting out. Reconciliation gives them another chance to act together, but their arguments over responsibility survive it. The Soulbroker's deadline presses on three of them; Vorel, who refused the contract, is still traveling beside its signatories.

The camp prepares to move after its location is reported compromised. Before leaving, Vorel speaks privately with the sightless former **Koromukishi** about opposing the Principal and closing **Esophageal Pass**. The orc would preserve the small resistance while the company seeks the means to block the route. It is a strategy for a future campaign, not an army ready to follow them into battle.

Stanley's first attempt to drive **Fate 4** makes an unsteady departure. Inside the vehicle they discover **Tedna**, a young stowaway determined to become a hero and fight the Principal. His enthusiasm earns little confidence from people already struggling to keep themselves alive. Before they can decide what place he has among them, a flying armored warrior intercepts the vehicle.

The stranger claims to serve the **Umbilical Order** and condemns Hope, Nash and Stanley for their demonic compact. Energy wings, firearms and mechanical attackers make his sentence a practical danger. Vorel refuses to surrender his friends. The resulting fight ends with the warrior killed and the RV overturned by an explosion. Tedna is found alive but unconscious inside; the companions right the vehicle and repair enough damage to continue.

At their desert camp, **Diptak of the Golden Land** challenges Vorel over an alleged claim involving priestess **Typalla**. The hunters and their raptors fight until Diptak yields, then pledge their clan's allegiance. Vorel gains the prospect of support, while Hope helps patch Fate 4's roof with raptor hide. Farther west they meet **Buttons**, an upright talking cat with glasses and a mechanical leg. He buys passage and shares knowledge of the Arcanum and **Tietkalla**, the fortress above **Teet and Trashburg**. Their growing company approaches the city carrying a roof held together by its latest battle and more promises than it can yet fulfill.[^two-2][^two-3]

## II. Two voices — Stanley and Diontalus

Camped outside **Tietkalla**, the travelers hear a song from the oil sands. Hope follows its call; by the time **Stanley** wakes, most of the company has vanished. Nash remains inside Fate 4, entranced and walking against the closed door. Stanley binds him to the workbench to prevent him following, then goes out after the others. Tedna's tracks lead onward, but the boy himself cannot be shaken free of the enchantment.

**Buttons**, apparently able to resist the music, joins Stanley. They descend through thorn-decorated doors into an abandoned prison and agree on an escape signal: **lemon tree**. Beyond the cells lies an underground oil lake, a beach strewn with bones and the creature whose singing has drawn their friends below. A monstrous body carries an extra mouth, while an aged hag treats the singer as her child. Hope, Vorel, Tedna and Bok Bok wait helplessly under its influence.

Stanley tries to negotiate their release. The captors speak of feeding, power and an old prisoner, but refuse to let the travelers go. Fighting them means facing companions who may turn against their rescuer without understanding why. Stanley uses magic and his animated sword **Diplomacy** to resist the pair; Bok Bok's dynamite adds a violent interruption. When the singer is destroyed, the enchanted company begins to stir.

Then a luminous blue, elven-looking figure crawls from the lake. He names himself **Diontalus, archfey of carnage**, and describes captivity, lost power and a future restored through elven supremacy. He wants Stanley's strength to recover his own. Stanley refuses another bargain, especially one offered by a being who speaks so readily of mass violence. **Diontalus answers the refusal by entering his body**, overwhelming him in blue light.

The prison begins to collapse around the waking survivors. Vorel carries Stanley and Bok Bok while Hope helps Tedna; an immovable rod is left beneath rising oil during the escape. They reunite with Nash, who has freed himself from his bonds, and reach Fate 4. Stanley conceals parts of what happened. Later, Diontalus openly speaks through him and admits the union was forced. The company now travels with two wills in one body, unable to trust that the friend who falls asleep will be the one who wakes.[^two-4]

## III. Trashburg — The promises beneath the keep

**Trashburg** greets the company with hungry children, cramped streets and the daily humiliations of poverty. Gang borders divide the settlement below the keep. At **Charlemagne's** they find lodging and a guide to local dangers, but the city's public celebration offers a clearer picture of who rules: **Suck Master Din** announces his son's wedding to **Jolt**, with food, festivities and a Blood Ball match to entertain the population. At the **Pit of Despair**, condemned people are thrown down to something with an enormous tongue. The crowd cheers.

The companions have several reasons to enter the fortress, including the promised revolvers **Fox and Scully**. Each proposed route carries another obligation. **Monsoon Cabbage**, leader of the **Twenty-Six Block**, offers a way inside if they kill the rival Toe Rippers' chief. Tedna investigates work serving the wedding banquet. The **Trashburg Gray Socks** offer a place on their Blood Ball team, and the adventurers train in the violent sport, collecting jerseys for a game they will never play.

In his room, Nash hears a tiny voice and finds **P0D**, a frightened pixie named for the label on her aquarium. She begs not to be returned to **Farmer Dewey**. According to her, captive pixies are crushed into dust to make **Pixie Blush**, the drug that helped drive Nash's destruction of Slavetown. His attachment to her makes the victims of that trade immediate and personal. Rescuing the others becomes one more urgent task competing with entry to the keep.

Trust is already failing within their lodging. Buttons presses Stanley to disclose what entered him beneath the oil sands; Stanley uses **Suggestion** to send him scouting instead. A visit to an arms dealer brings a fanged attacker and leaves Hope weakened, but also yields a rocket launcher. Vorel attacks Toe Rippers in the street, setting off a fight and a burning building without securing their leader.

At the **Golden Gargoyle**, **Angelashi's** music and intimacy offer Nash a brief refuge from these dangers. The Toe Rippers follow the company there too. What looked like a choice among clever entrances has become a widening conflict, with Monsoon waiting for his price and the city's authorities drawing closer.[^two-5]

## IV. The deal collapses — Hope rebuilt and the pit below

With the **Toe Rippers' leader Blunt Goliver** still alive, Monsoon's promised help remains conditional. He marks an underground approach and replaces weapons lost in the Golden Gargoyle escape. Charlemagne evacuates his family, leaving the inn's keys with Vorel. **Tedna writes from inside the keep**, reporting frightened tiefling statues, political intrigues and people connected to Dusthill's betrayal. Some of the letter remains Hope's private knowledge. The city's wedding now approaches over a network of threats the company barely understands. P0D's plea to rescue the other pixies still competes with every new emergency.

In the tunnels, Hope fires her recovered rocket at a hostile monk. He sends it back. The explosion drives her into a wall and fills the passages with smoke and falling rock. The others struggle to carry her toward breathable air while fighting through reinforcements. They finally kill Goliver and take his head to Monsoon, but Hope's injuries are beyond the help of a simple healing spell.

**Dr. Dickles** amputates her shattered left arm and leg. Nash, Vorel and Stanley assist through the surgery; **Metal Mandy** fits mechanical replacements. Hope wakes to test a new grip, altered movement and a weapon hidden inside her arm. Recovery brings another horror when Monsoon tells her he ordered the deaths of those who treated her, claiming they might betray him. The assistance that saved her has placed her more firmly inside his power.

Meanwhile, armed patrols announce that **Stanley, also called Diontalus**, is wanted. Reunited at Monsoon's base, the companions hear his stories of **Kefeli**, **Andreeste**, a lost tiefling kingdom and the **Gem of Tides**. His proposed road into the keep now runs beneath the execution pit. Reaching it means moving Hope through streets swept by spotlights, disguises failing as guards converge.

Below the bars, Vorel distracts the **froghemoth** while the others force a rusted grate. A frightened pursuing guard, **George**, follows them into the caves and depends on them for survival. Black puddings, narrow passages and old traces of petrification prolong the escape. Stanley wants George spared. After their rest, **Diontalus deliberately kills him**, making the division within Stanley's body a wound shared by the whole company.[^two-6]

## V. An older kingdom — The books beneath the city

The passages beneath the execution pit lead toward an underground river and a singer who calls himself its king. He demands a service before permitting passage. The company searches the caves, fights creatures that bring headache and dread, and gathers **enchanted beans and a skeleton-marked key**. Their route onward does not depend on winning the singer's favor after all. Planted in an old prepared patch, the beans raise a glowing green beanstalk toward a hatch. Hope's key fits the lock above.

Beyond lies an ancient tiefling complex, strewn with horned skeletons and protected by animated armor and stranger magics. A book in a ritual chamber shows an illustrated figure approaching its reader and pleading for help. The company shuts and binds it rather than immediately trust the invitation, then explores the library. There Hope finds poetry, irrigation knowledge and hints of old houses, while a trapped door unleashes a swarm of flying books. Flames spread among the shelves before the companions win clear.

**P0D scouts through ceiling passages** and reaches a door control, opening the way into an abandoned bureau. Hidden drawers, preserved remains and concealed corridors offer pieces of a history no single room explains. Eventually the search brings them back to the imprisoned figure in the book. Hope follows its written instructions, Vorel supplies a preserved head for the ritual, and Nash uses **Reduce** as they struggle to pull a living elder through the opening.

He first mistakes Hope for a lost queen. After examining her horns and hearing her name, he claims she belongs to **House Akmenos**, heirs of **Tietkalla**. His account brings together a vampire-tainted council, the Night Pasture and soldiers hidden through a **Soulbroker bargain**. The price he admits paying horrifies the company. He leaves through **Château Weir** to retrieve the force he says can restore the kingdom.

The others choose the old prisoner lift over a direct search for the crypt. Nash uses a ladder from his **Robe of Useful Items**, and P0D scouts the active cells above. There **Tedna and Bok Bok are found alive and freed**. Their joy is shadowed by Tedna's report that Angelashi was taken to the pit and killed. Nash has rescued friends, but fears he has reached the woman he loves too late.[^two-7][^two-8]

## VI. The price of betrayal — P0D, the Gem and the petrified throne

The prison rescue is followed by another trap. **Jolt, Buttons and guards** await the party in the keep's food stores, and Buttons discloses a competing deal around the Gem, Monsoon and the Principal. **Monsoon issues Order 26 by radio**: P0D is violently transformed into an enormous hostile creature. Her distress and Nash's attempts to protect her make clear that she is **not choosing freely to betray her friends**. In the fight **Vorel kills Buttons**.

The aftermath is more devastating. Despite Nash pleading for P0D to be calmed or saved, **Diontalus kills her**. Her body disperses as pixie dust. **Tedna**, rescued only recently, dies of severe wounds after a moment of temporary stabilization. A gunman called **Dashington** shoots Hope and is killed; Hope, deeply affected by pixie blush and its hallucinations, kills **Jolt**. Otherworldly visions mingle with Hope's grief and hallucinations. The losses leave the companions' relationship with Diontalus, and with each other, fundamentally shaken.

The party recovers the **Gem of Tides**, and Hope gains **Fox and Scully**, the enchanted paired revolvers, along with their ammunition belt. Vorel proposes the **Pillars of Decency**, a moral framework named for P0D. Behind the keep, an ancient crypt leads down to a pool and pipeworks where the Gem once belonged. The serpent-haired **Kefeli** claims the authority to rule Tietkalla and describes the curses that led her to turn tieflings to stone. Her powers are real, but her version of history and justification remains her own.

Negotiation fails. **Vorel and Hope are petrified** during the confrontation, and Kefeli also turns to stone through an ingenious mirror-and-gaze maneuver. Nash and Stanley destroy Kefeli's petrified body, but their companions remain statues. They enclose Hope and Vorel in protective hollow pillars and leave Bok Bok to guard them. Nash uses clairvoyance to check the old basilisk's remains before the two set out, searching for a cure while tremors run through the underground complex. The two survivors return to an earlier **basilisk corpse** and combine its remains with materials from the old bureau to make a desperate, improvised cure. **Both Hope and Vorel are restored**. The battle above them, however, is still raging.[^two-9][^two-10]

## VII. Through the beast — The Eye of Groxl and the last road to the keep

Back among the burning upper ruins, the party confronts transformed giant pixies and an enormous, changed **froghemoth**. Its massive body swallows them, and the adventure moves literally through its anatomy: mouth, throat, acid, parasites, heart, and finally its electrically active brain. Nash acquires a strange **small extra arm** amid unstable magical effects. Vorel touches the brain and tries to communicate with the intelligence inside.

Reaching that chamber has demanded more than a march through an enormous stomach. Nash ventures into the acid using an altered aquatic form, carrying a light that drives small digestive creatures away. The others swim after him and find another parasite waiting, drawing Vorel toward its silvery thread. Bile flushes threaten to carry them deeper; rope and a pole torn from Nash's robe help keep him from the lower outlet. They climb vein-like supports and force their way through fleshy valves, choosing to pass the electrically charged heart rather than attack it. Inside the brain chamber, Vorel's claim to be the creature's master meets a mind with ambitions of its own.

The beast describes a desire to ascend or transform. Then it divides: **an upper, cocoon-like mass rises into the sky**, while **the lower portion crashes into the desert** and begins dying around the trapped party. The airborne portion's ultimate fate remains unknown. The group fights its way through the failing lower body, and Vorel is drawn to an ancient **living eye**. The **Eye of Groxl embeds itself in Vorel's chest**. It grants formidable combat abilities but possesses an independent will. Vorel's new ability to change the color of his scales is a **separate alteration**, not a property of the eye. Stanley senses the Eye's disapproval when he examines its independent will.[^two-11]

Nash produces a rowboat from the finite patches of his Robe of Useful Items, and the adventurers escape the collapsing creature by the least dignified passage imaginable. They emerge alive in the desert. Vorel retrieves a cache of long-buried treasure—most of which is not carried away—and the group prepares a striking return: **Nash in young-black-dragon form, carrying Hope and the others toward the damaged city**.[^two-12]

## VIII. The crown — A queen and a city still in ruins

They arrive over **devastated Trashburg**. Giant pixies begin reverting to smaller forms. The keep stands breached, its authority broken, but no one can yet say what becomes of every inhabitant. Nash lands the party and smashes open a throne-room wall, bringing them face to face with **Monsoon Cabbage**. A prince lies dead; **Suck Master Din** is held captive; and, against the dreadful news brought by Tedna, **Angelashi is alive**, restrained by Monsoon's servant **Lazlo**.

Monsoon says he is willing to yield power. He warns that **Kefeli's death** has made the city vulnerable to a future claim by **Andreeste**. His warning gives the companions little comfort: even a victory may leave another enemy waiting. Vorel attacks; **Monsoon escapes in mist**, eluding the companions. The group breaks Lazlo's stone grip and **frees Angelashi**, who reunites with Nash. Monsoon's escape leaves the servant inert, but his stone hand still holds her fast. Hope supports her, Stanley works at the fingers and Nash breaks the arm with earth magic. Her rescue requires their cooperation after the supposed ruler has already fled. The dreadful report of her death was wrong.[^two-13]

Then the elder tiefling returns. A portal opens and **hundreds of armed tieflings** march into the hall. The elder has brought the long-hidden force he promised. Before the assembled company, **Hope Akmenos is crowned queen**. Her journey from Dusthill mechanic to the seat of a lost kingdom has become a reality.

Her reign begins in a morally fraught fashion. **Vorel executes the captive Suck Master Din with Hope's approval.** Armed leaders set about securing the ruined city, while others offer to organize farms and repair its buildings. Some openly object to the queen's non-tiefling companions. Hope insists that Nash, Stanley and Vorel have a place at her council, but a crown cannot settle old prejudice, heal the victims of war, or ensure that future orders are carried out justly.

The company speaks of clinics, reconstruction, abandoned belongings, the family of the murdered George, threats beyond the city and the obligations of power. **Most of that work remains to be done**. For now there is celebration, and the hope that peace will give them time to begin.[^two-14][^two-15]

## Epilogue — A kingdom to rebuild

A crown rests on Hope's head, but her friends are haunted and her city lies shattered, full of competing claims. The surviving companions have won the possibility of building something better. Whether they will agree on what “better” means is another story.[^two-16]
