# Arc 03 — Road to a Lost Kingdom

## I. The road from the Liberators

The name **Heroes of the Wastes** had scarcely been chosen before it began to weigh upon them. After the violence inflicted on **Slippery Brent**, **Stanley left the Liberators' camp alone**, unwilling to pretend that the cruelty was another heroic deed. The others sought him out, and their reunion made travel possible again. It did not settle their disagreement about what they might become. **Hope, Nash and Stanley** remained bound by the **Soulbroker's** deadline; **Vorel**, who had refused the compact, still chose to stand beside them.[^arc03-1]

Before departing, Vorel met privately with the **sightless former Koromukishi**. The nameless orc spoke of preserving a fledgling opposition to **the Principal** while the travelers sought a way to close **Esophageal Pass**. Their discussion pointed toward an alliance, not an army already assembled or a passage already sealed. The Liberators were preparing to move after reports that their hiding place had been compromised. With supplies from **Bertrand**, the company readied **Fate 4** and left a camp where even friends could not agree on what victory should mean.[^arc03-2]

**Stanley's first attempt at driving** sent the vehicle jolting forward. A young stowaway emerged from within: **Tedna**, desperate to fight the Principal and earn a place among the heroes. The company disputed his ability to survive their road, but they could not argue him back into the desert before a far stranger threat arrived.

A warrior in **powered armor** descended on mechanical wings. Calling himself **Sir Ranko of the Umbilical Order**, he accused Hope, Nash and Stanley of carrying a demonic stain, said his order could detect such things, and demanded their execution. **Vorel refused to surrender them.** Guns, magic, the armored stranger's small mechanical attackers and the desperate movement of Fate 4 turned the confrontation into a battle across the road. Even after the warrior had been overcome, he spoke of his order's power and released a brilliant signal whose purpose the party could not establish. He was killed in the clash. His claimed authority and any connection to the Principal remained unproven.[^arc03-3]

An explosion overturned Fate 4. The companions and **Bok Bok** survived. **Tedna was alive but unconscious** when they found him in the wreck, a reminder of how little shelter their company could offer a boy seeking adventure. They righted the vehicle and made repairs sufficient to continue, though the damage was far from gone.

That night, **lizardfolk hunters** and their raptor-like animals approached the camp. Their challenger, **Diptak of the Golden Land**, alleged that Vorel had claimed the priestess **Typalla** as a consort, and demanded an answer by combat. Whatever arrangement Diptak believed existed, **Vorel had not been shown agreeing to a marriage**. The hunter charged as the night tipped into battle.[^arc03-4]

The fighting continued through the darkness. Vorel struggled against Diptak and his hunters while the others fought the accompanying beasts. At last **Diptak yielded**. Some of his companions obeyed, though a wounded raptor kept attacking **Hope** until she killed it. Diptak acknowledged Vorel's strength and **pledged his Golden Land clan's allegiance**. It was a pledge spoken by the defeated hunter, not proof that a distant force had marched to war or that the disputed marriage had been settled.[^arc03-5]

The next morning, **Hope and Vorel patched the damaged roof with raptor hide**. It kept some of the desert out without making Fate 4 whole; an argument with Tedna soon left another hole from Stanley's magic. Farther west, the party encountered **Buttons**, an orange cat walking upright, wearing glasses and balancing on a mechanical leg. **Bok Bok** initially regarded him as food. Buttons instead paid for passage and made himself useful with scholarly conversation.

Buttons said he had once been an ordinary cat, altered by a wizard of **Arcanum Universalis**. He described **Trashburg**, the fortress above the oil sands, its gangs, public punishments and the roads around **T'ietkalla**. He copied an older map attributed to **Handsome Dan Dakota** and exchanged knowledge with Stanley. His stories offered leads, not a survey of a city the companions had yet to enter.[^arc03-6]

They **camped outside the Teet**, with no promise yet to explore the ruins Buttons hoped to study. On watch, **Hope heard singing** across the oil sands. Pale blue flashes glimmered in the distance, and invisible hands seemed to pull her away from Fate 4. She walked toward the music while the others remained behind, unaware of what awaited beneath the ground.

## II. Two voices

When **Stanley awoke**, **Nash** was entranced, pressing against Fate 4's closed door as if something outside called him. Stanley **tied him to a workbench for safety**, then discovered that **Hope, Vorel, Tedna, Bok Bok and Buttons** were missing. Tracks led away across the oil sands. Tedna, already drawn from the vehicle, could not be roused by Stanley's attempts to turn him back.

**Buttons** reappeared beside Stanley and joined the search. The little cat thought he might be less vulnerable to the song; neither knew whether that was a rule or a fortunate accident. Together they followed the tracks through a hidden entrance into a prison of old cells and **thorn-decorated doors**. They agreed that **“lemon tree”** would be their signal to flee.[^arc03-7]

Beyond the cells lay a dark **oil lake** and a bone-strewn shore. A monstrous singer, part human-like body and part crawling bulk, drew the missing travelers toward it. Another mouth sang from its flesh. An aged, sharp-eared **hag**, whom the creature called its mother, guarded the prisoners. **Hope, Vorel, Tedna and Bok Bok** stood under the song's hold, their wills unavailable to them even as their bodies could be turned against a rescuer.

Stanley tried words first. The captors talked of feeding upon people and harvesting **“mana waves”**, of ancient wards and a captive long kept below. Those were their explanations, not a history the companions could verify. Stanley refused to leave his friends as prey. When negotiation failed and the hag tried to charm him, he defended himself with spells and his animated sword **Diplomacy**. The captive companions posed dangers they could not knowingly choose. **Bok Bok's dynamite** tore into the singer's body, and Stanley's **Feather Fall** helped prevent a worse fall. The creature was destroyed. Stanley would later recount the hag's death as well, though his companions had not witnessed every blow.[^arc03-8]

As the spell's victims began to stir, a **luminous blue, elven-looking figure** emerged from the oil. He called himself **Diontalus**, an **archfey of carnage**, and spoke of old imprisonment, lost strength and a world he wished to reshape for elves. He offered Stanley power in exchange for the energy he believed the warlock could provide. The claims about ancient wars and his title were **Diontalus's own**.

**Stanley refused.** He would not bind himself to another power that spoke openly of slaughter. Diontalus answered not with persuasion but with force: the figure became blue light, **entered Stanley's body against his will**, and overwhelmed him. The others were only beginning to wake. The encounter did not make Stanley a willing signatory to anything, and his companions could not yet know what he had endured alone.[^arc03-9]

Elsewhere, **Nash** woke from his trance to find himself bound in Fate 4. He used **Reduce** to free himself and searched the oil field for the others. Belowground, the prison shook and began to collapse. Stanley called **“lemon tree”**; Vorel hauled **Stanley and Bok Bok**, while **Hope helped Tedna**, whose boot caught during the escape. Rising oil nearly trapped them. Stanley used an **immovable rod** to free Vorel from a flooded stair, but the device was **left submerged** as the survivors hurried upward. They crossed dangerous oil sands and reunited with Nash at the edge of the field.[^arc03-10]

The others could describe a strangely comforting dream that had held them, and Stanley could speak of the hag and her son. **He did not give everyone a full account of Diontalus's intrusion.** What happened in the prison had left different memories with different travelers. The voice now sharing Stanley's body had not been invited, whatever its plans for him.

Back aboard Fate 4, Buttons discussed his past and **gave Stanley written material for the Blink spell**; receiving it did not prove Stanley could already cast it. They reviewed their borrowed map and the cat's secondhand knowledge of **Trashburg**, its rulers, its violent arena and the keep. They still wanted the promised paired firearms, **Fox and Scully**, but none of Buttons's accounts gave them a safe way inside.

The group chose to **conceal the vehicle outside the Teet** and approach on foot. Beyond the oil sands stood the city that might answer some of their questions. They had not entered it yet. Stanley carried a secret from the prison toward streets in which other people would soon notice that **two wills were struggling for one body**.[^arc03-11]

## III. Promises beneath the keep

The travelers left **Fate 4** hidden outside **the Teet** and entered **Trashburg** on foot. Hunger greeted them before any answers did. Children begged for food, a resident emptied a bucket of waste onto **Vorel**, and **Tedna** limped in makeshift footwear after losing his boot. Men with **26** tattooed on them watched the streets. Buttons had described the city's poverty and competing factions; now the companions saw the conditions for themselves.[^arc03-12]

At **Charlemagne's** inn they found lodging and warnings about the city's gangs. A public celebration drew crowds to hear **Suckmaster Din** announce his son's upcoming wedding to **Jolt**, the woman associated with the title **Acrimonicar**. Food, festivities and a **Blood Ball** match were promised. At the **Pit of Despair**, condemned people were thrown into the dark; the party saw an enormous **tongue** drag down a victim as the crowd cheered. They could not identify the creature below. Neither the wedding nor the match had taken place.[^arc03-13]

Alone in his room, **Nash** found a frightened pixie called **P0D**, named for the label on her aquarium. She begged him not to return her to **Farmer Dewey**. P0D said imprisoned pixies were being turned into the drug **Pixie Blush**. The confession made Nash remember the destruction that drug had helped cause in Slavetown. He offered her shelter, while the companions debated using an already-produced drug to reach the rulers whose trade had exploited its victims. They had **not investigated the farm or rescued the other pixies**; P0D's history was her first-person testimony.[^arc03-14]

Their questions brought them to the **Twenty-Six Block**. Vorel nearly turned the introduction violent, but the gang escorted them through guarded barricades to **Monsoon Cabbage**. Monsoon claimed his crowded headquarters and communal kitchen served the neighborhood. He listened to their hunt for the revolvers **Fox and Scully** and their interest in the keep, then named his price: **kill the Toe Rippers' leader**, and he would reveal a way inside. It was an unfulfilled bargain rather than an alliance secured. **Tedna** separately reported a possible route through wedding catering staff, but had not obtained admission.[^arc03-15]

Other allegiances were less clear. **Bok Bok** wore a severed-toe necklace and spoke of new friends; he was not thereby proved a formally initiated Toe Ripper. **Buttons** privately confronted Stanley about the luminous being he had seen enter him beneath the oil sands. When the cat urged disclosure, **Stanley cast Suggestion** to send him scouting instead. Buttons had not freely chosen that silence. Stanley continued to conceal a violation he himself had never consented to.[^arc03-16]

Charlemagne asked the party to deliver a parcel to his arms-dealer friend **Jedel McCready**. At the damaged shop they found an unidentified body downstairs. Upstairs a twisted, **fanged stranger** sprang at **Hope**. The assault began at the end of one episode, but its long struggle followed in the next: the attacker endured punishment and seemed to regenerate. Hope's **maximum vitality fell** after a bite, though no one had proved she had become a vampire. The stranger was finally defeated and vanished. Hope recovered a **rocket launcher and two large projectiles** in a concealed case. Neither the corpse's identity nor the attacker's species was established.[^arc03-17]

The **Trashburg Gray Socks** then offered a possible route to the fortress through **Blood Ball** against the **Pounders**. They had invited the travelers, not yet played a match or granted access. Stanley's unsettling behavior continued, until a voice from his body declared that he was **Diontalus, not Stanley Billings**. He **admitted forcing his way into Stanley after Stanley rejected his offer**. His ancient history, motives and exact rules of shared control remained his claims, but the coercion was real. Stanley had not signed a second pact.[^arc03-18]

The company trained with the Gray Socks, struggled with the brutal sport and took **provisional jerseys**. A payment and opportunity to enter the keep were still conditional on future play. Monsoon awaited a gang chief's death, the wedding caterers had not admitted them, and **P0D's fellow pixies remained prisoners**. They had learned many possible entrances; they had completed none of them.[^arc03-19]

## IV. Music and the rocket's return

A card-playing promoter named **Fango** invited the companions to the **Golden Gargoyle**, where **Angelashi** would perform. Before they could seek that refuge, Charlemagne heard their account of the ransacked arms-dealer property and unidentified corpse. He feared the dead man was his friend. His grief was real, but the body still lacked an independent identification and nobody had proved who attacked the shop.[^arc03-20]

In Toe Ripper territory, **Vorel attacked a sentry first**. Fighters emerged from the buildings around him; blades, spells and **Nash's dragon-heart acid breath** turned the street into a battlefield. Fire spread and part of a structure collapsed. An alarm sounded from the Teet Guard, but the company did not know who raised it. The casualties were uncertain, and **the gang leader Monsoon demanded was not confirmed dead**. They reached the Golden Gargoyle at **E035's end**, their debt still unpaid.[^arc03-21]

Inside the nightclub, Angelashi's performance briefly drew **Nash** away from their struggles. Then the **Toe Rippers** forced their way inside. Their authority figure **Blunt Goliver**, a lizardfolk in a wheelchair, accused the group of destroying his people. **Vorel called himself the Lizard King**—a challenge, not a title anyone had accepted. **Nash transformed into a black dragon wyrmling** and the companions fought through the crowded room, while their enemies made unverified claims about power gained from consuming grisly trophies.

**Fango showed them a concealed exit** and they escaped through backstage passages and a sewer. They had **not killed Goliver or fulfilled Monsoon's requested assassination**. Angelashi accompanied Nash back toward the inn. Nash was exhausted from transforming; by E036's close, their private encounter was suggested but **not yet played**.[^arc03-22]

Afterward, **Vorel and Bok Bok** attended a clandestine meeting. An armed contact said **Jolt's crew had robbed an Oilbeard safe in Slavetown** during earlier fires, and handed Vorel a heavy **trunk of gold and jewels** as his supposed share. The payment occurred, while the robbery account was secondhand. Vorel took Bok Bok outside town and **buried the trunk under the desert sand**, concealing the disturbed ground. The valuables were hidden, **not carried in the party's packs**.[^arc03-23]

At Charlemagne's, **Nash and Angelashi** spoke of her people's history and a possible connection between his **dragon-metal heart** and the road to a lost home. The histories were her interpretation. Her touch brought forth a **magical flower** and briefly changed Nash's body. Their intimacy was played now, unlike the unfinished invitation at the end of E036. By morning she had departed and Nash had rested after the strain of his earlier transformation.[^arc03-24]

A servant brought **Hope a letter from Tedna**, working inside the keep. He described servants' passages, a cold cellar and a chamber of **terrified tiefling statues**, and reported hearing that **Softhead was alive** and implicated in Dusthill's betrayal. Some details were observed; others came from informants. **Hope kept some pages private**, so her friends did not automatically know every detail. **Charlemagne evacuated his family**, gave **Vorel the inn keys** and advised them to speak with Monsoon. Their lodgings were now an empty property rather than a staffed inn.[^arc03-25]

**P0D** still wanted the other pixies freed. She described seeing Charlemagne with someone resembling Hope, without confirming the person's identity. **Monsoon** arrived, acknowledged the damage inflicted on the Toe Rippers and insisted **Blunt Goliver's head was still required**. He marked an underground approach on their map and offered access to replacement weapons. **Vorel** chose a chainsaw-edged greatsword provisionally called **Mawstorm**. They entered abandoned tunnels, passed a **string-and-bell warning trap** and found a hostile monk ahead.[^arc03-26]

**Hope fired the recovered rocket launcher** toward him. The monk caught or redirected the rocket **back into Hope**. It exploded, hurled her into stone, injured the others and brought part of the cavern roof down. Smoke filled the passage. **Hope was critically injured, not confirmed dead**, and the group had neither escaped the collapse nor killed Goliver.

This was **E037's cliffhanger**. Carrying Hope through the smoke, killing the gang chief and treating her injuries would belong to the next chapter, not a victory already achieved here.[^arc03-27]

## V. The price of the rocket

The rocket had already come back at **Hope**. Its explosion filled the Toe Rippers' underground passage with falling stone, smoke and air so foul that breathing became a struggle. The launcher had been fired at the end of the previous night's action; there was **no second rocket attack** when the companions began searching for a way out. **Hope lay unconscious**, her left side shattered, while **Nash**, **Vorel**, **Stanley** and **Bok Bok** fought for breath and for space to carry her through the wreckage.[^arc03-28]

The danger did not end with the collapse. Enemy fighters pressed into the damaged passages, and the others had to protect Hope while finding air that would sustain them. Stanley acted and spoke as himself during parts of the ordeal, even though **Diontalus** still shared his body. Their efforts were not a simple healing spell that made the harm disappear: they could stop some bleeding and keep her alive, but the blast had ruined tissue, bones and movement in ways the companions could not repair beneath the city.

Deep within the tunnels they encountered **Blunt Goliver** and more Toe Rippers. The leader had survived both the burned streets and the Golden Gargoyle battle; **Monsoon's promised help still depended on his death**. Nash's fire struck the enemies. When Goliver was wounded and begged to live, **Stanley stabbed him** and **Vorel beheaded him**. The company took the severed head with them. Whatever danger Goliver had posed, his plea and the manner of the killing were part of what the heroes had done, not details to smooth away. This was the **E038** killing—not a belated claim that the earlier nightclub escape had finished the bargain.[^arc03-29]

They carried Hope out through dangerous streets. Shrapnel had torn into her face, horn and left side; her **left arm and leg** were gravely damaged, with movement and sensation affected. The others considered where help might be found while avoiding armed patrols. They chose **Monsoon Cabbage**. At his Twenty-Six Block headquarters they **delivered Goliver's head**, and Monsoon examined it closely. With the grim condition of his bargain now met, he arranged medical assistance instead of sending the adventurers back into the streets.[^arc03-30]

**Dr Dickles** assessed Hope and brought her to an improvised clinic for examination and surgery. He told them the shattered bones in her left arm and leg could not be saved using the treatment available. The companions questioned his ability to help but stayed. Through hours of operations, **Nash, Vorel and Stanley** assisted, using their own abilities where they could. Healing magic helped injured tissue and blood loss; **it did not restore the destroyed limbs**. The doctor amputated **Hope's left arm and left leg** as part of the effort to save her life. She was not made whole by an effortless miracle.[^arc03-31]

Near the end of the long surgery, **Metal Mandy** came to begin fitting **mechanical replacements**, and a healer called **Sister Sarah** was involved in Hope's care. The company could hope she would wake and learn to use them. The immediate medical ordeal, however, ended with Hope still recovering under care. The first real tests of her new mechanical arm and leg—and the danger that awaited her caregivers—were still ahead.

## VI. Hope rebuilt and Monsoon's stories

While **Hope** remained under treatment, **Stanley** helped care for patients at Dr Dickles's clinic. His gentleness with strangers stood in sharp contrast to the voice of **Diontalus**, which had spoken so casually of violence. **Nash** and **Vorel** watched the streets until an armed force moved through Trashburg and the sound of an official announcement changed their plans. The Teet authorities named **“Stanley Billings, also called Diontalus”** as a dangerous wanted spellcaster, claiming that an Arcanum Universalis mage had reported an attempted murder. That **public accusation** was real; the asserted crime had **not been independently proved**. The companions feared **Buttons** might have informed on Stanley, remembering how Stanley had compelled the cat with Suggestion, but the exact route by which the authorities learned his name remained uncertain.[^arc03-32]

The men withdrew to a **skull-marked safe house** connected with Vorel's earlier secret meeting. Hope was not there. They debated hiding, surrendering, and whether fighting through the city would endanger the very people they hoped to protect. Stanley resisted reckless destruction while the ruthless voice sharing his body argued differently. Armed patrols and curfews narrowed their options.

**Hope awoke apart from them**, under **Metal Mandy's** care. She was fitted with a **mechanical left arm and leg**, neither immediately comfortable nor a simple replacement for the body she had lost. Mandy adjusted their movement and demonstrated a grip stronger than ordinary flesh. A **concealed projectile mechanism** lay in the arm, making use of a recovered rocket. Hope could operate her new limbs, but learning to do so was a private and difficult beginning, not evidence that she had fought with them during the preceding cave escape. The original external launcher was no longer available according to this episode's account.[^arc03-33]

Rest and medication left Hope with fragmented impressions of another horror at the clinic. She remembered an assault in which **Metal Mandy** and **Sister Sarah** appeared to be attacked, then awoke in a guarded room under the Twenty-Sixers' control. **Monsoon Cabbage told her that he had ordered Dr Dickles, Sister Sarah and Metal Mandy killed**, arguing that they might betray his arrangements. Hope had heard an admission of responsibility; her sedated flashes were **not a reliable, complete view of every victim's fate or each attack**. The man whose protection had allowed her surgery now described the destruction of those who had cared for her as a useful precaution. A stone-bodied servant, **Lazlo**, helped her through her uncertain recovery.[^arc03-34]

As darkness came, the others made a diversion and slipped between patrols into **Twenty-Six Block** territory. They were at last **reunited with Hope** inside Monsoon's guarded headquarters. He claimed that Teet forces had raided **Charlemagne's** and that **Buttons had given away their identities**. He also said Tedna and others were detained in the keep. Parts of the city's official search were evident to the party, but Monsoon's intelligence about every captive and the cat's exact role remained his report, not a prison roster they had inspected.

Monsoon then spoke at length over **Blunt Goliver's head**. He asserted that a **Lactid exile** had been controlling the gang chief, explaining why his behavior and resistance to magic had seemed strange. He described a lost tiefling homeland called **T'ietkalla**, a figure called **Kefeli**, and **Andreeste**, the alleged dark power behind the Guzzle Gang's religion. Monsoon's account connected an old curse, the **Principal**, **Jesus Chrome** and a kingdom he wished to reclaim. It could help the company choose its next road, but these were the histories and interpretations of an ambitious political actor. Even the further name he offered as his own was not proof of every royal claim that accompanied it.[^arc03-35]

He proposed an approach into the fortress **beneath the Pit of Despair**, whose monstrous resident he called a **froghemoth**. He also speculated that **Jolt** intended to steal the **Gem of Tides** during the coming wedding, and that the gem could transform contaminated oil into water. The company had not seen the gem or witnessed a robbery. Monsoon wanted an alliance and the return of what he called his country; the travelers had reasons to doubt both his candor and his methods. **P0D's captive pixies** and **Tedna's uncertain situation** remained unfinished obligations.[^arc03-36]

They gathered supplies and chose clothes from Monsoon's storage rooms. The idea was to slip through streets watched by soldiers and reach the Pit. They ended **E039 with disguises and a plan**, not with a successful entry into the Teet or the rescue of anyone held there.

## VII. Toward the Pit

The company rode the **Twenty-Six Block's concealed elevator** back toward the dark streets. **Monsoon's proposed route** led south to the **Pit of Despair**, but Teet patrols and Guzzle Gang soldiers were searching the houses between them and the entrance. **P0D** scouted ahead, bringing reports of guarded routes. The fortress's great **spotlights** swung across rooftops and alleys, making each open crossing a risk. **Diontalus**, speaking through Stanley's body, cast an illusion to look like a **Teet Guard**. The uniform was appearance, not a transfer of loyalty.[^arc03-37]

**Hope's new mechanical left leg** could not yet carry her quickly. **Vorel carried her** as they advanced, making a noisy and unusual silhouette. **Nash** cast Darkvision and followed while the others briefly spread out. They passed a sleeping sentry and glimpsed soldiers searching civilian homes, looking for Stanley. Their route was not a triumphant infiltration: innocent people were being detained while the guards hunted one of the travelers.[^arc03-38]

Ahead of the others, Diontalus met a pair of **Guzzle Gang** searchers who despised the Teet Guard. He changed his **illusory uniform again**, now resembling a Guzzle member, and offered to help them look for the fugitive. A magical noise served as his diversion. It bought time, but his companions could not automatically recognize Stanley inside the borrowed appearance. The trick put one member of the company among their pursuers while the others struggled to pass outside.[^arc03-39]

A spotlight caught **Vorel, Hope and Nash**. They offered awkward false names, but the challenged guard summoned reinforcements. Vorel put Hope down and revealed himself. What had begun as stealth became a running **street battle**. Vorel grew larger and fought through gunfire, Hope fired her shotgun, and Nash used bolts of fire and a **Fireball** against approaching enemies. A magical sphere of darkness spread across part of the alley, cutting sightlines and confusing patrols. Diontalus remained disguised for part of the struggle, trying to turn enemies away from his companions rather than fighting openly beside them.[^arc03-40]

The Teet and Guzzle forces kept arriving. **Vorel's chainsaw-edged sword** and bursts of flame struck opponents; Hope fought despite the strain of her new limbs. Nash used more magic to slow the pursuit. Two guards jokingly called **“the two Phillips”** disappeared into the confusion of the dark area; the party could not inspect their fate, and no certain death should be added to their story. Diontalus persuaded some of his apparent Guzzle allies to circle away. When Stanley sent Hope a message identifying himself, it prevented her from mistaking his disguise for another enemy.[^arc03-41]

Finally, for a moment, **no hostile soldiers remained immediately in their path**. The larger search had not ended. With the fortress's **spotlight still following them**, the companions **ran toward the Pit of Despair**.

They had **not yet entered or descended into the Pit**. They had not faced the creature beneath its bars, found a hidden grate or met the pursuing guard **George**. Those events still waited beyond this episode's boundary. **Arc 03 ended with the heroes in motion, pursued and exposed, on the road toward the place Monsoon said might lead below the fortress.**[^arc03-42]
