### Recognition and stable features

The documented introduction presents a heavily modified RV on monster-truck
wheels, built by Hope with Jimster. Preserve its recognizable RV identity
and conspicuously oversized wheels. This is the supported broad form, not
a complete or independently inspected vehicle model.

The designer's sheet lists a 10-foot width and 30-foot length. Preserve
these as **design-document specifications**, not as measured played-scene
dimensions or a guarantee that every later vehicle state is identical.

### State: WASTES-FATE4-A01 — introduction

- **Drawable baseline:** A heavily modified recreational vehicle on
  monster-truck wheels. Keep the RV body and exaggerated wheel scale
  identifiable in the project's heavy-ink, slightly cartoony style.
- **Documented design reference:** S02 gives intended width/length,
  CB radio, roof access and passenger facilities. Exact body contours,
  wheel/axle count, suspension, markings, color and interior positioning
  have not been extracted from the images in this pass.
- **Color and materials:** Vehicle colors and specific materials remain
  unresolved from the sources. Trial D02 explores ivory/sage and coral;
  this is not an established Fate 4 paint scheme or selected model sheet.
- **Condition and equipment:** Do not add battle damage, later upgrades
  or occupants from later scenes. The broad reference alone does not
  specify every accessory present at unveiling.
- **Scene scope:** A neutral exterior study can test broad silhouette;
  a literal E006 scene needs a further check of surroundings and
  participants. An interior or precise multi-view reference needs
  direct comparison of the design sheets and floorplan.
- **Continuity limit:** A01 is not a standing instruction for all later
  Fate 4 scenes. The item index records later destruction, but that
  event and intervening changes are outside this pilot's evidence review.
- **Selected reference images:** None. Existing source images below
  are evidence leads, not newly accepted model sheets.
