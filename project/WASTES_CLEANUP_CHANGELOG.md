## When to add a new entry

Add one **brief dated row** only for a material milestone, decision, publication/approval, important source correction affecting several tasks, or significant blocker. Link the relevant `WCL-###`, [decision](DECISIONS.md) or evidence source. For routine source audits, readbacks, lint results and individual checkoffs, update **only the task entry**. Do not introduce a second task status, duplicate long change report, repeated SHA list, or a new `reports/` document without independent research value.
