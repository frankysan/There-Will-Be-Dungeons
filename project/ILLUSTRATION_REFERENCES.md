## Wastes: Dusthill

**Active subject reference:** [Dusthill — settlement appearance and continuity by story state](ILLUSTRATION_DUSTHILL_REFERENCE.md), v0.2 (2026-10-25). **ID:** `WASTES-DUSTHILL` (place); aliases `Dust Hill` (ASR/written variants). [Source audit](../reports/ILLUSTRATION_WASTES_DUSTHILL_AUDIT_2026-10-25.md).

**Recognition:** A **small, concealed village in a red-rock mountain alcove**; giant boulders form a natural defensive rampart around a **winding, easily missed entrance**. There is a defended gate and rock-top positions, **mud huts**, a few long-used **wood/metal communal barns**, a **central valve-operated water outlet**, pictograms on village rock, rear **cave farms** with irrigation, and a rock-excavated salvage garage. A tent-side clearing/fire pit is identified in the E005 scene. This is not a documented street/floor plan.

**Time states:** `WASTES-DUSTHILL-A01` (opening E001 village, before raid) and `WASTES-DUSTHILL-A02` (E005 attack and its immediate aftermath, through E006 assembly/departure). E005 refuge behind a barricade in the dark-farm caves, casualties and damage are established; exact ruined buildings or fires are **not**. The working surface outlet and Fate 4 departure are specifically **E006**. The town's later physical condition is unresearched. Use [the full state instructions](ILLUSTRATION_DUSTHILL_REFERENCE.md#appearance-state-timeline), not an undated generic-desert-town design.

**Verification / impact:** GM opening narration directly compared across two ASR exports; selected E005 primary windows read; other material inherited with explicit limits. **No original audio listened to.** Campaign-end JSON notes treated only as reference leads. **New:** the actual pixels of a handwritten Dusthill settlement plan at `local/images-wiki/maps/Dusthill.webp` (formerly `DVJ4V1JVwAErBl-.webp`) were inspected on 2026-10-25. Owner recollection (2026-10-25) suggests **probable GM origin for all six renamed maps, not confirmed**; author/date/era remain unverified. The complete current filename and checksum catalog is in [IMAGE_ASSETS.md](IMAGE_ASSETS.md#current-private-map-collection--renamed-2026-10-25). A **separate Dusthill Caverns map** at `local/images-wiki/maps/Dusthill Caverns.webp` was also visually inspected and should not be confused with the surface settlement. The map supports distinct gathering circle, well, school, Hillmaster hut, barracks, trading post, two farm sectors and garage/rock door as labeled sites. Consult the [map findings](ILLUSTRATION_DUSTHILL_REFERENCE.md#newly-inspected-dusthill-plan-2026-10-25). The previously owner-selected E001 aerial illustration is under [IRV-0002](ILLUSTRATION_REVISION_QUEUE.md#irv-0002--e001-dusthill-aerial-versus-newly-found-map) for **provisional layout review before publication**, not automatically rejected. Selected E006 Fate 4 garage image was checked by file identity/metadata, not pixels. No approved standalone Dusthill model sheet.

## Wastes: Jimster's garage in Dusthill

**Active subject reference:** [Jimster's garage — physical layout and scene states](ILLUSTRATION_JIMSTERS_GARAGE_REFERENCE.md), record v0.1 (2026-10-25).
**ID:** `WASTES-JIMSTERS-GARAGE` (place). This is Dusthill's cliff-excavated
mechanical shop, with an open shelf-divided town-facing workshop area and
a concealed mountain-face vehicle exit. The E067 retrospective scene
describes **two large rock-integrated garage doors**; the full physical
connection of these features is unmeasured and must not be represented as
a verified floorplan.

**Recognizable fixtures:** red-rock excavation, metal shelving, salvaged
mechanical and irrigation materials, secured weapons, workbench,
compressed-air tools, dust/oil, and makeshift crate seating at relevant
stages. The E001 workshop contains a tiny Smart-car-like vehicle and the
shabby, gigantic-tired RV in construction. Earlier E067 memory shows an
ordinary-tired RV at the back under canvas, with oversized tires in a
corner; E006 describes a secured, tarp-covered RV before the unveiling.
These are **different source-supported states**, not one simultaneous
canonical arrangement.

**States:** `WASTES-JIMSTERS-GARAGE-A00` (earlier Hope memory), `-A01`
(E001 construction), `-A02` (E005/E006 secured shop) and `-A03`
(E006 final preparation). See the [detailed timeline and evidence](ILLUSTRATION_JIMSTERS_GARAGE_REFERENCE.md#appearance-state-timeline)
for scene-specific Fate 4 state pairings. **ASR-text-based and
integrated**, no audio verification, no selected model sheet, and no
invented absolute measurements. Source-supported visual uncertainty is
retained as explicitly provisional creative decisions.
