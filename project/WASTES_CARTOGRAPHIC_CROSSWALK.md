## Principal City is not the mapped territory

**Principal City** is the owner-approved form for a city associated with the Principal, while **The Principal's Lands** is the much broader **map-inscribed territorial label**. The city name itself has **not been independently confirmed as a printed inscription on the v3 JPEG**. Do not map a city point from the territory label or collapse municipal limits into the land's regional borders. The older `Principle City` is a source/search alias, not a separate city. See the [compound-name audit](../reports/PRINCIPAL_RELATED_NAMES_AUDIT_2026-10-27.md).

Evidence for the city has **different epistemic tiers**:

- [E026](../episodes/E026.md): **Bertrand reports** Lactid Cartel activity below the city's spires. The companions **do not enter or survey** that undercity. A reported organization is not all Lactid people or the entire city's population.
- [E106](../episodes/E106.md): The Principal and Brett **claim or show illustrative visions** of attacks on the city. Their claims/illusions are not an independently explored city history.
- [S3E13](../episodes/S3E13.md): The heroes **actually fly toward the ruined city's northern vicinity and witness ruptures, lava and purple geysers**. Flying near a ruined city **is not walking through its streets or confirming every district**.
- [S3E019](../episodes/S3E019.md): Beau retrospectively describes **unused planned city exploration**, three finger-named spires, an underground Lactid society, a railway confrontation and a possible Arcanum Universalis citadel survivor. This is **unused creator preparation**, not an unrecorded played expedition, measured city layout or confirmed surviving citadel.

Because the maintained [places index](../world/PLACES.md#historic-secondary-and-mobile-sites) already distinguishes **Principal City / The Principal's Lands** and links to its controlling sources, no unsupported standalone city stub is required for this map-label pass.

## Other maps and outstanding questions

*The Known Wastes* v3 is **not the same artifact** as the two versions of *Beyond the Deadfingers*, the separately drawn *Dusthill* surface and cavern maps, *Slavetown*, or *The Teet and Trashburg*. They differ in scale, subject, provenance or annotations. Those six private, wiki-sourced images are individually listed in the [image asset register](IMAGE_ASSETS.md); their local file hashes there do **not** establish alignment to this large v3 map. The smaller *Beyond the Deadfingers — Annotated* image includes **Dusthill, Moag Ferhain, Slavetown and Weeping Dunes** in the earlier review, but has an **unverified annotation date**. [Source comparison](../reports/WASTES_KNOWN_MAP_ORTHOGRAPHY_2026-10-27.md#cross-reference-to-the-pre-existing-six-private-maps).

**Open for future direct verification:** other small-print inscriptions; production/depicted era and which handout was shown to players; exact coordinates and region boundaries; relationship of Moag Ferhain to the underground ruin; role of the mapped resource districts; possible map revisions; real-world illustrator and rights. **No new map artwork, asset publication, coordinates, routes or named settlements were created** for WCL-068/WCL-069.
