## Recognition baseline — exterior and identity

**For the E006 unveiling:** a **modified RV/camper body lifted high above
conspicuously oversized monster-truck wheels**, built by Hope with Jimster.
Its front carries a distinctive **fabricated metal dragon-skull-like feature**,
not an actual animal skull. The roof carries **solar panels** (number and
placement unverified), a usable **roof access route/hatch** and deliberate
**grab bars/handholds** extending to the sides. **Exterior metal railings**
allow occupants to hold on and move around the outside during combat. A **small
ladder at the rear** provides roof access. These are essential recognition
elements, not generic RV decoration. [S01, S04, S06]

E010 dialogue supports an approximately **10-foot width**; the **30-foot
length** is sourced only to the creator-design sheet. The precise make,
coach shape, paint, window/door positions, number and configuration of
axles, suspension, solar-panel pattern, hatch orientation and metal finish
are **not verified**. Do not substitute the E057 interceptor's rust-brown
paint, chained tires or exposed front engine for Fate 4. [S02, S06, S12, S15]

**Materials and color:** The dragon-skull front is described as metal;
a later character says the RV contains steel. Neither statement proves a
uniformly armored or steel-bodied vehicle. Its intrinsic paint colors remain
open. The ivory/sage/coral scheme is an experimental production decision
(D02), never show canon. The solar panels do not establish solar propulsion,
silent running or a specified battery arrangement.

## Interior and equipment at E006 introduction

Describe the E006 interior as an improvised, weapon-packed living space,
not a standard recreational vehicle showroom. The cab has **a driver's
seat but no ordinary front passenger seat**: that space contains guns.
A **removable skull-shaped gearshift** doubles as a spike, separate from
the large external dragon-skull front; the glove compartment contains
guns/ammunition and other spaces hold tools and scrap. A **wall magnet**
holds javelins. **Front and rear CB radios** connect occupants; there is
**no installed PA system** (E010). [S05, S06]

The vehicle has a **30-gallon water store**, roughly a month's food capacity,
**four places to sleep** formed using a convertible sofa-bed and floor
mattresses, a **hot plate** and a **toilet/bathroom**. A sofa near a window
and cupboards appear in later scenes; an interior **workbench near the door**
is identifiable by E029. A **cooler** is located by E028, but is not proof
of a fitted refrigerator. An RV-style table and seating are clearly used in
E065; do not automatically backdate their exact configuration to E006.
Jimster's compass and binoculars are supplied portable objects, not built-in
mechanisms. [S05, S06, S11, S13]

For an accurate interior diagram, use verified dimensional/layout evidence
from the actual images **only after direct inspection**. The creator
two-page design and the separately sourced floorplan have unresolved
correspondence, date and version; do not silently merge them. [S02, S03]

## Complete drawable state instructions

The matrix above defines applicable changes; use these standalone state
instructions for briefs. Details common to later states are kept only when
the scene supports the vehicle's identity, **not** as proof every roof
fitting survived every injury.

- **A00 — origin:** A worn, musty/greasy, ordinary-tired RV in disrepair,
  before its monster-wheel lift or Hope's established exterior modifications.
  The later dragon-skull front, solar fit and combat bars must not be
  backdated. Cassette-player use is shown in the memory; its continued
  installation in E006 is unconfirmed.
- **A00A–A00B — construction:** A shabby RV body being lifted onto oversized
  monster tires; at the E001 moment the last wheel is being installed. In
  E005 work continues. Exterior front ornament, final roof equipment,
  paint, passenger furnishing and build completion at these cutoffs remain
  unknown; avoid treating the finished E006 vehicle as a direct workshop photo.
- **A01 — introduction:** Raised monster-wheel RV with a metal dragon-skull
  front, roof solar panels, roof access and rear ladder, purpose-built
  exterior combat handholds/rails. Its home-built interior has one driver
  seat, weapons where a passenger seat normally sits, skull gearshift,
  radios, magnet, sofa/mattresses, stored food and water, hot plate and
  toilet. Paint and exact body geometry are undecided. **No later holes,
  raptor-hide patches or wreckage.**
- **A02 — breakdown:** The recognizable raised RV, its monster tires and
  front skull still the identity cues; now disabled with front-engine smoke,
  a recent explosion and shrapnel damage. Do not use E066 crushing/fire
  wreckage to depict this recoverable failure.
- **A03 — punctured and left:** The tall, skull-front, monster-wheeled RV
  with at least one punctured/damaged tire; substantial body otherwise
  described as intact when recovered in a lot. Exact puncture side, roof
  condition and roadworthiness unresolved. Avoid showing a fully repaired
  tire at the E017 abandonment.
- **A04 — repaired return:** Powered tall RV with large monster tires and
  a sunlit/shining skull front; visible former damage is repaired by E026,
  with restored mobility. Retain roof solar/rails only within conservative
  known-fit interpretation; their exact repaired state has not been
  individually checked.
- **A05 — torn-open roof:** Monster-wheel RV with its dragon-skull identity,
  overturned/blast-damaged and a major tear along the upper driver-side roof
  and windshield region. Depict the restored normal size unless showing
  the **short-lived external shrinking spell**. No raptor-hide cover yet.
- **A06 — hide patch:** Raised RV with a visible **stitched irregular
  raptor-skin/scale covering**, nailed over the earlier roof opening, not
  a neat riveted metal panel. A person cannot safely stand on it. In scenes
  after the later E028 blast, show an additional distinct hole in the wall
  if its actual visibility/orientation can be established; location beyond
  "behind Tedna" is not verified here.
- **A07 — described fixed-up vehicle:** Recognizable monster-wheel,
  skull-front RV said to be intact/repaired by E057/E059, but the extent and
  appearance of repaired roof, side openings, solar panels and interior are
  **unspecified**. Do not presume that a previous hide cover vanished
  or that every fitting was restored without a scene-specific check.
- **A08 — storm interior:** Tall monster-tire RV in wet sand, wheels
  slipping/sinking; interior seating/table visibly overloaded and
  damaged, driver's seat unstable. Do not reinterpret the surface traction
  as a permanently broken tire.
- **A09–A10 — destroyed:** No intact camper silhouette: immense crushing
  has folded the metal body, with wheels flung away. For A10 add later
  explosion/fire traces; keep salvageable tires, axles and a seat consistent
  with E066's later retrieval when composing an aftermath image.
