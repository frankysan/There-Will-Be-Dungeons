## Identity, source weighting, and recognition

- **Opening/played baseline:** E001 presents Hope as Dusthill's conspicuously **horned**, optimistic **tiefling woman** and a working **mechanic**, building a vehicle intended to hold a dragon engine. At approximately 00:49 the GM says she and others are likely in **work clothes**, contrasted with Stanley's fine clothes. No specific workwear cut or color is established by that exchange (S01–S02).
- **Character-entered D&D Beyond sheet:** both archival JSON exports identify the same sheet character ID and agree on **maroon skin, purple hair, gold eyes, 5 ft 6 in, 140 lb, age 32** (S03). These are **terminal snapshot** values, not proof that the full palette, precise weight, height or age was narrated in E001. A character-entered “Look” note says she has **armor constructed from old vehicle parts** and wears **a necklace, her sole keepsake from earlier life**. The note may reflect the player's long-held concept but has no sourced equipment-acquisition date; do not force conspicuous armor onto a scene where the GM says work clothes (S04).
- **Player-created artwork:** a blue monochrome drawing attributed by filename to **Scott Johnson** shows a smiling adult woman with two prominent outward-curled ram-like horns, pointed ears, swept-back/short-looking hair, round forehead goggles, practical layered costume with shoulder pieces, waist gear and high boots (S05). It is an undated **artist/player-adjacent impression**, not an observed E001 costume or palette, nor an owner-approved new production anchor. Scott is a cast member/artist but plays Nash, not Hope; this image must not be misidentified as Kristen's original design.
- **Other archived images inspected:** Ron Tang's undated frontal portrait has large ribbed horns pointing outward and forward, long dual braids, earrings and a work-style bib; the matching rear/three-quarter drawing has a single long tail, back pockets and dangling tools (S06). Casey Boyd's illustration has purple skin, gold eyes, **pale/whitish hair**, curled horns, long tail, blue top, tan work trousers and armament (S07). Dingo's *Cybernetic Hope* depicts mechanical pieces but mixes apparent anatomical sides (robotic arm on **Hope's right**, leg on **Hope's left** in a near-frontal composition); it is neither a side-accurate blueprint nor proof of the depicted date (S08). All are external fan/artist interpretations with undetermined era, reuse rights and permissions.
- **Tiefling anatomy as reference, not invented Hope canon:** 2014 D&D 5e official rules describe a broadly humanlike body, variable horns, long thick tail, pointed canine teeth, solid-color eyes and often dark hair; the palette allows red and ordinary human skin tones. The later 2024 rules add distinct Abyssal, Chthonic and Infernal legacy descriptions but **do not retcon The Wastes' own tiefling creation story or impose a bloodline/classification on Hope** (S09). In E000 Kristen describes the campaign's tieflings arising from a historical pact to fight dragons; preserve this setting-specific account rather than importing generic Forgotten Realms history (S10).
- **Height source ranking:** the external fan wiki lists **5 ft 8 in**, versus two matching D&D Beyond exports' **5 ft 6 in** (S11, S03). **New direct E088 check:** when asked about Hope's height while getting clothing, Kristen answers **“five six, I want to say”**, adding that she would have to check (~00:44:54–00:45:02, S19). This directly supports **approximately 5 ft 6 in** as the player-used dimension in E088, although the spoken value is explicitly tentative and not proof of E001 measurement. Preserve the wiki disagreement in provenance rather than averaging it.

**Stable recognition brief:** adult, practically built/competent tiefling engineer with **two natural horns before E037/E038**, distinctly nonhuman head silhouette, expressive warm confidence and an active mechanic's posture. Her literal skin/hair/eye colors are strong character-sheet **candidates** (maroon / purple / gold) that fit 2014 tiefling possibilities; there is no first-scene transcript confirmation of exact hues. A tail is strongly appropriate via both species reference and multiple specific fan designs, but its own initial-show narration is not yet located. Treat fixed tail shape, teeth prominence, ears, facial contours, horn geometry, haircut and workwear design as selectable visual designs unless verified separately.

## E088/A05 resurrection transformation — required independent variant

**The E088 transformation must not be modeled as the earlier A03 cybernetic Hope with her eyes open.** Kidigree's mirror produces a **new physical body** while her old inert corpse is still present on a stone slab. At ~00:32:02–00:32:36, the table explicitly checks her returned arm and leg, whether her scars were removed (GM: **no**) and whether the gun/metal prostheses still exist (GM: **no — “full organic”**). Kristen has Hope stare at her new limbs, moving the hand and inspecting the feet. Independently transcribed by Parakeet and Qwen (S16–S17); no audio listening in this pass.

**Complete drawable A05 anatomy:** recognizable adult female tiefling Hope, biological **left arm and leg**, biological right arm/leg, with retained older scars rather than pristine skin; **no mechanical left arm, mechanical left leg or built-in rocket gun**. Any metallic horn also needs careful review: the blanket “full organic” description points against retaining a metal horn, but **no explicit new natural horn shape, horn count, or scar pattern is individually narrated** in the inspected exchange. Preserve two-horned tiefling recognition as a working design assumption only; source-check the horn before approving A05 close-up. Physical identity resembles the formerly living Hope overall, but the removal of prostheses is an explicit exception. Her arm/leg scars are **not all erased**. Exact scar locations/extent cannot be invented from this scene.

**Costume milestones within A05 (not permanent additional anatomy states):** (1) **E088 mirror arrival:** naked by explicit table choice at ~00:33:03–00:33:13, with older inert body nearby; no old outfit or equipment on new body. (2) **E088 ~00:44:53–00:45:25:** Nothing supplies a simple **tunic and trousers**, slightly small, and Hope puts them on; the Soggy Feather cape stays with Stanley. (3) **Late E088:** Lohar tailors and delivers Hope a daring **black-and-gold** formal outfit with bag/jewelry preferences (uncertain “Manchester” style label; inherited E088 dossier); only use when that scene time applies. (4) **E089:** she also possesses practical clothes and **studded leather armor** during the later equipment-restocking period (inherited dossier); these should not appear during the mirror arrival. **Scully** is recovered only later in E089, not held at E088 emergence (S18).

**Profile/back-sheet requirements:** create a new **A05 organic-restoration variant**, retaining only justified identity anchors (face/eye color, horn geometry if established, tail, scale) from the original design, **without transplanting A03 machinery or early outfit**. Include evidence-led scar pattern once established, not random extra scars. The black/gold Sigil formal costume and practical armored outfit are separate clothing options on the same A05 body and need era-specific labels.

**Height note:** E088 Kristen gives **approximately 5 ft 6 in**, explicitly tentatively, agreeing with the same character's terminal D&D Beyond sheets and outweighing the fan wiki's 5 ft 8 in for design scaling (S19). No proof the dimension changed in the resurrection.

## E001/A01 drawable design-sheet brief

**Reserved sheet ID:** WASTES-HOPE-AKMENOS-DS-001, baseline research brief v0.2 (A01 unchanged; A05 resurrection clarified). **Status:** sheet-needed; the [E001 progressive sheet plan](../reports/ILLUSTRATION_HOPE_E001_SHEET_PLAN_2026-10-25.md) was **BRIEF_READY**; the [first E001 face-study attempt](../reports/ILLUSTRATION_HOPE_E001_FACE_ATTEMPT_2026-10-25.md) is **AWAITING_OWNER_REVIEW**, with no face anchor or model sheet approved. Recurring major PC who appears repeatedly; an owner-approved sheet is required before new scene/group depictions.

- **Face:** adult tiefling woman with symmetrical *natural* horns in A01, clearly separate from hair, eyebrow and ear silhouettes. Expression reads fundamentally optimistic, curious, competent and capable of firmness. The outward-curving ribbed horn family seen in Scott's and Ron Tang's images is a strong **candidate design language**, but no exact on-air horn dimensions/curvature were verified. Proposed skin **deep maroon**, iris/eye treatment **gold**, hair **purple** following final-sheet candidate fields; prompt/review must label these choices as provisional E001 continuity, not episode-established colors. **Hair length is disputed by art** (Scott short/swept, Ron Tang long twin braids, Casey light-color short).
- **Front / complete figure:** average humanlike tiefling stature, sturdy and athletic enough for physical garage labor (build beyond that is an art choice). Work-ready utilitarian top, trousers, tough boots and tool-access pockets, with modest salvage-metal details if desired. Clothing colors and silhouette are **unselected**. Car-part armor and keepsake necklace are **character notes**, not necessarily externally visible at the E001 meal; render as optional working design details pending owner selection. No automatic royal crown or heavy late-campaign combat hardware.
- **Both anatomical profiles:** preserve fixed horn implantation/curvature and ear length; show one coherent tail root/length; all limbs and both horns biological. Record tail placement and hairstyle chosen in the first approved anchor, then mechanically reproduce both left and right views rather than mirrored equipment.
- **Back:** same attire, horns, hair, boot structure and tail; the tool-bearing/workwear vocabulary can be guided by Ron Tang's rear sketch as a noncanonical reference. Avoid unselected weaponry and conspicuous external gear from E033 onward.
- **Palette:** house graphic-novel / modern adult-animation style v2, smooth variable-weight dark ink, muted workwear neutrals with restrained accents; exact body colors from the terminal JSON **proposed but not locked**. No new finished design or image selection is implied.
- **Preserve at E001:** adult female tiefling identity; visible **two intact organic horns**, **two organic arms and legs**; hands/feet functional for workshop labor; optimism and mechanic role. Any selected tail/eye/ear details must be consistent across later views.
- **Exclude from E001:** rocket launcher acquired E033; **all left prostheses and metallic left horn after E037–E039**; concealed forearm rocket; Tietkalla royal crown/regalia; right Hand of the Voice acquired E101; end-game upgraded armor, mask, rapier, magical items; appearance changes suggested solely by undated late fan art.
- **Opening dimensions:** Kristen estimates approximately 5 ft 6 in during E088, agreeing with the final sheet. The fan wiki differs at 5 ft 8 in; do not claim an exact measured E001 height. Different-era hairstyles and outfits should not be combined into a falsely sourced hybrid.
