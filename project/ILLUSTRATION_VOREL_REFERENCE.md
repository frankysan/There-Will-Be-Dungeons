## Identity and recognition

Vorel, whose full name is **Vorel Rasvim Kurik** in the owner-supplied late character sheet, is a **male-presenting lizardfolk barbarian**. Kyle's performed **E000 ~00:11:41** introduction describes **seven feet** of exceptional strength, **rocky scales and muscle**, **broad shoulders**, a **brawny swagger** and a body exposed to hard **sand and sun**. He arrives from desert lizardfolk lands with a **massive bloody haunch of meat on one shoulder**, trading for water. A recognizably reptilian, not human-headed, form is integral. A **long tail** is confirmed as actual anatomy in E022 (it catches a ritual glyph). At the beginning of the played campaign he wears **no loincloth or clothing**; Kyle and Beau explicitly discuss this at **E001 ~00:48:55–00:49:09**, with reptile-specific, folded external anatomy. This point is corroborated retrospectively in **September 2019 Q&A ~00:08–00:10**, where Kyle says his idea of formal innkeeper clothing was never performed.

**Appearance vs late sheet:** Two supplied D&D Beyond exports, `sources/json/vorel.json` and `sources/json/1075054--Vorel Rasvim Kurik.json`, report the same character ID and `dateModified=2022-10-15T23:22:44.333Z`. They are **overlapping final-snapshot sources, not independent period witnesses**. The owner-entered character fields read `data.name=Vorel Rasvim Kurik`, `gender=Lizardman`, `age=23`, `hair=None`, `eyes=Yellow`, `skin=Golden Scales`, `height=7 foot`, `weight=280` (presumed pounds, unit not stored in the visible field). `data.notes.personalPossessions` reads **“Butt naked nothing”**; `data.traits.appearance` is null. The `race` object is generic **legacy Volo's Guide** rules data, *not* custom description of Vorel. `data.classes` lists **Barbarian / Path of the Storm Herald, level 16** at snapshot time, not E001 level. The sheet's `Eye of Groxl`, +2 shield and +2 longsword are **late-sheet inventory**, not early props. **Golden scales are independently established for E001** by Kyle's direct answer in E001 ~00:30:52, corroborated by his E014 self-description and Beau's E017 bronze-gold narration (S17–S19). The **yellow eyes and age 23 remain final-sheet-only**, not explicitly established for E001. Keep those traits provisional; the original scale color is not a freely selected alternative.

## E000/E001 character design-sheet brief — owner-approved plan, appearance anchors pending

**Sheet ID:** `WASTES-VOREL-DS-001`. **Target:** `WASTES-VOREL-A01`; prospective status **sheet-needed**. One front, paired *anatomical* left/right sides and back, plus enlarged three-quarter facial study, in phased artwork production. Neutral full-body turnarounds should be unclothed, maintain exactly seven-foot adult stature in proportion to the other protagonists, and retain a substantial long tail without cutting it off at the page edge. **The written plan is owner-approved, but no Vorel appearance anchor is registered as approved in the repository; this update creates no image.**

- **Face study:** Strong reptilian skull, extended snout and hard jawline, **source-established golden-to-bronze scales** with a **provisionally chosen short fan-shaped occipital frill**; yellow eyes remain a *late-sheet-derived artistic candidate*. Choose a precise frill shape, jawline, tooth visibility, pupils, scales and brow geometry at the visual-design phase, then lock identity. Expression set: formidable confidence, hearty pride, excited contentment and serious protective resolve. Do not give him a generic dragonborn horn crown or decorative clothing without selecting that as a consciously separate design.
- **Front:** Broad trapezoidal shoulders, visibly muscular chest/arms, thick thighs, clawed feet, **coarse plated back/head scales contrasting with a smoother fleshy-looking chest**, long neck, erect yet relaxed swagger. **No loincloth or accessories** in the baseline; groin region organically flat/folded and nonexplicit. No chest Eye implant.
- **Two strict 90-degree profiles:** Camera sees Vorel's **own left side** while he faces right, and his **own right side** while he faces left. Maintain the chosen snout/crest/frill, chest contour, shoulder mass, and tail origin and curve without mirroring arbitrary asymmetries. Tail length must fit.
- **Back:** Continuous head/neck-to-spine scale pattern, shoulder and back musculature, tail attachment and continuation to its tip; bare back, **no concealed cloak/armor**. Avoid prominent arbitrary spines unless selected for every view.
- **Working palette:** **gold-to-bronze scales are directly established for E001** (S17–S22). The owner initially left artistic **palette alternatives open** pending research; this does not override the now verified hue family. Matte sandstone-gold/bronze with warm umber shadow accents is a *proposed graphic treatment*, not a literal source color specification. Yellow eyes are a late-sheet-derived candidate only, not E001 verified. The production style uses [fixed adult-animation graphic-novel v2](ILLUSTRATION_GUIDE.md): bold confident ink, simplified graphic shadow planes, matte colors, sparse texture and **1024×1024 sRGB PNG** pages with no writing/panels/watermarks unless explicitly specified. Technical compliance and owner stage approvals happen during generation.
- **Equipment:** None in neutral full-body model; the E000 introductory **bloody haunch of meat** may be a *separate contextual prop*, not a permanent weapon. Audie Norman's mace-and-shield depiction is fan interpretation, **not proof of E001 loadout**. Later Mawstorm, skull helmet, Eye, cape, anti-dragon suit and flame effects **must not** be retrojected.
