## Identity and evidence boundaries

Stanley is Jon's half-elf bard/politician, one of the four original Wastes PCs. His forced cohabitation with the archfey **Diontalus** does not make their decisions or appearance identical. His Elhandari birth name is reported in the played E118/E114+ story as **Valier/Vale Ornath (?)**; Vorel's late sheet says **“Valeer Ornnoth”**, an unresolved orthographic lead, not a replacement for his chosen name. Do not conflate Wastes with Delvers.

**Verification:** E000 reader draft and E001 complete reader draft examined; the **E020, E023, E045, E060, E120, Wastes S2E03, S3E03, S3E019**, and **March 2019 / May 2020 / April 2022 bonus** facial-hair passages were directly extracted and context-read from primary Parakeet SRTs, with **Qwen cross-checks** at the consequential passages. These full-file fetches used **pinned GitHub HEAD `bba3e150ed807966cd540e0c0c29218037f77c80`**, while the live local MCP worktree subsequently advanced to `6dad4986e01436784ce466aaf67809517539737d`. These revisions are not interchangeable. Other episode changes remain inherited from linked dossiers. **No source audio listened to.** Two ASRs are not listening verification. D&D Beyond is a **late/terminal sheet snapshot** (modified 2024-08-14), not proof of E001 or E128 equipment. Six private wiki image files were visually inspected; fan interpretation is distinct from Jon's historically attested Audie Norman commission. Scope is **targeted, not an exhaustive corpus sweep**.

## Appearance states

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A01 — Dusthill opening, E000 introduction / E001.**
A recognizable adult male half-elf **addressing residents as an assured politician** in the red-rock village. **In the played E001 feast**, a coat with usable pockets is specifically established; a rapier is positively established **later during the underground trip**. The E000 monologue speaks of an ancient tome that inspired him, but does **not** establish that he is holding or wearing the book in a given E001 frame. **Colors, hairstyle, beard shape, skin tone, eye shade, goggles, armor, boots and coat tailoring are not stated in the early read scenes**. Artist may provisionally choose neat brown hair, grey eyes, light skin, a restrained moustache/goatee and practical, somewhat dapper patched coat **using S09/fan-art inspiration as D01**, but must label these as chosen extrapolations pending approval, not as E001 transcript observations. The E088 bespoke formal scarf/square hat, E118 book/mask and E126 guitar **must not appear**. For a neutral full-body draft, a belted coat/vest, functional pockets and sheathed rapier are coherent interpretations. Frontal, profile and back projections must use *one* consistent chosen facial-hair style and pocket locations; asymmetries are not source established. State start is E001, **end unresearched**.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A01B — Groomed handlebar/goatee appearance after the E023 Château Weir barber (~01:40:21–01:40:50).**
The first **exactly player-described** version of Stanley's facial hair: **brown hair shaved close along the sides, longer on top and brushed back**, with the ears **clearly visible** (goggles referred to in relation to hair), a **Western-style moustache curled up at both ends**, and a **small goatee under the chin** (S15). This takes place **after** the E001 opening, **before** E045. Draw the face and moustache/goatee consistently from front, side and three-quarter view. Exact clothing beyond the local barber scene is not established by this haircut and should still respect per-episode sources. A later E045 removal is **not yet present**. The March 2019 Q&A establishes Jon's then-current preference for a facial-haired portrayal but does not prove the hairstyle applied before E023 (S16). State validity begins at E023 haircut and ends **no later than E045 appearance change**, with any intervening undocumented grooming left open.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A02 — Diontalus visibly modifies the shared body, E045 (~00:15:20–00:15:49).**
Begin from Stanley's then-current, **facial-haired** adult half-elf body. Under Diontalus's control the **facial hair disappears**, facial planes become more angular/defined, conventionally handsome, hair becomes a little longer, and **eyes brighten to silver** (S05, two ASRs agree). Change is **purposefully performed to seem a somewhat different person**. Keep body/identity broadly similar, rather than draw an unrelated ethereal elf, monster, or separate second body. Clothing color/style remain unknown here; do not transplant the later spectral Diontalus's long blue hair into his host form. **How long this cosmetic spell/alteration lasted is not established.** Separate his agency from Stanley's. E049–E050 continue bodily control conflict (S06) without a newly confirmed immutable facial palette.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A03 — Separation after E059 / no moustache confirmed E060.**
The Principle removes **Diontalus's presence into a crystal**, leaving Stanley's body physically intact (S07). **E060 00:11:38–00:11:47** then specifically says Stanley **“doesn't have a mustache anymore”** and “looks a lot different”; his clothing is described as better (S17). The table attributes the recognition problem to the change, but **does not play an actual shaving event**. **Do not automatically restore the E023 curled moustache following Diontalus's removal.** Whether the E045 sharp/silver host effects remained is still not verified by this remark. Continuing physically intact half-elf baseline, moustache absent at E060; exact facial features and clothing need independent scene evidence. Distinct temporary polymorphs in later episodes do not rewrite identity.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A04 — CHIDS aftermath / Sigil petitioner body, E081–E087.**
The GM declares Stanley among the four dead at E081; in E082 he is found in Sigil **cold, blue/bloated and motionless**, on/near the collectors' hovering slab (S08, inherited dossier). E083 finds a marked petitioner-like body, not a returned active adventurer. For the E082 recovery depiction: an adult half-elf **motionless and unnaturally discolored**, not upright and campaigning. **No speculative wounds, spectral replacement, or carried late loot.** Clothing and facial details at this point not established; confirm original E082 scene before frame-specific art. Do not present his original E082 body as merely sleeping or already restored.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A05 — Mirror restoration and improvised dress, E088.**
Stanley emerges from Kidigree's mirror **looking like his living self** while his old body still lies separately on a slab. He first emerges effectively **naked**, then **wraps Vorel's red Soggy Feather cape around himself as an improvised toga**; later Bill provides **old lawn-bowling clothes** (S10, inherited dual-ASR dossier). For a dressed restoration illustration, choose the *precise* moment: cape-toga **or** borrowed lawn-bowling clothes, not both as his obligatory lifelong costume. His neck carries Weir/Infernal markings visible **through a special ethereal spectrometer**; this does **not** establish permanently visible glowing letters to ordinary eyes. Hair, face and physical build are described as like the prior living man; exact contours remain artistic.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A06 — Tailored Sigil public appearance, late E088.**
At Lohar's tailor, three formal outfits are commissioned and subsequently delivered. Stanley's specified **sophisticated Witherford/Weatherford (?) look** is described as **blue/silver/white or purple-accented**, with a **scarf and square hat** (S10; precise name/palette phrasing requires audio). Complete drawable version: adult half-elf in fitted, comparatively clean urban formalwear with **a scarf and square hat**, and restrained blue/white/silver with possible purple detail, rather than an opening-era Dusthill coat. Choice between uncertain color phrases must be recorded, not simultaneously mandated as exact swatches. No presumption the costume is worn continuously in later expeditions. Owner-approved E001 sheet does not approve this variant.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A07 — Later Elhandar/Earth equipment, E118–E128 (partially audited).**
In E118 he gains father's ornate **key**, later the **Book of Adjacencies** and a dark gem-studded **mask**, which is then **not yet worn/attuned** (S11). E122 he has reunited sphere wand and recovered rapier; E121 **temporarily polymorphs into a giant ape**, returning to half-elf shape (S12). E126 **actually dons the obsidian/dragon-metal mask**, changing it to a preferred **eye-mask** shape with **eight movable chromatic gems**, but **attunement remains incomplete**; he seizes a powerful electric guitar from Henatar later in that combat (S13). Only include mask/guitar **in scenes after those actual uses**; no proof these are routine visible everyday accessories, and they do not exist as E001 costume requirements. Final D&D Beyond sheet reflects **another terminal source time**, not necessarily E128. Diontalus's later independent spectral form is a **different entity**, not Stanley's restored appearance.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A08 — Separate Unbreakable Majesty transformation, The Wastes S2E03 (~02:14:21–02:17:20).**
The GM **actually** awards Stanley the level-14 *Unbreakable Majesty* ability **early**, while the party is level 13, during his elevation as an Elhandari ruler (title spelling disputed in ASR). This is **not** the E045 Diontalus identity. Stanley floats bathed in rainbow light; facial planes become more **regal**, jaw sharpened, brow balanced, hair better groomed, ears prominent (GM). **Jon adds darker, longer hair, almost luminous/washed-light skin, fully/nearly reflective silver eyes and a subjective beauty effect:** other people see somewhat different appealing/androgynous or masculine features depending on their preferences (S19). The exact S2E03 played description **does not explicitly say moustache shaved off on-screen**, but **Jon's April 2022 retrospective says he wanted a neutral face *without it* for this transformation** and in S3E03 he confirms it is absent (S20–S21). Thus the recommended stable later visual anchor is **moustache-free, refined, silver-eyed**; the precise physical moment of losing any residual goatee remains an unresolved transition. Do **not** copy the early E023 handlebar moustache onto this later form. Distinguish personal eye color and subjective glamor from highlights/lighting. A separate state sheet is necessary before recurring scene depictions of this form.

**WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-A09 — Subsequent S3 moustache-free appearance (confirmed S3E03).**
During end-of-episode banter in **S3E03 02:40:45–02:41:05**, Scott asks whether Stanley can finally lose his moustache and Jon responds **“It's not there. It's gone.”** A joking reference to an earlier **baby/child** version having a moustache does not reverse the adult's appearance (S21). Continue the post-Unbreakable Majesty design without the early E023 curled moustache, unless a played subsequent physical change establishes a different state. In **S3E019** Jon speculates about Stanley growing a **long beard while living in secluded retirement**—a *future hypothetical*, not a depicted or completed appearance at the end of current play (S22).

## E000/A01 recurring-character design-sheet brief — first draft only

**Recurrence:** Stanley is an original PC present throughout the story and expected in group/location art repeatedly; requires a baseline sheet before new scene illustrations. **Reserved sheet ID:** WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS-DS-001, written brief v0.2, **sheet-needed**, **NO image generated/approved**.

- **Front:** self-confident adult half-elf speaker, upright, inviting-but-slightly-imperious expression, one hand able to gesture broadly. Wear a coat with obvious accessible pocket(s) (S03); sheathed rapier appropriate in late E001 (S04). **For an E001 sheet**, the exact facial-hair style is not yet early-scene verified, but a **moustache/goatee is now strongly supported as early-era design continuity by E023 Jon narration and March 2019 bonus** (S15–S16). Brown swept-back hair, grey eyes and light skin can draw on later sheet fields but are provisional for E001. Reserve the **precise upward-curled handlebar moustache, small goatee and shaved-side hairstyle** as *required* for an E023/A01B sheet; never transplant them into S2E03/Unbreakable Majesty.
- **Profile / three-quarter face:** same jaw, nose, hairline and facial hair as front. Expression library: beaming rallying smile, sincere concern, disapproval, brave resolve, later devastated grief. Do not switch to Diontalus's **bright silver eyes, hair-extension and cleanshaven face** in baseline.
- **Back:** same coat silhouette, hem length and collar as front. Sheath orientation and shoulder straps are **unselected design decisions**. No unearned purple superhero cloak, goggle strap or armor plating as compulsory E001 features.
- **Build/height:** male half-elf, plausible moderately tall humanlike proportions; **6′1″ / 188 lb are terminal sheet only**. No confirmed scars or species-standard giant ears. **No left/right bodily asymmetry is known**. A rapier may rest at a clearly consistent hip, side chosen and documented once visual approval occurs.
- **Palette/materials:** show-confirmed opening coat material and color **unknown**. Muted dusty cocoa/blue-grey/sand cloth, matte leather accessories, sparing brass accents are **proposed v2 style choices**, not facts. Keep shared ink-heavy modern adult-animation graphic-novel treatment and 1024×1024 sRGB master canvas.
- **Do not backdate:** silver Diontalus eyes; mirror-return cape-toga; tailored Witherford suit; Book of Adjacencies; enchanted mask/eye jewels; later electric guitar; campaign-end +2 equipment.

**Sheet design readiness:** Sufficient for a **provisional E001 baseline drawing**, and **stronger directly playable E023/A01B face/hair reference**. E001 specific beard shape and palette still need verification/artistic selection; E023 moustache and small goatee no longer merely fan-art choices. The March 2019 Q&A **confirms Jon paid Audie Norman for an “official” facial-haired depiction**, but matching that commissioned file to the archive remains unverified. **No owner-approved project sheet or image exists.** Later E045 and S2E03 variants need separate coherent artwork.
