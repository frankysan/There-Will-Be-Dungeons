# External references and chronology policy

**Checked:** 2026-10-21. **Scope:** References for *There Will Be Dungeons / The Wastes*, beginning with E000–E001 and supplemented by unverified E002–E012 dossiers. The fan wiki is not itself a transcript, and the linked character sheets are late-campaign snapshots.

## Source classes and appropriate uses

| Class | Example | What it supports | What it does **not** establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original audio (primary) | E000 and E001 MP3 | Spoken wording, speaker sounds, timing, actual-play events, and the GM's delivery | Whether a character's statements are objectively true in-fiction |
| Listener-verified corrections | Archive-owner checks | Confirmed local listening decisions such as Scott's performed voice and E000's final word | General speaker-ID mappings across every episode |
| Transcription outputs (derivative) | Raw/processed Parakeet and Qwen SRT | Search candidates, draft wording and segment positions | Canonical spellings or audio-verified truth |
| Creator-attributed background | Background credited to Beau Schwartz (with edits by Dershum) on the wiki home page | Strong corroboration for early setting descriptions, with explicit attribution | Unqualified proof of every later fan-wiki interpretation |
| Fan wiki articles and episode summaries | [There Will Be Dungeons Wiki](https://twbd.fandom.com/wiki/There_Will_Be_Dungeons_Wiki) | Candidate spellings, aliases, relationships, locations and **links to** later episodes | Word-for-word dialogue, guaranteed accuracy, or information already known in E000/E001 |
| Linked D&D Beyond character sheets | Links from the wiki's **D&D Beyond Character Sheets** section | Actual show character builds **at the end of the campaign**, per archive owner | Initial-level statistics, early equipment/abilities, dates when a feature first appeared, or a historical build timeline |

Attribution is not identical to independent verification: preserve URLs and any contributor/creator credit, and check claims with episode audio where their factual or chronological precision matters.

## Critical chronology and spoiler controls

1. **Two timelines:** (a) when an event or fact is established in the show; (b) when a reference page/snapshot was recorded. A late character sheet must be marked `campaign_end` rather than `E000` or `E001`.
2. **Do not retroactively spoil:** Later changes in equipment, skills, character condition, affiliations and plot must not be copied into opening-episode character descriptions. If needed, store them in clearly labelled later-campaign sections.
3. **No inferred initial build:** Never subtract levels, feats or equipment from the final D&D Beyond sheet to manufacture an episode-one character sheet.
4. **Respect narrative viewpoint:** GM-established exposition, narrated action, PC opinion and player out-of-character commentary are distinct evidence types. A later wiki interpretation does not overrule an earlier episode without a provenance note.
5. **Corrections are reviewable:** A spelling normalization in the readable transcript should retain original ASR form, wiki reference and — when ambiguous — an audio listening check; never modify the archived raw SRT.
6. **Prefer concise summaries and citations:** The fan wiki labels most community material CC BY-SA; avoid copying its prose and artwork wholesale. If text is reused, meet the applicable attribution and ShareAlike requirements. Separate the licensing of our editorial work, references, podcast audio and source content.
7. **Link rather than mirror character sheets:** D&D Beyond data and official show material may have separate access and reuse terms. Avoid automatically committing scraped sheet content or assets.

## E009–E012 expansion (2026-10-21)

The [E009](../episodes/E009.md), [E010](../episodes/E010.md), [E011](../episodes/E011.md), and [E012](../episodes/E012.md) dossiers continue the **machine-transcript-only reference expansion**. They are not reader-edited transcript publications or audio-verified evidence.

Retain important **source-disclosure boundaries**: E009 resolves E008's Leopold confrontation, but the gnomes' apparent loss under sand is not an established death; E009's giant black dragon is *observed* but its history/causal origin is not proven. E010 ends with the heroes' arena victory; E011 confirms *the heroes'* acquittal, but the Dusthill abductees remain enslaved. E011 first explicitly recognizes **Talia**, mother of Bruce Bane (family relation also [wiki-supported](https://twbd.fandom.com/wiki/Bruce_Bane)); E012's temporary accommodation does not resolve her captivity. E011 ends with Nash challenging the **Jeweler**, while E012 documents the confrontation and aftermath.

Most early **Slavetown names, street labels, businesses, titles and mine deeds** are ASR recognition candidates rather than corroborated spellings. Crucially E012 alternates between `copper mine` and `gold mine` for a sought deed; do not force a canonical metal until a contemporary map/deed or verified audio resolves it. **The Jeweler's allegation** that the Hillmaster and Softhead sold Dusthill out is a speaker claim and should not retroactively replace E001–E006 history. Slavetown maps, era-appropriate vehicle graphics, and original show assets—where license permits use—are better spelling/specification authorities than a current end-of-campaign character sheet.

The earlier fan-wiki and end-of-campaign sheet restrictions remain unchanged. Preserve raw-source provenance, decisions, source times and first-revelation episodes.

**License warning:** [Fandom's general licensing policy](https://www.fandom.com/licensing) explicitly distinguishes **non-text media** from its general text license; [its reuse support page](https://support.fandom.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035075654-I-want-to-reuse-text-or-images-from-a-Fandom-wiki) advises checking image-specific rights and uploader. **Do not assume the images are all CC BY-SA or cleared for publication** simply because the wiki hosted them or they are inside a private GitHub repository.
