## How to use this material

1. **Distinguish source roles:** (a) on-image **show/creator-authored design sheet**, (b) annotated **map**, (c) **fan artwork**, and (d) unknown/undated graphic. Image presence or filename may support search, but does not independently establish **canon**, pronunciation, episode timing, or character knowledge.
2. **Spoiler discipline:** A later portrait may show a later form, costume, companion or transformation. Do not introduce that appearance or relationship to an episode until the episode itself or explicitly dated contemporary creator material supports it. The maps are **not** automatically episode-era player maps.
3. **Copyright and release:** **Fandom's text license does not automatically cover individual image files.** See [Fandom licensing](https://www.fandom.com/licensing) and [Fandom's image reuse guidance](https://support.fandom.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035075654-I-want-to-reuse-text-or-images-from-a-Fandom-wiki). Each file's creator, original **wiki file page**, upload history and media-specific license/permission must be recorded before assuming external reuse rights. This **private repository's possession is not a reuse grant**; public redistribution (including compiled packages or publication pages) requires a separate rights review. Do not apply a single project CC BY-SA statement to all images.
4. **Credit and audit trail:** Preserve exact original paths and Git binary blobs. Filename-embedded names are **unverified attribution leads**, unless directly written in an image (e.g. Kristen Ashton on Fate 4 continuation). Record creator, source URL, image/file revision/date, exact license or permission and observed date; don't guess source URLs.
5. **Editorial traceability:** Cite individual file links in dossiers and specify **what the image visibly establishes**, separate from any ASR timestamp and wiki narrative. A visual label can suggest a spelling correction, but cannot change verbatim dialogue without listening. Prefer linking the files to embedding them on public pages until permissions are cleared.

**Still needed:** Recover each image's exact wiki **File:** page URL or original publisher URL, uploader/artist, original upload timestamp or revision and individual license. The archive owner supplied only the common origin site; the original URLs and grants were not included in this repository's filenames. Preserve that uncertainty.
