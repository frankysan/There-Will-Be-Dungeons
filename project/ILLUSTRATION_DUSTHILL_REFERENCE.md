## Identity, recognition and spatial baseline

Dusthill is a **small, lived-in settlement almost concealed in a naturally formed alcove beside a range of red rocky mountains**. Across what would otherwise be its exposed side, **giant red rocks provide a natural rampart**. The approach winds between them, hiding the village from inattentive travelers. Citizens have reinforced the naturally defensive site over centuries with **a defensible gate and elevated guard positions on the red rock**. This is not source support for a conventional, continuous stone city wall, a straight main road, a vast open settlement or a precise compass orientation. [S01]

Within the enclosure, the **village proper has mud huts** and a **smaller number of longstanding wooden-and-metal barns** serving as communal dining halls and supply storage. The important **dark farms are at the back, inside caves beneath the red-rock mountains**: animals include pigs, cows and chew worms; wheat, potatoes and beans survive with an ancient irrigation system. The E001 narration does not map the relative positions of individual huts, barns or cave mouths; a **newly examined undated settlement schematic** below supplies provisional relationships without authenticated E001 date or scale. Avoid replacing cave farms with large green outdoor fields simply because food crops exist. [S02]

The **central public water outlet is called a well**, but water is delivered to the surface by opening a **valve**; its deeper supply relies on long-hidden dwarven pumps and machinery. The narrator also mentions **pictograms on the village rock** as a record of the well's long history. Neither an ordinary rope-and-bucket well, an exposed dragon engine at street level, a particular pictogram composition, nor a specific pump housing has been verified. The settlement has a **garage/workshop for salvaged old machinery**. Its separately researched cliff-cut structure, furnishings and multiple time-specific vehicle states belong to [Jimster's garage](ILLUSTRATION_JIMSTERS_GARAGE_REFERENCE.md), not generic village set dressing. [S03, S09]

A **clearing with a communal fire pit**, used for gatherings and meetings, is described **outside/near the Hillmaster's canvas tent** when the protagonists look out in E005. Do not conflate this gathering space with the underground pump site or pretend that its exact distance from the central well is established. Residents' clothing, banners, signs, the gate's materials and mechanics, number of buildings, building arrangement, absolute dimensions, lighting, climate on a particular day and precise mountain shape remain unestablished. The environment is dusty and materially scarce, but the settlement is not a pristine fantasy village or a technologically uniform modern town. [S04]

**Recognition priorities:** concealed red-rock alcove; irregular protective boulder-rampart and winding approach; small muddy built environment; communal wooden/metal barns; central valve-operated water outlet; cave-farm mouths at the rear; rock-excavated garage; optional village-rock pictograms and tent-adjacent meeting fire pit if visible. None of the optional details must be crammed into every camera angle.

## Complete drawable state: A01 — intact opening settlement

- **Scene applicability:** E001, before the E005 attack. Suitable for a concealed approach view or a town-center street-level view. State only what the chosen viewpoint could reveal.
- **Complete description:** An irregular pocket of low, practical dwellings sheltered by high **rust-red rock**. Massive scattered rock formations pinch the entry into a **winding, hard-to-see path** with a defended gate; watch positions sit above. Inside, humble **mud huts** cluster loosely around functional communal places, mixed with a **few** weathered **wood-and-metal barns**. The center has an engineered **valve-operated well/outlet**; caves at the settlement's rear host the farms and an old irrigation arrangement. Another cliff excavation functions as a salvage garage. A gathering clearing and fire pit can be included if composing around the Hillmaster's tent, although that exact layout is described in E005.
- **Colors/materials:** **Red rock**, natural mud, aged wood and old metal are supported. Exact clay/roof hues, crops' color, gate material, water outlet finish, banners and lighting are **artistic choices**. Dusty muted pastels with selective accents per [style guide](ILLUSTRATION_GUIDE.md); do not mistake a palette choice for source canon.
- **Scale/orientation:** A **village**, not a metropolis; cave farms **at back** and water outlet **at center**. The opening-side boulder wall and inward winding approach are spatially established; cardinal directions, distances, heights, road widths, house count and sight-line diagram are not.
- **People/equipment:** Mixed peoples and trades described, not a mandatory crowd or uniform; no permanent municipal insignia established. A view of the garage may include the appropriately timed vehicles using the garage/Fate 4 state records.
- **Must preserve:** Natural boulder-rampart rather than generic manufactured city wall, semi-hidden approach, muddy dwellings and sparse wood/metal halls, cave agriculture instead of solely outside farmland, central *valve* supply.
- **Must not appear as established:** Large open grasslands, abundant freshwater lake/river on surface, generic hand-cranked stone well as the sole infrastructure, exposed dragon engine above ground, wide gridded roads, bustling modern service station, buildings burning before E005, E006-complete Fate 4 in an E001 construction scene.
- **Unknowns/design choices:** Perspective, exact building footprints, lanterns/sun angle, roof forms, gate form, number and nature of guards, artistic translation of pictograms and visible cave entrances. A handwritten settlement plan is now inspected, but its GM attribution, precise era and scale remain unverified.
- **Source support:** [S01–S03]; [S04] for the subsequent fire-pit setting; [S09] for garage's site.

## Complete drawable state: A02 — attack and immediate aftermath

- **Scene applicability:** E005 goblin incursion, survivors' emergence, or E006 next-day meeting/departure. Specify which moment before illustration.
- **Complete description:** Dusthill retains its **concealed red-rock alcove**, winding approach, defended heights, humble **mud huts**, wooden/metal communal barns and the rear **farm caves**. During the raid, hostiles come over the settlement's defenses and civilians flee or shelter behind a **makeshift barricade at the dark farms**. At the E005 aftermath, residents cautiously emerge, inspect bodies and damage, while the open meeting space near the Hillmaster's **tent and fire pit** remains a possible focal point. By E006, the **central outlet produces running water** again; the town meets after losses and prepares for continuing security, while Fate 4 is unveiled and departs from the garage.
- **Colors/materials:** Same stable materials as A01. Combat grime, scattered possessions, makeshift barricade and individual damage can be represented cautiously as scene interpretation; fire, smoke, burned roofs or a quantified extent of ruins are **not established by the cited passages**.
- **Scale/orientation:** Relative geography as A01. Do not depict the garage as entered from a through-road across town: [garage A02/A03](ILLUSTRATION_JIMSTERS_GARAGE_REFERENCE.md) describes an outer vehicle exit and no ordinary town-wide road for RV travel.
- **People/equipment:** Select scene-appropriate survivor and raider presence; neither a precise death toll's visible bodies nor the exact distribution of abductees can be inferred from meeting reports. Fate 4's final appearance belongs to **E006**, not the fighting portion of E005. The garage vehicles are **not established as stolen**. [S05–S07, S09]
- **Must preserve:** Same recognizable natural enclosure; damage/local evacuation rather than a magically transformed town; hidden farm-cave refuge; separation of surface water test from the E005 restart.
- **Must not show as established:** Total destruction of Dusthill, all residents dead/captive, particular houses burned, intact attackers occupying the town at the E006 meeting, Fate 4 present in town after the E006 departure without a later scene, rescued captives physically back in Dusthill in E059.
- **Unknowns/design choices:** Exact structural damage, repair progress, debris density, barrier construction and placement, spatial relation of tent/firepit to outlet. If a drawing depends on specific damage, do a targeted additional episode/audio check.
- **Source support:** [S04–S07, S09].

## Newly inspected Dusthill plan (2026-10-25)

**Map artifact:** `local/images-wiki/maps/Dusthill.webp` (formerly `DVJ4V1JVwAErBl-.webp`; same verified SHA-256) (166,822 bytes; SHA-256 `141731587b065a5271a6814c2d1cfd264f8b8a0a86b3fdbe41219266ef7bc5ce`), **pixels directly examined**. The map is handwritten on hex-grid paper, titled `DUSTHILL`, with a marginal note appearing to say `pop - 200–300 estimated` (uncertain); this is a **map annotation**, not an authenticated census or canonical population. **Provenance lead (owner recollection, 2026-10-25):** The project owner believes the map probably originated with the show's GM, but explicitly cannot confirm this. Record as `probable-GM-origin / owner-recollection / unverified`, **not** a verified creator attribution. Its author, date, episode-era applicability, scale and publication provenance remain unverified. The map is a potentially important original production reference; it should guide layout exploration, not override audio or automatically become a canonical E001 floorplan.

**Labeled features and placements as drawn:** A rock-/pictogram-associated perimeter on the upper-left; a `School` toward the left/upper village; a `Gathering Circle` slightly left of the `well`; multiple blocks of dwellings (some annotated `Talia's`); a broad `Barn` on the right next to a `hitching` area; top-edge buildings labeled `Hillmaster Hut`, `Food Stores`, and what appears to be `Town` or a town-related space (uncertain handwriting). Top-center `secret door` and bottom-center `guardhouse`/`guard post` annotations are drawn; several `Guard Post` labels or marks occur along the lower defensive edge. A `Trading Post` and `Barracks` are toward the bottom-right, with a `Man's Shop` or similarly written shop on the eastern village edge (reading unverified). To the right, two separately outlined areas are labeled `Dark Farm`, with a `Garage` further down/right and a `rock door` near the bottom-right corner. Specific handwriting readings must not be silently canonized as proper names.

**Layout implications:** The plan differentiates the **gathering circle from the central well**, explicitly locates several administrative/commercial buildings and the barn, and shows **two farm areas** beside the town enclosure and a garage off the village's eastern/right side. This is more specific than the transcript-only A01 account, but a drawn page orientation is **not cardinal north** and lines do not establish a measured building footprint or accurate topographic elevation. Its `secret door` may relate to subterranean passages, and its `rock door` may relate to the garage's outer vehicle exit, but **neither identification is proven**. Do not claim that all of the features were visible or built at E001 solely from this map. The pictograms' specific markings and positions in the generated aerial are artistic.

**Separate cavern map:** The newly renamed `local/images-wiki/maps/Dusthill Caverns.webp` (previously `DVJ4cN3VAAAkrng.webp`, 101,972 bytes; SHA-256 `13eca8a7c34d72daaf07e60394c9cc8fb4ba64cf7f257a2ff03b27bce82ce268`) was **directly viewed** in this pass. Some handwriting is ambiguous. Do not interpret it as the surface town or as definitive proof of which subterranean episode/level it depicts. The user's recollection of probable GM origin applies here too, without verification. This map can guide a separate underground appearance record after cross-checking E001–E005 dungeon passages.
