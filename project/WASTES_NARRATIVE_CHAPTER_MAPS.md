## 1. Counting convention and shared boundaries

- The proposed target is **10–20 source entries per narrative arc** (not necessarily the same number of fully played sessions). `E000` is introductory; record it explicitly.
- `E043` and `E127` are standalone holiday one-offs; `E107`–`E109` are the independent historical **Dunebreaker** prequel. These are excluded from the continuous main-party sequence.
- `E013.5` is a supplemental scene sequence to be placed in fictional time; it is **not** a new numbered main-story episode added to the arc count.
- `E101` is one recording spanning two current story arcs; the boundary is **Hope's departure from White Tower**. It is shown as belonging to **two** proposed arcs, but its dossier is counted **once** in campaign-total unique-source measurements.
- Season boundaries do **not** determine arc boundaries: `S2E12` continues into `S3E01`; this proposed grouping keeps them together through `S3E03`.
- `S3E019` is the separate concluding cast roundtable/Q&A, **not** a nineteenth played Season Three session or a sequel chapter.

## 4. Required coverage and balancing measurements before any rewrite

For **each** existing `ARC_ONE`–`ARC_SIX` source document:
- Measure prose words and paragraphs separately from footnotes, tables, headings and navigation; record old line/paragraph IDs or content hashes.
- Extract episode references, scene/topic outline, internal POV/private knowledge and significant cliffhanger/closure.
- Map every substantial old paragraph to a candidate new chapter, **source dossier range**, retained/summarized/archived disposition and a rationale if compressing it. Re-read relevant dossier evidence and alternative ASR when a high-impact claim is doubtful.
- Compare scene density and emotional weight: quiet changes, bonds, loss and consequences should not vanish beneath battle logs, nor should combat mechanics displace character choices.
- Calculate new chapter word distribution against the current Delvers **roughly 350–450 word diagnostic**, but allow justified exceptions.
- Check within-episode split at E101, nonplayed archive breaks, season transitions and alternate-knowledge branches.
- Verify all notes, anchors, inbound links, compatibility pages and in-world final statuses before accepting new canonical arc numbering.

**Stop condition:** If coverage mapping reveals an unnatural ending or arc 07's 20-episode ceiling harms readability, propose a revised boundary rather than forcing the table above.

## 5. Migration and preservation plan

The first structural pass is read-only: catalog every incoming link to `ARC_ONE.md`–`ARC_SIX.md`, every chapter anchor and each world subject's current internal arc labels. Do not overwrite these paths or change shared chapter names during planning.

Once approved, archive exact original arc bytes and hashes under `research/legacy/`, keep rebased reading copies, migrate each scene into the new sequence and either retain compatibility stubs or update all validated incoming links. The owner must be able to trace a sentence through old narrative → new chapter → episode evidence. Update the [narrative index](../narrative/README.md), root reader guide, [chronology](../world/CHRONOLOGY.md), subject-page arc headings, decisions and review-queue pointers **as one tracked change set**. Structural validation does not upgrade any source to audio-verified status.
