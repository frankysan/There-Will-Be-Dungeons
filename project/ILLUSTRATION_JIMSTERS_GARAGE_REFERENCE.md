## Identity, visual baseline and geography

Jimster runs Dusthill's community **garage and mechanical workshop**; the
texts use `garage`, `shop`, and the general description of a place for
mechanical work, without establishing separate facilities for these names.
The garage is **excavated into a red-rock mountain/cliff wall**, rather than
built as a freestanding shed or a fully natural cave. The GM specifically
describes it as **not quite a cave**. Its frontage inside the settlement is
**open and workshop-like**, with **metal shelving partially separating its
working space from the outside**, not one conventional domestic door.

A separate **somewhat camouflaged mountain-face exit** gives vehicles access
to the *exterior* of Dusthill; driving through the town proper is not an
ordinary route, as there are no suitable roads through its interior. A
retrospective Hope scene describes **two large garage doors in the rock face**.
Those may depict the vehicle-access frontage of the same workshop, but no
source yet measures the geometry or demonstrates exactly how the open
town-facing space, doors and rear/side access connect. **Preserve both
descriptions; never draw an invented authoritative floorplan.**
[S01, S02, S05]

The working space is **crowded with metal junk, salvaged machinery and old
materials**, especially irrigation-repair parts. **Metal shelving** is an
important visual motif; weapons are **secured under lock and key**, not
hanging freely on public display. There is a **workbench** and equipment
including **compressed-air tools/drill**, as well as **makeshift chairs and
milk crates** used for seating in E006. E006 explicitly characterizes it as
**dusty, oily, dirty and sandy**. The interior is plausibly large enough for
the described vehicles, but **exact dimensions, ceiling height, bay widths,
roof supports, light fixtures and tool placement are unestablished**.
[S01, S03, S05]

**Two vehicles at the E001 introduction:** the Hillmaster's very small,
Smart-car-like car and a shabby RV already set upon enormous monster-truck
wheels, the unfinished vehicle later called **Fate 4**. E001 narrates Hope
putting the **last giant wheel** on the RV. Neither vehicle is normally used
much; both are tinkering projects. E067's **subjective earlier memory**
also shows a car *like a Camaro* being worked on, but does **not** establish
that the E001-era inventory included an additional permanent vehicle.
A separate, previously unmodified RV stands **toward the back under a
large canvas/cloth cover**, and replacement giant tires are in a corner.
[S01, S04]

**Color and form limits:** Red rock and worn industrial metal derive from
Dusthill's setting and explicit garage construction/materials. No fixed
paint scheme, precise rock cut geometry, uniform workshop color, light
temperature or signage has been established. Do not import generic
neon-lit modern service stations, pristine showroom bays or a yellow
garage building as supposed established appearance. Unspecified lighting,
shelving colors and exact tool arrangement are documented artistic choices.

## Complete drawable state instructions

### A00 — earlier garage, before Fate 4 modifications

Use a **rough-hewn, excavated mountainside automobile workshop** with
**two large garage doors integrated into rock**, tools worked by compressed
air, an **oil-darkened workbench**, a large **cloth-covered boxy RV near
the back** with **ordinary road tires**, and **oversized monster tires in
one corner**. The RV is in disrepair. Keep the interior dusty, worn and
utilitarian, not pristine. The exact relative positions of workbench,
doors, spare tires and covered RV are **interpretive arrangements**;
apart from the RV being toward the back and the tires in a corner,
the transcript supplies no plan. No solar panels, dragon-skull front or
monster-tire installation on this vehicle yet. [S04]

This state is described in Hope's **retrospective subjective memory**.
A neutral source-based appearance study may omit figures, but should not
present the whole sequence as an objectively revisited place after E066.

### A01 — E001 construction workshop

A **rock-cut, cluttered community machine workshop**, open toward the
settlement behind **shelving partitions** and also possessing a
camouflaged route to the open world. The floor and work areas contain
**salvaged metals, irrigation-system parts, machinery and mixed
mechanical junk**. Keep the **tiny Smart-car-like vehicle** distinct
from the **small, shabby RV on very large monster tires**. Depict the
latter with work plausibly still in progress around its **last wheel**;
a loose wheel positioned beside it is an optional artistic staging,
not a fact about a specific unattended instant. Secure any firearms
rather than scattering a conspicuous arsenal around. Do not insert
the later dragon-skull nose/solar roof as established E001 equipment.
[S01, S02]

**For the queued no-people illustration:** choose an **unoccupied
environment/vehicle study inspired by E001**, not a literal frame
from the scene (which has mechanics and visitors). Use the corresponding
`WASTES-FATE4-A00A` vehicle state. Avoid inventing a fixed floorplan,
the workshop's exact number of lamps, a modern glass storefront or
signage. A single large square view may reveal the garage's rock-cut
interior, an open shelf-partitioned side, the unfinished RV and
select salvage; show the Smart car only when composition allows and
without contradicting its documented presence.

### A02 — secured garage after goblin raid

Keep the **same rock-cut, shelved, industrial interior** as A01 with
a **closed/liftable garage entrance**, dust, oil, stored metal parts and
improvised **milk crates/makeshift seats**; visible equipment can
include tools, storage cabinets or locked weapons. Fate 4, now later
in its modification work (`WASTES-FATE4-A00B` through E005, progressing
toward `A01` by E006), is **protected beneath a tarp at the E006
inspection**. Despite the raid, GM dialogue confirms things inside
have not been disturbed. Do not add goblin battle debris inside,
burnt interior walls, stolen tools or battle damage to the RV.
[S02, S03, S05]

### A03 — unveiling and departure preparation

Retain the garage's cliff-integrated, salvage-filled identity. Fate 4's
**E006 unveiling vehicle state** is a towering monster-wheel RV with
its **fabricated metal dragon-skull front, rooftop solar panels, rear
ladder and functional exterior combat railings**. A large **tarp is
removed during the reveal**; leave it nearby only as a scene-specific
composition choice. Supplies and tools are available but not proof of
particular fixed garage placements. No people may be shown in a
deliberately depopulated environment study, but a literal unveiling
scene in the episode involved participants. [S05]

### Unresolved source checks

- **Spatial relationship:** E001 says the garage is open/workshop-like toward the settlement and possesses a hidden mountain-face exit; E067 says two big garage doors are visible from the approach. They need not contradict one another, but neither ASR specifies a complete layout or exact number of interior openings. A future listening/handout search might resolve this.
- **E067's car:** A car like a Camaro appears in a retrospective memory. Do not replace E001's explicitly described **tiny Smart car** with a Camaro in a contemporary E001 depiction or declare a third permanent vehicle.
- **Dimensions and materials:** No internal garage measurements, ceiling height, garage door opening dimensions, floor paving, exact lighting, wall finish, colored paint, tool inventory plan or electrical scheme were located. Keep any such details provisional.

Also checked the existing Dusthill dossier, corresponding episode dossiers, Fate 4 state record and targeted repository references. **Not a full-corpus garage search**; other mentions or later changes may exist. No listening or direct picture inspection.
