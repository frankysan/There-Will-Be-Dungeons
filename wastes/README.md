# The Wastes — campaign overview

Nash, Hope, Stanley and Vorel begin in Dusthill, where failing waterworks and buried machinery point toward a much older world. Captivity, bargains and contested crowns draw them into threats that reach beyond the desert, through other planes and into the stars. Their changing loyalties and the cost of protecting Earth give the journey its shape.
