# Additional Discord handouts — spelling evidence audit (2026-10-27)

**Scope:** newly archived Discord channels and cached attachments, chiefly *Delvers*; also one Wastes character-related poem. This is a targeted **written-source spelling** audit, **not** a complete visual audit of all 491 newly recovered downloads and **not** an audio review. Files remain private in Git-ignored `local/`; only short spelling observations and source links are in this report.

## Evidence and editorial policy

- Archive message authorship follows the Discord archive's cast-account attribution, not independent authentication. **Kyle-attributed GM posts** support in-game map/handout orthography but may contain mistakes, evolving terminology or illustrative placeholders. **Written names do not certify dialogue, historical geography, character identities or player knowledge at an earlier episode**.
- **Highest weight for spelling:** names printed in a relevant GM-posted image/handout, checked visually. Filename-only forms, third-party prose, Discord previews and ASR have weaker or different roles. The *same underlying base map* with a quest overlay is corroboration of reuse, **not an independent second spelling witness**.
- **No changes to source SRTs or episode quotations.** Legacy Markdown slugs and already analyzed dossiers remain searchable. Normalize only after an explicit crosswalk and any necessary listening/identity checks.
