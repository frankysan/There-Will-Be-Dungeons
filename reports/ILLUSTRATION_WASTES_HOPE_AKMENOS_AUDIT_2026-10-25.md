## High-confidence source-supported requirements

- The earliest played E001 appearance is **a horned adult tiefling woman, named Oriphi, called Hope**, a notably optimistic Dusthill mechanic working on a dragon-engine-compatible vehicle. The GM contrasts Stanley's finery with Hope's likely **work clothes**. Exact head/eye/skin/hair and dress colors are not stated in the reviewed exchange.
- The final-sheet identity is unambiguously **Oriphi “Hope” Akmenos**, female, tiefling, with terminal **maroon** skin, **purple** hair and **gold** eyes. Additional terminal height **5'6\"** and weight **140 lb** do not authenticate E001 stature; the fan wiki's **5'8\"** is an unresolved discrepancy. The “Look” note is character-authored and says **armor assembled from vehicle parts** and **a memorial necklace**; the date/worn-state of both is unknown.
- Early two horns and natural original limbs must **not** be conflated with the left-sided prosthetic condition following E037/E038; **LEFT** arm/leg prostheses operate E039, and a **metallic LEFT horn** is later retrospectively confirmed. The post-E101 Hand of Voice replaces the anatomical **RIGHT hand**, independent of the earlier **LEFT** arm prosthesis.
- Fan/player-adjacent illustrations diverge: Scott Johnson's short/swept hair and goggle/workwear silhouette; Ron Tang's long braids and tail; Casey Boyd's pale short hair; Dingo's post-injury cybernetic image has its robotic arm on **Hope's right** rather than the played left. Therefore no existing external image is a canonical owner-approved baseline.
