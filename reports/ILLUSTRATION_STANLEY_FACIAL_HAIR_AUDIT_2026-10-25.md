# Stanley Billings — facial-hair and appearance chronology audit (2026-10-25)

**Campaign:** The Wastes. **Subject:** `WASTES-STANLEY-BILLINGS`. **Focus:** Stanley's moustache, goatee, wider facial hair, the Diontalus shift, and later Unbreakable Majesty transformation. **Research version:** 0.2 addendum to [integrated appearance reference](../project/ILLUSTRATION_STANLEY_BILLINGS_REFERENCE.md). **Original source identity:** GitHub `frankysan/There-Will-Be-Dungeons-Transcripts` pinned at `bba3e150ed807966cd540e0c0c29218037f77c80` for full SRT reads. The local MCP worktree advanced during the session to `6dad4986e01436784ce466aaf67809517539737d`; these are **not the same commit**, so the pinned SRT contents are explicitly attributed to the earlier revision. Source SRTs were not modified. **Two separately generated ASR transcripts read where specified, NO audio listened**.

## Main conclusion

**Stanley has a definite handlebar/Western curled moustache and small goatee at E023**, directly described by Jon in a played grooming scene. Jon subsequently endorsed an Audie Norman commission of a **facial-haired Stanley** as *his visual canon at the time* in the March 2019 cast Q&A; the exact corresponding archived image file is not independently authenticated. **Diontalus makes the host clean-shaven in E045.** In E060, characters explicitly acknowledge that Stanley **no longer has a moustache**, although the permanence of Diontalus's exact enchantment and the reason for no moustache remain unproven. A **later, separate, actually played transformation occurs in The Wastes S2E03**, when Stanley is awarded *Unbreakable Majesty*: more regal and sharper, hair darker and longer, light almost luminous skin, and **nearly reflective silver eyes**, with an observer-dependent attractiveness effect. **Jon's April 2022 retrospective states he wanted the moustache removed for this beauty/neutrality design, and S3E03 has Jon directly correct Scott: the moustache is gone.** Do not draw the E023 handlebar/goatee as a timeless character default. The two separate sources for silver eyes—Diontalus E045's temporary altered host appearance and Stanley's own S2E03 *Unbreakable Majesty*—are chronologically and causally **different**.

## Illustration-state corrections

1. **A01 E001:** first spoken-session character still insufficiently physically described to *require* exact moustache shape, but E023 and F04 confirm a facial-haired design in the **early arc** and favor a provisional moustache/goatee draft. Do not simply project a future bare-face onto E001.
2. **A01B E023 (new):** separate dated haircut appearance state; explicit **brown, shaved-side/longer-swept-back hair; Western curled moustache + small goatee; ears visible; goggles mentioned**, with Jon as narrator/owner of appearance choice.
3. **A02 E045:** shared-body appearance altered to **clean-shaven, silver-eyed**, Diontalus-controlled. Do not confuse with later silver eyes at S2E03.
4. **A03 E059 onwards:** E060 says **no moustache** and Stanley looks different; **no documented automatic reset** of appearance after Diontalus's extraction.
5. **A07 S1 late:** E120 moustache conductor joke conflicts with no-moustache evidence and is a **joke / research ambiguity**, not mandatory regrowth; do not copy it into E118–E128 as a deterministic visual requirement.
6. **A08 S2E03:** the **separately grounded Unbreakable Majesty transformation**. More regal, ears visible, darker/longer hair, softly light appearance, nearly reflective silver eyes, viewer-dependent attractiveness. By later creator report, **no curled moustache**. Source moment itself does not explicitly specify the precise shaving instant.
7. **A09 S3E03:** adult Stanley remains **without moustache** per Jon. The “baby with moustache” joke must not become ordinary age-reversal anatomy. The S3E019 long beard is only a speculative **future** variant, not a current state.

**Existing artwork impact:** The illustration catalog contains no approved/generated Stanley model sheet or named scene rendering; the no-person Fate 4 garage and aerial Dusthill images do not provide a confirmed Stanley mismatch. **No IRV item created**, no art regenerated. Existing **fan art with a moustache** is *valid as an early-E023 interpretation* even though post-Unbreakable Majesty portraits should be checked to ensure they do not accidentally preserve the early moustache.
