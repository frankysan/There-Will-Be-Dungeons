## Review and balance limits

The complete current main prose, including introductions and final status, was read for every profile. Every final-status source note was read, along with selected additional origin, response, upgrade, custody and ritual notes. This is not a claim to have reread every footnote or historical draft. Twenty entity-specific alias patterns were searched across all 37 dossiers. Candidate hits are recorded for review routing; an absent literal hit does not establish that no later scene exists.

Fifteen selected complete primary-dossier paragraphs in nine dossiers were read, focusing on detector construction/equipment, organ reuse and collapse, final ribbon, ring uncertainty, Chance agency/recognition tease, Drone theory/larger source, and globe/crank transfers. The exact paragraph selections are in the register. Other profiles received current-prose/status-note review without a fresh full primary-source pass. No new original ASR/audio, item/character sheets, rulebook, art, maps, remote URLs or rendered-site preview were reviewed.

Artifact balance follows meaningful changes rather than character word targets. The Drone's longer assembly/research/crisis phase, the urn set's three distinct recovery/reuse phases, the hilt's short post-destruction legacy, the ribbon's fuller enchantment/gift phase and the globe's uneven transfer phases have distinct source-supported purposes. No short phase was padded or meaningful change removed to equalize counts. Measurements and page-specific balance reasons remain in the register.

Unknown custody, untested powers, unresolved source discrepancies and missing final inventories remain open. Survival, a later equipped item, a generic matching name or campaign victory alone does not establish disposal, recharge, destruction, ownership or universal properties. The artifact record is independent of Wastes/Inverse continuity.
