## Counting and editorial boundaries

- Counts cover the prose beneath each numbered chapter heading. Headings, footnote markers and definitions, navigation, opening metadata, and unnumbered prologues/epilogues are excluded. Apostrophe-joined and hyphenated words count as one; Markdown link labels count as prose, while link targets do not.
- A target of about 400 words, normally 350–450, gives future chapters comparable reading weight. It does not imply equal in-world duration, equal episode coverage or a reason to invent events or pad descriptions.
- Added detail comes from the existing episode dossiers, particularly the early Dusthill/Slavetown/Black Mine research, the road to Tietkalla, and Sigil. This pass did not reprocess ASR or independently verify audio. The existing narrative footnotes retain source limitations, competing names and continuity questions.
- Condensed detail remains available in the dossiers and preserved pre-edit narratives. All pre-existing footnote IDs and linked local destinations were retained; additional source pointers cover the relocated storm/mine approach, Tylander/Catherine encounter and early Nash regrowth.
