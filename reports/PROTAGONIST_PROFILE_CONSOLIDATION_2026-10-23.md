- E048's report Angelashi died and E054's real rescue remain distinct; her later pregnancy and four children are followed through E114–E118.
- **Diontalus** is *extracted by the Principle in E059* and has **no new confirmed death, escape, presence in Sigil or space launch through E118**. The E081 sacrifice of the four original protagonists is not automatically a new disposition of the extracted crystal.
- E081 ends with a true GM death declaration; E082–E089 subsequently reveal survival/restoration.
- Hope's separation in **E101** divides Arc Four and Arc Five **inside one episode**; E116's replay of her E113 Gate cutoff is explicitly noted instead of inventing an intervening unseen journey.
- Hope's living **Hand of the Voice**, the separate **Fist gauntlet**, Vorel's **Eye of Groxl** and the still-sought **Eye of the Voice** retain distinct identities. The Eye of Groxl is one owner-confirmed artifact despite ASR aliases.
- The actual **E118 key/book/mask acquisition belongs to Stanley**, not to Hope or Nash. Dalit's research is a private, unverified theory.
- Vorel's **self-awarded titles**, official Razak Val office, E087 M-sports championship, post-loss despair and Omnom's repeated E118 “Great Fang” address are not interchangeable recognitions.
