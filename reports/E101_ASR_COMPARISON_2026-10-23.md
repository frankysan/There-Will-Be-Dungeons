## Source and editorial cautions

1. **Arc boundary is thematic and enacted, not a podcast file division.** [Arc Three](../narrative/ARC_THREE.md) ends with Hope's choice and the portal closure; [Arc Four](../narrative/ARC_FOUR.md) opens with what follows at White Tower and the Kaiser Casino. **E101 must appear in both arc navigation entries** and remain **one episode** in sequence.
2. **Anatomy and objects:** Vorel first picked up **Fist + severed hand** in E100; **Hope now fuses the severed Hand of the Voice to herself**, while **the Fist remains a separate gauntlet**. Vorel returns the loose gauntlet to the chest and Nash/Stanley re-lock it. Last **key with Stanley**, not in lock or with Vorel.
3. **Speaker/knowledge:** Hope hears patron privately, Vorel witnesses an apparition restore Groxl, others see some transformation; do **not** collapse all those into one shared reveal. In particular patron's identity is **never fully named in E101**, though “Voice” is used in magic and backstory.
4. **Interests:** Both **Spatulo** and **Ulvert** have their own incentives; Spatulo warns of Tobacho's claim but still accompanies Hope. The figure calling himself Asmodeus is friendly **by presentation**, not proven safe/credible. Hope's aspiration to godhood and requests to revisit Tietkalla/friends are **stated goals**, not already achieved.
5. **Source verification priorities:** Listen to **~00:01–00:06** prologue and proper nouns, **~00:33–00:39** private arm instruction, **~01:06–01:28** anatomical mechanics/patron/Groxl, **~01:43–01:59** departure and demon withdrawal, **~02:15–02:24** key/chest, **~02:23–02:46** Prith mark and casino host. Compare recording clocks/source copies, especially stage break. No voice identification, episode release date or spell exactness should be certified without audio/handout support.
