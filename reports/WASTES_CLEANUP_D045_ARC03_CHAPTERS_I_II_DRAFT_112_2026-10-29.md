# Road to a Lost Kingdom — Arc 03, Chapters I–II

## I. The road from the Liberators

The name **Heroes of the Wastes** had scarcely been chosen before it began to weigh upon them. After the violence inflicted on **Slippery Brent**, **Stanley left the Liberators' camp alone**, unwilling to pretend that the cruelty was another heroic deed. The others sought him out, and their reunion made travel possible again. It did not settle their disagreement about what they might become. **Hope, Nash and Stanley** remained bound by the **Soulbroker's** deadline; **Vorel**, who had refused the compact, still chose to stand beside them.[^arc03-1]

Before departing, Vorel met privately with the **sightless former Koromukishi**. The nameless orc spoke of preserving a fledgling opposition to **the Principal** while the travelers sought a way to close **Esophageal Pass**. Their discussion pointed toward an alliance, not an army already assembled or a passage already sealed. The Liberators were preparing to move after reports that their hiding place had been compromised. With supplies from **Bertrand**, the company readied **Fate 4** and left a camp where even friends could not agree on what victory should mean.[^arc03-2]

**Stanley's first attempt at driving** sent the vehicle jolting forward. A young stowaway emerged from within: **Tedna**, desperate to fight the Principal and earn a place among the heroes. The company disputed his ability to survive their road, but they could not argue him back into the desert before a far stranger threat arrived.

A warrior in **powered armor** descended on mechanical wings. Calling himself **Sir Ranko of the Umbilical Order**, he accused Hope, Nash and Stanley of carrying a demonic stain, said his order could detect such things, and demanded their execution. **Vorel refused to surrender them.** Guns, magic, the armored stranger's small mechanical attackers and the desperate movement of Fate 4 turned the confrontation into a battle across the road. Even after the warrior had been overcome, he spoke of his order's power and released a brilliant signal whose purpose the party could not establish. He was killed in the clash. His claimed authority and any connection to the Principal remained unproven.[^arc03-3]

An explosion overturned Fate 4. The companions and **Bok Bok** survived. **Tedna was alive but unconscious** when they found him in the wreck, a reminder of how little shelter their company could offer a boy seeking adventure. They righted the vehicle and made repairs sufficient to continue, though the damage was far from gone.

That night, **lizardfolk hunters** and their raptor-like animals approached the camp. Their challenger, **Diptak of the Golden Land**, alleged that Vorel had claimed the priestess **Typalla** as a consort, and demanded an answer by combat. Whatever arrangement Diptak believed existed, **Vorel had not been shown agreeing to a marriage**. The hunter charged as the night tipped into battle.[^arc03-4]

The fighting continued through the darkness. Vorel struggled against Diptak and his hunters while the others fought the accompanying beasts. At last **Diptak yielded**. Some of his companions obeyed, though a wounded raptor kept attacking **Hope** until she killed it. Diptak acknowledged Vorel's strength and **pledged his Golden Land clan's allegiance**. It was a pledge spoken by the defeated hunter, not proof that a distant force had marched to war or that the disputed marriage had been settled.[^arc03-5]

The next morning, **Hope and Vorel patched the damaged roof with raptor hide**. It kept some of the desert out without making Fate 4 whole; an argument with Tedna soon left another hole from Stanley's magic. Farther west, the party encountered **Buttons**, an orange cat walking upright, wearing glasses and balancing on a mechanical leg. **Bok Bok** initially regarded him as food. Buttons instead paid for passage and made himself useful with scholarly conversation.

Buttons said he had once been an ordinary cat, altered by a wizard of **Arcanum Universalis**. He described **Trashburg**, the fortress above the oil sands, its gangs, public punishments and the roads around **T'ietkalla**. He copied an older map attributed to **Handsome Dan Dakota** and exchanged knowledge with Stanley. His stories offered leads, not a survey of a city the companions had yet to enter.[^arc03-6]

They **camped outside the Teet**, with no promise yet to explore the ruins Buttons hoped to study. On watch, **Hope heard singing** across the oil sands. Pale blue flashes glimmered in the distance, and invisible hands seemed to pull her away from Fate 4. She walked toward the music while the others remained behind, unaware of what awaited beneath the ground.

## II. Two voices

When **Stanley awoke**, **Nash** was entranced, pressing against Fate 4's closed door as if something outside called him. Stanley **tied him to a workbench for safety**, then discovered that **Hope, Vorel, Tedna, Bok Bok and Buttons** were missing. Tracks led away across the oil sands. Tedna, already drawn from the vehicle, could not be roused by Stanley's attempts to turn him back.

**Buttons** reappeared beside Stanley and joined the search. The little cat thought he might be less vulnerable to the song; neither knew whether that was a rule or a fortunate accident. Together they followed the tracks through a hidden entrance into a prison of old cells and **thorn-decorated doors**. They agreed that **“lemon tree”** would be their signal to flee.[^arc03-7]

Beyond the cells lay a dark **oil lake** and a bone-strewn shore. A monstrous singer, part human-like body and part crawling bulk, drew the missing travelers toward it. Another mouth sang from its flesh. An aged, sharp-eared **hag**, whom the creature called its mother, guarded the prisoners. **Hope, Vorel, Tedna and Bok Bok** stood under the song's hold, their wills unavailable to them even as their bodies could be turned against a rescuer.

Stanley tried words first. The captors talked of feeding upon people and harvesting **“mana waves”**, of ancient wards and a captive long kept below. Those were their explanations, not a history the companions could verify. Stanley refused to leave his friends as prey. When negotiation failed and the hag tried to charm him, he defended himself with spells and his animated sword **Diplomacy**. The captive companions posed dangers they could not knowingly choose. **Bok Bok's dynamite** tore into the singer's body, and Stanley's **Feather Fall** helped prevent a worse fall. The creature was destroyed. Stanley would later recount the hag's death as well, though his companions had not witnessed every blow.[^arc03-8]

As the spell's victims began to stir, a **luminous blue, elven-looking figure** emerged from the oil. He called himself **Diontalus**, an **archfey of carnage**, and spoke of old imprisonment, lost strength and a world he wished to reshape for elves. He offered Stanley power in exchange for the energy he believed the warlock could provide. The claims about ancient wars and his title were **Diontalus's own**.

**Stanley refused.** He would not bind himself to another power that spoke openly of slaughter. Diontalus answered not with persuasion but with force: the figure became blue light, **entered Stanley's body against his will**, and overwhelmed him. The others were only beginning to wake. The encounter did not make Stanley a willing signatory to anything, and his companions could not yet know what he had endured alone.[^arc03-9]

Elsewhere, **Nash** woke from his trance to find himself bound in Fate 4. He used **Reduce** to free himself and searched the oil field for the others. Belowground, the prison shook and began to collapse. Stanley called **“lemon tree”**; Vorel hauled **Stanley and Bok Bok**, while **Hope helped Tedna**, whose boot caught during the escape. Rising oil nearly trapped them. Stanley used an **immovable rod** to free Vorel from a flooded stair, but the device was **left submerged** as the survivors hurried upward. They crossed dangerous oil sands and reunited with Nash at the edge of the field.[^arc03-10]

The others could describe a strangely comforting dream that had held them, and Stanley could speak of the hag and her son. **He did not give everyone a full account of Diontalus's intrusion.** What happened in the prison had left different memories with different travelers. The voice now sharing Stanley's body had not been invited, whatever its plans for him.

Back aboard Fate 4, Buttons discussed his past and **gave Stanley written material for the Blink spell**; receiving it did not prove Stanley could already cast it. They reviewed their borrowed map and the cat's secondhand knowledge of **Trashburg**, its rulers, its violent arena and the keep. They still wanted the promised paired firearms, **Fox and Scully**, but none of Buttons's accounts gave them a safe way inside.

The group chose to **conceal the vehicle outside the Teet** and approach on foot. Beyond the oil sands stood the city that might answer some of their questions. They had not entered it yet. Stanley carried a secret from the prison toward streets in which other people would soon notice that **two wills were struggling for one body**.[^arc03-11]
