# Road to a Lost Kingdom — Arc 03, Chapters V–VII

## V. The price of the rocket

The rocket had already come back at **Hope**. Its explosion filled the Toe Rippers' underground passage with falling stone, smoke and air so foul that breathing became a struggle. The launcher had been fired at the end of the previous night's action; there was **no second rocket attack** when the companions began searching for a way out. **Hope lay unconscious**, her left side shattered, while **Nash**, **Vorel**, **Stanley** and **Bok Bok** fought for breath and for space to carry her through the wreckage.[^arc03-28]

The danger did not end with the collapse. Enemy fighters pressed into the damaged passages, and the others had to protect Hope while finding air that would sustain them. Stanley acted and spoke as himself during parts of the ordeal, even though **Diontalus** still shared his body. Their efforts were not a simple healing spell that made the harm disappear: they could stop some bleeding and keep her alive, but the blast had ruined tissue, bones and movement in ways the companions could not repair beneath the city.

Deep within the tunnels they encountered **Blunt Goliver** and more Toe Rippers. The leader had survived both the burned streets and the Golden Gargoyle battle; **Monsoon's promised help still depended on his death**. Nash's fire struck the enemies. When Goliver was wounded and begged to live, **Stanley stabbed him** and **Vorel beheaded him**. The company took the severed head with them. Whatever danger Goliver had posed, his plea and the manner of the killing were part of what the heroes had done, not details to smooth away. This was the **E038** killing—not a belated claim that the earlier nightclub escape had finished the bargain.[^arc03-29]

They carried Hope out through dangerous streets. Shrapnel had torn into her face, horn and left side; her **left arm and leg** were gravely damaged, with movement and sensation affected. The others considered where help might be found while avoiding armed patrols. They chose **Monsoon Cabbage**. At his Twenty-Six Block headquarters they **delivered Goliver's head**, and Monsoon examined it closely. With the grim condition of his bargain now met, he arranged medical assistance instead of sending the adventurers back into the streets.[^arc03-30]

**Dr Dickles** assessed Hope and brought her to an improvised clinic for examination and surgery. He told them the shattered bones in her left arm and leg could not be saved using the treatment available. The companions questioned his ability to help but stayed. Through hours of operations, **Nash, Vorel and Stanley** assisted, using their own abilities where they could. Healing magic helped injured tissue and blood loss; **it did not restore the destroyed limbs**. The doctor amputated **Hope's left arm and left leg** as part of the effort to save her life. She was not made whole by an effortless miracle.[^arc03-31]

Near the end of the long surgery, **Metal Mandy** came to begin fitting **mechanical replacements**, and a healer called **Sister Sarah** was involved in Hope's care. The company could hope she would wake and learn to use them. The immediate medical ordeal, however, ended with Hope still recovering under care. The first real tests of her new mechanical arm and leg—and the danger that awaited her caregivers—were still ahead.

## VI. Hope rebuilt and Monsoon's stories

While **Hope** remained under treatment, **Stanley** helped care for patients at Dr Dickles's clinic. His gentleness with strangers stood in sharp contrast to the voice of **Diontalus**, which had spoken so casually of violence. **Nash** and **Vorel** watched the streets until an armed force moved through Trashburg and the sound of an official announcement changed their plans. The Teet authorities named **“Stanley Billings, also called Diontalus”** as a dangerous wanted spellcaster, claiming that an Arcanum Universalis mage had reported an attempted murder. That **public accusation** was real; the asserted crime had **not been independently proved**. The companions feared **Buttons** might have informed on Stanley, remembering how Stanley had compelled the cat with Suggestion, but the exact route by which the authorities learned his name remained uncertain.[^arc03-32]

The men withdrew to a **skull-marked safe house** connected with Vorel's earlier secret meeting. Hope was not there. They debated hiding, surrendering, and whether fighting through the city would endanger the very people they hoped to protect. Stanley resisted reckless destruction while the ruthless voice sharing his body argued differently. Armed patrols and curfews narrowed their options.

**Hope awoke apart from them**, under **Metal Mandy's** care. She was fitted with a **mechanical left arm and leg**, neither immediately comfortable nor a simple replacement for the body she had lost. Mandy adjusted their movement and demonstrated a grip stronger than ordinary flesh. A **concealed projectile mechanism** lay in the arm, making use of a recovered rocket. Hope could operate her new limbs, but learning to do so was a private and difficult beginning, not evidence that she had fought with them during the preceding cave escape. The original external launcher was no longer available according to this episode's account.[^arc03-33]

Rest and medication left Hope with fragmented impressions of another horror at the clinic. She remembered an assault in which **Metal Mandy** and **Sister Sarah** appeared to be attacked, then awoke in a guarded room under the Twenty-Sixers' control. **Monsoon Cabbage told her that he had ordered Dr Dickles, Sister Sarah and Metal Mandy killed**, arguing that they might betray his arrangements. Hope had heard an admission of responsibility; her sedated flashes were **not a reliable, complete view of every victim's fate or each attack**. The man whose protection had allowed her surgery now described the destruction of those who had cared for her as a useful precaution. A stone-bodied servant, **Lazlo**, helped her through her uncertain recovery.[^arc03-34]

As darkness came, the others made a diversion and slipped between patrols into **Twenty-Six Block** territory. They were at last **reunited with Hope** inside Monsoon's guarded headquarters. He claimed that Teet forces had raided **Charlemagne's** and that **Buttons had given away their identities**. He also said Tedna and others were detained in the keep. Parts of the city's official search were evident to the party, but Monsoon's intelligence about every captive and the cat's exact role remained his report, not a prison roster they had inspected.

Monsoon then spoke at length over **Blunt Goliver's head**. He asserted that a **Lactid exile** had been controlling the gang chief, explaining why his behavior and resistance to magic had seemed strange. He described a lost tiefling homeland called **T'ietkalla**, a figure called **Kefeli**, and **Andreeste**, the alleged dark power behind the Guzzle Gang's religion. Monsoon's account connected an old curse, the **Principal**, **Jesus Chrome** and a kingdom he wished to reclaim. It could help the company choose its next road, but these were the histories and interpretations of an ambitious political actor. Even the further name he offered as his own was not proof of every royal claim that accompanied it.[^arc03-35]

He proposed an approach into the fortress **beneath the Pit of Despair**, whose monstrous resident he called a **froghemoth**. He also speculated that **Jolt** intended to steal the **Gem of Tides** during the coming wedding, and that the gem could transform contaminated oil into water. The company had not seen the gem or witnessed a robbery. Monsoon wanted an alliance and the return of what he called his country; the travelers had reasons to doubt both his candor and his methods. **P0D's captive pixies** and **Tedna's uncertain situation** remained unfinished obligations.[^arc03-36]

They gathered supplies and chose clothes from Monsoon's storage rooms. The idea was to slip through streets watched by soldiers and reach the Pit. They ended **E039 with disguises and a plan**, not with a successful entry into the Teet or the rescue of anyone held there.

## VII. Toward the Pit

The company rode the **Twenty-Six Block's concealed elevator** back toward the dark streets. **Monsoon's proposed route** led south to the **Pit of Despair**, but Teet patrols and Guzzle Gang soldiers were searching the houses between them and the entrance. **P0D** scouted ahead, bringing reports of guarded routes. The fortress's great **spotlights** swung across rooftops and alleys, making each open crossing a risk. **Diontalus**, speaking through Stanley's body, cast an illusion to look like a **Teet Guard**. The uniform was appearance, not a transfer of loyalty.[^arc03-37]

**Hope's new mechanical left leg** could not yet carry her quickly. **Vorel carried her** as they advanced, making a noisy and unusual silhouette. **Nash** cast Darkvision and followed while the others briefly spread out. They passed a sleeping sentry and glimpsed soldiers searching civilian homes, looking for Stanley. Their route was not a triumphant infiltration: innocent people were being detained while the guards hunted one of the travelers.[^arc03-38]

Ahead of the others, Diontalus met a pair of **Guzzle Gang** searchers who despised the Teet Guard. He changed his **illusory uniform again**, now resembling a Guzzle member, and offered to help them look for the fugitive. A magical noise served as his diversion. It bought time, but his companions could not automatically recognize Stanley inside the borrowed appearance. The trick put one member of the company among their pursuers while the others struggled to pass outside.[^arc03-39]

A spotlight caught **Vorel, Hope and Nash**. They offered awkward false names, but the challenged guard summoned reinforcements. Vorel put Hope down and revealed himself. What had begun as stealth became a running **street battle**. Vorel grew larger and fought through gunfire, Hope fired her shotgun, and Nash used bolts of fire and a **Fireball** against approaching enemies. A magical sphere of darkness spread across part of the alley, cutting sightlines and confusing patrols. Diontalus remained disguised for part of the struggle, trying to turn enemies away from his companions rather than fighting openly beside them.[^arc03-40]

The Teet and Guzzle forces kept arriving. **Vorel's chainsaw-edged sword** and bursts of flame struck opponents; Hope fought despite the strain of her new limbs. Nash used more magic to slow the pursuit. Two guards jokingly called **“the two Phillips”** disappeared into the confusion of the dark area; the party could not inspect their fate, and no certain death should be added to their story. Diontalus persuaded some of his apparent Guzzle allies to circle away. When Stanley sent Hope a message identifying himself, it prevented her from mistaking his disguise for another enemy.[^arc03-41]

Finally, for a moment, **no hostile soldiers remained immediately in their path**. The larger search had not ended. With the fortress's **spotlight still following them**, the companions **ran toward the Pit of Despair**.

They had **not yet entered or descended into the Pit**. They had not faced the creature beneath its bars, found a hidden grate or met the pursuing guard **George**. Those events still waited beyond this episode's boundary. **Arc 03 ended with the heroes in motion, pursued and exposed, on the road toward the place Monsoon said might lead below the fortress.**[^arc03-42]
