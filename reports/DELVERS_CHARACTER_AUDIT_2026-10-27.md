## Findings and limits retained

- **Late references are not automatically new encounters.** Cheet's Academy assignment is reported by Broadside. Dumbledwark's later contribution is public advertising and a sealed letter, with one lead corroborated by Cassius's release. Margaret's correspondence/pamphlet request does not perform a later school meeting. Shrugs's later name appears in recollection of the heist.
- **Visions and objects retain their boundaries.** Nym's later appearance is a ritual vision after her body crumbles. The later radiant lance is Loran's weapon, not a returned knight. A family image in the tower is a keepsake, not physical attendance or relocation. The walking shrub Brad/Brador remains separate from Chad/Brad the minotaur; shared names and performers do not establish cross-campaign identity.
- **Rescue is not a complete personal epilogue.** Withers and Wildfire are rescued alive/stable without a later individual confession, legal outcome or family reunion. Cassius's practical restoration is not a formal acquittal. Hissia/Nopa reunite without a supplied marriage or future residence. Enzo's quiet reflection does not establish final metaphysical release.
- **Definite endings remain definite.** Khalazar is destroyed, Chad and the other minotaurs become inert bones, and the three main Delvers have completed one-year epilogues. Evelyn's wedding invitation remains future notice, the royal letter sealed. Creator defeat branches and hypothetical returns do not become lived events or biological immunity.
- **Proportion is not a quota.** Main-prose arc measurements are diagnostic. Broadside's raw largest-arc/median ratio is 2.376, so his complete main prose was reread: 202 words cover the substantial mercy/amends phase, while 85 and 51 cover briefer work/aftermath appearances. That difference reflects participation; no padding or cut was warranted. The major PCs' developed arcs remain comparable, without imposing their length on guests or children.

No audio, ASR comparison, original sheets/art/maps or complete campaign rereading was performed. Existing spelling, identity, mechanics, inventory and listening issues remain open. No source dossier/media, biography or unrelated identity was changed, and no evidence issue was closed.
