## Critical continuity and evidence guardrails

- **E101 is shared:** the earlier part closes Arc Four, and **Hope's departure starts Arc Five inside the same recording**. No invented episode-boundary transition.
- **E107–E109 and E127 are not main-party continuation episodes**. Linked for navigation but excluded from narrative event clock and main-chapter source coverage.
- **E113/E116 Gate discontinuity remains explicit**: E113 shows an apparent return to Tobacho while E116 resumes at the Council and does not take that return. No invented double-trip to repair the source difference.
- **Squirts dies E111, Brent revives/reconstitutes a body in a separately played E125 Hell scene**, and E128's spoken “Bok Bok has Squirts in tow” remains an **open roster/name contradiction**. No unshown meeting with Chunks or Mars ship.
- **Omnom's death (E121), Henatar's death after yielding (E128) and Diontalus's witnessed revival by Healing Word (E128)** retain their exact sequence, without applying later knowledge to an earlier episode cutoff.
- **Sole private revelations stay private until shared in play:** Hope's Voice commands, Stanley's father letter, Nash's heart visions and Soulbroker's interview; **Stanley tells the party about the Soulbroker only after they return from the storm**.
- **NPC history is attributed**, especially the Principle's Lich Project visions, Yoshalar's dragon/Weir account, the High Elf's archfey history and the Soulbroker's account of the Principle/Weir deal. **The actual planetary rift, buried creature, storm and successful escape are eyewitness events**.
- **Planned and completed activity stays separate**: Ruby not built, missing Tungsten Wheel not contacted, *Sending* not a completed engineer search, Villa Ornath's mask not fully attuned, Hope's ring not charged by corpses, Mind Master not confronted, no formal “king of space” rule, no Mars arrival witnessed.
