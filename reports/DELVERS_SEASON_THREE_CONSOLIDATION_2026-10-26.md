## Structural and factual decisions

- **Nine main chapters:** the proposed eight-chapter outline compressed gate entry, rescue, ritual, battle and return into one final unit. A separate gate/preparation chapter preserves the distinct approach and imminent rescue before the finale. There is no new arc or episode boundary.
- **Chronology and disclosure:** Evelyn/Krandal's private spa claims remain private. The specific Hissia and skeletal-curse conversations recalled in town are identified in footnotes as S3E10-presented retrospective spa scenes. The long rest, censer and expedition follow without inserting another day from the old narrative's repeated morning phrasing.
- **False lives and mental history:** the All Rat's foreclosure, public bounty, apparent Zoltar death, asylum and imagined divine rejection are projections. The finale's ritual history remains shared vision, and the restored city remains psychic space revealed as illusory. No ancestor genealogy is certified by resemblance.
- **Custody:** local metal keys differ from organ urns. First jar is installed, retrieved, joined by the second/third, transferred Evelyn→Koh, installed/released again and later produced from Koh's bag. Badge retrieval and exact jar rebagging are not invented to fill source gaps.
- **Rescue and victory:** no captive Remove Curse casts are inserted after the explicit retraction. Both circles operate, without an audited total of rescued people. Hissia is suspected then physically confirmed. Darmah's unshown revival remains a limit. Khalazar's actual final defeat is preserved, rather than confused with the earlier shade.
- **Final outcomes:** all ten minotaurs end as bones, with no living resurrection. The year's outcomes are fulfilled epilogue, not merely plans. Main prose shortens the groom to Lord Ethan Travail; Renald/Renault remains in the note rather than selecting an unverified middle rendering. Invitation does not become a ceremony, and the royal letter is not opened editorially.

These are presentation and evidence-boundary decisions from existing records. No new naming authority, identity merge, bonus-to-canon conversion or issue closure is claimed. The original `horizon` note key remains and now has a matching reference in the opening.
