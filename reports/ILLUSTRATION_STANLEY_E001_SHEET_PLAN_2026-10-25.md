## Planned progressive gates

1. **Face check / normalization:** Open and compare the exact v0 face pixels with the user's accepted first face direction and the source-backed early/late facial-hair distinction. If the likeness matches, normalize a *new versioned file* proportionally to **1024 × 1024 sRGB** (do not overwrite v0), visually inspect its actual pixels, record exact SHA/profile and obtain owner's approval of that precise derivative. Only then mark `FACE_APPROVED`. If v0 is not the accepted face, treat it as a separate candidate and confirm which image is the anchor.
2. **Front:** Generate a **single** complete head-to-boots front figure using the accepted actual face as reference. Define one practical slightly dapper coat and visible usable pocket(s), shirt/vest, trousers, boots; one period-appropriate sheathed rapier if included in this neutral E001 composite. Lock coat hem, collar, fastening, materials, color, hip side and strap geometry. No multi-view collage or on-image text. Approve and repository-verify before advancing.
3. **Side:** Generate **one** strict full-body profile with the *same* face, moustache placement if selected, hairline/ears, garment cut, pocket, sheathed rapier and proportions. State which of Stanley's **own left or right** is shown; do not mirror asymmetrical equipment. Owner acceptance + verified output precedes the next phase.
4. **Back:** One strict rear full-body view of the same exact outfit and head/hair construction. Back seams, coat hem, belt, scabbard location and boots must reconcile with front and profile. No additional packs or weapons. Owner acceptance + verified output precedes composition.
5. **Assemble:** Deterministically reuse the **actual four approved anchor images**, not a regenerated multiview figure. Default **page 1** contains front/profile/back at comparable body heights; **page 2** retains the accepted face in high detail, optionally supported by approved neutral expression and rapier/coat-pocket details. Every page text-free and separately **1024-square, PNG, sRGB**, inspected, hash-verified and individually owner-reviewed. The combined sheet becomes `OWNER_APPROVED` only after final layout approval and repository verification.

**Carry-forward:** The v0 face design may carry forward **only once identified and technically normalized**; the neutral full-body garment and equipment are *not* locked by an unseen v0 sheet. **Change later:** E023 barber result, E045 Diontalus interference, E060 no moustache, E088 tailoring/restoration, S2E03 silver-eyed transformation each require explicit state treatment, not silent revisions to A01. **Exclude from A01:** all later gear, cosmetic alterations and final character-sheet assumptions.
