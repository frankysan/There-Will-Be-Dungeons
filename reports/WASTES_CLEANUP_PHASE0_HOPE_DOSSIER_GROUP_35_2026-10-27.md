## Character history, evidence classes and finale

After her injury and later T'ietkalla coronation, she carries out engineering reforms **and** severe punishments; crown and civic work do not erase the observed costs of her actions. Omnom's allegations concerning the reason for her Razak Val rocket remain contested intentions rather than established fact. E081's CHIDS Drive beam results in the original four being declared dead, followed by their actual E088 return through the mirror rite. E101 Hope chooses a living Hand of the Voice and leaves the others; aspirations toward godhood, the Voice's promises and early attempts at reconciliation must not be described as accomplished transformations. She later returns to the group and makes a Harbor/Elhandar reconciliation, with Jon's 2022 “best friend” interpretation being retrospective, not a newly performed ceremony or independent Hope statement.

**Hope's personal and ethical scenes:** In S3E09 empathy grenades affect the mechanized General Motors, whom Hope physically kills; she subsequently kills a disarmed captive without learning the requested information. The records should not silently convert either act into lasting mercy or an exhaustive personality judgment. ## Physical custody, mechanics and episode-horizon reconciliation

**The Hand of the Voice is not the replacement hand:** Hope's living Hand is attached from E101 through S3E01; it can function in Pentel Weiss despite ordinary spell suppression, privately advises Hope to sing against the S2E11 eye, and is used to teleport the companions to Fate Six S2E12. The missing **Eye of the Voice** is not demonstrated acquired in those episodes. **S3E02 the Principal severs and steals the supernatural Hand**, leaving Hope at zero HP before Stanley heals her and later **Regenerate creates an ordinary biological arm**. That does not restore Hand powers. S3E12 the Principal passes the stolen Hand to Brett/Asmodeus and his assistant, not to Hope, and no subsequent recovery is shown. S3E03 Stanley *actually* transfers enchanted **Diplomacy** to Hope, separate from her later selected S3E04 +2 rapier, armor and shield. The sword repeatedly slips beyond her control range S3E06/S3E09 but is later used without a narrated retrieval, an inventory continuity lead only.

**Hollow Ring and planes:** In S3E11 the Principal physically returns Hope's recharged **Hollow Ring** after a long rest, and she uses its one charge for the portal to Weir. The ring is **depleted** during S3E12 and S3E13; Hope refuses the suggested evil act needed to recharge it, and Brett himself brings all four to Earth. The Principal's S3E15 portals personally take Hope to Sigil and back out of Worldbreaker's mouth; that is not a new ring charge, Hope spell or Fate Six capability. On S3E15 her windboard actually fails and is swallowed by the tarrasque while Stanley rescues her to his functioning board.

**Triangulosphere status by date:** The device is unfinished S2E02, completed but only locally demonstrated on Mars S3E04, taken toward Earth S3E05, and still physically **unactivated by S3E17**. Hope has the remote while the machine may remain in the Solar Mines chamber or Elhandari custody; orders to retrieve it are not performed retrieval until demonstrated. **S3E17 Hope personally carries invisible Nash aloft and shields him to finish ten full rounds of Banishment**, after which the Principal lunar copies collapse. **Full Earth renewal actually occurs only S3E18**. Hope receives a +2 crossbow S3E15 and uses it on the living tarrasque, but it is jammed/discarded in S3E16 per retrospective episode note and is not recovered S3E17.
