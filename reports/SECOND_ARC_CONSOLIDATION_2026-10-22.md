# Second-arc documentation consolidation — E055–E081

**Date:** 2026-10-22. **Source boundary:** corrected main *Wastes* E055–E081. **Scope:** Structural editorial migration after the ending of the CHIDS/Traversal Drive confrontation, not a rereading or independent listening to 27 recordings. **The source dossiers, raw Parakeet ASR files, spelling decisions and episode-era knowledge have not been rewritten by this task.** Full story spoilers.

## The central editorial decision

The research layer remains **one source-first episode dossier per recording**, plus the original SRT and evidence reports. The **live reference layer should not acquire a new appended episode section each time**; it presents current subjects and unresolved evidence. The **narrative layer** presents an organized story that need not recap each session in equal detail. **The review layer** lists actual listening/handout priorities, not all previously entered timestamped hypotheses.

The E081 ending changes the emphasis of Year Two: its throughline is **attempting to govern and reform, taking responsibility for violence at Razak Val, promising to save Sira, negotiating among unreliable explanations of CHIDS and the Drive, and rejecting Quizquaz's offer to abandon Earth**. The last act is **a four-person beam entry, disappearance and explicit GM declaration of death**. The aftermath and permanence remain unshown, rather than being filled in from a future sheet or wiki.

## Editorial and source invariants

- **Historical cutoff remains historical truth:** Sira was abducted at E066 and **released E081**; the Drive was not reached at E071 and **physically seen E079**; an E077 GM plan for Vorel's hand repair/rest precedes its **performed E078** resolution; old guests missing since E008–E009 were **rescued E078**, while **Chance disappears again E081**.
- **Demonstrated death versus permanent metaphysics:** Orp, Zercil, Follow and Rorf have clear played deaths. **Cerinthala's E079 body disintegrates**, but she revived earlier; **Dolo Popio's manifested body dies E081**, but an irretrievable demon essence is not demonstrated. **Four protagonists vanish and the GM calls them dead E081**; later permanent fate and planetary post-activation state are not shown.
- **Claims remain attributed:** The spectral dragon, Charles Dickens, Alvert, Cerinthala, the hive council, alleged future Wall Nash and Quizquaz offer **potentially contradictory testimony**. The observed devices, brains/vats, room transitions and beam activation are stronger evidence than assertions about the origin of Earth, lizardfolk, the Soulbroker or the Principle.
- **Identity and orthography:** One **Eye of Groxl** (owner-confirmed) even though a vessel reads **G-R-0-X-1**. **Dolo Popio, Ovaxiad, Cerinthala, Kitobol Prith, Andreeste** and other ASR labels require authoritative spelling review.
- **Source integrity:** No edits to `sources/*.parakeet.processed.srt`; no alteration of episode dossiers or retrospective Q&A. Archived snapshots were link-rebased, not treated as independent new evidence.
