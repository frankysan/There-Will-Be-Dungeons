## Result and evidence boundaries

The stated limit is one object up to sixty pounds; the actual hammer test establishes storage of that weapon, not a measured maximum, generic Bag of Holding rules, retrieval speed or damage behavior. Later references place the bag among hidden wedding arms and describe concealed warhammer storage at the Bronze Ball. The latter dossier's Wind Fan/Bag of Hammer Holding wording remains explicit in the note without an invented fan transformation or new capacity test. Evelyn's separately purchased general holding bag for javelins does not replace or enlarge Margaret's tote. Her last known bearer status does not inventory the bag after the finale.

The ring's concealed chest, stone-metal appearance, archaic Elven lettering, mental identification and visible resizing are preserved. The earlier suggestion that it lay there for ages is removed as an unverified storage history. Recognizing old letters does not translate them, and resemblance to other relics does not prove a shared maker or psychic-only operation. The described +1 Armor Class and +1 to all saving throws remain separate from an isolated test of every protection. Koh actually hands the ring to Krandal, who wears it on his left middle finger; the heavy scale mail left ashore is not sold.

The final ring state changes materially: Krandal is the last known wearer, but S2E05's sheet/inventory uncertainty prevents treating continued wear as verified. It does not prove a loss or return either. The Cloak of Protection and named Poison Resistance/Evasion/Free Action/Feather Falling rings remain separate, as do mental marriage rings and Evelyn's unidentified ring-assisted descent. The old underwater cutoff is historical rather than a claim that the party never left. No new owner, destruction or epilogue inventory is invented.
