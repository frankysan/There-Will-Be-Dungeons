## Factual and chronological corrections

- **Deed:** S2E01's evidence map places the physical handover before the later inspection-return/farm outing. The new text says the deed is already in hand rather than repeating the older narrative's later handover placement.
- **Larva:** S2E06 shows a dead worker with a burst chest and a larva fleeing the infirmary. The rewrite does not invent an observed emergence from that particular body.
- **Fire:** Suggestion at the heist, observed blaze in the next episode and later acknowledgment of Fern's release remain distinct disclosure stages.
- **Maynard:** The awakened-patient report is placed on the way to the Academy, before the assessment, and stays a report rather than five examinations by the Delvers.
- **Enemy and custody:** Anthela's physical rescue is preserved while Khalazar's true death remains unestablished. The S2E10 handover of the red shard and its unexplained S2E11 reappearance remain in the notes.
- **Arc ending:** The tables obstruct the pit but do not stop its sound or the arriving elves. The subsequent three-month transition and final campaign outcomes are not written into this arc.

These are dossier-grounded presentation corrections, not new listening resolutions. No new naming decisions, identity merges or ledger issue closures were made.
