## Editorial guardrails and follow-ups

1. Use these **canonical names in all new editorial narrative and world prose** and coordinate a reviewed correction of wrong *editorial prose* in existing live pages, not just captions. Do **not** bulk-rewrite `sources/`, generated transcripts, direct historical quotations, episode titles, frozen reports or established URLs/slugs. Preserve wrong old spellings as **legacy aliases** with this artifact/Discord citation. Name choice is settled; spoken wording is not thereby audio-verified.
2. **No retroactive disclosure:** the regional map shows locations and regional boundaries without identifying which version the players were shown in E001, E005, E054, E103 or S3. Large regional labels and geographic placement are cartographic claims at an **unknown production/story date**, not a verified character itinerary or historical political census.
3. **No automatic real-world credit:** the in-world Handsome Dan Dakota attribution is not the illustrator's legal identity. Beau's account posting a file is not an explicit statement of who drew it or who holds copyright.
4. **The Principal / the Principle is no longer an unresolved *spelling* dispute**: the owner authoritatively selected **The Principal** as the canonical title. Likewise use **Khaervilon**, not **Kervalon**. Listener checks may still be needed for precise utterances, source-scene referents and claims, but must not silently reopen a settled spelling choice or duplicate its entity.
5. The archive remains **Git-ignored/private**. Link to its local path and Discord provenance here rather than embedding or committing the copyrighted JPEG.
