## Cross-reference to the pre-existing six private maps

This map is **not a duplicate, crop or ordinary higher-resolution copy** of `local/images-wiki/maps/Beyond the Deadfingers.webp` or `Beyond the Deadfingers - Annotated.webp`. ## Editorial guardrails and follow-ups

1. Use the new exact forms for **new map-specific metadata, captions and curated geographic prose**, but do not bulk-rewrite `sources/`, episode source text, verbal quotations, published episode titles or legacy links. Record both preferred map spelling and index aliases, with this artifact/Discord URL cited.
2. **No retroactive disclosure:** the regional map shows locations and regional boundaries without identifying which version the players were shown in E001, E005, E054, E103 or S3. Large regional labels and geographic placement are cartographic claims at an **unknown production/story date**, not a verified character itinerary or historical political census.
3. **No automatic real-world credit:** the in-world Handsome Dan Dakota attribution is not the illustrator's legal identity. Beau's account posting a file is not an explicit statement of who drew it or who holds copyright.
4. **Listening still needed** where the spoken phonemes, entity equivalence or a conflicting scene-specific spelling matter. Investigate the distinction between **The Principal** as map title and **the Principle** as traditional antagonist title without generating duplicate characters.
5. The archive remains **Git-ignored/private**. Link to its local path and Discord provenance here rather than embedding or committing the copyrighted JPEG.
