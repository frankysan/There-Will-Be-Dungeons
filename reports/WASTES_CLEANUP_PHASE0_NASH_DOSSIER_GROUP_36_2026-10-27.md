## Cross-episode Nash body and identity timeline

**Living original character, distinct counterparts:** Nash begins as a Solar Mines survivor and Dusthill wanderer, subsequently a companion of Hope, Stanley and Vorel. He grieves **P0D's actual E050 death** while recognizing Diontalus's agency as distinct from Stanley's. He accepts the E081 CHIDS Drive sacrifice and is later restored. The 99 alternate Nashes gathered on Mars in S3E03 are not main Nash, nor are White Nash or Unfortunate Nash identical to him. **S3E07 Unfortunate Nash's reported *Meteor Swarm* kills 97 alternates**, with this death count attributed to survivor report, not independently inspected bodies; White Nash is then shot dead by Bertrand Bolg in S3E09, whereas main Nash survives and enters the mine with Unfortunate Nash. The **51 immediate copies, subsequent 3,280 and after-death figure 95,120 in S3E12** are a different episode's multiplication event and must not be used as retroactive count of S3E03's 99 variants. Which clone enters Hell in S3E12 cannot be objectively established as original just from appearance, though the maintained story treats the continuing Nash as the protagonist.

**Chronological anatomy:** From the previous heart/eye survival and S2E12 death, Stanley's *Regenerate* visibly rebuilds Nash's humanoid body during S3E01–02, when a separately speaking shoulder mouth/eye **Sprout** emerges, distinct from an existing extra baby hand; **its biological origin or independent soul is not established**. The metal dragon engine is still inside him during S3E12 Hell confinement. **S3E13 Brett physically removes and keeps the implanted dragon-metal heart**, while constructing a functioning **flesh heart from Nash's own tissues**, neither a donor transplant nor Nash's death; Nash immediately demonstrates functioning *Firebolt* and levitation. The original injured black dragon's separately harvested natural heart and spectral mother's earlier appearance must not be conflated with the removed engine or Nash's later regenerated heart. **S3E14 Nash actually attempts the old black-dragon transformation after removal, achieves only an incomplete flesh shape and reverts**; the failure is not proof he can never transform again, nor is the Principal's psychological explanation objective. S3E15 he eats *Heroes' Feast*, reconciles affectionately with Sprout, but cannot fully transform. In **S3E18 his normal anatomy temporarily disappears during forced transformation into new chrome Principal**; he resists Council orders, destroys the lunar facility and dies in that chrome body, then **Stanley's successful *Wish* replicating *True Resurrection* restores a living flesh Nash**, including red eye, extra arm/mouth and pocket, without equipment. He reunites with Angelashi and four children on **Planet Nash** and chooses retirement. The cupboard **second Nash** stinger has unknown identity, not verified replacement, hallucination or a second confirmed Principal.

**The Principal's parentage and provenance:** A childhood instructor, General Motors, the Principal and dream figures make conflicting or self-interested father/manufacture claims (including the “Jesus Chrome” story). The S3E03 apparent Principal narrative occurs inside a parasite-induced shared dream; the S3E11 claimed engineering/ancient timelines are antagonist testimony, not independently witnessed origins. GM/performer retrospective comments support the *story design* but do not independently demonstrate biological descent, a specific childhood rescuer or actual ancient time travel. Nash's S2E03 account of Solar Mines conditions is first-person traumatic testimony, not a physically played current mine survey or verified institutional census; the group later actually enters the mines in S3E10. The later playful personality and risk-taking discussed in cast bonuses are player interpretations, not newly played events or immunity to permanent death.
