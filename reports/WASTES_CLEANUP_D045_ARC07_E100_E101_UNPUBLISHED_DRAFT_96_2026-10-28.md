# The City of Doors — the last struggle at White Tower

The tower had become a battlefield without a safe direction. Above it hung an impossible city-bearing moon, and the night that had come with it. Demons swept between the walls and the airships; paladins fought to keep **Zorander's Fist** away from enemies who had once fought one another. Somewhere beyond the siege waited Tobacho's promise of a way back to Earth. To collect that promise, the companions needed the relic intact. They had already lost control of the plan that was supposed to secure it.[^07-ending-1]

## VII. The Fist exposed

**Stanley** found **Alentris** falling outside White Tower and slowed her descent to the ground, saving her life. The chest containing the Fist still floated nearby, unbroken despite the fighting. He helped her recover it and used magic to carry them back to the battlements. The reprieve lasted only moments.

More attackers came from three newly arrived airships: armed orcs, a concealed spellcaster and a four-armed minotaur whose interest in the chest was unmistakable. The defenders inflicted terrible losses on the attacking fliers, but fire, fear and spells that reversed gravity tore apart their formation. **Nash** lost his dragon shape. **Hope** fought free of a giant's grasp and survived a long fall. **Vorel**, bleeding from an earlier wound, kept fighting while the relic passed through too many hands.

The minotaur struck Alentris unconscious and seized the still-closed chest, leaping over the edge with it. The companions tried to follow their commander and one another through a confusion of falling bodies and suspended weight. Vorel encountered a hooded enemy spellcaster and pulled away the covering. **Hope recognized Spatulo**, the scarred tiefling who had directed her toward Tobacho in Sigil. The others had not shared her earlier meeting, and the caster's apparent place among the attackers explained neither his allegiance nor his purpose.

Nash and Hope found their way toward the tower entrance. A devastating spell left Stanley and Alentris unconscious amid the descending combatants. Vorel alone reached the floating case. Searching Stanley's belongings, he found the medallion key, fitted it into the chest and watched its panels unfold. Within the **Fist gauntlet** was a **severed hand**. Vorel lifted the gauntlet with that hand still inside, but he did not wear it or attach its contents to himself.

The chest was open. No one had delivered the relic, and the siege had not ended. **Hope** heard a summons meant only for her; **Vorel** experienced separate dark whispers from the object he held. Their companions could not simply know what either of them had heard. The decision waiting inside the glove was not yet anyone's victory.[^07-ending-2]

## VIII. The hand and the road apart

Outside White Tower, **Stanley** returned to consciousness when his body seemed closest to failure. He healed Alentris and brought her inside. Vorel, meanwhile, was stunned and dropped the exposed Fist. Hope sent her flying broom to retrieve it, but the tower's ward stopped the loose relic at the threshold. She caught it herself, wounded, as the unseen voice instructed her to abandon her own hand and take the one inside the gauntlet. The command was private; the others saw only what she chose to do.

**Hope** placed the gauntlet beneath her foot, severed her right hand and attached the exposed **Hand of the Voice** to her arm. The act nearly ended her life; then the new flesh joined, a shockwave swept the combatants aside, and her wounds vanished. Time appeared to stop around a hooded figure. He called her his champion. Asked what she wanted most, Hope answered that she wished to **become a god**. An answer was not yet an achieved destiny.

The figure's form was at most partly visible to others. **Vorel** witnessed the **Eye of Groxl restored** to his chest, though the patron's conversation with Hope was not generally heard. The figure detached the gauntlet, leaving Hope with the Hand but no longer wearing Zorander's Fist. Her altered body carried new cold power and a stranger's marks. She used **Finger of Death** against a demon, and the dead creature rose to carry her through the battle. The surrounding forces began to avoid her, responding to a transformation the companions could scarcely understand.

For the others, the bargain remained impossible to complete as planned. **Vorel returned the now-empty Fist gauntlet to the open chest**; an attempt to secure its mechanism failed. Nash helped tend Vorel's bleeding. Neither the gauntlet nor its original living contents could now be delivered to Tobacho as the party had promised. Hope had taken a power that might save her and might demand more of her than she could foresee.

The hooded Voice called her away from White Tower. She could bring her companions, he suggested, but she chose **to leave them behind**. She told Vorel to go back. To Nash, whose wife and expected child remained on Earth, the separation could only threaten another loss. Vorel had reason to believe in Hope's strength, but he could not decide her future for her. Stanley and the others had survived together only to watch their company come apart.

Beyond the tower, **Carthax** and **Ulvert** acknowledged that the Hand was no longer theirs to seize. Carthax reported **Orcus's order** for the demons to retreat; the forces actually began withdrawing. Their temporary alliance dissolved, but the wider Blood War was not ended. Ulvert offered Hope an invitation from **Asmodeus to the Kaiser Casino**. **Spatulo** warned that Tobacho's claim was still unanswered. Hope allowed the tiefling to accompany her.

A stone arch rose before them, its opening looking onto a fiery road. The hooded Voice passed through. **Hope followed, and Spatulo crossed behind her.** Then the gateway **closed and sank away**.

**Nash, Stanley and Vorel remained at White Tower.** The immediate siege had lifted, but they had not returned to Earth. They did not know the Voice's private demands, and they could not know whether Hope would come back. The close of that portal, rather than the moment she first spoke of leaving, was the end of their shared road here. Whatever happened afterward would be another part of the story.[^07-ending-3]
