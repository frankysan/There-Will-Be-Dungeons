## Required scene/custody and truth-status preservation

### S3E12 — the Weir battle and Hell's unsigned bargain, L563–595

- **L563–575:** Soulbroker / Kitobal Prith physically manifests as a huge goatlike threat after S3E11's cyclone. Nash intentionally receives **51 lightning damage** when he presses the Principal's barrier, giving **51 additional Nash copies**. Copy count increases through more lightning. Nash ***considers but does not cast Sunburst***, instead **actually casts an eighth-level Fireball**; GM allows duplicated simultaneous spells and adjudicates the tremendous combined strike. Soulbroker is **physically destroyed in the played battle**, but no demonstrated universal soul erasure, Weir population annihilation or planetary Worldbreaker extraction follows. Tens of thousands of Nash copies are scattered into adjacencies; original-versus-copied Nash identity **not conclusively resolved**.
- **L577–583:** The original four awaken **restrained in Hell**, not on Earth or at Solar Mines. Principal remains alive and offers to kill/sacrifice Nash to Brett/Asmodeus, a **proposal**, not a completed heart extraction. Brett is physically present. The Principal **actually gives the stolen Hand of the Voice to Brett**, who passes it to his **succubus assistant**; that assistant is the **last observed physical custodian of the Hand** (not Hope or the Principal). The **Eye of the Voice remains outstanding**, distinct from **Vorel's Eye of Groxl**.
- **L585–595:** Stanley negotiates against the Principal's proposed death payment. Vorel actually breaks stone restraints; Stanley actually uses *Dimension Door* to escape his table. Brett **snaps the Principal out of the room**, but his fate/location is *not* verified death. Brett offers Nash an engineered-heart exchange and further deals; none is accepted or performed in S3E12. Brett releases the heroes into an infernal casino VIP area and leaves them to discuss a contract. **No signature, Hollow Ring recharge, gate opening, departure from Hell, Ruby activation or Worldbreaker defeat** occurs by S3E12 cutoff. Hope's Hollow Ring is **uncharged** and its evil-act recharge requirement has not been met.
- **S3E12 original source paragraph L595 is an open decision point**: do not import S3E13's later answer or turn an unsigned negotiation into a consented death/heart transfer. Brett's accounts of cosmic ownership, divine power and future planetary restoration are interested claims, not observed effects.

### S3E13 — Nash's new heart, ship remanifestation and damaged Earth, L601–631

- **L601–611:** Time passes in Hell's casino. Stanley has a **private remembered cocoon instruction** concerning Nash's eventual death; this information is **not divulged to the other characters** and **not proof** the mandate is metaphysically true. The quartet investigates a Sinatra performance but **does not attend or hear one**; anonymous room note isn't a completed meeting. Vorel's apparent tarrasque meal becomes grotesque substitute matter under *Dispel Magic*; later food does not dispel, but no authenticated hunted tarrasque is shown. Hell's soul-coin manufacturing accounts remain NPC testimony. Brett has been disguised as bartender while they wait.
- **L613–617:** Brett proposes a replacement from **Nash's own tissue**, not a stranger's donated organ. He **actually removes Nash's engineered dragon-metal heart** after Nash chooses not to resist, shapes and installs a **working flesh heart from Nash's own tissue**, and retains the engineered metal heart in **Brett's custody**. Nash survives and is actually snapped back to Earth's **desert** with the three others by Brett, **not by the zero-charge Hollow Ring**. No new signed pact, Eye retrieval, killing of the Principal or new Ruby activation occurs. On Earth Nash successfully **casts Firebolt** and continues to float/levitate; **post-removal dragon transformation is not demonstrated** (not evidence of permanent inability either).
- **L619–623:** Stanley **actually summons the previously crushed Fate Six back using the Mask of Eltanfawr**, not a repaired permanent conventional ship or the Hollow Ring. Ghost crew reappear **incompletely**: Uncle Sever and Gorgeous George remain, **Crooked Hand Row is absent**; no claim all ghost sailors were revived. Hope takes navigation/helm responsibilities. Party flies toward Solar Mines and witnesses new red/purple lava fractures in Earth; Worldbreaker's continuing planetary injury is real, though its inner shape and complete origin remain unverified. Elhandari postbattle camp controls surface and mourns losses. Do not transfer S3E18's later four-day final ship reconstitution or captaincy into these earlier S3E13 scenes.

They prioritize fighting Worldbreaker **while Earth magic remains operational**. Stanley **issues an order to send guardians underground** to secure Ruby; the actual troop deployment is **not witnessed until the opening of S3E14**, so do not backdate it to S3E13. Ruby remains unactivated in the Principal's geothermal chamber, with its exact ongoing custody not completely filmed. Consultations with White Tower/Planet Nash/Sigil and other plans are **discussed, not carried out** in this episode; the Principal is still unaccounted for after Hell disappearance.
