# Road to a Lost Kingdom — Arc 03, Chapters III–IV

## III. Promises beneath the keep

The travelers left **Fate 4** hidden outside **the Teet** and entered **Trashburg** on foot. Hunger greeted them before any answers did. Children begged for food, a resident emptied a bucket of waste onto **Vorel**, and **Tedna** limped in makeshift footwear after losing his boot. Men with **26** tattooed on them watched the streets. Buttons had described the city's poverty and competing factions; now the companions saw the conditions for themselves.[^arc03-12]

At **Charlemagne's** inn they found lodging and warnings about the city's gangs. A public celebration drew crowds to hear **Suckmaster Din** announce his son's upcoming wedding to **Jolt**, the woman associated with the title **Acrimonicar**. Food, festivities and a **Blood Ball** match were promised. At the **Pit of Despair**, condemned people were thrown into the dark; the party saw an enormous **tongue** drag down a victim as the crowd cheered. They could not identify the creature below. Neither the wedding nor the match had taken place.[^arc03-13]

Alone in his room, **Nash** found a frightened pixie called **P0D**, named for the label on her aquarium. She begged him not to return her to **Farmer Dewey**. P0D said imprisoned pixies were being turned into the drug **Pixie Blush**. The confession made Nash remember the destruction that drug had helped cause in Slavetown. He offered her shelter, while the companions debated using an already-produced drug to reach the rulers whose trade had exploited its victims. They had **not investigated the farm or rescued the other pixies**; P0D's history was her first-person testimony.[^arc03-14]

Their questions brought them to the **Twenty-Six Block**. Vorel nearly turned the introduction violent, but the gang escorted them through guarded barricades to **Monsoon Cabbage**. Monsoon claimed his crowded headquarters and communal kitchen served the neighborhood. He listened to their hunt for the revolvers **Fox and Scully** and their interest in the keep, then named his price: **kill the Toe Rippers' leader**, and he would reveal a way inside. It was an unfulfilled bargain rather than an alliance secured. **Tedna** separately reported a possible route through wedding catering staff, but had not obtained admission.[^arc03-15]

Other allegiances were less clear. **Bok Bok** wore a severed-toe necklace and spoke of new friends; he was not thereby proved a formally initiated Toe Ripper. **Buttons** privately confronted Stanley about the luminous being he had seen enter him beneath the oil sands. When the cat urged disclosure, **Stanley cast Suggestion** to send him scouting instead. Buttons had not freely chosen that silence. Stanley continued to conceal a violation he himself had never consented to.[^arc03-16]

Charlemagne asked the party to deliver a parcel to his arms-dealer friend **Jedel McCready**. At the damaged shop they found an unidentified body downstairs. Upstairs a twisted, **fanged stranger** sprang at **Hope**. The assault began at the end of one episode, but its long struggle followed in the next: the attacker endured punishment and seemed to regenerate. Hope's **maximum vitality fell** after a bite, though no one had proved she had become a vampire. The stranger was finally defeated and vanished. Hope recovered a **rocket launcher and two large projectiles** in a concealed case. Neither the corpse's identity nor the attacker's species was established.[^arc03-17]

The **Trashburg Gray Socks** then offered a possible route to the fortress through **Blood Ball** against the **Pounders**. They had invited the travelers, not yet played a match or granted access. Stanley's unsettling behavior continued, until a voice from his body declared that he was **Diontalus, not Stanley Billings**. He **admitted forcing his way into Stanley after Stanley rejected his offer**. His ancient history, motives and exact rules of shared control remained his claims, but the coercion was real. Stanley had not signed a second pact.[^arc03-18]

The company trained with the Gray Socks, struggled with the brutal sport and took **provisional jerseys**. A payment and opportunity to enter the keep were still conditional on future play. Monsoon awaited a gang chief's death, the wedding caterers had not admitted them, and **P0D's fellow pixies remained prisoners**. They had learned many possible entrances; they had completed none of them.[^arc03-19]

## IV. Music and the rocket's return

A card-playing promoter named **Fango** invited the companions to the **Golden Gargoyle**, where **Angelashi** would perform. Before they could seek that refuge, Charlemagne heard their account of the ransacked arms-dealer property and unidentified corpse. He feared the dead man was his friend. His grief was real, but the body still lacked an independent identification and nobody had proved who attacked the shop.[^arc03-20]

In Toe Ripper territory, **Vorel attacked a sentry first**. Fighters emerged from the buildings around him; blades, spells and **Nash's dragon-heart acid breath** turned the street into a battlefield. Fire spread and part of a structure collapsed. An alarm sounded from the Teet Guard, but the company did not know who raised it. The casualties were uncertain, and **the gang leader Monsoon demanded was not confirmed dead**. They reached the Golden Gargoyle at **E035's end**, their debt still unpaid.[^arc03-21]

Inside the nightclub, Angelashi's performance briefly drew **Nash** away from their struggles. Then the **Toe Rippers** forced their way inside. Their authority figure **Blunt Goliver**, a lizardfolk in a wheelchair, accused the group of destroying his people. **Vorel called himself the Lizard King**—a challenge, not a title anyone had accepted. **Nash transformed into a black dragon wyrmling** and the companions fought through the crowded room, while their enemies made unverified claims about power gained from consuming grisly trophies.

**Fango showed them a concealed exit** and they escaped through backstage passages and a sewer. They had **not killed Goliver or fulfilled Monsoon's requested assassination**. Angelashi accompanied Nash back toward the inn. Nash was exhausted from transforming; by E036's close, their private encounter was suggested but **not yet played**.[^arc03-22]

Afterward, **Vorel and Bok Bok** attended a clandestine meeting. An armed contact said **Jolt's crew had robbed an Oilbeard safe in Slavetown** during earlier fires, and handed Vorel a heavy **trunk of gold and jewels** as his supposed share. The payment occurred, while the robbery account was secondhand. Vorel took Bok Bok outside town and **buried the trunk under the desert sand**, concealing the disturbed ground. The valuables were hidden, **not carried in the party's packs**.[^arc03-23]

At Charlemagne's, **Nash and Angelashi** spoke of her people's history and a possible connection between his **dragon-metal heart** and the road to a lost home. The histories were her interpretation. Her touch brought forth a **magical flower** and briefly changed Nash's body. Their intimacy was played now, unlike the unfinished invitation at the end of E036. By morning she had departed and Nash had rested after the strain of his earlier transformation.[^arc03-24]

A servant brought **Hope a letter from Tedna**, working inside the keep. He described servants' passages, a cold cellar and a chamber of **terrified tiefling statues**, and reported hearing that **Softhead was alive** and implicated in Dusthill's betrayal. Some details were observed; others came from informants. **Hope kept some pages private**, so her friends did not automatically know every detail. **Charlemagne evacuated his family**, gave **Vorel the inn keys** and advised them to speak with Monsoon. Their lodgings were now an empty property rather than a staffed inn.[^arc03-25]

**P0D** still wanted the other pixies freed. She described seeing Charlemagne with someone resembling Hope, without confirming the person's identity. **Monsoon** arrived, acknowledged the damage inflicted on the Toe Rippers and insisted **Blunt Goliver's head was still required**. He marked an underground approach on their map and offered access to replacement weapons. **Vorel** chose a chainsaw-edged greatsword provisionally called **Mawstorm**. They entered abandoned tunnels, passed a **string-and-bell warning trap** and found a hostile monk ahead.[^arc03-26]

**Hope fired the recovered rocket launcher** toward him. The monk caught or redirected the rocket **back into Hope**. It exploded, hurled her into stone, injured the others and brought part of the cavern roof down. Smoke filled the passage. **Hope was critically injured, not confirmed dead**, and the group had neither escaped the collapse nor killed Goliver.

This was **E037's cliffhanger**. Carrying Hope through the smoke, killing the gang chief and treating her injuries would belong to the next chapter, not a victory already achieved here.[^arc03-27]
