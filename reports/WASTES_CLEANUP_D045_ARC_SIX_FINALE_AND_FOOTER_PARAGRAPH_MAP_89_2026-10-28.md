The Principal's pleas to release the Worldbreaker on Earth and claims of divine threat are **interested NPC testimony**, not evidence the gods will conquer Earth. Five *Prismatic Wall* layers are stripped; Vorel's acquired chrome +2 AC is adjudicated, not proof his armor is sentient.
- **L751–757:** Stanley **actually uses an Elhandari rejuvenation syringe**, with an immediate full-rest benefit/level and lifespan cost; Fate Six **actually crashes against the Principal copies**, while Monsoon arrives to defend the group. Stanley drops *Polymorph* and casts *Greater Invisibility* on Nash, who remains concentrating on *Banishment*. Stanley makes an out-of-safe-bounds *Wish* producing a **modified, GM-accepted chain of 100 simulacra**; the original Stanley suffers **STR3 for seven days**, spell-stress injury and an **unrolled 33% potential permanent *Wish* loss**, *not* confirmed permanent loss here. Copies are fragile; don't transfer them to the ending as a permanent army.
- **L759:** The GM **actually declares the full minute completed**: the Worldbreaker/Tarrasque is **permanently banished from Earth** by this episode cutoff.
