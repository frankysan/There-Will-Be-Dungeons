**Portrayed by:** Scott. **Identity:** Wasteland adventurer, spellcaster and bearer of an implanted **dragon-metal engine heart**, capable of black-dragon transformation. **Spoilers:** Played *Wastes* through E128 (Season One finale) and retrospective cast Q&As. **Evidence:** Processed ASR and episode dossiers, not independent audio verification.

## E128 — Henatar's execution, family farewells and dragon rescue

**After Henatar/Henitar Grape yields**, Nash remains a black dragon and **tears off one side head**. Despite an effort/check to do so without killing him, the GM describes **Henatar's last breath**. Nash later **removes a second side head from the already-dead corpse** and leaves the middle head for Diontalus, who drains its visible soul-like energy. This is **an execution after surrender**, not merely killing an unresisting opponent with the E126 acid blast. Nash still wears his **manufactured dragon-metal heart**; a **distinct harvested natural dragon heart** is later **recovered by Elhandari** and transported toward Mars.

Nash **says goodbye to Angelashi and all four infants**, explaining that staying near him would put them in danger and promising to return. Angelashi, the children, Lyrielle, Bok Bok and surviving Harbor tieflings **actually board the Mars-bound Elhandari ships**, while Nash stays with the original party on Earth. When the Soulbroker opens a purple sky breach and releases a colossal burrowing creature, **Nash actually takes Hope, Stanley and Vorel onto his dragon back, flies above the desert sand tsunami and brings all three safely down**. The villa survives; no dragon fight against the creature occurs. Nash **returns to human form before an uneventful long rest**, and the whole group is officially **level 13**. No distinct new **improvised spellcasting verse** is identified in E128; the dragon rescue and head biting are not spell-rhymes. ## Life, memories and magical body

Nash introduces himself as a survivor of the **Solar Mines/Blood Canyons**, traumatized by coerced industrial labor, betrayal and the apparent destruction of his old life. His recollections and later histories sometimes conflict. The **implanted metal heart** is a real part of his body and magic, but his alleged earlier betrayer, biological origin and the sequence of implantation are **not fully reconstructed** from direct events. He begins in Dusthill, helps restore its old waterworks, and builds close relationships with **Hope, Stanley and Vorel**. [E000](../../episodes/E000.md) · [E001](../../episodes/E001.md) · [E006](../../episodes/E006.md) · [E024](../../episodes/E024.md).

His metal heart enables extraordinary magical output and **transformations into a black dragon wyrmling and then a young black dragon**. These forms consume effort and have practical costs. He also has unusual bodily changes, such as a smaller additional hand, and later wears a finite **Robe of Useful Items**. Recovered components and individual robe patches should not be assumed permanently present after their source episodes show use. In a post-arc Q&A, Beau explains he conceived the **heart** after Scott improvised uncertainty about Nash having a normal one: *production history*, not a new character memory. [Château Weir](../../world/places/chateau-weir.md) · [June 2019 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md) · [E048](../../episodes/E048.md)–[E053](../../episodes/E053.md).

An apparent E055 encounter with P0D is **a dream**, not her resurrection. He protects Tietkalla citizens from a deadly stonefall. ## Razak Val, alternate selves and the Drive (E057–E081)

While investigating the stonefall, Nash fights the **Wormfather** near an anomalous green crater in dragon form; the expedition later finds a brain parasite. He encounters apparently **future versions of Nash** who request a **Fengar egg** at Razak Val. Nash helps them steal it, but their claims of future origin and the rules of their “time barf” travel remain **claims**; this is not proof of a solved deterministic loop. The subsequent **Sun's Witnesses transformation crisis** and flight from Razak Val bring real losses, and in **E066 a T. rex destroys Hope's Fate 4** despite the party's spells. [E057](../../episodes/E057.md)–[E067](../../episodes/E067.md) · [Razak Val](../../world/places/razak-val.md).

At **Kervalon/CHIDS**, Nash uses terrain-altering spells, protective illusions, Fireball, dragon form and improvised devices. The first CHIDS funicular crash is a real physical event, not just an office simulation. A shared magical **cooling/flying garment** changes hands and is not an unlimited supply of equipment. The companions discover **a portable red/green portal device**, which Nash recognizes by analogy to an entertainment game; it is **not** the larger Traversal Drive. The living hive's apparent “future Nash” and cosmic warning remain potentially deceptive testimony. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md)–[E078](../../episodes/E078.md) · [CHIDS](../../world/places/chids.md) · [Portal device](../../world/technology/portal-device.md).

In **E078**, Nash receives a **Ring of Spell Storing** and loads *Chromatic Orbs*; he assists Vorel's actual limb reattachment. The group physically finds the **Traversal Drive E079**. **Nash kills the great illithid commander in E080**, while Hope destroys a manifested **Dolo Popio** in E081. Instead of sacrificing Earth, the four original friends **share the crimson beam**, vanishing into white light and being **GM-declared dead at E081 cutoff**. **Sira escapes alive with Eddie**; the planet's lasting condition is not fully observed. ## Sigil, regeneration and the White Tower (E082–E101)

**E082** reveals Nash's consciousness alive in **Sigil** as a **small eye protruding from his metal heart**, without his ordinary body. His gradual **rebuilding of hands, limbs and other anatomy** through subsequent episodes is physical development, not instantaneous restoration at E082. In **E089**, he has regained an ordinary mobile body; his personal account of the Solar Mines remains a report rather than a scene played in those earlier years. Beau's January 2020 bonus remarks anticipate **self-regeneration**, but those comments alone did not accomplish the process. [E082](../../episodes/E082.md)–[E089](../../episodes/E089.md) · [January 2020 Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-01.md).

Nash helps the party's Sigil investigations, battles and route planning. In E093 he **voluntarily becomes a young black dragon** while the **Traveler's Twin** crosses the Outlands. He and his companions enter the besieged **White Tower**; the danger includes a plague, a secured Fist relic and a growing moon. A later battle temporarily conjoins Nash's dragon body with a separate demonic body, but **the forms separate in E100**, so he does not permanently retain that shared physiology. The tower's plague is **actually cured in E096** by Walnut/Vorel's ritual. [E093](../../episodes/E093.md)–[E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [White Tower](../../world/places/white-tower.md).

**E101:** Nash survives the last siege, aids Vorel, witnesses Hope replace her own hand with the **Hand of the Voice**, and remains behind when she **leaves the original party**. Nash later works with Stanley and Vorel to **re-seal the separate Zorander's Fist gauntlet**; he removes a medallion key and returns it to Stanley. This is the **Arc Four/Arc Five transition within E101**, not Hope's permanent death. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [Hand of the Voice](../../world/technology/hand-of-the-voice.md).

## Separate paths and family reunion (E102–E118)

A **wild-magic-created, city-bearing Tungan moon** becomes a threat to the Outlands; Nash's attempts to reverse or outrun it are not automatically successful. **Squirts's Divine Intervention E104 removes the moon and attacking Tungan ships**. Orcs mistakenly credit Nash, acclaim him **Captain Dragon Lord** and rename their ship **The Great Angelashi** at his instruction. A wrongly executed fruit-portal ritual then **destroys that main ship**; the company survives aboard a smaller vessel. In E105 they reach **Sigil** and Nash receives a **100,000-gold liability claim**, not a paid debt. [E102](../../episodes/E102.md)–[E105](../../episodes/E105.md).

After the party helps deliver the **Fist gauntlet to Tobacho in E106**, an indirect route takes them to an **alternate Nash's asteroid tower**. Its host, **First Nash**, claims lost companions of **his** timeline and later calls the current Nash “Junior”; those memorials are **not deaths of today's party**. Nash uses an unusual vision in E110 to see pregnant Angelashi escaping danger at Tietkalla, but this is **not yet a physical reunion**. He then transports the party to the keep. [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [E110](../../episodes/E110.md).

**E111** brings the actual **reunion with Angelashi at Golden Sands**. Nash sketches a *Teleportation Circle* for a possible getaway, **without casting it** when Elhandari help arrives. During the battle with **General Carthax**, Squirts and Melgor die, while the family and other survivors board an Elhandari ship. In **E114**, Angelashi reveals **four living changeling children**—two boys and two girls—and speaks privately about Nash's own ancestry, which remains **her account** rather than a verified origin. Undead attackers disrupt the voyage, but the rescuers escape and **reach Elhandar E115**. [E111](../../episodes/E111.md) · [E114](../../episodes/E114.md) · [E115](../../episodes/E115.md).

At the end of **E116**, Hope **uses Gate to summon Nash physically into Dr. Spider's laboratory**. The arrival is real, not merely an imagined afterlife reunion. Nash is startled and asks questions; **Hope accompanies him back**, and in **E117 all four protagonists reunite at Elhandar**. Nash introduces Hope to his family, argues against violence in the dispute over her past conduct, and publicly recounts the Solar Mines uprising during the refugees' ceremony. This is a *present-day oral account*, not an enacted ancient flashback. [E116](../../episodes/E116.md) · [E117](../../episodes/E117.md).

In **E118**, Nash joins the cottage dinner and is among the group when severely injured **Omnom** confronts Hope; **Stanley's Hold Person**, not a blow by Nash, stops the attack. After the cottage-key investigation Nash and Vorel remain outside when **Hope and Stanley** enter the hidden study. The subsequent father's journal and Mask belong to **Stanley's E118 inventory**, not Nash's. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md).
