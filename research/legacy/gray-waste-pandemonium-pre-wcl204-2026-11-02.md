# Gray Waste, River Styx and Pandemonium — Hope's route

> **The Wastes Arc Five, played E112–E113 only.** [E112](../../episodes/E112.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md) · [E113 complete-ASR comparison](../../reports/E113_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md). These locations are visited by **Hope, Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider**, not by Nash/Stanley/Vorel. **All described events are source-ASR provisional, not independently listened to; broader Planescape lore must not silently substitute for campaign-specific scenes.**

## Gray Waste, from House of Delights to Pointless

At [E112](../../episodes/E112.md)'s end, a closet-and-carrot ritual in **Sigil's House of Delights** actually sends **all four travelers** to a flat and almost colorless waste. **Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider become gray and melancholy** immediately. In [E113](../../episodes/E113.md), **Hope also becomes gray during prolonged travel**, with Dr. Spider's despondency increasing. **Steerious retains colored gold details**. Mundane desert expertise and an assisted Survival attempt do not reliably find a direction in this magical environment.

The party meets a gray, apathetic orc who tells them they are going the wrong way and names **Pointless**. The orc's head **remains responsive when forcibly separated** and is named **Headsy/Hedzy (?)** by Steerious. The party drags his body/carries his head along the march; after Blood Skunk identifies a distant settlement, they eventually **physically arrive in Pointless**, a village of roughly **ten huts**, by black water and a fogbound wharf. **Headsy stays on the wharf**, as ferryman Hesterbrook will not transport him without his own valid payment. **He is not confirmed dead**.

## The Styx ferryman and passage

At Pointless the cloaked **Hesterbrook**, a being with shimmering **blue skeletal features**, approaches by boat and requires each passenger to surrender **something genuinely personally valuable**. The four completed payments are **Hope's pistol Fox**, **Steerious's greatsword**, **Blood Skunk's left little toe** (wound healed but toe regrowth not demonstrated), and **Dr. Spider's lab coat**. All are **actually accepted**, with goods hidden in Hesterbrook's cloak; **Headsy cannot pay and stays behind**. Hesterbrook claims he was formerly a prince and chose ferrying in the afterlife. This is **his account, not a verified origin**.

In the Styx a colossal serpent called **Velithon/Velothan/Velathon (?)** senses its long-absent former master through **Hope's Hand of the Voice**, threatens the ferry, then accepts Hope's praise. The **hooded Voice is seen privately by Hope** but cannot be seen by the serpent. Hope promises a future visit beneath the water, **unfulfilled in E113**. The boat crosses dangerous waters safely. The ferryman's claim of an old pact and the leviathan's collections of lost souls remain **NPC testimony**.

## Pandemonium, Council and the phlogiston lead

The ferryman actually lands beneath **immense wind-bored rock formations** in **Pandemonium** and the Gray Waste **color/negative effects lift**. A small imp **Voxil/Varkso (?)** guides all four by magic doorway and a **fast mine-cart ride** to a Council of Clowns entrance, then departs. **Dario the Impresario** first proposes a comically remote appointment but **actually allows an immediate hearing**.

In a grand, magically unfolding auditorium, **Bobo the Clown** and the other councilors hear Hope's request. **Bobo smells Hope's Hand** and says it belongs to **the Voice**. The clowns alternately deride her earnestness and enjoy her hostility, and **actually approve her petition**. According to their **unverified report**, the **Eye of the Voice** is in an ancient wizard-built **prison** variously recognized as **Pentel Weiss/Pentelvice/Pentalvise (?)**, drifting in **phlogiston space** between Prime Material worlds near the **Corinthian Nebula**. They **physically give Hope a wind-up, spring-headed clown compass**, claiming it points to the prison for **Hope alone**. The precise prison name, coordinates and compass reliability remain unverified. **No Eye is recovered**.

From the Council, **Hope actually opens her copper-ring Gate back to Tobacho's Sigil bar, and all four enter**. Thus the journey through Gray Waste, Styx and Pandemonium is **complete at E113 cutoff**; **the much larger Eye quest is not**, and Tobacho's signed E112 compact regarding the **Lady of Pain** remains unfulfilled.
