# Razak Val — lizardfolk clans, Great Fang and scaling

**Working name:** `Razak Val` (processed ASR also `Razak Fal`, `Razakvale`, `Razokval`). **Spelling not independently confirmed** by audio or an original map. First physically visited by the party in corrected [E059](../../episodes/E059.md), developed in [E060](../../episodes/E060.md)–[E065](../../episodes/E065.md). **Spoilers through played Wastes S3E09, including E104 follow-up testimony**, based on **unverified processed Parakeet SRTs**.

## Landscape and settlement

A **mountain-walled sunken valley** in the lizardfolk lands west of the group's Tietkalla journey; entrances into rock dwellings, tents, hunters' stalls, bone/leatherwork and guards occupy it. Its chief public components are the **Razak Duar arena** (provisional spelling), **Cave Fengar** for influential clan business and feasts, and **Tailway**, an open-air shrine with pillars and dragon-priest officiants. The characters **ride velociraptor-like mounts** alongside **Fate 4** to reach this region and use a **Tiny Hut** to wait out a huge bloodsucking desert swarm on the way. [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [E060](../../episodes/E060.md).

## S3E09 — Groxl's first Great Fang claim conflicts with Razak-the-Bronze tradition

In [played Wastes S3E09](../../episodes/S3E09.md) **~00:13–00:22**, Vorel experiences **the “Endless Dunes”** and hears a defeated supernatural figure identifying as **Groxl (Grojil/Grozil automatic variants)** assert that, after an artificial vat/soup origin and rejection by other lizardfolk, he **united the tribes of Razak Val and became its FIRST GREAT FANG / founded nation**. He says his **inability to sire eggs/offspring** led to his abandonment of the united clans and their decay. **This is GROXL'S FIRST-PERSON STORY, not independently researched oral history or a witnessed ancient sequence**. **Important explicit source conflict:** the priestly **S1 E060 Razak Val tradition identifies “RAZAK THE BRONZE” as the first Great Fang**, while the S3E09 speaker claims that role for himself. Do **NOT** replace Razak the Bronze with Groxl, equate the two without evidence, or silently treat either as verified omniscient fact; possibilities include incompatible traditions, mythic name/alias, an earlier/later reign or unreliable narrator. The conflict requires original-audio check and in-world source corroboration. Vorel **REJECTS Groxl's intergenerational vengeance**, intending lizardfolk to inhabit **Solar Mines peacefully** if the mission succeeds, **not a completed Razak Val reform or established peace treaty**. [S3E09 two-ASR audit](../../reports/WASTES_S3E09_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-27.md).

## Political organization

The highest title is **Great Fang**: held by **Orp the cyclops** through E061, then won by **Vorel in E062** and publicly reaffirmed in E063. Orp once met the party in Slavetown but has been adopted into the lizardfolk **Fengar** ruling clan after defeating its previous Great Fang in a duel. The club of local clans—**478 claimed in dialogue**—follows **scaling**, a single-combat succession rite; a formal challenge is intended to settle legitimacy. **Mirion/Mirian Fengar**, a small-bodied but politically skilled adviser called **“the Morsel,”** says he is the prior Great Fang's son and has Orp's ear. This is demonstrably an organized polity, not just a mob respecting the strongest punch. [E060](../../episodes/E060.md).

**Orp's rule is lethal and predatory:** members bring **living captives** for him to eat, and **he murders one or more captives before the party**. **Vorel challenges him in E060.** Orp **kills Diptak with a single blow**, and the court removes his body. During the evening feast, **Orp accepts a duel for the next sunrise**. **Orp is alive at the E060 cutoff**; the arranged sunrise duel is superseded when **Vorel challenges and kills Orp during E062**, after a terrible explosion in the Fengar lounge. **Omnom publicly acknowledges Vorel as Great Fang**. In E063 **Zercil's attempted succession and attack fail**; Hope kills her with an **Obsidian Nail**. Vorel remains acclaimed Great Fang but **her visibly assisted death complicates the formal honor of the contest**. [E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [E060](../../episodes/E060.md) · [E062](../../episodes/E062.md) · [E063](../../episodes/E063.md).

## Clans, allies and priesthood

**Diptak** is Vorel's earlier oath-bound lizardfolk ally from the Golden Lands; he brings Vorel here but is **killed E060**. **Mirion's brother Bramie/Braimie Fengar**, famed as a **Tyrant Slayer**, and sister/partner **Zercil/Zircal** are powerful hunters. **General Toxar** and **Skrot/Scrot** from another warrior clan (ASR `Mekatek`) voice distrust of powerful hierarchies while maintaining martial traditions. These names, kinship nuances and office titles await primary handout verification.

**E061–E063 lethal succession:** **Bramie/Baymie is murdered in the Fengar heirloom chamber E061**, with **Tyrant's Bane** left in his back; two apparent Nash doubles steal the Fengar egg but **the murderer is not independently identified**. **Zercil/Zershil**, who joins the scaling, **dies E063**, disintegrated by Hope. **Mirion, General Toxar, the Elder of Families, priestess Typalla, Mola Janda and other participants die in Hope's E062 rocket explosion**. **Omnom Cinderborn survives E062–E063** and becomes Vorel's grudging adviser; **his fate after E064 is initially unseen; E104 finds him alive in Tietkalla**. The wider city and its many clans survive the massacre; do not turn an approximate hall death count into the whole city's census. [E061](../../episodes/E061.md) · [E062](../../episodes/E062.md) · [E063](../../episodes/E063.md).

The **Council of Twelve** is the religious framework said to bless the city. The first Great Fang **Razak the Bronze**, **Fuxi/Fushi** the fire-father, **Rezar** and other named dragons are cited in tradition, with **one priestly emissary per god**. **Typalla** is said to speak for **Fuxi/Fushi**. The **Tailway** is an observable shrine, but the truth of each vision, creation myth and divine status is **NPC testimony**, not verified cosmology. [E060](../../episodes/E060.md).

## Food, monsters and local speech

Feasts serve **bloodberry drink**, **live snakes** that guests subdue and eat, and mostly raw **“chicken lord”** apparently hunted from the nearby **Demon Vold**. Hunting dangerous monsters there carries social prestige; **the Demon Vold itself has not been entered** by the party through E060. Vorel, having expected uncomplicated strength politics, expresses disillusionment at the place's patronage and plans to seek **Typalla**, but a completed meeting and prophetic judgment should not be assumed from E060 alone.

## Under the new Great Fang (E063)

After Orp's death, some lizardfolk **harvest his corpse for food** while the party recovers a **Necklace of Fireballs (five beads/charges)** concealed on his body. Vorel enters the battered throne cave, rejects feeding humans to the ruler and announces investigation of a possible **Dollo Poppio-associated infestation**. His aspiration to unite Razak Val with Queen Hope's Tietkalla **is a declared alliance goal**, not proof every clan has consented.

Beneath the hall is the **“man's pit,” containing roughly 200–300 surviving and visibly starving human captives**, including non-Common-speaking **sun worshippers** and a calm legless man naming himself approximately **Follow/Follo**. **Stanley declares they will be freed and begins screenings** with Nash's *Mold Earth* rooms. **Only the first few are examined by the E063 cutoff**; it is incorrect to mark the entire group safely escorted or released. Follow is detained separately after an unexplained magical impression. [E063](../../episodes/E063.md).

Vorel and Hope search Zercil's bedroom and **capture a moving black worm in the bed sand**. This is a separate observation from the wormlike motion **Vorel saw behind Zercil's eyes**, and from the brain leech previously recovered from a bugbear; it **does not itself prove who implanted them**. Three surviving **Fengar children**, including **Felcha** (provisional spelling), learn their parents are dead. Their own possible exposure and guardianship are still open. [E063](../../episodes/E063.md).

## Razak Val's spreading catastrophe (E064), and the killer identified later (E065)

**E064 turns a planned emancipation into a disaster.** Follow, a legless captive held separately, demonstrates **telepathic speech** and points to a particular **Sun's Witness** as carrying a strange mental signature. After Vorel calls on **Dollo Poppio**, the examined prisoner develops abnormal eyes and sprays **worms that start infiltrating Vorel and Omnom**; Vorel's fiery abilities burn the attached parasites. Then **the Sun's Witnesses transform into chicken lords** and outside many resident lizardfolk become **six-armed mutants** and fight other residents. **Nash fires a Fireball** into the prison pit and encloses a chamber, but some monsters survive. **Neither the planned freedom of the 200–300 captives nor the city's survival can be assumed**. The GM explains out of character that **Orp's tainted remains were a transmission concern**, not that every ordinary chicken-lord meal invariably contains worms. [E064](../../episodes/E064.md).

**The Zark question:** E064 guards attribute delivery of the Sun's Witnesses to **Zarks**, and Omnom believes they betrayed the city. **Complicity is still a suspicion**, not confession or observed deliberate poisoning. Vorel and friends leave for **Kervalon/Kaer Valon** on raptors as Omnom stays to defend the city; he receives the **Necklace of Fireballs recovered from Orp in E063** and stores it **under his tongue**. **E104 finds Omnom alive in Tietkalla; he reports Razak Val has fallen and his attempt to retake it failed** ([E104 dossier](../../episodes/E104.md)). This resolves his survival up to that appearance, not his fate after Tietkalla's later attack. The city's exact death toll, occupants and condition remain unverified testimony. The party's **Fate 4 reappears**, used without authorization by Follow and companions, but is not proven lost or formally ceded. [E064](../../episodes/E064.md).

**Separate murder disclosure:** **In E065**, **Rorf, a large orc shapeshifter accompanying Follow, explicitly admits killing Bramie/Baymie**. Follow says his group arranged deaths of elder claimants so other clan members could challenge Orp and facilitate escape. This is **participant confession** that supersedes Vorel's **unsupported E063 accusation against Zercil**; it does not retroactively grant the E061 investigators this knowledge. The true route by which the alleged future Nash visitors stole the egg remains separate. [E065](../../episodes/E065.md) · [chronology](../CHRONOLOGY.md).

## S2E02 — surviving clans and Great Fang's actual return

The now-functioning **[Fate Six](../technology/fate-six.md)** returns Vorel and his companions **to the canyon in person**. They see severe war damage, smoke from occupied encampments, a **group of roughly 30 from above**, and roughly **70–80 lizardfolk assembled at ground level**; the numbers are not a verified total census. **Orp's cyclops body has been stripped to bones**. Surviving residents **kneel to Vorel as their Great Fang**, directly reaffirming the title, and report that previous attackers and worm-borne assailants **suddenly died**, a claim they interpret as the clans' victory. Vorel tells them he defeated Dolo Popio; the exact causal connection, wider worm extinction and total losses are **not independently observed**. They describe the vast new planetary burrower as an omen. When Vorel explains his duty to continue to Mars, the locals **demand to accompany him**, and **many willingly board Fate Six and physically arrive on Mars**. This does not prove all clans emigrated or Razak Val became uninhabited. [Episode](../../episodes/S2E02.md) · [two-ASR comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S2E02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).
