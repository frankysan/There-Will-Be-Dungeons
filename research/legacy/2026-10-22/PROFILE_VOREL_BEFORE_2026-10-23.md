# Vorel

**Longer written name:** **Vorel Rasvim Kurik** in the fan wiki, not independently confirmed as a full name from Arc One spoken dialogue. **Portrayed by:** Kyle. **Identity:** Lizardfolk warrior. **Arc Two status:** Alive; protector of Bok Bok; bearing the sentient **Eye of Groxl**. **Spoilers:** narrative coverage through E118; the title register separately includes later seasons and the final Wastes roundup. Retrospective bonus remarks remain separate from played events.

## E118 — actual reunion with Omnom Cinderborn

[E118](../../../episodes/E118.md) resolves E117's unexplained lizardfolk scent. The Elhandari admiral privately tells Vorel that **Omnom has survived and been released after medical treatment**, but is hiding outside the cottage and failing to adjust. **Vorel calls out and physically meets Omnom Cinderborn alive**, grievously injured: **one side of face/mouth missing, casted limbs and damaged scales**. Omnom recounts **five nights fighting the chicken lords at Razak Val** and his shame at surviving retreat. The reunion is now **performed**, not merely the E115 report. On seeing Hope, **Omnom charges her**; **Stanley casts *Hold Person***, and Vorel **carries the paralyzed Omnom away** to forest privacy. **No attack lands**. Omnom accuses Hope of **the Razak Val rocket deaths, setting fire to the Principle-linked railway station and purposefully drawing a demon invasion to Tietkalla**. Hope **confirms firing the rocket and burning the station**, but says **the Hand attracted demons and she meant them to pursue her out of the city**. **Vorel is shocked by the rocket's true mechanism**, challenges Hope, but **does not issue a final judgment**.

Omnom begs Vorel to kill him out of dishonor and grief. **Vorel refuses**, reminding him that he carries **the lost stories of Razak Val**, and the two **actually agree to attempt a hunt together the following morning**, including Hope as a possible test of reconciliation. **They do not hunt or reconcile fully in E118**. Omnom **calls Vorel “Great Fang,” a repeat invocation of Vorel's already-recognized E062–E063 office, not a new coronation or title**. Vorel and Nash offer to help find Stanley's hidden keyhole but do **not themselves unlock the study**; Stanley and Hope do. [E118 ASR report](../../../reports/E118_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## E117 — Keeper of Stories, Chunks and a lizardfolk scent

In [E117](../../../episodes/E117.md), Vorel returns from hunting with Stanley and **Bok Bok/Chunks** after Nash reports **Hope has physically arrived** in Elhandar. He offers Hope comfort but also rejects Stanley's claim that the group was helpless without her. After discussing the upbringing of children, **Bok Bok announces he wants to stay in the cottage to protect Angelashi, the babies and the family while Vorel takes Chunks on future expeditions**. **Vorel actually carries Chunks to the Tietkalla refugee ceremony**. This revises the intended E115 caretaker arrangement for travel, not a legal or biological parentage change.

At the refugees' bonfire Vorel **performs Nobieri's ash rite** but first checks whether it might bind or curse him, inconclusively. He **publicly recites a long string of self-styled titles**, most already used historically. The phrase/source register below already lists the **E117 precise sequence and wording variants**, culminating in **“Keeper of Stories” (~02:08:39–02:08:46)**. He declares he will remember the survivors' stories and help restore the kingdom. **This is a new self-proclaimed role and sincere promise, not an official coronation or proof a recovered lizardfolk army exists**. Back near the cottage, **he smells lizardfolk in the forest**; an initial northward bearing is suggested but **his attempted track fails**, and he proposes a **search after supper**, not yet performed. **Omnom has not been physically found in E117**, despite E115's Nobieri report of survival. [Full ASR report](../../../reports/E117_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## E115 — lost remains, a memorial and “Fang of nothing”

Vorel **reaches Elhandar** after assenting to station fire that **destroys the withdrawing pirate vessel**. He sees the Principle’s display of power but declines to accept the elves’ passivity uncritically. In the biodome cottage, **Vorel confesses Squirts’s ashes are gone**, suggesting loss to space or his fiery sword during the ship battle. **E111 put the ashes in a vial**; E115’s hand/loss account leaves an **unresolved custody discrepancy and unwitnessed mechanism**, not an observed spill. He **performs a memorial**, supported by Nash and grieving Bok Bok; no resurrection occurs. **Bok Bok declares he will care for Chunks as a father**, and Vorel accepts that independence.

Over **four actual recovery days**, Vorel explores and hunts, choosing the firepit ash to sleep in. He wants an oversized mother raccoon preserved; **a finished trophy is not shown**. At breakfast he questions the party’s purpose after losing its communities and calls himself **no longer Fang / Fang of nothing**, a self-assessment rather than a new awarded office. **Nobieri urges him to lead**, without a coronation. Omnom’s recovery aboard is **Nobieri’s report**, not an actual reunion. **No quest departure, confirmed total extinction of Razak Val’s people or completed assault on the Principle.** [E115](../../../episodes/E115.md) · [comparison](../../../reports/E115_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## E114 — holding the boarding tube

In [E114](../../../episodes/E114.md), Vorel **still holds Squirts's ashes** and hopes the elves might revive her; **no resurrection occurs**. He repeats the established **Heroes of the Wastes** group style without a new office or distinct epithet. **Stanley heals Bok Bok conscious**, and Vorel gives the goblin a fire-retardant canister and orders him to protect the medical bay. Vorel **refuses Stanley's offered fast-rest pill**. He rages and fights **Fluffy** and undead pirates with a fiery sword/flame bursts; a proposed move is **rewound because he is grappled**, not completed alongside later attacks. Fluffy eventually **Commands him toward its mouth**, but **Stanley's heated Diplomacy kills it before entry**.

Vorel **grapples the giant skeleton and pushes it back into the boarding tube**. After **Cloudkill** comes down the passage, Stanley **Telekinetically pulls him to safety**. Vorel holds a handhold as the ship breaks away, sending the giant into space. **He survives aboard the ship still bound for Elhandar**, Squirts unrevived and injured Bok Bok alive; no ashes transfer or use is depicted. ## E111 — Bok Bok saved, Squirts lost, the general subdued

At [E111](../../../episodes/E111.md), **Vorel** steers the group stealthily from devastated keep to **Golden Sands** and **reunites with Bok Bok alive**, who had helped shelter pregnant Angelashi. When the **Elhandari elf army** arrives, he distrusts Captain Tenmuth's no-prison, therapy-oriented rhetoric. **Vorel states his established “Fang of Razak Val” title**, not a fresh coronation (see the title register). He reluctantly joins an attack on **General Carthax**; **Squirts heals Omnom, but Carthax's beams kill Squirts and Melgor**, leaving **Chunks alive**. Vorel deals heavy fiery melee damage, then **wins an Athletics contest to punch an elven empathy-grenade bandolier into Carthax's eye**. **Carthax is pacified and captured alive**, not slain. **Bok Bok dives into Carthax's mouth with dynamite**; after **Stanley's *Command* causes Carthax to vomit**, Vorel receives **Bok Bok alive but badly hurt and with one hand missing**. With Nash shielding the site and **Stanley supplying a component vial**, **Vorel physically gathers and keeps Squirts's ashes**. He **boards an elven ship with injured Bok Bok and the vial**, leaving Tietkalla for proposed refuge at Elhandar; **no arrival or resurrection shown**. [E111 comparison](../../../reports/E111_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## Character and development

Vorel arrives in Dusthill a powerful desert-wandering lizardfolk trying to trade for water and food. As the campaign unfolds he becomes a warrior increasingly willing to declare himself a **Lizard King**, but his leadership is not a uniformly recognized crown over every lizardfolk community. During the road adventures he brings along **Bok Bok**, a goblin whose persistent devotion starts as a surprise and becomes one of Vorel's closest bonds.

He fights through the slavery and terror of Slavetown and the Black Mine, including a lizardfolk religious/visionary ordeal tied to the name **Fuxi**. At the **Soulbroker compact**, Vorel is the one regular protagonist who **refuses to sign** the soul contract accepted by Nash, Hope and Stanley. His independent judgments are not always gentle: he kills enemies, coerces prisoners, contributes to hard choices and is often suspicious of the people who promise power. A later confrontation with **Diptak** ends with a pledge from the **Golden Land clan**, although no army is thereby on call at every subsequent scene.

As the company reaches Tietkalla, Vorel acquires the distinctive chain greatsword **Mawstorm** (provisional owner-suggested spelling; ASR often has `Ma Storm`) and fights the Toe Rippers and city defenders. His decisions frequently escalate danger, yet he is also among the companions most outraged by **Diontalus's** killing of P0D. He **kills Buttons** in E049, and later proposes a memorial code of conduct called the **Pillars of Decency** (POD). It is a **proposed party standard**, not a magical contract or a binding constitution.

**Kefeli petrifies Vorel** during E051. Nash and Stanley protect him and make a limited remedy from an earlier basilisk corpse, restoring **both Vorel and Hope alive**. In the giant froghemoth E052–E053, he mentally contacts the creature's brain but does not demonstrate lasting mastery over it. He then accepts the living **Eye of Groxl** into his chest. Its will, combat rage, shield and protection effects come with risks of displeasure and influence. The eye is a **single owner-confirmed artifact**, even though ASR transcribes `Grojil` and `Groxel`; the E067 `god killer Grojil` speech is **owner-confirmed as a reference to this same Eye of Groxl**, not to a separate deity or person; its alleged god-killing deeds remain unverified. Vorel's newly controllable **scale color-changing** arises separately from pixie/froghemoth effects, not the eye.

At E054 he attacks the fleeing **Monsoon** but cannot kill him, then **executes captive Suck Master Din at Hope's signal** after Hope's public coronation. Vorel's future ideas about raising Bok Bok as a squire are plans rather than completed training.

## Relationships and items

**Bok Bok** accompanies and protects him, including watching over his petrified form. **Hope** becomes a queen he has helped enthrone, not simply another opponent. **Stanley/Diontalus** remains a fraught relationship because of P0D and George; later cast discussion describes Vorel informally checking who has bodily control. Key equipment includes **Mawstorm** (spelling pending creator-written or audio confirmation), the **+1 AC lizardfolk-skull helmet** obtained in E047, and the later **Eye of Groxl**; **the helmet and eye are separate items**. The hidden desert cache Vorel retrieves in E053 is not equivalent to all its gold being distributed to the town.
