# Black puddings

## Introduction

Two creeping masses of black ooze inhabited the greasy caves beneath [Tietkalla's](../places/tietkalla.md) Pit of Despair. They approached through narrow passages from opposite directions, threatening to trap the travelers between them. Nash recognized the creatures as black puddings.[^appearance]

## Arc Two — corrosion in the caves

The puddings were dangerous to equipment as well as flesh. Hope's machete corroded as she struck them, and cutting the masses caused smaller pieces to split away. Wooden tables used as barriers also dissolved under their attack. These effects made cutting blades and wooden barriers unreliable defenses.[^corrosion]

Their position in the constricted tunnels made withdrawal difficult. Hope suffered serious injuries and damage to her protection while the others fought the spreading masses. Vorel used his fists rather than risk his valuable sword, and the company eventually reduced the puddings to fragments that Hope destroyed with gunfire.[^fight]

## Current status

The two puddings encountered in the caves are destroyed, including their final fragments. The wider extent of their presence beneath Tietkalla remains unknown.[^end]
