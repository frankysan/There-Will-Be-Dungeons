**Identity and earliest source:** [Wastes S3E02 — A New Mouth to Feed](../../episodes/S3E02.md) ~01:30–01:38, **named SPROUT by Nash ~02:08:12–02:08:35**. **Status:** physically living/speaking, still **ATTACHED TO NASH at episode cutoff**, full species/origin/physiology unknown. **Evidence:** Two machine-generated SRTs, no original audio heard. ## S3E03 — physically ensnared, still present and introduces itself to Nash's family

In [played S3E03](../../episodes/S3E03.md) ~01:09:29–01:12, when green **mind-fluff face parasites actually attach to Nash and companions aboard Fate Six**, a creature also has a tentacle **IN THE INDEPENDENT SPROUT SHOULDER MOUTH**, confirming its actual presence outside the shared dream and its continued physical attachment. **Nash's involuntary waking fire burst destroys parasitic creatures** without an observed permanent injury to Sprout. On Mars (~01:55–01:58), Sprout **SPEAKS INDEPENDENTLY TO ANGELASHI AND THE FOUR CHILDREN**, asks whether Nash's wife is also its wife; Nash and Angelashi reject that idea. **No new proof Sprout is the couple's offspring, sibling, alternative Nash or legally/romantically attached to Angelashi.** Sprout remains attached to Nash at episode cutoff in Mars augmentation lab; its creation remains unknown. ## Appearance and demonstrated capabilities

Nash **ACTUALLY REGAINS A COMPLETE HUMANOID BODY** when Stanley's seventh-level ***Regenerate* finishes** after the Hell-ship fight, having changed back from an adult black dragon. During the subsequent rest in his shared Fate Six cabin with Vorel, **a NEW, tiny autonomous EYE AND MOUTH physically grows on one shoulder OPPOSITE THE OLD ADDITIONAL BABY HAND**. Nash hears a voice in the dark asking that the light be switched off. Touching it earns him a small bite for **one damage**. Holding up a glowing vegetable peel, he **sees an eyeball and mouth of about FOUR TEETH**, some hairs, and a face-like protrusion; neither a separate head with neck nor full second body is described. The creature **can blink, speak independently, bite, eat food, dislike being poked in the eye and express fear**, but **has no own demonstrated limbs or ability to pick up food**. Nash uses his older baby hand to feed it.

The eye/mouth claims to know almost nothing, says it seems to have been alive for only minutes, and is hungry. Nash first offers **vegetable peels**, which it finds unappealing. The next morning it tries **grapes**, to its delight. It wonders what a book/name/father is and asks whether Nash might be its father; **Nash gives it the name “SPROUT”**, which it accepts. It greets Hope, Vorel and Stanley, asks about other children and displays its own questions. Nash protests that he did not ask to grow a second entity and wants to investigate; **he expressly promises NOT to cut Sprout out**. No future separation is performed.

## Limits and disputed origin

**Unknown:** Is Sprout a distinct engineered being, a complication of ***Regenerate***, influence from the Principle's chrome/bodies, a division of Nash's dragon-metal heart, or something else? The cast and Nash **speculate** about these options, but **none is verified by tests, surgery, creator admission or later episode review here**. The Principle's claim of Nash's fatherhood is also **not independently verified**, so “Sprout is Jesus Chrome's son” is NOT a supported fact. The GM treats Sprout as independently roleplayed; its mouth/eye are not an illusion, but **do not conflate it with Nash's original infant hand, his own red eye, or his engineered dragon heart**. **Played cutoff:** Sprout remains alive and attached while Fate Six leaves Blast Belly Rock **toward Mars**, not yet arrived.
