# Diptak — Golden Lands lizardfolk warrior

**Status:** **Killed by Orp in corrected E060**, directly described on scene; no later resurrection has been documented. **Working spelling:** `Diptak` (ASR variants `Diptack/Diphtak`), not audio-verified. **Scope:** Wastes through E060.

The warrior **Diptak** first confronts [Vorel](vorel.md) in the **Golden Lands** and **yields alive**, pledging his household/clan in corrected [E028](../../episodes/E028.md). He later reappears in [E058](../../episodes/E058.md), demanding that Vorel return west because an unworthy **Great Fang** rules a powerful lizardfolk polity, **Razak Val**. Diptak escorts Vorel there with velociraptor-like riding mounts while the party travels in **Fate 4**. [E059](../../episodes/E059.md).

In [E060](../../episodes/E060.md), the ruler proves to be **Orp**, cyclops and former party acquaintance, who has lizardfolk hunters bring him **human captives to eat**. Diptak insists on **single-combat “scaling”** tradition and the authority of a **Council of Twelve**, while Vorel resists rules that perpetuate mass captivity. During an argument over Vorel's formal challenge, **Orp strikes Diptak with a fist; the GM describes immediate death and an accordion-crushed body**. His remains are carried out. This outcome supersedes all **earlier living Diptak** descriptions, but **does not establish the fate of Diptak's clan**.
