# Pixie Hulks

## Introduction

Pixie Hulks are enormous, muscular transformations of small pixies, combining crushing physical strength with pixie magic. [P0D](../characters/p0d.md) first revealed the horror of this change when Monsoon Cabbage's command forced her into a giant, aggressive form. Her distress and confusion remained visible beneath the violence.[^change]

## The assault on Tietkalla

### Arc Two — weapons against the keep

Monsoon used P0D's transformation during the storeroom confrontation inside [Tietkalla's keep](../places/tietkalla.md). Nash tried to protect and calm her, but Diontalus killed her while she was still transformed. Her body burst into pixie dust; the other hulks that appeared afterward were different pixies.[^p0d]

The larger force could smash through walls and ceilings and rise into the air. Yet these powerful bodies were vulnerable to a still larger predator: an enormous altered froghemoth devoured hulks amid the ruined keep. Some remained alive within its mouth and digestive passages, sharing the adventurers' peril inside the creature.[^assault]

When the adventurers returned above the devastated city, more hulks rose toward them. Stanley appealed to the pixies with a promise of freedom, briefly affecting several. The huge forms then began shrinking back into small pixies, which vanished or scattered invisibly. The change could end, although the precise cause of that reversion was unknown.[^reversion]

## Current status

P0D is dead. The last hulks seen above Tietkalla reverted to small pixies and dispersed; the fate of all those used in Monsoon's assault remains unknown.[^reversion]
