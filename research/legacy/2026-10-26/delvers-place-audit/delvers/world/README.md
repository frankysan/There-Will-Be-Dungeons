## Research boundary

All 37 campaign entries have dossiers. Research remains ASR-provisional; this editorial pass adds no listening verification. Use [S3E12](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E12.md) for the finale evidence, and the [review queue](../../../../../../delvers/REVIEW_QUEUE.md) for open spelling, custody, source-identity and listening questions. Earlier cutoffs remain valid historical states, even where later outcomes are known.

Separately catalogued bonuses, including the [post-campaign retrospective](../../../../../../episodes/BONUS-2021-11-13.md), remain attributed commentary. They do not turn unused encounters into history or establish a campaign sequel. The [pre-cleanup portal](../../../delvers-campaign-cleanup/delvers/world/README.md) preserves the earlier episode-by-episode navigation history.
