# S3E10 — Atomer Guild public reckoning and Cassius's return

**Played [Delvers S3E10 — Sad in a boney kind of way](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E10.md)**, continuing **immediately** after S3E09 ~6pm magical tree arrival by the well. Evelyn plans a ~8pm protest, **Koh and Krandal hand over their Silver Guild badges to her** (despite S3E09 dropped/thrown badges, no retrieval scene), while **Evelyn hands all THREE collected URNS to KOH**, to separate prized objects from divination tracking. This is the *current S3E10 end custody*, superseding Evelyn's S3E09 bag.

Evelyn **ACTUALLY obtains Loran Radiant Armour** on school alarm and slays a hook horror, then rides giant elk through streets summoning locals to town meeting. Koh uses reporter Dumbledwark/Raisinbunch's sealed letter **ACTUALLY stating Cassius held at Wildfire estate**; Krandal returns from farm to join her. At Guild gate she **throws badges at Zoltar the Wizened** and accuses his Silver City commercial enterprise of tracking adventurers and ignoring missing people. **Zoltar explicitly ADMITS divination tracking of all adventurers as Guild practice** (stronger than S3E09 inference, though exact badge enchantment, operators, spell and individual surveillance sessions not tested). He defends academy campaign as creating many Gold Leaguers and blames Delvers for Bronzed Ball disaster. Koh, Krandal and **Helenar Clearstone (appears with written thirty-name missing elf list)** support Evelyn; the three **ACTUALLY READ all 30 names aloud**, but transcript does **NOT provide list of names**. Citizens, old Wood/Bronze/Silver Guild members including Boonfellow, Selenar and others **ACTUALLY JOIN public demonstration**. Zoltar cannot win over crowd and retreats inside.

**Koh ACTUALLY secretly follows Zoltar into Guild**, threatens consequences short of killing, **reveals reporter's letter location**; Zoltar **confirms Cassius at Wildfire estate**, joins Koh in riding there with the crowd. At Wildfire gate **Valentine Wildfire ACTUALLY EMERGES WITH CASSIUS, who is alive, clothed and walks free**. He had been contained, not necessarily tortured, because his objectives conflicted with academy priorities. Zoltar **ACTUALLY rides away from town on horse** after warning; **NO execution or verified formal Council legal exoneration**. Cassius **RETURNS TO GUILD DESK AND EXERCISES LEADERSHIP** on-screen, de facto restored as guildmaster, authorizing attack on Khalazar. He **ACTUALLY pays TWENTY THOUSAND GOLD EACH to Koh, Krandal and Evelyn**, offers shops for magical gear and supplies next morning (food/water/extended stay preparations are **promised**, not seen loaded). Advises **radiant, fire and protection versus necrotic**. He gifts Koh **RING OF FREE ACTION from Selenar**; asks him return it on surviving. Crowd goes back to Guild for campaign registration; individual recruited Silvers' commitment to march is **enthusiastic plan**, not descent yet.

**Status end S3E10:** CASSIUS PHYSICALLY FREE/restored in Guild, ZOLTAR GONE ALIVE, campaign authorization and money already distributed, expedition **planned for following morning only**. No new Guild court ruling shown; no proof all thirty missing have been found. Three urns held **BY KOH, NOT replaced in triangle altar**; metal gate remains sealed.
