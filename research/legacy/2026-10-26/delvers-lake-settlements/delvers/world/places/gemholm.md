# Gemholm — Lake Absul gem-dredging sandbar settlement

**Working spelling:** **Gemholm** (also heard by ASR as `Gemhold/Jenholm/In Gem Home`); precise name requires audio/GM-map review. **First directly played in [Delvers S2E06](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S2E06.md)**, not a Wastes location. Lore below is **ASR-provisional**; the Discord map mentioned on air has not been inspected.

## Site and access

An isolated village/mining colony built across shifting sandbars in **Lake Absul**. The Delvers travel **about six hours by small boat** from Atomer with a merfolk ferryman **Gil/Gilkeel**. Gil's large trained crab **Herman Snips** stays at Gemholm as a contact/signal for return transport; **no return voyage takes place in S2E06**. The lake route passes Krandal's riverside farm, **Noble Hollow** and a distant view of **Skull Keep**, but direct land travel or a permanent road is not documented.[^arrival]

The settlement has roughly **thirteen structures** on wooden stilts with exposed barnacle-coated bases; a central stone-supported warehouse is elevated above the tidal sand. The scattered signs name a **crab maintenance workshop, gem depository, Ambermoor house, infirmary, McNorris house, recreation building, mess hall and common office**, among other houses. **One boat has been deliberately burned and buildings are damaged, including a harpoon-riddled house associated with Hank Tom**. Since the island and tide shift, its exact map coordinates, building dimensions and durability are not yet independently checked.[^arrival]

## The S2E06 outbreak

Human survivor **Mack** claims dredging strange gems from the lake preceded the arrival of monstrous **slaadi**, including red and blue forms. He says red claw injuries implant larvae and blue assaults can transform victims into slaadi. This is **NPC testimony**; the gems' causal mechanism and the precise timing are **not proven**. The village burned its own merchant/escape boat to prevent a possible infected person reaching mainland Atomer. Mack and **Church**, a bandaged Goliath healer/priest, are the only survivors directly seen alive so far; the fates of **Ambermoor, Balmer, Destin, Neko, Goodwill** and other named workers remain uncertain.[^survivors]

The Delvers find **Caleb, a crab operator, dead in the infirmary** after a creature seemingly escaped from his chest. A **small purple-blue larva** escapes and is destroyed by Church following Koh's thrown psychic dagger. **Church admits a red slaad scratched him two days earlier** but believes Goliath ancestry protects him; **no medical determination confirms this**. Mack warns the larger creatures return with the tide, while the young are already on land.[^survivors]

In the harpoon-damaged residence of **Hank Tom**, a small **red frog-like transformed figure** flees under broken flooring. Koh's ranged psychic strike wounds it; Evelyn **kills it with repeated warhammer blows**, despite Krandal trying to obstruct and preserve it for questioning with a conjured python. Mack calls it Hank Tom; Church says the person is already lost. An apparent final **“thank you”** is present in both SRT exports, but speaker/meaning and any cure possibility **need audio or stronger evidence**. At the episode cutoff **tide is rising and ominous shadows circle offshore**. **No adult slaad battle, colony rescue/clearance or return ferry trip has been played.**[^cutoff]

## June 2021 cast commentary: origin of the crab controls

In [June 2021 supporter Q&A](../../../../../../../episodes/BONUS-2021-06.md), ~00:06–00:08, GM **Kyle identifies Gemholm's crab vehicles as the published *Apparatus of the Crab* (historically *Apparatus of Kwalish*)** and explicitly says **the lever list came from D&D Basic Rules**, not from his own invention. Kyle says he shared an older **third-edition illustration on Discord**, **not inspected** in this research; its artwork cannot automatically be treated as the actual appearance of the local machines or their numbered variants. Kyle recommends teaching controls once and then compressing routine operation: creator commentary about session management, **not additional played levers, keys or outcomes**. The location's directly played evidence remains in S2E07.

## S2E07: the infection source and laboratory

With tide flooding the village, the Delvers open the **crab maintenance workshop** using a **key #2 painted into existence** by Mack with Evelyn's magical pigments. Inside stand two **enchanted mechanical dredger-crabs (#2 and #3)**, swimming and walking vehicles with claws, collection hopper and ten levers; **#1 is absent**. The party finds **Hank Tom's diary and lever guide**, **Caleb's diary** in the **gem depository**, and **Ambermoor's diary** in the manager's lavish home. The two workers' journals mention contact with unusual gems and drinks supplied by Ambermoor; the manager's own writing describes **chaos gems processed into a transmissible phage**, acceleration of infections and a plan to monetize transformed creatures. His claimed transformation of **Destin into green slaad** remains a journal assertion. The actual GM-drawn journals and map are **not inspected**.[^s207diaries]

A **red slaad with a striped swimsuit marked Balmer** claws Koh; the party **kills it**, Krandal's twin face maces finishing the fight. Koh later feels a larva moving inside his leg, cuts it out with a psychic dagger and **crushes it under his hoof**, while neither Krandal nor Evelyn finds a similar infection. A large slaad had **regenerated earlier wounds** during the fight. **Evelyn successfully cures Church's early internal larva with Lesser Restoration**, visibly dissolving it. This does **not** prove reversal of a full transformation.[^s207combat]

Evelyn pilots **crab #2** into flooded wreckage of Destin's house and retrieves the missing **#3 operating key**. The group then uses two crabs to reach a worked **underwater cave**, with **#1** parked inside. A hidden flooded hatch leads around an alarmed main door. Koh infiltrates through that hatch and watches **Ambermoor** extract black liquid from a sedated giant **black slaad marked Neko**. When Evelyn triggers the front alarm, Koh **knocks Ambermoor unconscious nonlethally**. He is bound/gagged and his spell components/crab #1 key removed. Evelyn **casts Lesser Restoration on the black specimen without effect**, then **kills it with a javelin while still in its tank**. Koh takes the manager's **briefcase with red, blue, green and black reagent samples** for guild examination (not yet handed over). GM says the three exceptional source gems were **powdered/dissolved** into those mixtures; no intact originals recovered.[^s207lab]

**Two larger slaadi remain unaccounted for** when the party returns to the island; no final fight, eradication or proof of safety is played. The three metal crab machines are **returned to the workshop**, not taken on the ferry. **Herman Snips signals Gil/Gilkeel** for a return trip. Mack and newly cured Church continue toward the **Atomer guild**, while the **three Delvers, bound captive Ambermoor and evidence** leave the boat at **Krandal's farm**, avoiding the city for the night. **The guild has not received the prisoner/evidence and the Poppin' Slaads quest is not definitively completed at cutoff.**[^s207end]

## Current research status

**Through S2E07:** Gemholm's deadly outbreak is linked by journals and recovered reagents to **Ambermoor's deliberate experiments**; **Ambermoor alive/captive, Neko-labelled black slaad and Balmer-labelled red slaad killed**, Caleb/Hank Tom previously dead, **Church cleared of visible early-stage larva**. **Two adult slaadi still missing**. The **Delvers and captive are at Krandal's farm**, not back in city. Mack/Church continue to guild; no confirmed quest clearance, reward or full decontamination.
