# Skoogie — exiled kobold shaman of Chath Peak

**Working spelling:** **Skoogie** (ASR variants `Scoogie/Skooky/Scoogy`), a kobold shaman in **Delvers S2E08**. Pronouns and gender are **not defined by the creature**: when asked, Skoogie says such distinctions ceased to matter. Speaks **Draconic**, translated to the party by **Marsh**; character dialogue in transcripts should not be assumed intelligible to all Delvers.

## The shaman and lost worshippers

Skoogie approaches the Delvers wearing an enormous arching **feathered headdress**, long dragging cloak, carrying a **bell used for magic** and a **caged lizard**. Has a tiny dwelling hidden behind a rock in a lava tube, with a bed, fire, metal pipes and scraps of food. Skoogie says the villagers **worshipped the shaman until the red dragon arrived about a month earlier**, displacing that influence. The shaman wants the creature **killed** and the kobold settlement left for Skoogie to reclaim; those political motivations remain Skoogie's **claims**, not verified altruism.[^approach]

Marsh translates, after jokes about the shaman wanting to eat Marsh's weasel **Wheatley**. Skoogie ceremonially removes a feathered metal **Arrow of Dragon Slaying** from atop the hat and **actually gives it to Marsh**; this is an item transfer, not just an offer. There is discussion of future kobold foot massages, primarily joking negotiation, no evidence of a formal contract. Skoogie offers to lead the **secret way to the red dragon's lair**, guides the party past lava, and identifies the old chamber where the party finds **Radiant Lance inscriptions and Evelyn's forged Dragon Slayer's Longsword**.[^arrow]

## Later S2E09 retrospective outcome

**S2E09 GM recap** establishes **Skoogie survives** being crushed under the dragon as Forced Landing activates and later hurt by repeated dragon breath because **Evelyn heals Skoogie during the omitted combat**. After **Fredoroth is slain by Marsh's Arrow of Dragon Slaying**, **Skoogie happily departs to kobold settlement**. **Actually reclaiming religious leadership not witnessed**, despite shaman's ambition. Do not backdate dragon's death to the S2E08 recording itself, where Skoogie was still in live combat. [S2E09](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S2E09.md), ~00:00–00:09.

## Earlier S2E08 cutoff

Skoogie **accompanies the Delvers to the mountaintop chamber** and uses bell/spell **to grant 10 temporary HP each** in the fight. Dragon's fire apparently sets Skoogie alight and brings him to the ground; **Evelyn then heals the shaman for 3 HP**, so **NOT proven dead**. Later the shaman hands Evelyn a **lightstone** during heavy steam, and is still portrayed as a helper. **Dragon remains alive when active battle is paused mid-episode**, so Skoogie's wish for restored rule is **not fulfilled**. Skoogie's original arrow remains **unused in Marsh's custody** at cutoff.[^combat]
