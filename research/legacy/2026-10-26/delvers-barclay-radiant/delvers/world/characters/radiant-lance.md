# Radiant Lance — historic Pelor knight, trapped mental witness

**Previously remembered/represented hero, physically encountered as skeletal remains in [Delvers S3E08 — I Smell a Rat Ball](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E08.md)**. A bucket-helmed cavalier figure in old armour with **untarnished white/sun/horse/lance tabard**, associated with Pelor and the title **Radiant Lance**. In a projected farm takeover, **his apparent living form alone doesn't turn into rats** when other projected figures briefly waver. Koh uses psychic link and recognizes Radiant Lance from earlier **statues/markings**, and the figure speaks independently to Koh and Krandal inside rat mind-prison. This is **not evidence a living Radiant Lance walked above ground in present Atomer**.

## Speech inside the collective's psychic fabrication

Radiant Lance explicitly **calls the imprisoned place “Black Basilica,” claims to have been held there for some 150 years, knows he is DEAD and cannot physically leave**, and says consciousness survives inside his jailer's mind. He cannot free himself or the Delvers and expects **Evelyn (who resisted mind charms) to break their bonds**. He asks Krandal and Koh to play **War** to pass time; Krandal joins, seems to lose final hand, and **Radiant Lance thanks him for the final game before fading** as Evelyn cuts the controlling rat chains. **His story is character testimony and a direct psychic appearance, not a dated record of his life or confirmed divine resurrection**.

## Physical remains and custody

After rats disperse, heroes see **old skeletal armoured body in matching bucket helm** with preserved nervous system/brain residue and one hand resembling a **thumbs-up**. **Ordinary longsword and chainmail are rusted**, but **tabard and a physical long LANCE remain uncorrupted**. No Guild badge on corpse. GM says tabard can act as **Pelor holy symbol**, freeing **Krandal** from necessity of wearing separate oversized pendant solely as focus; **Krandal ACTUALLY DONS it** in S3E08. It **is not automatically attuned**, and any further magic not identified; discussion of Amulet of the Devout and attunement capacity is character/player mechanics only, not proof of extra item acquired. The physical lance is **placed in a party Bag of Holding**, but exact person carrying it not verbally resolved and lance is **not wielded yet**. Keep distinct from Loran's lance-mode weapon.

**Factual boundary:** “~150 years” is the knight's assertion, plausible from remains but **not independently dated**, and apparent retained mind is part of the All Rat's special prisoner system.
