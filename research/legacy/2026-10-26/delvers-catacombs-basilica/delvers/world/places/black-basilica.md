# The Black Basilica — cranium-rat prison and urn chamber

**Physically discovered during [Delvers S3E08 — I Smell a Rat Ball](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E08.md)** while party follows the **LEFT staircase** above the older [triangle-gate altar](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/triangle-gate-catacombs.md). The **name “Black Basilica” first comes from self-identified Radiant Lance's consciousness inside a psychic prison** and is adopted in GM/table narration for the real, columned long hall with crank-opened entrance, physical old armoured skeleton, altar and rat collection. Its broader link to Khalazar **is strongly suggested by urn/gate theme and All Rat claims but not fully established independent history**.

## Important reveal: false journeys, real location

The left stair and ancient corridors bring the heroes into a **psychic trap**, exposed after all three initially roll Survival and Intelligence saves. **Krandal's ostensible return to farm** (workers on strike, Goodwines buying property, snake ally accusing Hissia neglect, Chad Brad relocated), **Koh's ostensible criminal wanted posters and murdering Guild leader Zoltar**, and **Evelyn's ostensible confinement at Kingdom Saint asylum** are all **manufactured by cranium-rat chains attached to their necks**, **NOT physical events**. Similarly, visions of a city enslaved beneath a floating pyramid, the Skull Keep roof and Hopper wedding overlays are **projected images, not established historical or present scenes**. Fright/guilt generated by them affects characters' real future intentions; actual unpaid farm labour from S3E06 remains an unresolved concern.

**Evelyn** resists the illusion, using memories of her companions and Withers Mansion mirror; in physical room she finds **Koh and Krandal lying helpless on floor**, each with a **cranium rat biting nape and long trains of linked tail/teeth rats** leading to great hovering mass with exposed pulsing/flashing brains. Nearby **old armoured corpse** has a rat too. She stabs/removes their neck rats to break projected incarceration, liberating fellow Delvers. **The rat congregation styles itself “All Rat”** and claims it wishes to protect its organ-powered collective. It offers to surrender **golden hook-horror-capped urn** but wants to retain the **organ inside**, allegedly **“part of Khalazar”**—**interested NPC testimony, not independently verified origin**.

## Rat swarm's defeat, urn recovered

After rejected bargain, the All Rat uses whipping rat-chain tendrils, **psychic assaults and shifting appearance of battlefield**. Evelyn attacks with lightning and magical blade, Krandal heals/buffs, Koh attacks with psychic daggers. Final decisive result is **Koh leaping into airborne mass, grabbing urn (Athletics 18), throwing psychic dagger, and teleporting 50 feet away**. Swarm **IMMEDIATELY dissolves** into ordinary living fleeing rats whose heads cease glowing. **No complete physical extermination; no proven death of independent queen or actual Khalazar presence**. [All Rat creature record](../../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/all-rat-cranium-congregation.md).

## Radiant Lance's remains and recovered artefacts

Within false landscape **Radiant Lance** speaks to Koh/Krandal, claims **~150 years** trapping in rat brain / his body decayed, and spends time playing War with Krandal. He is **not actually alive in the street or at farm**. After Evelyn frees characters, Koh/Krandal find **PHYSICAL skeleton in old bucket-helmet and armour**, partially preserved nervous system/brain remnants, longsword/chainmail rusted, **tabard bearing Pelor-related sun, lance and horse design STILL PRISTINE, physical LANCE intact**. **Krandal puts on tabard**; GM confirms it functions as his **holy symbol in place of neck pendant**, while any other magical effects remain unknown. **Party stores physical long LANCE in Bag of Holding**; particular bag bearer not firmly audited. **No Guild badge from remains**. [Radiant Lance](../../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/radiant-lance.md).

## Exit and status after S3E08

Party **ACTUALLY rests here for one hour**; Krandal's baby hook horror (**Scroungy/Scroungie, tentative**) hunts ordinary rats, wears Evelyn's pink rope ribbon and exchanges cries with nearby adult hook horrors. Three adults investigate the rat buffet; lead threatens Krandal and **Koh instantly kills it**, **two others retreat alive**. Party exits cautiously, Koh **gives Drift Globe to Evelyn**, she recovers door crank, and they return down **Umbrel/Umbral Flight**. They **ACTUALLY REMOVE first bat-headed urn from older altar**, so both physically recovered urns are now in **Evelyn's bag**, leaving **all three slots vacant** until next visit. They go to [Deep Winds Spa](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/deep-winds-spa.md) to buy overnight room/meal for **1,000 gp each**, and no further lower staircase exploration occurs.
