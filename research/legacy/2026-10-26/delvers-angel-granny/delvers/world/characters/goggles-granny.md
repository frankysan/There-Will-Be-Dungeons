# Goggles Granny — visiting elderly catlike alchemist

**Played guest in [Delvers S3E07 — A Shirt Full of Mayo](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E07.md)** by **Carter Johnson**. The spoken name alternates between **“Granny Goggles”** (her own self-introduction in the Parakeet text) and **“Goggles Granny”** (other dialogue and the outro). **The latter is a provisional working headword until audio or written sheet confirms the intended ordering.** **Do not infer official D&D ancestry from ears/tail**, or whether she is the named proprietor of the earlier-reported closed **Goggles Granny's Alchemy Shop** in Atomer until dialogue, sheet or written resource verifies this identity. Her claim of being from **“my town”** and running a small alchemy shop has no confirmed geography; she sounds unsure whether her town is the Delvers' own Atomer or a different one. A definite shop-owner identification and business reopening would be premature.

## Appearance and equipment — directly described by the guest (~00:13–00:19)

Granny is an **elderly, hunched, approximately five-foot-tall feline-featured woman** with **cat ears, tail, small granny glasses, large scar running across an eye, and rather unfriendly/grumpy countenance**. From behind an **overstuffed backpack** nearly hides her figure; her footwear may be absent (she braces the ledge using cat claws). She provides her own **scroll/map and ancient-Elvish translation**, having navigated **three earlier puzzle rooms herself** before the party meets her. Guest says she had independently drawn art of her character, but **that drawing was not inspected**. **Do not invent coat/clothing colors, breed, pattern, eyes, outfit design or confirmed species/class beyond alchemical competence**.

## Independent explorer and reluctant fourth puzzle-solver

In the [black-slime key-and-lever chamber](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/black-slime-key-chamber.md), Granny accepts Krandal's **offered potato**, speaks ancient Elvish and uses Investigation to **complete the text translation**. Koh **actively links her by Psychic Whispers** with himself and other heroes. A worn but inventive alchemist, she tries tying a **dagger to a rope** to snag distant platform (two failed throws), produces **Potion of Diminution**, which she **gives to Krandal without clarifying he will SHRINK**, and **drinks Potion of Growth** to become roughly **ten feet tall**. With a tether she **physically throws shrunken Krandal** to distant platform, and later successfully performs a similar throw; he takes lightning shocks while solving puzzle. She also carries **potions of climbing, fire breath and healing, a merchant scale and an Alchemy Jug** whose lists include **mayonnaise**, but **only shrinking/growth potions are consumed**. Her Alchemy Jug **does not dispense mayonnaise in this episode**, despite jokes about a shirt filled with it. A successful **merchant scale** lets party measure heart vessel weight, and Granny's **two-pound reference weights plus a 23-pound rock** replace its pressure load; Granny and Koh complete dexterity coordination, trap never activates.

**Disposition:** Grumpy, brusque, dryly comic (“too old for this”), frustrated by younger adventurers but cooperative. When asked her objective she says she's trying to **save her town**; the reason for peril is not established. Krandal speaks as if the cities might face similar danger; **no verified shared-town location or curse**. She **initially turns toward home**, agrees temporarily to follow others and then **physically stops on long catacomb descent due to age/exhaustion**; the GM **narrates her safe return to town**. She is **NOT present when jar is set on S3E06 triangle altar**, not recruited as permanent party member or confirmed future return.
