# Marek Gideon — the researcher at the Noble Hollow

## Introduction

Marek is a researcher entrusted with examining the forest ruins by the Delvers Dungeon Guild. Silver-ranked fighters including Broadside and Solburn serve as guards and companions on his expeditions. His work draws him into depths whose sounds and ancient workings seem to change those who hear them. Whether he seeks knowledge for its own sake or comes to desire the power hidden below, his scholarship has become a danger to those who followed him.[^early]

## The darkmantle cowl

He appears to the Darkest Delvers not as a familiar researcher but as a mounted figure clothed in darkness, wearing a tall cowl stitched from the body of a darkmantle. Red glass ornaments replace two of the creature's eyes. He demands the conch-tipped staff of a fish wizard from the trader Glepo Ma, who obeys in fear. Marek threatens the whole kuo-toa village should anyone hide the adventurers from him. He leaves his armed companions to rob the Delvers and rides toward the distant bullywug encampment, where another group of bronze-ranked guild members is at work.[^marsh]

The First Sons Brotherhood is attacked, and high elf Faelan is taken captive. Later, beneath the magic that binds his words to truth, Broadside identifies Marek as the researcher he was meant to protect. The woman called Cheet says the old ritual the expedition uncovered needs an elf's life, the faith of devoted worshipers, and a summoning stone. She believes Marek means to bring forth a beast from the lake and unleash it against the city. Broadside remembers little of his own role, only a haze that followed their descent into the dark.[^truth]

## The interrupted summoning

At the petrified tree called the Noble Hollow, Marek stands before a green circle of flame with a darkmantle cowl upon his head. Faelan lies unconscious on a geared stone disc, and two cultists assist the ritual. Marek refuses to be drawn away, calling on Solburn and his followers to defend him. Koh wounds him repeatedly from the shadows, while Evelyn faces him openly with a hammer shining in sacred light. She finally strikes him down without taking his life. His ritual loses its voice, and the ancient disk breaks under Faelan. The earth then collapses, exposing a monstrous roper below; this creature's emergence does not prove the feared lake beast has been summoned.[^hollow]

## Current status

Marek has **been subdued alive**, and his immediate **midnight sacrifice and summoning are interrupted**. The Delvers later report his actions to Selenar Everbloom and Cassius. The guild promises **a formal trial at the Guild Academy** and a further inquiry into the assignment that placed a powerful researcher among those ruins, but **no trial, verdict or sentence is portrayed**. The underworld force that may have influenced him, the origin of the ritual and the fate of the conch-shell staff after the confrontation are not completely established. His precise surname and spellings remain subject to source verification in the footnote.[^status]
