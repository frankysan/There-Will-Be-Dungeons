# Hooper Estate — a wedding in the western woods

## Introduction

The Hoopers' mansion is a wealthy halfling house set beside deep woods on the southwestern outskirts of Atomer. Its stone frontage and grand buttresses announce generations of success; a curved drive receives carriages of guests and servants laden with drink, food and other celebrations. Within, the stairways, windows and furnishings are notably lower than those built for human visitors. The grounds hold a pavilion, ornamental lake, wooded burial plot and lawns large enough for all the joyful confusion of a halfling wedding.[^grounds]

## The joining of two houses

**Limwick Hooper** marries **Fresna Goodwine**, bringing together noble-seeming merchant families once divided by violent competition. Wedding captain **Autumn Dew** organizes the elaborate event: rows of guests, tables heaped with cheese, fine wines, playful mastiffs that carry children, and the traditional cornucopia fired high into the air to scatter coins and jewels through the woods. The ceremony is to end with white birds flying behind the officiant. Instead, the birds are found dead in their opened box. Evelyn hides their remains and brings forth an immense magical goat as a diversion, making two white bodies seem to fly for a few moments. The guests cheer, and the celebration continues.[^wedding]

## The unburied grandfather

Beyond the bright festivities lie a small walled family graveyard and a great leafless tree, but there is no stone for **Grandfather Aurelli/Orilli Hooper**. Elder Mr. Goodwine admits that he and his allies beat and hanged him fifteen years before during the trading wars, leaving his body in the woods. When he comes again, it is not as a gentle family shade. The grandfather's withered body wields a sword while suspended by the neck from a walking tree, and his magic gives the whole dead oak a dreadful gait. The tree's branches fall silent after Krandal shatters their connection to the body; the Great Daufante's stars then bring down the wight. The long-kept **Deck of Many Things** is taken from the corpse before Goodwine and the wizard's undead servant give the grandfather the burial he was denied.[^grave]

## Current status

**The Hooper–Goodwine wedding is held successfully and the wight is defeated.** The grandfather's remains are **actually buried in the family plot** by Goodwine and Connor, with no public announcement of the fifteen-year-old murder and **no independent proof of everlasting peace**. The guest wizard **takes the deck away without drawing cards**, having traded away his claim on the mission reward. A knife-bearing, red-eyed duck has also been stopped, but **the source of floating eyes, twenty dead ceremonial birds, a mysteriously screaming cheese-face and halfling-shaped black-sludge footprints is not independently identified**. **Black residue at the walking tree's roots** remains a fresh continuity lead, not proof of connection to the Dawnless Dunes.[^status]
