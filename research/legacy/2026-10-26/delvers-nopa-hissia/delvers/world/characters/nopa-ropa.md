Krandal's potato farm later expands, but Nopa's later employment/residence remains UNSPECIFIED. [S3E12](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E12.md).

He protests uncertainty where Princess Hissia is held, criticizes Koh's stealth and Krandal's leadership, follows his companions into windless desert and up pyramid approach. He does NOT find, rescue or physically recognize Hissia by cutoff; from far Krandal sees woman POSSIBLY Hissia fanning balcony occupant. All force takes shelter as Koh infiltrates.
