# Princess Hissia — Snake Island royal visitor

**Working spelling:** **Hissia** (Qwen “Hysia”, audio and original GM notes not verified), **claims to be daughter of King/Lord Hissia** and a **princess of Snake Island**, connected to [Krandal Splenderbelt](../../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/krandal-splenderbelt.md) during his **unremembered two-week absence** between Delvers first/second season. **FIRST directly observed/introduced to players in [S2E12 — The Bell of The Ball](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S2E12.md)** at Bronze Ball, ~01:46–02:04. **Second arc ends here; memories unresolved.**

## S3E12 FINAL: Confirmed and physically rescued, awake and kissed by Nopa

[Delvers S3E12 campaign finale](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E12.md) DIRECTLY CONFIRMS that woman in tattered purple dress with snake eyes, fanning Khalazar's bed, **IS Princess Hissia**: she is recognized in person by Krandal and especially Nopa Ropa. Koh and Dominic **ACTUALLY incapacitate Hissia and Keeves NONLETHALLY**, with Koh using psychic blades to render Hissia stable/unconscious; no death. Nopa Ropa **PHYSICALLY SWEEPS Hissia INTO HIS ARMS and escorts her downstairs to rescue circle**, while Corgan helps carry Keeves. After Khalazar is **ACTUALLY DEFEATED** and Darmah's last portal takes everyone to Guild basement, **Hissia ACTUALLY AWAKENS following healing magic and recognizes Nopa**. He calls her his Princess and his love, she calls the love “forbidden,” he replies it is forbidden too, and **THEY KISS**. Not merely a possible sighting or future plan, and NOT proof of Krandal's prior marriage to her on Snake Island. **Campaign ends one year later; no later Princess residence/marriage is played**. [S3E12](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E12.md).

## S3E11: Physically likely on balcony, still not positively identified or liberated

Inside [black pyramid / Dawnless Dunes](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/dawnless-dunes-pyramid.md), [S3E11](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E11.md) Koh physically uses Spider Climb to reach upper bedchamber, where he **SEES a long-haired female wearing tattered PURPLE dress with yellow-green snake slit eyes and scaled neck** fanning an **immobile bat-crowned withered figure**. Krandal first suggests that distant shape may be Hissia; with closer physical view Koh sees **strong resemblance to Princess Hissia's previous Bronze Ball appearance** and describes to allies, **but woman DOES NOT introduce herself or communicate, nor do they free or interview her**. Thus **likely but not unequivocally ID-confirmed Hissia**, do NOT record actual Princess rescued, dead or author of temple plot. **Keeves** also present anointing bed occupant. This physical candidate supports aboleth lead generally, but does not prove its entire claimed machine/location. [S3E11](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E11.md).

## S3E10: First explicit aboleth lead to Dawnless Dunes, NOT a witnessed rescue

In [Delvers S3E10](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E10.md) a flashback ACTUALLY plays Krandal's previously promised Deep Winds Spa questions to the **aboleth**. It **CLAIMS Princess Hissia is alive and forced to work on Khalazar's great machine in the deepest levels of Dawnless Dunes**. This is **enemy/NPC testimony**, NOT direct sighting, magical verification, liberation, confirmed machine specification or exact route. Krandal **ACTUALLY TELLS Nopa Ropa**, her Snake Island warrior advocate, who pledges to join next-morning hunt and calls it his mission. All three grick/hook-horror/bat-head urns recovered but transferred **Evelyn→KOH**, still NOT put into sealed triangle gate altar. **Princess does NOT physically appear in S3E10**, and final outcome remains unknown. [Nopa Ropa](../../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/nopa-ropa.md).

## Visual appearance

**Tall slender humanoid female, about Krandal's height**, long **straight DARK hair down back**; face/limbs **mostly human-looking**, NOT a literal snake head or visibly scaled serpent body, except **yellow-green irises with VERTICAL SNAKE SLIT pupils**. A **tight, flowing PURPLE SILK formal gown**, lavish **GOLD MEDALLION necklaces**, gold waist chain, thin additional hanging silk details, **small TIARA RINGED WITH SNAKE MOTIFS**. Arrives at Bronze Ball with **TWO SNAKE attendants/courtiers** beating drums and assisting her from coach; publicly **announced by Cassius as a surprise donor/benefactor**. Krandal notes touch cool/clammy; does not identify species as yuan-ti/naga.[^appearance]

## Relationship claim and gift provenance (NOT comprehensive memory recovery)

Princess greets Krandal affectionately as **“my beloved”**, pulls him into an ACTUAL dance and suggests intimate time together in the **missing Snake Island fortnight**. She calls **Staff of Python / Royal Staff of Snakes** a gift embodied in her father **King Hissia**, and says Krandal's snake armor belonged to her **brother who died during shared adventures**. Krandal guesses around her statements and privately tries to recover actual memory; the **ONLY described recollection** is a **snake crawling into his bed**, him jumping up/defeating the snake, **Hissia calling him beloved**. **No proven wedding, actual consummated romance, family death circumstances or missing-island chronology**; her claims are NPC testimony, not verified reconstructed narrative. Krandal excuses himself to washroom; princess's subsequent actions through monster attack not tracked.[^claim]

**Later S3E05 private lead (NOT confirmation of location/death):** In [S3E05](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E05.md) a three-eyed mind-reader at [Deep Winds Spa](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/deep-winds-spa.md) digs into **Krandal's** missing Snake Island memories, only to retreat amid **drums** that the being attributes to a curse. It says it **knows where Hissia is kept but does not tell Krandal**, and **corrects its own use of “dead” as idiomatic wording about where she lies/is kept**. **Hissia is NOT confirmed dead; no rescue, destination or factual proof of her current custody/location** is supplied. This knowledge stays in Krandal's private conversation, not automatically shared with Evelyn/Koh/Angel.
