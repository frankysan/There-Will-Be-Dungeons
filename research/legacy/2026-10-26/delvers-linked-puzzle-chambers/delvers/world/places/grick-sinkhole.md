# Grick-infested sinkhole — ruins and buried dungeon

## Introduction

In the forest outside Atomer, water-worn stone arches and rough steps lead into a limestone sinkhole. Roofless buildings surround a courtyard, well and cave mouth; an altar in one ruined room carries a low drone through a narrow opening. Woodland and hippogriffs border the old works, under the watch of the reclusive [Veggie Sticks](../../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/veggie-sticks.md).[^setting]

### Arc One — A dangerous threshold beneath the woods

A guild scouting assignment reveals gricks among the walls and undergrowth. Some carry old wounds, while others wait close to the altar and its inscribed tablet. The creatures turn the ruined courtyard into a trap for the explorers: a steep rim limits retreat, and an attempt to carry the heavy inscription out fails. The Delvers escape with their climbing sash, leaving the tablet behind. Krandal is dragged away near the rim before his companions kill the attacking grick and revive him.[^escape]

Veggie Sticks shelters the group in his hollow-stump home. His earlier claims to know everything in the woods give way to an admission that he had not known about the gricks. His concern is the hippogriffs and the disturbance a larger guild expedition might bring. With the immediate danger still unresolved, the Delvers return and kill the last grick they find near the crafted passage.[^return]

The dungeon beyond is dangerous even to its monstrous intruders. Grick corpses hang on wall spears or lie pierced by needles. Delayed controls govern rising floor spikes and pits, allowing brief safe crossings without making the whole route harmless. Behind these defenses lies an armory-like room with rusted fittings and a heavy stone chest. Evelyn takes a runic sword hilt from it, but the builders, inscriptions and deeper purpose remain unexplained.[^dungeon]

The guild accepts a scouting report, tentacle trophy and traced symbols. Cassius agrees to submit the Delvers' claim to explore farther, while retaining the option of sending stronger adventurers if urgent attacks demand it. Their discovery becomes a matter for guild investigation rather than a grant of control over the woodland or ruins.[^claim]

## Current status

Four gricks have been killed by the Delvers, but the site has not been established as clear. Traps remain, the tablet is unrecovered and the deeper structure unexplored. The last direct visit leaves the neighboring woodland and its guardian in place, with no later restoration or completed excavation known.[^status]
