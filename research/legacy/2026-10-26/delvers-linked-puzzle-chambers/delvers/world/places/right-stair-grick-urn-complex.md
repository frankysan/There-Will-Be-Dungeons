## Cross-linked two-person puzzle

All four **ACTUALLY stand on safe door plates** after Barclay's **Find Traps**, opening gates. Party separates physically into **Koh+Evelyn** (“Ko-keeper”) and **Krandal+Barclay** (“Splender-clay”). Koh **casts Psychic Whispers** for party communication and includes guest Barclay. At **35-ft chasm, ~300-ft drop**, floor switches on each pair's side raise/retract **the OTHER pair's bridge**; Koh uses **Psychic Teleportation (die 6 => 60 ft)** to leap to distant plate; the others walk across bridge and Evelyn uses Koh's **Sultan's Sash** to pull him across after shorter jump. No actual plunge. Next glyph rooms have **five unique pressure plates each plus two wall symbols**, wall symbols correspond to **OTHER lane's plates**. One incorrect set ACTUALLY triggers gas hiss/timer; party makes **Constitution saves**, corrects switches, opens further passage without recorded poison damage. Exact glyphs need original map, do not reconstruct from text approximations.

The next area has a ~50×50-ft central platform with perimeter falls and **two pressure plates per party**. Coordinated presses release **one stone golem PER LANE** plus poisonous floor gas. **Damage/action target appears on OPPOSITE golem**, so a strike in one room can visibly affect the other's creature. Crucial **GM reverses Koh's initial spectacular natural-20 psychic-dagger “kill”** because **stone golems are immune to psychic damage in this played ruling**; psychic blades do NOTHING to mindless constructs. Later **ordinary daggers do physical but reduced damage**; Krandal's face mace initially nonmagical/reduced until Barclay casts **Holy Weapon**, adding radiant effect. Other played attacks: Krandal **Spiritual Weapon (force)**; Barclay **Harm** after save (**25 necrotic in played instance**) and heals; Evelyn **Dispel Magic** removes poison on other side and **Lightning Javelin** strikes opposite golem, with Compelled Duel/Sanctuary tactic. **Both golems are ACTUALLY DESTROYED** when Krandal's force weapon finishes target; bridges and doors open. No player death. Spell interactions are **GM-played rulings**, not universal rulebook examples.

## Final chamber and urn

Two passages emerge above **~225-ft elongated hall** with middle **~50-ft spiral ramp/column**, **125-ft ceiling**, lower floor ~25ft down, small streams and **air SUCTION / outward BREATHING blast holes** all around spiral. Koh and Barclay spot some rocks are actually **hiding gricks including unusually bloated giant grick alpha**. Koh hides and readies strike; **Evelyn actually climbs the spiral** by timing wind rhythms (Athletics and repeated **Performance/dance** checks). At top she **physically removes a THIRD CLAY/CERAMIC URN with GRICK-shaped GOLD LID** from basin: **wind stops entirely**, and she **ACTUALLY STORES THIRD URN in her Bag of Holding** beside FIRST bat-skull and SECOND hook-horror urn. **No contents are opened/identified for third vessel**; Khalazar tissue remains inferred from family pattern, not seen. Gricks stir and fight; **Koh critical hits/slays a small grick and then large ALPHA, Krandal/Barclay dispatch others**, **AT LEAST ONE grick ESCAPES**. Subsequent return through **fully open bridges and doors** to the older forest-dungeon entrance, NO re-entry through triangle gate and **NO setting urns back on pedestal**.
