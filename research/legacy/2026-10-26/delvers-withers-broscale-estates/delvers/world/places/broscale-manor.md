# Broscale Manor — Wildfire's dragonborn household in Atomer

**First directly visited in [Delvers S2E09 — Potatopia](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S2E09.md)**, ~02:12–03:04. Dark, imposing **black-stone mansion on Atomer's prosperous outer estate circuit**, near the part of town where the previous noble wedding was held. **ASR-provisional** place spelling and family names; GM diagram not consulted. The household is **distinct from Withers Estate** and the earlier **farm-cave Broscale death**.

## Exterior, entry and sitting room

Well maintained grounds and self-closing gate; ornate wooden entry door with **large dragon-face knocker**. Deep red carpets, dark wooden floor and dark stone corridors create a dim interior; one sitting room has bright natural light, padded chairs and glass cases displaying trophies: **preserved eyes floating in jar, severed clenched ape's fist, chain-link symbol** and a barnacle-covered split stone used as coffee table. **Red drake named Bella** (~6 feet long as described) thrusts head from door when arrivals knock; housekeeper sends her upstairs to find Wildfire. **No drake attack occurs.** On dining hall mantel is stuffed **behir head**, which Wildfire attributes to grandfather **Elwood the Mighty Broscale** and **Georgina Purefire Broscale** in an old fight; **story/year as NPC account, not independently verified**.[^arrival]

## Household

**Wildfire/Buck Broscale** is red dragonborn, silver-ranked, Koh's earlier psionic instructor and the host. He wears ornate golden tunic and red sash, his familiar great red-black spiked **axe still strapped at his back but wrapped in cloth**. Wife **Valentine** is a brassy-toned dragonborn in gold-red flowing wrap, with small **Wall Nibbler** carried at chest. Children: **Climber** (roughly eight, keeps pet frog named **Boobola**, tells Koh about naming at 18), **Basher** (small toddler with cloth-padded stick and stuffed owlbear, hits Krandal's armored leg), **Dreamer** (older child with book **Gloomy Boy**). **Godwin Milner**, red-twisted mustache and short red goatee, is **servant/head cook/letter deliverer**; his dinner includes **crawfish bisque, roast pork with mushrooms and turnips/greens, caramel-rose mini cheesecakes**. The children's given names may be temporary until adulthood; no external written character sheet checked.[^family]

## S2E09 promotion dinner and Withers secret

All three Delvers invited to **18:00 dinner two nights after letter**. Evelyn arrives by school carriage wearing fancy **red dress with black accents as Lady Blackwood**, Koh appears as merchant through **Glamoured Studded Leather**, and Krandal wears his normal clerical clothes. Family meal is actually played, with Koh's bisque knocked over by Climber and the party making social checks; not an ambush. After the family withdraws, Wildfire reviews guild report cards, mentions **Bram Broscale** as brother whose badge Koh recovered, and judges moral dilemmas about prophecies, a vampire state, demon possession, monster-producing cities and bounty obligations. He **grants his recommendation for silver-rank promotion** but **does NOT issue badges, register rank or open Oracle Room** at this event.[^promotion]

At departure, **Wildfire physically stops Evelyn and privately tells her he knows she broke into Withers's basement**. Evelyn says they saw elementals imprisoned; Wildfire emphasizes **Withers's generous support of city and forthcoming Bronze Ball**, which celebrates the financier. There is **no performed arrest, open accusation before Koh or Krandal, or proof he himself was dorm intruder with great axe**, though Marguerite previously reported a red dragonborn intruder. Guests **leave the manor** at precise S2E09 cutoff.[^end]
