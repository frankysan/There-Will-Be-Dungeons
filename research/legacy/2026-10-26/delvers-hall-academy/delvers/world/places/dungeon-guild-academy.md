# Dungeon Guild Academy — the Radiant Lance Theatre

## Introduction

Far from Atomer, the Dungeon Guild Academy serves as a central gathering place for masters and adventurers drawn from guilds of every rank. The crowded hallways and common spaces form a living catalogue of creatures, magical equipment and ambitious heroes. Animal companions brush past unusual mounts; whispered spells mingle with the passage of scrolls and weapons. Cassius moves easily through the commotion, but the artificer Carrie Underworld, carrying her master's evidence, struggles to keep her things together.[^arrive]

## The theatre and its governors

At the heart of the Academy stands the Radiant Lance Theatre, a descending circular assembly room. Old gilt tables and chairs face a low central stone speaking place, while three powerful board members occupy an elevated enclosed seat. Their collective title sounds like **Numilari**; the exact word has not yet been settled. A tall, armed majordomo known in the surviving speech as **Ivorn or Iphorn the Just** announces the guildmasters whose petitions are heard. Spectators enter or leave at will, and assemblies respond to requests with murmurs, laughter and applause.[^theatre]

The guildmaster of Keld speaks about night patrols against vampires before Cassius's name is called. Other petitioners wait with equally extraordinary needs, from military threats to a far-off dragon-class guild trapped in a time crystal. Beneath the fantastic circumstances lies a hard division of authority: local guilds must ask the Academy for funds and have their claims judged by officials far from the danger itself.[^petitions]

## The plea for Atomer

Cassius asks for gold-ranked champions to clear the old Underdark passages beneath his city. Carrie lays out the recovered horn, psychic shafts and other trophies as evidence. Officials question the need, belittle her rank and remind Cassius of the researchers and silver guards already sent to inspect the forest ruin. Cassius says these researchers have shared nothing with him for almost two months, leaving the bronze ranks to negotiate with creatures at other ancient entrances while he struggles to manage the city. The board refuses to allocate more support and offers the future Bronze Ball as a financing opportunity, citing traditional Withers family support.[^petition]

The hearing reveals an unease within the guild's own belief in its chosen adventurers. Cassius doubts the three young Delvers despite their successes. A venerable diviner tells him the commune rituals cannot be wrong and that the party needs time. Such faith may govern decisions at the Academy, but it does not make its prophecies demonstrably true.[^chosen]

The board formally announces that **regional director Selenar Everbloom** will inspect the Atomer franchise to decide whether past aid has been properly handled. Cassius and Carrie depart in Aegis's coach with their request denied.[^inspection]

## Current status

The Academy has **refused the requested gold-ranked reinforcements for Atomer**, and **Selenar's inspection was ordered**. She subsequently appears during the **S1E12 post-Noble Hollow debrief**, but the Academy scene itself takes place earlier, during the **kuo-toa expedition**, despite its archive label **S2E00**. Exact spellings of governors, their hierarchy, the historical disasters Cassius cites and the identity of a diviner remain **ASR-provisional**. The Academy's own later decisions about Marek's trial remain **future proceedings** at the first-arc cutoff.[^status]
