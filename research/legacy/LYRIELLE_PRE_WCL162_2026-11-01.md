# Lyrielle — elven doctor (provisional name)

**Portrayed by:** Kristen. **Continuity:** The Wastes, Season 1, Arc Five. **First played appearance:** [E114](../../episodes/E114.md), ~00:24–00:27. **Name status:** ASR variants **Lyrielle / Liriel / Lirael**; the paged surname is garbled as **Korlani/Quarlani/Koralni (?)**. No creator-written spelling or spoken wording was verified. **Spoilers:** Through E128; **ASR-provisional, no audio listening**.

Lyrielle is an **elf doctor aboard the Elhandari rescue spacecraft** carrying the survivors from Tietkalla. Kristen describes golden hair, pale skin, green eyes, an off-white peasant-style dress, a small waist satchel, a white mink companion with red eyes, and a crystal rose necklace. **She is a newly introduced character**, not Squirts resurrected or Hope returning to this party.

## S3E019 Round Up — princess or well-read imaginative elf remains deliberately undecided

At the [out-of-game final cast Q&A](../../episodes/S3E019.md) **~02:06–02:08**, **Jon** asks if KRISTEN'S LIRIEL/LYRIELLE character was a **REAL PRINCESS** or merely imagined an adventurous princess history after staying home reading stories. **BEAU STATES HE FIRST BUILT HER AS A GENUINE PRINCESS**, then liked Jon's alternate interpretation so much **HE NEVER SETTLED ON WHICH STORY WAS TRUE**. **This is creator-retrospective confirmation that either interpretation is possible and the truth was left UNRESOLVED**, not evidence that she secretly is royal or definitively invented her entire biography. Her E115 seafoam/mermaid memories remain **the character's account**, and neither identity is a new post-campaign scene. Name spelling **Lyrielle/Liriel/Lirael still provisional**. ## E128 — survives station and departs with household

**Lyrielle survives the Elhandar impacts with Angelashi and the children inside Villa Or'Nath**. In [E128](../../episodes/E128.md), after the mercenary defeat, she helps **Angelashi and Nash's four infants board Elhandari transports**, alongside other surviving Harbor tieflings and Bok Bok. **The ships actually take off bound for the Mars terraforming station**; Lyrielle does **not remain with the four PCs on Earth**. No medical treatment, use of additional spells or arrival at Mars is directly demonstrated in this session. The confusing spoken “Squirts” alongside Bok Bok remains an unresolved roster/name issue and **does not identify Lyrielle as Squirts**. [E128 report](../../reports/E128_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md). **ASR-provisional**.

## E125 — family evacuation and protective spell

When the mercenary fleet **attacks Elhandar**, Lyrielle remains with **Angelashi and the four children** outside the High Elf's audience. She escapes with the family and the four main companions through the **freefalling spire elevator**, contributes a ***Mage Hand*** to steady **Vorel** as he cuts through the falling car roof, and survives the descent to the Rainbow Road. This is a **successful escape from the elevator**, not the end of the danger to the entire station.

She then **actually pilots one of three noble speeders**, carrying **Nash, Angelashi and the four children**, while Stanley and Hope pilot the other two with Bok Bok, Chunks and Vorel. When enormous biodome wreckage falls, **Lyrielle casts *Wall of Force*** as a protective canopy, allowing all three vehicles to reach **Villa Or'Nath**. This is an **actually performed spell**, not Stanley's considered but **unused** *Resilient Sphere* contingency. The household enters Stanley's father's hidden room and **buckles up before an impact**; their **post-impact state is not shown**. [E125](../../episodes/E125.md) · [ASR comparison](../../reports/E125_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-24.md).

## E124 — guide to the core and reception caretaker

**Lyrielle agrees to accompany the packed household**, hearing a limited confidential warning about danger, **not every Diontalus detail**. She guides the four, Angelashi, children and accompanying small companions through **Rainbow Road to the central core**, plays **Prestidigitation harp music** to soothe Vorel in the lift and cleans the resulting vomit with Stanley. **She remains in reception with the household while only the four enter the High Elf's chamber**. The roster repeatedly calls one small companion “Squirts”; this does not make Lyrielle a resurrected Squirts or resolve the Chunks/name conflict. ## E115 — cottage guide, Acorn and remembered seafoam

At [E115](../../episodes/E115.md), Lyrielle accompanies the family to **Elhandar**, then serves as **the assigned guide/host at a lakeside biodome cottage**. She helps the parents reconcile, cooks, uses **Mage Hand / Mending / Prestidigitation**, lights Squirts’s memorial fire with **Fire Bolt**, and cares for the household through **four recovery days**. The station offers therapist assignment but **attendance is voluntary**; Lyrielle is not shown conducting a formal therapy session. Her longer name is recognized approximately as **“Lirael Quirlia Har onihillu” / “Lyriel Quirlya Harhani Kenu” (~01:04)**; exact orthography remains open.

**The white mink familiar is named Acorn (~01:18)**. Beau acknowledges that he mistook some attempted familiar noises for Chunks during E114. This is a **bounded attribution correction**, not authority to relabel every earlier child sound. At day-five breakfast **Lyrielle recounts being a mermaid, bargaining with a sea witch, becoming seafoam, and returning through children’s prayers**. These are **her autobiographical claims**, not played scenes, independently verified history or a general resurrection rule. She confirms an **existing permanent Teleportation Circle in the science/magic district**, with no sigils or transit supplied. [ASR report](../../reports/E115_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-23.md).

## The four deliveries and the breached ship

At **~00:25–00:35**, she brings **Nash and Angelashi** to the medical bay, directs attendants, and reviews an unusual ultrasound: a baby with forms resembling **a fork, a boot and a smoking pipe**. A suggested genetic/pathology scan is **not shown being performed**. An attendant later provides a magical view inside the womb; **Lyrielle spends a slot on Animate Objects (~00:43–00:44), but the GM says it fails because the forms are not objects**. This does not establish a successful animation or extraction.

When a pirate attack opens the hull, she **casts Wall of Force over a breach (~00:50–00:52)**, allowing the medical group to regain their footing. She later has an attendant **clear enough of Bok Bok's canister barricade to close the medical blast doors (~01:16–01:17)**. At the ship's eventual disengagement, other elves seal the hull with foam; the continued journey does not depend on assuming her spell remains permanent.

She assists the deliveries of **fork (~01:47), pipe (~02:00), boot (~02:23), and baby-shaped boy (~02:35)**, preventing Nash from pulling the boot prematurely. Nash's improvised poems are **Performance attempts to comfort Angelashi**, not Lyrielle casting through him. At **~02:37–02:39**, the apparent objects change into babies, revealing **four living changeling children, two girls and two boys**, and Angelashi drops her elven form. The complete individual sex/form mapping and children's names are not supplied.
