# Dr. Spider

**S3E02 — survived, saved Union, departed on Blast Belly Rock:** In [played S3E02](../../episodes/S3E02.md), Doctor Spider survives his death saves and is held in Fate Six's guarded magical brig alongside Steerious Business. He agrees to help with emergency treatment for Union Wheel, and **ACTUALLY PERFORMS A SUCCESSFUL MEDICAL OPERATION** alongside Stanley and Dr. Daffodil, using his own equipment and spectral nurse Wilkins. Union survives. In the brig he joins the remains of deceased Squirts and Blood Skunk to Steerious's shoulders, creating a three-headed figure; **those two additional heads are inert, not resurrected**. Spider and Steerious are **ACTUALLY RELEASED at abandoned Blast Belly Rock**. Hope also **hands Spider her former Hell-issued appearance necklace and summoning ring** to return toward Brett; these are distinct from Pentel Weiss collector ring. His proposal to create a future practice in the settlement is only a plan. [S3E02 comparison](../../reports/WASTES_S3E02_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-26.md).

**Historical S3E01 cutoff:** This infernal arachnoid doctor **actually accompanies revived Squirts, Steerious and Blood Skunk aboard Fate Six** in Brett's attack party. His immense ***Sickening Radiance*** is mis-measured initially then **enlarged to correct 30-ft radius**, unintentionally hurting Hell allies including Squirts; Stanley ultimately **dispels it**. He **casts illusory *Mental Prison* on adult-black-dragon Nash**, briefly restraining him until Nash breaks free with great psychic pain. Spider also **actually uses *Healing Light* to revive fallen Squirts and Steerious**, and creates an aggressive ***Wall of Fire* on the mast that burns comrades as well as foes**. He announces a planned ***Dream of the Blue Veil*** but **is ACTUALLY DOWNED by Nash's 51-point dragon acid breath before completed planar travel is established**. **He remains in death-save state by S3E01 cutoff**—not confirmed permanently killed. ## E127 Christmas one-off — experiment, but not an Arc Five clock advance

In the special **[E127 — Spider Claws](../../episodes/E127.md)**, with **Kyle as guest DM and Davanos/Riley/Darnell as new player characters**, a laboratory loudspeaker declares a **“Dr. Spider Christmas”** before a presumed **Spinner's Council**. Three abducted Wastes residents are tested on dessert choice, tree-decoration accuracy, festive snow activity, mutated reindeer and a Santa-costumed ogre. The promised gift is in fact a coal-triggered floor trap above a crusher; the subjects **escape through maintenance vents**. They encounter experimental captive specimens and an armed **Professor Spider**, who objects to the Nice-list trick, fires a minigun and **is killed** after spell restraint and several longbow hits. A second voice previously reprimanded the professor by title. **This is not proof that Dr. Spider, the named experiment's organizer, is the dead professor**; the doctor's physical presence and whereabouts are not shown. The speaker's title also does not independently date this experiment relative to the Hell Squad's existing story. All three subjects later **use an Earth return code and survive**, but **the main quartet does not appear or advance beyond E126 in this special**.
