# Basilisk of the under-Pit caves

## Introduction

An eight-legged reptile with a petrifying gaze occupied a side chamber in the caves beneath [Tietkalla's](../places/tietkalla.md) Pit of Despair. Identified as a basilisk, it threatened anyone who met its eyes, forcing the adventurers to fight while averting their gaze.[^nature]

## Arc Two — dangerous eyes and useful remains

Vorel killed the basilisk by severing its head, then fashioned a javelin from its bone. The body remained in the caves, where its organs later became valuable for a very different purpose.[^death]

After [Kefeli](../characters/kefeli.md) turned Hope and Vorel to stone, Nash and Stanley sought a way to restore them. Nash located the old basilisk carcass through a clairvoyant draught, and the pair returned to collect material from it. They combined the basilisk ingredients with preserved tiefling eyeballs and liquid in two preparations. Applied to their petrified companions, the remedies returned both to living flesh.[^remedies]

## Current status

The basilisk is dead, and the two preparations made from its remains have been consumed. Hope and Vorel survived their petrification.[^status]
