# Malhya / Malhaya — the Fishbird

**Continuity:** The Wastes. **Working name:** Malhya, with Malhaya/Malhaiya/Malaya among ASR variants; exact orthography unverified. **Evidence:** Religious testimony in earlier dossiers and **directly experienced manifestation in E124**; ASR-provisional, no listening. **Spoilers:** Through corrected E124. [Emenil](emenil.md) · [Stanley](stanley-billings.md) · [Dossier](../../episodes/E124.md).

## Faith and the lake rite

Emenil serves the **Fishbird/Tidebearer faith**, invoking a savior god of water. E117's shrine destruction is an emotional act, **not proof of the being's death or a permanently renounced faith**. In E124 she says her late husband **Lelu/Leilu/Lelou/Lilu (?)**, a reverend, died under vampire persecution and left **fish flakes** for an emergency appeal. **That history is her account**; she admits she has never performed the rite before. [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) · [E123](../../episodes/E123.md).

All four companions and Emenil swallow flakes, **breathe beneath the Harbor lake**, and experience a shared transition to an apparently endless-water setting. The preceding **kraken departs without combat**; it is not independently established as Malhya's enemy, slain servant or second form. A **whale-sized, rainbow-scaled fish with birdlike wings** arrives and answers to Malhya, forming a **solid-water disc, explicitly not ice**, where the five stand. **The experience and actions are played; precise plane, godhood and cosmic authority remain attributed**.

## Demonstrated encounter outcomes

At ~00:50–00:55, the visitor **makes Stanley and Nash disappear, then restores them** after Vorel's intercession and mention of Emenil's faith. Both report isolated blackness with no time sense; **no death/resurrection is adjudicated**, and neither heard the intervening discussion.

It **takes a blue fish-scaled copy of Vorel's form**, cannot reproduce his chest Eye and touches **the Eye of Groxl with permission**. It recognizes the being, calls it feared among gods and asks whether Vorel has come to kill it. The following **“one he slew ... he ... unmaker”** warning has an **unresolved antecedent**; no certain named victim or universal divine history should be invented. [Eye of Groxl](../technology/eye-of-groxl.md).

**~01:02–01:04:** Malhya draws blue strands from **Vorel, then Stanley**, and the GM explicitly **cures their Diontalus debuffs**. This resolves the persistent E123 ability loss, **without supplying a new complete stat block**. It then swims away; the five share a transition back to the Harbor lake. **Emenil remains underwater but answers Stanley alive**. No new romantic outcome is established.

## Claims and unfinished engineering

Malhya says **dragon blight/heat and the old lords of creation's claim** prevent the gods from restoring Earth's waters. It connects the solution to the dying dragon's **device of impossible geometry** and suggests an **unnamed engineer**, allegedly hidden from the gods somewhere in the Prime Material, last near **`faerun` / `Favron` (?)**. It directs Stanley to the Elhandari leader/royal court. **This is a lead, not a verified Faerûn location, named engineer, supplied key/blueprint or completed Ruby**. [Dragon-heart device](../technology/nash-dragon-heart.md).

Statements about faith sustaining gods and mortals exceeding their nature are **the visitor's theology**. The episode restores the companions' abilities, **not Earth's climate**, and does not independently verify every claim made during the encounter.
