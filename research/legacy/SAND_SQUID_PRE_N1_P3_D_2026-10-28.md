# Sand squid

## Introduction

Sand squid are tentacled, bulbous-headed animals that travel beneath the desert sands. Their fatty meat provides inexpensive food, while a pale, creamy juice squeezed from their bodies can be added to coffee. They sustain travelers and townspeople alike, appearing as fresh meals, preserved rations and kitchen ingredients.[^food]

## Ecology and hunting

Sand squid travel in schools through sand and dirt, returning to feeding places where they eat insects. Hunters can recognize their tracks, wait at these feeding grounds and snatch the animals from the earth as they approach. The living animals have two eyes and tentacles extending from the front of the body.[^hunt]

Outside Slavetown, Vorel caught a sand squid by hand and killed it against a stone before carrying it back to Orp. At the same time, Stanley found sacks of dried squid in Lattlebing's hotel kitchen. Wild catches and preserved stores offered two ways to obtain the same familiar food.[^stores]

## Food and everyday life

In Slavetown, sand squid could be served in sandwiches, fried strips, eggs and soup. Its meat was considered cheap and of modest quality, although Vorel relished it. Lattlebing also squeezed juice from a dead squid directly into coffee, making a much richer cup for Vorel than for his companions.[^kitchen]

Drying reduced the animals' bulk and made them useful as travel rations. Canned squid appeared among general-store supplies, and fried portions fed people gathered at a camp. Sand-squid sandwiches later remained on an inn's menu in Tietkalla.[^provisions]

## Current status

Sand squid remain a familiar source of meat, preserved food and coffee cream. Far from home, Vorel asks for their juice in his coffee; its appearance also provides a familiar comparison when the companions encounter another pale drink in Elhandar.[^later]
