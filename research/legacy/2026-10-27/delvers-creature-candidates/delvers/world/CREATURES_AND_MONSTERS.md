# Creatures and monsters

The woodland, buried ruins and distant mountain keeps around the guild's city shelter creatures dangerous enough to draw the Delvers' attention.[^scope]

| Creature | Nature and place in the world |
| --- | --- |
| Ancient mummified elves of the triangle-gate catacombs | **Several fight the Delvers in [S3E06](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E06.md)** after rune-tombs spiral open; bodies ancient, surgically hollowed, **Krandal Turns Undead but does not instantly destroy them**. Multiple killed and party retreats; exact total/magic origins require recording/map check |
| Behi(r) of Dungeon C4 | Long many-legged lightning-breathing cavern predator **killed by Krandal's Spiritual Weapon in [S3E04](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E04.md)**. Its attack kills no observed Delver; earlier victim testimony says it squeezes prey and is lightning-immune. Unrelated unidentified sticky ceiling ropes/pale crab-like beings are **not proven part of behir**. |
| [Hook horrors](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/hook-horrors.md) | Beaked, hook-armed hunters of the deep tunnels. Wild adults retain their separate fates, while Krandal's hatchling Scroungy becomes a tame farm beast of burden.[^hook-later] |
| [Skeletal bat of Dawnless Dunes](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/skeletal-bat-balcony.md) | A giant bat skeleton enclosed by shadowy skin and fitted with a harness. Evelyn draws it away from the pyramid and destroys it in the dunes.[^bat-end] |
| [Gricks](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/gricks.md) | Scaled, grasping predators of the forest ruins and underground chambers, sometimes hidden among stone. The great wind-chamber specimen is killed, but others escape.[^grick-later] |
| [Noble Hollow roper](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/noble-hollow-roper.md) | Stone-skinned cavern predator that bears piercers. Evelyn kills it, while some of its brood escape.[^roper-form] |
| [Skull Keep's flameskull](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/flameskull.md) | Green-burning undead sentinel linked, by Enzo's account, to a failed immortality rite. Koh brings it down and Evelyn consecrates its remains.[^skull-form] |

Reasoning collectives and communities are routed through [Characters](../../../../../../delvers/world/CHARACTERS.md#reasoning-collectives-and-communities): [All Rat](../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/all-rat.md) has a character account, while the [Minotaur Mesa council](../../../../../../delvers/world/places/minotaur-mesa-bone-crypt.md) retains its community history and [Chad Brad](../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/chad-brad.md) his biography.[^routing]

[^scope]: This category primarily covers non-intelligent creatures and animal-like or monstrous behavior, rather than talking, reasoning NPCs. [Veggie Sticks](../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/veggie-sticks.md) and the walking plants remain with the character material; Studwell's apparition remains documented in the [crypt dossier](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E02.md). The grick article draws on S1E01, genuine S1E03 and S1E04 dossiers; [S1E09](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E09.md) provides a separate grick's attempt to eat a piercer and the observed hook horrors, without implying a proven life-cycle or kinship. [S1E12](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E12.md) **shows a roper producing piercers** in one collapsed cavern, confirming a local relationship but not universal reproductive biology, and distinguishes the roper from the hypothetical summoning target. [S1E06](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E06.md) provides the flameskull research, observed final banishment and Enzo's separately attributed ritual testimony; no audio-verified episode or audio research, species-wide intelligence ruling or D&D baseline comparison is claimed. Delvers continuity is independent of The Wastes and Inverse. [Category policy](../../../../../../project/WORLD_CREATURES_PLAN.md).

[^routing]: Category routing follows the [shared policy](../../../../../../project/WORLD_CREATURES_PLAN.md), not an invented intelligence score. [S3E08](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E08.md) gives All Rat a shared voice and a bargain; [S3E10](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E10.md) gives the minotaur council deliberation and voluntary alliance. [S3E12](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E12.md) observes all ten allies become inert bones after Khalazar's defeat, not restored flesh. Older aboleth/leader predictions remain distinct from that observed ending.
[^hook-later]: [S3E06](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E06.md) hatches the youngling; [S3E08](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E08.md) shows its ribbon, feeding and calls, with one of three wild adults killed and two retreating. It is not yet proved trained then. [S3E12](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E12.md), aftermath and one-year epilogue, later describes Scroungy as a tame farm beast. This is an individual development, not universal taming or a proved family relationship.
[^bat-end]: [S3E11](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E11.md) ends during the diversion; [S3E12](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E12.md), ~00:00–00:19, actually resolves the fight with Evelyn's solo destruction of the bat. The scream banishes her elk rather than killing it permanently. Harness, clicks and green gems do not identify a rider or establish exact senses.

[^grick-later]: [S3E09](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E09.md), ~02:00–02:46, gives concealment, the large grick's death and at least one escape; Koh's dominance and ninety-percent remarks are not ecological facts. [S3E12](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E12.md) shows butchered carcasses and later monster decline, not a population census or extinction. The subject page retains the separate early four-kill scouting report.

[^roper-form]: [S1E12](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E12.md), ~02:00–02:50, shows this individual's piercer production and death. It is not established as the intended summoned lake beast. Later ropers are separate encounters, and the later Hollow route/fertility does not establish brood eradication.
[^skull-form]: [S1E06](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E06.md), ~02:14–03:03: Enzo's divided-mind explanation is testimony; the skull's destruction and consecration are observed. His quiet reflection remains intact with uncertain awareness. [Enzo's biography](../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/enzo-vanderbuck-withers.md) retains reasoning agency and personal history; no species-wide intelligence or regeneration rules are inferred.
