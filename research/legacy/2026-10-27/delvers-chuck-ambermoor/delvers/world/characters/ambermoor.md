# Ambermoor — Gemholm operations manager and experimenter

**Working name:** **Ambermoor** (S2E07 ASR also gives `Ambermore`); canonical spelling unverified pending audio and the GM's written diary/map handouts. This is a **Delvers-only** character, not connected to Wastes. His first directly played on-screen appearance is in [Delvers S2E07](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S2E07.md). Earlier S2E06 survivors spoke of him without locating him.

## The prosperous man on the sandbar

Ambermoor administers a small gem-dredging colony on the exposed sandbars of **Lake Absul**, through an organization the workers call the **Drizzle corporation** (phonetic). Unlike their bare, windblown cottages, his own quarters are thickly carpeted and furnished in comfort. When the Delvers arrive, the settlement is in ruin: workers are missing, dead, or transformed into slaadi, and frightened survivors have burned their own boat to try to keep the threat from the mainland. The manager himself is missing from the island.[^earlier]

## The private diaries

The Delvers discover journals left by crab mechanic Hank Tom and gem worker Caleb. The mechanic writes of a red sliver from an unusual newly discovered gemstone embedded in his palm, followed by a drink from Ambermoor and strange symptoms over three days. Caleb found **three fragile gems in an underwater cave**, then drank a blue beverage Ambermoor gave him and experienced chest pain. These entries corroborate the existence of suspect drinks, but the GM's original visual journal sheets have **not yet been inspected**. Neither entry proves a precise microbiological mechanism alone.[^diaries]

Koh then uncovers Ambermoor's own diary beneath a pillow. Its author boasts of **chaos gems** containing something he can distill into a transmissible experimental substance called in the ASRs **chaos phage**. He expects military buyers, even speculating about a kingdom or vampire nation, and claims to have sped up transformations that normally take much longer. The journal alleges Caleb and Hank Tom transmitted the new substance, that Destin became a **green slaad**, and that **science officer Neko** would serve as material for a perfected being. These are **incriminating statements attributed to Ambermoor**, rather than independent proof of a completed sale, Destin's actual appearance or all claimed experiments.[^diary]

## Beneath the lake

An underwater map hidden in his desk leads the Delvers to a worked cave where **crab dredger #1** stands outside a metal door protected by a magical alarm. The party discovers a concealed flooded escape route. Koh swims through it, unseen, and observes Ambermoor in the huge laboratory, **an elf wearing dark reflective goggles and a swimsuit**, withdrawing a syringe of **black fluid** from a sedated **black, thorn-covered slaad** in a glass tank. **Red, blue and green prepared syringes** lie nearby; Ambermoor stores the fresh black sample in a case and exults in the value of his creation. The trapped creature wears a name label reading **Neko**, giving direct support to the diary's chosen subject but not to every other experiment.[^lab]

Evelyn opens the main door and triggers the alarm. Koh attacks from concealment, misses with the first psychic strike and lands a critical second blow to the head. **Ambermoor falls unconscious but is explicitly declared NONLETHALLY defeated**. The Delvers strip his **spell-component pouch and crab #1 key**, bind and gag him in Koh's **Sultan's Sash** and secure the four-color reagent samples in the briefcase, which Koh places inside a **Bag of Holding**. His unfinished villainous speech is table humor, not an unplayed action or admission of further results.[^capture]

## Later S2E08 continuity bridge (not an in-scene handoff)

S2E07 ends with **Ambermoor alive, bound/gagged, at Krandal's farm**. At the **opening of S2E08**, the GM retrospectively says the Delvers **delivered him to the guild holding cells** and that a **Zone of Truth** inquiry is arranged there, before the party was dispatched for the red dragon mission. **Delivery is established offscreen**, not a newly played interrogation, trial, confession, reward or vials/evidence handoff. The reagent briefcase's precise recipient remains unspecified. [S2E08](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S2E08.md), ~00:00–00:10.

## Earlier S2E07 cutoff (retained historically)

**Ambermoor is alive, unconscious or incapacitated, bound and gagged, in the Delvers' custody**. After leaving the laboratory and Gemholm, the group **actually disembarks from Gil's ferry at Krandal's farm with the captive** to hide from Atomer authorities investigating the Withers heist. **He has not yet been delivered to Cassius, examined, interrogated, tried, released, killed or paid for at the source cutoff.** His reagent briefcase stays with **Koh**; **two large slaadi from the shore remain unaccounted for**, leaving the safety of Gemholm uncertain. The original chaos gems were **pulverized and dissolved into experimental substances**, per the GM at the close, so no intact original stones are found. **Hank Tom, Caleb, Destin, Neko**, his corporate authority, and his proposed customers require written/source review.[^end]
