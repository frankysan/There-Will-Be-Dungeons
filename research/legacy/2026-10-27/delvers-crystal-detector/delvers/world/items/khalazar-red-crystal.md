# Khalazar red crystal fragment — dream-recovered shard

**New S3E02 development:** [Carrie Underworld](../../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/carrie-underworld.md) **explicitly says Evelyn handed her a red dream shard**, and she has **actually embedded the shard with Anathala's blood inside [Koh's newly made sensory amulet](../../../../../../../delvers/world/items/khalazar-sensory-amulet.md)**, which is physically given to Koh and later gains a directional indicator. This supports the physical transfer at S2E10 end **but still DOES NOT explain the apparent S2E11 recurrence of Evelyn holding a red gemstone**. Number of similar fragments/custody between episodes unknown; **do not erase the original discrepancy**. Carrie's proposed Dolbluth lineage is only an unverified hypothesis. **Origin/status through S2E11:** Red shard from a **crystal inside Khalazar's second, shrouded sand-form IN A DREAM** in [S2E10](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S2E10.md), ~02:09–02:19. **Carrier now explicitly DISPUTED:** Evelyn physically hands shard to Carrie at end of S2E10, but Evelyn physically produces crushed red gem again at immediate beginning S2E11 without a depicted return or distinct second shard. **Do not assign a resolved S2E11 final owner**. Red color is directly stated; **not a proven red gemstone from the Gemholm chaos-phage case** and **not confirmed a phylactery, soul gem, power core, blood ruby, alchemical compound or any established device**.

During a psychic battle in Anthela's bridal mindscape, **Evelyn Grinkeeper lands a natural-20 warhammer hit with Branding Smite**; something hard and glasslike **cracks in Khalazar's sand-filled chest**, and **red crystal pieces fall out**. He dissolves into an immense wave of black sand. **Evelyn explicitly stoops to PICK UP one RED SHARD before running to the escape light**, while Koh restrains the sand wave via psychic force. At exit, everyone returns to the guild lab; **Evelyn physically holds the fragment**, then **ACTUALLY hands it to Carrie**, who had **not traveled through Koh's link**. Carrie receives it but **does not analyze, name or explain it** before episode ends with **Anthela physically waking and embracing Koh**.[^source]

No verified size, shape beyond small fragment, material, magical aura, number of shards recovered, mechanism for transporting dream objects or permanent effect on Khalazar. **Avoid visually inventing engravings, glowing runes or metal setting in illustrations before more evidence**. Do not conflate with the **three chaos gems from Gemholm** previously pulverized by Ambermoor; source context and carrier are different.
