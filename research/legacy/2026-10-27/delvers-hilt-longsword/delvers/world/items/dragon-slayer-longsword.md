# Dragon Slayer's Longsword — Radiant Lance forge

**First obtained:** Played [Delvers S2E08 — Golden Potato Sword](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S2E08.md), ~02:03–02:17. **Current holder: Evelyn Grinkeeper.** The episode's **“Golden Potato Sword” is a joke/title**, *not* an item actually made with potatoes; the **half-eaten potato added by Krandal burned away**. Do not add potato peels, carved potato pommel or any tuber motif as canonical visual detail without new source evidence. **Exact written item sheet and owner/Discord drawing unverified**; `Dragon Slayer's Longsword` is the GM's on-air identification, not a guaranteed permanent formal equipment-sheet spelling.

## Creation and appearance

In a hidden **obsidian-and-clockwork forge at Chath/Chaff Peak**, an ancient inscription signed **Radiant Lance** invites dragon-slayers to demonstrate **freedom from greed**. Evelyn places **all approximately 250 gp of gold available on her person** into the accepting trough, and Krandal offers half his raw potato. When Evelyn touches a hand-shaped relief, bright gold light runs across the old Elvish text. The trough feeds the gold through channels visible beneath the transparent floor, and the machine **actually forges a golden longsword**. The potato goes into the open hot foundry **and is instantly destroyed**. The sword is one-handed with possibility of shield use discussed. Precise blade proportions, guard, runes, sheen and scabbard **are not described well enough for a definitive illustration**.[^forge]

Koh's later offering of **1,000 ball bearings** produces a worthless fused lump rather than another enchanted blade. Krandal's candles produce melted wax which may gum up clockwork. These are **distinct failed attempts**, not alternate versions of Evelyn's sword. Marsh does not receive this sword; he holds **Skoogie's Arrow of Dragon Slaying** instead.[^tries]

## Demonstrated properties and chronology

In the **mountaintop red-dragon lair**, the dragon immediately recognizes the weapon as **the blade of the dragon slayer**, and mocks Evelyn for carrying it. Evelyn later makes an **opportunity attack against the dragon**; first roll misses but **spends inspiration to reroll to natural 20**, with a second confirmation roll not also critical. GM resolves extra damage against a dragon—approximately **37 damage total** stated—with characteristic holy/golden glimmer. The sword's **exact magic bonus and written wording are not independently checked**; do not import abilities beyond those actually adjudicated. **Dragon is still alive at the S2E08 cutoff**, so this is a real successful hit, *not* the killing blow.[^fight]

**Current status:** Evelyn carries golden longsword in ongoing dragon fight. No theft, transfer, sale, breakage, stowing or subsequent change played before the end. The **forge's full rules and three lair-control buttons** are indexed at [Chath Peak](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/chath-peak.md).

**Evelyn retains the golden Dragon Slayer's Longsword**, which is described in S2E09's GM recap as **wounding Fredoroth during the omitted remainder of battle**. **Marsh's Arrow of Dragon Slaying is identified as the final attack**, not the longsword. **Evelyn later receives 250 gp at guild as quest reward**, but her share of the dragon hoard is **not quantified in narration**. No evidence she gives away the sword in this episode. The item's current visual design still has **no potato skin**; the title remains comic.
