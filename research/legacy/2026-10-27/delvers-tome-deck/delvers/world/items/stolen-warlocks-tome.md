# Warlock's tome from Cassius's former office — stolen by Dumbledwark

**Current status S3E03 (ASR-provisional):** **Dumbledwark of Raisinbunch physically holds this magical tome in his duffel**, after **actually stealing** it from a box in the room which had been **Cassius's office, now occupied by acting guildmaster Zoltar the Wizened**. **Unidentified owner, creator, exact title, writings, spell effects, patron affiliation and relationship to Khalazar.** Do not treat as proof Cassius was a warlock or that Zoltar owned it.

At ~01:57–02:00, while Zoltar leaves to retrieve refreshments, Dumbledwark **casts Detect Magic**, senses magic within a lower crate/box underneath another, gently lifts the top container and recognizes **a “warlock's tome”** from earlier adventuring familiarity; **no Identify ritual completed**. He initially closes the container and sits down.

At ~02:18:15–02:19:25, with Zoltar absent a second time obtaining marmalade, Dumbledwark **actually puts on Gloves of Thievery**, uses a **corrected Sleight of Hand total of 20**, **takes the book** and **deposits it in his duffel underneath his animated camera**. He removes his gloves before Zoltar returns. **This is completed theft and physical custody, not an idea, proposal or handoff from the guild.** He hypothesizes the nearby camera might obscure a magical search but **no magical detection test is played**.
