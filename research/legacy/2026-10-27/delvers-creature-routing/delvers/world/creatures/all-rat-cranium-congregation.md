# The All Rat — cranium-rat congregation

**Played adversary in [Delvers S3E08 — I Smell a Rat Ball](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E08.md)**. An enormous **hovering roughly 10×10-foot mass of linked rats, their exposed brains flashing in alternating colours as a collective voice**; many more rats stretch out as **linked chains, each biting the tail of the next, with terminal rats biting unconscious Delvers' necks**. It calls itself **“All Rat”** (some ASR forms “Owl Rat”) and **a cranium-rat congregation**. This name is its own self-presentation, not a verified natural-history taxonomy or imported D&D stat block.

It **physically traps Koh/Krandal and an apparently older knight** in projected fears: **Krandal's workers' revolt/farm foreclosure, Koh's wanted posters/Anthela accusation, Evelyn's asylum, city enslaved under flying pyramid** are NOT real events. Evelyn **resists**, severs rats controlling her party, then challenges main collective. All Rat **CLAIMS organ inside its golden hook-horror-topped urn is “part of Khalazar” and provides collective power**, offering to give urn without contents. Claim has **not** been scientifically or magically verified; party **does not open and examine organ**. It is physically located in Black Basilica beyond older triangle-gate left stair.

**Actual combat:** Group attacks with lightning, Branding Smite, psychic dagger, force spectral weapon and buffs/healing; creature retaliates with **rat-linked chain lashes causing psychic/bludgeoning injury, illusions of Skull Keep and Hopper wedding, and huge psychic bursts**. Koh **ACTUALLY grapples urn out of its body and psychic-teleports away**, whereupon **brains stop flashing and collective instantly disperses into live ordinary rats**. **No proof every rat died or that all rat conspiracies in world ended**. Scroungy and nearby adult hook horrors then eat some fleeing rats, but no wider extinction proven.

**Status:** Collective as magical/organized creature **dissolved at S3E08 cutoff**, urn kept whole, companions conscious/alive. Individual rats flee. Don't confuse with S3E05 black-sand filter ooze or Black Basilica's mummified elf attackers.
