# Creatures and monsters

The woodland, buried ruins and distant mountain keeps around the guild's city shelter creatures dangerous enough to draw the Delvers' attention.[^scope]

| Creature | Nature and place in the world |
| --- | --- |
| Ancient mummified elves of the triangle-gate catacombs | **Several fight the Delvers in [S3E06](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E06.md)** after rune-tombs spiral open; bodies ancient, surgically hollowed, **Krandal Turns Undead but does not instantly destroy them**. Multiple killed and party retreats; exact total/magic origins require recording/map check |
| Behi(r) of Dungeon C4 | Long many-legged lightning-breathing cavern predator **killed by Krandal's Spiritual Weapon in [S3E04](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E04.md)**. Its attack kills no observed Delver; earlier victim testimony says it squeezes prey and is lightning-immune. Unrelated unidentified sticky ceiling ropes/pale crab-like beings are **not proven part of behir**. |
| [All Rat cranium-rat congregation](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/all-rat-cranium-congregation.md) | **S3E08:** exposed-brained rat gestalt generates **illusions** of Krandal farm takeover/Koh bounty/Evelyn asylum, entraps heroes via linked rat neck chains and attacks with psychic pulses. **Koh actually removes the hook-horror-capped URN** and swarm breaks into live rats; **no real surface disasters or species-wide eradication** |
| [Hook horrors](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/hook-horrors.md) | Huge hook-armed, beaked hunters in deep tunnels; one killed by Koh, another observed alive. **S3E06 hatching imprints on Krandal, S3E07 juvenile eats rations in bag; S3E08 same fledgling provisionally called Scroungy gets a tracking pink ribbon, eats rats, and causes/simultaneously responds to nearby adult calls. THREE wild adults arrive, Koh ACTUALLY kills dominant/aggressive one by crit, TWO retreat alive. Hatchling returns to bag, NOT surrendered or trained/flying**. Universal senses/kinship remain uncertain. Their full ecology/senses remain uncertain. |
| Skeletal Minotaur Mesa allies | **S3E10 ACTUAL crypt audience**: 9 giant (~13ft) cursed skeletal minotaurs at old cavern below farm/Noble Hollow; Krandal's Chad Brad skull rejoined exhumed skeleton via `Create Undead`-like 150gp ritual, **10 TOTAL including Chad Brad**, **ACTUALLY DESCEND to Dawnless Dunes in S3E11**, help evacuate unconscious elves via FIRST teleport in S3E12, Chad Brad/Corgan shield party from collapsing pyramid and **ALL TEN SKELETONS CRUMBLE TO INERT BONES after Khalazar's defeat**. Curse-ending means final death per aboleth/leader claims, **not a tested Khalazar kill**. [Reference](../../../../../../delvers/world/places/minotaur-mesa-bone-crypt.md) |
| [Skeletal bat of Dawnless Dunes](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/skeletal-bat-balcony.md) | **S3E11 ~15ft giant flying BAT SKELETON with translucent SHADOW skin, green gem collar and saddle-like rigging**, clicks at black pyramid balcony; **Evelyn ACTUALLY draws it away using elk**, combat/death NOT played. Identity and echolocation mechanism unknown |
| [Gricks](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/gricks.md) | Scaled predators with grasping limbs and beaks, encountered around forest sinkhole and trapped dungeon. **S3E09:** some disguise themselves as rocks in wind/grick complex; **Koh actually crit-kills a very large alpha and smaller gricks**, Krandal/Barclay kill others, **at least one ESCAPES**. No proof entire population removed or Koh became literal pack leader. |
| [Noble Hollow roper](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/noble-hollow-roper.md) | Great stone-skinned creature beneath petrified hollow; **produces piercers**, killed by Evelyn. **Not proven summoned lake beast**. |
| [Skull Keep's flameskull](../../../../../../delvers/world/creatures/flameskull.md) | Green-burning undead sentinel linked, according to Enzo, to a botched immortality rite; destroyed by Koh and banished by Evelyn's holy water |
