# The Black Basilica — cranium-rat prison and urn chamber

## Introduction

The Black Basilica is a long, columned hall reached from the left-hand route above the triangle-gate altar. Its apparent abandonment conceals a prison of linked minds. An armoured skeleton lies among its furnishings, while chains of cranium rats connect helpless bodies to a hovering congregation. The place's name comes from a captive voice calling itself Radiant Lance.[^setting]

### Arc Three — A prison broken by removing its prize

The trap hides the hall from its victims behind familiar scenes. The Delvers seem to return to homes, streets and places of refuge, only to face accusation, confinement and betrayal. These landscapes are projections while their bodies remain in the Basilica. A trapped knight's voice reaches Koh and Krandal within the false world, claiming a long imprisonment in the rats' shared consciousness.[^prison]

Evelyn resists the deception and sees the physical chamber. Rats bite the backs of her companions' necks, each joining a chain that leads to the great floating mass. She severs those attachments and frees Koh and Krandal. The congregation names itself All Rat and offers a bargain: it will give up a hook-horror-headed urn if allowed to keep the organ inside, whose power it says sustains the collective.[^collective]

The Delvers refuse that division and fight the congregation amid further false views of the battlefield. Koh eventually seizes the intact urn and teleports away with it. All Rat immediately falls apart into ordinary living rats, their glowing heads dimmed. The hall's captor is undone through the removal of its prize, while the scattered animals survive.[^release]

The armoured skeleton remains as a physical trace of the earlier prisoner. Its mail and sword have rusted, but the tabard and lance are preserved. Krandal takes the tabard as a holy symbol, and the party carries away the lance. After resting in the hall, they encounter adult hook horrors feeding on the loose rats: one is killed and two retreat. The Delvers then leave with the urn, returning toward the older altar.[^departure]

## Current status

The Basilica no longer holds All Rat's unified congregation. Its urn, tabard and lance have been removed, but ordinary rats and surviving hook horrors remain in the surrounding passages. The hall's later inhabitants and condition after Khalazar's defeat are unknown.[^status]
