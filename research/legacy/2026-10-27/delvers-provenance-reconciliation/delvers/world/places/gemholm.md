# Gemholm — Lake Absul gem-dredging sandbar settlement

## Introduction

**New cartographic and handout spellings (2026-10-27):** Kyle-posted [Delvers regional map](../../../../../../../reports/DISCORD_NEW_SPELLING_2026-10-27.md#delvers-visually-verified-regional-map) labels **Lake Obsul** and **Obsul Swamplands**, contrasting with `Lake Absul/Abzul` in the audio-derived Gemholm dossier. The lake name is a **strong mapped spelling lead**, but its exact equivalence to the colony's named lake needs a geographic crosswalk. Three separately recovered [in-world diaries](../../../../../../../reports/DISCORD_NEW_SPELLING_2026-10-27.md#delvers-three-in-world-diary-handouts) consistently use **Ambermore**, strengthening this name without changing the existing `ambermoor.md` legacy slug. No original audio was heard.

Gemholm is a gem-dredging settlement on shifting sandbars in Lake Absul. Stilt houses, workshops and communal buildings stand above the exposed lakebed, with a central warehouse raised on stone supports. Boats connect the isolated colony to Atomer, while mechanical crab dredgers carry workers into the water. When the Delvers arrive, burned structures and a deserted shoreline interrupt the signs of ordinary working life.[^setting]

### Arc Two — A mining colony consumed by experiments

The settlement's buildings serve a small, specialized workforce: a maintenance workshop, gem depository, infirmary, common office, mess and recreation rooms stand among private houses. A six-hour ferry journey brings the Delvers here with Gil and his trained crab Herman Snips, who stays behind to signal for their return. The colony's own merchant boat has been burned. Mack says the residents destroyed it to prevent an infected person carrying their disaster to the mainland.[^arrival]

Mack and the healer Church survive among damaged homes and missing neighbors. Mack connects the trouble to newly dredged gems and describes creatures that implant larvae or transform their victims. The infirmary holds Caleb's body, its chest broken open, and a fleeing larva is killed. In another house, a small red creature identified by Mack as Hank Tom is slain. The colony has become a place where neighbors may be both victims and dangers to one another.[^outbreak]

Rising water makes the mining equipment a means of access and escape. The workshop's numbered crab machines can walk, swim and clear wreckage with their claws. Restoring access to a missing operating key lets the group travel together to an underwater cave marked on a recovered map. The machines connect the settlement to a concealed laboratory beyond its inhabited sandbars.[^machines]

Workers' diaries trace illness after unusual gem exposure and drinks supplied by Ambermoor. His own journal describes distilled phage, accelerated infections and plans to sell transformed creatures. Inside the laboratory, Koh sees him drawing black liquid from a sedated slaad. The writings and working equipment expose an experiment exploiting the colony and its people, even where individual victims' histories remain uncertain.[^laboratory]

The Delvers capture Ambermoor alive and take colored reagent samples. Church's early larva is dissolved by healing magic, while Koh removes an implant from his own leg. The black specimen labelled Neko does not change under the same restorative spell and is killed in its tank. Two larger slaadi remain unaccounted for when the group returns to the village. The three crab machines are left there as the ferry carries away the Delvers, prisoner, evidence, Mack and Church.[^departure]

Ambermoor subsequently reaches the guild's holding cells. Mack and Church establish a new place for themselves in Atomer as Wood League adventurers. The disaster has carried two of the colony's survivors into guild life.[^survivors]

## Current status

Gemholm's last known condition is a damaged settlement with its dredging machines left behind and two large slaadi still missing. Ambermoor is in guild custody and Mack and Church are living in Atomer. The island's later safety, remaining inhabitants and recovery are unknown.[^status]
