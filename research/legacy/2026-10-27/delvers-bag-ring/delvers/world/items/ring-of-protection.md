# Ring of Protection — a relic from the flooded ruins

## Introduction

A narrow band of mysterious stone-metal, inscribed in the old language used on other buried relics, rests for ages in a concealed chamber beneath the eastern lake. The ring seems slight enough to overlook, yet answers a familiar kind of power: the silent concentration with which Koh brings ancient weapons to life.[^finding]

## Discovery and awakening

The Darkest Delvers follow a submerged corridor that echoes the dungeons beneath the forest and Pelor's temple. Behind an algae-covered wall they uncover a heavy stone chest holding a single small ring. Krandal and Evelyn help raise its covering. Evelyn recognizes the letters as ancient Elven but cannot interpret them; Koh asks to hold it and channels psychic energy through its material. It shines along the inscriptions, revealing its nature without spoken explanation: the ring shields a wearer against harm and grants steadiness against threats both physical and magical.[^awakening]

## Offered rather than claimed

Koh finds the ring adapts to different fingers and need not belong permanently to its discoverer. Krandal has set aside his heavy scale mail to swim and feels particularly vulnerable below the water. On hearing this, Koh offers him the relic. It expands over the cleric's left middle finger until it fits his broad hand, leaving his movements unimpeded. It is an act of trust as much as an exchange of equipment.[^gift]

## Current status

**Krandal wears the Ring of Protection**, with **+1 Armor Class and +1 to saving throws** as described in play. The ring remains **transferable**, and its exact ancient maker, language and association with other psychic artifacts remain unknown. No later handoff or departure from the underwater cavern has been shown.[^status]
