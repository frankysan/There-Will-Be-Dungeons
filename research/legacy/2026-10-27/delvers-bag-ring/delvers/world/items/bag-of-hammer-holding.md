# Bag of Hammer Holding — Margaret's gift

## Introduction

A pink, jewel-studded tote bag seems an unlikely companion to Evelyn Grinkeeper's armor and great warhammer. Its bright decoration belongs more naturally in the rooms of her finishing school, which is precisely why Margaret, her friend and roommate, makes it for her. The little bag hides the heavy evidence of Evelyn's other life in a form that looks entirely respectable.[^gift]

## Enchantment

Margaret makes the bag from parts of other enchanted purses and moving bags, cutting them apart and stitching their magic into a new shape. It is roughly two feet long and one foot wide. She says that it can hold **one object weighing as much as sixty pounds**, a limit far more specific than the expansive promises of the best-known magical holding bags.

Evelyn tests the gift by lowering her **Vicious-enchanted warhammer** into it, hilt upward. The weapon sinks until the last portion seems to be drawn inside, leaving her free to pass between adventure and school without conspicuously carrying an armament. The enchantment is a successful favor from Margaret, not an item bought at the guild's magic shops.[^test]

## Current status

The Bag of Hammer Holding **belongs to Evelyn**, and its capacity to hold her large hammer is demonstrated. Its exact maximum dimensions, limits on other object types, retrieval speed and persistence when damaged are **not established** by this episode. No later loss or transfer is shown.[^test]
