## Method and reading weight

The maps account for all 37 archive IDs without equating archive order with story time. Season One was checked against its existing dossier evidence maps and all four earlier narrative studies. Season Two now has a [complete old-section coverage checklist](../../../../../reports/DELVERS_SEASON_TWO_CONSOLIDATION_2026-10-26.md) checked against its existing dossier scene maps and cutoffs. Season Three now has a [95-paragraph coverage ledger](../../../../../project/DELVERS_SEASON_THREE_COVERAGE.json), compared with the old narrative and targeted existing dossier evidence/continuity excerpts. It does not claim a new complete transcript interpretation.

Use roughly eight chapters per completed arc where the story supports them. The pilot uses the shared roughly 400-word planning reference, allowing denser sections where several distinct developments must remain. Measure prose separately from notes. No numerical target permits invented connective scenes or removal of unique evidence. Compare the actual pilot before making later chapter lengths binding.
