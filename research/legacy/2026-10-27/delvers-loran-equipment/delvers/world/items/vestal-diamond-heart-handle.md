# Loran Ilfelkyr's transforming sword — Lady Vestal's diamond-heart handle

**Later identification (S3E01–S3E02):** [Lady Vestal identifies the handle in S3E01](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E01.md) as **founder Loran Ilfelkyr's magical sword**. Evelyn **activates it using self-belief**, extending from dagger to sword lengths, but **cannot open the locked heart-marked underground chamber door**. The opening recap of [S3E02](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E02.md) explicitly states the blade provides **+2**, with its form depending on Evelyn's degree of belief. **No successful access to the founder's secret chamber and no further blade powers confirmed through S3E02.** Its magical identity **supersedes the historical S2E12 unknown-object status below** without backdating recognition to that episode.


**Status through S2E12 (second-arc finale):** **UNKNOWN object** transferred **physically from Lady Vestal to Evelyn Grinkeeper** during evacuation of finishing-school girls from invaded Bronze Ball. **Not named, activated, analyzed or shown to have specific magical effect**, despite GM allowing Arcana/History check (Evelyn fails). **DO NOT classify as wand, bell, drone remote, magic weapon, marriage sign, healing implement or GCT gift without future evidence**.

## Described form

A **small approximately SIX-INCH tubular HANDLE**, at top **two CURVED ARMS, each covered/crusted with DIAMONDS**, creating a **HEART-LIKE OUTLINE**. GM explicitly describes when Evelyn tries to re-don armor near garden bush as hook horror stalks. Evelyn attempts **History (4) and Arcana (9)** without identifying it, **pockets the handle before rushing back to protect civilians**. Earlier Lady Vestal places it into Evelyn's hand while **actively retreating from two hook horrors** and shares an intent look. **Why Vestal had it and what it does remain unknown**. No loss or subsequent transfer in episode.[^source]
