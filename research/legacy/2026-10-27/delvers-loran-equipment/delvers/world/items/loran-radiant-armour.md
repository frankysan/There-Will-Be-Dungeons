# Loran Ilfelkyr Radiant Armour — Evelyn's transformation and worn equipment

**ACTUALLY ACQUIRED and equipped in [Delvers S3E10 — Sad in a boney kind of way](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S3E10.md)**, at the hidden lower chamber of **Loran Ilfelkyr's school** reached by fountain/key and magic lance-blade. In S3E06 and later she entered but was **repelled by protective magical barrier**, so armour **NOT hers earlier**. During S3E10 she fails initial **Charisma 10, 4, Religion 8** efforts to claim it. **An actual school warning bell rings** as hook horror breaks through upstairs wooden floor: Evelyn's shift to protecting the students **ACTUALLY ALLOWS her hand to cross shield** (Charisma ~15), she seizes plate. She holds it to herself while rushing upstairs; **breastplate expands into full protective PLATE** around back, shoulders and skirt, overlays the blue flowered/dress aspect of Loran's vestments. She **ACTUALLY KILLS hook horror** before the schoolgirls, thereafter **wears the radiant armour into the ~8pm Guild confrontation**, publicly riding giant elk with shining armour, blue floral skirt/cloth. **Important visual continuity:** NO previous S3E06/S3E08 image should depict Evelyn wearing this complete set; transformation occurs only S3E10 after emergency bell.

Do not infer common homebrew/official item rules. The large magic lance/part blade remains separate prior equipment.
