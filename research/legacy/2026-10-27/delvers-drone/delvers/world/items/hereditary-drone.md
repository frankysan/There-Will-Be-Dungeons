## Observed and explained effects

A **stone music box with carved disc**; activating/playing melody causes a nearby **satyr Swiftclef** discomfort/headache during S2E05 Brotherhood inspection (directly observed). During guild study, artificer **Carrie Underworld** opens a **brain** for demonstration and explains that sound processing in the **auditory cortex** can drive/interfere with **motor cortex**, paralyzing or controlling susceptible victims, particularly **elves** through an inherited/hereditary aspect. She says **repeated non-elf exposure** can also affect motor pathways, analogizing Broadside's temporary mental confusion and earlier affected researcher. **This is Carrie's in-world scientific/magical interpretation**, not proof every black-goo case is solely sound-driven or that Swiftclef has a specific neural condition. **“Fulnomic/Fulmonic device”** on Withers's underground map is **only hypothesized related** to Hereditary Drone; link/method of activation unknown.[^discovery]

**Countermeasure planned:** Cassius worries **deafening/hearing protection** in dangerous pitch-dark Underdark ruins would leave adventurers uncoordinated, while **Koh's Psychic Whispers** might permit silent communications. Thus a **future guild-funded expedition** could exploit it once Bronze Ball raises funds; **no party goes on that expedition in S2E10**.[^lecture]

## Custody and status through S2E10

The **assembled music box and disc are still in guild/Carrie's artifact display custody** when she presents her findings. **Evelyn takes the black mirror** that reveals viewer's rear head, **not the drone**. Bell trousers and broken ritual staff also remain with guild. No subsequent drone activation, destruction, neutralization or removal from guild is depicted by S2E10's end. The item must not be placed in Koh's current carried pocket simply because he held it during S2E05.[^custody]
