The cases below do not establish species-wide traits or a departure from a D&D baseline. The Wastes evidence is ASR-provisional and has not been audio-verified.

## Evidence boundaries

These entries link to the canonical character pages rather than retelling their histories. No official creature-entry comparison has been made here, so observed behavior and table rulings are not presented as D&D deviations. Nash's transformation into a black dragon, by itself, does not establish a modified dragon form. The category does not imply that a listed being is hostile.
