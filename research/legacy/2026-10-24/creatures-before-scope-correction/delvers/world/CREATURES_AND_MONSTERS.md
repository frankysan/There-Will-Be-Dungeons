# Creatures and monsters

This index links notable Delvers beings and individual cases without treating ordinary encounters as campaign-specific variants. The material below is ASR-provisional and has not been audio-verified. Delvers is separate from The Wastes and Inverse.

## Notable cases

| Classification | Subject | Supported evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Baseline status unknown** | Walking plants, including [Veggie Sticks](../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/veggie-sticks.md) and `Brad/Brador` | Veggie Sticks emerges from a shrub, shelters the party in a hollow tree, and lives among other walking plants. He recognizes the small walking shrub the party brought as `Brad/Brador`, whom Evelyn returns to its home. The evidence does not establish the plants' origin, a species-wide account, or a D&D comparison. [S1E03](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E03.md) (~01:09–01:27) |
| **Baseline status unknown** | Studwell's crypt apparition | House attributes the strength-draining haunting to Studwell's despair after breaking his back; this is testimony. The party later fights a near-invisible figure identified as Studwell, which throws a kettlebell and is defeated by Koh. The evidence does not settle the apparition's nature or establish a wider haunting. [S1E02](../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E02.md) (~00:16–00:28, ~02:04–02:44) |

Common encounter creatures are not included merely because they appear in a session. No new creature-kind pages are needed for these cases: named, person-like beings remain linked to their character page, while the crypt and its encounters remain documented with the [place reference](../../../../../../delvers/world/places/grick-sinkhole.md) and episode dossiers.
