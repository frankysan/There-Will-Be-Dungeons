# Delvers Dungeon Guild — first employment

**Type:** Adventuring guild hall and workplace in the city rendered `Atamer/Atmer`. **Spoilers:** Delvers S1E01. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional; town spelling open.

## Overview

The hall combines a quest desk and job board, supply counter, tavern, trophy case and upper floor. It belongs to the lowest league in a wider hierarchy of adventurers described by Kyle. **Cassius** hosts new recruits, registers jobs and pays their rewards; **Clearstone**, a dwarf whose given name varies in ASR, supplies equipment; **Joe Groinhammer** runs the bar and can cover the quest desk.

For the first party the guild supplies an identity, employer and return point. Wooden badges number **404 Krandal, 325 Evelyn and 180 Koh**, each accompanied by a small guide. Cassius acknowledges little investment in delver-class badges; Koh's has scorch marks and reportedly previous holders. These numbers are not ranks or levels.

## Practices and first job

In [S1E01 ~00:24–00:43](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E01.md), the group selects the 120-gold farm-cave posting after rejecting Snake Island. Cassius says jobs post at seven, while newcomers arrive at nine, and estimates a fifty-percent success rate for the cave job. These are **NPC descriptions**, not measured guild statistics. He reads a foreword attributed to **Thun’Darious Storm**, whose spelling is supported separately by the [cast-posted handout register](../../../../../../../reports/DISCORD_SPELLING_2026-10-21.md). The famous hero does not appear.

On return ~02:22–02:28, Cassius accepts trophies and their report, pays forty gold to each member and receives badge 542. He says its missing owner had a different Underdark mission involving a door with a face. He checks the recovered [drift globe](../../../../../../../delvers/world/items/drift-globe.md), declares it nonsentient and explains that recovered items must be submitted for a sentience check. Neither this policy nor the handout establishes unplayed events.

## Current state and limits

The party's first quest is accepted as complete. Tomorrow's **crypt** job is assigned because the party has two divine casters. Evelyn and Krandal accept upstairs lodging; Koh declines to return home. Arrival upstairs, a completed long rest and the next morning are beyond the played cutoff.
