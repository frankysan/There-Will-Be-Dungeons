# Farm cave — first sealed passage

**Type:** Natural cave beside the sheep field at `Mickles/Mikkels` farm, east of the starting city. **Spoilers:** Delvers S1E01. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional; farmer spelling open.

## Overview

The party reaches the farm around **12:30 story time**, after passing Squelcher's Swamp. Four drained sheep lie beside a roughly ten-foot-square entrance beneath an uplifted slab. The descending cave contains puddles, stalactites and stalagmites, followed by a dead-end chamber with a **three-by-five-foot fissure** into a lower passage about fifteen feet below.

The stirge infestation is real in play, while the initial Underdark-origin explanation comes from Cassius's briefing. The party defeats six stirges outside, a piercer and a darkmantle inside. The darkmantle's discharged magical darkness persists after its death; the text does not establish when it finally disperses.

## Seal and recovered evidence

In [S1E01 ~02:06–02:22](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E01.md), Koh descends on a rope held by Krandal and recovers a glass orb and wooden badge from a mostly skeletal dragonborn. He alone sees a serpentine creature advancing as he climbs out. Evelyn wedges a dislodged rock into the fissure on her second attempt; Krandal adds concrete and its emptied sack, keeping the water container. The plug jolts under impacts but holds; a hiss recedes and a stirge part is trapped as the concrete dries.

The GM declares the hole sealed, and the [guild](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/delvers-guild.md) later accepts completion. **This closes one route**, not the entire Underdark. The pursuer remains alive/unfought; Cassius later calls Koh's description a grick. The skeleton remains below, with no cause/date of death established. Cassius identifies badge 542 with a different prior mission; linking these remains to Edgar is unsupported.
