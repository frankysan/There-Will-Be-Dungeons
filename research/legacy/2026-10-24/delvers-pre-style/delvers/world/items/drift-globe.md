# Drift globe — Koh's recovered light source

**Type:** Recovered magical glass orb. **Spoilers:** Delvers S1E02. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional; identification and capabilities explained by Cassius/GM, activation first demonstrated in S1E02 (ordinary light and once-daily Daylight). **542 identifies the recovered badge, not the globe.**

## Overview and custody

[Koh](../../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/koh-spiros.md) finds the orb beside the boots of a mostly skeletal dragonborn below the [farm cave](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/farm-cave.md). Around **02:15** he retrieves it and the wooden badge while keeping hold of the rope; back above, the orb is described as smooth and composed of many small joined glass panels. The ASR measurement is approximately six inches, with the exact dimension unresolved.

At the [guild](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/delvers-guild.md) ~02:24–02:27, Koh presents it for identification. Cassius invokes the recovered-item checking requirement, declares it nonsentient and clears its use. Krandal says Koh should keep it because he went down for it; **Koh is the supported holder at cutoff**. The badge is returned separately.

## Demonstrated light and changing significance

During the next day's crypt assignment Koh makes the globe shine as a portable light. When Shadows threaten to overwhelm his companions in the weight room, he activates its much brighter daylight power. The enemies recoil and become less effective under its radiance, allowing the three Delvers to survive the remainder of the trials. The globe's daylight does not itself deal confirmed direct damage, and its daily charge is expended until the next dawn.[^crypt]

[^crypt]: [S1E02 dossier](../../../../../../../delvers/episodes/S1E02.md), ~00:55 and 01:48–02:30. Recorded spell/effect wording remains ASR-provisional.

## Explained powers and unresolved name

Cassius/GM describes spoken commands, torch-like light, a **once-daily Daylight** effect, floating no more than **five feet above the ground**, and following if its user moves beyond **sixty feet**. These are this session's explanation, not a claim that all capabilities were tested or that a charge was spent.

Cassius suggests naming it for its former owner, rendered `Bran Broscale/Brosgail`, later `ram's/Rams` and `rosescale/rose scales`. The exact owner/name remains unresolved; no formal new item name is fixed here. His speculation about the dead delver's soul is not a confirmed afterlife outcome.

**S1E02 use:** Koh uses the at-will light in the cardio room (~00:55) and later triggers the once-daily **Daylight** charge (~01:48) to weaken Shadows; the glow does not automatically inflict damage. The same daylight persists through Studwell's fight. Further Daylight availability before next dawn is not shown.
