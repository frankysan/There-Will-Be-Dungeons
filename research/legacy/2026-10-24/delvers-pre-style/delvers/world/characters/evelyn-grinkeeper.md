# Evelyn Grinkeeper — paladin

**Identity:** Kristen's human paladin. **Spoilers:** Delvers S1E01. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional; working spelling follows the matching archived sheet-preview form **Evelyn Grinkeeper**. ASR aliases include `Greenkeeper`, `Grimkeeper` and `grin keeper`. Later expanded preview names are not imported here.

## Overview

Evelyn runs late to the [guild](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/delvers-guild.md), carrying a large dwarven warhammer and five javelins. Her skirt and blouse conceal clanking armor. Her urgency to close the dungeon repeatedly puts her ahead of [Krandal](../../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/krandal-splenderbelt.md) and [Koh](../../../../../../../delvers/world/characters/koh-spiros.md), who follow more cautiously. She carries bread and leads underground by torchlight.

Her impatience does not exclude concern for others: she leaves compensation for the owner of a horse she startled and heals Krandal during the stirge fight. By the cutoff she has helped plug the fissure, waited for the concrete to dry, received forty gold and accepted guild accommodation for tomorrow's crypt assignment. The crypt remains unvisited.

## Development and decisions

- **~00:14:38–00:22:** introduction, failed running check and runaway horse; meets the party and purchases bread. The horse's eventual recovery is unknown.
- **~00:31–00:59:** receives badge **325**, discards her guide, accepts its return from Cassius and discards it again. Unable to find the tradesman, she leaves **one gold beneath his cart seat**; receipt is not shown.
- **~01:05–01:24:** rushes the stirge, misses, then provides five-point Lay on Hands to Krandal. He heals her; the later correction makes that Cure Wounds **seven HP**, not its initial six. She finally punches the remaining stirge dead.
- **~01:26–01:55:** lights the torch, leads into the cave, receives Shield of Faith and damages the piercer. Mistakes Krandal's radiance for a creature's attack; fails to pull it off, then guides him into a puddle. Her Divine Sense does not locate the darkmantle because it is not one of the stated sensed creature types.
- **~02:01–02:22:** dislodges a rock formation while testing the ceiling, drags it to the fissure, succeeds on her second placement and waits for the concrete seal to dry. Shares bread during the offered short rest.

## Loyalty and divine courage in the crypt

Following the party's completed rest and advancement, Evelyn shows a firmer protective instinct, even when she expresses it impatiently. She cannot accept the refusal to sacrifice a rare strength potion while comrades remain missing, and persuades Crush to give up her second vial despite an unsuccessful impulsive slap. She willingly climbs the mountain steps a second time to learn the crypt's challenges and later lifts the emaciated Meatball out of a Shadow's grasp. Her support of Koh's cardio run combines encouragement and healing. In the weight room she revives Krandal after Koh accidentally wounds him, then suffers a debilitating Shadow attack herself; once he restores her, her Divine Smite dispatches the last threat. Evelyn helps pull the second chamber's chains and administers the third holy-water vial, restoring the interconnected fountains. Her compassion and urgency to finish the guild's work remain as characteristic as her formidable strength. [^crypt]

## Evidence and open questions

Her inference that the engulfing creature shoots lightning/fire is a **character misconception**, not an observed monster ability. The warhammer's dwarven description and her Dwarvish cursing do not establish a dwarven ancestry. Later XP banter about killing the horse owner is not performed action.
