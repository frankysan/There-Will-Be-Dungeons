# Koh Spiros — rogue

**Identity:** Jon's satyr rogue. **Spoilers:** Delvers S1E01. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional. **Koh** has cast-written support; **Spiros** appears in matching archived sheet previews. ASR aliases include `Co`, `Ko`, `Cole`, `co spiros` and `co-spyros`. Later preview nicknames are outside this horizon.

## Overview

Koh is already waiting inside the [guild](../../../../../../../delvers/world/places/delvers-guild.md) when his companions meet outside. He wears a large purple scarf, flowing white clothing with crimson leather and brass details, and a sword and dagger at his waist. He has dark hair, bright blue eyes and quiet hooves. He works at the edge of Evelyn's torchlight; Jon explicitly says he **does not have darkvision**.

His caution makes him the observer and opportunistic attacker of the first expedition. He collects proof from defeated creatures, consults his guide, and descends alone to recover a dead delver's badge and glass orb. At the cutoff he has earned forty gold, retains the identified [drift globe](../../../../../../../delvers/world/items/drift-globe.md), and says he must go home rather than take a guild room. His home itself is not shown.

## Crypt assignment and character development

At the next morning's reunion Koh has learned Cunning Action and admits that his unusually mysterious speaking voice on the first job was an affectation. The steep stairs to Kord's mountain crypt leave him exhausted, yet he recovers enough to run the ten laps required to reach its first basin. He risks further strength-draining blows but keeps moving while his companions defend him. During the more chaotic fight in the weight room, a sword thrust meant for a Shadow instead **drops Krandal**, whom Evelyn must heal; Koh recognizes the mistake and continues defending the group. His resourcefulness ultimately proves essential: he uses his recovered globe's Daylight power to expose and weaken the shadows, kills Studwell, and overcomes his damaged strength to ring the final bell. The others' help, particularly Krandal's Guidance, remains part of that success. Koh is the first to retrieve Sultan's enchanted climbing sash, but offers it to the party rather than asserting exclusive ownership. [^crypt]
