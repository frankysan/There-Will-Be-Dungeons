# Arc Two — The Road to the Crown

**The Wastes, second story arc — corrected E027–E054, excluding the separate E043 holiday one-shot**

**Full spoilers through E054.** **Status:** An editorial retelling synthesized from existing **processed-SRT episode dossiers**, **not an audio-verified transcript**. Historical claims, private memories and retrospective GM statements remain attributed. The separate [March 2019 Year One Q&A](../../../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md) supplies retrospective context, not another played chapter.

## I. The road from the Liberators — A challenge and a pledge

Leaving the **Liberators**, the group reconciles with a troubled Stanley and discovers **Tedna** stowed away in Fate 4. An armored pursuer attacks the three Soulbroker signatories; the party survives the fight and repairs its overturned vehicle. At their next desert camp, **Diptak** confronts Vorel and charges as E027 ends.

The confrontation ends with a pledge from the **Golden Land** rather than a final feud. **Tedna**, a young companion, travels with the party, and the talking cat **Buttons** joins them in Fate 4, carrying knowledge of the Arcanum and the fortress ahead. Soon the companions approach **Tietkalla**, the historically attested name associated with the fortress and its surrounding **Teet / Trashburg** settlements.

## II. Two voices — Stanley and Diontalus

Before they reach the town, something calls the group out into the oil sands. A strange song and a hidden encounter separate Stanley from the others and lead to the forced joining of his body with **Diontalus**, an archfey figure whose ambitions and methods differ profoundly from Stanley's. At first the experience is hidden from some companions. Later, Diontalus openly speaks while controlling Stanley's body. The question of which will awaken, and what one might do while the other watches, becomes one of the campaign's defining dangers.

## III. Trashburg — The promises beneath the keep

Tietkalla presents the party with apparently plentiful routes to power, but none can be trusted without cost. Its poor district, **Trashburg**, lies beneath an oppressive keep, gangs, a proposed Blood Ball match and the political wedding of **Jolt**. **Monsoon Cabbage** offers entry through gang assassination and presents himself as a useful ally. Nash befriends a little pixie named **P0D**, learning of the brutal trade in **Pixie Blush** and the captivity of other pixies. Hope becomes caught up in the city's old tiefling mysteries, and Nash draws close to **Angelashi**, whose music and brief intimacy offer relief from the surrounding violence.

Different routes open and close. The group accepts a Blood Ball invitation but never plays the long-planned game. Vorel attacks Toe Rippers, precipitating street fighting and a showdown in the **Golden Gargoyle**, and Hope acquires a weapon whose use will soon alter her life. Meanwhile the cat Buttons' attention to Stanley and Diontalus grows more urgent than it first appears.

## IV. The deal collapses — Hope rebuilt and the pit below

The hunt for Toe Ripper leader **Blunt Goliver** ends catastrophically when a missile Hope fires is returned against her in a cave. The party survives the explosion, kills Goliver and seeks Monsoon's promised help. Hope does not emerge unscathed: the injuries require major amputations, and a series of procedures leaves her with a **mechanical arm and leg**. What Monsoon calls assistance brings fresh deception, violence and fear of being turned into someone else's instrument.

The party becomes hunted by Teet authorities. Tedna's letters, competing versions of the city's hidden history, Monsoon's claims about **Kefeli** and **Andreeste**, and the **Gem of Tides** point toward a kingdom older than the keep's current rulers. Forced into the **Pit of Despair**, they encounter a great froghemoth and escape through forgotten passages. **George**, a guard who follows them down, becomes a moral test: Stanley wants him spared; after their rest **Diontalus deliberately kills him**. This is not a death caused by George's earlier accident, and the disagreement cannot be smoothed away by telling the story from the party's collective point of view.

## V. An older kingdom — The books beneath the city

In the caves, a mysterious singing river ruler demands a service before allowing passage. The party fights creatures and retrieves **enchanted beans and an engraved key**. These become an unexpected way upwards: the planted beans raise a giant green beanstalk, and the key opens an ancient hatch. The companions make their way among the skeletons and magics of an old tiefling complex, with its animated books, hidden archives, abandoned offices and strange ritual chambers.

One book contains a person. Through Hope's ingenuity, Nash's **Reduce**, and Vorel's strength, the group releases a frail **elder tiefling**. He first mistakes Hope for a long-lost queen, then examines her lineage and claims she belongs to **House Akmenos**, heirs to Tietkalla's forgotten rulers. He tells stories of a vampire-tainted council, a tiefling destiny, souls carried to the Night Pasture and a force he has hidden through a terrible **Soulbroker bargain**. His claims may explain much, but the party has not yet seen the army or proven every detail of his ancient history. He leaves through **Château Weir** to seek his soldiers.

At last the party reaches the keep's **prison level**, where **Tedna and Bok Bok are found alive and freed**. A report that Angelashi has been taken to the pit and killed is deeply troubling, but **it is secondhand information**, not a witnessed death. Later events will resolve it differently.

## VI. The price of betrayal — P0D, the Gem and the petrified throne

The prison rescue is followed by another trap. **Jolt, Buttons and guards** await the party in the keep's food stores, and Buttons discloses a competing deal around the Gem, Monsoon and the Principal. **Monsoon issues Order 26 by radio**: P0D is violently transformed into an enormous hostile creature. Her distress and Nash's attempts to protect her make clear that she is **not choosing freely to betray her friends**. In the fight **Vorel kills Buttons**.

The aftermath is more devastating. Despite Nash pleading for P0D to be calmed or saved, **Diontalus kills her**. Her body disperses as pixie dust. **Tedna**, rescued only recently, dies of severe wounds after a moment of temporary stabilization. A gunman called **Dashington** shoots Hope and is killed; Hope, deeply affected by pixie blush and its hallucinations, kills **Jolt**. The spectacular otherworldly images she sees amid Tedna's death are not confirmation of a literal steel heaven. Nor can the shock to the group be dismissed as an unfortunate battle mechanic: their relationship with Diontalus and with each other has been fundamentally shaken.

The party recovers the **Gem of Tides**, and Hope gains **Fox and Scully**, the enchanted paired revolvers, along with their ammunition belt. Vorel proposes the **Pillars of Decency**, a moral framework named for P0D. Behind the keep, an ancient crypt leads down to a pool and pipeworks where the Gem once belonged. The serpent-haired **Kefeli** claims the authority to rule Tietkalla and describes the curses that led her to turn tieflings to stone. Her powers are real, but her version of history and justification remains her own.

Negotiation fails. **Vorel and Hope are petrified** during the confrontation, and Kefeli also turns to stone through an ingenious mirror-and-gaze maneuver. Nash and Stanley destroy Kefeli's petrified body, but their companions remain statues. The two survivors return to an earlier **basilisk corpse** and combine its remains with materials from the old bureau to make a desperate, improvised cure. **Both Hope and Vorel are restored before the end of E051**. The battle above them, however, is still raging.

## VII. Through the beast — The Eye of Groxl and the last road to the keep

Back among the burning upper ruins, the party confronts transformed giant pixies and an enormous, changed **froghemoth**. Its massive body swallows them, and the adventure moves literally through its anatomy: mouth, throat, acid, parasites, heart, and finally its electrically active brain. Nash acquires a strange **small extra arm** amid unstable magical effects. Vorel touches the brain and tries to communicate with the intelligence inside.

The beast describes a desire to ascend or transform. In the following episode it divides: **an upper, cocoon-like mass rises into the sky**, while **the lower portion crashes into the desert** and begins dying around the trapped party. The airborne portion's ultimate fate remains unknown. The group fights its way through the failing lower body, and Vorel is drawn to an ancient **living eye**. The **Eye of Groxl**, owner-confirmed as the canonical name of the single artifact sometimes rendered `Grojil` or `Groxel` by ASR, **embeds itself in Vorel's chest**. It grants formidable combat abilities but possesses an independent will. Vorel's new ability to change the color of his scales is a **separate alteration**, not a property of the eye.

Nash produces a rowboat from the finite patches of his Robe of Useful Items, and the adventurers escape the collapsing creature by the least dignified passage imaginable. They emerge alive in the desert. Vorel retrieves a cache of long-buried treasure—most of which is not carried away—and the group prepares a striking return: **Nash in young-black-dragon form, carrying Hope and the others toward the damaged city**.

## VIII. The crown — A queen and a city still in ruins

They arrive over **devastated Trashburg**. Giant pixies begin reverting to smaller forms. The keep stands breached, its authority broken, but no one can yet say what becomes of every inhabitant. Nash lands the party and smashes open a throne-room wall, bringing them face to face with **Monsoon Cabbage**. A prince lies dead; **Suck Master Din** is held captive; and, against the dreadful news brought by Tedna, **Angelashi is alive**, restrained by Monsoon's servant **Lazlo**.

Monsoon says he is willing to yield power. He warns that **Kefeli's death** has made the city vulnerable to a future claim by **Andreeste**. That threat may be serious, but his explanation is the statement of an interested adversary, not an arrival by Andreeste or proof of the final terms of some ancient agreement. Vorel attacks; **Monsoon escapes in mist**, still alive as far as the episode can establish. The group breaks Lazlo's stone grip and **frees Angelashi**, who reunites with Nash. She was not resurrected: the earlier story of her death was never confirmed.

Then the elder tiefling returns. A portal opens and **hundreds of armed tieflings** march into the hall. Their presence makes one part of his extraordinary promise real, though it does not independently verify the alleged number of a thousand, the history of their imprisonment, or the price paid to the Soulbroker. Before the assembled company, **Hope Akmenos is crowned queen**. Her journey from Dusthill mechanic to the seat of a lost kingdom is now an enacted political fact, not simply a genealogical possibility.

Her reign begins in a morally fraught fashion. **Vorel executes the captive Suck Master Din with Hope's approval.** Armed leaders set about securing the ruined city, while others offer to organize farms and repair its buildings. Some openly object to the queen's non-tiefling companions. Hope insists that Nash, Stanley and Vorel have a place at her council, but a crown cannot settle old prejudice, heal the victims of war, or ensure that future orders are carried out justly.

The company speaks of clinics, reconstruction, abandoned belongings, the family of the murdered George, threats beyond the city and the obligations of power. **Most of that work remains to be done**. The GM closes the first campaign year with celebration and a proposed stretch of downtime. Nothing in the closing discussion means months of reforms have already occurred during the coronation day.

## Epilogue — What the cast later explained

The [**March 2019 Year One Q&A**](../../../episodes/BONUS-2019-03.md) follows the Arc Two finale in the source archive, but **does not continue in-world time**. It gives context that neither the characters nor the audience necessarily possessed when earlier scenes unfolded.

Among the disclosures, **Beau says Buttons had loved Stanley and tried to bring him to the Arcanum to remove Diontalus**. That retrospective revelation changes how his actions can be understood, but it **does not erase his betrayal or mean Stanley knew his intention at the time**. Beau also says an attempt to rescue the pixies was a **prepared but unchosen route**, that **Order 26** was Monsoon's planned transformed-pixie force, and that the froghemoth's internal dungeon grew from improvisation around an available map. Scott and Beau describe the collaborative development of **Nash's dragon-metal heart**. They intentionally leave mysteries around the Solar Mines, the gods, and the cast's future.

The first year has ended, then, with a crown, a haunted group of friends, and a shattered city full of competing claims. The surviving characters have won the possibility of building something better. Whether they will agree on what “better” means is another story.
