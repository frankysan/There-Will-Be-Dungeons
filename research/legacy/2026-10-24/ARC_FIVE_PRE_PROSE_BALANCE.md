# Arc Five — No Place Like Home

**The Wastes · fifth story arc · Full spoilers.**[^five-archive][^five-1]

## I. The door between friends

Hope leaves **White Tower** through a devil-opened portal with the hooded **Voice** and **Spatulo**. The door closes on Nash, Stanley and Vorel. The demons who besieged the tower have withdrawn under Orcus's command, but the victory is compromised: the living **Hand of the Voice** has gone with Hope, leaving the ornate **Fist gauntlet** in its chest. Nash feels abandoned when his wife and unborn child are still on Earth; Vorel believes Hope may yet come back. The tower's protection rejects the exposed gauntlet, so Nash carefully closes the case around it and returns its key to Stanley. Whatever these friends decide to believe about Hope, their immediate hope of reaching Earth still depends on delivering the Fist to **Tobacho Fleur**.

Hope comes instead to the fiery approaches of the **Kaiser Casino**, a place with slot machines, hotel service and a remarkably ordinary lounge. The Voice removes a marked scrap of flesh from her body and throws it into a burning lake, telling her that the old Soulbroker claim will no longer hold her. Hope must trust that his act can free her from the old claim. A smiling host in a white dinner jacket introduces himself as **Asmodeus**, offering the name **Brett**. He speaks of the Voice's supposed ambitions, the difference between devils and demons, and the benefits of cooperation. Hope wants to reach her kingdom and learn whether her friends are alive. Brett offers help and hospitality; **she does not sign a pact with him**. His hospitality comes from a power with ambitions of his own.

At White Tower, meanwhile, the **Tunga moon** Nash accidentally produced still fills the sky. A tiny flying saucer crashes during Nash's attempt to visit it, but he brings **Walnut** safely out of the wreck and makes an illusion of Euphemius to comfort him; the knights recognize the image as an illusion. **Alentris** prepares to answer to her superiors for the lost Hand. She asks Stanley to stay with her rather than continue the perilous journey. He refuses, then commits a more serious wrong: **Modify Memory** leaves her believing the true Fist is inside White Tower and that his party carries an empty box. The real relic remains in his keeping. With **Dimension Door** he brings Vorel and the case outside; Nash reaches the waiting orc-operated airships. All three depart. **Vorel is troubled by Stanley's choice**, and the relationship damaged at the tower is not repaired by their departure.

Brett offers Hope a magical disguise, the **Necklace of Infinite Appearances**, and a powerful **copper ring** that opens gateways. He introduces three employees who openly report to him: the towering minotaur **Steerious Business**, the erratic conjurer **Blood Skunk Hendrickson**, and **Dr. Spider**. Hope uses the ring to enter her own chambers at **Tietkalla**, accompanied by all three and the persistent Spatulo. The kingdom is alive, but visibly altered by her absence. **Celibus Stump**, a former Slavetown saloon keeper, sits on the throne as a Principle-backed chancellor, reporting political quarrels and railway troubles. Hope kills him with **Finger of Death**. Her new companions battle the guards; their brutality—including the killing of a captive—shows that escaping the devils' company will not be as simple as coming home.

Out among the citizens Hope learns from **Emenil** and others about the disturbed government, missing opponents and an unpopular railway arrangement. The Voice appears privately and demands an evil act. **Hope refuses to kill either Emenil or a random passerby**; instead Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider set fire to the **closed railway station**. People rush to extinguish the blaze. The station burns while Hope's struggle with the Voice remains unresolved. Elsewhere above the Outlands, the three men on the departing airship see the moon grow and a fleet of saucers close in. A larger craft decloaks and attacks. Separated by a single door, both parts of the former company are already drawing destruction toward places they hoped might shelter them.[^five-2]

## II. A moon, a false route home, and the price of a Hand

Hope briefly believes her return to Tietkalla can become a restoration. **Omnom Cinderborn**, alive after Razak Val's violence, speaks of his failed struggle to reclaim his home. She disavows the Principle's railway arrangement and tells citizens their queen has returned. Then an armed floating castle appears, **General Carthax** leads demons into the streets, and her city burns. The three companions Brett lent her help shepherd a few people toward the keep, but the scale of the invasion overwhelms any hope of saving the city by fighting here. Hope opens a gateway toward Brett, and **she, her three hired companions, Spatulo and two rescued tieflings escape**. This is a real withdrawal before Carthax reaches her; it does not establish the fate of every person left behind.

Above the Outlands, the others face their own invasion. **Stanley and Vorel** fight their way through one of the Tungan saucers while **Nash** discovers that **Squirts and infant Chunks** have stowed away in fruit crates. Giant machines attack the wooden ship; Nash assumes **black-dragon form**, Vorel cuts into a cockpit, and Stanley fights from the enemy's machinery. No ordinary victory can dispose of the extraordinary moon that Nash's wild magic made. **Squirts calls on Olidammara, and her Divine Intervention succeeds**: the moon, its advancing machines and remaining saucers vanish. The damage already inflicted is not undone. The orcs acclaim Nash as captain, and he names their ship **The Great Angelashi** after the woman he hopes to see again.

To reach Sigil they must sail through a **fruit-and-phrase portal ritual**. Nash gets the order wrong the first time. The opening leads to something monstrous; huge tentacles seize and destroy the ship. **Stanley, Nash, Vorel, Squirts, Chunks and survivors escape aboard a smaller boat**, but not everyone caught in the wreck is rescued. With the frightened servant **Snatch/Sam** explaining the correct sequence, they cut a window into the boat and try again. The second portal succeeds. They reach an industrial **Sigil dock**, where Nash discovers that inheriting his captain's title may also mean inheriting the wreck's **100,000-gold debt**. In the Hive they fall victim to a crowded freight-elevator ambush: disguised attackers and ogres strike in darkness, Nash's Fireball kills both enemies and the innocent lift operator, **Squirts heals Stanley** and saves others, and **Stanley's Feather Fall** helps them survive the shaft. The escape leads to a reconciliation between Vorel and wounded rickshaw driver **Fambo Joe** and finally to the door of Tobacho Fleur.

The great blue dragon receives the complicated **Fist chest**. Stanley finds the missing key unexpectedly in his boot and opens it before the crowd; the magnificent gauntlet is genuine, but **its living Hand is absent with Hope**. Tobacho notices. He accepts the diminished prize, yet **does not produce a direct route to Earth**. Instead he supplies a masked guide, **Badass T**, and displays an illusion of Hope while advertising a tremendous bounty for her Hand. The travelers have delivered the Fist, not betrayed Hope or brought home their friends. Following T, they reach a frog seller's house, perform a peculiar portal ritual and enter an alien spaceport. **T appears to destroy the route behind them** and takes them aboard a gnome-crewed ship, bound not for Earth but for a twin-asteroid tower. The cost of finding home is once more distance and danger.

Hope's escape lands at the **Kaiser Casino**, where Brett is conferring with **the Principle**. In dazzling projected scenes, the Principle depicts Earth's pre-Scorching wealth and oppression, dragons destroying cities, an early **CHIDS** machine, and the **Lich Project**—his claimed practice of growing bodies and consuming souls to sustain himself. Hope is appalled by this account yet agrees to consider cooperation against greater threats. Hope has only these powers' word for the history they show her. The Principle points her toward a second **Eye of the Voice** and says Tobacho might know where it is.

Spatulo insists the Hand was meant for him. The resulting quarrel ends in **his real death under Dr. Spider's Mental Prison**, followed by grotesque interrogation of his preserved brain. Hope and her entourage then **enter Sigil through the ring's gate**, separate from the men who have just left the Hive. Far away, Badass T brings the first party to a tower where a gigantic stone face resembling Nash greets its namesake as **“Junior.”** Their guide has not brought them to Earth, but he may finally be able to explain why.[^five-3]

## III. Alternate lives, lost lives

The speaking face in Badass T's tower belongs to **First Nash**, an older counterpart from another history. He says the Hope, Stanley and Vorel he knew died; their memorials stand inside his home. The present travelers stand alive before memorials to lives they never lived. He asks the younger Nash to help save Hope, then shows him how to look through his uncanny eye toward **Angelashi**. The vision shows her **pregnant, fleeing demons with Bok Bok in Tietkalla**. Nash needs no more reason to return. The house contains impossible counterparts to people they mourned—**Pod, alive with a family, and an alternate Tedna beneath Badass T's mask**—as well as near-copies of possessions lost in other travels. Stanley takes **Diplomacy**, his megaphone and dictionary; Nash retrieves a familiar staff. Pod is not the P0D killed by Diontalus in their own past, and the alternate Tedna is not their dead companion brought back. Those in the tower stay behind. Nash casts a **Teleportation Circle** and carries Stanley, Vorel, **Squirts and Chunks** back to the underground passages of Tietkalla.

The old keep is a ruin under demonic occupation. A terrified survivor recognizes the **Heroes of the Wastes**, only to be killed by a nycaloth before they can save him. Stanley's heated **Diplomacy** and Vorel's burning blade bring down the attacker, while Squirts's ***Silence* and *Pass without Trace*** let the company evade others. At the **Golden Sands** they find Bok Bok hiding in the cellar and, at last, **Angelashi physically reunites with Nash**. They also meet an extraordinary **Elhandari rescue mission**, whose soldiers employ psychotherapy and magical empathy grenades to trap demons. Among the arrivals are **Nobieri** and **Melgor**. Not all of their methods inspire trust, but they offer a way to escape.

That promise is tested by **General Carthax**, who has built a throne over the dead. Squirts heals the still-living **Omnom Cinderborn** while Nash and Stanley use magical restorative pills to return briefly to fighting strength; the pills carry a later cost in exhaustion. Elves and companions attack. Carthax fires destructive beams that **disintegrate Squirts and Melgor**, along with several elves. **Chunks survives**, fallen from his mother's arms. Vorel takes the loss into his furious assault, and Bok Bok hurls himself inside the monster's mouth with dynamite. Vorel thrusts a bandolier of **empathy grenades** into Carthax's eye; the general is **pacified and captured alive**, not slain. Stanley's magic helps get Bok Bok out. The elven survivors carry Angelashi, Nash, Stanley, Vorel, Chunks and Bok Bok onto their ship; **Vorel gathers Squirts's ashes in a vial** before the craft departs Earth. Hope is still far away.

In **Sigil**, Hope and her three Brett-employed companions seek **Tobacho Fleur**. Steerious kills a bounty hunter who tries to claim the price on her head. Tobacho has already received the Fist from the other company, and understands that Hope carries the missing living Hand. He offers information on the **Eye of the Voice** in return for something more consequential than gold: she must help unseat the **Lady of Pain** and grant him rule of Sigil if she gains such power. The Voice advises her not to sign. **Hope nevertheless signs**, and Tobacho gives a possible lead rather than the Eye: the **Council of Clowns** in Pandemonium. Her signature binds her to an ambition far beyond the search that brought her here.

The road to the Council first passes through a mansion claimed by Dr. Spider, where a dead accountant answers only through ***Speak with Dead*** and the party rests, and then through a grisly carrot-and-whip gateway to the **Gray Waste**. Its apathy drains color and spirit from the travelers. At **Pointless**, a talking severed orc head becomes Steerious's unwanted companion. **Hesterbrook**, the skeletal ferryman, demands cherished possessions for passage over the **River Styx**: Hope gives up **Fox** rather than Scully, Steerious his greatsword, Blood Skunk part of a toe, and Dr. Spider his lab coat. The ferryman takes their offerings. A colossal leviathan is diverted when Hope flatters it on the hooded Voice's behalf; it is neither killed nor brought into their service.

Past the river an imp guides the four through a furious cart ride and into the **Council of Clowns**. Its presiding figures mock Hope's grief and ambitions until her anger finally amuses them enough to hear her request. They say the **Eye of the Voice** is confined in an ancient hidden prison, a place whose name means little to the travelers, adrift in **phlogiston** near the **Corinthian Nebula**. A little **toy-box compass** points toward the supposed place. Hope has a direction for her search, but the prison and its captive Eye remain far away. Both parties are alive in different places. Neither knows the other's whole story.[^five-4][^five-5]

## IV. A forest in the stars

The Elhandari vessel lifts away from the ruined Earth with Squirts dead and **Angelashi pregnant**. As its crew debates an enormous explosion seen near the **Principle's city**, pirates attack and breach the hull. **Captain Toesalt** demands surrender. A monster called **Fluffy** seizes Vorel in its tentacles; **Stanley sends Diplomacy into its jaws and heats the blade until it dies**. A huge skeleton and undead boarders force the others into the corridors, and **Sensate Philanthalar** is killed. **Lyrielle**, a newly met elven doctor, uses ***Wall of Force*** to protect the medical bay from the vacuum as Stanley and Vorel fight to keep attackers out. Some elves are lost to space. The ship eventually seals the hull, though the pirates' wider fate is not resolved by a single boarding battle.

At the same time **Angelashi gives birth**, and the delivery exposes a secret she has kept from Nash: she is a **changeling**, not an elf by birth. Her four infants appear at first as a fork, a boot, a pipe and a more human-shaped baby. Lyrielle guides the difficult deliveries while Nash tries to be a father in the only way he knows, awkwardly and with enormous affection. All four survive; each is revealed to be a living shapechanging child, four infants with bodies as changeable as their mother's[^five-birth]. Nash's own heritage is less certain than Angelashi thinks.

The surviving ship reaches **Elhandar**, a vast orbital forest under a dome. Admiral **Chrysorius** leads the company along **Rainbow Road** to an audience with the **High Elf**, who recognizes Stanley as **Valier Ornath**, son of an Elhandari scientist who went to Earth. The leader offers to share his memories; Stanley, having endured too many intrusions into his mind, refuses, and the offer ends. **The Principle** unexpectedly manifests, displays overwhelming power and claims to have caused the explosion on Earth and ascended to demigodhood. He privately tells Stanley that a dangerous old acquaintance might have survived. Stanley is left to wonder which old enemy might return. Soon the travelers move into the abundant living woods and reach **Villa Ornath**, the apparently familiar cottage of Stanley's father. Nash and Angelashi struggle through the shock of her disclosure and reconcile. They name their babies **Fork, Boot, Pipe and Nash Junior Maggard**; Bok Bok agrees to help raise **Chunks**, whose mother has died. The household mourns Squirts, whose ashes have been lost.

Four quiet recovery days pass. Nash and Stanley clear the painful delayed exhaustion from the restorative pills, while Vorel explores Elhandar and asks what is left of his kingdom or duty. Nash hears a private dying voice connected to his heart but chooses rest. **Nobieri** finds the household and tells them **Omnom and Emenil survived**. Yet their new sanctuary cannot feel complete while Hope remains missing.

Hope's hunt for the missing Eye takes another turn. Still seeking a way onward from the Council cavern, she declines the offered return to Tobacho and tries to recharge Brett's ring. Hurting Dr. Spider fails to satisfy it, but **Blood Skunk volunteers an ear**, and the ring opens. At the Sigil docks the group attempts to seize a dwarven ship for phlogiston; **Dr. Spider kills the dockmaster**, the confrontation with the armed crew turns lethal, Steerious falls from a gangway but lives, and Blood Skunk collapses. Hope abandons the attempt and gates everyone back to Spider's infernal laboratory. **No vessel is captured, and a brief attempted Gate toward Kervalon does not land them on Earth.** Brett restores Steerious's sword but cannot identify the prison. The **Principle**, summoned against his wishes, also has no usable location, although he suggests Nash as a possible lead. After **Blood Skunk volunteers his other ear**, Hope summons Nash by name through a Gate. **Nash is drawn to her inside the Hell laboratory**, then the two step through into **Elhandar's forest**. Steerious, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider **remain behind**; no Eye or completed Tobacho bargain follows them.[^five-6]

The reunion is anything but simple. Nash brings Hope to the family cottage. **Stanley fears the Hand of the Voice and resents her decision to leave**, while Vorel and Nash insist that the group can at least hear her explanation. Hope weeps; their trust is wounded but their shared history remains. She visits the survivors of Tietkalla with the others, meeting **Nobieri and Emenil** at a small clearing. There she declines a queenly chair and admits that the Hand's pursuit endangered her people. A grieving household leaves the gathering, but the others choose to stay and try again. **A giant flaming tiefling figure identifying itself as Abegir manifests during Nobieri's ash rite**; offering encouragement and assurances about the afterlife to the grieving survivors. Nash recounts the Solar Mines, Stanley his own upbringing and sacrifices, and Vorel promises to remember such stories. **Hope names the settlement “the Harbor” and the people “Harborers,”** a genuine public act of founding rather than a claim that Tietkalla itself has already been restored.

**Emenil** privately tells Stanley she loves him. He cannot promise her a relationship, and she reacts painfully. In the forest Vorel detects another familiar lizardfolk scent. It belongs to **Omnom**, badly injured and furious at Hope for the disasters that struck Razak Val and Tietkalla. His accusations mix witnessed events and his own interpretation; Hope denies deliberately inviting the invasion. **Vorel refuses Omnom's wish to be killed** and offers him a place on a hunt the next day. At the same time a young elf, **Dilenoria (“Dilo”)**, asks Stanley to accept him as a pupil. The admiral's guests deliver a heavy **key from Stanley's father**. A childhood poem guides Stanley and Hope to a **hidden third-floor study** larger than the cottage should contain. There Stanley obtains the responsive **Book of Adjacencies** and **Mask of Eltanfawr**. A father's letter offers a map of possible connections among **N4SH, the Weir, the Solar Mines and the Principle**—his theories, not a verified time-travel explanation. A separate private intervention by the Voice still pulls Hope toward her unfinished Eye search. The refuge has become a place of answers, but not yet safety.[^five-7]

## V. The stranger in the mountain

The next day's hunt begins with a moment of ordinary family alarm: baby **Pipe** has vanished, only to turn up transformed into a smoking pipe in **Nash's own pocket**, safe and laughing. **Dilo** returns to learn from Stanley, and **Omnom** joins the expedition with Vorel. The would-be student speaks of **Chester**, a bizarre bear–dinosaur creature supposedly produced by old Elhandari experiments. Stanley is willing to teach him but afraid for a more private reason. He remembers the **Principle's warning** that an old enemy might have survived, suspects **Diontalus**, and shares that fear with Vorel. Stanley watches his new pupil with growing unease.

The six travelers follow Lyrielle's map north through the forest. They meet Fitz gathering berries, sociable squirrels and a friendly fishing elf. Vorel and Stanley catch fish; Nash saves heads and eggs for a lure. One peaceful night passes under ***Tiny Hut***. In the morning heavy footsteps approach as Vorel flings a burning fish-head bait into the brush; **Stanley extinguishes the small spreading fire**. Chester emerges: upright, antlered, part bear and part dinosaur. **Stanley becomes a Tyrannosaurus rex**, Nash speeds Vorel with ***Haste***, and a few furious blows send Chester scrambling up a tree. **Hope pulls the wounded creature down by its entrails; the fall kills it, but the body pins her underneath.** Vorel lifts Chester so she can escape alive. Omnom praises the Great Fang, and for a little while an old quarrel seems capable of healing.[^five-8]

Talking deer appear with congratulations and a lead toward glowing mushrooms. Stanley senses an illusion and casts ***Dispel Magic***: one deer becomes an armed elf, and the other five reveal themselves too. The six mages attack. **Mass Suggestion** compels Hope and Omnom to act like squirrels; cold and roots spread through the fight. **Dilo performs an apparent rescue before giving a prearranged signal**, and an enemy uses a wand to close Hope inside a floating, sealed blue sphere. The wand passes into Dilo's hands. He **runs away with the imprisoned Hope** while **Stanley's Destructive Wave kills all six attacking mages**. Hope's own attempt to ***Teleport*** out fails. Stanley takes **giant-eagle form** to follow the glowing prison to a mountain cave; Vorel, Nash and Omnom join the pursuit. In the mushroom grove Nash eats a strange luminous slice and his **open mouth becomes a bright lantern**. The urgency of rescue overwhelms any caution about the other wonders underground.

In the next chamber Dilo threatens to render Hope helpless with **empathy grenades** inside her prison. He demands Stanley's body or Hope with the Hand. Stanley recognizes his antagonist: **Diontalus**. The archfey says the Principle helped him escape his former confinement, offering no explanation of how he gained his freedom. Stanley bargains for a duel, transforms into a **giant ape** and strikes the elf's body. **Dilo's apparently lifeless body tumbles away, and a separate blue spectral Diontalus visibly emerges**. Now the possession is witnessed rather than feared. Hope tries desperately to disarm grenades from within the sphere; **Stanley fails to dispel it and cannot release her yet**.

The fighting tears across the cave. Diontalus's scythe drains strength from Stanley; Vorel's sword and Eye of Groxl reveal how difficult this ghost is to hurt. Nash becomes a black dragon. Omnom struggles to grab the apparition; roots imprison the company, and **Vorel empties Omnom's whole Necklace of Fireballs in one enormous blast**. The roots burn and friends suffer alongside the enemy. Then Diontalus's **chain lightning kills Omnom**, leaving only his feet. The newly renewed bond between the two lizardfolk ends in grief. A huge **scarred black dragon** suddenly bursts from deeper in the cave, savages Diontalus and forces him to **escape alive through the rock**. Nash's dragon form erupts with uncontrolled heat and acid; Stanley falls unconscious, Nash collapses into a private heart-vision, and **Vorel stabilizes Stanley exhausting his healer kit**. **Hope remains trapped**, even after the enemy flees.[^five-9]

The dying dragon heals the surviving men, then identifies itself as the **black dragon once imprisoned in Earth's desert**. It says Nash's manufactured heart carries the signature of **its mother** and asks to meet her. Nash speaks the mother's name; **the mechanical heart opens from Nash's dragon chest and a glowing orange-gold mother dragon appears**, tenderly greeting her wounded son. The reunion leaves Nash still wondering where he belongs in this strange family. The stranger describes the **Dragon Sons**, the Principle, body-making in **Clench's flesh pits**, ancient dragon resistance and Earth opened to the **Weir**. The companions listen to the dying creature's account of a war older than their own. It offers a last hope: construct a **Rubistic Triangulosphere** around **its own natural dragon-metal heart** and install/melt it at Earth's hottest point to help seal the world. The companions have never seen such a machine, but the dragon offers them a reason to try.[^five-10]

At last the black dragon dies; the spectral mother withdraws into Nash's engine and his chest closes. **Stanley finally activates the prison wand and releases Hope**, alive. **Vorel spends hours extracting the natural heart**, far heavier than Nash's implanted machine. Unable to carry the enormous organ safely, they hide it beneath earth near an ominous black spike deep in the cave. They gather grenades, mushroom samples and light-giving slices, leaving the fallen dragon's body to dissolve. The four emerge together and take refuge at the **Gatherers' Lodge**, where two embarrassed ghostly keepers offer beds, water and sweets. A ridiculous struggle over whether **Nash should eat ghost candy** interrupts their grief; Hope's disguise as an apparent other Nash is **a prank, not an actual time-traveler arrival**. No choice in the lodge has yet built the dragon's device or defeated Diontalus. The mountain has given them evidence of a larger war and a fragile reason to remain together.[^five-11]

## VI. The price of restoring a world

At the **Gatherers' Lodge**, Nash's imagined curse from the ghost sweets is finally put to rest in the least dignified way: his friends get him to swallow a bonbon, and he goes outside to **vomit it back up**. The lodge's custodians confirm the dead bearsaurus **Chester** and report that the other slain elves looked like council mages, leaving the reason for their attack a mystery. A spare **noble speeder** will help the four return. Over dinner they begin to name a task that sounds impossible: the dragon's proposed machine, the **Rubistic Triangulosphere**, becomes **Ruby** to Vorel. The dragon-metal heart remains buried in the mountain. They do not yet know who can construct Ruby, whether it really seals Earth or what will happen when it is placed in the planet's deepest heat.

Away from the others, **Hope and Stanley** share beers beside the speeder after she takes it for an unexpectedly destructive first spin through the barn. Their quarrel has lasted beyond White Tower. Stanley admits that he once made a bargain to save friends and cannot simply condemn Hope for choosing a different dangerous one. He **apologizes and welcomes her back**, though the **Hand of the Voice** and her patron remain terrifying. Hope believes trying to remove it could kill her. She offers a possible magical search for Stanley's father, but **they do not use the ring for that journey**. His father's book and her account of grief leave them wondering whether they have already failed in other possible histories. This private reconciliation cannot itself undo either old contract or make all four agree.

After an uneventful night's rest at the lodge, **Stanley's and Vorel's injuries from Diontalus remain**; sleep has not restored the strength stolen by his ghostly blade. Hope drives the group toward **the Harbor**, with Stanley behind her, Vorel balancing at the front and Nash towed by harness on his floating clothing. At the settlement they deliver the mushroom samples to **Nobieri**, who loosens the straps and fixes a finger Hope breaks trying to free Nash. No great new elven expedition is thereby completed. The party warns the Harbor of **Diontalus's possible return**. It would be wrong to drag another enemy to the survivors' refuge, Stanley and Hope concede. Their better task is to move danger away and seek help elsewhere; the tieflings are **not evacuated at this point**.

**Emenil** offers another kind of help, entering the Harbor's lake for a rite of **Malhya**, the Fishbird. The four follow. After taking strange fish flakes they can breathe underwater and find themselves in an endless ocean. A huge kraken passes **without battle**. Then a joyous rainbow-scaled winged fish appears, recognizing prayers to Malhya. Stanley and Nash vanish into solitary darkness, alarming Vorel and Emenil, but **are returned alive**. At Emenil's appeal the visitor touches **Vorel and Stanley**, and the injuries Diontalus inflicted are healed. The Fishbird recognizes the danger of **the Eye of Groxl** yet does not disclose every ancient origin it alludes to. It knows no finished sealing device, but points toward an **engineer outside the gods' easy reach**, possibly known to Elhandar's High Elf. The person and place are still uncertain.[^five-12]

Hope brings the restored group back to **Villa Ornath**. Nash embraces Angelashi and the children; this time the whole household prepares to travel with them. **Stanley retrieves the study key**. With Lyrielle guiding, they walk **Rainbow Road** to Elhandar's core and leave the family safely at reception while the four enter the High Elf's chamber. The leader recounts his people's old efforts to imprison **Diontalus**, and offers to summon him. **The ghost appears**, and Stanley tries to offer a future beyond forced possession or carnage. At the same time, **the Voice privately orders Hope to kill Diontalus and seize his power**. She refuses twice. Neither the others nor Diontalus hear the Voice's words, and Hope does not use the suggested undoing phrase.

Stanley eventually persuades Diontalus to **return to his own Feywild court, wait for Stanley and help against the Principle**. The yes is real, but the bargain is ugly. Diontalus promises to attack the elves if Stanley never comes or dies before doing so; and **neither return to the court nor eventual aid is seen during the negotiation itself**. The archfey then hands Stanley a **dark wooden ocarina**, claiming its music will call him. He admits compelling the deer-disguised mages to serve him and claims the older ghost-pirate attacks were his doing, making the agreement no absolution of his crimes.

With a troubled alliance secured, Stanley asks what might be done for Earth. The High Elf names **Tungsten Wheel**, an absent Elhandari engineer, and says someone close to him at the **Mars terraforming platform** might know where he went. The party receives **Mars and Elhandar teleportation-circle glyphs**. That is a useful lead, not the engineer's discovery or a device assembled. Hope has been freed from her sphere, Stanley and Vorel are healed, and for the first time in many adventures the companions are able to approach an ambitious purpose together. Before they can leave the station to pursue it, an enemy has already found them.[^five-13]

## VII. The fall of Elhandar

As the four speak with the High Elf, a **castle accompanied by a fleet of airships** advances upon Elhandar. Cannons hammer the station. A purple-robed **three-headed mercenary**[^five-names] teleports an army into the audience chamber and demands **Hope and Vorel**. He says the **Mind Master Gerbken Shartlinger** has made them targets after the M-sports cheating affair and has betrayed Tobacho to the Lady of Pain. **Stanley answers with Destructive Wave**; the ensuing battle pits the four against mercenaries, ogres and magic, while the High Elf calls living trees into the chamber and turns the castle's cannonfire back on its source. **Diontalus uses his spectral blade and later tears through hostile ships**, actual help beyond his recent promise. Even with the defenders' powers, **Elhandar begins to fall**.[^five-14]

The High Elf urges Stanley to get the children to his father's protected cottage. Their journey is frantic. A spire elevator breaks loose; ***Feather Fall*** slows the occupants, but its roof continues to crush them until **Vorel cuts a way out** with help from the others. Across **Rainbow Road**, the company races on three noble speeders toward **Villa Ornath**. A great piece of the biodome drops toward them, and **Lyrielle casts Wall of Force** to protect the route. In the cottage the family, Chunks, Bok Bok and the companions pile into **the hidden upper study**, where seats and restraints appear. They buckle in as the world outside tears apart. Impact and darkness swallow the household.

Far away in **Hell**, **Steerious Business, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider** are called to account for losing Hope. **Brent** brings **Mysteriosa** to an infernal laboratory. He says he obtained **Squirts's soul** from a soul stream and arranged a new body. A fluid-filled pod drains and **a living-looking Squirts awakens**, named by Brent as the new captain assigned to find Hope. The kobold lost at Tietkalla has awakened far from her surviving child.[^five-15]

Amid the wreckage, **Villa Ornath has survived**. The hidden study's seats release the household; Lyrielle retrieves Chunks from a chandelier. Outside the magical refuge lie **injured Harbor tieflings**, who are said to have been brought to the house by elves. Rubble seals the entrances. Stanley examines his father's journal and the dark **Mask of Eltanfawr**. The cottage is not simply a home: **it is a disguised spelljammer**, capable, according to the instructions, of flying between worlds or shrinking with its occupants. Stanley **puts the mask on**, and the house strains under a first attempt at flight—but **four consecutive nights of attunement** are required and it does not lift. A temporary opening lets Vorel scout Earth's desert. Before a more orderly escape is possible, the mercenary's **burning castle falls into the remaining station wreckage**. A second impact spins the house and **kills several tiefling refugees**. The other household members and the children survive, but the losses are real.

Outside, **Diontalus** attacks the mercenary with a spectral scythe. The mercenary answers with an **electric guitar**, magical chords that batter the archfey into a crater. **Diontalus lies motionless in the crater**. The hunter brings his music to the four. **Six orbiting blue spheres intercept magic**, and the guitar seizes **Hope and Vorel**, compelling them to fight each other. Nash casts spells, then becomes a **black dragon**. His acid breath bypasses the spheres. **Stanley takes the guitar with Telekinesis**, breaking the commander's musical grip, but his test strum misfires into an orb-absorbed Fireball. Nash flies after the mercenary and destroys his hovering disc with another acid blast. **The mercenary falls limp onto the desert floor.**[^five-16]

The fallen mercenary is **alive, conscious and surrendering** when Nash lands beside him. He begs for his instrument, names the Mind Master as his employer, and complains that Diontalus's intervention was not part of the job. Stanley jabs the prisoner with **Diplomacy**, explicitly nonlethally. Nash instead tears off one of the mercenary's side heads. **The mercenary draws his last breath, killed after surrendering.** Later Nash removes a second head, while **Diontalus—revived when Stanley casts Healing Word in his crater—returns alive** to drain **visible blue soul-like energy** from the mercenary's dead body. He then walks away, not back into Stanley. Hope touches one fallen blue orb and is flung across the sand as it discharges and explodes; **the set of orbs is not collected**. With ***Speak with Dead***, Stanley hears the dead mercenary's version of the **Mind Master's anti-Vorel campaign**, his position at **Tobacho's chair in the Hive**, and a return route through the now-crashed castle's console. Even in death, their enemy has given them more threats to weigh.[^five-17]

**Elhandari rescue ships** then land among the wreckage. The grieving **High Elf** and **Admiral Chrysorius** recover survivors and offer the **Mars terraforming platform** as refuge. Rescuers recover the **natural dragon-metal heart** hidden in the ruined mountain, while Nash's engineered heart remains within him. Chrysorius arranges **four noble speeders on Villa Ornath's exterior**. **Hope directs surviving Harbor tieflings to board**; **Angelashi, all four children, Lyrielle and Bok Bok** do likewise. Nash promises Angelashi he will return and that she and the infants will be safer away from the party's hunters. She kisses his dragon cheek before boarding. The ships **depart bound for Mars with the survivors and heart**. The four companions remain on Earth as the ships leave for their distant refuge.[^five-18]

Hope, tormented by the cost of magic and violence, tries to recharge her ring by desecrating **the mercenary's corpse** and then a deceased refugee. **Neither act is sufficient**, and **no Gate opens**; Hope buries the tiefling in remorse. Elhandar has fallen, some of its people have escaped, and the party's families are once again far away. Yet the ruin also leaves them with an intact cottage on Earth, the rescued heart's known destination, and the possibility of finally choosing the world's defense themselves.[^five-19][^five-20]

## VIII. The Burrow and the night after

When the last Elhandari ships vanish into the blue, **Nash, Hope, Stanley and Vorel** are alone again among the shattered remains of their refuge. **Villa Ornath** has survived where the station did not. Vorel, exhausted by worlds built and then demolished, storms away to sleep in the hot desert; Hope begins burying the dead; Nash remains in black-dragon form. Stanley sits apart and cautiously plays **the mercenary's electric guitar**. The notes carry with supernatural clarity, but **do not unleash the destructive chords the mercenary used in battle**. For a few moments the sound is merely music.

A familiar voice behind Stanley praises it. The goat-man of **Château Weir**, the **Soulbroker**, has come to claim a conversation. **Only Stanley hears his words here.** The figure asserts that **Hope found divine annulment of her original bargain**, **Vorel never agreed**, and **Nash's engineered dragon heart meant the Soulbroker never owned his soul**. He now considers **Stanley alone bound** and warns that his soul remains forfeit should he fail. He describes an earlier pact with a man of Earth who became **the Principle**, a deal supposedly made during the dragons' devastation of the planet. The Principle, the Soulbroker says, used the powers he received to resist the Weir and ultimately tried to escape the price. With the Principle now seeking godhood, he has allegedly neglected old protections that kept the Weir from Earth. Stanley hears the judgment of the very power that stands to claim his soul.

The Soulbroker offers a vision that is itself a warning. The **Weir**, he claims, once resembled ordinary people, journeyed to a realm between creation and the Far Realm and learned to draw sustenance from life and soul energy. He calls the gods careless children and promises a new order in which the world will be remade under his wisdom. He assures Stanley that this change will restore Earth and deliver justice. Stanley sees a ruler offering humanity salvation on the condition that humanity become his fuel. **He still intends to oppose the Principle**, but no longer accepts that doing so must mean handing the world to the Soulbroker. The threat that binds him is not the same as consent to his patron's vision.

Then words give way to an act the others **also witness**. The Soulbroker raises his hands. A great **violet rent** opens across the sky, and an enormous black creature, its six digging limbs armored by moving, almost gearlike scales, drops toward the far desert. Its size defies ordinary comparison. It **burrows deep into Earth's sand** as the Soulbroker pronounces the planet's **“conversion” begun** and disappears. A vast **tsunami of sand** spreads outward under purple-pink lightning. The ground shakes and cracks. **Nash flies up in his black-dragon form and carries Hope, Stanley and Vorel above the storm**; Vorel, furious enough to challenge the oncoming wave, climbs aboard last. Far below, the creature's mechanical tail sinks into the planet. After minutes the wave passes, leaving the desert briefly quiet. **All four survive**, and **Villa Ornath remains upright**, banked by sand. The creature has not been destroyed or even located precisely underground. Whatever change has begun beneath the sand, the companions have yet to find a way to stop it.

Back on the ground, **Stanley tells his companions what the Soulbroker said**. Until this disclosure the others knew the rupture and storm, **not his private conversation or the alleged fate of their bargains**. **Hope also recounts the journeys she made while separated from them**: the Voice and its Hand, devils who offered assistance, Tobacho and the Lady of Pain, the Council of Clowns, Pandemonium and the hidden prison said to hold the other Eye. At last the friends can weigh their separate experiences together. Hope has accepted Brent's help, though she has signed no pact with him. Neither revelation puts the **Eye of the Voice** in their hands.[^five-21]

Their enemies are not one another now, but there is no simple choice among them. **The Mind Master** has sent armies and may send more; **the Principle** wants to reshape the world according to his own plans; the **Soulbroker and Weir** have begun entering it. **Hope's Gate ring is still uncharged**, its price apparently a genuinely harmful act, not the desecration of a corpse. In theory it might reach Sigil, but **no Gate is cast**. Vorel has supporters among M-sports fans who might reject the Mind Master's propaganda, so the company imagines turning his own influence against him—even putting Vorel in power as **“king of space.”** This is an idea, not a coronation or decision enacted that night.

The planet-saving plan remains just as incomplete. The dragon's **natural metal heart is on its way to Mars with the Elhandari**, while Nash's **manufactured heart stays in his body**. The machine they call **Ruby** has **not been built**. They must meet **Dunglestar** at the Mars terraforming platform to learn the whereabouts of **Tungsten Wheel**, the engineer who might construct it. **Stanley's Sending** reaches the High Elf but yields no shortcut: he is told to speak to Dunglestar himself. Villa Ornath may one day fly or shrink, but the mask **still requires more attunement nights**. They discuss resting here for the remaining time, a possible trip to Mars and perhaps dealing with the Mind Master first; **for now those journeys remain ahead of them**.[^five-22]

At last, the four turn toward the battered cottage and the promise of sleep. **Nash returns to his ordinary form**, the companions find space to sleep within the battered cottage and **complete an uneventful long rest**. Far overhead the Elhandari and their dependents travel toward Mars. Far below lies something that may be changing Earth beyond recognition. The four companions separated at White Tower are **together again on Earth**, alive, grieving, and faced with a choice of what kind of world to defend.[^five-23][^five-24][^five-25]
