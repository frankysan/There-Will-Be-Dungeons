# Ancient catastrophe — dragons, gates and the Scorching

**Browse:** [Historical events](HISTORICAL_EVENTS.md) · [World portal](README.md)

**In-world period:** Long before the central Wastes campaign; **no verified precise calendar**. **Sources:** GM prologue and later contested testimony. **Research:** ASR-provisional.

## Opening account

The [E000 GM prologue](../episodes/E000.md) portrays a very different, more prosperous Earth: **dragons arrive through gates**, humans respond with sophisticated machines and pacts, and a destructive conflict leaves a shattered world. Later inhabitants scavenge **dragon engines**, extraordinary artifacts and facilities from these ages. This establishes the campaign's broad frame, not a dated military chronology or complete account of who built the gates. [World reference](WORLD.md).

## Surviving evidence versus claims

Old engineering is genuinely encountered beneath [Dusthill](places/dusthill.md), at the [Leopold facility](../episodes/E008.md), in [Tietkalla](places/tietkalla.md) and especially [CHIDS](places/chids.md). CHIDS contains **actual bodies, conditioning controls and a huge Drive apparatus**, demonstrating local ancient experimentation but not validating every claimed origin of dragons, lizardfolk, the Principle or godlike beings. [E073](../episodes/E073.md) · [E077](../episodes/E077.md) · [E079](../episodes/E079.md).

Later accounts differ: the spectral dragon suggests a **planet-moving machine**, the hive council claims human-mind archives and anti-dragon experiments, Quizquaz proposes Void mechanics, and Stanley's father describes prospective future-Weir-induced time adjacency in a journal found E118. Each is **attributed source testimony or theorizing**, not settled global history. [E067](../episodes/E067.md) · [E078](../episodes/E078.md) · [E081](../episodes/E081.md) · [E118](../episodes/E118.md).

**Open:** dates/duration, gate builders, actual dragon-war participants, nature of the Scorching, planet/Drive engineering and the competing accounts of the Weir. [Chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md) · [Uncertainties](CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
