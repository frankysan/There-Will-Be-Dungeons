# Hope Akmenos

**Also reported as:** Oriphi “Hope” Akmenos (fan-wiki form; spoken orthography unverified). **Portrayed by:** Kristen. **Identity:** Tiefling engineer, adventurer, **Queen of Tietkalla** and later bearer of the living **Hand of the Voice**. **Spoilers:** Through E128 (Season One finale) and retrospective cast Q&As. **Evidence:** Source-linked processed ASR; individual claims not audio-verified unless specified.

## E128 — evacuation, conscience and shared revelations

In [E128](../../episodes/E128.md), Hope searches the dead three-headed bounty hunter and **touches an orbiting orb**, which **shocks and flings her into the sand and self-destructs**; the full set is **not collected**. She **orders the surviving Harbor tieflings** to leave the exposed crash site with the Elhandari ships bound for Mars. **Nash's household and Lyrielle also actually depart**, while Hope and the other original adventurers remain on Earth.

Hope explores charging her powerful **evil-act-triggered Gate ring** by carving symbols in already-dead **Henatar's body** and later a deceased tiefling refugee. The GM **rules neither desecration recharges the ring**; **no Gate, live victim or successful sacrificial energy is played**. She **buries and mourns** the refugee. The party then witnesses a massive Weir-linked burrower from a **purple sky rift** and flees the sand tsunami **on dragon-form Nash**. After Stanley reveals his private Soulbroker conversation, Hope **actually tells the rest of the party** about the previously separate **Hand of the Voice, Eye, Sigil, Pandemonium, devils and Council of Clowns** quests, including offers of support from **Brent/Asmodeus-associated forces**. A table correction says there was **not necessarily a separate binding contract with Brent**; the disclosed **offers** do not automatically become accepted pacts. Hope suggests confronting the Mind Master and weighs the risks of assembling the Eye, **but these remain debate, not completed quests**. All four **complete rest and reach level 13**. ## E126 — refugees, blocked teleport and involuntary battle

**Hope survives the first Elhandar impact** and actively helps injured **Harbor tieflings** sheltered in Villa Ornath. During another impact, **several tieflings die**, but the immediate household survives. After **Diontalus is visibly beaten into the desert**, Hope tries to **Teleport herself and Vorel** close to Henatar/Henitar Grape: **the attempted spell is obstructed and no teleport occurs**. She charges, resists an initial stunning orb, then suffers the guitar's **compulsion to attack the nearest creature**, leading to involuntary fighting with Vorel. The enchantment ceases after Stanley seizes the guitar. **This is magical coercion, not a voluntary change of loyalty or an attack on her subjects**. The bounty hunter falls limp after Nash's later breath, not yet confirmed dead. [E126](../../episodes/E126.md) · [Villa](../../world/places/villa-ornath.md). **ASR-only**.

## Character, ambitions and loyalties

Hope begins in **Dusthill** as a resourceful mechanic whose **Fate 4** vehicle becomes a central part of the company's journeys. She hopes to use powerful old technology to alter a tragic past, but **wishing for time travel is not proof she has achieved it**. She becomes bound to the **Soulbroker compact** in E023 alongside Nash and Stanley (Vorel refuses), and later learns of a supposed connection to the old tiefling royal house **Akmenos**. The elder's full genealogy is *his testimony*, while Hope's actual coronation is directly played. [E001](../../episodes/E001.md) · [E006](../../episodes/E006.md) · [E023](../../episodes/E023.md) · [Tietkalla history](../../world/HISTORY_TIETKALLA.md).

Her body changes substantially over the campaign. After a catastrophic rebound of a **rocket in E038–E039**, Hope loses limbs and uses mechanical replacements and arm-mounted weapons. She later gains specialized **Fox and Scully firearms**, a **Broom of Flying**, appearance-changing magic and—much later—the **Hand of the Voice**. **These are different objects with different custody histories**, and the living Hand is neither **Zorander's Fist gauntlet** nor Vorel's Eye of Groxl. [E038](../../episodes/E038.md)–[E039](../../episodes/E039.md) · [E050](../../episodes/E050.md) · [Artifacts](../../world/ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md).

In a **June 2019 retrospective Q&A**, Kristen discusses the idea that Hope might eventually become powerful enough to **reach godhood and confront or destroy gods**. This is a developing **player ambition**, not accomplished ascension, confirmed godslaying or automatically an in-world declaration heard by the other characters. [June Q&A](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-06.md).

## From mechanic to queen (E001–E059)

Hope travels with **Nash, Stanley and Vorel** through Dusthill, Slavetown, the Black Mine and the contested settlements near **Tietkalla**. She resists exploitative power yet sometimes makes harsh choices of her own. The underground water-throne journey involves **Gem of Tides**, **Kefeli** and the bloody collapse of the Tietkalla power structure. **The party recovers the Gem in E050**, but the exact installation and sustained water restoration remain uncertain. In **E054**, the elder **Nobieri** arrives with actual armed tieflings; **Hope is publicly crowned queen**. She also frees **Angelashi alive**, contrary to earlier testimony that Angelashi had died. [E050](../../episodes/E050.md)–[E054](../../episodes/E054.md) · [Gem](../../world/technology/gem-of-tides.md) · [Nobieri](../../world/characters/nobieri.md).

After **three in-story months before E055**, she has initiated labor and sanitation reforms, rebuilt portions of city infrastructure and challenged the old serf-like social hierarchy. Yet shortages, rival noble houses and political violence remain. Hope orders **Softhead's tongue removed**, and people are executed under her authority; these actions must not be airbrushed away as a perfectly benevolent reign. A deadly **stonefall** threatens civilians, and her diplomacy with the **Principle** creates disputed trade and railroad prospects. **Actual Dusthill survivors return E059**, but that is not evidence every slave is freed or that a railway was already operational then. [E055](../../episodes/E055.md)–[E059](../../episodes/E059.md) · [The Principle](../../world/characters/the-principle.md).

## Razak Val, CHIDS and the shared sacrifice (E060–E081)

In **Razak Val**, Hope supports **Vorel's Great Fang challenge**, but her **rocket attack redirects through Vorel** and contributes to a feast catastrophe that kills named leaders and bystanders. The blast's victims and Omnom's later allegations are central to her moral story; her own claim about why she fired is not the same thing as Omnom's accusation of intent. She later offers shelter in Tietkalla to **Follow, Rorf and Sira**, but **Follow and Rorf die E066**, and Sira is abducted instead of reaching refuge. A T. rex **actually crushes and destroys Fate 4 in E066**; the improvised later rickshaw is not the repaired original vehicle. [E060](../../episodes/E060.md)–[E066](../../episodes/E066.md) · [Razak Val](../../world/places/razak-val.md) · [Omnom](../../world/characters/omnom-cinderborn.md).

At **Kervalon/CHIDS** Hope survives mind-flayer attacks, treacherous transport, spiders and hazardous biological containment. She **kills the giant spider matriarch with shotgun shots E074**, rather than it dying permanently from Stanley's earlier sheep transformation. She gains the **portable red/green portal apparatus E075** and **actually uses it to escape attackers E076**. Later the device demonstrates a dangerous alternate **necrotic-beam** setting, distinct from its paired wall gateways. Hope also acquires **two rockets E076** and expends **both during the E079 Drive battle**. The artifact and resulting limitations are documented separately. [E068](../../episodes/E068.md)–[E079](../../episodes/E079.md) · [Portal device](../../world/technology/portal-device.md).

**E081** concludes the CHIDS confrontation: Hope **destroys the manifested Dolo Popio**, and Sira escapes the Drive beam with Eddie alive. The original four **choose to share the dangerous crimson beam rather than destroy Earth**, vanish into light and are **GM-declared dead at E081's cutoff**. This is a played sacrifice, not proof that the planet is undamaged forever, that a soul contract is canceled, or that all four stay dead. [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [CHIDS history](../../world/HISTORY_CHIDS_DRIVE.md).

## Sigil restoration and White Tower (E082–E101)

**E082** shows Hope's **inert, apparently dead body** collected in Sigil after the sacrifice. **E088** actually restores her through a mirror rite; the original inert body remains on the slab. She is **alive afterward** and gains organic limbs; resurrection is a consequential event, not a refutation of the earlier GM death declaration or proof the Weir obligation is void. Later cast Q&A describes its contingency on the party's effort rather than unconditional immunity from death. [E082](../../episodes/E082.md) · [E088](../../episodes/E088.md) · [March 2020 bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-03.md).

She searches for an Earth portal in Sigil, crosses paths with **Tobacho Fleur** and the mysterious **Zorander's Fist** and obtains new magical means of travel. Her **Scully firearm** has several custody changes, including recovery and transfer; a pistol reported missing at one episode cutoff must not be restored to her inventory without an actual scene. At **White Tower** she is repelled by a protective ward on the first infiltration attempt, then becomes involved in the siege and a private **hooded Voice**. While a voice or charm seems to influence her, **her decisions and injuries are still events performed in play**, not proof someone else controlled every act. [E089](../../episodes/E089.md)–[E100](../../episodes/E100.md) · [White Tower](../../world/places/white-tower.md).

**In E101**, Hope severs her right hand and **fuses the living Hand of the Voice to her body**, receiving extraordinary power at a serious spiritual/moral cost. The **separate Fist gauntlet remains behind with Nash, Stanley and Vorel**, and the Eye of Groxl is restored to Vorel by the patron's intervention. Hope **chooses to leave her original companions** through a portal. This is **both the ending of Arc Four and opening of Arc Five**, not the middle of one unchanged group adventure. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [Voice](../../world/characters/the-voice.md) · [Hand](../../world/technology/hand-of-the-voice.md).

## Hope's separate path (E101–E116)

At the **Kaiser Casino**, Hope encounters a host calling himself **Asmodeus (“Brett”)**. In E101 he offers collaboration and access, but **she does not yet sign a formal bargain**. In E102 he introduces her new companions—**Steerious Business, Blood Skunk Hendrickson and Dr. Spider**—and she **Gates back to Tietkalla** with them. The party is physically divided; its two branches must be followed independently. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [E102](../../episodes/E102.md) · [Kaiser Casino](../../world/places/kaiser-casino.md).

Hope returns to an **inhabited Tietkalla E103**, encounters **Celibus Stump's acting chancellorship** and **sets the temporarily closed, empty railway station on fire** with her group. A blue fissure and rain are observed; the station's eventual fate and political consequences are not settled. The **hooded Voice accepts the fire E104**, but the destruction of Tietkalla soon escalates when **General Carthax's forces attack**. Hope opens a Gate addressed to Brett and **flees with her companions**, rather than being shown commanding the demonic invaders. **Omnom later accuses her of deliberately drawing the army**; he does not provide an omniscient verdict. [E103](../../episodes/E103.md) · [E104](../../episodes/E104.md) · [Tietkalla history](../../world/HISTORY_TIETKALLA.md).

In **E106**, Hope meets the **Principle/Vladimir** at Kaiser Casino and hears claims about ancient human processing and the Lich Project, some performed via Brett's illusions. Hope **agrees to cooperate**, then is directed toward the **Eye of the Voice**. Her group returns to Sigil; **Dr. Spider kills Spatulo** during a conflict, and the refugees accompanying the earlier Gate are **not among the four who later leave**. This Eye is **not Vorel's Eye of Groxl**, nor is it presently in Hope's possession. [E106](../../episodes/E106.md) · [The Principle](../../world/characters/the-principle.md).

In **E112**, Hope **signs Tobacho's written compact**, promising to help depose the **Lady of Pain** in exchange for an Eye lead and bounty terms. **No overthrow or Eye acquisition occurs**. She and her new party **actually traverse a House of Delights portal into Gray Waste**. In **E113**, they cross to Pointless, pay **Hesterbrook's River Styx ferry** (Hope relinquishes **Fox**, not Scully), and **reach Pandemonium**. The **Council of Clowns** gives Hope a **compass and a claimed prison lead near Pentelvice/Pentel Weiss (?)**. The E113 ending shows a Gate back to Tobacho's Sigil bar; **E116 replays the cutoff and has Hope instead decline that portal**. These are **different presentation/cutoff versions**, not proof she performed an unseen round trip. [E112](../../episodes/E112.md) · [E113](../../episodes/E113.md) · [E116](../../episodes/E116.md) · [Planar route](../../world/places/gray-waste-and-pandemonium.md).

After the altered E116 departure, the four try to **seize a Sigil cargo vessel** but withdraw without capturing it; **Blood Skunk survives** despite severe injury. From **Dr. Spider's laboratory in Hell**, Hope **casts Gate calling “Nash Maggard of the Solar Mines,” and Nash actually arrives**. This is a real reunion, not merely a promise to find him. She accompanies Nash toward Elhandar and the old group. [E116](../../episodes/E116.md) · [Nash](../../world/characters/nash-maggard.md).

## Elhandar, the Harbor and the father's study (E117–E118)

**E117:** Hope physically reunites with **Nash, Stanley and Vorel** at **Elhandar**, encountering Angelashi and Nash's **four changeling children**. **Stanley distrusts Hope**, objects to the attached Hand, cites her responsibility for Tietkalla's deaths and **does not forgive her**. Hope discusses her actions publicly before refugees from Tietkalla and **names their new community “the Harbor”**. Nobieri conducts a fire rite, while the deeper theological meanings remain uncertain. Naming Harborers is a **real founding/declaration**, not a reconstructed city on Earth. [E117](../../episodes/E117.md) · [Harbor](../../world/places/the-harbor.md).

In **E118**, severely scarred **Omnom** physically confronts Hope and attempts to attack her. **Stanley's Hold Person stops him**, so Hope is **not struck**. Omnom accuses her of killing kin through the redirected rocket, burning the railway station and **deliberately inviting demons**. Hope **acknowledges firing the rocket and the fire**, but says the **Hand attracted demons** and that she fled hoping they would follow her away from the city. Neither her explanation nor Omnom's accusation should be silently elevated to settled secret history. The company **plans**, but does not undertake, a joint hunt with Omnom. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Omnom](../../world/characters/omnom-cinderborn.md).

Later Hope helps Stanley interpret his father's childhood poem and **actually enters the hidden third-floor study with him**. The ornate key, **Book of Adjacencies** and **Mask of Eltanfawr (earlier “Elton IV”)** pass into **Stanley's** inventory, not hers. As she leaves, the hooded **Voice privately appears** and presses her toward **Pentelvice**, causing her attached Hand to hurt. **Hope does not Gate to the prison, acquire the Eye, or remove the Hand by the E118 cutoff**. [E118](../../episodes/E118.md) · [Stanley](../../world/characters/stanley-billings.md) · [Book](../../world/technology/book-of-adjacencies.md).

## Relationships, governance and open questions

**Nash, Stanley and Vorel** are longstanding companions, but reconciliation after the E101 break remains incomplete. **Tedna** dies in E050, **Angelashi** survives, and **Nobieri** supplies real soldiers and a contested claim of royal ancestry. **Steerious, Blood Skunk and Dr. Spider** are individually acting allies, not extensions of Hope's mind. **The Voice** is a supernatural interlocutor with observable influence, not a certified identity or justification for every act. [Tedna](../../world/characters/tedna.md) · [Nobieri](../../world/characters/nobieri.md) · [Voice](../../world/characters/the-voice.md).

Hope's major items include her changing **mechanical/organic limbs**, vanished **Fate 4**, **Fox/Scully** guns with different transfers, **Broom of Flying**, **Necklace of Infinite Appearances**, travel rings, **the attached Hand**, and the separate quest for the unpossessed **Eye of the Voice**. Custody and expended devices must remain episode-specific. The **Gem of Tides** recovery does not establish uninterrupted civic water service, and a proposed pact cannot be mistaken for completed fulfillment. [Artifacts](../../world/ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [Consistency ledger](../../world/CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).

**E119:** Hope joins **Vorel, Nash, Stanley, Omnom and Dilo** for the real Elhandar hunt. **Fitz of the Harbor actually recognizes her as queen** and shares berries; she feeds two rations' worth of nuts to the speaking squirrels. She travels with the party into the northern forest but **does not summon demons, acquire the Eye of the Voice, attack Omnom or complete reconciliation**. [E119](../../episodes/E119.md).

**E120:** Hope **pulls Chester down from a tree using its entrails**; the impact **kills the huge animal**, but she is **pinned alive beneath it until Vorel lifts the body**. She later receives misleading thanks from six illusory deer, **stabs a revealed hostile elf in the throat**, is blinded by blood, grapples another, and is briefly made to act like a squirrel by ***Mass Suggestion*** (ended by subsequent damage). **Dilo and an elf mage deliberately abduct her in a sealed floating sphere** while Stanley is preparing ***Destructive Wave***. In her separate played viewpoint she **remains conscious and tries *Teleport* via the Hand**, but **the spell fails and consumes the slot**; Dilo brings the sphere into a mountain cave. **She is not freed, killed, returned to her companions, or shown reaching Pentelvice/Eye**. [E120](../../episodes/E120.md).

She imagines potential branches in which Hope **embraces the Voice's agenda** or **turns against and kills her patron**. Beau discusses the struggle over a person's soul as dramatic potential. **Neither alignment choice, godslaying, patron defeat nor redemption is played in the bonus**, and E120 still ends with Hope **alive but abducted in Dilo's sphere**. [Bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2020-10.md).

**E121 — still imprisoned:** Hope is conscious inside the **sealed floating sphere**, which her captor fills with **multiple empathy grenades**. She examines one with engineering skills, identifies **red/white/green wires**, and **tries white**, triggering a blinding flash rather than release. She later disarms some grenades but **another detonates**, and stabbing/scratching the sphere **does not break it**. Stanley's ***Dispel Magic*, controller seizure and broken wand do not free her**. She sees Diontalus's conflict only through the soundproof sphere and remains airborne as a black dragon throws the sphere about. **She has not been made permanently catatonic, killed, released, or transported to the Voice's Eye**. [E121](../../episodes/E121.md).

**E122 — free, informed secondhand and playing a trick:** Stanley **presses the detonator**, ending Hope's sphere; she lands unharmed on a **20 Dexterity save**, and roughly forty grenades fall without detonating. Nash **recaps the dragon's account**, which she could not hear while enclosed. She gathers **one of each available mushroom type that fits her bag** for the tieflings, without testing them, and joins the four at the **Gatherers' Lodge**. With her **appearance-changing necklace** she impersonates an elf and then Nash, pretending the ghost custodians' sweets transformed her. **No candy transformation or actual future Nash is established**. She grapples Nash in the agreed play fight; at cutoff Stanley/Vorel are forcing bonbons toward his mouth. [E122](../../episodes/E122.md).

**E123 — reconciliation, piloting and the Harbor decision:** Hope privately tells Stanley that the Hand's forgotten/dead god speaks to her and **she believes removal would kill her**, then offers an **unperformed blood-cost use of a travel ring** to find his father. Stanley apologizes; she recounts bereavement and survival, with a connection-lagged passage **repeated rather than a second event**. She **crashes the noble speeder on first activation**, then successfully tests it with Stanley and **pilots all four back to the Harbor**, towing Nash. She **hands mushrooms to Nobieri**, breaks a finger while trying to untie Nash and has it **mended without HP restoration**. She gives cave directions/permission for a small inquiry; no outcome yet. After Emenil objects to evacuation, **Hope agrees the refugees should keep their home** and supports Stanley seeking Fishbird guidance. [E123](../../episodes/E123.md).

**E124 — a private refusal during a public negotiation:** Hope joins the lake rite and sees Malhya cure Stanley/Vorel. Nobieri **gives her a cellstone**. She pilots the speeder to the villa, then the household leaves it there and reaches the core by foot/Rainbow Road. **Alone in a headphone-separated scene (~02:15–02:18)** she twice refuses the Voice's command to kill Diontalus and take his essence. It gives her an uncertain supposed **undoing phrase**, **not spoken to the target or tested**. Stanley's later accord is a separate shared negotiation; its aid/departure remain promises. [E124](../../episodes/E124.md).

**E125 — bounty, battle, family flight:** A new **three-headed mercenary commander** invades Elhandar and **reports a contract from Gerbken Shartlinger targeting Hope and Vorel**; he does not clearly specify which target must die.
