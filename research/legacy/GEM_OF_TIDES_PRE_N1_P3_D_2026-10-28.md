# Gem of Tides

**Browse:** [Items and artifacts](../ITEMS_AND_ARTIFACTS.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Role:** Blue gem associated with Tietkalla's old water/oil transformation system. **Observed custody:** Recovered by the party **E050**. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional.

The party searches old chambers beneath the Tietkalla keep and eventually **physically retrieves the Gem in E050** after the ambush and deaths around the throne conflict. E051 explores the old water-throne installation. The artifact's retrieval is an established played event; **full installation, functioning water conversion and long-term civic restoration are not all independently demonstrated**. [E049](../../episodes/E049.md)–[E051](../../episodes/E051.md) · [Tietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md).

**Not to confuse with:** Dusthill's separate underground pump and dragon engine, the Eye of Groxl, or the Soulbroker key. **Open:** exact mechanism, current installed location, later ownership and sustained public impact. [Ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
