## Story routes — not a surveyed map

| Period | Principal path |
| --- | --- |
| **Arc One** | Dusthill → desert/Leopold → Slavetown → Black Mine → Château Weir → Liberators' refuge |
| **Arc Two** | Golden Land/oil sands → Trashburg and Tietkalla keep → underground throne and war → Hope's coronation |
| **Arc Three** | Tietkalla → Razak Val → loss of Fate 4 → Kervalon → CHIDS → Traversal Drive |
| **Arc Four** | Sigil → Hive investigations → Traveler's Twin/Outlands → White Tower → Hope departs inside E101 |
| **Arc Five through E126** | Parallel journeys through Sigil, Hell, Gray Waste/Pandemonium, devastated Tietkalla, an alternate Nash's asteroid and **Elhandar** |
| **Historical prequel** | **Dunebreaker E107–E109**, played **long before Dusthill**, though published between E106 and E110 |

The route arrows are **narrative transitions**, not claims that every transfer was direct, land-based or measured. Source dossiers distinguish *visited*, *seen remotely*, *proposed*, *reported* and *actually reached*. A Gray Waste portal proposed in E112 is not an E112 visit to Pandemonium; the party physically reaches Pandemonium in E113.

## Editorial boundary

**T'ietkalla** has an exact on-map spelling and **Tietkalla** has established cast-written support; retain both for search. Related **Teet/Trashburg** do not always denote precisely the same administrative area. The large map also prints **the Principal's Lands**; The canonical antagonist is **the Principal**; the old `the-principle.md` path and historic `Principle` spelling remain compatibility/search aliases, not an alternate approved name. [New map source audit](../reports/WASTES_KNOWN_MAP_ORTHOGRAPHY_2026-10-27.md). **Dunebreaker** is not a contemporaneous Arc Five ship. The separate *Inverse* campaign and E043 holiday special are **not** physical regions in the Wastes timeline. Handout/map orthography may require checking against original imagery.
