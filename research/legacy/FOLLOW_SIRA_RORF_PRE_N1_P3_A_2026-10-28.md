# Follow/Follo, Sira/Sera and Rorf — a persecuted traveling group

> **Working spellings:** `Follow`/`Follo` (ASR); `Sira`/`Sera`/`Sierra`; `Rorf`/`Worf`/`Rolf` (ASR). No creator-written character sheet or audio verification yet. First presence: [E063](../../episodes/E063.md). **Spoilers through E081.** Three individuals, not interchangeable identities or confirmed biological relatives.

## Follow — telepathy, influence, and an escape plot

In [E063](../../episodes/E063.md) (~02:30–02:53), **Stanley** interviews a surprisingly composed **legless, dark-skinned prisoner** on a **wheeled mobility base/cart**, physically healthier than the **Sun's Witnesses** sharing the pit. He says he once led a traveling troupe, and Stanley perceives a vague magical impression and keeps him separately for questioning. This **first-person story is not an established objective biography**.

In [E064](../../episodes/E064.md) (~00:20–00:46), Follow **speaks inside minds**, recognizes the young disappearing girl Stanley saw at the E063 duel, and claims the **Sun's Witnesses have strange internal thoughts**. He points to one as abnormal, after which Vorel's repeated call to Dollo Poppio triggers a horrific transformation. **Follow is last seen escaping the chamber**, later reappearing at **Fate 4** with the girl and a large orc. His powers are **depicted telepathy and claims of selective mind perception**, not grounds to assume all his predictions or suspicions were objectively accurate.

In [E065](../../episodes/E065.md) (~00:12–00:43), Follow says his group had a sheltering settlement named **Home**, lost to gobbear attackers, before lizardfolk hunters ambushed the survivors as **mutants** and sold/captured Follow as food for Orp. Follow says he can **compel people through mental influence**, and his associates used disguises, invisibility and killings to make clan challengers eligible to fight Orp and free Follow. His moral responsibility is not erased by their persecution. Hope offers refuge in **Tietkalla**, which Follow **accepts in principle**, but **they have not been resettled** by the end of E065.

## Sira/Sera — the quiet girl

The young girl **literally vanishes and reappears** near Stanley in [E063](../../episodes/E063.md), then at [E064](../../episodes/E064.md)'s Fate 4 doorway. In E065 (~00:18–00:31), Follow introduces her as **his daughter**, attributing **invisibility** to her. Her **ear tips are visibly scarred/cut**; Follow says former human guardians **mutilated her pointed elf ears** and abandoned her. **Stanley addresses her in Elvish and she initially responds nonverbally**, demonstrating comprehension. **Later in E065 (~01:48), she herself asks Stanley a question in Elvish about songs**, despite Follow's earlier suggestion that she barely speaks. Her genealogy and guardianship are not independently confirmed. She carries a **doll** and enjoys Stanley's sung/hummed old melody (~01:45? see [E065 index](../../episodes/E065.md) around 01:48) within Tiny Hut. She may be **a protected dependent**, not an assumed conscious participant in every operation adults carried out.

**Do not merge with Nash's dream-child:** the [July-archived bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-07.md) (~00:23–00:25) explicitly raises a comparison with an earlier little girl in **Nash's Solar Mines vision**. Scott had not made it; Beau **tentatively doubts they are the same child**, without providing a full canonical identity.

## Rorf — shapeshifter, driver, confessed killer

In [E064](../../episodes/E064.md)'s **played post-credit scene** (~02:49–02:57), **Rorf** is the enormous dark-skinned orc **driving the party's Fate 4**, having hotwired it, torn out the driving seat and damaged the interior to accommodate his body. He accompanies Follow and Sira.

In [E065](../../episodes/E065.md) (~00:22–00:27), **Rorf himself admits killing Bramie/Baymie Fengar**. Follow says they attacked eldest heirs so rival lizardfolk could issue scaling challenges to **Orp** and create an opening for Follow's rescue; he also claims Rorf **shapeshifts into small animals or lizardfolk** for stealth. Rorf's **confession is a direct in-character admission**, stronger than Vorel's mistaken E063 speculation against Zercil, but the precise murder mechanism/number of killings and his power limits remain unverified. During E065 **Rorf is frightened by the rare downpour**, makes rough progress at driving and eventually lets Hope retake the wheel. He refuses to join demon hunting and wants safety for their group.

## Evidence and editorial status

- **Three distinct people:** mind-sensing/influence belongs to **Follow**, invisibility attributed to **Sira**, shapeshifting and Bramie confession to **Rorf**. Do **not** project all abilities or all culpability onto every member.
- **Different evidence tiers:** observable disappearance, spoken telepathy, the written confession **as recognized by ASR** vs Follow's biographical claims vs GM tentative judgment on dream identity.
- **Political context:** the trio's efforts to free Follow contributed to a succession crisis but **are not identical to the alleged future-Nash theft of the Fengar egg**. Rorf's admitted murder was not solved by the E061 investigation.
- **Live status:** alive aboard/near **Fate 4** at E065 cutoff as the giant T. rex approaches; **no completed travel to Tietkalla**, no proof their reputation is safe there.
- **Review:** listen to E063 ~02:30–02:53; E064 ~00:20–00:47 and ~02:49–02:57; E065 ~00:08–00:43, ~01:40–01:50 and end. Seek official/cast spelling.

## E066 — abducted daughter, two deaths

While the party camps during the storm, **Sira disappears from Stanley's Tiny Hut**. A floating **mind flayer identifying itself approximately as Oubvayx/Ovaxiad** later holds **Sira aloft** as leverage against Hope, threatening Tietkalla. **Rorf shapeshifts into a large bird and attacks; the creature violently compresses him repeatedly and kills him outright**. **Follow levitates to save his daughter**, is hurled down with severe wounds, and **dies while asking Stanley to rescue Sira**. Stanley promises, and the companions **bury Follow**. **Sira is abducted alive as last shown**, carried away by the mind flayer toward **Kervalon**, not successfully resettled in Tietkalla. Their previously accepted sanctuary agreement **never culminates in arrival**. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md).

**Live status through E067:** **Follow dead E066; Rorf dead E066; Sira kidnapped E066 and not yet rescued by E067.** Sira's identity and abilities should not be merged with Nash's earlier dream child; the [July bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2019-07.md) supplies only a tentative GM judgment that they are different.

## Later arc outcome — Sira freed, Follow and Rorf remain dead (E081)

**Follow and Rorf remain dead after E066**; no revival is observed. **Sira stays abducted during the Kervalon and CHIDS descent**, but at the end of [E081](../../episodes/E081.md), after **Hope physically defeats Dolo Popio's manifested body**, the girl is **seen in the central Traversal Drive beam with Stanley and Eddie**. **Quizquaz enables egress**. Sira **leaves invisibly on Stanley's insistence**, then **reappears alive beside Eddie** outside the beam. She thanks Stanley, says goodbye, and **Eddie promises to protect her**. The girl **does not join the four protagonists' final absorption attempt**.

**Final E081 source cutoff:** **Sira is no longer trapped or abducted by the illithids' Drive machinery**; she is **alive outside with Eddie**. Her arrival in Tietkalla, long-term safety and the fate of her promised guardian after the beam event remain unshown. **Stanley, Hope, Nash and Vorel vanish into white light and the GM declares them dead**, with their permanence/afterlife unknown. Do not transfer the finale's deaths to Sira or revive Follow/Rorf by inference. [E066](../../episodes/E066.md) · [E081](../../episodes/E081.md) · [Consistency ledger](../CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md).
