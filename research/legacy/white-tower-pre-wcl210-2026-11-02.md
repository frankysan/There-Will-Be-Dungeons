# White Tower — besieged stronghold in the Outlands

**Browse:** [Places](../PLACES.md) · [World portal](../README.md)

**Type:** Celestial/Euphemian-affiliated fortress reached by the protagonists in **Arc Four**. **Spoilers:** E093–E101. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional.

## Approach and siege

The party observes the distant White Tower from the **Traveler's Twin** in E093. A besieging force and hostile aircraft are visibly present. In **E094** the group tries to drop into the tower: **Stanley, Nash and Vorel** use *Dimension Door* to enter; **Hope is expelled by a protective ward** and must find a different way. The Traveler's Twin's apparent crash and the captain's later repair report should not be conflated into an irreversible loss. [E093](../../episodes/E093.md) · [E094](../../episodes/E094.md).

## Plague and restricted library

**E095** shows an afflicted garrison, Knight Commander **Alentris Vayne (?)**, the gravely ill clergy and a tiered underground library. **E096** actually shows a successful soup/ritual cure spreading to infected survivors, with earlier deaths not reversed. In **E097** the previously barred restricted library opens by combined physical force; Alentris rescinds the execution sentence and a rooftop extraction plan is discussed. It is not an already completed departure at that cutoff. [E095](../../episodes/E095.md)–[E097](../../episodes/E097.md).

## The E101 break

The tower's crisis culminates in **E101**, when **Hope takes the living Hand of the Voice** and leaves the original company; her departure defines the boundary between Arcs Four and Five **inside that one episode**. The ornate **Fist gauntlet** is kept separately in a sealed chest afterward. Neither the tower ward nor its library establishes the true identity or purposes of every supernatural power involved. [E101](../../episodes/E101.md) · [Hope](../characters/hope-akmenos.md).
