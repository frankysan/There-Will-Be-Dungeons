# Kefeli / Kepheli (?)

**Role:** Serpent-haired claimant to Tietkalla's old water throne and contested ancient witness. **Names:** Orthography unresolved. **Last played physical disposition:** Petrified and destroyed in **E051**. **Research:** ASR-provisional.

Kefeli appears in the ancient chambers beneath the Tietkalla keep. Her power is not just rumor: she **petrifies Hope and Vorel**, who are later **restored alive within E051**. In the confrontation she herself is petrified and destroyed. These observed events differ from her explanation of why she changed. [E050](../../episodes/E050.md) · [E051](../../episodes/E051.md) · [Tietkalla](../places/tietkalla.md).

She asserts ancient royal and protective motives tied to **Andreeste/the Nourisher**, a vampire-cursed council and a threat to tieflings. **Monsoon** supplies a competing account and later warns of the consequences of her death, but the relevant ancient pacts, genealogy and purported divine claims remain contested.
