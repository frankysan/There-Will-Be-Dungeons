# The Wastes — world reference

A **subject-centered encyclopedia** for *The Wastes*: characters, antagonists, societies, places, objects and historical events. **Complete main-campaign spoilers through S3E18, which concludes cross-season Arc Six and The Wastes; S3E019 is a separate cast Q&A, not story**; the separately published E107–E109 *Dunebreaker* story takes place **long before E001**. The written research is based on processed ASR, **not independently verified by listening**. *Inverse* is [a separate reality](../../../../../../inverse/README.md).

**Completed-campaign editorial cleanup:** The [Wastes consolidation plan](../../../../../../project/WASTES_CAMPAIGN_CLEANUP_PLAN.md) and [migration manifest](../../../../../../project/WASTES_CLEANUP_MANIFEST.md) cover pending consistency, missing subjects and narrative restructuring. The **Races** domain (for intelligent peoples such as Lizardfolk, Tieflings, Lactids and Harlequinari) is **planned but not yet published**; do not infer that currently absent race pages are finished. New reference writing follows the [owner-confirmed canonical names](../../../../../../project/DECISIONS.md#owner-confirmed-wastes-map-orthography-2026-10-27), including **The Principal**, **Khaervilon**, **T'ietkalla** and the **Or'Nath** family names. Older path spellings remain search and link aliases.

- **Research uncertainty:** [Consistency ledger](../../../../../../world/CONSISTENCY_LEDGER.md), [review queue](../../../../../../project/REVIEW_QUEUE.md) and [editorial decisions](../../../../../../project/DECISIONS.md).
- **Machine-readable seed:** [entities.json](../../../../../../world/entities.json) and [schema](../../../../../../world/ENTITY_SCHEMA.md) are **not complete, synchronized encyclopedic data**.

[2026-10-23 reorganization report](../../../../../../reports/WORLD_REORGANIZATION_2026-10-23.md).

**Reference policy:** The [world narrative style directive](../../../../../../project/WORLD_NARRATIVE_STYLE.md) governs ordinary `world/` articles: **in-universe prose from first introduction, through arc developments, to current status**, with **episode citations, transcription uncertainty and editorial notes in Markdown footnotes**, not the main text. Preserve distinctions between witnessed actions, character testimony and later revelations; never invent canon or erase source conflicts. [Nash's spellcasting register](../../../../../../world/characters/nash-spellcasting-phrases.md), [Vorel's title register](../../../../../../world/characters/vorel-titles.md) and functional timelines, ledgers, indexes, schemas and compatibility pages are exceptions where research metadata is essential. Each subject still has one canonical page; follow the [page template](../../../../../../project/WORLD_REFERENCE_PAGE_TEMPLATE.md) and [documentation architecture](../../../../../../project/DOCUMENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md).
