# Inverse S1E04 — Inverse Part 4

> **Final published Inverse installment in the currently indexed four-part mini-campaign.** Provisional, source-indexed dossier from [`Inverse S1E4. Inverse Part 4.parakeet.processed.srt`](../../sources/Inverse%20S1E4.%20Inverse%20Part%204.parakeet.processed.srt), about **2 hours 51 minutes including introduction, break and outro**. This recording was **not independently checked against audio**; timestamps and non-spelled names remain ASR-derived. Full spoilers. [S1E03](S1E03.md) establishes the prior search and library fight.

**Continuity boundary:** Jon Jagger's *Inverse* takes place in **a different fictional reality from Beau's *The Wastes***. The *Inverse* characters' references to **Erivia** and a barrier-separated **Earth** are **internal to Inverse**; do not merge its history with the Scorching, CHIDS, the Traversal Drive or Wastes Earth. The episode's out-of-character promotion of the returning Wastes show is **broadcast scheduling, not an in-fiction crossover**.

## The end, in brief

**Baffo Saltz and Garpoon successfully retrieve Mondoro Sackt's cipher journal, escape an attack by masked members of the Bound on the Mines Monastery, and physically deliver the journal to Grizz de Wells in Serifax.** The apparent decoding key occupies roughly **ninety pages** of a thin leather journal marked **M/S**; the original journal, **not a finished copied transcription**, is concealed in Garpoon's bag and handed to Grizz. Their separate companions, **Lyalta Godfrey and Spores Meier**, **have not returned from the auction expedition**. Grizz sends Baffo and Garpoon to find them. The episode closes on the two riding away **toward another mission**, not on the cult's defeat or a complete explanation of the Anathema.

**The show's Inverse run ends with S1E04; the fictional plot is not fully resolved.** The performers explicitly frame this as closing the current second two-parter and returning to *The Wastes* on the following show. They muse about possibly playing these characters again; those remarks are **not a subsequently documented continuation or an in-world prediction**.

## Played events

### 1. Reunion, concessions and entry to the Dark Archive (00:03–00:24)

After the S1E03 split, **Baffo** returns stealthily through the southern stacks and finds **Garpoon alive** beside **Jeadar**, the elder scholar who knocked him down and subsequently helped heal him. Garpoon is upset that Baffo ran off while he was dying; Baffo describes his flight as improvising a clever plan. Their quarrel is brief. Jeadar will still not casually admit book-burning visitors into his most restricted collection. He listens as Baffo explains the purported **Bound threat**, the warlock **Mondoro Sackt** and the possibility that a scholar **Befren Trolley** kept or inherited Mondoro's effects.

Jeadar agrees to a **single supervised chance to locate and copy information**, conditional on surrendering their weapons. **Books may not be removed**, the visitors will be **permanently barred on departure**, and Garpoon's axes/sword and Baffo's darts must remain downstairs. Baffo successfully conceals that his **violin, Heartstring, can transform into a crossbow**, and Jeadar does not confiscate it. The elder personally unlocks an access door and brings them up a dark stairwell, about **two stories**, to the **Dark Archive**. He places a subordinate called **Noalis** (name provisional) to watch the confiscated items; no extra healing potion is given to Baffo.

The Dark Archive is physically shown as a **smaller, more tightly packed storehouse** of old works and restricted magical objects: a handful of **glass-cased books**, several chained books that **snap and bite**, and an isolated loose book that flips open and shuts within **a painted or marked enclosure**. Jeadar warns against reading potentially dangerous texts aloud. **These are separate animated/enchanted books, not Mondoro's cipher manuscript.**

### 2. Garpoon finds the cipher; Baffo steals a second book (00:24–00:53)

Jeadar takes the two into a likely **sorcerers' and warlocks' books** section; nothing is ordered under a single shelf for deceased scholars' effects. **Baffo's search is unimpressive**, and he is distracted by chained, biting volumes. He also secretly steals an illustrated book **showing a nude succubus**. He attempts to slide it into Garpoon's pack, but a suspicious Jeadar intercepts his approach. Baffo withdraws and successfully hides the book in **his own bag**, later transferring it to Garpoon's. The book is a **separate, unrelated theft**, not the sought magical key; its other contents and properties are unknown.

**Garpoon**, despite his dismissive attitude toward scholarship, performs the decisive search. A **thin flexible leather journal** falls from among the larger volumes; the letters **M** and **S** are stitched in the cover. **Baffo flips through it** and makes an Intelligence check. Within a central section he finds approximately **ninety pages of unfamiliar symbols, possible meanings and algorithms for determining a symbol's intended meaning**. The GM says he is **fairly certain the party has found the deciphering key**. This is **a discovery of a plausible functional cipher, not a completed decoding of The Creator's Bound** or a demonstration of what that book contains.

Baffo and Garpoon consider **stealing the journal** despite their agreement to make a supervised copy. Baffo eventually lets Jeadar inspect it, attributing it to **Befren/Mondoro**. Jeadar sees it as a method of decoding and, still suspicious of their earlier behavior, **offers paper, ink and trained copying assistance**. Before anyone copies the ninety pages, **metal-on-metal blows sound at the Dark Archive door**, which is violently forced open.

The exchange includes a GM reminder: the ancient account attributes creation of **the Anathema** to **Cathos Knull** and creation of the **barrier** to others. As established in [S1E02](S1E02.md), the Anathema's full origin is **accepted ancient lore that the GM explicitly says may be wrong**, not an externally verified fact.

### 3. Masked intruders and the battle in the archive (00:53–02:01)

The intruders wear **black robes** and **white porcelain-like masks**. Ordinary members wear **half-masks** with one eye slit and a placid mouth. One apparent leader wears a **full mask**, with one half calm and the other distorted into a grotesque grin. The GM and the final recap identify them as **cultists**; they are not members of the monastery. Some carry **crossbows**, and the leader fights with a **curved scimitar and dagger**. The scene begins with approximately **four intruders at the archive's entrance**, but other masked cultists are encountered elsewhere during the larger monastery assault.

Baffo and Garpoon have **surrendered their conventional weapons**. Baffo first tries to free and repurpose **a chained animated biting book** as an improvised weapon. The book **bites Baffo for one hit point**; Garpoon strains at another chain, is bitten repeatedly, and finally **tears its fixture loose**, leaving an airborne biting book tethered by a chain. **Baffo conceals the deciphering journal in Garpoon's backpack** during the confusion (and the other stolen book is also in the bag), so the central object is **not left with Jeadar to copy**.

**Jeadar initially faces the intruders directly**, killing or incapacitating two crossbow users but suffering severe wounds. Garpoon whirls his chained book at a surviving crossbowman; it hits and **returns to bite him again**. Baffo plays a loud diversionary melody on **Heartstring**, then uses its **crossbow configuration** to fire from the dark. Garpoon closes with the crossbowman and **knocks him down with a powerful unarmed strike**, his neck visibly injured; the GM depicts him falling dead. The **leader attempts to negotiate surrender**, repeatedly asking for the sought object, but continues attacking Jeadar.

Garpoon **grapples the full-mask leader** while Jeadar, already badly hurt, **stabs at him**. The cultist **cuts Jeadar down with his scimitar**; he falls and later **does not respond when Baffo checks him**. The scene strongly suggests death, but **neither a successful medical check nor an explicit GM confirmation of permanent death occurs**—record **Jeadar as motionless/possibly dead, not audio-proven permanently dead**. This differs from receptionist **Previs**, whose **murdered body with a crossbow bolt in his chest is explicitly shown later**.

Baffo's first bolts miss or wound the leader. Garpoon picks up **Jeadar's dropped longsword**, fails to disarm the leader and suffers more wounds. As Garpoon is nearly overcome, **Baffo kills the full-mask leader with a lethal Heartstring crossbow bolt through the head** (~01:55–01:56). The GM **explicitly describes the cultist dead**. A freed animated book continues to attack even after the leader's death until **Garpoon cleaves it in two with the longsword**; its red glyphs briefly glow and fade. **The enchanted book is destroyed**, not recovered as evidence of the cipher.

The heroes then ambush a further **half-masked intruder** who arrives after the leader is dead. **Baffo kills him immediately with another bolt**, searches his body and takes **fifteen crossbow bolts**. Baffo also prods Jeadar, who **remains unresponsive**; he leaves the scholar's money and mundane equipment behind. The companions remain **severely injured** and hear continuing combat through the library.

### 4. A last rescue from Murgit and the escape (02:12–02:40)

In need of a way out, Baffo first asks the **enchanted biting books for healing**, and Garpoon tries **intimidating** them. Neither attempt works. They contemplate **setting another fire as a distraction**, despite the obvious risk to the library, and Baffo fetches **four ordinary books** from the upper archive for tinder. **No second blaze is actually ignited**. At the lower Dark Archive entrance, **Garpoon spots a further full-mask cultist** near their confiscated weapons; those weapons are still where they were left, and their final retrieval is **not shown**.

As Baffo returns with books and the attacker moves to strike, **Murgit—the same frightened gnoll doorman whom they earlier mistreated—leaps onto the cultist, stabs him, and calls for the heroes to run**. Garpoon accepts the opening and races toward the exit, explicitly hoping **Murgit follows**. Baffo chooses to protect the gnoll, **attacking the masked foe's eyes through his mask** rather than immediately fleeing. His first and second successful strikes **blind the cultist** in the GM's description; meanwhile **Murgit continues stabbing**, takes a slash himself, then strikes the cultist in the throat. The GM specifies **the cultist falls but is not yet dead**, and Murgit runs after the heroes. The leader killed earlier and the **blinded, throat-wounded second full-mask combatant are distinct attackers**, so do not count both as permanently killed.

Jon expedites the remaining retreat: the **monastery defenders are overcoming the invading cultists**, and Baffo sees **Fulna** and a **small gnome defender** successfully fighting attackers. **Previs is found dead** in the reception area. The protagonists escape through the dented entry door. **Murgit refuses Baffo's offer of employment in Serifax** and stays at the monastery because **he is its doorman**. Baffo gives him **a business card and five more gold**; no relocation or death is shown.

The party's **concrete outcome**: they **leave the Mines Monastery alive with the original deciphering journal**, plus the unrelated illustrated book. The monastery has been attacked and suffered casualties; **the library is not declared completely destroyed, its scholars not all killed, and the Bound not exterminated**. The stolen journal's eventual removal violates Jeadar's **copy-only** condition; there is no scene of the copy being finished.

### 5. Delivery to Grizz and the recorded Inverse ending (02:40–02:50)

The GM summarizes their **several-day return journey to Serifax** by horse without the mysterious distant campfires that had appeared on the outbound journey. **They physically reach Grizz de Wells.** Garpoon draws the proper journal from his bag and hands it over. Grizz leafs through it and explicitly confirms **this is the cipher they need**. **The original manuscript is therefore both recovered and delivered** by the time the recorded series ends, strengthening Baffo's earlier inference in the archive. The GM does **not** play an actual translation of *The Creator's Bound*, disclosure of the Bound's final plan, or any ritual involving the two artifacts.

**Grizz has news:** **Lyalta and Spores have not returned from their auction assignment**, and the auction date is approaching. He believes **they may be in trouble** and asks **Garpoon to retrieve them and bring Baffo**. Baffo is distracted by questions of payment and expenses, but **the pair depart on another mission**. Grizz's concern does **not establish that the auction occurred, the book was stolen, or the two characters are dead/captured**.

For a final comedic scene, **Baffo tickles Garpoon with his tail from the opposite side while riding**. Garpoon detects the trick and **ties a temporary knot in the tail**. Jon then declares the chapter over as the two friends ride toward the next adventure. The hosts say the **Wastes cast will return the next week and Inverse will be put aside**, while speculating they might revisit it later. **This is the last documented Inverse played scene**, not a narrated final resolution of the cult and cosmology.

## Archival and verification notes

- **Voice is not spelling proof.** `Serifax/Sarafax`, `Erivia`, `Mondoro Sackt`, `The Creator's Bound`, `Alter`, `Zelganith`, `Jeadar` (an earlier in-world handwritten note actually spells **J-E-A-D-A-R**), `Previs`, `Fulna`, `Murgit`, `Befren Trolley`, `Grizz de Wells` require original audio/creator materials for final orthography.
- **Do not count a copy.** Jeadar *offers* copying; combat interrupts before any completed copy. **The actual original journal is stolen/carried away and delivered to Grizz**; that is supported by the finale.
- **Different death evidence:** Jeadar lies unresponsive, Previs is described as murdered, the first full-mask leader is explicitly killed by Baffo, and a later full-mask attacker is blinded and wounded but **not dead at the observed cutoff**.
- **S1E04 marks the end of recorded Inverse, not the total in-world end.** The expedition for the auction book is off-screen and unresolved; the two friends ride to find their companions. **No missing plot should be inferred from Wastes episodes, speculative crossover talk or listener-retrospective wiki summaries**.
