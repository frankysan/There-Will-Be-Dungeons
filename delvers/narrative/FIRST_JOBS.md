# First jobs

## A party on the clock

Krandal Splenderbelt stood outside the guild at nine, determined to be punctual. He had descriptions of two companions and a strong sense that work, once promised, ought to be finished. Inside, Koh Spiros had already arrived and was quietly watching the morning's business. Evelyn Grinkeeper reached them at a run, her weapons and armor clattering loudly enough to startle a horse. Krandal stepped out of its path; its owner ran after it. Their first meeting began with the sort of disruption Koh seemed content to observe from a little distance.[^premiere-opening]

The guild made adventuring look like ordinary employment, complete with supplies, paperwork and jobs that earlier arrivals had already taken. Two postings remained. Snake Island offered a warning about snakes and no apparent money. Krandal wanted no part of it. The other job promised 120 gold for dealing with a monstrous cave. Cassius, the guild's expansive host, welcomed the newcomers, issued wooden badges and read them an encouraging foreword from a celebrated hero. His enthusiasm did not conceal how little the organization spent on its lowest ranks. Koh's badge was already scorched.

Their task sounded straightforward: investigate the stirges troubling a farmer's sheep and close the opening believed to connect the cave to the Underdark. The supplier gave them concrete and explained how to use it with stone and water. Koh bought a shovel and a container for the water. Evelyn gathered bread. Krandal sought potatoes and, while waiting, heard unsettling advice from the patrons: watch the ceiling. One patron also told him about a party whose third member had not come back. Those were other adventurers' accounts, and his companions were outside when he heard them.

Before leaving, Evelyn tucked a gold piece under the abandoned cart's seat for the horse's owner. Whether he found it remained unknown. Cassius chased after her with the guide she had left behind; she accepted it and dropped it again once his back was turned. Krandal shouldered the heavy supplies, and the party set out east.[^departure]

## What lived above the torchlight

At the farm, drained sheep lay beside a cave beneath an uplifted slab. Evelyn hurried forward. When the first stirge appeared, her hammer missed it and struck the dead sheep instead. Koh's dagger killed the insect, but more came out of the cave. They attached themselves to the adventurers and drank. Krandal came close to collapse; Evelyn healed him, and he later returned the help. They survived by removing the creatures one at a time until Evelyn crushed the last one and the buzzing stopped.

She led the way below with a torch. Koh kept to its edge, relying on its light while trying to remain unseen. Krandal followed more noisily. The warning about ceilings soon acquired substance: what looked like a hanging stone stirred above Evelyn and fell. Krandal placed a protective spell on her, though the creature's first strike would have missed without it. The slug-like attacker tried to crawl away. Koh eventually cut it in half.

Another apparent rock opened eight yellow eyes. It sprang onto Krandal and wrapped him in a living darkness that left him unable to see or breathe freely. He called down radiant fire against it. Evelyn misunderstood the flash, believing the creature had attacked with fire or lightning; unable to pull it loose, she guided her companion into a shallow pool. Koh struck the creature repeatedly. It detached, discharged a cloud of magical darkness and hid.

They emerged separately from the cloud. Evelyn's divine sensing could not find this kind of creature, and Koh failed to spot it. Krandal followed Evelyn's voice out, noticed it hiding on the ceiling and brought it down with another burst of radiance. The guide later gave the creatures names: piercer and darkmantle. Koh gathered proof of the kills. Evelyn tested the remaining overhead formations with thrown stones, bringing one down for good measure.[^cave]

## A plug, a badge and a light

The final chamber contained the fissure they had come to close. While Krandal mixed concrete and Evelyn fetched the fallen stone, something shone below. Looking down revealed boots, a body and a glass object. Koh descended on a rope held by Krandal, with a little magical guidance to help the climb. The body was mostly a dragonborn skeleton, still wearing a numbered guild badge. He took the badge and orb quickly. As he climbed back, he alone caught sight of something serpentine approaching through the darkness.

Koh urged them to close the passage. Evelyn's first attempt left the stone poorly seated; the second drove it firmly into place. Krandal covered the plug with concrete, using the empty sack as reinforcement. Something struck from below. The stone shifted slightly but held, and the hiss beneath them receded. They waited for the mixture to dry, sharing bread and raw potato. A stirge's probing mouthpart and claw became caught in the setting surface. The way through was shut.

At the guild, Cassius accepted their report and trophies and paid the promised reward: forty gold each. The recovered badge was numbered 542. Cassius identified its missing owner as a delver sent elsewhere in the Underdark to find a door with a face. The party had seen no such door. Nor did his account establish that these remains belonged to the companion another group had lost that morning. From Koh's description, Cassius called the pursuing creature a grick and congratulated him on escaping it.

The orb was a drift globe. Cassius checked it, declared it nonsentient and described how it could provide light and follow its user. Koh had gone down for it, and the others agreed he should keep it. There was already another job waiting: a crypt, assigned for tomorrow because the party had two divine casters. Evelyn and Krandal accepted rooms upstairs. Koh needed to return home. Their first job was finished; the next had yet to begin.[^return]

## The cryptnasium and its survivors

After a night of rest, the three Delvers found themselves stronger and on a special assignment. They were to restore the fountains at a mountain sanctuary of Kord, a graveyard whose crypts had become places of athletic devotion. House, Crush and the gaunt Armada greeted them at the stairs. The devotees blamed a disaster in the weight room on Studwell, whose fatal attempt to squat in sandals had, they believed, left a spirit hungry for the strength of the living. Evelyn insisted that they surrender enough strengthening potion to rescue both missing companions. Her readiness to challenge Crush, followed by a successful appeal to loyalty, won the second vial.

The climb exhausted Koh, but not Evelyn, who descended again to learn the crypt's trials: ten laps, two chains and a rope leading to a bell. Under a fig tree they found Meatball, almost drained of strength, with a predatory Shadow waiting in the shade. Evelyn drew him away from its reaching fingers. Krandal recognized the weakness of darkness to sacred radiance and banished the creature. Strengthened by the first potion, Meatball fled to safety.

Koh ran the ten laps through a tomb-lined track while more Shadows closed in. His companions defended him, Krandal with sacred fire and Evelyn with thrown steel and healing. Koh suffered a draining touch but finished the circuit and poured his vial of holy water into the first basin. Foul water began to clear. In the next crypt, mirrors revealed a spectral body that refused ordinary sight. Chalk dust exposed its shape, and the ensuing struggle nearly destroyed the party: Koh mistakenly struck Krandal, Evelyn brought him back, and she in turn was felled by the Shadows. Krandal revived her. Koh called daylight from his rescued globe, and Evelyn's holy smite finally scattered the darkness. She and Krandal opened the chains and purified the second basin.

Yet the central fountain disgorged sludge, and its trail led into the last chamber. Another near-invisible enemy awaited them amid gymnastics equipment, hurling weights while the party fought weakened and injured. Krandal barely survived a kettlebell blow. Koh struck the final blow against Studwell. Nearby, Sultan lay scarcely breathing. The remaining strengthening potion restored him sufficiently to escape, and in gratitude he left his purple champion's sash for the party. Koh, unwilling to let his exhaustion decide the contest, leapt for the rope and rang the bell. Evelyn poured the last vial. With the third basin cleansed, clear water returned to the graveyard.

Meatball and Sultan were reunited with their fellow devotees, although Meatball's borrowed strength had already faded. The guild awarded the three adventurers three hundred gold between them and opened its full job board to their choice. Koh recovered Sultan's enchanted sash, but considered it a shared resource. Their second assignment had ended; the third had not been chosen.[^crypt]
