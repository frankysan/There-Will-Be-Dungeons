# Delvers — narrative histories

[Season Three — After the Broken Ball](SEASON_THREE.md) begins **on the same Bronze Ball night**, follows the next two mornings, and closes with seven afflicted elves actually treated while Evelyn travels home. The GM's proposed three-month interval is **not yet completed**. [S3E01 evidence](../episodes/S3E01.md).

[Season Two — The Forgotten Fortnight](SEASON_TWO.md) now covers the **COMPLETE SECOND STORY ARC, S2E01–S2E12 (owner-confirmed boundary)**: Snake Island amnesia and Potatopia, Withers heist, Gemholm slaad outbreak, Fredoroth dragon hunt, Koh's rescue of Anthela, silver-knight exam, and **actual Bronze Ball destroyed by an ACTIVE underground Hereditary Drone**. It **ENDS ON CLIFFHANGER**: Wildfire and Withers fall underground, elves still drawn compulsively to table-blocked pit. Future Season Three expedition and three-month time passage were **GM previews only**, not played here.

[The Darkest Delvers — The First Arc](SEASON_ONE_ARC.md) is the **complete eight-chapter narrative through the end of season one**. The four earlier chronological studies below remain available as detailed, source-linked narratives and are deliberately preserved rather than overwritten by the new arc synthesis.[^arc]

[First jobs](FIRST_JOBS.md) follows Krandal, Koh and Evelyn from their first guild meeting through a sealed cave passage, Kord's restored sanctuary and a perilous grick-scout mission that uncovers a trapped dungeon. Three assignments bring mutual dependence, new discoveries and a four-day pause.[^guide]

[The Rise to Bronze](THE_RISE_TO_BRONZE.md) follows the company's weeks of city service, Koh's newly awakened Soul Knife, Evelyn's struggle with her finishing school, Krandal's temple duties, and the eventual promise of bronze badges. The Darkest Delvers choose their name and Skull Keep as the next undertaking without beginning the castle adventure.[^bronze]

[Skull Keep and the Dawnless Dunes](SKULL_KEEP.md) follows the company's first completed bronze assignment: an unnaturally maintained estate, a restless lord in a mirror, signs of a darkness far below the lake, and a flameskull vanquished with Koh's Soul Knife. Enzo's revelations are treated as what he told the party, not established truth.[^tower]

[The Wells of Atomer](THE_WELLS_OF_ATOMER.md) follows the company through psionic training, a temple wedding, Evelyn's hidden school life and the guild's response to growing creature activity. The party descends the southwest well, uncovers old workings beneath Pelor's temple and defeats a cube and hook horror. The story continues through **Koh's psychic meeting with his unconscious wife and black-sand evidence**, Krandal's search for meaning in a sleeping elf's taps, and the guild's **peaceful mission** to a kuo-toa lakeshore village. In flooded ruins the company discovers a protective ring and **subdues a fish ritualist who claims a god sleeps in the Dunes**. The party **surfaces safely**, confronts a hostile mounted collector and two subordinates, and sets out for the guild with **three prisoners alive**; their eventual arrival and explanation remain open at this earlier narrative cutoff. The first-arc finale follows in [the completed first arc](SEASON_ONE_ARC.md).[^wells]
