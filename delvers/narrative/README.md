# Delvers — narrative histories

The Delvers' story follows three adventurers from the smallest guild commissions through the mysteries beneath Atomer. Read the three completed arcs in order. Each has its own source notes and preserves the knowledge available at that stage of the story.

## Primary reading order

1. [The Darkest Delvers — The First Arc](SEASON_ONE_ARC.md): early commissions, the rise to bronze, Skull Keep and the rescue at Noble Hollow.
2. [Season Two — The Forgotten Fortnight](SEASON_TWO.md): the return from Snake Island, a secret heist, distant expeditions and the Bronze Ball catastrophe.
3. [Season Three — After the Broken Ball](SEASON_THREE.md): the catastrophe's aftermath, a struggle over the guild and the final descent, followed by the one-year epilogue.

The campaign ends with the third arc. Season One ends at S1E12, Season Two at S2E12 and Season Three at S3E12. The narrated Academy cutaway archived as S2E00 belongs during the first arc's kuo-toa mission; it is incorporated there without renumbering the source.

## Editorial status

The [cleanup plan](../../project/DELVERS_CAMPAIGN_CLEANUP_PLAN.md) covers all three completed arcs. Season One has received a preserved consolidation pilot. Season Two and Three remain their existing extensive versions pending chapter consolidation; this portal does not claim that all three have passed the new acceptance checks. See the [manifest](../../project/DELVERS_CLEANUP_MANIFEST.md) for measurements and the [chapter maps](../../project/DELVERS_NARRATIVE_CHAPTER_MAPS.md) for the proposed next passes.

All narratives are source-based syntheses, not audio-verified transcripts. Exact clocks, ASR uncertainties and episode evidence belong in their notes and dossiers. Campaign completion does not establish a performed wedding from the final invitation, reveal the sealed letter or resolve every in-world mystery.
