# Delvers — Season Two: The Forgotten Fortnight

Season Two begins not on a boat or before the door of a dungeon, but with three adventurers returning home. Koh Spiros, Krandal Splenderbelt and Evelyn Grinkeeper walk through Atomer under the midday sun, dressed as though a serpent king had given them a hero's wardrobe. Krandal's metal armor has become a coat of living-looking snake scales. Koh's arrows resemble fossilized snakes, and Evelyn wears heavy golden jewelry patterned with coiling bodies. Helenar Clearstone meets them in astonishment. The Darkest Delvers have been gone for two weeks; the guild feared them dead. They remember setting out for Snake Island and a gigantic serpent rising from the lake. The days between are missing.[^return]

## A kingdom forgotten

Their gifts offer clues but no clear story. Krandal's staff becomes a great python when he casts it to the floor. He remembers waking in a silk-draped bed, setting the python against a viper, and a princess named Hissia calling to him with evident affection. Evelyn remembers being dressed in a flowered cape atop a stepped pyramid, proclaimed queen to a cheering throng of serpentfolk. Koh recalls replacing a precious golden snake idol with a clay copy, only to be confronted by an undead man with a snake living inside his mouth. Each new memory raises questions: who held the power there, what the adventurers promised, and why no one can recall their return.[^memories]

The guild treats the commission as complete and counts out a thousand gold pieces for each of them. Their strange prizes are taken for inspection. Cassius loosens the jewelry that clings to Evelyn, and the necklace and ring prove to have their own protective enchantments. Koh and Krandal also find scraps of letters, one addressed to a best friend and one marked by a green kiss. Krandal remembers a village where a princess was promised to him in return for a stolen idol. Koh pictures the three companions riding enormous snakes toward distant mountains, his own mount saddled specially to keep him aboard. But the bright flashes end as quickly as they come. Even the great fang they have carried home tells them little.[^inspection]

## The lives they left behind

Koh hurries toward his wife and daughter. Before he reaches them, a satyr leaps down from a roof: Chuck Abnaz, known as Nasty, comes from the Cloven Brotherhood, the forgers Koh once worked among. The two weeks of Koh's absence have nearly emptied the money meant to keep his wife under medical care. Chuck has paid the doctors and now demands his reward, along with Koh's return to the Brotherhood. Behind him three other satyrs step from concealment. Koh refuses to resume his former work, but at home he learns that Chuck did indeed provide the money. His daughter greets him gladly and his wife still sleeps; neither comfort has removed the threat.[^koh]

Krandal comes home to a temple that has spent the fortnight sealing the dungeon entrance and guarding the dark stair. Brother Maynard is short of helpers, for fear has driven several priests away. Rianu Keeves lies unawakened in the infirmary, his finger no longer tapping its old code. Krandal heals his bruised body without waking him. Trying a higher magic upon one who has not died, he hears a faint word—*Kalizar*, or something very like it—from the sleeper's lips. He keeps the clue to himself and retires, finding that his mouse friend's old hole has been plastered over. He breaks it open and rejoices when the creature emerges unharmed.[^elf]

Evelyn's absent weeks have placed Margaret Dupayton in a difficult position. Her painted sleeping double has lain ill on the school bed with one invented affliction after another, until reports reach Evelyn's family. A letter from Lord and Lady Blackwood announces the arrival of their physician. Evelyn changes her traveling clothes for something suitable and returns to the dormitory. An old family doctor examines her and finds no sickness, but his curious spectacles reveal a mark bestowed by a goddess of love and passion. When the doctor departs, Evelyn begins to tell Margaret why the discovery troubles her. Their words remain private, for the scene turns away before the tale is told.[^evelyn]

## The old land on the river

The next morning the Delvers receive their inspected possessions, together with the guild's reports and Krandal's long-promised deed to a watchtower near the Noble Hollow. The job board has grown into a great map filled with fresh tasks. The three select a wedding at which a dead grandfather may be determined to appear; they are to keep watch for the Hooper family without ruining the ceremony. Koh buys a ring to protect himself from the falls he seems to suffer so often, and after their private errands the three walk north with Krandal to see the land that may become his farm.[^guild]

Little of the place deserves a farmer's pride yet. Weeds choke two plots and an old irrigation ditch; a cottage has lost its roof, and the tower's walls have broken open toward the river. Still, Krandal sees the soil beneath the weeds and speaks of growing potatoes. As Evelyn approaches the ruined tower, she senses undeath in the scarecrow standing in the field. From within the stone appears a sea hag so frightful that even Evelyn turns away. A skeletal minotaur rises from the earth and steals the scarecrow's skull for its head, denying that any visitor owns the farm.[^tower]

The hag finds a fear Krandal has rarely spoken aloud. She makes him see himself without the light of Pelor, as though his devotion and his life had been a terrible mistake. The vision drives him unconscious. Evelyn takes a crushing blow from the skeleton but reaches Krandal and restores his strength. Koh's psychic blade and new serpent dagger tear the minotaur's head from its body. The hag sends lightning across the field, yet the three stand again and drive her toward the river; Evelyn's hammer and Krandal's shining face mace together end the hag's life. Eels and fish tumble from the empty shell of her body.[^fight]

The fields fall silent, but the minotaur's skull still speaks. It protests the naming of the holding, then suggests that it might at least frighten birds from the crops. Krandal names the land **Potato Grove** and buries the skull while he decides what to do with it. The buildings are still ruins and no potato has yet been planted. Nearby sits the hag's cauldron, and the sight of it brings another Snake Island image: a bonfire, armed little snakes and three bound adventurers facing what might have been their last meal. That memory too breaks apart before it can offer an answer.[^ending]

## The wight wedding

The next afternoon, the wealthy Hoopers prepare to marry their son Limwick to Fresna Goodwine. The Delvers come not as honoured guests but as guards expected to keep a troublesome grandfather's ghost away from the family celebrations. The house is built to halfling measure, its halls crowded with servers and invitations, its gardens filled with dogs, dancers and fine wine. A guest illusionist, the Great Daufante, comes separately at the guild's request with his undead assistant Connor and a spider familiar named Hamlet. In bright robes and a jewel-studded skull mask, he makes a stage of every conversation; he is interested less in modest guild service than in mysterious magic he might acquire.[^wedding]

Koh serves the wedding guests and catches unsettling signs behind the merriment, including dark footprints that appear without a body to make them. Krandal, watching from the tree line, hears of a burial place deep in the woods. The expected white doves fail to appear during the ceremony: Evelyn slips invisibly behind the stage and finds them all dead in their cage. With the quick invention of a frightened hostess, she conjures the immense white goat she has carried as an ivory figurine for weeks, and the crowd cheers its miraculous appearance instead. The wedding goes on, but the woods continue to watch it.[^doves]

Krandal sees red eyes drifting among the leaves and later entering a duck on the ornamental lake. The bird snatches a knife and dives toward old Mr. Goodwine until Krandal strikes it down with sacred flame. After the traditional cornucopia scatters jewels among the trees, Krandal joins the scramble; the riches lead him to an old family graveyard, where no memorial to Grandfather Hooper can be found. Hamlet has overheard servants whispering that Goodwine killed the old man in the long years of trade wars, and that Hooper carried a precious deck of cards into death. Daufante coaxes Goodwine into the woods with false friendship, makes him receptive to enchantment, and elicits a dreadful confession: the elder and his companions beat the grandfather and hung him from a tree, leaving him there without burial.[^confession]

The grandfather now approaches as a withered wight swinging from a noose attached to a walking oak. Krandal shatters the bough and stills the tree; Koh protects the living Goodwine; Evelyn tries to restrain the dead man. Daufante's robe sends a storm of blue stars into the wight and at last knocks it down. The wizard extracts a Deck of Many Things from the body without daring to draw a card. Evelyn demands a burial, Krandal agrees, and even Daufante finds it expedient to assent. The old murderer and Connor lay the corpse in the family earth. Near the roots of the fallen tree Koh sees black sludge, a fresh unanswered hint of the dangers that have followed the Delvers through other ruins.[^rest]

The marriage festivities survive their dangerous secret. Daufante leaves the next day with the deck in exchange for surrendering his portion of the reward to the three adventurers. He gives Evelyn an invitation to visit the theatre and departs before anyone accepts it. The families feast in apparent peace, though the murder that haunted their reconciliation remains a truth known only to a few.[^cutoff]

## A shark beneath the pavement

The city barely settles after the wedding before the guild diviners warn of another visitor. A great bulette is swimming through the soil beneath Atomer, threatening to surface anywhere among its people. The bronze guild companies fan out across the streets; the Darkest Delvers wait in the grand financial quarter near Withers Estate, amid the fountain and obelisk of a little public park. Koh makes a simple warning bell from a dagger and a scrap of string. When its chime rises alongside tremors in the water, Evelyn runs toward the creature and leads it away from the more crowded buildings. The earth bursts open behind her. Koh's twin psychic blades flash in the hard back of the land shark, Krandal's blessing sustains the battle, and Evelyn's enchanted javelin lights the creature with lightning.[^bulette]

The monster leaps above them and crashes onto Krandal, leaving him battered and helpless under its weight. Evelyn seizes its massive cheek and twists its head away from the fallen cleric. From the ground Krandal thrusts his carved serpent staff into an unarmoured eye. The beast breaks free and dives once more, with Evelyn clinging to its fin as it tunnels through the square. It rises again only to die, and the guild's other companies come running at the sound of victory. From the opened jaws Koh gathers teeth to commemorate their work. Edgar Boonefellow claims the fin for his celebratory soup.[^combat]

But the creature has left something more troubling than a broken street. Beside the Withers mansion, a narrow gap leads downward into a clean stone room. Koh climbs into it under Evelyn's watch and finds a great stone slab furnished with leather restraints. Fresh scraps of food and leather suggest another visitor has used the place recently. A heavy door leads toward the mansion but will not open. Withers himself walks out in house clothes and claims his old residence contains many rooms unknown to him. Koh finds this hard to believe, but he does not force the door. The Delvers bring the discovery to Cassius, who accepts their report with more concern than he cares to explain.[^estate]

## A farmer, a daughter and a secret

Two days pass after the guild's victory feast. Krandal returns to his neglected fields and at last begins the work he has so often imagined. He pulls old timbers from the collapsed cottage, clears the earth from the irrigation channels and plants pieces cut from potatoes. A voice rises beneath the soil. Krandal unearths the minotaur's talking skull and builds it a scarecrow's body out of wood and cloth. Placed above the new crop, the skull gratefully accepts a stone to fling at crows. The name **Spud Buds** pleases its farmer for now, though he has not settled on what the property shall be called.[^farm]

Evelyn returns to the quiet duties of finishing school. The Bronze Ball is drawing near, and Lady Vestal expects the students to show the adventurers what civilized hospitality can accomplish. Evelyn signs up for household and servant management, courtship and marriage, and horseback riding, glad of a course that offers practical training rather than ornament. Margaret speaks with her of family pressure, her future marriage and the classes she dreads. Neither girl can escape the duties their families have imagined for them, however different their ambitions may be.[^school]

Koh spends the same days with his wife and daughter. Penelope receives one of the land shark's teeth, supposedly found by her father at market, while her sleeping mother hears a far more honest account of the work Koh has done. He does not risk speaking mind to mind with her again. When Chuck Abnaz appears at his door, the former forger's past becomes impossible to hold outside the house. Chuck has an opportunity: the new hole beside Withers Estate offers a route to costly jade and powerful treasures. He wants Koh to coordinate the thieves by psychic thought and promises to forgive his claimed debt. Koh is tempted, agrees to help, and learns that the financier behind the theft goes by the peculiar name **Suis Sac**.[^s2e03koh]

## The job no guild will record

Koh writes a forged guild job card and slowly sees the financier's alias for what it is: **Cassius spelled backward**. At the guild, Cassius admits it freely and brings all three Delvers aside. He suspects that Withers, the financier of many guild endeavours, is gathering forbidden artifacts and that his interest in Skull Keep was not entirely philanthropic. The guildmaster asks the Delvers to work with the Cloven Brotherhood and seek proof from inside the house, hidden behind assumed identities. He will pay for evidence from his own purse, but no official guild mission will record the venture.[^secret]

Koh learns that his old underworld name is **Flatfoot Skippy**. Krandal settles on **Cobra Commander**, a suitable name for a man with a snake staff, and Evelyn names herself **Long Jump Jenny**. Between these preparations Evelyn admits a longer-hidden truth: the family that sent her to finishing school is **Blackwood**, and she is its only daughter. Her friends now understand at least part of the danger she risks by adventuring openly. Cassius lends them magical boots to soften their footfalls and sets the meeting at the exposed passage for half past nine that evening. Carrie takes Koh to find a way to fit such shoes to satyr hooves, leaving Evelyn and Krandal behind in the guild. The door opens and an unfamiliar elf enters before they can leave. For now, the job remains a promise rather than a journey inside Withers's house.[^end]

## A house guarded by four elements

The night of the burglary begins behind closed curtains in an inconspicuous cart. Allspice waits with the horseman's patience, while Chuck Nasty pores over his plans and the visiting wood elf Shrugs sits in quiet, careful readiness, dynamite wound beneath his cloak. Evelyn has bought matching disguises for the company, even tiny cloth masks for Krandal's own face-shaped maces. Koh reaches into the minds of the party's organizer and two of his accomplices, leaving Krandal to learn their messages secondhand. At the edge of Withers Estate, an inexplicable little black cloud snuffs the streetlamps one by one. The Delvers climb into the hole left by the slain land shark. Krandal reads a scroll of magical silence, and Shrugs blows open the barred way without a sound. Bells hidden beside the doorway shake furiously under the enchantment until Koh stills them.[^s204entry]

The dark cellars reveal an old evil wearing the costume of a rich man's wine stores. In the rooms once used as prisons are scratched names, worn shackles and a shrivelled kuo-toa corpse beneath a sheet. Krandal sees one capital letter where the maker of the wine labels should have left lower case, then more strange letters on other plaques. Together the signs give them the word **CROP**, and the great metal door beside them slides away. A roaring furnace awaits them; within its flames a voice calls them kindling. **Fern Fueler**, the fire guardian, says he has served the estate for a century and a half and will let them pass if they can amuse him with battle or puzzle. Evelyn gives the answer Blackwood when he asks for a family name, yet they cannot find the key implied by his next riddle. They fight. Shrugs hurls dynamite and Koh cuts at the heat with his psychic blades, suffering burning hands for his bravery. Krandal summons a torrent of water that makes the proud flame shrink to a tiny glowing creature. Fern survives, honors the fight and hands over a key.[^s204fire]

Beyond that door lies the pumping machinery and its lively watery keeper, **Dewey Liquis**. He too offers riddles, and a key. Rather than comb the house for a clock, the Delvers choose battle. The water surges out of its glass prison and swallows Krandal and Shrugs. Koh suddenly recalls another lost moment on Snake Island, a stone serpent whose glowing eyes he shattered by *throwing* the psychic blade he had always held in his hand. Now he can hurl two such blades at an enemy from a distance. Krandal releases the serpent in his carved staff, which grows into a real constricting snake and bursts his watery captor. His spiritual mace helps overcome the remaining form and Dewey yields the next key without dying.[^s204water]

The third guardian is a capricious storm called **Gale**. He blasts Krandal and Shrugs down the hall, poses another riddle, then lays into the heroes when they ask for combat. Shrugs falls unconscious in the gale, and Krandal himself is battered nearly to death, but the cleric sends Pelor's life-giving power through both of them and returns his ally to the fight. Evelyn stands where the wind is strongest, her hammer finding its purchase in air. Gale finally surrenders, calls her **the Unblown**, and returns to the house's ventilation. No one has died, but the group has spent too long in its underground labyrinth.[^s204air]

The last keeper is a great square of living stone. **Slate Cortez** raises a face from the lift and riddles of a garment made from fur or leather, something to keep out cold and rain. The answer is a coat. Koh and Shrugs sneak into the mansion proper to find the corresponding key while Evelyn and Krandal keep the speaking rock company. In the private museum Koh finds jade ornaments of every shape, and hurriedly fills his borrowed magical bag with twenty-three of the smaller pieces. Shrugs finds himself unable to search the overcrowded cloakroom efficiently, so he carries the coats away together. A servant catches sight of a walking pile of clothing and raises the alarm. Koh tears through the coats and discovers the key, and the four reconvene in haste, urged by Nasty to leave before their host returns.[^s204coat]

The stone platform lowers them past the cellar to a small room crowded with curious possessions. Koh and Shrugs empty its tables into their two bags. Among these treasures are a familiar conch-headed staff and trousers hung with bells. Evelyn smashes both, and Koh packs the broken remnants away. At the final locked vault, Krandal spends another scroll of silence and Shrugs blows the metal door apart. Jewel piles fill the opening; they scoop gems and strange black liquid into the magical bags while Koh keeps an eye open for evidence. He sees nothing he can positively call proof against Withers. As the adventurers climb back out and escape in Allspice's cart, Koh suggests to Fern that a little fire might be a fitting distraction. The idea is merely offered. Behind them, Withers arrives to find the mansion's windows brightening. The four thieves have escaped with their treasure, but the purpose of their guildmaster's secret commission remains unanswered.[^s204end]

## From the burning house to the hidden map

As the thieves flee, the Withers Estate glows with fire behind them. Nasty leads the Delvers from a warehouse into the Cloven Brotherhood's secret sewer hall, where satyrs fix their elaborate hairstyles with cockatrice eggs. Jade, jewels and odd objects spill across the table. Koh examines the book of guild payments and the music box whose sound hurts one nearby satyr; Evelyn finds Enzo Withers in a portrait whose painted surface hides another image. Koh exposes part of a map beneath it. The Brotherhood keeps the jade and divides the wealth. Evelyn purchases a Bag of Holding but does not want her portion of the stolen cash, so Koh and Krandal split what remains. The Delvers depart at dawn and defer their meeting with Cassius.[^s205new]

At home, Koh painstakingly uncovers and copies the remainder of Enzo's map. Tunnels lead toward a lakebed passage, underground monster lairs and a structure marked **Black Basilica**. Krandal hides his stones behind a loose tower brick and his money beneath a boulder overlooking his potato patch, where the talking minotaur skull reports seeing an unknown fishman. Evelyn earns praise for her Bronze Ball catering argument but fares poorly at horseback riding. When the party finally returns to the guild, Cassius brings them to Carrie Underworld's laboratory. Withers has reported the theft and employed the Broscale family to investigate. Koh acknowledges letting the furnace elemental loose, later explaining the blaze. Carrie accepts the strange book, old parchment, reassembled music box, mirror, black vials and broken summoning objects. She identifies a title rendered **Khalazar** in the old script and its recurrence in a phrase on the map, while warning that more reading and study are needed.[^s206new]

## Gemholm under the tide

Koh confides to Cassius that his wife remains trapped in an unresponsive state. Carrie wonders whether psychic communication could someday permit an intervention, perhaps tested with Rianu Keeves first; no experiment takes place. Cassius sends the Delvers out of town while the search for stolen items continues. They accept **Poppin' Slaads**, say temporary farewells and ride with a merfolk ferryman, Gil, to the sandbar settlement **Gemholm**, where his remarkable crab Herman Snips remains to signal for a return passage.[^s206sailnew]

The village is deserted and damaged. Mack, a frightened worker, reports creatures appearing after the miners dredged mysterious gems, and says red slaadi implant larvae while blue slaadi change their victims. In the infirmary they discover the body of Caleb, and a fleeing larva is destroyed by a Goliath healer called Church. He has a wound from a red slaad himself, though he insists he is safe. Searching the ruined home of Hank Tom, the companions find a small transformed creature that races toward the water. Koh strikes it with a psychic blade; Krandal summons a python to block it and favors capture, but Evelyn continues her lethal attack. Mack calls the creature Hank Tom; another survivor says the man is already gone. It falls, apparently speaking a final word of thanks, as dark forms begin circling offshore with the rising tide. The larger assault has yet to begin.[^s206endnew]

## The laboratory under the lake

With the tide climbing over Gemholm's sandbar and three shapes swimming offshore, the Delvers retreat to a crab-maintenance building. They find two enchanted metal dredging machines with claws, walking legs and bewildering rows of levers. Evelyn hands Mack her magical paints; he sketches the key he remembers, and his drawing becomes a real key that opens the door. Krandal finds a crab-operation guide and Hank Tom's diary. The mechanic's entries describe a sliver from a strange gem lodged in his hand, a drink from Ambermoor, and a growing fascination with flies and water. Evelyn retrieves Caleb's diary, which recalls three unusual gems from a drowned cave and a blue drink followed by terrible chest pain.[^s207diaries]

Koh finds Ambermoor's own journal. Its author boasts of distilling a substance from chaos gems and developing an infective weapon; he intends to experiment on science officer Neko. A red slaad bursts through the house floor, claws Koh and flees, but Evelyn's magical challenge keeps it near. Krandal brings it down with his face maces. The transformed body bears **Balmer's** name tag. Koh finds a marble-sized thing crawling under the skin of his leg. With remarkable precision he cuts out a tiny egg with his psychic dagger and stamps on the larva within. The other adventurers search their own wounds for similar signs.[^s207attack]

In the wreck of Destin's house lies the key to the second dredger. Evelyn pilots a machine beneath the rising water, Krandal riding behind, while Koh scouts and calls directions from a roof. Some large creature bumps the crab, but she returns with the key. Church confesses a lump beneath his chest where a red slaad scratched him. Evelyn casts **Lesser Restoration**, and the living shape inside him dissolves. This cure has worked before full transformation, but the fate of someone already changed remains uncertain. Two of the crab craft carry the Delvers and the two surviving workers to an air-filled cave, where another crab waits outside an enchanted laboratory door.[^s207cure]

Koh notices a magical alarm on the door and swims secretly into the laboratory through a flooded hatch. Ambermoor stands beside a glass tank, extracting black fluid from a motionless, thorn-covered slaad and preparing syringes of several colors. When Evelyn bursts through the main entrance, Koh lunges from hiding. His first psychic strike fails, but the second knocks Ambermoor unconscious **without killing him**. They bind and gag the researcher, remove his spell components and seize his case of experimental substances. The captive creature wears **Neko's** name tag. Evelyn tries the same restoration that saved Church; nothing happens. She then kills the sedated specimen with a javelin through its head.[^s207lab]

They return their mechanical crabs to Gemholm but cannot find the **two larger slaadi** that had been circling the island. The original unusual gems have been destroyed and dissolved into the experimental reagents, now in Koh's Bag of Holding. Their mission is therefore not proved complete. Herman Snips signals for Gil's return boat. Mack and the cured Church travel onward to Atomer and the guild, while the Delvers disembark earlier at Krandal's farm with their captive Ambermoor and evidence. The guild has not received either yet, and the farm must serve as a hiding place for the time being.[^s207cutoff]

## Marsh and the mountain of fire

The Delvers have barely had time to dispose of their dangerous prisoner. The guild takes Ambermoor into its holding cells, and Cassius wastes no time sending the three adventurers away from the eyes of the city again. A silver-ranked dragon hunter named **Marsh** has come in search of a red dragon at **Chath Peak**. He is a weathered wood elf with a badly scarred left cheek, a damaged ear, a farm-like brimmed hat and a bow made from dragon bone; his scruffy little weasel, **Wheatley**, rides with him. Aegis brings the Delvers out to meet him. All four receive guild fire-suppression armor, old equipment temporarily laid aside. Marsh soon sees that his allies are capable but noisy, and that Krandal considers a potato just as important as preparation.[^s208intro]

They climb through a barren red landscape where kobolds tread the hunting paths. Below a high waterfall they find those small workers fetching water, but the Delvers elect to go around them on a narrow ledge. Then a **huge red dragon** lands below to drink. It is far older than the guild's estimate, perhaps **450 years old** by Marsh's eye, and three Delvers freeze in terror. With ropes, a patient weasel and whispered encouragement they slip behind the falling water, until Krandal's clattering face maces alert the beast. Its fiery breath engulfs them on the exposed path, but the borrowed protective suits blunt the damage. The dragon leaves them singed and climbing, not dead. Higher on the mountainside the companions watch kobolds offer **three of their own**, tied to a log, to the dragon's hunger.[^s208falls]

A lone, extravagantly feathered kobold shaman meets them on the trail. **Skoogie** carries a bell and a caged lizard, and speaks Draconic to Marsh. Once revered by the settlement, the shaman has lost followers to the new dragon and desires the creature's destruction. After obtaining the party's agreement, Skoogie removes a massive feathered **Arrow of Dragon Slaying** from a headdress and hands it to Marsh. The shaman then leads the adventurers around the peak, through a hidden tunnel and over a falling river of lava. Marsh leaps with magic, Koh has a painful landing, and Krandal conjures enough cooling water to harden a temporary pathway for the rest.[^s208shaman]

Inside an obsidian chamber lies an intricate clockwork machine whose old Elven inscriptions are signed **Radiant Lance**. A raised model shows the dragon's lair above and three devices: **Lava Off**, **Sealed Gate** and **Forced Landing**. A message challenges dragon slayers to show that they are free of greed. Evelyn tips roughly **250 gold pieces** into a trough, and Krandal contributes half a potato. When her hand touches a carved imprint, the golden offering flows through the forge and comes out as a **Dragon Slayer's Longsword**. The potato has burned away. Koh tries to fool the mechanism with a bag of ball bearings; Krandal tries offering candles. Neither gains the artifact desired. Only Evelyn carries the golden blade as the floor rises and brings all of them into the dragon's chamber.[^s208forge]

The red dragon waits far above, resting among bridges, lava and an extravagant hoard. It laughs at the sword and the shaman's tiny champion. Skoogie rings the bell and blesses his helpers, while Krandal strengthens them with divine magic. The fight begins immediately. The dragon belches flame and swoops through the chamber; Marsh takes a bite, and Evelyn swings the new sword into its body. Koh rushes toward **Lava Off**. When he presses the switch, water blasts into the molten moat and a cloud of steam fills the lair. Marsh searches for the gate-sealing control, Evelyn hurries toward the device that will force the dragon to land, and Krandal clears a small patch of steam before seeking shelter in a wagon perched upon the hoard. They have only begun to work the ancient defenses. The dragon is **still flying and alive**, and battle stops in a haze before any winner can be declared.[^s208cutoff]

## The night of the dead dragon

What had been a tangled, unfinished fight in the steam ends as a tale told after the fact. The dragon is named **Fredoroth**. Marsh seals the old gate, Evelyn calls down the ancient mechanism to force the creature from the air, and Koh leaps upon its back with a flash of magic before he falls off. The trapped dragon lashes out, strikes Skoogie again and tries desperately to escape through paths that have been closed. The kobold survives Evelyn's care. At last Marsh's one precious **Arrow of Dragon Slaying** flies into the red dragon's neck, and it dies. It is a decisive victory, although the remaining combat is **recounted by the GM**, not played out at the table in the previous recording.[^s209dragon]

The hoard is immense. Evelyn's summoned mount helps bring an ancient cart down the mountain. Skoogie returns toward the kobolds and Marsh claims a laden cart, including magical objects whose precise division the Delvers never finish debating. Koh and Krandal each receive three thousand gold pieces from the treasure. At the guild, a ratfolk clerk, Jimmy the Soup, pays each adventurer another two hundred and fifty gold pieces. Krandal has also hidden a handful of loose coins in his pockets. By late night they separate for home, school and a ruined farm. The promotion to silver rank still awaits further approval.[^s209reward]

At the farm Krandal finally gives his scarecrow guardian a name. The cursed minotaur skull cannot recall his own and agrees to be called **Chad**. As more money goes beneath the familiar boulder, Chad reports that the wet-eyed fishman is still watching the potatoes from the lakeside. At school, Evelyn's roommate Marguerite is unnerved: a red dragonborn carrying a distinctive axe appeared behind her reflection and was gone when she turned. A letter with red and gold trim waits for **Lady Blackwood**, requesting dinner at **Broscale Manor** in two nights. Meanwhile, Koh keeps watch by his sleeping wife; by dawn a black sludge is physically running down her arm and mouth.[^s209night]

He reaches for her mind. The small field where he once found Anthela has become a shadow-filled stone chamber, where his wife is dressed for a wedding she believes will make her bride to **the Khalazar**, the ruler of the Lower Kingdoms. She does not know Koh, until he concentrates on the ring he bought for her years before and brings it into being within the dream. Her own ring appears, and for an instant she remembers. She says she has agreed to the marriage to keep everyone in Atomer safe. Koh insists that she and Penelope deserve a life together and promises to return. Then her recognition dissolves as quickly as it came, and she asks who he is once again.[^s209anthela]

## The price of potatoes and the wedding guard

Krandal wakes to someone tearing immature potato plants from the earth. A filthy, nearly toothless old man with a peculiar fluid-like hump is starving and hiding among the ruins. Krandal calls him back with the amplified authority of Pelor, but instead of punishing him he shares boiled potatoes and a conversation. The stranger says he sleeps in an Atomer alley. Krandal gives him eight gold pieces and a candle, and proposes another two gold upon his return and perhaps paid work when the potatoes can be harvested. The man thanks him, then leaves a chilling warning: the farm's walls will not stand when the darkness comes. No catastrophe occurs that morning, but Krandal has been given a reason to rebuild.[^s209stranger]

Evelyn practices courtly dancing for the Bronze Ball while Koh and Krandal receive matching dinner invitations. Koh presents his daughter Penelope with a dragon tooth and a necklace bearing a single crimson scale. At the guild, a plague of tiny fairy dragons has left staff compelled to speak in rhyme and hurts those who resist. Krandal catches one in a jar and helps collect the others. Koh, unharmed by the enchantment, demands answers from Cassius. In the quiet guild record room Cassius admits fear of a cult, perhaps aided by an insider, using the approaching Bronze Ball to strike at the assembled bronze adventurers. Carrie is studying the Withers documents but will not be ready to present her results until the day before the ball. There has not yet been any attack.[^s209guild]

The Temple of Pelor now shelters five unresponsive elves. Koh brings Krandal into the mental space of **Rianu Keeves**, who imagines himself training with shadow guards for the Khalazar's coming wedding. Krandal finds his own body dressed in a potato sack; controlling one's appearance here is more difficult than expected. **Command** forces Keeves to stop training, and **Lesser Restoration** in the dream sweeps away the marching shadows for a moment. Keeves remembers being a cleric and his life before collapsing. He wants to know whether his body has been cared for. Yet soon the false guards return and the spear rises again. They leave him there, still physically unconscious, carrying evidence that the mental enchantment can be disturbed but not yet broken. Carrie later suggests a more powerful **Remove Curse**, which nobody casts in this episode. Krandal also buys a package of farm implements from the tortle traders **Grapple and Tackle**; the tools have not arrived by the last scene.[^s209keeves]

## The family of Broscale

At the appointed dinner the three companions stand outside a dark stone manor. Evelyn arrives formally as Lady Blackwood, Koh's magical armor looks like merchant finery and Krandal comes in ordinary clerical attire. In the hall they encounter an eager red drake, family trophies and the noisy children of **Wildfire Broscale**. Wildfire's wife Valentine balances the baby Wall Nibbler, Climber shows Koh a pet frog, Basher keeps hitting Krandal's leg with a cloth-covered stick, and Dreamer prefers her book. A servant named Godwin Milner serves fine courses as Wildfire, a wrapped great axe still at his side, watches how his guests handle a crowded family meal.[^s209dinner]

When the household leaves the dining room, Wildfire opens the Darkest Delvers' guild records. He speaks of his fallen brother **Bram**, thanks Koh for finding his badge, and weighs the missions that have made the trio notorious. The Crypt of Cord brought an exceptionally high grade; their public conduct during the all-bronze mission earned a low one. Silver rank would grant new access to guild files and the Oracle Room, and his assessment is the final recommendation sought. He tests them with impossible-sounding moral problems, asking about prophetic children, the injustices of a vampire kingdom, possessed criminals and rewards owed for defeating evil. Their answers differ, but he finally gives **his recommendation for promotion to silver**. No badge or new rank is handed to them over dessert.[^s209silver]

As Evelyn follows the others out, Wildfire briefly holds her back. Quietly he tells **Lady Blackwood** that he knows she entered **Withers's basement**. She speaks of elementals trapped there, while he defends the financier whose money has supported Atomer and reminds her that the upcoming Bronze Ball celebrates Withers. They separate without an arrest or public accusation, but Evelyn knows the secret of the burglary is no longer entirely hers. The three Delvers leave the manor, with the Bronze Ball and Carrie's unfinished investigation looming over them.[^s209end]

## What the old books had to say

The Delvers have scarcely left Wildfire's manor when Aegis comes for them. The guild is busy: monster parts occupy the long tables, ready for the Bronze Ball's desperate fundraising auction. In the lecture hall, Carrie Underworld has at last deciphered the papers they stole from Withers. **Khalazar**, she says, bears titles including **Necromantis Rex**, **Lord of the Black Basilica** and **Undying Majesty**. The ancient Dolbluth were driven from the underground kingdom and built the dungeons the adventurers have explored as defenses against it. But creatures from above gradually overran those fortifications. Enzo Withers had instead dug straight down, connecting his estate to the Underdark. His map points to the Black Basilica and a strange device that Carrie cannot yet identify. She calls the music box **the Hereditary Drone**: sound can hijack vulnerable minds' motor control, especially among elves. Cassius hopes that Koh's telepathic link may eventually allow a protected expedition without the need to hear the drone; such an adventure would require money the guild does not yet possess.[^s210lecture]

For now the immediate task is more personal. Cassius urges Krandal to pray overnight for **Remove Curse** and asks Koh to bring his wife to the guild for a carefully watched attempt inside her dream. Carrie eagerly volunteers to observe. Evelyn takes the black mirror from the relics before leaving, while the unusual music box, broken staff and bell trousers remain behind. In the morning an outfitted Carrie and Aegis bring a stretcher to the Spiros home. Penelope hears that they may finally help her mother. At the laboratory Evelyn fastens a long pink ribbon around their wrists as a rescue precaution. Only then does Koh realize his power links at most three other minds: his wife, Evelyn and Krandal. Carrie, already equipped for adventure, must remain outside the dream, much to her disappointment.[^s210plan]

They arrive among green glowing crystals, fountains and stone corridors leading to two guards. One is **Rianu Keeves**, still playing the role forced upon him by the psychic prison. Koh copies the guards' uniform, Krandal tries to imagine a matching gold sash and braid, and Evelyn finds herself wearing a white gown. She introduces herself grandly as the **Queen of the Birds**, whose impossibly beautiful whistles Koh secretly enhances with psionic skill. The guards let the three pass. Beyond lies a bridal chamber, a white gown patterned like bat wings, caged bats and a balcony overlooking an immense subterranean city lit by a crystal above its roof. A fiery, long-legged mechanical shape strides through its distant streets. Whether that view reflects a real place, none of them knows. The bride lies on her bed, smiling vaguely, and has no memory of the husband who has come to save her.[^s210hall]

Evelyn keeps her attention with another whistle and calls Krandal forward for a blessing. Their hands meet. **Remove Curse** passes from the cleric's fingers into Anthela, and the bedroom begins to crumble into **black sand**. She knows Koh at last; he embraces her and tells her they must escape. Their way to the light is cut off by black ooze pooling in the doorway. A giant bat with a gilded saddle brings a slender pale elf to the balcony. His white hair is bound in an intricate braid, a deep violet cowl encircles his neck, and his face seems as smooth and cold as porcelain. Anthela knows him. **Khalazar** claims her as his bride and offers to spare the intruders if they surrender her. Koh's answer is a pair of psychic blades.[^s210curse]

## A storm made from black sand

Evelyn hurls a lightning javelin followed by another spear. The figure cracks apart like a statue, spilling black sand; the bat and distant city disintegrate as well. The chamber collapses onto them, and Khalazar rises once more from the dunes in a darker form, wrapped in ragged fabric with a monstrous mouth. His blasphemous word leaves Koh, Krandal and Anthela stunned, while Evelyn keeps fighting. She shields Anthela, defies a terrible necrotic utterance, and strikes with her hammer. Koh wraps his cloak about his wife and opens a magical door to the shaft of light. Anthela is swept toward the waking world, but he remains behind until his friends can escape.[^s210fight]

The battle's end is as uncertain as its beginning. Evelyn brings her hammer around in a mighty blow that shatters something hard and red within Khalazar's chest. The enemy's form breaks into shards and sand, then rushes across the chamber as an immense black wave. Evelyn grabs a piece of **red crystal**. Krandal and Evelyn run desperately toward the escape light; when they cannot outrun the tide, Koh forces the moving sand to slow through sheer will. They step into the glow and rise out of the dream while a threatening voice promises that it has seen them and they will meet again. Back in Carrie's laboratory, Anthela **opens her eyes in the physical world** and folds Koh into her arms. Carrie receives the crystal Evelyn managed to bring from the impossible place, but missed everything that happened there. The two lovers hold each other, and the episode ends.[^s210end]
