# Delvers — Season Two: The Forgotten Fortnight

Koh Spiros, Krandal Splenderbelt and Evelyn Grinkeeper return to Atomer beneath the midday sun, dressed in serpent ornaments and carrying weapons they scarcely remember acquiring. The guild feared them dead. Two weeks have passed since the Darkest Delvers departed for Snake Island, and almost everything between departure and homecoming has vanished from their memories.[^scope]

## 1. The forgotten fortnight

<a id="a-kingdom-forgotten"></a>

Their possessions offer glimpses of the missing days. Krandal's staff becomes a python, recalling a luxurious bedchamber, a defeated viper and a princess named Hissia who calls him beloved. Evelyn remembers standing above a cheering crowd, crowned queen in golden serpent jewelry. Koh sees himself replacing a golden idol with clay before an undead figure approaches with a snake inside its mouth. They remember riding enormous snakes, a poisoned princess and a terrifying crossing, but the flashes never become a complete account. The guild accepts their return and pays the promised reward, taking the strange equipment for inspection.[^island]

<a id="the-lives-they-left-behind"></a>

Their ordinary lives have not waited. Koh's family has exhausted its medical funds during his absence. Chuck Abnaz, called Nasty, has paid the doctors and now wants the former forger back in the Cloven Brotherhood. Koh refuses, but finds that the payment is real. His wife still lies unconscious, Penelope greets him gladly, and the household has become vulnerable to the people he hoped to leave behind.

At Pelor's temple, Krandal finds guarded passages, frightened clergy and the unresponsive Rianu Keeves. His healing eases the patient's injuries without waking him. Another attempt draws out a faint, unfamiliar word, which Krandal keeps to himself. At school, Evelyn discovers that Margaret has sustained the fiction of her illness for the entire fortnight. Her parents have sent a physician. The doctor finds no sickness, but his enchanted spectacles reveal a divine mark associated with love and passion. Evelyn begins explaining herself to Margaret; the rest of that confidence remains between them.[^homecoming]

<a id="the-old-land-on-the-river"></a>

With their gear inspected and Krandal's promised deed now in hand, they arrange a wedding-guard commission for the following day and walk north to inspect his land. The cottage is roofless, the watchtower broken and the fields choked with weeds. Krandal sees a future potato farm.

A sea hag and an animated minotaur skeleton dispute his possession. The hag turns Krandal's fear of a mistaken faith against him until he collapses. Evelyn revives him; Koh breaks the skeleton, and the companions kill the fleeing hag together. Yet the detached skull still talks. Krandal spares and buries it, considering its offer to frighten birds from the fields. The hag's cauldron recalls another island fragment: three bound adventurers, a bonfire and armed snakes. The memory stops before revealing their escape.[^farm-clearing]

## 2. The wight wedding

<a id="the-wight-wedding"></a>

The Hooper and Goodwine families expect a wedding untroubled by the groom's dead grandfather. The Delvers must keep any haunting away from the guests. A separately hired illusionist, the Great Daufante, brings his undead assistant Connor and spider familiar Hamlet. His bright robes and jeweled skull mask suit a man determined to command attention; his interest in whatever magic the dead man possesses makes him a less comfortable ally.

Koh serves drinks while Krandal watches the woods and Evelyn joins the celebration. Unseen footsteps leave black traces. Dogs bark toward the trees, and red eyes drift among the branches. When the ceremonial doves fail to appear, Evelyn finds them dead. Hidden by Daufante's invisibility, she substitutes an enormous white goat summoned from her ivory figurine. The crowd applauds and the ceremony continues.

The eyes enter a duck, which takes a knife and flies at old Mr. Goodwine. Krandal destroys it before the blade reaches him. A traditional shower of coins and jewels draws the guests into the woods, giving the investigators room to search. Hamlet brings a servants' rumor: Goodwine murdered Grandfather Hooper during the families' trading wars, and the victim carried a remarkable deck of cards into death.

Daufante charms Goodwine and draws him away for a private conversation. The old man admits that he and his companions beat Hooper and hanged him from a tree without burial. Before the Delvers can decide what to do with the confession, a leafless oak walks out of the woods. The grandfather hangs from its bough, a sword swinging beneath his noose.

Krandal breaks the branch connecting the wight to the tree. Koh shields the living Goodwine despite what they have learned, while Evelyn tries unsuccessfully to restrain the attacker. Daufante's magical missiles bring it down. He removes the Deck of Many Things without drawing a card, agreeing to surrender his share of the commission in exchange for keeping it.

Evelyn insists on a proper burial, supported by Krandal. Goodwine and Connor place the grandfather in the family earth. Black sludge remains at the tree's roots, another troubling resemblance to earlier discoveries. The guests continue feasting without learning the murder behind the haunting. Daufante departs with his untested deck and an invitation for Evelyn to visit the theatre, leaving the families' public reconciliation intact.[^wedding]

## 3. Beneath the financier's house

<a id="a-shark-beneath-the-pavement"></a>

A bulette approaches beneath Atomer, and the bronze companies spread through the streets to intercept it. The Darkest Delvers wait outside Withers Estate. Koh rigs a warning bell beside the fountain; when tremors disturb the water, Evelyn runs to draw the creature toward open ground. The armored land shark bursts through the pavement. Koh strikes with twin psychic blades while Krandal blesses his companions and Evelyn throws lightning.

The creature lands on Krandal and threatens to devour him. Evelyn wrenches its cheek aside, letting him drive his serpent staff into its eye. She clings to its fin as it tunnels away, emerging with it when it finally dies. Other guild companies arrive to celebrate, and Boonefellow claims a fin for soup.

The broken ground also exposes a clean stone chamber beneath the estate. Koh descends and finds a slab fitted with restraints, recent food scraps and a locked door leading inward. Withers claims not to know every room in his old house. Koh doubts him, but returns to report rather than force the door. Cassius receives the discovery with evident concern.[^bulette]

<a id="a-farmer-a-daughter-and-a-secret"></a>

Over the following days Krandal clears irrigation channels and plants potatoes. He raises the talking skull onto a scarecrow's body and gives it a stone to spit at crows. Evelyn selects school courses while Margaret worries about family expectations and marriage. Koh gives Penelope a bulette tooth, pretending it came through trade, then speaks more honestly to his still-sleeping wife.

Chuck returns with a burglary proposal. The chamber offers a way into Withers's valuable collection; Koh's telepathy can coordinate the thieves. Debt forgiveness and a share of the proceeds tempt him into agreement. The financier behind the operation calls himself Suis Sac.[^private-lives]

<a id="the-job-no-guild-will-record"></a>

Koh makes a false guild posting and recognizes Cassius's name reversed in the alias. Confronted, the guildmaster admits funding the Brotherhood. He suspects Withers of gathering forbidden artifacts and wants evidence from the house, with the venture concealed behind a theft. Koh's companions join the secret briefing and agree to help.

They prepare aliases and borrowed silent boots. Evelyn admits that she is the Blackwoods' only daughter, giving her friends another reason to understand her secrecy. Cassius intends the guild's involvement to remain hidden. The three will enter as thieves, pursuing an investigation for which no official job card exists.[^commission]

## 4. The job no guild will record

<a id="a-house-guarded-by-four-elements"></a>

At midnight the Delvers approach Withers Estate with Shrugs, a quiet wood-elf burglar carrying dynamite. Chuck coordinates from outside while Allspice waits with the cart. Their borrowed boots soften their steps; masks even conceal the faces on Krandal's maces. A moving black cloud extinguishes the streetlamps. Below the house, Shrugs uses explosives under Krandal's magical silence to breach the locked entrance.

Old prison cells have become wine stores. Scratched names, restraints and a mummified kuo-toa disturb the luxury above. Odd capitals on wine labels reveal the password to a boiler room guarded by Fern, a speaking fire elemental. His riddles offer another way forward, but the intruders choose combat rather than search further for a key. Krandal's torrent of water reduces Fern to a tiny flame. Defeated and alive, he yields passage.

Dewey, the water guardian, follows the same bargain. Koh recalls throwing his psychic blades on Snake Island and now hurls them through the water. Krandal's staff becomes a python that bursts his watery prison. Dewey retreats into the pumps and gives them another key. Gale, the air guardian, comes closer to killing them: Shrugs falls, Krandal barely remains standing, and the cleric's healing brings both back into the struggle. Evelyn's hammer wins the contest. Gale calls her the Unblown and withdraws into the ventilation.

Slate, the living stone lift, can be satisfied without battle. His clue sends Koh and Shrugs upstairs for a coat. Koh fills his bag with portable jade sculptures; Shrugs carries away the whole cloakroom when he cannot find the key. A servant sees the moving heap and raises the alarm. Koh finds the key among the coats, and they descend to the vault before Withers returns.

They seize curious objects, gems and black-fluid vials. Evelyn smashes a familiar-looking conch staff and bell-covered trousers, whose remnants are packed with the loot. Another silenced explosion opens the final vault. All four escape in Allspice's cart. Koh has encouraged Fern to create a fiery distraction, but their departure leaves its outcome behind.[^heist]

<a id="from-the-burning-house-to-the-hidden-map"></a>

Flames light the estate as they reach the Brotherhood's sewer hall. The stolen book mixes anatomical studies with guild-payment records; a painted-over portrait conceals a map, and a disc completes an unsettling music box. Krandal pockets useful trinkets. The Brotherhood keeps the jade and divides the wealth. Evelyn buys a magical bag but refuses the remaining cash, which Koh and Krandal split. Koh conceals the investigative objects for Cassius. Their theft has succeeded; what the evidence proves still awaits study.[^settlement]

## 5. Gemholm under the tide

Koh finishes uncovering and copying the hidden map while Krandal conceals his wealth at the farm. The skull reports a fishman watching from the river. Evelyn continues lessons despite exhaustion. When the companions finally meet Cassius and Carrie, they learn that Withers has hired the Broscales to investigate the burglary. The furnace fire may expose Evelyn, whose surname Fern heard. Koh acknowledges encouraging the elemental's release.

Carrie receives the strange papers, music box, map and other evidence. She identifies Khalazar among the old titles and proposes further research. Koh describes his wife's condition, bringing her suffering into the guild's investigation. Cassius wants the Delvers away from the city while the stolen goods are being sought. They accept a commission at Gemholm, a gem-dredging settlement reached by Gil's boat.[^debrief]

<a id="gemholm-under-the-tide"></a>

The sandbar village is burned, damaged and nearly deserted. Mack, a frightened survivor, describes slaadi and altered neighbors. Church, a Goliath healer, destroys a larva fleeing the infirmary, where a worker lies dead with a burst chest. He dismisses the danger of his own red-slaad wound. In Hank Tom's ruined house they find a transformed creature. Krandal wants it restrained and questioned, but Evelyn kills it. Whether the missing man could have been recovered remains unanswered.

<a id="the-laboratory-under-the-lake"></a>

As the tide rises, they seek shelter in the crab workshop. Mack uses Evelyn's magical paints to make a working key, opening the way to mechanical dredgers. Diaries describe unusual gems, drinks supplied by Ambermoor and the victims' changes. Ambermoor's own journal boasts of turning chaos-gem substances into infectious weapons. A red slaad interrupts the search. Krandal kills it; its name tag links it to another worker. Koh discovers an egg beneath his wounded skin, cuts it out and crushes it.

Evelyn pilots a dredger to retrieve the second machine's key. Church admits that something moves beneath his chest, and her restoration dissolves the larva before it matures. The survivors travel underwater to Ambermoor's laboratory. Koh slips through a submerged hatch while Evelyn triggers the main alarm. His surprise attack leaves the researcher unconscious and alive.

They seize his experimental reagents. In a tank, a sedated black slaad wears Neko's name tag. Evelyn's attempted restoration changes nothing; she kills the contained creature. Two other large slaadi remain unaccounted for when Gil collects the group. Mack and Church continue toward the guild, while the Delvers land at the farm with Ambermoor and the evidence. Capturing the researcher has not made the settlement demonstrably safe.[^gemholm]

## 6. The mountain of fire

<a id="marsh-and-the-mountain-of-fire"></a>

Ambermoor passes into guild custody, and the Delvers are sent out again with Marsh, a silver-ranked wood-elf dragon hunter. His scarred face and dragon-bone weapons suggest experience their own preparations lack. The guild supplies fire-resistant armor for the journey to Chath Peak. Marsh's weasel Wheatley accompanies him; Krandal brings his familiar concern for potatoes.

Kobold tracks lead toward a waterfall. The companions avoid the workers below, attempting a narrow ledge behind the falling water. Then a huge red dragon lands to drink. Marsh judges it far older than the guild expected. His three companions struggle against terror, and Krandal's clattering equipment gives away their escape. Fire washes over the ledge. Borrowed armor saves them from its worst effects, but the dragon remains free to fly away.

Higher on the mountain they witness kobolds offering three captives to the creature. Skoogie, a displaced shaman, seeks their aid against the dragon that has taken the community's devotion. He gives Marsh an Arrow of Dragon Slaying and leads them toward a hidden approach. Across flowing lava, Krandal's water creates temporary footholds while Evelyn carries their small ally to safety.

An obsidian chamber holds a model of the lair and an inscription signed Radiant Lance. Its challenge demands freedom from greed. Evelyn pours her ready gold into the forge and receives a Dragon Slayer's Longsword. Krandal's potato burns; later offerings of ball bearings and candles win neither him nor Koh a comparable weapon.

The platform rises into the lair. Bridges cross lava beneath the dragon's perch, and three controls promise to stop the lava, seal the gate and force a landing. Skoogie strengthens the adventurers while Krandal blesses them. Evelyn's new sword wounds the dragon, but its flame and teeth keep them scattered. Koh reaches the lava control, sending water into molten rock and filling the chamber with steam.[^dragon-approach]

<a id="the-night-of-the-dead-dragon"></a>

The remaining defenses finally turn the battle. Marsh seals the gate; Evelyn activates the mechanism that brings Fredoroth down, and tends Skoogie when the falling creature strikes him. Koh leaps onto the dragon through magical transport before losing his hold. Trapped within the lair, Fredoroth cannot escape. Marsh's precious arrow pierces its neck and kills it.

They return with treasure and the guild's reward, though the magical hoard is not simply divided object by object. Skoogie lives to return to the kobolds. Atomer welcomes the company home, but Koh's sleeping wife remains beyond the reach of a dragon-slayer's weapons.[^dragon-victory]

## 7. A voice brought home

<a id="the-price-of-potatoes-and-the-wedding-guard"></a>

At the farm, Krandal names his skull guardian Chad and shares food and money with a starving stranger stealing immature potatoes. The man warns that the ruined walls will not withstand the coming darkness. Evelyn receives an invitation to Broscale Manor, while Koh finds black sludge flowing from Anthela's still body.

Inside her mind, Koh sees a bride preparing to marry the Khalazar, ruler of the Lower Kingdoms. She fails to recognize him until he calls their old wedding ring into being. For a moment she remembers their family and the bargain she believes protects Atomer. Then she forgets him again. Koh brings Krandal to Keeves' mental prison, where the cleric imagines himself training as a royal guard. Restoration briefly returns his memory, but the imposed world soon reasserts itself. They need a stronger remedy.[^renewed-search]

<a id="the-family-of-broscale"></a>

All three attend Wildfire's dinner among his children, family trophies and fine food. Afterward he opens their guild records and questions them about mercy, justice and duty. Their answers differ, but he gives his recommendation for silver rank. Privately, he stops Evelyn and tells her he knows she entered Withers's basement. He defends the patron's generosity. She leaves knowing that her secret has reached the man assessing their rise through the guild.[^broscale]

<a id="what-the-old-books-had-to-say"></a>

Carrie's completed research gives the underground threat a clearer shape. She interprets the old defenses as the work of Dolbluth driven from Khalazar's kingdom. The music box is a Hereditary Drone, whose sound can seize susceptible minds' control of their bodies. Cassius hopes telepathy might allow an expedition using protected ears, but its funding still depends on the Bronze Ball.

First they will try to rescue Anthela. Krandal prepares Remove Curse, and she is brought to Carrie's laboratory. Evelyn ties their wrists with pink ribbon as a precaution. Koh can link only his wife and two companions, leaving the eager artificer outside their shared mental journey.

The three enter a green-lit palace, passing Keeves and another guard by presenting Evelyn as the Queen of the Birds. Anthela waits in a bridal chamber overlooking a vast subterranean city. Krandal touches her and removes the curse. Memory returns as the room dissolves into black sand. Koh embraces her, but their escape route fills with ooze. A saddled bat brings a pale, white-haired elf to the balcony. Khalazar demands his bride.[^rescue-entry]

<a id="a-storm-made-from-black-sand"></a>

They refuse. Evelyn's lightning cracks his shell and the city disintegrates, only for a darker figure to rise from the dunes. His power stuns Koh, Krandal and Anthela; Evelyn protects the bride and keeps fighting. Koh transports his wife to the escape light, then stays for his friends. Evelyn's hammer breaks a red crystal in their enemy's chest. She retrieves a shard as his form becomes a pursuing wave of sand. Koh holds it back long enough for everyone to escape.

In Carrie's laboratory, Anthela opens her eyes and embraces her husband. The fragment brought from the dream is real. The rescue has succeeded, even though the voice threatening them as they fled has promised another meeting.[^rescue-return]

## 8. The Bronze Ball

<a id="the-day-of-new-names"></a>

Anthela returns home to Penelope, walks to market and cooks for her family. Koh admits how their savings disappeared and how adventuring, including the dubious Withers venture, paid for his search. His original purpose is fulfilled, yet he chooses to continue helping others. At the farm, Krandal hires the watching fishman: Glepo Ma has been banished from his village and gladly exchanges gardening for shelter and potatoes.

Evelyn's rescue ribbon grows and shines. An armored reflection speaks of friendship and warns that Khalazar remains. She divides the ribbon among her friends, keeping a piece for the Traveler. Its pieces point toward one another when their bearers remember those they love.[^new-lives]

<a id="the-silver-assessment"></a>

Cassius announces their approved silver promotion. On the way to the Academy, Maynard reports that all five temple patients have awakened. The assessment records their abilities, purpose and chosen names: Splenderbelt the Efficient, Grinkeeper the Defender and One Shot. Koh speaks of heroism beyond his family's need; Evelyn recalls the injured Traveler whose healing helped shape her vocation. A portrait before Fredoroth's skull prepares the trio for public celebration.[^silver]

<a id="the-bronze-ball--finale-of-the-second-story-arc"></a>

At the governor's hall, Koh dances with the wife he feared losing. Krandal considers Goodwine's proposal for a future potato business. Evelyn stands openly when her hero's name is announced, meeting Lady Vestal's recognizing gaze, then changes into Lady Blackwood's dress without abandoning her adventurer's hairstyle.

A red portal brings the Gentleman Cavalier Traveler. He dances with Evelyn and describes a curse allowing him one night each year on the Material Plane, incurred while protecting minotaurs below Atomer. He evades her request for his name. Her ribbon becomes a rose on his lapel. Princess Hissia also arrives, calling Krandal beloved and recalling the island fortnight he cannot fully recover. Neither reunion answers every question.[^ball]

<a id="the-music-from-under-atomer"></a>

Then the maestro's eyes turn black. Khalazar's voice demands Anthela and moves through other unwilling bodies. Through Selenar it accuses Withers, who responds that he hired the Broscales and opened the doors. Koh joins his companions and wife in silent speech, anchoring her to their family. The floor erupts with the roaring drone. Roper tendrils carry compelled elves below; hook horrors, gricks and darkmantles break into the hall.

Evelyn opens a window for the schoolgirls. Vestal presses an unexplained heart-shaped handle into her hand before she reclaims her armor. Wildfire seizes Withers, demanding answers, and both fall through the collapsing floor. Koh saves Mack and Church, now guild recruits, while Krandal helps defend Zoltar. Evelyn fights beside the Traveler, their joined hands carrying them through the ruined hall in flashes of teleportation. When she accidentally strikes him, she heals him with a gentle squeeze instead of a slap.

The sound will not stop. Krandal's water drains into the tunnels; Koh's borrowed fireball fails to collapse the opening. Dominic and the others pile tables into the pit, blocking passage without silencing the call. Enchanted elves keep arriving and claw at the barricade. Anthela answers Koh's voice, Cassius carries the fallen Selenar, and the guild stands amid a catastrophe it cannot yet contain.[^catastrophe]
