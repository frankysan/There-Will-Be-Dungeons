# The Darkest Delvers — The First Arc

Three strangers enter the Adventurers' Guild in Atomer with little in common beyond a wish to work. Koh Spiros needs money for a family whose comfortable life is slipping away. Krandal Splenderbelt serves Pelor with boundless enthusiasm and dreams of a potato farm. Evelyn Grinkeeper carries a knight's weapons beneath the clothes of a young woman expected to pursue a different life. Their first commissions lead from troubled farms to ancient passages beneath the city, where the same strange workmanship and unsettling sounds await them again and again.[^boundary]

## 1. The guild's smallest promises

Krandal waits outside the guild at the appointed hour while Koh quietly watches its desks and job board from within. Evelyn arrives in a clatter of hidden armor, frightening a horse and sending its owner running. The three have scarcely met before ordinary obligations begin competing with the promise of adventure. Evelyn buys bread and leaves a coin for the inconvenienced tradesman; Krandal wants to keep to the schedule. Cassius, the guild's welcoming host, gives them wooden badges and guides. Koh's badge is already singed from earlier service.

Only two jobs remain. Snake Island advertises snakes without naming a reward; Krandal prefers the commission offering gold to close a farmer's monstrous cave. Cassius expects a passage into the Underdark. The supplier provides concrete, Koh buys a shovel and water container, and Krandal shoulders the materials. While seeking potatoes, Krandal hears patrons warn about ceilings and a companion who failed to return from another expedition. His friends are elsewhere when the warning is given.

Drained sheep lie beside the cave. Evelyn's first swing misses an emerging stirge, but Koh kills it and more follow. The creatures attach themselves to the companions and feed until all six are dead. Krandal nearly collapses, surviving through Evelyn's healing before returning the favor. Inside, torchlight reveals dangers hidden in the stone: a falling piercer and a darkmantle that wraps Krandal's head, blinding and choking him. Evelyn guides him toward shallow water while Koh strikes the creature. It releases a cloud of darkness, but Krandal escapes by following Evelyn's voice and kills it on the ceiling.

Beyond lies the fissure they must seal. Koh climbs down to recover a glass orb and badge from a dragonborn skeleton, then spots a serpentine creature approaching. Evelyn wedges a stone into the opening and Krandal spreads concrete over it. Blows shift the plug without dislodging it; the hissing recedes. They wait while the seal hardens, sharing bread and potato.

Cassius accepts their trophies and pays the promised wages. He calls the pursuer a grick and identifies the orb as a drift globe, which the companions leave with Koh. The dead adventurer, he says, had been seeking a door with a face. Their own next assignment is nearer: a mountain crypt whose fountains need cleansing.[^first]

## 2. The mountains, the crypt and a third job

The sanctuary of Kord turns devotion into feats of strength. Its graveyard holds athletic trials, but the people waiting below its stairs have lost their vigor. House, Crush and Armada blame Studwell, a strongman whose fatal lifting accident supposedly left a spirit hungry for other people's strength. Two companions, Meatball and Sultan, remain above. Evelyn presses the survivors to provide enough strengthening potion for both rescues, winning a second vial through her appeal to their loyalty.

Koh struggles with the climb and rests while Evelyn goes back for guidance. The three trials demand ten laps, the pulling of chains and a climb to a bell. Before beginning, they find Meatball barely alive beneath a fig tree. Evelyn carries him away from the Shadow concealed beside him. Krandal destroys the creature with sacred radiance, then gives the weakened devotee one of the borrowed potions. Meatball regains enough strength to flee down the stairs.

At the track, Koh runs while Shadows emerge among the tombs. One drains his strength, but his companions keep the attackers away long enough for him to finish. He pours holy water into the opened basin, and clear water begins replacing the filth. The next chamber is more dangerous. Mirrors reveal a presence invisible to ordinary sight; chalk dust exposes its shape, and more Shadows attack. Koh accidentally strikes Krandal unconscious. Evelyn revives the cleric, then falls herself, leaving Krandal to bring her back. Koh calls daylight from his globe, weakening their enemies until Evelyn's radiant blow destroys the last Shadow. Together she and Krandal pull the chains and cleanse the second basin.

Outside, black sludge still overflows the central fountain. Its trail leads to the final sanctuary, where Studwell hurls weights at the exhausted companions. A kettlebell almost fells Krandal, but he holds on, and Koh finally defeats the spirit. Sultan lies nearby, scarcely breathing. Krandal gives him the remaining potion; the restored strongman leaves his long purple sash in gratitude.

Koh completes the climb and rings the bell. Evelyn empties the last holy-water flask into the final basin, restoring clean water throughout the sanctuary. Below the stairs the rescued men rejoin their friends, though Meatball's borrowed strength has already faded. The guild rewards the rescuers, and the enchanted sash becomes another means of keeping one another safe. After rest, the companions choose a woodland scouting commission: confirm a grick sighting, report it and return.[^crypt]

## 3. Lives outside the badges

Between assignments, Koh changes back into the clothes his household recognizes. His wife lies unresponsive, his six-year-old daughter Penelope waits with her attendant, and neither knows how far the family's fortune has fallen. He has spent it seeking treatment. They believe his visits to the guild concern requests for healers; he conceals the dangerous work now paying their expenses. Before leaving again, he arranges their care and changes out of sight.

Krandal spends his free time watching birds and befriends Jimmy, the guild's ratlike night supplier. Evelyn sleeps, eats and finds a gym. At their next meeting Koh apologizes for striking Krandal in the crypt. The apology is accepted before they board Aegis's cart for the woods, where an abandoned trapper's wagon holds dead birds and one dying thrush. Krandal gives it water, names it Yuri and carries it in a pouch.

They detour around threatening hippogriffs and pass marked trees, sinkholes and ancient arches. A mud-streaked guardian named Veggie Sticks insists that he knows everything in the forest and that no gricks live there. Koh argues that an unanswered report will only bring more adventurers. Reluctantly, the guardian allows them into a deep hollow of roofless ruins.

Black slime clings to its well. An altar opening gives off a drone, and Koh copies nearby symbols while Evelyn studies an Elvish tablet referring to sky people. A camouflaged grick attacks. Evelyn kills it, but others emerge along the walls. Koh sends Sultan's sash toward the rim as their escape route. The heavy tablet cannot be secured and stays below; the sash rises slowly while the companions fend off pursuit.

Koh reaches the top and kills a pursuing grick, but another drags Krandal down. Koh's arrow stops it, and Evelyn returns to revive the fallen cleric. They retreat to Veggie Sticks, who admits that his certainty was a bluff intended to protect the hippogriffs. Sheltered in his hollow-stump home, they rest and decide to investigate a worked-stone passage he has glimpsed.

Back at the ruin, Krandal kills another grick. Others lie impaled by the dungeon's traps. Koh's attempt to cross a needle floor on a shield ends in painful poisoning, which Evelyn cures. She times the mechanisms, reaches an old armory and retrieves a runic sword hilt. They leave with the hilt and evidence for Cassius, who accepts the scouting report and submits their request for promotion.[^forest]

## 4. The road to bronze

The guild requires leave after three assignments, and each companion returns to a different discipline. Krandal volunteers at Pelor's temple, where Brother Maynard demands quiet service rather than theatrical healing. Buck Broscale, a dragonborn adventurer suffering from venom, teaches Krandal a practical lesson: closing wounds does not remove poison. A second, restorative blessing cures him. Yuri also needs attention after too long in Krandal's pocket; Jimmy takes the weakened thrush into his care.

Evelyn slips back into her finishing school, where a friend's excuses have covered her absence poorly enough to attract Lady Vestal's scrutiny. Her roommate offers more useful protection by weaving magic from enchanted dresses into Evelyn's chain mail, allowing it to be donned in moments. Koh returns to the medical bills and household expenses he must keep paying without revealing their source.

At the guild, Carrie Underworld has examined the ancient hilt. She calls it a Soul Knife and returns it to Koh. Her effort produces only a spark; his concentration draws out a cyan blade. It passes through wood without marking it, yet causes pain without a wound when touched. A masked mage calls Koh a scion and warns that others seek relics capable of awakening such powers. Carrie suggests that Buck may understand more.

Promotion is initially refused. Selenar Everbloom discounts the grick victories because the dungeon's traps had already weakened their foes. The guild wants visible service to Atomer, and weeks of smaller work follow: wasps, twig blights, a carrion crawler and supposed darkmantles that sometimes prove to be ordinary rodents. In the marketplace Koh exposes a hag disguised as a child selling tarts. Evelyn attracts the crowd while Krandal frightens people out of danger; their senior companion defeats the hag.

At a fire, Koh rescues trapped residents with the sash, Evelyn opens escape routes and Krandal brings rain. They clear a tannery, overcome cursed clothing and free a tunneling man from a cranium rat. Their earnings sustain Koh's household, encourage Krandal's farm ambitions and buy Evelyn greater independence. Their reputation grows through useful work as much as battle.

Cassius finally announces that Selenar has approved bronze rank. They choose Skull Keep as their next commission and arrange new equipment, some still awaiting collection. Koh proposes a name for the company they have become. His companions accept: the Darkest Delvers. The new badges will follow in the morning.[^bronze]

## 5. The lord who could not leave

Bronze membership brings a library key, better research and a public ceremony exchanging wooden tokens for chimera-shaped badges. Before departure Koh studies flameskulls with Carrie's help. Helena Clearstone supplies holy water and warns that killing the creature will not suffice: its remains must be consecrated before it rises again.

Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers accompanies them to his family's abandoned lakeside keep. He wants its antiques preserved and warns against enchanted mirrors. Across a bridge, the property looks remarkably well maintained. A green-flaming skull circles the tower, while an undead gardener tends the bushes without attacking. The companions try to behave as visitors until Krandal damages a chair. A skeletal repairer emerges, then screams and attacks when Koh addresses it. Evelyn destroys it.

Their climb draws the skull to an upper window. Fire spreads through the room, and Koh's attempt to snare the creature with the enchanted sash sends him falling into the courtyard. The servants continue their routines around the battle. A carpenter replaces scorched carpet while the adventurers search unused rooms for a way higher. Papers concealed in a desk reveal symbols matching Koh's earlier copy and the sequence for a locked stone doorway. They solve it, and Krandal's summoned water turns another fiery attack into steam as they escape up the tower stairs.

In an alchemical chamber a headless body sits before a mirror whose reflection still has a face. The speaker names himself Enzo Vanderbuck Withers. He says a failed attempt at immortality divided him between the mirror and the flaming skull, spreading the spell's consequences to his servants.

Enzo asks what brings them to such danger. Krandal speaks of Pelor, earnings and adventure. Evelyn admits that she seeks someone. Koh describes a person whom ordinary healing cannot restore, keeping the identity private. Enzo offers speculation rather than a cure. The copied symbols interest him more: he speaks of sky people who rose out of darkness and the Dawnless Dunes beneath the world. A heart is awakening there, he claims, driving predators upward. He also wants his own divided existence to end.

On the roof, the Delvers confront the skull together. Koh's arrows struggle against it, but his psychic blade proves effective. He lands the final strike and catches the bones; Evelyn washes them with holy water until ash and two garnets remain. Below, the servants lie inert and Enzo's reflection has faded without vanishing entirely. Their first bronze assignment is complete, and his troubling account travels home with them.[^keep]

## 6. Under the city's feet

Back in Atomer, Buck Broscale introduces himself to Koh as Wildfire. His brother carried the recovered globe before dying below ground. In private training he teaches Koh silent speech and warns of danger surrounding psychic relics. Krandal, meanwhile, recovers from a cockatrice's petrifying bite and conducts a wedding despite a shaky memory of the prayers. Evelyn's roommate Margaret gives her an enchanted bag that conceals her hammer. Preparations for the Bronze Ball bind school and guild together.

Cassius calls the bronze companies to investigate creatures emerging into the city. Carrie suspects that predators may be following other creatures upward. The Darkest Delvers choose the southwestern well, where a rust trail instead leads them into a smithy. A rust monster attacks Koh while a nearby barrel becomes a mimic. During the fight Koh's old hilt crumbles, but the blade he creates endures. Evelyn defeats the mimic, and the companions save an injured worker and a boy's dog. They return without examining the well itself. Cassius promptly sends them back.

Below the shaft, breached masonry opens into caves. They fight young rust monsters and stirges, then meet a stone-eating creature who accepts Krandal's potato and friendship. Worked passages beyond resemble the forest ruin. The First Sons Brotherhood disputes the explorers' claim before yielding passage; its members later become indispensable allies.

An armory contains psychic spear shafts and a horn. Evelyn's blast injures and deafens her and Krandal. After resting, the Delvers find six pressure platforms that require the First Sons' help. Together they open the stone above them into Pelor's temple, startling its monks and damaging the floor. Both parties return to their assigned task, sealing connecting passages with stone and concrete. Deeper caves remain unexplored.

Their curiosity leads to a gelatinous cube that engulfs the First Sons' sorcerer, Faelan. Evelyn's lightning javelin destroys it and releases him. A hook horror attacks during retreat; Evelyn turns back for the stumbling Krandal, then staggers the creature with the horn. Koh kills it with an arrow. Another horror approaches its body as he collects a trophy.

At the temple, Carrie receives the recovered objects. Cassius arranges guards, accepts responsibility for the floor and pays the Delvers. Further searching reveals a separate droning shaft beneath the main altar. An elf collapses; another unresponsive patient taps a repeating rhythm. Krandal records it, and Maynard entrusts him with a devotional amulet. Koh returns home wondering whether his new silent speech could reach his wife.[^wells]

## 7. The lake and the broken machine

Koh tries at dawn. His wife's still body gives way to a dark vision in which she recognizes and embraces him beside a flower. She fears unseen captors will discover his presence and believes her remaining there keeps the town safe. A drone swells as dunes surround them. Forced back into the bedroom, Koh sees black sand fall from her clenched hands and gathers it in a vial. She does not wake.

The guild sends its bronze companies to seek ruins among neighboring peoples through diplomacy. On the eastern ride, Evelyn tentatively interprets Krandal's recorded code as a funeral dirge. Koh admits knowing another unresponsive sufferer without naming his wife. At the kuo-toa village, the gifts of fish and pearls succeed more quietly than his telepathy: villagers hear his thoughts and worship him despite his protests. The trader Glepo Ma offers information for Koh's shovel and asks them to recover a staff from underwater ruins.

Leaving heavy equipment ashore, they drink water-breathing potions and descend through familiar architecture. A hidden ring goes to Krandal. Deeper down, a ritualist attacks beside a slime-streaked carving, drawing Koh into a violent whirlpool. The companions subdue him without killing him. He speaks of a sleeping god in the Dunes; they bind him for return to his people.

Elsewhere, Cassius and Carrie carry Atomer's discoveries before the Dungeon Guild Academy. Cassius asks for gold-ranked champions to confront the danger below his city. The board challenges his evidence, dismisses Carrie's standing and urges faith in the Delvers' selection. Cassius voices his doubts about the three. His appeal wins no reinforcements: the Bronze Ball must provide funding, and Selenar will inspect his guild. He and Carrie begin the long return.[^academy]

Beneath the lake, Koh identifies the captive's Wand of Secrets. It points Evelyn toward a geared quarter-disc concealed behind the carving. They press on into an abandoned market, where a floating, beaked creature attacks. Koh kills it after its tentacles seize him and Evelyn. Krandal retrieves a fog-emitting bottle, but the tooth-framed door beyond stays shut: their breathing magic is nearly spent.

They surface in time. At the village, Glepo Ma passes the recovered staff to a mounted, darkmantle-cowled collector who threatens the settlement and leaves two accomplices to rob them. The Delvers overcome both, preserving their lives. One man's altered face resolves into the familiar Broadside. Glepo Ma offers a gear-crank and coins in reparation, then departs to warn the guild party at the bullywug village. The Delvers ride home with three living prisoners.[^lake]

## 8. The Noble Hollow

On the road, a young bullywug helps a wounded dwarf out of the swamp. He is Boonefellow of the First Sons, whose company has been ambushed. Faelan was taken and Norman disappeared. Evelyn heals him, and he carries all three prisoners to the guild while the Delvers search the village. They find signs of struggle but lose the tracks and return to Atomer late at night.

The captives are already in separate cells. With Cassius and Carrie still absent, Jimmy and Clearstone permit the Delvers into the guild's truth-bound interrogation room. Broadside identifies the darkmantle-cowled rider as Marek, a researcher sent below with silver-ranked protection. The captured cleric Cheet describes a summoning rite at Noble Hollow, requiring a high elf's sacrifice at midnight. Broadside admits gaps in his memory and offers to help stop the danger. Cheet stays imprisoned; the exhausted Boonefellow joins the rescue.

They ride north beside the river as midnight approaches. Green fire marks a petrified hollow. Koh scouts ahead and sees Faelan unconscious within the rite, Norman bound nearby, Marek leading the ritual and Solburn guarding him. Broadside grapples with his former colleague, trying to reach him through the influence that has overtaken them. Evelyn and Koh press Marek while Krandal protects his companions.

Evelyn knocks Marek unconscious. The geared stone beneath Faelan breaks as he falls, ending the chanting and flame. Its construction recalls the fragment recovered under the lake. Then the ground collapses into a cavern, carrying almost everyone down. A towering, tendrilled creature opens its eye, and piercers emerge from a crevice in its body.

The roper seizes rescuers and former enemies alike. Krandal commands it to release its prey, while Koh's arrow finds its vulnerable eye. Evelyn descends on Sultan's sash and fights at close quarters. Solburn is badly bitten; Krandal extends his healing to the man who had opposed them, and the restored sorcerer joins the struggle. One fallen cultist is swallowed. Evelyn drives a javelin into the opening from which the young emerge, and the creature tears itself apart trying to remove it.

The piercers scatter, but the rescue is unfinished. Koh spots Faelan in the water. Evelyn reaches him with healing, then tends Norman, and both awaken. Marek's rite has been stopped and the First Sons recovered. The darkness beneath Atomer still extends far beyond the cavern where they have fought.[^hollow]

## After the first arc

Several days later, Selenar Everbloom and Cassius hear the Delvers' account. The investigation clears them of wrongdoing and recognizes their courage. The guild treats Broadside and Solburn as men acting under hostile influence; Marek is to face trial, while Cheet is destined for rehabilitation. No extra cash reward is available, with guild resources committed to war against northern vampires. Instead, Selenar promises Krandal the deed to an old tower in farmland, bringing his potato-growing ambition closer to reality.

The Bronze Ball still approaches. The tooth-framed door remains closed, Koh's wife still sleeps, and the carved symbols and dark sand have yielded questions rather than answers. Yet the three who began with wooden badges now have a name, allies and a shared history of returning for one another.

The guild's ordinary noise resumes until a short, bearded visitor arrives with buckets overflowing with coins and snakes. He demands adventurers for Snake Island. The offer awaits them as the first part of their journey ends.[^end]
