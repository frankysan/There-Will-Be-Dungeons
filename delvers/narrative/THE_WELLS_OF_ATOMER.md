# The Wells of Atomer

## Lives beyond the guild

The Darkest Delvers' new bronze rank brought more than prestigious work. Ordinary assignments continued to carry them through the city, and each adventurer returned to a life that few of the others fully understood. Koh gained an unexpected mentor in Buck Broscale, called Wildfire. In the quiet training yard behind the guild, the silver-ranked warrior taught Koh to speak through thought alone. He spoke of his brother, who had died carrying the globe Koh had recovered, and of strange psychic relics that could bring danger to those capable of using them. Koh learned to shape his powers more deliberately, though his teacher's warnings left him with unanswered questions.[^koh]

Krandal's duties were no less surprising. A cockatrice had turned him to stone during one of the week's quests, and he awoke at Pelor's temple as the spell wore off. Before he had fully recovered, Brother Maynard summoned him to lead a wedding at the governor's mansion. Krandal tried to keep the words of the ceremony in order and struggled with the lines he had written on his forearm. The prayer nevertheless found its blessing, leaving the newly married pair with radiant rings. At school, Evelyn found a different kindness: Margaret, her trusted roommate, gave her a small enchanted bag able to conceal her great hammer. Together they prepared for the demands of a forthcoming bronze ball, where guild and finishing school would meet.[^companions]

## Rumors from underground

Cassius called the bronze adventurers to a meeting. Carrie Underworld had marked reports of strange creatures across the city, noting that one kind seemed to follow another toward the surface. The patterns troubled her: stirges, darkmantles, piercers and gricks were appearing in places where they had not been expected. She wondered what could be following them. The strange dark ichor found on other assignments was another cause for unease. The guild divided the city's wells among several parties. The Darkest Delvers chose the southwestern quarter, where recent monsters had already given them reason to be wary.[^meeting]

The well stood in a sunny square beside homes, shops and a blacksmith's forge. Koh befriended a boy and asked him to shout if he saw peril. Evelyn kept watch while Krandal discovered a trail of rust leading away from the water bucket. Following it into the forge, Koh found a bizarre insect whose feelers ruined metal. Nearby, a barrel in a warehouse revealed itself to be a living monster. In the fight that followed Koh's old stone-metal sword hilt crumbled, but the blue blade he conjured endured through the strength of his own mind. Evelyn overcame the strange barrel creature, and the three rescued an injured workman and Bruno, the family's exhausted dog.[^square]

## Beneath the wells

They returned to Cassius with evidence that monsters had reached the neighborhood. Yet their report left something important undone. A stone thrown into the darkness had proved little about what lay below or the safety of the water above. Cassius sent the Brigand Patrol to guard the square, and the Darkest Delvers hurried back carrying a sack of concrete. Passing Pelor's temple, they saw another guild party treating the public well to a spectacular burst of fire. It did little to reassure them.[^cutoff]

Koh tied his enchanted climbing sash to the well frame and descended into the cold shaft, his glowing globe following behind. A deep wound in the masonry opened upon a natural cave. Evelyn struggled down after him with the heavy sack, and Krandal brought up the rear. The passage twisted through wet stone and narrowed until even Krandal had to crawl. Evelyn smashed a nest of young rust monsters, and the company fought off stirges spilling from a hanging sack. Deeper still, they met a little stone creature who spoke in broken Primordial. Krandal shared his potato with the rock eater, winning an improbable friend and an anxious warning whose meaning remained unclear.[^descent]

Beyond the creature's home the rough cave joined worked stone. Its old symbols and cruel traps were eerily familiar: another remnant like the forest ruin where the Delvers had encountered the gricks. Here they met the First Sons Brotherhood, who had already slain a darkmantle and felt entitled to explore first. Edgar Boonefellow urged civility while his companions disputed the claim. Evelyn and Krandal pressed on, and the two parties agreed, uneasily, to let the Darkest Delvers lead. Koh leapt over opening floor traps and pinned a needle-trap switch with an arrow; the others followed with considerable care, leaving a few burdens behind until the passage could be crossed.[^rivals]

## The door beneath the sanctuary

In an old armory the Delvers found long rune-marked shafts that answered Koh's mental power with shining spearheads. A stone horn waited in a chest. Evelyn blew it, and the blast struck her and Krandal with terrible force, robbing them briefly of their hearing. Koh's quiet mind-to-mind speech guided them through the pain. After resting they ventured up another stairway, only to find six stone platforms, each fitted with a socket for the ancient spears. The mechanism required more weight and more people than they could provide alone. Koh called the First Sons back, and their combined company took its places upon the six platforms.[^armory]

The stone above them split. Not into an imagined portal but through the very floor of Pelor's temple, where the sanctuary's furnishings shifted and startled monks scrambled aside. Brother Maynard stared down at Krandal and his companions with utter disbelief. The adventurers had uncovered a hidden connection beneath the city, and damaged the temple in the process. First they had a more immediate obligation: together the two guild parties carried stone and concrete back into the narrow passages and sealed the connections leading toward the southwestern well. The friendly rock eater lent his strength. The city's drinking water had still not been tested, and the tunnels beyond the buried ruin remained a mystery.[^temple]

Instead of abandoning that mystery, the six adventurers searched further. They entered a spacious wet cavern where shapes on the ceiling proved to be waiting piercers. An odd patch of air beyond the pool seemed to lift scattered pebbles from the floor. Evelyn extended a javelin, felt resistance and discovered what the light had hidden: a gelatinous cube filled the passage ahead. The creature had not yet struck. The deeper threat was only beginning to show itself.[^cube]
