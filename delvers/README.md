# Delvers — independent campaign archive

**Delvers is a separate campaign set in its own fictional reality.** Its continuity is independent of both [The Wastes](../world/README.md) and [Inverse](../inverse/README.md). Shared performers, familiar fantasy terms or similar names do not establish shared people, places, cosmology or history.

**Status, 2026-10-24: preparation only.** The local archive contains **37 entries across seasons 1–3**, with **74 ASR exports and 37 matching local MP3s**. No Delvers episode dossier, played chronology, character biography or narrative has been completed by this preparation. The researched-story horizon is **none**; source availability is a separate measure.

## Research order and scope

The intended sequence is to finish the current Wastes season-one research, then work through **Delvers season 1, season 2 and season 3**, using the verified archive entries. **First Delvers episode to research when that work starts: S1E01 — How Much Is Potato.** Preparation does not switch the active Wastes research pointer.

Delvers needs the same kinds of documentation as Wastes: source-first dossiers, a subject-centered world reference, a continuity ledger and chronology, and readable narrative histories. The [plan](DOCUMENTATION_PLAN.md) assigns those layers their own paths and criteria for creation. No Wastes arc numbering, character pages or timeline entries carry over. Seasons are archive divisions; story arcs will be determined from played evidence.

## Evidence boundary

The [guild-guide provenance register](../reports/DISCORD_SPELLING_2026-10-21.md) supplies Delvers setting-handout leads, not a played opening scene. Treat handout/planning material, played scenes, character testimony and retrospective cast commentary as distinct evidence. Future-season sources may be inventoried now without importing their revelations into an earlier episode's knowledge or spoiler horizon.
