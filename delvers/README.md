# Delvers

Three adventurers begin with wooden guild badges and small commissions in Atomer. Their work draws them into ancient ruins, a city's troubled institutions and a danger beneath the earth. Delvers is a separate campaign in its own reality, independent of The Wastes and Inverse.

## Evidence boundary

The [guild-guide provenance register](../reports/DISCORD_SPELLING_2026-10-21.md) supplies setting-handout evidence, distinct from played events. Character testimony, visions, hallucinations, retrospective commentary and unchosen plans retain their own attribution. Later revelations do not overwrite what characters knew at an earlier point.

The final invitation is shown without a performed wedding; the red royal letter remains unopened. Use the finale dossier for the detailed limits rather than completing these hooks editorially.
