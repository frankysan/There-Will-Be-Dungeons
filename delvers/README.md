# Delvers — independent campaign archive

**Delvers is a separate campaign set in its own fictional reality.** Its continuity is independent of both [The Wastes](../world/README.md) and [Inverse](../inverse/README.md). Shared performers, familiar fantasy terms or similar names do not establish shared people, places, cosmology or history.

**Status 2026-10-25:** **S1E01–S1E10** have ASR-provisional research. The authentic **S1E03 has been recovered and analyzed** from three `(corrected)` transcript exports, restoring continuous first-season text coverage. The older S1E03-labelled exports still contain S1E04 and are preserved as source-identity evidence. 37 episode IDs; 77 SRT files; 38 inventoried MP3 copies. No audio listening completed.

## Research order and scope
The formerly missing genuine **S1E03 — Raw End Of Joke Stick** has been recovered and analyzed at the proper episode ID, connecting the S1E02 guild return to S1E04's grick cliffhanger. **Season One / first arc completed through S1E12 — End Friends**. [Eight-chapter first-arc narrative](narrative/SEASON_ONE_ARC.md). **Next source: S2E00 — Cassius at the Guild**, whose actual recording type must be established before treating it as played. **S1E11** resolves S1E10's submerged cutoff: the party finds further Dawnless Dunes ruins and artifacts, surfaces safely, and later **departs the kuo-toa village toward guild with three living prisoners** after an ambush, without reaching guild. The darkmantle-cowled rider escapes with the fish wizard's conch staff. **S1E10** ends underwater with a captive; that prior cutoff remains historically correct. **S1E09** resolves the cube confrontation, brings the party back for the temple's second hidden shaft and twin unresponsive elves, and ends before the newly selected kuo-toa journey. S1E05 continues the completed grick assignment, advances the party to level three and approves bronze promotion; **S1E06 actually awards the badges and completes Skull Keep**; **S1E07 documents continuing creature incursions and sends the party back to an uninspected southwestern well**. Enzo's attributed Dawnless Dunes warning remains unresolved. The older misnamed S1E03 files remain preserved as actual S1E04 copies, and the corrected MP3 has not been listened to.

Delvers needs the same kinds of documentation as Wastes: source-first dossiers, a subject-centered world reference, a continuity ledger and chronology, and readable narrative histories. The [plan](DOCUMENTATION_PLAN.md) assigns those layers their own paths and criteria for creation. No Wastes arc numbering, character pages or timeline entries carry over. Season One concludes the **first Delvers arc** at S1E12, as designated by the archive owner and supported by the episode's explicit finale. Other seasons/arcs require their own research before boundaries are asserted.

## Evidence boundary

The [guild-guide provenance register](../reports/DISCORD_SPELLING_2026-10-21.md) supplies Delvers setting-handout leads, not a played opening scene. Treat handout/planning material, played scenes, character testimony and retrospective cast commentary as distinct evidence. Future-season sources may be inventoried now without importing their revelations into an earlier episode's knowledge or spoiler horizon.
