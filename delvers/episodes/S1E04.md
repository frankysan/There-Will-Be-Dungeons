## Entities, knowledge, custody, and continuity

**People:** [Koh](../world/characters/koh-spiros.md) remains a cautious rogue whose **bedridden wife, six-year-old daughter Penelope and concealed financial crisis were already established in genuine [S1E03](S1E03.md)**. His S1E04 plans for family time and an aristocratic public image build on that prior revelation; his family members are not shown again in S1E04. Evelyn's urge to save others and uncover hidden spaces drives decisions despite danger; Krandal combines stubborn persistence with reliance on his companions. **Veggie Sticks** protects the hippogriffs but admits his claimed omniscience about the woodland was false. **Brad/Brador** is a small animated plant welcomed back into Veggie Sticks's home; **Evelyn does not keep it**. **Aegis** is the centaur cart driver. Cassius accepts a guild trophy and holds the hilt for inspection. Rank authority's spoken name and the city spelling require audio/handout checks.

**Places and symbols:** [Limestone sinkhole and worked-stone dungeon](../world/places/grick-sinkhole.md) contain ancient carved walls, a loud altar opening, a 51-pound Elvish tablet, matching glyphs, traps with wall spears and rising floor pins, pits, stone alcoves and an armory chamber. The tablet provides a **partial reading of the wall symbols as 'sky people' in S1E03**; their fuller translation, significance and connection to the dungeon remain **unknown**. Evelyn does not finish deciphering them in S1E04. Veggie Sticks **claims** the gricks may be emerging from the dungeon; the party observes wounded gricks and trap-pierced corpses, supporting hazard exposure but not a fully explored origin, builder or purpose.

**Inventory:** [S1E03](S1E03.md) ends with Krandal carrying **Yuri, a rescued thrush**. S1E04 does **not** clearly establish a release, death or transfer of the bird, so its final whereabouts remain unverified; do not silently remove it from continuity. Koh keeps Sultan's **climbing sash** and the **drift globe**; he uses the former for extraction and the attempted shield-sled. An **ordinary rope** attached beneath the sash is intentionally abandoned at the sinkhole; **Krandal's shield is borrowed and returned/implied still with them**, with no explicit separate custody return line—do not make it a lost item. Krandal's priest vestments, two candles, four rations and tinderbox are lost; his damaged backpack is **repaired**. Koh speaks of **losing two daggers**; exact recovery/final inventory is not explicitly recounted. A tablet remains behind. Evelyn opens the chest and briefly holds the **runic hilt**, which is subsequently **deposited with Cassius** for a delayed inspection; no Sun Blade identity confirmed. Grick tentacle trophy is turned in. Lay on Hands points are depleted after curing poison; HP condition and full rest after return are not established.

**Guild status:** Three assignments completed (farm, Kord crypt, grick scout), third **180 gold** actually paid. Cumulative **600 gold total gross for the party / 200 per member** across these three stated rewards, **before any purchases/spending**, is arithmetic only; do not assert current carried purse amounts. Promotion to bronze is **requested**, dungeon exploration claim **submitted**, and XP threshold for level three **announced** without played character development/level-up details. The **four days** are the *available/expected* downtime, not four days already elapsed.
