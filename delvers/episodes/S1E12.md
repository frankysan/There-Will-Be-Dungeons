## Finale continuity and arc-level interpretation

- **First-arc resolved threat:** Marek's **immediate midnight rite is stopped** and the mother roper is killed; Faelan and Norman rescued alive; party/group returns to guild **by postscene retrospective**. It does **not** resolve the actual origin of the Dawnless Dunes, eliminate all connected beasts or establish objective cosmology. The creature exposed by collapse is **already physically beneath Noble Hollow**; do not automatically conflate it with the unmanifested **summoned “beast of the lake.”**
- **S1E11 cutoff refined:** Short rest, armor re-equipping and road journey **actually played in S1E12**. All three prisoners reach guild with **Boonefellow**, but **he takes them while PCs instead pursue abducted comrades**. No claim that Delvers themselves arrive first with all three.
- **Known silver identities:** Broadside and Solburn are **real guild silver colleagues**; guild ultimately treats them as affected by malevolent influence, *not definitively corrupt*. Cheet/Cheat admits she wanted to watch events unfold; the guild later plans rehabilitation. Earlier phonetic “Marek/Coal” name confusion is partly clarified: **Marek/Merrick** is the **darkmantle-hat leader/researcher**, not necessarily the robed captured woman; **Cheet** is the woman. Song-like incantation name **“Orgothel/Argir...”** ASR uncertain, do not make a deity/monster ID from it.
- **Important artifact:** Fragment and ritual disk have **matching gear-and-stone-metal construction**, stronger than prior hypothesis of connection, but **fragment/crank were not fitted** and ritual disk is **broken**. No evidence of item loss, magical healing of Koh's wife, or reopening submerged tooth-gate.
- **Moral choices:** Party deliberately **preserves prisoners**, recruits previously manipulated fighter and **heals another manipulated opponent**, interrupting ritual before elf sacrifice. One unnamed cultist is **actually swallowed by roper**; assertion “everyone survived” would be false.
- **Equipment custody clarified retrospectively:** The cast [April 2021 bonus](../../episodes/BONUS-2021-04.md), ~00:25–00:27, explicitly says the **Greatsword of Wounding was handed back to Broadside** when the party decided to trust him. This **postseason recollection** resolves prior dossier/profile uncertainty about the sword, **without adding an unplayed present-day transfer scene or assigning a precise in-game handover timestamp**.
- **Pay/status:** Base operation reward from earlier episode remains separate from **no supplemental finale cash payout**. Gear-crank and ~1,000 gp sack from Glepo Ma are **previous S1E11 compensation**, not guild payment. Farm deed is **future gift pledged**, no document handed. Snake Island job only offered at cutoff.
- **Unresolved cases after arc:** Wife's psychic imprisonment/black sand, unresponsive elves and dirge, Enzo's darker-world accounts, black slime and rune mechanisms, lower gated ruin, fish wizard's final status, trial results, and all future guest/season-two developments **not yet resolved**.
