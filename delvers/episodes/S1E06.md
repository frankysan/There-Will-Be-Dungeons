## Cross-episode continuity, custody and unresolved issues

**Promotion and route:** S1E05 ended with approved promotion/badges due next morning; **S1E06 physically awards all three unnumbered bronze badges** in exchange for numbered wooden ones, delivers the shared library key to **Koh**, and begins and completes the **Skull Keep** mission. The contract is no longer merely selected; the kill and return are completed.

**Items and expenses:**
- **Koh** physically retains the **Soul Knife**, his climbing sash after the failed window dive, shared **library key**, forgery kit and other supplies listed in the opening recap, and the **two orange garnets** recovered after banishment. His improvised door wedge **dagger is retrieved** on exit; the spell blade leaves no physical cuts.
- **Evelyn** receives **two holy-water jars from Helena** for the group, uses **one** on the skull's defeated remains, and **one remains unspent** at cutoff (exact personal final container custody after dousing not re-audited). She also uses her enchanted equipment in combat; don't assume spent javelins were recovered or lost without source.
- **Krandal** receives incense and uses **Create or Destroy Water** at least twice, both times as played magic rather than a physical holy-water crafting operation. He obtains **two mundane face maces** ordered in S1E05. GM states the earlier purchased enchanted gear was picked up before the journey, but **individual equipment delivery records need a detailed listening/handout review**. He heals Koh six HP at the keep.
- **350-gold guild contract** and Alexandre's promise of **up to 500 bonus gold each** are spoken earlier in the session. The GM later says **about 400 bonus gold** paid after damage assessment, plus the base contract. Preserve wording and verify final *per-member accounting* before consolidating purses or claiming the full 500 was earned.

**Character knowledge and revelations:** Koh shows copied ancient symbols to both friends and Enzo, but keeps the **identity of the person needing extraordinary healing** private in this exchange. Evelyn's reason for adventuring gains its first explicit clue—she is **seeking a person**, whose identity remains unknown to reader and companions here. Krandal's reluctance to lie conflicts with attempts to minimize the damaged furnishings; no actual false report to Alexandre is shown. The client's warnings about mirrors take on new significance, but Enzo's claim not to know his possible descendant leaves **the client's precise knowledge unproven**.

**Setting:** The keep's remarkable upkeep results from undead servants animated by a **failed ritual, according to Enzo**; their collapse after the holy-water victory is **directly observed**, supporting a shared magical dependency without verifying every detail of the ritual. The mirrored apparition persists faintly after the skull's defeat and no further disposition is shown. **Dawnless Dunes**, **sky people**, dark tunnels and the claimed “heart” may connect the older ruins and this lake. The exact old-Elvish inscription, origin of the phenomenon, timeline and truth of Enzo's prophecy are **open**.
