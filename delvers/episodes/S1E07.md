## Source and interpretation boundaries

**Three distinct layers of the same episode:** The tea presentation and network outro are real-world promotional framing. The exaggerated “tell us what happened at Skull Keep” exchange is a recap staged as a conversation at an unspecified point and **must not manufacture an in-world prison/interrogation**. Played downtime scenes, guild report and well assignment form the campaign continuity. Several hunts and a cockatrice fight occur in **GM-summary or immediately-before-cutoff flashback**, not as full combat scenes.

**Continuity resolutions:** S1E01's dead dragonborn associated with recovered globe is **Brom Broscale**, according to his **living older brother Buck/Wildfire**. S1E05 Buck's healing scene was therefore the brother, not the dead man. The important S1E07 artifact change is physical **Soul Knife hilt destroyed during Koh's critical strike**, but psychic ability **persists without hilt**. He also has the **Life Stealing shortsword**, not a documented switch to Corpse Slayer. Evelyn's Vicious hammer and Krandal's +1 scale mail now appear in played scenes. The guild has continued to pay new weekly earnings, **580 gold announced for the week's work**, precise per-member allocation unconfirmed. There is **no new confirmed level-up**.

**Who knows:** Wildfire and Koh conduct mind-speech training in private; Carrie arranged it, but Evelyn/Krandal are not shown learning its specifics. Evelyn's roommate Margaret knows her adventurer identity and aids her with a magic bag, while Lady Vestal still scrutinizes her social presentation. The guild discusses the creature trends and ichor broadly, but the **“bigger fish” predator chain and piercer metamorphosis are hypotheses**, and the Skull Keep ancestor's earlier prophecy was his own testimony; neither theory proves the other.

**Actual cutoff:** The party defeats one rust monster, one mimic and one stirge (also healing civilian and dog), **but the well itself remains uninspected**. Brigand Patrol takes temporary watch. The party carries an **unspent sack of concrete back toward the well**, with no claim of permanent repair or safe drinking water. The district's blacksmith is not a bugman, by direct appearance and explanation.
