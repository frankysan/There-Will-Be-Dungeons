- **Private / shared:** Krandal **pockets ~500-gp aquamarine from doll without disclosing** it. Koh holds **23 small jade artworks and vault room miscellany**, Shrugs holds separate items in borrowed bag; **exact gems counts, value, ownership after group exit not determined**.
- **Conch staff:** Earlier fish ritualist's conch-topped staff was **carried away by a mysterious rider in S1E11**; this vault contains **visually familiar conch-topped staff**. Evelyn **smashes this object**, and Koh **packs its broken pieces**. **Only probable identity match**, no explicit forensic identification or connected culprit proved.
