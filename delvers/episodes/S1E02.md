## Items, knowledge and last verified state

- **Holy water:** all three issued flasks used once each in the cardio, muscles and strength basins respectively. Evelyn retains purchased ritual silver as far as the transcript demonstrates; **no additional holy water made**. Empty vials' final custody is only explicit for Krandal's retained bottle.
- **Strength potions:** House's first consumed by Meatball; Crush's second consumed by Sultan. Neither remains with the party at episode end.
- **Drift globe:** Koh still carries/commands the recovered orb and **uses the once-daily daylight effect** against Shadows and Studwell. It cannot be assumed available again before the next dawn. Party did not take a new item from the Shadows.
- **Sultan's sash:** given as a reward and physically retrieved by Koh; confirmed by GM to function as **Rope of Climbing**. Its shared-party status differs from assigning Koh permanent exclusive custody.
- **Injuries:** Shadows inflict real necrotic damage and temporary strength reductions, Koh and Evelyn/Krandal briefly go down at different points, and spell slots/healing are spent. Neither a subsequent long rest nor full recovery of drained strength occurs on screen.
- **Knowledge:** House's theory of Studwell's genesis remains **House's inference**, not omniscient proof. The unseen figure at the guild supply desk is not identified. The party sees mirror-only apparitions and later fights creatures but does not establish a wider haunting beyond this crypt.
