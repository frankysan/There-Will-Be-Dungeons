Map inscriptions and ASR-derived names retain separate authority in the cited notes below. The [chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md) holds the detailed event order, and the [integration record](../../reports/DELVERS_CAMPAIGN_INTEGRATION_2026-10-27.md) explains this index's bounded reconciliation with canonical accounts.
