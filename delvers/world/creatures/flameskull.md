# Flameskull — the restless guardian of Skull Keep

## Introduction

A skull wreathed in green fire circles the highest tower of old [Enzo Keep](../places/skull-keep.md), guiding itself by two orange lights burning in its sockets. From the distant lake shore it resembles a strange sentry; near at hand its bone shows runes and the eyes resolve into fiery gems. Its scream is an alarm and a threat, and it defends the keep with rays of fire and spells capable of setting an entire room aflame.[^appearance]

## An unnatural life

The guild library describes flameskulls as floating undead heads with magical defenses and resistance to several types of harm. A warning note singles out their capacity to cast a great fireball. Helena Clearstone tells the bronze adventurers that such a creature may regenerate if a victory is not followed by consecration with an entire jar of holy water.[^lore]

The skull at Enzo Keep seems alert to visitors but does not immediately rush to fight. It watches the approaching party, resumes its patrol when they remain calm and responds to the ringing of a tower mechanism. It shatters a stained-glass window and hurls fire into an upper room. During the confrontation Koh tries to bind its mouth with an enchanted sash, but misses and falls into the courtyard. A later encounter creates a rolling sphere of fire that Krandal turns into steam with summoned water.[^encounters]

The figure in the tower's mirror claims that this flying head is part of himself: the result of an immortality rite that split [Enzo Vanderbuck Withers](../characters/enzo-vanderbuck-withers.md) into an unquiet spirit and a malevolent flaming remnant. After speaking with that trapped consciousness, the Delvers climb to the rooftop for a final confrontation. Koh's conjured psychic blade at last brings the skull down, its unnatural life extinguished by Evelyn's holy water. The nearby undead servants collapse with it.[^origins]

## Current status

The Skull Keep flameskull is destroyed. Its consecrated bones dissolved into ash, leaving two orange garnets that Koh retrieved. The mirror remained intact, its reflection quiet and diminished; whether Enzo had found rest remained uncertain. No later return of this skull is known.[^ending]
