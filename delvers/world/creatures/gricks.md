# Gricks

## Introduction

Gricks are dangerous predators with scales that resist ordinary strikes, grasping limbs and beaks. Koh first brought the guild a description of a creature he had avoided fighting; Cassius identified it as a grick. A later scouting assignment brought Koh, Evelyn and Krandal into direct conflict with several around a [forest sinkhole](../places/grick-sinkhole.md).[^introduction]

## The sinkhole predators

The creatures hunted among the limestone ruins and nearby brush, threatening both the adventurers' climb out of the sinkhole and their retreat through the woods. One tore open Krandal's backpack and consumed spilled rations, candles and scraps of clothing. Another knocked him unconscious and dragged him away before Koh killed it and Evelyn revived him. Their appetite made them dangerous even during an attempted escape.[^behavior]

Several gricks bore wounds before the company fought them. Deeper inside the worked-stone dungeon, bodies impaled on wall spears and floor needles showed that the ancient traps could kill these predators too. Veggie Sticks believed they might be emerging from the dungeon, but their deeper origin remained unknown.[^dungeon]

## Current status

The company reported four gricks killed and delivered a tentacle to Cassius as a trophy. Other carcasses had been killed by the dungeon's traps. The immediate scouting job was accepted, but the unexplored passages remained a possible source of further danger.[^status]
