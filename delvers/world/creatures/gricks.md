# Gricks

## Introduction

Gricks are scaled predators with grasping limbs and beaks, resistant to ordinary strikes. Koh first avoided one before Cassius identified it from his description. Further encounters revealed hunters among woodland ruins and beneath Atomer, sometimes concealed among the stone of the underground chambers.[^introduction]

## The sinkhole predators

Around the [forest sinkhole](../places/grick-sinkhole.md), gricks hunted among the limestone ruins and nearby brush. Their appetite endangered the adventurers even as they tried to leave: one tore into Krandal's backpack and swallowed rations, candles and scraps of clothing; another knocked him unconscious and dragged him away before Koh killed it and Evelyn revived him.[^behavior]

Some already bore wounds. Deeper in the worked-stone dungeon, carcasses impaled on wall spears and floor needles showed that the old defenses could kill them. Veggie Sticks suspected the creatures were emerging from below, but their deeper origin remained unknown. Evelyn, Koh and Krandal killed four during the scouting expedition and delivered a tentacle to Cassius; the guild accepted their report without the dungeon being fully explored.[^dungeon][^status]

## Predators beneath the city

The caverns beneath Atomer offered other prey. One grick seized a piercer and withdrew into another tunnel while the Delvers fought nearby. Gricks later emerged into the Bronze Ball alongside other underground creatures; the Gentleman Cavalier killed one during the rescue. Their presence reached beyond the forest ruins.[^city]

## Concealment in the wind chamber

In the [wind chamber](../places/right-stair-grick-urn-complex.md), apparently scattered rocks concealed gricks, including an unusually large, overfed creature. Removing the grick-headed urn stopped the chamber's artificial airflow, and the predators stirred in the ensuing silence. Koh killed smaller gricks and the great one; Krandal killed another with his enchanted face mace, while Barclay struck another with holy light. At least one escaped among the rocks and water, and the company left without pursuing every survivor.[^wind]

## Current status

Gricks survived the wind-chamber encounter. Freshly butchered grick and hook-horror carcasses later appeared among the meager provisions of Khalazar's captive workers. After his defeat the dungeon's monsters dwindled over time, but the surviving gricks' numbers and range remained unknown.[^aftermath]
