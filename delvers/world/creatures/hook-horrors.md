# Hook horrors — hunters of the underground passages

## Introduction

Hook horrors are large, bird-headed hunters with plated backs and great hooked limbs. The deep tunnels beneath Atomer shelter wild adults, while one young creature raised by Krandal eventually found a different life on his farm.[^sighting]

## First confrontation

Koh first glimpsed a pale-eyed, whiskered beak beyond the Delvers' battle with a gelatinous cube. His guild guide offered an old picture and a name, with little explanation. The company chose to retreat, but the creature charged after them.[^first]

Evelyn shielded Krandal and took a wound from one of its hooks. Her horn's thunderous blast disrupted the hunter's apparent navigation by sound, and Koh killed it with an arrow through its open beak. He cut free a hooked limb, later submitting it to Carrie for inspection.[^fight][^status]

A second horror approached the corpse and touched its head against the dead creature. Koh withdrew quietly. Their relationship remained unknown, and this survivor was left below the city.[^second]

## Wild hunters in Atomer

Two hook horrors emerged by the Bronze Ball's main exit during the underground outbreak. Koh killed one threatening Mack and Church. Later, an adult occupied the bank's document-storage floor, where its nest held a freshly laid leathery egg. The Delvers killed the adult; Krandal took the egg, named it Samuel and refused Yalora's request to surrender it, hoping to raise a farm worker.[^bank]

## Scroungy's early life

The egg hatched in Krandal's barn. A tiny, beetle-shelled, chicken-like fledgling rubbed against his ankle and cooed, already attached to him. It later climbed into his adventuring bag and ate his rations. Known as Scroungy, it fed on candles and rats as well; Evelyn supplied a pink ribbon cut from her enchanted rope so she could track it.[^hatchling]

Scroungy answered calls near an underground nest, drawing the attention of three adults feeding on loose rats. Krandal's attempt to introduce the hatchling failed: the adults began to flank him, and Koh killed the leading creature as it raised a hook. The other two backed away. Scroungy clambered back into Krandal's bag and stayed with him.[^adults]

Elsewhere in Atomer, another hook horror burst through the floor of Loran Ilfelkyr's school. Evelyn killed the intruder while protecting the pupils.[^school]

## Current status

After Khalazar's defeat, Scroungy became a tame beast of burden over the following months. A year later, the docile creature worked beside the donkey at [Splenderbelt Farm](../places/splenderbelt-farm-delvers-tower.md), pulling a cart. The old pink ribbon remained a keepsake in the rebuilt tower. The wild survivors' later lives remained unknown.[^farm]
