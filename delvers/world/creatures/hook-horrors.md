# Hook horrors — hunters of the underground passages

## Introduction

The hook horrors are enormous, bird-headed creatures that stalk the deep natural tunnels beneath the city. Their long bodies, plated backs and great hooked limbs give them an unnerving likeness to both predatory birds and hard-shelled cave beasts. They move with a confidence that makes even experienced adventurers hesitate to press farther into their territory.[^sighting]

## First confrontation

Koh first notices a pale-eyed, whiskered beak peering around a bend beyond the Delvers' battle with a gelatinous cube. His guild guide contains only an old image and the name *hook horror*, with few details. The company considers whether the beast is worth fighting, then decides to return to the guild rather than hunt a monster for which no reward has been arranged. The creature charges as they turn away.[^first]

The retreat falters on slick stone, and Evelyn places herself between the beast and Krandal. One great hook wounds her, but Krandal heals her as they flee. Evelyn's blast from the recently recovered horn disrupts the creature's apparent ability to track them by sound. Koh's arrow strikes through its open beak, and the horror falls. He later cuts one great hooked limb free as a trophy.[^fight]

When he returns to the body, another creature of the same kind emerges and bends over the dead one, touching its head against its companion. Koh withdraws quietly rather than start a second fight. The two creatures' exact relationship remains unknown, and the survivors are not pursued.[^second]

## Current status

**One hook horror is dead**, killed by Koh after Evelyn's thunder blast. Its **severed hooked limb was submitted to Carrie Underworld** with other specimens for guild inspection. **At least one additional live hook horror remains below the city** at the observed cutoff. The Delvers have not cleared the surrounding caverns or learned the creatures' broader ecology. Do not turn the characters' dramatic guesses about hearing or family bonds into general biological rules.[^status]
