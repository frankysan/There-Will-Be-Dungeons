# Skeletal bat with shadow-skin

<a id="skeletal-bat-with-shadow-skin--dawnless-dunes-balcony-s3e11"></a>

## Introduction

An enormous bat skeleton flies on almost silent wings over the black pyramid in the [Dawnless Dunes](../places/dawnless-dunes-pyramid.md), its bones enclosed by a dark, translucent outline of ears, nose and wings. Roughly fifteen feet long, it wears green jewels at the neck and chest and a saddle harness around its ribs. It settles on the upper balcony without a visible rider, turning its head and clicking as it searches the chamber.[^bat-introduction]

## Development by arc

<a id="s3e12-actually-destroyed-by-evelyn-alone"></a>

### Arc Three — The pursuit across the dunes

Koh suspects that the creature's clicks may find what his stealth conceals. With the captives' rescuers gathering below, [Evelyn](../characters/evelyn-grinkeeper.md) rides her elk Albert away from the pyramid and draws it after her.

In the dunes she turns to face it. Radiance from [Loran's lance](../items/vestal-diamond-heart-handle.md) disrupts its shadowed wings and brings the skeletal creature into the sand. Its scream banishes Albert, leaving Evelyn to fight on foot. She changes lance to sword, strikes through its ribs and finally shatters the framework. Its broken bones fall into the dunes. The elk is later called back intact.[^bat-defeat]

## Current status

The skeletal bat is destroyed. Its pursuit ends with Evelyn's victory before the captives' evacuation is completed. No rider emerges from the fallen creature, and it is not among the threats remaining at the pyramid.[^bat-status]
