# Ancient mummies of the triangle-gate catacombs

## Introduction

The [triangle-gate catacombs](../places/triangle-gate-catacombs.md) hold desiccated elven remains behind circular, rune-covered closures. One body drawn from a niche has dry, shedding skin and a surgically emptied interior. The people these remains once belonged to are unnamed, and the purpose of their preparation remains unknown.[^bodies]

## Occupants behind the spiral doors

As the closures open, other occupants animate and attack. More niches release foes during the confrontation, making the burial chamber dangerous beyond the first exposed body. Krandal's sacred turning drives some away, but the Delvers must still fight those that remain or return within reach.[^awakening]

The attackers wield cold, darkness and powers that restrain movement. One paralyzes Krandal and lifts him toward an empty recess until Koh breaks its concentration with a psychic strike. Another conceals itself, leaving dusty footprints that Evelyn follows before revealing it with radiant force. These powers make the tombs a threat of confinement as well as death.[^powers]

## Current status

Several awakened attackers are destroyed, and the Delvers withdraw alive with many closures still unexplored. A later expedition opens the great gate without a complete survey of the tombs. The remaining occupants' fate after Khalazar's defeat is unknown, as are their former identities and the origin of their animation.[^status]
