# The behir of Dungeon C4

## Introduction

The behir is a serpent-like cavern hunter with many small legs, a long tail and a mouth and tongue lit lightning-blue. It lurks within the dangerous passages of Dungeon C4, where missing adventurers have left both remains and rumors of a crushing, climbing predator.[^appearance]

## A hunter among the missing

Questioned through magic, a dead adventurer describes being crushed by the beast and recalls a companion fried in water. The witness also claims that lightning cannot harm it. Iron Monkey offers a living contrast to the dead: found hiding in a mound of dung, he has survived the dungeon's dangers.[^witness]

When Evelyn draws the hunter out, its attacks reveal both reach and force. Lightning sweeps along a line through the company; jaws and constricting coils hold Evelyn while the others fight to free her. The beast bites off one of Iron Monkey's arms, but he remains alive and retreats. Evelyn eventually breaks its hold.[^fight]

Krandal's summoned mace brings the behir down. As its body collapses, Koh seizes Evelyn and carries her clear with his teleporting cloak. The fallen corpse leaks acid that sparks with lightning.[^ending]

## Current status

The C4 behir is dead, and the adventurers leave through the entrance opened with a recovered blue crystal. Iron Monkey is last seen alive with an arm lost; no later restoration is known. The fate of other creatures in the unexplored passages remains uncertain.[^status]
