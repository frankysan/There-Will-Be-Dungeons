# The roper of Noble Hollow

## Introduction

Beneath the petrified trees of Noble Hollow waits a creature that looks at first like an enormous column of natural stone. Its main body rises nearly to the cavern's ceiling, sheathed in rocklike flesh, but its secrets are living ones. A great eye opens high upon its body, and a toothed mouth unfolds from below. Long tendrils reach in every direction, grasping whatever moves near its resting place.[^shape]

## Mother of the piercers

The rock surrounding its base is populated by sluglike piercers, creatures familiar from the Delvers' earlier journeys. The great roper has a crevice from which new piercers emerge, casting aside fleshy coverings as they begin to crawl. Its young cling to the cavern and climb its walls in search of height. In this particular hollow their relationship is visible, not merely an old guild theory about different monsters being connected. The creature itself becomes active when the ground above breaks under a disrupted ritual, exposing its home to the night sky and a waterfall of river water.[^birth]

The roper tries to draw the adventurers and their allies into its jaws. Its grasping limbs take hold of Krandal and several guild members, and its bite nearly kills Solburn. Twice Krandal uses the power of his faith to make it drop those it holds. Koh fires an arrow into its eye; Broadside, Boonefellow and even the recovered Solburn fight around its stone-hard base. When Evelyn realizes what the living crevice contains, she stabs the opening and pins one newly emerging piercer inside. The roper twists upon itself in distress, tearing at its body, until its great eye fails and it collapses dead. Some of its young crawl away; others perish in the fall.[^death]

## Current status

The **roper at Noble Hollow is dead**, destroyed by Evelyn's critical attack into the **piercer-bearing opening**. At least some **piercers survive** in the ruined cavern. During the battle, **one unnamed cultist is swallowed**, and the guild members Faelan and Norman Green are saved alive. The roper is **not established as the same entity as the feared summoned beast of the lake**, despite its appearance after the ritual breaks. Do not generalize this individual roper's reproductive behavior to every monster of its species without further source support.[^status]
