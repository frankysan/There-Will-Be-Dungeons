# The roper of Noble Hollow

## Introduction

Beneath the petrified trees of [Noble Hollow](../places/noble-hollow.md) stood a creature resembling an enormous column of natural stone. Its rocklike body rose nearly to the cavern's ceiling. A great eye opened high upon it, a toothed mouth unfolded below, and long tendrils reached outward to seize nearby prey.[^shape]

## Mother of the piercers

Sluglike piercers populated the rock around its base. More emerged from a crevice in the great roper, shedding fleshy coverings before crawling toward the cavern walls. The brood's relationship to this creature was visible when a disrupted ritual broke the ground overhead and exposed the cavern to the night sky and falling river water.[^birth]

The roper's tendrils dragged captives toward its jaws, and its bite nearly killed Solburn. Its stone-hard body resisted arrows, but Koh wounded the great eye. Krandal twice compelled it to release its prey through the power of his faith. Evelyn struck the piercer-bearing opening with a javelin, pinning a newborn inside; the roper tore at itself while trying to dislodge the weapon and collapsed dead. Some piercers escaped, while others perished in the fall.[^death]

## Current status

The Noble Hollow roper is dead, while some of its brood survived the battle. The collapse hole later served as a route underground. The land above eventually became fertile again, but the surviving piercers' fate and the cavern's condition remained unknown.[^status]
