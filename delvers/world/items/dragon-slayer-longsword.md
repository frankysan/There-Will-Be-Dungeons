# Dragon Slayer's Longsword — Radiant Lance forge

## Introduction

The Dragon Slayer's Longsword is a golden blade forged for Evelyn in the hidden machinery of Chath Peak. An ancient Elvish inscription signed Radiant Lance invites a would-be dragon slayer to prove freedom from greed.[^forge]

### Arc Two — A sacrifice of gold and a tested weapon

Evelyn places all the gold she carries into the forge's trough and presses its hand-shaped relief. Light runs across the inscription as the machinery consumes the offering and produces the longsword. Krandal's half-eaten potato burns away in the heat. Koh's ball bearings yield only a fused lump, and Krandal's candles melt into wax; neither attempt produces another enchanted weapon.[^forge][^tries]

The red dragon recognizes the blade and mocks its new bearer. When it passes Evelyn in battle, she strikes successfully, and the sword's particular force against dragons is revealed. The blow wounds the creature without killing it.[^fight]

The battle eventually ends with Marsh's Arrow of Dragon Slaying as the finishing attack. Evelyn retains the golden longsword afterward; the arrow is a separate weapon, given to Marsh by Skoogie.[^aftermath]

## Current status

The longsword is last known retained by Evelyn after the dragon expedition. Its later storage, condition and destination remain unknown.[^status]
