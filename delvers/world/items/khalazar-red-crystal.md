# Khalazar red crystal fragment — dream-recovered shard

## Introduction

The red crystal fragment falls from the chest of [Khalazar's](../characters/khalazar.md) shrouded apparition when Evelyn strikes it in Anthela's captive mindscape. She retrieves one piece as the surrounding vision dissolves into black sand. Its color and glasslike fracture are apparent; its substance and independent powers are not understood.[^origin]

## Development by arc

### Arc Two — A fragment carried out of the dream

Evelyn brings the shard back to Carrie's laboratory and hands it to her. The recovery gives the Delvers a physical remnant of an encounter within another person's mind, even though they cannot explain how it crossed that boundary. Its survival does not tell them what function it served inside the apparition.[^custody]

### Arc Three — A component of a detector

Carrie incorporates the received crystal and some of Anathala's blood into a [sensory amulet](khalazar-sensory-amulet.md) for Koh. The fragment becomes part of a constructed instrument whose light responds near afflicted people. Its significance now lies in that demonstrated use, while the source of the response remains uncertain.[^component]

## Current status

The shard is last known incorporated into Koh's detector. No separate loose-fragment location or independent use is known, and the assembled device's condition after Khalazar's defeat is untested.[^component]
