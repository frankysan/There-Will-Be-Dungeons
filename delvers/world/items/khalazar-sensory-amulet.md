# Koh's red-crystal sensory amulet — Khalazar-compulsion detector

## Introduction

<a id="creation-and-witnessed-form"></a>

Carrie makes this amulet for [Koh Spiros](../characters/koh-spiros.md) to help locate people affected by Khalazar. It combines the [dream-recovered red crystal](khalazar-red-crystal.md) with Anathala's blood, drawn with her agreement. A curved red dome sits on a large gold disc, initially suspended from a long gold chain.[^creation]

## Development by arc

### Arc Three — From hanging amulet to concealed guide

The amulet glows near afflicted elves at Pelor's temple but remains dark beside Anathala alone. Near his home, its light alerts Koh to an entranced elf. The signal stops when Koh knocks the elf unconscious; the victim later wakes lucid. This response gives Koh a practical way to notice a particular danger, without explaining every condition under which the crystal will shine.[^response]

Koh has the device mounted on a glove with a concealing flap. Carrie and Veggie Sticks then add a gold pinpoint that indicates the rough direction of a sensed presence. The instrument can now guide a search rather than merely announce proximity.[^upgrade]

Uncovered underground, it glows and directs Koh toward the urn chambers, at one point pointing almost straight down. He later wears the tracker as a bracer beneath a slitted leather cover. At the triangular gate, Shaolin Nym points to its blood-bearing contents when describing the ancestral blood she says Khalazar's restoration requires.[^later]

## Current status

The detector is last explicitly equipped on Koh's wrist for the final expedition. Its range, precision and exact target remain uncertain; its condition and usefulness after Khalazar's destruction are unknown.[^later]
