# Bag of Hammer Holding — Margaret's gift

## Introduction

The Bag of Hammer Holding is a pink, jewel-studded tote made for Evelyn by her friend and roommate Margaret. Its respectable appearance helps conceal the heavy warhammer that would otherwise betray Evelyn's adventuring life at finishing school.[^gift]

## Development by arc

<a id="enchantment"></a>

### Arc One — A handmade hiding place

Margaret cuts apart other enchanted purses and moving bags, stitching their magic into a new bag roughly two feet long and one foot wide. She describes room for a single object weighing up to sixty pounds.[^gift][^test]

Evelyn lowers her Vicious warhammer into it, hilt upward. The weapon sinks until its last portion is drawn inside, demonstrating that the little tote can hide her large weapon. The gift lets Margaret's craft support the secret she already helps Evelyn keep.[^test]

### Arc Two — Concealed equipment at formal gatherings

Evelyn brings the bag to the Hooper wedding, leaving it and her other hidden arms outside the fence while she enters in formal dress. At the Bronze Ball it again provides a way to conceal her warhammer among the trappings of a social occasion.[^later]

## Current status

Evelyn is the bag's last known bearer. Its later location and condition remain unknown.[^status]
