# Krandal's potato-headed Devotee's Censer

## Introduction

Krandal discovers the Devotee's Censer protruding from the soil at his farm at dawn. A short rod and silver chain lead to a rounded, potato-shaped flail head pierced with tiny holy patterns. When Krandal pulls it free, golden fragrant smoke escapes and an unseen voice pronounces a blessing upon his deeds.[^grant]

## Development by arc

### Arc Three — A blessing carried into the depths

The gift joins Krandal's devotion to Pelor with the crop he cultivates. It serves as both weapon and holy symbol, carrying the promise of radiant force in its blows and healing incense for those gathered nearby. The smoke that greets its discovery reveals its fragrant nature; its healing cloud remains a power to be called upon.[^powers]

Krandal takes the censer on the expedition into the Dawnless Dunes. Its potato head is visible among his equipment as the rescuers prepare to confront Khalazar.[^status]

## Current status

The censer is last directly described with Krandal on the expedition. Its later condition and storage remain unknown.[^status]
