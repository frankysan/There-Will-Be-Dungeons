# Krandal's potato-headed Devotee's Censer

**Actually received at DAWN at END of [Delvers S3E10 — Sad in a boney kind of way](../../episodes/S3E10.md)**, after overnight rest and Guild campaign authorization. In center of Krandal's farm, where potato plant stood, a **short roughly 2-ft rod and silver chain are protruding from soil**, partly concealing the item. **Krandal ACTUALLY pulls it from ground**, releasing golden fragrant smoke; an unseen divine-sounding voice says he has made good on his deeds and is blessed. It is **explicitly called “Devotee's Censer”** by GM, but has **potato-shaped rounded/perforated swinging FLAIL HEAD**, tiny holy holes and patterns, with silver chain and handle. A mace/flail **that doubles as Pelor holy symbol**, visually not an ordinary straight potato plant, decorative candelabra or permanently attached stationary sculpture. Do NOT confuse this item with **Nopa Ropa's separate newly-forged armour**, which he says is ready at same dawn but isn't the source of censer.

**Verbal GM effect:** attack hit with censer causes **+1d8 radiant**, and command word as bonus action creates **~10-ft incense healing aura** lasting **1 minute / ten rounds**, restoring **1d4 HP at start of Krandal's turn** for him and creatures inside cloud as played; no other attributes claimed, exact printed D&D source/attunement not inspected. GM uses official item name but potato aesthetic specifically belongs to this campaign. **ACTUALLY IN KRANDAL'S INVENTORY BEFORE UPCOMING EXPEDITION**, not a proposed crafting recipe nor a purchase from Guild.
