# Ring of Protection — a relic from the flooded ruins

## Introduction

The Ring of Protection is a small stone-metal band recovered from a concealed chamber beneath the eastern lake. Archaic Elven inscriptions mark its surface, linking its appearance to other buried relics without revealing who made it.[^finding]

## Development by arc

<a id="discovery-and-awakening"></a>
<a id="offered-rather-than-claimed"></a>

### Arc One — A responsive relic freely given

Behind an algae-covered secret wall, the Delvers find a heavy stone chest containing the ring. Krandal and Evelyn help raise the covering. Evelyn recognizes the old letters but cannot read their meaning; Koh takes the band and channels psychic energy into it. Light follows the inscriptions as he learns its protective nature.[^awakening]

The ring is understood to shield its wearer from harm and strengthen resistance to danger. Its use is not reserved for Koh: it adapts to different fingers and can pass to another bearer.[^awakening]

With Krandal's heavy scale mail left ashore for the swim, the cleric feels exposed. Koh offers him the ring with the others' consent. Krandal puts it on his left middle finger, and the band expands to fit his broad hand.[^gift]

## Current status

Krandal is the ring's last known wearer. Its later custody and condition remain uncertain.[^status]
