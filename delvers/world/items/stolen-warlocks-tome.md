# Warlock's tome from Cassius's former office — stolen by Dumbledwark

## Introduction

The tome is a magical book found among boxed possessions in Cassius's former office while Zoltar occupies the room. Dumbledwark recognizes it as a warlock's tome, but its title, contents and owner remain unknown.[^recognition]

## Development by arc

### Arc Three — From boxed object to concealed possession

Dumbledwark detects magic beneath a stacked container while Zoltar fetches refreshments. He examines the hiding place, then closes it without taking the book. The book remains in the crate during their interview.[^recognition]

When Zoltar leaves again, Dumbledwark puts on his Gloves of Thievery, removes the tome and hides it beneath the camera in his duffel. The transfer is a theft rather than a gift from the Guild. The book changes custody without its contents being read or its powers tested.[^theft]

## Current status

The tome was last placed in Dumbledwark's duffel. Its contents, purpose and later destination remain unknown.[^status]
