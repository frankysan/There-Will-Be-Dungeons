# The three urns of the triangle gate — recovered-key inventory and custody

## Historical 3-slot altar (S3E06)

The [triangle-gate catacombs](../places/triangle-gate-catacombs.md) have an altar with **THREE circular hollows** before a **still-sealed glowing-triangle dark metal gate**. Carrie hypothesizes that three missing objects open it. **Do NOT conflate** S3E07's three grick/hook-horror/bat-shaped metal keys, which were **consumed entirely inside a separate black-slime puzzle chamber**.

## S3E07: Bat-capped dried-heart urn, placed once

The [first vessel](dried-heart-jar.md) is a **ceramic-and-gold capped jar topped by BAT SKULL**, about a foot tall with ~3-inch base and **dry, shrivelled heart** inside. Audible beating **stops upon its lid being opened**; Krandal Medicine 18 recognizes anatomical heart; total jar weight **25 lb**. Recovered in separate [three-key puzzle room](../places/black-slime-key-chamber.md) using **23-lb stone + 2-lb merchant weights**, NOT the suggested mayonnaise-shirt. **Evelyn ACTUALLY CARRIES and PLACES it perfectly in ONE of older three altar recesses** at end of [S3E07](../../episodes/S3E07.md). **This installed state is historical, not current after S3E08.**

## S3E08: Hook-horror-capped organ urn taken from All Rat

After illusions and battle within [Black Basilica](../places/black-basilica.md), the **All Rat** collective presents a **SECOND gold-faced jar with HOOK HORROR head**, asserts the **ORGAN inside is “part of Khalazar” and empowers the rat congregation**, offers only empty container. Evelyn refuses; **Koh ACTUALLY LEAPS IN, GRABS WHOLE VESSEL and PSYCHIC TELEPORTS out**, immediately breaking organized rat mass. **Contents have NOT been directly examined or identified**; don't assume second contains the same dry heart, a different specifically named organ, or proved biologically Khalazar's. Initial custody with Koh post-teleport, later packed together with first urn.

**Critical end-of-S3E08 reversal:** On exiting, the party travels back through [Umbrel/Umbral Flight](../places/triangle-gate-catacombs.md) and **ACTUALLY RETRIEVES S3E07 BAT URN FROM ALTAR**. The GM explicitly confirms **TWO recovered urns now kept together in EVELYN'S BAG OF HOLDING** on the way to Deep Winds Spa. **THEREFORE ALL THREE OLD ALTAR SLOTS ARE EMPTY again**, even though the group possesses two of three proposed objects; **ONE THIRD URN UNRECOVERED at historical S3E08 cutoff**. Door never opened as of S3E08. No need for out-of-world teleporting or transmutation to account for first vessel; intentional re-collection played. [S3E08 research dossier](../../episodes/S3E08.md).

## S3E09: THIRD grick-headed urn recovered — all THREE carried but altar still EMPTY

Following an ACTUALLY COMPLETED night at [Deep Winds Spa](../places/deep-winds-spa.md), the party takes the **RIGHT** staircase from older catacombs, guided by Koh's glowing amulet bracer. In the [paired pressure-door/airflow-grick complex](../places/right-stair-grick-urn-complex.md), guest [Barclay](../characters/barclay-vandergeist.md) helps solve interdependent golem/switch puzzles. **Evelyn ACTUALLY ascends the ~50-ft periodic wind-suction spiral and removes the THIRD CLAY/CERAMIC urn crowned with the GOLD FACE OF A GRICK from a basin**. **Wind STOPS**; Evelyn **actually places it in her Bag of Holding** alongside both earlier vessels. **Contents of this third urn are NOT opened or examined**, even though all three heads and positioning suggest a related three-key set. Initial second hook-horror organ was only claimed as Khalazar's by All Rat in S3E08; **S3E09 GM recap NOW calls it “brain of Khalazar,” an attributed GM recap, though players never open specimen**. **Party DOES NOT return to original three-recess altar after third recovery**; instead exits **FOREST dungeon to Veggie Sticks**, tree-transports to Atomer near well. **ALL THREE urns physically recovered by S3E09 cutoff and in Evelyn's Bag; ALL THREE earlier altar slots REMAIN EMPTY; dark triangle gate REMAINS SEALED**. The key objects are *ready to attempt installation*, but it is not performed here; **Khalazar NOT confronted/killed**.

## S3E10 custody: Evelyn ACTUALLY hands all three urns to Koh

At ~6pm Atomer well after Veggie tree transport from S3E09, [Evelyn](../characters/evelyn-grinkeeper.md) **ACTUALLY HANDS ALL THREE ORNATE URNS to [Koh](../characters/koh-spiros.md)**, reasoning that guild divination tracking of her badges should not be co-located with three crucial relics. **This explicitly SUPERSEDES end-S3E09 custody in Evelyn's Bag of Holding**. Which personal bag/container Koh places them in isn't said; do **NOT infer the original Evelyn bag was also handed over**. Krandal/Koh hand Silver badges to Evelyn, but S3E09 showed Krandal throwing a badge away—recovery/retcon not narrated. Zoltar later openly ADMITS Guild's divination monitoring of adventurers, but badge-specific spell untested. **ALL THREE URNS now KOH custody at S3E10 episode end**; **NONE INSTALLED in altar**, gate sealed and Khalazar not confronted. Cassius's next-morning campaign funding/recruitment is PLAYED, while **the promised march to altar has NOT yet occurred**. [S3E10 dossier](../../episodes/S3E10.md).
