# Drift globe — Koh's recovered light source

## Overview and custody

The globe is a smooth glass orb made from many small joined panels. [Koh](../characters/koh-spiros.md) finds it beside the boots of a mostly skeletal dragonborn beneath the [farm cave](../places/farm-cave.md), retrieving it along with the dead delver's wooden badge.[^discovery]

At the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md), Cassius checks the recovered object, declares it nonsentient and clears its use. Krandal argues that Koh should keep it because he took the risk of descending for it, and the others agree. The badge is returned separately.[^custody]

## Explained powers and unresolved name

Cassius describes an orb that answers spoken commands, gives torch-like light, floats close to the ground and follows a user who moves too far away. It can also call forth daylight once each day. He suggests naming it for its former owner, but no settled new name emerges.[^powers]

## Light in the sanctuary

Koh first uses its ordinary light in the sanctuary's running chamber. Later, in the weight room, he calls forth daylight against the Shadows. They weaken in its glow, although the light does not itself burn them. The same daylight remains during the struggle with Studwell, giving the recovered object a demonstrated role in protecting its new companions.[^use]

## Current status

Koh retains the globe after returning to the guild. Its daylight has been used during the sanctuary assignment; another use before the next dawn has yet to be established.[^use]
