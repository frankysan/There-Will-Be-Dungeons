# Drift globe — Koh's recovered light source

## Introduction

The drift globe is a smooth glass orb formed from many small joined panels. [Koh](../characters/koh-spiros.md) recovers it beside a mostly skeletal dragonborn beneath the [farm cave](../places/farm-cave.md), together with the dead delver's wooden badge.[^discovery]

### Arc One — Recovered light and a former bearer

At the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md), Cassius examines the globe and clears it as nonsentient. Krandal argues that Koh should keep it because he took the risk of recovering it; the companions agree, while the badge is returned separately. Cassius describes an orb that answers spoken commands, floats near the ground, follows its user and provides ordinary light or daylight once each day.[^custody][^powers]

Koh uses its ordinary illumination in the sanctuary and its daylight against the Shadows. The creatures weaken without being burned by the light itself, and Studwell's attacks falter in its glow. On a later day's expedition, renewed daylight exposes a grick hidden among the stone. The globe becomes a practical means of revealing danger and protecting the group.[^use][^grick]

Wildfire later identifies the dead bearer as his brother Brom Broscale. The recovered object thus carries a family connection as well as a useful enchantment, though no agreed new name replaces its familiar description.[^brother]

### Arc Two — Entrusted to Anthela

Koh continues to carry the globe underground, but gives it to Anthela for protection at the Bronze Ball. She brings the orb with her among the guests.[^ball]

### Arc Three — Rescue light in another companion's hands

By the struggle at Deep Winds Spa, the globe is again in Koh's hands. He pushes it into the black mass engulfing Krandal and calls forth daylight. The encompassing ooze bursts apart, freeing the cleric and exposing the clawed creature within; Koh's subsequent psychic strike kills it. He then extinguishes the globe to spare the bathers its glare.[^spa]

After leaving the Black Basilica, Koh gives the globe to Evelyn. She later activates its daylight in the great windy chamber while the party seeks the third urn.[^transfer]

## Current status

The globe is last directly used by Evelyn after Koh's transfer. Its later custody and condition remain unknown.[^status]
