# Drift globe — Koh's recovered light source

**Type:** Recovered magical glass orb. **Spoilers:** Delvers S1E01. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional; identification and capabilities explained by Cassius/GM, no activation demonstrated. **542 identifies the recovered badge, not the globe.**

## Overview and custody

[Koh](../characters/koh-spiros.md) finds the orb beside the boots of a mostly skeletal dragonborn below the [farm cave](../places/farm-cave.md). Around **02:15** he retrieves it and the wooden badge while keeping hold of the rope; back above, the orb is described as smooth and composed of many small joined glass panels. The ASR measurement is approximately six inches, with the exact dimension unresolved.

At the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md) ~02:24–02:27, Koh presents it for identification. Cassius invokes the recovered-item checking requirement, declares it nonsentient and clears its use. Krandal says Koh should keep it because he went down for it; **Koh is the supported holder at cutoff**. The badge is returned separately.

## Explained powers and unresolved name

Cassius/GM describes spoken commands, torch-like light, a **once-daily Daylight** effect, floating no more than **five feet above the ground**, and following if its user moves beyond **sixty feet**. These are this session's explanation, not a claim that all capabilities were tested or that a charge was spent.

Cassius suggests naming it for its former owner, rendered `Bran Broscale/Brosgail`, later `ram's/Rams` and `rosescale/rose scales`. The exact owner/name remains unresolved; no formal new item name is fixed here. His speculation about the dead delver's soul is not a confirmed afterlife outcome.
