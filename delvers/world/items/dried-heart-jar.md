# The dried-heart jar — one of three triangle-gate altar objects

**Played discovery and custody:** In [Delvers S3E07 — A Shirt Full of Mayo](../../episodes/S3E07.md), **Koh's uncovered directional wife-blood/Khalazar-linked amulet** guides three protagonists beyond the previously explored mummy stairwell to a **separate, black-slime-covered, three-key puzzle chamber** (with guest [Goggles Granny](../characters/goggles-granny.md)). After all three keys turn successfully and slime drains, a small **gold-and-ceramic bat-skull-capped jar** is found on a pedestal near bottom of room. It **seems to pulse by sound** as approached, but when Evelyn **opens its lid** that heartbeat **stops**, and **Krandal's Medicine 18** establishes contents resemble **a shrivelled DRY HUMANLIKE HEART**. Species, individual owner, age and whether it ever animated remain **unknown**. Whole jar **with contents** weighs **25 pounds**, not just its heart. Vessel approximately a foot tall with roughly three-inch base, matching shape motif of slots; original prop unseen.

**Trap evasion:** Koh detects probable **pressure plate** under jar (Investigation 18); its actual consequence is **not known**. He lifts vessel with **Sleight of Hand 30 while maintaining pressure**; Granny measures 25 pounds. Proposed **shirt full of mayonnaise** (from **Alchemy Jug**) is a joke/possible alternative, **NOT enacted**. Evelyn selects **23-pound STONE**, Granny contributes **2 pounds of merchant counterweights**, which **Koh and Granny position successfully** on pressure plate. No trap fires. Evelyn closes/reseals jar and **carries it in her bag of holding**.

**Final custody/location, S3E07:** Evelyn and the other **THREE** Delvers return to [triangle-gate catacombs](../places/triangle-gate-catacombs.md) without Granny; the party **PLACES the jar onto the older altar, where it FITS ONE of THREE round slots perfectly**. **TWO slots still empty** and great metal triangle door **NOT opened**. Jar **is now on altar**, not merely inside Evelyn's bag. No connection to a named being's actual heart, Khalazar's blood or required mechanism has been proved; resemblance/correct fit is stronger physical evidence than NPC cosmological claims.

**Evidence:** [S3E07 source dossier](../../episodes/S3E07.md), clock ~01:53–02:37; [full P/Q ASR comparison](../../../reports/DELVERS_S3E07_ASR_COMPARISON_2026-10-25.md). Audio, item card and encounter maps unverified.
