# The dried-heart jar — one of three triangle-gate altar objects

## Introduction

The dried-heart jar is a narrow ceramic vessel bound with gold and capped by a bat's skull. It stands on a pedestal in a chamber newly drained of black slime. A heartbeat grows louder as Evelyn approaches, but stops when she opens the lid, revealing a small, dry, shrivelled heart.[^discovery]

## Development by arc

### Arc Three — From weighted pedestal to altar

The whole vessel and its contents weigh twenty-five pounds. Koh keeps pressure on the pedestal while Granny measures the load; a stone and merchant counterweights replace it without triggering the mechanism. Evelyn takes the resealed jar and carries it to the older catacomb altar. It fits one of three hollows perfectly, but the gate remains closed while the other two are empty.[^removal]

### Arc Three — Retrieved, reunited and emptied

The Delvers later retrieve the jar and place it with the hook-horror-capped vessel in Evelyn's bag. A third, grick-headed jar completes the [set](three-urns-triangle-gate.md). Evelyn transfers all three to Koh, who carries them on the final expedition.[^custody]

At the gate, each hero installs a vessel. Shaolin Nym opens the way and releases the jars for reuse. In Khalazar's chamber the Delvers open them and restore their organs to his body. The heart beats and grows near him, giving the dried remnant a role beyond its first place in the altar.[^use]

## Current status

The heart has been used in Khalazar's restoration, which ends with his destruction. The emptied jar's survival, holder and location after the pyramid's collapse are unknown.[^use]
