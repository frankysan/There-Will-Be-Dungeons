# Runic sword hilt — the object from the stone chest

## Overview

An old chest deep in a trapped ruin yielded an object shaped like the grip of a sword, though no blade extended from it. Runes marked its surface, which seemed to be stone or metal, or a mingling of the two. Its purpose was not apparent when Evelyn Grinkeeper first carried it out of the ruin.[^discovery]

## Recovery and questions

The hilt lay inside a heavy stone chest in a chamber fitted with long-rusted weapon racks, benches and hooks. With little time left before the ride back to the guild, Evelyn braced the room's doorway with her hammer, pushed aside the chest's lid and took the object without examining it. No other contents of the chest were described as recovered.

The company escaped before the dungeon could close around them again. On returning, they showed the hilt to Cassius, who accepted it behind the guild counter for a further examination. He did not identify its magic, translate its runes or return it to the party before their departure from the scene. The hilt's nature therefore remains an open question rather than a confirmed weapon or usable magical treasure.[^custody]

## Current status

The hilt is in **guild custody pending inspection**. Evelyn discovered and transported it, but it is not established as part of her available equipment. No blade, awakening command, sentience result, wielder or other ability has been revealed.[^status]
