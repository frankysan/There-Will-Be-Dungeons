# Soul Knife — the runic hilt from the stone chest

## Introduction

The Soul Knife first appears as a bladeless, rune-marked sword grip in a heavy stone chest. Its surface resembles stone or metal, and its purpose is unknown when Evelyn carries it from the trapped ruin.[^discovery]

### Arc One — From sealed relic to an awakened blade

Evelyn retrieves the hilt from an armoury-like chamber of rusted racks, benches and hooks, bracing the doorway with her hammer while she opens the chest. Back at the guild, Cassius takes the unidentified object for examination.[^custody]

Carrie Underworld later returns it to Koh as an old Elvish Soul Knife, also understood as a mind sword or psi blade. She judges it nonsentient but cannot work its substance or draw more than a tiny red spark from it. In Koh's grasp, concentration brings forth a long cyan-blue blade that can be dismissed and recalled.[^awakened]

The projection passes through a wooden table without marking it, yet hurts Koh's finger without cutting him. It gives his thoughts a harmful edge rather than an ordinary steel surface. A masked adventurer recognizes Koh as a scion and warns him of others who seek relics to strengthen similar gifts.[^scion]

The weapon proves effective against the flameskull at Enzo Keep, where Koh delivers the final psychic strike. Later, during his attack on a rust monster, the stone-metal grip crumbles. The blade remains: Koh can now shape it without the object that awakened it.[^tower][^freed]

### Arc Two — A legacy beyond the broken grip

Koh's recovered Snake Island memories reveal how to form two psychic daggers and later throw them. These growing abilities belong to him after the hilt's destruction; they do not restore the old artifact.[^s2e02][^s2e04]

## Current status

The physical hilt is destroyed. Koh retains his psychic blades independently; the relic's maker, original purpose and full workings remain unknown.[^status]
