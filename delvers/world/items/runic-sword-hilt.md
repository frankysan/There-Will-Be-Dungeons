# Soul Knife — the runic hilt from the stone chest

## Overview

An old chest deep in a trapped ruin yielded an object shaped like the grip of a sword, though no blade extended from it. Runes marked its surface, which seemed to be stone or metal, or a mingling of the two. Its purpose was not apparent when Evelyn Grinkeeper first carried it out of the ruin.[^discovery]

## Recovery and questions

The hilt lay inside a heavy stone chest in a chamber fitted with long-rusted weapon racks, benches and hooks. With little time left before the ride back to the guild, Evelyn braced the room's doorway with her hammer, pushed aside the chest's lid and took the object without examining it. No other contents of the chest were described as recovered.

The company escaped before the dungeon could close around them again. On returning, they showed the hilt to Cassius, who accepted it behind the guild counter for a further examination. He did not identify its magic, translate its runes or return it to the party before their departure from the scene. The hilt's nature therefore remains an open question rather than a confirmed weapon or usable magical treasure.[^custody]

## The blade awakened

The hilt passed through the guild's examinations and into the hands of an excitable artificer, Carrie Underworld. She called the old Elvish object a *Soul Knife*, acknowledging that a more literal name might be *mind sword* or *psi blade*. Its unusual mingling of stone and metal resisted her attempts to work it, and she could summon only a tiny red spark from the grip. She judged it nonsentient but could not know its true value.[^awakened]

When Koh took the hilt, a long cyan-blue blade appeared at his thought, bright and nearly solid-looking as crystalline light. He could dismiss it and summon it again with little effort. Tested discreetly, it passed through a wooden table without marking it, yet a gentle touch against Koh's finger caused real pain without a cut. The force struck his mind rather than his flesh. A white-robed masked adventurer recognized Koh as a scion and warned him that other wielders sought relics capable of strengthening their gifts, leaving Koh with questions he could not yet answer.[^scion]

## Victory at the keep

The blade proves itself not merely a curiosity when Koh faces the flameskull above Enzo Keep. Arrows glance against the flying bone, but the psychic edge pierces without leaving ordinary cuts. Koh strikes through the skull's defenses, destroying it with the last blow. The strange weapon does not leave the head marked as a steel blade would; after the creature loses its force, Evelyn's holy water reduces it to ash. Koh keeps both the weapon and the two fiery-colored garnets recovered from the remains.[^tower]

## The blade without a hilt

Koh's blade changes again in the blacksmith's shop. His decisive strike against the rust monster causes the ancient stone-metal grip to crumble, while the blue psychic projection remains. After his lessons with Wildfire, Koh can now shape the blade without the artifact that first awakened it.[^freed]

## A second blade remembered

Another part of Koh's growing psychic gift surfaces when a Snake Island memory returns at the Hooper wedding. He recalls clinging to the rock beside a volcano, keeping hold with a psychic knife while a little bearded man falls away beneath him. The scene is incomplete, but the recovered knowledge is not: Koh can now manifest **two psychic daggers at once**. Later, facing Grandfather Hooper's wight, he calls forth both blades while standing protectively before an endangered guest, though he chooses defense rather than landing a dual strike. His power no longer needs the old hilt to give it shape.[^s2e02]

## Two blades that leave the hand

Koh has already remembered making two psychic daggers on Snake Island and used both against the burrowing bulette in Atomer. In the water chamber of Withers Estate another forgotten image returns: from behind stone pews he had flung a psychic dagger into the emerald eyes of a giant moving snake statue. He now recognizes that he can throw either blade at a distant foe rather than keeping it at his hand. He immediately uses the discovery against the watery guardian, and again against the air guardian later in the mansion. These blades do psychic harm but dissipate instead of remaining lodged like steel.[^s2e04]

## Current status

The **physical Soul Knife hilt was destroyed in S1E07**, but **Koh's psychic blades exist without it**. **S2E02 confirms two simultaneously manifest daggers**, and **S2E03 demonstrates successful two-blade melee hits** against the bulette. **S2E04 further confirms Koh can throw psychic daggers at range** after a **Snake Island flashback** to a giant emerald-eyed serpent statue and **ACTUALLY uses thrown daggers against water and air elementals in combat**. This is a distinct ability revelation, **not retroactively used at earlier cutoffs**. His garnets and origin of the old runic hilt remain uncertain, and no material replacement hilt is discovered in the heist.[^s2e04][^s2e02][^freed]
