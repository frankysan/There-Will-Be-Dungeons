# Wand of Secrets and the broken geared disc

## Introduction

Two ancient objects emerge from the same strange encounter beneath the eastern lake. A bent wand fashioned from a curious mingling of stone and metal responds to Koh's silent power. A thick curved piece of the same forgotten craft, hidden in a black-slime-filled carving, suggests a once-larger mechanism. The first can find deliberate secrets; the second has not yet revealed its purpose.[^finding]

## The wand's pointing

The kuo-toa ritualist keeps a conch-tipped staff of his own making and a smaller wand tucked near his clothing. The Delvers take both when they restrain him after his violent defense of a subterranean shrine. Koh notices the little wand's familiar stone-metal substance and concentrates upon it; he feels it become an extension of his arm. He understands that its power detects places or objects made intentionally secret, rather than anything someone merely lost. He waves it before the carving of the Dawnless Dunes, and it points sharply toward the dark spiral opening.[^wand]

## A quarter of something larger

Evelyn puts a javelin through the opening and feels a hard obstacle; then she slides her arms into the black ooze and hauls out a curved, thick sector of the stone-metal. Along the outer arc run tiny gears. One inner seat lies empty where another gear seems to have belonged. Were there three other pieces of the same shape, they might form a broad circular device. The bound ritualist says he recovered this portion from a deeper place and concealed it behind the carving. He cannot show what the whole thing would accomplish, and no one assembles it.[^disc]

The Delvers later encounter a crank with a matching-looking stone gear on the shelf of Glepo Ma's store. After learning the trader passed the ritualist's staff to a dangerous mounted stranger, Koh accepts both the crank and a bag of coins as compensation. Whether the crank truly fits the disc, or whether either belongs to the massive unopened toothed gate seen farther below, is a question for a future journey.[^crank]

## Current status

**Koh holds the Wand of Secrets**, which was described as allowing **up to three uses per day**; one deliberate pointing activation is played. **Evelyn physically extracts the quarter-disc**, but its ultimate individual bearer at the end of the lakeshore battle is not separately confirmed. **Koh takes the gear-crank** from Glepo Ma, along with a bag roughly estimated at **1,000 gp**. **No one has fitted the gear, gathered the other three quarters, opened the ancient door or established the mechanism's purpose**. The ritualist's separate **homemade conch-shell staff is not part of this artifact group and is last seen with the mounted darkmantle-hooded collector**.[^status]
