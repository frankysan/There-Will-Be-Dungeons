# Wand of Secrets and the broken geared disc

## Introduction

The Wand of Secrets is a kinked stone-metal wand taken from a kuo-toa ritualist beneath the eastern lake. Its pointing leads the Delvers to a hidden quarter-circle of geared stone-metal. Similar construction invites comparison, but the objects remain distinct and the fragment's function is unknown.[^finding]

### Arc One — A discovered secret and an incomplete mechanism

Koh recognizes the wand through psychic concentration. He understands it to seek deliberately concealed things rather than objects merely lost. When he uses it before the Dawnless Dunes carving, it points toward the dark spiral opening. Evelyn probes the opening with a javelin, then reaches through the black ooze and pulls out the curved fragment.[^wand][^disc]

The fragment is thick and gear-edged, with an empty seat where another gear appears to belong. The ritualist says he found it deeper underground and hid it behind the carving. A complete circular mechanism remains only a possibility: the other sections are absent and the device is not assembled.[^disc]

At Glepo Ma's store, the companions notice a hand crank ending in a stone gear that resembles the fragment's workings. Koh later accepts it as part of the trader's compensation for handing the ritualist's separate staff to violent collectors. Its apparent suitability has yet to be tested against the quarter-disc.[^crank]

A larger geared stone appears beneath the captive elf at Noble Hollow. Koh recognizes its construction as the same type as the lake fragment. When the ritual is interrupted and the elf falls, that stone breaks and the green flames cease. The resemblance survives as a clue, without a demonstrated fit between the pieces.[^hollow]

### Arc Three — A working crank, without a completed disc

The carried crank eventually fits a gear socket beside a dungeon water wheel. It allows manual cranking, while water poured into a funnel drives the mechanism that raises the heavy gate. Evelyn retrieves the crank into her bag afterward. She later retrieves a gear crank from a doorway again; no assembly with the quarter-disc is known.[^wheel]

## Current status

Koh is the wand's last known bearer. The quarter-disc remains associated with the Delvers without a confirmed individual keeper, while the crank is last described being retrieved by Evelyn. Their later storage and condition remain unknown; no completed disc or proven fit among these parts is known.[^status]
