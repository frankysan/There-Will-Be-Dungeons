# Chance — Barclay's lantern spirit

## Introduction

Chance is the name [Barclay Vandergeist](../characters/barclay-vandergeist.md) gives to a spirit dwelling within his lantern. Its ghostly blue light accompanies the tortle at his mushroom camp below Atomer. Barclay says he met the spirit on an earlier expedition; its deeper identity remains unknown.[^identity]

## Development by arc

### Arc Three — A companion with a spectral hand

Chance reaches beyond the lantern with a spectral hand, shaking hands and scratching a rough greeting in the dirt. These small exchanges give Barclay a companion capable of answering others without spoken conversation. Krandal suspects an undead presence, but learns no certain account of who Chance was or how it came to inhabit the lamp.[^hand]

## Current status

The lantern remains with Barclay when he travels out with the Delvers to Atomer. Chance's later whereabouts and fate remain unknown.[^status]
