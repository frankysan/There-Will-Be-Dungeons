# Loran Ilfelkyr Radiant Armour — Evelyn's transformation and worn equipment

## Introduction

Loran Ilfelkyr's radiant armour waits in the hidden refuge beneath his school: blue-and-white formal plate with skirtlike plates, gloves, boots and a blue tie. An unseen barrier keeps it beyond Evelyn's reach even after she gains entry with the [transforming blade](vestal-diamond-heart-handle.md).[^refuge]

## Development by arc

### Arc Three — From guarded relic to protective armour

Evelyn's first attempts to claim the armour fail. The change comes when a school alarm announces danger to the pupils above. Turning from her own wish to possess the relic toward their protection, she reaches through the barrier and takes the breastplate. Held against her body as she hurries upstairs, it unfolds around her back, shoulders and skirt into full plate over her blue flowered dress. She kills the intruding hook horror wearing it.[^claim]

The armour's enchanted alloy turns critical blows into ordinary hits. Against magical effects that allow strength or endurance to halve the harm, a successful resistance can spare Evelyn the damage entirely. That protection helps her withstand the skeletal bat's scream during the final expedition. It does not make her invulnerable: Khalazar later wounds her and leaves her unconscious before her companions restore her.[^protection]

## Current status

The armour is last known worn by Evelyn during the expedition against Khalazar. No later transfer or loss is known; its condition after the Delvers' escape is not separately described.[^status]
