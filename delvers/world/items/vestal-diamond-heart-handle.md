# Loran Ilfelkyr's transforming sword — Lady Vestal's diamond-heart handle

## Introduction

<a id="described-form"></a>

Lady Vestal places a small tubular handle in Evelyn's hand while helping the schoolgirls escape the invaded Bronze Ball. About six inches long, it ends in two curved, diamond-covered arms forming a heart. Evelyn cannot identify the gift and pockets it before returning to protect the civilians.[^source]

## Development by arc

### Arc Two — An unexplained gift

The handle passes from Vestal to Evelyn without revealing its purpose. It produces no blade or other visible magical effect during the evacuation.[^source]

### Arc Three — A blade shaped by self-belief

Back at the school, Vestal identifies the gift as Loran Ilfelkyr's old sword. Evelyn's growing belief in herself draws a luminous blade from the hilt, lengthening from dagger to sword. Her first efforts still fall short of the reach needed to unlock the heart-marked door beneath the fountain; a later effort retracts the blade.[^recognition]

The weapon eventually extends into a lance roughly six feet long, allowing Evelyn to open the sanctuary door. Entry does not give her immediate possession of the [radiant armour](loran-radiant-armour.md) within: its protective barrier remains. The transforming blade already serves her in battle, and she later carries it as a lance while drawing Khalazar's skeletal bat away from the rescuers. Its radiant strikes disrupt the creature's shadowy wings before Evelyn destroys it.[^use]

## Current status

The sword is last directly described in Evelyn's hands during the final expedition. Its later storage, form and destination remain unknown.[^status]
