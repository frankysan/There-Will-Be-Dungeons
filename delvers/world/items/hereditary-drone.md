# Hereditary Drone — music box and droning effects

<a id="hereditary-drone--stone-music-box"></a>

## Introduction

The object later called the Hereditary Drone is a wind-up music box recovered from a hiding place beneath the Withers Estate's cellar floor. Its mechanism lacks a record. A carved stone disc found among the stolen valuables offers a way to make it play.[^origin]

## Development by arc

<a id="observed-and-explained-effects"></a>

### Arc Two — An assembled device and a widening threat

Koh fits the disc into the box. The quiet melody resembles an unsettling sound heard underground, and Swiftclef holds his head and nose while it plays. Repeated opening and closing reproduce his discomfort, while the nearby satyrs show no visible reaction. The assembly has a selective effect, though the reason for that difference remains unclear.[^test]

Koh brings it to the Guild rather than keeping it near his family. Carrie names it the Hereditary Drone and proposes that its sound can interfere with motor control, especially in susceptible elves. She also believes repeated exposure can affect other people. Her anatomical demonstration offers an explanation for the danger without establishing the full reach of the device.[^research]

<a id="custody-and-status-through-s2e10"></a>

A map's mysterious mechanism may be related, but remains unidentified. Cassius proposes hearing protection combined with Koh's silent mental communication for a future expedition. The small assembly stays among the Guild's research objects.[^custody]

<a id="s2e12-second-arc-finale-active-exposed-underground-installation"></a>

At the Bronze Ball, a much larger droning source erupts beneath the ballroom as the floor and glass roof break. Afflicted elves and half-elves converge upon the opening, where roper tendrils take them below. Its relationship to the portable box is unclear. Flooding drains away, a fireball fails to close the rock passage, and piled tables obstruct the opening without stopping the sound or the approaching victims.[^ball]

### Arc Three — An unresolved mechanism after the crisis

The barricaded opening and its aftermath remain hazards during the cleanup. Carrie later warns against renewed exposure while investigating the afflicted. Khalazar's eventual destruction breaks his hold over the captives, but does not reveal what has become of the small assembly or establish the larger source's physical condition.[^later]

## Current status

The portable box and disc were last left with the Guild for study. Their later holder and condition are unknown. No separate inspection, destruction or final functional test resolves the larger droning source.[^later]
