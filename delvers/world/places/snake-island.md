# Snake Island — the forgotten fortnight

## Introduction

Snake Island is a destination on Elmir Lake, known to the Delvers through a journey they can scarcely remember. A bearded gnome's commission draws them across waters haunted in their recollection by immense serpents. They return to Atomer after a missing fortnight with unfamiliar rewards and fragments of a world of snakefolk, priests and royalty.[^journey]

### Arc Two — A place remembered in fragments

The crossing returns first as an image of mist, a small boat and a great serpent overturning it. Other memories place the travelers ashore among villages with withered crops, giant riding snakes and a temple holding a golden serpent idol. The island appears both inhabited and dangerous, but these glimpses leave its routes and communities only partly known.[^landscape]

Krandal recalls a luxurious room, a viper and Princess Hissia calling him her love. Evelyn remembers a crowded step-pyramid ceremony in which she is adorned with gold and hailed as queen. Koh remembers substituting clay for the idol before a snake-driven corpse attacks. A volcano, a falling patron and captives beside a bonfire add further hazards without resolving their consequences.[^fragments]

The returned party offers firmer evidence of the journey's effects: serpent-styled weapons, armor and jewelry accompany them home, and the guild pays the commission. Yet neither the rewards nor their survival restores the whole fortnight. The patron's fate, the island's curse and the authority implied by Evelyn's crowning remain uncertain.[^return]

Hissia later comes to the Bronze Ball as a donor and again calls Krandal her beloved. Her account connects his staff and armor to her royal household and a dead brother. The island's relationships reach into Atomer, even though her words and Krandal's memories do not establish a completed marriage or a full history of the expedition.[^ties]

### Arc Three — Royal survivors beyond the island

Nopa Ropa arrives seeking Hissia and speaks of Krandal as an island hero. An aboleth's attempt to probe Krandal's missing memories is interrupted. Hissia is eventually found captive beneath Atomer, rescued and awakened at the Guild; her survival becomes known without another journey to her homeland.[^later]

## Current status

The Delvers and Hissia are alive, but Snake Island's later government, inhabitants and condition remain unknown. The missing fortnight is still incomplete, and no return establishes the outcome of every remembered danger or the island's response to its absent royalty.[^status]
