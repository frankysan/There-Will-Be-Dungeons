# Gemholm — Lake Absul gem-dredging sandbar settlement

**Working spelling:** **Gemholm** (also heard by ASR as `Gemhold/Jenholm/In Gem Home`); precise name requires audio/GM-map review. **First directly played in [Delvers S2E06](../../episodes/S2E06.md)**, not a Wastes location. Lore below is **ASR-provisional**; the Discord map mentioned on air has not been inspected.

## Site and access

An isolated village/mining colony built across shifting sandbars in **Lake Absul**. The Delvers travel **about six hours by small boat** from Atomer with a merfolk ferryman **Gil/Gilkeel**. Gil's large trained crab **Herman Snips** stays at Gemholm as a contact/signal for return transport; **no return voyage takes place in S2E06**. The lake route passes Krandal's riverside farm, **Noble Hollow** and a distant view of **Skull Keep**, but direct land travel or a permanent road is not documented.[^arrival]

The settlement has roughly **thirteen structures** on wooden stilts with exposed barnacle-coated bases; a central stone-supported warehouse is elevated above the tidal sand. The scattered signs name a **crab maintenance workshop, gem depository, Ambermoor house, infirmary, McNorris house, recreation building, mess hall and common office**, among other houses. **One boat has been deliberately burned and buildings are damaged, including a harpoon-riddled house associated with Hank Tom**. Since the island and tide shift, its exact map coordinates, building dimensions and durability are not yet independently checked.[^arrival]

## The S2E06 outbreak

Human survivor **Mack** claims dredging strange gems from the lake preceded the arrival of monstrous **slaadi**, including red and blue forms. He says red claw injuries implant larvae and blue assaults can transform victims into slaadi. This is **NPC testimony**; the gems' causal mechanism and the precise timing are **not proven**. The village burned its own merchant/escape boat to prevent a possible infected person reaching mainland Atomer. Mack and **Church**, a bandaged Goliath healer/priest, are the only survivors directly seen alive so far; the fates of **Ambermoor, Balmer, Destin, Neko, Goodwill** and other named workers remain uncertain.[^survivors]

The Delvers find **Caleb, a crab operator, dead in the infirmary** after a creature seemingly escaped from his chest. A **small purple-blue larva** escapes and is destroyed by Church following Koh's thrown psychic dagger. **Church admits a red slaad scratched him two days earlier** but believes Goliath ancestry protects him; **no medical determination confirms this**. Mack warns the larger creatures return with the tide, while the young are already on land.[^survivors]

In the harpoon-damaged residence of **Hank Tom**, a small **red frog-like transformed figure** flees under broken flooring. Koh's ranged psychic strike wounds it; Evelyn **kills it with repeated warhammer blows**, despite Krandal trying to obstruct and preserve it for questioning with a conjured python. Mack calls it Hank Tom; Church says the person is already lost. An apparent final **“thank you”** is present in both SRT exports, but speaker/meaning and any cure possibility **need audio or stronger evidence**. At the episode cutoff **tide is rising and ominous shadows circle offshore**. **No adult slaad battle, colony rescue/clearance or return ferry trip has been played.**[^cutoff]
