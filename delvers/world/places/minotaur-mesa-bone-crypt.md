# Minotaur Mesa bone crypt beneath Noble Hollow

## Introduction

A descent through Noble Hollow's old collapse leads among roots, earth and insects to an unlit burial chamber. Skeletal remains and Minotaur-script memorials line its circular perimeter, accompanied by rotted weapon hafts and surviving metal heads. This is a resting place for people who can still rise and speak, despite the loss of their flesh.[^route]

## Development

### Arc Three — A council choosing its release

Guided by Chad Brad's talking skull, Krandal reaches the graves and calls to their inhabitants. Bones gather into the towering figure of Corgan Greathorns, who summons the others. Nine skeletal minotaurs stand in council around the chamber. Chad Brad remains a separate skull accompanying the visitor.[^council]

The council describes an existence sustained by Khalazar's curse. Its members want the release that his defeat would bring, and willingly agree to accompany Krandal's expedition. He seeks their help while promising to honor their freedom. The burial chamber becomes a meeting place for an alliance founded on their wish for release.[^pledge]

Away at the farm, Krandal reassembles Chad Brad's buried body into another skeletal ally. The nine from the crypt and Chad Brad join the final expedition as ten. They carry captives to safety, and Corgan and Chad Brad protect the last rescuers from falling stone. After Khalazar's defeat, all ten settle into inert bones at the Guild, gaining the final rest they sought.[^rest]

## Current status

The crypt's nine council members have found release from their animated existence. Their last known remains lie at the Guild alongside Chad Brad's, with no return to these graves established. The burial chamber's later condition and its full relationship to adjoining tunnels remain unknown.[^status]
