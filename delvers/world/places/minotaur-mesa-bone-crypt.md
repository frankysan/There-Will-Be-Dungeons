# Minotaur Mesa bone crypt beneath Noble Hollow

**Physically explored by Krandal WITH Chad Brad's speaking skull in [Delvers S3E10 — Sad in a boney kind of way](../../episodes/S3E10.md)**. The Minotaur Mesa connection was previously proposed from farm/aboleth lore; **now actual explored location** accessed via **Noble Hollow** old vast collapse/open hole from earlier roper confrontation, underground roots, dirt and insects. Krandal succeeds high Survival (natural 20), Chad Brad aids direction imperfectly. **No confirmed precise map coordinate or continuous path to the Dawnless Dunes/triangle gate**.

An unlit, large underground chamber beneath the farm has **Minotaur-script memorials and skeletal burial piles** about **10ft apart** along its circular perimeter, with rotted wooden hafts and some surviving axe/mace heads. Memorial names as heard include **Corgan Greathorns, Bullyon, Nimblehoof, Thunder Hunter, Sausage, Goblin Runner, Steppenhoof**; these are **ASR-provisional**, do not treat as exhaustive nine-person roster. Krandal and **Chad Brad moo aloud**, causing one grave's bones to ACTUALLY form a giant **~13ft skeletal minotaur**, **Corgan Greathorns** (leader). Leader calls companions and **NINE skeletal minotaurs stand in council** including leader. Chad Brad is still a separate talking skull at this time and is NOT one of the nine crypt figures. Krandal asks to restore Chad Brad to freedom and avoid forced labour. The skeletons explain they have aged beyond flesh under **Khalazar curse** and will **PASS / finally die when Khalazar defeated**, willingly accepting that liberation. They **ACTUALLY PLEDGE to support Krandal's next expedition**; he promises to honor their freedom, not a guaranteed full resurrection. **No battle by crypt minotaurs during S3E10**; mission only promised for morning.

Krandal returns to farm, **ACTUALLY DIGS UP Chad Brad's buried original BODY**, and GM adjudicates a **`Create Undead`-like ritual using 150gp of specialty gems placed in eye sockets**; skull/bones reassemble into ANIMATED SKELETAL MINOTAUR (~13ft), not living flesh. GM announces **TEN skeletal minotaurs available in all: NINE crypt brothers/sisters + CHAD BRAD**, and **Nopa Ropa separately**. Do NOT add Chad Brad as an 11th. Their death upon ending Khalazar is **character testimony, not battle outcome**, and Krandal's further wish to resurrect remains aspiration. Chad Brad after ritual remains an undead ally and possibly rests as disassembled bones at dawn; no funeral/death at S3E10 end.
