# Governor's hall — Bronze Ball and the breached Drone pit

## Introduction

The governor's hall is a civic complex in Atomer's landscaped business district, close to [Withers Estate](withers-estate.md). Its meeting rooms and reception halls open onto a walled garden with trimmed trees, shrubs and a fountain. The Bronze Ball brings the city's officials, guild adventurers, donors and finishing-school students together beneath its glass roof. The same public rooms later become a battlefield and an entrance into the depths beneath the city.[^layout]

## Development

<a id="establishing-view-before-catastrophe"></a>

### Arc Two — A civic reception and a broken floor

Carriages deliver guests to a welcome from [Cassius](../characters/cassius.md) and the mayor. A coat and weapon check marks the transition from the street to the formal reception. Dance cards guide the evening's introductions, while the Gallery of the Fallen gives the festivities a memorial setting: inscribed names commemorate guild members who have died, alongside bellows-box portraits of bronze companies.[^reception]

The large wood-inlaid ballroom combines dining and dancing. A smaller auction hall holds a stage and Fredoroth's recovered dragon skull; glass-domed finds await written bids, alongside lots intended for a live auction. A smoking and card lounge, bar, kitchens and courtyard doors allow guests to move between formal entertainment and quieter conversations. The garden gives Evelyn space to change between armor and evening dress, and to meet the Gentleman Cavalier Traveler before they dance indoors.[^reception]

<a id="collapse-and-threats"></a>

The hall's open circulation becomes dangerous when Khalazar speaks through possessed musicians and guests. The garden fountain turns black. A great breach tears through the main ballroom floor and shatters the glass roof; the Drone sounds from below, drawing afflicted elves toward a roper that pulls them into the underground. Other creatures break into the reception rooms, while a separate collapse in the auction hall swallows Wildfire and Withers.[^breach]

Guests and adventurers fight through the damaged rooms. Evelyn breaks a Gallery window to open an escape for the schoolgirls. Water drains into the tunnels, and a fireball fails to break the rock or close the main pit. Dining tables become a barricade across the opening, but the Drone continues and compelled elves still try to reach it. The hall ends the night breached, with people missing below.[^barrier]

### Arc Three — From wreckage to dungeon entrance

Relief work begins among smaller holes and a fountain still oozing black fluid. Volunteers kill remaining creatures and block openings; Broadside helps move heavy furniture. These efforts make the wreckage more defensible without ending the underground threat.[^cleanup]

The surrounding governor's quarter is subsequently walled and placed under guild control. Public rules exclude elven and Sylvan people from governor's square and regulate licensed expeditions below. The former ballroom breach becomes a route into the dungeon network: the Delvers cross it, follow mirror-lit passages and descend by Gruber Lifton's lift. A ruined reception hall has become infrastructure for the guild's Silver City enterprise.[^access]

## Current status

The hall's last direct use is as an entrance to the occupied underground network. Khalazar's eventual defeat breaks his control and brings survivors home, but the building's repairs, the final condition of its openings and the fate of its furnishings and auction collection remain unknown.[^status]
