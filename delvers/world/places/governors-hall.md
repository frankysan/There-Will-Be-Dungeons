# Governor's hall — Bronze Ball and the breached Drone pit

**Atomer / Atamer (working city spelling)**, large civic complex with halls, meeting rooms and grounds in governor/mayor district. **Entire location ACTUALLY PLAYED in Delvers [S2E12 — The Bell of The Ball](../../episodes/S2E12.md), the Season Two / SECOND STORY ARC finale**, not just a planned fundraiser. **Original GM Discord floor plan/map referenced during episode but NOT inspected**; positions and scale below are described from speech, **not an exact cartographic reconstruction**.

## Establishing view before catastrophe

Grand municipal halls near landscaped **business district and Withers estate**, bordered by **walled courtyard/garden, manicured trees/bushes and fountain** which initially sprays pure water **~20 ft upward**. Formal guests arrive by carriage with Aegis offering assistance, are welcomed by guildmaster **Cassius** and military-looking **Mayor Malterby**. Inside: entrance **coat and weapon check**, **Margaret Dupayton issuing dance cards**, fork hallway leads to **Gallery of the Fallen** (stone names/shrine for ~15 guild dead, with bellows-box photographic plates of bronze teams), **vast wood-inlaid ballroom and dining hall ~225 ft across in GM description**, **smaller auction hall with stage bearing giant red-dragon skull**, separate **smoking/cards lounge**, bar and kitchens, many courtyard doors and tall glass windows/roof. Finishing-school students under **Lady Vestal** distribute formal dance cards and perform dances. The **Gallery includes names Elzar Soonish, Bram Broscale, Cameron family and Lilia Holmes** (ASR spelling provisional; inscriptions not visually verified). Gown and tux fashions appear late-Victorian/1880-inspired, but period/year and original Discord outfit plates unverified.[^establish]

**Silent auction** uses glass-domed finds and written bids; **live auction** uses podium under ornate drapes, prominently **Fredoroth's recovered dragon skull** (NOT sold in S2E12 cutoff). Other displayed lots include cranium rat in jar, grick tendrils, hook horror claw, banshee ectoplasm. **No final donor proceeds or completed sale documented**.

## Collapse and threats

**Cassius's ceremonial speech** seems to magically affect listeners (Wisdom saves and dreamlike smiles), source unknown. Later the **orchestra maestro's baton snaps**, he is used as the **voice of Khalazar**; dark eyes and smoke spreading across possessed musicians/guests. Outside fountain **blackens/thickens**. When **Selenar Everbloom**, temporarily possessed, confronts **Withers** in auction room, he claims he had hired Broscales/opened doors, Cassius catches her fainting body.

**The MAIN BALLROOM floor ACTUALLY BALLOONS THEN CRACKS/ERUPTS, revealing active sounding DRONE and subterranean network; glass roof SHATTERS**. From hole a great **roper** reaches upward and pulls countless black-eyed **compelled elves** into the underground. **Hook horrors** open further holes near main entrance and auction room, **darkmantles and gricks** spill from washroom/lower passages; crowd and guild parties fight/evacuate. **Wildfire drags at Withers in auction hall**, **SECOND HOLE opens beneath BOTH**, their physical fate **unconfirmed at cutoff**. Evelyn and schoolgirls escape by **NAT20 broken Gallery window**, later her GCT partner blinks around battlefield. **Lady Vestal hands Evelyn unidentified heart-shaped diamond-crusted six-inch object**, not used or identified.

Krandal's flood spell **DRAINS AWAY into tunnels**, Koh's recovered Fireball bracelet **ignites pit but fails to break rock**. Guild teams collectively dump **~25 dining tables into main entrance**, creating temporary access barrier. **DRONE CONTINUES SOUNDING and more enthralled elves try to climb/dig toward pit** when GM ends episode. **Do not depict hall repaired, portal sealed, elves cured, dragon trophy sold or Khalazar killed** before later source verifies.[^collapse]

**Illustration continuity:** one **BEFORE attack** establishing gala (warm evening, genteel crowds, decorated long ballroom, normal fountain); another **DURING attack** (broken wooden dancefloor + roof, central Drone pit + roper and black sand/compelled elf crowds, hook horrors in entrance). **No canonical map coordinates** until original GM plan read. Civilian appearances, exact table counts and device silhouette not established beyond narrative; avoid inventing a vast glowing sci-fi control console as certain identity of Carrie's small stone music box.
