# Delvers Dungeon Guild — first employment

## Overview

The Delvers Dungeon Guild offers adventurers a workplace and a place to return to. Its hall combines a quest desk, job board, supply counter, tavern, trophy case and upstairs lodging. Cassius welcomes recruits and manages assignments and payments; the dwarf Clearstone supplies equipment, while Joe Groinhammer tends the bar and can cover the quest desk.[^introduction]

Wooden badges and small guides identify the lowest league's members. The guild spends little on these badges: Koh's is already scorched, and Cassius speaks of previous holders. The trophy case and references to higher-ranked adventurers place the newcomers at the bottom of an established profession.[^badges]

## Practices and first job

Cassius says jobs are posted early, leaving the late-arriving recruits with limited choice. They reject Snake Island and accept paid work at the [farm cave](farm-cave.md). Their orientation combines encouragement with uncomfortable economics: Cassius reads a foreword attributed to the celebrated Thun’Darious Storm, yet estimates that only half of those attempting this cave job succeed. His estimate is part of the welcome he gives them, rather than an independently measured record.[^orientation]

When the party returns, the guild accepts its report and trophies and pays the promised reward. Cassius receives the dead delver's badge and says its owner had sought a door with a face elsewhere in the Underdark. He also checks the recovered [drift globe](../items/drift-globe.md) for sentience before clearing its use. Evelyn and Krandal accept upstairs accommodation, while Koh returns home.[^return]

## A wider choice of work

The party's two divine casters make it suitable for an assignment at Kord's mountain sanctuary. Successful cleansing of its fountains and rescue of the missing devotees earn a larger payment, including a rescue bonus. With that task complete, the guild opens its full job board to the three adventurers.[^crypt]

## Scouting, claims and a path to advancement

The guild's payment system becomes clearer when the three Delvers return from the wooded ruins. Their task was to verify the presence of gricks, not to exterminate them. Cassius accepts their trophy and discovery of a dangerous buried dungeon with admiration, but the extra gricks they slew earn no additional bounty. He explains that the guild sends cheaper wood-ranked adventurers to gather information before committing expensive, more experienced members. Exploration is sometimes valued more than immediate battle.[^scout]

As the discoverers, the three ask to claim the dungeon for future exploration. Cassius agrees to enter their request, while warning that another serious attack may require sending silver-ranked adventurers first. He also promises to put their names before a superior who can consider promoting them to bronze. The reward comes with a reminder of their present limits: wood-ranked members may undertake only three jobs in a week, so the party must spend the remaining days away from quests. Cassius still has the runic sword hilt Evelyn recovered, awaiting inspection.[^scout]

## Service, apprenticeship and the next rank

With the initial bronze promotion request rejected, the guild turns the three adventurers toward work that its neighbors can actually see. They clear pests, assist a senior member in protecting market-goers, combat a supernatural fire and even restore order at a laundry where garments have turned hostile. The ordinary residents whose paths cross theirs begin to recognize the group; its work is no longer hidden in distant dungeons. Rewards accumulate, though the guild's wood-rank limits still enforce rest days between strings of quests.[^service]

Cassius eventually announces that Selenar Everbloom has approved the move to **bronze rank**, to be formalized by new badges and orientation the next morning. The three choose a first bronze posting at **Skull Keep**, an old property called Witherskeep said to be haunted by a flameskull. As they prepare, Koh receives the inspected [Soul Knife](../items/runic-sword-hilt.md) from the acquisitions staff and the party visits the city's merchants. They also claim a new identity: **the Darkest Delvers**.[^promotion]

## The first bronze assignment

The guild's next ceremony is modest but memorable. Cassius summons the three friends, presents an adventuring guide with the words of Impossible Diamonds and entrusts them with a key to the guild's library. Later the numbered wooden badges that identified them as apprentices are surrendered. In their place they receive unnumbered bronze shields bearing the guild chimera: lion, goat and serpent-headed dragon. The little celebration has only a few witnesses, but the new rank marks an important threshold.[^bronze]

Cassius sends them north with Aegis and a client from the old Withers family. The keep they visit has been haunted by a flameskull and its animated servants. The Darkest Delvers return after sundown with the skull destroyed, news of an earlier party's fate and a new account of an old darkness beneath the lake. They receive payment for their work, reduced somewhat by the damage done to the furnishings their client wished to preserve.[^report]

## A citywide warning

The silver ranks become occupied investigating the forest dungeon while lesser horrors appear throughout the city's neighborhoods. Carrie Underworld studies sightings of darkmantles, stirges, piercers and gricks, proposing that prey and predators are following one another toward the surface. She and Cassius gather leaders from four bronze companies, allowing the entire Darkest Delvers group to attend because their past discoveries have become central to the problem. The guild sends teams to investigate public wells, keeping Brigand Patrol ready to respond.[^wells]

The first report comes from the southwestern quarter. It speaks of a rust monster, a mimic and a stirge, but the adventurers have not looked into the well itself. Cassius immediately dispatches a guard and insists that the matter remain open until the water source can be inspected. A victory over monsters in the street is no longer sufficient proof that the danger has passed.[^wells]

## The reckoning after the ruins

The guild's underground cells and magical circle of truth become a place of difficult questions when bronze-ranked adventurers return with silver-ranked colleagues in custody. Jimmy and Clearstone allow the Darkest Delvers to question their prisoners under supervision. Broadside and Cheet reveal that a researcher named Marek has taken the work of the forest expeditions toward a forbidden summoning at Noble Hollow. The guild makes an exception to rank restrictions, and Broadside leaves with the Delvers to help prevent a high elf's sacrifice.[^finale]

Several days after the hollow's collapse, Cassius and the senior officer Selenar Everbloom hear the Delvers' account. The guild thanks the adventurers for protecting its people, clears their names, and sets future proceedings in motion against those whose conduct endangered the city. War against vampires in the north has drained its available funds; instead of an additional purse, its officers promise Krandal the deed to an old tower. The bronze ball remains an important fundraiser for the next stage of investigating the earth below the city.[^finale]

## Current status

The guild has **completed its immediate All-Bronze emergency operation** and **cleared the Darkest Delvers** after their rescue at Noble Hollow. It believes **Broadside and Solburn acted under outside influence** and will clear them; **Marek will face a future trial** at the Guild Academy, and **Cheet is to be rehabilitated**. The guild's understanding of the **Dawnless Dunes, black residue, buried mechanisms and unconscious elves remains incomplete**, and further research funding is sought through the upcoming **bronze ball**. With money occupied by a **northern war against vampires**, there is **no extra cash for the Delvers' exceptional service**; officers **promise Krandal an old farmland tower deed**, not yet physically delivered. A new **Snake Island contract is offered** as the first arc closes, and no acceptance is shown.[^finale]
