## Introduction

Atomer's guild gives adventurers employment, supplies and somewhere to return after dangerous work. Its hall brings together a quest desk, job board, acquisitions counter, tavern, trophies and upstairs rooms. [Cassius](../characters/cassius.md) welcomes recruits and handles commissions; Clearstone supplies their equipment, while Joe Groinhammer tends the bar. Wooden badges mark the lowest league's members, who enter an established profession with little money or standing of their own.[^guild-introduction]

### Arc One — Employment and public duty

The guild's welcome combines encouragement with strict limits. Clients pay for defined tasks, and the reward follows completion rather than every monster killed along the way. Its least experienced companies gather information before more expensive adventurers are committed. Wood-ranked members can undertake three assignments each week, with enforced leave afterward. Supplies are accounted for, finds inspected and dead members' badges returned.

Advancement requires service as well as survival. Selenar initially refuses the Darkest Delvers' promotion, directing them toward work their neighbors can see. Bronze membership brings new badges, library access and greater responsibility. The hall is a meeting place where staff, novices and veterans exchange both practical help and warnings.[^guild-practices]

Increasing incursions expose the limits of the local organization. Cassius and [Carrie](../characters/carrie-underworld.md) coordinate companies at the wells and investigate the buried structures beneath the city. The guild can guard entrances and supervise prisoners, but its leaders need more resources. At the [Academy](dungeon-guild-academy.md), Cassius asks for experienced champions; the board refuses additional aid and points toward Withers patronage and the coming Bronze Ball.

The emergency at Noble Hollow tests its procedures. Jimmy and Clearstone allow bronze adventurers into the truth-bound interrogation facility, and a compromised senior colleague is permitted to help stop a sacrifice. The later inquiry clears the Delvers and recognizes their courage. Its officers cite the northern vampire war as the reason no extra cash is available and promise Krandal land instead. The institution survives through exceptions, personal judgment and obligations it cannot always meet in gold.[^guild-first-crisis]

### Arc Two — Patronage and its dangers

Routine commissions continue beside the growing underground investigation. The guild pays the returned Snake Island company, examines its strange possessions and fulfills the promised deed. It can supplement a difficult wedding assignment with a separately hired specialist, and coordinate the citywide defense against a burrowing predator.

Its dependence on wealthy patrons also creates conflicting loyalties. Cassius privately finances an investigation of [Withers](../characters/alexandre-vanderbuck-withers.md) through the Cloven Brotherhood, outside the ordinary guild record. The Delvers' burglary supplies Carrie with documents and relics to study, while Withers employs the Broscales to find the thieves. Cassius sends the company away on commissions as the inquiry threatens to expose them.[^guild-second-work]

Carrie's work identifies the mind-controlling Hereditary Drone and helps enable the rescue of Koh's wife. Silver approval follows recommendation, and an Academy assessment records the Delvers' abilities and hero names. Their portrait and recovered dragon skull become part of the Bronze Ball's public fundraising effort.

The celebration becomes an institutional disaster. Possession passes through its guests and officials, and Withers answers accusations about hired agents and opened doors. Creatures breach the hall while compelled elves are carried below. Guild companies save people and barricade the opening, but cannot silence its call. Cassius carries the fallen Selenar; Withers and Wildfire vanish underground. Public confidence and the guild's own leadership now face the consequences of a danger they have failed to contain.[^guild-ball]

### Arc Three — Silver City and the return to rescue

The Academy investigates Cassius and grounds the Delvers. Zoltar occupies the guildmaster's office while Atomer becomes Silver City: a walled, licensed gateway to the dungeons, drawing adventurers and business. He acknowledges to Dumbledwark that the Academy benefits from a continuing supply of dangerous expeditions. Specific C4 and diplomatic work can be approved while the trio remains excluded from ordinary commissions. Koh and Krandal's work freeing afflicted people continues beside these restrictions.[^guild-silver-city]

Suspicion becomes public confrontation when the Delvers return with the [three urns](../items/three-urns-triangle-gate.md). Zoltar admits routine divination of adventurers. Clearstone brings forward thirty names of missing elves, and the crowd hears the human cost behind the city's prosperity. Staff and members support the demand for Cassius's return. At Wildfire's estate, Valentine releases him; Zoltar leaves alive.

Back at his desk, Cassius funds an authorized operation against Khalazar. Lower leagues guard the approaches while trusted parties descend with the Delvers and their allies. Darmah's two circles make the guild basement a place of arrival for rescued captives. Healers receive people formerly enslaved in the pyramid, including Wildfire, Withers, Hissia and Keeves. The surviving Delvers return before the collapsing temple seals their escape. The guild's resources are finally directed toward ending the danger rather than indefinitely managing its opportunities.[^guild-return]

## Current status

Cassius has regained local leadership, and the guild remains a place of refuge and training after Khalazar's defeat. Its basement receives the rescued people, and its healers tend them. During the following year Koh teaches younger members and those developing psychic gifts, carrying the lessons of the Delvers' work into another generation of adventurers.[^guild-status]
