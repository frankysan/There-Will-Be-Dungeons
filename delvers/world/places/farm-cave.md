# Farm cave — first sealed passage

## Introduction

East of Atomer, beyond Squelcher's Swamp, a sheep field borders a natural cave beneath an uplifted slab. Drained sheep lie near its broad entrance. Puddles and hanging stone formations lead into the darkness, where the farmer's trouble reaches farther underground than the pasture suggests.[^setting]

### Arc One — A fissure beneath the pasture

Stirges feed near the entrance, while a piercer and a darkmantle hide among the stone formations inside. The Delvers kill these immediate threats, though the darkmantle leaves a patch of magical darkness behind. In the final chamber, a narrow fissure descends into a lower passage.[^threats]

Koh climbs down and finds a mostly skeletal dragonborn beside a wooden badge and a glass globe. He retrieves the objects, then retreats from an approaching serpentine creature. Evelyn wedges a fallen rock into the fissure and Krandal reinforces it with concrete. Impacts lift the plug slightly, but it holds as the hissing below recedes. The concrete hardens, cutting off this approach from the lower tunnel.[^seal]

## Current status

The fissure is sealed at the Delvers' departure. The skeleton remains below, and the approaching creature has not been fought. The guild accepts the work as complete; the cave's later condition and the wider passages beyond the plug remain unknown.[^status]
