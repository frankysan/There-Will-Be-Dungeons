# Right-stair twin-puzzle and grick wind-chamber complex

<a id="right-stair-twin-puzzle-and-grick-wind-chamber-complex-delvers-s3e09"></a>

## Introduction

The right-hand stair from the [triangle-gate catacombs](triangle-gate-catacombs.md) leads through roots and old stone toward a pair of great doors. Bones laid across the approach form an alarm for Barclay, a stranded researcher who has made a camp among mushroom beds and salvaged equipment. Beyond his refuge, matching passages divide visitors into pairs and make each pair responsible for the other's progress.[^approach]

## Development

### Arc Three — Shared defenses and a silent wind hall

Bat-wing and grick reliefs frame the two entrances. Pressure plates admit the Delvers and Barclay, then the doors close behind them. Each lane contains a deep chasm whose bridge is controlled from the opposite passage. Farther on, wall symbols in one room identify the floor plates needed in the other. The defenses demand communication across the dividing walls rather than independent movement through either route.[^paired]

The next chambers extend that dependence into combat. Two stone golems descend onto platforms surrounded by falls, while poisonous gas fills the floor area. Attacks directed at one construct damage the other. The group survives the linked defenses and destroys both golems, opening the way onward.[^golems]

The routes reunite in a high hall of streams, rocks and ledges surrounding a central spiral ramp. Openings around the column alternately draw air inward and blast it outward. Gricks hide among the apparent stones, including an unusually large alpha. Evelyn times her ascent to the wind and removes a grick-headed ceramic urn from the upper basin. The room's suction and blasts stop immediately.[^wind]

The silence rouses the predators. Several gricks are killed, including the alpha, but at least one escapes. With the urn carried away and the bridges and doors open, the party leaves the complex and backtracks to the historic forest dungeon entrance. Its repaired crossings provide a route out of the underground without opening the triangle gate on this journey.[^departure]

## Current status

Both golems are destroyed, the wind has stopped and the grick-headed urn has been removed. The opened route remains the party's last known way through the complex, with at least one grick surviving. Its later occupants and structural condition are unknown.[^status]
