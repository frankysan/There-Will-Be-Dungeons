# Pelor's temple — sanctuary over the forgotten ruins

## Introduction

Pelor's temple stands among the familiar streets of the guild's city, a refuge for prayer, healing and quiet acts of charity. To Krandal Splenderbelt it is both a home and a discipline: he sleeps in its spare dormitories, ministers to the wounded and endures Brother Maynard's stern expectations. Its sunlit halls and ancient artwork speak of the power of light, even while much of its older stonework remains hidden beneath later ornament.[^home]

## A cleric's duty

Krandal returns to the temple between guild adventures. Here he finds purpose in tasks that rarely bring public glory, and here a cockatrice's stone enchantment finally wears away after his companions bring him for care. When another celebrant falls ill, Brother Maynard presses Krandal into service at a noble wedding. Awkward and ill prepared, he nonetheless completes the blessing. This adds to Maynard's complicated judgment of his young colleague: genuine devotion accompanied by poor ceremony and an awkward tendency to argue over worldly payment.[^service]

## The old hall beneath the floor

The discovery of a worked underground dungeon forces the temple's long history into the open. Six adventurers stand upon hidden weighted platforms and a stairway rises as great stone slabs split the Hall of Penance floor. The displaced furniture and shattered mosaic horrify Maynard, but Krandal and his friends insist the opening was built long before their arrival. Carrie Underworld investigates on the guild's behalf, and guards are assigned to protect the new entrance. The guild later undertakes to spare the Delvers any personal liability for the damage.[^opening]

The disturbance does not end with the hall of penance. Carrie joins Evelyn and her companions in searching the larger sanctuary for traces of the same ancient design. Beyond a tapestry and a covering of plaster, they expose stone signs familiar from the woodland ruin. Beneath the altar's wooden lectern they uncover an old stone column pierced by a narrow shaft. A deep, insistent drone rises from the darkness when its covering is lifted. A monk collapses as the sound fills the sanctuary, and the wooden covering is put back before further exploration can begin. Whatever lies beneath the altar remains unseen.[^altar]

## The silent brothers

The temple's trouble becomes personal when Brother Maynard reveals that two elves cannot be awakened. One had been meant to officiate the wedding Krandal performed in his stead; the other fell ill in the sanctuary. Neither yields to the ordinary restorative aid tried by the priests. Krandal notices a recurring finger-tap in the first man's still hand and copies it down without discovering its meaning. Maynard, softened by the cleric's concern, gives him an old amulet of devotion said to have come from the temple's ancient founder. Krandal accepts it and puts it on.[^brothers]

## Current status

The **Hall of Penance entrance is exposed and under guild protection**, while the **distinct shaft beneath the main altar has been closed again by its removable wooden cover**. The old wall signs remain uncovered after investigation. The cause of the disturbing drone and the two elves' unresponsiveness has **not been demonstrated**, nor has either elf recovered. Brother Maynard now trusts Krandal more fully, and Krandal wears the given devotional amulet. The guild has promised that the Delvers will not personally pay for the accidental floor opening, though restoration of the temple has not yet been depicted.[^status]
