# Pelor's temple — sanctuary over the forgotten ruins

## Introduction

Pelor's temple is a place of healing, prayer and service near Atomer's guild. Krandal finds lodging in its spare quarters and work among the sick, under Brother Maynard's stern supervision. Its sanctuary and Hall of Penance give religious life a settled home, with room for worship, contemplation and the daily labor of its clergy.[^home]

### Arc One — A sanctuary built over another age

The temple provides practical care as well as religious discipline. Krandal heals injury and poison, serves in ceremonies and receives correction when he seeks tips or turns treatment into a performance. His companions bring him here after a cockatrice leaves him petrified; after he recovers, Maynard sends him to officiate a wedding in place of an ill cleric. The temple sustains its neighborhood through both routine duties and extraordinary emergencies.[^service]

Its buried architecture becomes visible when six adventurers activate weighted platforms underground. Great slabs split the Hall of Penance floor, displacing furniture and damaging the mosaic as a stair opens from below. Maynard confronts the intruders; Carrie surveys the entrance for the guild, which assigns guards and undertakes to handle the temple's financial claims.[^stair]

A separate investigation exposes old carvings beneath sanctuary plaster and a narrow shaft inside the stone column under the altar's wooden covering. A low drone rises from the darkness; an elf monk collapses while it sounds. Replacing the covering muffles the noise, leaving the shaft unexplored. This opening is distinct from the stair in the Hall of Penance.[^altar]

Two unresponsive elves bring the deeper danger into the temple's daily care. One is the absent wedding celebrant, Rianu Keeves; the other is the monk from the sanctuary. Ordinary restorative efforts fail. Keeves's repeating finger movements suggest a coded lament, but neither their purpose nor a cure becomes clear.[^sleepers]

### Arc Two — A guarded entrance and a ward of sleepers

The Hall of Penance entrance is boarded and guarded, but frightened clergy leave and Maynard struggles with too few staff. Keeves remains alive and unconscious. Healing eases his bodily injuries without waking him, and another magical experiment draws only a whispered word.[^security]

The ward grows to five sleeping elves. Koh and Krandal reach Keeves through a psychic link, finding him trapped in imagined royal service. Their intervention briefly restores his awareness within that world, then the compulsion resumes. Later, Maynard reports that all five patients have awakened and departed, with Keeves first to recover on the morning of Anathala's rescue.[^ward]

### Arc Three — A centre for relief after the Ball

After the Bronze Ball, the temple receives afflicted elves again. Its infirmary and isolated catacombs accommodate different forms of the disturbance. Koh carries Krandal into patients' dream spaces, where curse-breaking brings seven people back to awareness. Further patients remain, and treatment continues on following days.[^relief]

Carrie tests a detector among the afflicted, helping Koh find others in the city. One elf brought to the temple after a nonlethal psychic blow wakes lucid without a further dream intervention. This offers a way to relieve shallow compulsion, while the deeper sleeping cases remain a different problem. The temple becomes the receiving place for Koh's continuing rescue work.[^later]

## Current status

The temple's latest established role is as a centre for treating and receiving afflicted elves. The Hall of Penance entrance was secured, but completed mosaic repairs and a final investigation of the separate altar shaft remain unknown. Khalazar's defeat ends his control; the final survivors receive care at the guild. The temple's later staffing remains unknown.[^status]
