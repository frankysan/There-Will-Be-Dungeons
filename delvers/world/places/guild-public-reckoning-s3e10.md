# Atomer Guild public reckoning and Cassius's return

<a id="s3e10--atomer-guild-public-reckoning-and-cassiuss-return"></a>

## Introduction

Atomer's Silver City prosperity rests on expeditions beneath a city still missing people after the Bronze Ball. With Cassius absent and Zoltar directing the local guild, the Darkest Delvers bring their opposition into the streets. Their public challenge turns the missing citizens and the guild's responsibilities into a question the town must answer.[^context][^continuity]

## Development

### Arc Three — Public support becomes a rescue mandate

Evelyn gathers townspeople and confronts Zoltar at the guild. Koh and Krandal join her accusations, while Clearstone brings a list of thirty missing elves. The three read the names aloud. Zoltar acknowledges that the guild tracks adventurers by divination, defends the Academy's enterprise and blames the Delvers for the Ball. Guild members and citizens nevertheless give their support to the demand for Cassius's return.[^challenge]

When Zoltar retreats, Koh confronts him privately with a reporter's lead. Zoltar confirms that Cassius is being held at the Wildfire estate. Koh accompanies him there as the crowd follows. Valentine Wildfire brings Cassius out alive, and Zoltar leaves town on horseback after Koh's warning.[^release]

Cassius returns to the guildmaster's desk and directs the institution toward defeating Khalazar. He allocates twenty thousand gold to each of the three Delvers for equipment and authorizes preparations for an expedition. The confrontation has produced a practical transfer of local leadership and resources, turning public anger into an organized rescue effort.[^mandate]

## Lasting legacy

The authorized expedition reaches the Dawnless Dunes, frees captives and defeats Khalazar. Its survivors return to the guild for care, giving the public demand a concrete result. Cassius resumes local leadership, but a formal verdict on his confinement, Zoltar's legal position and the wider Academy's response remain unknown.[^legacy]
