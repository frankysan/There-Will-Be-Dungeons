# The triangle-gate catacombs — hidden beneath Delvers Dungeon Level E

**First discovered during played [Delvers S3E06 — A naked man can be slippery](../../episodes/S3E06.md)**, after the three core Delvers have stayed overnight at [Deep Winds Spa](deep-winds-spa.md). This is **a separate dark descent off the route back toward Gruber Lifton's lift**, not an occupied room of the spa and not proof of a formally opened Guild dungeon. **Actual position relative to Withers Keep/Black Basilica remains unresolved** until verified map evidence.

## Staircase and fallen Silvers

Koh notices a **tight, worn, steep staircase** the party failed to notice on arrival; the other heroes feel observed. Its carved **bat-and-humanoid skull/wing figurehead** seems to watch them as they move, but is described as **stone**, not a living monster. Along upper stairs lie **three fallen Silver League adventurers**, in atypical outfits: **Hypnomoth**'s **moth-like wing/flying suit** and helmet, Silver badge **95**; **Shadowblade**'s leather/throwing knives and a **flaming dark blade emblem**; and tattooed **Goliath “Black Bear”** with heavy hammer, black-bear badge **73**. **Names verified only after Carrie consults registry back at Guild.** They apparently died before the three present, but the precise killer and dates are not established. Evelyn **takes three badges and a moth HELMET**, not the wing suit, into her bag; Koh removes a **hummingbird-engraved ring with three emeralds** from leather figure and **gold necklace from Goliath**, carefully, for later identification. **Badge custody ends with Carrie**, item custody details separately recorded in [S3E06](../../episodes/S3E06.md).

## Hundreds of rune-discs and three-slot triangle door

Far below, the floor is dressed stone, walls lined with **hundreds of round spiral-slatted rune tablets**, like closable **sarcophagus/catacomb fronts**. At least two already exposed interiors bear scratched roofs, and one spot with an absent tablet has only solid stone behind it, even after Evelyn strikes it. Krandal and Evelyn recognize the pattern of a **stone tablet recovered after the [Noble Hollow](noble-hollow.md) fight**, already given to Guild; NOT still in Koh's bag. Koh and party reach altar showing figures, gricks and hook horrors venerating a **long-legged/spider-like figure**. Its **three circular shallow recesses** bear **black-sand residue**, while black grains seep under the entrance. Beyond a bat-skull/fanged arch is a **dark black/purple metal door with luminous triangle**. It physically resembles the image **Koh ACTUALLY saw inside an aboleth's mind** minutes earlier. Aboleth associated the visual with Khalazar's interests, but **the actual key objects, door function and original makers remain UNKNOWN**. **Carrie later proposes** three keys and tentatively equates stairs with **Umbrel/Umbral Flight** on a pre-existing Withers map; **neither guess confirmed by activation**.

## The tomb inhabitants and retreat

One sealed round tablet **spirals open** as the heroes examine the chamber. Evelyn pulls out a brittle, stretched-skin, **long-dead pale ELF mummy**; Krandal's medicine observation notes **removed organs/surgical alteration**, resembling the hollow Khalazar-associated attacker he killed near the farm, **not proved same person or cause**. More recesses later open, and multiple hostile mummified elves animate. **Krandal actually uses Turn Undead; monsters flee but are too powerful to be instantly destroyed by Destroy Undead**, his spectral mace inflicts **force**, and Koh/Evelyn together with him defeat emergent threats. One monster briefly **paralyzes and levitates Krandal** toward empty niche before **Koh breaks its concentration**. Evelyn's **Branding Smite reveals invisible fleeing monster**, whom she kills. Precise active and killed counts are ASR/map-uncertain. **The party retreats voluntarily, without taking a key, opening triangle door, or carrying bodies back.**

**Current status at S3E06:** Gate **sealed**, **zero keys confirmed possessed**, multiple remaining rune-tombs apparently unopened. The connection between **black sand, Khalazar, aboleth vision, Noble Hollow slab, Black Basilica, the old elves' surgical alteration and the Withers map** is a **multi-part investigational hypothesis**, not established ancient history. Party alerts Carrie, but Guild has not sent an authorized follow-up raid.
