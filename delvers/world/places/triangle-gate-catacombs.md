# The triangle-gate catacombs — hidden beneath Delvers Dungeon Level E

## Introduction

A narrow, steep stair leaves the tunnels near Gruber Lifton's lift and descends into an older burial complex. A stone bat-and-skull figure marks the approach. Far below, walls of circular rune-covered closures surround an altar and a dark metal door bearing a luminous triangle. The hidden chambers lie apart from the nearby spa and the guild's ordinary routes.[^approach]

### Arc Three — A sealed threshold and the gathered urns

Three dead Silver adventurers lie along the upper stairs, their unusual equipment contrasting with the worn stone. Recovered badges later identify them as Hypnomoth, Shadowblade and Black Bear. Their presence makes the descent a place of recent loss as well as ancient burial, though who killed them remains unknown.[^fallen]

Hundreds of round, spiral-opening fronts line the lower chamber. Some niches show scratches inside, while one missing plate reveals only solid rock. Reliefs depict figures and creatures gathered around a long-legged central form. Black sand rests in the altar's three shallow recesses and seeps beneath the closed door. Two stairways offer routes away from this threshold.[^hall]

Opening niches releases hostile mummified elves. The Delvers find bodies emptied of organs and fight occupants able to restrain, conceal themselves and draw victims toward the recesses. They survive and withdraw without a way through the gate, leaving many closures unexplored.[^occupants]

The altar's purpose becomes clearer through later visits. A bat-headed vessel recovered from the separate black-slime chamber fits one recess perfectly, but one vessel does not open the door. After exploring the left-hand route to the Black Basilica, the Delvers remove that first jar and carry it away with a second. The right-hand branch yields a third urn; the three objects remain with the party until its final authorized expedition returns.[^gathering]

This time all three vessels are placed in the altar. Their golden heads turn toward the gate, and an ancient woman emerges from a nearby tomb. Calling herself Shaolin Nym, an architect of Dolbluth, she instructs the Delvers to restore Khalazar's organs and defeat him in his true form. The urns lift free, the door opens and she crumbles to dust. The expedition enters the Dawnless Dunes, and the gate closes behind it.[^opening]

## Current status

The gate's last observed state is closed after admitting the expedition, with the three altar recesses empty again. Shaolin Nym is gone, and the urns have been carried onward. The remaining tombs and the catacombs' condition after Khalazar's defeat are unknown.[^status]
