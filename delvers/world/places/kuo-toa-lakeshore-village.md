# The kuo-toa lakeshore village

## Introduction

At the edge of the eastern lake, where cultivated fields become a tangle of warm freshwater swamp, a small community of catfish-like people maintains a life of fishing, trade and humble domestic work. Their homes cluster along a sandy strip between the trees and water. Docks and nets reach into the lake; smoking fires and simple looms provide for everyday needs. Smaller children play in the sand while their elders watch strangers with more alarm than hostility.[^setting]

## Strangers bearing gifts

The Darkest Delvers approach by borrowed horses carrying a basket of fish and freshwater pearls, the gifts their guild considers proper for peaceful negotiation. One villager flees at their first appearance, alarming the others. Evelyn sets out the offerings and struggles with a few words from a translation sheet. The village gradually comes forward, and its elder offers the most formal welcome.

Koh then tries to speak directly into the elder's thoughts. The voice seems to come from a divine presence rather than another mortal, and the old fisherman falls to the sand calling out that someone from above has come. His neighbors join him in reverence. Koh protests that he is only using a strange means of communication, but the belief persists. Even the horses draw prayers as the visitors enter the village.[^meeting]

## The trader by the forest

One inhabitant seems less frightened by such wonders. **Glepo Ma**, who wears human-made trousers and shoes modified for his finned feet, speaks enough Common to bargain. He trades unusual goods and objects gathered from the world outside his village, and tells of an upbringing close to a circus. He knows of old ruins beneath the lake, accessible more readily when the water recedes, and wants an old staff found there. Koh exchanges a shovel for directions and later gains a little fishing rod in return for letting the trader care for the company's horses. The villagers continue their ordinary tasks while the three strangers prepare to swim.[^trader]

## Current status

The **Delvers have entered the submerged ruin** after a peaceful meeting. The community's belief that **Koh is a god remains unresolved**, despite his denials. **Glepo Ma holds Koh's traded shovel** and is entrusted with the horses and left equipment; Koh received a small fishing rod. The **requested staff has not yet been delivered**, and the party has not returned to shore. One living ritualist from deeper chambers is being carried toward the village under restraint, but the villagers' response is unknown.[^status]
