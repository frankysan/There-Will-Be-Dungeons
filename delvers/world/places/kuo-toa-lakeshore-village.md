# The kuo-toa lakeshore village

## Introduction

At the edge of the eastern lake, where cultivated fields give way to freshwater swamp, a small kuo-toa community lives along a sandy strip between trees and water. Docks, fishing nets, smoking fires and simple looms support its households. Children play near the huts while their elders watch strangers cautiously. A trader's cluttered shop connects this lakeshore life to goods and visitors from farther away.[^setting]

### Arc One — Gifts, reverence and coercion

The Delvers arrive on borrowed horses with fish and freshwater pearls, seeking peaceful contact. Their first appearance alarms the villagers, but the gifts draw people forward. When Koh speaks into an elder's thoughts, the unexpected voice is received as divine revelation. The elder falls in reverence, and others join him despite Koh's insistence that he is no god. Even the visitors' horses become objects of fascination and prayer.[^welcome]

Glepo Ma is more comfortable with outsiders. He speaks some Common, wears adapted human clothing and keeps a shop of salvaged objects. For Koh's shovel he provides directions to ruins under the lake and asks for a staff recovered from them. He takes charge of the horses while the Delvers swim, and village life continues around the visitors' gifts: fish are smoked and pearls worn as ornaments.[^trade]

The return from the ruins exposes a harsher outside connection. Glepo Ma shelters the party and its living captive in his hut, but hands their conch-tipped staff to a mounted collector. Wearing a darkmantle cowl, the rider threatens to kill villagers who conceal the adventurers. He leaves for the bullywug settlement with the staff, ordering two armed followers to take whatever else the Delvers recovered.[^collectors]

The ensuing fight leaves both followers alive but defeated. One reveals the familiar face of Broadside. Glepo Ma acknowledges that he misjudged the guild visitors and offers goods as amends; Koh takes a stone gear-crank and a sack of coins. The trader then swims west to warn the other guild party. The Delvers ride away with their three living prisoners, leaving the settlement inhabited and its homes standing.[^departure]

<a id="later-s2e11-disposition-former-trader-now-works-on-potato-grove"></a>

### Arc Two — A trader displaced

Glepo Ma later appears at Krandal's riverside farm and reports that his community banished him for helping abduct its high priest. He finds shelter and work there.[^exile]

## Current status

The village's last directly known condition is intact and inhabited after the collectors' attack. Its subsequent leadership, response to the captive's removal and belief in Koh remain uncertain. Glepo Ma lives and works elsewhere; no later return establishes the settlement's condition.[^status]
