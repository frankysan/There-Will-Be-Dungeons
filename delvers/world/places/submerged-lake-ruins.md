# The submerged ruins of the eastern lake

## Introduction

Stone columns barely break the surface of the freshwater lake east of Atomer. Beneath them, moss, algae and drifting plants cover the walls of an old complex. The nearby [kuo-toa village](kuo-toa-lakeshore-village.md) knows routes into these submerged works, where familiar masonry disappears into deeper shafts and darkness.[^setting]

### Arc One — Flooded defenses and an older market

The Delvers find corridors and trap arrangements resembling the forest ruins and the passages beneath Pelor's temple. Water has changed how the defenses behave: spear traps stand extended and loose darts drift overhead. An algae-covered control conceals a side chamber with a stone chest. Its inscribed ring responds to Koh's mind and is passed to Krandal, while other ancient shafts remain on the walls.[^upper]

Flooded pits connect to a lower passage and a cavern with pockets of air. On a ledge, a kuo-toa ritualist tends a carving marked by black ooze. His conch-tipped staff draws the water into a dangerous whirlpool. The Delvers overcome him without killing him and bind him for transport. He speaks of a god sleeping in the Dunes, but the being's identity and the shrine's purpose remain uncertain.[^shrine]

The carving conceals another object. Guided by the captive's wand, Evelyn draws a geared quarter-disc from a narrow opening through the stone. It is a fragment of something larger, with a missing gear and no demonstrated complete function.[^disc]

Below, a lateral branch opens into the remains of a furnished market. Archways, baskets, woven floor coverings, benches and homes preserve traces of an older community. A floating, beaked predator feeds on stirges among the abandoned spaces. The Delvers kill it, and Krandal retrieves a fog-emitting bottle from a fountain shaped like a hook horror's face. Fresh water flows there despite the black residue around its basin.[^market]

A farther cavern holds stone plinths and a monumental closed gate beneath a carved face with bared teeth. Koh and Evelyn turn back before opening it. With their underwater breathing nearly spent, the group returns to the surface with the living captive and recovered objects, leaving much of the lower complex unexplored.[^return]

## Current status

The last explored market route is passable, but its gate remains unopened and its lowest passages uncharted. The ritualist has been removed alive, and the geared fragment, ring, wand and fog bottle have been carried away. The ruin's later occupants, structural condition and droning depths remain unknown.[^status]
