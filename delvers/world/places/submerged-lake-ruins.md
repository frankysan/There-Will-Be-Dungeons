# The submerged ruins of the eastern lake

## Introduction

Beneath a calm freshwater lake east of the guild's city, the columns of an old stone complex barely break the surface. Moss, algae and drifting aquatic plants have covered its walls. The ruin is one of several ancient works whose passages repeat a disturbing pattern of hidden rooms, weighted mechanisms and lethal traps. Whether the same forgotten builders made them all, and why they chose such places, remains a mystery.[^setting]

## Familiar passages underwater

The Darkest Delvers enter from the water after gaining permission from a lakeside trader and drinking potions that permit them to breathe beneath it. The familiar walls turn around the same corners they remember from the forest and the ruined stair beneath Pelor's temple. Spear traps stand extended under the weight of water; thousands of little darts hang above them. Under thick algae they find a concealed mechanism that opens a side room. Here, in a stone chest, lies a ring made of the ancient stone-metal alloy with inscriptions Evelyn recognizes as very old Elven. Koh awakens it through his mind and learns its protective power. He offers it to Krandal, whose finger it grows to fit.[^ring]

Other rune-marked shafts remain upon the walls; the party chooses not to carry them away while still exploring. Two open pits descend into flooded darkness, connecting into a deeper passage. Below the old masonry they find rough rock and a cavern in which an air-filled pocket glows with burning wood. There a fish-like ritualist kneels before the familiar carved signs, dark sludge seeping around the old marks, and uses a shell-tipped staff to stir the water into a violent whirlpool.[^below]

## The devotee of the Dunes

The creature tries to trap Koh in the turning water. Koh anchors an enchanted sash against the cavern ceiling, Krandal orders the ritualist to the ground, and Evelyn throws herself along the spinning current to strike without taking his life. When the magic breaks, the stranger can speak with them. He tells of a god sleeping in the **Dunes** and his desire to make contact again. The staff appears to be his own rough construction rather than an artifact of the ruin. The Delvers remove the staff and wand and bind the devotee with the sash, choosing to consult his village rather than execute him.[^ritual]

## Current status

**One Ring of Protection** has been discovered and is **worn by Krandal**. The **six ancient spear shafts remain in the side chamber**. The **aquatic mimic near the site survives**, and the **deeper black pit and its full extent remain unexplored**. The fish ritualist is **alive and bound on Krandal's back**, and neither the party nor its captive has reached the lake surface by the episode cutoff. The claim of a sleeping god in the Dunes is the ritualist's belief or testimony, **not established historical or divine fact**; its relationship to other dark-dune reports remains unknown.[^status]
