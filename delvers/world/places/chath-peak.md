# Chath Peak — red-dragon mountain

## Introduction

Chath Peak rises above steep red-rock trails and sparse mountain growth. Waterfalls and lava channels break the ascent, while kobold tracks lead toward a settlement beneath the summit. A red dragon has made the mountain its hunting ground and lair, drawing a guild expedition led by the experienced slayer Marsh.[^approach]

### Arc Two — A dragon's stronghold and an older defense

The approach offers little protection from the air. Behind a waterfall, a narrow ledge overlooks the basin where kobolds gather water and the dragon drinks. The creature's size alarms Marsh, who judges it far older than the guild expected. Its fire reaches the travelers even as they try to pass unseen.[^falls]

Higher up, the kobold shaman Skoogie describes a community whose allegiance the newcomer has taken from him. Living kobolds are carried away as offerings, and the dragon feeds on them. Skoogie guides the Delvers through a concealed volcanic passage, hoping the dragon's death will return the village to his authority.[^kobolds]

An obsidian chamber preserves machinery attributed to Radiant Lance. Its forge accepts gold beneath an inscription demanding freedom from greed, and a handprint control produces a dragon-slaying sword for Evelyn. A miniature of the summit lair identifies controls for the lava, gate and flying dragon. A lift carries the group from this hidden workshop into the stronghold above.[^forge]

The lair surrounds a lava moat with bridges, ramps and an elevated perch. Treasure and an abandoned wagon lie within. Koh's lava control releases cooling water, filling the chamber with obscuring steam. The remaining controls seal the escape and force the dragon down, turning the architecture against its occupant. Marsh's slaying arrow finishes Fredoroth, and the party carries treasure down the mountain. Skoogie leaves alive for his village.[^defeat]

The recovered skull later stands at the guild for a portrait and appears among the Bronze Ball's auction displays. Part of the mountain's defeated threat becomes an object of public celebration and fundraising in Atomer.[^trophy]

## Current status

Fredoroth is dead and the expedition has left with treasure and the skull. The lair's lava was cooled and its defenses used, but the mountain's later condition, the forge's continued operation and Skoogie's authority over the kobolds remain unknown.[^status]
