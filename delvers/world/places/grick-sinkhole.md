# Grick-infested sinkhole — ruins and buried dungeon

## Overview

In the forest outside the guild's city, worn stone arches lead down into a limestone sinkhole where the remains of an older structure lie surrounded by woodland. Broken waist-high walls stand around a weathered altar, whose narrow opening carries a strange droning sound from beneath the stone. This apparently abandoned place conceals a carefully constructed passage, lined with symbols and deadly devices. The surrounding woods also shelter hippogriffs and a reclusive guardian known as [Veggie Sticks](../characters/veggie-sticks.md).[^site]

The party reached the ruins while investigating reported gricks. The creatures appeared in numbers far greater than expected; several had already been injured by barbs or blades, and the lower chambers contained bodies impaled on ancient traps. The ruin proved less an empty lair than a dangerous threshold between forest and a worked-stone dungeon.

## A difficult escape

Koh, Evelyn and Krandal discovered that direct combat against the gricks was costly. Their scales resisted ordinary strikes, and their limbs and beaks inflicted severe wounds. An initial skirmish left the company trapped beside the altar, an inscribed stone tablet and a way up too sheer to climb unaided. Koh's enchanted purple climbing sash offered a route out. Evelyn defended the retreat while Krandal distracted one hungry creature inadvertently: after tearing open his backpack, the grick mistook falling vestments, candles and rations for the cleric's entrails.[^escape]

They reached the rim only through a succession of hurried climbs and arrow shots. One grick died while pursuing them upward. Another emerged from the brush and knocked Krandal unconscious, dragging him away until Koh's arrow brought it down. Evelyn returned to revive him, and the three made their way back toward the path. The heavy tablet remained behind in the ruins.

## The passage under the stone

After resting in Veggie Sticks's hollow-stump home, the adventurers returned and found the last known living grick hidden near the entrance to the crafted passage. Krandal killed it, ending the visible immediate threat. Behind it stretched a stairway cut into the limestone. A wall-speared grick corpse and another pierced by needles demonstrated what the party had escaped: the dungeon itself was injuring, and sometimes killing, its intruders.

One corridor held rising needles concealed in small floor openings; buttons activated a delayed mechanism. Another stretch contained pits that could be closed by further controls. Koh tested the floor with a severed grick tail and later a shield and climbing rope, an experiment that left him pierced and poisoned. Evelyn found a way to cross during a pause in the mechanism, then reached an abandoned armory-like chamber with rusted weapon fittings and a heavy stone chest.[^traps]

Inside she found an inscribed [sword hilt without a blade](../items/runic-sword-hilt.md). The party left the larger stone tablet, its Elvish writing and the repeating wall glyphs untranslated. They had seen only a portion of the buried structure, not its builder, purpose or full depths.

## Current status

The explorers returned to the guild with a grick tentacle and a tracing of the symbols. Cassius accepted their scouting report and agreed to submit their claim to investigate the dungeon themselves, while reserving the guild's right to send stronger adventurers if further attacks became urgent. The ancient doors, traps and inscriptions remain unsolved; the site has not been cleared. Veggie Sticks's woodland and hippogriffs remain nearby.[^status]
