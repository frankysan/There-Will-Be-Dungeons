# The southwestern well — a neighborhood water source

## Introduction

The southwestern well serves a busy neighborhood of the guild's city, set among workshops, houses and grassy ground where families gather. Its bucket hangs beneath a modest red roof, drawing water from the ground on which the residents depend. The blacksmith's open forge stands close by, with a warehouse and shops beyond it. Under warm skies it is an easy place to believe that the dangers of the Underdark remain far away.[^setting]

## The day of the patrol

The guild sends the Darkest Delvers to search the well because monsters have been found in the surrounding quarter. Krandal notices a faint rust-colored trail from the bucket toward the blacksmith's forge. Koh follows it into the shop and finds a giant insect with the power to ruin metal. A fourth barrel in a nearby delivery turns into a mimic and bites a warehouse worker, while a blood-hungry stirge is discovered attached to the elderly dog Bruno. All three creatures are defeated by the party, and the wounded worker and animal receive healing.[^encounters]

For the residents, this is both a rescue and a cause for anxiety. A child proud to know the word *peril* raises the alarm. An elder, Shmorth, begins to suspect people of hiding monstrous forms and smears strange blood upon a door in hopes of keeping monsters out. Krandal and Koh remind the neighbors that the blacksmith is an ordinary man, not a creature disguised as one. Yet even after the fighting ceases, no one has actually seen beneath the water.[^people]

## The breach below

The Delvers return with a sack of concrete as Brigand Patrol watches the square. Koh lowers himself into the well on his enchanted climbing sash and finds, far above the water, a gap where stones have been driven out of the masonry from the cave beyond. His companions climb down after him, and the three follow twisting wet tunnels through small rust monsters, stirges and a curious stone eater who becomes Krandal's friend. Farther along they reach ancient worked passages and, with another guild party, discover a hidden way up into Pelor's temple. Having found a route by which subterranean creatures could approach the well, both teams return to narrow sections of the passages and use rock and concrete to close the connections.[^return]

## Current status

The **well's damaged side passage has been explored and the accessible connecting route sealed with rocks and concrete**, with the First Sons Brotherhood sealing another approach. The original rust monster, mimic and stirge were defeated, along with young rust monsters and stirges farther underground. Nevertheless, **the actual drinking water has not been tested or pronounced safe on sufficient evidence**, and parts of the wider caves remain open and unexplored. Koh's enchanted climbing sash is still secured at the well and retrieval has been discussed, not shown completed. The immediate opening to the larger underground system is blocked, but the temple entrance and deeper caverns present further concerns.[^status]
