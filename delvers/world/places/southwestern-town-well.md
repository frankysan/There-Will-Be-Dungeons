# The southwestern well — a neighborhood water source

## Introduction

The southwestern well supplies a neighborhood of Atomer where houses, workshops and shops surround a grassy square. A small red roof shelters its bucket and frame; nearby stand an open forge and a warehouse. Families gather around this ordinary source of drinking water, close to the trades on which their daily lives depend.[^setting]

## Development

<a id="the-day-of-the-patrol"></a>

### Arc One — A neighborhood exposed to the underground

The square's apparent safety is broken by creatures among its businesses and homes. A rust trail leads from the bucket toward the forge, where the Delvers find a rust monster. A delivery barrel proves to be a mimic, while a stirge feeds on a local boy's dog, Bruno. The creatures are killed and the wounded worker and dog healed, but the disturbance leaves neighbors suspicious of one another. The elder Shmorth smears monster remains on a door as a warning, despite the Delvers' assurance that the blacksmith is an ordinary man.[^neighbors]

<a id="the-breach-below"></a>

Brigand Patrol guards the square while the Delvers investigate beneath the roof. Far above the waterline, displaced stones expose a side opening from natural caves into the well shaft. Wet, cramped passages lead past mineral seams, young rust monsters and stirges toward older worked stone. A friendly rock-eating creature lives along the route. Beyond it, the Delvers meet the First Sons Brotherhood and reach a stair that opens inside [Pelor's temple](temple-of-pelor.md).[^breach]

The discovery changes the well from a suspected source of monsters into a known point of access from the caves. Both teams block their accessible approaches with rock and concrete. Koh later returns to the square to recover his climbing sash, and residents are told the danger is over.[^closure]

## Current status

The explored routes between the larger cave system and the well are sealed. The surrounding caves have not been cleared in full, and the drinking water's safety remains untested. No later inspection establishes a changed condition for this particular well.[^status]
