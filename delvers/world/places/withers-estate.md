# Withers Estate — the financial-district mansion

## Introduction

The Withers Estate stands among Atomer's grand financial houses, its heavy limestone walls and high columns rising above a formal park. Statues of renowned heroes surround the mansion's upper edges, and barred windows suggest the expense and security of the treasures inside. The estate was once a financial building, and Alexander (Alexandre) Vanderbuck Withers, an important supporter of the Delvers Dungeon Guild, now occupies its old rooms. Its celebrated collection of jade is spoken of among thieves and wealthy patrons, though the full contents of its museum are not yet seen by the Delvers.[^estate]

## A chamber below the paving

When the city's bronze guild companies assemble to intercept a **bulette** tunnelling toward the financial quarter, the Darkest Delvers take their station by a fountain and obelisk close to Withers's front steps. The monster tears up cobblestones, uproots trees and strikes through the park before being killed. Where it has passed beside the house, the earth gives way in an opening scarcely three feet across. Koh lowers a glowing globe and climbs down, steadied by a sash that Evelyn holds above. Beneath the broken ground he finds an unexpectedly clean chamber of dressed stone, with traces of recently eaten food and a long slab fitted with old leather restraints. The locked door on the estate side cannot be opened from the room.[^room]

Withers himself comes outside in his evening clothes, disturbed by the damage. He says he knows little of his home's numerous older rooms, and promises to have the matter looked into. Koh cannot reconcile that claim with the evidence of recent use, though he has not seen anyone entering or leaving the room. The three Delvers later make a direct report to Cassius. His private suspicions of Withers, already developing from the recovery of Skull Keep and other artifact dealings, make him interested in further evidence without proving anything about the owner of the mansion.[^owner]

## The proposed entry

Koh's former associate Chuck Abnaz reveals that the Cloven Brotherhood is planning to exploit the opening before Withers can close it. Chuck describes a rich jade collection, recruited burglar Shrugs and centaur driver Allspice, and promises Koh payment if his psychic ability can coordinate the team. The financier behind the scheme calls himself **Suis Sac**, a name Koh recognizes as **Cassius** reversed. At a private meeting the guildmaster confirms he has indeed engaged the Brotherhood for an unofficial investigation. He wants the Delvers to examine the estate's artifacts and Withers's motives, without drawing the guild into a public burglary scandal. He supplies disguises, temporary magical footwear and instructions for a meeting at **9:30 that evening**, when the lamps by the new entrance should be extinguished.[^plans]

## Current status

**The estate remains occupied by Withers and has not been infiltrated during S2E03.** The **bulette was killed** and its digging **actually exposed a 15-by-25-foot underground room** with a **10-by-5-foot stone slab, leather restraint straps and fresh refuse**; **Koh entered, inspected and left**, but **the locked estate-facing door remains unopened**. Claims of a vast jade/artifact collection and questionable uses of Skull Keep are part of **Chuck's intelligence and Cassius's suspicions**, not inventories or proved wrongdoing. **Cassius's participation in financing the covert operation is personally confirmed**; the planned burglary has not begun, the contents of the mansion have not been recovered, and neither Withers's guilt nor the fate of the jewels is established.[^cutoff]
