# Withers Estate — the financial-district mansion

## Introduction

Withers Estate occupies a grand limestone mansion in Atomer's financial quarter. Tall columns rise beside a formal park with a fountain and obelisk, while heroic statues and barred windows give the house an air of wealth and security. [Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers](../characters/alexandre-vanderbuck-withers.md) lives among its older rooms and celebrated jade collection, describing the building as a former financial house.[^estate-introduction]

### Arc Two — Hidden rooms, stolen wealth and fire

A bulette's passage damages the grounds and exposes a narrow opening near the front steps. Below it lies a clean stone chamber containing a restraint-fitted slab and recent food scraps, with a locked door leading toward the house. Withers professes ignorance of the room. Its recent use leaves the purpose unsettled, and the opening gives a covert investigation backed by Cassius a way into the estate.[^estate-breach]

The lower house preserves another history beneath its wine stores: old prison cells, scratched names and a shrouded kuo-toa corpse. Beyond them, speaking elementals maintain heat, water, ventilation and a moving stone lift. Fern, Dewey, Gale and Slate call themselves brothers. Their keys and puzzles regulate movement through this service network; the intruders fight three until they yield, while Slate's challenge is answered without combat.[^estate-services]

The burglary breaches both collection and security. Portable jade works leave the upper museum, while larger sculptures remain. A servant raises an alarm after glimpsing Shrugs carrying coats. The lower vault is blasted open and its gems and smaller relics removed; a conch-topped staff and bell-covered trousers are damaged before being taken. The mansion's defenses have been overcome, but its rooms have not yielded a complete explanation of their owner's dealings.[^estate-burglary]

After the intruders escape, the estate is seen ablaze. Cassius later attributes extensive burning to Fern, and Koh acknowledges releasing and encouraging the fire elemental. Cassius also reports that Withers has listed stolen property and hired the Broscale family to investigate. The house becomes a damaged source of evidence as well as a victim of theft.[^estate-fire]

### Arc Three — Within the changed financial quarter

After the Bronze Ball, guild patrols cover the damaged financial quarter, and the district is later enclosed as Atomer becomes a center for Silver adventurers. The surrounding area changes its use and control, without establishing how the mansion itself has been repaired or occupied.[^estate-district]

## Current status

The mansion was burglarized and burned; no later detailed inspection establishes its repairs, surviving contents or current occupation. Its former service guardians survived their encounters with the burglars, but their fate after the fire is unknown. The recent use of the restraint chamber and the extent of the owner's knowledge remain unresolved.[^estate-status]
