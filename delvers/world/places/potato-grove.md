# Potato Grove — Krandal's riverside tower

## Introduction

Beside the river north of Atomer, not far from the ruined Noble Hollow, lies a neglected watchtower with a small holding of arable land. The stone walls rise only about twenty feet, with the top torn open to the weather; broken masonry lies in the grass at their foot. A wooden cottage has lost its roof and much of its floor, and its stove has rusted almost beyond recognition. Two small plots of soil wait beneath weeds, watered once by a narrow irrigation ditch running from the river. The place was given to Krandal Splenderbelt as a reward for saving guild members and the city, a beginning for his much-discussed potato farm rather than an established estate.[^place]

## The inheritance

The guild first promises Krandal an old tower but cannot give him its deeds at once. When the Darkest Delvers return from Snake Island after a baffling two-week absence, Helenar Clearstone places the actual deed and a schedule of repair prices in his hands. He visits the land the next afternoon with Koh and Evelyn. They find the ruined cottage empty, though its old stove and fallen timbers suggest former habitation. The river flows fast between narrower banks here, and the tower appears once to have guarded trade or river crossings.[^deed]

Before anyone begins repairs, an old cow skull decorating a scarecrow reveals an occupant's claim. A giant skeleton of a minotaur rises from the earth and takes the skull for its head, using the scarecrow's crossbeam as a cudgel. In the stone tower waits a hideously sodden sea hag. Her magic draws fear out of those who face her; Krandal sees a version of himself deprived of Pelor's light and collapses after his terror takes shape. Evelyn's healing brings him back to his feet. Koh tears the minotaur's head free with his psychic blade and venom dagger, and the hag is finally destroyed when Evelyn's hammer and Krandal's spectral mace strike as she flees.[^fight]

## The stubborn skull

The minotaur's body falls apart, but its skull speaks on. Krandal alone understands the creature's words. It protests the idea that anyone else should command the land and offers a more useful trade than the party expects: it might keep birds away from the potato rows. Krandal names the holding **Potato Grove**, puts down his weapons and buries the talkative skull for the time being. He has not yet accepted a binding agreement or tested its suitability as a guardian. The defeated sea hag leaves a cauldron containing a revolting mixture of food and spell ingredients, but the party does not formally take it away.[^skull]

## The first planting

The neglected farm has become Krandal's refuge whenever guild work permits. After the city survives a land-shark attack, he returns with his own tools to the banks of the river, pulls debris from the old ditch and gathers rotten roofing into a pile. He cuts ordinary potatoes so each piece bears an eye, then plants the pieces in the waiting soil. The tower and cottage still need work, but the seeds of an actual harvest now lie beneath the earth.

The skull he buried after the sea hag's defeat calls for help. He digs it out and, with great care, builds a crossbar from discarded planks and drapes an old jacket over it. Set above the fields, the talking minotaur skull can watch for birds and call out. It asks Krandal for a small rock to spit at trespassers; he grants the strange request, but no such attack has yet been witnessed. Krandal experiments with the name **Spud Buds**, noting that he has not decided on a final title for the estate. His willingness to improvise a living scarecrow is greater than his willingness to spend thousands on masonry, and for now the potatoes come first.[^s2e03]

## Current status (through S2E06)

Krandal **has the deed**, has **actually planted potato pieces and cleared a ditch**, but the cottage and tower are **still ruins**, not funded construction projects. His **animated minotaur skull remains installed as a scarecrow** and **reports a wet-eyed fishman watching from waterside shrubbery** in S2E06; the fishman's identity and motives are unknown. Following S2E05's payoff, Krandal **actually hides eight kinds of gemstones in the empty wall space behind a loose tower brick**, discovered beside the sea hag's old possessions, and **much of his large gold haul under a boulder east of the fields, in the skull's view**. He later asks the skull to watch while he goes to Gemholm. **No theft, harvest, paid rebuilding, complete restoration of minotaur body or defensive encounter at farm observed**. ## Earlier status (at S2E03 cutoff)

**Krandal still holds the deed** to the riverside farm once called **Potato Grove**; he now provisionally calls it **Spud Buds**, **not a confirmed permanent rename**. He has **actually planted cut potato pieces**, **cleared/partly restored the old irrigation channel** and **begun self-directed debris clearance**. The **talking minotaur skull has been dug up and placed on a newly made scarecrow pole**, dressed in jacket, with **a rock placed in its mouth**; its effectiveness as a bird deterrent has not been tested. The earlier **sea hag remains defeated**, original minotaur body destroyed. **Neither wooden cottage nor stone tower has undergone an actual full contracted restoration**—GM quotes **3,000 gp** and **10,000 gp** respectively, both **unpaid**. **No mature crops or harvest** exists yet. The hag's old cauldron was observed earlier but is not confirmed taken.[^s2e03]
