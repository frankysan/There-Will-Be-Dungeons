# Potato Grove — Krandal's riverside tower

## Introduction

North of Atomer, beside a fast-running river near [Noble Hollow](noble-hollow.md), a ruined watchtower stands above two weed-choked fields. Its broken stone walls and a roofless wooden cottage overlook an overgrown irrigation ditch. Granted to [Krandal Splenderbelt](../characters/krandal-splenderbelt.md) for his service to the guild, this neglected holding offers room for the potato farm he hopes to build.[^grant]

### Arc Two — Ruins made into a working holding

The property is occupied when the Delvers first inspect it. A sea hag shelters in the tower, and a skeletal minotaur takes the cow skull from a scarecrow for its head. The hag is killed and the skeleton's body falls apart, but its skull continues to speak. Krandal later places it on a new scarecrow overlooking the fields, turning a defeated claimant into the farm's watchful guardian.[^occupants]

He clears debris, opens the muddy irrigation channel and plants potato pieces. Building repairs remain expensive, so the tower stays open to the weather and he sleeps beneath its remaining stairs. The land begins producing through his labor before it has a comfortable farmhouse. Hidden recesses and a nearby boulder also serve as stores for his adventuring wealth.[^planting]

Gleepo Ma, a displaced kuo-toa, finds work here after asking Krandal for shelter. The river gives him a place to bathe and sleep, and his appetite for insects and small vermin serves the crops. A successful trial becomes a permanent arrangement for food and lodging. The skull, called Chad, gains a pointing hand as well as its place above the rows.[^gardener]

### Arc Three — Food, workers and a shared home

The farm grows into a mixed household. Nopa Ropa joins the irrigation work while seeking help for Hissia; Muck Muck takes on pest control, and the donkey Sean supplies animal power. Krandal accepts a plow and agricultural book from the Goodwines, but does not accept their proposed investment. After poor early results, better sprouting methods and a new barn improve the holding. Built with serpent-head gables, the barn provides space for seed potatoes and tools while the tower remains ruined.[^barn]

The farm is also vulnerable. A mummified attacker damages plants and tries to kill Krandal, claiming a promised reward; its defeat does not reveal who sent it. Later, an egg stored in the barn hatches into the hook horror Scroungy. What first seems another troubling presence becomes a young creature imprinted on the farmer.[^dangers]

A cart in Atomer sells the farm's potatoes at modest prices during rising inflation. Krandal explains the work as service and affordable nourishment rather than a drive for the greatest profit. Yet the workers go unpaid in coin for a time. A later shipment sale to the Goodwines brings money to the farm, and Krandal pays his three living employees. The sale concerns potatoes, while the holding stays his.[^economy]

The old collapse at Noble Hollow gives Krandal a route to the minotaurs' bone crypt, where he secures allies for the final expedition. After Khalazar's defeat, the curse ends and the neighboring land becomes fertile. Chad Brad and the other skeletal minotaurs find final rest as bones.[^release]

During the following year, potato production expands, debts are paid and new workers join the household. Scroungy becomes a tame beast of burden. The tower is finally rebuilt as the Delvers' shared home, overlooking fields worked by familiar hands and unusual helpers.[^home]

## Current status

The thriving farm supports Krandal's livelihood and occasional healing work for his neighbors. Its restored tower is the Delvers' headquarters. Within it, keepsakes of their lives share a table with a wedding invitation for Evelyn and a sealed red letter from the Kingdom; the letter's contents and what follows the invitation remain unknown.[^ending]
