# Hooper Estate — a wedding in the western woods

## Introduction

Hooper Estate is a wealthy halfling household on Atomer's wooded southwestern outskirts. A curved drive leads to a stone mansion whose low windows, stairs and furnishings suit its residents. Lawns, a pavilion, an ornamental lake and a family burial plot spread into the surrounding woods, giving a wedding room for both public celebration and private unease.[^grounds]

### Arc Two — A wedding and a place in the family graveyard

Limwick Hooper's marriage to Fresna Goodwine fills the grounds with guests, servants, wine and cheese. Children ride mastiffs, while a traditional cornucopia sends coins and jewels into the woods. The planned release of white birds fails when the birds are found dead; Evelyn conceals them and uses a magical goat to distract the guests. The ceremony and feast continue.[^wedding]

The woods conceal another disturbance. The family plot has no grave for Grandfather Aurelli Hooper. In a private, enchanted conversation, Mr. Goodwine admits that he and others beat and hanged Aurelli during the trading wars, leaving him unburied. The old rivalry intrudes upon the union of their descendants when the grandfather appears as a sword-bearing wight suspended from an animated oak.[^history]

Krandal breaks the noose's connection to the walking tree, stopping it, and Daufante brings down the wight. The Deck of Many Things is recovered from the body. Goodwine and Connor then bury Aurelli in the family plot, giving him the place among his relatives that he had been denied. The wedding carries on without public exposure of the murder or the fight.[^burial]

## Current status

The wedding is complete and Aurelli's remains are buried in the family graveyard. The immediate haunting is ended, but the cause of the estate's other strange signs and the permanence of the grandfather's rest remain uncertain. No later inspection establishes a changed condition for the house or grounds.[^status]
