# Broscale Manor — Wildfire's dragonborn household in Atomer

<a id="exterior-entry-and-sitting-room"></a>

## Introduction

Broscale Manor is the imposing black-stone home of [Buck Broscale, called Wildfire](../characters/buck-broscale-wildfire.md), on Atomer's prosperous outer estates. Well-kept grounds, a self-closing gate and an ornate dragon-face door knocker mark its approach. Red carpets, dark wood and stone corridors give way to a bright sitting room furnished for guests and lined with unusual trophies.[^manor-introduction]

### Arc Two — A family home and a place of assessment

The manor combines a veteran adventurer's display with an active household. Glass cases contain preserved eyes, an ape's fist and a chain emblem; a behir head hangs above the dining mantel. Wildfire attributes the head to his ancestors' exploits. His red drake Bella appears at the doorway, and a housekeeper sends her upstairs to find him.[^manor-rooms]

Valentine receives guests with baby Wall Nibbler, while Climber, Basher and Dreamer bring their pets, toys and books into family life. Godwin Milner handles invitations and serves the meal. The Delvers dine with them before the family withdraws and Wildfire turns to guild business. He reviews their reports, poses ethical dilemmas and gives his recommendation for Silver promotion. The house serves both as a private home and a setting for judging public responsibility.[^manor-household]

A quieter exchange at departure connects the household to the investigation of Withers. Speaking only to Evelyn, Wildfire says he knows about her entry into the basement and defends the financier's support of the guild. The other guests leave without that private conversation becoming a public accusation.[^manor-warning]

### Arc Three — The family estate and Cassius's release

While Wildfire is missing after the Ball, the household becomes involved in the guild's crisis. Cassius is found confined at the Wildfire family estate after a reporter's lead and Zoltar's confirmation. Valentine brings him out alive and asks the Delvers to recover her husband. How that confinement relates to the manor's familiar rooms, and who ordered it, remain unclear.[^manor-release]

## Current status

The manor's last described interior is the inhabited family home where the assessment dinner takes place. Cassius's later confinement at the family estate ends with his release. Wildfire is subsequently rescued to the guild, but no return to the manor or later change to its household, rooms or use is known.[^manor-status]
