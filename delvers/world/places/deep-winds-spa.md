# Deep Winds Spa — flumph hospitality beneath Atomer

## Introduction

Deep Winds Spa is a flumph-run bathhouse beneath Atomer, reached through the tunnels beyond Gruber Lifton's lift. Purple fungus-moss, running water and violet lamps mark its manicured entrance. Within, adventurers and creatures that might fight elsewhere share a place of bathing and rest under the hosts' rules of neutral hospitality.[^arrival]

### Arc Three — Hospitality, threatened water and paid refuge

The spa has changing rooms, a cool common pool, hot springs and private bathing or massage suites, supported by kitchens, laundry and filtration machinery. Staff offer towels and drinking water held in membranous orbs. Surface visitors are expected to respect the house customs and leave weapons outside the baths. Destroying Angel presents a gift of surface air while seeking lodging for future guild expeditions.[^customs]

The mixed clientele makes the bathhouse a rare meeting place. Skeletal minotaurs, elementals, trolls and other subterranean guests bathe alongside visitors from above. Private rooms also shelter a powerful three-eyed mind-reader. He draws on Evelyn's memories of the Traveler and probes Krandal's missing past, offering claims about curses and captives that remain his own account.[^guests]

The hospitality depends on less peaceful work behind the walls. Black sand accumulates in the filters, and the headmistress asks the visitors to clear the machinery. Light drives out a dark mass concealing a clawed undead-looking creature. It envelops Krandal, pins Vapo against a wall and races into the public baths. Koh's illuminated Drift Globe bursts the enclosing ooze and frees Krandal; he then kills the exposed pursuer. Vapo survives, and the inspected filter slats are clean.[^filters]

Afterward, Koh meets the private guest, an aboleth, and contests its mind. Its visions and ambitions reach well beyond the spa, but it observes the bathhouse's neutrality. The Delvers spend the night in comfortable rooms. By morning, the headmistress offers other adventurers accommodation for a price.[^firststay]

The party returns after its ordeal in the Black Basilica. This time the receptionist asks a thousand gold per person for a room, meal and night, invoking supply and demand. The three accept the charge and complete another stay before leaving for the right-hand catacomb route.[^return]

## Current status

The spa remains an operating refuge offering paid hospitality at its last known departure. Its immediate filtration threat has been removed, but no dedicated Guild encampment or permanent guarantee of safe passage has been established. Its condition after Khalazar's defeat is unknown.[^status]
