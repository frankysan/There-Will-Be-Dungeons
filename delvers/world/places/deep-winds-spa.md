# Deep Winds Spa — flumph hospitality beneath Atomer

Deep Winds Spa is a **manicured underground bathhouse operated by flumphs**, reached by descending **Gruber Lifton's lift toward dungeon level E** from Atomer's ruined financial district and following Guild-supplied directions. It is **neutral ground**: adventurers and monsters bathe together, even when their species clash outside. The Guild wants to use the colony to support an **overnight expedition base**, because its sunlight-mirror channels fail after sunset, making deep raids hard to sustain. **The desired base does not exist in the played episode**.[^arrival]

## First arrival and etiquette

The entrance has **purple fungus-moss carpet, shallow running water, violet lamps and a broad stone door with a flumph motif**. Undercommon signage identifies Deep Winds. A nervous telepathic flumph receptionist called **Poof** greets visitors; **Mistress Poo/Pooh/Hoo (?)**, recognizable by her flamboyant bow and hookah, oversees the operation. Exact headmistress spelling is ASR-provisional. Flumph staff include **Squeaker**, **Toot**, **Vapo**, **Flotus/Flatus (?)** and others whose names deserve listening review.

Visitors pass changing rooms and are offered **towels** and **water suspended in a membranous orb**, pierced for drinking. There is a large cool common pool and separate hot-spring sections, plus private baths/massage suites, kitchens, laundry and a **filtration room**. Angel notes that divided bathing rooms and changing facilities are accommodations to surface customers, not universal flumph customs; violent or insulting behavior is discouraged. **Mistress Poo accepts Destroying Angel's jar of surface air** and agrees to discuss the Guild's request further if the visitors first respect hospitality. **Krandal tries bringing his face maces to the bath but returns them to the changing room when staff objects**.[^manners]

## The guests and their private revelations

The baths host **skeletal minotaurs**, a **roper**, **grell**, **trolls**, **deep gnomes**, treasure-chest-like creatures with mouths, a **water elemental**, **gibbering mouther**, an **earth elemental** receiving a wash, a **beholder**, and a **weeping elf ghost**. Their apparent peaceful cohabitation is a feature of the spa, **not evidence that these creatures are harmless elsewhere**. A beholder actually **uses a sleep ray against Krandal** when the cleric becomes anxious in the male baths, but does not otherwise provoke a melee.[^guests]

One large private bath contains an **unnamed-in-reliable-writing three-eyed, catfish-faced, long-tentacled aquatic telepath**, addressed with ASR spellings approximating **Utharamat / Rutharamat / Utharimad**. He has the ability to read memories directly, first delving into **Evelyn's** private connection to Gentleman Cavalier Traveler and then **Krandal's** childhood, farm and missing Snake Island memories. He **claims** that the Traveler remains in astral space until **Khalazar is killed**; **claims** to know Princess Hissia's hiding place but **does not divulge it**. His remark about Hissia being “dead” is immediately **corrected as a figure of speech**, not a death declaration. A sound of drums stops his attempt to enter Snake Island memories. He also requests to speak to Koh. At episode cutoff, a **door telepathically invites Koh in**, but **its speaker is not positively identified and Koh does not enter on-screen**. The guest's **monster species is never named in dialogue** and must not be inferred as certified.[^mind]

## The filtration-room crisis

Mistress Poo explains that visiting **water elementals have brought black sand from below**, which apparently accumulates in the hot-spring filtration equipment and frightens attendants. She promises **future hospitality for Guild adventurers if the Delvers clear the problem**, but **does not sign/complete a permanent base agreement on-screen**. The four heroes rearm and visit the neglected equipment area with Vapo. **Evelyn's Daylight causes a black sandy ooze to rush out of the filtration slats**, revealing a thin, clawed undead-looking being. It traps Krandal in an enveloping ball and races through the facility, injuring him with necrotic magic; **Koh uses the Daylight ability of his Drift Globe to burst the ooze and free Krandal**, then **kills the clawed creature** before it enters the guests' pool. Angel returns to check the slats, which appear **clean at that moment**, and frees **Vapo alive** from a wall where the ooze had pinned him. Krandal accepts a flumph-assisted cleaning/massage, **not magically restored health**.[^crisis]

**Status through S3E05:** Spa remains inhabited/operating after the threat's death, and the immediate filters are clean by inspection. **No completed overnight Guild camp, written alliance, permanent safe route, or universal cure for black sand** is shown. Its origins and the connection (if any) to Khalazar remain under investigation.
