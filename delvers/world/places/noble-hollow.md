# Noble Hollow — the petrified tree

## Introduction

Noble Hollow lies north of Atomer beside the river leading toward the great lake. Pale, hardened ground surrounds twisted petrified trees whose holes and knots resemble faces caught in distress. Insects move over the dead forms, and a particularly great tree dominates the clearing. Guild accounts regard it as an ill-favored place of power, without explaining the origin of its condition.[^hollow-introduction]

### Arc One — A ritual clearing broken open

The clearing becomes a site of threatened sacrifice when [Marek Gideon](../characters/marek-gideon.md) brings Faelan there. A gear-edged stone supports the unconscious elf inside green flames, with attendants sustaining the rite and another captive bound nearby. According to Cheet, the site's power is part of a ceremony meant to call a beast from the lake. The place's use as ritual ground is witnessed; that larger purpose remains the participants' account.[^hollow-rite]

The intervention breaks the stone and extinguishes the rite, then tears open the setting itself. Tremors spread through the roots, the clearing collapses into a cavern and water pours into the exposed space. What had seemed solid ground conceals a great [roper](../creatures/noble-hollow-roper.md), with piercers emerging from its body and climbing the cavern walls. The roper is killed and the two captives are recovered alive, while piercers scatter. The broken opening remains the lasting physical consequence of the confrontation.[^hollow-collapse]

### Arc Two — A landmark beside new farmland

The collapsed Hollow lies close to the ruined riverside tower and plots granted to Krandal. As that neighboring property becomes a potato farm, the damaged site remains a landmark on the river rather than a restored clearing. The farm and the Hollow are connected parts of the landscape, but the grant does not establish ownership of the entire petrified tract.[^hollow-farm]

### Arc Three — A route below and land renewed

The old collapse gains another use when Krandal descends through it, guided by Chad Brad, and reaches the [Minotaur Mesa bone crypt](minotaur-mesa-bone-crypt.md). An earlier disaster has left an entrance into the underground burial place. This journey establishes a usable route, without mapping every adjoining chamber or a continuous connection to the Dawnless Dunes.[^hollow-route]

After Khalazar's defeat, the curse on Noble Hollow's land ends and the ground grows fertile over time. The recovery extends to Krandal's neighboring fields, supporting the farm's expansion during the following year. The tract's condition changes from corruption toward fertility, even though the fate of the broken clearing is less fully known.[^hollow-renewal]

## Current status

The cursed land has recovered its fertility. The clearing's last described structure is still the collapse used to enter the crypt; no later rebuilding or sealing is known. The old sacrifice was interrupted and the roper killed, but the surviving piercers' later fate and the great petrified tree's final condition remain unknown.[^hollow-status]
