# Dungeon Guild Academy — the Radiant Lance Theatre

## Introduction

Far from Atomer, the Dungeon Guild Academy gathers guildmasters and adventurers of every rank. Its busy halls are crowded with magical equipment, unusual mounts and animal companions. For local guilds, it is also a seat of authority: reinforcements, funding and oversight depend on officials who hear petitions at the Radiant Lance Theatre.[^arrive]

### Arc One — The theatre and the refused petition

The theatre descends in a circle around a low stone speaking platform. Old gilt tables and chairs face the petitioners, while three finance governors sit in an elevated enclosed box. An armed majordomo announces the speakers. Spectators come and go, answering requests with laughter, murmurs and applause. A plea for vampire patrols and news of a guild trapped in a time crystal sit alongside Atomer's emergency.[^theatre]

[Cassius](../characters/cassius.md) arrives with [Carrie Underworld](../characters/carrie-underworld.md), seeking gold-ranked champions to clear the passages beneath his city. Carrie's recovered horn, psychic shafts and other trophies become exhibits in an argument over whether Atomer deserves further help. The officials point to two researchers and silver guards already funded to investigate a forest ruin; Cassius complains that their work has left him without answers for almost two months.[^petition]

The hearing exposes a gulf between local danger and central confidence. Cassius questions the young Delvers chosen to meet the crisis, while an elderly diviner insists that the selecting commune cannot be mistaken. The board refuses more support, recommends a Withers-backed Bronze Ball as a source of money, and orders regional director Selenar Everbloom to inspect the Atomer franchise. Cassius and Carrie leave by coach with their request denied.[^decision]

The Academy also stands behind the guild's disciplinary machinery. After Noble Hollow, Marek Gideon's trial and an inquiry into his sponsorship are proposed there.[^discipline]

### Arc Two — Authority exercised away from the campus

Broadside later reports that Cheet has been reprimanded and assigned Academy clerical work.[^discipline]

The Delvers' silver assessment brings senior examiners to Atomer, near the governor's mansion. Selenar organizes their appearance before the panel, and the examinations establish titles and relative strengths after promotion has already been approved. Precise ordinal ranking is left for a later Academy assignment. This gathering extends the institution's reach without requiring the Delvers to visit its campus.[^assessment]

### Arc Three — The Silver City administration

After the Bronze Ball disaster, Zoltar occupies Cassius's office as acting guildmaster in Atomer. Academy authority is invoked to restrict access to Cassius, while the Delvers remain grounded. The new Silver City system organizes licensed expeditions beneath the damaged governor's quarter.[^administration]

In private conversation with a reporter, Zoltar says that the open dungeons bring the Academy gold and experience, whereas Cassius wants the danger ended quickly for the townspeople. His account makes the conflict of priorities explicit. Public opposition eventually forces him to relinquish local control; Cassius is physically released and returns to guild leadership.[^reckoning]

## Current status

The Academy remains the distant institutional centre last seen at the petition hearing. Cassius's return changes leadership in Atomer, but the campus's later condition, its governors' response and the outcomes of its promised proceedings remain unknown.[^status]
