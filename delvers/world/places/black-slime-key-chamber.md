# The black-slime three-key chamber — Delvers S3E07

**Played in [S3E07 — A Shirt Full of Mayo](../../episodes/S3E07.md)**. A separate cavern beyond **an OPEN bat-skull/wing gateway**, deeper than the previously found **side stairwell to triangle-gate catacombs**. Koh's **uncovered and glowing wife-blood/Khalazar-linked amulet actually leads here**. The prior [triangle gate](triangle-gate-catacombs.md) is a **DIFFERENT, still-sealed location**—three ornate keys in this room are not the three altarpieces needed there.

## Approach, appearance and occupants

The three grounded Delvers use an **ACTUALLY FORGED Gruber lift pass** to access dungeon level D, then follow Koh's now-uncapped directional light beyond the old catacomb staircase. Three prior puzzle rooms (shattered animated-statue debris, pressed glyph tiles, ancient Elvish levers) have been passed **already OPENED**, apparently solved by **[Goggles Granny](../characters/goggles-granny.md)**, an elderly catlike alchemist. The main chamber is **~240 feet across and 150 feet high**, with dripping **BLACK SLIME** obscuring its floor and broken staircases/bridges surrounding three elevated platforms. A flat center ledge displays three **six-inch ornate skeleton keys**: **grick**, **hook-horror** and **bat-faced “Necromantis”** emblems. Left platform has **teary face**; center has **face hiding its eyes and screaming**; right has **burning/fanged mouth consuming people**. An old **boat and pole** float in black slime. Krandal briefly sees an **unidentified spine-like body swimming**, never confirmed species/hostile motive or fought.

## Puzzle and observed solution

Ancient-Elvish inscription translated by Granny after Koh struggles is approximately:

> “Gricks escape the below. Necromantis hates the dawn. Horrors consume hope. Gricks consume all.”

**Punctuation and first line await original audio.** This is a **puzzle**, not reliable historical exposition about Khalazar or whole species. The party first selects keys/lever positions by guessing; several turns trigger lightning. Koh runs over railings using his **Sultan's Sash of Climbing**, Evelyn **uses Daylight, boat, and ultimately swims safely through goo**; Granny enlarges herself and throws **shrunken Krandal** to far ledge. Koh links guest/party using **Psychic Whispers**. **Krandal's Spiritual Weapon cannot manipulate key**. Observed successful light settings are **center screaming/covered-eye lever DOWN (dark)**; **right toothed/burning-mouth lever FULL UP (bright)**; **left crying-face lever MIDDLE (dim)**. On final middle-key turn **all THREE keys are ABSORBED and black slime DRAINS into corner basins**. Initial key/platform guesses may not be reliable final key mapping; preserve actual light settings, do not reconstruct missing mechanics as canonical.

## Recovered vessel and pressure-pedestal substitution

Beneath draining goo is a small floor altar with **ceramic/gold bat-head-capped jar**, beat of a **heart** increasingly audible as Evelyn approaches. She opens the cap and the beating stops. Krandal's **Medicine 18** recognizes **a completely dried and shrunken HEART**, not a living beating one; the **vessel with contents** weighs **25 pounds**. Koh detects probable pressure-activated mechanism beneath vessel (what it would do if set off **unobserved**). He **successfully holds plate** while Granny weighs vessel with her merchant balance. The table jokes about **Alchemy Jug mayonnaise poured in a shirt**, torches, candles and stones; **NO mayonnaise is actually made and NO shirt is used**. Evelyn supplies **~23-pound STONE**, Granny **2 pounds of weights**; the pair of **Koh and Granny** delicately transfers balanced objects to the plate and avoids triggering any trap. **Evelyn bags the closed jar and later actually PLACES it into one of three round altar slots back in the triangle-gate catacombs**, an exact fit. **Two remain missing**. No direct monster appears from the emptied basin and no unsealed doorway is shown in this episode.

**Status through S3E07:** This chamber's local three-key puzzle solved, goo largely drained; unique ceramic/gold heart jar **removed** and now located at the OTHER triangle-gate altar; guest Granny safely leaves before final placement. What heart represents, origin of black slime, unknown spine-shaped swimmer, remaining two altarpieces and whether pressure system would have triggered remain unresolved.
