# The black-slime three-key chamber

<a id="the-black-slime-three-key-chamber--delvers-s3e07"></a>

## Introduction

Beyond an open bat-wing and skull-decorated doorway lies a vast cavern filled with black slime. Broken stairways and bridges surround elevated platforms, while a boat floats above the hidden floor. Three ornate keys lie on a central ledge, each bearing a different creature emblem. This chamber is separate from the sealed triangle gate in the catacombs.[^approach]

### Arc Three — Light, keys and a buried vessel

Goggles Granny has reached the chamber ahead of the Delvers, having solved the rooms along its approach. Her ancient-Elvish translation helps them interpret the inscription. Three platforms carry faces: one weeps, one hides its eyes and screams, and one opens a devouring mouth. Their levers alter the light around the locks, while mistaken settings bring lightning.[^mechanism]

The broken crossings make even reaching the controls dangerous. Koh uses the remaining railings and his climbing sash; Evelyn lights the boat and swims through the slime when needed. Granny uses potions and rope to help Krandal reach the higher ledges. Together they discover a working arrangement of darkness, dimness and bright light. The three keys turn, are absorbed into their locks, and the slime drains toward corner basins.[^solution]

The exposed floor holds rubble and a small altar bearing a ceramic vessel with a golden bat-skull lid. A heartbeat sounds as Evelyn approaches, but opening the jar stops it and reveals a dry, shrunken heart. The vessel rests on a pressure-sensitive pedestal. Koh holds the plate while Granny weighs the jar, and they replace its weight with a stone and counterweights. The exchange succeeds without springing the trap.[^vessel]

Evelyn carries the jar away. Back in the triangle-gate catacombs, it fits one of the three round altar recesses, linking this local chamber's prize to the larger sealed entrance. The keys consumed here remain part of the chamber's mechanism, while the vessel begins a separate journey with the other urns.[^removal]

## Current status

The local puzzle is solved, the slime largely drained and the jar removed. Stone and weights maintain pressure on the pedestal, and no trap was sprung. The chamber's later condition and the fate of anything concealed in its remaining slime are unknown.[^status]
