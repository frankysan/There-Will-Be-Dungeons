The [S1E01–S1E09 source-first dossiers](../REVIEW_QUEUE.md) own precise time-coded evidence, with recovered genuine S1E03 linking S1E02 and S1E04. Opening campaign-transition talk and closing XP/murder-hobo banter belong to production/table commentary, not additional fictional events. S1E01 itself does not establish a level-up; the subsequent rest and advancement to level two are explicitly confirmed near the opening of S1E02. Off-air XP after S1E02 has no numerical amount. S1E04 announces enough experience to advance; **S1E05 plays the long rest and establishes level three**, then completes a montage of several weeks. Koh's specialized powers are being unlocked gradually; S1E06 **actually awards bronze badges and completes the Skull Keep mission**. XP was deferred off-air after S1E06; the GM **announces level four at the S1E09 close**, and the party departs at that level the next morning in S1E10. Do not backdate this to S1E07 or S1E08.
