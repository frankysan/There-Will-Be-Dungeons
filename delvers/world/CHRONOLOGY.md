Opening campaign-transition talk and closing XP/murder-hobo banter belong to production/table commentary, not additional fictional events. S1E01 itself does not establish a level-up; the subsequent rest and advancement to level two are explicitly confirmed near the opening of S1E02. Off-air XP after S1E02 has no numerical amount. S1E04 announces enough experience to advance; **S1E05 plays the long rest and establishes level three**, then completes a montage of several weeks. Koh's specialized powers are being unlocked gradually; S1E06 **actually awards bronze badges and completes the Skull Keep mission**. XP is deferred off-air and a further level-up has **not** been established.
