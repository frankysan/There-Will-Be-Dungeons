# Delvers — guild society and the world below

## Introduction

Atomer's adventuring guild connects ordinary civic life to the dangerous world beneath it. Work, patronage and public service shape the profession, while buried ruins draw the attention of researchers, merchants and distant authorities. The city's struggle over whether to contain, exploit or end that danger changes both its institutions and the people who depend on them.[^world-scope]

## Magic, craft and the wider kingdom

<a id="magic-and-an-1880-like-technological-baseline"></a>

Magic works alongside craft and machinery in the life of Atomer. The Daily Quest uses printing presses, while its correspondent carries an animated camera of wood, glass and crystal. Gruber Lifton's lift carries adventurers into the dungeon network; portable magical light and enchanted equipment accompany them below. These tools place practical magic beside the workshops, markets and services on which the city depends.[^craft]

Atomer belongs to a larger kingdom with other cities and competing adventuring guilds. Those distant communities broaden the world beyond the local crisis, though the Delvers' work remains centered on Atomer and its surroundings.[^maybonus]

## Adventuring as organized work

Adventuring in Atomer is organized employment, with numbered badges, defined commissions and leagues that govern access to work. The [local guild](places/delvers-guild.md) combines a quest desk, acquisitions counter, hospitality and lodging. Cassius assigns jobs and welcomes recruits, while Carrie examines finds and investigates threats. The Darkest Delvers begin among its least experienced members, dependent on institutional permission and supplies.[^society][^work]

The guild pays for agreed tasks rather than every creature killed. Wood-ranked companies face a three-job week and enforced leave; scouting can reserve a claim for later exploration without authorizing a complete clearance. Promotion requires visible public service as well as survival. The Delvers' denied bronze application eventually gives way to approval, and later silver standing brings assessment by senior examiners.[^ranks][^service][^revelation][^guild-later]

The profession's promises depend on scarce funds and patrons. Cassius's covert investigation of Withers through the Cloven Brotherhood sits outside ordinary commissions, exposing a tension between public procedure and private expediency. The fundraising Bronze Ball gathers donors and adventurers at the [governor's hall](places/governors-hall.md), but its destruction exposes the guild's inability to contain the danger beneath the city.[^guild-later]

## The Guild's faraway rulers

The distant [Dungeon Guild Academy](places/dungeon-guild-academy.md) exercises authority over funding, oversight and discipline. Cassius and Carrie petition its governors for experienced champions while Atomer's companies investigate the lake ruins. The officials cite researchers and silver guards already funded, recommend private patronage and the Bronze Ball, and refuse further support. Their diviner's confidence in the chosen Delvers offers Cassius little practical reassurance.[^academy]

Academy authority later supports Zoltar's administration of Atomer while Cassius is confined and the Delvers are grounded. Zoltar privately acknowledges that continuing expeditions enrich the Academy, whereas Cassius wants the danger ended quickly. The conflict becomes public when the Delvers demand Cassius's return. His release restores local leadership in practice; the central governors' response and any wider institutional settlement remain unknown.[^academy-later]

<a id="silver-city-after-the-bronze-ball-s3e02"></a>

## Silver City and licensed exploration

The three months after the ruined Bronze Ball transform Atomer into Silver City. A spiked wall and guild-marked gate enclose the dangerous financial quarter. Residents and merchants are displaced, while visiting adventurers fill lodgings and tent camps. Koh leaves the home caught within the enclosure for a smaller house near the guild. The damaged civic district becomes a regulated gateway into the ancient underground network.[^silver-city]

Public announcements promote daily silver-league expeditions, inspections and questing licences. They exclude elves, half-elves and people of Sylvan descent from governor's square, prohibit overnight stays below and recommend organized raid requests at major threats. Claims of plentiful treasure and a retreating Khalazar sell the enterprise's promise. The Delvers' licensed C4 expedition later passes the entry requirements, but specific permission to work does not end all restrictions on the company.[^silver-city][^licensing]

The resulting prosperity depends on an unresolved danger. At the guild's public reckoning, Clearstone brings forward the names of thirty missing elves while Zoltar admits the routine use of divination to track adventurers. Staff, members and townspeople support the call to restore Cassius. Zoltar yields local control and leaves alive; Cassius funds an operation intended to end Khalazar's threat.[^reckoning]

<a id="journalism-and-the-silver-dungeon-economy-s3e03"></a>

## Journalism and the dungeon economy

*The Daily Quest* serves the new adventuring economy from a newsroom and printing office near the Academy market. Dungeon news, guides and advertisements make exploration a public spectacle as well as a trade. Its reports of cleared passages, missing adventurers and a purchased prophecy carry the newspaper's authority without resolving the truth of every claim.[^press]

The paper hires [Dumbledwark of Raisinbunch](characters/dumbledwark-of-raisinbunch.md) to investigate the Delvers' suspension. His interviews and photographicals bring him into their homes, school and farm, then into Zoltar's office. There he gains an admission about the value of prolonged dungeon activity and arranges the licensed C4 expedition. His access also permits a theft: he leaves with an unidentified [warlock's tome](items/stolen-warlocks-tome.md), whose contents remain unknown.[^press]

Later advertisements announce his forthcoming exposé, *A Prison City*. A sealed newspaper letter gives Koh the location of Cassius's confinement at the Wildfire estate. Cassius's release there confirms that particular lead, though the book's eventual publication and the paper's wider allegations remain unresolved.[^press-later]

## Caverns and the Underdark

Natural caves and worked passages bring the underground close to Atomer's farms and streets. In the [sheep-farm cave](places/farm-cave.md), stirges feed near the entrance while a piercer and darkmantle hide among stone formations. A fissure in the final chamber descends to a lower tunnel, where Koh finds a dead adventurer's badge and globe before retreating from a creature Cassius identifies as a grick.[^below]

Rock and concrete close this fissure, securing one approach while leaving the wider passages unknown. The recovered badge links the work to an earlier missing guild member. Cassius says its owner had sought a face-bearing door elsewhere below; the cave expedition finds no such door, and the dead adventurer's fate remains only partly understood.[^door]

## Devotion and the strength of the living

Kord's mountain sanctuary joins worship to physical discipline. Running, chains and a bell reached by rope form trials within its crypts and graveyard. When its fountains become fouled, strength-draining Shadows threaten the devotees. House attributes the haunting to Studwell's fatal attempt to squat in sandals. The Delvers confront the apparition, restore clear water through the basins and bring the two missing devotees out alive.[^sanctuary]

[Pelor's temple](places/temple-of-pelor.md) serves Atomer through lodging, prayer and healing under Brother Maynard's supervision. Its care extends from ordinary injuries and poison to the elves left unresponsive by the deeper disturbance. The growing ward strains the clergy, and ordinary healing can ease bodily suffering without waking the sleepers.[^temple-care]

Treatment develops through particular cases. Koh's psychic access lets Krandal reach trapped minds, where curse-breaking brings seven patients back to awareness after the Bronze Ball. A later victim awakens after a nonlethal psychic blow and removal to the temple, offering a different route out of shallower compulsion. The temple continues receiving those Koh finds. When Khalazar finally falls, the last rescued captives receive care at the guild; the temple's later staffing and repairs remain unknown.[^temple-care]

## Forest ruins and old defenses

<a id="the-forest-ruins-and-the-limits-of-rank"></a>

The [forest sinkhole](places/grick-sinkhole.md) opens onto roofless buildings, an altar and a trapped worked-stone dungeon. Gricks hunt among the ruins, while other carcasses lie impaled on the old defenses. Inscriptions offer a partial reading of sky people; a heavy tablet remains behind after the Delvers fail to carry it out. Veggie Sticks fears that greater guild activity will disturb the woodland and its hippogriffs.[^grickwoods]

The first expedition establishes a scouting claim, not ownership or complete clearance. Cassius accepts its report and trophy, pays for the assigned work and agrees to seek further exploration rights subject to urgent intervention by stronger adventurers. Much later, the Delvers use the same forest entrance to leave the deeper puzzle complex. Repaired crossings and disabled old traps make it a usable exit, though the repairers and full extent of the works remain uncertain.[^ranks][^forest-route]

## Service and a wider city

Atomer's adventuring companies share a profession that reaches beyond dungeon fighting. Public service takes newcomers into inns, alleys, markets, sewers and workshops to remove pests, escort neighbors and restore ordinary life. The Darkest Delvers earn bronze standing after weeks of such work; more experienced members still handle threats beyond them.[^service][^revelation]

The First Sons Brotherhood, Five Emissaries, Viper Gang, Brigand Patrol and Darkest Delvers provide distinct companies for coordinated assignments. The guild distributes well inspections and holds a company in reserve, then sends teams to approach different communities with gifts and instructions to seek peaceful access. The shared guild coordinates collective work while each company receives its own assignments.[^companies]

Expertise also circulates through private encounters. Carrie identifies the recovered rune-marked hilt as a [Soul Knife](items/runic-sword-hilt.md), while a respected [Masked Mage](characters/masked-mage.md) calls Koh a scion and warns of awakened minds seeking relics. His unexplained advice leaves the identity and organization of those seekers uncertain.[^revelation]

## Enzo Keep and the warning below

<a id="a-shadow-beneath-the-lake"></a>

[Enzo Keep](places/skull-keep.md) preserves the comforts of a wealthy household on a spire above an upland lake. Animated servants maintain its gardens and rooms, while a green-burning skull patrols the towers. Enzo's reflection speaks from an upper laboratory, attributing the divided household to his failed immortality rite. The skull's destruction stills the servants and dims the reflection, leaving the building standing and Enzo's final awareness uncertain.[^keep]

Enzo interprets the old signs as those of people escaping the darkness below. He names the Dawnless Dunes and warns that an awakening heart is forcing predators upward. The Delvers later physically reach the [underground desert and pyramid](places/dawnless-dunes-pyramid.md), finding a ruined city and captive laborers. That journey gives his warning a tangible setting without settling every claim about the ruins' origins or the cause of each incursion.[^dunes]

## A city listening to its wells

Atomer's public wells sit among the homes, shops and workshops they supply. When underground creatures appear in streets and waterways, Carrie proposes a chain of hunters pursuing prey toward the surface and notes recurring black ichor. The guild treats these theories as grounds for investigation, assigning bronze companies to the wells while trying to avoid public alarm.[^city]

The [southwestern square](places/southwestern-town-well.md) reveals how local danger enters ordinary working life: a rust trail leads to a forge, a delivery barrel proves to be a mimic and a stirge feeds on a family's dog. The Delvers remove the immediate threats and heal the injured. Their later descent exposes a side opening from caves into the well shaft. Blocking the explored approaches limits access, but no water test establishes that the neighborhood's drinking supply is safe.[^city][^buried-limits]

## The buried city and its forgotten sounds

Below the southwestern well, natural caves lead into older worked stone resembling the forest defenses. Six adventurers activate weighted platforms that open a stair through Pelor's Hall of Penance. The discovery makes the buried architecture part of the city's daily life: the temple must contend with damaged flooring, a guarded entrance and the guild's promises of assistance. The accessible cave approaches are blocked, leaving other passages unexplored.[^under]

The main sanctuary conceals a separate narrow shaft beneath its altar. A deep drone coincides with an elf's collapse; replacing the covering muffles it without resolving the opening's purpose. The temple becomes a place of care for afflicted elves, while this covered shaft remains uninvestigated.[^under][^buried-limits]

Carrie's later translations connect the old works to Khalazar's underground dominion and describe the Dolbluth as people driven above. She interprets the surrounding dungeons as defenses later occupied by surface beasts. Her recovered texts and maps supply a historical account, while the actual passages establish only the connections the explorers traverse. Similar carvings and dark seepage do not by themselves reveal one complete underground plan.[^ancient-history]

## A living shore and a submerged faith

The [eastern kuo-toa village](places/kuo-toa-lakeshore-village.md) supports households through fishing, trade and craft at the edge of a freshwater swamp. The guild sends the Delvers with fish and pearls to seek permission to inspect nearby ruins. Their mandate favors peaceful contact and withdrawal if talks fail. Koh's unexpected voice within an elder's mind instead prompts reverence: villagers hail him as divine despite his denials.[^lake]

Glepo Ma's shop provides a more practical exchange. He speaks some Common and trades directions to the submerged ruins for Koh's shovel. Beneath the lake, a ritualist speaks of the Dunes and a sleeping god. His account offers a religious interpretation of the dark seepage and ancient structures, while his capture puts the visitors' peaceful purpose under strain.[^lake]

The settlement survives the later collectors' threats with its homes standing. Glepo Ma subsequently reports that his people banished him for helping abduct their high priest, and takes work at Krandal's farm. His exile leaves the village's later leadership and beliefs uncertain; his new life elsewhere supplies no return visit or final settlement with the community.[^shore-later]

## Hospitality and alliance below

The depths also contain places of negotiated hospitality and willing alliance. At [Deep Winds Spa](places/deep-winds-spa.md), flumph hosts expect visitors to respect bathing customs and leave weapons outside the pools. Guests who might fight elsewhere share the baths under the house's neutrality. The Delvers clear an immediate filtration threat and stay overnight, then return for another costly stay. Hospitality remains a paid service; a permanent guild base is never established.[^spa-community]

Beneath Noble Hollow, the [Minotaur Mesa council](places/minotaur-mesa-bone-crypt.md) speaks for people whose skeletal bodies remain animated by Khalazar's curse. Corgan and eight others choose to aid Krandal because they seek release. With Chad Brad they form ten allies, carrying captives to safety and shielding the last rescuers from falling stone. After Khalazar's defeat, all ten settle into inert bones at the guild, gaining the rest they sought.[^mesa-community]

## The collectors from the marsh

The Delvers' return from the deep ruins reveals an unexpected danger above. Their guide Glepo Ma passes the fish wizard's shell-tipped staff to a black-cloaked rider wearing the skin of a darkmantle as a high cowl. He threatens the quiet settlement and leaves two companions to take whatever the adventurers have found. The Delvers prevail, choosing to keep both strangers alive alongside the captured fish wizard. The larger of the two has hidden a familiar face: when his false features fade, he looks like Broadside, a man encountered around the guild. No one yet knows how the stranger came to wear that face, or to whom he answers.[^collectors]

Glepo Ma tries to make amends by giving the party a gear-crank and a bag of money. He also swims off to warn the guild adventurers farther west, where the rider claims to be heading next. The deeper ruins have yielded a broken circular mechanism and a wand capable of finding hidden things, but the company leaves a great tooth-framed portal unopened. All three living prisoners depart on the Delvers' borrowed horses toward the guild. Their explanations are still to come.[^collectors]

## The broken rite at Noble Hollow

A research mission under the guild's protection becomes a dangerous attempt to summon an old power. The man who has taken the fish wizard's staff seeks to use a high elf and a geared stone altar in a midnight rite beneath a petrified tree. Broadside and Solburn, silver-ranked guardians of the ruins, are drawn into the scheme under an influence they later struggle to describe. The Delvers intervene and halt the ceremony, but the shattered altar opens a cavern that conceals an enormous roper. Its breeding crevice reveals a direct living connection to the piercers found in other dungeons. The Delvers save Faelan and Norman and defeat the creature, yet the deeper power the researcher hoped to call remains unconfirmed.[^finale]

The guild eventually acknowledges that the bronze company has protected its people from the threat that emerged during the city's great investigation. Its officers promise proceedings against the researcher and recovery for those influenced; they offer an old tower in lieu of money. The city remains troubled by signs of things beneath its foundations, and the coming bronze ball is expected to fund further work. Even as these dangers recede, a new visitor arrives with money and snakes to revive an old offer concerning Snake Island.[^finale]

## The fortnight no one remembers

After the strange offer of Snake Island, the Darkest Delvers vanish for two weeks. They return to Atomer beneath the high sun, dressed in serpent scales, garlanded with flowers and carrying a huge fang. Their guild celebrates a job accomplished, but the three remember barely anything beyond crossing a lake and a serpent rising above it. Clues return as scattered images: a python brought forth from a staff, a princess greeting Krandal as her love, a queen's jewels placed upon Evelyn and Koh seizing a golden serpent idol from a temple. Even these glimpses leave too much untold for any of them to claim the island's story.[^season2]

Atomer has moved on without them. Koh's former criminal associates have paid for his wife's care and now demand that the debt bring him back among the Cloven Brotherhood. The elves sleeping in Pelor's temple have not awakened; Krandal hears only a half-whispered word from one of them. Evelyn's school absence prompts a visit from her family physician, who sees upon her a mark associated with a goddess of love and passion. The guild finally hands Krandal the promised deed to a neglected riverside watchtower. There he finds a sea hag, an animated minotaur and the beginnings of the potato-growing dream he names Potato Grove. The creatures are defeated or subdued, though the talking skull survives under the earth; no fields are restored yet.[^season2]

## The wedding protected

The Delvers attend the Hooper–Goodwine wedding under orders to avert a haunting without troubling the guests. A newly arrived magician, the Great Daufante, is hired separately to help with illusions. His mask, theatrics and undead assistant trouble the delicate wedding manners, yet the celebration becomes stranger still when its ceremonial white birds are found dead. Evelyn makes the great goat of her ivory figurine the unexpected centre of the ceremony, and the halfling guests rejoice. Outside the feast, Krandal destroys a glowing-eyed knife-bearing duck and Koh follows dark footprints with no owner he can see.

The cause of the old haunting is at last confessed by an elderly Goodwine: fifteen years ago he and his allies hanged Grandfather Hooper in the woods and never buried him. The dead man returns hanging from a walking oak as a wight. Krandal brings the tree down and Daufante's stars end the wight's attack. A card deck is removed from the grandfather's pocket, and Goodwine helps lay his former enemy in the family earth. The wedding continues, the entertainer departs with the dangerous cards, and the dark sludge under the fallen tree remains unexplained.[^s2e02]

## Beneath the financial quarter

The next guild call brings the Delvers to the wealthiest streets of Atomer, where a monster tunnels beneath the paving with the strength of an underwater shark. Evelyn lures it by the thunder of her armored feet, Koh drives two psychic blades into its hard hide, and Krandal uses Pelor's strength to strike the creature's bare eye. The bulette dies in the crushed garden outside Withers Estate. Behind its broken path, Koh finds a clean room with a restrained stone slab and a locked entrance into the wealthy collector's residence. Withers insists that many parts of his old mansion remain unknown to him; the adventurers bring the unsettling evidence to Cassius.[^s2e03]

A few quiet days bring changes of another sort. Krandal plants his first potatoes and sets the still-speaking minotaur skull above them as a scarecrow. Evelyn selects the subjects she will study and readies herself for the approaching Bronze Ball. Koh's old satyr associate recruits him to coordinate a theft from Withers's jade collection, promising to forget a debt Koh never freely accepted. Koh discovers the scheme's financier calls himself Cassius backward. Cassius confirms the disguise and privately orders the Delvers to accompany the thieves, seeking proof of what Withers collects and why. The group prepares disguises and magical footwear while Evelyn reveals to her friends the noble **Blackwood** family name she has hidden. The mansion remains unentered when an unfamiliar elf walks into the guild.[^s2e03]

## The vault beneath Withers

Koh's former associates join the Darkest Delvers for a moonlit robbery. A borrowed silence spell covers the explosions that open the way beyond the bulette's hole, and behind the wine racks the company finds the scars of an older prison. The four elemental guardians of Withers's hidden household test the thieves with riddles and terrible powers: Fern's fire, Dewey's water, Gale's wind and Slate's speaking stone lift. Koh discovers he can throw the psychic blades he once only wielded by hand, Krandal finally animates his snake staff to free himself from drowning, and Evelyn holds firm against the tempest that almost kills their guest. The keepers survive, surrendering keys, and the Delvers gain passage.[^s2e04]

Upstairs, Koh takes twenty-three pieces from Withers's jade museum. Shrugs is spotted carrying a mound of coats, setting a servant shouting, but Koh finds the hidden lift key. Below the cellar, Evelyn destroys a conch-headed staff that recalls the lost kuo-toa ritual staff; Koh gathers the pieces. Gems and mysterious bottled black liquid pour into the thieves' bags. All four escape in Allspice's cart just as Withers comes home, yet what Cassius truly wanted—proof of the financier's dangerous intentions—remains undiscovered.[^s2e04]

## Current status

Khalazar is defeated and his pyramid has collapsed. The guild receives rescued captives through two evacuation circles, and its healers tend the survivors. The dungeon's monsters wane over time, while the land at Noble Hollow becomes fertile again. These changes end the immediate crisis without settling the fate of every underground creature or the full condition of the ruins.[^world-ending]

Cassius has resumed local leadership. During the following year Koh teaches younger guild members, Krandal builds a thriving farm and Evelyn protects Atomer and helps its school. The rebuilt farm tower becomes the Delvers' home base. The Academy's response, Silver City's later licensing rules and the repairs to the governor's hall remain unknown.[^world-ending]
