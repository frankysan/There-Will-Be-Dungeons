# Creatures and monsters

The woodland, buried ruins and distant mountain keeps around the guild's city shelter creatures dangerous enough to draw the Delvers' attention.[^scope]

| Creature | Nature and place in the world |
| --- | --- |
| Ancient mummified elves of the triangle-gate catacombs | **Several fight the Delvers in [S3E06](../episodes/S3E06.md)** after rune-tombs spiral open; bodies ancient, surgically hollowed, **Krandal Turns Undead but does not instantly destroy them**. Multiple killed and party retreats; exact total/magic origins require recording/map check |
| Behi(r) of Dungeon C4 | Long many-legged lightning-breathing cavern predator **killed by Krandal's Spiritual Weapon in [S3E04](../episodes/S3E04.md)**. Its attack kills no observed Delver; earlier victim testimony says it squeezes prey and is lightning-immune. Unrelated unidentified sticky ceiling ropes/pale crab-like beings are **not proven part of behir**. |
| [All Rat cranium-rat congregation](creatures/all-rat-cranium-congregation.md) | **S3E08:** exposed-brained rat gestalt generates **illusions** of Krandal farm takeover/Koh bounty/Evelyn asylum, entraps heroes via linked rat neck chains and attacks with psychic pulses. **Koh actually removes the hook-horror-capped URN** and swarm breaks into live rats; **no real surface disasters or species-wide eradication** |
| [Hook horrors](creatures/hook-horrors.md) | Huge hook-armed, beaked hunters in deep tunnels; one killed by Koh, another observed alive. **S3E06 hatching imprints on Krandal, S3E07 juvenile eats rations in bag; S3E08 same fledgling provisionally called Scroungy gets a tracking pink ribbon, eats rats, and causes/simultaneously responds to nearby adult calls. THREE wild adults arrive, Koh ACTUALLY kills dominant/aggressive one by crit, TWO retreat alive. Hatchling returns to bag, NOT surrendered or trained/flying**. Universal senses/kinship remain uncertain. Their full ecology/senses remain uncertain. |
| [Gricks](creatures/gricks.md) | Scaled predators with grasping limbs and beaks, encountered around forest sinkhole and trapped dungeon. **S3E09:** some disguise themselves as rocks in wind/grick complex; **Koh actually crit-kills a very large alpha and smaller gricks**, Krandal/Barclay kill others, **at least one ESCAPES**. No proof entire population removed or Koh became literal pack leader. |
| [Noble Hollow roper](creatures/noble-hollow-roper.md) | Great stone-skinned creature beneath petrified hollow; **produces piercers**, killed by Evelyn. **Not proven summoned lake beast**. |
| [Skull Keep's flameskull](creatures/flameskull.md) | Green-burning undead sentinel linked, according to Enzo, to a botched immortality rite; destroyed by Koh and banished by Evelyn's holy water |
