# Creatures and monsters

The woodland, buried ruins and distant mountain keeps around the guild's city shelter creatures dangerous enough to draw the Delvers' attention.[^scope]

| Creature | Nature and place in the world |
| --- | --- |
| Behi(r) of Dungeon C4 | Long many-legged lightning-breathing cavern predator **killed by Krandal's Spiritual Weapon in [S3E04](../episodes/S3E04.md)**. Its attack kills no observed Delver; earlier victim testimony says it squeezes prey and is lightning-immune. Unrelated unidentified sticky ceiling ropes/pale crab-like beings are **not proven part of behir**. |
| [Hook horrors](creatures/hook-horrors.md) | Huge hook-armed, beaked hunters in deeper natural tunnels; one killed by Koh, **another seen alive**. Their ecology and exact senses remain uncertain. |
| [Gricks](creatures/gricks.md) | Scaled predators with grasping limbs and beaks, encountered around a forest sinkhole and within a trapped dungeon. |
| [Noble Hollow roper](creatures/noble-hollow-roper.md) | Great stone-skinned creature beneath petrified hollow; **produces piercers**, killed by Evelyn. **Not proven summoned lake beast**. |
| [Skull Keep's flameskull](creatures/flameskull.md) | Green-burning undead sentinel linked, according to Enzo, to a botched immortality rite; destroyed by Koh and banished by Evelyn's holy water |

[^scope]: This category primarily covers non-intelligent creatures and animal-like or monstrous behavior, rather than talking, reasoning NPCs. [Veggie Sticks](characters/veggie-sticks.md) and the walking plants remain with the character material; Studwell's apparition remains documented in the [crypt dossier](../episodes/S1E02.md). The grick article draws on S1E01, genuine S1E03 and S1E04 dossiers; [S1E09](../episodes/S1E09.md) provides a separate grick's attempt to eat a piercer and the observed hook horrors, without implying a proven life-cycle or kinship. [S1E12](../episodes/S1E12.md) **shows a roper producing piercers** in one collapsed cavern, confirming a local relationship but not universal reproductive biology, and distinguishes the roper from the hypothetical summoning target. [S1E06](../episodes/S1E06.md) provides the flameskull research, observed final banishment and Enzo's separately attributed ritual testimony; no audio-verified episode or audio research, species-wide intelligence ruling or D&D baseline comparison is claimed.
