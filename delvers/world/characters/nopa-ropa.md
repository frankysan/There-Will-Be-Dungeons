# Nopa Ropa — Snake Island warrior seeking Princess Hissia

## Introduction

Nopa Ropa is a snake-headed royal guard from Snake Island who seeks [Krandal Splenderbelt](krandal-splenderbelt.md) at his riverside farm. He calls the cleric his island's hero and reports that [Princess Hissia](princess-hissia.md) has been taken into darkness beneath Atomer. Cutting his own hand, he swears personal service and joins the work of digging an irrigation ditch. His service begins with an expectation that Krandal will help find her.[^nopa-introduction]

### Arc Three — Service, impatience and rescue

Nopa's oath draws him into the ordinary labor of the farm while the search waits. He harnesses himself to a plow before a donkey arrives, manages fieldwork and helps build a barn whose gables bear snake-head carvings. His readiness to work does not mean that the missing princess has ceased to matter. Krandal's continued absences leave both the search and his workers' wages unattended.[^nopa-service]

When Krandal finally returns with payment, Nopa throws the coins aside before gathering them again: money cannot satisfy the obligation that brought him there. A new lead changes the impasse. Krandal reports that a spa visitor has placed Hissia among captives in the Dawnless Dunes. Nopa renews his blood pledge, agrees to wait until morning and prepares to join the expedition. His fierce urgency becomes a shared undertaking rather than another demand for action.[^nopa-pledge]

He descends with the Delvers wearing new golden armor, a breastplate and headpiece shaped to his serpent head, and carrying the Staff of the Python Krandal has given him. He remains outspoken about their uncertain route and his companions' methods. Even on reaching the pyramid, rescuing Hissia requires restraint: Krandal repeatedly persuades him to hold back while they secure a safer escape.[^nopa-expedition]

When they recognize her among the upper chamber's attendants, Nopa receives her unconscious body after Koh subdues her without killing her. He carries her downstairs to the evacuation circle. His devotion finds its answer at the guild, where she wakes, recognizes him and returns his affection. They call their love forbidden and kiss, bringing his search to a living reunion.[^nopa-rescue]

## Current status

Nopa survives the expedition and is last known reunited with Hissia at the guild. His oath's immediate purpose has been fulfilled. Their future together, the reason their love is forbidden and his later connection to Krandal's farm remain unknown.[^nopa-status]
