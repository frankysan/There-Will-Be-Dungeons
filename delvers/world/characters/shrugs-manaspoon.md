# Shrugs Manaspoon — guest burglar and demolition specialist

## Introduction

<a id="introduction-and-appearance"></a>

Shrugs Manaspoon is a wood-elf burglar recruited by the Cloven Brotherhood to help rob the [Withers Estate](../places/withers-estate.md). Lanky, dark-haired and copper-skinned, he dresses neatly in gloves and a cloak. His quiet manner and habitual open-palmed shrug give little warning of the explosives strapped beneath his clothes.[^introduction]

## Development by arc

<a id="the-withers-estate-infiltration"></a>

### Arc Two — Precision, explosives and imperfect concealment

Shrugs brings both deft hands and destructive force to the burglary. Linked to Koh's mental communication and entrusted with a borrowed Bag of Holding, he works alongside the Delvers. Krandal's silence magic lets him blast through the first locked door; at another, he demonstrates the quieter alternative by picking its lock with exceptional speed.[^cellar]

His curiosity extends beyond the expected riches. He pockets an incomplete music box and a mummified kuo-toa toe, while sampling and returning a bottle of wine. The same reserve that marks his introduction does not restrain his methods in combat: his dynamite wounds Koh as well as the fire guardian. Later, an air guardian knocks him unconscious, leaving him dependent on Krandal's healing to continue.[^guardians]

His agility also has limits. Searching upstairs, he fails to find the required key and carries away a great heap of coats. A servant spots the moving pile and raises the alarm; Koh finds the key among the garments. Shrugs helps breach the final vault and gather its valuables, then escapes with the others in Allspice's cart.[^escape]

At the warehouse he makes an abrupt departure under cover of a smoke bomb. He leaves the borrowed bag and wooden music box behind, ending his work with the group before their later examination and division of the loot.[^departure]

## Current status

Shrugs has departed alive without the mission bag or music box. His later whereabouts and share of the proceeds are unknown.[^departure]
