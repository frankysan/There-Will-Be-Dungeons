# Margaret Dupayton — Evelyn's confidante

## Introduction

Margaret is Evelyn Grinkeeper's roommate at a distinguished finishing school. Where teachers expect polished manners, Margaret is happiest with a needle and thread, studying the concealed workings of enchanted garments. She has become Evelyn's ally in a secret life the school's authorities might not approve. She covers Evelyn's unexplained absences and helps her make adventuring equipment fit into a respectable wardrobe.[^intro]

## Gifts of quiet invention

Her first substantial favor is to weave the magic from easy-fastening dresses into Evelyn's chain mail, letting the paladin put on the armor at a moment's notice. Margaret follows that experiment with a pink, jewel-studded **Bag of Hammer Holding**, sewn from dismantled enchanted bags. The little tote can swallow one heavy item, allowing Evelyn to hide her warhammer before returning through the school's windows.

When Evelyn finds herself at a garden lunch in a dress and a hat decorated with crushed honeysuckle, Margaret discreetly threads magic into the flowers to restore their beauty. It is a small intervention, but no less loyal than the armored garment: she knows that Lady Vestal will be searching for signs of impropriety, and she protects Evelyn without demanding an explanation.[^gifts]

## A face drawn in a hurry

On the morning of the guild's eastern expedition, Evelyn returns to the problem of appearing at school while already departing for adventure. Margaret has suggested the possibility of a magical disguise, but the hour is too early for such a scheme. Instead she takes Evelyn's magical pigments and paints her friend's face on a pillow beneath the blankets. The drawing swells into an uncanny sleeping likeness. Margaret agrees to let Lady Vestal look in from the doorway without touching the illusion, giving Evelyn time to escape out the window. Whatever the school later believes, the two friends have made themselves partners in more than secret sewing.[^decoy]

## Earlier status (S2E01 cutoff)

Margaret has **successfully maintained Evelyn's painted sleeping double for two full weeks** during the missing Snake Island journey, supplying the school with fabricated ailments **including scarlet fever, whooping cough and typhoid**. Reports reached **Lord and Lady Blackwood**, Evelyn's true parents, who dispatched a physician. Margaret helps Evelyn **swap places with the dummy before Dr Ripart's examination**; he finds no disease but discovers a **mysterious love/passion goddess mark**. Once he leaves, **Evelyn begins telling Margaret her history from two years earlier**, but **the conversation is not performed to the audience**, so its contents cannot be written as known canon. Lady Vestal's exact knowledge of the adventuring secret is still unproved; Bronze Ball remains future.[^s2e01]
