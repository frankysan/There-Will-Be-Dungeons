# Margaret Dupayton — Evelyn's confidante

## Introduction

Margaret is [Evelyn Grinkeeper's](evelyn-grinkeeper.md) roommate at a distinguished finishing school. She shares notes from missed lessons and covers an absence by saying Evelyn has visited her family. Her skill with enchanted garments offers a practical answer to her friend's secret adventuring: she unthreads easy-fastening dresses and weaves their magic into Evelyn's chain mail, allowing it to be donned in moments.[^margaret-introduction]

### Arc One — Invention in a friend's service

Margaret's ingenuity makes Evelyn's two lives easier to sustain. She sews a pink, jewel-studded [Bag of Hammer Holding](../items/bag-of-hammer-holding.md) from dismantled enchanted bags, giving a heavy warhammer an inconspicuous place in a schoolgirl's wardrobe. Her attention extends to smaller embarrassments. At a garden lunch she discreetly restores the crushed flowers on Evelyn's hat with magic thread while Lady Vestal scrutinizes her presentation. She helps her friend meet the school's expectations without surrendering her adventures.[^margaret-gifts]

The same loyalty makes Margaret a partner in deception. When a school obligation clashes with a guild expedition, she considers a magical impersonation; instead, she uses Evelyn's pigments to paint a sleeping likeness on a pillow beneath the blankets. The likeness gives them a substitute to show from the doorway. Margaret's contribution is more than a useful possession: she must remain behind to maintain the story while Evelyn leaves through the window.[^margaret-decoy]

### Arc Two — The demands of keeping a secret

A short-lived excuse becomes a sustained responsibility when Evelyn's absence lasts two weeks. Margaret maintains the painted double and invents illnesses to explain it. The cover draws unwelcome attention: Evelyn's parents send a physician, and school concerns about lingering sickness threaten a change of room. Helping Evelyn exchange places with the dummy preserves the immediate deception, but cannot make it effortless. Margaret also reports a disturbing glimpse of an axe-bearing red dragonborn in the dormitory mirror, whose identity remains uncertain.[^margaret-cover]

Their confidence extends beyond hiding equipment. Evelyn begins telling Margaret about an earlier part of her life, and later entrusts her with a piece of the enchanted [friendship ribbon](../items/friendship-ribbon.md). They prepare a dress that can be folded beneath armor and put dance steps on Evelyn's fan. At the Bronze Ball, Margaret issues dance cards while Evelyn moves between her armored and formal identities. The preparations serve a friendship that has become part of Evelyn's ability to act openly.[^margaret-ball]

### Arc Three — A trusted contact

When Evelyn leaves Atomer to confront her family's expectations, she leaves letters for Koh and Krandal with Margaret. From home she writes to Margaret again and asks her to post anonymous pamphlets celebrating the heroes whose work has drawn suspicion upon Cassius. The pamphlets later appear around town. Evelyn continues to rely on her friend as a point of contact beyond her parents' household, although the distributor's identity is not established.[^margaret-letters]

## Current status

Margaret's last direct role is as Evelyn's trusted contact at the school when the letters are left with her. Later correspondence and the pamphlet request show Evelyn continuing to depend on that friendship. Margaret's own circumstances after the town's crisis remain unknown; Evelyn's later work rebuilding the school does not reveal what became of her roommate.[^margaret-status]
