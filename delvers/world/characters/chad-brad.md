He is NOT made a living fleshed minotaur, not already destroyed by curse, and not newly renamed. **Final GM count = TEN skeletal allies TOTAL (NINE crypt members plus Chad Brad)**; separate Nopa Ropa not counted as minotaur. Chad Brad can be resting/still bones at dawn but not declared dead; battle only planned. Krandal promises to attempt a further peaceful restoration if possible **in future**, no outcome yet.
