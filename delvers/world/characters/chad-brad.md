# Chad Brad — cursed skeletal minotaur and Krandal's farm companion

## Introduction

Chad Brad first confronts the Delvers as an unnamed skeletal minotaur at the abandoned riverside farm near [Noble Hollow](../places/noble-hollow.md). He claims the land as his own, takes a skull from its scarecrow as his head and wields the post against the intruders.[^encounter]

## Development by arc

### Arc Two — An enemy becomes the farm's watchman

When Koh's attacks bring down the minotaur's body, its detached skull continues to speak. Krandal nearly smashes it, but the skull warns of haunting and offers to scare birds instead. Their relationship begins as a bargain for survival. Krandal buries the skull, then later unearths it and mounts it on a jacketed scarecrow as he starts planting the fields.[^watchman]

The guardian takes an interest in his new work, asking for a stone to spit at crows. He watches the riverbank and reports a fishman visiting the farm. His limited body leaves him dependent on Krandal, but his voice makes him a participant in the growing household rather than a discarded remnant.[^watchman]

Unable to remember his own name through the curse, he accepts the name Chad from Krandal. The cleric later calls him Brad and fashions a pointing glove for the scarecrow's arm. Amid these improvised comforts, the former enemy becomes a familiar companion, while the possibility of restoring more than a speaking head remains a hope.[^name]

### Arc Three — A body, a choice and a farewell

Chad remains the farm's watchman as its workers and fields grow. Krandal protects his place among them, forbidding the new snake guardian to harm him. Their companionship gains a harder question when Krandal learns that defeating Khalazar may end the minotaurs' cursed animation. He promises Chad a choice and hopes to seek a fuller restoration afterward.[^choice]

Chad guides him to the ancient minotaurs' crypt and endorses him before Corgan Greathorns. Nine warriors agree to help. Back at the farm, Krandal reassembles Chad's buried remains into a towering skeletal body. He can move independently again, although he has not regained living flesh.[^body]

<a id="s3e11-actually-enters-dawnless-dunes-as-one-of-ten-cursed-skeleton-minotaurs"></a>

Armed with Krandal's gift of a Mace of Disruption, Chad joins the final expedition. His renewed strength serves the rescue of captives. While eight minotaurs leave with the first evacuation, he and Corgan remain beside the Delvers.[^expedition]

<a id="s3e12-final-carries-civilians-through-danger-cursed-skeletal-existence-ends"></a>

After Khalazar falls, the two shield the escaping rescuers from collapsing masonry. Chad reaches the Guild with the others. There he joins the minotaurs in a final silent acknowledgement to Krandal before all ten crumble into motionless bones.[^farewell]

## Current status

Chad Brad's animated existence has ended. His remains lie at the Guild with the other freed minotaurs; the living restoration Krandal hoped for has not occurred.[^farewell]
