# Shaolin Nym / Nim — ancient Dolbluth architect

<a id="shaolin-nym--nim--ancient-dolbluth-architect-s3e11"></a>

## Introduction

Shaolin Nym emerges beside the [sealed triangular gate](../places/triangle-gate-catacombs.md) when the Delvers place three organ urns in its altar. A mummified woman in ruined linen, with leathery skin and spiders weaving through her long, matted hair, she addresses the three heroes in words only they understand.[^appearance]

## Development by arc

### Arc Three — A final service to the liberators

Nym presents herself as an architect of the Dolbluth outcasts. When she recognizes that the Delvers seek liberation rather than service to their dark master, she bows low and offers the knowledge she has preserved. The other adventurers, unable to follow the exchange, reach for their weapons.[^history]

She describes an emperor who betrayed the Seldarine and was imprisoned by those who rejected him. In her account, an earlier generation dismantled his machines but failed to end his return. She instructs the heroes to reassemble Khalazar's organs, restore his true form with ancestral blood and defeat him permanently. Pointing to Koh's blood-bearing device, she places the next act in their hands.[^history]

Her aid is also physical. She opens the gate and releases the three urns from their recesses for reuse. Koh accepts them. Nym's fingers, arms and body crumble to dust, and the expedition proceeds without her.[^gate]

Later, as the heroes restore the organs to Khalazar's body, they share a vision of his ancient rule. A long-haired architect appears among those separating his organs and sealing them in jars. The revelation recalls Nym's part in the struggle she described, without returning her to the living expedition.[^vision]

## Current status

Nym's body has crumbled at the gate. Her later appearance belongs to the heroes' vision of the past; her fate beyond that physical ending remains unknown.[^vision]
