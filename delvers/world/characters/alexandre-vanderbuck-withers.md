# Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers — claimant of Skull Keep

## Introduction

Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers enters the Delvers' lives as a prosperous, talkative aristocrat intent on recovering his family's holiday residence. Wine on his breath, fine velvets, warm cheeks and a magnificent mustache give his business the air of jovial patronage. From his comfortable coach he makes clear that adventurers should preserve valuable furniture as carefully as they clear undead.[^withers-introduction]

## Development by arc

<a id="family-interests-and-anxiety"></a>

### Arc One — Family property and guarded knowledge

Withers calls [Enzo Keep, also known as Skull Keep](../places/skull-keep.md), his ancestral property, repurchased after a long abandonment. An earlier Bronze company has gone missing, yet he is particularly insistent that his new agents spare the antiques. He promises an additional reward for preservation and repeatedly warns them against looking into the mirrors. His caution suggests knowledge of danger without revealing how much comes from family history or recent reports.[^withers-contract]

He leaves the fighting to the Delvers, waiting outside with Aegis. His claim upon the keep draws them into its surviving magic, but does not itself explain the old household or his precise relationship to its former lord.[^withers-keep]

### Arc Two — A benefactor under suspicion

The same mixture of wealth and guarded knowledge follows Withers home to Atomer. When a bulette exposes a chamber beneath his estate, Koh finds recent signs of use and a restraint-equipped slab. Withers says the building was once a financial house and that he does not know every old room. The polite explanation leaves the Delvers doubtful. [Cassius](cassius.md) suspects a darker purpose behind his collection and privately finances an investigation disguised as burglary.[^withers-chamber]

The intrusion costs Withers jade sculptures, gems and unusual relics, and his house is subsequently seen burning. According to Cassius, he reports stolen property and hires the Broscale family to investigate. The theft and fire are real losses, while the discovered rooms and artifacts still leave the extent of his knowledge uncertain.[^withers-burglary]

At the Bronze Ball he appears publicly as a donor. That standing is shaken when Khalazar speaks through the afflicted Selenar and accuses him of failing to deliver. Withers answers that he hired the Broscales and opened the doors. His response gives the accusation weight without explaining the terms of his involvement. [Wildfire](buck-broscale-wildfire.md) grabs him and demands an explanation, but both fall through the collapsing floor before the confrontation can continue.[^withers-ball]

### Arc Three — From missing patron to captive

The fall leaves Withers missing until the rescuers find him in the [black pyramid's](../places/dawnless-dunes-pyramid.md) lower workshop. Disheveled and apparently compelled, he labors with Wildfire and other captives on an immense unfinished statue of Khalazar. His place among its workers reveals his survival, without establishing that he designed the structure or freely chose the work.[^withers-captivity]

He is subdued without being killed and carried through Darmah's first completed circle to the guild. Whatever his earlier dealings, the rescue brings him out as a living captive rather than leaving him beneath the collapsing pyramid. It supplies no further account from him of the promises invoked at the Ball.[^withers-rescue]

## Current status

Withers is alive and has been evacuated to the guild, last carried unconscious and stable with the rescued workers. His subsequent recovery, property settlement and legal fate remain unknown. His earlier statement has neither been withdrawn nor explained by a later confession or judgment.[^withers-status]
