# Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers — claimant of Skull Keep

## Introduction

Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers is a prosperous, talkative aristocrat who arrives in the Darkest Delvers' lives with wine on his breath and a determination to recover his family's ancestral holiday residence. His magnificent mustache, carefully chosen velvets and warm cheeks make him appear as much a jovial patron as a serious man of business. He travels in a comfortable coach and expects his employees to recognize the importance of preserving fine furniture while fighting undead.[^introduction]

## Family interests and anxiety

He calls the long-abandoned **Enzo Keep** his property and says he has repurchased it after some hundred and thirty years. A previous bronze group has gone missing, and Alexandre is impatient for results. He promises a generous premium for returning his antiques intact, yet repeatedly cautions his new agents not to stare into the keep's mirrors. These warnings could reflect reports he has heard, family lore or something more; his precise knowledge of the old magic remains unknown.

For all his talk of danger, Alexandre does not attempt the expedition himself. He and Aegis retire to a guest house beyond the keep, leaving the adventurers to negotiate its inhabitants and the flameskull.[^contract]

## The rooms he does not know

In Atomer's financial district, Withers's home is an imposing limestone building with fine columns and a collection of statuary. It was once a financial house, and he acknowledges that its many old rooms may still hold secrets beyond his acquaintance. A burrowing bulette tears the pavement open before its entrance and exposes a chamber below ground. Koh enters and finds a clean stone slab fitted with restraint straps, recent traces of visitors and a thick door into the estate. When asked, Withers politely disclaims knowledge of the room and offers to see to it. The Delvers leave unwilling to accuse him on evidence they cannot yet fully interpret.[^room]

The guildmaster Cassius quietly fears Withers's collection of costly jade and other artifacts may mask an illicit purpose. His earlier contract to clear Skull Keep and his financing of guild ventures now look less innocent to Cassius than they once did. That suspicion provokes a covert investigation involving former thieves, but nothing recovered in the chamber establishes who used it or why. Withers himself remains unaware, at least in played dialogue, of the clandestine assignment intended to enter his estate.[^plot]

## Current status

Alexandre (also **Alexander** in S2E03 narration) **occupies the financial-district Withers Estate and sponsors the approaching Bronze Ball**. His **bulette-damaged grounds** physically expose an **old but recently used restraint chamber**, which **Koh inspects**; the estate-facing door remains **locked and unopened**. Withers **claims not to know its contents** and promises to handle the breach. He is **suspected by Koh and Cassius**, especially because of a **large jade/artifact collection and disputed Skull Keep interests**, but **no physical evidence yet proves Withers's participation in a crime, the purpose of the slab, or his knowledge of any occult operation**. The **Cloven Brotherhood and the Delvers plan an unauthorized covert entry for tonight**, which **has not yet begun**. [Estate](../places/withers-estate.md).[^s2e03]
