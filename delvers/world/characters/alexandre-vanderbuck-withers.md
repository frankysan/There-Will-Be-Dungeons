# Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers — claimant of Skull Keep

## Introduction

Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers is a prosperous, talkative aristocrat who arrives in the Darkest Delvers' lives with wine on his breath and a determination to recover his family's ancestral holiday residence. His magnificent mustache, carefully chosen velvets and warm cheeks make him appear as much a jovial patron as a serious man of business. He travels in a comfortable coach and expects his employees to recognize the importance of preserving fine furniture while fighting undead.[^introduction]

## Family interests and anxiety

He calls the long-abandoned **Enzo Keep** his property and says he has repurchased it after some hundred and thirty years. A previous bronze group has gone missing, and Alexandre is impatient for results. He promises a generous premium for returning his antiques intact, yet repeatedly cautions his new agents not to stare into the keep's mirrors. These warnings could reflect reports he has heard, family lore or something more; his precise knowledge of the old magic remains unknown.

For all his talk of danger, Alexandre does not attempt the expedition himself. He and Aegis retire to a guest house beyond the keep, leaving the adventurers to negotiate its inhabitants and the flameskull.[^contract]

## Current status

Alexandre receives the returning Darkest Delvers after they destroy the skull and leaves with them for the guild city. Damage to the estate is evaluated over the following days, and the adventurers are told of a **reduced preservation bonus**. He is not shown recovering the keepsakes, inspecting Enzo's mirror or explaining any family connection to the old necromancer beyond their shared surname.[^aftermath]
