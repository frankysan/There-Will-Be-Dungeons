# Krandal Splenderbelt — cleric

<a id="overview"></a>

## Introduction

Krandal Splenderbelt is an imposing half-orc cleric of Pelor who believes promised work ought to be finished. He prefers Splenderbelt or Sir Splenderbelt until friendship warrants his first name. Punctual and prepared, he brings patience and heavy supplies to his first [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md) assignment. His seriousness coexists with a fondness for potatoes, difficulty remembering names and an unease about what might be waiting overhead.[^krandal-introduction][^krandal-commentary]

### Arc One — Service and the care it requires

Krandal's resolve depends on others more than his formidable appearance suggests. [Evelyn](evelyn-grinkeeper.md) saves him from blood-draining creatures and later from the gricks; he restores and protects her in turn. [Koh's](koh-spiros.md) mistaken blow in the mountain crypt makes their dependence painfully clear. Krandal survives through stubbornness and healing, while giving his companions the guidance, shelter and practical help that allow them to continue.[^krandal-first-trust]

His affection for vulnerable creatures can exceed his attention to them. He revives a trapped thrush, names it Yuri and carries it away, only to forget the weakened bird in his pocket. When he recognizes the neglect, he accepts Jimmy's help rather than insisting on keeping it. The incident complicates his wish to be a caretaker: goodwill alone does not finish the work.[^krandal-yuri]

At [Pelor's temple](../places/temple-of-pelor.md), service brings both discipline and trust. Brother Maynard rebukes his showmanship and requests for payment, but comes to rely on his concern for two unresponsive elves. Krandal records a tapping finger and studies its possible meaning instead of pretending that faith has supplied an answer. He wants to be useful even when the sacred institution around him cannot explain the suffering before it.[^krandal-temple]

That concern extends to enemies. He insists on carrying a captured fish wizard alive, staunches Broadside's wounds after defeating him and heals Solburn beneath Noble Hollow. Mercy means taking responsibility for people whose safety is inconvenient. Alongside these duties he saves toward a potato farm, imagining another way to sustain life beyond the dungeon.[^krandal-mercy]

### Arc Two — Doubt and a place to grow

A deed to the ruined riverside tower gives Krandal's farming ambition a physical home. Where his friends see wreckage, he sees land to cultivate. A sea hag confronts him with an image of himself abandoned by Pelor, exposing doubts that his imposing manner cannot hide. His companions help him out of the despair. He begins clearing irrigation, planting potatoes and turning a talking minotaur skull into an improbable watchman.[^krandal-land]

The desire for security also draws him into compromised work. As **Cobra Commander**, he enters Withers Estate with Evelyn's little masks covering his face-carved maces. He heals his fellow burglars, privately pockets a valuable gem and later hides stolen wealth on his property. His compassion does not place him outside the theft. At Gemholm he asks for a transformed villager to be restrained, but the request is not relayed and he does not physically prevent the killing.[^krandal-choices]

His temple work eventually finds a breakthrough through cooperation rather than certainty. With Koh opening a path into his wife's dream and Evelyn protecting the effort, Krandal removes the curse that restores her recognition. Her awakening turns a long, unresolved duty into help he can see. He receives Silver standing as **Splenderbelt the Efficient**, although neither a title nor success answers every question about his calling.[^krandal-awakening]

He also begins becoming an employer, accepting the exiled [Glepo Ma](gleepo-ma.md) as a farmhand. The strange affection of [Princess Hissia](princess-hissia.md) confronts him with a different unfinished obligation: a fortnight on Snake Island that he cannot fully remember. Her claims of their intimacy do not restore the missing history.[^krandal-obligations]

### Arc Three — Making nourishment a promise kept

Farming offers Krandal a tangible purpose as Atomer's prices rise. He wants to sell potatoes affordably while supporting the land and its workers. He adds a barn, hires more help and resists a large investment proposal, but his absences expose a serious failure: the workers receive food and shelter while their wages remain unpaid. [Nopa Ropa](nopa-ropa.md) also waits for him to honor the search for the missing Hissia.[^krandal-farm]

A cranium-rat prison turns those failures into an imagined revolt and eviction. The workers have not actually rebelled, yet the fear forces Krandal to confront what he has left undone. The imprisoned [Radiant Lance](radiant-lance.md) becomes an unexpected companion there. After Evelyn frees him, Krandal wears the dead knight's surviving tabard and resolves to pay his workers and attend to his promises. [Barclay's](barclay-vandergeist.md) cheerful devotion to Ptah later sharpens his questions about his own religious life without making him abandon Pelor.[^krandal-reckoning]

He finally returns to distribute wages and gives Nopa the lead he has received about Hissia. He recruits nine ancient skeletal minotaurs and restores Chad Brad's skeletal body, accepting that ending Khalazar's curse will also end their animation. Pelor's gift of a potato-headed [Devotee's Censer](../items/devotees-censer.md) joins his agricultural hopes to his work as a healer.[^krandal-repair]

The rescue brings those obligations together. Hissia is recovered, and Krandal helps evacuate the enslaved townspeople. Khalazar nearly kills him; Koh's potion saves him in time to heal Evelyn. Together the three strike the tyrant's heart. When the curse breaks, all ten minotaurs return to inert bones. His farm companion's farewell is part of the freedom they have won.[^krandal-finale]

## Abilities and care

Krandal's magic heals injuries, protects companions and turns undead, while a spectral face mace carries his familiar fighting style beyond arm's reach. These gifts have limits: an early attempt to relieve a sleeper does not wake him, and healing cannot repair every injury. His gentleness extends to animals, though care must be learned. The hook-horror egg he keeps eventually produces Scroungy, whose attachment precedes any training. A visiting temple mouse remains a free-roaming stray.[^krandal-abilities]

## Current status

Over the year after Khalazar's defeat, the cleansed land supports a flourishing potato farm. Krandal pays his debts and workers, hires more help and continues healing townspeople. Scroungy becomes a docile cart-puller alongside his donkey. The rebuilt [Delvers tower](../places/splenderbelt-farm-delvers-tower.md) stands on the farm as a headquarters. He survives as both cleric and farmer, with practical nourishment an enduring expression of his care.[^krandal-status]
