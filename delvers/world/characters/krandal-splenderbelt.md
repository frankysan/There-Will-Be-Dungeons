# Krandal Splenderbelt — cleric

**Identity:** Scott's half-orc, half-human cleric. **Spoilers:** Delvers S1E01. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional; working spelling **Krandal Splenderbelt** comes from matching archived sheet-title evidence, not listened dialogue. ASR aliases include `Crandall`, `Splendorbelt`, `Splunderbelt` and `splender belt`.

## Overview

Krandal is a tall, imposing but even-tempered newcomer to the [Delvers guild](../places/delvers-guild.md). Scott describes him as determined to finish work he has promised, even when others have not been fully candid with him. He prefers **Splenderbelt** or **Sir Splenderbelt**; his first name is for close friends. His devotion to the god rendered `Palor/Paylor` is represented by a large necklace normally tucked beneath his clothes. The deity's spelling remains open.

His serious approach coexists with a fondness for potatoes, difficulty remembering names and an aversion to ceilings that becomes acute after Riley's warning. By the end of his first job he has survived repeated blood-draining attacks, helped seal the farm fissure, received forty gold and accepted a room at the guild. The overnight rest has not been played through.
