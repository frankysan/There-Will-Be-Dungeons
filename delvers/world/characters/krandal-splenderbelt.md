# Krandal Splenderbelt — cleric

## Overview

Tall and imposing, Krandal is a half-orc, half-human cleric whose seriousness rests on a simple obligation: work promised ought to be finished. He prefers Splenderbelt or Sir Splenderbelt until a friendship warrants his first name. A large holy necklace usually stays beneath his clothes. His even temper coexists with a fondness for potatoes, difficulty remembering names and a growing distrust of ceilings.[^identity]

## Development and relationships

### The first guild job

Punctuality and preparation distinguish Krandal from his more hurried companion [Evelyn](evelyn-grinkeeper.md). He waits with descriptions of his assigned partners, declines unpaid work and shoulders the heavy supplies needed to seal the [farm cave](../places/farm-cave.md). A patron's warning about ceilings unsettles him, although he does not share it with the others until they are underground.

His resolve proves compatible with dependence on others. Blood-draining creatures nearly overcome him, and Evelyn's healing keeps him on his feet; he in turn heals and protects her. Engulfed by a darkmantle, he follows her voice out of its darkness and brings it down with sacred radiance. He later steadies [Koh's](koh-spiros.md) descent with rope and divine guidance, completes the concrete seal and supports Koh's claim to the recovered globe. Responsibility, for Krandal, includes both finishing the job and acknowledging another person's risk.[^cave]

### Faith, fallibility and mutual care

At Kord's mountain sanctuary, Krandal's faith becomes a practical defense against creatures that drain the living. He recognizes their vulnerability to sacred radiance, but knowledge does not make him invulnerable. Koh's mistaken strike fells him, and Evelyn must restore him; when a Shadow later incapacitates her, he returns that care. His endurance also carries him through a kettlebell blow that would otherwise have left him helpless.

His contribution reaches beyond surviving combat. He helps cleanse the fountains, tends the barely breathing Sultan and lends Koh the guidance needed to complete the final trial. He is attracted by better-paid work, yet accepts the guild's requirement that a chosen job be undertaken promptly. His developing partnership depends on the same mixture of stubbornness, healing and cooperation that sustained their first assignment.[^crypt]

## Current status

Krandal has returned to the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md) after the sanctuary's cleansing and received his share of the reward. The party has access to a wider job board, but has yet to choose its next undertaking.[^status]
