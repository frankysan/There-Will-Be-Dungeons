# Cheet — a cleric's dangerous curiosity

## Introduction

Cheet is a human spellcaster of the Delvers Dungeon Guild's silver ranks, distinguished by the title she has earned for cheating death. Her fair, curly blond hair and smaller frame conceal a willingness to look where more cautious guild members would turn away. She names herself a cleric of the death-associated goddess Wee Jas. In conversation her curiosity about ancient magic seems stronger than her concern for what might happen if it is used.[^name]

## Robes, prisoners and the truth circle

Near the kuo-toa lake she joins a heavily armored fighter on orders from a darkmantle-cowled researcher. The two are to wait for the Darkest Delvers and seize whatever they have brought from beneath the water. Cheet rummages through their saddlebags and pockets a healing potion, unaware that Koh is approaching silently from behind. His first psychic strike overwhelms her but deliberately leaves her alive. The party restrains and gags her and later puts her inside a prisoner carriage with Broadside and the fish wizard. She rouses once during the long journey, only to be knocked unconscious again before she can escape.[^ambush]

At the guild she sits within a circle of truth. Broadside seems frightened by what happened to him, while Cheet talks openly of discovering a ritual deep underground. She explains that a sacrificed elf, devoted priests and an ancient summoning stone could call up a dreadful creature, yet insists she only wished to witness the result. When the Delvers are preparing to stop the researcher at the Noble Hollow, she volunteers to accompany them and perhaps interfere at the last moment. The others refuse, judging her fascination more dangerous than useful.[^zone]

## Current status

Cheet is **alive and held by the guild**, having **not been released to join the midnight pursuit**. After the threatened summoning is broken, the guild decides that **rehabilitation should follow**, rather than treating her exactly as the two silver-ranked colleagues it believes were coerced. No actual rehabilitation, recovery or discharge is played. Her true intentions beyond what she professes and the degree of magical influence over her actions are unresolved. Her guild name is ASR-provisional: the exports also render **Cheat**, with **Mary/Merry** as an ordinary name.[^status]
