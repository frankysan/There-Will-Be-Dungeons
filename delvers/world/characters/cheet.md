# Cheet — a cleric's dangerous curiosity

## Introduction

Cheet first encounters the Darkest Delvers as a robed spellcaster searching their belongings near the kuo-toa lake. Smaller than her armored companion, she pockets one of Koh's healing potions before he reaches her. Her identity and reasons for waiting there remain unknown to the Delvers.[^cheet-introduction]

## Development by arc

<a id="robes-prisoners-and-the-truth-circle"></a>

### Arc One — Curiosity, captivity and judgment

Koh's psychic strike leaves Cheet alive but unconscious. The Delvers restrain her and bring her toward the guild; when she wakes during the journey, Evelyn knocks her out again. There, the fair-haired human identifies herself as a cleric of Wee Jas and a Silver guild member. She and [Broadside](broadside.md) had been following the instructions of researcher Marek Gideon, but her explanation of the danger differs from his frightened account of lost memories.[^cheet-capture]

Inside the guild's truth circle, Cheet describes a ritual that uses an elf's sacrifice, priestly devotion and a summoning stone to call a creature from the lake. She says curiosity drew her to the discovery and that she wanted to witness its result. Her explanation emphasizes the discovery rather than the safety of those it would endanger. When she volunteers to accompany the rescuers and perhaps interfere, the Delvers refuse to release her. Her fascination has cost her their trust.[^cheet-curiosity]

After the rite is interrupted, the guild chooses rehabilitation for Cheet while accepting coercive influence as grounds to clear Broadside and Solburn. The different decision leaves questions about her responsibility unresolved rather than establishing that every part of her account was either honest or false.[^cheet-judgment]

### Arc Two — A reported reassignment

Broadside later tells the Delvers that Cheet has been reprimanded and sent to clerical duty at the Academy. Cassius also recalls her reprimand while considering the possibility of an insider threat. She remains a subject of concern, without appearing again to explain her conduct or show what that work has changed.[^cheet-reassignment]

## Current status

Cheet is last directly known alive in guild custody. The later report places her in Academy clerical work, but her rehabilitation, subsequent choices and circumstances at the campaign's end remain unknown.[^cheet-status]
