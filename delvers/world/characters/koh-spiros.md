# Koh Spiros — rogue

## Overview

Koh is a quietly observant satyr, already inside the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md) when his assigned companions arrive. Dark hair, bright blue eyes, a large purple scarf and flowing white clothing with crimson leather and brass details distinguish him. A sword and dagger hang at his waist. His quiet hooves suit his preference for remaining unnoticed, although he needs light to see underground.[^identity]

## Development and custody

### Caution and initiative

On the first expedition, Koh keeps to the edge of [Evelyn's](evelyn-grinkeeper.md) torchlight and takes opportunities to strike while the creatures are occupied. He also handles the less dramatic business of adventuring: buying tools, consulting his guide, gathering proof of kills and submitting it at the guild. Cassius hints at earlier dealings with him, but Koh offers no account that establishes a criminal past.

His caution allows decisive risks. With [Krandal](krandal-splenderbelt.md) holding the rope and lending divine guidance, he descends beneath the [farm cave](../places/farm-cave.md) to retrieve a dead delver's badge and glass orb. He alone sees a serpentine creature approaching during his climb back and urges the others to seal the passage. He later describes it to Cassius, who calls it a grick. His companions agree that the recovered [drift globe](../items/drift-globe.md) should be his. He declines a guild room because he must return home, though that home remains unseen.[^cave]

### Resourcefulness and reliance

The next morning, Koh admits that his earlier mysterious voice was an affectation. His reserve gives way to a more visible mixture of ingenuity and fallibility. Exhausted by the sanctuary stairs, he still runs the ten laps required by its first trial while his companions defend him. In the weight room, a strike meant for a Shadow instead fells Krandal, leaving Evelyn to restore their companion.

Koh's recovered globe becomes crucial when he calls forth daylight against the Shadows. He later finishes Studwell and, despite his diminished strength, rings the final bell with help from Krandal's guidance. The trials expose how much his individual successes depend on cooperation. He retrieves Sultan's enchanted sash but offers it to the group, treating a new reward as a shared resource rather than insisting that finding it makes it his alone.[^crypt]

## Current status

Koh has returned to the guild with the drift globe and has physically retrieved Sultan's sash for the party. Exclusive long-term ownership of the sash remains unsettled. The second reward is paid, and the next assignment has yet to be chosen.[^status]
