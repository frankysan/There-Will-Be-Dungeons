# Koh Spiros — rogue

**Identity:** Jon's satyr rogue. **Spoilers:** Delvers S1E01. **Evidence:** ASR-provisional. **Koh** has cast-written support; **Spiros** appears in matching archived sheet previews. ASR aliases include `Co`, `Ko`, `Cole`, `co spiros` and `co-spyros`. Later preview nicknames are outside this horizon.

## Overview

Koh is already waiting inside the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md) when his companions meet outside. He wears a large purple scarf, flowing white clothing with crimson leather and brass details, and a sword and dagger at his waist. He has dark hair, bright blue eyes and quiet hooves. He works at the edge of Evelyn's torchlight; Jon explicitly says he **does not have darkvision**.

His caution makes him the observer and opportunistic attacker of the first expedition. He collects proof from defeated creatures, consults his guide, and descends alone to recover a dead delver's badge and glass orb. At the cutoff he has earned forty gold, retains the identified [drift globe](../items/drift-globe.md), and says he must go home rather than take a guild room. His home itself is not shown.
