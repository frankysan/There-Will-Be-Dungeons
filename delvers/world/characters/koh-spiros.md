# Koh Spiros — rogue

<a id="overview"></a>

## Introduction

Koh Spiros is a quietly observant satyr who prefers to notice danger before it notices him. Dark hair, bright blue eyes and a large purple scarf distinguish his white-and-crimson clothing. His quiet hooves help him remain unobtrusive, although the darkness beneath Atomer initially leaves him dependent on his companions' light. At the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md) he watches, asks practical questions and attends to equipment while his new partners make a more conspicuous entrance.[^koh-introduction]

### Arc One — Secrecy and shared trust

Koh's reserve protects a household in distress. [His wife](anthela-spiros.md) lies unresponsive, and the former family fortune has been spent on unsuccessful treatment. He conceals both their poverty and his dangerous employment, returning to [Penelope](penelope-spiros.md) in clean clothes with adventures disguised as stories. The search for a cure gives his resourcefulness an urgent purpose that his companions do not initially understand.

Working with [Krandal](krandal-splenderbelt.md) and [Evelyn](evelyn-grinkeeper.md) exposes the limits of self-reliance. Koh's mistaken strike injures Krandal in the mountain crypt; he later apologizes. His climbing sash and arrows help save the cleric among the gricks, while Evelyn's healing rescues Koh from his own trap-crossing experiment. He shares useful discoveries and learns when retreat serves his friends better than another risk.[^koh-first-trust]

The ancient [Soul Knife](../items/runic-sword-hilt.md) gives shape to a talent that grows beyond its physical hilt. [Carrie](carrie-underworld.md) helps him understand the weapon, and [Wildfire](buck-broscale-wildfire.md) teaches him silent communication. Koh questions warnings about psychic hunters rather than treating them as certainty. His greatest use for the new gift is personal: reaching his wife's mind, where she recognizes him before fear and droning darkness force him out. He keeps the encounter private. Elsewhere he argues for living prisoners, offers protection to Krandal and notices the endangered Faelan when the others might be celebrating. His caution increasingly serves people rather than simply keeping himself unseen.[^koh-first-powers]

### Arc Two — Old obligations and a new purpose

The missing Snake Island fortnight leaves Koh's household exposed to an old associate. [Chuck Abnaz](chuck-abnaz.md) has paid the doctors and uses that help to draw him toward the Cloven Brotherhood. Koh refuses a return at first, then accepts a role coordinating the Withers burglary. Discovering Cassius behind its funding gives him an investigative purpose, but does not erase his participation in theft. He takes jade, conceals a magical bag and accepts money Evelyn declines. His gifts serve both his friends and the criminal connections he cannot easily dismiss.[^koh-second-choices]

His preference for taking captives has limits in practice. At Gemholm he captures Ambermoor alive, yet does not relay Krandal's request to restrain the transformed Hank Tom before Evelyn kills him. Coordinating the party through thought makes Koh influential in deciding what information reaches whom.[^koh-second-mercy]

Returning to his wife's mind, he finds her preparing for a coerced wedding. A remembered ring restores her recognition only briefly. He brings Krandal into another patient's dream to test a remedy, then accepts the danger of a shared attempt to free his wife. When the cure succeeds, Koh carries her toward escape and stays for his companions. Their survival depends on his protection as well as theirs.

Her physical awakening changes his purpose. He tells her how the family's money vanished and how guild work, including the dubious Withers venture, sustained his search. Choosing the Silver name One Shot, he resolves to keep helping others. At the Bronze Ball his silent reassurance keeps her connected to their family amid Khalazar's renewed assault.[^koh-second-rescue]

### Arc Three — Protection, defiance and responsibility

Koh's rescue of his wife does not make all his choices straightforward. Grounded by the guild, he steals from wealthy deposit boxes at the damaged bank despite Krandal's objection, then seeks the Brotherhood's help fencing the gems. He also tells Penelope the truth about One Shot. The heroic identity he concealed at home becomes something his daughter can knowingly share.[^koh-third-family]

Helping afflicted elves gives his powers a wider use. He supports Krandal's dream rescues and learns that some shallower compulsions can be broken without entering a mental prison. Anathala willingly helps Carrie develop a blood-and-crystal detector, and Koh seeks Veggie Sticks's aid in improving it. A smaller home and nightly mental companionship give the recovering family a different stability; a missed night later shows how much reassurance his wife still needs.

Koh remains guarded with Dumbledwark, refusing a truth-bound interview, and forges a pass to investigate while general guild restrictions persist. The All Rat exploits his fear of becoming a hunted criminal and losing his family. Evelyn frees him from that false life. His subsequent seizure of the collective's urn turns a growing ability to teleport into a means of escape and recovery.[^koh-third-trust]

He backs Evelyn's public challenge to the guild and helps bring Cassius home without killing Zoltar. In the final expedition, stealth serves rescue: he distinguishes hostile guards from compelled civilians and disables the latter without killing them. When Krandal nearly dies, Koh's potion gives the cleric the chance to heal Evelyn. He joins both friends in the final thrust through Khalazar's heart. The work that began with his family's need has become a shared obligation to Atomer.[^koh-third-service]

## Abilities and limitations

Koh's psychic blades can hurt without leaving ordinary cuts, and his silent speech links companions across separation and noise. His gift develops from a recovered hilt into blades he can summon, throw and use in teleportation. These abilities do not make him self-sufficient: the paired stone golems prove impervious to his psychic attacks, his mental links have limits, and healing another person's imprisonment requires allies. He combines these gifts with forgery, careful scouting, magical tools and ordinary steel.[^koh-abilities]

## Current status

Koh survives the fall of Khalazar and returns to Anathala and Penelope. During the following year he chiefly teaches younger guild adventurers, helping novices and those with psychic potential. Anathala's Will rests above the family hearth as a memorial to the struggle that brought them home. His enduring role is now to pass on what he learned while remaining present for the family who first led him to the guild.[^koh-status]
