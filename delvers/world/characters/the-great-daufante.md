# The Great Daufante — illusionist of Teppensteppe Hall

## Introduction

The Great Daufante cultivates a theatrical presence: purple-blue robes, a jewelled skull mask and a dark beard threaded with silver turn his arrival into a performance. His unusual watch pulses toward what he seeks. He travels with the undead Connor, his former assistant, and the spider familiar Hamlet, expecting attention and service as readily as he offers a flourish.[^introduction]

### Arc Two — Showmanship, persuasion and a prized deck

The Hoopers hire Daufante separately to conceal supernatural disturbances at their wedding. His interests extend beyond keeping the guests amused. He claims magical artifacts should belong to a wizard able to use them, provoking objections from the Delvers. The job begins with cooperation under disputed terms about who should keep its discoveries.[^contract]

Performance serves both his public and private purposes. He entertains guests with an invented adventure while Hamlet gathers rumors and Connor searches the grounds. He helps Evelyn move unseen when the ceremony falters, but treats Aegis more imperiously, demanding that the driver taste his tea first. The entourage supports both his spectacle and his investigations.[^entourage]

When a rumor points to Goodwine's old crime, Daufante uses charm and a staged rescue to draw the elder into a private confession. His comforting manner helps obtain the account of Aurelli Hooper's murder. The encounter shows persuasion operating through deception and magical influence, with the confession kept from the wedding crowd.[^confession]

Daufante's star-bright missiles defeat the wight after Krandal stops its walking tree. He then recovers the Deck of Many Things without drawing a card. Keeping it matters more to him than the contract money: he accepts Krandal's demand to surrender his reward share in exchange for the deck. His appetite for artifacts survives the compromise with his fellow adventurers.[^deck]

## Current status

Daufante leaves the following day with the deck packed away, Connor and Hamlet still associated with him. His theatre invitation to Evelyn remains an offer. Any later meeting or use of the cards remains unknown.[^status]
