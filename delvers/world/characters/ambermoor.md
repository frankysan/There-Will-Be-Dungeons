# Ambermoor — Gemholm operations manager and experimenter

## Introduction

<a id="the-prosperous-man-on-the-sandbar"></a>

Ambermoor manages the gem-dredging settlement of [Gemholm](../places/gemholm.md) on Lake Absul. His carpeted, comfortable quarters stand apart from the workers' exposed cottages. When the Delvers reach the damaged colony, its people are missing, dead or transformed, and the manager himself is nowhere to be found.[^earlier]

## Development by arc

<a id="the-private-diaries"></a>

### Arc Two — Experimental ambition brought into custody

The workers' journals connect Ambermoor to drinks given before frightening changes in their bodies. His own writings reveal an ambition to distill a substance from chaos gems, accelerate transformation and sell the resulting creatures as weapons. He writes of colleagues as carriers and of science officer Neko as material for a perfected being. Commercial prospects matter more in that account than the lives placed at risk.[^diaries]

<a id="beneath-the-lake"></a>

The Delvers find him in a laboratory beneath the lake: an elf in dark goggles and a swimsuit, drawing black fluid from a sedated, thorn-covered slaad. Other prepared syringes lie nearby. The creature bears Neko's name, while Ambermoor celebrates the value of his work.[^lab]

An alarm interrupts that control. He threatens the intruders with a crossbow, but Koh's attack knocks him unconscious. Stripped of his component pouch and vehicle key, bound and gagged, the experimenter becomes their captive. His samples are taken as evidence. The Neko-labelled specimen remains in its tank and is killed after restorative magic produces no visible change.[^capture]

<a id="earlier-s2e07-cutoff-retained-historically"></a>

Ambermoor is taken first to Krandal's farm, while the surviving colony's safety remains uncertain. His removal ends his immediate control of the laboratory without repairing everything his experiments have set in motion.[^farm]

<a id="later-s2e08-continuity-bridge-not-an-in-scene-handoff"></a>

He subsequently reaches the Guild's holding cells, where an inquiry under truth magic is arranged. The intended examination leaves the outcome of his captivity unresolved.[^custody]

## Current status

Ambermoor remains alive in Guild custody; the outcome of the intended inquiry and his later fate are unknown.[^custody]
