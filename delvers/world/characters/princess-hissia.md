# Princess Hissia — Snake Island royal visitor

<a id="visual-appearance"></a>

## Introduction

Princess Hissia arrives at the Bronze Ball as a surprise donor from Snake Island, escorted by two snake attendants beating drums. Tall and slender, with long dark hair, she has a largely human appearance distinguished by yellow-green eyes with narrow serpent pupils. A fitted purple silk gown, gold medallions and chains, and a small snake-decorated tiara mark her formal entrance. She greets [Krandal Splenderbelt](krandal-splenderbelt.md) with an affection he cannot fully explain.[^hissia-introduction]

## Development by arc

<a id="relationship-claim-and-gift-provenance-not-comprehensive-memory-recovery"></a>

### Arc Two — Familiarity without shared memory

Hissia calls Krandal her beloved and draws him into a dance. She speaks as someone whose history with him is intimate and recent, while he struggles to reconstruct the fortnight he lost on Snake Island. She identifies her father as King Hissia, links the royal serpent staff to him and says Krandal's snake armor belonged to her brother, who died during their adventures. Her confidence meets his uncertainty rather than a fully shared recollection.[^hissia-claims]

His fragments of memory include an island promise involving the princess and a frightening snake in a bedroom, followed by her affectionate greeting. They leave the nature of the relationship unresolved. Hissia's presence at the Ball makes her a real visitor beyond those memories, but does not supply the missing history. When the celebration falls into violence, her whereabouts are lost amid the attack.[^hissia-memory]

### Arc Three — Captivity and a forbidden reunion

[Nopa Ropa](nopa-ropa.md), a royal guard devoted to her, reports that Hissia has been taken below Atomer and presses Krandal to find her. The search initially depends on testimony. A mind-reader at [Deep Winds Spa](../places/deep-winds-spa.md) claims to know where she is held, then later tells Krandal that she labors on Khalazar's great machine in the Dawnless Dunes. Those words give the rescuers a direction before they have seen her.[^hissia-search]

Inside the [black pyramid](../places/dawnless-dunes-pyramid.md), a woman in a tattered purple dress, with slit eyes and scales at her neck, fans the withered figure on the upper bed. Resemblance first raises hope; closer recognition establishes that she is Hissia. Koh renders her unconscious without killing her, and Nopa carries her down to the escape circle. She reaches the guild alive after Khalazar's defeat. Waking under healing, she recognizes Nopa and acknowledges a love they both call forbidden. Their kiss reveals a bond distinct from the uncertain history she shares with Krandal.[^hissia-rescue]

## Current status

Hissia is alive, freed from captivity and last known reunited with Nopa at the guild. Their later residence and future together remain unknown. Neither the reunion nor her earlier account of Snake Island establishes a marriage to either man.[^hissia-status]
