# Carrie Underworld — artificer of the guild

## Introduction

Carrie Underworld works behind the acquisitions counter of Atomer's [Delvers Dungeon Guild](../places/delvers-guild.md), eager to examine discoveries that ordinary adventurers struggle to understand. A red-horned tiefling with brown hair, purple eyes, large goggles and stained work clothes, she meets an unfamiliar artifact with delighted curiosity. Her knowledge makes her useful to the newcomers, while their discoveries give her opportunities to test what she knows.[^carrie-introduction]

### Arc One — Curiosity brought into the field

The hilt recovered beneath the forest gives Carrie an early encounter with the limits of her own skill. She tentatively calls it the [Soul Knife](../items/runic-sword-hilt.md) and can coax only a spark from it. When [Koh](koh-spiros.md) summons a cyan blade, her astonishment becomes encouragement. She returns the weapon and points him toward Buck Broscale as someone who may understand his developing psychic gifts. She is a guide willing to recognize abilities she cannot reproduce.[^carrie-blade]

Her work soon reaches beyond the counter. At [Pelor's temple](../places/temple-of-pelor.md), she examines ancient stonework and accepts the Delvers' specimens for study. When they expose a droning shaft and an elf collapses nearby, she agrees to cover it again while its danger remains uncertain. Curiosity does not require immediate descent.[^carrie-temple]

At the Academy, that practical experience earns little respect. She accompanies [Cassius](cassius.md), weighing down unruly scrolls with recovered artifacts as the board belittles her standing. The evidence she has helped gather fails to secure the aid he requests. She returns toward Atomer with him, still facing questions that institutional authority has not answered.[^carrie-academy]

### Arc Two — Research in service of a family

Withers's recovered records and relics give Carrie a larger task than identifying another useful treasure. She studies damaged writing, strange fluid and a stone music box, then presents an account connecting the ruins, Khalazar's titles and the [Hereditary Drone](../items/hereditary-drone.md). Her explanations offer the guild a direction, while incomplete translations and an unidentified device leave important gaps. Her enthusiasm produces sustained research rather than immediate certainty.[^carrie-research]

The same willingness to experiment supports Koh's search for his wife. Carrie suggests curse-breaking after an earlier treatment brings only temporary relief. She volunteers to enter Anathala's dream, arrives equipped for danger and helps transport her to the laboratory. Koh's limited psychic link leaves Carrie outside, watching the bodies instead. When Anathala wakes, the success depends on work Carrie helped prepare but could not witness. Evelyn gives her a red shard brought back from the dream, opening another question for study.[^carrie-rescue]

### Arc Three — Useful experiments and quiet support

With Anathala's agreement, Carrie conducts repeated blood tests and uses her blood with the recovered crystal to make a [detector](../items/khalazar-sensory-amulet.md) for Koh. It responds near afflicted elves, and Veggie Sticks helps add a directional indicator. Her hypothesis about Anathala's ancestry remains uncertain, but the device gives the search a practical tool. Koh declines riskier sleep experiments; the collaboration continues within that limit.[^carrie-detector]

She also helps interpret the threats returning from below. A hollowed attacker brought from Krandal's farm prompts examination rather than a certain identification of its master. Later, recovered badges let her name three lost Silver adventurers, and she identifies their surviving equipment. She suggests a connection between the discovered stairs and an old map without treating it as settled. When the grounded Delvers discuss another unauthorized descent, she leaves room for their initiative while acknowledging the restrictions. Her support has become discreet as well as technical.[^carrie-support]

## Current status

Carrie is last known working at the guild laboratory, helping the Delvers understand their discoveries and prepare for further exploration. Koh holds the detector she helped make. Her own location and work after the final rescue are unknown; no separate outcome is known for her in the following year.[^carrie-status]
