# Carrie Underworld — artificer of the guild

## Introduction

Carrie Underworld works behind the acquisitions counter of the Delvers Dungeon Guild, among objects ordinary adventurers rarely know how to judge. A red-horned tiefling with brown hair, large goggles, purple eyes and the stained clothing of her craft, she is quicker to delight in a discovery than to conceal her curiosity. She carries a stranger's artifact as carefully as a gift, and her eager manner betrays how seldom she gets to present an important examination herself.[^introduction]

## The Soul Knife

When the unusual hilt recovered from the forest dungeon reaches her, Carrie finds that neither its stone-metal substance nor its runes resemble familiar work. She offers the name **Soul Knife**, while acknowledging alternative translations such as *mind sword* or *psi blade*. The hilt seems unsentient. Carrie can call forth only a tiny flicker of light, but recognizes that it would be capable of more in other hands.

Koh Spiros shows her how much more. A bright cyan blade springs from the hilt at his thought, to Carrie's delighted astonishment. She has no answer to every question it raises, but speaks with pleasure of its material and psychic nature. She later identifies the watching masked figure as a high-ranking mage and suggests Buck Broscale might know something about awakened psionic gifts. Her fascination makes her a useful guide, though her explanations of relics and classes are not all proved beyond her own experience.[^blade]

## A city of unanswered machines

When the floors of Pelor's temple open over old worked stone, Carrie brings her instruments to examine the ancient threshold. She delights in finding a familiar design beneath the sanctuary, yet she does not pretend that the hidden structures have yielded their purpose. The Delvers bring her further evidence from below—a fragment of a monstrous hooked limb, the strange gelatin from a slain cube, an ancient psychic weapon and a horn that releases thunder. She accepts the findings for the guild to examine and encourages the party to help search for the same symbols hidden in the temple's main sanctuary.[^s9]

There Carrie watches Evelyn reveal an old stone carving, and Krandal expose a deep, droning shaft beneath the altar. Her curiosity is checked by the sudden collapse of a nearby elf and by the uncertainty of the shaft's depth. She agrees that the wooden cover should be replaced until the guild can give the discovery proper attention. More than ever, her work has become a search for the forgotten relationship between the city's faith and the stone chambers below.[^s9]

## Standing before the Academy

Carrie's most daunting task takes her away from the familiar safety of the acquisitions counter. She travels through the night with Cassius to the Dungeon Guild Academy, carrying rolls of evidence and the curious objects recovered by the bronze companies. The grandeur of its Radiant Lance Theatre and the crowd of accomplished guildmasters offer little welcome to a young artificer who can scarcely make her scrolls lie flat. She weighs them down with the horn and the rune-marked shafts the Delvers have entrusted to her. When a board member calls her merely a low-level artificer and orders her to be silent, the rebuke reveals the distance between the people discovering dangers and those deciding how much protection they deserve. She bears the dismissal and later asks Cassius what they will do. Together they set out for home without the gold-ranked help he sought.[^s2e00]

## Earlier status (through S2E06)

In the **played S2E06 debrief** Carrie examines **Withers's anatomical/financial record book, mysterious ancient parchment, assembled music box, mirror, black vials and smashed ritual implements** in her Atomer lab. She identifies a **Hat of Mending**, **kuo-toa summoner staff** and **bullywug shaman bell trousers**. She interprets `Khalazar/Kalizar` as an old-Elvish **title/position** and the map phrase as essentially **“Khalazar rise again”**; exact phonetic spelling and deeper historical referent remain open. Her tests on **previous** black sludge samples suggest the substance dries to **black sand and water** and responds to probing and nearby emotion. She promises **Mending of damaged parchment and a two-night reading/presentation of the book**; those results **have not yet occurred**. Her hypothesis that Koh's psychic link could help Rianu Keeves or his sleeping wife **has not been tested**. [S2E06](../../episodes/S2E06.md).

Carrie is a **guild artificer and examiner** entrusted with the Delvers' unusual discoveries. The **Horn of Blasting and at least one psychic spear shaft are physically carried to and exhibited before the Academy board** during a narrated interlude set while the Delvers are at the lake. Other specimens previously entrusted to guild staff remain under guild examination unless returned in a later played scene. **She and Cassius depart the Academy in Aegis's coach**, but their actual arrival in Atomer is not shown in S2E00.
