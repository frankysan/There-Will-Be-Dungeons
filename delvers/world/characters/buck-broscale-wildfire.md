# Buck Broscale, called Wildfire — psionic mentor

## Introduction

Buck Broscale, known among the [guild's](../places/delvers-guild.md) Silver adventurers as Wildfire, is a towering red dragonborn with a formidable axe and a hard gaze. He first encounters Krandal as a patient at Pelor's temple, where the cleric cures the fiendish poison troubling him. His imposing presence belongs to an experienced adventurer, though he too has needed someone else's help.[^wildfire-introduction]

### Arc One — Grief and a demanding mentorship

Wildfire seeks out [Koh](koh-spiros.md) because the globe recovered underground belonged to his dead brother Brom. He admits that he pushed his brother toward dangers for which he was unprepared. The discovery touches guilt as well as grief, yet he allows Koh to keep the light-bearing relic. The living Buck and the dead adventurer are separate people, connected by a loss the newcomer has unexpectedly brought back to him.[^wildfire-brother]

Recognizing Koh's psychic potential gives their meeting another purpose. Wildfire tests whether the satyr can speak without words, then offers private instruction. He briefly reveals his own stone circlet but keeps it, warning that some practitioners seek power at others' expense. His reserve makes the invitation guarded rather than effortless trust.[^wildfire-whispers]

Their lessons proceed largely in silence. Wildfire demands punctuality and concentration, then shows satisfaction as Koh develops new forms of psychic expression. What begins through Carrie's referral becomes a sustained relationship between an exacting practitioner and a pupil learning to use an unfamiliar gift.[^wildfire-training]

### Arc Two — The mentor as assessor and patron's defender

At [Broscale Manor](../places/broscale-manor.md), Wildfire receives the Delvers among his wife Valentine, their children and the household's trophies. The family setting gives way to a formal assessment once the others leave. He reviews the group's record and asks how they would judge difficult cases involving duty, danger and innocent lives. His recommendation for Silver standing depends on more than their ability to defeat monsters. He thanks Koh again for recovering his brother's badge, keeping their personal bond present within the evaluation.[^wildfire-assessment]

Cassius reports that Withers has hired the Broscale family to investigate the estate burglary. Wildfire privately tells Evelyn that he knows she entered the basement and defends Withers's longstanding financial support of the guild. Mentorship coexists with loyalty to a patron whom his pupils suspect.[^wildfire-warning]

At the Bronze Ball, that defense meets a disturbing answer. When Withers responds to Khalazar's accusation by speaking of hired Broscales and opened doors, Wildfire seizes him by the collar and demands an explanation. The floor collapses beneath both men before the confrontation can resolve what either knows.[^wildfire-ball]

### Arc Three — A captive needing rescue

Valentine later asks the Delvers to bring her missing husband back. Their search finds him alive in the [black pyramid](../places/dawnless-dunes-pyramid.md), still bearing his axe but laboring in tattered clothes beside Withers and other captives on Khalazar's unfinished statue. His presence there supplies no free explanation of the Ball or the work he is compelled to do.[^wildfire-captivity]

The rescuers subdue the workers without killing them. Wildfire is carried through Darmah's first completed teleportation circle to the guild, alive and stable. The instructor who helped Koh find his strength now depends on the expedition his former pupil has joined.[^wildfire-rescue]
