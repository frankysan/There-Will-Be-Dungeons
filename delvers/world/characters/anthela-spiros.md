# Anathala / Anthela Spiros — Koh's rescued wife

**Name review:** In S3E01's introductory recap Kyle **explicitly settles the previously variable pronunciation/name as “Anathala,”** and it is used throughout S3E02 by speaker and ASR, while **Anthela** remains the older working written file slug and historic source reading. **Do not silently rename every historic citation or claim an independently verified official character-sheet spelling; original sound/written provenance is still pending**. [S3E01](../../episodes/S3E01.md) · [S3E02](../../episodes/S3E02.md).

## S3E02: Voluntary research and recovered family life

Recovered Anathala **actually agrees to Carrie Underworld's repeated blood tests** to understand Khalazar's original interest in her. Koh protects her by maintaining telepathic contact; after ~three weeks, Carrie fashions a [red-crystal detector](../items/khalazar-sensory-amulet.md) using the shard Evelyn handed her and **Anathala's blood**. Carrie suggests **possible Dolbluth ancestry** and a role in Khalazar's planned rite, but **this is tentative research, not confirmed genealogical fact**. Anathala remains physically awake and accompanies Koh in the lab. Koh declines proposed renewed hypnosis/exposure to the Drone because of danger. Over the episode's completed three-month montage, the family relocates when Koh purchases a smaller home by the guild; **not a return to coma**.


## S2E12: awake, dancing, then resisting new compulsion

**Anthela PHYSICALLY ATTENDS Bronze Ball WITH Koh and dances**, in ornate **GREEN and BLUE Victorian dress with GOLD scale-like accents and dark-green hip silks tied at neck**, recently recovered from three-month coma. Koh **GIVES her Dagger of Venom and Driftglobe**, which she conceals as best she can for protection. She **recognizes Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers only as seeming familiar from her captive dreams** (her testimony, NOT a proven real-world meeting), unnerved by his presence. **Khalazar's smoky voice inhabits orchestra leader and other guests**, orders her to come; Koh connects minds via **eight-hour Psychic Whispers** and continuously affirms family memories. **Anthela responds coherently** and stays beside Koh through closing scene despite **other elf/half-elf town residents compulsively entering Drone pit**. **This does NOT prove permanent immunity**, recovery of all earlier dream memories or Khalazar's death. No witnessed harm/abduction/return to coma at **S2E12 end of second arc**, and no evidence she entered underground pit. [Finale](../../episodes/S2E12.md).

**Working spelling:** **Anthela Spiros** (ASR sometimes `Anthila/Anthea`); exact spelling is **not audio/character-sheet verified**. [Koh Spiros](koh-spiros.md)'s wife and mother of **Penelope**. She is living but has been unresponsive for an extended period; Koh spent much of the family's fortune trying to find effective treatment. **Delvers only**: do not connect to persons or mental realms from Wastes/Inverse.

## Physical condition and earlier visions

Anthela lies in the family home with caregivers tending her; she **breathes** yet fails to wake/respond normally. Koh's **Psychic Whispers** enables direct but uncertain contact: in an earlier mental scene she sat in a little lit grass field and rejected his effort to intrude. Physical healers had not found a cure. The guild's Carrie Underworld suggested studying whether Koh can **join another affected person's dream with a helper and try mental spells**, potentially first with the comatose **Rianu Keeves** in the Temple of Pelor. At [S2E08](../../episodes/S2E08.md) cutoff **this experiment had not happened**.[^prior]

## S2E09: Khalazar's bride

**Physical change actually observed the morning after Dragon Mountain:** **black goo/slime drips from Anthela's hand, chest and mouth**, and she has **shifted/tossed overnight**, unlike prior unmoving sleep. **Koh actually enters her mind** and sees stone columns, shadowy faceless figures, and Anthela in a **formal open-backed gown, long black metal hairpin through an intricate updo**, preparing a **wedding to “the Khalazar,” described as emperor/king of the Lower Kingdoms**. She repeatedly calls Koh a stranger. After a Wisdom save, Koh realizes this mindscape has malleable elements and summons the **engagement/wedding ring he gave her**. Its counterpart appears on her hand and she **recognizes him briefly**, revealing she believes the marriage is necessary to **protect Atomer and Penelope** and that the Khalazar promised only she would be taken. Koh objects because **other cases of apparent affliction exist**, but Anthela's memory resets quickly. **She has not physically awoken or left her room, and the wedding has not actually happened in external world.** Khalazar's identity and genuine control mechanism **unproven**; these are her statements inside the apparent psychic prison.[^wedding]

## S2E11: household reunion, confession and persistent fears

**Anthela is PHYSICALLY UP AND WALKING in S2E11**, albeit weak and fatigued. Supported by husband **Koh** as they leave Carrie's office, she **embraces daughter Penelope** at their Atomer home, goes with them to market and **ACTUALLY COOKS HER FAVORITE STEW** with ingredients and chicken. She learns from Koh that he **became a guild adventurer after costly unsuccessful doctors drained family resources and later undertook illicit Withers work involving former associate Chuck**; **Anthela says “Chuck's been here,”** but time/intent of visit remains NPC testimony, not a played Chuck scene. She sees Koh manifest psychic dagger, then is visibly anxious when reminded of his blade thrown toward **dream Khalazar** and asks Koh to remain mentally connected as she sleeps. He casts **Psychic Whispers for one hour**, **without entering her dreamscape**; she **falls asleep normally, not recollapsing into coma**. Next day she intends to **ATTEND the Bronze Ball with Koh**, while Penelope only goes dress-shopping (not attending ball). **Whether she is fully cured of all mental traces, or whether the threatening shade can affect her again, is unknown.** [S2E11](../../episodes/S2E11.md), ~00:31–01:01.

## S2E10: ACTUAL rescue and physical awakening

**Anthela is no longer comatose at the S2E10 cutoff.** Carrie and Aegis actually transport her physical body on stretcher from Koh's home to the guild; Koh links **Anthela, Krandal and Evelyn**, **not Carrie**, into the dream. The bridal hall overlooks a subterranean city of green crystal, which is **seen in mindscape only**, not independently visited. Under Evelyn's **Queen of Birds** ruse, Krandal **touches Anthela and actually casts Remove Curse in her mental realm**; she **recognizes Koh**, while bed and furnishings crumble into black sand. A giant bat carries a pale elf calling himself **Khalazar**, who demands his bride. **Koh Dimension Doors with her to escape shaft, Anthela alone initially rises into the exit light**, while Koh remains behind to rescue his companions. **At the end she PHYSICALLY opens her eyes inside Carrie's Atomer laboratory and embraces Koh** after three months in coma. **This is a performed, observed bodily recovery**, unlike the S2E09 brief recognition; no proof Khalazar has been permanently destroyed, that other sleepers will respond or that her recovered awareness can never relapse. **Penelope is informed in morning that a hopeful treatment will be attempted, but does NOT appear at physical reunion.** [S2E10](../../episodes/S2E10.md), ~00:50–02:19.
