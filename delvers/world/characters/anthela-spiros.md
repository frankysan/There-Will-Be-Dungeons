# Anthela Spiros — Koh's unconscious wife

**Working spelling:** **Anthela Spiros** (ASR sometimes `Anthila/Anthea`); exact spelling is **not audio/character-sheet verified**. [Koh Spiros](koh-spiros.md)'s wife and mother of **Penelope**. She is living but has been unresponsive for an extended period; Koh spent much of the family's fortune trying to find effective treatment. **Delvers only**: do not connect to persons or mental realms from Wastes/Inverse.

## Physical condition and earlier visions

Anthela lies in the family home with caregivers tending her; she **breathes** yet fails to wake/respond normally. Koh's **Psychic Whispers** enables direct but uncertain contact: in an earlier mental scene she sat in a little lit grass field and rejected his effort to intrude. Physical healers had not found a cure. The guild's Carrie Underworld suggested studying whether Koh can **join another affected person's dream with a helper and try mental spells**, potentially first with the comatose **Rianu Keeves** in the Temple of Pelor. At [S2E08](../../episodes/S2E08.md) cutoff **this experiment had not happened**.[^prior]

## S2E09: Khalazar's bride

**Physical change actually observed the morning after Dragon Mountain:** **black goo/slime drips from Anthela's hand, chest and mouth**, and she has **shifted/tossed overnight**, unlike prior unmoving sleep. **Koh actually enters her mind** and sees stone columns, shadowy faceless figures, and Anthela in a **formal open-backed gown, long black metal hairpin through an intricate updo**, preparing a **wedding to “the Khalazar,” described as emperor/king of the Lower Kingdoms**. She repeatedly calls Koh a stranger. After a Wisdom save, Koh realizes this mindscape has malleable elements and summons the **engagement/wedding ring he gave her**. Its counterpart appears on her hand and she **recognizes him briefly**, revealing she believes the marriage is necessary to **protect Atomer and Penelope** and that the Khalazar promised only she would be taken. Koh objects because **other cases of apparent affliction exist**, but Anthela's memory resets quickly. **She has not physically awoken or left her room, and the wedding has not actually happened in external world.** Khalazar's identity and genuine control mechanism **unproven**; these are her statements inside the apparent psychic prison.[^wedding]

## Status through S2E09

Still **bedridden/unresponsive** in Atomer home. Koh later **enters Rianu Keeves's dream along with Krandal**, and Krandal's **Lesser Restoration** there briefly dispels guard hallucinations and restores Keeves's awareness, **not his physical consciousness**. This suggests a method might help Anthela but **it is not tested on Anthela in S2E09**; **Remove Curse** only proposed by Carrie. Cassius is warned of Khalazar and fears a cult action at the forthcoming Bronze Ball, again **not a confirmed mechanism or completed future event**.
