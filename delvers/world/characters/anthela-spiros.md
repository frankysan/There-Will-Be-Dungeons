# Anathala / Anthela Spiros — Koh's rescued wife

## Introduction

Anathala Spiros is [Koh's](koh-spiros.md) wife and [Penelope's](penelope-spiros.md) mother. She lies unresponsive in the family's Atomer home, where caregivers tend her while Koh searches for a treatment ordinary healing has not supplied. His efforts have exhausted their fortune, though he conceals the household's hardship and his dangerous new work. Her illness has become the urgent center of a family life she cannot openly share.[^anathala-introduction]

## Development by arc

<a id="physical-condition-and-earlier-visions"></a>

### Arc One — A voice behind the silence

Koh's psychic contact reveals awareness that her still body cannot express. In a small grassy mental refuge, Anathala embraces him and speaks, but fears that his presence will be discovered. She believes that remaining there will keep the town safe because her captors want only her. The belief gives her confinement a purpose in her own mind, without showing that the promise can be trusted. A drone and encroaching sand force Koh out; she remains physically asleep. Their affection survives the contact, but it has not freed her.[^anathala-contact]

### Arc Two — Recognition, recovery and renewed danger

Her condition becomes more visibly disturbing when black fluid appears on her body and Koh finds her preparing for a wedding inside a shadow-filled hall. She repeatedly forgets him and Penelope. When their marriage ring briefly restores recognition, she explains that she means to protect both her family and Atomer by accepting Khalazar. The recognition fades again. Her apparent willingness belongs within the coercion and unstable memory of the prison.[^anathala-wedding]

A later rescue reaches beyond that brief exchange. Krandal removes the curse within the mental bridal chamber, restoring her memory and recognition. Koh brings her to the escape light, staying to help his companions. She opens her eyes in Carrie's laboratory and embraces him, then returns home to Penelope. Weak and easily tired, she nevertheless walks to market and cooks a favorite stew. Familiar acts become part of reclaiming her life.[^anathala-recovery]

Koh now tells her about their lost money, his guild work and the burglary involving Chuck. She sees his psychic blade but fears returning to the realm he describes, asking for his mental companionship at bedtime. She still chooses to attend the Bronze Ball and dance with him. When Khalazar calls through the afflicted guests, their psychic connection and shared memories help her remain herself beside Koh while others are drawn underground.[^anathala-home-and-ball]

### Arc Three — A life resumed with fears still present

Anathala agrees to Carrie's investigation of why Khalazar wanted her. Repeated blood tests contribute to a [detector](../items/khalazar-sensory-amulet.md) that Koh can use against the continuing threat. Carrie's proposed ancestral explanation remains uncertain. The family moves to a smaller home near the guild, where Anathala later welcomes a visiting reporter and offers refreshments. Her place in the household is again active rather than confined to a sickbed.[^anathala-research]

Recovery has not removed every fear. When Koh misses a night of their usual psychic reassurance, she barricades the door, closes the curtains and cannot sleep. His return brings conversation, tea and comfort, and she hands him an unopened letter that provides a lead to Cassius's whereabouts. The frightened homecoming is real, but it is not a rejection of her husband or a return to the earlier coma.[^anathala-homecoming]

## Current status

Anathala remains with Koh and Penelope after Khalazar's defeat. Over the following year, Koh returns to family life while teaching at the guild. No renewed captivity is known, but her later memories, health and daily work are not separately described. The family's home is not known to have moved to the rebuilt farm tower.[^anathala-status]
