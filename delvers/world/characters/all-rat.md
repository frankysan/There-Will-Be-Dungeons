# The All Rat — cranium-rat congregation

## Introduction

The All Rat speaks as a single presence within a hovering mass of cranium rats in the [Black Basilica](../places/black-basilica.md). Exposed brains flash in alternating colours while chains of biting rats connect it to captives on the floor. The congregation presents itself through a shared voice, keeping others within fears shaped from their own lives.[^voice]

## Development by arc

### Arc Three — A bargain for the source of its power

The collective holds Koh and Krandal in projected disasters while another trapped mind appears as Radiant Lance. Evelyn resists her own false refuge and cuts the rats attached to her companions, releasing them. The All Rat loses its hold over those captives but still speaks for the mass gathered around the golden hook-horror-capped urn.[^captivity]

When confronted, it offers the container while insisting on keeping the organ inside. It claims that this part of Khalazar sustains its power. The bargain exposes the limit of its willingness to yield: the Delvers may take an empty prize, but not what it wishes to retain. Evelyn rejects the offer.[^bargain]

The All Rat defends that possession with linked-rat lashes, psychic force and changing images of earlier dangers. Koh instead seizes the whole urn and teleports clear. The flashing brains cease and the congregation falls apart into scattered rats. Its organized voice and resistance end with the loss of the object it had refused to surrender.[^fall]

## Current status

The collective has broken apart, while surviving rats scatter. No later reunion of its shared mind is known.[^fall]
