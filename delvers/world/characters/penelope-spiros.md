# Penelope “Penny” Spiros — Koh and Anathala/Anthela's daughter

## Introduction

Penelope Spiros is the young daughter of [Koh](koh-spiros.md) and [Anathala](anthela-spiros.md). She lives in Atomer with her unresponsive mother and the caregivers her father struggles to afford. Koh keeps his injuries, depleted fortune and dangerous employment from her, preserving the appearance of a more secure household.[^penelope-introduction][^penelope-design]

<a id="played-continuity-vs-retrospective-intentions"></a>

## Development by arc

### Arc One — Stories around an absent truth

Penelope draws her father wielding daggers while he turns his encounters with underground creatures into stories for her. The adventures enter their home as tales before he admits that they are his own work. She remains part of the life he wants to protect, but does not yet share his knowledge of the risks he takes for her mother's recovery.[^penelope-stories]

### Arc Two — Her mother comes home

While Koh is away, medical support runs short and Chuck pays the doctors, drawing the household into obligations Penelope has not chosen. She remains at home with her nanny. When the guild offers another treatment for Anathala, Penelope hears the hope without receiving a promise that it will succeed.[^penelope-care]

Her mother's actual return changes the household. They embrace, go to market with Koh and share a dinner Anathala cooks despite her fatigue. Penelope also goes dress-shopping, though she is not a guest at the Bronze Ball. The reunion restores ordinary family moments without giving her knowledge of everything her parents have endured.[^penelope-reunion]

### Arc Three — A hero's daughter and unsettling dreams

After the Ball, Koh finally tells Penelope that he joined the guild as **One Shot** and helped defeat a dragon. His stories become an acknowledged part of his life. The family moves to a smaller home near the guild, but its danger has not vanished: Penelope later describes dreams of a desert when her father returns after an unexplained absence. The dreams are troubling experiences, without establishing that she has traveled underground.[^penelope-truth]

## Current status

Penelope remains with her parents after Khalazar's defeat and the following year of rebuilding. Koh's return to family life and a family image kept at the rebuilt Delvers tower preserve her place in that ending. Her later activities and circumstances are not separately known.[^penelope-status]
