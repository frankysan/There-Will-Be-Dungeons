# Penelope “Penny” Spiros — Koh and Anathala/Anthela's daughter

**Campaign:** [Delvers](../../README.md), NOT Wastes or Inverse. Child of **satyr rogue [Koh Spiros](koh-spiros.md)** and **elf-descended [Anathala/Anthela Spiros](anthela-spiros.md)**. **Penelope** is the established full name in played dossiers; **“Penny”** is explicitly spoken by the cast in [BONUS-2021-11-13](../../../episodes/BONUS-2021-11-13.md) (~19–21 and ~34–37min). Do not confuse this with an unrelated Wastes person.

## Played continuity vs retrospective intentions

**Already played:** Anthela and Penelope physically reunite with Koh in [S2E11](../../episodes/S2E11.md) after the mother wakes; daughter lives with parents at Atomer family home. [S3E10](../../episodes/S3E10.md) daughter speaks of **dreams of desert**; actual offscreen journey to Khalazar not witnessed. S3E12 defeats Khalazar and final one-year epilogue shows family still central to Koh, with daughter represented on a small family **LOCKET** in rebuilt farm tower. **No visually detailed character introduction or kidnapping physically played**. She is NOT proven genetically immune to the Drone.

**GM clarification [BONUS-2021-11-13 ~18–21min](../../../episodes/BONUS-2021-11-13.md):** Asked why Penny seemed spared, Kyle says it was **deliberate boundary around frightening child harm and the players' earned success**, not a discovered race immunity. Penny **had begun having related dreams**, so “never affected” is false. The potential story **if the heroes LOST the earlier dream battle** could have induced her to walk toward the Underdark, or allowed Anthela's abduction to Khalazar's pyramid. But **Koh protected family and heroes WON the dream**, so Kyle rejected arbitrary later abduction; these routes are **UNPLAYED**. Jon explains that as a new parent repeated harm to Penny would have caused real distress. No biologically guaranteed protection established.

## Performer-confirmed visual baseline — 2021-11-13 retrospective

At ~34–37m, Jon says that during an **earlier, later RETCONNED character-development concept** he had imagined Koh **half-elf/half-satyr**, from which he first pictured Penny as **a mostly young elf-looking child, possibly with an unruly mane of hair**. The father's original half-elf concept was DROPPED before the played identity. Jon then **EXPLICITLY APPROVES/SELECTS “little hooves” for Penny** in response to listener question; this is the clearest performer-approved physical feature. **Do not draw her as ordinary human feet** if following the latest design intent. **Wild hair and elf-like face are early loose mental references, not individually fixed current design choices**. **No confirmed horns, leg-fur extent, skin/eye/hoof colours, build, exact age or clothing**, and no played close-up/full-body appearance. Her mixed satyr and elf ancestry and hooves can inform concept art, while everything else should be proposed, not misattributed to source.
