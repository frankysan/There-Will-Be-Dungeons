# Glepo Ma — kuo-toa riverside farmhand

## Introduction

Glepo Ma is a trader in the [kuo-toa lakeshore village](../places/kuo-toa-lakeshore-village.md), more at ease with outsiders than many of his neighbors. Catfish-like in appearance, he wears adapted human clothing and speaks enough Common to bargain. His shop holds salvaged objects and curious goods, giving visitors a practical point of contact with the lake community.[^introduction]

## Development by arc

### Arc One — Bargains, compromise and amends

When the Delvers arrive, Glepo Ma meets their needs with trades. Koh's shovel buys directions to the submerged ruins. Glepo Ma gives him a fishing rod in return for letting the trader care for the borrowed horses. While villagers marvel at Koh's psychic voice, the trader asks for a staff from below the lake. His dealings offer the party a more ordinary relationship than the reverence surrounding them.[^trader]

That confidence falters when armed collectors arrive. Glepo Ma passes the recovered conch staff to their mounted leader, who threatens the village and leaves followers to take the adventurers' finds. After the Delvers overcome them, he admits that he had misjudged the guild visitors. He gives two shop items as amends and immediately sets out to warn the guild party at the bullywug settlement. The encounter turns a convenient bargain into an attempt to repair the harm done through it.[^amends]

### Arc Two — Exile and a place to work

Glepo Ma later approaches Krandal's farm, reporting that his village has banished him for helping abduct its high priest. He asks for shelter and employment from someone he remembers as kind and holy. Krandal offers a trial with food, lodging and river access. Glepo Ma's appetite for insects and small vermin becomes useful pest control, and a productive first day earns a permanent place. The displaced trader finds a new household through practical work.[^farm]

His bright pink-purple coloring, blue dorsal fins, feathered hat and carried bag make him recognizable beside the fields. He sleeps in the river and uses the farm's tools, adapting to the holding while its buildings remain unfinished.[^appearance]

### Arc Three — Responsibility, trade and a continuing home

Glepo Ma brings Muck Muck into the farm's growing workforce and supervises pest control. He also carries his trading experience into Atomer, selling potatoes from the farm's market cart. During rising prices, the stall remains busy and affordable. His warning that something is living in the barn is borne out when Krandal finds a newly hatched hook horror there.[^responsibility]

Food and shelter precede cash wages. The workers remain unpaid in coin for a time, but a later potato shipment sale brings money to the holding and Krandal pays Glepo Ma and the other two employees. He continues working alongside Muck Muck during the following year's flourishing farm life, remaining part of the household he joined in need.[^wages]

## Current status

Glepo Ma is alive and working at Krandal's thriving farm in the year after Khalazar's defeat. His return to village life, reconciliation with its leaders and any separate shop of his own remain unknown.[^status]
