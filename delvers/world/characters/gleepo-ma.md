# Glepo Ma — kuo-toa riverside farmhand

**Working canonical spelling:** **Glepo Ma**, already used in the earlier [kuo-toa village](../places/kuo-toa-lakeshore-village.md) reference; **S2E11 ASR often renders “Gleepo Ma.”** Spoken pronunciation and GM-written notes not checked. **Delvers-only character**, a fish-person from earlier **kuo-toa lake village**, now directly encountered near [Krandal's Potato Grove](../places/potato-grove.md) in **[S2E11 — New High Score](../../episodes/S2E11.md)**. Previously only an **unidentified fishman watching the farm** was reported by minotaur skull Chad in S2E06/S2E09; **direct visual identification now occurs**, distinct from **Gil**. Do not assume every earlier unidentified lakeside figure was Gleepo without further source alignment.

## Appearance and presentation

GM describes **vibrant pink-purple amphibious scales/skin**, **blue dorsal fins down his back**, **moist/slimy hands**, breathing rasp, carrying a **bag**, miscellaneous utensils and a **floppy Robin Hood-style hat with a feather**. Seen first **crouching in marshy bushes 50 feet from farm**, peering toward Krandal before emerging. He **recognizes Krandal as especially nice and holy** and fidgets with his holy necklace. Comic misunderstandings about tools and “room and board,” but character **actually performs work**. No demonstrated specific height, facial markings, webbing form, shoes or magical abilities; original artist artwork absent.[^appearance]

## Role and events

Gleepo **claims he has been banished from lake community for helping kidnap the high priest**, who was held at the adventuring guild; this is **his testimony** (prisoner's current location not personally revisited). Homeless, he asks to stay with Krandal. Krandal hires him **on trial**, offering **food, farm shelter, potato crops and access to river for two dips daily** rather than an explicitly agreed coin salary, and explains use of **newly arrived farming tools** from Grapple & Tackle. **Krandal Nature 17, Gleepo ~12**: work productive; Gleepo devours bugs, rat pups and moles as pest control, a practice Krandal permits despite slight horror. At sundown **trial becomes permanent labor**, he receives first potato and **jumps into river to sleep**; no new house built or rent paid. [S2E11](../../episodes/S2E11.md), ~00:19–00:32 and ~00:56–01:00.[^employment]

**Separation of NPCs:** The **ragged elderly human with a liquid-like hump**, given **8 gp and a Pelor candle in S2E09**, was an entirely **different farm visitor** and is **not Gleepo**. The skull stays **Chad** despite Krandal mistakenly saying Brad later.
