# Marsh — silver-ranked wood-elf dragon hunter

**Working name:** **Marsh**, performed by guest **Garrett** in [Delvers S2E08 — Golden Potato Sword](../../episodes/S2E08.md). **Full name, guest surname, original sheet, exact item identities and native chronology not independently verified**. Preserve this Delvers character separate from Wastes/Inverse names. S2E07 outro teased a returning guest dragon slayer, **not a played introduction**.

## Arrival at Atomer

A weathered wood elf of ordinary height, salt-and-pepper hair, **bad scarring like chemical burns along left side of face and part of left ear missing**. He wears a practical broad-brimmed farm/cowboy-style hat, a coat resembling dark denim, and **dragon-bone bow** with dragon-bone-hilted daggers. His weathered, scruffy weasel **Wheatley (Wheatly, provisional)** has a dark body and contrasting pale bandit-like eyes and watches carefully from his shoulder. His **silver guild badge** makes rank clear, higher than the bronze Darkest Delvers. Marsh presents as laconic and wary of the noisy novice escort.[^first]

Marsh enters the Atomer guild independently and chooses **Dragon Mountain at Chath/Chaff Peak**. The clerk advertises a **young red dragon** and **1,000 gp reward shared by four (~250 each)**; extra hoard division negotiable, **none paid yet**. Last known peak dragon was said to be **green, defeated ~150 years ago by Radiant Lance**, but the name and history are clerk testimony. Marsh takes a guild horse to meet Aegis and the three Delvers at a mountain road ~3 hours from town. **GM says Ambermoor was already delivered to guild and Cassius sent Delvers immediately on the hunt**, but this is a retrospective transition, not newly played guild debrief.[^first]

## An unexpectedly ancient quarry

Aegis supplies Marsh **guild fire-resistant studded leather** as a replacement for his previous **acid-resistant armor** (old suit not destroyed). Marsh exchanges awkward jokes with Krandal, and with the group climbs a game trail where he recognizes kobold tracks. He guides a dangerous narrow ledge behind a waterfall using pitons and Koh's enchanted sash. A **very large red dragon** lands below; Marsh estimates it **~450 years old**, far older than the guild's ~100-year estimate, and recognizes the **open-air disadvantage**: a flying dragon will burn them and escape, while a cave/lair may permit them to seal exits. Unlike the other three he resists its **frightful presence**; the dragon nevertheless **breathes fire on the party**. Marsh survives with resistance suit/healing, urges Krandal to muffle his face maces, and leads ascent.[^waterfall]

He speaks **Draconic** and interprets for the kobold shaman **Skoogie**, who claims worshippers deserted him for the dragon. The shaman hands Marsh an **Arrow of Dragon Slaying**, removed from his feathered headdress, in return for help defeating the dragon. Marsh has **NOT fired the arrow** through the episode cutoff; no unconditional future foot-massage obligation established by party jokes. Marsh casts **Jump** to cross mountain lava, scouts the dragon eating the kobold sacrifice, notices a claw-scratched entrance, and helps uncover the chamber signed by old dragon slayer **Radiant Lance**. He **does not put a valuable item into the forge** or receive Evelyn's sword.[^mountain]

## Current status (S2E08 cutoff)

Marsh fights in the **Chath Peak dragon lair**, wearing loaned **fire-resistant studded leather**, **Potion of Fire Resistance actually consumed**, and carrying the **unused Arrow of Dragon Slaying**, bone bow and Wheatley. He takes **a dragon bite (~17)**, hits with dragon-tooth dagger opportunity attack, and **casts Pass Without Trace in the steam** while moving toward the **Sealed Gate** button. **The gate has not been pressed**, the dragon remains alive and airborne, and the fight ends **mid-battle** for guest scheduling; host says Garrett will return next episode, an **out-of-game intention, not a completed return**. His full eventual fate is outside this page's spoiler horizon. [Source clock and uncertainty register](../../episodes/S2E08.md).
