# Marsh — silver-ranked wood-elf dragon hunter

## Introduction

Marsh is a weathered wood-elf dragon hunter whose silver badge places him above the bronze-ranked Darkest Delvers. Salt-and-pepper hair, a scarred face and damaged left ear accompany practical travelling clothes and dragon-bone weapons. The watchful weasel Wheatley rides with him. He brings a restrained manner and an experienced eye to the expedition against Chath Peak's dragon.[^introduction]

### Arc Two — Caution, cooperation and a killing arrow

Marsh approaches the hunt as a problem of terrain and escape. He recognizes kobold tracks, leads the narrow waterfall crossing and judges the dragon far older than the guild expected. He urges a fight inside the lair, where a flying quarry can be prevented from escaping. His attention to noise and positioning gives the younger adventurers practical direction.[^judgment]

He also depends on cooperation. Evelyn heals him after the dragon's fire, and the group uses Koh's climbing sash to negotiate the mountain. Marsh's Draconic allows him to translate Skoogie's bargain, accept the shaman's slaying arrow and follow the concealed route. Local knowledge and the companions' abilities become part of his plan.[^alliance]

In the lair, Marsh works toward sealing the gate while the others control the lava and the dragon's flight. Once the creature is grounded and trapped, he fires Skoogie's arrow into its neck and finishes Fredoroth. The partnership ends with a negotiation over the hoard: Marsh agrees to an equal overall division in principle, then departs with a large cart containing magical equipment.[^outcome]

## Current status

Marsh is last known alive after the dragon's defeat, leaving with treasure. His later travels, continued dealings with the Delvers and the final distribution of individual magical items remain unknown.[^status]
