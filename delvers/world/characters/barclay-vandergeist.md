# Barclay Vandergeist / Bendergeist — guest tortle cleric

## Introduction

Barclay Vandergeist is a tortle cartographer and cleric of Ptah whom the Darkest Delvers find living in the dungeon with a lantern spirit called [Chance](../items/chance-lantern.md). Bluish-green, weathered and underfed, he nevertheless greets them with a smile. His improvised camp and dismantled equipment bear the marks of a prolonged struggle to survive.[^identity]

### Arc Three — A solitary researcher finds companions

Barclay says he has been underground for three or four months after becoming separated from his research team. Mapping has given way to necessity: he has burned his maps and cut up his leather armour for camp equipment. Mushroom beds, cooking arrangements and alarms made from bones and bells help sustain him. Chance provides company, answering with a spectral hand and crude writing.[^camp]

He first challenges the approaching Delvers with frightening voices and tremors, then welcomes them, shares mushrooms and accepts Krandal's potato. His readiness to join is frank: he would rather risk dying with companions than remain alone. Frequent invocations of Ptah accompany a practical faith in knowledge and craft.[^camp][^design]

Working beside Krandal, Barclay makes that faith useful to others. He helps assess traps and depths, strengthens Krandal's mace against the stone golems and heals him after the fight. Later, he shapes a stone rope anchor so they can descend safely. His resourcefulness now supports a shared expedition instead of merely maintaining his solitary refuge.[^cooperation]

After the group recovers the last urn, Barclay helps them leave through the forest entrance. At Veggie Sticks's refuge he casts Nondetection on the trio. Their Silver badges fall away, prompting suspicions about Guild surveillance. He then accompanies them through the trees to Atomer, completing his own return above ground.[^return]

## Current status

Barclay is alive in Atomer with Chance after travelling out with the Delvers. His missing research companions' fate and any reunion remain unresolved.[^return]
