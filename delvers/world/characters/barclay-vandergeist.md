## Appearance — player narration ~00:10–00:20

**Bluish-green tortle**, roughly **5′10″**, bulky shell-bearing tortle body, smiling and friendly, weathered, hungry, dirty and undernourished. **Plain simple clothes** (player explicitly says **NOT naked**), no intact visible armour: his old **leather armour is dismantled for camp equipment**, including rope, bell alarm/straps and repairs. Carries **numerous pots and pans/pack**, a **ghost-blue glowing lantern** with a spirit he calls [Chance](../items/chance-lantern.md); hand appears as a quasi-physical ghostly hand, can shake hands and write crudely in dirt. **Clothing colours, shell design/markings, face shape, eyes, scars, beard, precise tools and exact lantern silhouette are NOT described**, not suitable to invent as confirmed illustration traits. Has been underground for **self-estimated three/four months** after separation from research team, most friends gone or lost according to his account—**guest testimony, not verified deaths/times**.

## Skills, religion, behaviour

**Cartographer/researcher**; says mission was mapping cave routes, but burned his old maps and cannibalized equipment to survive. **Cleric of Ptah**, whom he calls deity of **knowledge, crafting and research**. Has camp with deliberately scattered **small animal bones as noise alarms**, mushroom beds, cooking spit/roast and bell-strung double grick-wing doors; lives alone with Chance. Uses **Thaumaturgy** to create frightening guardian-voice, tremors/whispers before meeting party, then welcomes strangers, shares mushrooms and Krandal's roasted potato, gratefully joins. Cheerfully says dying with companions beats dying alone; has no permanent Delvers contract. Krandal silently compares own Pelor devotion unfavourably to Barclay's frequent invocations; no actual theological conflict between Ptah/Pelor witnessed.

**Played spellcraft:** **Find Traps** identifies four initial door-trigger floor plates safe; **Light** on leather scrap dropped in chasm lets him approximate **~300-ft pit depth**; **Stone Shape** creates stone anchor for rope into enormous air tower and **Knowledge of the Ages** temporarily grants athletics proficiency. In paired corridor, he is paired with Krandal (while Koh+Evelyn take other). **He is added to Koh's actually cast Psychic Whispers network.** He knows golems immune to charm by Arcana **26**, uses mundane mace, **Toll the Dead**, **Harm** (one sixth-level slot, cast for 25 necrotic damage after successful save in his case) and **Holy Weapon** to imbue Krandal's ordinary mace with magical/radiant power, enabling attacks that otherwise suffer resistance. **He heals Krandal 22 then 21** during subsequent transition; survives both golems, no player death. In grick wind chamber, identifies concealed predators, attacks fleeing grick with **Guiding Bolt**, then escorts original trio back out through **forest dungeon** from which his research team entered.

At [Veggie Sticks](veggie-sticks.md), **Barclay actually casts Nondetection on Koh, Krandal and Evelyn** (GM permits three targets in play); **all three silver guild badges physically FALL OFF**, suggesting a magical relationship/tracking effect but without confirmed enchantment, active Guild scrying or source. He accompanies tree transport to **Atomer well near Atlas cedar (~6 p.m.)** in story; **no separate guest-led legal Guild assignment, permanent party recruitment, or confirmed return to research team**.
