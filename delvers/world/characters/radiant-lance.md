# Radiant Lance — historic Pelor knight, trapped mental witness

## Introduction

Radiant Lance is a remembered knight whose name is borne by the [Dungeon Guild Academy's](../places/dungeon-guild-academy.md) circular theatre. His reputation also reaches those preparing to hunt a dragon on Chath Peak, where an older victory and an inscription bearing his name connect him to the mountain's history.[^history]

## Development by arc

### Arc Two — A reputation attached to a trial

A Guild clerk credits Radiant Lance with slaying a green dragon roughly a century and a half earlier. On the mountain, an ancient Elvish inscription bearing his name challenges would-be dragon slayers to demonstrate freedom from greed. Evelyn offers her gold and receives a Dragon Slayer's Longsword from the mechanism. The knight's name thus accompanies a demand for sacrifice at the threshold of a dragon's hoard.[^history]

<a id="speech-inside-the-collectives-psychic-fabrication"></a>

### Arc Three — Companionship inside a prison

Koh and Krandal later meet Radiant Lance as a captive within a rat collective's mental prison. Appearing as a bucket-helmed knight, he says he is dead and has been held in the [Black Basilica](../places/black-basilica.md) for about 150 years. He cannot free himself or the newcomers, but recognizes that Evelyn remains outside their captivity and can help.[^prison]

His response to helplessness is companionship. He invites them to play cards, and Krandal joins him for a game of War. When Evelyn severs the rats binding the prisoners, Radiant Lance thanks Krandal for that final game and fades from their shared mental space.[^prison]

<a id="physical-remains-and-custody"></a>

The Delvers find an armoured skeleton matching the knight's appearance. Rust has ruined its chainmail and sword, while its tabard and lance remain preserved. Krandal takes the sun-, horse- and lance-marked tabard as a symbol of Pelor; the lance goes into the party's storage.[^relics]

## Current status

Radiant Lance's mental presence has faded, and his skeletal remains lie in the Black Basilica. Krandal wears the tabard, while the physical lance is stored with the party. The fate of the knight's consciousness is unknown.[^relics]
