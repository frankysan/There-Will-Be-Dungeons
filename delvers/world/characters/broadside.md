# Broadside — the familiar face beneath the armor

## Introduction

Broadside is a fighter whose face the Darkest Delvers recognize from the orbit of the dungeon guild. Koh first knows him as one of the figures moving among the guild's trainees and later sees the same recognizable presence leaving Arcane To Go. He is not established as a member of the Darkest Delvers, and his formal rank, employer and exact history have not yet been learned.[^earlier]

## The ambush at the village

When the Delvers return to a kuo-toa lakeshore settlement after searching a sunken ruin, two strangers lie in wait for them. One is a robed caster rummaging through the adventurers' belongings; the other is a broad-shouldered warrior encased in heavy plate, his face darkened and bordered with pale painted lines. He carries a great shield carved with a monstrous tooth-rimmed face and a long serrated sword marked by a red line. His skill at turning aside a blow and his heavy strikes make him an alarming opponent even for the reunited party.[^battle]

Koh strikes the caster down with a deliberate nonlethal psychic blade attack. The armored fighter rushes in, but Krandal's sacred flame and floating face mace, Evelyn's lightning and javelins, and Koh's agile blows wear down his defenses. When he finally falls, Krandal stops his bleeding instead of letting him die. As Evelyn removes the warrior's equipment, magic seems to release its hold upon his face. It shifts and returns to the features Koh knows as Broadside. The discovery is unmistakable to the characters, yet it leaves them no clearer about whom this man truly serves or why he came in disguise.[^reveal]

## His return

At the guild Broadside describes a troubling gap in his memories after the expeditions beneath the forest. He had been protecting researcher Marek, but no longer understands why he fought the Delvers. When news comes that Faelan may be sacrificed, he offers to guide the company north. At Noble Hollow he grapples with Solburn, an old colleague, attempting to bring him away from the rite instead of taking his life. Both later fight beside the Delvers against the creature discovered beneath the collapsing earth. Broadside's conduct earns the party's trust, although he acknowledges that the same dark influence may overcome him again.[^s12]

## Current status

Broadside **survives the events at Noble Hollow**, assists the rescue of Faelan and Norman, and helps fight the immense roper. The guild later **clears him and Solburn of wrongdoing**, believing they were affected by the darkness encountered during Marek's investigations. Broadside admits he may be vulnerable again. His seized **Greatsword of Wounding** was identified by Evelyn during the village rest; return of his sword, distinctive shield and former heavy armor is not established. His next assignment remains unknown.[^s12]
