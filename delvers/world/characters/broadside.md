# Broadside — the familiar face beneath the armor

## Introduction

Broadside is a large fighter familiar to [Koh](koh-spiros.md) from the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md), where he has been seen training near Wildfire and leaving Arcane To Go. He belongs to the circle of adventurers the newcomers recognize without yet knowing well. His formal role and experience are not yet clear to the newcomers.[^broadside-introduction]

## Development by arc

<a id="the-ambush-at-the-village"></a>

<a id="his-return"></a>

### Arc One — Mercy and a chance to make amends

At the kuo-toa village, an armored warrior attacks the Delvers while a robed companion searches their belongings. His tooth-faced shield, serrated sword and heavy blows make him formidable. When he falls, Krandal stops his bleeding and later heals him. Removing his equipment reveals a magical change in his face and the familiar features of Broadside. The recognition raises questions that surviving the fight cannot answer.[^broadside-capture]

At the guild, Broadside says he had been guarding researcher Marek Gideon and now has gaps in his memory. He cannot account for the attack and admits he may be vulnerable to the same influence again. Rather than asking only for release, he offers to help stop the threatened sacrifice at [Noble Hollow](../places/noble-hollow.md). The Delvers accept his help and choose to trust him.[^broadside-return]

His response to that trust is practical. He grapples with his former colleague Solburn, trying to bring him away from the ritual instead of killing him, and later fights beside the company against the roper. The guild clears both men on the belief that they were coerced. His seized Greatsword of Wounding is returned after the decision to trust him; the relationship has moved from mutual violence toward cooperation without erasing his admitted vulnerability.[^broadside-mercy]

### Arc Two — Ordinary work and renewed danger

Broadside later thanks the Delvers and returns to mostly solitary, lower-tier guild work, taking a cranium-rat assignment. He also reports Cheet's reprimand and reassignment. Their shared capture has led to different outcomes, rather than a common judgment about everything they did.[^broadside-work]

At the Bronze Ball, danger reaches the guild's assembled members again. Broadside is among the adventurers around whom Evelyn and the Traveler fight and assist others during the monster attack. His presence there does not end the threat from the sound beneath the hall.[^broadside-ball]

### Arc Three — Helping despite the sound

In the aftermath, Krandal finds Broadside hiding from the sound rather than simply from the creatures. The large goliath nevertheless helps move heavy furniture as the Delvers block openings and clear the wreckage. The man who warned that the influence might return remains wary, yet still contributes to the city's defense.[^broadside-aftermath]

## Current status

Broadside is last known alive and helping with the Bronze Ball's aftermath. He remains identified as a Silver guild adventurer despite taking lower-tier work. His later assignments, lasting susceptibility and circumstances after Khalazar's defeat are unknown.[^broadside-status]
