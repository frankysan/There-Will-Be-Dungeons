## Overview

Evelyn is a human paladin whose urgency is hard to miss. She arrives at the [guild](../places/delvers-guild.md) at a run, with armor clanking beneath her skirt and blouse, a large dwarven warhammer and five javelins. Where her assigned companions pause to observe or prepare, she wants to get moving and close the dungeon.[^identity]

## Development and decisions

### Impatience and care

Evelyn's first morning reveals both her disregard for ceremony and her concern for other people. She repeatedly discards the guild guide, yet leaves a gold piece beneath a cart seat after startling its owner's horse. Whether the owner finds it is unknown. She supplies bread for the journey and leads the descent into the [farm cave](../places/farm-cave.md) by torchlight.

Her readiness to charge ahead exposes her to danger, but she also keeps [Krandal](krandal-splenderbelt.md) alive when the stirges nearly drain him. He returns the care. Faced with the creature engulfing him, she mistakes his own radiance for an attack and tries to free him before guiding him into a shallow pool. Her divine sensing cannot locate the escaped darkmantle. Neither mistake stops her from helping: she tests the ceiling with thrown stones, brings a fallen rock to the fissure and finally wedges it firmly into place. Her determination becomes most useful when joined to her companions' work.[^cave]

### Loyalty and divine courage in the crypt

At Kord's sanctuary, Evelyn's impatience takes a more openly protective form. She challenges Crush's reluctance to surrender a rare strengthening potion while companions remain missing. An impulsive slap fails; an appeal to loyalty secures the second vial. She climbs the stairs again to learn the trials and carries the weakened Meatball away from a Shadow's reach.

She supports [Koh's](koh-spiros.md) run with encouragement and healing, then restores Krandal after Koh accidentally wounds him. When a Shadow incapacitates her in turn, Krandal brings her back. Her holy smite destroys the remaining Shadow in the weight room, and her strength helps open the second basin. She completes the cleansing with the last vial of holy water. Compassion and urgency remain intertwined: Evelyn presses the work forward because people are still in danger.[^crypt]

### Protecting others and pursuing the unknown

Evelyn does not seek difficulty for its own sake, yet the ruined woodland's mystery steadily overcomes her initial caution. She helps the party avoid a confrontation with two hippogriffs, then explores the settlement within a limestone sinkhole. A humming aperture and an ancient inscribed slab draw her toward the mystery of its former inhabitants. She begins to discern a reference to **people of the sky**, but a grick emerges from the wall before she can decipher more. She answers its attack on Krandal with a radiant hammer blow that shatters its beak and kills it. More gricks move in, and she resists leaving without the heavy slab, even as Koh's climbing sash rises toward the rim.[^firstgrick]

The grick expedition tests Evelyn's insistence on leaving no one behind. She fights to hold open an escape while Koh brings the magical climbing sash up the cliff; when a grick fells Krandal, she turns back to heal him and helps move the fallen beast from his body. Her compassion extends beyond the party. She picks up a small walking shrub by the ruins and carries it safely back to its keeper, Veggie Sticks, who recognizes it as one of the plants from his woodland home.[^gricks]

Even after the group has endured serious injuries, Evelyn cannot easily leave a dangerous dungeon unexplored. She persuades the others to return for a limited search, challenges the traps ahead of them and finds a way past the corridor's delayed needles and concealed pits. She saves Koh from poison, retrieves a rune-covered sword hilt from a stone chest and takes it back for the guild's inspection. Her willingness to act quickly sometimes exposes everyone to risk, but it also brings hidden things to light. Back in town she intends to train and improve her equipment, and her enthusiastic account of the company's deeds catches a bard's ear.[^discovery]

### Between duty and freedom

Evelyn's schooling has never taught her to remain quiet or satisfied with other people's expectations. She returns from the grick adventure to an immaculate finishing school, sneaking through a window in chain mail. A friend covers her absence and secretly weaves the magic of easy-donning dresses into her armor, helping her move between her two lives. Lady Vestal suspects her deception and expects an accounting. Evelyn's gratitude toward her friend stands alongside her disregard for formal propriety.[^school]

As the company's work moves into the city, Evelyn discovers that heroism can mean holding a crowd's attention as much as striking a monster. She draws on strength and showmanship to distract shoppers while a more experienced adventurer fights a hidden hag. She also opens paths for people trapped by a fire. Her confidence grows, and she delights in equipment that offers new ways to act—especially a lightning-bearing javelin, clever paints and a more fearsome hammer. Rather than try to hide her wealth in elegance, she buys practical adventuring clothes, which are exotic enough for someone accustomed to formal attire.[^service]

### In the tower, a purpose kept secret

Evelyn approaches the haunted estate as she approaches most danger: with a ready step and a curiosity stronger than prudence. She would rather announce herself at its doors than creep through rooms unnoticed. Her boldness draws a flying skull's attention to the tower and leaves her with smoke and ruined furnishings to reckon with. But she does not confuse courage with indifference to others; when a flaming sphere threatens Krandal, she seizes his arm and pulls him behind a bedroom door, taking him out of its path.[^tower]

In the room of mirrors, Evelyn offers a smaller and more revealing answer than her companions expect. She tells Enzo that she is **looking for someone**, then refuses to say who. No name or relationship escapes her. Her usual open enthusiasm suddenly yields to deliberate privacy. When she leads the party to the roof, that private need remains beside her public courage: she lights her hammer, confronts the skull and uses consecrated water to ensure the victory will last.[^tower]

### A friend at school and a square in peril

Evelyn finds the most dependable help at her finishing school not in its strict lessons but in Margaret, the roommate who understands the life she conceals. Margaret gives her a small, bright bag made from repurposed enchantments, deep enough to swallow her formidable hammer. Later, when Evelyn's chosen flowers fall short of Lady Vestal's standards, a touch of Margaret's magic rescues the arrangement. The school is preparing a bronze ball with the guild, and Evelyn can already sense the difficulty of navigating both worlds at once.[^margaret]

On the streets, her enthusiasm becomes a form of guardianship. She faces a creature disguised as a barrel and keeps its attention away from a wounded workman, even when it holds her hammer fast. She finishes the fight with a common javelin, then joins Koh to help the exhausted dog Bruno, sharing her healing light rather than merely celebrating another victory. She remains eager for adventure, but she has begun to make the safety of those living around the guild part of her own purpose.[^well]

### A shield in the dark

Deep beneath the city, Evelyn's boldness now has a clear loyalty at its center. She carries difficult supplies down a well, confronts the old ruins with impatient curiosity and bears the blast of a horn whose strength she has not yet measured. When a sorcerer is swallowed by a gelatinous cube, she turns her lightning javelin upon its trembling form and frees him alive. Later, as her companions try to flee from a monstrous hook-bearing creature, she runs back for the fallen Krandal and holds herself between her friends and danger. Her second blast upon the beast buys them the opening Koh needs to kill it.[^s9]

Her appetite for useful marvels leads her to buy a small ivory goat figurine said to summon a giant beast, a delight she has yet to test. She hands the strange horn to Carrie for guild examination rather than keeping it after the battle. Back in her other life Margaret is still trying to help her meet Lady Vestal's demands. A formal meeting about the bronze ball now competes with a guild mission, and Evelyn still has no satisfactory way to be in two places at once. Each obligation matters to her, though she increasingly lives for the friendships made beyond school walls.[^s9]

### Two lives on the same morning

Evelyn's friendship with Margaret deepens from quiet sympathy into active conspiracy. When the morning's guild assignment collides with a formal meeting at finishing school, she wakes early and asks Margaret to paint a likeness of her face upon the bed. The magical pigments rise into an eerily convincing sleeping form, and Evelyn slips away through the window. The scheme is bold and imperfect, just like the girl who devised it; Lady Vestal has not yet tested the deception.[^s10]

### A voice to the strangers

She enters the lakeside village bearing the fish and pearls the guild sent as gifts, and is among the first to try the strange words in the translation sheet. The people's reverence for Koh is bewildering, yet she does not mock their fear. Later she recognizes Krandal's old Elven word as a funeral lament, an unexpected use for the learning her school gave her. In the submerged ruin she leaves her heavy new armor and beloved hammer behind so she can swim, and retains only the tools she needs. She agrees that an ancient protective ring should go to the unarmored Krandal. When a fish priest hurls Koh into a whirlpool, Evelyn turns the current into the force of her own flight, striking the priest without trying to kill him. Victory matters less to her here than bringing everyone out alive.[^s10]

### Hands inside the dark

Evelyn refuses to let the strange slit in the temple-like stone remain a mere warning. As Koh's wand points, she forces her arm through the oily aperture and hauls forth a great curved piece of a broken mechanism. She holds it in the dim light, marveling at its gears and the empty place where another should be. In the old market beyond, her confidence has a more immediate use: the beaked beast catches her in its barbed tentacles, but she seizes its jaws and holds them open while the others attack. Her strength creates the chance for Koh to finish the fight.[^s11]

### A promise worth a risk

Back in the fish village Evelyn presses a fist into the captive wizard's mouth rather than betray her companions' hiding place. When the remaining collectors discover them, she is quick to step between the deadly armored stranger and Koh, meeting his sword with hammer and lightning javelin despite having left her armor onshore. After Krandal prevents their fallen foe's death, she joins in the difficult work of binding prisoners instead of executing them. Glepo Ma's fearful apology cannot hide his betrayal, but Evelyn still lets him depart to warn the distant frog-people. The village remains standing behind her as the Delvers set out for home.[^s11]

### The decisive blow and the lives it spares

Evelyn's strength is not always matched by grace, especially on horseback. But the way she depends on Krandal's steadier riding during the hurried journey shows how readily she lets a friend help when the greater purpose requires it. She will not leave Faelan behind, nor allow a threat to the city to proceed while the Delvers deliberate. At the petrified Noble Hollow she invokes the light of her sacred weapon, a shimmer of pink petals around her hammer, and throws herself against the man keeping the ritual alive. He falls under her blow, unconscious rather than dead. In the collapse that follows she hangs above the danger while the others fall, then descends into the underground chamber to stand by them again.

When the great roper's armored exterior seems to frustrate every attempt, Evelyn realizes that its living brood is a vulnerable place. Her javelin pierces the opening from which its young emerge; the beast's desperate reaction brings its death. Even then she does not turn first toward trophies. Koh calls out that Faelan is drowning, and Evelyn brings him awake by touch. Her final healing also returns Norman Green to consciousness. Her need to find an unnamed person and her school duties remain, but the guild can no longer overlook the paladin's heroism, however uneasy she is about being widely known.[^s12]

### The marks beneath another name

Evelyn's missing weeks on Snake Island have made her both more illustrious and more vulnerable. In a half-remembered ceremony she is crowned before serpentfolk, wearing snake-jewelry that clings too tightly until Cassius frees it. Back at school, Margaret has spent a fortnight maintaining a painted likeness in the bed, inventing one illness after another to cover her friend's absence. The tales reach Evelyn's parents, Lord and Lady Blackwood, who send the family physician. Evelyn hurriedly returns to a respectable nightgown before he can discover her adventuring gear.

The doctor finds no trace of the illnesses Margaret reported, but his spectacles reveal a divine mark he associates with a goddess of love and passion. Evelyn is shaken, recalling something from two years before, and begins to explain it to Margaret after he leaves. Their conversation remains private. At Krandal's land, she bears an actual fright and a heavy minotaur blow, yet brings her cleric companion back from unconsciousness and helps finish the sea hag that claimed the tower.[^s2e01]

### A bride's strange blessing

When the wedding of the Hoopers and Goodwines is threatened by an ancient haunting, Evelyn arrives as a lady in a fine dress with her warhammer concealed in Margaret's magical bag. Daufante, the guest illusionist, offers her his arm; beneath his courtesy she senses a man always weighing what he might gain. They investigate the dogs' strange agitation together. During the vows the white doves prepared for the celebration fail to appear. The guest makes Evelyn invisible, and she slips behind the stage to find twenty dead birds. Rather than let the procession fail, she summons the immense goat promised by her little ivory figurine and raises two white bodies to imitate the flight of doves. The crowd receives the spectacle as a blessing.

Later, when Grandfather Hooper comes out of the woods as a wight, Evelyn attempts to seize and restrain him rather than strike blindly, though the dead man tears free. After the wizard finishes the creature she asks the elder Goodwine to bury the victim, not merely conceal a crime but give the old man the dignity his killers denied him. The wedding survives, and Daufante departs offering an invitation that Evelyn has yet to accept.[^s2e02]

### A name spoken among friends

Evelyn throws herself between a bulette and the financial quarter, running loudly enough in armor to draw it out from under the streets. A lightning javelin arrests its flight through the ground; later she holds the monster's great jaws away from a fallen Krandal and rides its back as it plunges into the soil for the last time. The guild celebrates, but another sort of duty calls her back to school.

The Bronze Ball is close, and Lady Vestal expects her students to demonstrate graceful management of the guild's rough adventurers. Evelyn signs up for the practical classes she thinks may prove useful—household management, servant and employee oversight, courtship and marriage, and riding lessons—without knowing how she will fit them around the guild. When a covert job is proposed, she chooses **Long Jump Jenny** as her second alias and at last tells Koh and Krandal who she really is: the only daughter of the **Blackwood** family. Her mother believes her too delicate to fight monsters. She trusts her companions with her family name, but the more private secret behind her divine mark still remains her own.[^s2e03]

### Long Jump Jenny in a burglar's colors

Evelyn prepares the group for the unlikely role Cassius has offered them, buying coordinated stealth clothes and placing small masks on Krandal's face-shaped maces. Her outfit is pink. She enters Withers Estate as Long Jump Jenny, thinking to Koh through a psychic link that does not include Krandal. In the abandoned prison cells she discovers the shrouded body of a kuo-toa, dried with great age. At the first great furnace she answers a riddle by offering her true family name, Blackwood, and the voice of fire agrees.

The basement's guardians quickly make an evening of disguise into something deadly. Evelyn stands her ground before living flame, water and the tempest, breaking watery restraints with her hammer and drawing the anger of the air elemental. Shrugs is briefly knocked unconscious and Krandal narrowly survives, but the combined team prevails; the guardian of the winds calls Evelyn **the Unblown** in grudging admiration. In the lower treasure room she recognizes a conch-headed staff and trousers hung with bells, relics too ominous to leave whole. Evelyn smashes both. They escape with the broken pieces among their mysterious loot, carrying more questions than answers about the family they have robbed.[^s2e04]
