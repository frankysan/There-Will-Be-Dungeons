# Evelyn Grinkeeper — paladin

<a id="overview"></a>

## Introduction

Evelyn Grinkeeper arrives at the [Delvers guild](../places/delvers-guild.md) in a hurry, with chain mail concealed beneath a blouse and skirt, a dwarven warhammer and little patience for instructions. She is a human paladin whose eagerness to help often runs ahead of preparation. Her commanding manner can startle strangers, but she offers compensation when she frightens a horse's owner and readily spends her strength on wounded companions.[^evelyn-introduction][^evelyn-commentary]

### Arc One — Impatience and mutual care

Evelyn's first partnerships turn urgency into a more demanding kind of loyalty. She repeatedly restores [Krandal](krandal-splenderbelt.md) when underground dangers overwhelm him, and he returns that care when she falls. Her confidence does not make every rescue succeed: an attempted healing slap fails to rouse Crush in the mountain crypt, while an appeal to him reaches where the blow cannot. Even when frightened or frustrated, she keeps trying to bring people home.[^evelyn-first-trust]

Curiosity can pull her friends toward danger. She helps interpret an ancient slate and presses for another descent into the grick-infested ruins despite their injuries. Yet she returns for the fallen cleric and cures [Koh](koh-spiros.md) when his improvised crossing leaves him poisoned. Her boldness is inseparable from a willingness to share the consequences. Later, she interrupts a ritual without killing its leader, returns into a collapsing cavern and heals rescued guild members. Protecting others increasingly gives her recklessness a purpose.[^evelyn-rescuer]

Her private life requires a different courage. At finishing school, Lady Vestal's scrutiny competes with guild work, while [Margaret](margaret-dupayton.md) becomes the confidante who makes both lives possible. Enchanted clothing, a bag that conceals her hammer and a painted sleeping decoy help her evade the expectations placed upon her. These disguises preserve freedom, but also keep her companions at a distance. She admits that she is searching for someone without yet sharing the full reason. The outspoken paladin remains guarded about the life she leaves behind each morning.[^evelyn-school]

### Arc Two — A name shared and a purpose chosen

The wealthy Blackwood family's only daughter gradually lets her identities meet. She tells Koh and Krandal her family name while preparing to burgle Withers Estate as **Long Jump Jenny**. The trust is personal, although the work itself compromises any simple image of righteousness. She fights through the estate's guardians and destroys ominous relics; afterward she accepts a magical bag but refuses the remaining cash offered to her. Her unease does not undo her participation in the theft.[^evelyn-heist]

Mercy likewise remains a choice she does not always make. At Gemholm she kills a transformed villager before the party has established whether recovery is possible. Krandal's wish to restrain him does not reach her. Later she cures another man's developing infection, while her attempt to restore a fully transformed captive fails. Her healing has limits, and force can close questions she has not answered.[^evelyn-gemholm]

Her bonds nevertheless become a source of strength. She spends her gold at Radiant Lance's forge, protects the vulnerable Skoogie and helps Koh recover his wife from Khalazar's dream prison. A pink ribbon used to keep friends connected becomes an enchanted [friendship ribbon](../items/friendship-ribbon.md). Her accepted knightly name, **Grinkeeper the Defender**, gives public form to that protective purpose.[^evelyn-bonds]

At the Bronze Ball she faces Lady Vestal openly, wearing Lady Blackwood's dress beneath her armor. The [Traveler](gentleman-cavalier-traveler.md) she has long sought returns for their dance. When disaster interrupts, she fights beside him and heals him with a gentle touch rather than another impulsive slap. Her affection and her calling begin to fit within the same life.[^evelyn-ball]

### Arc Three — Freedom joined to responsibility

Returning to her parents tests the independence Evelyn has found in Atomer. They plan a marriage she refuses and initially dismiss her account of being a paladin. When vampires attack, she fights in her nightclothes and helps defend the household. Her parents finally acknowledge her calling and freedom. She can return to her friends without merely escaping a life imposed upon her.[^evelyn-family]

The Traveler's curse gives her a continuing private quest. She vows to help him, but the leads she receives remain claims rather than a completed rescue. A cranium-rat prison attacks her confidence with an imagined asylum in which her powers and identity have been stripped away. Recognizing the deception lets her resist it and free her companions. The friendships she has chosen outlast the false world built to discredit them.[^evelyn-freedom]

Her allegiance to the school also changes. When a hook horror threatens its children, she answers the danger and claims Loran Ilfelkyr's [radiant armor](../items/loran-radiant-armour.md), previously beyond her reach. Protection takes precedence over hiding from school authority. At the guild's reckoning she rallies people around their missing neighbors and supports Cassius's return, helping turn private investigation into a shared rescue effort.[^evelyn-defender]

In the final expedition she draws a monstrous [skeletal bat](../creatures/skeletal-bat-balcony.md) away from the rescuers and defeats it herself. Against Khalazar she remains dependent on the same reciprocal care that began her guild life: she falls, Krandal restores her, and the three companions strike his real heart together. Her courage contributes to a victory that none achieves alone.[^evelyn-finale]

## Abilities and bonds

Evelyn combines armored endurance, restorative touch and radiant force with practical improvisation. Javelins and a warhammer serve her before Loran's [heart-shaped hilt](../items/vestal-diamond-heart-handle.md) reveals a transforming blade and lance. The later armor is a separate attainment. Her summoned elk, Albert, is a trusted companion; his banishment in the bat fight does not prevent his intact return. Margaret's ingenuity and her fellow delvers' support remain as important to her freedom as her own magic.[^evelyn-abilities]

## Current status

Evelyn survives Khalazar's defeat and spends the following year protecting Atomer, helping rebuild the school and taking up Blackwood family responsibilities. She continues to seek the Traveler. An invitation announces the future wedding of Evelyn Blackwood and Lord Ethan Renald Travail, signed **GCT**; it leaves his identity and the marriage itself unresolved. The accompanying royal letter remains sealed.[^evelyn-status]
