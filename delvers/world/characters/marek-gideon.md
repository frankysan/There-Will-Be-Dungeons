# Marek Gideon — the researcher at the Noble Hollow

## Introduction

Marek Gideon is a researcher associated with the guild's examination of the forest ruins, accompanied by Silver adventurers including [Broadside](broadside.md) and Solburn. The Darkest Delvers first encounter him as a mounted figure wearing a darkmantle cowl with red glass ornaments set among its eyes. His scholarly appointment is not apparent to the villagers facing his demands.[^marek-introduction]

## Development by arc

### Arc One — Research turned toward coercion

At the kuo-toa settlement, Marek demands the fish wizard's conch-tipped staff from Glepo Ma and threatens the village if anyone conceals the adventurers from him. He leaves two companions to seize whatever the Delvers have recovered, then rides toward the bullywug camp. His conduct makes the expedition's interest in ancient objects a danger to the people living among them.[^marek-demands]

The captured Broadside later identifies him as the researcher he had been assigned to guard, while [Cheet](cheet.md) describes the ritual the expedition found underground. She attributes the change in Marek and Broadside to a mental drone. Her explanation offers a possible influence, without establishing how much of Marek's conduct he chooses or understands. Boonefellow's account of another ambush, with Faelan taken, gives the rescuers an immediate reason to follow.[^marek-testimony]

At [Noble Hollow](../places/noble-hollow.md), Marek maintains a midnight rite over the unconscious captive despite repeated attacks. He relies on Solburn and two followers to defend the ceremony. His concentration on completing it persists until Evelyn knocks him unconscious without killing him. The geared stone breaks beneath Faelan and the rite stops. His actions have carried him from a research assignment to an attempted sacrifice, but his own account of that change remains unknown.[^marek-rite]

## Current status

Marek survives his nonlethal defeat and is referred for trial at the Guild Academy, alongside an inquiry into the research sponsorship. No completed transfer, hearing, verdict or sentence is known. Later guild discussion still treats him as removed from the work, without supplying a personal account or resolving the influence upon him.[^marek-status]
