# Cassius — guildmaster of Atomer

## Introduction

Cassius keeps the Delvers Dungeon Guild running amid a perpetual procession of frightened clients, reluctant recruits and more dangers than his limited adventurers can readily answer. He is practiced in assigning work, judging trophies, balancing rewards and keeping his members within the rules. His manner can be wry, impatient and severe, but he knows that a guildmaster's decisions reach beyond the people in the hall. Even a cheap wooden badge can become a memorial to someone he sent into the ground.[^first]

## An overburdened guild

Cassius recruits the Darkest Delvers when they are little more than strangers. He explains the guild's economy without romantic flourishes: the inexperienced scout for a fraction of what trained silver members cost; the successful must still finish their reports, and a dangerous dungeon is not something to claim merely because one has survived its first room. He pays according to contracts and seeks higher approval when the company asks to advance. As their discoveries spread from woodland ruins to public wells and Pelor's temple, Cassius assigns the bronze teams to protect ordinary residents and directs his limited higher ranks toward the most dangerous investigations.[^guild]

## Before the Academy board

His concern eventually drives him and the artificer Carrie Underworld to travel overnight to the great Dungeon Guild Academy. Before its ruling financiers, Cassius requests a force of gold-ranked adventurers, or the funds to hire one, to clear the dungeon below Atomer. Carrie struggles to arrange the recovered horn and psychic shafts as evidence. The officials belittle her standing and ask why the earlier researchers have not delivered results. Cassius protests that the city cannot wait while dark creatures rise through old entrances and his stronger guild members remain occupied.[^hearing]

He also shows how severe he can be toward those he is trying to protect. Asked whether the specially chosen Darkest Delvers are not already sufficient, Cassius complains of Krandal's interest in payments, Koh's desperation to cure his wife and Evelyn's concealed life as a schoolgirl. Yet the words are spoken to an indifferent board, not to his companions; his disapproval does not erase the help he has repeatedly offered them. A white-haired diviner urges him to trust the choice of the young party. Cassius receives no gold-ranked aid. The board directs him toward the coming Bronze Ball and announces that regional director Selenar Everbloom will inspect his guild. He leaves with Carrie to begin the long journey home.[^hearing]

## After the first arc

The Academy's refusal does not change the danger. The researcher it previously funded eventually becomes the center of an attempted sacrifice at Noble Hollow, and the young party Cassius criticized helps prevent it. Several days later Cassius stands beside Selenar as the guild clears the Delvers of wrongdoing and recognizes their service. With guild funds devoted to a northern war against vampires, there is still no extra purse for them. Krandal is instead promised an old tower in the farmland. The Bronze Ball remains to be held, and the Delvers have yet another offer to consider.[^finale]

## A dangerous private commission

The Delvers' report of a recently used underground chamber beneath the Withers estate troubles Cassius in a way that ordinary guild paperwork does not. The wealthy Alexandre Vanderbuck Withers, long a supporter of guild undertakings and a patron of the Bronze Ball, has collected old treasures and taken an interest in Skull Keep. Cassius fears that some of the money paying for civic safety may hide a different purpose. Instead of authorizing a public investigation through the guild's usual board, he retains the Cloven Brotherhood to inspect the estate in secret. A financier calls himself **Suis Sac**, a clumsy reversed cipher for Cassius's own name, and waits to see whether Koh notices.

Koh does. He arrives with a forged job card and a careful question, and Cassius ushers him and his companions into the lecture hall. Here the guildmaster admits he is the patron of the covert operation. He asks the three to join the thieves, investigate Withers's artifacts, and bring back evidence without wearing their guild badges. He offers money from his own pocket for meaningful information and temporarily equips them for quiet movement. He is willing to place them among untrustworthy criminals because he believes delay may cost lives, though his judgment of Withers is suspicion rather than proven fact.[^s2e03]

## Current status

Cassius **remains Atomer guildmaster** and **personally confirms that “Suis Sac” is his deliberately reversible alias as secret financier of the Cloven Brotherhood's planned Withers Estate break-in**. He **receives Koh's real report** of a clean subterranean room with restraints, questions the relationship of Withers's **jade/artifact collection** and **Skull Keep** to darker schemes, but **has not proven Withers guilty or opened the hidden locked door**. He **tasks all three Delvers with an off-books investigation and report**, not an official guild contract or an already completed theft, **offers personal payment for evidence with no fixed sum**, and orders secrecy, aliases and **temporarily loaned Boots of Elvenkind**. A **9:30 p.m. meeting that same night** is scheduled; it has **not occurred**. Silver promotion still **requires an Academy exam and more advancement**, neither awarded now. His previously unspecified “condition” remains unexplained.[^s2e03]
