# Cassius — guildmaster of Atomer

## Introduction

Cassius runs Atomer's [Delvers Dungeon Guild](../places/delvers-guild.md), assigning work, judging discoveries and paying adventurers whose experience often falls short of the dangers awaiting them. Wry, impatient and attentive to procedure, he explains what the guild expects without dressing employment as romance. New recruits must finish reports and honor contracts; surviving a room does not give them ownership of a dungeon. His decisions carry consequences for the people he sends underground.[^cassius-introduction]

### Arc One — Responsibility under pressure

As the Darkest Delvers' discoveries expose danger beneath public wells and [Pelor's temple](../places/temple-of-pelor.md), Cassius directs scarce teams toward protecting residents. He activates reserves, arranges guards and asks the newcomers to help [Carrie](carrie-underworld.md) investigate. Rewards and advancement remain subject to rules and higher approval, even when the young party believes its achievements deserve more. His care for the city operates within an institution he cannot command alone.[^cassius-service]

That limit becomes stark at the [Dungeon Guild Academy](../places/dungeon-guild-academy.md). Cassius asks for gold-ranked help to clear the threat beneath Atomer, but the board dismisses his evidence and directs him toward fundraising. Frustration also exposes his severity toward his own adventurers: he complains about Krandal's interest in payment, Koh's desperation for his wife and Evelyn's concealed school life. These judgments coexist with his repeated help. He argues that the city cannot wait, yet must leave without the force he sought.[^cassius-academy]

After Noble Hollow, he stands beside Selenar Everbloom as the Delvers are cleared of wrongdoing and their service recognized. Limited money still shapes what he can offer. Krandal receives a promise of land rather than another purse of gold; Cassius remains dependent on the institution whose priorities he has challenged.[^cassius-hollow]

### Arc Two — Suspicion and compromised methods

Cassius's concern about [Withers](alexandre-vanderbuck-withers.md) leads him outside ordinary guild procedure. Through the alias **Suis Sac**, he privately finances the Cloven Brotherhood's investigation and recruits the Delvers to enter the estate without their badges. He suspects that a civic benefactor's wealth conceals dangerous dealings. The secrecy reflects distrust of the available public route, but it also places the young adventurers among thieves on the strength of suspicions still unproved.[^cassius-commission]

After the burglary, he receives the recovered evidence and warns them about identifiable stolen property. Protecting his investigators now includes directing them away from town while Carrie studies the artifacts. His support remains practical and morally complicated: the desire to discover a threat has not made the means lawful or established every allegation against Withers.[^cassius-evidence]

Koh's wife's illness gives that support a personal focus. Cassius helps arrange the attempt to free her through psychic contact and Krandal's curse-breaking magic, then confirms the Delvers' approved Silver standing. He also fears disruption at the approaching Bronze Ball. During its catastrophe he catches the afflicted Selenar and carries her as others begin evacuating. The hoped-for fundraiser becomes a disaster he cannot contain.[^cassius-ball]

### Arc Three — Confinement and a return to action

The aftermath removes Cassius from the office he has struggled to keep effective. He is investigated, the Delvers lose access to ordinary guild work, and Zoltar takes his place. Zoltar blames the earlier dream rescue for the disaster while acknowledging a deeper conflict: Cassius wanted Khalazar defeated quickly, whereas the Academy benefits from continued dungeon expeditions. The accusations do not supply a settled account of responsibility.[^cassius-custody]

Public pressure and Koh's confrontation with Zoltar finally lead to Cassius's release from the Wildfire estate. Valentine brings him out alive, and he returns to his desk as Zoltar leaves town. He uses regained authority to fund each of the three Delvers, provide a protective ring for Koh and authorize the rescue campaign. His preparations include assigning Darmah to bring captives home through teleportation circles. The task he could not secure from distant financiers is entrusted to the adventurers whose judgment he once criticized.[^cassius-return]

## Current status

Cassius is last known back in guild leadership, having funded and organized the expedition against Khalazar. His release and practical restoration are established; a formal acquittal and the Academy's later judgment remain unknown. No separate personal outcome is known for him in the year after the rescue.[^cassius-status]
