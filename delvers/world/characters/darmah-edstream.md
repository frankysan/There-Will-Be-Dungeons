# Darmah Edstream — Five Emissaries rescue wizard

<a id="darmah-edstream--five-emissaries-rescue-wizard-s3e11"></a>

## Introduction

Darmah Edstream is a halfling Guild wizard whose fire magic first draws the Delvers' attention during maintenance at a well outside Pelor's temple. She favors a conspicuous, direct approach to the work.[^early]

## Development by arc

### Arc One — An emphatic demonstration

Travelling with another adventuring group, Darmah sends a fireball down the well and declares it clean. The Delvers continue past. Her confidence is evident, although the fate of anything below the flames remains unknown to them.[^early]

### Arc Three — Firepower placed at the service of rescue

Darmah later joins Cassius's expedition as a member of the Five Emissaries. Small beneath trailing robes and a large blue hat, she cheerfully advertises her fireballs, but her assigned responsibility is bringing captives home. She carries materials for two teleportation circles and insists that the magic needs firm, flat ground instead of loose sand. Her practical requirements shape the rescue plan.[^assignment]

She also helps Koh reach the pyramid's upper rooms by enabling him to climb its walls and ceilings. His scouting and the search for suitable ground turn her abilities into a shared means of reaching and recovering prisoners. In the lower workshop she prepares the first circle, sends captives and their carriers to the Guild, and stays for those still inside.[^rescue]

<a id="s3e12-campaign-finale-actually-teleports-captives-and-party-home-fights-khalazar"></a>

The mission draws her into danger beyond that role. Through Koh's mental link, she and Dominic are pulled into Khalazar's vision of his restored city. Darmah strikes the tyrant with two fireballs before his attacks leave her unconscious within the confrontation.[^vision]

After Khalazar's defeat, with the real pyramid collapsing, Darmah announces the second evacuation circle. The remaining rescuers and captives reach the Guild basement. Her promised route home has become the means of escape for both groups.[^escape]

## Current status

Darmah has survived the expedition and completed both evacuations to the Guild. Her later activities are unknown.[^escape]
