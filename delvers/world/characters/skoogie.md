# Skoogie — exiled kobold shaman of Chath Peak

## Introduction

Skoogie is a kobold shaman living apart from the settlement on [Chath Peak](../places/chath-peak.md). A sweeping feathered headdress, dragging cloak, magical bell and caged lizard give the shaman a conspicuous presence. Behind the display lies a grievance: Skoogie says a newly arrived red dragon has taken the villagers' worship and displaced the shaman's influence.[^introduction]

### Arc Two — An alliance to recover lost influence

Skoogie approaches the Delvers with a clear bargain: kill the dragon and leave the kobold community for the shaman to reclaim. Marsh translates the Draconic conversation, and Skoogie supplies more than a demand. An Arrow of Dragon Slaying is removed from the great hat and given to Marsh, while local knowledge opens a concealed route toward the lair.[^bargain]

The alliance makes Skoogie both guide and participant. Evelyn carries the shaman across lava, and the group follows the hidden passage through the old forge to the summit. During the fight, bell magic bolsters the companions and a lightstone helps Evelyn navigate the steam. Dragon fire wounds Skoogie, who receives her healing and remains involved in the effort.[^danger]

The dragon's forced landing brings further injury when its body falls onto the shaman. Evelyn treats Skoogie again, and the eventual defeat of Fredoroth removes the rival at the centre of the bargain. Skoogie leaves alive for the kobold settlement. The desired return to religious authority remains beyond what the Delvers witness.[^outcome]

## Current status

Skoogie survives the dragon's defeat and departs toward the village. The shaman's later reception, influence and relationship with the Delvers are unknown.[^status]
