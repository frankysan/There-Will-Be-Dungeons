# Goggles Granny — visiting elderly catlike alchemist

## Introduction

Goggles Granny is an elderly alchemist exploring the dungeon independently when she meets the Darkest Delvers. Hunched beneath an oversized pack, with cat ears, a tail, small glasses and a scar across one eye, she brings a gruff manner and a formidable store of useful supplies. She says she is trying to save her town.[^identity]

### Arc Three — An independent explorer accepts company

Granny has made her own map and passed earlier puzzles before meeting the trio. She accepts Krandal's potato and joins their efforts in the [black-slime chamber](../places/black-slime-key-chamber.md), where her ancient Elvish helps complete an inscription Koh struggles to read. Koh brings her into the group's whispered mental conversation. Her irritation with younger adventurers coexists with a readiness to contribute expertise.[^explorer]

Her improvisation can be disconcerting for those she helps. After failed attempts to snag a distant ledge with a tethered dagger, she gives Krandal a shrinking potion while letting him expect enlargement. She enlarges herself instead, then throws the smaller cleric between platforms with a rope attached. He is displeased, but her unusual method helps the group work across the chamber.[^alchemy]

Granny is also essential to a more delicate collaboration. Her merchant balance establishes the weight of a heart-bearing vessel on a pressure-sensitive pedestal. Combining her counterweights with Evelyn's stone, she coordinates with Koh to exchange the load without triggering the mechanism.[^vessel]

Her willingness to help has a physical limit. She briefly follows the others toward the catacombs, then finds the descent too exhausting and turns back. Her departure leaves the trio to carry the recovered vessel onward.[^return]

## Current status

Granny returns safely to town. The danger she hoped to avert and the outcome of her own mission remain unknown.[^return]
