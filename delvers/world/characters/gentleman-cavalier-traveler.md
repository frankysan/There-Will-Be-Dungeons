# Gentleman Cavalier Traveler — annual visitor to the Material Plane

<a id="appearance-at-the-bronze-ball-01390215"></a>

## Introduction

The Gentleman Cavalier Traveler enters the Bronze Ball's garden through a thin red line that opens into a triangular portal. He appears as a pale, youthful man with swept blond hair, dark eyes and a clean-shaven face. A top hat, polished black formal clothes, white gloves and a black cape lined in deep red lend his arrival a theatrical elegance. Behind a white eye mask, he bows and kisses [Evelyn's](evelyn-grinkeeper.md) hand; his true name remains unspoken.[^traveler-introduction]

## Development by arc

<a id="evelyn-and-his-curse"></a>

<a id="defense-of-the-ball-02400300"></a>

### Arc Two — Courtesy, attachment and courage

The Traveler recalls meeting Evelyn at another ball two years earlier, when a stolen kiss earned him a slap that also healed him. Their reunion offers a gentler intimacy. He dances with her, throws his mask into a shower of sparkles and accepts a piece of her [friendship ribbon](../items/friendship-ribbon.md), folding it into a pink rose for his lapel. Yet he evades her request for his real name. Affection does not remove the secrecy surrounding him.[^traveler-dance]

He tells Evelyn that a curse allows him only one night each year on the Material Plane. Otherwise, he says, he travels the Astral Plane helping others; his confinement followed an attempt to save minotaurs beneath Atomer from corruption. The account gives Evelyn a reason to seek his freedom, without explaining everything about his past.[^traveler-dance]

When monsters interrupt the Ball, his gallantry becomes action. His cane reveals a narrow fencing blade, and he takes Evelyn's hand to carry her through successive short teleports while they fight and aid endangered guests. Disoriented by the first jump, she accidentally strikes him, then heals him with a squeeze of his hand. They continue as partners in the defense.[^traveler-defense]

<a id="a-new-alleged-curse-lead-s3e05-not-independent-confirmation"></a>

### Arc Three — A promise across the planes

After the attack, the Traveler describes an evil heart beneath Minotaur Mesa and a curse of soul rot or accelerating age, dating his attempt to help the minotaurs to roughly five years before. Evelyn promises to seek an end to it and declines his offered kiss for the time being. He leaves through a red-purple triangular portal, with their hoped-for future still dependent on a freedom neither can yet secure.[^traveler-departure]

Later, a mind-reader at [Deep Winds Spa](../places/deep-winds-spa.md) privately tells Evelyn that killing Khalazar will release him. It is a lead she receives in his absence, rather than knowledge he shares or a demonstrated cure.[^traveler-lead]

## Current status

The Traveler's last witnessed appearance ends with his departure through the portal. After Khalazar's defeat, Evelyn still searches and waits for him during the following year. A later invitation names her and Lord Ethan Travail and bears the signature **GCT**, suggesting a connection without settling the groom's identity. His release, a reunion and a completed marriage remain unconfirmed.[^traveler-status]
