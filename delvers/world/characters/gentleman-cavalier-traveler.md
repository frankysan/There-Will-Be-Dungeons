# Gentleman Cavalier Traveler — annual visitor to the Material Plane

**Working identity:** **Gentleman Cavalier Traveler**, shortened **GCT**; **true name NOT given by Delvers S2E12 cutoff despite Evelyn asking him directly**. Linked to [Evelyn Grinkeeper / Lady Blackwood](evelyn-grinkeeper.md). **Actual, in-person arrival and visually described on-screen in [S2E12 — The Bell of The Ball](../../episodes/S2E12.md)**, not merely a hopeful future visitor. Episode ends the **second story arc**.

## Appearance at the Bronze Ball (~01:39–02:15)

A **thin bright-red vertical line appears in air about seven feet above garden**, opens into a **triangle portal**. Out steps a **youthful (~21-year-old-looking) handsome pale/human-looking male**, **blonde hair swept into lock rising above front forehead**, **dark eyes and NO facial hair**, wearing **BLACK TOP HAT**, **polished formal black tuxedo with shining shoes**, **dramatic large BLACK CAPE LINED DEEP RED**, **white eye mask**, **white gloves** and carrying an elegant **cane**. He tips/tosses cane with flourish and bows, **kisses Evelyn's hand**. During dance he **tosses white mask which EXPLODES INTO SPARKLES** revealing bare youthful face. **Actual race/species, age and planar nature NOT confirmed**; reference dress is **on the specific Bronze Ball evening**, not universal eternal costume. GM uses actor/celebrity analogies for general youthful handsome look, not exact likeness licensing.[^arrival]

## Evelyn and his curse

The Traveler recalls meeting Lady Blackwood around **two years before** at a demon-interrupted ball: he stole a kiss, she **slapped him and healed him**. In S2E12 he **ACTUALLY dances with Evelyn** after her switch from Grinkeeper armor to Lady Blackwood dress. He describes **a curse allowing ONLY ONE NIGHT EACH YEAR on the Material Plane**, and says he otherwise roams the Astral Plane helping people. By his account, the curse followed sacrificing his freedom to save **minotaurs living in a subterranean town below Atomer** from dark corruption; **no divination/independent witness of ancient story or specific culprit**. Evelyn wishes to break curse but **does not actually do so**. She **GIVES him one six-inch piece of magical pink [friendship ribbon](../items/friendship-ribbon.md)**. It coils into **rose shape which he WEARS ON LAPEL**, physical transfer completed, not future promise.[^dance]

## Defense of the ball (~02:40–03:00)

GCT fights a grick with **CANE UNFOLDING/REVEALING A THIN, CURLING FENCING BLADE / rapier**, deftly cuts four facial tendrils and stabs head/beak. Takes Evelyn's hand and **ACTUALLY BLINK-TELEPORTS WITH HER around battlefield**, fighting gricks, hook horrors, darkmantles and rescuing people near **Warden, One Dee Dee, I Spy, Broadside, Solburn, Gruber Lifton**. **Evelyn accidentally strikes GCT when disoriented from first teleport**, then **ACTUALLY heals him for three points through a HAND SQUEEZE**, not her familiar healing slap. They continue defending during the drone crisis. **He does NOT disappear or have annual curse resolved within cutoff**; do not infer later location/return.[^battle]

**Illustration time-variation:** Prior unseen meetings ~two years earlier have **no safely described costume or face**; only S2E12 ballroom/garden look is described. Thin sword in cane appears **later in combat**, not before; mask removed **while dancing**, therefore illustration should consider pre-unmask vs post-unmask timestamp. **No true name**; no actual future return promised as fulfilled.
