# Chuck Abnaz — Nasty of the Cloven Brotherhood

## Introduction

Chuck Abnaz, called Nasty, is a satyr from [Koh Spiros's](koh-spiros.md) criminal past and a representative of the Cloven Brotherhood. He greets Koh with the familiarity of an old associate, praising his forgery and insisting that the Brotherhood remains his family. The welcome carries an expectation of obedience.[^return]

## Development by arc

<a id="the-debt"></a>

### Arc Two — Help made into leverage

During Koh's unexplained absence, Chuck pays the doctors caring for his sleeping wife. On Koh's return, he turns that assistance into a demand for service. Three satyrs emerge when he calls, reinforcing his refusal to accept that Koh has left criminal work. The medical help is real, but it gives Chuck a hold over a household already short of money.[^family]

<a id="a-financier-in-disguise"></a>

He later names a debt of 750 gold and offers to cancel it if Koh helps rob the Withers Estate. He wants Koh's silent communication, with Shrugs supplying burglary skills and Allspice an escape cart. Cassius secretly backs the investigation, while Chuck pursues the valuables. His bargaining converts an old personal connection into a profitable operation.[^recruitment]

From outside the mansion he supplies equipment, follows the crew's progress and urges withdrawal when a servant spots Shrugs. After the escape he leads the Delvers through concealed passages to the Brotherhood's storehouse. His caution protects the organization's refuge even while he welcomes Koh back under an old nickname.[^heist]

The Brotherhood takes the jade and arranges the gem settlement. Chuck offers further work and gives Koh a pan flute for contacting the gang. Payment does not make their relationship a clean separation: Koh's family later reports further contact, renewing his alarm about Chuck's access to them.[^settlement]

### Arc Three — Continued business under conditions

Koh eventually seeks Chuck out again with stolen bank valuables to fence. He offers information about Cassius, Withers and an inquisitor while insisting that the Brotherhood stop approaching his family unexpectedly. The ensuing agreement leaves the connection transactional. The gang promises to leave for Kingdom with the Withers jade.[^fencing]

## Current status

Chuck remains a Brotherhood contact after agreeing to further business with Koh. His promised departure, compliance with the family boundary and the final fencing settlement are unresolved.[^fencing]
