# Chuck Abnaz — Nasty of the Cloven Brotherhood

## Introduction

Chuck Abnaz, sometimes called **Nasty**, is a satyr from Koh Spiros's past. He moves with the easy familiarity of someone who has long believed himself part of the same family of outcasts and forgers. His words are friendly enough to begin with, yet his companions remain hidden until he summons them, and his requests carry the weight of a man who does not believe anyone may simply leave the organization he represents.[^return]

## The debt

When Koh returns from Snake Island after two unexplained weeks, Chuck waits for him on a rooftop near the family home. He greets Koh as the finest forger the **Cloven Brotherhood** ever knew and remarks that the guild must pay well now that the satyr has chosen honest work. Koh insists that his criminal days are finished. Chuck smiles at this declaration but refuses to let it end the conversation. Koh's family had run short of funds for his sleeping wife's medical care during his absence, and Chuck says he has paid the doctors. The help must be repaid, he says, and the Brotherhood needs Koh's services again.

Three other satyrs emerge from the shadows when Chuck calls. They do not attack, but the threat is plain. Chuck promises another visit and speaks of payment due for his care of the family. At home, Koh learns that the doctors were indeed paid and his daughter remains well. He can take comfort in the latter fact without trusting the motives of the man who helped them.[^family]

## A financier in disguise

Chuck returns as Koh is spending rare quiet days with his family. This time he no longer simply hints at a debt. He claims that Koh owes seven hundred and fifty gold pieces for the care provided during the forgotten Snake Island weeks, but he will forget the balance if Koh helps with a profitable burglary. A giant subterranean beast has opened a way beneath the house of the wealthy Withers family. Chuck knows that a trove of valuable jade lies within, or believes it does, and he plans to enter while the damage remains unrepaired.

He has recruited a burglar named **Shrugs** and a centaur getaway driver called **Allspice**. Koh's talent for speaking silently to several people would make him an excellent coordinator. The financier behind the scheme has the peculiar name **Suis Sac**; Koh recognizes the disguise as Cassius reversed and soon obtains the guildmaster's own confirmation. The scale of the predicted haul tempts Koh, but Chuck's promise to leave him alone is already compromised by the threat that future gifts might again make him useful.[^s2e03]

## Current status

Chuck Abnaz/Nasty **ACTUALLY directs the S2E04 Withers Estate burglary** from an Allspice-driven cart outside the building, coordinating **Koh's Psychic Whispers** with Koh, guest burglar **Shrugs** and Evelyn (not Krandal). He provides **two Bags of Holding and three Scrolls of Silence** for the operation, warns the group as the owner approaches, and dispatches **Allspice to get everyone out**. The crew **successfully infiltrates, steals 23 portable jade pieces plus unappraised gemstones and assorted objects, and escapes in the cart**, even though a servant sees Shrugs. **No distribution of treasure, Koh's promised payment, cancellation of Chuck's claimed 750-gp debt, formal reconciliation or post-escape conversation is played**. Cassius's role as off-books financier is confirmed from S2E03, but Chuck's profit motive does not prove that any criminal scheme by Withers existed.[^s2e04][^s2e03]
