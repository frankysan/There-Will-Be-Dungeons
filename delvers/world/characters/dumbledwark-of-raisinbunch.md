# Dumbledwark of Raisinbunch — visiting bard and dungeon correspondent

**First played appearance:** [Delvers S3E03 — Thank You For The Deposit](../../episodes/S3E03.md). **Performer: Bo Schwartz** (spoken cast introduction; sometimes spelled Beau elsewhere). Character name **Dumbledwark of Raisinbunch** is **ASR-provisional**: alternate segmentations include “Dumbledewalk/Dumbledwalk of Raisin Bunch,” and he distinguishes his name from a similarly named person **of Lake's End (?)**. **Guest/visiting character, not automatically a permanent fourth Darkest Delver**.

## Appearance and equipment (S3E03)

He describes himself as an **elderly ~3½-foot halfling** with **large beard**, hair spilling under a **top hat**, **small darting eyes**, brownish **age/liver spots**, wrinkled skin, and several missing teeth. Wears **leather jerkin, dungarees and worn boots**, smokes a **cigar**, carries a **backpack, duffel and lute**. A veteran **wartime correspondent** and former adventurer, now a **bard** and specialist in reporting heroic expeditions. His comic identity is built around chronic hunger, sore knees, frequent second meals, extravagantly old-fashioned journalistic etiquette and receipts for expenses. **Do not invent facial scars, tattoos, precise hair color, monocle or weaponry**.

He animates a **large wood/glass camera with moving crystal elements** out of his duffel using the Bard **Animating Performance** class feature. The camera floats near his shoulder, crystal optics align, and its exposure creates a large rainbow flash. **Photographical capture is played; immediate printed/developed plates and publication are not**. Photographed the editor, **Koh**, assembled finishing-school students and **Krandal**; **did not photograph Evelyn** on this visit. Holds or is seen using a **lute, pen/quill, journal, marked expenses, Gloves of Thievery**, **Zone of Truth**, **Detect Magic**, and persuasive/deceptive interviewing skills. **Speak with Animals considered but not cast**, Identify not cast; no automatic attribution of other possible spell list.

## Investigation, ethics and custody

Hired by editor **James “Chroma” Kramerson (?)** at **The Daily Quest**, on retainer/publication terms, to investigate why the three young Silver Delvers remain barred from missions despite gossip and an **unverified prophecy** supposedly purchased from a ratlike guild insider. Reporter first visits Koh in his newly purchased small house (and meets awake wife **Anathala**), persuades a reluctant Koh to a photo but **not Zone of Truth**, then interviews Evelyn at her finishing school (receiving a hint about Cassius/Selenar relationship), then Krandal at his productive potato farm.

At farm gives Krandal **an actually commissioned gilded-potato trophy**, takes Krandal's photograph, and obtains **voluntary signed consent before actually casting Zone of Truth**. Under the spell, **Krandal discloses that Koh took bank money**, journalist now personally knows this. Krandal **refuses** to help coerce Koh into follow-up magical questioning. The in-character reporter requests access to farm restroom and **actually uses Krandal's compost box**, source of the episode's title. This establishes the comedy beat but **does not prove future fertilizer effectiveness**.

At Chimera Guild reporter declines ratlike **Jimmy the Soup's implicit bribe**, gains interview with temporary guildmaster **Zoltar** in Cassius's former office, covertly spots a magical warlock's tome via Detect Magic, and gets Zoltar to **openly describe profitable incentives to prolong the Khalazar dungeon**—separate from Zoltar's unsupported accusations of guilt against Delvers and Cassius. The reporter **proposes controlled public heroic “victories”** and agrees to keep further exchanges covert; **actual story publication, ethical future conduct and detailed agent agreement not yet performed**.

**Actual custody at S3E03 cutoff:** **Dungeon C4 licence physically handed to Dumbledwark**, and he **ACTUALLY STEALS a [magical warlock's tome](../items/stolen-warlocks-tome.md)** from boxes in Zoltar-occupied former Cassius office, hides it under his photographic equipment in duffel. The book is **not identified/decoded**, nor is Cassius proven former owner. Zoltar **promises to release the Delvers from grounding next day** and let Dumbledwark chaperone a planned C4 expedition; **neither party notification nor actual dungeon entrance has occurred**. Future visits by centaur rickshaw **Allspice** arranged; next scene not yet played.
