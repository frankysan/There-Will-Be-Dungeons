# Dumbledwark of Raisinbunch — visiting bard and dungeon correspondent

## Introduction

Dumbledwark of Raisinbunch is an elderly halfling bard and veteran war correspondent hired by The Daily Quest to investigate the Darkest Delvers' suspension. Beneath his top hat and beard is a reporter of restless appetite, aching knees and elaborate professional manners. His floating crystal camera makes the gathering of photographicals part of his performance.[^identity]

### Arc Three — Access, compromise and field reporting

Dumbledwark pursues access through courtesy, gifts and persistence. He visits Koh at home, Evelyn at school and Krandal at the farm, collecting interviews and pictures while keeping careful receipts. Koh resists truth magic; Krandal signs consent before Dumbledwark casts it. Krandal's account reveals Koh's theft of bank money, but the cleric refuses to help pressure his friend into another interview. The reporter gains knowledge without securing unlimited cooperation.[^interviews]

His professional restraint has sharp limits. He declines to buy Jimmy the Soup's inside information, then draws Zoltar into admitting the dungeon's value as a continuing source of profit. Dumbledwark proposes managed public victories that preserve that economy. While arranging a licensed expedition, he also steals an unidentified warlock's tome from the occupied Guild office and hides it beneath his photographic equipment.[^bargain]

<a id="s3e04-photographicals-in-c4"></a>

In the dungeon, his curiosity becomes practical assistance. He identifies Krandal's boots, questions moss and the dead, and discovers Iron Monkey alive where the missing adventurer has hidden in dung. He willingly adopts the same filthy concealment. During the behir's attack he keeps taking pictures and inspires his companions, maintaining his role as correspondent amid real danger.[^field]

<a id="s3e10-book-advertisements-and-sealed-cassius-location-letter"></a>

His later reporting reaches beyond the expedition. Advertisements announce an exposé called *A Prison City*. A sealed letter from The Daily Quest gives Koh the location of Cassius's confinement at the Wildfire estate; Cassius's eventual release there confirms that particular lead.[^later]

## Current status

Dumbledwark's book has been advertised as forthcoming, but whether it reaches readers is unknown. His own later whereabouts are unestablished, and the stolen tome's contents remain unresolved.[^later]
