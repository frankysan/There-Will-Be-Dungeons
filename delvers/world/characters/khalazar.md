# Khalazar — the Undying Majesty

## Creator-confirmed race and visual design (BONUS-2021-11-10, ~22:25–24:25)

Kyle explicitly says OUT OF GAME that **Khalazar's ancient underground faction and the Dolbluth/Dobluth opposing culture are both his versions of “DROW.”** He visualizes **PALE-WHITE / GHOSTLIKE subterranean elves** drawing on *Hellboy*-like elf aesthetics, deliberately **without standard spider-queen worship and without simply applying all conventional D&D dark-skinned drow imagery**. This is unusually important for future visual character/civilization studies: **producer-verified ART / RACIAL INSPIRATION**, not an in-character racial declaration by everyone involved or support for a common Wastes universe. The cast briefly compares drow/draugr/Skyrim sounding words; **not a claim the civilizations are Norse undead or literally Skyrim creatures**.
