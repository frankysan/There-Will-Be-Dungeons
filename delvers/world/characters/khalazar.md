# Khalazar — the Undying Majesty

## Introduction

Khalazar is an ancient ruler known first through damaged texts and titles, including the Undying Majesty and Lord of the Black Basilica. Carrie's translations describe an underground dominion from which the Dolbluth broke away. His identity initially reaches the Delvers through fragments, interpretation and the experiences of captive minds.[^history]

## Development by arc

<a id="texts-and-unverified-old-history"></a>
<a id="anthelas-captive-wedding"></a>

### Arc Two — A ruler's claim upon a captive mind

Within her psychic prison, Anthela believes she must marry Khalazar to protect Atomer and her daughter. Her memory of Koh repeatedly slips away. The proposed marriage expresses a claim of possession: the emperor demands a bride whose consent has been shaped by fear and forgetting. Keeves, another captive, believes himself a guard for the wedding.[^captivity]

<a id="s2e10-dream-projection--first-form"></a>
<a id="battle-and-second-form"></a>

When Krandal breaks Anthela's curse, Khalazar appears upon a great bat, pale and porcelain-faced beneath long white hair and purple-and-gold finery. He demands her return and calls Koh a Dolbluth warrior. Resistance strips away that grandeur: blows crack his shell, releasing black sand, and he rises again as a towering, wrapped figure with a luminous maw. He answers defiance with stunning words, insects and necrotic magic.[^apparition]

<a id="s2e11-gm-clarifies-defeated-form-as-a-shade"></a>

The Delvers free Anthela and shatter the apparition's red core, but a threatening voice promises another meeting. The defeated presence proves to be a shade, leaving Khalazar's power beyond the dream unresolved.[^shade]

<a id="s2e12-present-voice-and-citywide-active-drone--not-a-confirmed-physical-body"></a>

At the Bronze Ball, his voice speaks through unwilling hosts and again calls Anthela to him. The Hereditary Drone draws afflicted elves toward the opening beneath the hall. His reach has survived the loss of the shade, while Koh's continuing mental reassurance helps Anthela retain herself.[^ball]

### Arc Three — The body behind the promised return

Accounts of Khalazar's restoration draw attention to the blood of the Dolbluth and organs preserved in urns. The aboleth claims he seeks ancestral blood; the All Rat claims power from one organ. Shaolin Nym identifies him as an emperor who betrayed the Seldarine and instructs the Delvers to reassemble him before ending his return. These accounts offer a means of confronting the ruler whose influence persists beyond his shade.[^restoration]

<a id="s3e11-gate-opened-huge-statue-and-immobile-gilded-figure-no-actual-encounter-with-awake-khalazar"></a>

Beyond the gate, his dominion rests upon captives and machinery. Enslaved workers build a colossal statue bearing his face, fed by black ichor. Above them lies a desiccated, ornamented body attended by Keeves and Hissia. The commanding figure of the dream has a motionless counterpart, dependent on what the urns contain.[^body]

<a id="s3e12-final-reconstituted-and-actually-destroyed-third-arc-and-entire-campaign-end"></a>

Restoring the organs brings a shared vision of the young emperor's persecution, destroyed shrines and rebellion. Khalazar then confronts the Delvers amid an apparently renewed city, once more presenting himself as a restored sovereign. His attacks nearly kill Krandal and Evelyn and overwhelm Darmah, but the allies keep fighting.[^final-battle]

Koh's strike breaks the city's illusion. Back in the real bedchamber, Koh, Krandal and Evelyn together drive a psychic dagger into Khalazar's heart. It bursts, and he disintegrates. The pyramid collapses as the surviving rescuers escape; the unfinished statue never rises to defend its maker.[^destruction]

## Current status

Khalazar is destroyed. His hold over the captives is broken, the ten cursed minotaurs settle into inert bones, and Noble Hollow becomes fertile again. His threatened restoration has ended in defeat.[^aftermath]
